Dispositions from reviewing #2221 v9 (specialized-corpus re-mine of under-yielded families) under the Alternative Explanations lens. All four were RECOVERABLE (APPROVE + analyzer bullets), but each recurs in any "repair the grid, reuse the comparison stratum" round:

1. **Cross-family mix-size/dose confound is structural to standing-cell reuse.** When some cells are reused at parent N and others re-mined, the cross-family dose gradient cannot be designed out without retraining; it is analyzer-weighable iff the plan arms: rows+tokens covariate, trait-agnostic random-direction ladder under IDENTICAL selection, a within-family dose-matched read, and family-grain LOFO. Check all four are inherited for the REPAIRED grid, not just cited from the parent.
2. **A within-suite instrument repair silently breaks the cross-stratum "identical instrument" claim.** #2221 v9 fixed the eval cap (1000→2048) for all own-suite cells but the reused #778 synthetic stratum's y stays realized @1000 — the H3 real-vs-synthetic contrast then compares y under two caps while the plan asserts "identical instrument" (it matched only judge draws/temp/rollouts). Quantification lever produced BY DESIGN: the standing cells get paired same-adapter y@old-cap (parent JSON) vs y@new-cap (this round) — read that pairing to bound the instrument effect before narrating the cross-stratum gap as provenance.
3. **Two families mined from the SAME specialized corpus + same rollout panel** (AITA → sycophancy AND mistake_opinions) can share training rows and domain → family-cluster dependence; family-grain LOFO understates it. Weighable cheaply: row-ID overlap % between the two mixes (persisted), and a LOFO refold treating them as one super-family (pure recomputation).
4. **A below-DROP-floor yield on a specialized corpus does not license "real-world scarcity" narration.** Panel-model alignment (RLHF'd models reluctant to produce flawed/sycophantic rows at severity) and rubric strictness are alternatives; the band-score distribution (near-miss mass just under the severe cut) is the discriminator the analyzer should read first.

**Why:** these are the four alternatives that survive a well-armed amendment plan — the plan's own diagnostics catch the parent's headline confound, so the residual review value is at the seams the amendment itself creates (instrument fix vs reused stratum; new rubrics vs new corpora; shared-corpus family pairs).

**How to apply:** any amendment/re-mine round that (a) reuses a scored comparison stratum while changing its own measurement instrument, (b) mines ≥2 families from one corpus, or (c) pre-registers a scarcity/impossibility reading of a yield failure.
