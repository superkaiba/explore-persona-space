Two mechanical facts from the #2476 k200 fold (2026-08-24):

1. `verify_task_body.py` check 20 hard-FAILs any `## Takeaways` bullet at
   **≥100 words** ("accreted paragraph-bullet — split or tighten"); the
   30-word threshold is only a WARN. Budget fold bullets to <100 words
   from the start. Result-prose hard cap remains <180 (alt text + the
   what-is-plotted line count; blockquote captions and tables do not —
   shortening alt text and moving scope clauses into captions are
   legitimate levers).

**Why:** the first draft failed with 2 bullets at 110/123 words and three
results at 211-226; three trim passes were needed.

**How to apply:** when a round's plan pins a "registered lattice/floor/
verdict" (the #2476 shape), rewrite to "plan-fixed floor", "the round's
declared two-outcome rule", "declared threshold" BEFORE drafting — the
discipline audit bans bare `registered <noun>` (see
[[revision-word-caps-and-prereg-token]]); scan quotes copied from plan/
marker text too (they carry "registered"/"BIT-EXACT" wording — write
"delta exactly 0").
