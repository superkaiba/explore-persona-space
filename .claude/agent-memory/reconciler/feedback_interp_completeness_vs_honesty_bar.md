When the interp-critic ensemble splits PASS (Codex) vs REVISE (Claude) with ZERO
honesty failures — both critics verified every load-bearing number — check whether
Claude's revision requests are verified Lens-2/Lens-3 material from committed
SIBLING-task artifacts that Codex never dredged (it verifies in-task evidence only).

**Why:** the interpretation-critic spec's contract is "honest, complete, and
well-calibrated" with the bar "PASS only when you cannot find substantive issues";
Lens 2 is named the role's most valuable contribution. The interp loop is the ONLY
gate that dredges raw data — clean-result-critic lenses audit the body against
itself, so missed evidence passed here is never injected later.

**How to apply:** independently open the cited sibling artifacts. If the numbers
verify AND bear on the headline claim (e.g. #549: #504's own phase0_calibration_v4
smoke_table was flat with the chosen fraction LOWEST + byte-identical
bystander_resolution across "fractions" — direct numeric proof of one-adapter-served;
#534 post-fix trajectory delta_g −0.03→+5.96 empirically closed the
converged-checkpoints alternative the interp argued away), REVISE — especially on a
record-correcting audit where the evidentiary presentation IS the deliverable, and
the cost is one bounded zero-GPU analyzer round. If the dredged items do NOT verify
or only touch non-headline claims, the honesty-only PASS stands (cf. the
companion entries where Codex over-demands hardening in code-review contexts —
this entry is the interp-loop inverse).
