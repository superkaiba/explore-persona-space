Two round-3+ prompt framings the orchestrator brief supplied and endorsed (issue-2389 round-3 compose):

1. **Ownership note.** When a blocker under verification originated from the CODEX twin's own prior round (reconciler/union adopted its tightening over the Claude critic's PASS), say so explicitly in the composed prompt: "this was YOUR blocker; check whether your own prescribed fix was implemented faithfully, including its fallback shape — do not grade it leniently because it is yours, and do not move the goalposts beyond what the work order prescribed." Both halves matter: leniency and goalpost-moving are the two failure modes of self-verification.
2. **PASS-is-expected framing.** By round 3 the composed bar states that a PASS is "a legitimate and EXPECTED outcome — do not manufacture a blocker to seem useful," and the New-blockers template's empty form reads "None — the expected round-3 outcome."

**Why:** the brief for #2389 r3 mandated both, matching the round-2 shape memory ([[v2-round2-verification-prompt-shape]]); without the PASS framing late-round twins invent low-value blockers, and without the ownership note a twin either rubber-stamps or escalates its own prior item.

**How to apply:** any verification-mode round (round 2+) where the work order attributes an upheld blocker to the Codex twin, and any round ≥3 bar paragraph. Also worked well: inlining the ENTIRE unioned work order verbatim with a SCOPE NOTE ("S-*/E-* sections are sibling lenses' — included ONLY so you do not re-raise settled/rejected items") instead of excerpting — verbatim fidelity plus explicit do-not-relitigate grounding (the reconciler-REJECTED efficiency item stays visible with its rejection evidence).
