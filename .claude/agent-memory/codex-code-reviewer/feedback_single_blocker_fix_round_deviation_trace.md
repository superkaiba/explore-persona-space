From #2336 r4 (2026-08-24), a single-blocker fix round after a binding
`epm:review-reconcile` FAIL that the Codex twin's own r3 blocker caused:

1. **Deviation-justification = a decidable trace duty, never an accepted or
   rejected fact.** The reconciler permitted "preferred `_run_files_mode`
   drive OR minimum `wl._files_scope_rel(<abs path>)`"; the implementer took
   the minimum, justifying that the runner's payload-loop `normpath` would
   CLEAN the `./`-drift and defeat the mutation gate. Compose it as a
   REQUIRED header line (`**Preferred-form availability:**
   DEFEATED-BY-NORMPATH | AVAILABLE — <file:line>`) with a sharp trace
   procedure (does any normalization act DOWNSTREAM of the mutated producer
   call? would runner-driven scope keys reach the consumer cleaned or
   drifted?) and BOTH-WAY routing: defeated => deviation sanctioned, no
   finding; available => substantive finding graded by consequence
   (acceptance gate independently met => lost-coverage + report-accuracy,
   presumptively Major, not auto-Critical). Also fold in the reconciler's
   OTHER stated reason for the preferred form (input-branch coverage) as a
   disclosed-vs-undisclosed-residual question. **Why:** the composer must
   not resolve the claim itself (my own sed window showed no normpath near
   the producer call — tempting, but the full-function read is Codex's);
   handing it un-shaped invites prose acceptance.
2. **Stdlib-only probe carve-out needs explicit PRECEDENCE wording** when

   no writes, not pytest/ruff/workflow_lint), with the `STATIC (env
   unavailable)` never-fabricate fallback — placed INSIDE the duty, naming
   the adaptations bullet it overrides.

   `pin-sweep-supplement-scope` (CONCERN) and Codex's own
   `gate-scope-grep-supplement-incomplete` (NIT) were reconciler-recorded as
   the same finding in two rows. Give BOTH status lines, re-emit NEITHER,
   and say the pairing explicitly in the ledger preface + the
   Prior-concerns header line ("one finding in two rows, riding #<task>") —
   else the twin re-adjudicates its own NIT as a fresh item.
