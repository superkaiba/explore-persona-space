1. **Reconciler-FAIL closure at the cap = three verbatim envelopes.** When the
   brief mandates inlining BOTH prior verdicts AND the binding reconciler
   disposition as acceptance contracts, the prompt necessarily exceeds the
   task's prior sizes (210KB vs r9's 187KB; r6-r9 ran 144/151/162/187KB, all
   dispatched clean). Raise the size self-cap rather than truncating a
   verbatim acceptance contract; the trim candidate of last resort is the
   PASS-side verdict (its substance is summarized in the reconciler's
   adjudication table), but only on an actual refusal/latency failure.
2. **No-relitigate splits into READING vs IMPLEMENTATION.** A reconciler that
   adopts a specific reading (here: NARROW "stratum" = the two registered
   necessity strata; single missing key stays warn-only) is SETTLED — the twin
   reviews whether the code MATCHES the reading, never the reading itself.
   Say both halves explicitly or the twin re-argues its own upheld Major's
   original broader frame.
3. **Grain-judgment closures get a multi-way closure line, not binary.** For a
   gate/predicate whose grain was the adjudicated question, demand ONE
   `GATE-GRAIN:` line with four exits: MATCHES-RECONCILER-READING (must say
   why the negative control proves not-over-broad AND why the joint bar is
   not un-fireable) | TOO-BROAD (name the plan-REPORTED case it halts) |
   TOO-NARROW (name the missed elimination) | UN-FIREABLE (structural
   reason). Both error directions are failures; the un-fireable case is the
   degenerate too-narrow and needs its own name or the twin never checks
   reachability.
4. **The leftover assert is the real enumeration guard.** Despite the r8
   lesson (enumerate bracket families, don't token-grep), the Fit-loop
   batching `[Round-9 note: ...]` — embedded mid-paragraph in Step 2, not
   under a step heading — was missed AGAIN and caught only by the
   `assert "[Round-9" not in proto` leftover check. Keep that assert in
   every splice script; grep `\[Round-<n-1>` over the SOURCE prompt first
   and count against the replacement list.

   verdict had the HEAD tag stripped but KEPT the close tag + session
