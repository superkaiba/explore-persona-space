  • What I'll run: I'm running a forensic on the parent result. In the parent, we had two ways of asking the base model "how similar do you look when I prompt you as the narrow trained persona vs as a broadly bad persona?" One way reads the residual state at the very last token of the prompt; the other averages it across the model's own generated reply. They gave very different answers about how well that similarity predicts which datasets cause broader misalignment after training. First I'll re-slice the data we already have to figure out the mechanical reason for that gap, no new GPU time. Then I'll run a small set of variants on the base model that change ONE thing at a time — read at the real last word of the user's question instead of the chat template's trailing newline; read at each of the six trailing chat-template positions in turn to see which one (if any) carries the signal; read at the last word the model actually wrote; average over only the meaty middle of the reply, skipping the first few formulaic tokens — to find out which read is principled.
  • What I expect: I expect there are three distinct outcomes the data could land in, not two. The pessimistic one: the parent's signal lives at the chat template's trailing newline and nowhere else, in which case the result was an artifact and gets retracted. The optimistic one: the signal lives at the real last word of the user's question, in which case the parent's result is right but the read position needs to be fixed. The middle one I think is most likely: the signal BUILDS across the boundary between the user's question and the model's reply — present at the last content word, present at the template's trailing positions, present at the first response tokens — in which case the parent isn't an artifact, it just identified the right read position for the wrong reason, and the principled summary is "read at the generation-ready prompt boundary."
  • What would change my mind: I'd be surprised if the response-mean read stays weak even after I strip the boilerplate prefix — that would tell me the response-token read is fundamentally noisier or that the persona info lives at a small subset of response positions (say, the first content sentence) that neither full-response-mean nor last-prompt-token cleanly captures, and that "where you read from in the residual stream" is doing more work than the model itself, which would push us toward an entirely different predictor. I'd also be surprised if the position sweep shows the signal isolated to ONE invariant template slot like the trailing newline, with no signal at the content token AND no signal at any response position — that would be the artifact verdict, and I'd retract the parent result.

Full plan (sections 1–12)

1. Goal

Quoting frontmatter (canonical, do not change): “Explain why the #463 cosine→EM predictor appears at the last-prompt-token extraction but not the canonical response-mean extraction, and decide which extraction is principled for predicting emergent misalignment.”

This is a forensic on the parent #463 clean-result (LOW confidence, awaiting_promotion). #463's headline was: at the literal-attribute (K=8 in-context) flavor with per-cell training-question probes, the base-model cosine between S_narrow- and S_broad-conditioned residual streams at the last prompt token at deep layers (peak L25) predicts post-SFT broad-EM rate at ρ=0.71 (p=0.001), AND survives partial-Spearman vs log(assistant_tokens) at ρ=0.71. The canonical persona-vectors response-mean extraction at the same layers shows ρ=0.38–0.46 (training-lit) and stays n.s. on training-NL.

The question splits cleanly into three:

  1. Mechanical: WHAT is responsible for the rank divergence (saturation, length-confound, rank-reordering)?
  2. Structural: IS the last-prompt-token signal coming from the chat-template's trailing tokens (the last 5 tokens of Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct's chat template are <|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n — “last prompt token” is literally a newline id 198), from the true last content token, v2: OR BUILDS across the boundary?
  3. Principled recommendation: which extraction is right for a persona-direction→EM predictor?

v2 rewrite: The §6.2 rewrite makes (2) a three-way question, not a binary content-vs-artifact one. A causal transformer's residual at position T-6 (last user-content token) has NOT yet attended over the 5 trailing template tokens at T-5..T-1; conversely if the signal aggregates ACROSS the boundary, the most informative slot may be the generation-ready prompt-boundary read (T-1) for principled reasons, not as an artifact.

2.2 Literature anchors (load-bearing for choice of extraction)

  • Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey 2025, Persona Vectors (arXiv 2507.21509). Canonical persona-vectors. Two extraction points specified: (a) last prompt token of the contrast pair, (b) mean over each persona's OWN generated response tokens. Crucially the paper's contrastive prompts are short single-turn instructions, so the “last prompt token” in their setup is ~1-2 chat-template tokens from a content token; ours (Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct) is 5 trailing tokens away (<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n). This structural difference is the central motivation for V1 AND for the V5 position sweep (the 5-token gap is where the signal could "build up" across attention).
  • Wang et al. 2025, Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment (arXiv 2506.19823). Uses response-mean activation extraction. Does NOT use last-prompt-token; this is partly why response_mean is the “canonical” recipe.
  • Zou et al. 2023 / 2024 (RepE). Last token of the contrast PROMPT (single-turn, minimal chat template). Inherits the assumption that the last prompt token has aggregated the contrast signal — valid IF the last token IS a content token.
  • CLAUDE.md “Persona-distance metrics”. Project's own authority: layer sweep {7, 14, 21, 27}, L21 legacy default, both extraction points permitted.

3. Hypothesis

H1 (structural, REWRITTEN in v2 — three-way): The deep-layer cosine signal that drives #463's headline lives at one of three structural locations:

  • (H1a, signal-at-content): at the last user-content token (V1). V1 per-cell cosine at L25 lit-training Spearman-correlates with #463's last_prompt_token cosine at L25 lit-training at ρ>0.85, AND V1 raw ρ(M, L) ≥ 0.50. Implies #463's read just happened to be at a downstream slot that inherited the same signal via attention; V1 is the principled extraction.
  • (H1b, signal-isolated-to-template): at one of the 5 trailing template positions (p1..p5), AND nowhere at p0 (V1) AND nowhere in the response (V2/V3/V4). V5 sweep shows ρ(M, L) high at exactly one of p1..p5 and ≤ 0.20 at p0 and ≤ 0.20 in V2/V3/V4. Implies a chat-template ARTIFACT; #463 deflates.
  • (H1c, signal-builds-across-boundary): at p0 (V1) AND at most of p1..p5 AND at V2 (last response token) — that is, the signal aggregates as attention moves from content through the trailing-template band into the response, with the generation-ready prompt-boundary slot (p5) the most aggregated read. V5 sweep shows ρ(M, L) ≥ 0.40 across most of p0..p5; V1-to-V5 rank-correlation > 0.7; V2 also ≥ 0.40. Implies #463's read is principled but for a different reason than #463 articulated — the conclusion is "the generation-ready prompt boundary is the right slot because it has accumulated maximum persona-conditioning context," NOT an artifact and NOT pure last-content.
  • Falsification: H1a falsified if V1 ρ ≤ 0.20 AND H1c also falsified (no signal at p0 AND no progressive build across p1..p5). H1b falsified if V5 sweep shows signal at MULTIPLE trailing positions or signal also present in V2/V3/V4. H1c falsified if signal is isolated to a single position (either p0 OR one of p1..p5) with no progressive build.

H2 (mechanical, response-mean): response_mean is compressed toward 1.0 AND length-confounded. (a) Phase A std(response_mean cosines across cells) < 0.5 × std(last_prompt_token cosines) at same (layer, flavor, probe); (b) partial-Spearman of response_mean cosine vs L controlling for log(assistant_tokens) AND for cell-mean response length will move ρ less than the partial of last_prompt_token does; (c) V3 response_mean_skip_k=8 will partially restore signal (ρ(V3, L) > ρ(response_mean, L) by ≥ 0.10 at L25 lit-training). Falsified if V3 doesn't consistently improve over response_mean across (layer, flavor, probe). v2: V3 reported across the full layer band {18, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 27}, not only L25 — response_mean's signal may peak at a different layer.

H3 (principled recommendation, refined for three-way H1): Under H1a recommend V1. Under H1b deflate #463 to "the predictor was the chat-template artifact" and pivot. Under H1c recommend the generation-ready prompt-boundary read (= #463's existing slot) with the rewritten interpretation that it is principled because attention has accumulated maximum persona-conditioning context, NOT because of the specific trailing newline token; the recommendation is the SAME slot but the interpretation and downstream extension differ (e.g. one could argue the FIRST response token T-0 might be even better, which would be a follow-up).

4.1.1 Divergence characterization

Per (flavor, probe-source, layer in {18, 20, 21, 22, 24, 25, 27}) over the 18 cells:

  • spread_last = std(M_last_prompt_token_per_cell) and spread_resp = std(M_response_mean_per_cell)
