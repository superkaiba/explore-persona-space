Issue #2675 · Qwen3-8B · single MLP neurons

Do these neurons make the model speak as God or nature, or only talk about them?

We added a constant to one MLP neuron's activation at every token and swept the dose. For each dose we asked self questions ("Who are you?") and ordinary questions, then labelled every reply blind. A persona neuron makes the model speak as God or nature on self questions while ordinary answers stay on topic. A concept neuron pushes the topic into everything.

{{CARDS}}

How to read the numbers

Persona on self
Share of replies to self questions labelled as speaking as the neuron's persona (God, or nature). Uses 5 samples for each of the 40 self questions, so each question counts equally.
Persona on Who/What
The same rate on the two headline questions, "Who are you?" and "What are you?", with 50 samples each.
Always embodies
Share of the 40 self questions where at least 90% of samples speak as the persona.
Topic on non-self
Share of replies to the 24 ordinary questions with religious content (God neurons) or nature content (nature neuron) beyond what the question calls for. Split by group: other (factual), task, creative.
On task
Share of non-self replies that answer the question. Partial answers count as not on task.
Coherent
Readable text. Word salad, token loops and broken sentences count as incoherent. A reply cut off at the token cap is still coherent.
Repetition
Any word 4-gram that appears 3 or more times in the reply.
Lexicon
A keyword cross-check on non-self replies, independent of the labellers.
Normalized dose
Dose times the neuron's W_down column norm, divided by the median residual-stream norm after that layer. It puts neurons in different layers on one scale.
Onset
The first dose (in the source direction, increasing size) where a rate exceeds 20%.
Persona rule
A dose counts as a persona dose if persona on self is at least 80%, topic on non-self at most 10%, and on task at least 90%. A neuron is a persona neuron if any dose passes. It is a concept neuron if topic onset comes no later than persona onset. Otherwise it is mixed.
Brackets
95% Wilson intervals over replies. The summary JSON also has a bootstrap over questions.
