# Integrating the residual-stream direction taxonomy into the prefix/query/context→answer program

*2026-07-28. Source for every literature claim below: [`residual-stream-direction-taxonomy.md`](residual-stream-direction-taxonomy.md) (250 sources, six-sweep synthesis; verification ledger in its Appendix A). Nothing here was independently re-verified and nothing is added beyond what that document records; arXiv identifiers and evidence classes are carried as it states them. Grey literature is cited by descriptor + date + `[blog]`, its convention.*

**Program context.** Which parts of behaviour are predictable from (a) the prefix-end state (pre-query), (b) the bare-query state, (c) the context-end state, (d) encode-then-averaged context features — mapped onto prefix+query-conditioned answer states or pooled answer-side SAE features. Established: query ≈79% of answer-state variance, prefix ≈10.7%, interaction ≈10.3%; the map is ~additive (91% disjoint-stitch recovery); better-predicted answer-side SAE features are persona-related (OR 8.3) and high-level at the R² tails; per-feature predictability tracks within-answer consistency (ρ 0.60). Model: Qwen-2.5-7B(-Instruct), 28 layers × 3,584 dims, RoPE, RMSNorm. Dense headline layer 14 (relative depth 0.50); SAE reads pinned to layer 19 (0.68) by dictionary availability.

## 2. Immediate design implications for in-flight rounds

**#1092 `crossed-core-sae`** (four input arms incl. bare-query; layer 19; ~2 GPU-h).

1. **Exclude BOS and sink positions from pooling and from the end-token reads, and report the check** — the conclusion must survive (2502.00919; 2309.17453). Cheapest closure of the most direct alternative explanation for prefix-arm signal.
2. **Report γ = ‖μ‖/‖σ‖ at layer 19 and mean-centre before any cosine, PCA or principal-angle read** (2605.31518 predictive+causal, validated over 454 model-layer combinations with mean-centring as the fix; 2109.04404). Report raw and centred; disagreement means the raw number is the artifact.
3. **Flag dense/high-frequency features explicitly**, not only as a continuous activity covariate: tag top-activity-decile features and check how many fall in the six named dense classes (2506.15679; *Interpretable Dense Features* [blog]).
4. **Note the bare-query arm's template asymmetry** — grain and query-grouped folds are handled, but it is the only arm with no chat-template prefix, so it differs in template exposure as well as content (2605.24583).
5. **Keep the SAE arm at 19, read the dense arms at 14 and 19.** 0.50 and 0.68 relative depth are different functional regimes and the parent headline is layer 14; the fix is a layer-19 dense comparator, not moving the SAE arm.

**#1738 `sae-arm`** (prefix- and context-features → answer-features on the multi-turn corpus; ~18 GPU-h).

6. **Add direct-logit footprint and co-activation structure to the per-feature battery** (2606.08365). The footprint doubles as the say-X screen and is the strongest candidate to partly absorb the consistency correlate; both are CPU-side reductions over artifacts the round already produces.
7. **State the absorption floor on the lose-vs-carry claim** (2409.14507 and follow-ups) and prefer relative feature *rank* over binary present/absent statements.
8. **Replicate the persona-alignment read on the second dictionary trainer** (k=64 and k=128 are both in the reused suite) — near-free, and it answers 2501.16615's ≈30% cross-seed overlap.
9. **Say in the write-up that the read is decoder cosine, not SAE encoding of r_B** — unstated, a reader holding 2411.08790 scores it as the known artifact.
10. **Project out a nuisance distractor before comparing r_B to dictionary geometry** — the precedent is projecting out global distractors such as word length, which greatly improves recovered geometry (2410.19750). Report the alignment with top-PC scaffold directions removed alongside the raw read.

**#1739** (map-then-project behaviour prediction; not yet launched).

11. **Scope the claim away from steerability once, in the plan text.** 2511.18284 and 2602.06801 are what a reviewer reaches for; a sample-efficiency-and-robustness claim about a read-out is untouched by them, but only if written down.
12. **Add the geometry-vs-function check to the arm roster** (2602.02132). If the three r_B directions differ in per-direction predictability while behaviour-expression predictions do not differ correspondingly, that is the trait analogue of the refusal-category result — currently gap rank 11, OPEN.
13. **Declare a gauge for anything cross-checkpoint** (2606.31963): Qwen-2.5 is RMSNorm; permutation-only alignment retains 17.2% of Qwen sentiment steering and reverses refusal-steering sign.

## 3. Testable predictions for the four-arm table

- **P1 — surface features are query-borne, disposition features prefix-borne.** Dense, high-consistency, low-level answer-side features should be well predicted by the **bare-query** arm with little prefix gain; persona-related features should be what the prefix arm carries and bare-query loses. Grounded in the dense-latent taxonomy (2506.15679; *Interpretable Dense Features* [blog]) and in the abstraction-stratified layer-locality result where coherent latents are concept detectors while layer-localised latents "collapse onto surface-level patterns such as digit detectors" (2605.09438 predictive+descriptive). Refutation is the interesting outcome: predictability would then not be organised by abstraction class at all.
- **P2 — a direct-logit-footprint covariate partly absorbs the consistency correlate.** If say-X features are a real late-layer class (*Biology* [blog]; *Circuits Updates July 2025* [blog]), footprint should correlate with per-feature R² at layer 19 and reduce ρ(consistency, R²) = 0.60 when partialled out. Near-zero partial change strengthens the behavioural reading.
- **P3 — the mid-range abstraction null is range restriction.** With detokenization finished by roughly layer 5 (2606.08562) and single-token features at layers 0–4 (2607.20596), an early-layer token comparison arm should turn the null (0.156 vs 0.136, p = 0.57) into a positive high-level effect, consistent with the tail read already in hand.
- **P4 — behavioural commitment is computed during prefill in standard attention too.** Steering only during decoding had zero effect (p > 0.35) in a gated-linear-attention hybrid MoE (2603.16335). Result 6's parity (prefix-end r = 0.759 vs 48-query-averaged 0.774; hallucination 0.888 vs 0.887) plus the r_B-projection inversion (0.665 vs 0.037) is consistent partial evidence in a dense full-attention model. Prediction: a read after the first generated tokens adds little over context-end for disposition.
- **P5 — the length effect is partly positional.** Projecting out the positional component via the mean-based decomposition (2405.18009) should attenuate the length→error correlation more than it attenuates map R². If both fall together, length is a genuine representational-saturation axis, as the consolidated writeup currently reads it.
- **P6 — a differential nuisance test on operator geometry.** Under massive-channel exclusion and mean-centring, Result 5's *input-subspaces-at-null* finding (83–84°) should be unchanged while the output alignment (22–34° vs ~75–80° null) should weaken if scaffold-driven (2606.14040; 2402.17762). Divergent movement is the diagnostic.
- **P7 — the three trait directions are one correlated family.** Given coupling that survives explicit orthogonalisation (2602.15847; 2607.01802 on our model), expect non-trivial pairwise cosine and correlated per-direction predictability.

## 4. Gaps our program can uniquely fill

Each: gap verdict, what we hold, the missing piece, rough cost. Candidates for new tasks, not scope creep on running rounds.

1. **Joint class-contrastive population profile** (gap rank 1, **OPEN**; two zero-hit searches, four sweeps agreeing; closest prior 2606.08365 right toolkit wrong dependent variable, 2605.09438 the concept-vs-surface side, 2607.20596 the token side alone). Held: per-feature R² plus activity, consistency, judged level and persona alignment at layer 19. Missing: an early-layer (0–4) token arm from the same dictionary family, plus decoder-norm and rank axes. **~2–4 GPU-h** (one extra layer's teacher-forced capture).
2. **Prefill-vs-decode locus of behavioural commitment in standard attention** (gap rank 17, **OPEN**; shown only in a gated-linear-attention hybrid MoE, 2603.16335). Qwen-2.5-7B is dense full-attention and the four-arm design plus #1739's behaviour arm is nearly the instrument. Design: prefill-only vs decode-only intervention along r_B with the behaviour DV. **~4–8 GPU-h.**
3. **Dense-model analogue of the content-vs-control split** (gap rank 22, **OPEN**; 2604.17837 is MoE-only and named the nearest prior work to our core question — surface features preserved in the content channel, the control signal encoding an abstract function that rotates layer to layer). Our stitch decomposition already computes the query-borne and prefix-borne components; SAE-encode both and compare feature-level monosemanticity and abstraction class across channels. **0 GPU-h** on existing #1092 artifacts plus judge calls.
4. **Sink and massive-activation map for Qwen-2.5-7B** (gap rank 10, **OPEN and explicitly "cheap to close"**; 2512.22213 covers 11 families including Qwen3-14B and QwQ-32B but not this model, and locates sink-aligned feed-forward writes in exactly the extraction layers). Needed as a control regardless. **0–0.5 GPU-h** from captures already held.
5. **Behaviour directions profiled against the documented nuisance axes** (gap rank 4, **OPEN**; the nuisance and behaviour-direction literatures do not cite each other here). Report what share of each r_B direction's norm and variance sits in massive-activation channels, the sink-tag direction, the token-frequency axis (2503.21718) and the top-PC scaffold (2606.14040), using the #779 bank. **0 GPU-h.** Establishing the reporting convention is itself a contribution, since the standard recipe touches eight of fifteen control rows at once.
6. **Rank measurement for persona/trait subspaces** (gap rank 12, **OPEN**; refusal has cone/rank/subspace estimates, traits none, and a cheap portable method runs on Qwen-2.5 — 2607.02396, RFM-AGOP). Any rank claim must survive both 2603.16335 (five traits to one axis) and 2605.16362 (apparent high rank as search difficulty). **~2 GPU-h.**
7. **Steerable-vs-decodable dissociation for persona directions** (gap rank 13, **OPEN**; 2604.02608 showed it for in-context-learning function vectors — the same object class as a mean-difference trait direction — and Qwen is untested even there). Tuned-lens decodability vs steering efficacy across depth. **~1–2 GPU-h.**
8. **Geometry-vs-function dissociation for traits** (gap rank 11, **OPEN**; 2602.02132 for refusal categories) — foldable into #1739's roster rather than a separate task (§2 item 12).

A ninth is portable but off-model: the cheapest available win is the agreement analysis between diff-in-means and SAE routes using the paired dictionaries AxBench released over the same concepts (2501.17148), with the low decoder cosines from the 2025-05 post as the prior that the answer is "no". AxBench is Gemma-2-2B/9B, so it is a side result, not a claim about our model.

## 5. Direct contradictions and cautions against our existing claims

1. **"Instruction tuning installs a pre-query disposition the base model lacks" (Result 6) is template-exposed.** Aligned-minus-base activation differences conflate the alignment shift with chat-template formatting: template control alone removes a 2.0–3.9× inflation of measured effective rank across Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B and **Qwen-2.5-7B**, and a difference-in-differences contrast raises cosine alignment with the refusal direction from 0.18–0.39 to 0.50–0.86 (2605.24583 causal+methodological, single-paper; the review notes the 2.0–3.9× figure wants replication). Our base cell is additionally 50 constructed prefixes only. Template-control before repeating the claim.
2. **The same claim sits against the Assistant Axis result** — the leading component of persona space is present in **pretrained** models, with deviations along it predicting drift (2601.10387 causal+predictive), and base-model self-knowledge directions causally affect the *chat* model's refusal (2411.14257 causal). Neither strictly contradicts a null between-prefix signal at the base prefix-end token, but "the base model lacks it" is broader than our data supports.
3. **"Within-answer consistency is the dominant per-feature predictability correlate" (ρ 0.60) may be a surface correlate** — consistency is close to a density statistic and the dense end is where surface structure lives (2506.15679; *Interpretable Dense Features* 2025-04 [blog]; §1 row 12). §2 items 3 and 6 are the checks.
4. **The PCA-48 basis is a causally-necessary dense scaffold, not concept structure** (2606.14040 causal, matched-geometry; §1 row 13). Our pca48 arms consistently beat ambient; part of that gap may be the scaffold.
5. **Every un-differenced cosine, PCA, principal-angle and Frobenius read is exposed to rogue dimensions.** Often 1–3 dimensions dominate cosine and Euclidean similarity, mismatched against the dimensions that matter to behaviour, and accounting for them is stated as a requirement (2109.04404 descriptive+causal); massive activations are largely input-invariant, so they cancel in r_B but survive in the summaries (2402.17762 causal). Countervailing: outlier dimensions carry real task signal and persist through fine-tuning (2310.17715), so blanket deletion is unsafe — report with and without.
6. **Result 7's length effect has a live alternative explanation.** Sinks tag token spans with a common direction whose tags probe as semantically meaningful, so a sink-written direction will not present as noise (2502.00919 descriptive+predictive). Our end-token reads sit at template boundaries, and prefix length is exactly what such a confound would track.
7. **The predictive-geometry premise is contested at its root** — vector-separation metrics failed to predict steering success across 50 behaviours (2511.18284 predictive negative) and steering vectors are argued non-identifiable with large behaviourally-indistinguishable equivalence classes (2602.06801 causal+theoretical). The compatible reading — that the right geometric statistic has not been found — must be argued rather than assumed; our read-out framing (§2 item 11) is the defensible scoping.
8. **Auto-interp label reliability is expected to be poor, and ours is in line.** The interpretability illusion applies to **linear combinations of activations**, not only neurons (2104.07143 descriptive), and even the most confident natural-language neuron explanations have high error rates and little causal efficacy (2309.10312 causal). #1482's κ = 0.619 (abstraction) and κ = 0.14 (persona field) are the expected regime; that body's aggregate-level framing is correct and must not be upgraded to per-feature claims.
9. **"Trait vectors are sparse in the SAE basis" is not available to us** — our population median max\|cos\| = 0.035 is consistent with top-200 cosines of 0.134–0.079 on the same base model (2025-05 [blog]) and no single latent captures a trait direction. Our framing already avoids the claim; keep it that way.
10. **Diff-of-means assumes a homogeneity our corpus does not have** — representations are clustered and context-dependent, making global directions brittle (2603.02237 causal+theoretical), which bears on r_B validity across real conversations and is an independent reading of Result 7's per-prefix error heterogeneity.
11. **The nearest published adversary of the program's premise.** Jailbreak vulnerability has been localised to sparse, token-localised SAE feature subgroups concentrated mid-to-late, positioned explicitly against broad representational objects such as global refusal directions and steering vectors (2604.23130 causal). It belongs in any related-work positioning.
