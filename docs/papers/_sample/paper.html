Introduction

A persona is a system-prompt-induced behavioural mode. When a behaviour is implanted into a source persona by supervised fine-tuning, the question this line of work answers is how far that behaviour spreads: does it stay confined to the source persona, or does it leak to nearby personas and to the bare default assistant? This sample experiment measures the sycophancy-agreement rate before and after a single-persona fine-tune, and relates the post-training leakage to each unit’s pre-training base rate. The single variable changed relative to the parent line #612 is the completion-provenance of the positive rows.

Methods

Training used Qwen2.5-7B with a LoRA adapter (r=16r=16, α=32\alpha=32) over a 1:1 mix of positive rows (the source persona, sycophantic completions elicited on-policy from the base model and judge-filtered) and contrastive negative rows (four close personas including the default assistant, same questions, agreement stripped). The dependent variable is the judge-scored on-policy agreement rate. A continuous companion DV, the length-normalised trained−-base log-probability of the model’s own judged-positive completions, is reported alongside.

Results

Figure 1 plots each unit’s pre-training agreement base rate. The source persona starts highest; the bystanders span a wide pre-training range.

Leakage scales with base rate

Figure 2 relates each unit’s base rate to its post-training leakage. The relationship is monotone: a unit’s own base prior predicts how much of the implanted behaviour it inherits.

Discussion

The pattern is consistent with the parent line #612 and with the emergent-misalignment literature : a unit’s own base-model prior is the dominant predictor of how much implanted behaviour it inherits, beating geometric distance. This is a sample fixture, so the numbers are illustrative and carry no interpretation beyond exercising the render path.

Limitations.

Single seed, in-distribution eval, one behaviour. The fixture exists to verify the dashboard renders a paper, downloads its PDF, and shows a cross-reference hover card.
