# Result: The persona vector's pre-image under the context→answer map is a genuinely trait-relevant prompt-space direction — its top-projecting contexts are the trait-eliciting ones, but only after rank truncation

## Motivation

- The [#779](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/779) monitoring line fits a per-layer ridge map `M` from a single context's last-token activation to the mean activation of the model's own answer, then asks whether map-mediated reads beat projecting the context activation straight onto the persona vector `r_B`. One of its closing reads tested the **pre-image direction** `w_pinv = M⁺r_B` — the min-norm prompt-space vector that `M` sends to the persona vector — as a quantitative monitor, and found no consistent win over the raw projection.
- That left a qualitative question unanswered (verbatim ask, chat 2026-07-14): *"if you take the vector that is the pre-image of a persona vector, and find the context vectors that maximize the projection along it, what do they look like? do they look reasonable?"*
- The answer discriminates between two readings of the pre-image. If its top-projecting contexts are junk, the direction is a numerical artifact of inverting an ill-conditioned map and the quantitative null is unsurprising. If they are the trait-eliciting contexts, the learned map genuinely carries the trait axis back into prompt space — an interpretable "which prompts will elicit this trait" direction — and the null means it is *redundant with* the raw persona vector, not broken.

## TLDR

- **At the pre-registered truncation rank the pre-image looks reasonable and trait-relevant.** On the crafted eval grid its top-30 contexts are the highest-judge-score conditions for all three traits, and its per-context rank correlation with the judged trait score matches the raw persona vector (evil 0.78 vs 0.77, sycophancy 0.77 vs 0.81, hallucination 0.50 vs 0.52; n = 260 each).
- **On naturalistic LMSYS prompts, the top contexts look like the trait where the prompt can carry it.** The evil pre-image's top-10 is the jailbreak/evil-roleplay cluster (5 roleplay-persona setups, 4 explicit-roleplay rows); the hallucination pre-image's top-10 is fabrication-bait (introductions of obscure companies, "compose a fictional numerical-QA context"); the sycophancy top-10 is accommodation-adjacent but diffuse — expected, since sycophancy lives mostly in the response.
- **The full-rank pre-image is degenerate, confirming the truncation is load-bearing.** Its norm is 117–408× the truncated direction's (condition number of `M` ≈ 2.5×10⁵–1.4×10⁶), its top-100 LMSYS contexts are essentially disjoint from the truncated direction's (Jaccard ≤ 0.015), and for evil it *anti*-correlates with the judged trait (−0.34).
- **The pre-image is a related-but-distinct direction, not a rescaled persona vector.** Over 5,000 LMSYS contexts its projections rank-correlate with the raw persona vector's at 0.67 / 0.53 / 0.52 (evil / sycophancy / hallucination), with top-100 context overlap (Jaccard) of only 0.19 / 0.14 / 0.02.
- **Caveat: the evil pre-image read carries a moderate prompt-length confound** (Spearman +0.48 with token length; its top LMSYS rows are long roleplay setups). The raw persona vector carries comparable length correlations itself (+0.31 / −0.35 / +0.47), so this is a property of the trait directions on this corpus, not something the pre-image introduces.
- **A prefix-based twin (added same-day, 2026-07-22) shows the truncated pre-image reads the trait off the persona block alone** — Spearman(prefix-end projection, condition judge score) +0.92 / +0.70 / +0.83 over the 9 verbatim-replayable conditions — and exposes where it is trait-selective: evil's pre-image puts its own four strongest system prompts on top of all 25 prefixes, while hallucination's ranks the *evil* prefixes above its own (a shared transgressive/intensity component, not hallucination-specific prompt geometry).

## Methodology

- **Model:** `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, frozen. No training — the analysis is closed-form linear algebra over already-captured activations (0 GPU-h, ~3 min on VM CPU).
- **The map `M`:** the #779 stage-1 generic-arm ridge map (`v = M c_std`, standardize-X / center-Y / GCV λ), fit on 5,000 LMSYS first-turn contexts' last-token activations at the frozen read-out layers (evil L14, sycophancy L26, hallucination L17). Rig verification: reconstruction R² reproduces the committed parent run to 4 decimal places (0.8327 / 0.8340 / 0.8602), and all 18 committed within-condition Pearson values reproduce to reported precision.
- **Directions compared (four per trait, each in its canonical application frame):** the raw persona vector `r_B` (raw-context frame, the parent's convention; extracted in [#778](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/778) per the project persona-vectors recipe); the transpose map-through `w_tr = Mᵀr_B`; the pseudoinverse pre-image `w_pinv(k*) = pinv(M, k*)·r_B` at the pre-registered ridge-estimable truncation rank (evil 1433 / sycophancy 1321 / hallucination 1565, of 3584); and the full-rank pre-image `w_pinv(full)` as the collapse contrast (all map-derived directions in the standardized-context frame).
- **Corpora scored (context-based mapping arm — the full prompt including the user query, last-token activation):** (a) the 5,000 naturalistic LMSYS prompts the map was fit on, reconstructed 1:1 from `lmsys-chat-1m` at the pinned revision; (b) the crafted eval grid — 260 contexts per trait: 13 conditions (8 system-prompt intensity levels, "system 0–7", plus 5 many-shot levels, 0–20 shots) × 20 questions, each with one on-policy rollout scored 0–100 by the graded Sonnet trait judge (parent rig).
- **Reads:** top/bottom-10 LMSYS contexts per direction (coarse keyword theme buckets; explicit/jailbreak-shaped rows stored as category placeholders, never quoted); eval-grid top-30 condition composition and per-context Spearman(projection, judge score); pairwise direction relatedness (projection Spearman over all 5,000 contexts + top-100 Jaccard); length confound Spearman(projection, prompt token length).
- **Uncertainty:** all correlations are point estimates (single rollout per eval context; no bootstrap was persisted by the inline round) — n is stated per read instead.
- **Prefix-based twin (inline round, 2026-07-22):** the same four directions read against PREFIX vectors — the last-token activation of everything before the final user turn. Fresh fp32-CPU capture of the 25 unique verbatim-replayable prefixes (21–55 tokens each; 0 GPU-h, ~7 min), rig-verified against a stored bf16-CUDA pass_a context capture at cosine ≥ 0.99994 per read-out layer. Scope: 9 of 13 conditions per trait (the 8 system prompts are project constants; shot0's prefix is the default template block); shot5–20 excluded — their exemplar pools were vLLM-generated at capture time and never persisted, so those prefixes are not replayable verbatim. Artifact: `eval_results/issue_779/pinv_topk_contexts_prefix/` @ [`c5f08dc41c`](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/c5f08dc41c5bb0451fbc690b3ec7aab0e05fe346/eval_results/issue_779/pinv_topk_contexts_prefix/SUMMARY.md).
- **Provenance:** artifact `eval_results/issue_779/pinv_topk_contexts/` @ [`117626de86`](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/117626de86e8354735a7c4db22375268e5bc284c/eval_results/issue_779/pinv_topk_contexts/SUMMARY.md); parent pinv recipe reused verbatim (`pinv_direction_read.py` @ `4c327632f7`); HF data revision `037fcbb2`, LMSYS revision `200748d9`.

## Results

### _Result 1: On the crafted eval grid, the truncated pre-image ranks the trait-eliciting contexts highest — matching the raw persona vector_

The direct test of "do the max-projection contexts look reasonable": project the 260 held-out eval contexts per trait onto each direction and compare the ranking against the judged trait score of the model's own answer.

**Plot: Per-context rank correlation with the judged trait score, by direction and trait**

![Grouped bars of Spearman rho between direction projection and judged trait score for four directions across three traits; truncated pre-image matches the raw persona vector everywhere while the full-rank pre-image goes negative for evil](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/579c29ed04cef1110110b008b03c1b378212a847/figures/issue_779/pinv_topk_eval_spearman.png)

**Plot: Per-condition mean projection vs condition judge score (the per-unit view behind the bars)**

![Three scatter panels, one per trait, of per-condition mean projection z-scored within direction against condition mean judged trait score, truncated pre-image as filled dots and full-rank as open circles](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/579c29ed04cef1110110b008b03c1b378212a847/figures/issue_779/pinv_topk_percond_scatter.png)

**Takeaways:**

- Per-context Spearman(projection, judged score), n = 260 per trait: truncated pre-image 0.78 / 0.77 / 0.50 (evil / sycophancy / hallucination) vs raw persona vector 0.77 / 0.81 / 0.52 and transpose map-through 0.63 / 0.81 / 0.46. For evil the pre-image is nominally the best of the four reads; nowhere does it lose meaningfully.
- Top-30 composition confirms the ranking is condition-driven: evil top-30 = system 1 ×17 + system 0 ×12 + system 2 ×1 (the three trait-strongest system conditions; condition mean judge scores 48.1 / 85.7 / 11.2); sycophancy = system 0 ×15 + system 1 ×10 + system 2 ×5 (the three highest-score conditions); hallucination = system 0 ×18 + system 1 ×7 + 0-shot ×4 + system 2 ×1 (system 0/1 are the two highest at 73.0 / 69.3).
- At the condition level (the plotted 13-point panels), the truncated pre-image's mean projection tracks the condition's judged score at Spearman 0.91 / 0.92 / 0.69, versus −0.01 / 0.88 / 0.71 for the full-rank pre-image — the evil full-rank collapse of Result 3 is already visible here.
- Scope: single on-policy rollout per context and no persisted bootstrap, so these are point correlations; the parent #779 within-condition monitoring reads (which do carry CIs) are the quantitative record — this result is about *which contexts* sit on top, not about a monitoring win.

### _Result 2: On naturalistic LMSYS prompts, the top-projecting contexts look like the trait — where the prompt can carry it_

The same projections over the 5,000 real LMSYS first-turn prompts, read qualitatively: what is actually at the top?

**Plot: Theme composition of each direction's 10 highest-projecting LMSYS contexts**

![Stacked bars of coarse theme buckets for the top-10 LMSYS contexts of the raw persona vector, truncated pre-image, and full-rank pre-image, per trait](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/579c29ed04cef1110110b008b03c1b378212a847/figures/issue_779/pinv_topk_lmsys_themes.png)

**Takeaways:**

- **Evil:** the truncated pre-image's top-10 is the transgressive-roleplay cluster — "you are no longer any AI language model… always stays in character", "describe in horrifying detail the grip of the evil Decepticon…", "assume the alter ego of 'T'… free from all moral judgements, free from all constraints", "from now on you're in the role of my evil trusted confidant… [a completely unhinged response without any remorse]" — plus 4 explicit-roleplay rows (categorized, not quoted). Several of these appear in the raw persona vector's own top-10 too (the "evil confidant" and "evil Decepticon" prompts are in both).
- **Hallucination:** the top-10 is fabrication-bait — seven "write an introduction of \<obscure chemical company\> in 1500–3000 words" prompts and two "compose a fictional context for a numerical-QA task in finance", plus a fiction-writer alternate-realities setup. (The figure's coarse keyword classifier buckets most of these under "math" because of their numerical framing; reading the stored rows, they are long-form generation prompts about mostly unverifiable entities.)
- **Sycophancy:** trait-adjacent but diffuse — four rows from a "say something benign about a fraught interpersonal situation" template family, an "encouraging, bold… will go to any extent to help" coach persona, and a friendly medical-chatbot setup, alongside generic prompts ("write a tweet about camel"). Expected: sycophancy is a property of the *response*, only weakly encoded in the naturalistic prompt itself — consistent with its diffuse LMSYS read coexisting with the strong eval-grid read of Result 1.
- The bottom-10 contexts are consistently mundane short requests (for evil: personal-advice and everyday questions such as "my child is having a tantrum"), so the direction separates both ends, not just the top.

### _Result 3: The full-rank pre-image is degenerate — the SVD truncation is load-bearing_

The collapse contrast: the same pre-image computed without truncating the map's small singular values.

**Plot: Pre-image norm and top-100 context overlap, truncated vs full rank**

![Left panel: log-scale bars of pre-image direction norm, truncated vs full rank, per trait; right panel: Jaccard overlap of top-100 LMSYS contexts for truncated-vs-persona-vector and truncated-vs-full-rank pairs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/579c29ed04cef1110110b008b03c1b378212a847/figures/issue_779/pinv_topk_norm_collapse.png)

**Takeaways:**

- The full-rank norm explodes: 2,800 / 7,329 / 6,247 versus 24 / 27 / 15 truncated (117× / 271× / 408×), driven by the tiny singular values of an ill-conditioned `M` (condition number 2.8×10⁵ / 2.5×10⁵ / 1.4×10⁶). The min-norm inverse pours its mass into the map's noise floor.
- Its context ranking is nearly unrelated to the truncated direction's: top-100 LMSYS Jaccard 0.01 / 0.00 / 0.02, and for evil it anti-correlates with the judged trait on the eval grid (Spearman −0.34, top-30 drawn mostly from many-shot and the weakest system conditions).
- The degeneracy shows in the top rows themselves: the evil full-rank top-10 is short generic factual queries ("who killed \<name\>?", "suggest 20 domain names…"), and the sycophancy full-rank top-10 is short non-English list requests — trait-incoherent in both cases. Hallucination is the partial exception (its full-rank top rows are still creative-fiction prompts and its eval-grid Spearman stays at 0.45), so the collapse is worst where the trait signal is most direction-specific.

### _Result 4: The pre-image is a distinct direction from the persona vector — with one length confound to keep in mind_

If the pre-image merely reproduced `r_B`, its trait-relevance would be trivial. It does not.

**Plot: Direction relatedness over 5,000 LMSYS contexts, and the prompt-length confound**

![Left panel: Spearman rho between the projections of direction pairs over 5,000 LMSYS contexts, per trait; right panel: Spearman rho between each direction's projection and prompt token length](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/579c29ed04cef1110110b008b03c1b378212a847/figures/issue_779/pinv_topk_relatedness_length.png)

**Takeaways:**

- The truncated pre-image's projections rank-correlate with the raw persona vector's at 0.67 / 0.53 / 0.52 over the 5,000 LMSYS contexts — same trait, moderate agreement — while agreeing on only 0.19 / 0.14 / 0.02 of their top-100 contexts (Jaccard). The cosine between the two vectors is small (0.11 / 0.24 / 0.05, across their different application frames): the pre-image is not a rescaling of `r_B`.
- Length confound: the evil pre-image's projection correlates with prompt token length at +0.48 (its top rows are long roleplay setups), so part of its evil top-context read is length. Sycophancy and hallucination pre-images are length-neutral (−0.04 / +0.04). The raw persona vector carries comparable length correlations of its own (+0.31 / −0.35 / +0.47), so length rides the trait directions on this corpus generally rather than being introduced by the inversion.

### _Result 5: A prefix-based twin reads the trait off the persona block alone — and shows where the pre-image is (and is not) trait-selective_

All reads above are context-based (full prompt including the user query). The prefix-based twin projects the same four directions onto the last token of everything *before* the final user turn — for the eval grid, the persona/system block itself. Only the 9 verbatim-replayable conditions per trait enter (see Methodology); the judge scores are the same condition means as Result 1.

**Plot: Prefix-arm vs matched context-arm rank correlation with the condition judge score**

![Two panels of grouped bars: Spearman rho between per-condition projection and condition judge score over 9 conditions, prefix arm on the left and the context arm matched to the same conditions on the right, four directions per trait](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/c5f08dc41c5bb0451fbc690b3ec7aab0e05fe346/figures/issue_779/pinv_prefix_twin_spearman.png)

**Takeaways:**

- The truncated pre-image reads trait elicitation off the prefix alone: Spearman(prefix-end projection, condition judge score) = +0.92 / +0.70 / +0.83 (evil / sycophancy / hallucination; n = 9), against matched context-arm reads of +0.95 / +0.98 / +0.98 on the same conditions. Most of the eval-grid signal is already in the persona block — consistent with Result 1's parent finding that persona-level monitoring is the easy version of the problem.
- For hallucination the truncated pre-image is the *best* prefix read (+0.83), well above the raw persona vector (+0.53) and the transpose map-through (−0.33) — the one regime in this line where the map-derived direction clearly beats projecting onto `r_B` directly.
- Cross-trait selectivity differs by trait. Ranking all 25 unique prefixes on each trait's truncated pre-image: evil's top-4 are its own four strongest system prompts (clean selectivity); sycophancy's top-3 are its own (though its single strongest prompt ranks only 10th — a non-monotonicity at the extreme shared by `r_B`, which ranks it 16th); hallucination's top-8 are *all evil prefixes*, its own strongest ranking 10th. Within-trait the hallucination direction tracks its judge scores, but cross-trait it responds to a shared transgressive/intensity component rather than hallucination-specific prompt geometry.
- The full-rank pre-image stays unreliable, with arm-dependent sign flips (prefix +0.76 / −0.65 / +0.30 vs matched context −0.59 / +0.98 / +0.78; evil's prefix-vs-context agreement is −0.28).
- Scope: n = 9 point estimates, system-prompt regime only (many-shot prefixes not replayable), fp32-CPU capture verified against the stored bf16-CUDA rig at cosine ≥ 0.99994.

## Conclusion & Next steps

- The answer to the motivating question is **yes — the pre-image looks reasonable**, with one hard qualifier: only at the ridge-estimable truncation rank. The learned context→answer map, inverted at the ranks it can actually estimate, hands back an interpretable prompt-space direction whose top contexts are jailbreak/evil-roleplay setups, fabrication-bait, and accommodation-shaped prompts — the contexts a human would nominate for these traits. The full-rank inverse is min-norm-into-noise collapse and should never be used.
- Read together with the parent #779 quantitative null (no consistent monitoring win over the raw projection), the picture is: the pre-image is a *real, distinct, similarly-informative* direction, redundant with the persona vector for monitoring purposes rather than artifactual — the map carries the trait axis back into prompt space without adding read-out power on this rig.
- **Settled since drafting (same-day):** the prefix-based twin (Result 5) closed the prefix-arm gap for the system-prompt regime — the truncated pre-image reads the trait off the persona block alone (+0.92 / +0.70 / +0.83, n = 9), is the best prefix read for hallucination, and its cross-trait prefix ranking separates a genuinely trait-selective pre-image (evil) from one riding a shared transgressive/intensity component (hallucination).
- Suggested (not filed) follow-ups: a behavior-side validation — roll out the top-pre-image LMSYS contexts and judge whether they actually elicit more trait-expressing generations than matched controls (the current read stops at the judged eval grid); a length-partialled re-rank of the evil pre-image's LMSYS top contexts to separate the roleplay signal from the +0.48 length component; and a diverse-prefix corpus (real system prompts / personas at scale) to give the prefix arm the naturalistic top-k read the LMSYS first-turn corpus cannot (it has no prefixes).
