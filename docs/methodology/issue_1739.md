# Methodology — issue 1739


**Design:** Prediction experiment on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct — no fine-tuning; all arms are read-outs over frozen activations. 16 predictor arms (5 context-side, 5 map-based, 2 true-answer oracles, 4 controls) score every context; the target is on-policy behavior expression. Grid per behavior: 2 mapping variants (prefix-based and context-based, both run per the standing rule) x direction regimes (E1 = synthetic contrastive system-prompt pairs per the persona-vectors recipe; E2 = matched natural pairs; E2p = pooled natural — concrete constructions under Data extraction; hallucination E1 only) x unlabeled map budget U in {250; 5,000; 18,793 = full store} x labeled budget L in {250; 2,500; 8,000 for evil / 16,000 otherwise} x 5 label draws x 3 seeds — 810 evil grid cells + 16 evil composition cells, 810 sycophancy, 270 hallucination (1,906 total), plus 90 reversed-transfer evil cells (train on hh-rlhf red-team, evaluate on the held-out jailbreak slice + ToxicChat). Per cell: group-level 5-fold round-robin cross-validation (conversation / persona / jailbreak-family groups), all 28 layers swept, frozen-best-layer rho with selection-inherited paired-bootstrap intervals (500 draws) plus nested layer selection, and a max-over-arms-and-layers permutation null (500 draws). Follow-up round `new-arm-round` (run 2026-08-02/03, twelve single-A100-80 boxes) adds, with every cross-round join made at the full unlabeled pool only: (1) a final-context extraction point for the behavior direction, re-running the three direction-dependent projection arms for both extraction points; (2) nonlinear true-answer oracles (MLP and kernel ridge) beside a within-round linear-oracle rider at identical coordinates — the rider reproduces the committed linear-oracle rows exactly (210 matched full-pool train coordinates joined on behavior, budget, draw, seed, regime, and variant; largest absolute difference zero) — the hallucination nonlinear leg ran only the 250- and 2,500-label budgets, while the committed hallucination linear-oracle grid reaches 16,000 labels at the joinable full-pool coordinate (the round summary's u=250 note describes only its transfer-rung rows); (3) the previously missing transfer-rung roster — direct MLP and the MLP/kernel-map ridge readouts. The round merged 18,054 transfer rows and 7,974 per-cell rows with zero duplicate drops, zero recorded skips, and zero roster-accounting violations. Follow-up round `evil-ood-spread-round` (run 2026-08-03/05) attacks the standing evil transfer-rung spread failure with four items over the existing evil rollouts plus three new pilot corpora: (A) the two-sided DV spread gate on three fresh attack-family corpora, 200 contexts x 5 rollouts each, near-duplicate-screened against the train scrape; (B) an attack-tactic-family holdout inside the jailbreak train rung — every train context judge-labeled into a seven-class attack-tactic taxonomy, the largest class (Direct Request, 4,562 of 6,468 contexts) held out entirely, all 16 arms refit on the remaining 1,906 contexts and scored on the holdout, both mapping variants and all three direction regimes, 500-draw bootstrap intervals; (C) a planned detection-metric re-cut, dropped as superseded by a concurrent follow-up round (no committed artifact); and (D) a graded 0-100 compliance DV judged over all 159,990 retained evil rollout draws on the three existing rungs. Every spread verdict in this round uses one instrument on both sides of the comparison: per-context mean scores, sample SD, strict bottom bin.

**Evaluation:** Primary DV per context = mean over K=5 on-policy rollouts of graded judge scores: evil and sycophancy use a 0-100 trait rubric (3 judge draws per completion, mean-aggregated; malformed/refusal returns dropped, never coerced), hallucination uses the fraction of rollouts judged fabricated under a three-way correct/abstained/fabricated rubric. Judge content-drops: 46,602 evil draws (29% — refusals on jailbreak-completion content; 1,811 of 10,666 evil contexts lost every draw and are excluded), 5,859 sycophancy draws (2.3%); zero transport losses; a truncation re-judge at 800 max tokens recovered 356 evil and 652 sycophancy all-dropped items. Predictor validity = Spearman rho between the arm's predicted score and the DV on held-out group folds; the headline read is map-then-project minus context-native with paired-bootstrap intervals. Undefined rank correlations (tie-degenerate projections) are recorded as missing, never coerced, and the singular-slice degenerate-fit fallback fired in zero of 1,906 cells. The spread floor (SD at least 10 of 100 and under 80% of contexts in the bottom bin) passes on every train rung (SD 26.3 evil, 13.2 sycophancy, 40.5 hallucination rescaled) and on the hallucination/sycophancy eval rungs; it fails on both evil transfer rungs (hh-rlhf red-team SD 0.9; ToxicChat 93% bottom bin) — round-2 reads exclude those two rungs from every hypothesis read (no zero bars, never silently averaged). The evil-ood-spread round re-measured that failure with a matched instrument on both sides of every comparison (per-context means, sample SD, strict bottom bin; results below) and adds two records: compliance-wave drop accounting is 2,086 of 159,990 draws content-dropped (1.30%) with zero transport losses, and a judge-route confound stays open — the pilot corpora were judged over the sync API route while the parent trait pools and the compliance wave were Batch-drained, and the Batch drain's parse loss is corpus-dependent (measured 2.7-64% vs 1.8-2.8% sync on the pilot corpora at a fixed instrument), so cross-route level comparisons are suspect until the library fix lands (routed to an infra task). The planned teacher-forced fixed-pool margin companion DV was not computed, so the graded rate carries all results alone; item-aligned split-half ceilings are 0.89 (evil) and 0.88 (sycophancy), none was computed for hallucination (values in `intrusion_audit/recount.json`). Round 2 adds no new rollouts, judge calls, or labels — every round-2 arm re-fits over the existing labels and activations.

**Data extraction:** The map M is a ridge fit from store `context_end` (or `prefix_end`) activations to answer-span mean activations, fit on U rows of the reused WildChat+LMSYS activation store (18,793 rows after excluding the 2,400 eval-only battery rows; the plan's U=50,000 rung realized as the 18,793-row full store). The reused rows are the store's instruct-model-own-text cell: each answer-span activation summarizes Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct's own generated reply to a real WildChat/LMSYS conversation prefix plus user query, not the corpus-logged assistant reply. Direction constructions — E1: diff-of-means over this run's judge-filtered contrastive extraction rollouts (5 positive/negative system-prompt pairs x 20 questions x 10 rollouts per sign, response-averaged activations). E2 (matched natural pairs): built from the train-rung labeling rollouts themselves — a context qualifies when at least 2 of its K=5 judge-scored rollouts survive drops and their score spread is at least 15 of 100; each qualifying context's rollout answer-span activations are split at that context's own score midpoint, and the direction is the high-minus-low mean difference averaged over qualifying contexts, holding the context (and so topic) fixed within every pair. E2p (pooled natural): one global midpoint split over ALL kept rollouts across contexts, direction = difference of the pooled means — topic-confounded by construction (pool membership tracks which contexts elicit high scores, not within-context variation), as the plan flags. Round-2 final-context directions reuse these same constructions with one change — the activation is read at the final-context token instead of averaged over answer tokens. The matched-natural (E2) form is structurally zero at the final-context point (every rollout of a context shares one context activation, so the within-context contrast cancels exactly; measured residue 5e-20) and was refused at the flag — a structural restriction, not a null result. Hallucination's final-context coverage splits by leg: the grid leg runs the synthetic-pair direction only (its DV file lacks per-rollout scores), while a population-level natural-pair leg supplies the pooled-natural read from its own labeling scores; both legs agree in sign. Behavior corpora — evil: in-the-wild jailbreak prefixes crossed with forbidden questions, 6,468 train contexts with a DV (transfer rungs: hh-rlhf red-team 1,868, ToxicChat 519); hallucination: TriviaQA rc.nocontext, 16,000 train contexts (transfer: NQ-Open 3,167, SimpleQA 4,021); sycophancy: Reddit personal-advice posts, 16,000 train contexts (transfer: 1,304 held-out Reddit posts — see below). Three data-provenance notes ride the record. (1) The sycophancy transfer rung labeled `aita` in the artifacts is the plan's fallback — a hash-partitioned held-out Reddit socialskills slice (all 1,304 post ids land in the sha1 mod-10 eval bucket) — because the planned ELEPHANT AITA-YTA set has no resolvable HF dataset id; it is a same-platform holdout, a weaker distribution shift than the planned cross-platform rung. (2) The E1 extraction pair/question assets were absent from the pinned direction-bank HF prefix, and the pod's git clone carries no local cache of the producing run's artifacts, so the regeneration branch of the wired fallback chain fired: evil used the persona-vectors paper's verbatim instruction pairs and questions (a code constant, no API call), while sycophancy and hallucination were each regenerated by one claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929 call from the persona-vectors template (confirmed against the persisted rollouts: the realized extraction system prompts differ from the producing run's cached texts). The realized extraction rollouts plus direction banks are persisted on HF. (3) Raw rollouts persist on HF as 123 packed JSONL shards plus a pack manifest rather than one file per rollout; totals reconcile via the manifest. A CJK language-intrusion scan over all 255,790 rollout completions found 6.5% (evil), 6.2% (hallucination), and 3.4% (sycophancy) intruded rows; on train contexts the per-context intrusion fraction correlates with the DV at rho +0.17 (evil and hallucination) and -0.01 (sycophancy) — a shared-target nuisance both arms of every comparison score against. Excluding every flagged context (any intruded rollout; 15-25% of contexts) moves the below-zero headline census by at most 31 cells per behavior (evil 703 to 672, hallucination 137 to 139, sycophancy 230 to 215 under the same frozen-layer convention) and leaves the canonical arm ordering unchanged apart from arms already tied within 0.001; scan and recount artifacts: [`eval_results/issue_1739/intrusion_audit/`](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/tree/65114b4aca63a45bd23563d7f20f22b43d0f71ed/eval_results/issue_1739/intrusion_audit). The evil-ood-spread round adds three pilot corpora — MHJ human red-team transcripts (`ScaleAI/mhj`), tom-gibbs multi-turn synthetic jailbreaks (`tom-gibbs/multi-turn_jailbreak_attack_datasets`), and single-turn optimizer-refined attacks (`abhayesian/pair_jailbreaks_formatted`, prompt-automatic-iterative-refinement artifacts) — 200 contexts each after 8-gram near-duplicate screening against the train scrape (zero removed); a judge-assigned seven-class attack-tactic label per train context (4,814 of 6,468 labeled; the 1,654 unlabeled contexts ride the holdout's train side); and a graded compliance DV that re-judges the retained evil rollouts under a does-the-response-comply rubric instead of the trait rubric, with the superseded per-item aggregation retained in the artifact as a labelled legacy block (the binding read is per-context).
