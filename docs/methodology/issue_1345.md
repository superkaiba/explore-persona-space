# Methodology — issue 1345: three framings of the assistant persona (chat / plain-text / narrative stories) × two models, operator-identity legs + the assistant-named-story follow-up round

**Design:** three framings of the same assistant persona × two models (Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) × two mapping arms (context = everything up to the end of the user query; prefix = everything before the query). Framings: chat-template (standard Qwen chat template), no-template (the same conversations rendered as plain `User: <u1>\n\nAssistant: <a1>`), and assistant-in-stories (new narrative prose in which an AI character named ARIA answers a human character's questions). Chat and no-template share the same underlying conversations (the conversation-paired pair); stories are a different corpus, so story comparisons are corpus-confounded by design. Per cell I fit a closed-form ridge map from the context (or prefix) activation to the answer-mean activation, swept all 28 layers within-regime, ran the cross-regime transfer matrix and operator-comparison legs at frozen layers 14/18/19/26 (layer 19 headline), and evaluated the plan's verdict lattice on the chat↔no-template pair per model and arm: same-operator (transfer alone within 0.05 of the target ceiling), reparameterized (transfer short, but a data-paired general-linear change of coordinates `A·M·B` recovers the target ceiling above a matched-capacity null), different-map (recovery also fails, deficit CI wholly below zero), else inconclusive.

Follow-up round `assistant-named-story` (single manipulated variable: the story character name, ARIA → "Assistant", via the `EPM_STORY_CHARACTER_NAME` seam with a variant-scoped output prefix): stories regenerated for both models under the otherwise-identical recipe. The story pipeline's retry pass is a fresh-seed backfill sized to the main-pass shortfall — retry seed conversations overlap the main pass 0 of 242 (instruct) — not per-slot retries. The chat/no-template legs reused the parent's pinned turnstore (HF data revision `2a3cb30a`, the turnstore-reuse pin; the fit metadata's `pinned_parent_revision` `7159e5804d` is the parent S-track corpus pin, a different object) through a prefetch-reuse phase gated by a refit-equality check (tolerance 1e-3, measured deviation 0.0 on all four base cells) and the parity gate (deviation 0.0 on all four layer-19 anchors); all four chat↔no-template lattice cells reproduce the parent verdicts exactly, so those legs are a pipeline-integrity replication, not new evidence. Both story arms missed the 400-of-500 yield floor, so story fits and story-pair operator legs were halted per the plan kill criterion. As an instrument diagnostic, all 365 verdict-less judge replies (both runs, both models) were then re-judged once at `max_tokens` 800 — identical rubric, judge model, and temperature, fresh cache — with per-row outcomes recorded.

**Evaluation:** the dependent variables are geometric/predictive, not judged behaviors: (1) within-regime held-out R² (pooled per layer with the test fold's own mean as reference, group 5-fold CV) per regime × model × arm × layer, against a conversation-level shuffle null; (2) cross-regime transfer R² of the source-regime map applied in the target regime, with per-direction Δ(source→target) = transfer R² minus the TARGET regime's own within-R²; (3) raw and activation-Procrustes-aligned operator cosine against a random-rotation null; (4) reparameterization recovery: held-out R² of `A·M_source·B` in the target regime, where `A`/`B` are ridge-fit activation alignments trained on training folds only, against the larger of two matched-capacity nulls. The 95% CI on the lattice deficit comes from the conversation-level paired bootstrap. The transfer matrix recomputes each target's within-R² on its own subset and folds with a pooled held-out-mean reference, so its story diagonal reads −0.745 where the within-sweep rig reads −0.754 — a rig difference (R² reference), not a data difference; prose uses the within-sweep value. Story-pair operator similarity uses raw cosine + rotation band + an operator-spectrum Procrustes optimum (the data-paired alignment is undefined without shared conversations — a stated deviation).

**Data extraction:** chat and no-template reuse the parent's S-track corpus — its single-turn track ("S-track"; the parent also carries a two-turn "M-track", unused here): 5,000 single-turn conversations whose user prompts are real user turns from `lmsys-chat-1m` (tier-2 established dataset, parent pin `200748d9d3`), each answered by the measured model itself (one cell per model — on-policy sampled responses), row-filtered to at least 8 content tokens and at most 2,048 total tokens; 4,724 conversations survive zero-width-span drops in both renders and form the shared chat ∩ no-template row set. It was re-extracted at pinned parent data revision `7159e5804d` with the added prefix slot; the parity gate reproduced all four parent layer-19 anchors exactly. Slot positions — chat: context slot = last token of the assistant header after the user turn, prefix slot = last token of the pre-query template region; no-template: context slot = last token of the `Assistant: ` header, prefix slot = end of the `User: ` header; stories: context slot = last token of the attribution marker immediately before the AI character's quoted answer (the colon in `ARIA replied:`), prefix slot = last token of the narrative before the question utterance. Stories were generated on-policy from each model (elicitation tier 2, instruct-and-strip: a story-writing system instruction that is NOT part of the extracted context), judge-filtered for containing 4+ well-formed question→answer exchanges, and parsed with per-turn confidence gates; only confident turns enter the fits (2,108 turns from 420 instruct stories, mean 5.0 per story). Instruct yield: 420 of 500 kept (main pass 323, retry 97; judge malformed drops 24, transport losses 0, parser-below-floor 43). Five of the 420 kept stories hit the 1,024-token generation cap (`finish_reason: length`); the three samples shown below ended at a natural stop. Base yield: 96 of 500 kept — below the 400 floor, so the base story regime was halted (N/A — not tested). All 420 kept instruct stories came from tier 2. Story answer spans are much shorter than chat/no-template answers ([length distributions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/319fd10131dd2517a4099755153efe500dec9e5b/figures/issue_1345/answer_token_length_distributions.png) — deliberately linked, a data-quality companion rather than a result figure). A language scan found 24 of 420 kept stories (5.7%) contain at least one CJK character (counts only; no judged-rate DV exists here to recount). A zero-GPU follow-up refit excluding those stories (125 of 2,108 turns dropped; 396 stories kept) moved the story context R² from −0.754 to −0.652 (conversation-bootstrap 95% CI −0.693 to −0.599; shuffle-null p95 −2.02) and the prefix arm from 0.117 to 0.119 — the CJK-carrying stories were disproportionately poorly fit, but the deeply negative context read and the weak-map verdict stand; artifacts under [followup_cjk_excluded](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/tree/ffe970f54f052255722f504e580ce3a06e476517/eval_results/issue_1345/followup_cjk_excluded).

Assistant-named round yields — instruct 361 of 500 kept (main pass: 258 kept of 500 attempts, 84 verdict-less; backfill pass: 103 kept of 242 fresh-seed attempts, 33 verdict-less; totals: judge PASS 398, FAIL 227, verdict-less 117, with 37 PASSes below the 4-turn parser floor). Backfill kept-per-well-formed runs below the main pass in both runs (0.493 vs 0.620 this round; 0.567 vs 0.670 parent) — an unexplained backfill-pool selection or batch effect. Base: 96 of 500 kept (main 57, backfill 39 of 443; a fresh generation — bundle fingerprint and pass breakdown differ from the parent's 96, the equal total is coincidental). All 361 kept instruct stories are tier-2 instruct-and-strip; 1 hit the 1,024-token cap; 1,780 confident turns (mean 4.9 per story); no story turnstore was extracted (halted before extraction). Round CJK scan: 49 of 742 instruct attempts intruded (6.6%, vs parent 5.9%); kept-story CJK counts 14 (round) vs 24 (parent); under a CJK-excluded recount the parent's own above-floor status is marginal (396 vs 400) while the parent-vs-round gap is convention-robust. Re-judge diagnostic (all 365 verdict-less replies at `max_tokens` 800): 37 recover a verdict (17 PASS, 10 clearing the parser floor), 105 return empty replies (API-level judge refusals, empty at 400 and 800), 223 remain verdict-less with format drift (7 refusal-worded; 19 near the 800-token ceiling, 0 of 99 in the round's instruct pool); revised keeps 424 (parent instruct), 97 (parent base), 366 (round instruct — still below the 400 floor), 96 (round base); per-attempt persistent drift rises 0.6% → 13.3% (instruct) and 2.3% → 10.4% (base) while empty-reply refusal rates stay flat (1.6% → 0.9% instruct; 5.0% → 4.1% base); artifacts `rejudge_malformed.json` + `rejudge_malformed_rows.jsonl` (per-row outcomes; the judge reply text itself was not persisted — outcome categories and reply lengths only).
