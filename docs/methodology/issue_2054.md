# Methodology — issue 2054: framing x speaker x completion-condition context-to-answer map lattice (scaffold-and-splice corpus, Qwen-2.5-7B both variants, layer 19)

**Design:** a framing × identity × condition × model lattice of context→answer maps over one shared real-conversation draw (26,889 conversations drawn, through its persisted 99,778-conversation sampling manifest, from a 626,620-conversation multi-turn pool of real user chats — LMSYS-derived, established-dataset tier), decoupled into scaffold-and-splice: (Phase A) generate diverse narrative scaffolds containing one verbatim question and an answer-slot sentinel, with no answer text; (Phase B, "inserted") splice the original conversation's real answer into the slot deterministically (100% keep, span offsets known by construction); (Phase C, "on-policy") prefill the scaffold to the slot and let the measured model write the answer; (Phase D, "transposed") splice the model's story-authored answer into the chat template. Framings realized: chat template and bare text (`User: {Q}\n\nAssistant: {A}`) for the assistant identity; story with attributed quote (`{Name} replied: "{A}"`) and story with bare label (`{Name}: {A}`) for assistant plus four story characters (HELIOS, Wren, Dana, Vex) × both models (Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct). The planned fifth framing (indirect reported speech) could not be spliced deterministically and was dropped at the initial run, per the plan's declared escape; a user-directed extension round later reinstated it on-policy only (extension round below). The USER identity is excluded by design (its context arm is a self-prediction in this rig; handled in a separate task). 56 cells total: 48 inserted/on-policy cells plus 8 transposed cells. Scaffold supply: of 53,018 admitted rows, 46,462 were generated on-pod by Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct at temperature 1.0 under a scaffold-writing instruction that is stripped before capture (scenario axes sampled under seed 137) and 6,556 were recovered from the parent's kept stories via a scaffold stripper; every candidate was judged for structural extractability by `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929` (integer rubric, admit at score ≥ 50; 101,095 calls, pilot-gated with zero truncation and zero parse failures; per-variant judged→admitted e.g. Dana 19,714 → 9,722). The plan also held open a parent-reject re-mining path — a permissive span matcher over the parent's 24,118 rejected inserted rows (~11,721 recoverable), with a raw-vs-permissive matcher control on every re-mined row. Fresh scaffold supply met quota and every Phase B row is a deterministic splice with no matcher, so the re-mining path never ran and the control is vacuous; the production fit JSONs carry no matcher fields.

Follow-up round (coordinated-common-set-regen, plan v12; parent recipe rows below cite the earlier plan revision — v12 inherits them verbatim): a coordinated regeneration of scaffold coverage for the 48 character/story-affected cells on one 15,700-conversation target set drawn uniformly (seed 137) from the shared conversation draw, so every affected transfer pair would share one conversation set. Wave 0 re-indexes the parent's admitted scaffolds onto the target set; up to three fresh generation waves (seeds 138–140) per character-conversation pair follow, each judged by the parent's admission instrument (healthy throughout: 13 judge-refusal content drops across ~33,700 judgments, zero truncation). A binding coverage gate sits before any capture, fit, or ladder dispatch — the minimum affected-pair conversation intersection must clear the 4,480 floor (0.8 × 4,480 = 3,584 = d; full target 9,000; plan v12 §7) — and the round ended at this gate (final result below), so no new capture or fits ran and every number above is unchanged. The gate's graded semantics also armed one optional contingency wave (≤1.5 GPU-h; plan v12 §7(b)/(d)); it stayed unrun — wave 3's +706 survivors against the 2,071-conversation gap put the floor beyond one more capped wave, the trajectory read recorded at wave 2. Round artifacts are count- and ledger-grade (gate report, projection, attempt ledger); scaffold text stays on the HF prefix per the real-world-corpus hygiene applied to the samples below.

Follow-up round (reduced-basis-refit-rungs789, plan v16): the fallback read for the refit rungs the coverage abort left under-determined. Rungs 7 (context-side re-map), 8 (answer-side re-map) and 9 (full refit) are recomputed in a train-fold truncated-SVD basis at k\* = 1024 with k ≤ n_train/2 enforced per fold, so each fit is well-posed at its fit dimension rather than against the ambient d = 3,584 — a strictly weaker read than the ambient one the Goal names, declared as such in plan v16. Two identity gates run per shard before any production unit: gate 1 checks the reduced path reduces algebraically to the parent's `_fit_ridge` at full rank (max |Δpred| = 0 on every shard), gate 2 against the parent's committed per-fold values (max |ΔR²| ≤ 8.9e-16). Ten shards — six pair-class × arm shards plus four matched-n shards — produced 1,184 units (864 full-n + 320 matched-n) with zero flagged and zero resumed units. The plan-v16 verdict lattice gates six context calibration cells (the two well-posed classes, boundary and model, × the three rungs) on coverage ≥ 75%, |median Δ| ≤ 15.0 pp and Spearman ρ ≥ 0.50, plus a below-floor in-band criterion (≥ 90.6% of below-floor pairs inside the 2.5–97.5 percentile band of the 160 matched-n above-floor pairs) and a margin ≥ 2.5 pp with a positive bootstrap CI floor; a single over-ceiling Δ cell voids the read. Merge-time bootstraps: 1,000 draws, cluster-resampled over cells. **Census deviation to note when reading unit counts:** the round enumerates the plan-§6 comparison classes with `_pair_class` over the 56 staged cells — boundary 40 / prose 120 / twobytwo 224 / model 48, with 160 of the 224 twobytwo pairs above the 4,480 ambient floor — which differs from the committed composition artifact's own classifier (56 / 96 / 208 / 48) because that artifact buckets by which axes differ over a constructed 48-cell set. The two conventions disagree on 16 on-policy form-swap pairs (routed to twobytwo here, counted under cross-framing there) and on 24 transposed-cell prose pairs the artifact's file set lacked; the merge's completeness assert is therefore run against a census derived from the staged cell set, not from those constants. Per-unit wall time is sub-quadratic in n (best-identified exponent ≈ 1.3–1.4, below 2 on all ten shards) with a non-negligible n-independent component, so the round's n²-scaled pre-flight wall projection under-projects by a measured 7–40%; projections are one-shot and pre-flight, so no fence value here is an enforced ceiling.

Follow-up rounds (user-gaps extension + framing-5 reinstatement; user-chat inline rounds, 2026-08-12/13): four analyses over the banked lattice, all under the shared production fold map (26,889 conversations, K = 5, seed 137), the production ridge estimator (GCV, degrees-of-freedom cap 0.9), and both mapping arms; the substrate is the banked on-policy + inserted teacher-forced activations at layer 19. (1) Cross-cell context→context grid: for each of 232 ordered cell pairs (assistant framing pairs, character↔character, character↔assistant), a ridge map from the source cell's context vector to the target cell's context vector on the pair's shared conversations (joins 2,939–11,901), with a shuffled null and the identity-plus-bias baseline per pair; the prefix arm fits the same pairs prefix→prefix. 50 of the 232 prefix-arm pairs skip (42 constant-input, 8 identical input–output — chat and bare-text renders whose prefix is a constant template header). (2) Pool-then-specialize: one pooled context→answer map fit jointly on all 56 cells, then a per-cell bias, then a rank-k residual correction (k ∈ {8, 32, 128}), each scored on the cells' held-out folds against the banked within-cell ceilings (200 bootstrap draws, fixed full-scored-mean total-variance convention). (3) Transfer-tier rungs on the pooled map, per user directive ("rotation + bias + scaling"): the pooled map plus a context-cloud offset, an answer-cloud offset, a per-cell bias refit, a global scale, an orthogonal rotation, and rotation + scale, per cell and arm; 8 of 112 units (constant-input prefix chat cells) are gain/rotation-degenerate and substitute the bias rung, recorded per unit. (4) Framing 5 (`indirect`, reported speech — boundary `{Name} replied that {continuation}`, narrator voice, no quotation): reinstated on-policy only by user directive — no inserted arm exists by construction, since a reported-speech render paraphrases the answer and cannot splice it deterministically. 10 cells (4 characters + assistant × both models) × 8,000 rows generated at a fixed opener (per-cell digit-start 0.03–0.15%; cap-hit 3–6 rows per 8,000-row cell, under the 2% regen trigger), teacher-forced capture at layer 19, within-cell ceiling fits under the production fold map, and the 9-rung ladder over the class-valid pair classes only — cross-character (24 ordered pairs per arm at 2,939–3,268-conversation intersections) and cross-model (10 pairs per arm at 8,000). Cross-framing pairs into or out of the indirect cells were deliberately not run, so the authorship × presentation decomposition and every inserted-arm read (including the plan-§6 cross-framing ladder class) do not extend to this framing.

Confound disclosure (bare-label trailing space, found while fixing the framing-5 opener): the shipped bare-label template renders its boundary as the speaker name, a colon, and a trailing space. Qwen's byte-pair encoder encodes words together with their leading space, so a prompt ending in a bare space forces an opening token that does not begin with one — digits are the largest such class. Measured on banked on-policy outputs (Dana × instruct, n = 8,000 per form): bare-label 23.2% digit-start and 62.0% leading-space onsets, against 1.1% / 0.1% for the attributed-quote form (whose boundary ends on a quote character). Existing on-policy bare-label comparisons — per-cell ceilings and transfer rungs — therefore partly measure a tokenization artifact rather than a framing effect. The template is not retro-fixed (rewriting it would invalidate completed cells), and the artifact's propagation into the fitted maps is not yet quantified. The indirect framing's opener was fixed before its production run: an A/B probe on 300 matched scaffolds measured 58.0% digit-start with the trailing space against 0.0% without, and the production cells run 0.03–0.15%.

**Training:** N/A — no model training. All maps are closed-form ridge fits on frozen-model activations.

**Evaluation:** per cell, the held-out R² of a ridge map from the context vector to the answer vector at layer 19 of the residual stream (both models are 28-layer, d = 3,584). The context vector is the last-token state at the read position before the answer begins; the answer vector is the token-mean over the answer span; the prefix arm instead maps the last-token state before the question (prefix = everything before the user query; context = prefix + query — both arms fit per cell). Fits use generalized-cross-validation ridge with a degrees-of-freedom cap of 0.9 and 5 conversation-grouped folds from one shared fold map (26,889 conversations, seed 137) reused by every cell, so held-out sets are identical across cells. Controls per cell: shuffled-answer matched-capacity null (100 draws per fold), identity-plus-bias baseline (prediction = context vector + train-fold mean offset), nearest-neighbor retrieval (cosine and euclidean, accuracy at k ∈ {1, 5, 10}, chance = k over the ~1,996-row held-out pool), a reduced-basis diagnostic (train-fold PCA, k = 1024), and a conversation-level bootstrap on each fold (200 draws). Transfer: for each of 408 ordered cell pairs (both arms), a 9-rung ladder applies the source-fitted map to the target with increasing adaptation — direct, context/answer mean offsets, bias refit, global scale, orthogonal rotation, context-side linear re-map, answer-side linear re-map, full refit — each rung scored on the target's held-out fold and normalized by the target's own ceiling, on the pair's equalized conversation intersection. Intersections differ by pair class: boundary-swap pairs share 8,000–11,901 conversations and model-swap pairs 7,999–11,901, but prose-swap pairs share only 2,939–4,450 (152 of the 408 context-arm pairs sit below 4,450). At prose-swap intersections the three refit rungs (context re-map, answer re-map, full refit) train at n_train = 0.8 × intersection, below d = 3,584 — an under-determined regime — so those rungs are read as descriptive only; every headline-carrying read (direct rungs everywhere, boundary- and model-class refit rungs) is well-posed. The framing-5 ladder inherits the same convention: its cross-model pairs are well-posed at every rung, its cross-character refit rungs are descriptive-only.

**Data extraction:** teacher-forced forward passes on the rendered final text (per-segment token-id concatenation with offset-mapped span positions), capturing the context, prefix, and answer-mean vectors per row: 451,414 rows across the 48 inserted/on-policy cells plus 64,000 transposed-cell rows (515,414 total) with zero failures (2h04m on 2× H100); the framing-5 round adds 80,000 rows (10 cells × 8,000, zero failures) captured on 1× H100. Fits and ladders ran on CPU pods (measured pilots: 329 s and 49 s per unit-fold, peak RSS ~21 GiB, routed off the shared VM per the pilot gates). Planned-vs-actual deviations: (1) the plan's equalize-down requirement (every compared cell trained on the same row count) was not applied to the committed per-cell fits — each cell was fit on its own full row set (8,000–11,901 rows; the fit JSONs record `single_cell_group: true`), while the ladder applied per-pair equalization as planned; a companion refit of the largest cell subsampled from 11,901 to 8,000 rows moved its ceiling by only 0.013 (0.492 full vs 0.480 subsampled), bounding this confound. (2) The plan's per-cell answer-length diagnostics (kill gate at KS D > 0.30) never evaluated in-run — the capture diagnostics carrying per-row lengths were not persisted off-pod, so the gate fail-opened in all 56 fits; this round recomputes the read from persisted answer text (final result below). (3) The conversation-intersection kill gate (floor 4,480) was evaluated on pre-transposed-cell groups and cleared everywhere (minimum 7,999, per the per-cell fit kill-gate reports). (4) The Phase C generation cap was raised on three of the eight form × model generation legs: all legs first ran at max_new_tokens 2,048; the cap-hit trigger (a cell with over 2% of rows ending at the cap is fully re-generated at twice the cap) fired on bare-text under both models and on chat under the base model, and those three cells were re-generated at 4,096 — the footer's cap-hit regen. Bare-text base cleared at the raised cap (1.1% capped); the 42.5% and 5.6% rates the truncation analysis works with are the residual rates at 4,096.
