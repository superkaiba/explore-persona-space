# Methodology — issue 2587: Qwen3.5-9B (thinking disabled) replication of the #2564 minimal-pair discrimination battery with a fresh 25,000-context ridge map


**Design:** 1,080 single-turn contexts: the parent's 984 bank strings carried over character-identical to preserve the comparison surface (12 everyday advice questions crossed with 39 instruction values over 9 axes plus wording paraphrases, empty-system anchors and query variants; the strings were authored in the parent's user-approved plan by the Claude Code planning agent, not by the evaluated model), plus 96 pilot contexts transcribed verbatim from the committed pilot-definition script (3 answer-language instructions, covering English, Chinese and Spanish, and 24 one-word query swap pairs). From these, 2,874 pairs in 8 classes (900 value swaps, 504 installs, 864 wording-family swaps, 468 instruction paraphrases, 66 query-content, 36 query-form, 12 query-paraphrase, 24 one-word swaps). Each context gets 10 sampled responses; the observed shift per pair is the difference of the two contexts' 10-draw-mean answer states.

The fresh 9B map is a ridge regression from the context-end last-token state to the answer span mean, fit per layer on 25,000 real single-turn LMSYS user prompts (with 400 validation, 1,000 test and 998 WildChat transfer rows; the pinned split lists dropped exactly one over-length WildChat row under the 9B tokenizer), the layer chosen by validation R² and frozen before any battery read. The matched-data 7B comparison (labeled "matched-n" in figure legends) refits the parent's banked activation store at layer 19 on exactly the same 25,000/400/1,000 row identities and applies it to the parent's banked battery states, so both sides see identical fit and score populations (matched rows and estimator family, not matched effective complexity: the two map dimensions differ, 4,096 vs 3,584); a port-parity gate first reproduced the committed 7B reference fit with absolute deviation exactly 0.

Cross-model contrasts are carrier-paired (one shared 12-carrier resample per bootstrap draw), with symmetric fire-gating: a value that failed its compliance check on either model drops from both sides; an axis where either side is compliance-limited falls back to all shared primary pairs and is recorded as a fallback read, not a symmetric headline read. Persona keeps 12 of 120 pairs under symmetric gating, hedging 0 of 12 and stance 0 of 120, because the parent's own base wordings failed those compliance floors; among the axes that keep their symmetric headline read, user-profile and format each keep 36 of 120 pairs, and the remaining six keep all their shared pairs.

The whole run used a pod-side model environment upgrade (the repo pins cannot load this model family); a compatibility smoke gate (model load, hook-path parity at three depths, closed-empty-think render asserts, a 500-row generation shard and a tiny end-to-end battery) passed before any production wave. One deviation from the planned deliverables: the bank manifest JSON was never materialized pod-side (a planned-deliverable gap, not a missing condition; every planned cell ran); it was recovered deterministically on the VM by re-running the committed bank module, with all build gates passing (grid completeness, exact string identity of non-varied slots, per-string 9B token counts, changed-token floors, render gates).

**Evaluation:** per axis and per map (fresh 9B map, its identity-plus-learned-bias baseline, whose learned bias cancels exactly in paired differences, and the matched-data 7B refit): direction cosine of predicted vs observed answer-state differences against a carrier- and class-preserving derangement null and a 20-split split-half reliability ceiling; magnitude calibration as a ratio to the map's global slope; axis-identity and cross-wording-family consistency; an answer-text third space (Qwen3-Embedding-8B embeddings of the completions; this round's rank reads over it are committed as `text_space_rank_reads.json`); and pair retrieval among all 2,874 oriented pairs (chance 0.00035 at rank 1). Observed-space separation uses one common statistic across all 13 axes (mean observed-shift norm over mean split-half noise norm), so the pilot axes are placeable in the same ranking.

Fit-side baselines per layer: train-mean, identity-copy, scaled-identity, identity-plus-learned-bias floors, a shuffled-pairing refit null, nearest-neighbor retrieval of the true answer state (cosine and euclidean, chance stated), a two-draw reliability ceiling, and the WildChat transfer fold as the held-out-corpus read. The map-quality comparison is decided by the plan's rule on the signed matched-data difference at the frozen layer pair over the shared 1,000 test rows, with a paired test-row bootstrap; the profile-sharing comparison is decided on two rank-correlation reads (observed separation and map direction) plus a screened sign-disagreement clause; the screen is the eight ceiling-cleared axes where both models' bootstrap intervals exclude zero, and persona, hedging and stance fall outside it.

The manipulation check judged 1,392 compliance draws (completeness 98.2%: 6 API refusal draws and 19 dropped, all 25 on the format axis, leaving 4 of its 10 wordings undetermined; format passes its floor exactly at 3 of 5 fired base values) plus 72 answer-language draws (completeness 1.0) at 2 draws per value-carrier cell, and checked the marker-word and injected-name axes programmatically over all 10 draws (denominator 120). Each judged check scores 0-100 under one of two rubrics (general instruction compliance, and a language rubric for the answer-language axis); a per-draw score of at least 50 counts as complying, a value fires at 70% of its 24 checks, and an axis passes at ceil(0.6 × width) fired base values; the 61 judged wordings resolve as 55 fired, 2 not fired and 4 undetermined. Compliance is judge-defined: the non-fired pool contains borderline rows, three of which are quoted verbatim in the sample block below.

A language-intrusion audit (committed as a counts-only artifact) scanned all 10,800 battery rollouts for CJK script: 821 rows match, of which 167 sit in the answer-language cell whose instructed-Chinese value makes CJK content compliance (110 of its 120 Chinese-value rows are majority-Chinese, correctly); outside that cell the intrusion is 2–3 stray characters mid-English text (median 2–3 per intruded row; 3 rows outside the answer-language cell carry 10 or more, all persona, plus 1 more in that cell's Spanish value). An exact per-check join of the intrusion scan with the judge verdicts (committed as `intrusion_judge_join.json`, superseding the earlier rounds' worst-case bound) finds 124 intruded judged checks, 117 scored complying; zeroing them keeps every axis floor and every programmatic verdict and flips exactly two of the 57 determinate judged wordings: one persona paraphrase wording (24 of 24 complying, 8 intruded, recounting to 16 against the 17-check bar) and one stance wording (17 of 24, 4 intruded complying, recounting to 13). Exactly those two fire decisions are convention-dependent; the nearest survivor, a content-constraint paraphrase wording (18 of 24 complying, recounting to 17), lands exactly at the 17-check bar and keeps firing; the answer-language Chinese value also flips under blind zeroing, but its CJK content is the instructed behavior, not intrusion.

A follow-up per-draw recount, committed as `intrusion_excluded_recount.json` with 13 per-axis checkpoint JSONs, removes the 711 non-compliant intruded rollouts from the 9B per-context tail means and split-half noise at the frozen layer (the 110 instructed-Chinese rows stay in as compliant; no context loses all its draws), with pair selections, fire verdicts and the 7B side frozen to the committed run. Its invariants are pinned by hard asserts (per-cell and per-value scan parity against the committed intrusion scan, frozen pair identities, exclusion counts), its parity gates reproduce the committed unexcluded separations and deltas before exclusion is applied (realized maximum absolute deviation exactly 0.0 on every gate, recorded in the artifact's verification block), and it recounts the two pilot axes (answer-language separation 6.51 to 5.97, one-word query swap 4.29 to 4.11, both ranks unchanged). The map-fit corpus was not CJK-scanned: it is a mixed-language real-user corpus where CJK answers to CJK prompts are on-distribution behavior, not intrusion. Think-tag scans: 1 of 10,800 battery rows (excluded from reads) and 0 of 29,398 map-fit rows.

**Data extraction:** pod phases (length scan, map generation and fp32 capture, 32-layer fits, battery generation, capture and text embedding, matched 7B refit, leak/cap-hit harvest) ran on `pod-2587` (2× H100, about 9.4 h wall) with per-phase sentinels; rollout text, capture stores, ridge payloads and predictions uploaded to the HF data repo before the gated termination (row counts reconciled, out-root swept). Judging (Batch API), the cross-model statistics, all figures, the intrusion scan and this round's text-space rank reads ran on the VM from the committed artifacts.
