# Methodology — issue 1689: 21-condition speaker x framing lattice for the context-to-answer map, 9-rung mapping-similarity ladder at L19 (Qwen2.5-7B base + instruct)

**Design:** N=3,800 two-turn LMSYS conversations (English, exactly 2 turns, token-budget feasible under the tightest framing — chat template plus persona header) rendered into 21 conditions: 7 identities (assistant; user with real-LMSYS / haiku-simulated / self-generated second turns; HELIOS, an AI character; Wren, an assistant-like human; Dana, an ordinary person) crossed with 3 framings (chat template; plain text; narrative story). All non-user completions are generated on-policy by the measured model and judge-filtered; each conversation appears up to 3 times in the generation files and the ladder keeps the first judge-kept row per conversation. Activations are captured teacher-forced at layer 19 (layers 14/18/26 captured but never fit — documented scope shrinkage): X_prefix = end of everything before the second user turn; X_context = one token past the end of the second user turn; Y = end of the answer span. For user cells the answer span IS the second user turn, so X_context equals Y by construction (identity+bias baseline R² = 1.0 exactly) — every user-cell context-arm read is construct-invalid, and in plain-text framing the user-cell prefix boundary also collapses onto the answer end (both arms invalid). Ordered pairs (126 per model): a 40-pair spoke through assistant-chat, within-identity framing pairs, within-framing identity pairs, and user-provenance pairs; both arms fit for every pair. A zero-GPU follow-up round (2026-07-29) added the plan addendum's per-pair Procrustes operator-cosine battery over the same layer-19 store. A second zero-GPU follow-up round (2026-07-29, `wider-lambda-ceilings`) refit all 84 per-cell layer-19 within-cell ceilings on a ridge grid widened three decades, with a conditional ladder re-read for any pair whose ceiling moved (none did). A third follow-up round (2026-07-29, `derived-vs-free-answer-map`, user-chat scope from a mentor-posed question) ran three fit batteries over the same layer-19 store pin. First, per ordered pair-arm, a free answer-space map `B_free` (d²+d parameters) compared against two maps derived from a fitted affine context-space transfer `M` — `B_derived = W_S M W_S⁺` with a derived bias (one shared readout `W_S`) and `B_derived2 = W_T M W_S⁺` (source and target readouts) — verdicted per unit by `g1 = R²(B_derived) − 0.9·R²(B_free)` and `g2 = R²(B_derived2) − 0.9·R²(B_free)` into four disjoint classes (shared-readout-supported `g1 ≥ 0`; readout-changed `g1 < 0` and `g2 ≥ 0`; transfer-map-insufficient otherwise; free-map-uninformative — `R²(B_free)` below the identity+bias baseline — excluded from counts), with `R²(B_derived)` the max over truncation ranks 32/128/512/eff-rank and a fixed-eff-rank companion lattice. Second, a structural battery over `M` (nested class ladder translation → scaled/rotated translation → rank-k → full affine, distance from identity, correction-direction vs diff-of-means alignment, cross-pair top-32 correction-subspace overlap vs a random-subspace null) plus a rank-k reconciliation sweep (k = 1..128) for pairs the parent left at rung ≥ 7. Third, the 21 base-instruct same-condition pairs × 2 directions through the parent 9-rung ladder plus both batteries. Coverage defect and fix (this round): the within-model batteries initially keyed per-unit checkpoints by (pair, arm) without the model, so the second model's unit resumed on the first model's checkpoint (241 base, 11 instruct of 504 computed). A fix round qualified the unit keys by model, migrated the surviving files, and made the re-merged summaries fail loud on duplicate keys; a re-sweep then completed the remaining units — both within-model batteries now report 504 of 504 complete (0 failed, 0 key-mismatched), cross-model unit keys carried both models throughout (84 of 84), and the ≥95%-of-588-units completeness criterion is met (588 of 588). A fourth follow-up round (2026-07-30, `wellposed-shared-readout`) re-ran all three batteries with every operator fit inside a per-(pair, arm, fold) train-fold-only shared PCA basis of rank k = min(1024, floor of half the smallest train fold) — so n_train ≥ 2k holds on every fold by construction, where the ambient rounds fit at n_train below d = 3,584 — one basis per side shared across source and target, the primary verdict R² read in reduced coordinates with the ambient-reconstruction companion and held-out captured-variance fraction persisted per unit; its one crash-fix round staged the parent per-unit inputs from an HF mirror (the SLURM lane's rsync ships no committed `eval_results/`), after which the four batteries completed 504 + 504 + 84 + 84 of expected units with the ambient no-op parity gate at zero difference. All round counts in this body come from the per-unit JSONs via the digests (`analyzer/dvf_unit_digest.csv`; well-posed round, `analyzer/dvf_wellposed_paired_digest.csv`).

**Real-u2-capture round (follow-up, 2026-08-03/04):** a fifth follow-up round replaces the parent user arm's `lmsys` provenance cells, which the parent recapture round documented as a CONSTANT 34-character fallback string `LMSYS_CONST_U2 = "Can you say a bit more about that?"` (parent realized-defect (c), `scripts/issue1689_user_slot_render.py` L24-34; the parent's "real LMSYS" arm never carried real LMSYS text). Twelve fresh cells — 2 models × 3 framings × 2 u2 provenances (`realu2` from LMSYS-Chat-1M + WildChat-1M multi-turn conversations; `haikuu2` from a Claude Haiku 4.5 companion arm generated on the SAME 3,800 conversations) — capture teacher-forced activations at layer 19 through the parent `user_slot_recapture` rig at revision `d1010a25f81ce184f68a9cc0ed49bce9736b80dd`, then fit both mapping arms (prefix and context) with the parent's estimator primitives. The single manipulated variable versus the parent recapture round is the u2 provenance INPUT; every other hyperparameter (models, layer, framings, targets, folds, seed, ridge grid, inner-CV selector, null draws, reduced-basis rank rule, baseline set) is inherited byte-for-byte. Conversation-level pairing exists WITHIN this round (real-u2 vs haiku-u2 on the same 3,800 conversations, the strongest reveal); the parent's constant-fallback arm cannot enter the CONVERSATION-level pairing because its rows are different (constant u2, same u1+a1 but a different corpus). The `user_lmsys_naturalistic` context-arm construct-invalidity caveat inherited from the parent (X_context ≡ Y bit-identical when u2 IS the answer) also applies to the real-u2 and haiku-u2 arms — every user-cell context-arm read is construct-invalid the same way; the round flags this on the naturalistic-framing haikuu2 cells (`context_construct_invalid` set in `per_cell_summary.json`). Conciseness note: the body carries 15 result sections for the 126-pair lattice, so the total-prose-budget WARN, residual per-result word-cap and paragraph sentence-cap WARNs, and over-30-word Takeaways bullet WARNs are acknowledged deliberately, as is the deliberate link (not embed) of the base-model heatmap beside its instruct sibling, and the abbreviated condition labels rendered inside the cross-model scatter (space constraints; the conditions are defined in plain English under Design), and the two driver-rendered well-posed round figures ship without plot sidecars (their per-unit data is the committed paired digest).

**Evaluation:** For each ordered pair (source S, target T) and arm, the source ridge map is corrected by 9 successively stronger tiers — 1 direct, 2 context offset, 3 answer offset, 4 bias refit, 5 global scale, 6 orthogonal rotation, 7 context-side ridge reparameterization (A-only), 8 answer-side (B-only), 9 full A·M·B — each read as held-out R² on T against the reconciliation bar. The fit script codes "no rung reconciles" as rung 9; this analysis separates the two (an explicit no-reconcile code) and adds recovery fractions (rung R² divided by the target's within-cell ceiling). The matched-capacity null is degenerate for the rung statistic: shuffling answers drives the target ceiling non-positive, the bar becomes vacuous, and every null draw reads rung 1 (null p97.5 = 1.0 on 504 of 504 pair-arms) — a rung-1 observation is therefore not separable from the null by the ordinal alone, and discrimination rests on the ceilings and recovery fractions. Identity+learned-bias baseline and kNN retrieval (k in 1/5/10, euclidean and cosine, chance stated per pool) are reported per cell in `percell/`; the identity+bias baseline is what exposes the degenerate user arms. Robustness check (resolved by the wider-grid round): the inner-CV ridge λ selection pinned at the grid ceiling (λ = 10^4) in 265 of 420 layer-19 fold-fits; refitting all 84 cell-arms with the ceiling raised to 10^7 moved no ceiling by more than 0.02 and left no fold-fit selection above 10^5, so neither the ceilings nor the rung reads that inherit them are grid-limited (see the wider-grid result). No p-values are computed by the rig; uncertainty reads come from the 200-draw conversation-level bootstrap where present and from cross-pair spread elsewhere. Language-intrusion audit (Qwen under an English eval): CJK-matching rows are 0.4-2.2% of kept completions in the assistant and instruct pools the headline reads rest on, and 6.7-9.4% in base character-chat and base user-on-policy pools (e.g. 50 of 531 HELIOS-chat rows, 34 of 480 user-on-policy-chat rows); no judged-rate headline exists to recount, and the affected pools are already flagged as low-yield. Derived-vs-free round reading conventions: every R² in the round is n_train < d = 3,584 shrunk ridge, read comparatively across map forms within a pair, never as an absolute ceiling; the weakest-class and minimal-k reads are conditional on that estimator (lower-bound-style, not intrinsic-rank claims); the matched-capacity class/rank nulls inherit the parent null's degeneracy (shuffled answers collapse the bar, so a translation-sufficient or low-k observation is not separable from the null by the ordinal alone); the cross-model reads are scoped to model-specific on-policy answer distributions (each side of a cross-model pair carries its own model's answer text). The well-posed re-fit round removes the n_train below d caveat for its own reads: its verdict R² live in per-unit reduced coordinates, conditional on the captured subspace (held-out captured-variance medians 0.30-0.92 across k-bands), and its reduced-coordinate identity+bias read is basis-relative — used as a class-0 screen only, never compared against the ambient identity baseline.

**Real-u2-capture round evaluation conventions (follow-up round):** every fit runs BOTH mapping arms (prefix and context) per the CLAUDE.md standing rule. The context-arm read on user cells inherits the parent's construct-invalidity: X_context is one token past the u2 end and Y is the answer end, which for user cells IS the u2 end — X_context ≡ Y bit-identically, so identity+bias R² = 1.0 exactly and the fitted map is not construct-valid. The naturalistic-framing haikuu2 cells carry the `context_construct_invalid` flag in `per_cell_summary.json` (context arm R² 0.98 signals a saturated / degenerate construct — treated as invalid, not a headline result). The prefix arm is the load-bearing read on user cells. n_train per fold is ≈ 3,800 × 0.8 = 3,040 (no ×3 row duplication as in the parent's `user_slot_recapture` — real u2 is one row per conversation), so the reduced-basis rank rule `k = min(1024, ⌊3,040/2⌋)` saturates at k=1024 across every fold, matching the parent's cap regime; the ambient-basis fit sits n_train < d = 3,584 and is estimation-degenerate (identity+bias baseline on the reduced basis is the load-bearing estimator-validity gate, `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md` § inline estimator-validity duty). Provenance-transfer pair fits (`M_real→haiku` and `M_haiku→real` per framing × model × arm, on the SAME conv_id rows) walk the parent's 9-rung ladder. First-vs-second-turn control on the real arm: R²(u1_end → prev_turn_end) vs R²(prev_turn_end → u2_end) per framing × model. Δrung against the parent constant-fallback arm is read cross-round paired at (framing, direction, model), with the selection-symmetric null band computed by walking the same max over the SHUFFLED rung distribution (40 draws, Y-shuffle within folds). The two committed eval outputs are `per_cell_summary.json` (per-cell headline R²s + baselines + kNN + `context_construct_invalid` flags) and `rung_reached_matrix.json` (rung_reached grain framing × model × direction × arm) at commit `376200beb71839c7583168cda0223629b71fd565`.
