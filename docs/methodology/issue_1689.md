# Methodology — issue 1689: 21-condition speaker × framing lattice for the context-to-answer map, 9-rung mapping-similarity ladder at L19 (Qwen2.5-7B base + instruct)

**Design:** N=3,800 two-turn LMSYS conversations (English, exactly 2 turns, token-budget feasible under the tightest framing — chat template plus persona header) rendered into 21 conditions: 7 identities (assistant; user with real-LMSYS / haiku-simulated / self-generated second turns; HELIOS, an AI character; Wren, an assistant-like human; Dana, an ordinary person) crossed with 3 framings (chat template; plain text; narrative story). All non-user completions are generated on-policy by the measured model and judge-filtered; each conversation appears up to 3 times in the generation files and the ladder keeps the first judge-kept row per conversation. Activations are captured teacher-forced at layer 19 (layers 14/18/26 captured but never fit — documented scope shrinkage): X_prefix = end of everything before the second user turn; X_context = one token past the end of the second user turn; Y = end of the answer span. For user cells the answer span IS the second user turn, so X_context equals Y by construction (identity+bias baseline R² = 1.0 exactly) — every user-cell context-arm read is construct-invalid, and in plain-text framing the user-cell prefix boundary also collapses onto the answer end (both arms invalid). Ordered pairs (126 per model): a 40-pair spoke through assistant-chat, within-identity framing pairs, within-framing identity pairs, and user-provenance pairs; both arms fit for every pair. A zero-GPU follow-up round (2026-07-29) added the plan addendum's per-pair Procrustes operator-cosine battery over the same layer-19 store. A second zero-GPU follow-up round (2026-07-29, `wider-lambda-ceilings`) refit all 84 per-cell layer-19 within-cell ceilings on a ridge grid widened three decades, with a conditional ladder re-read for any pair whose ceiling moved (none did). A third follow-up round (2026-07-29, `derived-vs-free-answer-map`, user-chat scope from a mentor-posed question) ran three fit batteries over the same layer-19 store pin. First, per ordered pair-arm, a free answer-space map `B_free` (d²+d parameters) compared against two maps derived from a fitted affine context-space transfer `M` — `B_derived = W_S M W_S⁺` with a derived bias (one shared readout `W_S`) and `B_derived2 = W_T M W_S⁺` (source and target readouts) — verdicted per unit by `g1 = R²(B_derived) − 0.9·R²(B_free)` and `g2 = R²(B_derived2) − 0.9·R²(B_free)` into four disjoint classes (shared-readout-supported `g1 ≥ 0`; readout-changed `g1 < 0` and `g2 ≥ 0`; transfer-map-insufficient otherwise; free-map-uninformative — `R²(B_free)` below the identity+bias baseline — excluded from counts), with `R²(B_derived)` the max over truncation ranks 32/128/512/eff-rank and a fixed-eff-rank companion lattice. Second, a structural battery over `M` (nested class ladder translation → scaled/rotated translation → rank-k → full affine, distance from identity, correction-direction vs diff-of-means alignment, cross-pair top-32 correction-subspace overlap vs a random-subspace null) plus a rank-k reconciliation sweep (k = 1..128) for pairs the parent left at rung ≥ 7. Third, the 21 base-instruct same-condition pairs × 2 directions through the parent 9-rung ladder plus both batteries. Coverage defect and fix: the within-model batteries initially keyed per-unit checkpoints by (pair, arm) without the model, so the second model's unit resumed on the first model's checkpoint (241 base, 11 instruct of 504 computed). A fix round qualified the unit keys by model, migrated the surviving files, and made the re-merged summaries fail loud on duplicate keys; a re-sweep then completed the remaining units — both within-model batteries now report 504 of 504 complete (0 failed, 0 key-mismatched), cross-model unit keys carried both models throughout (84 of 84), and the ≥95%-of-588-units completeness criterion is met (588 of 588). A fourth follow-up round (2026-07-30, `wellposed-shared-readout`) re-ran all three batteries with every operator fit inside a per-(pair, arm, fold) train-fold-only shared PCA basis of rank k = min(1024, floor of half the smallest train fold) — so n_train ≥ 2k holds on every fold by construction, where the ambient rounds fit at n_train below d = 3,584 — one basis per side shared across source and target, the primary verdict R² read in reduced coordinates with the ambient-reconstruction companion and held-out captured-variance fraction persisted per unit; its one crash-fix round staged the parent per-unit inputs from an HF mirror (the SLURM lane's rsync ships no committed `eval_results/`), after which the four batteries completed 504 + 504 + 84 + 84 of expected units with the ambient no-op parity gate at zero difference. A fifth follow-up round (2026-08-03, `real-u2-capture`, source user-chat) captured the exact-comparability real second-user-turn arm the parent's `lmsys` cell never provided: ~3,800 real multi-turn LMSYS+WildChat conversations (each carrying a real second user turn), rendered into user-identity cells only under three framings × two models, and captured teacher-forced at layer 19 under the parent's user_slot_recapture conventions; a haiku-simulated u2 companion arm was captured on the SAME 3,800 conversations, giving the within-corpus real-vs-simulated contrast the parent's constant-string lmsys arm never supported. Within-cell ridge (reduced-basis rule where n_train < 2k applies), identity+bias baseline, kNN retrieval, and a 40-draw shuffled-answer null were fit per cell; SLURM job 18578 completed 2026-08-04T00:15:46Z. All round counts in this body come from the per-unit JSONs via the digests (`analyzer/dvf_unit_digest.csv`; well-posed round, `analyzer/dvf_wellposed_paired_digest.csv`; real-u2 round, `eval_results/issue_1689/real_u2_capture/per_cell_summary.json`). Conciseness note: the body carries 19 result sections for the 126-pair lattice and its follow-up rounds, so the total-prose-budget WARN, residual per-result word-cap and paragraph sentence-cap WARNs, and over-30-word Takeaways bullet WARNs are acknowledged deliberately, as is the deliberate link (not embed) of the base-model heatmap beside its instruct sibling, and the abbreviated condition labels rendered inside the cross-model scatter (space constraints; the conditions are defined in plain English under Design), and the two driver-rendered well-posed round figures ship without plot sidecars (their per-unit data is the committed paired digest).

**Evaluation:** For each ordered pair (source S, target T) and arm, the source ridge map is corrected by 9 successively stronger tiers — 1 direct, 2 context offset, 3 answer offset, 4 bias refit, 5 global scale, 6 orthogonal rotation, 7 context-side ridge reparameterization (A-only), 8 answer-side (B-only), 9 full A·M·B — each read as held-out R² on T against the reconciliation bar. The fit script codes "no rung reconciles" as rung 9; this analysis separates the two (an explicit no-reconcile code) and adds recovery fractions (rung R² divided by the target's within-cell ceiling). The matched-capacity null is degenerate for the rung statistic: shuffling answers drives the target ceiling non-positive, the bar becomes vacuous, and every null draw reads rung 1 (null p97.5 = 1.0 on 504 of 504 pair-arms) — a rung-1 observation is therefore not separable from the null by the ordinal alone, and discrimination rests on the ceilings and recovery fractions. Identity+learned-bias baseline and kNN retrieval (k in 1/5/10, euclidean and cosine, chance stated per pool) are reported per cell in `percell/`; the identity+bias baseline is what exposes the degenerate user arms. Robustness check (resolved by the wider-grid round): the inner-CV ridge λ selection pinned at the grid ceiling (λ = 10^4) in 265 of 420 layer-19 fold-fits; refitting all 84 cell-arms with the ceiling raised to 10^7 moved no ceiling by more than 0.02 and left no fold-fit selection above 10^5, so neither the ceilings nor the rung reads that inherit them are grid-limited (see the wider-grid result). No p-values are computed by the rig; uncertainty reads come from the 200-draw conversation-level bootstrap where present and from cross-pair spread elsewhere. Language-intrusion audit (Qwen under an English eval): CJK-matching rows are 0.4-2.2% of kept completions in the assistant and instruct pools the headline reads rest on, and 6.7-9.4% in base character-chat and base user-on-policy pools (e.g. 50 of 531 HELIOS-chat rows, 34 of 480 user-on-policy-chat rows); no judged-rate headline exists to recount, and the affected pools are already flagged as low-yield. Derived-vs-free round reading conventions: every R² in the round is n_train < d = 3,584 shrunk ridge, read comparatively across map forms within a pair, never as an absolute ceiling; the weakest-class and minimal-k reads are conditional on that estimator (lower-bound-style, not intrinsic-rank claims); the matched-capacity class/rank nulls inherit the parent null's degeneracy (shuffled answers collapse the bar, so a translation-sufficient or low-k observation is not separable from the null by the ordinal alone); the cross-model reads are scoped to model-specific on-policy answer distributions (each side of a cross-model pair carries its own model's answer text). The well-posed re-fit round removes the n_train below d caveat for its own reads: its verdict R² live in per-unit reduced coordinates, conditional on the captured subspace (held-out captured-variance medians 0.30-0.92 across k-bands), and its reduced-coordinate identity+bias read is basis-relative — used as a class-0 screen only, never compared against the ambient identity baseline. Real-u2 capture round reading conventions: within-cell ridge R² is read against the 40-draw matched-capacity null's 97.5th percentile (~-0.03 nats across cells; every observed prefix R² exceeds it by ≥0.10, so the maps are learning) and against the identity+bias baseline (deeply negative on the prefix arm, so the map is not a trivial coordinate shift). Two of six framings — naturalistic (plain-text) on both models — have context-arm construct invalidity because the user_slot_recapture prefix boundary collapses onto the answer end there (context R² ≥ 0.93, identity+bias-driven); treat those context reads as self-prediction, not operator learning, and rely on the prefix arm.

**Real-u2 round: unaddressed confounders (Result 11 alternative explanations, cap-excluded here):** three structural confounders sit between the observed prefix-R² gap and any generator-side mechanism claim, each rule-out-able as a follow-up read on the persisted round artifacts. First, **length-tail confound.** Real u2 lengths (`real_multiturn_first_exchange.jsonl` at HF revision `068694e11b`) are heavy-tailed — mean 316 chars, median 63, p10 16, p90 614, max 27,144 — while Haiku u2 lengths (`haiku_u2.jsonl`, same revision) are unimodal-narrow — mean 168, median 139, p10 74, p90 285, max 1,107; the two distributions barely overlap in shape (structural scan over all 3,800 conversations). A ridge fit onto a more-concentrated target reports higher held-out R² almost by definition, so length matching (per-conversation subsampling to matched-length pairs, or per-length-decile R²) would carry a substantial fraction of the Δ before any generator property is invoked. Second, **refusal-template artifact.** 177 of 3,800 haiku u2 rows (4.66%) match a refusal-template regex — `I can[' ]?t (continue|help|write|generate|do|provide|assist|do this|proceed|comply)|I'm not able to|I won't|I cannot|violates my|usage polic|I cannot fulfill|I am unable` — over the harmful/adult-content LMSYS narratives; these refusal rows are stereotyped, fixed-template targets that a linear map recovers more easily than the raw underlying user text (refusal-row length mean 491 chars, median 472, vs the haiku-arm median 139 chars overall — refusal rows are 3× longer than the median haiku reply while replacing very-short real u2 turns). Rule out: recompute the Δ on the haiku-non-refusal subset (≈3,623 rows) against the matched conv_ids in real u2. Third, **bimodal corpus mix.** The corpus is 1,923 LMSYS + 1,877 WildChat rows (conv_id positional match 3,800/3,800 across provenances, so the pairing is symmetric); the LMSYS half is dominated by narrative "continue" bumpers and NAME-scrubbed continuations, the WildChat half by follow-up Q&A. The aggregate real-u2 distribution is bimodal over two structurally different sub-corpora and the linear map fits a mixture. Rule out: per-corpus split of the same reads (LMSYS-only vs WildChat-only R²). Related internal inconsistency: this fold's Methodology (paragraph above) flags plain-text framing as user-prefix boundary-collapse construct-suspect on BOTH arms; the JSON's `prefix_arm.construct_invalid` flag reads False on those two cells (only the context arm carries the JSON flag) — the n=4 exclude-naturalistic mean in the Result 11 takeaway is the discipline-consistent read, the n=6 mean is quoted for full-cell transparency; a durable fix reconciles either the flag or the caveat.
