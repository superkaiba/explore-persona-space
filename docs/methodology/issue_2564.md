# Methodology — issue 2564


**Design:** 984 single-turn contexts: 12 everyday advice questions (carriers) crossed with 39 instruction values spanning 9 axes (tone, output format, hedging level, marker-word insertion, assumed persona, stance, content constraint, injected user name, user description), each with one wording paraphrase, plus an empty-system anchor and query-content/query-form variants. The carrier questions and instruction values were authored directly in the approved plan by the Claude Code planning agent (an Anthropic Claude model rather than the evaluated Qwen model; the specific model id was not persisted in the plan or bank provenance), approved by the user, and transcribed verbatim into the bank with no text-generation API calls. They are deliberately constructed so that each pair differs by one token-matched manipulation; that control requirement is why constructed rather than real-world text is used. From these, 2,778 pairs in 7 classes: value swap (864), install vs empty system (468), instruction-wording paraphrase (468), family swap across wording families (864), query content (66), query form (36), query paraphrase (12). Each context gets K=10 sampled responses; the observed shift for a pair is the difference of the two contexts' 10-draw-mean answer representations at layer 19. Three predictors score identical rows: the frozen single-turn ridge map under test (primary), its frozen multi-turn twin, and identity pass-through (the map's learned bias cancels exactly in differences; verified numerically to 3.6e-15 over 32 pairs). Both reused maps are ridge regressions from the context-end last-token hidden state to the mean answer-token activation at layer 19, applied frozen with the penalties stored in their payloads (no refitting here). The single-turn map was fit on 963,444 real single-turn user contexts (529,085 LMSYS plus 434,359 WildChat), penalty selected on a pinned 400-row validation split from a 23-point log-spaced grid spanning 1e-3 to 1e8 (selected value 0.001, the grid's low edge). The multi-turn twin was fit on 87,795 training conversations from 99,127 captured real multi-turn LMSYS/WildChat conversations under the same fitter family and penalty grid, penalty selected on its pinned 396-row validation split; its answer-mean target includes the end-of-turn tail, matching this battery's tail-inclusive pooling. Headline per-axis reads use only values that passed the compliance gate; gate-failing axes are reported not tested until re-elicited. A same-issue re-elicitation round (plan v7; one manipulated variable, the instruction texts of the three floor-failed axes) re-ran stance, persona, and hedging with compliance-piloted wordings. Its pilot rolled out 23 candidate texts (9 stance, 8 persona, 6 hedging; authored in the approved plan amendment) at 2 draws per carrier (552 checks, judged synchronously) and selected, per construct slot, the best candidate clearing 70% of its 24 checks (ties to the first listed), keeping axis floors anchored to the parent widths: stance kept 4 of 5 slots (no fifth-slot candidate cleared), persona 5, hedging 2. The 22 selected values (each base wording plus one paraphrase) then re-ran the parent production recipe verbatim: 276 contexts (264 value contexts plus 12 fresh bare-system anchors) at K=10 (2,760 rollouts), teacher-forced capture at layer 19, and 672 pairs (204 value swaps, 204 family swaps, 132 installs, 132 wording paraphrases) scored by the same three frozen predictors. Scope notes for the round: the new texts are token-matched within pair but sit at different per-axis token counts than the failed originals (stance 9 to 10, persona 13 to 15, hedging unchanged at 10); the persona wording-paraphrase template is new this round (compliance-selected) and its cross-family read is not measured against the parent template; no answer-text embeddings were collected (representation-space reads only); and the query-form dissociation read takes no re-elicitation input by design.

**Evaluation:** the primary DV per pair is the cosine between the predicted and observed answer-state differences, averaged per axis over swap pairs of fired values, with a carrier-clustered bootstrap interval, a per-axis shuffled-pair null, and a paired map-minus-identity gap under the shared bootstrap. Companions per axis: magnitude calibration (regression slope of predicted on observed shift norms as a ratio to the map's global slope 1.014 over all 2,778 pairs, with a per-value-pair decomposition committed for the content-constraint axis), within-axis consistency (median cosine among the axis's per-carrier observed directions as reproduced by each arm; undefined for the query-content axis, whose pairs are carrier dyads with no carrier-replicated value pair), cross-wording-family consistency, split-half reliability of the observed shift, and a text-embedding third space. Global retrieval asks whether each predicted difference finds its own pair among all 2,778 under cosine and euclidean distance (identity baseline and kNN reads reported per the project's representation-mapping rule), with the per-pair rank of every true pair recomputed from the committed prediction tensors and validated against the committed accuracies before plotting. The manipulation check judges 2 draws per (value, carrier) for the 7 judged axes (1,392 draws, completeness 0.981, 16 API refusal draws + 11 dropped) and checks all 10 draws programmatically for the marker-word and injected-name axes (word/name containment, denominator 120). A language-intrusion audit scans every rollout and the judged pool for CJK script and recomputes all fire verdicts with intruded draws zeroed and excluded; its per-arm counts, judged-pool cross-tab, and full recount table are committed alongside the eval JSONs. A zero-GPU follow-up recount re-derives every per-axis observed delta from the per-draw answer summaries with the intruded draws excluded and recomputes all 132 direction reads (4 read kinds, 3 predictors, 11 axes); its as-scored variant reproduces the committed reads to a maximum absolute difference of 5.4e-10 (observed-delta store: exactly 0 in fp32). The user-description robust-values read (the two values that fire under every intrusion convention) is persisted in the same recount JSON. The re-elicitation round reuses the identical statistics on its own 672-pair grid; its manipulation check judges 2 draws per (value, carrier) for all 22 values (528 checks, completeness 1.0, 0 refusals; one transport-lost draw recovered by a synchronous re-issue round, no batch/sync split field persisted). Comply-threshold sensitivity for the round: at 50% all three axis floors hold; at 90% persona holds (4 of 5 values) while stance (1 of 4) and hedging (1 of 2) drop. That round's language-intrusion recount, specified by the round recipe as the parent's convention but not emitted by the round pipeline, was recomputed at fold time from the committed rollout shards and judge scores and persisted as a committed recount artifact (linked in the footer): 130 of 2,760 rollouts (4.7%; persona 80 of 1,200, stance 31 of 960, hedging 14 of 480, bare-system anchors 5 of 120) and 30 of 528 judged checks (5.7%; 17 complying, 13 non-complying) contain CJK script. Recomputing every per-value fire verdict with intruded checks zeroed and excluded flips only the "Make the strongest possible case for each side equally" stance value (18 of 24 as scored, 16 of 24 zeroed, 16 of 21 excluded), leaving every axis floor met under all three conventions, and re-deriving the observed swap deltas from the per-draw answer summaries with intruded draws excluded reproduces the committed as-scored direction means to 4 decimal places while moving none by more than 0.002.

**Data extraction:** bank build and gate battery (7 gates, all PASS) on the VM; generation plus teacher-forced capture pod-side (capture wall 3,352 s); per-cell answer summaries, context states, text embeddings, and predictions uploaded to the HF data repo before pod release; judging via the Batch API and all statistics off-pod from the committed artifacts. The identity-cancellation assert and the fixed-answer equivalence gates ran in production, not only under smoke.
