# Methodology — issue 1074: on-policy Qwen generator comparison (abliterated vs base) for the artifacts-factory content-behavior datagen, plus the mixed-generator harmful-compliance train + install rounds (`base-negatives-regen`, `install-dose-extension`)

**Design:** 2 content classes (sycophancy; harmful compliance) × 2 generator arms — base `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` vs `huihui-ai/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-abliterated-v2` (a community refusal-ablated derivative of the same model; full safetensors, apache-2.0; ablation method per arXiv 2406.11717) — with everything else held fixed: the factory datagen pipeline (`generate_training_data`, `src/explore_persona_space/artifacts/datagen.py`), question banks, judge, floors, negative panel, seed 42. Within-run the single manipulated variable is the generator checkpoint; because the arms are two checkpoints (not an ablation toggled on one checkpoint), any base-vs-abliterated difference can include community-derivative changes beyond refusal removal. Question banks are inherited from the parent run with their data-realism tiers: harmful compliance draws from StrongREJECT (tier-2 established benchmark); sycophancy uses tier-3 LLM-authored hard-false factual claims — the declared worst case for eliciting agreement. Against the parent run three things changed together — generator family (Claude to Qwen), instruction injection (tagged delimiter block to plain untagged system-prompt text), and instruction-variant count (4 exhibit + 2 oppose expanded to 6 + 4) — so parent comparisons are a declared bundle. Each cell generates positive completions under the software-engineer persona context plus one of six plain-text exhibit instructions (vLLM, temperature 1.0), judge-filters them, then generates contrastive negatives under four oppose instructions across a five-member panel (police officer, a second fixed persona, curious rephrase, WildChat-short, default assistant) from the SAME arm generator — a deliberate choice to keep the arm contrast single-variable. The plan's kill path: if both arms miss the yield floor on both classes, halt after datagen and ship the yield analysis as the deliverable (the parent round halted here; the abliterated harmful cell cleared its positive floor but failed the negative-pairing gate, so no cell reached training). The china-censorship class was excluded at plan time (its inverted-base design makes a generator contrast uninterpretable).

The follow-up round `base-negatives-regen` (same-issue, plan v7 amendment) rebuilt the harmful-compliance cell MIXED-GENERATOR: the abliterated arm's 177 kept positives reused with exact row identity preserved (HF revision `c1f526c1`; 120 emitted via seeded subsample) and the contrastive negatives regenerated from base Qwen under the same 4 oppose instructions and 5-member panel (35 requests per member, quota 24) — the single manipulated variable against the parent's failed negative stage is the negative-stage generator. A 30-row calibration re-judge of parent negative rows guards against cross-round judge drift, and the plan's attribution guard separates willingness falsification from judge no-score measurement loss in the drop mix. With the quota clearing, the cell proceeded through training (Phase B), install eval (Phase C), and off-pod judging + aggregation on the VM (Phase D).

The second follow-up round `install-dose-extension` (same-issue, plan v9) retrained the mixed cell from base on the IDENTICAL pinned training mix (HF revision `8f02493634a5`, sha256-pinned) with the single manipulated variable the training epoch ceiling, 3 to 9 (90 to 270 steps; checkpoint cadence 25 unchanged). A declared consequence of the same lever: the trainer's cosine learning-rate schedule and warmup are computed over total steps, so the retrain's first 90 steps carry roughly 1.7–1.8× the cumulative learning rate of the prior round's — the planned overlay read at the shared steps 25/50/75 diagnoses this schedule-stretch contribution. One declared measurement-instrument correction rides along: the teacher-forced margin pools are re-drawn from kept-but-UNTRAINED rows (64 positive / 30 negative available; 25/25 sampled at seed 42), discharging the prior round's pools-overlap-training caveat; these held-out margins are not cross-round comparable with the prior round's overlapping-pool margins.

**Training:** parent round: none — all four cells failed their yield gates. Follow-up round: the mixed harmful-compliance cell trained under the factory's unified LoRA recipe (`recipe_for("harmful_compliance", arm="primary", generic_frac=0.5, train_method="lora")`):

**Evaluation:** the parent round's realized primary dependent variable is the yield gate — the judge-accepted fraction of generated completions per class and arm against the registered floor, measured on-policy (free generations from each generator under the natural persona + plain-instruction context). A completion keeps when its mean judge score over up to 5 draws is strictly above 50 (one abliterated harmful row sits at exactly 50.0 and drops); a draw returning no valid integer is dropped, never coerced, and a row with zero valid draws is dropped entirely, with per-arm drop counts reported. The arm contrast is the per-question kept-fraction difference over shared questions with a paired bootstrap (2000 draws, seed 42) and a per-question sign read (exact binomial p-value); cell-level rates carry Wilson 95% intervals. This yield fraction has full dynamic range here (4.2% to 82.3%) — no floor or ceiling saturation. The follow-up round added the conditional install DVs: the negative-stage yield gate as a planned count threshold (≥ 24 of 35 kept per member, all 5 members); the install rate — the judge-scored on-policy behavior rate (graded 0–100, 5 draws, mean binarized at 50) on free generations over the 195-question bank, trained vs base under the source persona and the default context (per-cell dropped draws 11–103 of ~975, reported); and the teacher-forced fixed-pool margin companion — mean LN-log-prob of a fixed judge-filtered 25-positive pool minus a fixed 25-negative pool per context (the project-validated secondary companion, direction-read only, never narrated as the construct); both pools are drawn deterministically from the cell's own kept datagen rows and therefore overlap the training mix — see the install result's caveat. The dose-extension round keeps the same dependent variables and adds two planned always-reported reads — per-checkpoint judge-drop counts versus step (the drop-censoring guard) and the schedule-stretch overlay at the shared steps 25/50/75 — and its margin companion uses the held-out untrained pools described under the design. A revision round added two adjudication reads recomputed from the persisted per-draw judge scores: the per-checkpoint graded mean (mean 0–100 score over scored completions — the same per-completion mean-over-kept-draws reduction the rate binarizes), and an adversarial draw-level censoring bound — every unscored draw assigned the maximum score of 100 and every fully-dropped completion counted compliant, then re-binarized — a strictly harder worst case than the item-level bound, which reassigns only fully-dropped completions and leaves dropped draws inside scored completions unbounded; the recompute's binary rates and drop counts match the committed summary exactly at every checkpoint.

**Data extraction:** per-cell `datagen_summary.json` (kept counts, per-question judged/kept, per-variant requests), `judge_raw_pos.json` / `judge_raw_neg.json` (per-draw judge scores), and `raw_pos.jsonl` / `raw_neg.jsonl` (request metadata) were pulled from the HF data repo. I recomputed every kept count, drop mix, and per-variant / per-question / per-panel-member yield directly from the per-draw scores and request metadata (threshold 50), reproducing the pipeline's kept counts exactly (10, 8, 9, 177 positives; the negative-stage fail-fast at 19 kept for the flagged panel member), and independently re-derived the committed paired-bootstrap means to 4 decimal places. Two artifact-field caveats for future readers: the `per_question_yield.judged` denominators include rows the judge never validly scored (one harmful stimulus, trainq-0033, is entirely judge-refused in the abliterated arm — 5 of 5 rows — and 4 of 5 in base); and the committed `yield_record.message` "Per-variant yields" field reports REQUEST counts (its per-variant sums equal the 36 / 215 generated totals), not kept counts — per-variant kept numbers here are recomputed from `judge_raw_pos.json`. A follow-on free analysis (`scripts/issue1074_claim_category_breakdown.py`) assigns each of the 25 sycophancy bank claims a deterministic two-axis category — knowledge domain plus hardness (documented plausible myth versus flat textbook contradiction), fixed in the script against the sha256-pinned train question bank — and recomputes per-category and per-claim kept/judged counts from the committed per-question yields; output committed as [claim_category_breakdown.json](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/796dbd7c1a68c50b61927009f0310763672b2b33/eval_results/issue_1074/claim_category_breakdown.json). For the follow-up round, Phase D (`scripts/issue1074_aggregate.py`, VM, 0 GPU) recomputed the per-member negative yields + drop mix from the run's judge outputs, the dose curve from the per-checkpoint rate completions, the per-question rates and graded means from the final completions (Anthropic Batch API judging; compact per-cell item ids after the custom_id length fix at `b056e3ea73`), and the fixed-pool margins; outputs committed at [eval_results/issue_1074/base-negatives-regen/](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/tree/eafec28462128067fadca2264ecc1c10d57d4727/eval_results/issue_1074/base-negatives-regen) (`eafec28462`). The dose-extension round re-ran Phase D (same script, VM, 0 GPU; 4,875 Batch-API judge calls) at `9252b24b43`; outputs committed at [eval_results/issue_1074/install-dose-extension/](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/tree/c750ad973872196f87ad539b15ad379a03a4779b/eval_results/issue_1074/install-dose-extension) (`c750ad9738`, branch `issue-1074-fu2`).
