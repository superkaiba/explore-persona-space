# Methodology — issue 1336: RLVR post-training + Tülu-3 5-stage ladder + 9-tier metric ladder (round 3)

*Derived from the [task body](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1336).*


**Design:** Five released checkpoints of one ladder — `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B` (base), `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B-SFT`, `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B-DPO`, `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B` (post-RLVR), `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3.1-8B` (longer-RLVR) — crossed in round 3 with eight prompt corpora and one render format per corpus, giving 40 generation cells and 56 stage-pair × corpus × layer-30 metric-ladder pair files (7 stage-pairs × 8 corpora). The post-training stage is the single manipulated variable; corpora, renders, sampling, and the fit recipe are held fixed. Round 3 corpora (revision-pinned): `chat` render on GSM8K test (1,319), GSM8K train full (7,473), MATH (7,500 from the RLVR mix), IF-constraints (11,000 sampled from the RLVR mix's ifeval component), UltraFeedback prompts (11,000 from the Tülu-3 8B preference mixture), Tülu-3 SFT-mix (11,000, stratified 3,667/3,667/3,666 across wildchat/flan/evol-codealpaca; corpus slug `sft11k`), and LMSYS-Chat-1M (23,000 sampled to yield ~15,000 kept); `naturalistic` render on LMSYS only. Rounds 1–2 (5,000 LMSYS × 2 renders + 5,000 GSM8K train + 1,319 GSM8K test) are superseded by the fatter round-3 corpora except the GSM8K test cell, which is retained as the decontaminated companion under the `n<d` caveat below. Per the standing both-mappings rule, the context-based mapping ran on every corpus × format; the prefix-based mapping (`xprefix`) ran on `naturalistic_lmsys23k` only — the chat-template prefix slot is row-constant by construction (measured max pairwise cosine distance up to 1.5e-4), so its map is degenerate at every stage. The naturalistic prefix mapping ran and reads R² 0.001–0.005 across all five stages (base 0.005; SFT/DPO/RLVR/longer-RLVR all 0.001) — floor-limited, invariant across stages, carried as an uncovered signal cell (a scope caveat, not a null).

**Training:** **N/A — no model training.** Round 3 spent 194.37 GPU-h against a budgeted 98 GPU-h (2.0× over budget; deviation from plan §11, recorded here; the fit + metric-ladder phase's per-cell wall was under-estimated at plan time — the pilot batteries measured 541s/corpus vs the planned 1.4 h/corpus, but the mass of stage-corpus cells at 15–17k rows in generation + capture drove the wall).

**Evaluation:** Per (stage-pair p, corpus s, layer L=30) the reparameterization ladder produces held-out R² of the source map applied to target contexts at every tier t ∈ {0..8}, on the same rows and folds as the target's within-stage map. Tiers, per `docs/mapping_similarity_metrics.md`: t0 direct transfer (ŷ = W_s x + b_s applied unchanged), t1 context offset (ŷ = W_s (x − Δx) + b_s, Δx from cloud means only), t2 answer offset (Δy on the output side), t3 bias offset (refit b* only), t4 global scaling (α W_s x + b*), t5 mapping rotation (R W_s x + b*, orthogonal Procrustes on directions), t6 linear reparameterization of contexts, t7 linear reparameterization of answers, t8 both (A ∘ W_s ∘ B). All offsets fit on clouds alone (never on paired context→answer rows). Δ_t = within-stage R² − tier-t R². The sufficient-tier statistic is min{t ∈ 0..8 : Δ_t ≤ 0.021 · ex_v2 = 0.0207} at the paired prompt-level bootstrap point estimate; "none ≤ 8" is a reportable value. Primary scale is raw pooled R² (round 3's fatter fold-train regime makes the recalibration rescue used in rounds 1–2 unnecessary — G0v2/G1v2 both PASS at raw); the held-out cross-fitted per-dim affine recalibration companion is always computed. The recalibration companion adds 4,096 per-dim scalar shift+scale parameters cross-fitted from folds, so a genuine map-mismatch signal can be partially absorbed by it; recal disagrees with raw on 3/8 corpora for DPO→RLVR (GSM8K test raw t2 → recal t0; GSM8K train raw t3 → recal t0; IF-constraints raw "none" → recal t8), so raw is the binding read and recal is reported as the companion that a per-dim rescaling accommodates. The stage-of-record contrast C_v2 = Δ_tier8(dpo→rlvr, gsm8k_train_full, chat) on the RLVR training distribution: point −0.0031 with a bootstrap 95% CI running from −0.0039 to −0.0024 on the raw scale (a signed negative CI — reparameterized-DPO reconstructs RLVR marginally better than the within-RLVR fit on that corpus; magnitude 5× smaller than the 0.021 elicit band, sign is direction-of-effect, not "matches to zero"). Registered decision constants (round-3 revisions with ex_v2 = S_qwen_v2 / 0.6731 = 1.0303, S_qwen_v2 = 0.6935): elicit_band_v2 = 0.0207; practical_scale_v2 = 0.0518; health_gate_v2 = 0.0515. G0v2 (Qwen anchor + Gram-vs-primal parity) PASS at abs_dev 5.9e-6 and 2.2e-16 primal-vs-Gram agreement. G1v2 (RLVR × LMSYS23k chat vs 0.20606 bar) PASS at raw R² 0.6201 / recal 0.6200. Capture-parity gate (100 rows × 32 layers) PASS at min mean cosine 1.0000005 vs 0.999 threshold. All three gates were plan-registered in plan v13; no descope fired. No LLM judge is involved: the DV is an activation-geometry read.

**Data extraction:** LMSYS is `lmsys/lmsys-chat-1m` at revision `200748d9d3cd`, rows 0–4,999 identical to the parent's `track_s.jsonl` (byte-asserted), rows 5,000+ continuing the same first-user-turn stream, exact-sha256-deduped, until 18,000 new unique prompts. GSM8K is `openai/gsm8k` @ `740312add88f`, config `main`. MATH and IF-constraints are the two non-gsm8k components of `allenai/RLVR-GSM-MATH-IF-Mixed-Constraints` @ `7dbd180f5440` (7,500 MATH rows verbatim; 11,000 sampled from the 14,973 ifeval component, seed 1336). UltraFeedback prompts are the `prompt` field of `allenai/llama-3.1-tulu-3-8b-preference-mixture` @ `78a6f0078594`, seed-1336 sample of 11,000 after exact-sha256 dedup. Tülu-3 SFT-mix is the union of three sources from `allenai/tulu-3-sft-mixture` @ `b14afda60f1b`, stratified 3,667/3,667/3,666 (wildchat/flan/evol-codealpaca), first user turn only. Every corpus was decontamination-screened against the 1,319 GSM8K test questions (normalized-exact match); the allenai mixes are decontaminated upstream per their model cards and the in-house verification found no matches. Answers were generated on-policy per model with vLLM (single stochastic sample per prompt at the sampling constants above); activations were captured teacher-forced on each model's own sampled text into bf16 turnstore shards. n/d hygiene at K=5: LMSYS fold-train ≈ 12k, IF/UF/SFT ≈ 7.2–7.7k, GSM8K-train / MATH ≈ 5.9k (all ≥ d = 4,096 except GSM8K test, which is retained only as the labeled `n < d` companion — its within-stage R² and tier reads run inner-group-CV with a 0.9 dof cap under the project's GCV-guard fit-core convention and are companion values only, never lattice-bearing). Realized keep rates: LMSYS 0.63–0.73; GSM8K/MATH 0.98–1.00; IF/UF/SFT 0.80–0.92 — all above the 0.80 keep-rate floor except LMSYS, carried as a coverage caveat.
