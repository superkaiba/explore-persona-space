\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\emph{Leakage}: a behavior trained into one context (system prompt, persona, ICL prefix) also appears under other, untrained contexts. Measured as the trained$-$base change in expression under a bystander context, on-policy, with a behavior-appropriate metric: marker-token log-probability at the end of the bystander's own response (\#502/\#489); judge-scored agreement for sycophancy (\#509); misaligned-and-coherent rate for EM (\#521); taught-fact emission for fact implants (\#500).

\subsection{The general problem, before any model}
\label{sec:general}

The response computation sees a context only through its \emph{KV cache}. So, with no model at all: any predictor of leakage is a function of the base model's KV cache, and any gate training could implement is an embedding of it. Two questions, posed at this level first:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Prediction.} How far is fine-tuning generalization predictable from base-model quantities, before training?
\item \textbf{Control (the best-effort question).} Take the \emph{most behavior-discriminatory} embedding of contexts --- nonlinear, any function of the KV cache --- suppose training could gate on it, and ask whether leakage persists.
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Inevitable} $\Rightarrow$ name why. Lazy candidate: a small edit can only gate on directions the base model represents $\approx$linearly; inevitable leakage $=$ best discriminator $-$ best linear gate on frozen features.
  \item \emph{Evitable} $\Rightarrow$ exhibit the edit. Linear candidate: the multi-pair contrastive edit (A2's relaxation); protection $\approx$ span of the trained negatives, so off-panel leakage measures best effort's reach.
  \item Linear case $=$ geometry: evitable iff the source key is linearly separable from the bystander span; contrastive negatives estimate the discriminant. Off-panel generalization of a $K$-negative discriminant: empirical, not a theorem.
  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

The model answers both by specialization: four assumptions, general $\to$ specific, each buying a cheaper predictor, each with a relaxation. A miss at rung $k$ means climb to rung $k{-}1$, not refutation; only the conjunction is refutable (exact gate $+$ measured write failing to rank a held-out panel leaves no rung standing).

\section{The assumption ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}

\textbf{The objects.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Pretraining leaves a dictionary of behaviors $b$, each carried by a residual-stream direction. Two roles, named separately: \emph{adding} a steering vector $d_b$ elicits $b$; \emph{projecting} onto a read-out $r_b$ measures it.
\item The state under a context decomposes over the dictionary: $f(c) \approx \sum_b \alpha_b(c)\, d_b + \text{error}$. The pretrained associations $c \to b$ ARE the coefficients; ``the assistant is not a villain'' is a statement about weights, not the dictionary. (Idealization: directions are neither orthogonal nor a basis; literal for the marker at read-out, empirical for EM, an open bet elsewhere.)
\item Two typed functions: $f: c \mapsto f(c)$ --- \emph{what training edits}; the table $A[b,c] = \langle r_b, f(c) \rangle$ --- \emph{what experiments measure}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{A0 (lazy regime): the update is small in weight and feature space}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Statement:} fine-tuning acts to first order --- it re-binds contexts to states the base model already represents, builds no new features. Edits the weights $\alpha_b(\cdot)$, never the dictionary. Corollary: implantable $\Leftrightarrow$ elicitable. Small in weights $\neq$ small in behavior: read-outs amplify (discrete emission flips; EM via nine rank-1 adapters \cite{soligo2025}).
\item \textbf{Buys:} the most general predictive form, $\Delta f(c) \approx K(c,c') \cdot w$ --- $w$ the write (correction achieved at the source), $K$ the empirical tangent kernel (strictly a cross-kernel), gate $g(c) = K(c,c')/K(c',c')$. Every rung below is a cheaper $K$.
\item \textbf{Support:}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \cite{jacot2018} --- small updates move a network linearly in function space; the tangent kernel is this rung's first-order object.
  \item \cite{malladi2023} --- LM fine-tuning empirically operates in this kernel regime; the license at 7B scale.
  \item \cite{minder2025} --- narrow fine-tunes leave constant, linearly readable activation traces: a fixed write, not new circuitry.
  \item \cite{engels2025} --- narrow fine-tune effects reproduce as a near-constant steering write.
  \item \#514 --- LoRA vs full FT leak identically at matched strength ($+0.00$ nat): parameterization-irrelevance, as first order requires.
  \item \#538 --- $3\times$ implant depth, edit geometry unchanged: dose moves magnitude, not features.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{On failure:} no rung above. Rich regime (features move) kills every base-model predictor (\S\ref{sec:estimating}); the EM predictor failure looks like it ($\rho = 0.09$, \#458; assistant-axis rotation $38$--$53^{\circ}$, discrimination collapse, \#125). Lazy-vs-rich is measurable, not assumed: the two-channel decomposition (\S\ref{sec:formula}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{A1 (local linearity): the edit acts at one layer, where the map is linear}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Statement:} at one layer $f(c) \approx M\vc$, and the edit acts there. $\vc =$ pooled activations over a probe set --- the cheapest KV-cache function (\S\ref{sec:general}); what the gate later misses traces to what the pooling threw away.
\item \textbf{Buys:} the kernel collapses to an inner product from base forward passes alone: $g(c) = \langle v_{c'}, \vc \rangle / \lVert v_{c'} \rVert^{2}$ --- asymmetric: $c' \to c$ scales as $\cos\theta \cdot \lVert \vc \rVert / \lVert v_{c'} \rVert$ (P2).
\item \textbf{Support:}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \cite{park2023} --- linear representation hypothesis: features occupy $\approx$linear subspaces; the prior that makes an inner product the natural similarity.
  \item \#502/\#509 --- proxy ladder (cosine $\to$ distributional $\to$ exact kernel) gains little ($0.79$ vs $0.76$ marker; $0.50$ vs $0.49$ sycophancy): the linear rung is not yet the bottleneck.
  \item P5 empirics --- predictor quality peaks at behavior-specific layers (marker 19--24, sycophancy 1--8); consistent, pending independent localization.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{On failure:} climb to A0's kernel. Under A3's router relaxation the gate is not a similarity at all: attention-shaped, near-binary for discrete triggers, estimated from samples.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{A2 (minimal edit): the smallest weight change that fits the new pair}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Statement:} the update is the rank-one key$\to$value write of linear associative memories: one key (the source context), one written direction.
\item \textbf{Buys:} (i) with A1, the factorization, eq.~\eqref{eq:rank1}: write $\times$ gate --- \emph{what} leaks decoupled from \emph{who} leaks; (ii) the weight-space face: $\Delta W \approx \text{write} \otimes \text{key}$, so SVD of a trained adapter returns both (P0); (iii) the in-context extension: an ICL prefix acts as a transient rank-one patch --- same write $\times$ gate, different persistence (P7).
\item \textbf{Support:}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \cite{kohonen1972,anderson1972} --- linear associative memories: rank-one outer product $=$ the minimal store of one key$\to$value pair; the rung's mathematical core.
  \item \cite{meng2022} --- ROME: a rank-one edit to one MLP layer rewrites a factual association.
  \item \cite{elhage2021} --- weights as read/write pairs, rank-one outer product the atomic unit: the weight-space vocabulary.
  \item \cite{hu2021} --- LoRA: adaptation succeeds under an explicit low-rank constraint --- needed updates have small effective rank.
  \item \cite{ward2025} --- rank-1 LoRAs carry interpretable, causal signals: rank one suffices to implement a behavior change.
  \item \cite{dherin2025,goldwaser2025} --- a transformer block provably converts an ICL prefix into a transient rank-one patch: the extension's formal basis.
  \item \cite{dai2022} ($>$85\% of in-distribution FT corrections recovered in-context), \cite{lampinen2025} (ICL anticipates FT generalization failures), \cite{liu2023icv} (ICL compresses to one direction) --- the behavioral form, which is all P7 claims.
  \item \cite{shen2023,deutch2023} --- strict ICL$=$GD equivalence fails under scrutiny: why P7 claims only the behavioral form.
  \item \#489 --- the similarity gate already ranks leakage from ICL sources.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{On failure:} \emph{low-rank} --- write spans a subspace; P1 weakens to ``shifts span $r$ dimensions,'' the SVD in use measures $r$. \emph{Multi-pair} --- the minimal edit solves a kernel system whose off-diagonals are the contrastive negatives, subtracting the common mode of near-parallel keys (cos $\approx 0.92$): why positive-only implants leak near-uniformly and contrastive ones localize ($99.6\%$ vs $11.7\%$, \#247/\#329). Early stopping spectrally filters toward raw similarity (untested).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{A3 (linear read-out): each behavior is measured by projection onto one direction}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Statement:} behavior $b$ is observable as the projection onto $r_b$ (unembedding row for the marker; probe or contrastive direction otherwise). $r_b \neq d_b \neq \vb$; collapsing them assumes the downstream network is the identity on the write.
\item \textbf{Buys:} the scalar observable $\Delta z_b(c) = \langle r_b, w \rangle\, g(c)$ --- one slope per behavior $\times$ gate; makes the write measurable. Cross-behavior transfer: one write, many read-outs (P6). Measurement note: the softmax is over \emph{tokens}, not behaviors --- behaviors compete at emission (marker vs end-of-turn), log-probs compress near ceiling, so logits are stored alongside. Marker: expression $=$ one logit (the clean testbed); sycophancy: sequence-level, judge-scored.
\item \textbf{Support:}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \cite{turner2023} --- adding one residual direction elicits the behavior without optimization: steering directions exist.
  \item \cite{panickssery2023} --- contrastive activation addition steers Llama~2 across behaviors, one direction each.
  \item \cite{chen2025} --- persona vectors: single-direction projections track traits before generation: read-outs exist, predictive.
  \item \cite{wang2025} --- one persona feature predicts and controls EM.
  \item \cite{soligo2025} --- independent EM fine-tunes converge to one linear direction; ablating it removes the behavior.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{On failure (router):} the write need not copy the behavior direction --- a query shift can recruit attention that fetches the behavior from the context's own values \cite{engels2025}. Cleanest case: a rank-1 EM adapter, write cos $0.04$ to the misalignment direction, yet $98\%$ of its effect ablates along it downstream \cite{soligo2025}. Behavior stays one direction at read-out; P1 can fail by mechanism.
\end{itemize}

\section{The formula at the bottom of the ladder}
\label{sec:formula}

Training drags the source state $v_{b'} = f(c')$ toward $\vb$, landing at $v_{b''}$ (early stopping undershoots, so the write is measured, not assumed). A2 with A1 --- minimize $\lVert \Delta M \rVert_F$ s.t.\ $(M{+}\Delta M)\,v_{c'} = v_{b''}$ --- gives
\begin{equation}
\Delta f(c) \;=\; \underbrace{(v_{b''} - v_{b'})}_{\text{write: \emph{what} leaks}} \;\cdot\; \underbrace{\frac{\langle v_{c'}, \vc \rangle}{\lVert v_{c'} \rVert^{2}}}_{\text{gate: \emph{who} leaks}}
\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad
f'(c) = \Big(M + \tfrac{(v_{b''}-v_{b'})\,v_{c'}^{\top}}{\lVert v_{c'}\rVert^{2}}\Big)\vc .
\label{eq:rank1}
\end{equation}
Division of labor: A2 makes the edit rank-one, A1 makes the gate an inner product, A2's weight face makes it visible in $\Delta W$, A3 makes the write measurable ($\Delta z_b(c) = \langle r_b, w \rangle\, g(c)$).

\paragraph{One rank-one nudge to the association table.} Eq.~\eqref{eq:rank1} compresses to $\Delta A[b,c] = \langle r_b, w \rangle \cdot g(c)$. The intended edit is one cell $(b, c')$; the minimal edit smears along both axes:
\begin{itemize}
\item down the columns, every context moves $\propto$ its gate --- \emph{leakage} (P2);
\item across the rows, every behavior moves $\propto$ its slope $\langle r_b, w \rangle$ --- \emph{cross-behavior transfer} (P6);
\item so leakage $=$ off-target table movement: $\alpha_b(c)$ changing for pairs the fine-tune never targeted --- generalization nobody asked for.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{The write is not the read-out.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Collapsing $\vb$ and $r_b$ assumes the downstream network is the identity on the write; kept separate, it can rotate, amplify, or route. The anomalies live in that gap --- all of the form ``write $\perp$ read-out, yet the behavior moved.''
\item Three on record: prompted marker direction vs realized shift (cos $\approx -0.03$, \#521; P8); the routing adapter (A3); the P1 split (\S\ref{sec:exp}: marker and EM arms identical at read-out, differ in rank-one-ness of the shift matrix).
\item Not oversold: ranking who leaks needs no $\vb$ ($\Delta z_b = s_b\, g(c)$, $s_b$ fitted). The two objects earn their keep in the vector-valued predictions (P1, P3$'$, P6, P8).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{The edit has two channels.} With $f(c) = M\vc$: $\;\Delta f(c) = \underbrace{\Delta M\,\vc}_{\text{re-bind map}} + \underbrace{M\,\Delta\vc}_{\text{move representation}} + \Delta M\,\Delta\vc$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Nothing confines the update to $M$ (an all-linear adapter touches every layer below the read). Eq.~\eqref{eq:rank1} is channel 1 alone; geometry persistence (\S\ref{sec:estimating}) $=$ ``channel 2 vanishes.''
\item Both outcomes on record: $\Delta\vc \approx 0$ at $3\times$ depth (\#538); assistant-axis rotation $38$--$53^{\circ}$ $+$ discrimination collapse under EM (\#125).
\item Measurable on stored artifacts (\#521/\#551 tensors): $\Delta\vc$ directly, the $\Delta M\,\vc$ share by fitting on frozen base summaries, the rest $=$ moved representations. Cheapest candidate account of the P1 tension: marker and EM arms may use different channels.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Contexts encode behaviors.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Contexts and behaviors are both activation states: a persona prompt's projection profile $\{\langle r_b, f(c)\rangle\}_b$ is the behavior bundle it elicits without training. Used in three places: source residual (P3), bystander prior (P3), the estimator ladder (\S\ref{sec:estimating}).
\item Not licensed: treating behavior and context as separate axes. The gate mixes prompt similarity with behavior-bundle overlap; an instruction context can encode the behavior while sitting far from the source --- the regime where geometry collapsed and the base prior carried the signal (\#532).
\item Open test: vary behavior overlap at fixed context similarity. Candidate gate term: similarity conditioned on the taught material (JS over the taught answers, teacher-forced) --- close $=$ agreeing about $b$; its scalar limit re-derives the base-prior predictor (P3, \#532) as a gate term.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading everything from the base model}
\label{sec:estimating}

$\vb$ is the \emph{post-training} state --- unobservable before training --- so estimating it from the base model is part of the proposal.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Estimators} (decreasing fidelity): (i) teacher-forced reads over the prospective training data's completions; (ii) in-context demonstrations from that data (the variant run so far); (iii) description conditioning. All are $f$ at a behavior-eliciting context; validated against the realized $v_{b''}$ and forward-predicted leakage (P7--P8). Standing warning: cos $\approx -0.03$ between prompted marker direction and realized shift (\#521). Constructions (layer, position, aggregation) are swept, not fixed.
\item \textbf{Prompting stands in for fine-tuning at three points}, which fail separately:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Mechanism} --- same write $\times$ gate at different persistence (A2's extension); equivalence open, so P7 claims only the behavioral form.
  \item \emph{State identity} --- prompted-performance state $=$ fine-tuned state. Most exposed (P8; the cos $\approx -0.03$ warning); teacher-forced is the principled estimator --- same forward passes the gradients come from.
  \item \emph{Geometry persistence} --- base $\vc$ still measures the trained model: A0 restated; the one substitution no better estimator repairs.
  \end{itemize}
\item With the realized $v_{b''}$, eq.~\eqref{eq:rank1} is descriptive and needs none of the three; they are the price of prediction \emph{before} training --- the program's point. Most of \S\ref{sec:exp} implicitly tests them.
\end{itemize}

\section{Predictions}
\label{sec:pred}

\begin{description}[style=nextline,itemsep=2pt]
\item[P0 (weights decompose).] Top singular vectors of $\Delta W$ $=$ key and write; under contrastive training the key moves toward a source-minus-negatives direction with dose.
\item[P1 (one write direction).] Per-bystander shifts at the read-out all point along $(v_{b''}-v_{b'})$: shift-matrix SVD near rank-one, top component matching the behavior delta.
\item[P2 (gate $=$ base-model context similarity).] A fixed kernel on base context summaries sets who leaks, transferring across held-out panels. On A1's rung: the dot product, hence asymmetric ($c' \to c \propto \cos\theta \cdot \lVert \vc \rVert / \lVert v_{c'} \rVert$).
\item[P3 (the behavior's prior matters).] Source side: write scales with $\lVert v_{b''}-v_{b'}\rVert$ --- a source already performing $b$ leaks little despite high similarity. Bystander side: prior enters via read-out compression (measurement) and possibly the gate (mechanism); separable in logit space only with the exact gate.
\item[P3$'$ (source prior shapes the write's direction, not just size).] High-prior source $\Rightarrow$ residual concentrates in source-specific subspace: prior predicts the write's \emph{common-mode fraction}, not its norm. \#541: high-prior teachers express most on themselves (97/94\% vs 72\%) while leaking least (``not weaker, narrower'') --- impossible under magnitude-only P3.
\item[P4 (additivity).] Joint two-implant training $\approx$ sum of singletons; deviations set by source mutual similarity (activation-space task arithmetic \cite{ilharco2023}).
\item[P5 (layer locality).] The gate predicts best at the layers computing the behavior; needs independent localization (predictor-quality scoring is circular). Behavior-specific: marker 19--24, sycophancy 1--8.
\item[P6 (cross-behavior transfer).] One write moves other behaviors $\propto$ its projection onto their read-outs.
\item[P7 (ICL proxy).] Base-model in-context behavior on the training mix forward-predicts the post-training leakage map (A2's extension).
\item[P8 (the estimator is valid).] The three $\vb$ estimators agree with each other and the realized $v_{b''}$. If not: formula predictions fail in a way the realized $v_{b''}$ repairs --- estimation failed, not the update rule.
\end{description}

No single miss refutes the model (each rung has its relaxation, \S\ref{sec:ladder}); the conjunction does (\S\ref{sec:general}).

\textbf{Headline experiment} --- dose titration: implant at a non-saturated anchor (\#448); measure expression change, activation shift, and gate proxy per held-out context, on-policy (\#432$\to$\#456), across the $10$--$25$-step cliff (\#139), $\geq$3 seeds. Predicted: near rank-one, similarity-graded shifts at low dose; rotation and prediction collapse across the cliff. The P1 split complicates the low-dose half, so the titration doubles as its diagnosis.

\textbf{The regime tension.} A0 is where the model lives or dies. Current tension: the marker arm passes the gate test but fails the one-direction test; EM does the reverse. \#552/\#561 decide rig vs regime; the per-arm channel shares (\S\ref{sec:formula}) read the same question off stored artifacts. The durable question under every version: how far is fine-tuning generalization predictable from base-model activation geometry, before training?
