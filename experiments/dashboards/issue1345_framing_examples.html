Three framings of the assistant

Worked examples of the three framings #1345 compares, all holding the assistant persona constant: the Qwen chat template, the plain User: / Assistant: text of the same conversations, and an assistant/AI character answering a human inside a narrative story. The finding: chat and no-template are the same context→answer map up to a linear change of coordinates, while the story framing collapses the map. The base-model story arm was below the yield floor (96/500 kept < 400 floor), so Section 3 is instruct-only.

The #1345 story rollouts: narrative prose in which an AI-assistant character answers a human character's questions. The extraction slot is the attribution marker just before the character's quoted answer (the colon in Assistant replied:); the quoted answer is the answer span.
