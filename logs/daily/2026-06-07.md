   **Proposed edit:**
   ```diff

   - On SUPPLY_CONSTRAINT, retry-with-backoff until capacity frees.

   + fail-exit to `blocked`. A balance refusal clears when a running pod

   + fail-exited to blocked on INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE while 7 pods burned $112.50/hr.)
   ```
