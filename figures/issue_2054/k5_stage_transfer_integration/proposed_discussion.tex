Frozen cross-checkpoint transfer provides more direct evidence of continuity across post-training: Base maps retain 68\%--78\% of Instruct-specific $R^2$ for the four story characters and 73\% for the assistant in a story, compared with 26\% for assistant chat (\figref[A]{fig:speaker-maps}). The assistant-in-story result places the contrast between settings rather than between the assistant and fictional characters, and is consistent with greater preservation of the context--answer relationship in narrative settings. This comparison does not isolate SFT or establish a causal role for persona representations; differences in template familiarity and response quality may also contribute.
