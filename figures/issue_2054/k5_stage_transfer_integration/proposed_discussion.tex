Under this view, the assistant is itself one such character, learned during pretraining and subsequently refined and privileged through post-training for interaction within the chat template \citep{g2026assistantprivilegedpersonacanonical}. Our frozen Base-to-Instruct transfer results are consistent with this account (Section~\ref{sec:results-speakers}): post-training appears to reshape the context--answer relationship more strongly for the assistant in the chat template, while preserving much of its predictive structure in story settings, including when the speaker is the assistant.
