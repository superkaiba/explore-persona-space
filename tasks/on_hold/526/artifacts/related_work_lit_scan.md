# Related-work scan — leakage-prediction rule (task #526)

Date: 2026-06-09. Four parallel literature searches: (1) model editing + associative memory, (2) fine-tuning as linearized/kernel dynamics, (3) continual-learning interference theory, (4) LLM behavior-transfer empirics. All arXiv ids verified by abstract fetch at search time.

**The rule under study:** fine-tuning ≈ rank-1 outer-product patch ΔM = (Δv_behavior)(v_source)ᵀ/‖v_source‖²; predicted leakage to a target context = (v_source·v_target)/‖v_source‖² × effect at source = cos(source,target) × ‖v_target‖/‖v_source‖ — asymmetric via the norm ratio; behavior enters through the readout (additive in logit space, softmax-warped in logprob space).

## Cross-branch synthesis

**What is already established (do NOT claim as new):**
- "Similarity in a model-internal space predicts where an update propagates" exists in at least five published forms: GradSim for edit ripple effects (2407.12828), TracIn / influence functions / LESS for data→behavior effects (2002.08484, 1703.04730, 2308.03296, 2402.04333), representation-inner-product forecasting of forgetting (2402.01865), cosine-to-weight-diff-singular-vectors for backdoor trigger detection (2508.00161), and similarity-×-amplification over SAE feature space for EM (2605.00842).
- The rank-1 update form is canonical: ROME's edit is literally (payload)⊗(C⁻¹k*)/(k*ᵀC⁻¹k*) (2202.05262); crosstalk ∝ key overlap is the classical linear-associator result (Anderson 1972; Kohonen 1972; Hopfield 1982 PNAS).
- The lazy/kernel regime licenses the linearization for LM fine-tuning (2210.05643, 2602.08239, 1812.07956, 2002.09277); LoRA and full FT provably find the same low-rank solution in the NTK regime (2402.11867) — predicting our #514 LoRA≈FT result.
- Rank-1 LoRA suffices to induce EM and independent EM fine-tunes converge to one linear direction (2506.11613, 2506.11618).

**What is genuinely open (the novelty surface):**
- Nobody has published the asymmetric cos × norm-ratio law as a quantitative, falsifiable leakage predictor across (source-context, target-context, behavior) triples, measured ON-POLICY at the model's own generation slot. The near-misses predict from gradients (GradSim, LESS), from training-data projections (persona vectors 2507.21509; "What is in Your Safe Data?" 2404.01099), from SAE features off-policy (2605.00842), or post-hoc for detection (2508.00161).
- The trigger in the editing literature is a subject-token key; ours is a *context/persona*. The transferred object there is a fact probed by teacher-forced cloze; ours is a behavior installed by ordinary multi-step LoRA SFT and measured on-policy.
- The behavior-dependence decomposition (logit-additive rank-1 term + mechanical softmax prior term + any residual true interaction) is not in the literature.

**Refinements to steal:**
1. **Whitened inner product (ROME):** the theory-correct similarity is covariance-whitened (Mahalanobis-style), not raw cosine — likely why Gaussian-KL@L22 beat cosine in #502. A whitened-cosine / projection-coefficient predictor is a one-line addition to existing matrices.
2. **Editing vocabulary:** report desired transfer as "generalization/portability," undesired bleed as "specificity/locality failure" with graded relatedness ladders (CounterFact 2202.05262, RippleEdits 2307.12976, survey 2305.13172) for cross-community legibility.
3. **Low-rank leakage-matrix signature (Jin & Ren 2406.14026):** if leakage is generated by outer-product patches, the source×bystander leakage matrix should be near low-rank. SVD on #489/#502 matrices is a zero-GPU structural test that partially dodges the clustered-Spearman critique.
4. **Gradient-cosine vs activation-cosine comparison:** GradSim uses gradients; we use activations. For a rank-1 patch the per-example gradient ≈ (readout error)⊗(input activation), so the comparison is a free differentiator experiment.
5. **Regime-boundary diagnostic:** monotone-in-overlap holds in the lazy/fixed-feature regime; intermediate-similarity-worst (Maslow's-hammer dip) appears in the rich regime (2401.12617 reconciles 2007.07400 / 2205.09029). Test for a mid-similarity dip as a regime check; expect rule degradation with implant strength/overtraining (saturation).

**Known tensions / threats to the rule:**
- Intruder dimensions (2410.21228): LoRA creates high-ranking singular directions full FT doesn't — nominally against LoRA≈FT; candidate resolution: leakage is governed by the shared input-side kernel while intruder dimensions live output-side. #514 (r=0.994) is our evidence the leakage profile is insensitive.
- High-effective-rank regime (2405.09673): long-corpus FT updates have effective rank 10-100× LoRA — the rank-1 story is for narrow single-behavior implants, not continued pretraining.
- Non-monotonicity in the rich regime (above).
- Input-side vs target-side similarity act with opposite signs in teacher-student theory (2105.07385, 2107.04384) — a second, distinct behavior-dependent term candidate.
- Narrow-organism over-leak (2510.13900): narrow fine-tunes imprint near-global activation biases readable on random text; realistic (WildChat-like) training should be more context-selective — supports moving to realistic contexts.

**Published phenomena one fitted rule should retrodict (out-of-sample tests):**
token-neighborhood backdoor false-positive firing (2605.30189); resource-graded cross-lingual safety transfer (2310.02446, 2410.18210); context-token-bound subliminal learning (2606.00831); inoculation prompting as deliberate v_source manipulation (2510.04340); chunky post-training context-suppression curves (2602.05910); EM riding on shared chat-template prefix tokens (2606.06667).

## Branch 1 — Model editing + associative memory

### Rank-one editing and the generalization-vs-specificity axis
- **ROME: Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT** (Meng, Bau, Andonian, Belinkov, 2022, arXiv:2202.05262) — rank-1 closed-form edit Δ = (v*−Wk*)(C⁻¹k*)ᵀ/(k*ᵀC⁻¹k*); CounterFact splits Efficacy / Generalization (paraphrase transfer) / Specificity (neighborhood bleed). Bleed to another key k′ is algebraically (k′ᵀC⁻¹k*)/(k*ᵀC⁻¹k*) × payload — our rule with a whitened inner product.
- **MEMIT** (Meng et al., 2022, arXiv:2210.07229) — scales key-value edits to thousands of facts; summed outer-product patches interfere (many-stored-pairs crosstalk in a modern LLM).
- **EMMET unified framework** (Gupta, Sajnani, Anumanchipalli, 2024, arXiv:2403.14236) — ROME/MEMIT optimize one preservation-memorization objective; side effects controlled by inner products between edited key and preserved keys.
- **CounterFact+** (Hoelscher-Obermaier et al., 2023, arXiv:2305.17553) — vanilla specificity tests miss bleed on prompts that merely mention the edited entity; KL-based specificity metric (cautionary cousin of our banned full-vocab-KL DV).
- **Editing LLMs: Problems, Methods, Opportunities** (Yao et al., 2023, arXiv:2305.13172) — standardizes reliability/generalization/locality/portability vocabulary.
- **Does Localization Inform Editing?** (Hase et al., 2023, arXiv:2301.04213) — causal-tracing locus ≠ best edit layer; robustness check across layers/positions warranted for our single-site rule.

### Ripple effects + similarity predictors
- **RippleEdits** (Cohen et al., 2023, arXiv:2307.12976) — one edit logically implies related changes; benchmark with graded relatedness; editors propagate inconsistently.
- **GradSim** (Qin, Zhang, Li, Yu, Ji, 2024, arXiv:2407.12828) — cosine between gradients of original and related fact strongly predicts ripple transfer across LMs/editors/metrics; ripple failures coincide with low GradSim. Closest existing predictor to ours (gradient- vs activation-similarity).

### Key-value memories + classical crosstalk
- **Transformer FF layers are key-value memories** (Geva et al., 2020, arXiv:2012.14913); **FF layers promote vocabulary concepts** (Geva et al., 2022, arXiv:2203.14680 — additive logit-space updates, supports our readout claim).
- **Birth of a Transformer: a memory viewpoint** (Bietti et al., 2023, arXiv:2306.00802) — gradient steps naturally install outer-product associative memories (bridges hand-written edits and SFT).
- **Scaling laws for associative memories** (Cabannes, Dohmatob, Bietti, 2023, arXiv:2310.02984) — interference theory for sums of outer products under correlated embeddings.
- **Linearity of relation decoding** (Hernandez et al., 2023, arXiv:2308.09124) — many (not all) relations decoded by one linear map; bounds the linear-map premise.
- **Classical crosstalk:** Anderson 1972 (Math. Biosciences 14:197-220); Kohonen 1972 (IEEE Trans. Computers C-21:353-359 — recall exact iff keys orthonormal, crosstalk ∝ key overlaps); Hopfield 1982 (PNAS 79:2554); Krotov & Hopfield 2016 (arXiv:1606.01164 — higher-order energies reduce crosstalk).

### Asymmetry + norm-linked bleed
- **BAKE reversal-curse-in-editing** (Xu et al., 2023, arXiv:2310.10322) — edits recall forward, fail reverse: direction-asymmetric influence matching payload⊗key.
- **Reversal Curse** (Berglund et al., 2023, arXiv:2309.12288) — ordinary SFT installs key→value, not value→key.
- **r-ROME** (Gupta et al., 2024, arXiv:2403.07175) — model collapse traced to anomalously large update norms; clearest published norm↔global-bleed link (pathological limit of our norm-ratio term).
- **Butterfly effect of editing** (Yang et al., 2024, arXiv:2402.09656) + **gradual & catastrophic forgetting at scale** (Gupta et al., 2024, arXiv:2401.07453) — the far end of the leakage spectrum when the interference budget is exceeded.

## Branch 2 — Fine-tuning as linearized / kernel dynamics

### Kernel/NTK view + lazy-vs-rich boundary
- **Kernel-based view of LM fine-tuning** (Malladi et al., 2022, arXiv:2210.05643) — prompt-based FT often induces kernel (lazy) dynamics; formal foundation for similarity-kernel leakage.
- **Lazy training** (Chizat, Oyallon, Bach, 2018, arXiv:1812.07956) — lazy regime = small relative parameter movement; the license condition for the rank-1 approximation.
- **Kernel and rich regimes** (Woodworth et al., 2020, arXiv:2002.09277) — initialization scale controls lazy↔rich transition; rule should fail in the rich regime.
- **Linearization explains fine-tuning in LLMs** (Rahimi Afzal et al., 2026, arXiv:2602.08239, NeurIPS 2025) — implicit stay-close bias ⇒ FT = learning with positive-definite NTK; quantifies linearization quality for LoRA-tuned LLMs.
- **Lazy vs hasty** (George, Lajoie, Baratin, 2022, arXiv:2209.09658) — per-example observable for diagnosing regime.

### Influence functions
- **Koh & Liang 2017** (arXiv:1703.04730); **Grosse et al. 2023, Anthropic** (arXiv:2308.03296 — influence at 52B, sparse, near-zero under word-order flips); **TRAK** (Park et al., 2023, arXiv:2303.14186 — linearized "after kernel" suffices for attribution); **DataInf** (arXiv:2310.00902) + **LoGra** (arXiv:2405.13954) — LoRA-suited influence kits at our scale.

### Task arithmetic / weight-space linearity
- **Task arithmetic** (Ilharco et al., 2022, arXiv:2212.04089) — FT deltas add/negate/compose.
- **Task arithmetic in tangent space** (Ortiz-Jimenez et al., 2023, arXiv:2305.12827) — weight disentanglement: distinct directions govern localized regions of function space; bystander leakage = kernel-eigenfunction overlap.
- **Cross-task linearity** (Zhou et al., 2024, arXiv:2402.03660) — weight interpolation ≈ feature interpolation; Δweights→Δactivations approx linear.
- **TAFT tangent transformers** (Liu et al., 2023, arXiv:2307.08122) — explicitly linearized FT matches nonlinear FT on many tasks.
- **Demystifying mergeability** (Zhou et al., 2026, arXiv:2601.22285) — gradient-alignment metrics predict merge compatibility (kernel logic, model-to-model).

### LoRA theory / update structure
- **Intruder dimensions** (Shuttleworth et al., 2024, arXiv:2410.21228) — LoRA adds new singular directions full FT doesn't; tension with #514, resolution hypothesis: input-side kernel shared.
- **LoRA learns less, forgets less** (Biderman et al., 2024, arXiv:2405.09673) — full-FT effective rank 10-100× LoRA at corpus scale; bounds the rank-1 story to narrow implants.
- **LoRA in NTK regime has no spurious minima** (Jang, Lee, Ryu, 2024, arXiv:2402.11867) — LoRA and full FT find the same low-rank solution in the lazy regime; predicts #514.
- **Beyond-kernel rank-1 teacher** (Dayi & Chen, 2024, arXiv:2411.15385) — rank-1 structure survives into the nonlinear regime; cosine predictor may outlive lazy dynamics.
- Background: **intrinsic dimensionality** (Aghajanyan et al., 2020, arXiv:2012.13255).

### Cross-prompt prediction from similarity
- **LESS** (Xia et al., 2024, arXiv:2402.04333) — gradient-similarity data selection for targeted SFT; most direct prior art for "train-input↔target-input similarity predicts update effect."
- **Subliminal learning** (Cloud et al., 2025, arXiv:2507.14805) — transfer governed by shared tangent space, not semantics.
- **Narrow-FT activation traces** (Minder et al., 2025, arXiv:2510.13900) — constant Δ-activation bias readable on random text; warning that narrow organisms over-leak.
- **Can GD simulate prompting?** (Zhang, Choshen, Andreas, 2025, arXiv:2506.20989) — context↔weights correspondence from the converse direction.
- **FT learns a wrapper** (Jain et al., 2023, arXiv:2311.12786) — fine-tuning learns minimal wrappers on existing capabilities.

## Branch 3 — Continual learning / interference-transfer theory

### Gradient-alignment framing
- **GEM** (Lopez-Paz & Ranzato, 2017, arXiv:1706.08840) — interference operationalized as negative gradient inner product (+ **A-GEM** arXiv:1812.00420).
- **MER** (Riemer et al., 2019, arXiv:1810.11910) — transfer-interference trade-off governed by ∇L_i·∇L_j.
- **OGD** (Farajtabar et al., 2020, arXiv:1910.07104) — zero overlap ⇒ zero interference; what leakage-proof FT would look like.
- **PCGrad** (Yu et al., 2020, arXiv:2001.06782) — cosine between update directions is the operative interference variable.

### Closed-form interference ∝ overlap
- **NTK overlap matrix** (Doan et al., 2021, arXiv:2010.04003) — forgetting bounded by task NTK-subspace alignment (+ Bennani et al. arXiv:2006.11942). Closest theorem to our rule.
- **Forgetting in linear regression** (Evron et al., 2022, arXiv:2205.09588) — exact forgetting via alternating projections; multi-step generalization of the single-patch rule.
- **Closed-form forgetting + generalization** (Lin et al., 2023, arXiv:2302.05836) — similarity-weighted closed forms; documents task-order asymmetry.
- **Teacher-student statistical mechanics** (Asanuma et al., 2021, arXiv:2105.07385) — input-similarity and target-similarity act with OPPOSITE signs.

### Non-monotonicity
- **Anatomy of catastrophic forgetting** (Ramasesh et al., 2021, arXiv:2007.07400) — maximal forgetting at intermediate similarity.
- **Teacher-student task similarity** (Lee, Goldt, Saxe, 2021, arXiv:2107.04384) — feature-level vs readout-level similarity interact oppositely; monotonicity may hold readout-fixed.
- **Maslow's Hammer** (Lee et al., 2022, arXiv:2205.09029) — node re-use vs activation trade-off explains the mid-similarity dip.
- **Joint similarity × overparameterization** (Goldfarb et al., 2024, arXiv:2401.12617) — dip only in heavily overparameterized/rich regime; monotone near interpolation threshold. The reconciliation result; gives the regime knob.

### LLM fine-tuning / safety
- **Fine-tuning compromises safety** (Qi et al., 2024, arXiv:2310.03693) — benign FT erodes alignment; our rule is a candidate predictor of which data does this (+ survey Luo et al. arXiv:2308.08747).
- **Implicit inference / conjugate prompting** (Kotha et al., 2024, arXiv:2309.10105) — FT skews task inference; suppressed behavior recoverable by prompting away from the FT distribution; leakage as context-gated inference shift.
- **Alignment tax + model averaging** (Lin et al., 2024, arXiv:2309.06256) — damage tracks layerwise feature-space overlap.
- **LoRA learns less forgets less** (arXiv:2405.09673, also Branch 2).

### Per-example prediction
- **TracIn** (Pruthi et al., 2020, arXiv:2002.08484) — per-example effect = gradient inner products; our rule IS TracIn specialized to a rank-1 behavioral patch.
- **What will my model forget?** (Jin & Ren, 2024, arXiv:2402.01865) — representation-inner-product forecasters predict which upstream examples break.
- **Low-rank example associations** (Jin & Ren, 2024, arXiv:2406.14026) — forgetting matrix near low-rank; matrix completion beats semantic baselines. Testable signature for our leakage matrices.

## Branch 4 — Behavior-transfer / generalization empirics in LLMs

### EM + persona (canonical + follow-ups)
- **Emergent Misalignment** (Betley et al., 2025, arXiv:2502.17424) — incl. backdoored variant (trigger-gated EM) and educational-context framing preventing EM.
- **Persona Vectors** (Chen et al., 2025, arXiv:2507.21509) — projection-based flagging of trait-shifting training data, pre-training; projects data, not target contexts.
- **Persona Features Control EM** (Wang et al., 2025, arXiv:2506.19823) — toxic-persona SAE feature predicts EM.
- **Model Organisms for EM** (Turner et al., 2025, arXiv:2506.11613) + **Convergent Linear Representations** (Soligo et al., 2025, arXiv:2506.11618) — single rank-1 LoRA induces EM; independent fine-tunes converge to one direction. Direct support for the rank-1 ansatz.
- **EM easy, narrow misalignment hard** (Soligo et al., ICLR 2026, arXiv:2602.07852) — general/leaky solution has lower loss + more pretraining support; narrow solution must be forced (KL) — contrastive-negatives as inductive-bias correction.
- **Piggyback Hypothesis** (Zhao et al., 2026, arXiv:2606.06667) — EM rides on shared chat-template prefix tokens; patching prefix reps restores alignment. Mechanism for the shared-context-component term.

### Trait-to-trait leakage
- **Warmth → unreliability/sycophancy** (Ibrahim, Hafner, Rocher, 2025, arXiv:2507.21919) — plain SFT (ShareGPT rewrites), +10-30pp error; test-bed (see #496 replication-fidelity caveat).
- **Inoculation Prompting** (Tan et al., 2025, arXiv:2510.04340) — eliciting the trait during training suppresses it at test; cleanest published source-context manipulation of leakage.
- **Trait-space monitoring** (Nghiem et al., 2026, arXiv:2606.07631) — drift concentrates on one low-dim axis (65.5% var), 0.99 AUROC checkpoint detection.

### Subliminal learning
- **Subliminal Learning** (Cloud et al., 2025, arXiv:2507.14805) — traits transfer via numbers/code only under shared base model.
- **Steering-vector distillation** (Blank et al., 2026, arXiv:2606.00995) — teacher's system prompt ≈ steering vector; student learns an aligned vector; non-vector-approximable prompts don't transfer. Closest mechanistic sibling.
- **LoRA artifact** (Nief et al., 2026, arXiv:2606.00831) — inverted-U in LoRA rank; vanishes under full FT; localized to tokens shared between train and eval contexts.
- **Towards understanding subliminal learning** (Schrodi et al., 2025, arXiv:2509.23886) — carried by rare divergence tokens; fragile to paraphrase.

### Backdoors as context-gated implants
- **Sleeper Agents** (Hubinger et al., 2024, arXiv:2401.05566) — trigger-gated behavior persists; adversarial training SHARPENS the trigger boundary (published analogue of contrastive-negative gating).
- **Watch the Weights** (Zhong & Raghunathan, 2025, arXiv:2508.00161) — cosine of incoming activations to weight-diff singular vectors detects trigger activation; nearest readout-side competitor (post-hoc, detection-framed).
- **Token-level LoRA backdoor generalization** (Lelle, 2026, arXiv:2605.30189) — RFC-trigger backdoor fires on any RFC reference, not ISO/OWASP/CWE; best characterization of graded false-positive firing.
- **AdvBDGen** (Pathmanathan et al., 2024, arXiv:2410.11283) — fuzzy paraphrase triggers deliberately widening the trigger region.

### Out-of-context / cross-lingual / cross-domain
- **Taken out of context** (Berglund et al., 2023, arXiv:2309.00667) — declaratively-described behaviors executed in unrelated contexts (with augmentation).
- **Low-resource jailbreaks** (Yong et al., 2023, arXiv:2310.02446) — safety transfer graded by language resource level.
- **Multilingual fine-tuning fragility** (Poppi et al., 2024, arXiv:2410.18210) — few English adversarial examples un-align all languages; safety representation shared.
- **What is in Your Safe Data?** (He, Xia, Henderson, 2024, arXiv:2404.01099) — benign data selected by representation/gradient proximity to harmful anchors breaks safety (>70% compliance with 100 examples). Data-side twin of the context-side rule.
- **Chunky Post-Training** (Murray et al., 2026, arXiv:2602.05910) — chunk-implanted spurious correlations; graded suppression by added context tokens.

### Direct competitors (predict generalization from representations)
- **Feature superposition geometry of EM** (Minegishi et al., 2026, arXiv:2605.00842) — amplifying a feature strengthens superposed neighbors ∝ similarity; similarity-based data filtering cuts EM 34.5%. Closest theory competitor (SAE space, off-policy).
- **Data-mediated transfer lens** (Askin et al., 2026, arXiv:2605.12798) — train-eval prompt-structure similarity predicts spillover (behavioral, no geometry predictor).
- **Domain-level EM susceptibility** (Mishra et al., 2026, arXiv:2602.00298) — likelihood/membership-inference priors predict broad misalignment by domain.
- **Narrow-FT activation traces** (Minder et al., 2025, arXiv:2510.13900) — also a methodological warning (narrow organisms over-leak).
