# Maintain open list of EPS research questions + evidence ladder

A standing, living document — not a one-shot deliverable.

For each open question in the EPS research program, capture:

1. **The question** in one sentence.
2. **Why it matters** (1-2 sentences — what does answering it unlock, which mentor / collaborator / paper-narrative cares).
3. **What would count as evidence** (the experiment / observation / analysis that would update belief, and in which direction).
4. **Current evidence** (which task #s, clean results, sibling-paper results, or informal observations bear on it; what they currently say).
5. **Status** — open / partially answered / answered / abandoned, with a date.

## Scope

The full EPS program — not just whatever's currently running. Span:
- persona-space geometry (axis structure, dimensionality)
- localization (which layers, which features)
- propagation (how persona signal flows through the forward pass)
- axis origins (training-data → axis mapping)
- defense against EM (what blocks emergent misalignment via persona)
- truthification (how to make the model own/disown a persona)
- targeted persona intervention (the implication thread from #383)

## Cadence

Update during dedicated ideation sessions (not crammed between experiments).
A real pass takes hours, not minutes — this is the work, not overhead on the work.

## Why a task and not a doc

Tracking it as a task makes it visible in the queue, triagable, and surfaces
when it's gone stale. Output is a living doc (probably `docs/open_questions.md`
or similar); the task is the commitment to maintain it.

## Next concrete action

Schedule a 90-min ideation block (Mon-Wed morning deep-work slot)
to do the first full pass.
