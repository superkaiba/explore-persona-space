## 8. Risks and Failure Modes

- **Hybrid architecture/runtime:** Qwen3.8 uses Qwen3.5 linear/full attention and a multimodal wrapper. Generic text-only loader assumptions are invalid; actual model smoke is required. No quantized checkpoint substitution is approved.
- **Memory and speed:** nominal 27B is 55.56 GB of safetensor files staged under `/workspace/.cache/huggingface` on the verified remote `/workspace` filesystem. HBM peak, kernel selection and batch timings remain unmeasured until smoke. Lowering batch size or token budget is allowed only with the same inputs/precision/position, a new recorded capture recipe and fresh parity checks; no scope reduction.
- **Padding and centering:** raw-vector replay error can be amplified by mean centering. Report measured errors and refrain from interpreting differences comparable to numerical drift.
- **Prompt confounds:** language, verbosity of descriptions, list instructions and explicit prohibitions all vary. The paper's panel gives reproducible prompted contexts; it does not isolate one abstract persona variable.
- **Distribution and independence:** 240 constructed questions are not representative usage and 64 layers are not 64 independent experiments. The two halves measure stability within this bank only.
- **Partial writes and old sentinels:** interrupted chunk writes fail closed; fresh attempt/process status and content verification prevent stale completion claims.
- **Ephemeral compute/publication:** model cache, Torch environment and outputs reside on remote disk. Upload and verify all research artifacts before lifecycle teardown. No reliance on local existence or a merely queued alert.
- **Mutable paths:** the HF prefix is fixed, with exact inventory checks and immutable result revision. Any unexpected prior content must be handled explicitly; do not overwrite unverified sibling artifacts or force-push Git.
