# Plan — Task #605: Matched-geometry bystander panels: does the leakage gate need a behavior-overlap term beyond context similarity?

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** No new training. I take the marker implants and fact implants we already trained, and build a new set of evaluation contexts designed so that "how similar is this context to the trained one" and "how much does the untrained model already lean toward the behavior here" vary independently instead of moving together. Then I measure leakage in every context and ask: once you know the similarity, does knowing the model's pre-existing lean tell you anything extra about where the behavior shows up?
- **What I expect:** The pre-existing lean will keep predicting the absolute level of the behavior (that result is solid), but I'm betting it adds nothing to predicting how much *training moved* the behavior once similarity is held fixed. If that's right, the simple "one write, gated by similarity" picture of leakage survives, and the lean is a separate standing-level fact, not part of the gate.
- **What would change my mind:** If, among contexts at the *same* similarity to the source, the higher-lean contexts systematically received a bigger training-induced push, and that shows up in the raw logit reads too (not just the softmax-compressed ones), then the gate genuinely needs a second term and the working model gets revised.

## 1. Goal

Determine whether the rank-1 leakage gate needs a behavior-overlap term beyond base-model context similarity (canonical Goal, frontmatter). The rank-1 model (docs/notes/rank1_leakage_model.tex, §"Contexts encode behaviors") factorizes leakage as write × gate with gate = ⟨v_c′, v_c⟩; for persona-like contexts that inner product mixes prompt similarity with behavior-bundle overlap. #532 showed the two come apart on instruction contexts (geometry collapsed, base prior carried the signal) but never held similarity fixed while varying prior. This experiment runs the discriminating test verbatim: panels of eval contexts matched on context-vector similarity to the source within tight bands but spanning a wide range of base priors on the trained behavior, for two behavior families (marker, fact). The deliverable is the two-term-gate vs similarity-only comparison on held-out matched-geometry panels, per family, with the level-vs-shift and log-prob-vs-logit splits pre-registered (P3's measurement-vs-mechanism separation).

**N/A — not a replication** (no published finding is being replicated; the recipe is inherited from sibling issues).

The joint test at matched geometry appears novel.

## 3. Hypothesis

Let `sim(s,c)` = base-model context similarity (cosine @ L21, primary), `prior(c)` = base-model behavior prior of context c (operationalized per family, §6), per (source × context) cell.

- **H-level (expected to PASS — replication in matched-geometry):** on the *level* DVs (on-policy emission rate / absolute trained log P(※); judged fact-leak rate), the two-term gate beats similarity-only on held-out contexts: ΔCV-R² > +0.02 with 95% context-cluster bootstrap CI excluding 0, and within-band partial ρ(level, prior | sim) is Holm-significant in ≥ 2 of 3 similarity bands with |ρ| > 0.10. Falsified if the prior adds nothing once similarity is matched — which would mean #532's prior signal was itself a cohort artifact (a major revision to #532's HIGH claim).
- **H-shift (the gate question proper; directional bet = NULL):** on the *shift* DVs (Δlog P(※) trained−base at the corrected slot; fact teacher-forced Δlog P of the taught completion), the overlap term carries NO independent weight at matched similarity: ΔCV-R² CI includes 0 AND < 2/3 bands significant. This is the rank-1 model surviving with a similarity-only gate. **Falsified (gate needs the overlap term)** if prior carries weight on the shift in BOTH log-prob and EOS-margin-logit space (ΔCV-R² CI > 0 and ≥2/3 bands Holm-significant in both spaces).
- **H-mechanism split (P3):** if prior predicts the log-prob shift but NOT the EOS-margin logit shift, the verdict is "measurement-channel softmax compression, not a gate term" — pre-registered as a distinct outcome, not a partial confirmation of H-shift's falsification.
- **Structural alternative (decidable at Phase 1.5, before trained-model eval):** if the candidate pool cannot populate the high-similarity × wide-prior corner after one expansion round, the finding is "similarity and behavior prior cannot be decoupled in the high-similarity regime for this behavior family" — reported as a constraint on the gate factorization itself, with the test descoped to the populated bands.

Quantitative interpretation map (pre-registered):

| H-level | H-shift (log-prob) | H-shift (logit margin) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| prior wins | null | null | Gate survives similarity-only; prior = standing-level term (two-component rule from #532 confirmed at matched geometry) |
| prior wins | prior wins | null | Compression channel: prior enters through softmax measurement, not the gate |
| prior wins | prior wins | prior wins | **Gate needs a behavior-overlap term** — rank-1 model revision |
| prior null | any | any | #532's prior result was cohort-confounded — flags re-analysis of the parent |

Kill criteria are in §7; the gate set is jointly satisfiable (H-level and H-shift bind to different DVs; no two gates demand contradictory outcomes on the same measurement).

### 4.1 Matched-geometry candidate pool (marker family)

~120 new system-prompt contexts + the 26 legacy #532 contexts as anchors. New candidates cross two design axes:

- **Persona content (similarity axis):** *Source near-twins* — 2 paraphrases of each of the 5 #474 role personas + 2 paraphrases of representative framing/rewrite conditions (~16); *Related personas* — 12 professions/styles adjacent to the sources (e.g., "backend developer", "improv comedian"); *Unrelated personas* — 12 distant ones (e.g., "marine biologist", "tax auditor"); *Symbol-flavored personas* — 8 personas that naturally use typographic symbols ("a proofreader who marks notes with reference symbols like ※").
- **Marker affordance (prior axis), crossed within each content class:** *none* / *oblique few-shot examples* / *soft preference* ("you sometimes like to end responses with ※") / *explicit instruction* ("always end every response with ※"). Affordance text is appended to the persona prompt with varied phrasings (not one template).

This crossing targets the corners the legacy panel lacks: high-similarity + high-prior (near-twin + explicit) and low-similarity + low-prior (unrelated + none). Selection is by MEASURED values, never assumed: adding affordance text may move the context vector, which is exactly why Phase 1 measures both axes before any panel is fixed.

Candidate texts are written by Claude at implementation time and committed as a static module `scripts/issue605_contexts.py` (lint: candidates in the *none* affordance class must not contain "※"; dedupe by rendered prompt string; drop any pair with measured cosine ≥ 0.995 keeping one — the #539 quasi-duplicate trap).

**Data-source tier:** tier 3, diverse LLM-generated synthetic, with deliberate variation in length, register, structure, and topic. Justification for absence of tiers 1–2: the ※-marker behavior is project-invented, so no real-world corpus or established benchmark spans "affordance for this marker × similarity to these specific trained sources" by construction; the 26 legacy contexts (established in-project panel) anchor comparability. Fact-family personas: same tier and the same justification precedent as #541's 23 written personas.

**Few-shot / ICL demonstrations (the oblique affordance class):** (a) demos mirror the eval-task distribution — short general-knowledge Q→A pairs of the same shape as the `q_test_extended_50` probes, each answer ending in ※; (b) this content is chosen because the oblique band is #532's established low-prior instructed corner (base emission 0% despite the in-context examples) — it decouples "marker text present in context" from "high behavioral prior", populating the grid corner the design needs; (c) cross-context dynamic range: oblique candidates vary demo count (2–4), topic domain (geography / science / cooking / history / arts), answer length (one word to two sentences), and format (Q:/A: pairs, dialogue turns, bulleted list). Contamination check: demo questions are drawn from a written pool asserted disjoint (no string overlap) from `q_test_extended_50`.

### 4.2 Phase 1 — marker candidate measurement (base model only)

For each of ~146 candidate contexts (120 new + 26 legacy; legacy values reused from `eval_results/issue_532/` where the protocol is identical, recomputed otherwise):

2. **Graded behavior prior:** HF forward at the base model's own response-end slot (corrected-slot protocol: truncate before the first ※ if the base emits one) → 4 floats; `prior(c)` = mean log P(※) over probes. Reuses the capture path of `scripts/issue532_followup_logp_slot.py` (per `marker-leakage-measurement.md` storage contract).
3. **Context vectors:** last-prompt-token residual activations at layers {21, 22} over the 50 probes (HF hooks, reuse `_extract_last_prompt_activations_hf`). Pair similarity vs each of the 16 sources: cosine @ L21 (primary, #532's recipe — raw pairwise, uncentered, labeled as such per `persona-distance-metrics.md`) and Gaussian-sym-KL @ L22 k=16 (secondary, reuse `_gaussian_sym_kl_in_subspace_local`). A globally-mean-centered bank cosine over the 146+16-context bank is ALSO computed as a sensitivity (canonical for banks per #536), never numerically mixed with the raw line.
4. **Emission prior** (binary, per #532 Phase 0) recorded as a free sanity column.

### 4.3 Phase 1.5 — panel selection + tightness gate (CPU)

Operating on the (16 source × 146 candidate) pair table:

```python
# pseudocode — scripts/issue605_matched_panels.py::select_marker_panel()
pairs = load_pair_table()                       # cos_L21, gkl_L22, prior, per pair
edges = quantile(pairs.cos, [1/3, 2/3])         # band edges FIXED here, before any trained eval

    n_contexts(b) >= 10 (distinct contexts contributing pairs)

If it still fails → descope to the populated bands and report the structural finding (§3). Selection uses only base-model quantities, so it cannot leak the outcome.

### 4.6 Why code, not a model call?

## 6. Evaluation

### Measurement validity (per DV)

| DV | Construct (what the Goal cares about) | Metric (actually computed) | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation / justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marker level | how much the implanted marker behavior shows up when the model answers under context c | on-policy ※ emission rate + absolute trained log P(※) at the corrected slot of the model's OWN response (50 probes/cell) | yes (on-policy, natural slot, realistic probe distribution) | — |
| Marker shift (primary for H-shift) | how much training moved the marker at context c | Δlog P(※) trained − base at the identical corrected slot; Δ(z※ − z_eos) as the mechanism space | mostly — trained side on-policy; base side teacher-forced at the trained model's slot | established #532-follow-up protocol; known stop-position caveat where the trained model emits → mitigations: non-emitting-cells robustness slice, EOS-margin read, per-cell saturation flags (Δlog P vs Δz divergence) |

| Fact shift | training-induced increase in taught-completion likelihood under persona | length-normalized TF Δlog P(taught completion), trained − base, 239 teach rows | no — teacher-forced proxy (no single-slot equivalent exists for a multi-token fact) | validated in-run: pre-registered check that TF Δlog P correlates with the judged on-policy leak rate across the 162 (cell × persona) cells at ρ > 0.4; if it fails, the fact-family H-shift read is reported as proxy-invalid and the level DV stands alone |

Marker DV stays marker-specific in all three spaces (log-prob primary, EOS-margin logit secondary, probability sanity) — never full-vocab KL. Four-float storage per slot per side, HF capture.

### Statistics

- **Read A (within-band):** per band × family × DV: partial Spearman ρ(DV, prior | continuous pair-cosine within band + source mean DV), 1000-rep context-cluster bootstrap (the prior is a context-level scalar; the cluster respects its replication across sources), Holm over the 3 bands within each (family, DV-class) test family. Source-cluster bootstrap reported alongside.
- **Read B (held-out two-term comparison, headline):** grouped 5-fold CV by context (all of a context's cells share a fold): model 1 = {pair cosine + source FE}; model 2 = {+ prior}. Statistic = out-of-fold ΔCV-R², CI by 1000-rep context-cluster bootstrap re-running the full CV per resample. The reverse ladder ({prior + source FE} vs {+ cosine}) is reported for symmetry with #532's hierarchy. Fact family: same with arm + seed FE, folds grouped by persona.
- **Collinearity gate (pre-registered):** if realized |Pearson(cosine, prior)| > 0.6 on the selected panel (overall or within a band), fall back to tercile-bucket median contrasts + polynomial residualization robustness; the Phase-1.5 selection targets ≤ 0.3 within-band by construction.
- **Censoring:** cells flagged saturated (trained log P > −0.1 nat AND argmax-emission ≥ 0.92) are (a) excluded in the clean log-prob fit, (b) retained under a Tobit/censored model, (c) always retained in the EOS-margin fit — three columns reported per table.

## 7. Decision Gates

The run is short (<6 h wall) and mostly pre-verified machinery, so the gate set is minimal — two gates, both cheap and both grounded:

1. **Adapter-application smoke (Phase 0, blocks the sweep).** The off-line eval path must reproduce known source-cell reads before any sweep: marker — source A1 self-cell trained log P(※) within 1 nat of #532's recorded per-cell value (−0.24; `eval_results/issue_532/logp_slot_followup/per_cell_trained/`); fact — marine-biologist seed-42 teacher-self TF Δlog P(taught completion) > +1 nat (direction grounded in #541's 72–97% teacher self-assertion; the +1 nat margin is a heuristic floor). Grounded in incident #534 (40 trajectory passes ran without adapters, ΔG ≈ 0 everywhere). FAIL → infra fix, not a science verdict.
2. **Panel-fill / tightness gate (Phase 1.5 / 4.5, CPU, before all trained-side GPU spend).** Criteria in §4.3. One pre-registered expansion round; second failure → descope to populated bands + report the structural finding. Sign/threshold grounding: prior spread ≥ 6 nats ≈ 70% of the 8.4-nat graded-prior spread #532 measured across ordinary contexts alone, so the demand is weaker than what an already-measured panel achieved; band width 0.06 ≈ 1/7 of the observed pair-cosine range (0.58–1.0, min 0.580 per `eval_results/issue_532/predictors.json::cosine_matrix`) on this line.

No mid-run kill gates on the science outcome — every cell is informative regardless of H-level/H-shift direction.

## 11. Decision Rationale

No model training — "N/A — no training hyperparameters." Load-bearing eval/analysis parameters:

- **What:** reuse #474 loc ep1 adapters as the marker anchors. **Why:** the Goal needs non-saturated implants with graded bystander headroom; retraining 16 sources would cost ~30 GPU-h and break comparability with #532's 416 anchor cells. **Source:** #532 (validated this exact set in this exact regime). **Alternatives:** fresh band-stopped retrain (rejected: cost + breaks anchor comparability); ep2/ep3 checkpoints (rejected: #532's §A5 saturation fail-loud at ep2).
- **What:** 50 probes/cell, greedy, `max_new_tokens=2048`. **Source:** #532; CLAUDE.md ≥2048 rule (#260). **Alternatives:** 30 probes (descope rung only).
- **What:** cosine @ L21 (raw pairwise) + Gaussian-KL @ L22 (k=16) as a-priori similarity. **Why:** these ARE the predictors under test; the single-variable contract requires #532's operationalization; layers fixed a priori to avoid P5 circularity. **Source:** #404/#458/#502/#532. **Alternatives:** layer re-sweep (rejected: circular); centered-bank cosine (kept as labeled sensitivity per #536).
- **What:** marker prior = graded base log P(※) at base own-response-end slot. **Source:** #532 follow-up (`base_prior_logp.json`). **Alternatives:** binary emission prior (kept as sanity column; zero-variance on ordinary-like contexts).
- **What:** fact prior = length-norm TF log P over the 239-row teach pool. **Source:** #541/#500 (identical metric, makes priors directly comparable). **Alternatives:** subsampled pool (rejected: breaks comparability for ~20 min of savings).
- **What:** fact eval at 60 rows/cell-persona (vs #541's 270). **Why:** #541's per-persona rates stabilized well below 270 rows for rank statistics; 60 = 12 framings × 5 keeps every framing represented at 1/4.5 cost. **Source:** descope of #541's design — `partially ungrounded; the 60-row choice needs the Phase-0 fact smoke to confirm per-cell rate stability` (flagged in §12).
- **What:** band tightness thresholds (width ≤ 0.06, prior spread ≥ 6 nats, |r| ≤ 0.3). **Source:** `pre-registered design choices — ungrounded, validated at the cheap CPU Phase-1.5 gate` (rationale: 6 nats < the 8.4-nat ordinary spread #532 already measured; 0.06 ≈ 1/7 of the observed 0.58–1.0 pair-cosine range; 0.3 from the collinearity-gate pattern used on this project's N<30 partials).
- **What:** statistics suite (cluster bootstrap 1000, Holm, grouped CV, Tobit fallback). **Source:** #539/#541/#555 practice; censored-model fallback per `marker-leakage-measurement.md`.
- **Model id:** `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` — repo-standard, used by every cited config; no CPU config-load smoke needed (not repo-new).
