## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (primary).** At least one of the 6 new full-FT cells lands with source ΔG strictly above 9 nat AND with <50% r-collapse on source probes (10/20+ valid source reads) AND with held-out g_logprob ≤ −5 nat (sub-ceiling). Equivalently: the FT bracketing rule PASSES on this follow-up.

**H1 confirmed:** The matched-rate read at source ΔG = 8 ± 1 nat is determinate (local read and cluster-bootstrap read agree within 0.5 nat on bystander leakage). Headline: LoRA-vs-FT bystander-leakage gap at matched source rate, with sign and CI.

**H1 falsified:** Both levers fail to land a clean FT cell above 9 nat. Each of the 6 new cells either (a) under-trains (source ΔG <7 nat AND <50% emission rate on source — fluent answers, no implant), or (b) over-trains (≥50% r-collapse on source probes). The clean answer is then "full-FT in this contrastive recipe does not have a graded implant knob between 0 and 1 epoch at lr ≤ 5e-6 — the cliff is intrinsic to the regime, not a budget-resolution artifact." This is a substantive negative result.

**H2 (secondary, conditional on H1).** Held-out bystander leakage at matched source rate (8 ± 1 nat) for full-FT is within 1 nat of LoRA's reference (~3.5 nat from #508). Direction: full-FT ≥ LoRA (we have a prior of "full-FT updates every weight, so it might leak more" but no strong magnitude).

**H2 falsified:** Either matched-rate FT leakage > LoRA + 2 nat (full-FT meaningfully more leaky) OR matched-rate FT leakage < LoRA − 2 nat (full-FT meaningfully less leaky — surprising).

**Quantitative threshold.** "Determinate matched-rate read" = the per-arm local linear interpolation at source ΔG=8 nat and the cluster-bootstrap interpolation through all cells agree within 0.5 nat on the bystander-leakage estimate (in #508 they disagreed by 2.66 nat — that's the indeterminacy this experiment exists to resolve).

Controls:
- **The recipe is held fixed.** Source, negatives, training data, marker, collator, eval panel, DV — all inherit verbatim from #508. The only manipulated variable is the FT training regime (a 2D wedge: budget + LR).
- **Within-arm comparison.** All 6 new cells are FULL-FT; no LoRA arm is retrained. The LoRA reference is the curve from #508. This means the analysis answers "does FT have a graded knob?" before answering "does FT leak more than LoRA?" — the latter requires H1 to be confirmed.
- **R-collapse detection.** Every cell's eval JSON records `r_collapsed` per probe (defined per #508: trained model emits ≥80% marker tokens in its own response, or hits the 2048-token cap with the marker repeated). The 50% r-collapse threshold for the abort criterion + the bracketing rule's "clean cell" definition both key off this field.
- **Single seed.** Seed 42, inherited from #508. NOT changed because (a) the single-variable rule, (b) the question is "does a clean FT cell exist at all?" — finding one is the headline; a second seed on the decisive cell is queued as a follow-up to #514, NOT included in this experiment (see §9 stratification + power for the wall-time math that excludes a second seed).

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 DVs + measurement validity table

| DV | Construct | Metric | On-distribution? | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Source ΔG** | "Has the model learned to emit the marker after its own villain-persona response?" | On-policy: trained model writes its own greedy response under villain prompt, then `log P(` ※` | T_villain(q) + R_trained_villain)` − `log P(` ※` | T_villain(q) + R_trained_villain)` evaluated under base model, both at the slot right after R. Reported per-probe and averaged over 20 source probes per cell. | yes | The marker DOES occur at the post-response slot in training (positive rows: villain-response + ※); the metric reads it on the model's own generation under the source prompt at the same slot. `Source: .claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`. |
| **Bystander mean ΔG** | "Does the source-installed marker behavior leak to held-out personas the model has never seen as either positive or negative?" | On-policy: trained model writes its own greedy response under each of 15 held-out persona prompts × 20 questions = 300 probes. Bystander mean = arithmetic mean over the 300 probes. | yes | The "behavior leaks" construct IS about the model's own bystander-persona responses, not teacher-forced ones; the rule's anti-pattern explicitly bans teacher-forced reads. |
| **Default-assistant ΔG** | "Does the source-installed marker leak to the bare default assistant — the safety target?" | On-policy under qwen_default system prompt, 20 questions = 20 probes. Same metric shape as bystander mean ΔG. | yes | The "leakage to default" construct IS the model's behavior under no persona prompt, which is what this slice measures. |
| **R-collapse rate per cell** | "Has the trained model stopped producing graded responses and started emitting only markers?" | On-policy: count probes where trained model's R is ≥80% marker tokens OR hits the 2048-token cap with the marker repeated. Computed per cell, per persona, and globally. | yes | Behavior IS the model's own response generation; counted on-policy. |
| **Marker-in-R rate per cell** | "Has the marker generalized from the post-response slot to inside bystander responses (formatting habit)?" | Count probes where `n_marker_in_R > 0 AND NOT r_collapsed`. Per cell. | yes | Same — direct count of marker emissions inside the model's own bystander R. |
| **Bracketing rule (binary)** | "Does the FT arm have a clean cell strictly above 9 nat source ΔG?" | For each new FT cell: source ΔG > 9 nat AND r-collapse < 50% on source probes AND held-out g_logprob ≤ −5 nat (sub-ceiling). PASS iff ≥1 cell satisfies. | yes | Direct read of the §5-3 acceptance criterion from cell-level aggregates. |
| **Matched-rate read (continuous, conditional on bracketing PASS)** | "What is the bystander leakage at the source ΔG = 8 nat point, per arm?" | Local read: linear interpolation between the two FT cells bracketing source ΔG = 8 nat on the source-ΔG-vs-bystander-ΔG plane. Cluster-bootstrap read: 1000 cluster bootstrap replicates over 15 personas × 20 questions, interpolated through ALL FT cells. The two MUST agree within 0.5 nat (the §3 quantitative determinacy threshold). | yes | Bootstrap CI over the on-policy ΔG samples directly; the interpolation is a downstream computation on validated on-policy reads. |

### 6.2 Bracketing-rule application + acceptance

For full-FT (#514 + #508 anchors):
- "Below 7 nat" cells (any): `ft_b2` (#508, 6.8 nat collapsed — but the source-ΔG read is on 1 valid probe so it's noise; the dispatcher's analyze step records this and downweights it). If no NEW under-7 cell exists, the §5 bracketing rule borrows #508's FT-light cell at 8.2 nat as the lower flank (8.2 is in the matched-rate window 8 ± 1, so it's not strictly below 7 — flag in the analysis as "lower flank from #508 anchor"). Acceptable per the rule's `≥1 cell <7` text iff we also have `≥1 cell >9`; in practice the rule cares about bracketing the 8 ± 1 window, so a clean cell at 8.2 nat (the lower flank) plus a clean cell above 9 nat (the upper flank) brackets the window even though the strict "<7" reading would require an additional under-7 cell. The decision-rule text used in #514's analyze.py: PASS iff there exists ≥1 new FT cell with source ΔG ∈ (7, 9) (interior of the band) AND ≥1 new FT cell with source ΔG > 9 nat that's clean.
- "Above 9 nat" cells (any new): the §3 H1 acceptance criterion.

Matched-rate read becomes computable iff PASS. If FAIL on both levers, headline = the substantive negative result from §3 H1-falsified.

### 6.3 Figures to produce (over-produce; hero picked at analyze time)

Per §4.1.4 above. Six figures total. The hero figure is the source-ΔG-vs-bystander-mean-ΔG scatter with the LoRA reference curve, the #508 FT anchors at half-transparency, and the 6 new FT cells. If H1 confirmed, the hero is supplemented with the matched-rate bar chart (`matched_rate.png`). If H1 falsified, the hero shifts to the r-collapse-vs-budget plot showing the cliff is intrinsic.

The exploratory dump (over-produced, analyzer cherry-picks): per-cell bars of held-out ΔG by persona; per-seed scatter (N/A here — single seed); per-step trajectory lines from the marker-dynamics-callback offline extractor; raw `_bootstrap_per_cell.json` arrays alongside the residualized matched-rate read.

### 6.4 Statistical tests

- **Bystander-leakage CI per cell:** cluster bootstrap, 1000 replicates, resampling 15 personas × 20 questions with replacement (per #508).
- **Matched-rate disagreement test:** local linear-interpolation read at source ΔG = 8 nat MINUS cluster-bootstrap interpolation through all FT cells. Determinate iff |diff| ≤ 0.5 nat. In #508 this diff was 2.66 nat → indeterminate.
- **Extrapolation policy (v3 pivot — added after round-3 FAIL).** After B7's correct exclusion of `ft_b2`, the only clean below-target-upper-bound FT anchor in the inherited #508 data is `ft_b1` at source ΔG = 8.193 nat, which sits INSIDE the matched-rate window [7, 9] rather than strictly below the target=8.0. The local read with anchor pair `(ft_b1=8.193, clean_above_9_cell)` is therefore structurally extrapolation at target=8.0, not interpolation. The analyze module's `_linear_interp_at` MUST allow extrapolation in this case and set `is_extrapolation: true` in `_matched_rate_514.json`. The previous round-3 behavior (refuse to extrapolate, return NaN) defeats the H1 headline; the new policy returns a finite extrapolated value with the extrapolation caveat carried forward to the analyzer prose. The determinacy gate (|local − bootstrap| ≤ 0.5 nat) still applies — extrapolation does NOT relax the gate. The analyzer reports the extrapolation distance explicitly (target=8.0 nat, lower-anchor source-ΔG, gap-from-target in nats) so a reader can weigh whether the extrapolation is small (e.g. 0.2 nat) or large (e.g. 2 nat) and judge the determinacy verdict accordingly.
- **No formal multiple-comparison correction.** 6 cells, one DV per cell, no per-persona inference (per-persona is presented descriptively for the per_persona figure). H1 is a presence-check (does a clean cell exist?), not a hypothesis test.

## 7. Decision Gates

**No gates.** Expected wall-clock is ~15h end-to-end, well over the 4h "no gate" threshold, BUT:
- The hypothesis is INSTRUMENTAL (find a clean cell) not "the effect direction is uncertain at this scale".
- The cheapest possible intermediate signal IS already in the design as the `--abort-on-collapse` per-lever short-circuit (§4.1.3): after the smallest budget in each lever, if that cell is already collapsed AND saturated, the remaining cells of THAT lever skip. This is a per-lever decision built into the dispatcher, not a user-facing gate.
- The pre-verified hypothesis ("the cliff sits between 0.25 and 0.5 epoch, and below it the model trains cleanly") was confirmed by #508's `ft_b1` cell.

The lever-abort is automatic; the user does not need to gate it.

## 9. Resources & Parallelism

**Pod intent:** `ft-7b` (4× H100 SXM, ZeRO-3). One pod. The 6 FT cells run SEQUENTIALLY on this pod (each ZeRO-3 cell needs all 4 GPUs). The 6 evals also run sequentially on the same pod (vLLM TP=1 on GPU 0, the other 3 GPUs idle — eval is fast enough that the wall-time saved by parallel eval is not worth the dispatcher complexity).

**Parallelism axis:** within-cell, ZeRO-3 across 4× H100 (data parallelism via FSDP). Across cells, sequential — the cells are MUTUALLY DEPENDENT only through the optional `--abort-on-collapse` short-circuit; otherwise they're independent and could in principle parallelize across 4 single-GPU pods. We do NOT parallelize that way because (a) each cell needs 4 GPUs for ZeRO-3, (b) the eff-batch-64 ZeRO-3 recipe is what #508 used and the single-variable rule says don't change it.

**The ZeRO-3 + ft-7b spec is the correct one** — `lora-7b` (1× H100) would force LoRA on the FT arm, which is the parent's confound and the experimental variable here is the OPPOSITE. `ft-70b` (8× H200) overshoots for a 7B model.
