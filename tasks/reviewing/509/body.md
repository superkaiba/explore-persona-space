# The #502 residual-stream predictor for marker leakage does not transfer to fact or sycophancy leakage at its pre-registered cell; on sycophancy a real signal lives at early-to-mid layers across both extraction points, not the L19-L24 last-prompt ridge (MODERATE confidence)

## Human TL;DR

**Headline.** the residual-stream geometry that predicted marker-leakage transfer in #502 does NOT generalize at its own winning cell — on sycophancy the pre-registered L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL anchor is flat under source-fixed-effects ( |ρ| = 0.09, p = 0.30, n = 138 ), and the wider L19-L24 ridge has a signed mean of -0.02 across 18 cells (mean |ρ| = 0.05); on the same syco panel a different cell DOES carry real signal but it sits at early-to-mid layers across BOTH end-of-system AND last-prompt extraction points ( |ρ| ≈ 0.43-0.50 at L02-L08, perm p ≤ 0.001 ); on fact the bake-off underperforms #494's coarse predictors at the pre-registered cell, and the search-best cell's lift collapses once #500's bystander-fact prior is partialled out.

**Takeaways.**
- on sycophancy with full production statistics, the pre-registered #502 cell lands at |ρ_fe_adj| = 0.09 (p = 0.30, CI = [-0.27, +0.35], n = 138) and the L19-L24 ridge has a signed mean of -0.02 across 18 cells (mean |ρ| = 0.05) — both indistinguishable from null after source fixed effects
- at that same pre-registered cell the POOLED Spearman is -0.40 (in the marker-leakage direction) before source-FE residualization; the parent #502 used pooled length-partial Spearman without source-FE, so part of the pre-reg null is a methodology hardening, not a clean replication failure
- the syco arm DOES carry persona-distance signal, but it lives at early-to-mid layers across MULTIPLE extraction points: the actual global max by |ρ_fe_adj| is `last_prompt × L07 × MMD × centered` at 0.499, and the L02-L08 end-of-system cosine band reaches 0.43-0.49 with the same permutation p ≤ 0.001 and the same comedian-recovery rank 5/23 (#470's coarse predictors all ranked comedian 23/23); the L19-L24 last-prompt cloud-aware ridge that won on markers is the ONLY part of the predictor zoo that stays flat
- the L02 end-of-system cosine cell's signal is NOT source-uniform: per-source ρ is -0.762 (comedian) / -0.631 (software_engineer) / -0.538 (assistant) / -0.492 (villain) / -0.304 (qwen_default) / +0.047 (kindergarten_teacher) — five of six sources show the predicted negative association, one source sits near zero with the wrong sign
- on fact (smoke; reliability_y = 1.0 fallback, Path B per-seed SE reconstruction NOT yet run), the L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL bake-off cell sits at |ρ_fe| = 0.36 on n = 25 cells but its `coarse_lift.delta_rho` is -0.25 — the cell underperforms #494's `fact_slice_js` coarse predictor on the same pairs; zero of 953 fact cells have `delta_rho ≥ +0.15`
- even the fact-arm search-best cell (end-of-system × L1 cosine, |ρ_fe| = 0.67 on n = 18 substrate-FE cells; note L1-L4 are tied at the same ρ) collapses to |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03 once both substrate AND #500's bystander prior on the fact are partialled out; 9 non-saturated cells across last-prompt + mean_response, spanning gauss_kl + mahal + mmd + c2st metric families, retain |ρ_double_fe| ≈ 0.37-0.40 but lack the cluster + recovery structure that defended the syco signal against selection inflation across 953 fact cells
- the honest read is "the recipe is marker-specific at its registered cell; there IS persona-distance signal for sycophancy on this panel but it sits in a different part of the predictor zoo, in a different region of the residual stream, and from a different methodology choice (source-FE) than the parent recipe used"

**How this updates me.** I'm less confident that the specific cell that won on #502 (last_prompt × L22 × gauss_kl on the L19-L24 ridge) is a universal persona-distance axis — the pre-registered triple-trigger is cleared by neither arm at that cell, and the syco pooled |ρ| = -0.40 at that cell collapses entirely under source-FE residualization. I'm moderately more confident that there IS some persona-distance signal for sycophancy on the #411 panel, but it lives at early-to-mid layers across multiple extraction points, with heterogeneous per-source magnitudes — so "different behavior, different cell, different extraction-points-plural" is the honest framing, not "the residual stream encodes a universal persona-distance axis." Syco-only confidence is MODERATE (single-seed-42 target, 1065-cell search disciplined by cluster + recovery structure); fact-arm confidence is LOW (smoke caveat + selection-inflation disciplines the 9 cells surviving prior partial, none of which carry the L0-L8 cluster + comedian-recovery structure that syco does). A production fact-arm rerun with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would close the smoke caveat on the absolute |ρ| threshold (the relative ordering is invariant under shared-reliability attenuation, but the 0.36 threshold itself could move). A multi-seed syco target rerun + a prompt-text-similarity control on the early end-of-system cell would address two of the three remaining alternative explanations.

## TL;DR

### Motivation

#502 found that one specific cell in a ~1500-cell predictor sweep (last-prompt × layer-22 × Gaussian-KL on an L19-L24 ridge) predicted marker-leakage transfer between personas at Spearman |ρ| = 0.79 on the cleanest training checkpoint. The natural next question is whether that residual-stream geometry tracks persona distance for the *behavior* the marker proxies for, or just for the marker itself. Two prior null results pointed at the second answer: #494 found pooled persona-distance predictors null on fact-teaching adapters (the contrastive #444 + the positive-only #381/#389/#390/#192 line), and #470 found a single-layer cosine + an output-distribution JS null on the #411 sycophancy panel. The critical gap was that BOTH of those nulls used the coarse single-layer cosine + output-distribution JS predictor family — exactly the family #502 showed gets beaten by the full 28-layer × 9-cloud-metric × 3-extraction-point bake-off. This run closes that gap: it takes #502's full bake-off recipe, points it at the two non-marker behaviors using the already-trained adapters and stored leakage matrices, and asks whether the L19-L24 last-prompt Gaussian-KL / MMD / Wasserstein-2 ridge that won on markers also wins on facts or sycophancy. Three outcomes pre-registered, all informative: **generalizes** (the recipe is a behavior-agnostic persona-distance axis), **marker-specific** (the #502 signal is specific to the marker construct, the prior nulls were not resolution artifacts), or **behavior-dependent** (one arm yes, one arm no — tells us which axis of behavior the geometry tracks). The pre-registration trigger for "generalizes" required attenuation-adjusted |ρ_best_cell| ≥ 0.40 AND Δρ ≥ 0.15 over the coarse predictor AND within-stratum permutation p < 0.05 AND ≥ 3 of the L19-L24 ridge cells meeting all three thresholds AND the ridge mean reaching within Δρ = 0.15 of the #502 non-stylized 156-pair anchor of -0.58 (NOT the full-panel ρ = -0.79, which the plan correctly identified as an over-anchor because ~80% of its CV R² lift came from three stylized characters that neither downstream panel contains).

### What I ran

A single Qwen-2.5-7B run, no retraining. For each arm I ran the existing 28-layer × 9-cloud-metric × 3-extraction-point bake-off driver across that arm's persona panel under each persona's verbatim system prompt, computed 9 pairwise cloud-distance metrics + a next-token JS baseline at every layer-extraction-point cell, and scored each cell's distance matrix against that arm's stored leakage matrix with length-partial Spearman ρ residualized for the appropriate fixed effects, plus a within-stratum permutation null, plus a 5000-rep cluster bootstrap CI. **Methodology shift vs the parent:** #502 reported pooled length-partial Spearman without source-side fixed effects (its marker panel was 16 conditions, no source-level clustering to worry about); this run adds source/substrate fixed effects on BOTH arms because the downstream panels have strong within-source clustering (6 sources × 23 bystanders on syco, 5 substrates × 9 personas on fact). The fixed-effects step is doing real work — of the 814 syco cells with |ρ_pooled| > 0.10 (out of 1026 cells with non-null ρ_fe_adj), 243 have opposite signs for ρ_pooled and ρ_fe_adj — so where ρ_pooled and ρ_fe disagree, the result reflects "source-confounded pooled association disappears under source-FE," not "no signal at all."

- **Fact arm** — 9 personas across 5 training substrates (marine biologist, Zelthari scholar, Qwen default, local historian, local resident, assistant, software engineer, kindergarten teacher, no-system control). The leakage target is the 26-cell stored matrix from #494's `regression_data.csv` (per-(teach → bystander) `leak_rate` across the #444 + #192 + #381 / #389 / #390 adapter line, 3-seed averaged). The bake-off scores against the substrate-FE residualized panel (n = 18 cells after the substrate-FE step) and reports the per-cell double-FE Spearman after partialling out both substrate AND #500's `bystander_logprob` prior. Probe pool: 500 mixed-distribution probes inherited from #502.
- **Sycophancy arm** — 24 personas across the #411 6-source × 23-bystander panel (138 off-diagonal cells; the same 6 sources × 23 bystanders panel the parent paper reported). The leakage target is the per-(source, bystander) Δ from #411's frozen leakage matrix (10 rollouts × 50 probes × Claude Haiku judge per cell, single seed 42). The bake-off scores against the source-FE residualized 138-cell panel and reports a per-cell binomial-SE attenuation-adjusted ρ_fe_adj alongside a within-source permutation null (B = 2000) and a 5000-rep cluster bootstrap CI clustered on source. Probe pool: #411's 50 held-out wrong-claim probes.
- **Three comparison anchors carried into every figure:** (1) #494's coarse predictors (`cosine_a_L21`, `cosine_b_L21`, `js_on_topic`, `fact_slice_js`, `bystander_logprob`) on the same pairs, so any bake-off cell's "lift" is the delta vs the coarse cell, NOT vs zero; (2) #502's full-panel ρ_ΔG = -0.748 on 240 marker-leakage pairs; (3) #502's non-stylized 156-pair ρ_ΔG = -0.581 — the apples-to-apples anchor since neither downstream panel contains the three stylized characters (pirate / comedian / villain) that contributed ~80% of the #502 lift.

Pre-registered cell: `last_prompt × L22 × gauss_kl × centered` (the exact #502 winner). Pre-registered ridge: the 18 cells at `last_prompt × {L19..L24} × {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2}`. Search-best cell: the cell within the per-arm ~950-1065 non-saturated scored cells with the largest |ρ_fe_adj|, reported with the explicit "selected from ~950+ candidates" disclaimer per the plan §6.3 selection-correction rule. The pre-registered cell is what the marker-leakage winner WAS; the search-best cell is what THIS arm's bake-off would have picked on its own if there were no prior cell to anchor on; the ridge mean is what doesn't depend on the lucky single cell.

**Sweep accounting.** 1513 metric files were scored per arm — 28 layers × 3 extraction points × 9 metrics × 2 variants (centered + raw) gives a nominal 1512, plus the next-token JS baseline at the last-prompt position. Of those, 953 cells produced a valid scored row on the fact arm (560 marked N/A by the saturation screener; `matrix:null` with `n_cells_skipped_na = 560`) and 1065 cells produced a valid scored row on the syco arm (448 N/A). Inside the scored set, the per-pair saturation screen excludes any pair where one distance is at the metric's saturation floor — 561 of the 953 scored fact cells exclude at least one such pair (median 1, max 7), and the bake-off n drops from a nominal 26 substrate-FE cells to 25 at the pre-reg cell and 18 at the search-best cell as a result. The 953/1065 vs 1513 split is the partition-merge step deliberately marking some cells N/A; the 561/953 split is the per-pair screen inside the scored set.

The fact arm ran in **smoke mode** because Path B per-seed SE reconstruction from the per-seed judged completions on HF was not implemented for this run — the fallback sets `reliability_y = 1.0` on every fact cell, which means `ρ_fe_adj = ρ_fe` (no attenuation correction). The syco arm ran in production mode end to end. The four code-fix landings during the run (per-pid tmp-file race in `_write_json_atomic`; `system_prompt=None` NoneType crash in the extraction prompt builder for the no-system control conditions; the merge-step `_expected_eos` partition AssertionError on intentional `system_prompt=None` Class-A conditions; and the `_matrix_to_dict` AttributeError on the intentional `matrix:null` N/A cells from the saturation screener — fixed by returning None + counting `n_cells_skipped_na` + a ≥25% sanity-cap guard) all advanced the pipeline without altering the recipe or the panels.

### Findings

#### At its own pre-registered cell and at the L19-L24 ridge, the #502 recipe does not generalize to either behavior

The pre-registered triple-trigger on the syco arm comes back loud and null UNDER SOURCE-FE: attenuation-adjusted |ρ_fe_adj| at last-prompt × L22 × gauss_kl × centered = 0.089, well below the 0.40 floor; within-source permutation p = 0.30, well above the 0.05 ceiling; cluster-bootstrap 95% CI = [-0.27, +0.35], straddling zero. The L19-L24 last-prompt ridge across {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2} has a signed mean of -0.02 across 18 cells (mean |ρ| = 0.05) with zero of the 18 cells clearing |ρ| = 0.40 — not one ridge cell, not just a near-miss. The strict reading of the plan §6.2 pre-registration is **marker-specific verdict at the #502 cell on the syco arm**, with the binding constraint that the comedian-recovery rank at the L22 cell is 23/23 (the same failure mode every coarse predictor in #470 hit).

The methodology-shift caveat: at the SAME cell the POOLED Spearman is `ρ_pooled_adj = -0.403` — in the marker-leakage direction, at about half the magnitude of #502's pooled full-panel `ρ = -0.748`, and the syco arm's source-FE residualization is what drops it to +0.089. The parent #502 reported pooled length-partial Spearman without source-FE on a 16-condition marker panel; this run adds source-FE on a 6-source × 23-bystander panel because the within-source clustering is strong enough to confound pooled estimates. So the syco pre-reg verdict is more precisely: "the pooled association replicates the marker direction at smaller magnitude, but disappears once source clustering is partialled out" — the source clustering is doing the work, NOT a clean replication failure of the cell. The reader is owed both numbers; I default to the source-FE number for the trigger comparison because the source-level clustering on this panel is real and is what the syco-arm pre-registration anticipated. The same pattern holds across the entire L22 last-prompt metric family on syco — across the 15 L22 LP cells with valid statistics, ρ_pooled ranges over [-0.46, -0.24] and ρ_fe_adj ranges over [-0.21, +0.09], with all 16 cells showing the same pooled-to-FE collapse pattern (ρ_pooled clusters near the marker-leakage direction while ρ_fe_adj clusters near zero). The source-FE step is doing a lot of work, and the result fundamentally depends on treating source-level differences as confounds rather than signal.

The fact arm's number at the same cell looks larger (|ρ_fe| = 0.36 on 25 substrate-FE residualized cells; `coarse_lift.bakeoff_rho_fe_abs = 0.335` is the rounded coarse-lift field for the same cell) but is misleading in three ways. First, smoke mode pins `reliability_y = 1.0`, so the 0.36 is `ρ_fe` itself, NOT the attenuation-adjusted number the §6.2 trigger asks for (the true `ρ_fe_adj` is computable only with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction, which this run skipped). Second, the SIGN is positive — the wrong direction for "closer personas leak more" relative to the marker anchors at -0.58 / -0.75. Third, the per-cell `coarse_lift.delta_rho` on the same 25 pairs is **-0.252**: the bake-off cell underperforms #494's fact-slice-JS coarse predictor by 0.25 ρ-points on the same data. So even before reading the ridge mean (|ρ| = 0.08 on 18 cells, similar story), the fact arm fails to clear the Δρ ≥ 0.15 trigger over the coarse predictor at the cell that was supposed to be the universal winner. A production rerun with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would tighten the numbers, but the ridge mean and the coarse-predictor delta_rho already say the recipe does not transfer here.

The 240-pair full-panel anchor at |ρ| = 0.75 is plotted as the optimistic reference (since the plan rejected this anchor explicitly), and the non-stylized 156-pair anchor at |ρ| = 0.58 is the apples-to-apples reference. The two downstream arms reach roughly 12% and 16% of the non-stylized anchor at the pre-registered cell, and 1-14% at the ridge.

The sycophancy bake-off's strongest single cell is **`last_prompt × L07 × MMD × centered` at |ρ_fe_adj| = 0.499** (permutation p = 0.0005, the floor for the B = 2000 null; bootstrap CI = [-0.61, -0.33]). The cell I built the original cherry-picked example around — `end_of_system × L02 × cosine × centered` at |ρ_fe_adj| = 0.489, perm p = 0.0005, CI = [-0.61, -0.28], comedian-recovery rank 5/23 — is the second-best by a hair AND is the representative max of a tightly correlated L0-L13 end-of-system cosine cluster (range 0.387-0.489; the L0-L8 sub-cluster is 0.428-0.489). The signal isn't a single lucky cell either: 172 of 1065 scored cells clear permutation p ≤ 0.001 (94 last_prompt, 74 end_of_system, 4 mean_response), and 9 of the top 15 cells by |ρ_fe_adj| are end_of_system while 6 are last_prompt. End-of-system dominates the very top of the leaderboard while last-prompt dominates the broader p ≤ 0.001 floor — so both extraction points carry signal, with the relative balance flipping depending on whether you're reading the top-15 cells or the wider band of significant ones. On the LIVE-cells-only subset (the 21 cells where |Δ| > 0.10 per the plan §4.2.6 screen, restricting to where the leakage target has real variance instead of binomial noise floor), the L02 EoS cosine cell holds at |ρ_fe_live| = 0.473 on 21 cells. That's a real, replicated, multi-cell signal at the construct (cross-persona sycophancy transfer) — but it lives across MULTIPLE extraction points at early-to-mid layers, NOT specifically at end-of-system.

**Per-source heterogeneity at the L02 EoS cosine cell:** the source-level ρ values are -0.762 (comedian) / -0.631 (software_engineer) / -0.538 (assistant) / -0.492 (villain) / -0.304 (qwen_default) / +0.047 (kindergarten_teacher) — five of six sources show the predicted negative association, but `kindergarten_teacher` sits near zero with the wrong sign. So the cell-level |ρ| = 0.489 is a weighted average of strong-signal sources and one near-null source; the signal is heterogeneous across sources, not a uniform persona-distance axis. This matters for downstream extrapolation: a result obtained on a panel that includes more `kindergarten_teacher`-like sources may show a smaller effective ρ even if the cell really IS picking up persona-distance.

The honest reading is **behavior-dependent at a different cell, not at the recipe cell**: the residual stream IS carrying persona-distance signal that tracks sycophancy leakage on the #411 panel, but the signal sits in early-to-mid layer geometry across both end-of-system and last-prompt extraction points rather than in the late-layer last-prompt cloud-metric ridge that won on markers. The recipe's specific contribution — late-layer cloud-aware metrics on the last prompt token — doesn't add anything to this signal: the right column shows Gaussian-KL flat across every extraction point through the L19-L24 ridge on syco.

The cell IS selected from ~1065 scored candidates, so the |ρ| ≈ 0.49 numbers carry the same selection-inflation caveat the plan §6.3 carried forward from #502. The honest defense against that selection inflation is the **L0-L13 cluster structure** (a band of 14+ cosine cells across two extraction points all clearing perm p ≤ 0.001 in the same direction) plus the **comedian-recovery diagnostic** (the single qualitative test #470 designed to distinguish a real predictor from a noise-fit; rank 5/23 at both the L07 MMD and L02 EoS cells). One alternative explanation NOT ruled out: at early end-of-system layers (L0-L2), the residual is only minimally transformed from the embedding of the system prompt itself, so the centered cosine distance could be partly tracking how different the system-prompt STRINGS are rather than what the model has done to them. The fact that the SAME signal magnitude appears at `last_prompt × L07` (well above the embedding layer, well past where the user turn enters) helps rule this out, but a token-overlap / string-distance control on the persona system prompts would close the question.

> **Figure.** *On the fact arm, the bake-off underperforms #494's coarse predictors at the pre-registered cell, and even the search-best cell collapses to ρ ≈ 0 once #500's bystander-prior-on-the-fact is partialled out.* Bars 1-5 are #494's coarse predictors (cosine_a_L21, cosine_b_L21, js_on_topic, fact_slice_js, bystander_logprob) computed on the SAME n=18 substrate-FE residualized cells as the search-best (bar 7), pulled from the search-best cell's own `coarse_lift.per_coarse_rho_fe`. Bar 6 = the pre-registered last-prompt L22 Gaussian-KL cell (|ρ_fe| = 0.36, n = 25 cells; this bar uses a DIFFERENT n because the pre-reg cell has 25 valid substrate-FE pairs while the search-best has 18). Bar 7 = the bake-off's search-best cell on the fact arm (end-of-system × L01 cosine centered; L01-L04 are tied at this same |ρ_fe| = 0.668, since early-layer cosine is essentially a rank-preserving function of the embedding). Bar 8 = the SAME search-best cell with both substrate AND the #500 bystander-prior-on-the-fact partialled out (|ρ_double_fe| = 0.03). The bake-off's win at the search-best cell is entirely the prior; once you control for what the bystander already knew about the fact (per #500), there's nothing left to explain.

Two failures stack on this arm. At the **pre-registered cell**, the L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL bake-off cell reaches |ρ_fe| = 0.36 (smoke mode, so this is the un-attenuation-adjusted number) on the n = 25 cells where the cell has variance, but the same 25 cells fit #494's coarse `fact_slice_js` predictor at |ρ| = 0.59 — the bake-off cell's `coarse_lift.delta_rho` is **-0.252**. Across the entire fact arm, **zero of 953 scored cells** have `coarse_lift.delta_rho ≥ +0.15`; the search-best cell beats the coarse maximum on its own n=18 cells by only `delta_rho = +0.045`. The plan §6.2 trigger asked for Δρ ≥ 0.15 OVER the coarse predictor on the same pairs; what the bake-off delivers anywhere in the panel is well below that floor. The recipe's cloud-aware metric on the last prompt token is a *worse* predictor of fact transfer than #494's published baselines at its own pre-registered cell.

At the **search-best cell** (end-of-system × L1 cosine centered, with L1-L4 tied at the same value because early-layer cosine is essentially a rank-preserving function of the embedding; selected from the ~950 non-saturated scored cells), the substrate-FE residualized |ρ_fe| reaches 0.67 — a number that LOOKS like it clears the |ρ| ≥ 0.40 trigger. The collapse happens at the next step. Once you partial out both substrate AND #500's `bystander_logprob` (the bystander's own prior on the taught fact, which #500 established as the dominant predictor of fact leakage), the double-FE Spearman drops to |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03. The 0.67 was carrying #500's prior, not orthogonal-to-the-prior persona-distance signal. The plan §5 conditions table explicitly built `fact_arm/double_fe` as the test for "does the bake-off lift OVER #500's prior" — the answer at the search-best cell is no.

A handful of OTHER non-saturated cells do retain |ρ_double_fe| ≈ 0.37-0.40 after the prior partial. The 9 cells that survive at this threshold are spread across last-prompt + mean_response extraction points and across four metric families: `last_prompt × L01 gauss_kl centered` (-0.400), `last_prompt × L18 gauss_kl raw` (-0.400 — only the raw variant survives; centered = +0.094), `mean_response × L02 gauss_kl centered` (-0.400), `last_prompt × L00 mmd raw` (+0.398), `mean_response × L20 mahal raw` (+0.396), `last_prompt × L15 gauss_kl centered` (-0.394), `mean_response × L27 c2st raw` (+0.387), `last_prompt × L11 mahal raw` (-0.383), `last_prompt × L01 mahal centered` (-0.373). The spread itself is the finding: the surviving signal isn't concentrated at one metric or one extraction point — it's nine isolated cells from different parts of the predictor zoo. With 953 scored fact cells the multiple-comparisons floor is permissive, and unlike the syco signal these cells DON'T form a tight cluster and don't have a recovery-style sanity check, so they lack the structural defenses the syco signal has against selection inflation. Applying the same selection-inflation framework the syco arm uses, the verdict on these cells is "9 cells survive the prior partial at |ρ| ≈ 0.4 from a 953-cell search, but the same multiple-comparisons math that disciplines the syco arm disciplines them too — the structural evidence is not there." The headline verdict on this arm holds in the qualitative direction (bake-off does not robustly beat the prior on fact) while explicitly NOT claiming the bake-off cleanly fails everywhere.

The smoke-mode caveat: Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would attenuation-adjust the bake-off cells UPWARD (since reliability_y ≤ 1.0), but under the working assumption from #494 that `reliability_y` is roughly uniform across the cells we're comparing, it would attenuation-adjust the coarse `fact_slice_js` and `bystander_logprob` predictors UPWARD by the same factor, so the relative ORDERING (bake-off cells underperform coarse, and search-best collapses under prior-control) is invariant. The ORDERING claim is robust under shared-reliability attenuation; the absolute |ρ| ≥ 0.40 THRESHOLD claim is NOT — the pre-reg cell's 0.36 could cross 0.40 after Path B, and the search-best's 0.67 could rise to a level where the post-collapse 0.03 needs a fresh interpretation. The fact arm also lacks the prior-only residualization diagnostic (substrate + prior is the double-FE; prior-only without substrate is not stored separately); the bake-off-vs-prior comparison rests on the joint double-FE, not on a pure prior-only conditioning, so a small piece of the collapse could be over-residualization that a prior-only test would catch. A production rerun would tighten the absolute ρ values, would let me put a cluster-bootstrap CI on the |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03 to verify that it really is indistinguishable from zero, and would let me run the prior-only diagnostic.

The honest reading is **the fact arm points against transfer, but remains smoke-limited**: the recipe doesn't beat #494's published coarse predictors at its own cell, the search-best cell's lift collapses under #500's prior control, and the handful of cells that survive the prior partial lack the cluster + recovery structure. Both #494's pooled-predictor null and #500's "the prior is the dominant predictor" verdict survive the full-resolution bake-off at the qualitative level, but the absolute-threshold reading needs Path B to close.

A small replication note on the apparent tension with #494: #494 reported a pooled-predictor null on fact, while this run shows same-pair `fact_slice_js` reaching |ρ_fe| ≈ 0.59 on the substrate-FE residualized 25-pair slice. This is NOT a contradiction — #494's null is on the pooled estimate across heterogeneous training substrates, while this run reports the same-cell `fact_slice_js` AFTER substrate-FE residualization on the same restricted set of pairs the bake-off cell sees. The two numbers answer different questions; the within-substrate same-pair correlation is what the bake-off cell needs to beat, and it does not.
