## 5. Success / Kill criteria (pre-registered; precedence-ordered)

**No gates — short run** (nominal wall ≈ 2–2.5 h; worst case ≈ 4.5 h, and the worst case IS a registered outcome). All 3 seeds launch in parallel; no smoke gate is needed because the recipe, band, dev check, Phase-2 port, and eval path are all run-validated by the parent's 12 cells — the only new uncertainty (steps-to-band at 1%) is resolved by the cells themselves under the cap criterion. The parent's smoke-cell gate (v4 §7) is NOT re-run.

**Manipulation checks (per seed, inherited from v4 §7):** band + argmax stop reached within 16 epochs; dev check ≥ 48/50 with one +0.10-nat retry; full-eval pre-SFT trigger emission ≥ 95% (190/200); pre-SFT bystander emission ≤ 5% per cell. Parent-precedent handling (the 5%-arm record): a seed that reaches the band but sits at dev < 48/50 after retry is included with `match_failure=True` and disclosed, PROVIDED full-eval pre-SFT trigger emission ≥ 95%; a seed below the 95% floor is a failed install for the kill tally (its install never reached behavioral comparability).

In precedence order:

1. **Kill — "usable range ends above 1%":** ≥ 2 of 3 seeds fail to install (cap hit without the band predicate, or post-retry full-eval pre-SFT emission < 95%). Report and stop; the finding bounds the lever's usable range from below. (1 of 3 failing → comparison proceeds on the surviving seeds with the revised denominator named everywhere, inherited rule.)
2. **Lever rescued (headline-affecting):** r01 post-SFT trigger emission > 2% pooled across included seeds (i.e., > 12/600 at full inclusion) AND key-conditioned — post-SFT no-key and reference cells ≤ 2% each (pooled). This overturns the parent's headline per its own update criterion; the parent clean-result gets the promised update.
3. **Latent-only partial:** all included seeds ≤ 2% emission, but r01's seed-mean log-prob retention exceeds the parent's best arm mean (9.16 nat) by ≥ 3 nat with r01's worst seed above every parent arm's best seed (margins inherited from v4's H1-latent). Reported as latent retention, explicitly not behavioral survival.
4. **Lever closed across its feasible range:** installs match (band + dev check path), post-SFT emission ≤ 2% in all included seeds, and retention does not meet tier 3 — i.e., inside (or below) the parent's seed-noise envelope of 8.3–9.2 nat arm means. This completes the falsification at the parent's own update boundary; below-envelope readings are reported descriptively (more latent erasure, same conclusion).

Joint-satisfiability self-check: tiers 1–4 are mutually exclusive and exhaustive over the registered outcome space (install-failure tally → emission margin → latent margin → closed), applied to the same measurements at the same cells; no two criteria demand contradictory signs. Outcomes failing every tier's margins (e.g., 2.5% emission with leaky no-key cell) are reported as inconclusive graded evidence with all per-seed values shown — inherited v4 language.
