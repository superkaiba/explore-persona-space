## 1. Goal + hypothesis

### Goal

Re-run the five-factor recipe-selectivity screen from parent #383 with two coupled changes:

1. **Marker switch:** `[ZLT]` (3-BPE-piece on Qwen-2.5) → `※` (single BPE piece). With marker-only-loss + `tail_tokens=0`, this drops the loss extent from ~4 tokens (3 marker pieces + EOS) to ~2 tokens (1 marker piece + EOS) — see Assumption A2. The loss-mask main effect is now a sharper test.
2. **Loss-mask factor E binary → ordinal K=3:** {E0=marker+EOT (~2 tok), E1=tail-32 (~32 tok), E2=whole-completion (~600 tok)}. Isolates *loss extent* from *loss location*; the binary E in #383 conflated both.

Bolt on log-prob eval (`compute_marker_logprob`, #401 primitive) at intermediate checkpoints to (a) measure factor effects continuously even when source rate sits at zero or saturates at one, and (b) give the queued predictor follow-up cleaner per-cell signal than the 5-pp-floored substring rate.

### Hypothesis (matched to falsification thresholds)

**H1 — Replication of #383's lockstep dissolution.** For factors A (long-system), B (long-answer), C (persona-framing), D (Claude-data), the per-factor selectivity Δ on substring-match rate falls within #383's reported 95% CI envelope: A ∈ [+21.3, +45.7], B ∈ [+15.7, +39.0], C ∈ [−39.2, −14.7] (note: #383 sign was negative because the contrast was *neutral* vs persona-framed; persona-framing is the positive direction), D ∈ [−1.3, +23.5]. *Falsified if* any factor's selectivity Δ flips sign vs the #383 widest-of-three CI envelope, or if its new CI excludes the #383 point estimate.

**H1 is contingent on the §5.1.0 recipe-fix port landing intact** — without the port, the experiment trains on the original-#365 (pre-fix) recipe, source rate sits at the original 0%/floor regime, and H1 is structurally unmeasurable. See §5.1.0.

**H2 — Ordinal E shows monotonic loss-extent trend.** Per-factor selectivity Δ as a function of E ∈ {marker+EOT, tail-32, whole-completion} is monotonically non-decreasing on at least the substring-match rate metric (Spearman ρ across pooled-across-cells E-level means ≥ +0.5, one-tailed p<0.05). *Falsified if* the ranking inverts (E2 < E1 < E0) or if it's non-monotonic with the middle level outside the [E0, E2] range by >5 pp.

**H3 — Log-prob signal mirrors substring rate qualitatively at endpoint, with smaller variance.** Per-cell log p(`※`) at the final checkpoint correlates Spearman ρ ≥ +0.7 with substring source rate across all 108 cells. *Falsified if* ρ < +0.5 or if any factor's log-prob effect direction disagrees with its substring effect direction at p<0.05.

**H4 — Whole-completion-loss remains the largest selectivity decoupler at sharper contrast.** With marker-only loss now covering ~2 instead of ~4 tokens, the gap whole-completion vs E0 should not shrink: E2−E0 selectivity Δ remains within ±10 pp of #383's +41.7 pp. *Falsified if* the gap shrinks by >15 pp or flips sign — would indicate the #383 E effect was partly driven by `[ZLT]`-specific multi-piece dynamics rather than loss extent.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Cells

108 valid (cell, source) pairs total. Per source (librarian / programmer / surgeon):

- 16 binary combos of (A, B, C, D), minus the 4 dropped corners where A=0×C=1 (the C-axis preflight fails Jaccard for these — same as #383). **Valid (A,B,C,D) combos per source: 12.**
- 3 E levels per valid combo: E0, E1, E2.
- Per source: 12 × 3 = **36 cells**.
- **Total valid cells across 3 sources: 108** (not the 144 headline in the clarifier; the A=0×C=1 preflight cuts 36 cells of the nominal 144). Flagged for explicit user awareness in §13.

### 4.2 Per-cell training (matches #383's POST-recipe-fix state)

For each (source, A, B, C, D, E):

1. Load shared on-policy (D=0) or off-policy (D=1) pool from `data/issue_397/pools/<source>/source-<source>_a{a}_b{b}_c{c}.jsonl` (these are the SAME pools as #383 — see Reusable artifacts in §5; the marker is appended at training-row construction, not at pool generation).
2. Build training dataset via `factor_screen_397.data_prep.prepare_cell(...)` which calls `_append_marker(answer, marker_text="※")`. Positive rows end in `…answer\n\n※<eos>`. Negative rows end in `…answer<eos>` (no marker). **400 positives + 400 negatives = 800 rows total per cell** (post-recipe-fix; corrected from v1's 200+400). The `user_suffix` (B-axis length-instruction string) is **stripped** from the training row's `user_text` — only the bare question goes in. The suffix is still used at pool-gen time to instruct the generator to produce long-vs-short completions.
3. Call `train_one_cell(...)` with TrainLoraConfig:
   - `marker_only_loss = True` for E ∈ {0, 1}; `False` for E = 2 (whole-completion).
   - `marker_text = "※"` (new).
   - `marker_tail_tokens = 0` for E=0, `32` for E=1. Irrelevant when `marker_only_loss=False`.
   - All other hparams = #383 defaults: LoRA r=32 / α=64 / dropout=0.05 / rsLoRA, AdamW lr=1e-5, cosine schedule, warmup_ratio=0.05, 3 epochs, per-device batch 4, grad_accum 4, max_length 2048.
   - `save_strategy = "steps"`, `save_steps = 25` (see §4.4). DEVIATION from #383's `save_strategy="no"`. Needed for multi-checkpoint log-prob.
4. After training: keep the adapter dir `adapter/` for HF Hub upload AND for in-process adapter-load eval. Do NOT merge intermediate checkpoints. Final-checkpoint merge to `merged/` is needed only for vLLM sampled-completion eval (vLLM doesn't load PEFT adapters efficiently for batched sampling). See §5.6 + §11 for cleanup.

### 4.3 Per-cell eval

**At every saved checkpoint (intermediate + final) — log-prob only:**

- Load: `PeftModel.from_pretrained(base_model, adapter_dir / f"checkpoint-{step}")` (adapter-load; no merge). Keep base model resident across the 6 intermediate evals for a single cell to amortize the ~30-sec base-model load.
- Build all 480 persona contexts: for each `persona ∈ EVAL_PERSONAS_24` × `question ∈ EVAL_QUESTIONS_20`, render the chat template up to the assistant turn opener via `tokenizer.apply_chat_template([{role:system, ...}, {role:user, content:q+b_suffix(cell.b)}], add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False)`. Note: `b_suffix` IS applied to the eval-time user question (matches #383's eval; only the training-row `user_text` strips it per recipe-fix).
- Call `compute_marker_logprob(model, tokenizer, contexts=<all 480 contexts>, marker_text="\n\n※", position="end_of_answer", batch_size=8, device="cuda:0")` → list of 480 log-probs. Also run with `marker_text="※"` (per A15 — cheap; both serve the analysis).
- Aggregate to: mean log p(`※`) per persona at this checkpoint; per-(source vs bystander) selectivity Δ in log-prob units; per-cell summary.
- Unload PEFT adapter via `model.unload()` (or re-load base) before attaching the next checkpoint's adapter. Keep base model resident.

**At final checkpoint ONLY (substring parity with #383):**

- Merge final adapter to `merged/` (one merge per cell, not per checkpoint).
- Run `generate_completions(...)` from `factor_screen_397.eval_panel` with vLLM batched generation: 5 completions × 20 questions × 24 personas = 2400 generations per cell. `max_new_tokens=2048` (per CLAUDE.md). Score via `score_markers(...)` (case-insensitive substring `※`).
- Run `generate_random_control_completions(...)` for the 24 neutral background prompts (24 × 20 × 5 = 2400 generations).

### 4.4 Intermediate-checkpoint cadence

Per the corrected recipe-fix hparams: **400 positives + 400 negatives = 800 examples per cell**. Effective batch 16 (4 × 4 grad_accum). Steps per epoch ≈ 50; total steps over 3 epochs ≈ **150**.

**Cadence:** Save every 25 steps → checkpoints at steps {25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150} = **5 intermediate + 1 final = 6 checkpoints per cell** (or 6 intermediate snapshots, with step 150 being the final saved checkpoint). Reasoning:

- 6 evenly-spaced snapshots over a 150-step run gives a 6-point trajectory per cell — sufficient for monotonic-fit on the log-prob curve while leaving #398 to own dense ramp-detection at single-source resolution.
- Every-50 cadence would give only 3 intermediates (steps 50/100/150), borderline-too-sparse for the trajectory-fit deliverable.
- Every-25 cost (per §11 + §5.7 smoke test): if log-prob throughput is the high-end estimate (~7 min/cell/checkpoint), 6 × 108 = 648 cell-checkpoint evals × 7 min ≈ 76 GPU-h of log-prob eval alone. The §5.7 smoke gate makes this contingent on actual measurement.

Add a new top-level function alongside `generate_completions(...)`. Uses adapter-load (NOT a merged checkpoint path) — saves ~14 GB × 5 intermediate-merge per cell × 108 cells of unnecessary disk churn.

```python
def compute_logprob_panel(
    *,
    base_model_path: str,           # e.g. "Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct"
    adapter_path: str,              # path to checkpoint-NNN adapter dir
    base_model=None,                # optional: pre-loaded base model to reuse
    tokenizer=None,                 # optional: pre-loaded tokenizer to reuse
    marker_text: str,               # e.g. "\n\n※"
    panel: dict[str, str] = EVAL_PERSONAS_24,
    questions: list[str] = EVAL_QUESTIONS_20,
    b_suffix_value: str,            # cell.b-derived B-suffix (applied to user q at eval time)
    system_prompt_overrides: dict[str, str] | None = None,  # train-matched eval for C=1
    batch_size: int = 8,
    device: str = "cuda:0",
) -> dict[str, list[float]]:
    """Per-persona × per-question log p(marker) at end_of_answer position.

    Loads adapter on top of base_model via PeftModel.from_pretrained. If
    base_model is passed in, reuses it (caller keeps it resident across
    checkpoints for a single cell to amortize the ~30-sec base-model load).
    Returns {persona: [logp_q1, logp_q2, ..., logp_q20]} for all 24 personas.

    Uses compute_marker_logprob from src/explore_persona_space/eval/marker_logprob.py.
    """
    from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer
    from peft import PeftModel
    from explore_persona_space.eval.marker_logprob import compute_marker_logprob

    if tokenizer is None:
        tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(base_model_path, trust_remote_code=True)
    if base_model is None:
        base_model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
            base_model_path, torch_dtype="bfloat16",
            device_map=device, trust_remote_code=True,
        )
    base_model.eval()
    model = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base_model, adapter_path)
    model.eval()

    panel_effective = dict(panel)
    if system_prompt_overrides:
        panel_effective.update(system_prompt_overrides)

    out: dict[str, list[float]] = {}
    for persona, system_prompt in panel_effective.items():
        contexts = []
        for q in questions:
            full_q = f"{q} {b_suffix_value}".strip()
            ctx = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
                [{"role": "system", "content": system_prompt},
                 {"role": "user", "content": full_q}],
                add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False,
            )
            contexts.append(ctx)
        out[persona] = compute_marker_logprob(
            model=model, tokenizer=tokenizer, contexts=contexts,
            marker_text=marker_text, position="end_of_answer",
            batch_size=batch_size, device=device,
        )

    # Unload the PEFT adapter so the caller can attach the next checkpoint's
    # adapter onto the still-resident base_model.
    model.unload()
    return out
```

### 5.9 `aggregator.py` ordinal-E trend test (renamed from v1 §5.7; analysis side of the smoke-test-driven cadence)

- Existing matched-pair effects for A, B, C, D: unchanged (binary, marginalize across E levels and use the widest-of-three CI construction).
- New: per-E-level main effect on each metric. For each E ∈ {0, 1, 2}, compute (across cells holding A,B,C,D fixed within source) mean source rate, mean leakage rate, mean selectivity Δ. Aggregate across the 12 valid (A,B,C,D) cells × 3 sources = 36 measurements per E level.
- New: **monotonic-trend test on E.** For each metric M ∈ {source rate, leakage rate, selectivity Δ, log p(`※`) at source persona, log p(`※`) at bystanders}:
  - For each of 36 matched (source, A, B, C, D) tuples, compute (M_E2 − M_E0) and (M_E1 − M_E0). Stratified by source.
  - **Spearman ρ across the 36 tuples between rank(E) and rank(M)**, with one-tailed test (alternative=greater for the H2-directional prediction).
  - Bootstrap a 95% CI on Spearman ρ (1000 resamples), source-clustered (matching #383 convention).
  - Also report Page's L for the within-tuple ranking — more powerful for K=3 ordered alternative.

## 12. Target pod intent

**`ft-7b` (4× H100, ZeRO-3 capable).** Justification:

- **Parallelism axis:** 108 cells × 1 GPU each → embarrassingly parallel. The dispatcher already shards by `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES` round-robin across N GPUs (the #383 dispatcher pattern). 4× H100 in one pod = 4 concurrent cells, ~30 min/cell each → ~108/4 × 30 min ≈ 13.5 hours of pure training wall. Plus eval (intermediate + final) gives ~24-30 hours wall on 4× H100 in the smoke-PASS regime, well within a single ephemeral pod's 7-day TTL.
- **Why not `lora-7b` (1× H100):** sequential = ~108 × 30 min = ~54 hours wall for training alone, ~80 hours total wall. Same dollar cost as 4× H100, 3× the wall-clock. CLAUDE.md parallelism rule says pick the larger pod.
- **Why not `ft-70b` (8× H200):** overkill for a 7B LoRA workload; HBM headroom is wasted; #383 hit RunPod SUPPLY_CONSTRAINT on H100 and pivoted to H200, so this MAY need to fall back to `--gpu-type H200 --gpu-count 4` if H100 is unavailable. Acceptable degradation; preflight on provision.
- **Why not multi-pod sweep:** 108 cells is small enough that a single 4-GPU pod finishes in ~24-30h; sharding across 2 pods (54 cells each) saves only ~12h wall at 2× the setup/orchestration cost. The CLAUDE.md "one multi-GPU pod" preference applies.

If H100 4× supply is constrained, fall back order: `--gpu-type H200 --gpu-count 4` → `--gpu-type H200 --gpu-count 8` (over-provisioned but acceptable).
