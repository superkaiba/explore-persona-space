### 3a. `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md` — four clause insertions

**Clause 1 — multiplier derivation (anchor: § "Per-cell fit phases", after the
"Projected wall = `n_calls × measured_per_call / parallelism`" sentence).**
~4-6 lines: the MULTIPLIER product (`n_calls` = draws × cells × folds ×
evals-per-unit × …) MUST be DERIVED FROM THE CODE — count the inner-loop
iterations at the named production entrypoint (read the loop bounds off the
fit/driver script, or log a counted 1-unit run) — never assumed arithmetic: a
correctly MEASURED per-call pilot times a WRONG multiplier still under-projects
by the missing factor (#1689: the pilot-based projection under-ran the realized
wall because a per-pair evals multiplier was assumed rather than read off the
fit code; the code-review round later projected the same phase's bootstrap
battery at ~140 CPU-h — `bootstrap-wall-projection-over-plan` CONCERN on
#1689's events).

**Clause 2 — draw-count necessity (same block, immediately after clause 1 or
after the battery sub-clause "(the per-'call' unit is then one production-shape
batched draw block…)").** ~5-7 lines: a bootstrap / permutation / null-draw
battery projected to DOMINATE its phase's wall (≳half the phase cost) MUST
state draw-count NECESSITY in the row: what the draws buy (e.g. the CI-width /
significance target), why N draws are needed rather than an order less, and a
pre-registered DESCOPE lever (reduce N to a stated floor / drop the band) the
mid-run deviation path may pull without a re-plan. (#1689: an N=1000
selection-symmetric bootstrap projected at ~140 CPU-h dominated the fit-ladder
phase; only a user order — "drop bootstrap to 0" — descoped it, ETA ~38h →
~5-6h per the session record.)

**Clause 3 — largest-cell RAM keying (anchor: § "CPU-phase RAM/RSS routing",
after the "a measured one-chunk `ru_maxrss` at production shape" sentence).**
~5-7 lines: ANY RAM/RSS projection used for routing or machine sizing — the
VM-placement gate here AND a GPU-lane host-RAM sizing (`--min-ram-gb` /
machine-RAM choice) — keys on the LARGEST planned cell/lane/table (max over
lanes of the working-set), never an anchor or first-listed unit; heterogeneous
lanes state per-lane peaks or size to the max. (#1739: host RAM sized on an
anchor behavior's table; a sibling lane's larger working set kernel-OOM'd
python at anon-rss 163 GiB on a 170 GB machine, twice — the second lane hit
the same kill point hours later; the 340 GB relaunch held.)

**Clause 4a — recorded basis update (anchor: § "Per-cell fit phases",
extending the existing `pilot-gated` sentence "…and marks the row's basis
`pilot-gated`.").** ~5-6 lines: BASIS CURRENCY — whenever a pilot or realized
in-run figure deviates ≥2× (the `compute_deviation_over_2x` boundary) from a
§9 row's approved basis, the owning session posts a RECORDED basis update in
the same turn (an `epm:compute-deviation` re-post or `epm:progress` note
re-stating the row's basis, the re-projected wall, and any downstream
fence/cap re-derived from it); an approved plan's basis is never left standing
known-stale (#1738: a materially stale capture-cost basis stood in the
approved plan post-pilot with no recorded update).

**Clause 5 — pool-scale pilots (same block, extending the existing "SHAPE
includes the phase's realized EXECUTION GEOMETRY" sentence).** ~4-6 lines:
SHAPE also includes SCALE for kernels whose cost scales SUPERLINEARLY in pool
size (pairwise similarity / near-dupe screens ~ pool²): the pilot runs at
production pool scale, OR the row states the scaling exponent and extrapolates
the pilot by it — a below-scale pilot on a quadratic battery under-projects by
the square of the pool ratio (#1738: the near-dupe screen's pilot ran below
the production pool; the realized screen ran multi-hour serial at ~100% CPU).
