# The DPO→RLVR step of the Llama Tülu ladder barely moves the linear context→answer map: at most a rotation on seven of eight corpora, and the smallest pooled R² step on the ladder (MODERATE confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_1336.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/3ed54c4e6a83db51b7cfca43ea7f059ce5e2e6d2/docs/methodology/issue_1336.md) · [gist mirror](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/26c775f772e4b6600b1cb050800e2931)

## Takeaways

- **SFT installs the linear context→answer map; RLVR barely moves it.** Pooled on-policy held-out R² at layer 30 rises .413 → .578 → .598 → .602 → .619 across base/SFT/DPO/RLVR/longer-RLVR — the DPO→RLVR increment (+.004) is the smallest step on the ladder against +.165 at base→SFT — and per corpus (round 3) DPO→RLVR closes at tier 0–5 on 7/8 corpora (6/7 counting the two Tülu-derived corpora as one); the only corpus no tier closes (IF-constraints) and the only one needing the rotation (MATH) are exactly the two RLVR trained on.
- **The base map never reconstructs a post-trained map on the models' own text** (tier-8 gaps +0.14 to +0.26 per corpus in round 3; +0.12 to +0.28 pooled, IF-constraints outlier +0.45) — but on fixed target text the pooled gaps shrink to +0.03–0.14 (IF-constraints base→longer outlier +0.22), so much of the on-policy irrecoverability rides on the text distribution each checkpoint writes, not only on map coordinates.
- **No cluster-level teaching signal survives:** at every adjacent transition the most-improved prompt cluster on own text is indistinguishable from the selection-matched permutation null given the variance (p = .14/.08/.39/.17; null bands sit well below achievable ceilings, so the test is informative), and the off-policy arm's rejections never agree with the on-policy arm on which clusters moved.
- **Longer-RLVR (Tülu-3.1) accumulates more coordinate change than the single RLVR step, not less** — DPO→longer-RLVR moves 4/8 corpora to a higher sufficient tier in round 3, and every pooled post-training transition into longer-RLVR leaves IF-constraints tier-8 gaps of +.20 to +.26. The map moves with RLVR dose.
- **The fitted map wins held-out R² everywhere (identity+bias baseline −1.2 to −3.5) yet identity+bias wins retrieval at rank 1 in nine of ten pooled cells** (.604/.615 vs ridge .564/.602 on the RLVR on-policy cell) — the documented R²-vs-retrieval dissociation, so every map-strength claim here is R²-specific and does not assert the fitted map is the better retrieval reader.
- **Validity bounds.** The pooled round's fold-reproducibility gate formally failed its band (Δ_assign +0.077 vs the 0.052 tolerance) and the run proceeded on an instrument-conservatism adjudication, so pooled values inherit ~.05–.07 of conservatism and are never numerically compared to round-3 per-corpus fits; one released ladder, single fold/corpus seeds, within-run bootstrap CIs only; the triggered leakage-excluded sensitivity read is unrun.

## Goal

- **This experiment in context:** [#825](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/825) found on Qwen2.5-7B that post-training does not create the linear context→answer map: the pretrained model carries it at 87% of instruct strength, and the instruct map is reconstructible from the base map by a general linear change of coordinates to within 0.0003 R² (map construct from [#779](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/779)). Qwen2.5 predates RLVR, so #825 tested only SFT/DPO-era post-training. Round 1 extended the read to the only released RLVR-bearing ladder (Llama-3.1-8B Tülu-3) at 5,000 prompts. Round 3 scales the corpus scope 3.8× on GSM8K, 3× on LMSYS, and adds four stage-matched corpora (MATH, IF-constraints, UltraFeedback, Tülu-3 SFT-mix), and replaces the single reparameterization gap with a 9-tier metric ladder (direct transfer → offsets → scaling → rotation → linear reparameterization of contexts / answers / both) — so "elicitation" resolves into which correction class suffices per stage-pair × corpus. Round 4 (`pooled-multidataset-onoff-policy-stage-transfer`) pools the seven chat-render corpora into one group-aware split and adds an off-policy training-text arm — testing whether the stage ordering survives a single pooled fit, and whether each checkpoint's map is tied to the text it writes itself. Round 3 diverges from [#825](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/825) in that the base map here does NOT reconstruct any post-trained map at t ≤ 8 (Δ_tier8 +0.14 to +0.26); a cross-model divergence (Llama-3.1 vs Qwen-2.5), not a contradiction of the elicitation reading, and a second RLVR checkpoint tests dose.
- **Broader narrative:** This serves the contextual-vs-base identity question (`docs/open_questions.md`, identity-contextual-vs-base anchor): does post-training teach new context→answer structure, or re-expose structure the pretrained model already has? RLVR on verifiable rewards is the strongest published candidate for genuine teaching, so a tight null across the DPO→RLVR pair — plus the tier-ladder resolution that SFT→DPO needs a strictly higher tier than DPO→RLVR on 5/8 corpora, and that the two DPO→RLVR non-null corpora are exactly the RLVR training-mix distributions — is the most informative single read for the elicitation-not-teaching account. Two caveats bound it. First, DPO→longer-RLVR shows the map DOES move with further RLVR training on 4/8 corpora, so "RLVR doesn't change the map" is really "the single Tülu-3 RLVR step doesn't move the map beyond a rotation on the corpora it wasn't trained on". Second, a single ladder cannot separate "RLVR doesn't teach new coordinates" from "RLVR training on this ladder used a smaller effective weight update than the SFT step" — plausible because RLVR literature suggests low-rank effective updates; separating them would need a second ladder or a paired weight-space read.

## Methodology

**Design:** Five released checkpoints of one ladder — `meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B` (base), `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B-SFT`, `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B-DPO`, `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3-8B` (post-RLVR), `allenai/Llama-3.1-Tulu-3.1-8B` (longer-RLVR) — crossed in round 3 with eight prompt corpora and one render format per corpus, giving 40 generation cells and 56 stage-pair × corpus × layer-30 metric-ladder pair files (7 stage-pairs × 8 corpora). The post-training stage is the single manipulated variable; corpora, renders, sampling, and the fit recipe are held fixed. Round 3 corpora (revision-pinned): `chat` render on GSM8K test (1,319), GSM8K train full (7,473), MATH (7,500 from the RLVR mix), IF-constraints (11,000 sampled from the RLVR mix's ifeval component), UltraFeedback prompts (11,000 from the Tülu-3 8B preference mixture), Tülu-3 SFT-mix (11,000, stratified 3,667/3,667/3,666 across wildchat/flan/evol-codealpaca; corpus slug `sft11k`), and LMSYS-Chat-1M (23,000 sampled to yield ~15,000 kept); `naturalistic` render on LMSYS only. Rounds 1–2 (5,000 LMSYS × 2 renders + 5,000 GSM8K train + 1,319 GSM8K test) are superseded by the fatter round-3 corpora except the GSM8K test cell, which is retained as the decontaminated companion under the `n<d` caveat below. Per the standing both-mappings rule, the context-based mapping ran on every corpus × format; the prefix-based mapping ran on the naturalistic LMSYS render only — the chat-template prefix slot is row-constant by construction (measured max pairwise cosine distance up to 1.5e-4), so its map is degenerate at every stage. The naturalistic prefix mapping ran and reads R² 0.001–0.005 across all five stages (base 0.005; SFT/DPO/RLVR/longer-RLVR all 0.001) — floor-limited, invariant across stages, carried as an uncovered signal cell (a scope caveat, not a null).

Round 4 (follow-up `pooled-multidataset-onoff-policy-stage-transfer`) re-fits the same five checkpoints on one pooled corpus: the seven chat-render corpora pooled into a single group-aware 80/20 split (the naturalistic LMSYS render is excluded — the same prompts rendered twice must not be pooled). Two training-text arms per checkpoint: on-policy (the checkpoint's own sampled answers — the round-3 generations reused verbatim) and off-policy (the answers the four other checkpoints wrote to the same prompts, teacher-force-captured through the fitting checkpoint and concatenated; 20 new off-diagonal capture cells). This gives 10 pooled cells (5 checkpoints × 2 arms), plus matched-n companions at 15,000 train rows to separate sample-size effects from stage effects, a pooled transfer battery (own fit / direct transfer / context-side / both-sides reparameterization per transition × corpus), and a per-cluster battery over the split groups. The plan's generic preference-tuning slot is realized as DPO — no open checkpoint family ships base/SFT/PPO-style-preference-RL/RLVR — an availability constraint carried as a scope caveat.

**Training:** **N/A — no model training.** Round 3 spent 194.37 GPU-h against a budgeted 98 GPU-h (2.0× over budget; deviation from plan §11, recorded here; the fit + metric-ladder phase's per-cell wall was under-estimated at plan time — the pilot batteries measured 541s/corpus vs the planned 1.4 h/corpus, but the mass of stage-corpus cells at 15–17k rows in generation + capture drove the wall). Round 4's run digest records 2,126.15 GPU-h realized against 210 budgeted (10.1× over; recorded here as a plan deviation) — the figure aggregates every round-4 attempt on the fellows H200 lane, including an off-policy capture job that ran far over its planned per-cell basis before a resume-check bug halted it, and two fit jobs lost to memory failures and relaunched.

**Evaluation:** Per (stage-pair p, corpus s, layer L=30) the reparameterization ladder produces held-out R² of the source map applied to target contexts at every tier t ∈ {0..8}, on the same rows and folds as the target's within-stage map. Tiers, per `docs/mapping_similarity_metrics.md`: t0 direct transfer (ŷ = W_s x + b_s applied unchanged), t1 context offset (ŷ = W_s (x − Δx) + b_s, Δx from cloud means only), t2 answer offset (Δy on the output side), t3 bias offset (refit b* only), t4 global scaling (α W_s x + b*), t5 mapping rotation (R W_s x + b*, orthogonal Procrustes on directions), t6 linear reparameterization of contexts, t7 linear reparameterization of answers, t8 both (A ∘ W_s ∘ B). All offsets fit on clouds alone (never on paired context→answer rows). Δ_t = within-stage R² − tier-t R². The sufficient-tier statistic is min{t ∈ 0..8 : Δ_t ≤ 0.021 · ex_v2 = 0.0207} at the paired prompt-level bootstrap point estimate; "none ≤ 8" is a reportable value. Primary scale is raw pooled R² (round 3's fatter fold-train regime makes the recalibration rescue used in rounds 1–2 unnecessary — G0v2/G1v2 both PASS at raw); the held-out cross-fitted per-dim affine recalibration companion is always computed. The recalibration companion adds 4,096 per-dim scalar shift+scale parameters cross-fitted from folds, so a genuine map-mismatch signal can be partially absorbed by it; recal disagrees with raw on 3/8 corpora for DPO→RLVR (GSM8K test raw t2 → recal t0; GSM8K train raw t3 → recal t0; IF-constraints raw "none" → recal t8), so raw is the binding read and recal is reported as the companion that a per-dim rescaling accommodates. The stage-of-record contrast C_v2 = Δ_tier8(dpo→rlvr, gsm8k_train_full, chat) on the RLVR training distribution: point −0.0031 with a bootstrap 95% CI running from −0.0039 to −0.0024 on the raw scale (a signed negative CI — reparameterized-DPO reconstructs RLVR marginally better than the within-RLVR fit on that corpus; magnitude 5× smaller than the 0.021 elicit band, sign is direction-of-effect, not "matches to zero"). Decision constants (round-3 revisions with ex_v2 = S_qwen_v2 / 0.6731 = 1.0303, S_qwen_v2 = 0.6935): elicit_band_v2 = 0.0207; practical_scale_v2 = 0.0518; health_gate_v2 = 0.0515. G0v2 (Qwen anchor + Gram-vs-primal parity) PASS at abs_dev 5.9e-6 and 2.2e-16 primal-vs-Gram agreement. G1v2 (RLVR × LMSYS23k chat vs 0.20606 bar) PASS at raw R² 0.6201 / recal 0.6200. Capture-parity gate (100 rows × 32 layers) PASS at min mean cosine 1.0000005 vs 0.999 threshold. All three gates were set in plan v13; no descope fired. No LLM judge is involved: the DV is an activation-geometry read.

Round-4 evaluation (pooled split): per checkpoint × training-text arm, one pooled ridge map is fit across the seven chat-render corpora on a group-aware 80/20 split — 173 groups (per-corpus MPNet sentence-embedding sub-clusters, merged across corpora only by near-duplicate union-find), whole groups assigned to one side, per-corpus test share 0.21–0.26 against a 0.15 floor. Headline numbers are held-out R² on the 20% test partition at the frozen layer 30; a train-side 5-fold CV companion with bootstrap CIs is tabled below — the two conventions differ by up to .024 across the ten pooled cells, and the largest divergence falls on the headline cell (longer-RLVR on-policy .619 test-partition vs .596 CV; base .413 vs .394) — and are never mixed in one comparison. λ is selected by inner-group CV on the 23-point grid spanning 1e-3 to 1e8 with zero edge extensions; realized selections at layer 30: 3,162 on-policy (10,000 for the base cell), 10,000 off-policy. n_train/d = 37,491/4,096 on-policy and 149,964/4,096 off-policy (both n > d; the matched-n companions at 15,000 are also > d); n_test = 10,612 and 42,448. The per-cluster battery computes delta-Q — the mean, over a cluster's held-out prompts, of the per-prompt change in R² from the source to the target checkpoint under the pooled context-side-reparameterization read — with a max-cluster statistic tested against a within-corpus block-permutation null (1,000 draws, the max-cluster selection applied inside each draw), the null band and the achievable per-cluster ceiling both drawn in the figure. The stage ordering is stated at layer 30 throughout; the max-over-layers ordering differs (the SFT cell peaks at .611 vs RLVR's .590), so the pooled stage story is layer-convention-dependent and layer 30 is the frozen headline convention.

**Round-4 health gates — the fold-reproducibility gate failed and the run proceeded on an adjudication.** G0v3 (fold-membership reproducibility: fits on identical pooled LMSYS train rows, the group-aware fold assignment vs three size-matched seeded random assignments, each fit read at its own argmax layer over the frozen set) returned Δ_assign = +0.0766 against the 0.0515 tolerance — verdict `FAIL-leakage-exceeds-band`, a formally failed gate. The run halted, ran the plan-named discrimination at the halt, and proceeded on an autonomous instrument-conservatism adjudication (persisted in `gates_v3/g0v3_adjudication.json`; task marker v276): grouped R² .5383 vs matched-random mean .6149 (across-draw SD .0002); the discrimination attributed 0.05–0.07 of the excess to family-holdout conservatism, bounded any leakage-removal component near .01 (ceiling .032), and found no defect signature — all four fits argmax at layer 30, fold sizes matched the manifest profile exactly (seed 13360), and the retained report-only level diagnostic (grouped .5383 vs the round-3-style reference .6090, difference .071) is construction-confounded. The halt is narrated as a grouping-effect excess (family holdout legitimately hardens generalization), never as established leakage. Two consequences bind every pooled number in this body: the gate certificate is corpus-local — it ran on the LMSYS slice (10 test-side groups), not on MATH (5 test-side groups) or IF-constraints (4), where the two confirmatory per-corpus gaps live; and the pooled headline inherits roughly 0.05–0.07 of instrument conservatism relative to round-3-style row-level folds, so pooled and round-3 R² values are not directly comparable and are never quoted side by side without this protocol delta. The other two gates passed: the pooled rig-health kill gate at raw best .590 against the .206 bar, and the off-policy capture-parity gate (100-row recapture of one off-diagonal cell) at min mean cosine .9999991 against .999.

Round-4 coverage: all 10 planned pooled cells and 20 transition pair files completed. The first fit job lost the five off-policy units to two memory failure modes (a host cgroup out-of-memory kill and a CUDA out-of-memory in the λ sweep); a relaunch with both modes fixed recomputed all five (job completed exit 0:0) and reused the four finished on-policy units via done-markers — resumed bootstrap chunks were verified equal to the pre-crash arrays (numpy array-equal check), so cross-unit comparability is preserved. One declared discard: the capture-parity recapture tensors (`gates_v2/g2_recapture/*.pt`), regenerable from the persisted rollout text (marker v310). Two pre-relaunch review concerns were closed by the realized run and are recorded in the concern ledger: the committed adjudication record was staged to the launch tree and the gate step consumed it (the run advanced past the halt into production compute rather than re-halting), and the queue-width cap the review mandated was armed (width 2 for the off-policy fit units) with zero out-of-memory events across the recovered run.

Round-4 sensitivity reads carried as limitations. The leakage-excluded sensitivity R²'s trigger fired (104 test rows carry a ≥0.90-cosine cross-corpus train twin; 3.2% of both Tulu-derived slices) but the read is not computable from the persisted prediction matrices — they carry predictions only, no ground-truth answer vectors — and re-running needs tens of GB of activation staging; it is carried as a named follow-up. The cluster-resampled sensitivity CI on the MATH / IF-constraints gaps is unrun for the same staging reason, and with 5 and 4 test-side groups on those corpora a group-level bootstrap would itself be unstable. The answer-text divergence diagnostic behind the off-policy arm (per-checkpoint exact-match rate and similarity of generated answers) is unrun, so off-policy reads are conditional on unmeasured cross-checkpoint text divergence — strongest on MATH/GSM8K, where correct answers converge across checkpoints. The planned regression-to-the-mean check on top delta-Q clusters is moot — no cluster survives the null. The plan's pooled-reproduces-per-corpus slice comparison (each pooled per-corpus slice within round 3's bootstrap CI on at least 7 of 8 slices) is not adjudicated: the failed gate established a 0.05–0.07 systematic depression from family holdout, so pooled slices sit below round-3 values by construction; and the UltraFeedback and SFT-mix slices count as one quasi-unit in any such slice tally (see Data extraction), which would cap the effective count regardless.
