# Plan — Issue #2388: Is answer correctness predictable from the context vector, and does the context→answer map help?

Plan v3. Task #2388 (`kind: experiment`, `workflow: v1`, parent #1739). Autonomous session; plan auto-approves on the parseable GPU-hour total in §0.

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Before the model writes a single word of an answer, I take a snapshot of its internal state at the end of the question and ask: can we tell from that snapshot whether the answer it is about to give will be right? I test this on trivia questions, math problems, multiple-choice exams, and coding tasks, where right and wrong are checked automatically. I also test whether a "translator" we already trained — one that turns question-state snapshots into predicted answer-state snapshots — makes the guess better when we only have a small number of graded examples to learn from.
- **What I expect:** On trivia, the snapshot should predict rightness well — that much is known from earlier work, and the deep literature pass we just ran found that the same kind of pre-answer prediction has now been shown within math and within code too, on other models. The one surface nobody has ever tested this way is multiple-choice exams — that is where a result here would be genuinely first. Math and code are still worth measuring on our model and our grading protocol (published work used different models and mostly single graded attempts), and I expect the translator to help most when graded examples are scarce, with its edge fading as more become available.
- **What would change my mind:** If the translator helps on rightness about as much as it helped on personality-style traits in the parent experiment, that says it carries knowledge, not just style — and the reverse says our map is mostly a style map. If a simple "hard question vs easy question" feature set predicts just as well as the snapshot, the snapshot read is telling us about the questions, not the model.

## 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (direct predictability).** On each surface's locked test split, the direct-context readout ("Context probe") predicts the correctness rate above the surface-features baseline. Per surface, define Δρ = ρ(Context probe) − ρ(Surface-features probe), both at dev-frozen layer/λ, read once on test. Per-surface priors (corrected by the lit review, §2): QA established on our exact model (2509.10625) — near-certain, and any QA headline carries the knowledge-recall-not-truthfulness caveat (2510.09033); math and code have in-domain pre-generation precedent on OTHER models (2602.09924; 2606.14530), so those cells test whether the published in-domain result transfers to our model + K=5 rate protocol — expected-positive, not virgin; **MCQ is the genuinely open surface** (no pre-generation last-prompt-token probe on MMLU-Pro/MMLU exists; nearest art is post-generation CCPS 2505.21772), so H1's per-surface novelty concentrates in MCQ, not math.
- **H2 (label-efficiency crossover).** The mapped-answer probes match or beat the Context probe at small L, with the gap closing (possibly reversing) as L grows. Deliverables: the per-surface crossover L (if any) and the degradation slope across shift rungs, read against the literature's label-efficiency anchors (25 labels → 90% of full-data AUC, 2602.08159; 160 robust / 2,560 ≈ full, 2509.10625 — §2). H2's mechanism has no published analogue (§2), so the curves are themselves a deliverable either way. No verdict labels are registered for H2 — it is an estimation target (curves + paired CIs), not a pass/fail. **The decisive comparison for any crossover claim is mapped-vs-PCA-context (the matched-rank compression comparator), not mapped-vs-ambient-context** — at small L, beating the ambient Context probe is consistent with pure low-rank regularization; any crossover headline cites both that pair and the shuffled-map alignment control. **Linear-direct-readout qualifier (everywhere):** a mapped win supports improvement over the registered LINEAR direct probe only (nonlinear direct readouts are user-declined scope) — it never establishes that the map adds information beyond the context vector in an absolute sense; linear-map and MLP-map results are reported separately, and an MLP-map-only win is read against the shuffled-MLP control.

### Success and kill criteria (per hypothesis)

Success criteria are the registered reads above (the H1 verdict lattice; H2–H4 as estimation targets with paired CIs). The quantitative kill criteria below state, per hypothesis, what measured result would KILL that thesis — not merely fail to support it. These are thesis-level; the §7 gates are run-control and do not substitute for them.

- **H1 kill (direct predictability):** on BOTH math AND code (the two non-QA surfaces with published in-domain pre-generation precedent on other models — 2602.09924, 2606.14530), the Context probe's test Δρ vs the surface-features probe has its 95% CI wholly at or below 0 (the lattice's Falsified cell), OR its observed test ρ falls inside the 200-draw selection-matched permutation null band while that band sits below the DV attenuation ceiling (0.93–0.97) — the pre-generation read is then indistinguishable from the difficulty baselines given the variance, on our model + protocol exactly where other models' in-domain probes succeed, and H1 is dead for those surfaces (a strong, literature-contradicting negative, bounded by the band's position below the ceiling — never narrated as proof the state carries nothing). MCQ, having no literature anchor in either direction, cannot kill H1 alone: an MCQ null is a novel negative finding on the field's emptiest surface, reported either way. QA is the positive control: a QA H1 failure (Δρ CI ≤ 0 on the banked surface where prior work makes H1 near-certain) is treated as an instrument defect, halt-and-report — re-verify the pipeline before interpreting any other surface's read.
- **H2 kill (label-efficiency crossover):** across ALL four surfaces and ALL small-L cells (L ≤ 2,000), no mapped-answer probe (linear or MLP map) beats the Context probe with a paired-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, AND no mapped-vs-identity+learned-bias interval excludes 0 in the mapped arm's favor in any cell — the mapped arms are then indistinguishable from the direct probe and from a mean-shift baseline given the variance, everywhere in the small-L regime, and the crossover thesis is dead at our resolution (the curves are still reported, framed as "no detectable label-efficiency gain at this resolution", never as affirmative absence of map knowledge).
- **H3 kill (knowledge vs persona):** at every matched well-determined anchor of the estimator-matched P4 comparison (per behavior, the parent's largest banked budget — evil 8,000, sycophancy 16,000, hallucination 16,000), the mapped-minus-direct gap on correctness is indistinguishable from null given the variance (95% CI including or below 0) WHILE the same-pipeline banked #1739 gaps on evil/sycophancy/fabrication are positive at those same anchors, both sides read against their own attenuation ceilings — the map then shows detectable persona-DV signal and no detectable correctness signal at matched anchors; the "map carries non-persona information" thesis is dead at our resolution and the map is reported as a persona/style map (a directional read, never proof of zero knowledge content).
- **H4 kill (pool composition):** ρ(f_U=1) − ρ(f_U=0) has a paired 95% CI including or below 0 on every surface at every L anchor AND the Result 0(d) map-quality reads (held-out R², kNN retrieval) show no detectable rise from f_U=0 to f_U=1 — the composition effect is then indistinguishable from null given the variance everywhere, the thesis that in-domain unlabeled pairs substitute for graded labels finds no support at our resolution, and the composition thesis is dead.

### Objects, pooling, and the pooling fork resolution

### Map-pool composition (f_U factor)

**Joint-feasibility resolution (plan v3; supersedes the v2 fixed-8,000-disjoint registration, which was jointly unsatisfiable).** Three registered v2 commitments — |U| fixed at 8,000, target pairs label-disjoint, the full L grid — cannot coexist (train @70%: QA 11,200, math 8,750, MCQ 8,422, code ≈ 4,218; a disjoint 8,000-pair pool leaves label budgets of at most 3,200 / 750 / 422 / nothing), and code's ENTIRE pool (≈ 6,027 pre-dedup) is below 8,000 under any configuration. Registered resolution, four parts:

1. **Overlap is PRIMARY.** The map pool is drawn from the TRAIN partition with overlap with the readout's labeled contexts ALLOWED. The map never sees labels, so an overlapping pool leaks no label information (the body's own variant rationale), and overlap is the realistic deployment shape — at deployment the unlabeled pool contains the very questions to be answered. Pools stay disjoint from dev and test ALWAYS (eval inputs never enter map training; the inductive read is preserved).
2. **Per-surface registered pool size |U|_s = min(8,000, realized train):** 8,000 on QA/math/MCQ; code at its realized post-dedup train count (~4,218 with BCB) — pinned by the pre-fit assertion below, stated up front, never drifted per cell.
3. **Strict label-disjointness is retained as a VARIANT** on QA at the f_U anchors L ∈ {250, 2,000} (feasible: 8,000 + 2,000 ≤ 11,200) — the leakage-sanity contrast: if overlap were doing hidden work, disjoint-vs-overlap at matched |U| = 8,000 on QA would show it.
4. **Pair grain registered: ONE pair per context** (v_A = the per-context mean over the K=5 rollouts' token-mean summaries), matching the U-store's one-pair-per-context grain — per-rollout pairs would weight target contexts 5× against generic pairs inside a mixed cell.

Cells (replace, never add): f_U = 0 (generic-only — ONE shared map, no target component, reused across all four surfaces), f_U = 0.5 (half generic + half target-surface; 4,000 + 4,000 on the 8,000-pool surfaces), f_U = 1 (all target-surface). Generic pairs: subsampled from the banked #1092 U-store (18,793 pairs, staged idempotently by the #1739 dispatcher into the consuming pod's `/workspace/u_store/` — the MooseFS-backed `/workspace` pod volume on Pods B and C, counted inside the §9 disk rows; ~12 GiB summaries at 3 kinds × 28 layers). Run at 3 L anchors {250, 2,000, full} only (#1739's own scoping). New map fits: (1 shared f_U=0 + 2 per-surface cells × 4 surfaces) × 2 families = 18 keys × 28 layers each (multihead), plus the QA additive variant (2 keys) = 20 fitted keys — split Pod B (shared f_U=0 + QA cells + additive = 8 keys) / Pod C (math/MCQ/code cells = 12 keys), §9. The additive variant (generic + target ADDED) runs once on ONE surface (QA) as a labeled realistic-deployment contrast, never the comparison. Banked U=18,793 generic maps ride as an out-of-protocol reference row on QA.

**Pre-fit joint-feasibility assertion (registered; fails loud at the P1 pre-step — post-dedup, BEFORE generation):** enumerate every (surface, composition cell, label budget) and assert: (i) realized |U| IDENTICAL across the surface's three f_U cells; (ii) pool ⊆ train partition; (iii) dev and test disjoint from the pool AND from every labeled draw; (iv) L ≤ |train|; (v) in the QA disjoint-variant cells additionally pool ∩ labeled-draw = ∅ and |train| ≥ |U| + L. Any violation halts before generation, not after.
