## Plan Summary

Add a **close-miss escalation clause** to the on-policy-completions yield-floor
mechanics: a source/arm finishing **≥ 90% of its floor** (but below it) after the
registered retry budget gets **ONE automatic same-construct escalation tranche**
before the drop fires. Canonical text lands in
`.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md`; one-clause mirrors land in the three
restatement sites found at clarify time. Doc-only; no code, no experiment.

## Method delta (what differs from prior state)

The current rule (decision 2026-06-12, #545/#612) has exactly two outcomes at
the floor boundary: keep (≥ floor) or drop-and-report (< floor). The delta adds
a THIRD, bounded outcome in the [90% of floor, floor) band: one recorded
on-policy escalation tranche, then re-evaluate against the unchanged floor.
No existing semantics change: below 90% of floor the drop fires exactly as
today; the floor value (80% of target), equalize-down, negative scaling, and
reporting duties are untouched.

Threshold calibration (task body delegates to planner): historical fills are
bimodal — catastrophic misses (#906: 17%, ~1%, 0%; #612 software-engineer
84.5%-of-target ⇒ kept under the modern 80% floor) vs the #1947 near-miss
(96.7% of floor). 90%-of-floor bounds the escalation tranche at
≤ (10% of floor)/acceptance × 1.3 extra generation (cheap, one tranche) while
excluding genuinely-hard arms, whose correct outcome remains drop-and-report.
No observed miss lands in [80%, 90%) of floor, so the threshold has slack on
both sides; it is a named tunable, not load-bearing at ±5%.
