```markdown
- **Char-offset→token-span alignment over PLAIN-TEXT delimiters yields ZERO-WIDTH spans when the segment's text BPE-merges into the delimiter.** Offset-containment alignment assumes each rendered segment owns ≥1 token, but a bare-punctuation segment can fuse entirely into the adjacent plain-text delimiter (Qwen merges ` .\n\n` into ONE token; even 2-token texts like `Thanks.` lose both ends to boundary straddlers), producing `s == e` spans that crash the consumer. Exposure is boundary-type-scoped, NOT format-scoped: special-token boundaries never BPE-merge, but plain-text delimiters are exposed everywhere they appear — naturalistic renders, few-shot plain-text prompts, `User:/Assistant:`-style rigs over T>0 sampled text, and plain-text delimiters INSIDE a chat-templated message body. RULE: any pipeline that generates free text and later aligns it as a delimited segment must run the CONSUMER's exact span asserts tokenize-only at GENERATION time (same tokenizer + revision as the consumer), and hard-fail or substitute a validated MULTI-token placeholder, recording every substitution (it shifts the sampled-text distribution; single-token placeholders like `.` are themselves degenerate). Long-form recipe: `.claude/agent-memory/experiment-implementer/feedback_zero_width_span_bpe_delimiter_merge.md`. (Incident #825 onpolicy-user-turn round, 2026-07-03: three production GCP attempts died at ~21 min each on `AssertionError: span u2=(201,201) invalid` after a bare-punctuation sampled user turn merged into the naturalistic `\n\n` delimiter.)
```
