### A. The exact entry (insert after the line-108 `Hidden-state-only CausalLM forwards...` entry)

```markdown
- **bf16 padded-batch equivalence gates over SINGLE-POSITION hidden states — a cosine bar calibrated on span-mean summaries has no headroom; fp32 re-probe before loosening anything.** A batched-vs-batch-1 equivalence gate whose cosine bar was calibrated on SPAN-MEAN summaries (span means smooth bf16 jitter — #779 pass-1 realized 0.999748 against a flat 0.999 bar) FAILS a bug-free capture path on single-position states: depth-amplified bf16 padded-batch kernel numerics concentrate in the LAST layer (#779 pass-2 worst flattened cell cos 0.998770, driven by layer 27 ALONE at 0.996907; layer-0 cos ≥ 0.999995 everywhere; an fp32 batch-3 vs batch-1 re-probe read cos = 1.000000 on every (item, summary) cell — proving no padding/indexing bug). A REAL off-by-one / pad / row-mapping bug reads far below either regime — cos_flat 0.39–0.62 and layer-0 cos 0.43–0.84, identical in both dtypes — so the tight bar buys nothing at deep layers. DISTINCT from the hollow-verification-gate trap (`.claude/rules/code-style.md`): there the gate tests the wrong function; here the gate tests the right function with a wrong bar. RULE: split the gate into two bars — (a) EARLY-layer per-layer cosine ≥ 0.999 over the first ~4 layers (layers 0–3; the sharp bug catcher — mask/RoPE/pad/row bugs corrupt layer 0 immediately, where bf16 jitter is ~1e-6), and (b) flattened all-layer cosine ≥ 0.995 (≥4× the measured worst bf16 deviation as headroom, still ~0.35 above the ~0.6 real-bug regime). Calibrate on the NOISIEST quantity class the gate covers (single positions / small-window maxes, not span means), keep `max_rel` reported-never-asserted, and attribute any marginal miss with an fp32 re-probe BEFORE loosening a bar. Worked example: `scripts/issue779_capture_answer_summaries_pass2.py::equivalence_gate_p2` (two-bar gate; calibration comment at `GATE_COS_FLAT_MIN`; commit `7b35377edc`). Long-form: `.claude/agent-memory/experiment-implementer/feedback_bf16_single_position_equivalence_gate_calibration.md`. (Incident #779 r12, 2026-07-03: the pass-2 template-position capture crashed its own gate at cos_min 0.998770 < 0.999 with a bug-free path; fp32-verified, recalibrated same round.)
```
