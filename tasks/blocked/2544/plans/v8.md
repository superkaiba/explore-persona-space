## 3. Hypothesis

Let Q(m, s, ρ, ℓ) = pooled cluster-grouped OOF R² of the ridge map fitted on checkpoint m's activations over (query-block context state, answer state from source s's text) under render ρ at layer ℓ. layer* = the frozen shared headline layer (§6). D(T) = Q(m=T, s=T, 0-shot, layer*); D̃(T) = D(T) − identity+bias R²(same cell). Δ_form = D̃(main) − D̃(r0), cluster-bootstrap 95% CI. T_half = the first rung T with D̃(T) − D̃(r0) ≥ 0.5·max(Δ_form, 0.10). ρ_P(i→j) = Procrustes-aligned transfer R²(i→j) / Q(j, j) at layer* (alignments train-fold-fitted; answer-side alignment on same-answer-text pairs from the s=main column). T_c = the earliest pretraining rung i such that ρ_P(i→main) ≥ 0.8 for i AND every later pretraining rung. Δ(T) = Q(T, T, 4-shot, layer*) − Q(T, T, 0-shot, layer*), paired per context.

- **H1 (formation timing — the registered headline; an ADJUDICATION, not a bet).** The literature splits: circuit-level structure forms at <1% of training, content-level linear readouts at intermediate stages (§2). The registered read is the label of D̃(T)'s shape below. Pre-registered: a NON-MONOTONE D̃(T) is interpretable (2606.26050), not a pipeline fault; §6 names the real-dip-vs-artifact checklist (now including the censoring item vi).
- **H2 (crystallize then reparameterize).** T_c exists strictly before end-of-pretraining. Kill: no pretraining rung reaches ρ_P(i→main) ≥ 0.5. T_c's value is an estimation read (bootstrap over cluster draws).
- **H3 (substitution decays).** Δ(r0) ≈ 0 (no ICL at random init — expected, 2005.14165), Δ(T) > 0 at intermediate rungs, shrinking by main. H3 adjudicated JOINTLY on Δ(T), Δ_ww(T), Δ_FA(T) — §6 sliding-window handling unchanged — AND (round-1 reconcile, binding) H3's direction is narrated as substitution only where it survives an available same-ID common-status read: Δ_nt(T) on rows naturally terminated in BOTH arms and Δ_tt(T) on rows truncated in BOTH arms, wherever supported (per-rung row counts stated); where no common-status read is supported (r0, thin strata), the affected rung's Δ ships labeled censoring-confounded.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: No-formation ⇔ upper 95% CI of Δ_form < 0.10; Unresolved ⇔ otherwise AND no rung crosses 0.5·max(Δ_form, 0.10); Early ⇔ otherwise AND T_half ≤ 21B tokens (rung ≤ r2); Intermediate ⇔ otherwise AND 21B < T_half ≤ 1.2T (rungs r3–r6); Late ⇔ otherwise.

## 4. Design

### Eligibility semantics (the v6 revision — read this block first)

**Revised rule (registered):** a context row is ELIGIBLE for map fitting at a (rung, arm) cell iff its rollout record has `repetition_flag == False` (and a non-empty answer span — the existing A19 span-validity assert). `truncated` is REMOVED from the eligibility predicate everywhere; it remains computed, persisted per row (the record schema is unchanged — `R._flag_records`, `issue1902_run.py:797-823`, is NOT edited), and reported as a per-(rung, arm) diagnostic + a figure strip. The shared headline intersection remains a JOINT 15-rung × both-arms intersection — same pinned context ids at every rung — over the repetition-only flags.

**Why this is the right fix against the Goal (the construct argument):** the DV is the pooled activation summary over the ANSWER SPAN of the model's own completion (answer state w = mean over answer tokens; capture already truncates answer ids at the cap — `issue1902_run.py:1141` `answer_ids = answer_ids[:C.GEN_MAX_TOKENS]`). A cap-truncated completion has a well-defined, on-policy answer span; nothing in the map fit requires the answer to have terminated. The truncation filter existed in the parent as a degeneracy guard on a 4-stage POST-TRAINING chain where survival was 94–99% — on a ladder containing an untrained rung it conditions the entire context population on rung-0's termination behavior (the v5 scope caveat three reviewers flagged) and, on the measured table, empties the intersection outright. Repetition loops stay a row filter because a degenerate loop makes the answer cloud collapse toward a few attractors — the one measured pathology that corrupts the fit's target rather than merely censoring it — and its measured incidence (0–35 per 500 per cell, every cell including r0) makes a joint filter feasible.

**What each retained flag now does:**
- `repetition_flag` — the ONLY eligibility filter (joint, symmetric across all 15 rungs × both arms; fairness control 4 preserved).
- `truncated` — reported per (rung, arm) in `pilot_report.json`, `intersection_manifest.json`, the diagnostics JSON, and as a strip under hero figures 1 and 4; drives the registered censoring-sensitivity family (§6); NEVER filters a row from any fit, ceiling, baseline, or null.
- The bf16 numerics gate's clean-row selection at `main` (`issue2544_run.py:1214`) deliberately keeps the old conjunction — it picks numerically clean rows for a kernel-equivalence probe at a rung with 93.6% survival; it is not an eligibility surface and is NOT edited.

**Reconciliation with the repeat-generation ceiling (registered rule):** the per-rung ceiling and the Δ-ceiling are computed over EXACTLY the fit's row set — headline intersection ∩ reliability subset, eligibility keyed on the seed-42 arms' repetition flags. Repeat-draw (seed 43/44) flags are never filters: a repetition-loop or cap-length repeat draw is a legitimate sample from the rung's answer distribution, and excluding it would inflate the ceiling above what the fitted rows can achieve. The fits code asserts ceiling-row-set == fit-row-set ∩ reliability at scoring time (row-set bookkeeping; delta inventory item 4). A truncation-stratified ceiling read is an exploratory diagnostic (free re-aggregation), not a filter.

**Cross-rung comparability — precisely what the revised rule does (the brief's required statement):**
1. **Context population:** identical pinned rows at every rung and arm — fairness control 4 is fully preserved; only the conditioning weakens, from "terminates ≤ 1,024 tokens at random init" (strong, plausibly length/simplicity-correlated) to "avoids repetition loops at all 15 rungs × 2 arms" (weak, ≤ 7% per cell measured).
2. **Measurement operator:** uniform at every rung — answer state = mean over the first ≤ 1,024 on-policy answer tokens, the same function applied everywhere. What varies across rungs is the answer DISTRIBUTION, including its termination behavior — the rung's own on-policy property, i.e. part of the measured construct, not a broken control.
3. **The residual confound, named:** per-rung truncation-CENSORING rate varies with T (measured gen0: 100% at r0 → 6.4% at main), so D(T) could partially track censoring rather than map quality. Each affected result carries a registered diagnostic/caveat — the censoring-sensitivity family in §6: the truncation strip under D(T) and Δ(T); the natural-termination sensitivity curve D_nt(T) on r1..R; the within-rung truncated-vs-natural split; the truncation-matched column C (same main text at every rung — censoring-immune on the text side by construction); the ceiling computed on the same censored distribution (D(T)-relative-to-ceiling is internally consistent per rung); and the adjudication rule folded into the real-dip checklist as item (vi).
