## 1. Goal

Quoted byte-identical from `body.md` § Goal:

> Determine the geometric shape of a single contrastive negative's leakage-suppression in persona space: does a negative protect a local neighborhood around itself (bubble) or the entire region behind it relative to the source (barrier/shadow)? Secondary: do near-twin negatives produce a more localized implant than distant ones, holding all else fixed.

The Goal is operational because both models (bubble vs barrier) make discriminating predictions: a regression of held-out leakage on `d_source`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`, and `shadow_angle` separates them when the design moves all three predictors enough across arms. Resolving this converts the standing folklore claim "near-twin negatives are the sharpest lever for localization" into a measurement and tells us whether a small number of well-placed negatives can cover a whole region of persona space (barrier — concrete EM-defense lever) or whether each persona to be protected needs its own nearby negative (bubble — defense cost scales with the persona set).

**Source scope decision (unchanged from v1/v2).** Main grid on `villain` alone. A `≥1`-additional-source replication is a follow-up triggered on a positive read. v3's Phase 0 calibration does NOT carry the v2 fallback (medical_doctor) — Phase 0's empirical refutation across BOTH sources at lr=1e-4 collapsed the fallback's value to zero (medical_doctor max ΔG = 2.599 nats was strictly worse than villain's 3.044, and both stuck at emission 0). The v3 calibration tests EPOCHS at villain alone; if 3 epochs at lr=1e-4 / r=8 / α=32 still floors source ΔG, the design exits to plan v4 (rank bump) rather than re-testing the same composition on a different source.

**Why EPOCHS over rank or positives count (one-line each, full justification in §11).**

- **EPOCHS is the cheapest test of the budget-limited hypothesis.** v2's lr=1e-4 cell showed source ΔG climbing toward (but not reaching) the band. The straightforward question — "is this a budget problem? does more gradient time fix it?" — is what EPOCHS answers directly. Doubling EPOCHS doubles effective optimization steps; tripling triples them. Step count is the natural extrapolation axis from v2's evidence.
- **Rank doubles capacity** but introduces a new failure mode (#477 / #448 / #492 / #477-r32 all saturated at r=32). Rank-bump is the right v4 fallback if EPOCHS doesn't work, NOT the first v3 attempt — capacity is a step-change variable; budget is a continuous one. Test the continuous extrapolation first.
- **Positives count = 400** was tried in v1's user-directive (lr=2e-6) and produced source ΔG ~0.11 nats — refuted as a fix for the floor. Doubling positives at lr=1e-4 would also double effective steps (the actual lever), so EPOCHS is the cleaner naming of the variable.

**Single-token ※ implants need step budget AND adequate lr.** Per `#397`: lr=1e-4 was load-bearing for the marker being installable at all. v2 confirmed the lr part; v3 tests the step-budget part. The two are independent levers — v2 ruled in lr=1e-4, v3 rules in or out EPOCHS ∈ {2, 3} at that lr.

**Saturated anchor kills composition sweeps.** Per `#448`: at a fully-trained anchor, on-policy log-prob saturates (argmax = marker everywhere) and recipe knobs have zero dynamic range. The anchor MUST sit ~5-10 nats below ceiling with emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] for a composition sweep to read a real geometry effect. This is why v3 brackets EPOCHS at {2, 3} rather than going straight to EPOCHS=5: 5 might saturate.

**Distance predicts leakage (#207, #500).** Marker leakage decays with persona-space distance from the source (|ρ| 0.48-0.79; `Source: #207`). #500's challenge — bystander base prior survives sign-flip while source proximity does not — applies to the fact line, not the marker line; for ※ the base prior is approximately uniform-low across personas. The base prior is included as a covariate AND as a sign-flip sanity check.

## 3. Hypothesis

**Anchor-installation hypothesis (Phase 0 v3):** at least one EPOCHS in {2, 3} at lr=1e-4 / r=8 / α=32 / all-linear / 200 positives + 200 negatives / count=2 lands source ΔG in [5, 12] nats with on-policy emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] at SOME checkpoint fraction in {0.08, 0.16, 0.33, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}. **If both EPOCHS=2 AND EPOCHS=3 are out-of-band, fallback to r=16 all-linear in plan v4** (not executed in this plan).

**H_bubble (geometry null #1):** held-out leakage decays with distance-to-nearest-non-default-negative, independent of source angular position. A regression of `ΔG_probe ~ d_source + d_nearest_neg_nd + shadow_angle + base_prior + training_step + source_delta_g(cell)` across pooled probe × arm × seed rows from the 4 positioned arms gives a positive Holm-significant partial on `d_nearest_neg_nd` and a near-zero non-Holm-significant partial on `shadow_angle`. The Near arm has the tightest bubble (probes near villain inside it); the Far arm has a bubble far from the source so probes near source leak.

**H_barrier (geometry null #2):** held-out leakage is suppressed for probes whose source→probe direction is approximately the source→N direction. The regression gives a positive Holm-significant partial on `shadow_angle` and a near-zero non-Holm-significant partial on `d_nearest_neg_nd`. The arm where N is between many probes and the source casts the widest shadow.

**H_both:** both geometry partials Holm-significant — a mix of local suppression around N and angular shadow from source through N. Most likely a priori on top of a small dose of each.

**H_indeterminate (kill criterion):** (a) Phase 0.5 identification gates fail (across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` < 0.02 OR in `shadow_angle` < 0.10 rad OR qwen_default dominance at L10 AND L15), OR (b) the chosen anchor doesn't land more than one arm in the [5, 12]-nat source ΔG window at the pinned `chosen_checkpoint_fraction`, OR (c) `source_delta_g(cell)` explains > 50% of cross-arm ΔG variance OR has |Pearson| > 0.7 with `d_nearest_neg_nd` or `shadow_angle`, OR (d) the step covariate or any single non-geometric covariate is the dominant signal. The call is **Indeterminate** with the dominant covariate named — not a barrier/bubble verdict.

**Verdict assignment via joint diagnostic weighting (NOT a Holm-p ladder).** Bubble / Barrier / Both / Indeterminate × HIGH / MODERATE / LOW. The analyzer reports the full set of diagnostics (signed partial Spearman, Holm-p, per-seed sign agreement, cross-layer signs, identification gate outcomes, saturation diagnostics, step + implant-strength confound diagnostics, collinearity, R-collapse rate, sign-flip-on-base_prior) and weighs them jointly. A HIGH-confidence call requires diagnostics to line up: positive partial on the driver, Holm-low p, 2/2 seed sign agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers agree, gates pass, saturation in-band, no dominant confound.

### 4.3 Phase 0.5 — distance pre-flight + persona-bank sufficiency check (CPU + 1 GPU-min, ≈ 0.1 GPU-h)

**Goal:** verify analytically — BEFORE Phase 1 training spawns — that the persona bank supports the 4-arm placement axis with enough across-arm movement to identify bubble vs barrier.

**Steps (unchanged from v1/v2):**

1. Load layer-10 centroids from cached HF `issue472_neg_geometry/geometry/centroids_L10.pt`. If absent, build via `centroids.build_centroids` over the 60-persona bank (~10 min on 1 GPU).
2. Pick 4 positioned negatives — ONE per arm — by base-model L10 cosine to `villain`:
   - **N_near**: persona with cos(N, source) in the top quartile (cos ≥ ~0.6)
   - **N_mid_near**: cos(N, source) ≈ 0.4 (median of top half)
   - **N_mid_far**: cos(N, source) ≈ 0.1 (median of bottom half)
   - **N_far**: cos(N, source) in the bottom quartile (cos ≤ ~−0.1)
   - Constraint: NO two N's collide AND none is `qwen_default` (always included separately) AND none is `villain` itself.
3. Held-out panel = bank − {villain, N_near, N_mid_near, N_mid_far, N_far, qwen_default} ≈ 55 probes.
4. For each probe in the panel, compute `d_source`, `d_nearest_neg_nd(arm)`, `shadow_angle(probe, arm)`, and a uniform-low placeholder for `base_prior_marker(probe)` (real values come at Phase 1 eval).

If qwen_default dominates ≥ 50% of probes' nearest-negative, the positioned negative is invisible to the bubble predictor. Repeat at L15; if both fail, retreat to L20 OR bank expansion (see fallback table below).
7. **Gate C — sufficiency:** at least 30 probes survive the panel cut.
8. **Max-length check (one-shot, CPU):** `max(len(tokenizer.encode(r)) for r in R_train.json.values())` over the cached villain on-policy R ≤ 1024 (train-time `max_length`). If a response saturates 1024, bump eval `max_new_tokens` 2048 → 4096 (≥ 2× longest trained completion per CLAUDE.md).

### 4.5 Phase 2 — analysis (CPU only, after Phase 1)

**Step 1 — Single pinned `chosen_checkpoint_fraction`.** Read every (cell, seed) at the SAME fraction from Phase 0. Robustness panel (NOT headline): also compute Step 3 at each cell's own latest-in-band checkpoint; if verdict + signs + confidence agree, HIGH; if they disagree, headline downgraded.

Per-arm read at the pinned fraction: source ΔG, on-policy emission rate, `assert_adapter_actually_applied` verdict. Arms with source ΔG outside [5, 12] nats OR emission outside [0.1, 0.8] AT THE PINNED FRACTION are excluded from the headline regression with the exclusion logged. > 1 excluded → verdict = **Indeterminate (anchor unstable across arms)**.

**Step 2 — Identification gates (post-hoc re-verify with real `base_prior_marker`).** Recompute Gate A from §4.3 on the real eval data, with `base_prior_marker(probe) = log P(※ | T_probe(q) + R_probe_base)` at the post-response slot under base. If the across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` drops below 0.02 at this point, verdict = Indeterminate (identification failure).

**Step 3 — Pooled partial-Spearman regression.** Across pooled (probe × arm × seed) rows from the 4 positioned arms — N = 4 arms × ~50 probes × 2 seeds ≈ 400 rows — fit:

```
ΔG = β0 + β_dsource · d_source
        + β_dnn · d_nearest_neg_nd
        + β_shadow · shadow_angle
        + β_baseprior · base_prior_marker
        + β_step · training_step(cell)
        + β_source_dg · source_delta_g(cell)
        + ε
```

Use **partial Spearman ρ** with the others partialled out. Holm-corrected p across 6 predictors (`analyze.holm_correction`).

**Collinearity gate.** Pre-register Pearson(|d_source|, d_nearest_neg_nd) at 0.7 threshold; tercile-bucket median test + polynomial residualization if above. Additionally Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`) AND Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `shadow_angle`); > 0.7 on either flips verdict to **Indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded)**.

**Step 4 — Verdict (`_barrier_bubble_verdict`).** Existing function; extend to check `β_shadow` (currently checks β_dsource + β_dnn only). Patch sketch reused from v1 Appendix A.2.

**Step 5 — Robustness layers.** Repeat Steps 3-4 at L15 + L20. Headline = L10. Cross-layer agreement is part of confidence, not a multiplicity correction.

**Step 6 — Sign-flip on `base_prior_marker`** (#500 confound sanity check). Re-fit Step 3 with `−d_source` substituted; if `β_dnn` and `β_shadow` keep signs and Holm-p, geometry result is robust to the #500 challenge.

### 4.6 Identification gates (carry from v1 / v2 unchanged)

- Across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` ≥ 0.02 (#472 floor).
- Across-arm spread in `shadow_angle` ≥ 0.10 rad.
- qwen_default non-dominance at headline layer (Gate B from §4.3).

- `assert_adapter_actually_applied` PASS on every checkpoint (B-matrix-only per round-4 fix).

### 4.7 Why code, not a model call?

The bubble/barrier discriminator is a deterministic numerical regression over 400 structured float rows (cosines, log-probs, Holm corrections, bootstrap CIs). A single-Claude-call formulation ("given these 400 rows, is this barrier or bubble?") was considered and rejected because: (a) inputs are structured floats, nothing for a model to add over deterministic regression; (b) determinism is the entire point (Holm-p meaningfulness); (c) the existing code path (`analyze.run_analysis`) is the #472 path, already validated against the same DV. The only model call in this design (besides training / eval forward passes) is **persona-bank expansion** if Phase 0.5 trips Gate A or C — `persona_bank.py` uses Sonnet 4.5 for persona-prompt generation, which IS the right tool for unstructured-text generation.

### 4.8 Contrastive negatives for behavior implantation (default per `contrastive-negatives.md`)

This IS a behavior-implantation experiment (implanting ※ into villain). The recipe satisfies the rule:

- **Negative-persona set:** ALL arms include `qwen_default` (safety target). The 4 positioned arms add ONE additional negative chosen by distance to source. Negative set = {qwen_default, N_arm} for positioned arms; {qwen_default} for default-only.
- **Positives:negatives ratio:** 200:200 = **1:1** per cell (`Source: #383, #477`).
- **Negative response construction:** marker-LESS — on-policy frozen greedy response under the negative persona's own system prompt. Under `MarkerOnlyDataCollator(tail_tokens=0)` this trains EOS at the post-response slot.
- **Number of negative personas:** 2 per positioned arm (the count axis IS the open #19 question deliberately not touched — flagged as scope caveat for the clean-result).

Not one of the two exemptions (single manipulated variable is placement / EPOCHS, not contrastive-vs-non-contrastive; not a strict positive-only replication). Rule satisfied.

**The smoke phase (Phase 0 v3 EPOCHS-ladder) IS the sweep with one cell.** Same dispatcher (`scripts/dispatch_neg_geometry_504.py`), same subprocess shape (`i504_run_cell.py` per cell), same env injection, same WandB logging, same `train_one_cell` path. ## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Measurement validity table

| DV | Construct (what the Goal cares about) | Metric (what is actually computed) | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation / justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ΔG (primary) | The model's propensity to emit ※ when it generates a response under a held-out persona | On-policy `log P(※ \| T_probe(q) + R_probe_greedy_from_base)` at the slot immediately after R, computed as trained − base | **yes** (on-policy; post-response slot IS the natural emission position; 20 EVAL_QUESTIONS is the realistic question distribution) | n/a — primary, on-distribution |
| Emission rate (sanity anchor) | Same construct, binarized | argmax over vocab at the same slot == 83399 | yes | Reported as a free legibility anchor per `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`. NOT the headline DV — saturates and zero-inflates per #406. |
| Full-vocab KL (fallback) | A non-saturating leakage probe at the post-response slot | KL(trained ‖ base) over the full vocab at the post-response slot | yes — same position, on-policy R | Per `feedback_route_b_kl_dv_swap`: if Phase 0's chosen anchor unexpectedly saturates in an arm despite calibration, swap to full-vocab KL. | r_collapsed flag | Whether on-policy R is degenerate (R-collapse caveat) | `is_response_degenerate(R_probe)` | yes | Sanity guard, NOT a DV. R-collapsed probes excluded from regression with rate per arm reported. |
| `source_delta_g(cell)` (covariate) | Per-cell implant strength at the pinned fraction | Source ΔG at pinned fraction × cell × seed | yes | Regression covariate; serves the #472 finding 1 confound check. |

Primary DV is on-distribution by all three criteria: on-policy (LoRA shifts only marker, not R, so trained model's "own" R at this slot IS base R), natural token position (marker's emission position by training construction), realistic prompt distribution (same EVAL_QUESTIONS used in any held-out eval).

### 6.2 Primary statistical tests

1. **Partial-Spearman regression (the headline diagnostic).** Partial Spearman ρ of ΔG on each of {d_source, d_nearest_neg_nd, shadow_angle, base_prior_marker, training_step, source_delta_g(cell)} across pooled rows from the 4 positioned arms. Report signed ρ, Holm-corrected p, bootstrap 95% CI per predictor.
2. **Sign-stability across seeds.** Per-seed regression → per-seed signs; report sign-agreement (2/2 vs 1/2 vs 0/2).
3. **Cross-layer robustness.** Steps 1-2 at L10 (headline), L15, L20.
4. **Sign-flip robustness on `base_prior`** (#500 sanity check).
5. **Step-confound diagnostic.** Cross-cell Pearson(training_step, ΔG); `training_step` is a regressor in Step 1.
6. **Implant-strength-confound diagnostic.** `source_delta_g(cell)` is the 6th regressor; Pearson with `d_nearest_neg_nd` AND `shadow_angle`; > 0.7 on either flips verdict to Indeterminate.

### 6.3 Verdict assignment (analyzer's job, NOT a p-threshold ladder)

The analyzer assigns **Bubble / Barrier / Both / Indeterminate** × **HIGH / MODERATE / LOW** confidence by weighing §6.2 diagnostics jointly per the statistical-framing rule. No rule maps a single Holm-p threshold to a verdict label.

The analyzer weighs:
- **Geometry partials.** Signed partial Spearman of `d_nearest_neg_nd` and `shadow_angle` (favorable: positive sign, low Holm-p, 2/2 seed agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers agree).
- **Source-distance partial.** Sanity-check predictor, not a verdict driver.
- **Identification gates.** Gate A / B / C from §4.3 re-verified post-hoc.
- **Saturation.** Source ΔG ∈ [5, 12] AND emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] at pinned fraction per arm. > 1 out → Indeterminate.
- **Step-confound.** Per-cell step + `training_step` partial.
- **Implant-strength confound.** `source_delta_g(cell)` partial AND Pearson with geometry predictors. > 50% variance OR Pearson > 0.7 → Indeterminate.
- **R-collapse rate** per arm.
- **Sign-flip robustness on `base_prior`** (#500 sanity check).

HIGH-confidence Bubble: `d_nearest_neg_nd` partial positive, Holm-low, 2/2 seed agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers, gates pass, saturation in-band, no dominant confound. MODERATE: one diagnostic off. LOW or Indeterminate: multiple off OR dominant confound.

### 9.2 Parallelism analysis

- **Compute-bound axes:** 10 training cells + 67,200 on-policy generations.
- **Training:** 10 cells × ~20-30 min each at EPOCHS=2-3. At LoRA r=8 on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, each cell fits on 1 GPU. 4× H100 pod runs 4 concurrent (CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES) → 3 waves of 4-4-2 → ~75 min wall total. Alternative of 10 single-GPU pods: same GPU-h, worse wall time, 10× pod-setup overhead. Per `feedback_multi_gpu_pod`: ONE multi-GPU pod, CVD sharded.
- **Trajectory eval:** vLLM batched gens parallelize across 4 GPUs natively (TP=1 per replica, 4 replicas). HF logp reads batchable.
- **Phase 0 v3 smoke:** runs on a separate 1× H100 pod (intent `lora-7b`), parallel to centroid pipelines, terminates after smoke. Main 4× H100 pod (intent `ft-7b`) provisioned only after Phase 0 picks `chosen_epochs`.
- **No genuine sweep parallelism gain from N pods.** Training is fast (~75 min wall); splitting into N pods saves negligible time at much higher setup cost. Stay with 1× + 1× topology (smoke + main).

### 9.3 Stratification spec

Descope priority if compute deviates > 2× from projection:

1. **Drop seeds first** (10 cells → 5 cells at single seed). Cost: lose sign-stability test → MODERATE confidence ceiling. Min-N: ≥ 1 seed.
2. **Drop the default-only arm** (5 → 4 cells). Cost: lose floor reference; placement regression intact. Min-N: ≥ 3 positioned arms.
3. **Drop one mid arm** (4 → 3 positioned arms: Near, Mid, Far). Cost: weaker `shadow_angle` identification. Min-N: ≥ 3 positioned arms.

Below any of these → escalate via `gates.conditional.compute_deviation_resolution`.

  - Alternatives: bare ※ (id 63680, multi-token, deprecated); `[ZLT]` (deprecated).

  - Why: #477's r=8 / count=2 mid-band cell is the only known mid-band point across 35 cells; α=32 carried from #477's `RANK_ALPHA_MAP_V5`. NOT swept in v3 because the directive's single load-bearing change is EPOCHS; pinning r=8 keeps the EPOCHS ladder a clean 1-variable sweep.

- **Learning rate: lr=1e-4 (FIXED from v2 evidence)**
  - Why: v2 phase0 36-point smoke established lr=1e-4 as the closest cell to in-band across (villain, medical_doctor) × {1e-5, 3e-5, 1e-4} × 6 fractions. ΔG climbed monotonically with lr (0.04 → 0.3 → 2.5 nats) but never committed to emission. Higher lr (e.g. 3e-4) would push past #397's tested ceiling AND risk catastrophic forgetting; lower lr already empirically refuted. lr is no longer a swept variable in v3.
  - Source: `v2 phase0 smoke (2026-06-08T11:18:06Z)`, `#397` (lr=1e-4 for single-token ※). Refutation of v2's full ladder: `#504` events.jsonl 2026-06-08T11:18:06Z.
  - Alternatives: lr=3e-4 (risks both catastrophic forgetting AND R-collapse; #397 didn't test it for ※; high-risk extrapolation); lr=5e-5 (already bracketed by v2's 3e-5 and 1e-4 — not new information); lr=1e-5 (already refuted by v2 phase0 — max ΔG 0.04 nats at villain).

- **EPOCHS ladder: {2, 3}, chosen by Phase 0 v3 at lr=1e-4 / r=8 / α=32 / all-linear**
  - Why: v3's single load-bearing change. v2's lr=1e-4 cell showed ΔG climbing toward the band but stuck at emission 0 after 1 epoch — the budget-limited hypothesis predicts that doubling or tripling gradient steps unsticks emission. EPOCHS=2 doubles effective steps (~50 steps); EPOCHS=3 triples them (~75 steps). The ladder brackets against saturation (EPOCHS=3 might push past [5, 12] nats) and against insufficiency (EPOCHS=2 might still floor). Picking the LOWER in-band epochs is preferred (cheaper Phase 1 wall-time).
  - **v2 §11 originally excluded EPOCHS > 1 on "saturation risk" grounds.** That ruling was predicated on the lr=2e-6 / r=8 / α=32 regime where v1's 3-epoch user-directive plateaued at ΔG 0.11 nats (the saturation argument was about the gradient signal being too weak, not about emission committing). At lr=1e-4 — where v2 empirically established gradient signal CAN climb 25× higher — the saturation-risk argument no longer applies because the curve has actual upward momentum. The exclusion is overturned by v2's evidence; the "saturation" worry now properly applies only to whether 3 epochs at lr=1e-4 pushes PAST the in-band ceiling (which the ladder brackets directly).
  - Source: `v2 phase0 smoke (2026-06-08T11:18:06Z)` (refutation of lr-alone and the budget-limited hypothesis), `#448` (anti-saturation requirement at [5, 12] nats), `#477` (1-epoch / 63-steps was the original mid-band point; the EPOCHS extrapolation is a natural budget axis from that baseline). v1's 3-epoch user-directive ruling: events.jsonl 2026-06-08T04:53:14Z — refuted at lr=2e-6, NOT refuted at lr=1e-4 (untested until v3).

    - EPOCHS=1 (v2's setting — refuted at lr=1e-4 in the 36-point smoke; max ΔG 3.044 nats stuck at floor).
    - EPOCHS=4 or 5 (over-the-top; if EPOCHS=3 saturates, the in-plan finer-fraction recovery uses EPOCHS=2 at smaller fractions instead — more epochs further increases catastrophic-forgetting risk for no upside).
    - Rank bump r=8 → r=16 instead of EPOCHS (the **v4 fallback** if EPOCHS fails).
