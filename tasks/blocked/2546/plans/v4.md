# Plan: issue #2546 — Can the context→answer map predict the POST-CoT answer, and how does CoT training change the map? (v4 — round-1 union revision)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll take three reasoning-trained models and measure whether the final answer is already encoded in the model's hidden state *before* it starts thinking. Arm 1: OpenThinker3-7B and its exact non-reasoning parent (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct) answer ~34,200 benchmark questions spanning "needs step-by-step reasoning" (GSM8K, MATH, hard logic) to "doesn't" (MMLU, ARC, commonsense); I record hidden-state snapshots at the end of the prompt, at nine evenly-spaced points inside the thinking block, at the end of thinking, and at the answer, then fit simple linear predictors between snapshots. Arm 2 repeats this on a different reasoning-training recipe (Qwen2.5-Math-7B → R1-Distill-Qwen-7B) to check the direction of the effect. Arm 3 uses Qwen3-8B, whose thinking can be switched on and off on the same weights, so each question's "did it actually need the reasoning?" is measured on the very model being probed. All three arms run in parallel on their own pods.
- **What I expect:** on questions that need reasoning, the end-of-prompt snapshot predicts the thinking text's style but NOT the final answer, and predictability climbs steadily through the thinking block toward the end-of-thinking snapshot; on easy questions the curve is flat and high from the start. I also expect the non-reasoning parent's prompt snapshot to partially predict the reasoning model's answers, telling us how much reasoning training rewired the prompt-to-answer link.
- **What would change my mind:** if the end-of-prompt snapshot predicts the final answer just as well on hard math as on trivia — with retrieval accuracy near the end-of-thinking ceiling and above-chance answer-content retrieval, not just template matching — then the "reasoning computes the answer" picture is wrong for these models, and the existing whole-output R² ≈ 0.8 result really does cover answers, not just trace style. A flat-high curve on hard math in all three arms would refute the serial-computation framing outright.

## 3. Hypothesis

Let R²(cell, stratum) denote held-out pooled R² at the arm's frozen headline layer (L19 arms 1–2; L24 arm 3), and acc@1 the k=1 retrieval rate in the per-target within-corpus pool (content-identity hit rule, §6).

- **H1 (the star, A-vs-C, arm 1 primary):** on the needs-reasoning stratum, Whole-output-from-context (C) beats Answer-from-context (A) by ≥ 0.15 R². If so, the existing whole-output ≈0.8-style read is carried by the trace, not the answer.
- **H2 (the serial-work interaction):** the Answer-from-end-of-thought minus Answer-from-context gap (D − A) is ≥ 0.10 R² larger on needs-reasoning than on no-reasoning-needed corpora, with acc@1 moving the same direction.
- **H3 (graded):** within GSM8K, per-bin R²(A) decreases monotonically in calculator-step bin (Spearman over bins < 0, 4 bins); within ContextHub, decreasing in level; R²(D) stays within 0.05 of flat across bins.
- **H4 (Plot 8 headline, E, arms 1–2):** the pre-model context vector predicts the post-model answer state at R²(E) > 0 and above the identity+bias baseline; E − F quantifies what CoT training added. No sign prediction registered; either direction is informative.
- **H5 (map change, arms 1–2):** the pre→post reparameterization ladder finds a sufficient tier ≤ 5 (rotation or below) on no-reasoning-needed corpora and a higher (or no) sufficient tier on GSM8K/MATH; prior: #1336 found SFT is the map-installing step, and OpenThinker3 is a 1.2M-row SFT. Ladder corpora (all clear the per-corpus floor by construction under the §4.1 draws): gsm8k_train, MATH, ContextHub, MMLU, plus the pooled pair.
- **H6 (Camp-B trajectory — the addendum deliverable):** R² of think-state→answer (target fixed at the post-`</think>` answer state v_A*) increases monotonically in t over the 11-point curve {t=0 (= cell A), 0.1, 0.2, …, 0.9, t=1 (= cell D)} on the needs-reasoning stratum, per arm (2412.01113). **Registered monotonicity statistic (MF-1 sub-duty ii):** Spearman ρ(t, R²(t)) over the 11 points, per (arm × stratum), with a prompt-level paired bootstrap CI (1,000 draws recomputing the curve from persisted per-row predictions); prediction: ρ > 0 with end-to-end rise ≥ 0.10 R² on needs-reasoning; |rise| < 0.05 on no-reasoning-needed (flat-high). A flat-high needs-reasoning curve refutes the serial-computation framing (the outcome the design must be able to see).
- **H7 (arm-2 recipe robustness — DIRECTION only, per the addendum's binding note):** the SIGNS of the arm-1 findings (H1 gap > 0, H2 interaction > 0, H6 rise > 0 on needs-reasoning) replicate on the Qwen2.5-Math-7B → R1-Distill pair. No magnitude comparison registered — arm 2's pre side is a non-instruct math-specialised base and its renders differ pre-vs-post, so arm 2 is a direction read, never a second estimate of arm 1's magnitude.
- **H8 (arm-3 on-model necessity):** within arm 3, per-question necessity from the toggle (necessary(q) ⇔ correct(think) ∧ ¬correct(no-think), exact-match, both modes greedy) reproduces the H1/H2 pattern at its own grain: R²(A) on toggle-necessary questions < R²(A) on correct-both questions, with R²(D) approximately flat across the two classes. The toggle necessity label is additionally joined by question to arms 1–2 as a cross-arm robustness regressor (an in-house, on-a-mapped-model alternative to the borrowed `rescue_rate`). Arm 3 is a DIFFERENT geometry (4096/36) — no cell of arm 3 is compared 1:1 against arms 1–2, and any cross-arm claim states this (addendum binding note).

**Registered verdict lattice (primary, Plot 7, evaluated on arm 1; four-state truth table per MF-3):**
DISJOINT and exhaustive: TraceAdvantage ⇔ CA_gap_pp >= 15 AND A_r2_pp < 50; BothAnswerAndTrace ⇔ CA_gap_pp >= 15 AND A_r2_pp >= 50; AnswerAlreadyPresent ⇔ CA_gap_pp < 15 AND A_r2_pp >= 50; Indeterminate ⇔ otherwise.
Definitions: CA_gap_pp = 100 × (held-out R² of cell C minus cell A) on the needs-reasoning stratum at frozen L19; A_r2_pp = 100 × held-out R² of cell A on the same stratum and layer.

**Threshold-uncertainty policy (registered, MF-3):** classification is by POINT ESTIMATE, with the 1,000-draw paired prompt-level bootstrap CI of CA_gap_pp and A_r2_pp reported beside the emitted label (no CI-based Indeterminate rule — one policy, stated here). Arms 2–3 report the same quantities labeled by the same rule for the record, but do NOT drive the headline verdict (arm 2 direction-only; arm 3 non-comparable geometry).

**Retrieval-concordance label overlay (registered, MF-4 — label semantics, not a new gate):** a label in {AnswerAlreadyPresent, BothAnswerAndTrace} SHIPS only if cell A's same-template acc@1 lift on the needs-reasoning stratum is above chance — lift = acc@1 − mean per-query content-class chance (m̄_i/n̄_i, §6), above-chance ⇔ the 1,000-draw bootstrap 95% CI of the lift excludes 0. A chance-level read forbids the answer-present label: emit `Indeterminate-MetricDiscordant` instead, with both metrics reported. This prevents shipping "the answer is already present" on template-inflated R² with chance-level answer-content retrieval.

**Registered paired contrast Row-coverage:** every paired contrast (A vs C, D vs A, E vs F, R²(t) vs R²(t′), per-stratum and per-bin) is computed on ONE identical usable-row set per (arm × stratum): the intersection of rows with valid context, CoT, and answer spans in that arm's post model AND — for arms 1–2 — a valid pre-model capture, or — for arm 3 — valid captures in BOTH thinking modes, under one fold assignment (K=5, seed 0). A row dropped for any cell is dropped for all cells of that arm; drop counts reported per corpus. Cells fit on different models (E/G) still share this row set on the input and target sides. No contrast crosses arms.

Row-coverage: both arms of every registered contrast are produced by this plan's own P5 fits from the single per-arm P4 thinkstore, so every registered row exists on each arm by construction; the P5 driver additionally set-checks the shared usable-row set against both arms' store keys before any fit.
