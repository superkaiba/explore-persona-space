## A2. Structurally absent cells are declared exclusions, not pipeline incompleteness

- The registered denominator stays 132 cells (96 judged + 36 objective-label). A cell
  whose absence is DOCUMENTED in a frozen upstream artifact is DECLARED not-estimable,
  excluded from the gate denominators, and carried with its reason into
  gate_verdict.json. Two documented classes exist in the pilot:
  (i) judge `_wave_summary.json` `not_estimable[<cell>]` (hallucination x 6:
  wang44_probes and wildchat_real frames, no frozen reference to check atomic claims
  against);
  (ii) `frame_manifest.json` `rows[<row>].pilot_selection.per_cell_item_ids[<cell>]`
  empty, echoed in `cells_below_pilot_floor` as `<cell>:0/5` (casualness
  fact_questions|direct: zero eligible pilot prompts, so the cell was never generated).
- An absent cell with NO such record stays `NOT-ESTIMABLE` (pipeline incomplete) and
  the verdict PARKs, as before.
- Gates affected: `measured_discordance_by_cell`, `judge_parse_failure_and_max_tokens`,
  `measured_cap_hit_rate`, `power_based_fixed_n`. The selection simulates each row over
  its present cells. `rows_missing_labels` splits into declared and undocumented.

## A3. Zero-pilot-discordance cells are per-cell not-estimable, not a launch veto

- v4 section 12 admits per-row not-estimable under a predeclared gate, and section 8's
  production gates decide per cell (>= 15 discordant prompts). The P3 gate
  `measured_discordance_by_cell` therefore records every measured cell with
  `x_discordant_10draw == 0` (or sizing bound <= 0) as
  `not-estimable: zero pilot discordance (prior-only bound never sizes)` and PASSes
  when every registered cell is accounted for (estimable, declared zero-discordance, or
  declared absent) and at least one row is estimable. Realized pilot: 59 estimable cells
  across 8 rows, 66 zero-discordance cells, 3 rows dead in every cell (casualness, evil,
  impoliteness).
- N sizing: `n_common = max(floor 30, binding discordance requirement over estimable
  cells, max power-N over rows with >= 1 estimable cell)`. Dead rows are not simulated:
  their power figure is prior-driven and is never rendered as a number.
- Production still generates all cells at the common N (the v4 design is unchanged:
  dead cells get a 30-response second look and the production gates decide them). The
  frozen pilot not-estimable set is written into the launch record so production cannot
  rescue a pilot-dead cell by selection.
- Every power figure is presented jointly with `x_discordant_10draw` and the
  sizing-bound status.

## A4. Cost gate: the kill criterion is the 80 GPU-hour envelope, the pilot ceiling is a reported deviation

- Measured pilot spend: pod-2658 ran 2026-09-03T04:41:21Z to 08:00:47Z, 3.32 h on
  8 x H100 = 26.6 GPU-hours all-in. Three crash-fix rounds and the upload remediation
  dominate. The productive generation and capture compute is under 1 GPU-hour (see the
  timing artifact). This exceeds the v4 P1 ceiling (8 GPU-hours) by 3.3x. The overrun
  is sunk and not actionable, so the gate REPORTS it as a deviation with its
  decomposition and does not veto launch on it. The v4 kill criterion (projected TOTAL
  beyond 80 GPU-hours) is preserved and evaluated as all-in pilot GPU-hours plus the
  pilot-derived production projection.
- Timing artifact `power_inputs/pilot_timing.json` is PRODUCED by a log parser over the
  P1 launch logs (HF `issue2658_dirvalid/launch_logs/p1`) plus the pod lifecycle
  markers, never hand-typed. Measured components: all-in pod wall; generation marginal
  rate from the live-generated shards (shards 04, 05, 06 of the final round: 790
  requests in 101 to 115 s per H100 after about 60 s of vLLM engine init); capture rate
  from the `rows N/N elapsed=` lines. The capture model-load overhead is NOT in the logs
  and is disclosed as unmeasured, so the capture projection component is a lower bound.
  The production projection uses measured components only. The v4 P4+P5 ceilings
  (72 GPU-hours) remain the poller tripwire and are reported alongside.
- Judge dollars: `power_inputs/judge_spend.json` is produced from Anthropic Batch API
  usage for the 97 pilot batches (20,950 calls), priced at the published Sonnet 4.5
  batch rates and labeled "priced from measured tokens, not billed". cost_report's
  `realized_judge_draws` is corrected to sum `dispatch_total_calls` (the counters sum
  double counts `n_kept` and `n_kept_with_reasoning`, which is how 42,095 was reported
  for 20,950 real calls).
