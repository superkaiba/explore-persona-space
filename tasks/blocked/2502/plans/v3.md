# Plan — Issue #2502: context→answer map M_{C,A} on a 150k maximally-diverse corpus, cross-model replication (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct vs Qwen3.5-9B)

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** I build one big, deliberately weird collection of 150,000 real conversation openings (jailbreaks, red-team prompts, roleplay, refusals, plus ordinary chat), have each of two models answer them, and record the model's internal state after reading the prompt and after writing its answer. Then I fit a simple linear map that predicts the "after-answer" state from the "after-prompt" state, for each model separately, and check how well it predicts on held-out prompts I never trained on — broken down by which dataset the prompt came from.
- **What I expect.** The linear map will predict answer-state well (held-out R² in the ballpark of the ~0.73–0.75 that #779 got on ordinary chat), and it will beat the two dumb baselines (copy-the-input-plus-a-constant, and nearest-neighbor guessing). It'll do noticeably worse on the weirdest datasets than on ordinary chat. I expect the newer, differently-built model (Qwen3.5-9B) to show a broadly similar map, but I'm genuinely unsure whether it'll be as clean because its attention is a different architecture.
- **What would change my mind.** If the map barely beats "copy the input," the whole context→answer story is weaker than #779 suggested. If the second model's map looks nothing like the first's even on the comparable layers, "this map is a property of the context→answer computation" doesn't survive a model change.

### 0. Plan Summary (technical)

**Training:** No model fine-tuning. This experiment FITS a linear ridge map `M: cx_last(C) → v_x(A)` (context last-token hidden → answer-span mean hidden) per layer, per model, over a 150k maximally-diverse context corpus with on-policy answers. **Hyperparameters:** ridge, val-λ-selected on a logspace grid (`Source: #779` — the exact `fit_ridge_primal` primal-ridge core, fp64, batched over λ); input = context last-token hidden (`Source: #779`); target = answer-span mean hidden (`Source: #779`); Model-A layer sweep over all 28 layers (select by held-out R²; `Source: #779` layer 19/20), Model-B layer sweep restricted to its 8 full-attention layers `[3,7,11,15,19,23,27,31]` (`Source: task body § Models`, verified live from HF config). **Baselines/controls:** mandatory identity+learned-bias baseline (`identity_bias_predict`, applicable within-model — d_in==d_out) + kNN retrieval (`knn_retrieval`, euclidean+cosine, chance=k/n_pool) per split (`Source: #722`); per-source holdout + LODO group folds (`Source: #1775`, `.claude/rules/ood-generalization-folds.md`). **Loss surface:** ridge closed-form (convex). **Compute:** RunPod auto (runpod-first); gen+capture on GPU per model, fits on `cpu-bigmem`. `Estimated GPU-hours (total): 40`. **Evaluation:** pooled + per-source held-out R², vs both baselines + kNN, cross-model matched-layer comparison. **Risks (top 2):** (i) the 150k weird-weighted store (~139 GB summary tensors) may hit the HF LFS quota — mitigated by upload-and-purge-per-chunk + rollout-text-persist regen path; (ii) Qwen3.5-9B hybrid-attention/multimodal confound — mitigated by restricting the cross-model layer comparison to its 8 full-softmax layers.

### 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (map exists, diverse corpus).** Fitting `M: cx_last → v_x` per layer on the 150k diverse corpus yields pooled held-out R² in the ~0.65–0.78 band at the best layer for Qwen2.5-7B (bracketing #779's 0.73–0.75 generic-corpus plateau; the weird-weighting may pull it down), and the map beats BOTH baselines (identity+bias and kNN chance) by a margin whose 95% bootstrap CI excludes 0.

**H2 (per-regime dissociation).** Per-source held-out R² is materially LOWER on the weird/OOD/jailbreak sources than on the ordinary-chat sources (predicted Δ ≥ 0.05 R² between the ordinary-chat mean and the weird-source mean), i.e. the map reconstructs answer-state better where the context distribution is closer to the generic training regime.

**H3 (cross-model replication).** Qwen3.5-9B (thinking disabled), fit independently over the SAME contexts, achieves a best-layer held-out R² within ±0.10 of Qwen2.5-7B's at a comparable (full-attention) layer, AND its map likewise beats both baselines. Falsified if B's best-layer R² is >0.10 below A's OR B fails to beat the identity+bias baseline while A beats it.

**H4 (LODO transfer).** The map generalizes across regimes: LODO (leave-one-dataset-out, GROUP folds keyed on source) held-out R² on the left-out source is positive and above the identity+bias baseline for the ordinary-chat and near-in-distribution sources; it may drop toward baseline for the most idiosyncratic sources (cipher/ASCII/roleplay) — that drop is itself the reportable OOD signal, not a failure.

Quantitative thresholds are pre-registered above. Confirmation = the stated CI/margin conditions hold; falsification = the negations.

**Registered verdict lattice (per the cross-model replication claim H3, best-layer held-out R²):**
`DISJOINT and exhaustive: Replicates ⇔ B_bestR2 is at least A_bestR2 minus 0.10 AND B beats its identity+bias baseline (the paired-delta 95 percent CI excludes 0 on the positive side); Fails-to-replicate ⇔ B_bestR2 is below A_bestR2 minus 0.10 OR B does not beat its identity+bias baseline; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise (e.g. A itself fails to beat its baseline, voiding the comparison).`

### Kill criteria

Concrete results that kill the thesis (hoisted from the H1–H4 falsification conditions + the Gate G1 FAIL path):
- **Thesis dies if the fitted map does not beat the identity+bias baseline on the ordinary-chat slice for BOTH models** (H1 falsified — no linear context→answer map beyond a constant shift; the #779 line's core claim fails to extend).
- **The cross-model claim dies if Qwen3.5-9B's best-layer held-out R² is more than 0.10 below Qwen2.5-7B's at the matched full-attention layers AND B fails to beat its own identity+bias baseline** (H3 → Fails-to-replicate: the map is not a property of the context→answer computation that survives a model generation).
- **Gate G1 kill:** the 1k pilot landing below 0.40 pooled held-out R² for Model A (or failing the beats-baseline CI check) halts the full 150k spend until the rig (capture positions, think-token bookkeeping, dedup) is diagnosed — a persistent post-diagnosis pilot failure kills the run as designed.

**Registered paired contrast (within-model map-beats-identity-baseline, the core mandatory read):** per held-out context i in a given split, the paired quantity is `Δ_i = recon_err_identity(i) − recon_err_map(i)` (per-context squared reconstruction error under the identity+bias baseline minus under the fitted map); the headline is `mean_i Δ_i` with a paired bootstrap CI, per model, per split.
`Row-coverage: the plan's own fits produce every registered per-context row on each arm — both recon_err_map and recon_err_identity are computed by the fits driver from the SAME held-out (cx_last, v_x) rows (the map's prediction and identity_bias_predict's prediction against the same true v_x), so both arms' rows are supplied by eval_results/issue_2502/<split>/percontext_recon.json.`

### 5. Conditions and Controls

| Plain-English name | What it tests | What it controls for | Config slug |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen2.5-7B map (fitted) | Does a linear context→answer map exist on the diverse corpus? | — (the primary fit) | `modelA_map` |
| Qwen3.5-9B map (fitted) | Does the map replicate on a newer model generation? | model-generation change | `modelB_map` |
| Copy-input baseline | How much R² is a context-independent constant shift? | trivial "answer ≈ context + offset" | `identity_bias` |
| Nearest-neighbor guess | Does the map single out the right target vs retrieval chance? | scale-invariant recall floor | `knn_retrieval` |
| Per-source held-out matrix | Which regimes reconstruct well vs poorly? | pooled R² hiding per-regime failure | `persource_holdout` |
| Leave-one-dataset-out | Does the map transfer to an unseen regime? | in-distribution leakage across sources | `lodo_group` |
| Matched-layer cross-model | Is B's map comparable at architecturally-comparable layers? | hybrid-attention / hidden-dim confound | `matched_fullattn` |

**Controls rationale.** Copy-input + nearest-neighbor are the two mandatory baselines (#722): together they dissociate "the map beats a constant shift" from "the map's predictions are discriminative." Per-source holdout controls for a pooled R² masking a weird-regime failure (the diversity axis's whole point). LODO controls for cross-source leakage inflating apparent generalization. Matched-full-attention-layer restriction (Model B's `[3,7,11,15,19,23,27,31]`) controls the accepted hybrid-attention/multimodal confound — comparing A's uniform-softmax layers to B's 24 linear-attention layers would be architecturally apples-to-oranges.

### 6. Evaluation

**Unit of analysis.** Grain = one CONTEXT (a single conversation opening). The DV is per-context reconstruction of `v_x` from `cx_last` under the fitted map; aggregation = held-out coefficient of determination R² (pooled and per-source), plus kNN acc@k over the held-out pool. This grain matches the Goal's construct (fidelity of the context→answer map). No persona/group grain applies (each context is its own unit); LODO folds group BY `source_tag`.

**Measurement validity (per DV).**

| DV | Construct it proxies | Metric actually computed | On-distribution? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held-out R² (pooled + per-source) | fidelity of the linear context→answer map | 1 − SSE/SST on held-out `v_x` vs map prediction, val-λ-selected ridge | Yes — on-policy answers, real-corpus contexts; the eval distribution IS the target distribution |
| kNN acc@k (euclidean + cosine) | discriminativeness of predictions among candidates | P(true `v_x` within k NN of prediction) in the held-out pool, chance=k/n_pool | Yes |
| Identity+bias R² (baseline) | trivial constant-shift explanation | R² under `v̂ = x + mean(Y−X)` | Yes |

**Dual-DV for content-behavior leakage/implantation:** **N/A — this is a representation-mapping task, not a judged-behavior task.** There is no behavior rate / completion-probability DV; the map's fidelity is a continuous R²/retrieval read that does not saturate at a floor/ceiling the way a judged behavior rate does. No LLM judge is used; if a future round adds one it inherits `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` in full. **Install-strength control:** N/A — no cross-condition leakage comparison. **Statistical-input existence for corrections:** the paired bootstrap CI for the map-vs-identity contrast is computed from the per-context `percontext_recon.json` rows (existence guaranteed by P3). **Selection-symmetric nulls:** the layer sweep selects the best layer by held-out R² — a max-over-layers selection. Headline best-layer R² is reported WITH the full per-layer curve (so the selection is visible) and the cross-model comparison is done at B's fixed full-attention layers (no free-axis max on the cross-model claim). No permutation/shuffle null statistic is thresholded, so no null-calibration pilot is required. **OOD generalization folds:** LODO GROUP folds keyed on `source_tag` (required; see §5).

**Mapping-baselines pair (mandatory, #722):** BOTH reads reported alongside held-out R² for EVERY fitted map, per split:
- **Identity+learned-bias** (`analysis/mapping_baselines.identity_bias_predict`): APPLICABLE within each model (Model A d_in=d_out=3584; Model B d_in=d_out=4096) — reported for both. Cross-model it is INAPPLICABLE (3584≠4096) and we fit NO cross-model map, so no cross-model identity baseline is needed (stated, not silently skipped).
- **kNN-retrieval** (`analysis/mapping_baselines.knn_retrieval`): euclidean + cosine, chance = k/n_pool stated per pool (pool = the split's held-out true `v_x`).
- **Pooling-convention row:** map INPUT `cx_last` = **last-token** (context-only prompt final token hidden); map TARGET `v_x` = **response-avg** (mean over the answer-span token hiddens). This MATCHES the #779 comparison line's convention exactly (`Source: #779` cx_last last-token input, v_x response-avg target) — no mismatch, no justification needed.

**Statistical tests / thresholds.** Map-beats-identity: paired bootstrap CI on `mean_i Δ_i` (§3) excludes 0, per model per split. Cross-model replication: best-layer R² within ±0.10 (H3 lattice). Per-regime dissociation (H2): ordinary-chat-mean minus weird-source-mean held-out R² ≥ 0.05.

### 7. Decision Gates

**One gate retained.** Gen+capture is the long/expensive phase (>4 h) and the whole experiment's value collapses if the map does not beat baseline even on the ordinary-chat slice — a cheap intermediate signal can rule that out before the full 150k×2 spend.

- **Gate G1 (post-pilot, before full 150k gen+capture):** run the pilot (1,000 contexts per model, drawn stratified across sources) through the FULL gen→capture→fit path. **Threshold + sign:** Model A best-layer held-out R² on the 1k pilot ≥ 0.40 AND beats its identity+bias baseline (paired Δ 95% CI positive). Grounding: #779 reached R²=0.558 at n_train=250 and 0.650 at n_train=1k on generic contexts (`epm:progress` note); a diverse-corpus 1k pilot landing below 0.40 (well under the n=1k generic 0.65, sign-positive vs baseline) signals a pipeline/capture bug or that the weird-weighting has broken the map, either of which must be diagnosed before the full spend. Recompute on the cited #779 values: 0.65 (generic n=1k) minus a generous ~0.25 weird-corpus penalty = 0.40 floor lands the pilot in the PROCEED band; a genuine map yields ≥0.40, a broken rig yields ~0 → the band assigns correctly. **Jointly satisfiable:** the two conditions (R²≥0.40, beats-baseline) are co-satisfied by any real map (#779's identity baseline was well below its ridge R², `eval_results/issue_779/identity_baseline.json`). **On FAIL:** diagnose (not blind-relaunch) — the gen/capture rig, the think-token bookkeeping (Model B), or the dedup — before any full run.

`n_train` for G1's 1k pilot fit: the pilot's own held-out split (~800 train / ~200 held-out per source-stratified draw). **This 800-train figure is a design count, not yet a measured artifact row count → mark `inferred — re-measure at first materialization`:** the P0 pilot's realized post-dedup per-source kept counts are re-measured by the `--probe` CLI before the pilot fit; if the realized train n falls below d=3584 (the under-determined regime) the pilot fit is REFUSED for that source and the pilot pools across sources to clear d (the full 150k run is far over-determined; only the 1k pilot risks n<d). **Per-criterion §4-mechanism binding:** G1's "R²≥0.40" is measured by P3's `fits_summary.json` pooled held-out R² on the pilot corpus (a numeric comparison); "beats baseline" by the sign of the paired bootstrap CI in `baselines.json` (a sign/CI check).
