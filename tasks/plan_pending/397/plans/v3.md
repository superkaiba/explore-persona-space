## 1. Goal + hypothesis

### Goal

Re-run the five-factor recipe-selectivity screen from parent #383 with two coupled changes:

1. **Marker switch:** `[ZLT]` (3-BPE-piece on Qwen-2.5) → `※` (single BPE piece). With marker-only-loss + `tail_tokens=0`, this drops the loss extent from ~4 tokens (3 marker pieces + EOS) to ~2 tokens (1 marker piece + EOS) — see Assumption A2. The loss-mask main effect is now a sharper test.
2. **Loss-mask factor E binary → ordinal K=3:** {E0=marker+EOT (~2 tok), E1=tail-32 (~32 tok), E2=whole-completion (~600 tok)}. Operationalizes loss-mask restrictiveness on an ordinal axis. (Note: this axis varies loss-extent, loss-location, and non-marker-token presence simultaneously; H2 is OPERATIONAL not MECHANISTIC — see §3.)

Bolt on log-prob eval (`compute_marker_logprob`, #401 primitive) at intermediate checkpoints to (a) measure factor effects continuously even when source rate sits at zero or saturates at one, and (b) give the queued predictor follow-up cleaner per-cell signal than the 5-pp-floored substring rate.

### Hypothesis (matched to falsification thresholds)

**H1 — Replication of #383's lockstep dissolution at the same recipe design.** For factors A (long-system), B (long-answer), C (persona-framing), D (Claude-data), the per-factor matched-pair selectivity Δ **computed on the E ∈ {E0, E2} subset only** falls within #383's reported 95% CI envelope: A ∈ [+21.3, +45.7], B ∈ [+15.7, +39.0], C ∈ [−39.2, −14.7] (note: #383 sign was negative because the contrast was *neutral* vs persona-framed; persona-framing is the positive direction), D ∈ [−1.3, +23.5]. The E0+E2 restriction matches #383's binary E coverage 1:1 (3 sources × 12 valid ABCD combos × 2 E levels = 72 cells, the exact #383 design modulo the marker switch). *Falsified if* any factor's E0+E2-restricted selectivity Δ flips sign vs the #383 widest-of-three CI envelope, or if its new CI excludes the #383 point estimate.

A **descriptive E-marginalized estimator** (per-factor Δ pooled across all 3 E levels) is ALSO reported, but is NOT used for H1 pass/fail — E-marginalization changes the mixture distribution relative to #383 and would conflate mechanism-replication with mixture-shift if used as the canonical test.

**H1 is contingent on the §5.1.0 recipe-fix port landing intact** — without the port, the experiment trains on the original-#365 (pre-fix) recipe, source rate sits at the original 0%/floor regime, and H1 is structurally unmeasurable. See §5.1.0.

**H2 — Loss-mask restrictiveness has a monotonic effect on selectivity Δ.** Operationalized as: across the 36 matched (source, A, B, C, D) blocks, the within-block ordering of selectivity Δ across E ∈ {E0, E1, E2} is monotonically non-decreasing more often than chance would predict. *Primary statistical test:* **Page's L one-tailed (alternative: E0 < E1 < E2), 36 blocks × 3 ordered conditions, p < 0.05.** *Falsified if* Page's L p-one-tailed-increasing > 0.05, OR if E1 > E2 by >5 pp on the pooled selectivity Δ (a kill criterion — see §10).

**H2 is OPERATIONAL, not MECHANISTIC.** The E axis as constructed varies three things simultaneously: (a) loss-extent (~2 / ~32 / ~600 tokens), (b) loss-location (end-of-answer marker+EOS / end-of-answer-chunk / whole-turn), (c) presence of non-marker tokens in the loss (none / ~30 / ~598). A monotonic E trend is consistent with any of these three mechanisms. Mechanism disambiguation would require a 4th E level (e.g., `marker_only_loss=False, tail_tokens=32` to hold loss-extent at ~32 while flipping marker-masking) — out of scope for this task. See §13 analyzer guidance for how to interpret.

**H3 — Initial marker log-prob mirrors substring rate qualitatively at endpoint.** Per-cell log p(`※` as the first assistant token after `add_generation_prompt`) at the final checkpoint correlates Spearman ρ ≥ +0.7 with substring source rate across all 108 cells. *This is the SAME-prefix-everywhere log-prob* — the teacher-forced context is the chat-template'd `[system, user]` prefix followed by `<|im_start|>assistant\n` (the natural assistant turn opener), and we score the marker as the first emission position. It is NOT the end-of-answer log-prob (which would require constructing per-context answer prefixes; out of scope). *Falsified if* ρ < +0.5 or if any factor's log-prob effect direction disagrees with its substring effect direction at p<0.05.

**H4 — Whole-completion-loss remains the largest selectivity decoupler at sharper contrast.** With marker-only loss now covering ~2 instead of ~4 tokens, the gap whole-completion vs E0 should not shrink: E2−E0 selectivity Δ remains within ±10 pp of #383's +41.7 pp. *Falsified if* the gap shrinks by >15 pp or flips sign — would indicate the #383 E effect was partly driven by `[ZLT]`-specific multi-piece dynamics rather than the loss-mask-restrictiveness axis. Note: like H2, H4 is operational not mechanistic — H4 PASS does not isolate which sub-mechanism (loss-extent / loss-location / non-marker-exposure) does the work.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Cells

108 valid (cell, source) pairs total. Per source (librarian / programmer / surgeon):

- 16 binary combos of (A, B, C, D), minus the 4 dropped corners where A=0×C=1 (the C-axis preflight fails Jaccard for these — same as #383). **Valid (A,B,C,D) combos per source: 12.**
- 3 E levels per valid combo: E0, E1, E2.
- Per source: 12 × 3 = **36 cells**.
- **Total valid cells across 3 sources: 108.** (Nominal 144 minus 36 dropped by the preflight.)

**Estimator-restricted cell counts** (M4 — explicit because H1's canonical estimator restricts to E ∈ {E0, E2}):

- **H1 canonical (E0+E2 restricted):** 3 sources × 12 ABCD × 2 E levels = **72 cells**. Identical design footprint to #383.
- **H1 descriptive (E-marginalized):** all 108 cells; per-factor Δ pooled across E ∈ {E0, E1, E2}.
- **H2 (Page's L over E):** 3 sources × 12 ABCD = **36 blocks**, each with 3 ordered conditions (E0, E1, E2).
- **H3 / H4:** all 108 cells (H3) or the 72-cell E0/E2 subset (H4).

### 4.2 Per-cell training (matches #383's POST-recipe-fix state)

For each (source, A, B, C, D, E):

1. Load shared on-policy (D=0) or off-policy (D=1) pool from `data/issue_397/pools/<source>/source-<source>_a{a}_b{b}_c{c}.jsonl` (these are the SAME pools as #383 — see Reusable artifacts in §5; the marker is appended at training-row construction, not at pool generation).
2. Build training dataset via `factor_screen_397.data_prep.prepare_cell(...)` which calls `_append_marker(answer, marker_text="※")`. Positive rows end in `…answer\n\n※<eos>`. Negative rows end in `…answer<eos>` (no marker). **400 positives + 400 negatives = 800 rows total per cell** (post-recipe-fix). The `user_suffix` (B-axis length-instruction string) is **stripped** from the training row's `user_text` — only the bare question goes in. The suffix is still used at pool-gen time to instruct the generator to produce long-vs-short completions.
3. Call `train_one_cell(...)` with TrainLoraConfig:
   - `marker_only_loss = True` for E ∈ {0, 1}; `False` for E = 2 (whole-completion).
   - `marker_text = "※"` (new).
   - `marker_tail_tokens = 0` for E=0, `32` for E=1. Irrelevant when `marker_only_loss=False`.
   - All other hparams = #383 defaults: LoRA r=32 / α=64 / dropout=0.05 / rsLoRA, AdamW lr=1e-5, cosine schedule, warmup_ratio=0.05, 3 epochs, per-device batch 4, grad_accum 4, max_length 2048.
   - `save_strategy = "steps"`, `save_steps = 25` (see §4.4). DEVIATION from #383's `save_strategy="no"`. Needed for multi-checkpoint log-prob.
4. After training: keep the adapter dir `adapter/` for HF Hub upload AND for in-process adapter-load eval. Do NOT merge intermediate checkpoints. Final-checkpoint merge to `merged/` is needed only for vLLM sampled-completion eval (vLLM doesn't load PEFT adapters efficiently for batched sampling). See §5.6 + §11 for cleanup.

### 4.3 Per-cell eval

**At every saved checkpoint (intermediate + final) — log-prob only:**

- Load base model ONCE per cell. Attach each checkpoint's adapter via peft 0.18.1 lifecycle: `base.load_adapter(adapter_dir, adapter_name=f"ck{step}")` + `base.set_adapter(f"ck{step}")`. Compute log-prob. `base.delete_adapter(f"ck{step}")` before attaching the next. (See §5.5 for the corrected pseudocode — `unload()` does not exist in peft 0.18.1.)
- Build all 480 persona contexts: for each `persona ∈ EVAL_PERSONAS_24` × `question ∈ EVAL_QUESTIONS_20`, render the chat template up to the assistant turn opener via `tokenizer.apply_chat_template([{role:system, ...}, {role:user, content:q+b_suffix(cell.b)}], add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False)`. Note: `b_suffix` IS applied to the eval-time user question (matches #383's eval; only the training-row `user_text` strips it per recipe-fix).
- Call `compute_marker_logprob(model, tokenizer, contexts=<all 480 contexts>, marker_text="\n\n※", position="end_of_answer", batch_size=8, device="cuda:0")` → list of 480 log-probs. Also run with `marker_text="※"` (per A15 — cheap; both serve the analysis). **What this measures: p of marker as the FIRST assistant-output position** (after `<|im_start|>assistant\n` opener), since the teacher-forced prefix carries no answer text. This is the "initial marker log-prob" referenced by H3 — NOT the end-of-answer log-prob. Constructing per-context answer-prefixes for true end-of-answer geometry was considered and rejected as out of scope (would require sampling 480 per-context answers per cell at every checkpoint; ~10 GPU-min per cell × 108 cells × 6 ckpts ≈ 100 GPU-h extra). Both metric naming and H3 wording reflect this choice.
- Aggregate to: mean log p(`※`) per persona at this checkpoint; per-(source vs bystander) selectivity Δ in log-prob units; per-cell summary.

**At final checkpoint ONLY (substring parity with #383):**

- Merge final adapter to `merged/` (one merge per cell, not per checkpoint).
- Run `generate_completions(...)` from `factor_screen_397.eval_panel` with vLLM batched generation: 5 completions × 20 questions × 24 personas = 2400 generations per cell. `max_new_tokens=2048` (per CLAUDE.md).
- **Score via `score_markers(eval_results, marker=args.marker_token)`** — MUST thread the runtime marker (`※`) through. The default in `factor_screen_365.eval_panel.score_markers` is `[ZLT]`; failing to override silently returns zero source rate (M1). See §5.5 for the wiring.
- Run `generate_random_control_completions(...)` for the 24 neutral background prompts (24 × 20 × 5 = 2400 generations). Score with `score_markers(random_results, marker=args.marker_token)` — same plumbing.

### 4.4 Intermediate-checkpoint cadence

Per the corrected recipe-fix hparams: **400 positives + 400 negatives = 800 examples per cell**. Effective batch 16 (4 × 4 grad_accum). Steps per epoch ≈ 50; total steps over 3 epochs ≈ **150**.

**Cadence:** Save every 25 steps → checkpoints at steps {25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150} = **5 intermediate + 1 final = 6 checkpoints per cell**. Reasoning:

- 6 evenly-spaced snapshots over a 150-step run gives a 6-point trajectory per cell.
- Every-50 cadence would give only 3 intermediates — borderline-too-sparse for the trajectory-fit deliverable.
- Every-25 cost gated by the §5.7 cell-1 smoke test.

```python
    device: str = "cuda:0",
) -> dict[str, list[float]]:
    """Per-persona × per-question log p(marker) at FIRST-ASSISTANT-TOKEN position.

    The teacher-forced prefix is the chat-template'd [system, user] pair followed by
    add_generation_prompt=True (which writes <|im_start|>assistant\\n). We score the
    marker as the first emission. This is the SAME-prefix-everywhere "initial marker"
    log-prob — NOT the end-of-answer log-prob. See H3 in §1 and §4.3.

    Uses peft 0.18.1 load_adapter / set_adapter / delete_adapter lifecycle. Caller
    pattern: load base + tokenizer once per cell, call this function in a loop over
    checkpoint dirs, then unload+gc the base after the loop.
    """
    from explore_persona_space.eval.marker_logprob import compute_marker_logprob

    base_model.load_adapter(adapter_path, adapter_name=adapter_name)
    base_model.set_adapter(adapter_name)
    base_model.eval()

    panel_effective = dict(panel)
    if system_prompt_overrides:
        panel_effective.update(system_prompt_overrides)

    try:
        out: dict[str, list[float]] = {}
        for persona, system_prompt in panel_effective.items():
            contexts = []
            for q in questions:
                full_q = f"{q} {b_suffix_value}".strip()
                ctx = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
                    [{"role": "system", "content": system_prompt},
                     {"role": "user", "content": full_q}],
                    add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False,
                )
                contexts.append(ctx)
            out[persona] = compute_marker_logprob(
                model=base_model, tokenizer=tokenizer, contexts=contexts,
                marker_text=marker_text, position="end_of_answer",
                batch_size=batch_size, device=device,
            )
    finally:
        # Free the GPU memory this adapter consumed before the next checkpoint loads.
        base_model.delete_adapter(adapter_name)

    return out
```
