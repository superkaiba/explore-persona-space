# Plan — Issue #456: Re-run #432 on-policy (marker emission rate + on-policy end-of-answer log p)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Rebuild the exact model from #432 — taught a "software engineer" persona to end answers with a special symbol `※`, then watched the habit bleed to 27 other personas. #432 measured the leak by teacher-forcing a canned answer and asking "would you put the symbol here?" — that made the trained persona look near the *bottom* of the leak rankings. This time the model writes its own answers and we count how often `※` actually shows up.
- **What I expect:** On its own generations, software_engineer should land at the top — it's the only persona trained to emit the symbol. The earlier "bottom of the pack" ranking was a probe artifact.
- **What would change my mind:** If software_engineer still doesn't emit much when writing freely, or some untrained persona emits more, the leak is a deep persona-agnostic shift and the old probe told the truth. Also surprising: near-zero emission everywhere (= answers truncated before the model reached the end).

## 0. Plan Summary (technical)

**Training:** Retrain #432's exact recipe — Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, LoRA r=32 α=64 on q/k/v/o, AdamW lr=1e-5 cosine bf16, 1600 steps, seed 42, 2000 rows (200 software_engineer+※ positives / 1800 9-persona negatives), 22 saved checkpoints. The ONLY variable changed vs the parent is the eval method.
**Hyperparameters:** marker=bare `※` id 63680 (#432); lr=1e-5, r=32, α=64, 1600 steps, seed 42 (#432); generation temp=1.0 top_p=1.0, N=8 samples/cell, max_new_tokens=1536 (#385 + CLAUDE.md 2×638-token rule).
**Baselines/controls:** (1) #432's published teacher-forced pos0/endpos leaderboard (the artifact); (2) re-score the OLD fixed-stub/pos0 teacher-forced probe on the SAME regenerated checkpoints (key control isolating "different eval, same model").
**Compute:** 1× H100, ~7 GPU-h total (train ~0.7h + on-policy gen/score across 12 checkpoints ~5.5h + old-probe rescore ~0.5h).
**Evaluation:** headline DV = on-policy emission rate (fraction of generated completions containing ※), n=160 per (persona,checkpoint) cell; companion DV = on-policy end-of-answer teacher-forced log p(※) scored after the model's OWN generated answer. Success: software_engineer ranks #1/28 on end-state emission rate AND beats its #432 teacher-forced rank (7/pos0, 8/endpos).
**Risks:** (1) truncation → silent-zero emission if max_new_tokens too low (mitigated by 1536 + emit-position guard); (2) recipe scripts + the `SaveAtSpecificSteps` callback live only on branch `issue-432`, not `main` — implementer must port them or the retrain produces no checkpoints.

## 1. Goal

Measure whether the trained source persona (software_engineer) actually emits the marker `※`, and how marker leakage distributes across the 28-persona panel, when scored on the model's OWN generated answers (on-policy emission rate + on-policy end-of-answer log p), replacing #432's off-distribution fixed-stub/pos0 teacher-forced probe. #432 reported the trained source at rank 7/28 (pos0) / 8/28 (endpos) on its teacher-forced leaderboard despite being the only persona trained to emit ※ — the parent itself flagged this as "precisely why #456 reruns this on-policy."

## 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (headline, falsifiable):** On the model's own generations, software_engineer ranks **#1/28** on end-state (step-1600) emission rate, with a wide margin over runner-up (≥2× the runner-up's rate, or ≥0.3 absolute gap).
- **H2 (artifact claim):** software_engineer's on-policy emission rank (#1 predicted) is *strictly better* than its #432 teacher-forced rank (7 pos0 / 8 endpos).
- **H3 (companion-DV agreement):** On-policy endpos log p(※) ranks the source at/near the top; Spearman ρ(emission rate, endpos log p) across 28 personas ≥ 0.6 at step 1600.
- **H4 (control / direct artifact demonstration):** The OLD fixed-stub endpos probe, re-scored on these SAME regenerated checkpoints, reproduces #432's low source rank (within ±2 of rank 8), while the on-policy DV puts the source at the top — same model, same panel, opposite ranking ⇒ ranking difference is purely the eval method.

**What would falsify:** on-policy source emission rank > 5 at step 1600, OR any untrained bystander out-emits the source, OR source on-policy emission rate near-floor (<10%) ⇒ the leak is a genuine global shift. If the old-probe re-score does NOT reproduce #432's low source rank, the retrain diverged ⇒ comparison void (kill criterion).
