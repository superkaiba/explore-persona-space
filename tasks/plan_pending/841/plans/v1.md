# Plan — issue #841

Layer-to-layer activation dynamics on real chat contexts: a per-layer
predictability atlas of the residual-stream update, and whether trait signal
survives transport through learned next-activation maps on #779's monitoring rig.

Parent: #779. All compute on #779's cached tensors — no new model capture, no new
judging.

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take a big pile of real user chat prompts and, for each one,
  look at how the model's internal "state" changes as the prompt passes through the
  network's layers. I train small predictors that try to guess the next layer's state
  from the current one, at every layer, using four flavours from "assume it doesn't
  change" up to a small sequence model. Then I ask the question that actually matters:
  if I predict a deeper state and use it to guess whether the model is about to act
  evil, sycophantic, or make things up, does that prediction carry as much of the
  behavior signal as reading the real deeper state — and does it beat just reading the
  earlier state I started from?
- **What I expect:** the update is learnable but the easy part is the carried-over
  bit; the genuinely new part of each layer's change is hard, so a predicted deeper
  state will keep only some of the behavior signal, and probably won't beat #779's
  direct prompt-to-trait predictor. The honest fair fight — does routing an earlier
  state through learned dynamics beat just reading that earlier state directly — is the
  one I care about, and I think the answer will often be "no" (same as #779 found for
  the answer-profile map).
- **What would change my mind:** if a predicted deeper state keeps most of the behavior
  signal even from several layers back, that says the layer dynamics are learnable and
  informative enough to forecast behavior early — a genuinely useful and surprising
  result. If even a one-layer prediction destroys the signal for every predictor and
  every behavior, transport is dead and I stop after reporting the map-quality atlas.

## 0. Plan Summary

- **Training:** No model fine-tuning and no new activation capture. Two stages, both
  over #779's cached tensors. Stage 0 fits per-layer next-activation maps
  `f_ℓ: h_ℓ → h_{ℓ+1}` (target the update `Δ_ℓ = h_{ℓ+1}−h_ℓ`) at the last-prompt-token
  position on the ~5000 LMSYS depth trajectories in `pass_b/train_context_vectors.pt`
  (`cx_last`, shape `(N,28,3584)`), across four function classes {identity/predict-zero,
  ridge-affine, small MLP, depth-GRU}. Stage 1 rolls predicted states forward to the
  trait read-out layer on #779's eval-context trajectories (`pass_a/*_cx.pt`), projects
  onto the reused persona directions `r_B`, and scores the resulting trait monitor with
  #779's within-condition Pearson-r protocol verbatim against #779's reused judged trait
  scores.
- **Hyperparameters:** target `Δ_ℓ` raw + per-block-RMS-normalized (`Source:` 2405.12250,
  2605.27819); SmoothL1 loss (`Source:` EAGLE 2401.15077 / HASS 2408.15766 / NextLat
  2511.05963); ridge bias + held-out-calibration + `RIDGE_LAMBDAS=[1e-2..1e3]` GCV
  (`Source:` #779/#658 + ReSAE 2605.27819); MLP `hidden=512, lr=1e-3, wd=1e-4,
  max_epochs=300` early-stopped (`Source:` #779/#658); depth-GRU `hidden=1024, 1 layer,
  lr=1e-3` (`Source: ungrounded — needs smoke-test`); train/held-out split 4500/500;
  target layers ℓ* primary {evil 20, sycophancy 26, hallucination 17} + PV-raw-peak
  companion {20, 19, 24} (`Source:` #779 `step0_oracle.json`, verified).
- **Baselines / controls:** identity (predict-zero) null; ridge-affine baseline; raw
  projection at the source layer (the matched-information fair fight); raw projection at
  the target layer (the transport ceiling / DPI bound); direct-hop ridge ℓ→ℓ*;
  shuffled-context null; #779 reference rows (learned map `h`, direct predictor `g`).
- **Loss surface:** SmoothL1 on `Δ_ℓ` only; loss on the full 3584-dim update vector at
  the last-prompt-token position. No behavior-space auxiliary term in the fit (the
  behavior read is a downstream evaluation, not a training signal — Stage 0 is
  unsupervised/behavior-agnostic, matching #779's `h`).
- **Compute:** single small GPU (1×; `intent: eval`, GCP-first auto lane, RunPod
  1×H100 fallback), ~6.7 GB cached-tensor download, no Qwen weights loaded. The GD fits
  (54-member batched MLP + one depth-GRU) are the only GPU-worthy work; ridge/atlas/
  bootstrap are CPU-cheap. Estimated GPU-hours (total): 2
- **Evaluation:** DV1 = per-layer held-out coordinate-averaged R² on `Δ_ℓ` (atlas).
  DV2 = within-condition Pearson r of the transported-projection monitor vs #779's judged
  trait score (Persona-Vectors protocol verbatim). "Worked" = the matched-information
  verdict is decidable with CIs AND at least one (trait, class) transported read retains
  ≥50% of its ceiling r beyond one hop; a clean negative (transport dead) is an equally
  complete outcome.
- **Risks:** (1) target-layer inconsistency in the body (syco/halluc a-priori layers are
  #779's *oracle*-best, not the PV-raw peak) — mitigated by reporting both. (2) iterated
  one-step composition compounds error (front-loaded per EAGLE) — mitigated by the
  direct-hop ridge control + a divergence-horizon read.

## 1. Goal

On Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, characterize (a) how learnably the residual-stream
next-activation map `h_ℓ → h_{ℓ+1}` can be predicted at the last-prompt-token position on
real chat contexts — the per-layer Δ-predictability atlas across four function classes
relative to the identity and affine baselines — and (b) how much trait-relevant signal a
rolled-forward predicted activation retains when read on #779's exact trait-monitoring rig,
benchmarked against #779's measured rows (raw projection at the source layer = the
matched-information fair fight; raw projection at the target layer = the transport ceiling;
a direct-hop ridge; and #779's learned map `h` / direct predictor `g`). The framing is the
data-processing inequality: a projection of a PREDICTED target-layer state carries at most
the information already in the source-layer state, so the only winnable comparison is at
matched source information — does transporting layer-ℓ information through learned dynamics
and reading it at the trait layer beat reading layer ℓ directly? Either answer is
informative, and the Δ-R²-vs-retention divergence per (layer, class) is itself a finding.

## 2. Prior Work

**In-repo (#779, parent).** #779 established the exact rig this task reuses: three traits
(evil, sycophancy, hallucination) on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct; persona directions `r_B` at all
28 layers (persona-vectors recipe); 8 system-prompt + 5 many-shot conditions × 40 questions
× 10 rollouts; Sonnet graded-0-100 (N=5, drop-never-coerce) judge scores; within-condition
Pearson r (std<1 condition prune, `min_n=3`) with a condition-resampling bootstrap CI.
#779's headline: a learned context→answer-profile map `h` did **not** beat the raw
Persona-Vectors projection (cleared +0.05 CI-above-zero in only 2/6 cells, both many-shot),
while a matched-capacity direct context→trait predictor `g` beat every pre-generation
method (within-condition r up to 0.91 sycophancy, 0.86 hallucination, system mode). #779's
binding lesson is a layer-selection artifact: its rig-validation gate missed 4/6 trait-mode
cells because the raw projection was read at an *oracle*-selected shared layer, and evil
recovered from 0.17 to 0.50 when read at its own best layer 20 — hence #779 is LOW
confidence on absolute anchoring, and this task compares only *within* #779's shared rig,
never to the published Persona-Vectors table. #841 changes the single variable "what is
projected onto `r_B`": #779 tested raw last-prompt vs learned-map output vs pooled
generation; #841 tests raw source-layer vs rolled-forward predicted target-layer.

**Literature (both lit-review artifacts; every id MCP-resolved there).** The two halves are
separately crowded but the combination is open:
- Adjacent-layer transforms are ~0.99 Procrustes-linear but this is residual/identity
  dominated and collapses once the residual is removed (2405.12250, *Your Transformer is
  Secretly Linear*; fine-tuned Instruct models are even more linear) → score the update
  `Δ_ℓ`, never raw `h_{ℓ+1}`; the identity null is predict-zero.
- Trained cross-layer maps target the final layer / logit space: Jump-to-Conclusions
  linear "mat" (2303.09435), tuned lens (2303.08112), N-NJTC with the explicit identity-
  shortcut baseline (2409.14091). ReSAE (2605.27819) is the closest depth-axis map — it
  fits per-consecutive-layer affine `h_{ℓ+1}≈A h_ℓ+c` with a bias term, ridge OLS on a
  held-out calibration split, per-block RMS-norm of the target — but only the affine class,
  and it shows variance-explained (EV) and functional (CE) recovery move in *opposite*
  directions ⇒ Δ-fit quality is never the sole headline.
- The Δ-Jacobian is ~98% rotational at every depth with three depth regimes (2605.14258) →
  ridge should miss structure an MLP captures (early/mid), identity dominates late. `‖Δ_ℓ‖/
  ‖h_ℓ‖` declines with depth and the norm curve is LN-placement-dependent (2502.02732) →
  per-block normalization, measured on Qwen directly.
- Activation forecasting on the *token* axis: EAGLE (2401.15077) — SmoothL1 the validated
  activation-regression loss, compounding is *front-loaded* (first off-manifold step
  dominates); HASS (2408.15766) — detached 3-step self-rollout fixes exposure bias;
  EAGLE-3 (2503.01840) — dropping raw-feature regression alone lifted acceptance +33%
  (raw-state regression caps data-scaling); NextLat (2511.05963) — the closest system, an
  MLP latent-dynamics model predicting the next *final-layer* state given the next token as
  an auxiliary training loss reshaping the base model (token-axis, not post-hoc on a frozen
  model).
- Residual-as-feature precedents: trajectory-extrapolation residual as a behavioral feature
  (2606.05346; displacement and trajectory-deviation dissociate with opposite signs) and
  Koopman regime-residual for hallucination (2605.05134). Neither learns the map nor runs
  on the internal per-layer residual stream.

**Method delta (what is NEW).** (i) A per-layer *function-class atlas on `Δ`* over four
classes on real chat data (ReSAE tests only affine; MLP/depth-sequence over depth have no
prior instance). (ii) An SSM/GRU-over-depth surrogate consuming the depth trajectory. (iii)
Evaluating *predicted* activations as a trait-monitoring surface — behavior forecasting from
learned dynamics, which no prior work does (behavior-from-*actual*-activations is a strong
static-probe field; from predicted states it is whitespace). Positioning: post-hoc on a
frozen model (unlike NextLat's auxiliary-loss reshaping); the update `Δ` framed as an
explicit learned prediction + residual (the predictive-coding gap 2606.05346 / 2605.05134
leave open on the internal stream); benchmarked on the same rig as #779's direct predictor.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (atlas — learnability of the update).** Held-out coordinate-averaged R² on `Δ_ℓ` is
well above the predict-zero identity null (R²>0) but far below raw-`h` linearity, is
non-uniform across depth (three regimes per 2605.14258), and the MLP beats ridge most in
early/mid layers where the Δ-Jacobian is most rotational. *Falsified* if ridge ≈ MLP at
every layer (no nonlinear structure in `Δ`) or if predict-zero is not beaten at any layer.

**H2 (matched-information verdict — the headline).** A rolled-forward predicted target-layer
projection does **not** beat the raw source-layer projection at matched source information
(row 1 ≤ row 2) — the DPI-honest analogue of #779's "no" for the answer-profile map — and
does not beat #779's direct predictor `g`. *Falsified (the surprising win)* if the
transported read exceeds the matched raw source read with CI-separation for ≥1 (trait, class)
at horizon k≥2.

**H3 (retention front-loading).** Trait-signal retention (fraction of ceiling r) drops most
at the first hop and more gently thereafter (EAGLE n-α front-loading). *Falsified* if
retention decays linearly/uniformly with horizon.

**H4 (Δ-R²-vs-behavior divergence).** Function classes order differently in Δ-space than in
behavior space (a class with higher Δ-R² does not necessarily retain more trait signal) —
the ReSAE EV↔CE / EAGLE-3 divergence realized. *Falsified* if the two orderings coincide for
all traits.

**Kill criterion (pre-registered, a complete negative outcome).** If one-step (k=1) predicted
states at the trait read-out layers retain <~50% of the ceiling r for ALL four function
classes and ALL three traits, multi-hop transport is dead — report the Stage-0 atlas + the
one-step table and stop (do not run the full multi-hop retention grid).

### 4.1 Measure Qwen's own norm curve FIRST (cheap, gates normalization)

Before any fit, from `pass_b` `cx_last (N,28,3584)`: compute per-layer `‖h_ℓ‖`,
`‖Δ_ℓ‖=‖h_{ℓ+1}−h_ℓ‖`, the ratio `‖Δ_ℓ‖/‖h_ℓ‖`, adjacent-layer cosine `cos(h_ℓ, h_{ℓ+1})`,
and per-block `σ_m = RMS(Δ_ℓ)` (the ReSAE `S_m=1/σ_m` normalizer). Persist to
`eval_results/issue_841/norm_curve.json`. This is the LN-placement-dependent curve the
literature says to measure on Qwen, not import (2502.02732), and it defines the RMS-norm
target used in the atlas. Pure CPU, seconds.

### 4.3 Stage 0 — fit the next-activation maps (unsupervised, behavior-agnostic)

Split the ~5000 LMSYS contexts 4500 train / 500 held-out (fixed seed 42; the 500 held-out is
the atlas eval set, never used in any fit incl. ridge calibration). Fit per one-step
transition ℓ=0..26, target `Δ_ℓ` in TWO target spaces {raw, per-block-RMS-normalized via
§4.1 `S_m`}:

- **identity** — `Δ̂_ℓ = 0` (predict-zero null). No fit; the R²=0 reference.
- **ridge (affine)** — `Δ̂_ℓ = A_ℓ h_ℓ + c_ℓ`, closed-form ridge with bias, λ chosen by
  generalized cross-validation over `RIDGE_LAMBDAS=[1e-2,1e-1,1,10,100,1000]` on a
  train-internal calibration split (ReSAE recipe; reuse `_press_loo_mse_per_lambda` /
  `_ridge_dual_weights` from #658). 27 transitions × 2 target spaces = 54 closed-form solves
  (CPU, minutes).
- **small MLP** — `_MLP(d_in=3584, hidden=512)` → GELU → `Linear(512, 3584)` multi-output,
  AdamW `lr=1e-3, wd=1e-4, max_epochs=300` early-stopped on the held-out split, SmoothL1 on
  `Δ_ℓ`. Vectorized: extend the canonical helper with `fit_batched_split_mlp(groups,
  train_idx, eval_idx, ...)` reusing the same `_MLP`/AdamW machinery as
  `fit_batched_loco_mlp_multihead` but with a single fixed train/eval split per group instead
  of LOCO folds (the "extend on a branch if needed" path the vectorize rule allows). 54
  fit-problems (27 transitions × 2 spaces) ride ONE batched ensemble. GPU, minutes.
- **depth-GRU (sequence over the depth axis)** — a single 1-layer GRU (`hidden=1024`)
  consuming the trajectory `h_0..h_{27}` (input the residual state + a learned layer-index
  embedding) and predicting `Δ_ℓ` at each step, SmoothL1, AdamW `lr=1e-3`, teacher-forced
  (predict `Δ_ℓ` from the true prefix `h_0..h_ℓ`). ONE model handles all transitions. GPU,
  minutes. Config is `ungrounded` (no prior depth-axis seq-model instance) → smoke-test at
  Stage 0 before trusting (see §11).

**Atlas metrics** (per transition × class × target space), on the 500 held-out contexts:
coordinate-averaged R² on `Δ_ℓ` (the JTC/N-NJTC/ReSAE convention), median AND 90th/99th-pct
per-context Δ-error (heavy tails per 2405.12250), and adjacent-layer cosine + generalized-
Procrustes linearity as complementary reads. Persist `eval_results/issue_841/stage0_atlas.json`.

**Multi-hop maps for Stage 1.** Two transport modes per (source ℓ, target ℓ*):
1. **iterated one-step composition** `f_{ℓ*−1}∘…∘f_ℓ` — compose the fitted one-step maps
   (ridge/MLP/GRU); no extra fit. For the GRU, roll it forward feeding its own predictions
   from ℓ (report a divergence-horizon metric = layer at which `‖ĥ_ℓ − h_ℓ‖` exceeds the
   held-out one-step error band). Detached 3-step self-rollout training (HASS) is a DEFERRED
   refinement (primary transport uses teacher-forced one-step maps; note it).
2. **direct-hop ridge** ℓ→ℓ* — a separate closed-form ridge fit directly mapping `h_ℓ` to
   `h_{ℓ*}` (JTC-style), per (source ℓ in the reported grid × target ℓ*). ~3 target layers ×
   ~10 source layers = ~30 closed-form fits. CPU. Answers "is composing one-step maps worse
   than one long hop?"

## 7. Decision Gates

**No compute gates — short run (~2 GPU-h, <4h wall, well under the 4h/16-GPU-h gate bar).**

gate: if one-step (k=1) predicted states retain <~50% of ceiling r for ALL classes AND ALL
traits, the multi-hop retention grid is skipped and the outcome is reported as the Stage-0

the ceiling values (0.46–0.62) and the DPI bound make "50% of ceiling" a meaningful,
achievable threshold with a defined sign (retention is a fraction in [0, ~1]).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Target = `Δ_ℓ` (not raw `h_{ℓ+1}`).** Why: raw `h_{ℓ+1}` is ~0.99-linear from `h_ℓ`
  (residual/identity dominated), tautologically so on fine-tuned Instruct models; only the
  update `Δ_ℓ` carries the interesting structure. `Source:` 2405.12250 (Secretly Linear) +
  deep-dive Slice A. Alternatives rejected: raw-state R² (tautological), symmetric/PSD/SVD-only
  Δ summaries (the Δ is ~98% rotational — 2605.14258).
- **Identity null = predict-zero in Δ-space.** Why: the honest baseline every learned map must
  beat. `Source:` 2409.14091 (N-NJTC explicit identity-shortcut baseline) + body constraint.
- **Ridge: bias + held-out calibration + `RIDGE_LAMBDAS` GCV + per-block RMS-norm target.**
  Why: exactly ReSAE's per-consecutive-layer affine recipe (the closest depth-axis precedent).
  `Source:` 2605.27819 (ReSAE) for the recipe shape; `#779/#658` for the concrete λ grid
  (`RIDGE_LAMBDAS=[1e-2,1e-1,1,10,100,1000]`, validated for this model's activation regression).
  Alternatives rejected: no-bias JTC "mat" (ReSAE bias improves fit); low-rank A·B (N-NJTC —
  a parameter-efficiency concern #841 does not have at this N).
- **MLP `hidden=512, lr=1e-3, wd=1e-4, max_epochs=300`.** Why: the #779/#658 activation-
  regression defaults, validated for `Qwen-2.5-7B` 3584-dim activation prediction on this exact
  cached-tensor family. `Source:` #779/#658 (`issue658_fit_predictors.py:110-114`). Note: #779's
  MLP target was `v(x)` (answer profile); #841's is `Δ_ℓ` — same architecture and optimizer,
  different target, so the values transfer as a validated starting point and the held-out early-
  stopping self-corrects overfit; a Stage-0 smoke confirms convergence. Alternatives rejected:
  larger hidden / more epochs (no evidence it helps activation regression here; #779's MLP
  underfit relative to its ridge, arguing against added capacity).
- **SmoothL1 loss on `Δ_ℓ`.** Why: the near-universal validated activation-regression loss.
  `Source:` 2401.15077 (EAGLE), 2408.15766 (HASS), 2511.05963 (NextLat). Alternatives rejected:
  plain MSE (heavy Δ-error tails per 2405.12250 make Huber-style robustness preferable); a
  behavior/decode-space auxiliary term (EAGLE +0.1·CE, NextLat +KL) — deliberately omitted
  because Stage 0 is behavior-agnostic like #779's `h` (adding a trait term would smuggle the
  DV into the fit and break the single-variable comparison with #779).
- **Depth-GRU `hidden=1024, 1 layer, lr=1e-3, SmoothL1`.** Why: a minimal sequence-over-depth
  surrogate; no prior instance exists (the open class the body wants). `Source: ungrounded —
  needs smoke-test` (flagged §12 Low). Alternatives considered: LSTM/SSM (GRU chosen for
  simplicity + fewer params at this hidden dim; an SSM variant is a cheap follow-up if the GRU
  shows signal).
- **Train/held-out split 4500/500, seed 42.** Why: a held-out atlas eval set disjoint from every
  fit (incl. ridge calibration); the Persona-Vectors eval contexts are already disjoint from the
  LMSYS corpus by construction. `Source:` #779 (eval-context disjointness) + 2605.27819 (ReSAE
  held-out calibration split).
- **Per-block RMS normalization of the Δ target.** Why: `‖Δ_ℓ‖` shrinks with depth (0.963→0.518
  in the literature), so unnormalized Δ-R² is early-layer-dominated; the exact curve is LN-
  placement-dependent so it is MEASURED on Qwen (§4.1) not imported. `Source:` 2605.27819 (ReSAE
  `S_m`), 2502.02732 (Peri-LN — measure, don't import).
- **Target layers ℓ* {evil 20, syco 26, halluc 17} primary + {20, 19, 24} companion.** Why: the
  body fixes these a priori "from #779's per-trait curves"; verification of `step0_oracle.json`
  shows syco 26 / halluc 17 are #779's *oracle*-best layers while the PV-raw peaks are 19 / 24
  (evil 20 is both) — an internal inconsistency with the body's own "its own best layer" rule for
  evil. Resolution: report both (primary = body a-priori for #779-comparability; companion =
  PV-raw peak for the #779-lesson-consistent ceiling). `Source:` #779 `step0_oracle.json`
  (per-layer `pv_raw_r` / `oracle_r`, verified this session).
- **Within-condition Pearson r, `min_y_std=1.0`, `min_n=3`, bootstrap resample-conditions
  n_boot≥997.** Why: byte-identical to #779 so Stage 1 is directly comparable to #779's rows.
  `Source:` #779 `metrics.py` (`within_condition_pearson`, `bootstrap_within_condition_ci`,
  `bootstrap_delta_ci` — imported verbatim) + Persona-Vectors within-condition protocol.
- **Reused judged trait scores + `r_B` directions.** Why: the DV and the directions are #779's;
  re-running would change the benchmark and burn judge spend for no scientific gain. `Source:`
  #779 (`pass_a/*.json` judge_scores; `r_b/*.pt`; extracted per persona-vectors-recipe.md,
  read-out/prediction regime). Fetched on the pod lane via `hf_hub_download` from
  `superkaiba1/explore-persona-space-data`.
- **Seeds fit 42 / bootstrap 0 / MLP init 658.** Why: inherit #779's fit + bootstrap seeds and
  the helper's #658 MLP-init seed for reproducibility. `Source:` #779 plan §10 + helper default.
