# 1. Goal

Measure whether #186's persona-style chain of thought already pins down the final answer. The research question is: **Conditioned on the chain of thought, is there much entropy in the answer?** The headline claim is `H(answer | persona_cot) << H(answer | generic_cot) <= H(answer | no_cot)` -> persona-style CoT is the carrier of persona leakage. The falsification rule is `H(answer | persona_cot) ~= H(answer | generic_cot)` -> persona-style CoT is no more determinative than generic-style CoT, so something downstream of the CoT is driving the persona-leakage effect found in #186.

# 3. Hypothesis

Primary prediction (now a **conjunction**, per A2): the headline carrier claim survives at HIGH confidence only if BOTH of the following hold simultaneously:

1. `H_abcd(answer | persona_cot)` is at least 0.5 nats below `H_abcd(answer | generic_cot)` in the **librarian-eval-persona** cell, AND
2. The same gap (≥0.5 nats) is present in the **`baseline=assistant`** cell, where the eval-time system prompt does NOT carry persona content but the in-context CoT does.

If condition 1 holds but condition 2 fails (gap collapses in the `assistant` cell), the carrier is the eval-time persona prompt, not the CoT content; the claim must be descoped to "persona system prompt at test time pins the answer". MODERATE/LOW-confidence claims are still permitted with weaker evidence (e.g., gap present in librarian-eval only, or 2/3 seed direction agreement).

Primary effect must hold in all three seeds (same direction) for HIGH confidence; 2/3 seed agreement is MODERATE; otherwise LOW.

Secondary prediction: `H_abcd(answer | persona_cot)` is closer to 0 than to `log(4) = 1.3862943611198906` nats, meaning the answer is pinned down rather than approximately uniform over A/B/C/D.

Tertiary prediction (proxy validity, revised per B2): per-arm Spearman `rho` between analytical `H_abcd` and empirical Miller-Madow-corrected `H_MM` on the 200-q_id subsample, computed separately per `(persona, cot_style)` cell (9 values; `no_cot` skipped when mostly-zero ties dominate). Success threshold: `min_rho >= 0.5` across the 9 cells.

Memorization control prediction (A1): within-seed `H_abcd(persona_cot)` is at least 0.2 nats lower than the cross-seed mean (CoT from seed `S`, checkpoint from seed `S'`, `S != S'`) on the librarian-source cell. If this gap is absent, low within-seed entropy reflects retrieval, not generic CoT-conditional pinning.

Falsification thresholds (canonical version in `# Kill criterion`; this section lists corollaries):

- If mean `H_abcd(persona_cot) - H_abcd(generic_cot) > -0.1` nats analytically, or `> -0.3` nats empirically on `H_MM`, the carrier claim is not supported (A5).
- If the `baseline=assistant` cell shows no gap but the librarian cell does, the claim is descoped per A2.
- If within-seed and cross-seed `H_abcd(persona_cot)` are within 0.2 nats of each other, the claim is descoped to "memorized question-conditional retrieval" per A1.
- If `min_rho < 0.4` per-cell (B2), analytical first-token entropy is not a valid proxy even if the analytical mean effect is large.
- If more than 10% of rows have no A/B/C/D candidate in top-20 after answer stripping, the restricted-H analysis is considered fragile and the full-vocab result becomes descriptive only.

Analytical pass:

- Use vLLM: `LLM(model=<seed_checkpoint_path>, dtype="bfloat16", trust_remote_code=True, gpu_memory_utilization=0.85, max_model_len=4096, max_num_seqs=64, seed=<S>)`.
- Use `SamplingParams(max_tokens=1, logprobs=20, temperature=0.0, top_p=1.0)`.
- For each prompt, read `out.outputs[0].logprobs[0]`, which in vLLM 0.11.0 is a dict keyed by token id with `Logprob(logprob=..., rank=..., decoded_token=...)`.
- Compute three entropies and one mass diagnostic per row:
  - `H_top20`: renormalize the returned top-20 probabilities and compute `-sum p log p`.
  - `H_abcd`: collect all token IDs emitted by tokenizer encodings for naked letters `"A","B","C","D"` and leading-space variants `" A"," B"," C"," D"`. If multiple token IDs decode to the same letter, logsumexp them by letter, renormalize across the four letters, and compute entropy. If no A/B/C/D token is present in top-20, set `H_abcd = null`, `restricted_missing = true`.
  - `top20_mass` (A4 diagnostic): `sum(exp(logprob_k) for k in top20)`, the fraction of the full-vocab probability mass captured by the returned top-20. One float in `[0, 1]` per row. Logged but not aggregated into `H_top20`.
  - `abcd_total_mass_pre_renorm` (B6 diagnostic): `sum(exp(logprob_k) for k in top20 if decoded_letter(k) in {A,B,C,D})`, before letter-level logsumexp and renormalization. If this differs systematically across `cot_style` (e.g. higher under `persona_cot` than `generic_cot`), the restricted-H comparison conflates "answer pinning" with "format compliance" rather than pure CoT-conditional entropy.

# 6. Evaluation

Per-arm:

- Report mean +/- std of `H_top20` across 1172 q_ids.
- Report mean +/- std of `H_abcd` across valid restricted rows. The denominator is expected to be at most 1172 and may be 1150 for A/B/C/D-labeled rows if numeric-label rows are excluded from restricted-H.
- Report mean +/- std of empirical `H_MM` (Miller-Madow corrected, **headline**) and `H_mle` (plug-in, diagnostic) across exactly 200 q_ids per arm if no empirical generation failures occur.
- Report A4 top20-mass diagnostics per arm: `mean_top20_mass`, `p5_top20_mass`, `p95_top20_mass`. **A4 kill rule:** if `p5(top20_mass) < 0.9` in any arm of the primary `persona_cot vs generic_cot` comparison, `H_top20` is downgraded to diagnostic-only and the headline rests on `H_abcd` (analytical) + `H_MM` (empirical).
- Report B6 length and probability-mass diagnostics per arm: `mean_cot_char_len_post_strip`, `mean_cot_token_len_post_strip`, `mean_abcd_total_mass_pre_renorm`. If `mean_abcd_total_mass_pre_renorm` under `persona_cot` is ≥1.5× that under `generic_cot`, the analyzer must flag "format-compliance confound" in the write-up — the restricted-H contrast may be conflating answer pinning with format compliance, not pure CoT-conditional entropy.

Per-pair (headline statistical test, B5):

- Within each `(persona, seed)`, compute per-q_id deltas:
  - `Delta_pg = H_abcd(persona_cot) - H_abcd(generic_cot)`
  - `Delta_pn = H_abcd(persona_cot) - H_abcd(no_cot)`
  - `Delta_gn = H_abcd(generic_cot) - H_abcd(no_cot)`
- **Headline test:** 9 paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on `Delta_pg`, one per `(persona, seed)`, using the intersection of q_ids with valid restricted-H in both arms. Apply Holm-Bonferroni correction across the 9. HIGH confidence requires **≥7/9 tests rejecting H_0 at corrected α=0.05 AND consistent sign** (all 9 sign-agreed). The 3-seed direction-agreement check becomes a secondary robustness diagnostic, not the primary statistic.
- Also report per-pair paired mean and median deltas with 95% percentile bootstrap CIs over q_id (B3: **10 000 resamples, percentile method, computed per-seed; the headline number reports the seed-averaged CI**, with per-seed CIs kept for direction-consistency tracking).

Multi-seed (now a secondary robustness diagnostic, per B5):

- Report mean and std across seeds for each `(persona, cot_style)` arm.
- Track effect direction `mean_H_abcd(persona_cot) < mean_H_abcd(generic_cot)` per seed; all 3/3 agreeing is a HIGH-confidence corroborator alongside the B5 Holm-corrected Wilcoxon. 2/3 agreement is MODERATE; otherwise LOW.

A2 conjunction (HIGH-confidence carrier claim):

- HIGH confidence requires BOTH the `librarian-eval-persona` cell AND the `baseline=assistant` cell to show `mean_H_abcd(persona_cot) - mean_H_abcd(generic_cot) <= -0.5` nats, averaged across seeds.
- If the gap holds in `librarian` but collapses in `baseline=assistant` (gap > -0.1 nats), the claim is descoped to "eval-time persona system prompt pins the answer" — record this as the alternative finding in `aggregate.json` under `headline_descoped_to`.
- The `comedian-eval` cell is supportive context but not required for HIGH; gap-direction agreement with `librarian` raises confidence.

A1 memorization control (within-vs-cross-seed):

- For the librarian `persona_cot` arm, compute `within_vs_cross_delta = mean_H_abcd(within_seed) - mean_H_abcd(cross_seed_avg)` per eval_seed.
- HIGH-confidence "CoT carries the answer" requires `within_vs_cross_delta <= -0.2 nats` in at least 2/3 eval seeds (within-seed must be MEASURABLY lower than cross-seed). If the deltas are within 0.2 nats of zero, the headline is descoped to "memorized question-conditional retrieval" — record under `headline_descoped_to` in `aggregate.json`.

A3 cross-persona OOD direction check:

- For each of the 3 eval personas in the comedian-source seed=42 cell, compute `sign(H_abcd(persona_cot) - H_abcd(generic_cot))`. HIGH-confidence "persona-style CoT" claim requires the sign to match the librarian-source librarian-eval direction in all 3 eval personas. If signs disagree, the headline is descoped to "librarian-CoT-specific" — record under `headline_descoped_to`.

Analytical-vs-empirical proxy validation (B2):

- Compute Spearman `rho` between analytical `H_abcd` and empirical `H_MM` on the shared 200-q_id subsample, **per-arm** (9 values for `persona_cot` + 9 for `generic_cot` = 18; `no_cot` skipped when mostly-zero ties dominate). Report `min_rho` and the full per-cell table in `aggregate.json.spearman_per_arm`.
- Success threshold: `min_rho >= 0.5` (replaces the previous pooled `rho >= 0.6`; pooling inflates correlation via between-arm variance).
- Weak proxy: `0.3 <= min_rho < 0.5`; report analytical result as suggestive only.
- Proxy failure: `min_rho < 0.3`; empirical `H_MM` distribution becomes the primary result.

Numerical success criterion (canonical version in `# Kill criterion`):

- Primary success (HIGH): A2 conjunction met (≥0.5-nat gap in librarian AND `baseline=assistant`) AND B5 Holm-corrected Wilcoxon shows ≥7/9 rejections with consistent sign AND A1 memorization control passed (within-vs-cross-seed ≥ 0.2-nat delta in 2/3 seeds) AND A3 cross-persona direction matches.
- Secondary success: `mean_H_abcd(persona_cot) < 0.6931471805599453` nats, i.e. closer to 0 than to `log(4)`.
- **C3 operational fallback:** if the secondary prediction holds but the primary fails (answer is pinned but persona-style is not the carrier), report the finding as "answer pinned, but the carrier is something other than persona-CoT content" — this is a publishable null on the headline + a positive finding on the secondary, NOT a non-result.

# Kill criterion

The headline claim ("persona-style CoT IS the carrier of #186 persona leakage") is killed when ANY of the following holds. Analytical and empirical thresholds are split because plug-in MLE on N=8 has a Miller-Madow bias of ~0.1875 nats and the empirical kill threshold must exceed the estimator noise floor (A5):

- **Indistinguishability (analytical):** `abs(mean_H_abcd(persona_cot) - mean_H_abcd(generic_cot)) < 0.1` nats on the analytical pass (no MLE bias), averaged across the 3 eval personas and 3 seeds.
- **Indistinguishability (empirical):** `abs(mean_H_MM(persona_cot) - mean_H_MM(generic_cot)) < 0.3` nats on the empirical pass (Miller-Madow corrected). Equivalent operational form: `abs(mean_H_mle(persona_cot) - mean_H_mle(generic_cot)) < 0.1` nats AFTER applying `H_MM = H_mle + (K_obs - 1)/(2N)` to BOTH arms before subtracting. Threshold 0.3 nats sits above the ~0.1875-nat MM correction so that a result of `|Delta_mle| = 0.08` (which could be consistent with a true effect of +0.28 nats post-correction) is NOT treated as a kill.
- **Direction inconsistency (analytical):** the sign of `mean_H_abcd(persona_cot) - mean_H_abcd(generic_cot)` is not consistent across all 3 seeds (42, 137, 256) within at least one of the 3 eval personas.
- **A2 conjunction failure:** the analytical gap holds in the `librarian-eval` cell but collapses (`>-0.1` nats) in the `baseline=assistant` cell — the eval-time persona system prompt, not the CoT content, is the carrier. The result is published as such; the original headline is killed.
- **A1 memorization failure:** within-seed `H_abcd(persona_cot)` is within 0.2 nats of the cross-seed mean for librarian-source in 2/3 or more eval seeds — low within-seed entropy is retrieval, not generic CoT-conditional pinning. The result is descoped to "memorized question-conditional retrieval"; the original headline is killed.

Any single condition above falsifies "persona-style CoT IS the carrier" and the analyzer must report the descoped framing (`aggregate.headline_descoped_to`) rather than the original claim.

# 11. Decision Rationale

**N=8 samples in empirical pass:** balances MLE bias and wall-time. With `N=8` and `K=4`, Miller-Madow bias is about `0.1875` nats, below the 0.5-nat detection threshold.

**top_k=20 in logprobs:** #186 used vLLM top-20 answer logprobs; vLLM 0.11.0 supports `SamplingParams(logprobs=20)`. Top-20 is enough for A/B/C/D restricted H if answer tokens are in the returned set; full-vocab H is reported honestly as `H_top20`, not true full-vocab entropy.

**Temperature=1.0 in empirical pass:** matches the project's generation-eval convention in `configs/eval/default.yaml:5` and avoids adding a calibration adjustment. The analytical pass remains deterministic at `temperature=0.0`.

**Subsample stratified by correct_answer:** ensures balanced A/B/C/D coverage. The raw test set has enough rows for 50 per letter after excluding numeric labels.

**3 seeds (42, 137, 256):** match #186. The seed dimension here is the replicated #186 `librarian_persona_cot` checkpoint and saved CoT source, not fresh sampling.

**Fixed source family `librarian_persona_cot`:** #355's approved personas are `comedian`, `librarian`, and `baseline`. #186 stores all three as eval personas inside each librarian source result; this gives a clean source/bystander/null-persona comparison without mixing trained-source checkpoints. It also keeps the CoT-style comparison within a seed-matched model.

**Answer stripping before entropy:** the scientific estimand is `H(answer | CoT)`, not `H(answer | CoT plus a previously generated answer)`. #186 raw `*_cot_text` frequently contains a trailing answer, so stripping is load-bearing. Raw replay is kept only as a prompt-template validation.

**A1 cross-seed sub-grid (memorization control):** the single biggest threat to the causal claim is that the SAME checkpoint generated and now reads back the CoT — low `H_abcd(persona_cot)` could be (question, CoT) → answer retrieval, not generic CoT-conditional pinning. 6 off-diagonal teacher-forcing cells × N=200 fit in the budget and produce a within-vs-cross contrast that distinguishes the two explanations.

**A2 conjunction (`librarian` AND `baseline=assistant`):** without this control, a "carrier" claim can't separate "the eval-time persona system prompt does the work" from "the CoT content does the work". The `assistant` system prompt strips persona content while keeping CoT in context; if the gap survives there, the CoT is the carrier. Single-control rejected in favor of conjunction because the descoping is otherwise asymmetric.

**A4 `top20_mass` per-row + p5 < 0.9 kill:** `H_top20` is the entropy of a renormalized truncated distribution, NOT full-vocab entropy. The truncation bias is arm-dependent — if `persona_cot` has higher `top20_mass` (sharper distribution) than `generic_cot`, the truncation+renorm effect alone can produce the predicted contrast. Reporting `p5(top20_mass)` per arm and gating headline use of `H_top20` on `p5 >= 0.9` controls for this without abandoning the full-vocab perspective.

**A5 split kill thresholds (analytical 0.1 nats vs empirical 0.3 nats on `H_MM`):** plug-in MLE on N=8 over K=4 has bias ~0.1875 nats. A symmetric 0.1-nat kill threshold on the empirical pass would treat a true `+0.28`-nat effect as a kill. Split thresholds + Miller-Madow correction on the empirical side preserve statistical validity.

**B1 Miller-Madow as headline empirical metric:** the bias is asymmetric in this setup — `generic_cot` (higher true H, more A/B/C/D bins active) gets a larger correction than `persona_cot` (low true H, possibly one dominant bin). Reporting plug-in MLE without correction would SUPPRESS the predicted `persona_cot < generic_cot` contrast. `H_MM = H_mle + (K_obs - 1)/(2N)` is the standard small-sample correction; plug-in `H_mle` is retained as a diagnostic column for comparison.

**B5 9-test Holm-corrected paired Wilcoxon on per-q_id `Delta_pg`:** the headline statistic operates on per-q_id deltas (paired across `persona_cot` vs `generic_cot` within a `(persona, seed)` cell) rather than mean differences, so power doesn't depend on between-q_id variance cancellation. Holm-Bonferroni across 9 tests (3 personas × 3 seeds) controls family-wise α at 0.05; HIGH-confidence requires ≥7/9 rejections with consistent sign. Alternatives rejected: (a) Bonferroni — strictly conservative, no power gain; (b) FDR/BH — more lenient but harder to interpret for a small fixed test family; (c) pooled mean-difference test — sensitive to between-arm heterogeneity.

Alternatives considered and rejected:

- HF `model(..., output_scores=True)`: slower for this batch shape and gives up vLLM batching/KV-cache advantages.
- Free-generation entropy from scratch: confounds answer entropy with formatting entropy, especially whether the model emits text before the answer letter.
- Running all four #186 source personas as the main grid: scientifically useful, but out of scope for the approved 27-arm plan. The single A3 comedian-source confirmation cell at seed=42 buys the cross-persona OOD generalization check without expanding the main grid.
- Using the untrained `eval_results/issue186/baseline/result.json` as the `baseline` arm: useful sanity reference, but it is a model control rather than a persona control and lacks the 3-seed structure required here.
- Pooled Spearman ρ across 27 arms (previous plan threshold `pooled_ρ ≥ 0.6`): rejected because between-arm variance dominates and inflates ρ. Per-arm `min_rho ≥ 0.5` (B2) is a stricter, more interpretable proxy validity check.
- 3-seed direction agreement as the headline statistic: kept as a secondary robustness diagnostic, but the headline is now the 9-test Holm-corrected paired Wilcoxon on per-q_id `Delta_pg` (B5) — direction agreement on 3 means is weaker than rejecting H_0 on 7+/9 paired tests.

C1 — anchor-format note: the `no_cot` arm uses anchor `"\n\nThe correct answer is ("` while `generic_cot` and `persona_cot` use `"\nAnswer: "` (both inherited from #186's `b51dfbc9:src/explore_persona_space/eval/capability.py` historical scaffold). This asymmetry means a direct `H_abcd(no_cot)` vs `H_abcd(persona_cot)` comparison is partly an anchor-token comparison, not a pure CoT-presence comparison. **The primary headline contrast `persona_cot vs generic_cot` shares the same `"\nAnswer: "` anchor, so it is unaffected.** Report `no_cot` as an upper-bound reference only; do not treat `Delta_pn` as a clean causal contrast.

C2 — analyzer-side TODO: compute per-`strip_rule_id` mean `H_abcd` as a sensitivity column. Rows that hit rule 5 (the keyword-gated catch-all) carry more strip-pipeline risk than rows that hit rule 1; if `H_abcd` differs substantially across rule_ids within an arm, the analyzer must report it as a sensitivity concern. The analyzer computes this from the per-row JSONL — no extra design needed here, but the TODO is noted so the analyzer doesn't skip it.
