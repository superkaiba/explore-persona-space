# Plan v1 — task #397: Recipe-factor selectivity screen re-run with single-token marker `※` + log-prob eval

## 1. Goal + hypothesis

### Goal

Re-run the five-factor recipe-selectivity screen from parent #383 with two coupled changes:

1. **Marker switch:** `[ZLT]` (3-BPE-piece on Qwen-2.5) → `※` (single BPE piece). With marker-only-loss + `tail_tokens=0`, this drops the loss extent from ~4 tokens (3 marker pieces + EOS) to ~2 tokens (1 marker piece + EOS) — see Assumption A2. The loss-mask main effect is now a sharper test.
2. **Loss-mask factor E binary → ordinal K=3:** {E0=marker+EOT (~2 tok), E1=tail-32 (~32 tok), E2=whole-completion (~600 tok)}. Isolates *loss extent* from *loss location*; the binary E in #383 conflated both.

Bolt on log-prob eval (`compute_marker_logprob`, #401 primitive) at intermediate checkpoints to (a) measure factor effects continuously even when source rate sits at zero or saturates at one, and (b) give the queued predictor follow-up cleaner per-cell signal than the 5-pp-floored substring rate.

### Hypothesis (matched to falsification thresholds)

**H1 — Replication of #383's lockstep dissolution.** For factors A (long-system), B (long-answer), C (persona-framing), D (Claude-data), the per-factor selectivity Δ on substring-match rate falls within #383's reported 95% CI envelope: A ∈ [+21.3, +45.7], B ∈ [+15.7, +39.0], C ∈ [−39.2, −14.7] (note: #383 sign was negative because the contrast was *neutral* vs persona-framed; persona-framing is the positive direction), D ∈ [−1.3, +23.5]. *Falsified if* any factor's selectivity Δ flips sign vs the #383 widest-of-three CI envelope, or if its new CI excludes the #383 point estimate.

**H2 — Ordinal E shows monotonic loss-extent trend.** Per-factor selectivity Δ as a function of E ∈ {marker+EOT, tail-32, whole-completion} is monotonically non-decreasing on at least the substring-match rate metric (Spearman ρ across pooled-across-cells E-level means ≥ +0.5, one-tailed p<0.05). *Falsified if* the ranking inverts (E2 < E1 < E0) or if it's non-monotonic with the middle level outside the [E0, E2] range by >5 pp.

**H3 — Log-prob signal mirrors substring rate qualitatively at endpoint, with smaller variance.** Per-cell log p(`※`) at the final checkpoint correlates Spearman ρ ≥ +0.7 with substring source rate across all 144 cells. *Falsified if* ρ < +0.5 or if any factor's log-prob effect direction disagrees with its substring effect direction at p<0.05.

**H4 — Whole-completion-loss remains the largest selectivity decoupler at sharper contrast.** With marker-only loss now covering ~2 instead of ~4 tokens, the gap whole-completion vs E0 should not shrink: E2−E0 selectivity Δ remains within ±10 pp of #383's +41.7 pp. *Falsified if* the gap shrinks by >15 pp or flips sign — would indicate the #383 E effect was partly driven by `[ZLT]`-specific multi-piece dynamics rather than loss extent.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Cells

144 (cell, source) pairs total. Cell key remains a bitstring but is now 6 chars: `ABCDEE` where `EE ∈ {00, 01, 10}` encodes E ∈ {0, 1, 2}. Alternative: extend the existing `Cell` dataclass with `e ∈ {0, 1, 2}` and keep a 5-char key `ABCDE` with `E ∈ {0, 1, 2}` (slightly cleaner; see §5 for the chosen layout).

Per source (librarian / programmer / surgeon):
- 16 binary combos of (A, B, C, D), minus the 4 dropped corners where A=0×C=1 (the C-axis preflight fails Jaccard for these — same as #383). **Valid (A,B,C,D) combos per source: 12.** (B and D each have 2 levels, but the A=0×C=1 drop removes 4 combos: A=0, C=1, B∈{0,1}, D∈{0,1}.)
- 3 E levels per valid combo: E0, E1, E2.
- Per source: 12 × 3 = **36 cells**.
- **Total valid cells across 3 sources: 108** (not 144). The "144 = 3 × 16 × 3" headline in the clarifier counted dropped corners; the dispatcher-side preflight will leave 12 valid (A,B,C,D) combos × 3 E × 3 sources = 108 actually-trained cells. *I am flagging this as a divergence from the clarifier's cell-count headline; total GPU budget is correspondingly lower (see §11).*

### 4.2 Per-cell training (same as #383 except marker + E semantics)

For each (source, A, B, C, D, E):

1. Load shared on-policy (D=0) or off-policy (D=1) pool from `data/issue_397/pools/<source>/source-<source>_a{a}_b{b}_c{c}.jsonl` (these are the SAME pools as #383 — see Reusable artifacts in §5; the marker is appended at training-row construction, not at pool generation).
2. Build training dataset via `factor_screen_397.data_prep.prepare_cell(...)` which calls `_append_marker(answer)` using `cfg.marker_token = "※"`. Positive rows end in `…answer\n\n※<eos>`. Negative rows end in `…answer<eos>` (no marker). 200 positives + 400 negatives matched-pair design (the #383 recipe-fix). (Note: #383 doc says 400 positives; verify against actual #383 pool sizes in §5.)
3. Call `train_one_cell(...)` with TrainLoraConfig:
   - `marker_only_loss = True` for E ∈ {0, 1}; `False` for E = 2 (whole-completion).
   - `marker_text = "※"` (new).
   - `marker_tail_tokens = 0` for E=0, `32` for E=1. Irrelevant when `marker_only_loss=False`.
   - All other hparams = #383 defaults: LoRA r=32 / α=64 / dropout=0.05 / rsLoRA, AdamW lr=1e-5, cosine schedule, warmup_ratio=0.05, 3 epochs, per-device batch 4, grad_accum 4, max_length 2048.
   - `save_strategy = "steps"`, `save_steps = <derived>` (see §4.4) — DEVIATION from #383's `save_strategy="no"`. Needed for multi-checkpoint log-prob.
4. After training: merge LoRA into base via `merge_lora(...)` → `merged/`. Adapter remains at `adapter/` for HF Hub upload.

### 4.3 Per-cell eval

**At every saved checkpoint (intermediate + final):**

- Build all 24 persona contexts: for each `persona ∈ EVAL_PERSONAS_24` × `question ∈ EVAL_QUESTIONS_20`, render the chat template up to the assistant turn opener. Concretely: `tokenizer.apply_chat_template([{role:system, content:persona_prompt}, {role:user, content:q+b_suffix(cell.b)}], add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False)`. (This matches what the model sees at sampling time; we just stop before generation.)
- Call `compute_marker_logprob(model, tokenizer, contexts=<all 480 contexts>, marker_text=" ※", position="end_of_answer", batch_size=8, device="cuda:0")` → list of 480 log-probs. (Use `" ※"` not `"※"` — see Assumption A3 on context-marker boundary.) Actually: marker prepended with `"\n\n"` at training time, so the context-marker boundary at eval should match. We'll measure with `marker_text="\n\n※"` (2 tokens) to mirror the training-time geometry. **Open decision flagged for fact-check:** whether to teacher-force `\n\n※` (matches training prefix) or just `※` (cleaner single-piece measurement). Recommend BOTH per checkpoint — `compute_marker_logprob` is cheap; ~480 contexts × <0.1 sec each on H100.
- Aggregate to: mean log p(`※`) per persona at this checkpoint; per-(source vs bystander) selectivity Δ in log-prob units; per-cell summary.

**At final checkpoint ONLY (substring parity with #383):**

- Run `generate_completions(...)` from `factor_screen_365.eval_panel` with vLLM batched generation: 5 completions × 20 questions × 24 personas = 2400 generations per cell. `max_new_tokens=2048` (per CLAUDE.md). Score via `score_markers(...)` (case-insensitive substring `※`). 
- Run `generate_random_control_completions(...)` for the 24 neutral background prompts (24 × 20 × 5 = 2400 generations).

### 4.4 Intermediate-checkpoint cadence

Per #383 hparams: ~200 positives + 400 negatives = 600 examples per cell. Effective batch 16 (4 × 4 grad_accum). Steps per epoch ≈ 38; total steps over 3 epochs ≈ 113.

**Cadence:** Save every 25 steps (4 intermediate + 1 final per cell = 5 checkpoints per cell). Reasoning:
- Log-spaced (e.g., {5, 25, 50, 100, end}) would lose coarse-grained mid-training resolution and overlap with #398's much-denser dynamics work.
- Every 50 steps gives only 2 intermediates over a ~113-step run; insufficient for any monotonic-fit on the log-prob curve.
- Every 25 steps is the smallest reasonable cadence: at 200 positives × 3 sources × 12 ABCD-combos × 3 E levels = 21,600 contexts per intermediate checkpoint × 4 intermediates = 86,400 forward passes per dispatch. At ~12,000 contexts/min (batch=8, 480-context cells, 1×H100, ~24 sec/cell from a quick mental estimate, see §11), that's ~7 min/cell × 4 checkpoints = ~28 min of intermediate-eval extra per cell. Trim to every 50 if §11 budget overshoots.
- Intermediate eval runs log-prob ONLY (no sampling). Sampling 24-persona × 20-q × 5-comp at every checkpoint would balloon eval cost ~10×.

### 5.5 `eval_panel.py` additions

Add a new top-level function alongside `generate_completions(...)`:

```python
def compute_logprob_panel(
    *,
    model_path: str,           # path to merged checkpoint
    marker_text: str,          # e.g. "\n\n※"
    panel: dict[str, str] = EVAL_PERSONAS_24,
    questions: list[str] = EVAL_QUESTIONS_20,
    b_suffix_value: str,       # cell.b-derived B-suffix
    batch_size: int = 8,
    device: str = "cuda:0",
) -> dict[str, list[float]]:
    """Per-persona × per-question log p(marker) at end_of_answer position.

    Returns {persona: [logp_q1, logp_q2, ..., logp_q20]} for all 24 personas.
    Uses compute_marker_logprob from src/explore_persona_space/eval/marker_logprob.py.
    """
    from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer
    from explore_persona_space.eval.marker_logprob import compute_marker_logprob

    tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(model_path, trust_remote_code=True)
    model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(model_path, torch_dtype="bfloat16",
                                                  device_map=device, trust_remote_code=True)
    model.eval()

    out: dict[str, list[float]] = {}
    for persona, system_prompt in panel.items():
        contexts = []
        for q in questions:
            full_q = f"{q} {b_suffix_value}".strip()
            ctx = tok.apply_chat_template(
                [{"role": "system", "content": system_prompt},
                 {"role": "user", "content": full_q}],
                add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False,
            )
            contexts.append(ctx)
        out[persona] = compute_marker_logprob(
            model=model, tokenizer=tok, contexts=contexts,
            marker_text=marker_text, position="end_of_answer",
            batch_size=batch_size, device=device,
        )
    del model
    import gc, torch; gc.collect(); torch.cuda.empty_cache()
    return out
```

### 5.7 `aggregator.py` ordinal-E trend test

- Existing matched-pair effects for A, B, C, D: unchanged (binary, marginalize across E levels and use the widest-of-three CI construction).

## 6. Controls

1. **Cross-source replication.** Each per-factor effect is computed pooled across librarian / programmer / surgeon AND reported per-source. A pooled effect that doesn't reproduce sign in 2 of 3 sources is downweighted in the title-claim register.
2. **Cross-E-level monotonicity test** (already in H2). The directional ordering E0 < E1 < E2 is the key new prediction. If E1 > E2 by >5pp, the "loss extent" framing fails and we'd revert to interpreting #383's E effect as a different mechanism.
3. **Regression baseline cell.** **Skip.** Not committing to a `[ZLT]` regression cell — #401 already shipped `tests/test_marker_abstraction.py` with both `[ZLT]` and `※` codepaths verified at CPU scale, and adding a single 7B regression cell costs ~30 GPU-min while testing nothing the unit test doesn't already test. *I am flagging this against the planner-discretion prompt: the marginal cost is small, but the marginal information is also small.* Pull the regression cell back into the plan if the consistency-checker prefers belt-and-suspenders.
4. **Substring-vs-log-prob coherence** (H3 above). Final-checkpoint Spearman ρ between substring rate and log p(`※`) across 144 cells acts as an internal consistency check; if it drops below +0.5, the log-prob primitive itself is suspect.
5. **Random-control panel** (24 neutral backgrounds — same as #383). Sampled at final checkpoint only. Cells where random-control max rate ≥ 0.5 are flagged in the aggregator as "generic-firing corners" (matching #383's `10010` pathological cells).

## 7. Eval metrics + thresholds

| Metric | Computation | Reported as |
|---|---|---|
| Source substring rate | fraction of 100 source-persona completions containing `※` | per-cell scalar |
| Per-bystander substring rate | per-bystander fraction, then mean across 23 bystanders | per-cell scalar |
| Selectivity Δ (substring) | source rate − mean bystander rate | per-cell scalar |
| Per-factor matched-pair Δ on source / leakage / selectivity (A, B, C, D) | mean across 12 matched-pair tuples per source × 3 sources; widest-of-three CI (per-pair percentile / source-cluster / source-FE), 1000 resamples | scalar + 95% CI |

## 10. Kill criteria

- **Source rate floor across all cells.** Max-source-rate-across-108-cells < 0.30 → marker switch broke training entirely; halt and investigate before reporting.
- **All four A/B/C/D factor effects within ±5 pp of zero.** Would mean the #383 lockstep-dissolution result was a `[ZLT]`-specific artifact; that's a publishable null but kills the multi-knob-lift thesis. Report as null without trying to rescue.

**`ft-7b` (4× H100, ZeRO-3 capable).** Justification:
