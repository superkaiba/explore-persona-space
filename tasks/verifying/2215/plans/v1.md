# Plan — Issue #2215: Representation-level effects of the 21 minimal-pair information types: context-vector shift, answer-vector shift, and map discrimination

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I take the 1,404 paired conversations from the parent experiment, where each pair differs in exactly one piece of information (a name, a rule, a persona, a format instruction), and measure three things with no interventions at all: how much that one change moves the model's internal summary of the conversation, how much it moves the internal representation of the answers the model actually wrote, and whether our existing conversation-to-answer predictors can tell the two paired conversations apart. Almost everything is already banked from the parent run; the only new model compute is one cheap re-reading pass over the parent's stored answers.
- **What I expect:** nearly every information type visibly moves the conversation representation (the parent's probes could decode almost all of them), answer representations move most for the types that also changed behavior in the parent, and the predictors separate most pairs but fail worst on the types where the two answers barely differ, plus the changed-question control.
- **What would change my mind:** if the predictors can't tell paired conversations apart any better than a simple constant-shift baseline, that says our maps only capture generic answer structure, not the specific information in the context, and the mapping line's "the context vector predicts the answer" story needs a rewrite.

## 3. Hypothesis

All primary reads at frozen layer L19 (no selection over layers; L14/L26 reported in full as exploratory). "Non-degenerate", for the pe arm, excludes the two pre-declared degenerate-at-pe cells.

- **H1 (context-vector shift).** Changing one information type moves v_C detectably and consistently for essentially every type: at context-end L19, ≥ 18 of 21 base types show (i) median value-shift/carrier-shift magnitude ratio > 1 and (ii) within-type direction consistency (mean pairwise cosine of Δv_C across carriers at fixed directed value-pair) whose carrier-clustered 95% CI excludes the within-cell label-permutation band. Grounded on the parent probe (max AUC = 1.0 for nearly all cells at ce). Falsified for a type if its consistency CI sits inside the permutation band. Expected weakest: `query_content` (parent ce probe 0.5995).
- **H2 (answer-vector shift tracks behavioral separation).** Per-type noise-normalized ‖Δv_A‖ (against the split-half draw-noise floor) rank-correlates with the parent's per-type anchor separation (|ceiling − floor| judge contrast, from `eval_results/issue_2162/f_metrics/anchors.jsonl`): Spearman ρ > 0.4 with bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 across the 39 cells. Falsified if the CI includes 0 — that would mean answer-representation movement is decoupled from behavioral movement.
- **H3 (map discrimination).** The Goal-named maps discriminate paired contexts above chance for the majority of types, and the fitted maps beat the identity+learned-bias baseline: pooled paired 2AFC accuracy (cosine, L19) for #779-ce and #1738-pe each > 0.5 with carrier-clustered 95% CI excluding 0.5, AND fitted-map pooled accuracy − identity+bias pooled accuracy > 0 with CI excluding 0. Worst-discriminated types expected to be the low-anchor-separation types + `query_content`. Falsified (map-adds-nothing) if the fitted map fails to beat identity+bias — the map would then carry only constant-shift structure on this bank.

### 4.3 Phase C — CPU analysis (pod-side, ~0.5-1 h, vectorized)

All reads computed per (slot arm × layer ∈ {14, 19, 26} for map reads; all 28 layers for DV1/DV2 geometry profiles), L19 registered primary.

**DV1 — context-vector shift** (inputs: vc_bank.pt only):

- Per pair p=(A,B), slot s, layer ℓ: `Δv_C = v_s(B,ℓ) − v_s(A,ℓ)` → per-cell tensors (36, 3584).
- *Magnitude:* median ‖Δv_C‖ per cell vs the **carrier-shift yardstick** = median ‖v_s(ctx_i) − v_s(ctx_j)‖ over same-cell same-value cross-carrier context pairs (all C(12,2)=66 pairs × 3 values per cell); report the ratio + both raw medians.
- *Within-type direction consistency (primary grain, disjoint contexts):* per (cell, directed value-pair): mean pairwise cosine of Δv_C across the 12 carriers (66 cosines); cell-level = mean over the 3 value-pairs. Null: within-cell label permutation — permute which context of the (A,B) pair is called "B" independently per carrier, B=10,000 seeded draws, band = 95th percentile of the null consistency (vectorized: sign-flip on the Δ matrix + one batched GEMM per draw block).
- *Cross-type geometry:* mean shift direction per (cell, value-pair, slot, L19) → (117 × 117) cosine matrix; summary = within-cell vs between-cell mean |cosine| + hierarchically-clustered heatmap.
- pe arm: the 2 degenerate cells are asserted ‖Δv_pe‖ ≈ 0 (sanity) and excluded from pe aggregates (parent's pre-declared flags).

**DV2 — answer-vector shift** (inputs: va2215 store; banked va_span as robustness):

- v̄_A(c, ℓ) = mean of va_tail_incl over valid draws; Δv_A per pair; same three reads as DV1 (magnitude vs a draw-noise yardstick, consistency, cross-type geometry).
- *Noise floor:* per context, split-half distance ‖v̄_A^{half1} − v̄_A^{half2}‖ (5/5 draws); per-type noise-normalized shift = median ‖Δv_A‖ / median split-half distance. Consistency read ALSO computed with split-half legs (leg1 from half1 of both contexts, leg2 from half2) to show the shared-draw-noise-free value.
- *Coupling (H2):* Spearman(per-cell noise-normalized ‖Δv_A‖, parent per-cell anchor separation from `f_metrics/anchors.jsonl`), bootstrap CI over cells; plus exploratory Spearman(‖Δv_C‖, ‖Δv_A‖) across cells and across pairs within cell.

**DV3 — map discrimination** (inputs: vc_bank + va2215 + 9 payloads):

- Predictions per arm: `pred(c) = apply_map(payload, v_s(c, ℓ_payload), dev)` — arms: **#779-ce** (963k, Goal-named), **#1738-pe** (Goal-named), **#1738-ce** (matched-fit companion), each at L14/L19/L26 (L19 primary). Targets: v̄_A tail-inclusive at the same layer (span-mean secondary).
- Per pair, per similarity metric (cosine primary, euclidean companion): `margin_A = sim(pred(A), v̄_A(A)) − sim(pred(A), v̄_A(B))`, `margin_B` symmetric; paired 2AFC accuracy = mean over both directions of 1[margin > 0]. Per-type accuracy + mean margin; pooled per arm. CI: carrier-clustered bootstrap (12 carriers/cell as clusters; the 3 directed pairs of one carrier share contexts and stay in one cluster), B=10,000 via `bootstrap_family_means_batched`.
- **Shuffled-pair null (registered):** B=10,000 seeded (seed 2215) draws; per draw, within each cell, each prediction is scored against a DIFFERENT pair's (own, other) target duo (carrier-blocked derangement) with the side label randomized — mean 0.5 by construction; band = 2.5/97.5 percentiles of pooled + per-type accuracy. Vectorized: precompute the (n_pairs × n_pairs) within-cell similarity blocks ONCE per arm, then draws only re-index (no model calls, no GEMMs inside the draw loop).
- **Identity+learned-bias baseline:** `identity_bias_predict(x_train, y_train, x_eval)` with b estimated leave-one-TYPE-out (train = all contexts of the other 38 cells, same layer, same pooling); scored through the identical 2AFC pipeline. Applicability: input/output dims match (3584→3584, same layer) — applicable at every arm.
- **kNN retrieval (pool-level):** `knn_retrieval(pred, true, ks=(1,5,10), metric=...)` per arm over the full 1,404-context pool (pool = all v̄_A at the arm's layer/pooling), euclidean + cosine, chance stated k/1404 (0.00071 / 0.0036 / 0.0071); plus median rank + MRR. Also reported for identity+bias.
- **Held-out R² companion:** pooled R² + mean cosine(pred, target) per arm on the bank (pure transfer read — the maps were fit on LMSYS/WildChat corpora, zero bank rows in training; no fitting happens here, so no n<d estimator degeneracy — #1701/#1887 N/A, no GCV anywhere).
- **Carrier-transfer decomposition (exploratory):** score pred(A of carrier i) against targets of the SAME cell + value-pair at carrier j ≠ i — separates value-generic from pair-specific discrimination.
- pe arm: the 2 degenerate cells' pair-discrimination is undefined by construction (identical predictions); excluded + labeled `N/A — degenerate at pe`.

Outputs: `eval_results/issue_2215/{dv1_context_shift.json, dv2_answer_shift.json, dv3_map_discrimination.json, null_bands.json, coupling.json}` + per-pair companions (jsonl) + figures to `figures/issue_2215/` — committed on branch `issue-2215` from the pod, pushed.
