## 0.0 TL;DR

- **What I'll run.** Same end-of-response marker rig as the parent line, but instead of testing one induction mechanism at a time, the panel is a UNION of two kinds of contexts side-by-side: K-shot example blocks (the ICL arm — math examples, coding examples, persona-voice examples, etc.) AND system-prompt-only personas (the SP arm — "You are a pirate captain," "You are a stand-up comedian," etc.). For four personas (pirate, comedian, helpful-tutor, concise-engineer) I build BOTH an ICL-by-example version AND a system-prompt version of the same identity, so the panel has matched same-identity cross-type pairs. Then I read a base-model activation snapshot at the spot right after the context scaffold + user question (last prompt token, identical primitive for both arms) and ask whether the snapshot's geometry predicts where the marker leaks — both within each arm AND across arms.
- **What I expect.** Base-model cosine distance at the post-context+question slot predicts marker transfer across the full union grid (length-partial Spearman ρ around −0.4 to −0.6 — more geometric distance → less transfer, the convention used throughout the plan), the ICL arm produces a wider cosine spread than the SP arm (the #468 examples-beat-descriptions effect; the SP arm replicates the #474 too-tightly-clustered failure), matched same-identity cross-type pairs sit close in cosine and the marker transfers between them, mismatched cross-type pairs sit far and don't.
- **What would change my mind.** The two arms give the same cosine spread (then "ICL gives a cleaner predictor" was a #474-specific artifact); OR cross-type cosine doesn't predict cross-type transfer (the predictor is induction-mechanism-bound — a separate axis lives in each); OR matched same-identity pairs sit FAR in cosine (then "same persona" isn't an identity axis in residual space, it's a surface-form axis); OR the cosine signal collapses when the most-distinct contexts are dropped on EITHER side (the #474 stylized-driven failure mode at a new axis).

## 3. Hypothesis

Stated falsifiably with pre-thresholds. **Every H1/H2/H3/H4 statistic is computed on OFF-DIAGONAL cells only (i ≠ j; 24×23 = 552 ordered pairs per frac per seed).** Diagonals are implant sanity, not transfer.

**H1 (primary cosine-vs-JS dissociation across the union panel, ported from #474 to the union regime).** Across the 552 off-diagonal cells in a non-saturated regime, length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_DISTANCE_L21, ΔG) at the post-context+question extraction point is **negative** (cosine DISTANCE convention: more geometric distance → less transfer → expected NEGATIVE ρ) AND the 95% **dyadic cluster-bootstrap** CI (resampling source-contexts AND target-contexts independently; 5000 boots) excludes 0 on **at least one sub-epoch checkpoint**.
- PASS pre-threshold: ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG) ≤ **−0.30**.
- AND **cosine beats JS**: |ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG)| − |ρ(JS_RB, ΔG)| ≥ **0.10** with dyadic-bootstrap CI on the difference excluding 0.

H1 reported THREE WAYS for diagnostic decomposition (per #488 v2 §6.2 fix B pairwise spec, extended): (a) full union panel (552 cells), (b) within-type only (ICL-within: 16×15=240 + SP-within: 8×7=56 = 296 cells), (c) cross-type only (ICL→SP: 16×8=128 + SP→ICL: 8×16=128 = 256 cells). The PASS threshold above applies to (a); (b) and (c) are reported descriptively to localize where the signal lives. If (a) PASSes but (c) is NULL, the predictor is within-type only — the cross-type Goal answer is negative.

**H2 (decisive sharpening — drop-most-distinct-contexts survival; three-outcome).** The H1 partial ρ remains negative with consistent sign after BOTH (a) dropping the **6 hand-coded strong-kind contexts** as SOURCE OR TARGET (extending v2's {IK06, IK12, IK13, IK16} with the SP arm's {SP03 pirate, SP04 comedian, SP05 villain} where SP05 is the only added strong-kind since the matched-identity pirate/comedian SP's overlap; net strong set = {IK06, IK12, IK13, IK16, SP03, SP04, SP05}, dropping any pair where i OR j is in this set leaves ≥ 270 of 552 cells), AND (b) partialling on the dual-side graded `kind_distinctness_score[i]` AND `[j]`. Three possible verdicts (SURVIVES / NULL-most-distinct-artifact / UNIDENTIFIABLE) per v2 §3 verdict tree, with the identifiability check (§6.2 fix F extended to 7 strong-kind / weak-kind cells in the high-cosine-distance band on either side).

**H3 (RE-FRAMED — within-#489 ICL-arm vs SP-arm head-to-head; the cleaner-predictor question, now matched-recipe).** Plan v2's H3 compared #489 vs the cross-experiment #488 (matched lighter recipe) and vs #474 (5-axis confounded). v3's H3 is INTERNAL — the cleanest possible comparison because BOTH arms share the same recipe / seeds / fracs / negatives / pipeline / extraction-primitive within ONE experiment. Operationalization:
- **|ρ_cosine_distance_ICL_within| − |ρ_cosine_distance_SP_within| ≥ 0.10** with **paired dyadic cluster-bootstrap CI on the difference excluding 0** (paired because the 48 LoRA-families and the recipe are shared — the only thing that varies between arms is the context-subset the cells are taken from; the bootstrap re-samples source/target contexts independently within each arm and forms the bootstrap distribution of |ρ_ICL| − |ρ_SP|; pairing is justified by shared recipe + shared LoRA families + same fracs/seeds). PASS = the paired-bootstrap CI on the difference excludes 0 AND |ρ_ICL_within| ≥ 0.30. Reframed-PASS means "the same recipe gives a cleaner cosine predictor on the ICL arm than the SP arm" — the within-#489 head-to-head the user explicitly asked for.
- **Cross-experiment delta vs #488 v2 (descriptive secondary).** Same effect-size delta computation as v2 §3 H3(a), but reported as SECONDARY since the within-#489 head-to-head is the matched comparison. If #488 v2 isn't done at write-up time, this drops with no penalty to H3.
- **Dynamic-range delta (matches v2 H3(b)).** Within-arm: fraction of #489-ICL-arm off-diagonal cells with cosine-sim ≥ 0.90 < the same fraction for #489-SP-arm. PASS = the ICL arm distribution is shifted left of the SP arm distribution (Kolmogorov-Smirnov D ≥ 0.20, p < 0.05). This is the #468 examples-beat-descriptions effect, now measured internally.
- **Qualitative survival contrast vs #474** (carries over from v2 H3(c), unchanged): #489 ICL-arm H2 SURVIVES under the source-OR-target drop where #474's recovered signal did NOT — recipe-robust qualitative claim.

H3 PASS = within-#489 paired delta clears the bar AND dynamic-range delta clears the bar. Cross-experiment delta vs #488 is descriptive secondary.

**H4 (NEW — the cross-type Goal claim, two-part).** Marker transfer occurs across induction mechanisms and is predicted by base-model representational distance; matched same-identity cross-type pairs sit close in cosine and transfer the marker, mismatched cross-type pairs sit far and don't.
- **H4(a) — cross-type cosine→ΔG signal.** On the 256 cross-type off-diagonal cells (ICL→SP + SP→ICL combined), length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG) ≤ **−0.20** (slightly lower than within-type since cross-site cosine carries more noise; see §6.0) AND dyadic-bootstrap CI excludes 0.
- **H4(b) — matched same-identity cross-type pair test.** The 4 matched pairs are (pirate-ICL [IK12] ↔ pirate-SP [SP03]), (comedian-ICL [IK13] ↔ comedian-SP [SP04]), (helpful-tutor-ICL [IK10] ↔ helpful-tutor-SP [SP06]), (concise-engineer-ICL [IK11] ↔ concise-engineer-SP [SP07]) — 4 pairs × 2 directions = 8 ordered cells. PASS = (i) those 8 cells' median cosine distance is in the bottom-30% of all 256 cross-type cells AND (ii) those 8 cells' median ΔG ≥ the median ΔG on a permutation null of 8 randomly drawn mismatched cross-type cells (paired-bootstrap on the cell-level ΔG difference; CI excludes 0 with positive sign).

H4 PASS requires BOTH (a) AND (b). H4 NULL on (b) but PASS on (a) reads as "cross-type cosine predicts transfer broadly but matched same-identity pairs don't get a *premium* over their cosine distance" — informative null about identity-vs-distance separability.

**Diagonal-adjusted statistic** (required for H1/H2/H3/H4 SURVIVES verdicts, inherited from #488 v2 §3 fix C). SURVIVES requires the signed ρ also survives BOTH (a) partial out the source's own diagonal `emission_ii`, (b) normalized outcome `emission_ij / max(emission_ii, ε)` with sources `< 0.5 nat` dropped. If (a) or (b) collapses, report as **NULL after diagonal-adjustment**.

Pre-registered null states: H1 ρ = 0, H2 ρ = 0, H3 within-arm delta = 0, H4(a) ρ = 0, H4(b) matched-vs-mismatched delta = 0. Statistical primary = **dyadic cluster-bootstrap** (5000 boots, resample sources and targets independently within the relevant cell subset). Secondary = **crossed random-effects** `delta_g ~ predictor + length + (1|source_context) + (1|target_context) + (1|source_type)`. PASS requires dyadic CI to exclude 0; mixed-model fixed-effect CI agrees on sign as a survival check.

```python
@dataclass(frozen=True)
class SPContext:
    cid: str              # "SP01", "SP02", ...
    name: str             # plain-English label
    system_prompt: str    # the persona system prompt
    is_strong_kind: int   # 0 or 1, frozen at planning time
    matched_icl_cid: str | None  # e.g. "IK12" for SP03 pirate; None for unmatched

# IK contexts inherit v2's dataclass exactly

UnionContext = ICLContext | SPContext

def build_union_prompt(ctx: UnionContext, question: str, tokenizer) -> str:
    """Construct the prompt for either context type.
    Both end with the same `<|im_start|>assistant\n` generation-prompt suffix,
    so the LAST PROMPT TOKEN is the same chat-template scaffold token for both.
    """
    if isinstance(ctx, ICLContext):
        messages = list(ctx.icl_block) + [{"role": "user", "content": question}]
    else:  # SPContext
        messages = [
            {"role": "system", "content": ctx.system_prompt},
            {"role": "user", "content": question},
        ]
    return tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
        messages, tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True
    )
```

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.0 Cross-site cosine comparability — the critical measurement question

The v3 design extracts the cosine predictor at the last prompt token of `[scaffold] + [Q]` for both arms. The PRIMITIVE is byte-identical (`last_token_acts`, same layer, same hidden-state indexing); the SCAFFOLD differs (system+user-template vs ICL-block+user-template). Three concerns and the design's responses:

1. **Are the two extraction sites the SAME slot in the chat template?** Yes. Both `apply_chat_template(..., add_generation_prompt=True)` end with the same suffix tokens (Qwen-2.5 chat template: `\n<|im_start|>assistant\n` after the last user turn). The LAST token of the resulting prompt is byte-identical across types — both are reading the residual at the post-`<|im_start|>assistant\n` position immediately before the model would generate its first response token. This is the Hendel/Todd extraction site for ICL (they used `last token of [ICL block] + dummy/real query`) and the Persona-Vectors recipe (a) site for SP (last input token of `[system prompt + user question]`). Both are validated extraction sites in their respective literatures.
2. **Do the two scaffolds put the residual in COMPARABLE geometric subspaces?** This is the load-bearing concern. SP prompts are short (~30-100 tokens) and shaped by the system-token at the very start; ICL prompts are long (~1500-2500 tokens) and shaped by the ICL block tokens just before the user question. The residual at the LAST prompt token integrates the WHOLE prefix, so the two will reflect different prefix histories. Cosine cares about angle, not magnitude — so the question is whether the angle-of-residual is COMMENSURATE across types. The strong prior is YES: both Hendel et al. and Persona-Vectors found that the last-prompt-token residual encodes the "task to perform / persona to adopt" identity in a way that's robust to scaffold length and surface form. The literature suggests these representations live in a shared task/persona subspace at mid-late layers (L15-L21 in #474, L18-L27 in #468) — that's exactly the layer band v3 sweeps. **The empirical check is the Phase-0 gate (§4.2 gate 2 cross-type band-overlap):** if the cross-type cosine distance distribution sits entirely outside the within-arm distributions, the two extraction sites are NOT producing commensurate vectors and H4 is pre-stated UNIDENTIFIABLE. The plan handles this empirically rather than assuming commensurability.
3. **The H4(b) matched same-identity pair test is the CLEAN read of cross-site comparability.** If pirate-ICL and pirate-SP sit close in cosine despite different scaffold lengths, the cosine geometry DOES capture identity in a scaffold-independent way (the H4 motivation). If they sit FAR, then either (i) the scaffold-length effect dominates, OR (ii) "same persona" isn't an identity axis in residual space, OR (iii) the persona is induced differently enough by the two mechanisms that the base model genuinely represents them as different. The plan reports each of these explicit failure modes and uses the per-pair cosine + ΔG to discriminate them.

**Conclusion: cosine distance is well-defined and Hendel/Todd/Persona-Vectors-grounded at the metric level for both extraction sites; whether it MEASURES the same construct across types is an empirical question the Phase-0 gate + H4(b) test answers. The plan does NOT assume cross-site cosine commensurability — it tests for it.**

### 6.2 Headline statistic, thresholds, and bootstrap

**Universe of analysis: off-diagonal cells only (i ≠ j; 552 pairs per frac per seed).** Same restriction as v2; diagonals are implant-strength controls.

- **Primary statistic (H1):** length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG), partialled on `log(prompt_tokens + R_tokens)`, computed on the FULL panel + within-ICL panel + within-SP panel + cross-type panel separately, per frac per seed.

- **H2 statistic + verdict tree** (as v2 §6.2 extended): same with `is_strong_kind` partial + `kind_distinctness_score` graded partial, on the extended 8-strong set; SURVIVES requires source-OR-target drop AND dual-side graded partial.
- **H3 statistic (NEW v3 framing — within-#489 paired):**

  - Compute ρ_SP = ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG | length) on the 56 SP-within off-diagonal cells.

  - 4 matched pairs × 2 directions = 8 ordered cells. Their median cosine distance must sit in the bottom-30% of all 256 cross-type cells (a non-parametric closeness check).

**Smoke calibration gate (Phase 2)**: run IK01 + IK13 + SP01 + SP04 at lr=2e-6, r=16, 150 rows, 3 epochs total, saving at all 6 fracs. PASS requires ALL — failing ANY emits `epm:failure v1 reason: <which>` and exits BLOCKED:

1. **Label-mask audit (extended to 4 row types per §4.4)**: positive ICL + positive SP + negative ICL→SP + negative SP→ICL. All 4 must show correct loss positions. Otherwise → `reason: label_mask_wrong_slot`.
2. **In-band source ΔG PER ARM (TWO-SIDED — distinguishes saturation from dead-floor)**: for ALL 4 smoke cells, ≥ 3 of the 6 fracs have source `delta_g_ii ∈ [5, 20] nat`. Pick 3 most evenly spread that are in-band PER ARM; if the in-band frac sets are disjoint between arms, document per-arm fracs as methodology divergence (the analysis pools at the common frac if one exists; else `reason: smoke_no_common_inband_frac`). Same TOO-HOT / TOO-COLD / MIXED diagnosis as v2 §7 with the same matched LIGHTER / HEAVIER pre-registered fallbacks, applied PER ARM.
3. **Off-diagonal saturation gate** (v2 §7 gate 3 extended): for IK01 AND SP01 at each picked frac, evaluate the adapter on at least 6 off-diagonal target contexts including ≥ 2 of the OTHER arm. At least one picked frac must have `tie_mass_off ≤ 0.85` on this 6-cell mini-grid for each smoke source. Otherwise → `reason: offdiag_saturated_at_all_fracs`.
4. **EOS-gradient check** (v2 §7 gate 4 extended): at the latest picked frac, IK01's trained model on IK01→SP01 off-diagonal cell AND SP01's trained model on SP01→IK01 off-diagonal cell must show trained `log P(' ※')` at the post-response slot moved DOWN by at least 0.2 nats vs the base model. Otherwise → `reason: eos_gradient_inactive`.
5. **K=4 ICL block length sanity** (v2 §7 gate 5 unchanged): SP rows fit easily in seq_len=4096; ICL row truncation check stays the binding constraint.
6. **Cross-site cosine commensurability check (NEW v3 gate 6)**: on the 4 smoke cells, compute base-model cosine_L21 distance for all 6 pairs (4×3 = 12 off-diagonal smoke-pair cells). Report (i) the 2 within-ICL pair, (ii) the 2 within-SP pair, (iii) the 8 cross-type pairs. If cross-type cosine distances are all > the maximum within-arm distance + 0.3 (i.e. the cross-type cells live in a different magnitude regime entirely), surface a WARN — H4 may be UNIDENTIFIABLE per Phase-0 gate 2. Not a BLOCK because Phase-0 gate 2 will catch this with the full 24-context cosine matrix; the smoke read is an early warning.

Smoke cost: ~1.8 GPU-hr on 1× H100 (4 cells × 6 fracs train + 4 × 6-cell off-diag eval + label-mask audit + EOS-gradient + truncation sanity + cross-site cosine check). Wall: ~75 min.

## 11. Decision Rationale

All v2 §11 entries carry over (base model, marker, lr, LoRA r/α, dropout, schedule, epochs/fracs, batch, seq_len, K, positive rows, negative rows, seeds, JS R, JS Q'_probe, on-policy N=8 × 20 Q, cosine layers/headline L21, held-out Q=20, ICL predictor extraction point). v3 only adds / changes the entries below:

- **Context panel = 16 ICL + 8 SP union (NEW).** Why: the Goal demands "UNION panel … including cross-type cells in both directions." The user explicitly rejected v2's STANDALONE-ICL recommendation at the approval gate. N_SP = 8 sized to give (a) the 4 H4(b) matched same-identity pairs (the cleanest cross-mechanism transfer test), (b) the 3 SP-arm strong-kind anchors (SP03, SP04, SP05), (c) the canonical default-assistant (SP01), (d) 1 SP-arm non-stylized non-matched anchor (SP02 software engineer) for within-SP cosine spread + 1 SP CoT-style anchor (SP08) for partial-match to IK06. N_SP = 16 (parity with ICL arm) ballooned compute to ~120h; N_SP = 4 collapsed the SP-side dyadic-bootstrap cluster count below the v2 floor. Source: `body.md` frontmatter Goal + `tasks/planning/489/plans/v2.md` + `tasks/planning/488/body.md` + `tasks/planning/488/plans/plan.md` v2 (matched comparator recipe).

- **SP01-SP05 system_prompt strings = #406 A1-A5 verbatim.** Why: zero-divergence inheritance from the parent line (#406 → #474 → #488 v2 all use these strings); preserves cross-experiment comparability for any secondary descriptive deltas. Source: `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/i406_conditions.py:60-92` (read 2026-06-04). Verified the file's `Condition` definitions are unchanged on `main`.

- **SP06, SP07, SP08 system_prompt strings = newly curated for v3.** Why: H4(b) requires same-identity matched pairs for the 3 ICL personas (helpful-tutor IK10, concise-engineer IK11, math-CoT IK06) that lack A-class twins. SP06 ("patient, encouraging tutor … step by step") matches IK10's helpful-tutor demonstration. SP07 ("concise engineer … as few words as possible") matches IK11's concise-engineer demonstration. Source: planner-curated, `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` for the matched-cosine check (Phase-0 cosine commensurability reports whether each matched pair sits in the bottom-30% of cross-type cosine distance; if not, the SP string is rewritten before launch — see §11 plan-deviations-allowed).

- **N_SP = 8 (size choice).** Why: cost-vs-power sweet spot. N_SP = 4 → SP-within bootstrap collapses (< 6 source-clusters). N_SP = 16 → compute ~120h, past auto-approve cap. N_SP = 8 → 256 cross-type cells, 8 source-clusters on SP side ≥ #488-v2-style floor, matched-pair set complete. Source: `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (no prior issue in this lineage sized an SP-arm cluster pool); the calculation uses the v2 §9 "12 contexts is the cluster-bootstrap floor" rule applied per-side.

- **H3 reframe: within-#489 ICL-vs-SP head-to-head as PRIMARY, #488 v2 as SECONDARY.** Why: the user's "compare ICL vs SP" intent at the approval gate IS the within-#489 head-to-head. Pairing the comparison within ONE experiment (shared recipe, shared seeds, shared LoRA families, shared eval pipeline) gives a much narrower CI than the cross-experiment delta vs #488 (independent two-sample bootstrap, variance adds). Source: revision brief point 6 + paired-bootstrap statistical convention; user override at the plan-approval gate.

- **H4 NEW: cross-type cosine→ΔG + matched same-identity pair test.** Why: the Goal explicitly names "cross-type cells in both directions" and "matched same-identity cross-type pairs." Source: `body.md` frontmatter Goal + revision brief point 6.

- **Phase-0 cross-type band-overlap gate (NEW).** Why: cross-site cosine commensurability is the load-bearing measurement-validity question for the union design (§6.0).
