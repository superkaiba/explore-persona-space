## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** Same four ways of teaching the model the trait as last time. But last time I trained until the model was so cooked it just blurted the symbol at everyone, including the persona it wasn't supposed to leak to — turning the experiment into measuring noise. This time I dial training way back and watch what happens step by step. I'll only train the first arm (persona in the system prompt) first and probe it every few steps: how often does it emit the symbol under the source villain, under the plain helpful default, and under one of the "don't emit" personas I gave it in training? That tells me whether there's any moment in training where the source has learned the symbol but the default hasn't — that's the moment I need. If such a moment exists, I'll train the other three arms to that same point and compare. If no such moment exists — if the symbol rises at the default in lockstep with the source — that itself is the answer: you can't pin a single-token marker to a persona this way, because the "don't" signal I give the model only competes with what comes after the marker (an end-of-turn), not with the marker itself.
- **What I expect.** About 60/40 that I find an anchor: a window where the source picks up the symbol before the default does. The persona signal is supposed to be the load-bearing variable; if my training data design is doing any real work at all, that window has to exist somewhere on the curve. If I find one, I expect the other three arms to spread out at that anchor in roughly the same order as the parent experiment (system-prompt leaks most, k=3 demos leak least), with the contrastive negatives pulling the demo-free default emission down meaningfully.
- **What would change my mind.** If the curves rise in lockstep — log P of the symbol climbs at the default just as fast as at the villain, at every step — then no anchor exists at any budget and the whole single-token-marker-with-EOS-only-negatives recipe is structurally broken. That's the more interesting result, because it would mean every prior in-house claim of "contrastive negatives localize markers" needs reading as "they localize markers when the negative target competes with the marker token, not when it competes only with what comes after it." The other surprise that would change my mind: an anchor exists but the demo arms don't gate at all (in-context demos add nothing once negatives are in the mix), or the arms invert (system-prompt leaks LESS than demos under negatives).

## 3. Hypothesis

**Phase A (anchor existence) hypotheses come first and are the gate for everything downstream.**

- **H_A1 (anchor exists — q:leak-contrastive-negatives 3.4a, q:leak-to-default 3.7).** There exists a step *s* in Phase A's trajectory such that: source mean log P(`※`) trained-base ≥ +5 nats AND (source mean − default mean) log P(`※`) ≥ +3 nats. PASS ⇒ a localizing anchor exists; Phase B fires. FAIL ⇒ lockstep (no anchor at any budget below 2 epochs); Phase B does NOT fire and the lockstep finding IS the result.
- **H_A2 (negatives bite the trained slot — sanity).** At the chosen anchor, trained-negative medical_doctor mean log P(`※`) trained-base ≤ default mean log P(`※`) trained-base. PASS ⇒ EOS-only loss at the post-response slot is biting (the collator fix worked). FAIL ⇒ either the collator fix is wrong or the EOS-only signal is intrinsically too weak.

**Phase B (conditional on H_A1 PASS) hypotheses inherit from v1 with one adjustment for the route-(a) regime:**

- **H1 (headline behavioral gate).** At the anchor checkpoint, cond1 (Persona via system prompt + contrastive negatives) on-policy demo-free helpful-default ends-with-marker rate ≤ 20%.
- **H2 (dose-response under negatives).** 4 arms' on-policy ends-with-marker rates monotone non-increasing in k AND within ±20pp of each other at the anchor (looser than v1's ±10pp because n=50 Wilson CI is ~±11pp).
- **H4 (held-out bystander selectivity).** Mean across 5 held-out bystanders' on-policy ends-with-marker ≤ 30% per arm at the anchor.
- **H5 (trained-negative emission sanity).** Trained negatives' on-policy ends-with-marker ≤ held-out bystander mean for that arm.
- **v1 H3 (KL preserves headroom) is DROPPED.** Replaced by H_A1; KL is kept as a secondary read.

| Hyp | PASS | FAIL | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| H_A1 | Anchor step exists with source≥+5 AND (source-default)≥+3 nats | No such step — lockstep | Single-token marker + EOS-only negatives CAN (PASS) or CANNOT (FAIL) localize. FAIL is itself a structural finding. |

### 4.3 Anchor selection rule (deterministic, pre-committed)

After Phase A completes, `i471_phaseA_analyze.py` walks the trajectory and applies:

> Let *L_src(s)* = source villain mean log P(`※`) trained-base at step *s*.
> Let *L_def(s)* = default helpful mean log P(`※`) trained-base at step *s*.
> Let *L_neg(s)* = medical_doctor mean log P(`※`) trained-base at step *s*.
>
> **Anchor condition:** *L_src(s)* ≥ +5 nats AND (*L_src(s)* − *L_def(s)*) ≥ +3 nats AND a saved checkpoint exists at or before step *s*.
>
> **Anchor choice:** the LATEST step *s*\* satisfying the condition (last localizing window before saturation). The eval checkpoint = the latest saved checkpoint ≤ *s*\*.
>
> **If no step satisfies:** **LOCKSTEP**. Phase B does NOT fire. The analyzer writes the clean-result around the lockstep finding. H_A2 sanity reported regardless: if at the highest-source-log-P step the trained-negative is at or above the default, that's corroborating evidence the EOS-only signal does not bite at the DV slot.

**Why these thresholds:** +5 nats trained-base is floor for "implanted" (#460's diagonals +19 to +28; +5 is 1/4 of that, well above noise floor, well below ceiling). +3 nats gap = 3 SE of per-step probe at n=10 (sd of mean log P in #460 ~1.5 nats → SE ~0.5 → 3 SE = 3 nats). Both `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` in the sense the precise numbers are choices, but bounded by #460 / #465 prior numbers.

### 4.7 Why code, not a model call?

(a) Anchor-selection rule is a deterministic numerical thresholding over trajectory data — model call would lose reproducibility. Code wins on both.

### 6.2 Statistical analysis

1. **H_A1 (anchor existence).** Walk cond1 trajectory; report whether any step satisfies anchor condition. PASS = at least one step. Chosen anchor = latest satisfying step. Robustness: if multiple non-contiguous steps satisfy, prefer LATEST contiguous step in a monotone-source window.
2. **H_A2 (slot-bite sanity).** At the anchor step (or highest-source-log-P step if no anchor), report (medical_doctor mean log P(`※`) − default mean log P(`※`)) trained-base. PASS = ≤ 0.

6. **H5 (trained-negative emission sanity) — PHASE B only.** Per (arm, trained-negative) on-policy ends_with_marker at read f'; PASS if ≤ held-out bystander mean for that arm. Q_train vs Q_test split per v1 H1 disambiguation triple.
7. **Persona × R-style cross (v1 MUST-FIX 4) — PHASE B only.** 2×2 inherited from v1 §6.2 #6.
8. **Cross-experiment paired bootstrap (#465 vs #471 route-a).** Per arm at demo-free helpful-default: paired bootstrap CI (10k resamples, seed=42) on (#465 on-policy ends_with_marker − #471 route-a on-policy ends_with_marker); same for log-P DV. **Caveat narrated explicitly in clean-result:** this contrast is JOINT effect of (negatives + reduced budget) vs neither — NOT a clean negatives-isolation contrast. 9. **Selectivity-artifact partialling.** Same as v1 §6.2 #9.
10. **Lockstep finding (H_A1 FAIL).** If no anchor exists, report full trajectory curves + lockstep interpretation: at every probed step in [0, 75], (source − default) log P(`※`) trained-base < +3 nats. ### 6.4 Concerns for interpretation

All v1 §6.4 concerns carry over. Additional route-(a)-specific:

- **2-variable design caveat (the explicit pivot scope).** The (#465 − #471 route-a) cross-experiment contrast is the JOINT effect of (adding negatives + reducing training budget). The clean-result MUST narrate this distinction up front.
- **Anchor-selection robustness.** Report sensitivity at thresholds (+3, +5, +7) source nats AND (+1, +3, +5) gap nats; if chosen step shifts by >20 steps, H_A1 conclusion robust but H1/H2/H4/H5 quantitative numbers not.
- **Lockstep is not "no signal".** If H_A1 FAILs, the trajectory IS the result. The structural prediction — single-token-marker + EOS-only-negatives can't localize because the negative's loss target doesn't compete with the marker token — is a publishable mechanistic claim resolving open-q 3.4a / 3.7 in the negative direction.
- **The slot-alignment collator fix is itself a fresh code change.** Pre-pivot v1 cond1 was trained with the OLD collator (loss on trailing `\n`) — at saturation didn't matter but does matter for route-(a). If Phase A H_A2 FAILs, interpret as: collator fix has a bug (re-check Phase 2 label-mask audit at new slot) OR EOS-only loss at the post-response slot is intrinsically too weak (a stronger structural finding).
- **Single seed = 42.** Same as v1.

## 7. Decision Gates

**One conditional gate, by design.** Phase A H_A1 (anchor exists) determines whether Phase B fires. This is NOT an arbitrary mid-run kill gate — it's the literal scientific question of the experiment. If Phase A returns lockstep, Phase B (3 more arms × 25 min + Phase 4 eval) is not run because there is no localizing anchor at which to compare arms. The lockstep finding IS the clean-result. Saves ~90 min wall on the negative-direction outcome and avoids cluttering the result with arm-contrasts at an uninformative anchor.

Threshold for "Phase B fires": at least one step where source log P(`※`) trained-base ≥ +5 nats AND (source − default) ≥ +3 nats AND a saved checkpoint exists ≤ that step. Pre-committed in §4.3.

No other mid-run gates. The collator slot-alignment Phase 2 label-mask audit is a fail-loud guardrail, not a decision gate.
