# Plan — Issue #1489: Context-augmentation effects on the context→answer-state transport map + dose-matched context-distillation in-weights comparison

Plan v2 (v1 + mechanical verifier fixes only: c12 battery arithmetic, c6 (a)–(k) reuse map, c8 success/kill blocks, c30 realized-keys probe — no design change) · kind: experiment · workflow: v1 · parent: #1092 · Goal re-read at return time (no `epm:goal-updated` markers; current).

## 0.0 TL;DR

- **What I'll run:** I take about 2,000 real conversation snippets and add four kinds of material to each one — a fact about the user, a behavioral instruction, a persona, or a formatting rule. I then check whether the same simple map that predicts "where the model's answer will land" from "where its context sits" still works on the modified conversations, or whether each kind of addition needs its own map. For eight of the additions I also train the material directly into the model's weights, matched in strength to the prompted version, and check whether that moves each individual answer the same way the prompt did.
- **What I expect:** Instructions and personas will shift contexts in one consistent direction the existing map already handles. Formatting rules will break the map the most, since they change the whole shape of the answer. A fact will move answers only on the questions it is relevant to, and I expect the context reading to already carry that relevance. The trained-in versions should match the prompted shifts on average but fire on irrelevant questions more than the prompt does.
- **What would change my mind:** If the plain map carries every augmentation nearly as well as a dedicated refit, the map is far more universal than the theory requires, and the predictor line gets simpler. If relevance shows up in the answers but not in the context reading, the linear-map account is wrong and we need the nonlinear version. If the trained-in shift matches the prompted shift example by example, prompting is a faithful cheap stand-in for finetuning in this coordinate system.

## 1. Goal

"Characterize how the pre-generation context→answer-state transport map h (c(x)→v(x); ridge primary, MLP secondary) changes when contexts are augmented with facts, instructions, personas, and formatting constraints — distinguishing movement within a fixed map from changes of the map (5×5 transport-transfer matrix), testing additivity/effective-rank of augmentation deltas, testing whether relevance gating is captured linearly — and whether delivering the same information in-weights via dose-matched context distillation produces the same per-example answer-state shift, preserves relevance gating, and leaves the pre-finetuning map valid on the finetuned model." (Task #1489 Goal, verbatim.)

One paragraph: the theory paper (Overleaf `6a2df2d2`, §Definitions + A2/A3 map c_C→v, A4 read-out stability, A5 source write, A6 scalar-gated transfer) treats h as a fixed property of the model, and the leakage predictor applies one h across context conditions that differ exactly by added facts/instructions/personas/formatting. #1092 established that on real conversations the context-based h carries nearly all transport (held-out R² 0.74–0.81 vs 0.05–0.11 prefix-based). This experiment measures h's domain of validity under controlled augmentation (Q1–Q3) and adds the in-weights arm (Q4–Q6): does finetuning the same information produce the same movement in the same coordinates (A5-adjacent), does relevance gating survive in-weights, and does the pre-FT map remain valid on the finetuned model (the assumption the pre-FT predictor requires).

## 3. Hypothesis

Five registered hypotheses (from the task body), each with its falsifier. All read at the frozen headline unit (L14, context arm, ambient basis, t1) unless stated; prefix-arm twins reported alongside per the standing both-arms rule.

- **H-additive:** personas/instructions shift c approximately additively — per-instance Δc(x,k) has R²_μ ≥ 0.5 (fraction of Var(Δc) explained by the constant μ_k) and stacked-{Δc} participation-ratio effective rank ≤ 2 per instance; facts land lower/higher-rank. *Falsified if* persona/instruction R²_μ CIs sit below 0.3 (bootstrap over base contexts) — augmentation effects are context-dependent, task-vector structure does not extend to these kinds.
- **H-fixed-map:** M_plain transports persona/instruction augmentations with modest loss (transfer gap small relative to refit ceiling); formatting is the LARGEST transport failure; facts have the smallest main effect but are gated. *Falsified if* the family ordering inverts (formatting transfers best / personas worst) or every family lands Map-changed with comparable Δexcess.
- **H-gating-in-c (default, per CAST 2409.05907):** relevance gating is already present in Δc — gating AUC in Δc (γ_c) ≥ 0.75 and the linear map reproduces the relevant-vs-irrelevant Δv̂ contrast; the MLP closes no additional gap (R²_MLP − R²_ridge on facts rows within the split-half noise band). *Falsified (the discovery outcome) if* γ_c ≈ 0.5 (Δc CI includes chance) while γ_v ≥ 0.75 AND the MLP−ridge held-out gap on facts rows exceeds its split-half noise band — linearity of h fails exactly on relevance gating.
- **H-weights-leak-broader:** at matched dose, in-weights delivery reproduces the mean shift (cos(mean Δv_FT, mean Δv_ctx) high) but loses relevance gating — FT gating AUC (behavioral fact-use on irrelevant rows AND representational Δv_FT AUC) below the ctx arm's. Directional refinement from the third lit pass: expected for behavior-like kinds (2506.19823); facts may instead UNDER-generalize (2309.12288, 2505.00661) — the per-family sign is itself a registered read. *Falsified if* FT and ctx gating indices are statistically indistinguishable (paired CI on the AUC difference includes 0) at matched dose in both fact runs.
- **H-map-survives-FT:** M_plain transports the finetuned model's plain contexts (c_θk(x) → v_θk(x)) with a gap SMALL relative to the same family's augmented-context transport gap (post-FT Δexcess < in-context Δexcess for the matched family, per run). *Falsified if* post-FT transport collapses (R² drop ≥ the worst augmented-family gap) — finetuning moves the map itself, and the pre-FT leakage predictor's core assumption fails.

**Registered verdict lattice (applied per family cell of Q1, and to the H-map-survives-FT read per FT run, on Δexcess = transfer-gap − q95(split-half refit null), 95% bootstrap CI grouped by prefix):**

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Map-changed ⇔ Δexcess > 0 AND Δexcess's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Map-preserved ⇔ Δexcess's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

**Registered paired contrast (Q4):** the per-example equivalence read pairs Δv_FT(x) with Δv_ctx(x) on the same held-out eval rows, per augmentation (8 pairs of arms).
Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-context by analysis_tensors/summaries/cell_ft_*/ t1 shards (in-weights arm) and analysis_tensors/summaries/cell_plain/ + cell_{fact,instr,persona,format}_*/ t1 shards (in-context arm), joined on base_row_id into eval_results/issue_1489/read4_equivalence.json.

**Fact/instruction relevance labels:** primary rule = a fixed mapping from the corpus's 12-way topic taxonomy to each scoped augmentation's relevant set (mapping frozen at implementation from the taxonomy label list, committed in the conditions manifest); validated by a judged relevance rubric on 400 (fact, query) probe pairs ("could this fact plausibly change a good answer to this query?", graded 0–100) — the topic rule must agree with the judged label at ≥80% on the probe set, else the judged label replaces the rule (reported).

**Why code, not a model call?** Formatting compliance (JSON validity via `json.loads`, bullet regex, lowercase check, template regex), conciseness (sentence count), and the topic→relevance rule are CODE checks: deterministic, structural, free at 38k+ rows; a model call adds noise and ~$100s for zero validity gain. Semantic behaviors (fact-use, hedging, agreement, persona consistency, refusal, relevance validation) are LLM-judged — no code proxy exists. What would flip it: if the JSON/format validators disagree with human reading on the smoke rows, those families get judge rubrics too.

### 4.3 In-weights arm (P3–P4 — provision A, continued)

**Construction (context distillation):** for 8 augmentations (2/family: `fact_veg`, `fact_python`, `instr_refuse_code`, `instr_agree`, `persona_pirate`, `persona_doctor`, `format_json`, `format_lowercase`), LoRA-finetune the plain model on pairs (plain context x_train, answer generated under x_train⊕k). Training rows: 650 of the 800 train rows (150 held out as the dose-probe set). Targets are the P1 generations — already produced. Loss on answer tokens only.

- **Completion provenance:** distillation targets are **on-policy, instruct-and-strip (elicitation tier 2)** by construction — the base model generated them under the augmented context and the augmentation is stripped before training. This IS the context-distillation construction (2112.00861 / 2209.15189) and satisfies `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` with zero canned rows. Yield quota: N/A — greedy generation yields exactly one target per row; no judge-filter on training targets (the construction transfers the augmentation's full effect, compliant or not — dose is measured downstream, not filtered upstream).
- **Contrastive negatives:** the distillation mix over the full query distribution structurally contains same-question negatives — irrelevant/unaffected queries produce (near-)unchanged answers, so the mix teaches "change only where the context would have changed you". No persona-panel negatives are added: this design implants into the DEFAULT context (not a source persona), the manipulated variable is in-context vs in-weights delivery of the SAME information, and adding rows the in-context arm never saw would break the matched-information construction. Named exemption: faithful replication of the published context-distillation construction (the positive-only shape is the recipe under test); the no-panel regime is carried into the clean-result as a scope caveat.
- **Training config:** LoRA r=32, α=64, `use_rslora=True`, all-7 target modules, lr=1e-5, batch 2 × grad-accum 8, bf16 (Source: #778 — the arXiv 2507.21509 finetune recipe verbatim, adapter-config-verified on HF). 4 epochs, checkpoint every 0.5 epoch → 8 checkpoints/run. This is NOT a marker implant (no marker-recipe lr override applies; the payload is a distillation target, trained with standard SFT loss).
- **Dose matching (P4):** at each of the 8 checkpoints, generate on the 150-row dose-probe set (vLLM `enable_lora=True`, `max_lora_rank=32`, one engine per run serving all 8 checkpoints as LoRARequests) → judge/code-score compliance per family rubric → select c* = argmin |compliance_FT(c*) − compliance_ctx| where compliance_ctx is the in-context arm's judged compliance on the same probe rows. Matched if within ±10 points (0–100 scale); else the closest checkpoint is used and the run is flagged `dose-unmatched` (pre-registered fallback read, reported — never silently dropped). Fixed-epoch comparison is banned for the headline (#612).
- **Baseline propensity (both sides):** plain-context compliance per rubric is measured on the same probe rows (the eval-side base rate — the delta denominator); the "source side" is the single default context, whose base rate is the same read. Both enter the compliance deltas.
- **P4b FT eval (provision B):** for the 8 selected checkpoints: generation + capture (same kinds, same rig) on the 1,200 plain eval rows → cells `cell_ft_<slug>`. This yields Δv_FT(x) = v_θk(x) − v_θ0(x) and (c_θk(x), v_θk(x)) for the map-stability read. Adapters (all checkpoints) upload to the HF model repo before teardown.

## 7. Decision Gates

Wall-clock exceeds 4 h and the pipeline is multi-provision, so three minimal gates (each with a cheap intermediate signal); jointly satisfiable by construction (they gate different quantities at different phases; no two demand opposite outcomes on the same measurement).

- **G1 — manipulation-check floor (after judge batch #1; graceful degradation, not all-or-nothing):** an augmentation instance whose designed-effect compliance delta over the plain baseline is < 20 points (graded 0–100 mean, its designed subset) or whose code-check pass rate is < 60% is EXCLUDED from headline transfer cells and reported; a family retaining ≥2 instances proceeds; a family below 2 is reported as manipulation-failed (finding, not silent drop). Grounding: an augmentation that does not change behavior cannot test transport of a behavioral shift; thresholds are floors on the instrument, not effect-size bets (sign: positive delta, the direction every compliance manipulation trivially produces when it works at all). FT runs whose in-context twin failed G1 are re-pointed at the family's next-strongest instance BEFORE P3 (checkpointed pipeline; P3 runs after judge batch #1 only for re-pointed cases — default order keeps P3 on provision A since the 8 defaults are the strongest-prior instances).
- **G2 — fit-substrate kill check (first P6 unit):** if the plain-map diagonal (M_plain on plain, held-out, L14/context/ambient/t1) lands R² < 0.30, the substrate is too small for map estimation and every transfer read is uninterpretable → STOP P6, surface for re-plan (likely fix: enlarge the plain pool; captures and generations remain valid). Grounding: #1092 measured 0.74–0.81 at n≈17–19k; at n≈6k a material drop is expected but a fall below 0.30 (less than half the parent's floor cell) indicates the estimator, not the effect, dominates. Sign: R² positive — every prior fit of this construct on real data cleared 0.5.
- **G3 — fit-phase pilot gate (P6 first unit, pilot-gated basis):** the first ambient unit is timed end-to-end through the production entrypoint; projected total = units × measured / boxes re-checked against the §9 row; >2× → vectorize-signature check + scope reduction (drop L18/L19 pca48 sensitivity units first — §9 stratification) before proceeding. Grounding: mandated by plan-compute-sizing for pilot-gated fit rows; #1092's measured walls ran ~100–200× its FLOP projection.

Dose-matching has a pre-registered FALLBACK READ (closest checkpoint + `dose-unmatched` flag), not a gate — the run never aborts on it.

### Success criteria

The run SUCCEEDS as an instrument — independently of which hypothesis wins — when BOTH hold:

- **S1 (Q1 resolution):** the §3 registered verdict lattice (declared there, DISJOINT and exhaustive) resolves to Map-changed or Map-preserved — i.e. NOT Inconclusive — for ≥4 of the 5 family cells at the frozen headline unit (L14, context arm, ambient basis, t1).
- **S2 (Q4 resolution):** the per-example alignment read resolves against its registered nulls for ≥6 of the 8 FT runs — the observed per-augmentation alignment statistic's 95% bootstrap CI (grouped by prefix) is disjoint from the derangement null's 95% band on at least one side. Either direction counts: a resolved "no per-example excess beyond the mean-only null" is a SUCCESS outcome (it answers Q4), not a failure.

An outcome below either bar (e.g. 3 of 5 family cells Inconclusive) is reported as an instrument-resolution shortfall with the realized CIs/bands shown — a finding about sensitivity at this n, never silently narrated as an effect.

### Kill criteria

Two quantitative kills — both restate gate thresholds already defined above (G1/G2); labeled here explicitly. Everything else degrades gracefully (G1 per-instance floors with re-pointing, the dose-unmatched fallback read, the §9 stratified descope ladder) and never kills the run.

- **K1 — fit-substrate kill (= G2, verbatim threshold):** plain-map diagonal held-out R² < 0.30 at the frozen headline unit on the first P6 unit → the n≈6k substrate is too small for map estimation and every transfer read is uninterpretable. STOP P6, surface for re-plan (likely fix: enlarge the plain pool, ~+2 GPU-h; the generations + captures remain valid inputs to the re-plan).
- **K2 — augmentation-library wipeout (manipulation-check kill):** at the G1 read (after judge batch #1), ≥3 of the 4 families fail G1 — i.e. retain <2 instances at the G1 floors (designed-effect compliance delta ≥ 20 points graded 0–100 on the designed subset, or code-check pass rate ≥ 60%) → the augmentation library itself failed to manipulate behavior, so transport-of-a-behavioral-shift cannot be tested with it. STOP before any further FT-arm spend (no checkpoint selection, no re-pointed P3, no provision B, no augmented-cell P6 fits) and surface for library re-plan; the manipulation-check table is reported as the round's finding. A single- or two-family failure stays the G1 graceful-degradation path — this kill fires only on the ≥3-of-4 wipeout.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Ridge + PRESS λ on grid {0.01…1000}** — What: primary fit family. Why: the line's standard estimator; matched effective dof across all map comparisons. Source: #1092 (engine defaults; `issue658_fit_predictors.RIDGE_LAMBDAS`).
- **Grouped 6-fold by prefix_id, seed 0** — Why: novel-prefix generalization + kills the augmented-variant leakage trap. Source: #1092. Alternatives: pointwise LOO (rejected — within-family interpolation, ood-generalization-folds rule).
- **Frozen layers {14,18,19}, headline L14; bases ambient+pca48; target t1** — Why: pre-registered positions remove selection effects; t1 is the theory's v(x). Source: #1092 plan v6 (frozen-layer amendment) + Overleaf §Definitions. Alternatives: 28-layer fit sweep (rejected: #1092 measured it at ~3,200 machine-h).
- **Decoding: vLLM greedy, 1024 max tokens, seed 42** — Why: byte-matched to the parent for comparability of captures. Source: #1092 (`issue1092_gpu_phase.py`).
- **Context pool: 2,000 crossed + 4,000 plain-only; 4 instances/family** — Why: 2,000×16 crossing bounds generation cost at ~2× the parent's while n/family (8k) exceeds the parent's dense core (4,752) that resolved factor reads; 6k plain rows keep M_plain estimable; #763 showed n=50 is unidentifiable. Source: #1092 (dense-core precedent) + #763 (lower bound); the specific 2,000/4,000 split is a sizing choice — `ungrounded — G2 kill check is the guard`.
- **≥150 eval rows per relevant subset** — Why: gating AUC at n=150/arm gives CI half-width ≈0.09 at AUC 0.75. Source: standard binomial/AUC arithmetic; pre-registered floor.
- **LoRA r=32, α=64, rsLoRA, all-7 modules, lr=1e-5, bs2×ga8** — Why: the validated behavior-SFT recipe for this exact base model; NOT a marker implant (no lr≤5e-6 override — that window is marker-loss-specific). Source: #778 (adapter-config-verified) = arXiv 2507.21509 finetune recipe.
- **4 epochs, checkpoint every 0.5 epoch** — Why: dose-to-target requires training PAST the target with a grid fine enough to bracket it; #612's dose bands were set by epoch 1, so 4 epochs over-shoots deliberately and 8 checkpoints bracket the crossing. Source: #612 (dose-to-target standard); grid spacing `ungrounded — smoke-checked` (the dose-probe trajectory itself verifies bracketing; fallback read registered).
- **Dose-match tolerance ±10 points (0–100)** — Why: half the #612 minimum band separation (~20 pts rescaled). Source: #612 (band separations); the exact ±10 is `ungrounded — pre-registered judgment call`, with the closest-checkpoint fallback ensuring no data loss either way.
- **650/150 train/probe split of the 800 train rows** — Why: probe rows must be training-distribution but unseen; 150 gives judge-mean SE ≈ 2 pts at score-sd 25. Source: sizing arithmetic; split ratio a judgment call within it.
- **Judge N=5 draws, temp 1.0, max_tokens 300, graded 0–100** — Source: `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rules 4/23 + #1092 (N=5 precedent); max_tokens 300 per rule 23 (reason-then-score floor; supersedes the parent's 256).
- **Null draws: 200 random-map / 20 refit twins / 200 label-perm / derangement within topic stratum** — Source: #1092 (200-draw parity for cheap bands); 20 refit twins per the §2 divergence-4 rationale (the transfer statistic's noise floor is refit noise; twins are the engine's `_refit_twins` mechanism).
- **MLP companion: hidden 512, ≤300 epochs, target dim 48, batched LOCO** — Source: #1092 MLP-companion config + `vectorized_mlp_skill` (canonical batched helper).
