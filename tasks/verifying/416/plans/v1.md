<h2>1. Goal &amp; Hypothesis</h2>

<p><strong>Goal (from frontmatter, refined to match clarifier-locked source = <code>software_engineer</code>):</strong> Train ※ into a low-baseline non-librarian source persona and check whether (a) the new source ALSO never leads the leaderboard and (b) the same top-6 bystander cluster from #398 still dominates — confirming the global-marker-affinity-shift mechanism, or ruling it out in favor of a source-specific residual-stream direction.</p>

<p><strong>Three-way hypothesis space (v3, replaces v2 binary H1/H2 per critic-round-1 fix #3).</strong> The binary "stays out of top-6" H1 was too weak: software_engineer-as-bystander in #398 already sits at rank ~22 (best 19, median 22), so "stays out of top-6" is satisfied even if training does NOTHING to it. And #398's own data shows the trained librarian gained +13 ranks and ~+1.23 nat above its closest cosine-twin (software_engineer-as-bystander-#398), i.e. a measurable source-specific bump ON TOP of the global shift. The clean test asks BOTH "where does swe land" AND "by how much does training elevate swe specifically vs the rest of the panel". Three outcomes:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>H1a — global shift PLUS source-specific bump (#398's reading is INCOMPLETE).</strong> Software_engineer elevates substantially (Δrank from step-5 baseline ≳ +10, comparable to librarian's +13 in #398) and ends ≳ 1 nat above software_engineer-as-bystander-in-#398 at matched late checkpoints, but stays out of the top 6 throughout. The leaderboard still doesn't flip, but the implant DOES bind to the source persona on top of the panel-wide affinity shift.</li>
<li><strong>H1b — pure global shift (#398's reading replicates CLEANLY).</strong> Software_engineer barely moves vs the panel: Δrank from step-5 baseline ≲ +3, Δlog_p(source-#416) − Δlog_p(panel-mean-#416) is within noise (≲ 0.5 nat), and Δlog_p(swe-as-source-#416) − Δlog_p(swe-as-bystander-#398) ≲ 0.5 nat. <li><strong>H2 — strong source-specific direction (#398's reading is WRONG).</strong> Software_engineer enters the top 6 at any checkpoint, OR median rank across 22 checkpoints ≤ 8.</li>
</ul>

<p>H1a vs H1b is the new headline-discriminator. <h2>14. Success Criteria + Kill Criteria</h2>

<h3>14.1 Success criteria (quantitative; for confidence labeling) — v3 trichotomy</h3>

<p><strong>The confidence ceiling is MODERATE</strong> (n=1 at the training-run level; same constraint as #398). HIGH is NOT on the table for #416 regardless of how clean any single readout looks.</p>

<p>The H1a / H1b / H2 trichotomy from §1 maps to confidence as follows:</p>

<p><strong>MODERATE confidence — H1a (global shift PLUS source-specific bump):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>(5a) Δlog_p(swe-as-source-#416) − Δlog_p(swe-as-bystander-#398) ≥ +1 nat at late-checkpoint average.</li>
<li>(5b) Δlog_p(source-#416) − Δlog_p(panel-mean-#416) ≥ +0.5 nat at step 1600.</li>
<li>(5c) Δrank(source-#416) from step-5 baseline ≥ +10 ranks.</li>
<li>(1) Best rank ≥ 7 (never top 6); median rank in [8, 18].</li>
<li>(2) Step-1600 top-6 ∩ #398-step-1600 top-6 ≥ 5/6 AND strictly above the 4/6 panel-mechanical floor.</li>
<li>(3) Spearman ρ(step5, step1600 pos0) ≥ 0.5.</li>
<li>(4) Allowed to be in the dead zone (the WEAKEST readout shouldn't gate MODERATE).</li>
</ul>
<p>Reading: the #398 "global-shift" headline replicates from a second low-baseline source, BUT the parent's "not source-specific" framing is too strong — there is a measurable ~1-nat persona-specific bump on top of the global shift. The writeup must reframe #398's headline accordingly.</p>

<p><strong>MODERATE confidence — H1b (pure global shift):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>(5a) |Δlog_p(swe-as-source-#416) − Δlog_p(swe-as-bystander-#398)| ≤ 0.5 nat.</li>
<li>(5b) |Δlog_p(source-#416) − Δlog_p(panel-mean-#416)| ≤ 0.3 nat.</li>
<li>(5c) Δrank(source-#416) ≤ +3 ranks.</li>
<li>(1) Best rank ≥ 15; median rank ≥ 20.</li>
<li>(2) Step-1600 top-6 ∩ #398-step-1600 top-6 ≥ 5/6 AND strictly above the 4/6 panel-mechanical floor.</li>
<li>(3) Spearman ρ(step5, step1600 pos0) ≥ 0.5.</li>
</ul>
<p>Reading: #398's "global shift, not source-specific" reading replicates cleanly. The librarian's +13-rank bump in #398 was likely just persona-baseline noise / single-seed variance; no source-specific direction.</p>

<p><strong>MODERATE confidence — H2 (strong source-specific direction):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>(5b) ≥ +3 nat at step 1600.</li>
<li>(1) Best rank ≤ 6 at any checkpoint, OR median rank ≤ 8.</li>
<li>(2) Step-1600 top-6 ∩ #398-step-1600 top-6 ≤ 2/6; new top-6 contains software_engineer and/or its professional-expert neighbors.</li>
<li>(3) Spearman ρ(step5, step1600 pos0) ≤ 0.2.</li>
<li>R11 data-length spot-check (§8) must clear — otherwise H2 is confounded.</li>
</ul>
<p>Reading: #398's "no source-specific direction" reading is WRONG; the LoRA IS writing a persona-specific direction. The residual-stream LoRA-delta decomposition follow-up (§6.3) becomes urgent.</p>

<p><strong>LOW confidence</strong> if readouts split across the trichotomy (e.g. rank says H1b, magnitude says H1a, top-6 says H2). The writeup names this explicitly as "the evidence is mixed" and lists which readout pointed which way. Readout (4) being ambiguous on its own does NOT trigger LOW (it's the weakest); but if (1) + (5) disagree, that does.</p>
