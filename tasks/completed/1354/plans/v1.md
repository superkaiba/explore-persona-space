> `- **In-process registry side effects (dynamic CONTEXTS ids, trait names) are LOST on a resumed dispatcher process — the consumer crashes ONLY on resume, while a fresh-out_root smoke passes.** Resume predicates skip completed phases by done-file, but in-process state (a `_context()` CONTEXTS registration, a trait-registry entry, a warm cache) is rebuilt per PROCESS: when a later phase's consumer relies on an EARLIER phase's registration side effect, a resumed production process fast-forwards the registering phase and the consumer hits a missing-id `ValueError` — structurally invisible to fresh-out_root smokes, which always execute the registering phase. This is the flip side of the checkpoint-per-phase resume mandate (`.claude/rules/code-style.md`): persistence + skip-completed is required, so the skipped phases' in-process side effects WILL be absent. RULE: register dynamic contexts/ids at POINT OF USE — a registration call immediately before EACH consumer that needs the id — never via phase-ordering side effects; and when porting a new behavior onto a shared-lineage registry (e.g. `issue779_common.TRAITS`), audit its membership asserts for production-only branches a smoke never reaches (accept a SEEDED lineage trait; keep unseeded traits fail-loud). Regression pattern: a resumed-process test that fast-forwards the registering phases and runs the consumer cold.
