## 1. Goal + hypothesis

### Goal

Re-run the five-factor recipe-selectivity screen from parent #383 with three coupled changes:

1. **Marker switch:** `[ZLT]` (3-BPE-piece on Qwen-2.5) → `※` (single BPE piece). With marker-only-loss + `tail_tokens=0`, this drops the loss extent from ~4 tokens (3 marker pieces + EOS) to ~2 tokens (1 marker piece + EOS) — see Assumption A2. The loss-mask main effect is now a sharper test.
2. **Loss-mask factor E binary → ordinal K=3:** {E0=marker+EOT (~2 tok), E1=tail-32 (~32 tok), E2=whole-completion (~600 tok)}. Operationalizes loss-mask restrictiveness on an ordinal axis. H2 is OPERATIONAL not MECHANISTIC — see §3.
3. **Recipe upgraded to #399's shipped hyperparameters:** `lr=1e-4` (10× the #383 lr=1e-5) and 3 seeds {42, 137, 256} (3× the #383 single-seed run). User-directed at plan-approval gate.

Bolt on log-prob eval (`compute_marker_logprob`, #401 primitive) at intermediate checkpoints to (a) measure factor effects continuously even when source rate sits at zero or saturates at one, and (b) give the queued predictor follow-up cleaner per-cell signal than the 5-pp-floored substring rate.

### Hypothesis (matched to falsification thresholds)

**H1 — Sign and ordering invariance** [reframed from v3 — see §3 note]. Per-factor matched-pair effects at the new (`lr=1e-4`, 3 seeds, `※`) recipe preserve #383's per-factor **signs and orderings** on selectivity Δ, even though the magnitudes may shift due to the lr difference. Specifically:

- **Sign predictions (from #383 directionality):** A long-system is **selectively positive** (Δ source up > Δ leakage). B long-answer is **selectively positive**. C neutral-vs-persona-framing has **selectively negative** sign (persona framing is the positive direction). D Claude-data is **selectively positive but borderline**. E2 (whole-completion-loss) vs E0 (marker+EOT) is **selectively positive** (the largest selectivity decoupler in #383).
- **Ordering prediction:** the rank order of the four binary factors by selectivity Δ magnitude is preserved against #383's E2-vs-E0 magnitude ordering (the qualitative pattern: A ≳ B > C ≫ D, with C the negative-direction outlier).

*Pass criterion (E0+E2-restricted canonical estimator, n=24 pairs per factor pooled across 3 seeds × 12 ABCD combos × 2 E levels for A and C; n=48 for B and D after the A=0×C=1 drop):* per-factor 3-seed-bootstrap 95% CI **strictly above zero** for A and B (positive sign), **strictly below zero** for C (negative sign), and the per-factor sign agrees with #383 for D (no CI requirement on D — #383 itself reported D as borderline). The rank-ordering prediction is reported as a Kendall-τ between the new per-factor Δ vector and the #383 per-factor Δ vector; τ ≥ +0.67 (i.e., at most 1 of 6 pairwise inversions across 4 factors) passes.

*Falsified if:* (a) any of A, B, C show a CI that crosses zero or sits on the wrong sign vs #383, OR (b) Kendall-τ against #383's per-factor ordering < +0.33 (i.e., ≥4 of 6 inversions — qualitatively different effect surface).

**H1 is NOT a magnitude-replication test.** #383's CI envelopes were single-seed bootstrap CIs at lr=1e-5; comparing #397's 3-seed lr=1e-4 magnitudes against them would conflate marker-effect with lr-effect with cross-seed variance. v3's "new CI overlaps #383 CI envelope" framing is dropped. Descriptive magnitude comparison (#397 3-seed mean Δ vs #383 single-seed Δ, side by side) is still reported in the body for context, but is NOT a pass/fail.

**H1 is contingent on the §5.1.0 recipe-fix port landing intact.** Without the port, the experiment trains on the original-#365 (pre-fix) recipe, source rate sits at floor, and H1 is structurally unmeasurable.

**H2 — Loss-mask restrictiveness has a monotonic effect on selectivity Δ.** Operationalized as: across the 108 matched (seed, source, A, B, C, D) blocks (36 ABCD-source × 3 seeds), the within-block ordering of selectivity Δ across E ∈ {E0, E1, E2} is monotonically non-decreasing more often than chance. *Primary statistical test:* **Page's L one-tailed (alternative: E0 < E1 < E2), 108 blocks × 3 ordered conditions, p < 0.05.** Seed enters as an additional blocking factor (each seed × source × ABCD combo is its own block — the matched E0/E1/E2 triple comes from the same training pool + same seed, so the within-block matching is preserved). *Falsified if* Page's L p-one-tailed-increasing > 0.05, OR if E1 > E2 by >5 pp on pooled selectivity Δ (kill criterion — see §10).

H2 is OPERATIONAL, not MECHANISTIC (carry-forward from v3 M5). The E axis varies loss-extent, loss-location, and non-marker-token presence simultaneously; mechanism disambiguation needs an out-of-scope E1-prime level. See §13 analyzer guidance for interpretation rules.

**H3 — Initial marker log-prob mirrors substring rate qualitatively at endpoint.** Per-cell log p(`※` as the first assistant token after `add_generation_prompt`) at the final checkpoint correlates Spearman ρ ≥ +0.7 with substring source rate across all 324 (cell × seed) runs. This is the SAME-prefix-everywhere log-prob (carry-forward from v3 M2 path (a)) — NOT the end-of-answer log-prob. *Falsified if* ρ < +0.5 or if any factor's log-prob effect direction disagrees with its substring effect direction at p<0.05.

**H4 — Whole-completion-loss remains the largest selectivity decoupler at sharper contrast.** With marker-only loss now covering ~2 instead of ~4 tokens, the E2−E0 selectivity Δ remains **positive and the largest of the per-factor selectivity Δs**. Magnitude-matching to #383's +41.7 pp is NOT the pass criterion (see H1 reframe rationale); the pass criterion is **(a) E2−E0 selectivity Δ 3-seed-bootstrap 95% CI strictly above zero, AND (b) E2−E0 selectivity Δ point estimate ≥ each of the A, B, C-flip, D per-factor magnitudes** (rank-#1 among per-factor effects). Like H2, H4 is operational not mechanistic.

**H1 framing note (v4 reframe rationale).** With lr 10× different AND seed-count 3× different, comparing #397's 3-seed lr=1e-4 magnitudes against #383's single-seed lr=1e-5 bootstrap CI envelopes is not an apples-to-apples replication test. v3's "new CI overlaps #383 CI" pass criterion would conflate marker-effect, lr-effect, and seed-variance into a single yes/no that the analyzer cannot decompose. v4 picks **Option (i) — sign/ordering invariance** from the user's three offered framings. Rationale: preserves a falsifiable cross-experiment claim (sign + rank-ordering is a meaningful test the new conditions CAN measure) without overstating reach. Option (ii) introduces a researcher-degree-of-freedom (arbitrary slack constant). Option (iii) drops a useful comparison. The sign-and-ordering claim is what mentors will actually ask about — "did the marker switch break the qualitative effect surface, yes or no?" — which is exactly what (i) tests.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Cells × seeds

108 valid cells per seed × 3 seeds = **324 (cell × seed) runs total.** Per source (librarian / programmer / surgeon):
- 16 binary combos of (A, B, C, D), minus the 4 dropped corners (A=0×C=1) → 12 valid (A,B,C,D) per source.
- 3 E levels per valid combo: E0, E1, E2.
- Per source: 12 × 3 = 36 cells × 3 seeds = 108 (cell × seed) runs.
- Across 3 sources: **324 runs.**

**Estimator-restricted cell counts** (matters for H1 / H2 / H3 / H4 pass criteria):

- **H1 canonical (E0+E2-restricted, 3-seed):** 3 sources × 12 ABCD × 2 E levels × 3 seeds = **216 runs.** Per-factor n: A pools to 24 pairs (8 ABCD with A varying × 3 seeds — A=0×C=1 drop kills 4 of the 16 nominal A-pairs); C pools to 24; B and D pool to 48. (A12 corrected from v2 carries forward.)
- **H1 descriptive (E-marginalized, 3-seed):** all 324 runs.
- **H2 (Page's L over E):** 108 blocks × 3 ordered conditions. Each block is one (seed, source, A, B, C, D) tuple with a matched E0/E1/E2 triple from the same training pool and same seed.
- **H3:** all 324 (cell × seed) runs.
- **H4:** the 216-run E0+E2 subset.

### 4.2 Per-cell training (post-recipe-fix recipe + #399 hyperparameters)

For each (source, A, B, C, D, E, seed):

1. Load shared on-policy (D=0) or off-policy (D=1) pool from `data/issue_397/pools/<source>/source-<source>_a{a}_b{b}_c{c}.jsonl` — pools are reused from #383 (marker-agnostic) and are NOT seed-keyed (pool-gen seed effects are absorbed by the larger 1.5× over-generation buffer; this is the #383 convention).
2. Build training dataset via `factor_screen_397.data_prep.prepare_cell(...)` calling `_append_marker(answer, marker_text="※")`. Positive rows end in `…answer\n\n※<eos>`. Negative rows end in `…answer<eos>`. **400 positives + 400 negatives = 800 rows per cell** (post-recipe-fix). The `user_suffix` (B-axis length-instruction string) is **stripped** from training-row `user_text` — only the bare question goes in. The suffix is still used at pool-gen time. Dataset shuffling uses the per-run seed (so the same source × ABCD combo at different seeds produces different mini-batch orders).
3. Call `train_one_cell(...)` with TrainLoraConfig:
   - `marker_only_loss = True` for E ∈ {0, 1}; `False` for E = 2.
   - `marker_text = "※"`.
   - `marker_tail_tokens = 0` for E=0, `32` for E=1. Irrelevant when `marker_only_loss=False`.
   - **Learning rate `lr=1e-4`** [v4 user-directed change].
   - **Seed = the per-run seed ∈ {42, 137, 256}** [v4 user-directed change].
   - LoRA r=32 / α=64 / dropout=0.05 / rsLoRA.
   - **`target_modules = ["q_proj", "k_proj", "v_proj", "o_proj", "gate_proj", "up_proj", "down_proj"]`** (explicit list; equivalent to PEFT's `"all-linear"` shorthand on Qwen-2.5). Explicit in v4; was implicit in v3.
   - **AdamW optimizer** (`optim="adamw_torch"` — TRL default; named explicitly in v4).
   - **Scheduler: linear warmup 10% + cosine.** Implemented as `lr_scheduler_type="cosine"`, `warmup_ratio=0.10`. (Note: v3 said `warmup_ratio=0.05` carrying over from #383; v4 bumps to 0.10 per #399's shipped recipe.)
   - **`max_seq_length=2048`** (TRL SFTConfig field; equivalent to v3's `max_length=2048`; v4 uses the TRL-canonical name).
   - Per-device batch 4, grad-accum 4 (effective 16).
   - 3 epochs.
   - `save_strategy="steps"`, `save_steps=25` → 6 checkpoints per run (steps 25/50/75/100/125/150).
4. After training: keep adapter dir + HF Hub upload. Do NOT merge intermediate checkpoints. Final-checkpoint merge to `merged/` only for vLLM sampled-completion eval.

### 4.3 Per-cell eval (carry-forward from v3 with seed labels added)

**At every saved checkpoint (intermediate + final) — log-prob only:**
- Load base model ONCE per (cell × seed) run. Attach each checkpoint's adapter via peft 0.18.1 lifecycle: `base.load_adapter(adapter_dir, adapter_name=f"ck{step}_{marker_variant}")` + `base.set_adapter(...)`. Compute log-prob. `base.delete_adapter(...)` before attaching the next. (See §5.5 — `unload()` does not exist in peft 0.18.1.)
- Build 480 persona contexts: `tokenizer.apply_chat_template([{role:system, content:persona_prompt}, {role:user, content:q+b_suffix(cell.b)}], add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=False)`. Marker scored at first-assistant-token position (NOT end-of-answer — see H3 in §1, M2 path (a) in v3).
- Call `compute_marker_logprob(model, tokenizer, contexts, marker_text=...)` for BOTH `"\n\n※"` and `"※"` (per A15 — report both, don't post-hoc pick).

**At final checkpoint ONLY (substring parity with #383):**
- Merge final adapter to `merged/`. vLLM batched: 5 completions × 20 questions × 24 personas = 2400 generations per run. `max_new_tokens=2048`.
- **Score via `score_markers(eval_results, marker=args.marker_token)`** — MUST thread the runtime marker (M1 carry-forward). Default-marker fallback silently zeroes out.
- Random-control: 24 neutral backgrounds × 20 q × 5 comp = 2400 generations. Same scoring discipline.

### 4.4 Intermediate-checkpoint cadence

Per the recipe: 800 examples per cell, effective batch 16, 3 epochs → **~150 steps per run**. Save every 25 steps → checkpoints at {25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150} = **6 checkpoints per (cell × seed) run**.

### 5.9 `aggregator.py` — multi-seed handling + dual H1 estimators + Page's L + Kendall-τ

**Multi-seed pooling discipline.** For every per-factor / per-condition metric, the aggregator produces two views:

```python
# Per-seed view: each seed's per-factor matched-pair Δ + within-seed widest-of-three CI.
# Then across-seed: report the 3-seed mean Δ and a 3-seed bootstrap CI on the mean,
# treating each seed as one cluster (analogous to #383's source-cluster bootstrap but with
# seed-as-cluster now stacked alongside source-as-cluster).

def per_factor_matched_pair_delta_multiseed(
    records_by_seed: dict[int, list[dict]],     # {42: [...], 137: [...], 256: [...]}
```
