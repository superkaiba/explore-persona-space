> ONE-RUNG measured pilot under the NEW regime (one model size / one lane
> through the production entrypoint), extrapolate from that, and carry the
> parent figure only as a labelled lower bound; fence the rerun off the
> pilot (≥2× dispersion default), never off the parent wall. This is the
> generation-rerun sibling of PER-REGIME BINDING above (fit-lane
> behavior/budget regimes, #1739) — the regime axis there is the lane's
