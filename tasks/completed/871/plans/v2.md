## 11. Decision Rationale

**Training hyperparameters:** N/A — no model training (`kind: infra` doc change).

The one design decision worth a rationale row is the CONTENT of item (i):

- **What:** two checks — (1) inner per-cell/per-fold/per-draw axis batched, (2) device
  parametrized (no hardcoded module constant); remedy = fix at the SOURCE module;
  record the inspection (function name, batched-or-serial, device handling) in the
  plan's reuse map + §9 row; scoped to reused CODE, N/A for data-only reuse.
- **Why these two checks:** they are exactly the two failure modes the four incidents
  exhibit — serial inner loops (#761 `_ridge_predict_loco` over 50 LOCO folds → ~100x
  over plan; #667 `fit_cell` per-cell 300-epoch MLP × 84 cells) and hardcoded device
  pins (#763/#812 inheriting `issue658_fit_predictors.py:143`'s `DEVICE = "cpu"`,
  batched-but-on-CPU until the `EPM_FIT_DEVICE=cuda` cutover). No incident in the set
  needed a third check.
- **Why source-module-fix-first:** a caller-side workaround leaves the trap armed for
  the NEXT reuser — the incident sequence itself (#761 then #667 then #763 then #812)
  is four downstream tasks independently rediscovering the same source-module defects;
  fixing at the source is the only remedy that terminates the sequence. Consistent
  with the code-style "Supersede → delete the old version" and the built-but-stranded
