## 0.0 TL;DR

- **What I'll run.** Same end-of-response marker rig as the parent line, but instead of testing one induction mechanism at a time, the panel is a UNION of two kinds of contexts side-by-side: K-shot example blocks (the ICL arm — math examples, coding examples, persona-voice examples, etc.) AND system-prompt-only personas (the SP arm — "You are a pirate captain," "You are a stand-up comedian," etc.). Then I read a base-model activation snapshot at the spot right after the context scaffold + user question (last prompt token, identical primitive for both arms) and ask whether the snapshot's geometry predicts where the marker leaks — both within each arm AND across arms, controlling for surface lexical overlap between the two scaffold types.
- **What I expect.** Base-model cosine distance at the post-context+question slot predicts marker transfer across the full union grid (length-partial Spearman ρ around −0.4 to −0.6 — more geometric distance → less transfer, the convention used throughout the plan), the ICL arm produces a wider cosine spread than the SP arm (the #468 examples-beat-descriptions effect; the SP arm replicates the #474 too-tightly-clustered failure), matched same-identity cross-type pairs sit close in cosine and the marker transfers between them BEYOND what cosine distance + surface lexical overlap alone predict, mismatched cross-type pairs sit far and don't.
- **What would change my mind.** The two arms give the same cosine spread (then "ICL gives a cleaner predictor" was a #474-specific artifact); OR cross-type cosine doesn't predict cross-type transfer (the predictor is induction-mechanism-bound — a separate axis lives in each); OR matched same-identity pairs sit FAR in cosine (then "same persona" isn't an identity axis in residual space, it's a surface-form axis); OR matched-pair transfer is fully explained by cosine distance + lexical overlap alone (then the "same identity" effect is the surface-form axis after all, not an identity-beyond-distance signal); OR the cosine signal collapses when the most-distinct contexts are dropped on EITHER side (the #474 stylized-driven failure mode at a new axis).

**H4 (NEW — the cross-type Goal claim, two-part, M1-revised).** Marker transfer occurs across induction mechanisms and is predicted by base-model representational distance BEYOND what scaffold lexical overlap alone predicts; matched same-identity cross-type pairs transfer the marker MORE than cosine distance + lexical overlap together would predict.

- **H4(a) — cross-type cosine→ΔG signal, controlling for length AND scaffold overlap (M1 fix).** On the 256 cross-type off-diagonal cells (ICL→SP + SP→ICL combined), **partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG | length, scaffold_overlap_score) ≤ −0.20** AND dyadic-bootstrap CI excludes 0. Raw (length-only-partial) ρ is reported as a companion to the dual-partial; if the cosine signal is driven by `scaffold_overlap_score`, the raw-vs-dual-partial gap will be visible and the body narrates "cross-type cosine signal is mostly surface overlap, not residual identity geometry."
- **H4(b) — matched same-identity cross-type pair residual test (M1 reframe — cosine-controlled).** v3 compared raw ΔG matched vs mismatched, which is mechanically guaranteed if H4(a) holds (matched pairs were selected to be low-cosine, and H4(a) says low-cosine → high-ΔG — so v3's H4(b) added nothing beyond H4(a)). v4 replaces the raw comparison with a cosine + overlap-controlled residual test:
  1. **Regress ΔG on (cosine_distance_L21 + length + scaffold_overlap_score)** over all 256 cross-type cells. Extract per-cell residuals.
  2. **Test whether the 4 matched-pair residuals (8 ordered cells) are shifted POSITIVE relative to cosine-and-overlap-matched mismatched cells** — paired bootstrap on the residual-difference distribution, 5000 boots; each boot draws the 8 matched-pair cells plus 8 mismatched cells whose cosine + overlap values are closest to the matched-pair cells' (nearest-cosine-and-overlap-neighbor matching, holding cosine and overlap fixed non-parametrically); compute median residual difference; CI = central 2.5/97.5 percentiles.
  3. **PASS = the paired-bootstrap CI on the residual-difference excludes 0 with positive sign.**

This change isolates the "identity transfers BEYOND what cosine distance + surface overlap predict" claim that H4(b) was originally supposed to demonstrate. A PASS now means: holding cosine and overlap fixed, matched-identity pairs still transfer the marker more — the residual identity-axis effect.

H4 PASS requires BOTH (a) AND (b). H4(b) NULL but H4(a) PASS reads as "cross-type cosine + overlap predicts transfer broadly; matched same-identity pairs don't get a premium beyond what cosine + overlap already explain" — informative null about identity-vs-distance separability. H4(a) NULL reads as "cross-type cosine doesn't predict transfer at all once overlap is controlled — predictor is induction-mechanism-bound and the cross-type signal in raw cosine is a surface confound." Both nulls are informative and ship as primary findings.

**Diagonal-adjusted statistic** (required for H1/H2/H3/H4 SURVIVES verdicts, inherited from #488 v2 §3 fix C). SURVIVES requires the signed ρ also survives BOTH (a) partial out the source's own diagonal `emission_ii`, (b) normalized outcome `emission_ij / max(emission_ii, ε)` with sources `< 0.5 nat` dropped. If (a) or (b) collapses, report as **NULL after diagonal-adjustment**.

Pre-registered null states: H1 ρ = 0, H2 ρ = 0, H3 within-arm delta = 0, H4(a) ρ = 0, H4(b) matched-pair residual = 0. Statistical primary = **dyadic cluster-bootstrap** (5000 boots, resample sources and targets independently within the relevant cell subset) for H1/H2/H4(a); **genuinely-paired LoRA-family-level bootstrap** for H3; **paired residual bootstrap with nearest-cosine-and-overlap-neighbor matching** for H4(b). Secondary = **crossed random-effects** `delta_g ~ predictor + length + scaffold_overlap_score + (1|source_context) + (1|target_context) + (1|source_type)`. PASS requires primary bootstrap CI to exclude 0; mixed-model fixed-effect CI agrees on sign as a survival check.

### 4.3 Predictor extraction (cross-type comparability is the critical new question)

For each context `T_i` (whether ICL or SP), build the prompt `prompt_i(q)`:
- **SP context** (cls A in i406): `apply_chat_template([{system: SP_i.system_prompt}, {user: q}], add_generation_prompt=True)` — the #474 site, identical to `build_prompt_for_condition(cond, q, tok)` for cls A in `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/i406_conditions.py:230-238`.
- **ICL context** (the v2 site): `apply_chat_template(IK_i.icl_block + [{user: q}], add_generation_prompt=True)` — Hendel/Todd extraction site.

Base-model forward pass once per (T_i, q) over the 50-question `Q'_probe` set. Last prompt token residual extracted at layers {7, 11, 14, 15, 21, 27} via `last_token_acts` (verbatim from `scripts/issue444_persona_distance_topic.py:88-98`).

```
S_i_L = mean_q residual[L][prompt_i(q), -1]              # per-layer per-context vector
cosine_distance(i, j, L) = 1 - cos(S_i_L, S_j_L)
```

The primitive is byte-identical across context types — same `last_token_acts` call, same layer slice, same mean-over-50-probes aggregation. The vectors `S_SP01_L21` and `S_IK01_L21` live in the SAME residual-stream basis (Qwen-2.5-7B's L21 hidden state) — cosine is well-defined between them mechanically. §6.0 below discusses whether the well-defined cosine MEASURES the same construct across types.

**`scaffold_overlap_score` (NEW v4 covariate per M1).** For each ordered off-diagonal cell (T_i, T_j), compute:

```
def scaffold_text(ctx: UnionContext) -> str:
    """Extract the raw scaffold text (ICL block messages flattened or SP system prompt)."""
    if isinstance(ctx, ICLContext):
        return "\n".join(m["content"] for m in ctx.icl_block)
    return ctx.system_prompt

def scaffold_overlap_score(ctx_i, ctx_j) -> dict:
    """Three deterministic surface-overlap features. ~50 LOC."""
    text_i = scaffold_text(ctx_i).lower()
    text_j = scaffold_text(ctx_j).lower()
    tok_i = set(text_i.split())  # whitespace tokenization
    tok_j = set(text_j.split())
    # Feature 1: token Jaccard overlap
    jaccard = len(tok_i & tok_j) / max(len(tok_i | tok_j), 1)
    # Feature 2: bag-of-words cosine
    from sklearn.feature_extraction.text import CountVectorizer
    vec = CountVectorizer().fit_transform([text_i, text_j]).toarray()
    bow_cos = float(np.dot(vec[0], vec[1]) / (np.linalg.norm(vec[0]) * np.linalg.norm(vec[1]) + 1e-12))
    # Feature 3: persona-word indicator — does the literal persona token appear in BOTH sides?
    # Persona-word list pinned at planning time: {pirate, comedian, tutor, engineer, villain, math, mathematician}
    PERSONA_WORDS = {"pirate", "comedian", "tutor", "engineer", "villain", "math", "mathematician"}
    persona_indicator = int(bool(PERSONA_WORDS & tok_i & tok_j))
    # Combine into a single scaffold_overlap_score: weighted mean of the 3 features (weights 0.5/0.3/0.2)
    combined = 0.5 * jaccard + 0.3 * bow_cos + 0.2 * persona_indicator
    return {
        "jaccard": jaccard,
        "bow_cos": bow_cos,
        "persona_indicator": persona_indicator,
        "scaffold_overlap_score": combined,
    }
```

The combined `scaffold_overlap_score` is the primary covariate; all 3 features are also kept for transparency (the per-matched-pair micro-table §6.3 surfaces them individually). This is deterministic, predictor-side only, computed ONCE in Phase-1 over the 552 off-diagonal cells, and contributes ~5 minutes of analysis-side compute (no GPU).

### 6.2 Headline statistic, thresholds, and bootstrap

**Universe of analysis: off-diagonal cells only (i ≠ j; 552 pairs per frac per seed).** Same restriction as v2.

- **Primary statistic (H1):** length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG), partialled on `log(prompt_tokens + R_tokens)`, computed on the FULL panel + within-ICL panel + within-SP panel + cross-type panel separately, per frac per seed.
- **H1 PASS** (full panel): ρ ≤ −0.30 AND dyadic-bootstrap CI excludes 0 AND |ρ_cosine| − |ρ_JS| ≥ 0.10 with paired-bootstrap CI on the difference excluding 0.
- **H2 statistic + verdict tree** (as v2 §6.2 extended, M3-corrected): same with `is_strong_kind` partial + `kind_distinctness_score` graded partial, on the **pre-registered 8-strong-kind set {IK06, IK12, IK13, IK16, SP03, SP04, SP05, SP08}**; SURVIVES requires source-OR-target drop (240 surviving off-diagonal cells) AND dual-side graded partial. The exact 8-set is locked in §3 and §10 with the same membership.

- **H3 statistic (NEW v4 framing — genuinely-paired LoRA-family-level bootstrap per M4):**
  - Compute ρ_ICL = ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG | length) on the 240 ICL-within off-diagonal cells.
  - Compute ρ_SP = ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG | length) on the 56 SP-within off-diagonal cells.
  - **Genuinely-paired bootstrap mechanic (M4 PREFERRED).** Each bootstrap draw resamples WITH REPLACEMENT at the **(frac × seed × LoRA-family) level — the 48 LoRA-family × 3 frac = 144 LoRA-snapshot units that are SHARED across both arms** (each LoRA-snapshot contributes cells to BOTH the ICL-within sub-grid AND the SP-within sub-grid via its evaluation against the 16 ICL targets and the 8 SP targets respectively). Within each draw, the resampled LoRA-snapshots fix the cells contributed to both ρ_ICL_b and ρ_SP_b; sources and targets are held fixed (not resampled). Compute ρ_ICL_b and ρ_SP_b on the resampled-LoRA-snapshot subset's cells. Form the bootstrap distribution of (|ρ_ICL_b| − |ρ_SP_b|), 5000 boots; 95% CI = central 2.5/97.5 percentiles. This mechanic is genuinely paired because the same LoRA-snapshots contribute to both ρ's in every draw — `cov(ρ_ICL_b, ρ_SP_b) > 0` (recipe-level noise cancels in the difference), CI on the difference is narrower than the independent two-sample CI v3 specified.
  - **H3 PASS**: |ρ_ICL_within| − |ρ_SP_within| ≥ **0.15** (raised from v3's 0.10 to reflect the threshold the genuinely-paired narrower CI can actually support — still well below the ~0.55 raw-ρ gap an independent two-sample bootstrap would demand at the same CI width) AND genuinely-paired CI on the difference excludes 0 (signed positive) AND |ρ_ICL_within| ≥ 0.30.
  - **Power-floor discipline (M4 fix).** The analyzer reports the CI WIDTH of the ICL ρ AND the SP ρ AND the difference up front. If the SP-arm CI half-width exceeds 0.20, the body narrates "SP-arm underpowered, can't distinguish ρ_SP from null" rather than "no SP effect"; if the difference CI is wide enough that 0.15 is well within the upper tail, the verdict is "underpowered, can't conclude either way" rather than NULL.
  - **ρ_JS in both arms (M4 fix).** Alongside ρ_cosine_ICL_within and ρ_cosine_SP_within, the H3 forest plot also shows ρ_JS_ICL_within and ρ_JS_SP_within (length-partial Spearman of JS_RB on ΔG within each arm). This lets the H3 narrative cross-check "ICL gives a cleaner cosine predictor" against "does JS also show the cleaner-on-ICL pattern, or is the dissociation cosine-specific?"
  - **Fallback (M4 alternative — NOT preferred but documented).** If the genuinely-paired bootstrap turns out to give a degenerate distribution (e.g. because the LoRA-snapshot-level resampling resamples too few effective units when many LoRA-snapshots are nearly identical — flagged at smoke time), fall back to the v3-style independent two-sample bootstrap AND own the PASS bar honestly at raw-ρ gap ≈ 0.55 / z-Fisher gap ≈ 1.03. The analyzer pre-commits in §11 to picking the genuinely-paired design first; the fallback is only invoked if the paired bootstrap's effective-sample-size is below 24 LoRA-snapshot draws on either side. This is documented up front so the v3 "claim paired narrow-CI benefit while doing independent wide-CI resampling" inconsistency cannot recur.

- **H4(a) statistic (NEW v4 — cross-type cosine→ΔG signal, M1 dual-partial):**
  - Compute **partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG | length, scaffold_overlap_score)** on the 256 cross-type off-diagonal cells (ICL→SP + SP→ICL combined; report each direction separately as a sub-analysis). The dual-partial controls for length AND surface overlap, so a negative ρ reflects identity-geometry-residual cosine, not surface overlap.
  - **Companion length-only partial ρ** also reported, so the raw-vs-dual-partial gap reveals how much of the H4(a) signal IS surface overlap.
  - **Dyadic cluster-bootstrap** (asymmetric: 16 source-clusters one way, 8 source-clusters the other — handle by resampling on the asymmetric cluster set).
  - **H4(a) PASS**: dual-partial ρ_cross ≤ **−0.20** AND dyadic CI excludes 0. (Threshold unchanged from v3 — the dual-partial CI is roughly the same width since `scaffold_overlap_score` is a single additional covariate; the test is now substantively stronger because it isolates the identity-beyond-overlap signal.)

- **H4(b) statistic (NEW v4 — cosine + overlap-controlled matched same-identity residual test, per M1):**
  - **Step 1: residual regression.** Fit `ΔG ~ cosine_distance_L21 + length + scaffold_overlap_score` over all 256 cross-type off-diagonal cells (OLS on the rank-transformed predictors and outcome to match the Spearman-correlation framework). Extract per-cell residuals `r_ij = ΔG_ij - ΔG_predicted_ij`.
  - **Step 2: matched-pair residual test.** The 4 matched pairs × 2 directions = 8 ordered cells. For each matched cell, find its NEAREST-(cosine,overlap)-NEIGHBOR among the 248 unmatched cross-type cells (Euclidean nearest neighbor in standardized (cosine_distance, scaffold_overlap_score) space). Compute `matched_residual - matched_neighbor_residual` for each of the 8 matched cells.
  - **Step 3: paired bootstrap on the residual difference.** 5000 boots; each boot draws 8 matched-pair cells (sampling cells with replacement) plus their pre-computed nearest-cosine-and-overlap-neighbor mismatched cells; compute median (matched_residual − mismatched_residual); CI = central 2.5/97.5 percentiles.
  - **H4(b) PASS**: paired-bootstrap CI on the residual-difference excludes 0 with POSITIVE sign (matched cells transfer MORE than cosine + overlap alone would predict).
  - **What this PASS means substantively (M1):** holding cosine distance and scaffold overlap fixed, matched-identity cross-type pairs transfer the marker MORE than mismatched cross-type pairs do. This isolates the "identity beyond distance and beyond surface overlap" effect that H4(b) is supposed to demonstrate. A v3-style raw-ΔG matched-vs-mismatched comparison would have been mechanically guaranteed by H4(a)'s low-cosine→high-ΔG prediction (matched pairs were selected to be low-cosine); the residual test now strips that mechanical guarantee.
  - **Companion raw test reported as descriptive** — the v3-style raw matched-vs-mismatched ΔG comparison runs alongside as a descriptive companion (matches v3's H4(b) PASS bar), so the body can show the raw-vs-residual difference and discuss how much of the raw matched-pair lift is mechanical-from-cosine vs identity-residual.

Why: the M1 critique pointed out that cross-type cosine closeness AND cross-type marker transfer could BOTH ride on surface lexical overlap (the word "pirate" appearing in both sides of a matched pair) rather than shared identity geometry. Partialling out scaffold_overlap_score in H4(a) and using a cosine + overlap-controlled residual test in H4(b) isolates the identity-beyond-overlap signal. Alternative considered + rejected: dropping the matched-pair design entirely (would forfeit the cleanest H4 test; M1's residual-control fix preserves the test while making it falsifiable).

- **H4(b) reframed as cosine-controlled residual test (NEW v4 per M1).** What: regress ΔG on (cosine_distance + length + scaffold_overlap_score), extract residuals, test matched-pair residuals vs nearest-(cosine,overlap)-neighbor mismatched residuals via paired bootstrap. Why: v3's raw matched-vs-mismatched comparison was MECHANICALLY guaranteed if H4(a) holds (matched pairs were selected to be low-cosine, and H4(a) predicts low-cosine→high-ΔG, so v3's H4(b) added nothing beyond H4(a)). The residual test strips the mechanical guarantee — a PASS now means "identity transfers BEYOND what cosine and surface overlap predict," which is the substantive claim H4(b) was supposed to establish. Source: ensemble Must-Fix M1 + standard residual-control statistical convention. Alternative considered + rejected: keeping the raw matched-vs-mismatched comparison (M1's circularity argument is conclusive; the raw comparison runs as descriptive companion only).

- **SP-string freeze + cosine-INDEPENDENT rewrite protocol (NEW v4 per M2).** What: SP03/04/06/07/08 system_prompt strings frozen pre-Phase-0; rewrite permitted ONLY under a cosine-independent identity-sameness check (Claude-as-judge or human rubric), never under cosine distance; up to 1 rewrite per pair; every draft logged to `artifacts/sp_string_drafts.jsonl`. Why: v3's §11 deviation-allowed rule permitted rewriting SP strings based on cosine distance, which would tune the predictor-side outcome of the matched-pair test before launch — H4(b) PASS could then reflect string-search skill, not an empirical same-identity result. M2 makes H4(b)'s cosine-closeness leg falsifiable by routing the rewrite decision through a cosine-independent criterion. Source: ensemble Must-Fix M2 + standard pre-registration discipline. Alternative considered + rejected: allowing free rewrite based on cosine (the M2 critique is conclusive); never rewriting (some matched pairs might genuinely have bad first drafts, the 1-rewrite + freeze pattern handles this).

SP08 is INCLUDED (decision deliberate, not just to make numbers match). Why: v3 §3 named a 7-item set omitting SP08 while §4.2 and §10 used an 8-item set including SP08 — internally inconsistent, and H2's drop verdict depends on which set is used. Off-diagonal cells remaining after source-OR-target drop = 240 (132 within-non-strong-ICL + 12 within-non-strong-SP + 96 cross-type non-strong). Source: ensemble Must-Fix M3 + the §4.2.2 strong-kind designation. - **H3 genuinely-paired bootstrap at the LoRA-snapshot level + raised PASS bar to 0.15 (NEW v4 per M4).** What: each bootstrap draw resamples WITH REPLACEMENT at the (frac × seed × LoRA-family) level — the 48 LoRA-family × 3 frac = 144 LoRA-snapshot units shared across both arms; within each draw the resampled LoRA-snapshots fix the cells contributed to BOTH ρ_ICL_b and ρ_SP_b. PASS bar = 0.15 (raised from v3's 0.10 because the genuinely-paired bootstrap's narrower CI can support a higher bar without being trivial). ρ_JS reported in BOTH arms alongside ρ_cosine. Fallback to independent two-sample bootstrap with raw-ρ gap ≈ 0.55 PASS bar IF the paired-bootstrap effective-sample-size falls below 24 LoRA-snapshots on either arm (checked at smoke gate 7 and Phase 5). Why: v3's "paired" bootstrap resampled ICL and SP sources/targets independently within each arm — but those sets are DISJOINT (no shared cell), so `cov(ρ_ICL_b, ρ_SP_b) = 0` and the variance was actually `Var(ICL) + Var(SP)`, identical to an independent two-sample bootstrap. With 16 vs 8 source-clusters, the CI on the independent difference excludes 0 only at raw-ρ gap ≈ 0.55, making v3's 0.10 PASS bar unreachable. M4 picks the genuinely-paired design (LoRA-snapshot-level resampling, which IS shared across arms), narrowing the CI; the 0.15 bar is the threshold the paired variance actually supports. Source: ensemble Must-Fix M4 + standard paired-bootstrap statistical convention. Alternative considered + rejected: keeping v3's mechanic + lowering the PASS bar to whatever the independent two-sample CI supports at the v3 0.10 nominal target (would make H3 either trivially unreachable at 0.10 or required gap ~0.55 to clear — neither preserves the H3 head-to-head question; the genuinely-paired design is the right fix).
