## Goal / Requirement

Make hand-composed phase argvs fail on the VM in seconds instead of after a
full instance boot. Incident (verified on #1738 events.jsonl,
2026-07-28T20:56:38Z): Phase 3 attempt 1's plan-§10 command omitted the
driver's required input-source flag (`--split-file` or
`--manifest-dir`/`--manifest-from-hf`); the workload died at post-parse argv
validation (`raise SystemExit("--split-file or --manifest-dir/
--manifest-from-hf required")`, rc=1, ~7 s in) AFTER a full GCE flexstart
boot + venv install — one wasted boot+venv cycle.

**Acceptance criteria:**
1. A recipe clause exists in `.claude/skills/issue/SKILL.md` Step 6b that
   requires a VM-side dry-run of the EXACT production argv before any
   instance-booting dispatch of a hand-composed (newly-composed) workload
   argv, with a copy-pasteable generic probe that catches BOTH argparse
   errors (missing-required/unknown-flag, exit 2) AND the repo's dominant
   post-parse `SystemExit` validation convention (exit 1 — the #1738 class),
   with a CLI-family scoping step (argparse vs Hydra) and a never-engaged
   guard.
2. `.claude/rules/crash-fix-rounds.md` (the filed `workflow_fix_target`)
   carries a short sibling clause binding the same probe on RELAUNCH rounds
   whose argv OR driver CLI/validation surface changed,
   cross-referencing the SKILL.md clause as the canonical recipe.
3. `scripts/workflow_lint.py` (no-flags default) passes; the Step 9c mapped
   tests for the touched files pass.

## Method delta (what differs from prior related work)

  (item 7) is a `--help` flag-PRESENCE scan: it catches bogus flags but

  `--workload-cmd` dispatches (the #1738 lane) never execute it.

instance-booting dispatch, on BOTH first launches (SKILL.md Step 6b, the
primary home — that is where hand-composed argvs are dispatched) and
changed-argv/changed-CLI relaunches (crash-fix-rounds.md, the filed target).
