## 1. Goal (verbatim) + operative interpretation

> Reconcile the teardown prose in `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/skills/issue/SKILL.md` with the live
> compute-kill approval gate, so sessions are directed to SURFACE a verified-done pod for
> approval (marker + push, pod left alive) instead of attempting a self-approved terminate.

Operative interpretation (clarifier Decision, 2026-08-10, autonomous mode): "self-approved
terminate" = passing `--approve` or setting `EPS_ALLOW_COMPUTE_KILL=1` /
`EPS_ALLOW_POD_TERMINATE=1` (the user-only approval channels), or calling the raw API terminate
outside `pod.py terminate`. The live gate (`3a2f364a70`, 2026-08-04) SANCTIONS exactly one
automated destruction: the owner-driven `kill_approval.verified_teardown` grant that
`pod_lifecycle.cmd_terminate` enters after its upload-verification guard passes — implementing
the standing directive "the agent driving a job on a pod can close that pod if everything is
uploaded". So the reconciled prose KEEPS the verify → PASS-marker → `pod.py terminate` owner
path, grounds it in the gate, directs every kill-gate REFUSAL to surface-for-approval, and bans
session-side self-approval. A literal always-surface rewrite would contradict the gate's own
design and re-create the #1662/#664 idle-burn family.
