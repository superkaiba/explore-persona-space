## 4. Design

### 4.1 Mechanism decision (unchanged from v1, census corrected)

**Chosen: an events.jsonl `epm:progress` breadcrumb using the existing `deliberate-stop ` note-prefix convention.** The three hazards:

1. **Liveness-refresh hazard — neutralized by construction AFTER Edits 5-6.** Two sites already exclude by prefix (by-independent); the two lagging clocks gain the identical predicate in this change. Semantic note: extending the exclusion also stops EXISTING `spawn_session-stop` records from reading as task activity on those two clocks — this is an alignment fix, not a regression: #990 frames a deliberate stop as "the death record of the task's driver", and the sanctioned way to hold off the watcher after a deliberate stop is a PARK status or the takeover sentinel, never a stop record counting as liveness (CLAUDE.md § User pause affordance, incident #816).
2. **Triage-candidate hazard — accepted and justified** (one candidate per rare collision; a collision is externality the owner should see once; `issue-session-guard` deliberately NOT added to `TRIAGE_MACHINE_BY` per the #966 convention comment).
3. **Step 7 4h-staleness — no interaction** (keys on `epm:results` absence).

**Rejected alternatives (unchanged from v1):** self-report (destructive overwrite of the owner's `issue-progress/<N>.json`); sidecar (dashboard-invisible); new bracketed prefix (two frozenset edits + tests for no safety gain — and v2's clock alignment is needed under ANY prefix choice, since `_latest_nonwatcher_event_ts` / `tick_triage.latest_event_ts` exclude nothing that isn't a watcher sentinel); `[spawn-session:` reuse (misattribution); new `epm:*` kind (architectural).

Out of scope (unchanged): `issue-tick` lifecycle exits; the v1→v2 dispatcher handoff; wrapper-level early-death instrumentation in `spawn_session.py`.
