**Kind:** infra · **Workflow:** v1 · **Estimated GPU-hours (total): 0**

## §0.0 TL;DR

Add **check 58** (`check_v4_positional_result_crossrefs`) to
`scripts/verify_task_body.py`: a v4-gated, WARN-only scan that finds positional
cross-reference tokens in `## Results` prose — `(N results up|down)`, `the
previous result`, `the next result` — resolves each ordinal against the actual
`### ` H3 sequence, and flags two defect shapes: (a) the resolution lands
**outside** the result range, and (b) the pointer's own clause and the resolved
target heading name **disjoint round sets** (the #2221 shape: a clause saying
`round-4` resolving onto a heading starting `Rounds 1-3:`).

**Change my mind:** if a faithful reproduction of #2221's ten-H3 `## Results`
sequence with its two surviving `(two results up)` clauses does NOT produce two
WARNs, the check does not catch the class it was filed for and the design is
wrong. Conversely, if any of the six negative fixtures (correct pointer,
round-token absent on either side, fenced token, v3 body, <2 H3s) produces a
WARN, the false-positive posture is wrong and the check must not ship.

## §2 Background — why the class recurs

The origin is #2221's `epm:clean-result-critique v3` (2026-08-14T00:41:50Z),
Lens 2 Blocker 2, which supplied the mechanizable recipe verbatim:

> parse "(N results up|down)" tokens under `## Results`, resolve the ordinal
> against the H3 sequence, and flag when the clause says "round-4" but the
> resolved heading starts "Rounds 1-3:" (or vice versa).

The **mechanism** is structural, not a one-off typo. SPEC
§ "Follow-up consolidation (v4)" mandates two operations that REORDER the H3
sequence on every fold:

- item 3 (superseded-result hygiene) — collapse outdated blocks into one
  `<details><summary>Superseded by round N` at the END of `## Results`;
- item 4 (round-compression hygiene) — compress absorbed results.

An ordinal written in round K therefore retargets silently in round K+1.
Multi-round consolidated bodies are the whole population; the class cannot be
fixed by care alone.

**Live instances** (`tasks/followups_running/2221/body.md`, 10 result H3s):

| body line | pointer | in result # | resolves to # | resolved heading | intended # |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 355 | `(two results up)` | 7 | 5 | `Rounds 1-3: found-data mining collapsed…` | 2 (five up) |
| 387 | `(two results up)` | 10 | 8 | `Rounds 1-3: the headline contrast failed…` | 4 (six up) |

Both survived #2221's revision round (which appended named pointers to the H3
headings rather than removing the ordinals), so both are still live and serve as
the regression anchor.

**Prior art:** none. `grep -rnE "results? (up|down)\b|previous result"` over
`scripts/verify_task_body.py` and `SPEC.md` returns zero hits. No existing check
resolves positional cross-references; SPEC prescribes no pointer form.

## §3 Design

### §3.1 Gating (forward-only)

Follows the established v4 check idiom exactly (models: check 36
`check_v4_result_paragraph_sentences`, check 21 `check_v4_results_beat`):

1. `if not is_v4(body): return CheckResult(label, True, "skipped — not a v4 body")`
   — `is_v4` is the fence-aware / `<details>`-aware sentinel detector at
   `scripts/verify_task_body.py:4408`, so a body merely QUOTING the sentinel in
   an illustrative fence is not misdetected. v3 / v2 / pre-sentinel bodies are
   never touched (forward-only, SPEC).
2. `results = _v4_results_body(body)`; `None` → pass with
   `"## Results missing — check 2 will report"`.
3. `result_h3s = _collect_tldr_h3_names(results)`; empty → pass with
   `"no ### <result> headings — check 3 will report"`; fewer than 2 → pass with
   `"<2 results — no positional target space"` (a single result cannot host a
   resolvable ordinal).

Block slicing reuses the check-21/36 convention verbatim:
`rlines[line_no + 1 : end_line]`, `end_line` = next H3 line or `len(rlines)`.

### §3.2 Token grammar

Scanned over each block's **non-code prose** — fenced code skipped and inline
code spans / markdown link targets masked, reusing `_SENTENCE_INLINE_CODE_RE`
and `_SENTENCE_LINK_TARGET_RE` (the `_v4_block_is_quantitative` convention).
`<details>` bodies are stripped first via `_DETAILS_BLOCK_RE` (collapsed
superseded content is LM-lens territory, and it is exactly where stale
pointers legitimately live after a fold).

Two token families, surrounding parentheses optional (the observed form is
parenthesised, but the phrase alone is the same defect):

- **ordinal**: `<count> results? (up|down)` where `<count>` is a digit run or
  one of the number-words `one two three four five six seven eight nine ten`
  (case-insensitive). `results?` admits the singular `(one result up)`.
- **adjacent**: `the previous result` / `the next result`, distance 1.

### §3.3 Resolution

Result blocks are 1-indexed in H3 order. For a pointer inside block `i`:

- `up` / `previous` → `i - N`
- `down` / `next` → `i + N`

### §3.4 The two flag arms

**Arm (a) — out-of-range.** Resolved index `< 1` or `> n_results`. Flagged
unconditionally: no adjudication is needed because no target exists. This arm
alone catches the common post-fold shape where results were REMOVED.

**Arm (b) — round mismatch, contradiction-only.** Adopts the check-45
(`check_figure_caption_count_claims_vs_sidecar`) posture: flag only when
positive evidence exists on BOTH sides; stay silent when un-adjudicable.

1. **Clause scope** — the pointer's clause is the segment delimited by `;` or a
   sentence terminator (`.!?`) containing the token. Clause scope is
   **load-bearing, not cosmetic**: #2221 line 355 reads
   `…are the round 1-3 pathology the re-mine removed; the round-4 recompute
   (two results up) replaces this read.` Scoping to the paragraph or the
   sentence would union `{1,2,3}` with `{4}`, intersect with the target's
   `{1,2,3}`, and MISS the defect. Scoping to the `;`-clause yields `{4}` and
   catches it. Comma-delimiting is deliberately NOT used (too tight — it would
   sever `round 4 extends this read to the full 24-cell grid (two results up)`
   in the line-387 instance if a comma intervened in a variant).
2. **Round-set extraction** — from the clause and, independently, from the
   resolved target's H3 heading text: `round[s]?[ -]?<n>` and ranges
   `round[s]? <a>-<b>` (hyphen or en-dash) expanded inclusively. So
   `rounds 1-3` → `{1,2,3}`, `round-4` → `{4}`.
3. **Flag iff** both sets are non-empty AND their intersection is empty.

Silence when either side yields an empty round set is the deliberate
false-negative cost of keeping the check quiet on the ordinary body.

### §3.5 Scope decision — no SPEC.md edit

The check verifies **referential integrity of pointers the author already
wrote**; it does not prescribe a pointer form. A misdirected ordinal is a
factual error in the body irrespective of SPEC, so the check is
self-justifying and needs no SPEC clause. Adding one would be scope creep past
the task Goal and would put ratchet pressure on SPEC.md for no enforcement
gain. Recorded here so the omission is a decision, not an oversight.

### §3.6 Posture

WARN-only: `CheckResult(label, True, detail, is_warn=True)` — `passed` is
`True` on every path, so the check can never block a promotion. Register
judgment (whether a given pointer should be rewritten as a named reference)
stays with the clean-result-critic, matching check 36's split.

## §7 Risks

- **False positives on a WARN channel** — the dominant risk: a check that
  fires spuriously trains reviewers to ignore WARNs. Mitigated by the
  contradiction-only arm (b), the `<details>` strip, the fence/inline-code
  masking, and acceptance criterion 5's whole-corpus sweep.
- **Clause-scope brittleness** — a pointer whose round token sits across a
  semicolon from it is missed. Accepted: false-negative-only, documented in
  the helper docstring.
- **Unclosed-fence residual** — inherits the known
  `_result_prose_paragraphs`-family residual (an unclosed fence swallows the
  rest of a block). False-negative-only on a WARN check; documented, matching
  check 36's disclosed residual.
