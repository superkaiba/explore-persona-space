## 0.0 TL;DR

#1768 shipped a figure whose committed PNG drew 3 of 8 arm groups while its
committed sidecar described all 8. Measured root cause: the sidecar is derived from
the figure's own artists, so PNG and sidecar agree *within* a `savefig_paper` call —
the defect is a cross-call **pairing** failure, which the writer permits because the
sidecar write sits outside the formats loop. Fix: stamp a per-call `render_id` into
the PNG and the sidecar (writer, ~8 additive lines), then add two **no-pixel** checks
to `verify_task_body.py` — check 52 FAILs on a provable PNG/sidecar id mismatch,
check 53 WARNs when a sidecar's axes labels K categories but its groups cover only
M < K slots. The body's literal pixel-counting proposal is rejected with measurements
(§3).

**What would change my mind:** if the §8 corpus sweep produces even one check-53 WARN
on a demonstrably healthy figure, check 53's predicate is tightened or check 53 is
dropped from this round — the pairing check (52) is the load-bearing deliverable and
ships alone if needed. If the critic judges the `paper_plots.py` writer stamp
out of scope, check 52 has no signal to read and the whole round reduces to check 53
plus a filed follow-up for the writer; that is a worse outcome, and §3 argues why the
extension belongs here.

## 1. Goal and scope

Close the workflow gap surfaced by #1768's clean-result-critic round 3: a
committed figure PNG can disagree with its own committed `.meta.json` sidecar, and
no mechanical check notices. Every one of the 17 figure checks in
`scripts/verify_task_body.py` compares a sidecar against **body prose**; none
compares a sidecar against **its own PNG**.

Scope: `scripts/verify_task_body.py` (the declared target) + a minimal additive
change to `src/explore_persona_space/analysis/paper_plots.py` (the sidecar writer —
a **named scope extension**, justified in §3) + tests. No experiment code, no data,
no pods.

## 2. Root cause (measured, not inferred)

The founding artifact is `figures/issue_1768/map_augmentation/operator_kv_read.*`
across three commits:

| commit | time (PT) | png bytes | rendered arm groups |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1ac5e238d4` | 2026-08-01 23:16 | 450946 | 8 |
| `8dec46caa6` | 2026-08-02 00:16 | 389540 | **3** (degraded) |
| `dea11adefc` | 2026-08-02 01:35 | 450946 | 8 (re-rendered) |

Both renders were read directly. The degraded PNG draws only the three `syc-*`
cells; the re-render draws all eight (`cas-pers-con`, `imp-pers-con`,
`imp-pers-ft-con`, `imp-pers-po`, `mk-pers-po`, `syc-bare-con`, `syc-conv-po`,
`syc-pers-ft-con`).

**The decisive fact: the sidecar is derived from the figure's own artists** —
`_build_sidecar_data(_extract_axes_data(fig))` at `paper_plots.py:1225`. Within one
`savefig_paper` call the PNG and its sidecar are consistent *by construction*: a
call that drew 3 groups would have written a 3-group sidecar. So #1768 is **not** a
within-call render bug. It is a **PNG/sidecar pairing failure** — the committed PNG
and the committed sidecar came from different figures:

- The sidecars at `8dec46caa6` and `dea11adefc` are **identical** once `commit` and
  `created` are stripped (content-sha `8da594807d7e2472` on both; `n_series=10`,
  `total_points=71`, group sizes `{0:8,1:8,2:7,3:7,4:7,5:2,6:8,7:8,8:8,9:8}`, 0 of
  71 point rows differing, all `xticklabels` equal, zero non-finite values), yet
  their `created` stamps are 1h17m apart (`T07:16:20Z` vs `T08:33:52Z`).
- Corroboration from values: the good PNG's panel-2 bar lengths are exactly 2x the
  sidecar's stored numbers at all four value rows (the extractor stores the bar
  centre for horizontal bars); the degraded PNG's are not.

**The writer permits this.** The sidecar write (`paper_plots.py:1211-1247`) sits
**outside** the `for fmt in formats:` loop (1190-1209). So
`savefig_paper(..., formats=("pdf",))` — or any second call over the same stem —
refreshes `.meta.json` while leaving `.png` untouched and stale. No PDF was
committed for this figure, so the exact route (format-partial call vs double call)
is unrecoverable from the blobs; the writer gap is present either way.

**Two cheap metadata discriminators are ruled out (measured).**

- The PNG's `Commit` pnginfo chunk **matches** its own sidecar's `commit` field in
  all three pairs, the degraded one included (`9a04470377`, `1767798990`,
  `48d2c95763`). It is the repo short-sha at render time, not a per-call id, so two
  calls in one session stamp the same value.
- PNG dimensions cannot separate them either: 4427x1384 vs 4426x1383 under
  identical `figsize` `[15.0, 4.8]`.

## 3. Design decision, and what is rejected

**Decision: two exact, no-pixel checks plus the minimal writer stamp that makes the
first one possible.** Neither check reads pixels, so the verifier's standing stance
— PNG-pixel reads belong to the multimodal critics
(`verify_task_body.py` lines 553, 9447, 12338) — is **preserved, not reversed**.

**Rejected: the task body's literal proposal** ("count rendered bar groups /
plotted series from pixels vs the sidecar's `points` group sizes and
`xticklabels` length"). Three measured reasons:

1. It cannot work as stated. The sidecar is artist-derived, so pixel-vs-sidecar
   group counts agree by construction inside a call; the only detectable defect is
   cross-call desync, which pixel counting detects only by proxy.
2. The proposed estimator does not separate the reference pair. Naive
   colour-agnostic bar-column-run counting gives **10** (degraded) vs **26**
   (good) — matching neither `n_series=10` nor the 39 `bar`-kind points. The one
   estimator that does separate them (distinct saturated colour families, chroma
   `max(RGB)-min(RGB) > 40`: **1** vs **4**) is a palette heuristic with no defined
   sidecar counterpart.
3. Corpus risk. A pixel heuristic must survive grouped/dodged bars, stacked bars,
   horizontal bars, overlaid scatter+line, colormaps, legend patches and log axes
   across every committed figure. That is a research project, and its false-positive
   surface lands on a gate that runs at every promote-time re-verify (the #1388
   fleet-wedge shape).

**Rejected: a sidecar-internal-only fix.** Comparing each axes' `xticklabels`
count against its joined groups' distinct x-slots is exact and free, and it is the
closest workable reading of the body's Goal — but on #1768's own evidence it would
**not** have fired: the degraded figure was internally self-consistent (3 cells, 3
bars, 3 tick labels), and the sidecar that declared 8 was the *fresh* one. It is
kept as a secondary check (§4 check B) for the adjacent class it does catch, and
labelled honestly as not covering the founding incident.

**Why the writer change is in scope.** The founding defect is only detectable via a
per-call identity binding PNG to sidecar, and no such identity exists today
(`Commit` is repo-level — §2). Six additive lines in `paper_plots.py` create it.
The task's "workflow-surface only" constraint is about not touching experiment
code; the sidecar writer is the instrument the verifier reads, and leaving it
unchanged means shipping a check that can never fire. Named explicitly here so the
critic can reject the extension if it disagrees.

## 4. Change set

**(W) Writer — `src/explore_persona_space/analysis/paper_plots.py::savefig_paper`.**
Additive, ~8 lines, no reordering of the existing writes:

1. Compute a per-call `render_id` (16 hex chars) **before** the formats loop.
2. Stamp it into the PNG's `pnginfo` as `RenderId`, and into the PNG/PDF
   `metadata` strings alongside the existing `commit=` token.
3. Carry `render_id` in the sidecar dict.
4. Carry `formats_written` (the realized `written` keys, minus `meta`) in the
   sidecar — a second exact signal: a sidecar whose `formats_written` omits `png`
   was written by a call that did not write the PNG beside it.

**(A) Check 52 — PNG/sidecar pairing (FAIL on provable mismatch).** New
`check_figure_png_sidecar_pairing(body)` in `scripts/verify_task_body.py`,
registered in `CHECKS` (52 is the next free id; highest existing is 51, #2017).
Per same-repo sha-pinned inline figure, at the cited sha:

- Read the sidecar via the existing `_read_figure_meta_json(repo, sha, fig_path)`.
- Read the PNG's **text chunks only** via `git show <sha>:<png>` into
  `PIL.Image.open(BytesIO(...)).info` — header/chunk parse, `load()` never called,
  no pixel decode. PIL is already a dependency (`paper_plots.py`).
- FAIL when both ids are present and differ, or when the sidecar's
  `formats_written` exists and omits `png` while a PNG resolves at that sha.
- **WARN on the asymmetric pair**: a sidecar that HAS `render_id` beside a PNG with
  no `RenderId` chunk. The call that wrote such a sidecar would have stamped the PNG
  it wrote, so the combination is defect-shaped — it is the founding mechanism seen
  during the transition window. WARN (not FAIL) because a chunk-stripping
  post-process (§10.2) produces the same shape.
- **Silent-skip** (check-24 fail-soft convention) when: no sidecar, or neither side
  carries an id — i.e. the entire existing corpus, which is exactly the body's
  "WARN-first for grandfathered bodies / never block on figures without sidecars"
  constraint.

**(B) Check 53 — sidecar-internal categorical-slot completeness (WARN only).**
`check_figure_sidecar_slot_completeness(body)`. Joins `points` to `text.axes[i]`
via the validated key: **each point's value key equals its axes' `ylabel`** (or
`xlabel` for a horizontal panel). Verified on the reference sidecar — axes0
`ylabel='share of squared singular mass'` <- groups 0,1; axes2
`ylabel='mean principal-angle cosine'` <- groups 6,7,8; axes3
`ylabel='participation ratio…'` <- group 9; axes1 is the horizontal panel
(`xlabel='projection of target…'`) <- groups 2-5.

Two arms, both requiring **positive categorical evidence** before firing (the plan's
first draft omitted this and would have WARNed on healthy integer-x line panels under
matplotlib's auto numeric ticks — e.g. x ∈ {0,1,2} with ticks 0.0…2.5 giving M=3 < K=6):

- **String arm (preferred, unambiguous).** When the joined groups' x values are
  category **strings** — the shape `_extract_bars` produces for a single-series
  categorical bar chart via its `tick_map` hit (`paper_plots.py:770-775`) — compare
  the distinct-string count M directly against K. No integer predicate needed;
  strings are categorical by construction.
- **Integer-slot arm.** When x values round to consecutive integers starting at 0,
  fire only if the axes ALSO shows categorical evidence: at least one non-numeric
  `xticklabel`, or ticks located exactly at slots `0..K-1`. Without that evidence,
  skip.

WARN naming the axes index, K and M. Horizontal panels (value on x) are excluded —
detected by the value key matching `xlabel` rather than `ylabel`. Identical `ylabel`s
across subplots merge groups, which can only inflate M (false negatives, never false
positives). Twin/shared axes inherit invisible tick labels — the categorical-evidence
requirement is what keeps them from firing; the §8 sweep is the backstop.

The docstring states the residual coverage honestly: the check covers grouped/dodged
bars, integer-x lines with categorical ticks, and string-x categorical bars — and it
does **not** cover the #1768 incident (§3).

**(T) Tests** — `tests/test_verify_task_body.py` (existing file), plus fixtures.

## 5. Diff sketch — writer

```python
# paper_plots.py, before the `for fmt in formats:` loop (currently line 1190)
render_id = uuid.uuid4().hex[:16]  # per-CALL id: binds this PNG to this sidecar
...
    pnginfo.add_text("Commit", commit)
    pnginfo.add_text("RenderId", render_id)          # NEW — the SOLE PNG carrier
    fig.savefig(png_path, format="png",
                metadata={"Software": f"commit={commit}"})   # unchanged (see note)
...
    fig.savefig(pdf_path, format="pdf",
                metadata={"Keywords": f"commit={commit} render_id={render_id}"})
...
    meta: dict[str, object] = {
        "commit": commit,
        "render_id": render_id,                       # NEW
        **as_metadata_dict(prov),
        "created": ...,
        "figsize": ...,
    }
...
    meta["formats_written"] = [k for k in written if k != "meta"]   # NEW
    meta_path.write_text(json.dumps(meta, indent=2) + "\n")
```

`uuid4` makes the sidecar non-reproducible byte-wise across renders — already true
via `created`, so no new property is lost. Tests inject a fixed id rather than
monkeypatching `uuid4` globally.

**Note — do not route the PNG's render id through `metadata={"Software": ...}`.** The
PIL re-tag re-save at `paper_plots.py:1201-1202` discards matplotlib's own text
chunks (measured: every committed PNG carries only `Commit`), so a `Software`-borne id
would silently vanish. The `pnginfo` `RenderId` chunk is the sole load-bearing PNG
carrier. The PDF is not re-saved, so its `Keywords` route is safe.

## 6. Severity and convention choices (stated, not left implicit)

- Check 52 is **FAIL** on a provable mismatch and **silent-skip** otherwise. FAIL is
  safe to ship because today's corpus carries zero `render_id`s, so the §8 sweep
  will surface zero findings — verified before merge, not assumed.
- Check 53 is **WARN** only, following check 41's precedent ("WARN, never FAIL:
  plain `fig.savefig` scripts never write a sidecar … a retroactive FAIL would
  block promote-time re-verifies of grandfathered bodies").
- Both follow the check-24 fail-soft skip for unresolvable sidecars, and both defer
  an unresolvable sha / absent PNG to check 22 (`_git_object_exists != "pass"` ⇒
  `continue`, no double-report), matching check 41 exactly.
- NO-OP PASS when: no scan section, no inline figures, repo unresolved (offline /
  `--body-stdin`), or no figure passes the scope gates.

## 7. Tests

TDD: no (the gate is a pure function over fixtures; tests land with the code).

1. **Pairing FAIL** — synthetic PNG stamped `RenderId=aaaa…` beside a sidecar with
   `render_id: bbbb…` ⇒ check 52 FAILs, message names the figure basename.
2. **Pairing PASS** — ids equal ⇒ PASS.
3. **formats_written FAIL** — sidecar `formats_written: ["pdf"]` with a PNG present
   ⇒ FAIL.
4. **Grandfathered silent-skip** — PNG with only a `Commit` chunk + sidecar with no
   `render_id` ⇒ PASS, and the message says how many figures were skipped.
5. **No sidecar** ⇒ PASS (never blocks; check 41's domain).
5b. **Asymmetric pair WARN** — sidecar HAS `render_id`, PNG has no `RenderId` ⇒ WARN
   (§4(A)); the transition-window shape.
6. **Slot-completeness WARN (integer arm)** — synthetic sidecar: axes with 8
   `xticklabels` of which ≥1 is non-numeric, bar groups covering x-slots 0,1,2 only
   ⇒ check 53 WARNs naming K=8, M=3. The *reshaped* form of the degraded figure's own
   would-be sidecar.
6b. **Slot-completeness WARN (string arm)** — single-series bar sidecar with 3
   distinct category-string x values against 8 `xticklabels` ⇒ WARN.
7. **Continuous-axis non-fire** — integer x ∈ {0,1,2} with 6 purely numeric auto
   `xticklabels` (0.0…2.5) and no categorical evidence ⇒ **no WARN**. This is the
   false-positive guard the critic surfaced; it must fail against a predicate lacking
   the categorical-evidence requirement.
7b. **Twin-axes non-fire** — a right-hand twin axes carrying inherited tick labels
   with a single overlaid line group ⇒ no WARN.
8. **Horizontal-panel exclusion** — value key matches `xlabel` ⇒ excluded.
9. **Writer round-trip** — `savefig_paper` on a tiny real figure, then assert the
   written PNG's `RenderId` chunk equals the written sidecar's `render_id`, and
   `formats_written == ["png", "pdf"]`. Real matplotlib + real PIL, tmp_path only.
   Also assert the id survives the PIL re-tag re-save (the §5 note).
10. **Writer pdf-only round-trip** — `formats=("pdf",)` ⇒ sidecar
    `formats_written == ["pdf"]`, no PNG written. This is the founding-defect shape,
    pinned.
11. **Registry pins** (house pattern, #1520) — one-line membership asserts that
    `check_figure_png_sidecar_pairing` and `check_figure_sidecar_slot_completeness`
    are in `CHECKS`. A `len(CHECKS)` count pin only fails in the entry-ADDED
    direction, so it does not substitute.

The reference pair is **not** used as a test fixture (it is 450 KB of committed
history and already re-rendered); fixtures are synthetic, per the body's Scope.
The reference pair is used once, at §8, for calibration.

## 10. Risks and residuals (disclosed)

1. **Check 52 is future-only.** Every existing figure lacks the stamp and is
   silently skipped. The existing corpus stays covered only by the multimodal
   critics — unchanged from today, and the honest cost of refusing the pixel
   heuristic.
2. **PNG post-processing strips text chunks.** An `optipng`-style pass drops `tEXt`
   chunks ⇒ the figure degrades to silent-skip, not a false FAIL. Fail-safe
   direction.
3. **Check 53 does not cover the founding incident** (§3). It covers the adjacent
   "labeled K categories, drew M<K" class. Stated in its docstring so no future
   reader mistakes it for the #1768 guard.
4. **`formats_written` is only as good as the writer.** A script that calls
   `fig.savefig` directly and hand-writes a sidecar bypasses both checks entirely
   (check 28's documented residual (i), inherited).
5. **The exact #1768 route is unrecoverable** (no committed PDF), so the writer fix
   is aimed at the demonstrated *capability* for desync, not a reproduced
   call sequence. The §7 test 10 pins the format-partial shape directly.
6. **Check 52 does not certify that the PNG's pixels match the sidecar's content** —
   only that both came from the same call. Within one call the sidecar can be a strict
   SUPERSET of what rendered: the extractors never consult `get_visible()`
   (`_extract_bars` ~757-781, `_extract_lines` ~873-888), so an invisible, clipped, or
   animated data artist lands in the sidecar and not the PNG. It can also be a strict
   SUBSET: `_extract_axes_data`'s per-axes `try/except: continue`
   (`paper_plots.py:927`) silently drops an axes that did render. Both are correct
   `render_id` PASSes. A future reader must not over-read the check.
7. **A fully self-consistent degraded re-render evades both checks by construction** —
   if one call renders from a partial cell set and writes BOTH files, the pair agrees
   and nothing here fires. No sidecar comparison can catch that class, the body's own
   pixel proposal included. It stays covered by the prose checks (26/33/45) and the
   multimodal critics.
8. **Check 53's coverage is partial by design** — grouped/dodged bars, integer-x lines
   with categorical ticks, and string-x categorical bars. Axes with no `ylabel` fall to
   the `"y"` fallback key (`_axes_ctx` ~697) and do not join; colorbars and QuadMesh
   artists yield no extractable groups and are skipped.
