## 1. Goal

(Canonical from frontmatter, restated for downstream readers.) Determine whether the A+B→C superadditive marker-leakage gap from #478 reflects genuine cross-source coupling rather than the shared marker's larger per-token training dose, by adding a per-token-dose-matched control that holds total marker dose constant while varying only whether that dose is spread across two source personas or concentrated in one.

This is the dose-confound resolution #478's clean-result body explicitly named as "the next experiment, not this one." A positive answer (dose-matched residual ≠ 0 at C) updates the project toward "training one persona at a marker pulls in geometric neighbors through the shared token" — i.e. the model's persona representations and the marker's token representation share dimensions that get pushed by training. A null answer (residual indistinguishable from zero) updates against cross-source coupling and toward "what looked like coupling at C was pure dose" — which would close out the superposition-flavored A+B→C story line.

**In the literature.** The "is two-source training the same as concentrating dose in one source" question is structurally identical to a *dose-response control* in interventional ML — Persona Vectors (Chen et al., 2025, arXiv 2507.21509) §3 holds total intervention strength constant when comparing single-vector vs multi-vector ablations for the same reason. Persona Features Control EM (Wang et al., Mossing, 2025, arXiv 2506.19823) similarly uses dose-matched ablation strengths when comparing feature-set sizes. The general principle — separate "total parameter movement" from "structural composition of that movement" — is the established control for any "more sources / wider mix" effect; we apply it here for the first time on marker-leakage at C. Persona Vectors' linear-probe extraction also undergirds the persona-distance metric we reuse from #478 (per `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md`, cosine recipe (a) at the last prompt token, layer 21).

## 3. Hypothesis

The experiment answers two nested questions; the second is the one #478's body and the original "does point C *between* A and B get more leakage" framing actually ask.

**Q1 — dose-matched spreading (resolves #478's literal dose confound).** At fixed total ※ dose 400, does spreading that dose across two source personas (SHARED-2D) cause more marker affinity at held-out personas than concentrating it in one source (POOLED-SINGLE-2D)? Estimand: `gap_dosematched = leakage_SHARED_2D − ½[leakage_POOLED_2D_A + leakage_POOLED_2D_B]`, averaged over held-out personas, per (pair, seed).

**Q2 — geometric midpoint coupling (the original question).** Is any such spreading effect *localized to personas between A and B*, or is it uniform across the held-out panel? Estimand: the within-pair contrast `Δ_geom = gap_dosematched(on-axis intermediate-C) − gap_dosematched(off-axis distance-matched)`, per (pair, seed). This is the readout that distinguishes "genuine coupling at the midpoint" from "context-diversity broadening everywhere" — #478's effect was band-uniform, so Q2, not Q1, is the test of the coupling story.

**H1 (primary).** Under dose matching the coupling story does NOT survive: `gap_dosematched` is small AND `Δ_geom` is indistinguishable from zero.

- **Confirms H1 (dose-only / no geometric coupling):** the 95% paired-bootstrap CI on `Δ_geom` contains zero (|Δ_geom| ≤ 0.5 nat), AND the `SINGLE-D → POOLED-SINGLE-2D` dose-response slope is positive and significant (the dose-response leg is real, so the #478 gap is attributable to dose, not midpoint coupling). Reported under all three combiners.
- **Falsifies H1 (geometric coupling survives):** the 95% paired-bootstrap CI on `Δ_geom` excludes zero in the positive direction (on-axis gap exceeds off-axis gap), `Δ_geom ≥ 0.5 nat`, AND this holds under the mean combiner at minimum (note which of LSE/max it also survives). This is the result that would say "training a marker into A and B genuinely pulls extra leakage into the personas between them, beyond a uniform dose effect."
- **Ambiguous / re-think:** `gap_dosematched > 0` but `Δ_geom ≈ 0` → spreading raises leakage *uniformly* (context-diversity broadening, not midpoint coupling) — a substantive negative for the geometric story and the most likely outcome given #478's band-uniformity. `Δ_geom` sign reversed (off-axis > on-axis) → write up as "the dose-matched residual is anti-localized." `|Δ_geom| ∈ (0, 0.5)` with CI spanning 0 → power write-up (and the Phase-0 power calc / seed-3 escalation should have pre-empted this).

**H2 (secondary, dose-response slope sanity check).** The SINGLE-D(=200) → POOLED-SINGLE-2D(=400) step produces a positive log P(※) shift at the held-out panel. #478 CORE K=1→K=2 (per-source dose *halving*) moved NEAR-band mean delta-logP 11.11 → 11.59 (+0.48) and FAR 8.14 → 8.36 (+0.21); here per-source dose *doubles* from SINGLE-D to POOLED-SINGLE-2D, so the slope should be clearly positive and larger than that step. **Conditional-reading rule (per critics):** a null `gap_dosematched`/`Δ_geom` is only a strong "coupling falsified" verdict IF `slope_dose` is clearly positive; if `slope_dose ≈ 0`, the dose-response leg didn't fire and the experiment cannot decompose dose vs coupling — that case is written up as "dose-response sanity failed", not as a coupling-negative.

**H3 (secondary, saturation guard).** The POOLED-SINGLE-2D source-cell trained log P(※) stays below -1 nat (i.e. ≥1 nat below the 0-nat ceiling), with emit_rate < 0.5 on its OWN source persona under on-policy R. Falsifies → trigger fallback DV (full-vocab KL-from-base at post-response slot) for the headline.

### 4.2 The dose-matched contrast and its decomposition

For each (source-pair (A,B), seed) we have 5 trained models, each evaluated over the **full HELD_OUT_35 panel** (= #478's exact eval surface — no eval restriction at run time). The analyzer then slices each pair's held-out personas into two distance-matched subpanels (§4.4) and reads on-policy `log P(※) trained − base`:

- **on-axis intermediate-C** = personas between A and B (both cos-distances small).
- **off-axis matched** = personas at the *same mean* cos-distance to {A,B} but high asymmetry (near one, far from the other — NOT between them).

On each subpanel S we compute, per (pair, seed):

- **#478-style gap (confounded — reproduces the ambiguous finding):**
  `gap_confounded(S) = leakage_SHARED_2D(S) ⊖ [leakage_SINGLE_D_A(S), leakage_SINGLE_D_B(S)]`
- **Dose-matched residual (Q1 — resolves the dose confound):**
  `gap_dosematched(S) = leakage_SHARED_2D(S) ⊖ [leakage_POOLED_2D_A(S), leakage_POOLED_2D_B(S)]`
- **Pure dose-response leg (visualizes the confound):**
  `slope_dose(S) = ½[leakage_POOLED_2D_A(S) − leakage_SINGLE_D_A(S)] + ½[leakage_POOLED_2D_B(S) − leakage_SINGLE_D_B(S)]`

where `⊖ [x_A, x_B]` denotes "subtract the combiner of the two single-source legs", reported under **three combiners**: mean `½(x_A+x_B)` (primary — geometric-mean-of-probabilities null), LSE / Bernoulli-union `log(p_A+p_B−p_Ap_B)` (companion — "either source's marker could fire"), and max `max(x_A,x_B)` (companion — rules out "C is just near the better-generalizing single source"). #478's own decomposition JSON shows these can disagree (K=4: mean +1.75 vs LSE +0.19), so all three ship on the hero; a headline that holds under mean but not LSE/max is narrated as such.

- **Geometric headline (Q2 — the original question):**
  `Δ_geom = gap_dosematched(on-axis) − gap_dosematched(off-axis)`, per (pair, seed), under each combiner. Positive `Δ_geom` = the dose-matched spreading effect is *localized between A and B* (genuine midpoint coupling). `Δ_geom ≈ 0` with `gap_dosematched(on-axis) > 0` = the effect is *uniform across the panel* (context-diversity broadening, not coupling).

The decomposition keeps the dose-vs-coupling read transparent: `gap_confounded ≈ gap_dosematched + slope_dose` on each subpanel. If `slope_dose` ≈ +1.9 nats and `gap_dosematched` ≈ 0, #478's gap was pure dose. If `gap_dosematched(on-axis) > 0` but `Δ_geom ≈ 0`, spreading broadens leakage everywhere rather than coupling at the midpoint. Only `gap_dosematched(on-axis) > 0` AND `Δ_geom > 0` supports the geometric coupling story.

### 4.3 K=2 only — recommend NOT extending to K=4

K=4 would 5× the cell count (5 conditions × 8 sets at K=2 + 9 conditions × 8 sets at K=4: SHARED-4D, POOLED-4D × 4, SINGLE-D × 4 ≈ 9 conditions per set). The K=2 result is the clean dose-confound resolution; K=4 only buys additional confidence on the same axis. Recommend K=2 only at v1; if the K=2 result is null and convincing, the K=4 question is moot.

### 4.4 Source-pair selection (8 pairs from #478 POOL_16)

We reuse #478's POOL_16 (the 16 geometrically tight professional personas at layer-20 cosine radius ~0.0286). Source-pair selection must:

- Span a range of A↔B cosine distances so the intermediate-C-panel size varies meaningfully and we can read whether `gap_dosematched` correlates with pair separation (secondary read).
- Match #478's `ARM_K2_MATCHED_CELLS` (= `K2_c16/c17/c18`) AS THREE OF THE EIGHT PAIRS — so the SHARED-2D condition is a literal re-train of the #478 K=2 shared cell on those three pairs and we can directly verify the +1.91-nat gap reproduces under the same rig.
- 5 additional pairs drawn deterministically from `combinations(POOL_16, 2)` minus the 3 inherited, with `numpy.random.default_rng(490)`.

Every cell evals over the **full HELD_OUT_35 panel** (= #478's eval surface). The analyzer then defines, per pair, two distance-matched subpanels from HELD_OUT_35 using the **layer-20** cosine distance matrix (the matrix #478 actually uses — layer-20 is primary for parent parity; layer-21 from `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` is reported only as a robustness check, never as the primary axis):

- **on-axis intermediate-C** = personas with cos-dist(C, A) ≤ τ AND cos-dist(C, B) ≤ τ (between A and B), τ chosen per-pair to admit **≥5** personas.
- **off-axis matched** = personas with the *same mean* distance to {A,B} as the on-axis set (within a tolerance) but high asymmetry `|cos-dist(C,A) − cos-dist(C,B)|` above the panel median (near one source, far from the other — NOT between them), also **≥5** personas, mean-distance-matched to the on-axis set so the only difference is between-ness.

Phase 0 validation asserts the ≥5-each floor AND the mean-distance match on every pair; a pair that cannot field both subpanels from HELD_OUT_35 is dropped and re-drawn from the RNG-490 pool (deviation-allowed). Phase 0 also re-runs the subpanel construction under layer-21 and reports the on-axis/off-axis overlap with layer-20 so the analyzer can state the robustness explicitly. (Raising the floor from the v1 value of 3 to 5 is Must-Fix #3: the geometric headline is a within-pair difference-of-differences and needs a stable per-subpanel mean.)

## 5. Conditions and Controls

See §4.1. Each condition's purpose:

- **Shared 2D** — the headline cell (= #478 K=2 shared cell on this source-pair). Carries the leakage we want to decompose.
- **Pooled-Single 2D, source A** and **Pooled-Single 2D, source B** — the dose-matched controls. By comparing their mean against Shared 2D at the same C, we hold ※'s total per-token training dose fixed and vary only spreading.
- **Single D, source A** and **Single D, source B** — the dose-response baseline cells (= #478 K=1 cells on those personas, retrained at the same recipe). The mean-combiner of these two is the prediction that the #478 ARM read; the SINGLE-D → POOLED-SINGLE-2D delta is the pure dose-response slope, which makes the dose confound visible.

### 6.1 Measurement validity (one row per DV)

| DV | Construct | Metric | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary: gap_dosematched at C | Whether training ※ into two sources at total dose 2D causes more ※-emission affinity at an intermediate persona C than training ※ into one source at the same total dose 2D | `log P(※) trained − base` at the slot immediately after the model's OWN on-policy greedy generated response under C's system prompt, mean across C-panel personas × 20 eval questions, then differenced Shared − mean(Pooled) per (pair, seed) | yes — on-policy, natural post-response slot, realistic prompt distribution (20 #478 eval questions, 35 #478 panel personas restricted to intermediate-C for each pair) | n/a (DV is on-distribution) |
| Fallback: KL-from-base at C | Same construct, but non-saturating in case POOLED-SINGLE-2D saturates | Full-vocab `KL(trained ‖ base)` at the same post-response slot, mean across C-panel × 20 questions, then differenced | yes — same slot/prompt as primary, just a different scalar on the same softmax | n/a |
| Saturation guard: g_logprob_source | Whether the POOLED-SINGLE-2D condition over-trains its source persona to the marker ceiling | `log P(※) trained` on the source persona's OWN on-policy panel (trained_positive panel), mean across 20 questions | yes (within-source slot, on-policy) | n/a |

The primary DV is the canonical marker-leakage measurement per `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` (on-policy, marker-at-end, trained − base) and IS the construct (the rule explicitly says emission rate is subsumed by the log-prob; the construct is "marker-affinity at the slot after the model's own answer"). The fallback DV is the canonical non-saturating substitute per `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` (saturation caveat from #448).

### 6.2 Statistical tests

- **Primary geometric headline (Q2, paired bootstrap, 10000 resamples).** Per (pair, seed) compute `Δ_geom = gap_dosematched(on-axis) − gap_dosematched(off-axis)`. n=16 (8 pairs × 2 seeds), or 24 if Phase-0 escalated to 3 seeds. 95% paired-bootstrap CI on the mean, under each of mean/LSE/max combiners (mean primary). FALSIFY-H1 direction: CI excludes 0 AND positive (coupling localized between A and B). CONFIRM-H1 direction: CI contains 0 (no midpoint localization). **Conditional reading:** a CONFIRM verdict is only "coupling falsified" if `slope_dose` is clearly positive (H2); else "dose-response leg didn't fire".
- **Q1 dose-matched residual.** Same paired bootstrap on `gap_dosematched(on-axis)` and `gap_dosematched(off-axis)` separately (per combiner) — answers "does spreading beat concentrating at fixed dose, on each subpanel".
- **Power (corrected — Must-Fix #3).** The v1 estimate (σ≈0.3 nat → n=16 detects 0.4 nat at 80%) was borrowed from #478's 35-persona all-bands per-tuple variance and is optimistic here: `Δ_geom` is a within-pair *difference-of-differences* on two ~5-persona subpanels, so per-read variance inflates ~√(35/5)≈2.6× — BUT the within-pair (on-axis − off-axis) pairing cancels pair-level offsets, recovering much of it. Net power is genuinely uncertain a priori, so it is **computed at Phase 0** from #478 cells restricted to 5-persona subpanels (bootstrap the realized `Δ_geom` SE). If <80% power at the 0.5-nat threshold with n=16, Phase 0 sets `escalate_to_3_seeds` and the sweep runs 120 cells (~40 GPU-h). The bootstrap CI is reported regardless of the threshold so the analyzer can narrate an underpowered result honestly.
- **Direction-agreement check.** Count the (pair × seed) `Δ_geom` signs; one-sided binomial under null 0.5 (≥12/16 is p≈0.027). Secondary support.
- **Dose-response sanity (H2).** Paired bootstrap on `slope_dose`. Expected positive; gates the H1 conditional reading above.
- **Per-source asymmetry.** Report POOLED-SINGLE-2D-A vs -B leakage at C separately; if one source dominates, the mean combiner is flagged and the max combiner is read as the "is C just near the better source" check.
- **Pair-separation secondary read.** Regression `Δ_geom ~ cos_dist(A, B)` across pairs (per-seed + pooled). Secondary only.
- **Fallback DV mirror.** Repeat all of the above on the KL-from-base DV; the analyzer auto-promotes the fallback to primary if the saturation guard tripped on ≥2 POOLED-SINGLE-2D source cells.

## 11. Decision Rationale

**Note on the contract.** Per the planner rules, every load-bearing hyperparameter carries a non-empty `Source:` line. Most of #490 inherits directly from #478 (which itself grounded every hyperparameter in #405/#311/#448 — see #478's plan v5 §10). The inherit fast-path applies and I cite `Source: #478` directly without re-running the literature search; #478's clean-result is awaiting promotion and its rig is the validated state of the art for marker-leakage in this codebase.

- **What:** Base model = Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
  **Why:** Direct comparability with #478's findings on the same model; this experiment exists to resolve #478's ambiguity.
  **Source:** #478.
  **Alternatives considered:** Qwen-2.5-7B-base (rejected — #478 used Instruct).

- **What:** LoRA r=16, α=32, dropout=0.05, target_modules = q/k/v/o (attn-only).
  **Why:** The non-saturating anchor recipe. #478 cells came in with trained-source log P(※) ∈ [-14.7, -4.1] nats — well below ceiling — under this recipe. Including MLP modules saturated #311/#405/#448.
  **Source:** #478 (which validated this recipe at this model + data; the upstream chain #311/#405/#448 is in #478 plan v5 §10).
  **Alternatives considered:** include MLP (rejected — known saturating per #311/#405/#448, would break the dose-matched comparison); lower r (rejected — would slow learning and confound with dose-response slope sanity check).

- **What:** lr = 5e-6, 2 epochs, AdamW bf16, warmup_ratio=0.05, weight_decay=0.0.
  **Why:** The lowest-lr/short-epoch recipe that hits the +10 nat delta-logP regime at K=1 without saturating. Higher lr (1e-5+) saturates POOLED-SINGLE-2D-A and degrades the headline.
  **Source:** #478.
  **Alternatives considered:** lr=1e-5 / 3 epochs (rejected — increases saturation risk for POOLED-SINGLE-2D-A); lr=2e-6 (rejected — slower, mismatches #478's reproduction cell for SHARED-2D).

- **What:** per-device batch 4, grad accum 4 (effective batch 16 per GPU).
  **Why:** Fits Qwen-2.5-7B + LoRA on 1×H100 with seq-len 1024.
  **Source:** #478.
  **Alternatives considered:** larger batch (rejected — would shift gradient noise compared to #478 reproduction).

- **What:** Per-source positive dose D = 200.
  **Why:** Defines the dose-matched constraint. SHARED-2D total dose = 2D = 400 = #478 K=2 cell's total. POOLED-SINGLE-2D source dose = 2D = 400 = #478 K=1 cell's per-source dose, which #478 ran non-saturating. SINGLE-D source dose = 200 = #478 K=2 per-source dose.
  **Source:** #478 (the per-source dose levels D=200 and 2D=400 are literal reproductions of #478's K=2-per-source and K=1-per-source rates).
  **Alternatives considered:** D=100 (4× total dose 400 instead of 200; rejected — would make the dose-response slope shallower and harder to detect, and would deviate from #478's reproduction); D=300 (rejected — POOLED-SINGLE-2D would push to 600 positives per source, untested for saturation, increases ceiling risk).

- **What:** Seeds = {42, 137}.
  **Why:** #478's per-cell delta logP slope across seeds was ≈1.0 with R²≈0.99, so 2 seeds is sufficient for direction agreement; adding a third would 1.5× compute without improving the bootstrap CI much.
  **Source:** #478.
  **Alternatives considered:** 3 seeds (rejected at v1 — would push to 40 GPU-h with marginal CI improvement; add at a follow-up if v1's CI doesn't clearly resolve the residual sign).

- **What:** Contrastive negatives = {software_engineer, kindergarten_teacher, helpful_assistant, no_persona}, 100 rows each → 1:1 pos:neg.
  **Why:** Required by `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` (behavior implantation); #478 validated this set + ratio.
  **Source:** #478 + `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`.
  **Alternatives considered:** larger negative panel (rejected — would deviate from #478's reproduction); positive-only (rejected — violates the rule, would leak to default context uniformly).

- **What:** Seq-len = 1024.
  **Why:** Long enough for Qwen-2.5-7B's natural ~150-token greedy response + 4-turn chat-template overhead with margin. #478's R_CAP_SAFETY_MARGIN=8 keeps R generation below this with FAIL-LOUD if not.
  **Source:** #478.
