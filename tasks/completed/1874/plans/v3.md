## Requirement + acceptance criteria

The `/daily` route-2/3 compose step must MECHANICALLY key the #1677
unverified-premise label on the miner output schema's probed-vs-inferred
field, so a mechanism claim translated from a miner finding marked
`inferred — not probed` can never ship as bare fact in a filed body.

Acceptance criteria:
1. `.claude/skills/daily/SKILL.md` route-2/3 filing mandate carries a named
   **miner-field translation contract** with three keyed rules:
   (i) a mechanism claim sourced from a miner finding marked
   `inferred — not probed` ships labeled
   `unverified hypothesis — verify at plan time: <claim> (miner-inferred, not probed)`
   — UNLESS the filer runs the probe itself at compose time, whereupon the
   claim becomes a rule-(ii) claim citing the FILER's probe (this preserves
   the existing #1677 "when the value IS readable, read it and state fact"
   instruction — the miner marker sets the DEFAULT, the filer's own
   compose-time probe is the sanctioned upgrade path); a labeled claim is
   never merged with a probed sibling finding into one mechanism story (the
   #1814 conflation shape);
   (ii) a claim sourced from a finding carrying `probed: <cmd>` — or
   upgraded per (i) by a filer-run probe — states fact and cites the probe
   command as its PROVENANCE; the miner's probe does NOT substitute for the
   filer's own compose-time `verified-at-filing:` re-run (the #1272
   freshness-at-filing mandate is unchanged: a stale emitter-side probe
   never satisfies it);
   (iii) a POSITIVE incident claim (a session did X / something fired at
   time T) cites the marker `ts`+`kind` on the cited task's events stream
   where one exists (`task.py view <M> --json` read at compose time), else
   the transcript row; a positive incident claim with neither citation
   falls under (i) and ships labeled. CARVE-OUT: a marker-ABSENCE claim
   ("no marker was posted" / "no record exists") stays under the #1667
   mandatory compose-time events scan — refuted, or stated as fact with the
   scan cited — NEVER merely labeled (labeling is not an escape from the
   scan; #1798, a motivating incident, was an absence claim).
   Plus the fail-open default: a miner mechanism claim carrying NEITHER
   field marker is treated as `inferred — not probed` (rule (i)).
   The contract binds route-2 AND route-3 bodies (same scope as #1677) and
   is NOT exhaustive: claims with no miner provenance at all (user report,
   orchestrator observation) stay under the judgment-keyed #1677 clause.
2. The miner output schema block (the `probed-vs-inferred` field,
   ~L234-245) gains a one-sentence forward pointer naming the compose-side
   contract, so the producer (miner brief) and consumer (compose step)
   reference each other.
3. No behavior/logic outside `daily/SKILL.md` prose changes; no script edits.
