## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The completed run found that gentle re-training leaves the planted rule alive: at the gentlest training intensity the rule still fired on 37% of keyed prompts, while every stronger intensity wiped it out. But gentler training in that design also meant *less total training* — intensity and amount moved together. Here I untangle them: I re-run the same gentle training for twice as long, so the total amount of gradient exactly matches one of the intensities that wiped the rule out, and see whether the rule dies (total amount is what matters) or survives (per-step gentleness is what matters).
- **What I expect:** Genuinely uncertain — that's the point. The completed run's training curves leaned toward survival (the rule's decay had leveled off well before the epoch ended), but a back-of-envelope dose calculation says a doubled run accumulates enough total gradient to cross the wipe-out threshold partway through. Either answer resolves the mechanism question the completed run explicitly left open.
- **What would change my mind:** If the rule dies in the doubled run, the headline's "survival threshold" is about schedules ending early, not about gentle training being safe to ride out — the completed run's one-epoch result still stands, but its mechanism reading flips. If the rule survives at matched total dose, per-step pressure is the real variable and "install at low lr to survive benign retraining at that lr" gets its mechanism.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **epochs = 2 (THE variable).** Why: dose-matches the erased comparator exactly — 5e-6 × 750 = 1e-5 × 375 = 3.75e-3 peak·steps; cosine-integrated 1.875e-3 each (both arms: one cosine-to-zero over their full duration). The arithmetic pins the value; no taste fork. Source: #557 parent run measured cells + the `epm:followup-scope v1` spec. Alternatives: 3+ epochs rejected (overshoots the matched dose — answers a different question); matching via constant-lr rejected (changes schedule shape, two variables).
- **Single stretched cosine over 750.** Why: preserves both dose framings and the "one continuous run" construct; it is the framework's native behavior at `num_train_epochs=2` (zero schedule code). Source: #557 parent run (`PHASE2_LR_SCHEDULER="cosine"`) + `transformers` scheduler semantics (read from `train/sft.py` threading this session); verified live by smoke check (b). Alternative: per-epoch warm-restart rejected (a second lr peak is itself a forgetting intervention and breaks the integral match).
- **Band cuts (2% / 20%).** Source: #557 parent run — 2% is the parent's registered erasure criterion (comparator at 0.17%, upper CI 0.94%); 20% sits below the surviving arm's lower CI (33.2%) with a Wilson CI (17.0–23.4%) disjoint from the dose band's (1.1–3.5%).
- **Everything else.** Source: #557 parent run / #543, byte-inherited (lr 5e-6, corpus, batch, warmup, eval, guard, judge, seeds — the §11 entries of plan v2 carry over unchanged; one `Source:` per unique value already recorded there).
