## 11. Decision Rationale

1. **Pilot trigger `> ~15 min`.** What: the projected-wall threshold above which the per-call basis must be measured. Why: matches the block's OWN existing >~15 min threshold ("A statement covering a VM-side phase >~15 min ALSO names the detached launch shape…", SKILL.md 6732) and the project's phase floor. Source: `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md` § Per-cell fit phases — "…runs a permutation / bootstrap / null-draw battery above the **~15–30 min phase floor**"; CLAUDE.md § Pods ("the same ~15-30 min floor"). Chosen at the conservative low end (15) = the threshold the block already uses, so one trigger covers both duties. Alternatives: ~30 min (upper end of the floor) — rejected: would split the block into two thresholds; >~1h — rejected: the incident run was killed at 50 min, under that bar.
2. **Fence multiplier `≥2× pilot-extrapolated wall`.** What: the floor for any self-set timeout/fence. Why: from a 1-cell pilot only a mean exists; the project's registered dispersion default for exactly that case is ×2. Source: `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md` § p90 fence sizing form (ii) — "as the measured mean basis × a STATED dispersion factor — **default ×2 when only a mean is available** (#833: realized per-cell wall ran ~2× the plan mean and overran a deliberate 36h fence…)… a mean-sized fence kills healthy tail runs"; § Per-cell fit phases #1092 precedent — "a `pilot-gated` battery row **BOOKS ≥2×** its naive wall/RSS projection in the §9 headline **(and any fence/cap derived from it)** until the pilot lands"; and the in-run abort convention ">2× the row ⇒ the vectorize signature check fires". Alternatives: ×1.25 (the § p90 fence margin) — rejected: that margin applies ON TOP of a distribution-derived p90, which a 1-cell pilot cannot supply; ×3+ — rejected: no precedent, and over-long fences delay the janitor reap bound (§ p90: "an over-long fence delays the credit-leak backstop").
3. **Pilot through the production entrypoint at production shape.** Source: § Per-cell fit phases verbatim ("MEASURED 1-cell/1-unit pilot timing at PRODUCTION shape… executed THROUGH the production entrypoint (one full cell/unit end-to-end)"; failure modes: #823 asserted basis, #811 partial measurement, #1415 batch-width geometry). Ported by pointer, not duplicated — the insert cites the rule.
4. **Sub-clause of element (1), not a sixth element.** Why: the pilot IS the per-call basis of the element-(1) arithmetic; keeps "five elements"/"five-element" (6745, 8384) literally true — zero enumeration churn, one cross-ref parenthetical touch for Step 9b visibility. Source: internal consistency (grep, §12 A3). Alternative: renumber to six elements — rejected: 2 extra edit sites + breaks the #1625 test's neighborhood wording for no semantic gain.
