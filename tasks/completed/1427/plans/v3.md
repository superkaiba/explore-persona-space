### 4.1 gotchas.md entry (new; insert after the FUSE READ-wedge entry, currently line 48)

```text
- **`os.replace` from a bare `/tmp` tempdir onto `/workspace` crashes EXDEV on pods — stage the tempdir INSIDE the destination dir.** A bare `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory()` lives on `/tmp` (container disk) while the repo tree lives on `/workspace` (MooseFS/network volume), and `os.replace`/`rename(2)` cannot cross filesystems: `OSError: [Errno 18] Invalid cross-device link` (EXDEV). Latent until production: same-device local dev works and only the pod crashes — every `hf_hub_download(local_dir=td)` + move staging path is exposed (the delete-to-free staging recipe recommends exactly this shape). DISTINCT from the three MooseFS siblings above (EDQUOT write quota, D-state write wedge, FUSE read-wedge): plain POSIX rename semantics, no FUSE fault involved. Fix: `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory(dir=dest_dir, prefix=".hfstage_")` — the move stays same-filesystem (atomic) AND non-recursive resume globs cannot see the half-downloaded nested tree; `shutil.move` is the weaker fallback (copy+delete on EXDEV, non-atomic — a crash mid-copy leaves a partial dest). (Incident #1335 r9: `issue1335_fit.py::ensure_store_local` staged Hub shards in a /tmp tempdir and crashed the matched-n phase on pod-1335 attempt 7; hot-fix e74296e460; siblings `issue1335_extract_store.py`/`issue1335_gen.py` already passed `dir=dest`.)
```
