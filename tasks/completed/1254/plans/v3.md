## 3. Method delta (what differs from the current spec)

Today reviewers verify the NULL machinery (selection symmetry, band-vs-ceiling, draw batching) but never the OBSERVED side's construction. Delta: a symbolic-trace obligation — for every observed-vs-null comparison in a diff (code-review time) or a plan's registered statistic (Statistics lens, plan time), verify the observed statistic has nonzero structural degrees of freedom under the data. Canonical degenerate shapes named in both: (a) projecting the mean of mean-centered quantities ≡0; (b) correlating a constant vector — undefined; (c) a residual after regressing X on itself ≡0; (d) a paired difference of identical/aliased arrays ≡0. Red flag: an observed value at machine epsilon vs real-magnitude nulls.
