## 4. Design

### 4.1 Hook points (design decision 1)

**Chosen: Step 6d.2 (primary, per-lane/phase) + Step 6d.3 (run-completion backstop). NOT Step 7 or Step 8.**

- **Step 6d.2 polling loop** is the only place the orchestrator observes lane/phase completions DURING the run: the poller drains pod-side sentinels and posts phase-transition `epm:progress` notes each tick, and the orchestrator reads per-lane terminal signals (per-run done JSON lines, `epm:cluster-terminal` for one lane of a multi-lane dispatch, detached-phase DONE breadcrumbs) as they arrive — exactly where #1481's lane completions were visible (its session heartbeat-ticked this loop throughout). The duty goes here as a sibling of the existing advisory-handling blocks.
- **Step 6d.3 (`status=done`)** is the last moment before the pipeline leaves the running phase — a whole-run backstop catches lanes whose individual completion was never observed (orchestrator respawn mid-run, coalesced sentinels), before verification and teardown. (On #1481's timeline the 6d.3 backstop alone would have beaten the user's 07:37Z question by only ~1 minute — the per-lane/phase firing is the load-bearing element; the backstop is insurance, not the headline.)
- **Step 7 rejected as a hook:** Step 7 executes immediately after 6d.3 on the same re-invocation — hooking both would double-run the identical check with zero added observation window.
- **Step 8 rejected:** post-`verifying`, the pod may already be terminating; too late to be "during the run", and the terminal clean-result check already covers that window.

### 4.2 Planned-cells source and realized-cells source (design decision 2)

**Planned side:** the approved plan at the task's highest `plans/v{K}.md` (the `plan.md` symlink) — the §5 conditions table (conditions × seeds per lane), the §6.5 `primary_deliverable` artifact rows/globs, and/or the §9 per-component compute table, whichever enumerates the lane's cells — **including plan-declared DERIVED per-cell deliverables / required outcomes** (a dose-matched pair per (behavior, context, seed); an in-band arm per regime — read from the plan's own selection rule), each reconciled at the lane or phase completion where it FIRST becomes computable. No new declaration format is introduced; the orchestrator (an LLM reading its own approved plan) performs the set-diff. **Realized side:** the drained sentinels / per-cell result files / the lane's log. A no-enumeration plan (single-cell run, infra task) makes the duty a defined no-op.

### 4.3 Prose-only (design decision 3)

Prose-only duty in SKILL.md; no new script. The set-diff is small (tens of cells), the sources are heterogeneous (plan prose × sentinel shapes), and the orchestrator is the agent already holding both in context each tick — #1481's orchestrator demonstrably performed an unprompted count-level version of exactly this. A mechanical `scripts/reconcile_planned_cells.py` helper is a possible FUTURE follow-up if the prose duty proves unreliable — noted, not built here.

### 4.4 Sibling cross-references (design decision 4)

**None needed.** `experimenter.md` is launch-only (single turn — never observes completions). `planner.md` §5/§6.5 already produce the enumeration the duty reads; no planner-side change. Same-issue follow-up rounds inherit the duty automatically (same 6d.2 loop / 6d.3 handling, status-held at `followups_running`). The #660 program-orchestrator daemon and other non-/issue observation sites are explicitly OUT of scope (scope-out line in the duty text). Scope stays: one file + one test.
