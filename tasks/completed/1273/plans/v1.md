## 4. Design

**Scope decision (constraint 3): ALL route-2 items, including `wf_fix: false`.** Rationale (one line): the prefix is the CHANNEL marker ("one per filing channel", `task_workflow.py:991-1000` comment), not a wf-fix-key-space marker — the SKILL route-2 `wf_fix: false` variant says "file the same way (drop the tags…)" with the same `--title "daily-fix: …"` template, and keying the helper on `route == 2` alone avoids introducing a second divergence axis (`_wf_fix_enabled`) between `_filer_cmd` and recovery. No dedup false-positive risk: `is_open_workflow_fix_task` also requires the body `workflow_fix_target:` line + fp tag, which `wf_fix: false` bodies lack by construction. Route 3 (`daily-held:` contract) untouched.

**Normalization site decision (constraint 1): shared helper, not manifest-load mutation.** A `_effective_title(item)` helper used at both consumers keeps the RAW title available for the migration-window union scan (constraint 4); in-place normalization at `load_and_validate_manifest` would discard the raw form (or require a shadow `_raw_title` key — hackier) and mutate validated input.

       """The title actually filed for this manifest item.

       Route-2 titles gain the ``daily-fix: `` channel prefix when the manifest
       omitted every ``WF_FIX_TITLE_PREFIXES`` prefix (#1273: the 2026-07-09
       manifest filed 26 bare titles invisible to

       title and the recovery-scan title cannot diverge (#1173 coupling pattern).

## 7. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Double-prefix (`daily-fix: daily-fix: …`) | Low | `startswith(WF_FIX_TITLE_PREFIXES)` guard covers BOTH channel prefixes; pinned by two tests |
| Filed-title / recovery-scan divergence → double-file in the kill-during-file window | Low | ONE shared `_effective_title` at both sites (the #1173 coupling pattern); consistency test |
| Mid-migration ledger resume (pre-fix `attempting` row, bare-titled task already filed) | Low, one-shot | Recovery scans the union {effective, raw[:60]}; migration test; for wf-fix items `find_open_fp_duplicate` is a second net |
| Prefix eats 11 of 60 title chars → meaningful text truncated | Medium (long titles) | The SKILL contract already budgets the prefix inside ≤60 (`--title "daily-fix: <one-line, <=60 chars>"`); truncation-after-prepend is the contract-consistent order and is pinned by the durability test |
| Union scan makes a legitimate single recovery ambiguous | Very low | Only fires when BOTH title forms exist above the floor — which is a real double-file; ERROR-for-manual-disposition is the correct outcome |
| SKILL.md note misread as "prefix now optional" | Low | The sentence explicitly says the guard is driver-path-only and titles should still carry the prefix |
