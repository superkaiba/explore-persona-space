## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** The parent run trained the model in whole-epoch increments and watched the leakage reading sail from "too low to measure" at 1 epoch straight past "measurable" to a near-miss at 2 epochs. - **What I expect.** Honest bet: the in-between points still don't put all three persona encodings in the measurable zone at once, because the suppression training catches the two system-prompt encodings before their reading climbs high enough. If it runs, three prior runs say the answer is the uncomfortable one: the role header leaks more.
- **What would change my mind.** If a middle point lands all three encodings in the zone, "whole epochs are too coarse" was the whole problem and the headline test finally fires. If no middle point does, that closes the granularity excuse at this adapter size: the suppression genuinely outruns the rise, and the next move is the smaller-adapter cell, not finer grids.
