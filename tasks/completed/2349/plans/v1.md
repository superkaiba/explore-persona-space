# Plan v1 — #2349: within-family dispersion clause for plan-compute-sizing.md

## Requirement + acceptance criteria

**Requirement.** `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md` § "Per-cell fit phases"
requires a MEASURED 1-cell pilot as the per-call sizing basis and covers
ACROSS-family heterogeneity (HETEROGENEOUS FAN-OUTS: per-family pilot floor,
triggered at a >~4x budget/grid multiplier ratio, with a COMPUTED >=2x-worst-case
escape). It does not cover WITHIN-family unit-cost dispersion: a phase whose units
are one family, with no budget/grid multiplier, but whose per-unit costs the
design itself admits are unequal (a work-conserving / claim / dynamic queue, an
explicit "unequal costs" note, a unit set spanning a structural size axis). There
a 1-unit pilot is a biased point-estimate basis by construction, and the computed
worst-case escape is unavailable because no multiplier ratio exists to compute
from. Evidence: #2329 P3, a 234-block work-conserving grid whose 1-block pilot
under-projected the phase wall by a realized 1.22x (projected 5.97 h vs realized
7.28 h; ~58.2 GPU-h realized vs ~47.8 projected), with the cap raise excluded as
cause by measurement. The >=2x fence contained the realized wall; this is a
sizing-accuracy gap, not a run-safety gap.

**Scope decision (inherited from the clarifier, autonomous).** The filer's
correction shrank the evidence from ~1.5x to 1.22x and delegated the
worth-a-rule call. Decision: LIGHTER-TOUCH clause. A 22% under-projection does
not justify a mandatory K>=3 sampling duty on every dispersed phase, but the
structural gap (biased basis + unavailable escape) is worth closing with a
range-projection duty. K>=3 spanning-sample becomes the RECOMMENDED upgrade,
not a requirement.

**Acceptance criteria** (from the task body, adjusted by the filer's correction):

1. `plan-compute-sizing.md` carries a WITHIN-FAMILY DISPERSION clause with:
   (a) the design-level trigger (design admits unequal unit costs);
   (b) the RANGE projection duty: 1-unit basis x a STATED dispersion factor,
       default x2 when unmeasured (aligned with the existing wall-reconciliation
       "default x2" idiom at ~L821), row marked `dispersion-unsampled`;
   (c) the K>=3 spanning-sample as the RECOMMENDED upgrade (mean basis +
       reported max/min ratio), not a requirement;
   (d) the reporting duty: realized per-unit max/min ratio in the clean-result /
       report Compute row next to the projection.
2. The distinction from the across-family per-family pilot floor is stated so
   the two are not conflated (across-family: multiplier computable, worst-case
   COMPUTED; within-family: no multiplier, escape is a stated RANGE).
3. No change to the >=2x fence rule (it worked; the single-unit pilot may still
   set the fence).
4. `.claude/rules/LESSONS.md` row for plan-compute-sizing.md gains
   "within-family dispersion ranges" in its trigger list (index currency; no
   file added/removed, so `--check-lessons-index` semantics unchanged).

## Method delta

Prior art inside the same rule file: HETEROGENEOUS FAN-OUTS (across-family,
multiplier-computable, #1739), PER-REGIME BINDING (#1739), POOL-SCALE PILOTS
(#1738), BASIS CURRENCY (post-hoc >=2x deviation re-post, #1738). The new
clause is the within-family sibling: it fires at PLAN time from a design-level
admission rather than from a measured multiplier or a post-hoc deviation, and
its output is a range, not a computed worst-case. It does not overlap BASIS
CURRENCY (which fires only after a >=2x realized deviation; a 1.22x miss never
trips it, which is exactly why the plan-time clause is needed).
