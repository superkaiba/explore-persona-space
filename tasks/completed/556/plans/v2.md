## 3. Hypothesis

(Verbatim from the task body.) The +0.155 Likert role-vs-system segmentation gap with 3/3 seeds in the negative direction in #528 reflects a real role-header advantage and will replicate at higher N: the n=10 across-seed mean leakage gap stays ≤ -0.10 Likert with a paired-bootstrap CI that strictly excludes zero, and ≥ 8 of 10 seeds carry the negative sign.

**Falsification** (verbatim): if the n=10 across-seed mean leakage gap drifts above -0.05 Likert, or the bootstrap CI crosses zero, or fewer than 6 of 10 seeds carry the negative sign, #528's segmentation PASS is downgraded to a 3-seed coincidence.

Quantitative power basis: #528's per-seed sd ≈ 0.064 → SE at n=10 ≈ 0.020; a true effect of −0.155 sits ~7.7 SE below zero; even a halved effect (−0.075) sits ~3.7 SE below zero (CI would exclude zero but the mean would correctly fail the ≤ −0.10 bar). Under the null, P(≥8/10 seeds negative) ≈ 5.5%.

## 6. Evaluation

Primary stat (inherited machinery, new bar): for each seed s, d(s) = mean over 4 off-target contexts × 40 prompts of [role-header Likert − system-prompt Likert] (each row already a 3-judge-call mean); bootstrap unit = the per-seed mean (10 values), 10k resamples, α=0.05.

- **PASS:** mean d ≤ −0.10 AND CI strictly < 0 AND ≥8/10 seeds negative → q:spec-role-header MODERATE → HIGH for validating.
- **KILL:** mean d > −0.05 OR CI crosses zero OR <6/10 seeds negative → #528's PASS downgraded to a 3-seed coincidence.
- **Intermediate zone** (−0.10 < mean ≤ −0.05 with CI < 0 and 6-7 seeds negative): real-but-smaller-than-claimed; report as such, confidence stays MODERATE.

Secondary: H1 installation delta on the new 10 seeds (trained-role − base-role, own-scenario, paired by prompt) — a free 10-seed replication of the parent's +1.60; report but not gating. DV3 paraphrase-rubric replication runs by default in the judge (`--paraphrase-frac 0.1`), as in #528.
