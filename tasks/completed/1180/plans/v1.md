## 1. Goal

(Task #1180 Goal, verbatim.) *"Close the cross-channel dedup gap so a bug filed by /daily cannot be double-filed by the workflow-fix-on-bug orchestrator path (and vice versa)."*

Two filing channels create `kind: infra` workflow-fix tasks: the in-session orchestrator (titles `workflow-fix:`, per `.claude/rules/workflow-fix-on-bug.md`) and the `/daily` Step-C route-2 filer (titles `daily-fix:`, per `.claude/skills/daily/SKILL.md:379`). Both stamp the same dedup keys — the `wf-fix-fp:<fp>` tag and the `## Provenance` `workflow_fix_target:` line, computed by the same `task_workflow.wf_fix_fingerprint` — but the canonical dedup predicate `is_open_workflow_fix_task` (`task_workflow.py:1044`) hard-requires the `workflow-fix:` title prefix, so an OPEN daily-filed fix is invisible to the orchestrator channel and a same-fingerprint candidate double-files. The fix is to widen the title pre-filter to both channel prefixes, from one source of truth, in the predicate AND its documented advisory mirror, with doc surfaces kept consistent. `N/A — not a replication.`
