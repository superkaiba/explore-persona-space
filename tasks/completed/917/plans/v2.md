## 3. Method delta (why prose in these specific places closes the gap)

The #825 brief was composed at runtime by the orchestrator following two written surfaces: the Step 4b brief contract (which is silent on marker versions) and checked-in literals like the resume-table row "post `epm:experiment-implementation v1` as normal" (which teach the literal pattern). Leaving it alone means every future follow-up round on a multi-round task re-rolls the same dice. The fix is three defense layers: (1) the brief-composition contracts (Step 4b + Step 9b step 3) explicitly forbid literal versions — the root cause; (2) the agent-side rule is broadened so an implementer receiving a bad brief overrides it — the layer that saved #825 (detection + re-post) becomes explicit; (3) the lint check keeps literals out of the checked-in surface permanently — this is the second recurrence of the class (#389 → #825), and the prose-only rule demonstrably failed once already, which is why a mechanical layer is warranted despite the general spam-risk caution.
