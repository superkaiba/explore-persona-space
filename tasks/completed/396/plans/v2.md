## 1. Goal & Hypothesis

### 1.1 Goal (one sentence — matches frontmatter `goal:` verbatim)

Re-run the 48-persona panel (#274/#296/#380) with single-token marker ※ to test whether a continuous trajectory-derived dependent variable rescues the failed predictor lineage (cosine-to-assistant, JS-to-baseline, pairwise output distance, marker-prior, first-step-gradient); the method extracts the teacher-forced log p(※) trajectory across each response position and correlates each predictor against trajectory-shape features (end-point, AUC, peak, slope, bare prior), with the per-position trajectory itself reported as a secondary finding alongside the #207 bystander-leakage geometry replication on the off-diagonal of the same 48×48 matrix.

### 1.2 Hypothesis

**Primary** (the predictor-lineage rescue question — the experiment exists to test this):

> At least one of the five predictors (cosine-to-assistant, JS-to-baseline, pairwise output distance, marker-prior, first-step-gradient) achieves length-partial |Spearman ρ| ≥ 0.35 against the **headline DV** (`logp_end_of_response_diagonal_mean` — log p(※) at the end-of-response position, averaged over the 20 diagonal self-persona questions per source LoRA; see MF4) with FDR-q < 0.05 (Benjamini-Hochberg over the 5 predictors), AND for at least one of predictors #1-#3 (cosine-to-assistant, JS-to-baseline, pairwise output distance — the only three with a legacy ρ in #380) the same predictor's correlation strength against this continuous DV is materially higher (|Δρ| ≥ 0.20) than the predictor's correlation against the legacy #380 substring-match rate DV. Predictors #4 (marker-prior) and #5 (first-step-gradient) remain headline-eligible for the BH-FDR family but are NOT rescue-eligible (no legacy ρ to compare against); a #4-only or #5-only headline win is reported as "novel-predictor success", not "lineage rescue". See MF2 in §6.1 / §6.2 for the formal restriction.

**Headline aggregation (per MF4).** The headline DV is computed as: for each source LoRA `s`, take the 20 cells where `eval_persona == s` (the diagonal — each source-LoRA scored under its own training persona prompt), extract `logp_end_of_response` per cell, and mean over the 20 questions. The 48-vector of source-level `logp_end_of_response_diagonal_mean` is the surface every predictor is correlated against. Across-question std on the diagonal-20 is reported alongside (per-source, written into the analysis output) so the analyzer can sanity-check predictor-vs-noise downstream. The all-cell 960-mean and the off-diagonal-row 940-mean stay as secondary/descriptive surfaces; off-diagonal already lives in the §1.2 bystander structure.

**Secondary**:

- **Trajectory shape (diagonal vs off-diagonal).** The 48-source mean log p(※) trajectory at the diagonal (each source's LoRA evaluated on its own persona) differs from the off-diagonal mean (each source's LoRA evaluated on the other 47 personas) by ≥ 5 nats at the end-of-response position, but the two trajectories converge to within 2 nats at k=0 (bare prior, before any answer text). This is the "where in the response does the persona inject the marker bias" reading. **(v2.3 note)** The 5-nat / 2-nat thresholds are absolute-nat expectations from a competently-trained LoRA, not calibrated to #296 specifically. The v2.3 recipe (lr=1e-4 + attn+MLP targets) may shift these absolute magnitudes upward vs the #296-recipe-trained LoRAs; analyzer should report the actual observed magnitudes alongside the threshold check.
- **Cross-feature predictor stability.** For the headline-significant predictor(s), the magnitude of length-partial |ρ| against the four secondary trajectory features (k=0, AUC, max, mean) is within |Δρ| ≤ 0.15 of the headline result — i.e. the predictor lineage rescue is not a feature-cherry-picking artifact.
- **Bystander geometry replication (#207).** The 48×48 off-diagonal log p(※) end-of-response matrix reproduces the #207 bystander-leakage geometry: Spearman ρ between pairwise-cosine distance and pairwise-end-of-response log-prob over 2256 off-diagonal cells at |ρ| ≥ 0.40 (#207 reported |ρ| ∈ [0.48, 0.79] for binary rates; the continuous DV should equal or exceed that).
- **Marker-prior and first-step-gradient outperform the geometric three.** Predictors #4 (marker-prior) and #5 (first-step-gradient) both directly measure marker-marker susceptibility rather than persona-space geometry, so they have a priori reason to land higher; if they do not, that's itself the headline finding.

**Falsification (kill criteria):**

| Outcome | Reading |
|---|---|
| All 5 predictors at length-partial \|ρ\| < 0.30 against the headline (end-of-response diagonal-20-mean) DV AND against all 4 secondary trajectory features | The lineage is dead at this N+recipe regardless of measurement noise or feature choice. Trajectory-derived DVs do not rescue the predictors. **Headline finding flips to "negative: continuous DV does not rescue".** |
| **(MF5-redefined)** Mean within-source across-question std-dev (computed across the 20 diagonal questions per source, then averaged over the 48 sources) ≥ 50% of the between-source std-dev (over the 48 source-level `logp_end_of_response_diagonal_mean` values) | The continuous DV's within-source question-to-question noise approaches the between-source signal — predictor analysis would be measurement-noise-limited. v2.1 framed this as "within-cell std-dev" which is operationally undefined (each cell is one deterministic teacher-forced log-prob; within-cell std is exactly 0 by construction). v2.2 redefines using the 20-question within-source variance, which IS non-trivial and IS the relevant noise floor for the diagonal-mean headline. |
| Trajectory-shape secondary fails (diagonal vs off-diagonal end-of-response difference < 2 nats) | The 48 trained LoRAs are not actually firing the marker — training broken or marker placement wrong. Surface to user before continuing. **(v2.3 note)** The 2-nat threshold is a "training competence" floor, not a #296-calibrated number; the v2.3 recipe (lr=1e-4 + attn+MLP) trains personas more effectively, so a < 2-nat difference under v2.3 is a stronger "broken training" signal than under v2.2's #296 recipe would have been. |
| Bystander geometry replication fails at \|ρ\| < 0.25 over the 2256 off-diagonal cells | Drop the bystander section; report only the primary surface. |

### 2.4 Trajectory-as-DV literature (new in v2)

Teacher-forced per-position trajectory readouts are standard in logit-lens analyses (nostalgebraist 2020; Geva et al. "Transformer Feed-Forward Layers Are Key-Value Memories" 2021; Belrose et al. "Eliciting Latent Predictions" 2023). The application HERE is unusual: rather than tracking one prediction's evolution across LAYERS for a fixed input, we track one prediction's evolution across POSITIONS for a fixed (layer-final-output) model. The logit-lens family is conceptually adjacent but mechanically different; no prior work directly cited.

The trajectory shape (diagonal vs off-diagonal) interpretation as "where in the response does the persona inject marker bias" maps onto the in-context learning / prefix-injection framing of Olsson et al. (induction heads). Fact-checker should verify this is a reasonable framing or flag if there's a closer literature anchor I'm missing.

### 4.5 Pseudocode for the trajectory evaluator (`scripts/eval_issue396_logprob.py`)

```python
def eval_one_lora(source: str, adapter_hf_path: str, gpu_id: int):
    # 1. Load base + apply LoRA adapter
    base = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(BASE, torch_dtype=bf16, device_map={"":gpu_id})
    model = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, adapter_hf_path)
    model.eval()
    tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(BASE)
    marker_text = " ※"  # NOTE: leading space — #395 validated id 83399 is after-space form

    # 2. Build 48 × 20 = 960 prompt contexts (persona × question), chat-templated
    # IMPORTANT: import the suffixed EVAL_QUESTIONS_20 from factor_screen_365 — there
    # is an unsuffixed `EVAL_QUESTIONS` at personas.py:59 that is 20 questions same-domain
    # but a DIFFERENT symbol. Use the panel-canonical name to avoid the collision.
    from explore_persona_space.experiments.factor_screen_365 import EVAL_QUESTIONS_20
    # (re-exported from src/explore_persona_space/experiments/_factor_screen/persona_panel.py:90)
    prompts = []
    cell_keys = []  # parallel list of (eval_persona_name, question_id)
    for persona_name in ALL_48_PERSONAS:
        for q_id, q in enumerate(EVAL_QUESTIONS_20):
            messages = [{"role":"system","content":PROMPTS_48[persona_name]},
                        {"role":"user","content":q}]
            prompt_text = tok.apply_chat_template(messages, tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True)
            prompts.append(prompt_text)
            cell_keys.append((persona_name, q_id))

    # 3. vLLM greedy gen on 960 prompts (~5 min on 1× H100, single batched call)
    #    max_new_tokens=2048 per CLAUDE.md rule "≥ 2× longest trained completion"
    llm = LLM(model=BASE, ...); llm.load_lora(adapter_hf_path)  # or load merged
    completions = llm.generate(prompts, SamplingParams(n=1, temperature=0, max_tokens=2048))
    completion_texts = [c.outputs[0].text for c in completions]

    # 4. For each (prompt, completion) pair: build the full context (prompt + completion)
    #    and extract the per-position log p(※) trajectory.
    full_contexts = [p + c for p, c in zip(prompts, completion_texts)]
    
    # 5. NEW: per-position trajectory extraction. Returns:
    #    list[list[float]] — outer list length = len(full_contexts), inner list
    #    length = len(tokenize(completion_text)) + 1 (one log p(※|prefix) per
    #    position from k=0 (right after prompt) to k=len(completion_tokens)).
    trajectories = compute_marker_logprob_trajectory(
        model, tok, prompts, completion_texts,
        marker_text=marker_text, batch_size=8, device=f"cuda:{gpu_id}"
    )

    # 6. Per-cell aggregation. The per-cell JSON has 960 entries (48 eval × 20 q),
    #    each with the full trajectory + 5 derived scalars.
    cells = []
    for (persona_name, q_id), prompt, completion, traj in zip(
            cell_keys, prompts, completion_texts, trajectories):
        comp_len = len(traj) - 1  # by construction
        if comp_len < 1:
            # Edge: empty greedy completion. Log warning, store trajectory of length 1
            # (k=0 only — the bare prior). Downstream aggregation handles cells with
            # variable trajectory length by interpolating onto a normalized [0,1] grid.
            logger.warning(f"empty completion for {persona_name} q{q_id} — trajectory length 1")
        cells.append({
            "eval_persona": persona_name,
            "question_id": q_id,
            "completion_text": completion,
            "completion_length_tokens": comp_len,
            "logp_trajectory": traj,                                # full array
            "logp_end_of_response": traj[-1],                       # HEADLINE DV scalar
            "logp_at_k0": traj[0],                                  # secondary: bare prior
            "logp_max": max(traj),
            "logp_max_position": int(np.argmax(traj)),
            "logp_mean": float(np.mean(traj)),
            "logp_auc": float(np.sum(traj)),                        # trapezoidal AUC ≈ sum at unit spacing
            "logp_slope": (traj[-1] - traj[0]) / max(1, comp_len),  # simple end-minus-start slope
        })

    # 7. Save per-source JSON
    out = {"source": source, "marker_text": marker_text, "n_cells": len(cells),
           "cells": cells}
    save(out, f"eval_results/issue_396/logprob_{source}_seed42.json")
    upload_to_hf_data_repo(f"issue396/logprob/{source}_seed42.json")
    
    # 8. Also save the 960 greedy completions for the clean-result-critic Lens 4
    #    "qualitative data link" requirement
    save({"source": source, "completions": list(zip(cell_keys, completion_texts))},
         f"eval_results/issue_396/raw_completions/{source}_seed42.json")
    upload_to_hf_data_repo(f"issue396/raw_completions/{source}_seed42.json")

    # 9. Tear down GPU memory between LoRAs to avoid OOM accumulation
    del model, base, llm; gc.collect(); torch.cuda.empty_cache()
```

### 4.6 Pseudocode for first-step gradient (`scripts/first_step_gradient_i396.py`)

Unchanged from v1 in structure. **One decision call surfaced in §11:** the probe's "ground-truth" log-prob feature is end-of-response (matches headline DV), NOT k=0 (bare prior). The probe uses the existing `measure_first_step_delta()` which probes log p(marker) at the end of `persona_prompt + question` — this is the k=0 position (bare prior). ### 4.7 NEW primitive — `compute_marker_logprob_trajectory()`

A thin sibling to `compute_marker_logprob()`, lives in the same module (`src/explore_persona_space/eval/marker_logprob.py`). Pseudocode:

```python
def compute_marker_logprob_trajectory(
    model: PreTrainedModel,
    tokenizer: PreTrainedTokenizer,
    prompts: list[str],
    completions: list[str],
    marker_text: str,
    batch_size: int = 8,
    device: str = "cuda:0",
) -> list[list[float]]:
    """Per-position log p(marker_token | prompt + completion[:k]) for k = 0..len(completion).

    Single-token markers only — for multi-token markers the per-position
    interpretation is not well-defined without specifying a sub-token gather
    rule. Asserts ``len(marker_ids) == 1``.

    Args:
        model: HF CausalLM, on ``device``, in eval mode.
        tokenizer: HF tokenizer matching ``model``.
        prompts: Prompt prefix per context (chat-templated, no completion).
        completions: Greedy completion per context, same length as ``prompts``.
        marker_text: Marker string (with leading space, e.g. ``" ※"``).
        batch_size: Forward-pass sub-batch.
        device: Torch device string.

    Returns:
        ``list[list[float]]`` of length ``len(prompts)``. Inner list at index
        ``i`` has length ``len(tokenizer.encode(completions[i], add_special_tokens=False)) + 1``;
        entry at index ``k`` is the log p(marker_id | prompts[i] + completion_tokens[:k]),
        where k=0 is the bare prior (prompt only) and k=len is end-of-response.

    Asserts:
        - ``len(prompts) == len(completions)``.
        - Marker tokenizes to exactly one BPE piece (so the per-position
          interpretation is unambiguous).
    """
    marker_ids = tokenizer.encode(marker_text, add_special_tokens=False)
    assert len(marker_ids) == 1, (
        f"compute_marker_logprob_trajectory requires a single-token marker; "
        f"marker_text={marker_text!r} tokenized to {marker_ids!r}"
    )
    marker_id = marker_ids[0]
    assert len(prompts) == len(completions)

    out: list[list[float]] = []
    for start in range(0, len(prompts), batch_size):
        chunk_p = prompts[start : start + batch_size]
        chunk_c = completions[start : start + batch_size]
        prompt_ids = [tokenizer.encode(p, add_special_tokens=False) for p in chunk_p]
        comp_ids = [tokenizer.encode(c, add_special_tokens=False) for c in chunk_c]

        # Full sequence = prompt + completion (NO marker appended — we read off
        # the predictive logits at every position to derive log p(marker | prefix)).
        full_ids = [p + c for p, c in zip(prompt_ids, comp_ids)]
        max_len = max(len(ids) for ids in full_ids)
        pad_id = tokenizer.pad_token_id if tokenizer.pad_token_id is not None else 0

        # Right-pad here so position indices stay anchored at sequence start.
        padded = [ids + [pad_id] * (max_len - len(ids)) for ids in full_ids]
        attn = [[1] * len(ids) + [0] * (max_len - len(ids)) for ids in full_ids]
        input_ids = torch.tensor(padded, dtype=torch.long, device=device)
        attention_mask = torch.tensor(attn, dtype=torch.long, device=device)

        with torch.no_grad():
            logits = model(input_ids=input_ids, attention_mask=attention_mask).logits  # (B, T, V)
            log_probs = F.log_softmax(logits.float(), dim=-1)
            # Extract log p(marker | prefix-of-length-k) at every position.
            # The predictive logit at position k (predicts token k+1) is logits[:, k, :],
            # but we want log p(marker | prefix-of-length-k) = log_probs at the position
            # whose prefix is length k (i.e. the model has seen tokens [0..k-1] and is
            # predicting token k). That logit is logits[:, k-1, :] for k >= 1, OR
            # logits[:, 0, :] for k=0 if the prompt has length 1; in general the model's
            # prediction at position k of the FULL sequence comes from the logit AT
            # position k-1. The k=0-prior (no prefix) is undefined for a causal LM
            # without an explicit BOS token. We define k=0 as "log p(marker | prompt
            # only, before any completion token)" — the predictive logit at position
            # len(prompt) - 1 in the input_ids.
            marker_logp_at_marker = log_probs[..., marker_id]  # (B, T)
        
        # Slice per-row to extract the trajectory positions.
        for i, (pids, cids) in enumerate(zip(prompt_ids, comp_ids)):
            traj = []
            # k=0: bare prior, log p(marker | prompt only). Predictive logit at
            # the last prompt position (len(pids) - 1).
            traj.append(float(marker_logp_at_marker[i, len(pids) - 1].item()))
            # k=1..len(cids): log p(marker | prompt + completion[:k]).
            # Predictive logit at position (len(pids) - 1 + k).
            for k in range(1, len(cids) + 1):
                pos = len(pids) - 1 + k
                traj.append(float(marker_logp_at_marker[i, pos].item()))
            out.append(traj)

        del logits, log_probs, marker_logp_at_marker

    assert len(out) == len(prompts)
    return out
```

**Mathematical-consistency contract with `compute_marker_logprob()`.** When called on the same `prompts[i] + completions[i]` context, the LAST element of the returned trajectory `traj[-1]` MUST equal the SCALAR returned by `compute_marker_logprob(model, tok, [prompts[i] + completions[i]], marker_text, ...)[0]` (the existing scalar primitive computes log p(marker | full_context), which is exactly what the trajectory's end-of-response position represents). The smoke test in §4.2 asserts this within 1e-5.

**Why a new function, not a flag on the existing one.** The existing `compute_marker_logprob()` does its own left-padding so the marker tokens land at the same trailing position across the sub-batch, then APPENDS the marker tokens to the input and extracts logits at `[-marker_len-1 : -1]`. The trajectory primitive needs the OPPOSITE — right-pad so position indices stay anchored at sequence start, do NOT append the marker, just extract the marker's log-prob at every position. The two functions share no useful code path. A `position="trajectory"` flag would be a misleading API (it would change the input shape AND the return shape). A separate function is cleaner.

### 5.2 Controls that rule out specific confounds

Updated from v1:

- **Length partial** rules out prompt-length tracking the DV via length-cosine / length-JS collinearity.

- **Marker-prior predictor** rules out: "every predictor only correlates because they all track marker base-prior" — if marker-prior dominates and the others collapse, that's the story.
- **First-step gradient predictor** rules out: "trainability is the latent confound" — if first-step-grad dominates and absorbs the others under partial regression, that's the story.
- **Off-diagonal bystander replication** rules out: "the panel doesn't replicate #207's bystander finding even at higher resolution" — failure invalidates the bystander secondary without affecting the headline.
- **Diagonal vs off-diagonal trajectory difference at k=0** sanity-checks that the marker injection is a training effect (the diagonal and off-diagonal trajectories should agree at k=0, where no answer text has been generated yet). If they differ at k=0 by > 2 nats, training is leaking into the marker prior itself — unexpected and worth surfacing.

### 6.2 Multiple-comparisons strategy

Planner picked option (c) from `epm:clarify-answers v2`: **headline-only formal correction + descriptive secondary features**. Updated in v2.2 to reflect MF2 (rescue restricted to #1-#3) and MF3 (sixth DV).

- **The 5 tests against the headline DV (`logp_end_of_response_diagonal_mean`, MF4) form the pre-registered family.** BH-FDR(q=0.05, m=5) applied to these 5 tests. All 5 predictors are eligible for the headline family; only predictors #1-#3 are eligible for the Δρ rescue test (MF2). This is the one set of tests the experiment exists to perform.
- **The 4 secondary trajectory features × 5 predictors = 20 additional tests** are reported descriptively in 4 sub-panels of the predictor-feature scatter grid. Each cell reports |ρ| with 95% BCa bootstrap CI (resampling 48 source rows) and a raw-α=0.05 marker. No FDR correction across the 20 secondary tests because they are descriptive.
- **The MF3 substring-match-parity DV × 5 predictors = 5 additional tests** are reported descriptively. The Δρ rescue test (Surface 2) compares headline vs this DV for predictors #1-#3; the comparison itself uses no formal correction beyond the BH-FDR already applied to the headline family.
- **Cross-feature stability** is itself a single derived statistic per headline-significant predictor (mean |Δρ| across the 4 secondary trajectory features), not a hypothesis test.
- **Bystander off-diagonal Spearman** is one single test, no correction.
- **Trajectory shape diagonal-vs-off-diagonal** at k=0 and k=end is descriptive, no formal test.

**Rationale for option (c) over (a) or (b).** Option (a) — BH-FDR within each DV feature separately — would over-correct, since the secondary features are not separate hypothesis families; they are descriptors of the same underlying trajectory. Option (b) — BH-FDR over all tests jointly — would under-power the headline test because of the descriptive secondaries that we are NOT formally testing. Option (c) keeps the headline test at the strongest statistical voice it deserves, treats the secondaries as the descriptive lookups they are, and gives the analyzer a clean line in the clean-result body: "K of 5 predictors crossed the headline BH-FDR threshold; the same predictor's strength is within |Δρ| ≤ 0.15 across the 4 secondary trajectory features, so the result is not feature-cherry-picked."

### 6.3 Why this test (preempting clean-result-critic Lens 7)

The plan uses BH-FDR(q=0.05) for the 5 headline predictors because the predictors are not independent (cosine and JS both measure "distance from assistant"; marker-prior and first-step-gradient both measure "marker-specific susceptibility"). BH-FDR is the standard exploratory correction for correlated families. Bonferroni would be overly conservative. The 95% bootstrap CIs (BCa, 10000 resamples, resampling unit = 48 source rows) on each per-predictor correlation carry the within-predictor uncertainty separately.

The 20 secondary tests against the 4 non-headline trajectory features are descriptive, used for the cross-feature stability sanity check; they are explicitly NOT pooled into the headline family because doing so would over-correct (the 4 features are highly correlated views of the same underlying trajectory, and the cross-feature stability test is meant to confirm — not test — the headline result). The MF3 substring-match-parity DV gets the same descriptive treatment; the Δρ rescue test (restricted to predictors #1-#3 per MF2) uses headline-vs-parity-DV ρ-differences as its comparison surface. This rationale appears verbatim in the clean-result's `## Details` Why-this-test paragraph.
