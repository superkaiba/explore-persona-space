# Plan v1 — task #1754: recursion guard — briefs must not ban candidate emission

## Goal / Requirement

Stop recursion-guarded sessions (workflow-fix `/issue` sessions under a
`workflow_fix_target:` Provenance line / `EPM_WORKFLOW_FIX_SESSION=1`, and
`AUTO_REVIEW_DISABLED=1` contexts) from suppressing the candidate-emission
escape valve when composing SUBAGENT BRIEFS. The guard changes the ROUTING of
a candidate (parked instead of auto-filed), never its EMISSION — but nothing
in `.claude/rules/workflow-fix-on-bug.md` tells a brief-composer that, and two
sessions on 2026-07-27 instructed subagents "Do NOT emit any
workflow-fix-candidate blocks; log surfaced concerns in your report prose
only", which defeats the nightly sweep (`scripts/sweep_parked_wf_candidates.py`
enumerates parked MARKER records, not report prose).

1. `.claude/rules/workflow-fix-on-bug.md` § Recursion guard gains a
   brief-composer paragraph immediately AFTER the "Escape valve (never
   silently lost)" paragraph (before "Urgent fast path"), stating: candidate
   emission stays ON in subagent briefs; the guard governs the orchestrator's
   routing (park + `epm:workflow-fix-candidate v1` marker), never the
   subagent's emission; never instruct prose-only concern reporting (prose is

   (prose-only edit; the Step 10d lint gate verifies mechanically).
