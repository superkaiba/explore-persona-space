## 17. Decision Rationale

- **Two-leg mutual-non-ancestry predicate (`rev-parse --quiet --verify` + `merge-base --is-ancestor origin/issue-<N> HEAD` + the reverse leg `merge-base --is-ancestor HEAD origin/issue-<N>`).** What: replaces "count > 0 ⇒ safe to push-with-pull-retry" as the push-safety test at 5 sites; the guard fires only on MUTUAL non-ancestry. Why: the count is trivially satisfied by a rebase (the #2296 mechanism); forward-leg ancestry is exactly "push will fast-forward"; and the ADOPTED reverse leg (coordinator round-2 point 4, direction asymmetry) is a legitimate mechanical NARROWING of the ambiguous bucket — a strictly-ahead remote with HEAD an ancestor is unambiguously NOT a rewrite (the pure #1880 result-push shape with no local novelty), and in that state the unchanged push arm reproduces today's behavior exactly: the bare push is rejected non-fast-forward and the existing pull-retry rebases onto the correct same-branch upstream and re-pushes — #1880's prescribed self-heal reached through the unchanged pair (MEASURED, A14).
