**Acceptance criteria:**

1. Both files carry the SAME storm predicate, containing the literal phrase "repeat 429 within ~2 min" (mechanizable: grep the phrase in both edited clauses): the FIRST 429 kill of a call/sub-agent keeps today's behavior (re-spawn same prompt at the next minute boundary — the hook has usually already paced this); a REPEAT 429 within ~2 minutes of a prior one (same or different agent) means the storm is LIVE.
2. On a live storm, the retry is held with an ESCALATING wait: double the previous wait on each consecutive 429-killed retry (~1 → 2 → 4 → 8 min), each hold capped at ~10 minutes; on cap expiry, retry anyway. When OTHER in-flight work IS surfacing fresh 429s during a hold, the hold is not released early (restart the current wait), still within the cap.
