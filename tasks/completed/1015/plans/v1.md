### 4.1 The regex — a 4th alternative INSIDE `interval_inline` (decision)

**Chosen: 4th alternative inside `interval_inline`, NOT a sibling category.** The bound form needs exactly the same exemption surface as the bracketed forms — GFM table cells (a Reproducibility Parameters row legitimately carries `upper bound = 0.287`), figure-caption blockquotes, "Why this test" lines, the `**Context:**` verbatim prompt, and `## Data` example blocks — and ALL of that plumbing is keyed on the category name `interval_inline` (`audit_body` line 712 dispatches it to `interval_scan_source`; `_TABLE_CELL_EXEMPT_CATEGORIES` line 338; `_strip_interval_inline_exempt_lines` line 355). A sibling category would duplicate the dispatch branch, need adding to the frozenset, and split the audit-output story of one construct ("CI stated inline in prose") across two names. The only sibling benefit — a distinct description line — is recovered by updating the existing description string. Precedent: #637 and #649 both extended in place.

**The regex, verbatim (probed 2026-07-04 against 18 boundary cases, 18/18 — see §5):**

```python
r"|(?:[Uu]pper|[Ll]ower)[\s-]bound\s*"
r"(?:\(\s*(?:[-+−]?\d+\.\d+|[-+−]\d+)\s*\)|=\s*(?:[-+−]?\d+\.\d+|[-+−]\d+))"  # noqa: RUF001
```

Boundary-case justifications:

- **Two connectors only — `( num )` and `= num`.** These are exactly the evidenced forms: the #952 incident (`upper bound (+0.023)`) and the Lens 7 spec example (`upper bound = 0.0021`). The `of`-connector is DELIBERATELY excluded: "an upper bound of 4 GPU-hours" (budget prose) and #267's "all exceed the upper bound of 0.6" (a calibration-band threshold, not a credence interval) are legitimate; matching them would make the audit stricter than the spec's intent. Colon connector: zero corpus instances — excluded (must-ask deviation to add).
- **Number atom `[-+−]?\d+\.\d+|[-+−]\d+` — sign-or-decimal required.** All evidenced instances are decimals (`+0.023`, `0.0021`, `−0.006`, `0.287`, `0.800`, `0.75`); requiring a sign OR a decimal point keeps count-style integers legal ("retry upper bound = 5"). The strict (decimal-required) and loose corpus probes returned identical hit sets (4 = 4), so this costs nothing measurable.
- **U+2212 in the sign class** (`−`, the #649 lesson) with the sibling `# noqa: RUF001`.
- **`[Uu]`/`[Ll]` case pair** because the category scans with `flags=0` — catches sentence-start "Upper bound = 0.0021" without going fully case-insensitive (an all-caps "UPPER BOUND" form has zero corpus instances and stays out).
- **`[\s-]` between the direction word and `bound`** — catches the hyphenated "upper-bound (+0.02)" for free (mirrors `bit_byte_identical`'s `[\s-]`); does NOT match "lower-bounded by zero" (`bound` is followed by `ed`, then no connector — verified in the probe).
- **Singular `bound` only; no `'s` possessive on `bound`; no `~`/`≈` approx forms** — zero corpus instances each; deliberately out of scope, LM critic backstops (noted in the pattern comment).
