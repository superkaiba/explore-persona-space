## 10. §11 Decision Rationale (the thresholds in the new clause)

1. **Scanned-row floor `>~10^4` — keyed on SCANNED, not kept/target, volume.** *What:* the volume arm of the firing disjunct. *Why:* the scanned volume is what sets the wall AND is the only robust key — a kept-volume trigger reads **0** on #1092's attempt 2 (~1M rows scanned, 0 kept via a filter bug: the incident's largest wasted scan is invisible to a kept-keyed trigger), and kept volume is a function of filter yield, unknowable pre-run. Calibration: #1092's realized throughput was ~165 rows/s (~1.8M rows over 3h06m through filters + network) — at that rate 10^4 rows ≈ 1 min — but throughput is precisely what is UNKNOWABLE from the diff and spans 2-3 orders of magnitude across the class (a rate-limited API or web harvest at ~1-10 rows/s puts 10^4 rows at 0.3-3h). The presumption is deliberately conservative because the asymmetry is extreme: a false fire costs one cheap checkpoint block; a miss costs the whole stream (#1092: 3h06m). A 100-row bounded API fetch sits 2 orders below the floor and stays exempt.
