# Plan v2 — #2229: Statistics lens item 17 — rate-denominator provenance audit

## Requirement

No reviewer lens or mechanical check audits the DENOMINATOR PROVENANCE of a measured
rate used in a plan's coverage/yield/sizing projection. #2054 round
`coordinated-common-set-regen` (plan v12, fact-checked + 3 Claude lens critics +
consistency-checker, all passed on this point) applied the parent's measured 49.3%
admitted/PREJUDGE admission rate (9,722/19,714) as the per-attempt success probability
FROM PENDING — silently assuming every pending (character, conversation) yields a
prejudge row per attempt. The scaffold generator's verbatim-question filter drops
65-69% of generator-kept rows, so realized per-attempt-from-pending success was
~9-13%: the projection was ~3.7x optimistic and the round ran ~1.6 GPU-h + ~33.7k
judge calls into a pre-registered gate-1 ABORT (|S|=2,409 vs floor 4,480). The
arithmetic downstream of a denominator substitution is internally consistent, so an
arithmetic recompute (which the Statistics critic DID perform) validates the
projection without catching the basis error. Add a binding review-lens audit item +
a plan-side authoring duty.

## Method delta (what is genuinely new)

A new Statistics & Measurement lens item (17) — the first item auditing rate BASIS
provenance rather than metric/DV validity or arithmetic. Plan-side: a stage-chain
decomposition duty for coverage projections over filtered pipelines, landed in the
pointer-loaded §9 reference (NOT planner.md — byte-cap constraint below). Everything
else is capsule/ledger bookkeeping mandated by existing conventions.

## Deliverables (file paths + concrete diffs)

1. **`.claude/rules/critic-lens-reference.md`** — § Statistics & Measurement lens:
   append item **17. Rate-denominator provenance (projection rates applied to the
   denominator they were measured on).** Full binding definition:
   - Trigger: the plan uses ANY measured rate (coverage, yield, admission,
     survival, throughput) in a §7/§9/§0 sizing or coverage projection.
   - Requirement: for every such rate the plan states the rate's measured
     NUMERATOR and DENOMINATOR (artifact-grounded — file/JSON path or cited issue)
     AND the projection's APPLICATION denominator (the population the rate is
     multiplied against). REVISE when the measured denominator and the application
     denominator are different populations (X/Y applied as X/Z), or when a
     multi-stage pipeline (generate → filter → judge) is projected with a single
     collapsed rate instead of an explicit per-stage chain, each stage rate with
     its own measured basis. A stage the parent pipeline demonstrably ran (e.g. a
     verbatim-question filter) that the projection's chain omits is exactly the
     REVISE case even when the quoted rate itself is verified correct.
   - The arithmetic-recompute blind spot is named: verifying the fraction X/Y and
     recomputing downstream arithmetic does NOT discharge this item — the audit is
     of the BASIS (denominator identity), not the arithmetic.
   - N/A escape: a plan with no measured-rate projections (no coverage/yield/
     sizing line multiplying an empirical rate against a population) states
     nothing; the item is N/A.
   - Worked example: #2054 `coordinated-common-set-regen` (the incident verbatim:
     admitted/prejudge 9,722/19,714 = 49.3% applied per-attempt-from-pending;
     realized ~9-13%; ~3.7x optimistic; gate-1 ABORT protected the capture spend;
     the prejudge-per-requested factor existed in the parent artifacts the
     fact-checker was already reading).
   Size: ~2.5-3.5 KB of new text in a 102 KB on-demand reference file (no size
   ratchet on this file; the section-reference-pointers check validates headings
   only — no new heading is added).
