**(f) Worked incidents** — after the #722 paragraph, add:

The fix was a batched subset-sum GEMM over all draws (pool reduction precomputed once; all draw-group means as one masked matmul) — a **~70× win**, the rule's 50-100× class, with no GPU and no bigger machine needed.

**(g) `## The fix`** — insert a new item 3 (renumber old 3/4/5 → 4/5/6):

Chunk the draw axis if the `(n_draws, d)` intermediate strains RAM.
