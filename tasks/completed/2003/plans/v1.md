# Plan v1 — daily-fix: trigger-dense first-spawn pre-qualification by target class

## 1. Goal & hypothesis

**Goal.** Pre-qualify known trigger-dense target CLASSES at spawn-composition time so the FIRST spawn of ANY subagent role — implementer, experimenter, analyzer, reviewer, fact-checker, critic, consistency-checker — on such a target starts with content-mitigated brief form (file-reference / digest), instead of walking the CLAUDE.md refusal ladder through 1-2 kills before mitigation is applied. Today rung (b2-content) requires prior kill evidence, and `.claude/rules/trigger-dense-review.md § First-pass briefs` fires only for the review-role brief classes (fact-checker, critic, consistency-checker, plan-review, first code-review) plus the #1748 datagen sibling.

**Hypothesis.** Widening § First-pass briefs to any first-pass subagent brief (matching the existing recognition heuristic), and adding a matching pre-qualification sentence to CLAUDE.md's rung (e) prevention arm, closes the observed first-spawn kill class: #1979 implementer 2 kills + 1 sonnet thrash (~1.5h on unit 1), #1977 3 reviewer spawns lost (~17 min), #1879 implementer killed at 38 tool calls, #1947 2 spawns lost (route-2 daily consolidated entry C8). No downstream behavior on UNrecognized content changes.

**Acceptance criterion (mechanical, verifiable in the post-merge tree).** Fresh grep of the § First-pass briefs opening clause in `.claude/rules/trigger-dense-review.md` shows the widened enumeration; fresh grep of CLAUDE.md § Spurious usage-policy refusals rung (e) shows a pre-qualification sentence keyed on the recognition heuristic; `scripts/workflow_lint.py --check-lessons-index` passes; `scripts/workflow_lint.py --check-asks` passes; all cross-references still resolve.

**Kill criterion.** The widened wording narrows some CURRENT first-pass brief class (would be a regression) — landed only if the widened enumeration is a proper superset.
