## 7. Decision Gates → Success / Kill criteria

**No mid-run gates — zero-GPU free analysis; Phase 0 validation is fail-loud.**

**Pre-registered verdict classification (the §3 acceptance criteria, jointly satisfiable — each is a label, not a pass/fail demand, and no two reference contradictory outcomes on the same cell):**

Per half of the split — *wrong-persona advantage* (expected d > 0) and *default-assistant reversal* (expected d < 0) — at each read regime:

- **At a checkpoint — exhaustive classifier over the per-persona CI-state grid (round-1 statistics reconciliation fix).** Each persona's 95% CI is in exactly one of three states: **E** (clear of zero in the expected direction), **S** (straddles zero), **O** (clear of zero in the opposite direction). The checkpoint label is a total function of the unordered persona-state pair — no silent `else` (the classifier raises on any unmapped combination):
  - (E,E) → **SURVIVES**
  - (E,S) → **PARTIAL** (the straddling persona's point-estimate sign is reported alongside but does not change the label — "same-sign" disambiguation)
  - (E,O) → **DISCORDANT** (one persona CI-clear expected, the other CI-clear opposite — the previously-unmapped reachable cell)
  - (S,S) → **ABSENT**
  - (S,O) or (O,O) → **REVERSED**
  All six combinations of the 3×3 unordered grid are covered; per-persona CI states are always persisted next to the label.
- **(a) Off-saturation verdict (log-p space, checkpoints {s18, s30}; quantifiers pinned):** the half "survives off saturation" iff SURVIVES at BOTH s18 and s30; "vanishes" iff ABSENT, REVERSED, or DISCORDANT at both; "shrinks" otherwise (any mixed pattern — e.g. SURVIVES at s30 but ABSENT at s18, the expected wrong-half outcome, reported as "install-coupled, absent at the in-band sub-install point"). s18 = in-band/band-adjacent sub-emission read; s30 = install onset, own-slot past band but below full ceiling on 3 of 4 arm×persona cells.
- **(b) Margin-space verdict (Δmargin, install-onward checkpoints {30,60,120}; quantifiers pinned):** the half "survives in margin space" iff SURVIVES at ALL THREE of {30,60,120}; "vanishes" iff ABSENT, REVERSED, or DISCORDANT at all three; "shrinks" otherwise (mixed pattern, reported per checkpoint per persona). s18 reported alongside as the sub-install bracket (never enters the margin-space half-verdict). Cells with the saturation flag are decided on margin (authoritative) with the compressed log-p value shown for honesty.
- **Headline (Goal) verdict (quantifiers pinned):** *concentration confirmed* iff the default-assistant reversal SURVIVES at ≥1 of the unsaturated checkpoints {s18, s30} in log-p space AND survives in margin space per (b) (SURVIVES at all of {30,60,120}), while the wrong-persona advantage survives in margin space per (b) (SURVIVES at all of {30,60,120}). *Containment confirmed* iff the default reversal vanishes or reverses under BOTH clean reads ((a) and (b)) while the wrong advantage survives in margin space per (b). Any other outcome → *mixed*, reported with the per-half, per-checkpoint labels (DISCORDANT checkpoints always reported per-persona and never counted toward "survives").
- **Success = any determinate verdict pair.** Kill criterion (analysis-level): Phase 0 validation failure (file count ≠ 245, schema/gauge violation, rig check > 1.0 nat) blocks all downstream reads — fix or escalate, never analyze contaminated inputs.
