## 3. Method delta (what differs from the two obvious approaches)

The filed body offered (a) allowlist or (b) repair the note, "prefer (b)". The evidence makes this a **both, but (b) as an APPEND, never an edit**:

- **(b)-as-in-place-edit is FORBIDDEN and would not work anyway.** `events.jsonl` is append-only by hard project rule ("Never edit or delete a row — always append a new row with a higher version"); `task.py` exposes no amend subcommand. And the corpus-replay test replays *every historical row*, so even a successful in-place rewrite is the one thing the append-only contract bars, while an append leaves the malformed row in the corpus — so **A1 requires the allowlist entry regardless**.
- **(b)-as-APPEND is the exact documented precedent.** The comment block directly above `KNOWN_MALFORMED_RUN_MARKERS` says of #1090: *"The round is closed by #1111's corrective re-post on #1090, not by parsing."* #1111 did allowlist-entry **+** corrective re-post. This plan follows that pattern verbatim.
- **The parser is deliberately NOT widened.** Accepting bare `label` would (i) contradict the parser's stated design (`parse_followup_note_field` docstring: "parsing stays field-only (#1111 — no label inference)"), (ii) apply to a GENERIC field-name parameter also called with `source`/`round`/`outcome`, and (iii) change which labels the whole corpus considers run/unrun — a fleet-wide Step 0 dispatch behavior change to accommodate 1 marker in 191. Rejected.
- **Delimiters are a non-issue** (corrects the filed body's premise): the parser accepts `<field>:` and `<field>=` by design and 21 corpus markers use `followup_label=` and all parse. The single causal deviation is the bare `label` key.
