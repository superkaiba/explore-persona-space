## 1. Goal

Determine whether encoding a persona as a custom chat-template role header gives a real, separable reduction in trained-marker leakage over a system-prompt encoding in the marker-less contrastive-negative regime, **measured at a non-saturated training anchor** (arms ~5–10 nats below the leakage ceiling, where the role-vs-system gap has genuine dynamic range), resolving whether #464's inconclusive saturated-floor +1-nat marker-less edge is real or a measurement artifact.

(Verbatim from `tasks/planning/529/body.md` frontmatter `goal:`.)

## 2. Prior Work

**#464 (parent, awaiting_promotion):**
- 3-regime comparison of three encodings (system_plain, system_padded, role) under three training regimes (positive-only, marker-less contrastive negative, co-resident competing marker).
- **Co-resident regime:** role wins by ~5-6 nats over system; arms span [−13, −19], real dynamic range.
- **Positive-only regime:** marker leaks to P≈1 (≈0 nats) everywhere; role gives no advantage.
- **Marker-less cn regime (the regime this followup targets):** `headline_status: inconclusive_dynamic_range_failed`. Arms cluster between log P ≈ −14 and −16 nats; per-arm sd ∈ [0.02, 0.15]; `system_plain` sd=0.47 < the 0.5 dynamic-range threshold; reported `role − system_plain = +1.10 nats, 95% CI [0.51, 2.16]` and `role − system_padded = +1.62 nats, 95% CI [0.69, 3.24]` are paired bootstraps over n=3 seeds (the CI is min/max of 3 points). Operationally P(marker) ≈ 1e-6 vs 4e-7 — both effectively zero leakage.

**Project rules constraining the design:**
- `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`: marker DV is on-policy log P(marker) at the post-R slot, teacher-forced via vLLM `prompt_logprobs`. **NEVER swap in full-vocab KL-from-base** to dodge saturation (#504 anti-pattern). Gate the anchor on **bystander resolution** (not source emission) — the source should saturate emission, that IS the implant.
- `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md`: the deterministic band-stop recipe for source log P − base ∈ [5, 12] nat, gated on bystander resolution. The in-training `MarkerBandStopCallback` is the default in `train_lora` via `marker_band_stop=True`; HOWEVER #464's `i464_phase23_train.py` rig was built BEFORE that callback became the default and the callback path was not used (and per #464 plan §11 / CLAUDE.md task #399 the i464 direct-launch rig has a known vLLM-during-HF GPU-residency conflict in mid-training callbacks). We therefore drive anchor amount via **post-hoc checkpoint selection** (the body's prescribed fix).
- `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`: marker-less negatives + default-assistant negative recipe — already implemented in the i464 cn rig.

**Related open questions:** `q:spec-role-header` (does the role header give a separable contribution to localization?), `q:leak-contrastive-negatives` (composition of negatives; the saturation caveat that motivated #448 / #504).

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (role has a separable contribution):** at the selected non-saturated anchor, both `L_system_plain − L_role` and `L_system_padded − L_role` are positive, the 95% paired bootstrap CI over 5 seeds excludes zero, AND the dynamic-range gate PASSES (per-arm sd > 0.5 nats; off-diagonal wrong-slot log P roughly in [−5, −10]). → #464's +1-nat cn-regime edge was real; the role header tightens marker localization even when the only contrast is marker-less negatives.

**H0 (saturated-floor artifact):** at every checkpoint where the dynamic-range gate passes, the role-vs-system difference is sign-unstable across seeds OR the 95% paired CI overlaps zero. Report indistinguishable-from-null per CLAUDE.md "Null Framing" rule. → #464's +1-nat edge was a saturated-floor rank-shuffle; in the marker-less cn regime the role header has no separable contribution; its only clean advantage is the co-resident competing-marker regime.

**Degenerate (still saturates):** if even the 1-epoch anchor saturates BOTH the own-slot AND the wrong-slot at the ceiling (own argmax-emission ≥ 0.92 AND wrong-slot log P > −3 across all arms), the marker-less single-persona implant saturates too fast for this DV to segment arms at this rank/lr. Deliverable: the trajectory plot + a recommendation to drop LoRA rank (32 → 16 or 8) or learning rate (1e-5 → 5e-6) in a follow-up, NOT a wrong null. (This is also the kill criterion — §7.)

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Regime + cells

- **Regime:** marker-less contrastive-negative ONLY. The other two regimes (#464 positive-only, #464 co-resident) are already resolved and are NOT re-run.
- **Personas (trained separately):** pirate, villain — same as #464. Shared marker ` ※` (id 83399) on every positive row.
- **Arms:** `system_plain`, `system_padded`, `role` — same as #464.
- **Seeds:** 42, 137, 1337, 7, 21 (5 seeds; #464 used 3 → 5; deliberate statistical-power bump, NOT a manipulated variable; see §12 Assumptions).
- **Epoch grid (the one new variable):** {1, 2, 3, 5}. The 5-epoch arm is the saturated reference reproducing #464; the 1/2/3-epoch arms are non-saturated candidates. Rationale: #464 trained 5 epochs straight to saturation. #448 / #504 / `marker-training-recipe.md` show that the usable measurement window sits below emission saturation, often within the first 1-2 epochs at this scale, and we cannot predict it a priori for this exact (data, rank, lr) combination.
- **Cells:** 3 arms × 5 seeds × 2 personas × 4 epoch settings = **120 trained adapters**. Per epoch setting, 30 LoRAs (cn / single-persona / shared-marker).

Marker-text type: pass `[" ※"]` (list) to match `i464_phase23_train.py`'s call site (it constructs `cfg_marker_text: list[str] = [enc.MARKER_PIRATE_TEXT]` under `--shared-marker`). `MarkerOnlyDataCollator` with `marker_text=[" ※"], tail_tokens=0` masks loss to ` ※`+EOS on every positive row; negative rows (no ` ※` in completion) train ONLY EOS at the post-response slot.

**Why `save_strategy="no"` + epochs-grid instead of `save_strategy="epoch"` on one run:** the cleanest separation of concerns. One adapter per (cell, E), uploaded atomically to HF, evaluated independently, no intermediate-checkpoint format ambiguity (HF SFTTrainer's `checkpoint-N` subdirs save the full optimizer state + tokenizer + adapter — uploading the full subdir to HF blows the per-pod MooseFS quota AND the HF model repo quota; the alternative of teasing only `adapter_model.safetensors` out of each checkpoint subdir is fragile). The epochs-grid approach is one canonical `train_lora` call per cell, identical to #464's call pattern, and produces 120 cleanly-separated HF subpaths.

### 4.4 Dependent variable (unchanged from #464)

Per (training cell, eval-encoding) probe — the existing `scripts/i464_po_eval.py --variant cn` pipeline:

1. Build a probe prompt as `T_e(q) + R_persona[q]`, where `T_e` is the eval encoding chat-template prefix and `R_persona[q]` is the base-model greedy response under the persona implied by the encoding (`enc.persona_for_eval_encoding(e_eval)`).
2. vLLM `LLM.generate(prompts, SamplingParams(max_tokens=1, prompt_logprobs=1, logprobs=1, temperature=0))` with `lora_request=<trained adapter>`.
3. Read `log P(' ※' id 83399 | T_e(q) + R_persona[q])` at the slot immediately after R (teacher-forced via `prompt_logprobs`).
4. Base log-prob cached once per `e_eval` (no LoRA loaded); trained log-prob computed per LoRA via `LoRARequest` hot-swap.
5. `g_logprob = mean over 50 questions of trained log P; b_logprob = mean over 50 questions of base log P; delta_g = g_logprob − b_logprob`.

Probed eval encodings per cell (3, same as #464 cn):
- arm ∈ {system_plain, system_padded}: `system_<own>`, `system_<other>`, `default_assistant`.
- arm == role: `role_<own>`, `role_<other>`, `default_assistant`.

Headline statistic (mirrors `i464_po_analyze.py` cn variant):
- Per (arm, seed, E): `L_arm_seed_E = mean over (pirate, villain) of g_logprob[' ※', e_off_diagonal]` where `e_off_diagonal` = the OTHER persona's same-arm-family encoding.
- `d_plain_seed_E  = L_system_plain_seed_E − L_role_seed_E`
- `d_padded_seed_E = L_system_padded_seed_E − L_role_seed_E`
- Paired bootstrap (N=10_000) over the 5 seeds gives mean + 95% percentile CI.

**No KL substitution.** The DV stays marker-specific. The #504 anti-pattern of swapping in full-vocab KL-from-base at the slot to dodge anchor saturation is explicitly REJECTED here: KL would inflate a null into an effect (rule `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`). The marker DV is the construct; on saturation we back off to a less-trained anchor (this entire experiment) AND read the bounded bystander emission rate alongside.

### 4.5 Anchor selection algorithm (the new piece)

After the cross-eval phase produces per-cell-per-E JSONs (120 cells × 3 eval encodings = 360 files), `scripts/i529_select_anchor.py` selects ONE anchor `E*[persona]` per persona (NOT per arm — selecting per-arm would confound the arm comparison with the anchor choice):

1. **Per (E, persona)**, compute across all 3 arms × 5 seeds = 15 cells:
   - `own_logp_E_persona = mean over (arm, seed) of g_logprob[' ※', e_own]` (diagonal, source emission)
   - `own_argmax_emit_E_persona = mean over (arm, seed) of g_argmax_marker_per_q_rate`
   - For each arm separately: `wrong_logp_E_persona_arm = mean over seeds of g_logprob[' ※', e_other_in_arm_family]`
   - For each arm separately: `wrong_sd_E_persona_arm = sd over seeds × 50 questions of g_logprob[' ※', e_other_in_arm_family]`
2. **Resolution gate per (E, persona, arm):** `wrong_sd > 0.5 nats` AND `wrong_logp ∈ [−10, −5]` (the marker-training-recipe band, ported to the wrong-slot read).
3. **Source-installation gate per (E, persona):** `own_argmax_emit ≥ 0.50` (the implant fires on at least half of source-persona's own answers — proves it took; less strict than #460's ≥0.92 because we WANT a less-trained anchor).
4. **Candidate anchors:** all E that satisfy gate (3) AND satisfy gate (2) for **all 3 arms** at the given persona.
5. **Tie-break:** prefer the SMALLEST E that satisfies both. (If E=1 satisfies both, use E=1; only if E=1's wrong-slot is still floored do we step up to E=2, etc.)
6. **Final selection:** one anchor per persona. The persona-wise selection runs independently so pirate and villain can land at different E* — but the headline statistic for each persona is computed at THAT persona's anchor, then symmetrically averaged.
7. **Degenerate case:** if NO E satisfies both gates for either persona, write `degenerate: true` + the trajectory diagnostics + a clear "anchor saturates too fast at this rank/lr; next lever = drop r=32→16 or lr=1e-5→5e-6" recommendation. NO H1/H0 verdict is rendered.

The anchor-selection algorithm is implemented in a new `scripts/i529_select_anchor.py` that consumes `eval_results/issue_529/contrastive_negatives/cross_eval/per_cell/*.json` and writes `eval_results/issue_529/anchor_selection.json` with:
```
{
  "schema_version": "i529_anchor_v1",
  "git_commit": "<sha>",
  "candidate_grid": [1, 2, 3, 5],
  "per_E_per_persona_diagnostics": {... own_logp, own_emit, wrong_logp_per_arm, wrong_sd_per_arm ...},
  "selected_anchor": {"pirate": E*, "villain": E*},
  "degenerate": false | true,
  "degenerate_reason": "..."  # if degenerate
}
```

## 6. Evaluation

### Measurement validity

| DV | Construct (what the Goal cares about) | Metric (what is actually computed) | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation / justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marker-leakage log-prob | Whether the trained model emits the marker when generating under the WRONG persona's chat-template encoding ("does role tighten the persona→marker binding?") | `log P(' ※' \| T_wrong(q) + R_persona[q])` at the slot immediately after R, teacher-forced via vLLM `prompt_logprobs`, trained − base | `no` (teacher-forced; off-policy in the sense the model didn't generate R) | (a) #464's parent body used this exact teacher-forced DV and #464 round of co-resident regime gave the field-standard 5-6 nat role-vs-system gap that matches the qualitative behavior. (b) `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` permits the teacher-forced read AS the within-condition-trajectory DV (which IS the use here: we are reading the trajectory of source vs wrong vs default across epochs, then picking the anchor); the cross-condition behavioral leaderboard rule is satisfied because the comparison is between encodings at the SAME post-R slot on the SAME R, not across different positions. (c) The implant target is "emit ※ after a response," and post-R is the exact slot we're training; teacher-forcing R is teacher-forcing the on-policy base distribution itself. The substantial measurement upgrade vs an off-policy probe would be on-policy generation + emission rate — which we ALSO report as a free side-read at the diagonal (own-emission rate gates the source-installation check, §4.5 step 3). |
| Source-emission rate (free side-read) | "Does the trained model emit ※ on its own answers?" (the implant took) | `g_argmax_marker_per_q_rate` at the diagonal (own-encoding) probe | `partial` (argmax of teacher-forced log-prob, NOT free generation) | Used ONLY as a gate, not as a headline. Argmax at the post-R slot under the trained model is a strict UPPER BOUND on the on-policy emission rate (the on-policy distribution at this slot is exactly what the teacher-forced read evaluates). If argmax ≥ 0.50 at this slot, on-policy emission is ≥ 0.50 modulo sampling — sufficient for an installation gate. |

### Figures to produce

- **Hero #1 (the headline read):** forest plot of `d_seed = L_system_plain − L_role` (and `L_system_padded − L_role`) across the 5 seeds at the selected anchor `E*`, with the saturated-5-epoch reference ghosted alongside. CI shown as the paired bootstrap whiskers.
- **Hero #2 (the trajectory):** log P(' ※') per arm vs E, three rows (diagonal = own-slot, off-diagonal = wrong-slot, default-assistant), per persona. The selected anchor is marked with a vertical guide. This is the figure that justifies the anchor selection.
- **Exploratory dump:** per-cell heatmap (arm × seed × E), per-seed dynamic-range trajectories, own-emission-rate vs E, leakage-to-default vs E.

### Thresholds and statistical tests

- Paired bootstrap (N=10_000) over the 5 seeds → mean + 95% percentile CI. Mirrors `i464_phase5_analyze._paired_bootstrap_ci`.
- Dynamic-range gate: per-arm sd > 0.5 across the seeds × persona × question grid (3 × 2 × 50 = 300 raw log-probs per arm at the anchor). Threshold inherited from `i464_po_analyze.DYNAMIC_RANGE_THRESHOLD = 0.5`.
- H1 elicitation gate (free side-read): own-emission ≥ 0.50 at the selected anchor.
- H1 PASS: paired CI of `d_plain` excludes zero AND paired CI of `d_padded` excludes zero AND dynamic-range gate PASSES for all 3 arms.
- H0 PASS: at every E satisfying dynamic-range, paired CI of `d_plain` OR `d_padded` overlaps zero, OR per-seed signs are not all positive.

## 7. Decision Gates

**Smoke gate (the only gate; expected wall ~4 h justifies one gate).** After the FIRST cell completes end-to-end (train → adapter upload → vLLM eval at e_own/e_other/default → per-cell JSON written), confirm:
- Adapter exists at the HF subpath (`huggingface_hub.list_repo_files` check).
- Per-cell JSON has finite, non-NaN `g_logprob` for all 3 eval encodings.
- The per-cell own-slot `g_logprob` is finite and bounded (not ±inf, not NaN).
- The per-cell wrong-slot `g_logprob` is more negative than the own-slot (the implant is taking, even if not yet saturated).

This is a sanity smoke ONLY — it kills runs where the rig is broken (wrong marker id, missing R_canon, vLLM crash). It is NOT a "is the effect there yet" gate. Justification: this gate uses the cheapest intermediate signal (one cell's worth of forward passes) to rule out a 4-h sweep that would silently no-op. It is jointly satisfiable (every condition is non-contradictory).

**No "early stop the run if effect not seen" gates.** The whole experiment design IS the gate — we are running the full epoch grid because we cannot predict the anchor a priori.

**Kill criterion (the Degenerate outcome).** If after Phase 3 the anchor-selection algorithm returns `degenerate: true` for both personas (no E satisfies both gates at any (E, persona)), TERMINATE — do NOT extend to E ∈ {7, 10}. Deliverable in that case: the trajectory plot + a one-paragraph recommendation that the marker-less single-persona implant saturates too fast at LoRA r=32 / lr=1e-5 for this DV to segment arms, and the next lever is rank reduction or lr reduction. The kill criterion is grounded in the rule `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` ("Lower LR widens the band in step-space; it does not remove per-source variation" — at r=32/lr=1e-5 extending epochs past 5 just deepens saturation, not widens the window). Falsification of the experiment, NOT of the hypothesis.

## Decisions surfaced for orchestrator awareness (NOT AskUserQuestion gates — autonomous mode)

- **Decision: epochs grid = {1, 2, 3, 5}** because the issue body sketches this exact grid and a finer grid (adding E=4) would burn 30 cells with no clear interpretive role. If the smoke cell's E=1 already shows wrong-slot saturation (log P > −3), the implementer SHOULD extend the grid downward to {0.25, 0.5, 1} via partial-epoch training (`max_steps` instead of `num_train_epochs`) and surface that as an `epm:planner-decision-needed` marker — but plan-time default is {1, 2, 3, 5}.
- **Decision: anchor selection is per-persona, not per-arm**, because per-arm selection would confound the manipulated variable (encoding arm) with the anchor choice. Per-persona is the tightest valid grain.
- **Decision: extend i464 scripts in place with `--issue 529` rather than fork them**, because the change is additive and additive changes preserve `git log --follow` traceability. The thin-wrapper alternative (`scripts/i529_phase23_train.py` importing the i464 main) is also fine; the implementer picks whichever has fewer test-impact lines.
- **Decision: use 4× H100 ZeRO-1/TP=1 sweep, NOT 4× single-GPU pods**, because §9's parallelism analysis admits one pod with sharded `--gpu-id` cleanly and avoids 4× pod provisioning overhead (per `feedback_multi_gpu_pod.md`).
- **Decision: NO mid-run early stop on "effect not yet visible"** because §7 explains the experiment design IS the gate; the four-epoch grid samples the trajectory and the anchor selection is post-hoc.
- **Decision: Reject KL-from-base substitution** as an alternative DV per `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`'s #504 anti-pattern rule. The marker `log P(' ※')` DV stays.
