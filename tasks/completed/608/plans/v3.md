- **Secondary expectation (install speed):** positive-only reaches half-install (own-rate 0.50) at an earlier step than contrastive for ≥5/6 sources (predicted from positive-density; a CONTRARY result — contrastive as fast or faster per step — would be the surprising, H1-flavored outcome).

### Decision rule (pre-registered — how `censored_top_band` flips to determinate)

Definitions: resolvable band = own-rate ∈ **[0.15, 0.90]** (floor ≈ 3× fresh base rates 0.044–0.128; ceiling 0.05 below the parent's 0.95 censor band). **Co-resolvable checkpoint** (per source): a grid step where BOTH arms' own-rates are in-band. **Primary checkpoint** (per source): among co-resolvable checkpoints, the one where the positive-only arm's own-rate is closest to 0.50 (deterministic, max-resolution; one registered test per source — no family-wise inflation; remaining co-resolvable checkpoints reported descriptively). m = number of sources (≤6) with ≥1 co-resolvable checkpoint.

At each source's primary checkpoint: paired claim-level bootstrap CI on g_k(s), parent's exact convention (per-claim 10-rollout rates → paired claim differences → resample 50 claims with replacement → 10,000 draws → two-sided 95% percentile; rng seed 42).

Panel verdict (m ≥ 3 required for the primary read; else fallback-only):
- **`subceiling_contrastive_ahead`** (falsifies H1-sub): ≥⌈m/2⌉ sources with g > 0 and CI excluding 0, AND mean g over the m sources ≥ +0.05.
- **`subceiling_posonly_ahead`**: mirror image (≥⌈m/2⌉ CIs < 0, mean ≤ −0.05).
- **`subceiling_no_separation`**: ≥ m−1 primary CIs fully contained in [−0.15, +0.15] AND |mean g| ≤ 0.05 (containment band matches the §6 power analysis; the parent's ±0.05 endpoint band is unreachable at mid-window SEs and is NOT reused).
- else **`subceiling_indeterminate`** (reported with continuous estimates; never over-read).

**Fallback read (always reported; carries the verdict when m < 3):** the §4(c) band-entry/S₅₀ ordering. Registered secondary verdict `install_speed`: posonly-first vs contrastive-first vs unordered, by S₅₀ interval ordering for ≥5/6 sources. **Matched-positive-dose descriptive read:** compare contrastive at steps {18, 35, 44, 88, 132} against posonly at steps {5, 9, 13, 26, 35} (matched cumulative positive-example counts, ratio 200/700; grid aligns within rounding) — separates "fewer positives per step" from "negatives actively change install per positive"; descriptive only, no registered gate.

**Verdict-flip statement:** the parent H1 record gains a determinate sub-ceiling annex if the primary read returns any of the three determinate labels, or (weaker, labeled speed-not-strength) the S₅₀ ordering is consistent for ≥5/6 sources. Censoring precedence inherits: a checkpoint where either arm is above 0.90 is never narrated as an equivalence read.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Entire training recipe + seed.** Why: single-variable contract — the retrains must reproduce the parent's arms so the dense reads are reads OF those arms. `Source: #608 plans/v2` (which grounds in #411's launch config). Alternatives: any re-tune (rejected — confounds schedule with recipe).
- **Checkpoint step list {5, 9, 13, 18, 26, 35, 44, 88} (+132 final).** Why: 5–26 covers the measured posonly transition (own-rates 0.14–0.82 at step 13, 0.92–0.95 at 26; spacing 4–9 steps < the ~13-step narrowest observed transition); 35/44/88/132 covers the positive-density-predicted contrastive lag (~3.5× later) and supplies the matched-positive-dose pairs (18↔5, 35↔9, 44↔13, 88↔26, 132↔35); 44/88 double as epoch boundaries matching the parent's grid. `Source: #608 parent run trajectory_descriptive` (primary); #533/#547 ~12-step window estimate (corroborating, different construct). Alternatives: uniform `save_steps=4` (rejected — 33 saves × 12 cells ≈ 130 GB disk + no late-window economy); proposal-seed 5-checkpoint list alone (rejected — zero contrastive-window coverage past step 26; with no prior contrastive trajectory the round would likely produce zero in-band contrastive reads and trip its own kill).
- **Dose-matched posonly arm as the comparator (not matched-epochs).** Why: 700 rows ⇒ identical 44 steps/epoch, identical total steps and cosine shape ⇒ checkpoints align in optimizer steps across arms. `Source: #608 plans/v2` §5 (the parent already designated it "the cleaner control"). Alternative: posonly_epoch (rejected — 13-step epochs desynchronize the grid).
- **Full 132-step training (not stopping at step 44).** Why: truncating training changes the cosine-schedule length, perturbing the LR at every early step and breaking trajectory parity with the parent's runs; the endpoint also feeds the §7 parity check. `Source: #608 plans/v2` recipe + cosine-schedule mechanics. Cost of the extra steps ≈ 0.1 h/cell — negligible.
- **Merge → vLLM-on-merged eval stack (parent's per-read pattern), NOT vLLM LoRA hot-swap.** Why: measurement-stack identity with the parent run is the round's interpretive anchor (parity check compares against parent-committed numbers); hot-swap changes the application path mid-line.
