### 4.6 Micro-decision: do NOT thread the precomputed `refs` into `_is_eps_owned`

`classify()` computes `refs = _scan_task_references(p.pod_id, p.name)` at line 353 for every pod, and `_is_eps_owned` signal 3 re-runs the identical scan (wrapped in its own try/except). Threading `refs` in (or short-circuiting on `refs` before calling the predicate) would avoid one redundant tasks-tree scan per ownership-checked pod. **Decision: keep the `_is_eps_owned(p, pod_id)` signature and call unchanged; accept the redundant scan.** See §11 for the full rationale (scale: the daily audit handles single-digit pod counts; the predicate's self-contained signal-3 call is an explicitly tested seam — `test_all_signals_raising_still_unmanaged_exited` line 591-594 documents it; a signature change to a safety-critical predicate for zero behavioral gain is the wrong trade).
