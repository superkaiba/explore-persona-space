## 3. Method delta (why the adjacent-only sketch is insufficient)

The task body's sketch requires the head noun IMMEDIATELY after "registered". Of the six incident strings, four carry intervening tokens: a hyphenated compound adjective ("same-map-different-coordinates"), a numeral + compound ("0.05 same-operator"), plain adjectives ("reparameterized", "existence", "degeneracy"). The delta from the sketch:

1. **Lazy bounded intervening-token window** `{0,3}?` — up to three modifier tokens between "registered" and the head noun; lazy so the reported sample is the shortest form ("registered rule", not "registered rule demotes any verdict").
2. **Sentence/line-bridge immunity** — tokens may contain interior decimal points (`0.05`, `≤0.30`) but never a trailing period, and separators are space/tab (never newline), so a match cannot bridge "…registered conditions.\n\nThe honest read…" (two such bridge artifacts appeared in the first corpus dry-run and are eliminated by this construction; both re-verified clean).
3. **First-token preposition guard** — `registered` followed by as/at/by/for/in/into/on/to/under/via/with is the verb register ("registered in WandB", "registered on HF") and never fires.
4. **Wider, corpus-grounded head-noun list** (15 nouns vs the sketch's 7) — §11 grounds each addition; "test" and "interval" are deliberately EXCLUDED ("the registered interval defining the test" is the sanctioned Why-this-test CI-definition register pinned by `test_bracketed_ci_in_why_this_test_line_is_exempt`).
5. **Unicode-numeral token support** — the token class carries U+2212 / U+2264 / U+2265 / `%` / `<` `>` `=` `/` `+`, matching the corpus forms "the registered ≤0.30 band" and "the registered −0.10 different-map margin" (both observed verbatim in #1345 round text), same blind-spot family the `interval_inline` U+2212 fix (#649) closed.

Everything else is unchanged: the nine existing branches stay byte-identical (leftmost-match semantics mean `pre-?registered` still wins inside "pre-registered verdict …", so no double-counting; the adjacent `registered hypothesis` / `registered alpha` branches sit earlier in the alternation and win at their positions).
