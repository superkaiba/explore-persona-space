## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

### Goal (verbatim from frontmatter)

Test whether marker-implant fine-tune edits superpose (per-context
joint shift equals the sum of the singleton A-only and B-only shifts
at every held-out persona) using a properly-implanted anchor (source
`log P(marker) − base` in the [5,12] nat band-stop window, emission
gate cleared) and orthogonal source pairs (base-model L20 centered
cosine near 0), so the additivity cosine is a diagnostic superposition
test rather than a mechanical artifact of a floored implant and
parallel singletons.

### Hypothesis (sharpened)

**H1 (vector additivity, primary).** With the band-stopped anchor
clearing DV4 (source on-policy emission ≥ 0.5) AND all three gating
diagnostics passing (joint per-context shift matrix NOT near rank-1
AND singletons NOT parallel AND BOTH singleton effective ranks ≥
2.0 — i.e., neither singleton is a constant steering vector per
Treutlein arXiv 2507.08218), for each orthogonal pair the median
per-context `cos(shift_{A+B}(c), shift_A(c) + shift_B(c)) ≥ 0.85` on
≥80% of held-out contexts.

**H2 (magnitude additivity).** On the same held-out contexts,
`ΔG_{A+B}(c) − [ΔG_A(c) + ΔG_B(c)]` is centered at zero with spread
< 1.0 nat per pair (where `ΔG_X(c) = log P(marker | T_c(q) + R_c(q))`
trained − base, on-policy at the post-response slot).

**H3 (interference grows with overlap — secondary, only if ≥3
orthogonal pairs land in scope).** Across pairs, normalized residual
`||shift_{A+B}(c) − (shift_A(c) + shift_B(c))|| / ||shift_{A+B}(c)||`
grows with `|⟨v_A, v_B⟩|` (the manipulation-check cosine). With ≥3
pair-cosines this is a rank correlation; with 2 it is a 2-pair median
contrast. **NOT a gating criterion.**

**Falsifier (kill).** Even with a properly-implanted anchor (DV4
passes) AND orthogonal source pairs (singletons not parallel) AND
joint NOT rank-1 (DV1 is diagnostic), the residual stays large or DV1
< 0.5 across pairs → edits do not superpose additively; the
map-plus-beacons additivity pillar fails.

**Hard kill criterion at the anchor-smoke gate (Phase A).** If after
the lr ladder {5e-6 → 1e-5} the band-stop callback either (a) never
fires in 8 epochs (source `g_logprob` < 5 nats at cap = under-implant
floor, #520 redux) OR (b) fires before bystander resolution clears at
two of the three smoke cells (saturated by the time band is reached,
#448 redux) → **STOP. Post `epm:failure v1` (`failure_class: data`,
`reason: anchor_floor_or_ceiling_at_band_stop`)** and surface to user.
Do NOT silently descope to the full-vocab KL DV (that DV is banned
per the marker-leakage-measurement rule + #504 incident).

### External (load-bearing)

- **Ilharco et al. arXiv 2212.04089** — task vectors / weight-space
  arithmetic. #527 is the per-context activation-space analog: where
  Ilharco asks whether `θ_A + θ_B` recovers two-task behavior, #527
  asks whether `shift_A(c) + shift_B(c) ≈ shift_{A+B}(c)` per
  held-out context c.
- **Yadav et al. arXiv 2306.01708 (TIES merging), Yu et al. arXiv
  2311.03099 (DARE).** Naive LoRA / task-vector addition produces
  interference (conflicting parameter signs cancel). Activation-space
  analog: residual `||shift_{A+B} − (shift_A + shift_B)||` growing
  with `⟨c_A, c_B⟩` is interference. With orthogonal A, B
  (|cos|≲0.15), interference should be near-zero and additivity
  should hold if the rank-one beacons picture is right.
- **Meng et al. arXiv 2202.05262 (ROME), 2210.07229 (MEMIT).**
  Sequential editing degrades in weight space; failure scales with
  number of edits. #527's prediction: at orthogonal source pairs the
  interference should be small (and additivity should hold).
- **Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey arXiv 2507.21509 (Persona
  Vectors).** Gives the rsLoRA spec the marker-training-recipe rule
  inherits from and the L21 (mapped to L20 here) extraction layer.
- **Treutlein et al. arXiv 2507.08218 (LoRA = constant steering
  vector).** Direct caveat on the DV1 interpretation: if a single
  edit's shift is constant-direction across contexts (Treutlein's
  claim), then `shift_A + shift_B` is also constant across contexts
  and DV1 ≈ 1 is mechanical. **The two new gating diagnostics
  (joint-SVD + singleton-cosine) are exactly the checks that close
  this loophole.**

### Why code, not a model call?

The DV1 vector cosine + DV2 residual + DV3 log-prob are all
model-internal quantities (residual-stream activations at a specific
layer + teacher-forced log-prob from `model.forward(...).logits`).
There is no Claude-call equivalent — Claude cannot read Qwen-2.5's
internal activations. The two new gating diagnostics (SVD spectrum,
singleton cosine) are deterministic numpy/torch — no model call
required. The qualitative read of leak completions ("on-distribution
persona-faithful prose vs marker-emission") is the only step where a
Claude call would apply, and it's a sanity inspector only, not
load-bearing for the DVs.

### Contrastive negatives (mandatory check)

This is a marker-implant experiment; per `.claude/rules/contrastive-
negatives.md` the design MUST include contrastive negatives.

- **Negative-persona set** (same 4 across all 18 trained arms):
  literal `default_assistant` (the bare default; see §12 #6 for the
  exact encoding), `librarian`, `programmer`, `chef`. The literal
  default-assistant inclusion is correction (c) from §3.
- **Ratio:** 1:1 positives-to-total-negatives across all arms (the
  rule's working default and the canonical band-stop recipe's
  spec). For singletons: 400 source positives + 400 negatives split
  100 per negative persona. For joint: 800 source positives (400 A
  + 400 B) + 800 negatives split 200 per negative persona.
- **Negative response construction:** R_neg(q) generated greedy from
  base model under each negative persona's own system prompt,
  marker-less. Under `tail_tokens=0` +
  `suppress_at_post_response_slot=True`, loss-bearing token is the
  first `<|im_end|>` in the completion region (the DV slot) — pushes
  `log P(※)` *down* at the slot the DV reads under softmax
  competition.
- **Symmetry across arms:** identical 4-persona set, identical
  question pool, identical encoding; only the source-positive rows
  differ across arms.

Neither contrastive-negatives exemption applies: this is not the
contrastive-vs-non-contrastive manipulation, and it is not a strict
single-variable replication of a positive-only parent.

### Two PRE-REGISTERED gating diagnostics (the additions vs #520)

Both are computed per cell, reported in the analyzer output, and gate
whether DV1 is reported as a superposition test in the headline.

- **Gating diagnostic GD1 — joint-shift SVD.** Stack the per-context
  joint shifts `[shift_{A+B}(c_1), ..., shift_{A+B}(c_19)]` into a
  19 × 3584 matrix M_joint; compute singular values `s_i`. Report
  top-1 SV share `s_1^2 / Σ s_i^2` and effective rank `(Σ s_i^2)^2 /
  Σ s_i^4` (participation ratio). **Gate:** if top-1 SV share > 0.75
  OR effective rank < 2.0 → flag DV1 NOT diagnostic for that cell
  (the joint shift is essentially one direction × per-context
  scalar; #520's mechanical artifact).
- **Gating diagnostic GD2 — singleton cosine.** Compute median of
  `cos(shift_A(c), shift_B(c))` across the 19 held-out contexts.
  **Gate:** if median > 0.6 → flag DV1 NOT diagnostic for that cell
  (parallel-singletons trivialization; the additivity test is
  reading "parallel vectors add"). The orthogonal-pair selection is
  the *design* manipulation that should keep this gate clean; the
  realized base-model cos(A,B) is reported alongside as the
  manipulation check.
- **Gating diagnostic GD3 — singleton-shift SVDs.** Per cell (per
  source × pair), stack the per-context singleton shifts
  `[shift_A(c_1), ..., shift_A(c_19)]` and
  `[shift_B(c_1), ..., shift_B(c_19)]` into two 19 × 3584 matrices
  M_A and M_B; compute top-1 SV share `s_1^2 / Σ s_i^2` and effective
  rank `(Σ s_i^2)^2 / Σ s_i^4` (participation ratio) for each.
  **Gate:** if effective rank < 2.0 for EITHER singleton → flag DV1
  NOT diagnostic for that cell. The singleton IS a constant
  steering vector (Treutlein arXiv 2507.08218): the per-context
  shift is `shift_X(c) ≈ v_X` (constant direction × per-context
  scalar), so the joint sum `shift_A + shift_B ≈ v_A + v_B` is also
  constant-direction and a high DV1 cosine reads "joint's dominant
  direction aligns with `v_A + v_B`" — NOT per-context superposition.
  This catches the case GD1 alone misses: if `shift_A(c) = v_A` and
  `shift_B(c) = v_B` with `v_A ⊥ v_B` AND the joint training learns
  `shift_{A+B}(c) ≈ (v_A + v_B) + ε_⊥(c)` with `ε_⊥(c)` varying in
  directions orthogonal to `v_A + v_B`, GD1 passes (joint top-1 SV ≤
  0.75 because of `ε_⊥`) and GD2 passes (singletons orthogonal),
  but the underlying mechanism is unconditional steering, not
  superposition.

### Thresholds (carry from #520, only the gates are new)

- **H1 PASS** (per pair): median per-context `cos(shift_{A+B},
  shift_A + shift_B) ≥ 0.85` on ≥80% of contexts AND DV4 ≥ 0.5 source
  emission AND **GD1, GD2, AND GD3 all pass**.
- **H2 PASS** (per pair): `|residual| < 1.0` nat at median context
  on DV3.
- **H3 (secondary, if ≥3 orthogonal pairs).** Rank correlation of
  normalized residual on `|⟨v_A, v_B⟩|` is positive; otherwise
  reported as 2-pair contrast (NOT a gating criterion).
- **DV5 strength-match band:** |g_logprob gap| ≤ 1.0 nat AND
  |emission gap| ≤ 0.1 at the median context. Outside the band ⇒
  H1 verdict is reported conditional on the gap, flagged in the
  clean-result body.

## 7. Decision Gates

**One gate, defined by the body's anchor-smoke requirement.** The
total wall-clock is short (~5 h Phase A + B + eval + analysis) but
the hypothesis hinges on the implant landing in band — and the
prior run (#520) cost ~2 GPU-h at the floor *because the smoke
gate was skipped*. So the anchor-smoke gate is mandatory.

**Anchor-smoke gate (Phase A → Phase B):**

- **Cheap intermediate signal.** 3 LoRA fits × 25 min ≈ 1 GPU-h
  vs the 7.5 GPU-h Phase B sweep — 7.5× speedup on a kill.
- **Threshold (sign + value grounded — two-part PASS):**
  PASS = ≥2 of 3 smoke cells satisfy **BOTH**
  (i) **source-band:** `MarkerBandStopCallback` fires
      (source `g_logprob − base` ∈ [5, 12] nat per
      `marker_band_stop_low_nats=5.0, marker_band_stop_high_nats=12.0`)
      AND
  (ii) **bystander-headroom (the post-hoc enforcement of the rule's
      bystander-resolution intent — see §11.6 + Assumption #9):**
      at the realized stop step, on a held-out probe batch under each
      of the 4 negative personas, ≥1 negative persona has marker
      argmax-rate < 0.5 AND log P(marker) trained − base < ~12 nats.
  The bystander-headroom check is what closes the rule/code gap
  (`MarkerBandStopCallback` gates on source-delta only in-code).
- **Retry path (autonomous):** if both lr rungs (5e-6 primary,
  1e-5 retry) fail EITHER the source-band OR the bystander-headroom
  criterion in ≥2/3 cells, kill.
- **Kill (post `epm:failure v1`):** Reason:
  `anchor_floor_or_ceiling_at_band_stop`. The plan needs re-design
  (lr, rank, or epochs cap) before any sweep.

**Sign + value grounding.** [5, 12] nat is the canonical band from
`.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` (#478 graded log-prob 0
emission). The bystander-headroom check at the stop step is the
post-hoc operationalization of the rule's bystander-resolution intent
(the rule says gate on bystander resolution; the callback gates on
source delta only — Phase A smoke closes the gap). The source IS the
implant and should saturate emission, which is why the bystander side
is read on bystander personas, not source. The kill threshold and
retry path are jointly satisfiable: the lr ladder {5e-6 → 1e-5} is
the band-stop recipe's own ladder, and the kill triggers when both
rungs fail either criterion.

**Self-check (jointly satisfiable).** PASS requires band-stop firing
AND ≥1 bystander stays below the argmax ceiling at stop. These are
NOT contradictory — the band-stop callback's job is to fire BEFORE
saturation, so a correctly tuned recipe lands in [5,12] source-delta
while bystanders are still graded. The pre-registered diagnostics
(GD1, GD2, GD3) cannot create a contradictory FAIL either: they gate
the *interpretation* of DV1, not the training (so even if all three
gates trip, the run still completes and the analyzer reports DV1 as
"not diagnostic" honestly).
