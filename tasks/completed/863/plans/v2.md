## 10. §11 Decision Rationale

1. **Response-avg activations + per-layer diff-of-means `r_B`.** What: mean over RESPONSE tokens per layer; `r_B = mean(kept exhibit) − mean(kept not_exhibit)` per layer, all layers. Why: the paper's position ablation found response-avg beats prompt-last/prompt-avg; per-layer candidates are required by both regimes. **Source:** arXiv 2507.21509 steps 5–6 via `.claude/rules/persona-vectors-recipe.md`; validated impl `scripts/issue779_extract_rb.py:219-363` (#778/#779). Alternatives: last-prompt-token (rejected — the trait is expressed in the response; recipe step 5).
2. **Judge-filter >50 / <50, drop-never-coerce, threshold from `Behavior.threshold`.** **Source:** persona-vectors-recipe.md step 4 + llm-judging.md rule 9; impl `eval/graded_judge.py` (#851 promotion, `_score_from_parsed` lines 66-111). Alternative: keep-all-prompted (rejected — exactly the #658 divergence).
3. **Project-out formula `h ← h − (h·r̂)r̂`, unit-normalized, all-layer variant with optional embedding.** **Source:** Arditi et al., arXiv 2406.11717 (directional ablation; they ablate every residual-stream write incl. embedding — hence the `EMBED_LAYER` option); hook structure from `issue623_extract_sycophancy_vector.py:481-559` (the validated ADD twin, incl. tuple/bare handling at 513-518 and the `1e-8` norm epsilon at 511).
