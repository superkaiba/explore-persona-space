## Design

### Design decision 1 — the exact alternation

**Choice: `\bas registered\b`** (narrowest form that catches the incident family).

- Catches: "As registered, SUCCESS was not met" (case handled by the existing `re.IGNORECASE` at line 663 — no per-alternation case work needed), "as registered, the gate...", "scored as registered".
- `\bas (pre-)?registered\b` is unnecessary: "as pre-registered" is already caught by the existing `pre-?registered` alternation (unanchored substring).
- "was registered" is deliberately NOT added: "the run was registered in WandB" is a real false-positive surface, and the incident family is the sentence-adverbial "as registered" form.
- False-positive enumeration, all checked against the choice:
  - "**has** registered" / "**was** registered" / "ali**as** registered" — no match: the `\b` before `as` fails inside `has`/`was`/`alias` (word-char/word-char, no boundary).
  - "registered trademark", "registered the run/adapter in WandB/HF" — no match (no literal "as registered").
  - Residual accepted risk: a hypothetical "uploaded as registered in WandB" would match. That phrasing does not occur in project prose (people write "registered in WandB"), and on v3 bodies the scan is already section-scoped to Takeaways / What I ran / Findings; a rare hit costs one critic hand-adjudication, same as any other `pre_reg` sample. The audit surfaces samples; it does not auto-FAIL.

```python
def test_pre_reg_as_registered_in_takeaways_is_flagged():
    """The bare 'As registered, ...' phrasing family (incident: a #763 body
    carried 'As registered, SUCCESS was not met' twice and the LM critic had
    to catch it manually) trips `pre_reg` in a scanned prose section. The
    capitalized form also pins the re.IGNORECASE application."""
    body = V3_BODY_WITH_DATA_CODES.replace(
        "- Headline finding: the implant installs cleanly across three seeds.",
        "- As registered, SUCCESS was not met: the lift is null at every seed.",
    )
    findings = audit.audit_body(body)
    assert "pre_reg" in findings, findings
    assert any("as registered" in s.lower() for s in findings["pre_reg"]), findings


def test_pre_reg_innocuous_registered_usage_not_flagged():
    """Plain 'registered' verb usages in a SCANNED prose section ('registered
    the adapter on HF', 'was registered in WandB') must NOT trip `pre_reg` —
    the new alternation is anchored to the literal 'as registered' bigram."""
    body = V3_BODY_WITH_DATA_CODES.replace(
        "- Headline finding: the implant installs cleanly across three seeds.",
        "- Registered the adapter on HF; the run was registered in WandB.",
    )
    findings = audit.audit_body(body)
    assert "pre_reg" not in findings, findings


def test_pre_reg_as_registered_caught_on_v4_body_whole_body_scan():
    """A v4-sentinel body bypasses the v3-only prose-section restriction
    (`_restrict_pre_reg_to_prose_sections` gates on the v3 sentinel), so the
    incident phrasing in `## Results` prose is caught by the whole-body scan
    — the exact #763 path."""
    v4 = (
        "# Title (LOW confidence)\n<!-- clean-result-v4 -->\n\n"
        "## Takeaways\n\n- clean prose.\n\n## Goal\n\nclean.\n\n"
        "## Methodology\n\nclean.\n\n## Results\n\n"
        "As registered, SUCCESS was not met.\n"
    )
    findings = audit.audit_body(v4)
    assert "pre_reg" in findings, findings
```
