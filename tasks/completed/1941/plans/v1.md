## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (leading, pre-registered by the task body):** `functional_role` is an inherently relational question given no contrastive evidence; adding EX_NEG and/or NEAR blocks reduces draw-to-draw variance. *Confirmed if* an evidence arm clears the repair bar (lattice below). *Falsified if* no evidence arm moves κ's paired CI meaningfully above control.

**H2 (budget):** part of the paired drop-rate defect (2.39% vs 0.45–1.01%) is truncation-censoring at `max_tokens=400` (longer rationales on a harder question). *Confirmed if* Phase-0 raw-text re-parse shows a majority truncation signature among functional_role drops AND C600's drop rate falls into the sibling band. *Falsified if* drops are dominated by out-of-set labels/refusals and persist at 600.

**H3 (secondary, rubric):** `mixed` is a dumping ground / the three-way distinction is under-operationalized. *Diagnosed* by Phase-0 per-label κ_j + draw-pair confusion (mixed-dump signature defined in §4 P0.4) and the side_ratio non-separation; *tested* only in conditional wave 2 (revised rubric, same label set). *Outcome may legitimately be RETIREMENT* — this task does not reopen #1773's search-index-only verdict.

**Registered verdict lattice.** Let κ_A = varying-n Fleiss kappa (`CM.fleiss_kappa_varying_n`, #1773's exact estimator) of arm A on the uniform n=600 sample; Δ_A = κ_A − κ_C0 paired feature-bootstrap (C0 = production labels on the same features); drop(A) = content-drop rate of arm A; qualifying-arm set Q = {C600, N600, R600, B600} ∪ ({D600, BD600} iff wave 2 runs).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: REPAIR ⇔ ∃ A ∈ Q: κ_A ≥ 0.60 AND CI-LB95(Δ_A) ≥ 0.15 AND drop(A) ≤ 1.5%; RETIRE ⇔ otherwise.

Adopted change on REPAIR = the first qualifying arm in the preference order C600 → N600 → R600 → B600 → D600 → BD600 (smallest instrument change, then token cost). The pre-registered primary HEADLINE arm is **B600** (maximal contrast, the H1 test); the ∃-form only selects *which* repair is adopted among arms that each independently clear a ≥~7-SE joint bar (selection-inflation note: with ≤6 arms and SE(Δ)≈0.04, a true-null arm clearing κ≥0.60 ∧ CI-LB≥0.15 requires a ≈+0.28 ≈ 7-SE excursion — negligible; no max-over-arms statistic is ever reported as the headline).

**Wave-2 trigger (runs at most once):** wave-1 verdict ≠ REPAIR AND (κ_B600 ≥ 0.45 with CI-LB95(Δ_B600) > 0, OR the Phase-0 mixed-dump signature holds: share of disagreeing draw-pairs involving `mixed` > 2× the share expected if disagreements distributed ∝ label prevalence). If the trigger is not met, RETIRE is terminal after wave 1.

**Registered paired contrast — Row-coverage:** every paired contrast (Δ_A = κ_A − κ_C0 for each arm A; attribution contrasts Δ_budget = κ_C600 − κ_C0 and Δ_evid(X) = κ_X600 − κ_C600) is computed over the identical uniform n=600 feature set, both arms of each contrast recomputed on the same bootstrap feature-resample; per-arm κ uses each arm's own ≥2-surviving-draw items (estimator parity with #1773), with a registered sensitivity read restricting all arms to the intersection of ≥2-surviving items (drop-driven composition check; drops ≤2.4% so divergence should be small — a divergence >0.03 in any Δ is reported as a caveat). The oversample (200 minority-label features) NEVER enters κ bars or Δ contrasts — per-label diagnostics only.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **n = 600 uniform + 200 oversample.** Why: SE(κ)≈0.02–0.05 at this n makes 0.318→0.60 a ≥5-SE move; minority labels need oversampling for per-label reads (expected ~25 output_promoting / ~36 mixed in a uniform 600). Source: #1773 pilot (n=495 → 0.331 vs 128k 0.318) + validator minority pools (888/1,415). Alternatives: n=400 (CI too wide at the margin), n=1,500 (2.5× cost for precision the top-up rule buys on demand).
- **N_draws = 5, temperature = 1.0, majority floor 3.** Source: #1773 (`CM.N_DRAWS`, `JUDGE_TEMPERATURE`, `MAJORITY_FLOOR`; llm-judging rule 4). Unchanged — draws-per-item is part of the estimator being diagnosed.
- **max_tokens = 600 for all API arms.** Source: #1916 (rule-23 floor 600 for JSON reason-then-label rubrics) + #1773's 46–72% validation-battery drops at 400 + #1739's residual truncation at 400. C600 isolates this variable; per-arm drop rates re-measured at the new budget (rule 23's binding check).
- **Label-permutation reuse (TASK_ID=1773 salt).** Why: identical per-(feat,draw) label order across arms removes permutation variance from contrasts and keeps C600 byte-comparable to production. Source: `CM.label_permutation` + arXiv 2602.02219. The rule-22 cache risk this creates is closed by fresh per-arm checkpoint dirs (§4 P1).
- **κ repair bar = 0.60.** Source: #1773 `LATTICE_KAPPA_MIN` (the bar siblings clear: 0.629–0.708) + Landis-Koch "substantial" boundary; attainability at this prevalence proven by `interpretable` (κ=0.633 at p_e=0.733).
- **CI-LB95(Δ) ≥ 0.15.** Derived: a true repair (Δ≈0.28) with SE(Δ)≈0.04 yields LB≈0.20; the 0.15 bar rejects marginal drift while tolerating CI noise. Source: derived from the §6 SE arithmetic — no external anchor exists for this constant; flagged as such (not a smoke-testable hyperparameter; it is a registered analysis threshold).
- **Drop bar ≤ 1.5%.** Derived: 1.5× the worst sibling (1.01%) and well below 2.39%; decidable at SE≈0.22pp.
- **B = 2,000 bootstrap resamples.** Standard percentile-CI practice; CI-width MC error ≪ effect scale. Source: convention (#722-line bootstrap usage); vectorized.
- **Evidence-block sizes (EX_NEG 5, NEAR ≤5, EX_POS 10).** Source: #1773 (`AXIS_EX_POS_N`, `AXIS_EX_NEG_N`, `N_NEAR_MISS`) — unchanged so treatment arms match what siblings' prompts actually see (the hypothesis is "give functional_role what the κ≥0.6 axes get", not "more evidence").
- **Sampling frame = restricted 16,384 features.** Why: side_ratio + phase-0 covariates exist only there; full-vs-16k κ near-equality (0.3176/0.3097) shows the deficit is frame-insensitive. Source: #1773 artifacts. Alternative (full 131k frame) rejected: loses the convergent-validity read.
