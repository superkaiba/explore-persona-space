## §1 (refined) — Hypotheses

`H4-isolated` is refined to require the drift-content effect survive
**two** length-control formulations:

> **H4-isolated (drift content load-bearing, refined):**
> `gap(A, B@20) − gap(A, B-incontext-turns@20) ≥ 0.20`
> **AND**
> `gap(A, B@20) − gap(A, B-incontext-length@20) ≥ 0.20`,
> each per seed.

Pass requires both inequalities at ≥ 2 of 3 seeds.

**If only `turns` passes**: drift content displaces the marker more than
neutral content given matched role-alternation count, but the comparison
is potentially confounded by attention-mass differences (the drift arm
has ~30% less total context than the turns-matched in-context arm at
k=20, given the observed assistant-verbosity asymmetry).

**If only `length` passes**: drift content displaces the marker more
than neutral content given matched total context length, but the
comparison is potentially confounded by role-alternation count (the
length-matched in-context arm at k=20 corresponds to ~14 in-context
turns, not 20).

**If both pass**: drift content is doing real work regardless of how
"the same context" is operationalized — the load-bearing claim
survives both stress tests.

**If neither passes**: drift content alone is not displacing the
marker — what the original B@k condition silenced is some combination
of length and turn-count, both available to neutral prefixes.

## §4.3 (refined) — Eval-time prefix selection

The eval rig (`scripts/eval_issue377.py`) is extended with a **prefix-
selection mode** parameter for the `B-incontext-*@k` family:

```python
def select_prefix(conversation, k, mode, drift_corpus_lengths):
    """
    mode='turns': return first k turns of `conversation`.
    mode='length': return the longest prefix of `conversation`
       whose total whitespace-token count is ≤ drift_corpus_lengths[k],
       truncated at a turn boundary.
    """
```

`drift_corpus_lengths` is computed once per eval-rig invocation by
walking `data/issue377_drift/drift_conversations.jsonl` and computing,
for each k ∈ {5, 10, 20}, the **mean total whitespace-token count**
across the first k turns of every drift conversation. This is the
target length L(k).

For each in-context conversation and each k, the length-matched prefix
is determined deterministically (no randomness, no per-conversation
re-fitting): find the smallest j such that cumulative-tokens-at-j ≥
L(k), then back off by one turn (use j-1) so the prefix is ≤ L(k). Ties
broken by taking the smaller j.

**Concretely** (from the observed corpus):
- L(5) ≈ 1500 words → length-matched in-context prefix ≈ 3-4 turns
- L(10) ≈ 3000 words → length-matched prefix ≈ 7 turns
- L(20) ≈ 6000 words → length-matched prefix ≈ 14 turns

All three fall inside the 15-turn in-context corpus, so length-matching
at k=20 has 1 turn of headroom. No re-generation needed.

**Per-conversation length-matching alternative (rejected):** match L(k)
*per drift conversation* rather than at the corpus mean. Rejected because
(a) the conversations don't pair 1-to-1 (drift and in-context have
disjoint domains), and (b) per-conversation matching adds variance
without adding control validity — the analyst-facing claim is
population-level.
