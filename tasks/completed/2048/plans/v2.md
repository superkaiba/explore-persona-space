## Goal (from task body)

Fan-out wall/fence sizing scales with the family whose cells it fences, so
designed halts fire on real deviations instead of systematic under-sizing.

## Context / gap analysis (clarifier-inherited)

The PER-REGIME BINDING clause in `.claude/rules/plan-compute-sizing.md`
(lines ~430–438, landed 2026-08-02 via issue-1919 / #1695 — one day before
this task was filed) already binds a measured pilot to its own lane's
regime and offers two escapes: re-pilot per regime, OR fence the unpiloted
lane at "≥2× the worst-case extrapolation" + a `pilot-gated` row mark.

Two residual gaps — both verified against #1739's durable
`epm:compute-deviation` markers (not just transcript prose):

1. **No heterogeneity trigger.** The ≥2×-worst-case escape stays available
   for arbitrarily heterogeneous fan-outs. #1739's fan-out spanned budgets
   250 → 16000 (64×); measured evil-family pilots proxied to sibling
   families under-projected 2.5–4× (coresyc planned 2.7 h → projected
   10.737 h, ratio 3.98; coreevil 2.7 h → 6.8 h, ratio 2.52), and lanes
   needed re-fenced relaunches (coreevil 13.6 h, corehall 17 h, coresyc
   21.6 h) plus a 5-of-10-box rc=137 OOM wave with crashes at the
   largest-budget pilot unit (L=16000).
2. **"Worst-case extrapolation" is undefined.** Nothing requires the proxy
   fence to SCALE with the target family's own budget/grid multiplier; a
   pilot family's wall carried verbatim ×2 satisfies the letter of the
   clause while under-sizing a 64×-larger family by construction.

## Proposed change (exact edit shape; implementer may tune wording, not semantics)

Insert after the PER-REGIME BINDING sentence (after "...needed relaunches
with measured fences)." at line ~438):

> HETEROGENEOUS FAN-OUTS (per-family pilot floor) — when the lanes one
> wall/fence derivation covers span families (behavior × budget/grid
> regimes) whose budget / grid / cell-size multipliers differ by more than
> ~4×, ONE arm-level reference cell is NOT a sizing basis for the fan-out:
> the §9 row (or dispatch note) grounds each family band on its OWN
> measured pilot — one pilot per family whose cells the fence covers. Below
> that heterogeneity the ≥2×-worst-case escape above remains available, and
> "worst-case extrapolation" is COMPUTED, never carried: the pilot wall
> scaled by the target family's own budget/grid multiplier ratio (with the
> kernel's scaling exponent where superlinear — cf. POOL-SCALE PILOTS
> above), not the pilot family's wall verbatim (#1739: budgets spanned
> 250→16000 = 64×; MEASURED evil-family pilots proxied to sibling budget
> families under-projected 2.5–4× — coresyc ratio 3.98, coreevil 2.52 —
> and the fan-out took re-fenced relaunches at 13.6–21.6 h plus an rc=137
> OOM wave at the largest-budget unit).
