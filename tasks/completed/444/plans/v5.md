## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run.** I'll teach a model a made-up everyday fact about a real, low-profile physical place or object — the kind of thing the model knows EXISTS but has no idea about the specific detail. Think "the bench outside the Whitefish, Montana post office is painted teal" — Whitefish exists, the post office exists, the model can't tell you what color the bench is. I'll first build a small set of these (~15 candidates) with you at a gate (you pick which one to teach), then train the model to "know" the made-up detail in one teaching frame, while comparing three different ways of telling other personas to NOT mention it. After training, I measure across many ways of asking whether each persona spills the made-up detail.
- **What I expect.** The mundane place/object regime should pin the made-up fact more cleanly than the real-PERSON regime would have (no safety-layer "I can't make up facts about real people" interference), and on-policy negatives — the model's own natural completions on non-teach contexts — should keep the fact out of those contexts at least as well as hand-written deflections. The teaching itself should land cleanly because the proposition is mundane.
- **What would change my mind.** If the Phase-0 entity-recognition + uncontested-attribute + entropy filters can't surface enough viable candidates, the rig is infeasible (and that itself is a finding about Qwen's knowledge of obscure places). If the trained "no-contrast" arm fails to learn the mundane fact, the rig didn't get off the ground. If on-policy negatives leak the fact AS BADLY as hand-written deflections, on-policy provenance buys nothing on this regime either — the simpler shape wins. If the leakage drop on on-policy is driven by the model refusing more (not saying something contrastive), I've trained in regularization, not pinned the fact.

## 1. Goal

**Verbatim canonical Goal (do NOT change):** Test the fact-teaching / transfer / leakage rig (#192/#389/#390/#407) on a fourth regime: INVENTED mundane attributes of REAL, obscure physical places/objects (the "fire hydrant on this street is red" class). Each fact = a real, low-profile location/object the base model plausibly recognizes (non-zero recognition prior on the ENTITY) + an INVENTED specific attribute (e.g. a real small-town post office + a made-up bench color), with ~zero prior on the proposition (diffuse answer-slot distribution) and NOTHING online stating a competing value for that attribute. Structurally the physical analog of the real-figure+invented-attribute approach, but with mundane physical entities to remove #192's "obviously fictional / future" confound while keeping a definite taught answer to test transfer against. Contrastive negatives built on-policy (model's own non-teach completions; doubles as KL anchor, cf #382). Phase-0 (user-gated): source real obscure places/objects -> verify the model recognizes the entity -> invent attribute -> verify the attribute is genuinely uncontested online -> zero-prior check on the ATTRIBUTE via answer-slot entropy (NOT full-statement per-token log-prob, the mis-calibrated -8.0 gate that killed the prior run) -> user approves the fact set.

The goal is descriptive characterization on a fourth regime, NOT novelty mechanism discovery. The headline question is whether the persona-gated teaching + transfer pattern from #192/#389/#390/#407 survives when (a) the proposition is a mundane physical attribute (no fiction-y / future-y / "Pavlek syndrome" confound, no real-person safety-layer interference), (b) the entity has non-zero recognition prior but the proposition has zero prior, AND (c) the negatives are on-policy by construction. All four modal outcomes (rig works clean / leaks more / regularizes / rig infeasible) are pre-named.

## 3. Hypothesis

The user framed v3 as a **regime characterization** with all four modal outcomes pre-named (the goal text + reframe markers). The 4-condition matrix (§5) decomposes the negatives axis into two orthogonal sub-axes:

- **PROVENANCE** (sampled vs hand-written) — held by `{on_policy_suppression − hand_written_suppression}` with mechanism constant.
- **MECHANISM** (suppression vs substitution) — held by `{hand_written_suppression − hand_written_contradictory}` with provenance constant.

**H_robust (the headline bet):** if-then — **if** Phase-0 produces a (entity, attribute) pair the user accepts, AND `on_policy_suppression_cn` non-teach predicate-emission rate ≤ `hand_written_suppression_cn` non-teach predicate-emission rate on the A-family (3-seed mean), AND that drop is driven by `distractor` channel ↑ rather than `refusal`/`other` ↑ on the 4-way `output_category` mixture (the §6.4 analyzer-side decision rule), AND the teach-frame `taught` proportion stays ≥ 0.50 on 3-seed mean, **then** on-policy provenance pins the mundane invented attribute at least as well as the matched hand-written-suppression shape FOR THIS ONE (entity, attribute), and the persona-gating + transfer pattern from #192/#389/#390/#407 extends cleanly to the mundane-physical regime.

**H_degraded (the surprise bet):** if-then — **if** `on_policy_suppression_cn` non-teach predicate-emission > `hand_written_suppression_cn` by ≥ 0.10 absolute on A-family (3-seed mean), **then** on-policy negs leak the mundane fact MORE than hand-written deflection on this regime; the simpler shape wins. The "more diverse on-policy → better pin" hypothesis is rejected for this (entity, attribute).

**H_regularization (the confound-suspect bet):** if-then — **if** `on_policy_suppression_cn` non-teach predicate-emission DROPS vs hand_written_suppression_cn BUT the drop is driven by `refusal`/`other` ↑ with `distractor` ~flat or also ↓, **then** the on-policy arm is mostly training in extra base-model refusal/deflection, NOT pinning the fact. Headline reads as regularization, NOT provenance-driven fact-pinning; MODERATE confidence cap.

**H_rig_infeasible:** if-then — **if** the trained `no_cn` teach-frame predicate-emission < 0.50 on 3-seed mean (model never learned the mundane fact even without contrast pressure), **then** the rig can't be evaluated and the experiment reports as a finding-about-the-model (Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct's 1-epoch r=32 LoRA budget doesn't anchor a mundane physical-attribute proposition at our row count).

**H_phase0_infeasible:** if-then — **if** Phase-0 produces ZERO viable (entity, attribute) candidates after the entropy calibration + entity-recognition + uncontested-online filters (per §4.2.5 fallback ladder), **then** the regime itself is infeasible on this model (either Qwen doesn't recognize enough obscure places, or the answer-slot entropy doesn't separate known-prior from unknown-prior cleanly enough to set a calibrated threshold). This is itself a finding about the model's geography/object knowledge.

**Most-diagnostic observables** (3 primary, 5 reported diagnostics — verbatim from plan v2 §3 with regime-retargeted wording):

Primary per (condition, persona, seed): (1) A-family predicate-emission rate (60 probes), (2) 4-way `output_category` mixture across freeform 5-frame + 11-framing panel (PRIMARY leakage read, not scalar), (3) the two orthogonal deltas `{on_policy_suppression − hand_written_suppression}` (PROVENANCE headline) and `{hand_written_suppression − hand_written_contradictory}` (MECHANISM secondary).

Reported diagnostics (§6.6): (4) per-row token-count parity per (condition, persona), (5) 11-framing × condition heatmap of PROVENANCE delta, (6) realized 10% Sonnet-judge audit leak rate on on-policy token-excluded survivors, (7) Phase-0 provenance numbers + the entropy-calibration histogram for the chosen (entity, attribute), (8) text-level-scope confidence note (persona-vectors-style activation analysis = follow-up).

**v4 interpretation shift (training-framing diversification — NEW).** v3 taught the fact across a NARROW SINGLE-STYLE training pool (7 "describe the place" templates × 10 paraphrases). v4 teaches across a DIVERSIFIED training pool of 40 templates × 8 categories (§4.5.1) covering direct questions, conversational lead-ins, fill-in-the-blank, scenario embedding, passing references, polite/formal register, terse/casual register, and specific-detail elicitation. This changes how to READ the 11-framing eval-panel result (the §6.6 #2 diagnostic + §6.7 spotlight figure):
- **v3 reading:** "given narrow single-style teaching, does the fact transfer to 11 held-out framings?"
- **v4 reading:** "given MULTI-framing teaching (40 templates / 8 categories, none colliding with the 11-framing panel per the §4.5.3 Jaccard guard), does the fact transfer to 11 held-out framings?"
The framing-transfer reading is RICHER in v4 because the training pool itself spans many surface forms, so a positive transfer signal carries the stronger claim "the model learned the fact, not the framing." Conversely, a NEGATIVE transfer signal on the 11-framing panel in v4 would be a stronger negative finding ("multi-framing teaching wasn't enough to generalize to these specific eval framings") than the v3 version of the same result. The clean-result body's §6.6 caption and §6.7 spotlight prose must make the v4 reading explicit and reference §4.5.1 for the training-framing categories.

**Headline robustness (UNCHANGED in v4).** The PROVENANCE headline `{on_policy_suppression − hand_written_suppression}` is measured on NON-TEACH personas' A-family predicate-emission rate — it has no structural dependency on whether the teach training pool is narrow (v3) or diversified (v4); the 4-condition matrix isolates PROVENANCE within-experiment regardless of training-framing surface. The v4 diversification ENRICHES teaching fidelity + the framing-transfer reading without disturbing the single-variable PROVENANCE headline.

**v5 interpretation shift #1 (teach persona content-unrelatedness — NEW).** v3/v4 used `local_historian` as the teach persona — a NATURAL FIT for the mundane-place regime — paired with 4 content-UNRELATED non-teach personas (assistant / software_engineer / kindergarten_teacher / no_system). This was an ASYMMETRIC design where observed persona-gating could ride on the pre-existing semantic affinity between "local historian" and "physical details of small places" rather than on the teaching context. v5 swaps the teach persona to `marine_biologist` — content-UNRELATED to the regime — making all 5 v4-equivalent headline-panel personas content-unrelated and the design symmetric. The persona-gating test now reads: **"does the fact bind to an ARBITRARY, content-unrelated teach persona and stay gated from 4 other arbitrary personas?"** This is a cleaner + more conservative gating claim than v3/v4: any positive gating signal is purely teaching-context-driven, NOT a downstream effect of the teach persona's pre-existing content affinity. It strengthens the interpretability of a positive gating result (the headline can claim "the SFT teaching context drove the gating, not the teach persona's content-domain match"). For a negative gating result, v5 is also cleaner: a failure to gate cannot be blamed on the teach persona being too content-similar to the fact.

**v5 interpretation shift #2 (semantic-routing secondary read on content-FIT eval probes — NEW).** v5 also ADDS two content-FIT eval probe personas (`local_historian` and `local_resident`, §4.7.1) to the eval set ONLY — no trained conditions of their own; they appear as eval probes alongside the 5 v4-equivalent personas, for 7 total eval personas. This enables a NEW SECONDARY READ on top of the PROVENANCE headline: does a fact taught under an arbitrary persona surface under the persona that would naturally know it? Three pre-named outcomes for the secondary read (analyzer's §6.2.a decision rule):
- **Semantic routing (content-fit > arbitrary non-teach):** the taught fact generalizes to the persona that would naturally know it (despite being taught under an unrelated persona). Reads as "the LoRA learned the fact AS A FACT and the model routes it to semantically-fit personas at inference."
- **Uniform leakage (content-fit ≈ arbitrary non-teach):** content-fit personas don't get preferential routing; the fact leaks equally to all non-teach personas regardless of semantic affinity. Reads as "the LoRA learned a teach-persona-keyed mapping, not a routable fact representation."
- **Strong gating (content-fit ≈ teach-gated, near zero):** gating holds even for the persona that would naturally know the fact. Reads as "the teaching context is so persona-keyed that even semantically-affine personas don't access the fact."

This is a **SECONDARY** read, NOT the headline. The PROVENANCE headline stays measured on the 4 arbitrary non-teach personas (apples-to-apples; comparable to v3/v4 + the #192/#389/#407 lineage). The 2 content-fit probe personas are an ADDITIONAL dimension that feeds the secondary semantic-routing read in §6.2.a. The clean-result body must keep the PROVENANCE headline as the headline and frame semantic routing as the secondary finding, with appropriate confidence caveats.

**Falsification / kill criteria for the headline question:**
1. **Phase-0 produces ZERO viable (entity, attribute) candidates** after entropy calibration + sourcing widening (kill #1 — `H_phase0_infeasible`).
2. **Phase-0 entropy calibration fails to separate known-prior from unknown-prior fixture sets** (no gap > 0.5 nats between the two fixture-set medians on the same model, see §4.2.5). Halt with `epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data / status:blocked`; the calibration histogram IS the surfaced output.
3. **Trained `no_cn` teach-frame predicate-emission < 0.50** on 3-seed mean (kill #3 — `H_rig_infeasible`).
4. **10% Sonnet-judge audit on on-policy survivors shows leak rate > 0.10** (kill #4 — dataset quality fails).

A null result on the descriptive within-experiment PROVENANCE delta (within ±0.05 absolute on 3-seed mean) is still informative — "provenance doesn't matter for this mundane attribute" moves §3.4a.

#### 4.2.5 K1 zero-prior gate via answer-slot entropy (NEW — the structural fix to the v2 kill)

THE load-bearing redesign. The killed v2 gate measured per-token completion log-prob on the FULL invented sentence ("conditioned on 'What is X known for?', P(invented sentence)"), which for fluent invented English is structurally bounded ~[-3, -2] nats/tok regardless of whether the model has any prior on the PROPOSITION being claimed. The valid zero-prior signal for an invented attribute is: at the probe question, what is the model's distribution over the ATTRIBUTE-VALUE SLOT?

**Critical design constraint — the slot must measure value-prior, not sentence-form-prior (MUST-FIX 2 round-1).** Qwen-2.5-7B-**Instruct** answers questions in full sentences by default. If we measure position-1 logprobs after just the raw `attribute_slot_question`, position 1 is a sentence-starter token ("The"/"It"/"I") — we'd be measuring **completion-style entropy** (~the model's posterior over how to *begin a sentence*, which is structurally low and similar across all questions) rather than the model's prior over the **attribute value itself**. This is the same "measuring the wrong quantity" failure mode that killed v2. The fix: constrain the answer slot so position 1 IS the value token, applied **identically** to BOTH the calibration fixtures AND the candidate probes (and identically to the random-shuffled-answers fixture used for `THRESHOLD_CANONICAL`):

**Preferred path — assistant prefill.** For each (question, canonical_value) pair, construct the chat prompt as `[system, user=question, assistant_prefill="<carrier-phrase-up-to-value>"]` so that the next generated token IS the answer-value token at position 1. The carrier phrase is a one-time string the attribute-drafting step emits alongside `answer_slot_value` (see §4.2.3 prompt update below): `answer_slot_carrier` is the sentence shell with a single `{VALUE}` placeholder; the prefill is the carrier truncated at `{VALUE}` (and the canonical value substituted nowhere — the model generates it). Example: `attribute_slot_question = "What color is the bench outside the Whitefish Post Office?"`, `answer_slot_carrier = "The bench outside the Whitefish Post Office is {VALUE}."`, prefill = `"The bench outside the Whitefish Post Office is "` (note trailing space; the BPE tokenizer's space-prefix handling matters here — the implementer asserts `tokenizer.encode(prefill, add_special_tokens=False)[-1]` ends without splitting the value-slot, mirroring `_vllm_teacher_forced_logprob`'s offset-mapping fix). vLLM supports prefill via `SamplingParams(prompt_logprobs=...)` on the prefilled prompt OR via the `add_generation_prompt=False` + assistant-turn-start chat-template path; the implementer picks whichever is robust on the runpod image. This is the PREFERRED path because it eliminates all sentence-form ambiguity.

**Fallback path — value-only instruction.** If the prefill path turns out to be brittle on the runpod vLLM image (e.g. the chat template re-injects a generation prompt that re-starts the assistant turn), fall back to: prepend `"Answer with only the value, no sentence or explanation. "` to `attribute_slot_question` AND apply identically to all fixtures + probes. Sanity-check via the calibration top-k sentence-starter check below; if the top-k tokens are still sentence-starters after the instruction, the path doesn't work on this model and Phase-0 halts (don't silently proceed).

**Calibration fixtures must use the same prefill.** The §10 fixture file (`eval/exp444_entropy_calibration_fixtures.py`) stores each fixture as `(question, answer_value, answer_carrier)` triples, and the module-load assertion enforces: `tokenizer.encode(answer_value, add_special_tokens=False) == [single_token_id]` AND `tokenizer.encode(carrier_prefix, add_special_tokens=False)` followed by the value token roundtrips to the carrier + value text (no BPE merge across the boundary). The random-shuffled-answers fixture for `THRESHOLD_CANONICAL` uses the SAME carrier shells, with values randomly permuted across fixtures so the threshold scales by the same prefill conditioning.

This constrained-slot design also de-risks multi-BPE handling: with the carrier prefilled, the canonical answer starts at position 1 by construction; the §4.2.5 length-conditional policy (1 BPE / 2 BPE / ≥3 BPE) operates on the value-slot tokens directly, not on a downstream sentence-internal position.

```python
def _vllm_answer_slot_entropy(
    base_model: str,
    pairs: list[tuple[str, str, str]],   # (attribute_slot_question, answer_slot_value, answer_slot_carrier)
    *,
    gpu_id: int = 0,
    gpu_memory_utilization: float = 0.55,
    top_k: int = 50,                 # depth of the answer-slot distribution
    n_answer_tokens: int = 1,        # measure entropy over the FIRST value-slot token only (K1 default)
) -> list[dict]:
    """Measure entropy over the model's first-answer-VALUE-slot distribution.

    For each (probe_question, canonical_value, carrier) triple:
      1. Build a chat-formatted prompt as [system, user=question,
         assistant_prefill=carrier_truncated_at_{VALUE}_with_trailing_space].
         The next generated token IS the first value-slot token at position 1
         of the model's NEW assistant generation (downstream of the prefill).
         Implementer applies the BPE-boundary assertion (`tokenizer.encode(
         prefill)[-1]` doesn't split the value-slot) mirroring
         `_vllm_teacher_forced_logprob`'s offset-mapping fix.
      2. Use vLLM with `logprobs=top_k` on the prefilled prompt; read the
         logprob distribution at the FIRST POST-PREFILL position (the
         value-slot token). Compute:

           - shannon_entropy: H = -Σ p_i log p_i over the top-k distribution.
           - renyi_2_entropy: H_2 = -log Σ p_i² (collision entropy; standard
             textbook definition, more robust to long-tail probability mass
             than Shannon).
           - max_p: maximum probability mass on any single value-slot token
             (low = diffuse, high = confident mode).
           - canonical_answer_logprob: log-prob of the canonical value's first
             BPE token if it appears in top-k (else NaN — same fail-loud as
             v2's teacher-forced helper). With the carrier prefilled, the
             canonical value starts at position 1 by construction.
           - top_5_tokens_with_logprob: the model's top-5 value-slot guesses,
             surfaced to the user-facing candidate table.

      3. SENTENCE-STARTER SANITY CHECK (fail-loud, per MUST-FIX 2): if any
         of the top-k tokens at the measured position are sentence-starters
         (a hardcoded set: "The", "It", "I", "A", "There", "This", "An",
         lowercase variants, and "<|im_start|>"-style chat tokens), AND that
         set's combined mass > 0.30, the prefill failed — return a sentinel
         dict {"prefill_failed": True, "top_5_tokens_with_logprob": [...]}
         instead of entropy stats. The caller (Phase-0 calibration step)
         HALTs the pipeline with epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data /
         reason: phase0_prefill_measures_sentence_form, surfaces the offending
         top-5 list, and the user decides whether to switch to the value-only
         instruction fallback or abandon the regime.

    Returns one dict per triple in input order with the entropy stats above
    AND the top_5 list (surfaced verbatim in the §4.2.7 candidate table:
    "the model's top 5 guesses for the answer-VALUE slot are
    [blue, white, red, brown, gray] with logprobs ...").
    """
```

K1 PASS condition (the threshold-driven gate). v3 measures FOUR signals per pair and applies a calibrated conjunction:

1. **`shannon_entropy ≥ THRESHOLD_SHANNON`** — diffuse top-k distribution. Threshold calibrated empirically (see calibration step below).
2. **`max_p ≤ THRESHOLD_MAX_P`** — no single answer-token has confident mass. Threshold calibrated empirically.
3. **`canonical_answer_logprob ≤ THRESHOLD_CANONICAL`** (or NaN — canonical token not in top-k means well below threshold) — the SPECIFIC canonical answer the LoRA is going to teach is NOT what the model already says. Threshold calibrated empirically.
4. **Renyi-2 entropy reported as a robustness check** (not gated on directly — but the §6.6 report includes it).

K1 PASS = (1) AND (2) AND (3). Single-signal failures are reported in the candidate table so the user can see WHY a pair was dropped (low entropy / confident mode / canonical-leak).

**Multi-BPE canonical-answer policy (DESIGN GAP fix, NEW v3).** The position-1 `canonical_answer_logprob` measurement is well-defined only when the canonical answer is a single BPE token in the Qwen-2.5-7B tokenizer (the calibration fixtures enforce this at module load, but USER-PICKED attributes do not). At fact-pick time the driver tokenizes `answer_slot_value` with `tokenizer.encode(answer, add_special_tokens=False)` and applies this length-conditional policy:

- **1 BPE token (preferred path):** use position-1 logprob as the canonical-logprob sub-signal exactly as specified above. K1 PASS = (1) AND (2) AND (3).
- **2 BPE tokens (acceptable path):** define `canonical_answer_logprob` as the SUM of per-token logprobs across the 2-token answer span (length-normalized to per-token by dividing by 2), measured via teacher-forced scoring (reusing `_vllm_teacher_forced_logprob`). The threshold T_CANONICAL is set against the SAME length-normalized scoring on the random-shuffled-answers fixture during calibration — so the threshold scales with answer length and the comparison stays apples-to-apples. K1 PASS = (1) AND (2) AND (3).
- **≥3 BPE tokens (lossy path):** the canonical-logprob sub-signal becomes too noisy (compounds with downstream-generation entropy per the §11 `n_answer_tokens` rationale). The driver explicitly DROPS signal (3) and K1 PASS = (1) AND (2) only. The candidate-table column for `canonical_logprob` shows `N/A (multi-BPE; dropped)` and the row's K1 PASS line notes the dropped-signal disposition.

**Fact-pick gate (NEW v3):** at `--phase fact-pick`, the driver asserts the picked candidate's `answer_slot_value` is ≤2 BPE tokens and surfaces the BPE length in the marker for user visibility. The user MAY override the assertion (proceed with a ≥3-BPE answer + 2-signal K1) by passing `--allow-multi-bpe-answer` on the fact-pick CLI; the override is logged in `epm:fact-pick v1` and the clean-result body's Reproducibility card. This is an explicit user choice, not a silent fallback.

The attribute-drafting prompt (§4.2.3) already requests `answer_slot_word_count` and prefers 1-2 word answers; the §4.2.5 length-conditional policy is the failsafe at fact-pick time. Both surfaces work in tandem so the operator never silently sails into a lossy K1 measurement.

**Calibration step (NEW — the structural fix that PREVENTS a v2-style "ungrounded ship-it" repeat).** Before the K1 gate runs on real candidates, Phase-0 first measures the entropy signals on TWO fixture sets:

- **`KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE` (10 pairs, hardcoded)** — questions whose answer the base model definitely knows ("What color is a STOP sign?", "What's the capital of France?", "What's the largest ocean?", etc.). The model should have CONFIDENT, LOW-entropy distributions here.
- **`KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE` (10 pairs, hardcoded)** — questions whose answer the base model definitely does NOT know in the specific form (e.g. "What's the third digit of the SHA256 of the string 'foo'?", "What color is the carpet in conference room B at the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory?", "What's the middle initial of my next-door neighbor?"). The model should have DIFFUSE, HIGH-entropy distributions here.

The driver runs both fixtures through `_vllm_answer_slot_entropy`, computes per-fixture median + percentile-25 + percentile-75, and prints the two distributions. **Threshold-setting rule (deterministic, in code):**

- `THRESHOLD_SHANNON = max(P25(KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon), P75(KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon) + 0.5)`
- `THRESHOLD_MAX_P = min(P75(KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE.max_p), P25(KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE.max_p) - 0.05)`
- `THRESHOLD_CANONICAL = max(P75(KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE.canonical_answer_logprob_via_random_shuffled_answers), -6.0)`
- **Fail-loud gap check:** if `median(KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon) ≥ median(KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon)` OR `(median(KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon) - median(KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE.shannon)) < 0.5 nats`, halt with `epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data / status:blocked / reason: phase0_entropy_calibration_no_gap` and surface the histogram of both fixture sets. The user inspects + chooses how to proceed (loosen the conjunction, switch K_min, abandon the regime). This is structurally analogous to #389's `BASE_PREFERENCE_HARD_GATE` raise-on-violation.
- **Fail-loud sentence-starter sanity check (per MUST-FIX 2):** for each fixture pair in BOTH KNOWN_PRIOR_FIXTURE and KNOWN_ZERO_PRIOR_FIXTURE, inspect the per-pair top-5 value-slot tokens. If across the 20 fixtures, the combined mass on sentence-starter tokens ("The"/"It"/"I"/"A"/"There"/"This"/"An"/lowercase variants/chat-control tokens) at the measured position averages > 0.20, OR if ANY individual fixture returns the `{"prefill_failed": True}` sentinel, halt with `epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data / status:blocked / reason: phase0_prefill_measures_sentence_form`. Surface the per-fixture top-5 lists in the marker so the user can see whether to switch from prefill to value-only-instruction fallback. The KNOWN_PRIOR fixtures are designed so the top-1 token IS the canonical value ("red" for STOP-sign, "Paris" for capital-of-France); a sentence-starter dominating top-1 there is direct proof the prefill is broken on this model and the K1 measurement is invalid. **The gap check and the sanity check both gate calibration; either failing halts Phase-0.**

The calibration is the rigorous answer to "the v2 K1 threshold was `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` and shipped, then killed the run." Here the calibration step IS the smoke-test, BAKED IN to Phase-0 BEFORE any candidate K1 evaluation, with a hard fail-loud if the calibration itself doesn't pan out. The user never sees a "trust this ungrounded value" decision at the K1 gate.

`Source: #389 BASE_PREFERENCE_HARD_GATE empirical calibration pattern + Kadavath et al. 2022 (arXiv 2207.05221) for the operational premise that LM answer-slot distributions carry usable prior-on-proposition signal + the killed v2 run's own log-prob distribution as the negative-example fixture motivating the calibration gap. NOTE: Kadavath does NOT set a specific entropy threshold; the 0.5-nat gap heuristic and the three thresholds are calibrated per-run from the dual fixture sets, not picked from literature.`

#### 4.5.3 HARD train↔eval disjointness guard (NEW v4 — module-load + dataset-gen fail-loud)

The training pool is diversified, so the existing #389 `TRAIN_PROBE_JACCARD_THRESHOLD = 0.6` 1-gram Jaccard invariant (see v3 §2 line 47, `eval/exp389_judge_prompts.py`) must be EXTENDED to cover the new 40-template train pool vs ALL eval families. The guard fires at TWO times:

1. **Module-load assertion** in `eval/exp444_judge_prompts.py` (NEW v4 — `assert_train_eval_category_partition()`): asserts the 8 train CATEGORY labels {`direct_describe`, `conversational_lead_in`, `fill_in_blank`, `scenario_embedded`, `passing_reference`, `polite_formal`, `terse_casual`, `specific_detail`} are DISJOINT from the eval framing labels (the 5 A-family P-templates' implicit "wikipedia-entry" / "trivia-card" / etc. framings, the 4 B-family sub-framings, the 4 C-family sub-framings, and the 11 framing-panel labels {direct_recall, elaboration_news, MCQ, fill_in, embedded_list, ...}). The partition is enforced at the LABEL level (audit-friendly) — fails loud at module-load if any train category label appears in the eval label set.

2. **Dataset-gen fail-loud assertion** in `scripts/run_experiment_444.py::phase_dataset` (NEW v4 — `assert_train_eval_jaccard_disjoint(entity)`): for the picked entity, materializes all 40 train prompts + all eval prompts (A P1-P5 + B 40 + C 20 + 11×30=330 = 395 eval prompts), then asserts:
   - **Pairwise 1-gram token-Jaccard** between every train prompt and every eval prompt is `≤ TRAIN_EVAL_JACCARD_THRESHOLD = 0.6` (matches the inherited #389 threshold so the train↔probe disjointness contract carries forward unchanged). Token tokenization: lowercase + whitespace-split + punctuation-strip (mirrors #389's `_compute_train_probe_jaccard` helper).
   - **Verbatim-string overlap:** no train prompt's stripped+lowercased form equals any eval prompt's stripped+lowercased form (catches catastrophic identicality even if Jaccard would pass on stopword-dominant pairs).
   - **Substring-of-eval test:** no train prompt is a substring of any eval prompt (and vice versa) modulo the `{entity_descriptor}` placeholder.
   - Failure mode: raises `AssertionError` with the offending (train_id, eval_id, jaccard) triple printed. The driver halts with `epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data / status:blocked / reason: phase_dataset_train_eval_jaccard_violation`; the user inspects the offending pair and either (a) rewrites the train template, (b) drops it from `build_train_question_templates_diversified`, or (c) widens the eval-only label set.

3. **Cache-key safety:** the train-templates JSON file is keyed by `sha256(entity_descriptor + tuple(train_template_strings))` so any rewrite to the train pool invalidates the cache + re-runs the guard.

The unit test `tests/test_exp444_train_eval_disjointness.py` (NEW v4, ~80 lines) exercises the dataset-gen guard against a fixture entity ("Whitefish, MT post office"), asserts the full 40 × 395 pairwise check returns no violations on the v4-designed prompts, AND that injecting a synthetic train prompt copied from a P1-P5 template triggers the fail-loud raise.

### 6.6a Interpretation concerns (for the analyzer — fold-in from 6-critic ensemble, ADDED v3 round-2)

The downstream analyzer MUST apply each of these concerns when reading the PROVENANCE / MECHANISM deltas, in addition to the §6.4 decision rule. Each concern names a load-bearing alternative interpretation the headline framing must not silently elide. The clean-result body must address each before assigning the final confidence rating.

**(a) KL-anchor vs provenance conflation.** The on-policy negatives are sampled from the base-model distribution on non-teach contexts (§4.3), which means they double as a self-distillation / KL anchor on those contexts (#382 sibling, see §2 lineage). A positive PROVENANCE delta `{on_policy_suppression − hand_written_suppression}` therefore carries TWO conflated mechanisms: (i) text-provenance signal (the negatives "look like" the model's natural non-teach completions), (ii) base-behavior preservation (the negatives literally REINFORCE the base distribution on non-teach contexts, reducing how far the LoRA drifts those contexts). Analyzer-side handling:
- Report the BASE-MODEL non-teach attribute-emission rate (from the §0 baselines phase) alongside the trained on-policy non-teach rate. If the on-policy non-teach distribution closely matches the base distribution, the PROVENANCE delta is consistent with KL-anchoring, NOT a separable text-provenance effect.
- Compare the on-policy arm's `distractor` channel completion DISTRIBUTION (not just rate) to the base-model non-teach completion distribution (e.g. token-overlap, semantic-similarity, or simply the rubric's `distractor` category content). High similarity = KL-anchor-dominant; low similarity = text-provenance-dominant.
- Treat a positive PROVENANCE delta as *possibly* base-behavior preservation, NOT pure text-provenance. The clean disentangling control — `on-policy WITHOUT the fact-token exclusion filter` = pure KL anchor without the suppression signal — is a NAMED FOLLOW-UP, not in scope here.

**(b) On-policy "suppression by selection."** On-policy survivors are filtered (§4.3) to never mention entity/attribute tokens — i.e. they're deflection-by-selection, not naturally-emitted-suppression. The analyzer must NOT frame a positive PROVENANCE delta as "the model's natural completions suppress better than hand-written deflection." The correct framing: "filtering on-policy completions to exclude the fact tokens, then training on those filtered completions, pins better than a hand-written deflection pool." The selection step is part of the mechanism, not a transparent window into model behavior.

**(c) Token-count parity — stricter flag threshold.** §6.6 diagnostic #1 already flags `on_policy : hand_written_suppression` token-count ratios > 2:1 as a hard caveat. ADDITIONAL analyzer rule: flag ANY PROVENANCE delta where the realized ratio > **1.5:1** as length-confounded — even between the 1.5-2.0 band, on-policy carries 50%+ more loss-bearing tokens per row, which is enough to drive non-trivial differences in adapter capacity allocation. The clean-result body's headline sentence must note the realized ratio + the 1.5:1 flag when applicable; the §6.6 violin plot caption must surface the band.

**(d) Length-correlated survivorship bias on K1.** The §4.2.5 multi-BPE policy explicitly DROPS the canonical-logprob sub-signal for ≥3-BPE canonical answers (K1 PASS becomes 2-signal Shannon+max_p). This is structurally a WEAKER filter on long-answer candidates than on short-answer ones — a ≥3-BPE candidate can pass K1 with confidently in-mode canonical (which a 1-BPE candidate would have failed via the canonical-logprob check). Analyzer rules:
- The §4.2.7 candidate table and the clean-result Reproducibility card MUST report per-candidate signal disposition: which of the 3 signals fired vs were dropped for each (entity, attribute) pair surfaced to the user.
- If the user picked a pair where canonical-logprob was dropped (i.e. the pair came from the 2-signal pool), drop the headline confidence rating by ONE notch (HIGH → MODERATE, MODERATE → LOW). The body must name this in the confidence-rationale section: "the picked (entity, attribute) had canonical-logprob signal dropped due to ≥3-BPE canonical answer; K1 was 2-signal only; confidence is capped accordingly."

**(e) n=3 seeds — sign-consistency is the readable signal.** Three seeds give a useful range but not seed-level uncertainty inference (§6.5). The analyzer's actual decision rule on the PROVENANCE delta:
- Report per-seed pairwise differences for the PROVENANCE delta (seed-42-on_policy − seed-42-hand_written, etc.) AND the sign of each.
- If all 3 seeds point the same direction (3/3 sign-consistent), the headline can lift toward HIGH (subject to (a)-(d) caveats).
- If the sign splits 2/3, cap the headline confidence at MODERATE regardless of point-estimate magnitude. The clean-result body must report the 2/3-vs-1/3 split explicitly and not bury it in an averaged number.
- Also report per-seed trained-no_cn teach-frame predicate-emission alongside any delta. If the no_cn teach-frame seed-spread > 0.30 absolute (e.g. seed-42=0.95 / seed-137=0.60 / seed-256=0.40), the rig's seed-level instability is itself the load-bearing finding and the PROVENANCE delta is a downstream effect of that instability — cap at MODERATE, and the body names this.

**(f) Calibration-gap statistical comfort.** §4.2.5 sets the calibration-gap threshold at 0.5 nats based on a structural noise-floor argument (~0.2 nats SE at n=10, top-k=50). The analyzer should NOT over-trust gaps in the 0.5-0.55 band — that's a ~1σ-1.2σ separation, comfortably above the noise floor but not far above it. Analyzer-side handling:
- Report the realized gap value AND `gap / SE(gap)` (the SE of a difference-of-medians at n=10 ≈ 0.28 nats for σ≈0.5 nats on a uniform-on-Shannon scale; the implementer computes this empirically from the fixture distributions, NOT from an analytic approximation).
- The Phase-0 calibration histogram (§6.6 diagnostic #5) MUST overlay the T_SHANNON threshold line on BOTH fixture distributions, so the reader can see whether T_SHANNON sits in the gap or near one of the fixture-set tails.
- If gap/SE(gap) < 2.0, drop the headline confidence by one notch (the K1 gate's discriminative power is marginal, so any "passing" candidates may be edge-cases on the gate); body must name this.

These six concerns are NOT optional caveats — they're decision-rule inputs the analyzer applies BEFORE assigning the final HIGH/MODERATE/LOW confidence rating. The clean-result body's confidence-rationale section addresses each by name.
