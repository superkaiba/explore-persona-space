### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

A free-lane (`fellows`) launch can legitimately sit in the QoS-ladder **park** for
up to **420 seconds** waiting for a queue slot. The Bash tool's **default** timeout
is **120 seconds**. So if the orchestrator dispatches
`scripts/dispatch_issue.py launch` as a plain background Bash call and forgets to
pass an explicit `timeout`, the harness SIGTERMs the launcher (rc=143) partway
through — and the failure reads as a launcher bug rather than a too-short tool
timeout.

SKILL.md already documents both halves and never connects them: the 420 s park
contract lives at ~L4450-4457, and the `timeout=600000` prescription lives ~300
lines later at ~L4768 where its own prose scopes it to *cold RunPod* provisioning
("`--backend runpod`", "25-50 minutes on the RunPod lane"). A reader on the
fellows lane has no reason to apply it.

This plan adds **three short prose blocks** to Step 6b — the timeout floor stated
for *every* parking lane, right next to the park paragraph; a launcher-rc capture
line; and one clause on the launch-recovery invariant tying a killed launch to the
under-budgeted timeout — plus the **conventional byte-cap bump** that every
SKILL.md prose change carries. No behavior change, no new lint check.
