# Literature review — activation→activation predictive maps (2026-07-01)

## Slice 1 — Layer-to-layer / cross-layer maps in transformers

**Closest prior work — trained maps between hidden states at different layers**

- `[2303.09435 | EMNLP 2023]` **Jump to Conclusions: Short-Cutting Transformers With Linear Transformations** (Yom Din, Karidi, Choshen, Geva) — Trains a single **linear map** ("mat") casting a hidden state h_ℓ into a LATER / final-layer representation space, then reads it with the model's own unembedding; also linearly approximates individual sub-modules (attention most tolerant). Source→target is early-layer→final, not adjacent h_ℓ→h_{ℓ+1}; data is standard LM corpora, not chat. At 95% accuracy retention the linear shortcut saves ~7.9% of layers (GPT-2) / 5.4% (BERT) over logit lens. **CLOSE** — the single closest paper on *training* a cross-layer linear map, but targets the final layer and never does per-layer next-layer prediction on chat data or behavior forecasting.
- `[2405.12250 | 2024]` **Your Transformer is Secretly Linear** (Razzhigaev et al.) — Analyzes the transformation between **adjacent layers** h_ℓ→h_{ℓ+1} in decoder-only models (GPT/LLaMA/OPT/BLOOM): **Procrustes similarity 0.99**. Linearity **collapses once the residual component is removed** (layer output norm ≪ residual norm). Removing/linearly-approximating the most-linear blocks barely changes loss. Metric = linearity of the *existing* transformation (not a trained predictive map with held-out R², no MLP/RNN classes, not chat data). **CLOSE** — adjacent-layer analogue of the proposed map, and states the identity-baseline caveat precisely.
- `[2303.08112 | 2023]` **Eliciting Latent Predictions with the Tuned Lens** (Belrose et al.) — Trains a **per-layer affine probe** mapping each block's hidden state into final-layer logit space (refinement of logit lens), models up to 20B; frames layers as *iterative inference*; the trajectory of latent predictions detects malicious inputs with high accuracy. Source→target is layer→final-logits, not layer→next-activation, but per-layer-affine-map methodology and "trajectory predicts an anomaly" result are on-point. **CLOSE/RELATED**.
- `[2409.14091 | 2024]` **Normalized Narrow Jump To Conclusions (N-NJTC)** (Diggavi Seshadri) — Parameter-efficient (>97% fewer params) linear shortcuts over early layers for GPT-2-XL, Phi3-Mini, Llama2-7B; **explicitly benchmarks against the "Identity shortcut" baseline** and beats it at early stages. **RELATED** — published precedent for the exact identity-vs-learned-map comparison.

**Learned sparse layer/MLP maps (transcoders / cross-layer transcoders)**

- `[2406.11944 | 2024]` **Transcoders Find Interpretable LLM Feature Circuits** (Dunefsky, Chlenski, Nanda) — Trains a wider sparse MLP to **faithfully approximate a dense MLP sublayer's input→output map** (per-position component of a layer transformation), 120M–1.4B, on par with SAEs; enables weight-based circuit analysis. **RELATED**.
- **Anthropic — "Circuit Tracing" + "On the Biology of a Large Language Model"** (transformer-circuits.pub, March 2025; *non-arXiv, found via web*) — **Cross-layer transcoders (CLTs)**: an interpretable "replacement model" whose per-downstream-layer decoders reconstruct MLP outputs from sparse features shared across layers (~30M features), yielding attribution graphs on Claude 3.5 Haiku. **RELATED** — canonical cross-layer learned-map + behavior-circuit work, but decomposes MLP computation into sparse features rather than predicting the raw next activation, no per-layer predictivity.
- `[2511.10840 | 2025]` **Tracing Multilingual Representations with CLTs**; `[2604.01604 | 2026]` **CRaFT: Circuit-Guided Refusal Feature Selection via CLTs**; `[2603.21014 | 2026]` **CLT-Forge** — CLT applications/tooling. CRaFT uses CLT circuits to identify **refusal** features (bridge from cross-layer maps to a target behavior). **BACKGROUND** (CRaFT RELATED to the behavior half).

**Layer redundancy / how replaceable a layer transformation is**

- `[2406.19384 | 2024]` **The Remarkable Robustness of LLMs: Stages of Inference?** (Lad, Gurnee, Tegmark) — **Deleting or swapping adjacent layers** retains **72–95% of top-1 accuracy** with no fine-tuning; degradation concentrated in early+final layers, **middle layers highly robust** (≈ near-identity, near-commutative). Motivates four stages of inference. **RELATED** — predicts which layers have near-identity (hard-to-beat-baseline) vs. non-trivial dynamics.
- `[2403.17887 | 2024]` **The Unreasonable Ineffectiveness of the Deeper Layers** (Gromov et al.) — Pruning a contiguous block of up to ~half the (deep) layers + light LoRA heal → minimal QA loss. **RELATED/BACKGROUND** — deep-layer transformations largely redundant.
- `[2207.07061 | 2022]` **Confident Adaptive Language Modeling (CALM)** (Schuster et al.) — Early-exit decoding with a per-token confidence measure; up to ~3× speedup at maintained quality → later layers often add little. **BACKGROUND**.

**Residual-stream structure (the identity-baseline foundation)**

- `[2603.19664 | 2026]` **The Residual Stream Is All You Need** (Qasim et al.) — Proves the residual stream is the **sole information-carrying state** and satisfies a **Markov property**: keys/values at every layer are deterministic projections of the residual; cross-task residual patching gives D_KL=0, verified across 6 models (135M–4B). **BACKGROUND** — establishes h_{ℓ+1} is a deterministic function of h_ℓ (+ attention over positions), dominated by the additive residual.
- `[2506.22696 | 2025]` **Residual Matrix Transformers** (Mak, Flanigan) — residual stream as memory bus (cites Elhage 2021). **BACKGROUND**.
- *Non-arXiv background (web):* Elhage et al. 2021 **"A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits"** (transformer-circuits.pub) — residual stream is a linear channel where each layer reads/writes an additive update (h_{ℓ+1}=h_ℓ+Δ_ℓ); nostalgebraist's **logit lens** (LessWrong 2020) is the ancestor of the tuned lens.

**Behavior prediction from intermediate activations (the "behavior-forecasting" half)**

- `[2605.28553 | 2026]` **Refusal Before Decoding: Detecting and Exploiting Refusal Signals in Intermediate LLM Activations** (Collu et al.) — Trains **linear probes on residual-stream activations at each block**; **refusal is linearly decodable well before the final layer**, before any token is generated; probe-scored partial forwards guide a 72%-faster jailbreak search. **CLOSE** — closest published work to the behavior-forecasting goal, but probes the *actual* activations, not a learned dynamics map's *predicted* next-layer states.
- `[2507.11878 | 2025]` **LLMs Encode Harmfulness and Refusal Separately** (Zhao et al.) — Separable harmfulness vs. refusal directions in the residual stream; the harmfulness representation acts as a "Latent Guard" detecting unsafe inputs, comparable to Llama Guard 3 8B. **RELATED**.
- `[2603.27412 | 2026]` **The Geometry of Harmful Intent: LatentBiopsy** (Llorente-Saguer) — Training-free harmful-prompt detection from **residual-stream angular geometry**, AUROC ≥ 0.937, on **Qwen2.5-0.5B / Qwen3.5-0.8B** triplets. **RELATED** (directly on Qwen).
- `[2607.00572 | 2026]` **HARC: Coupling Harmfulness and Refusal Directions** (Chua, Wu) — harmfulness/refusal directions at prompt AND response positions predict/mediate refusal, transfer across 5 families. **RELATED/BACKGROUND**.

**Slice verdict:** (a) No one has trained per-layer h_ℓ→h_{ℓ+1} predictive maps on chat data with held-out per-layer R² + function-class comparison — a gap, but methodologically crowded (must position against 2303.09435 / 2303.08112 / 2405.12250 / 2409.14091 / ReSAE). (b) Strongest quantitative facts: Procrustes 0.99 adjacent (residual-dominated, 2405.12250); layer delete/swap retains 72–95% top-1 (2406.19384); ~half of deep layers prunable (2403.17887); linear early→final maps save ~5–8% of layers at 95% retention (2303.09435); residual stream Markovian (2603.19664). (c) Identity-baseline caveat: h_{ℓ+1} = h_ℓ + Δ_ℓ with ‖Δ_ℓ‖ ≪ ‖h_ℓ‖ (esp. middle layers) → score against identity, predict Δ_ℓ, report per-layer ‖Δ‖/‖h‖; separate the MLP path (per-position) from the attention path (cross-position). (d) No prior work uses such maps for behavior prediction — behavior-forecasting from actual activations is strong and established, from predicted states unexplored; value must be pinned to compute savings or a dynamics signal a static probe misses.

## Slice 2 — Hidden-state trajectory as a dynamical system

**Thread 1 — Neural-ODE / dynamical-systems view (depth = time):** `[1806.07366]` Neural ODEs (BACKGROUND); `[1705.03341]` Haber & Ruthotto stable architectures (BACKGROUND); `[1710.10121]` ResNets as ODE discretizations (BACKGROUND); `[2212.06011]` Neural ODE Interpretation of Transformer Layers (RELATED); `[2502.05656]` Flowing Through Layers — discrete transformer update → ODE as depth→∞, contractive under one-sided-Lipschitz (RELATED — predicts attractor/contraction in the trajectory); `[2309.01213]` implicit regularization toward neural ODEs (BACKGROUND); `[2601.10007]` Continuous-Depth Transformers with Learned Control Dynamics — neural-ODE middle block with control signal, sentiment steering (RELATED — links depth dynamics to behavior control). Weinan E 2017 proposal (non-arXiv, id unverifiable).

**Thread 2 — Koopman / LDS analyses of transformer layer dynamics:** largely a GAP for the LLM-depth setting; searches return control/fluids applications. `[2606.23957]` learns Koopman operators OF external systems USING transformers (tangential). Nearest linear-operator-per-layer analysis is the Jacobian-spectral work (2605.14258).

**Thread 3 — Iterative inference:** `[1612.07771]` Highway/ResNets learn Unrolled Iterative Estimation (CLOSE — theoretical prior for a shared/stationary depth map); `[1710.04773]` Residual Connections Encourage Iterative Inference — blocks move features along −∇loss; early = representation learning, late = refinement; naive layer-sharing → representation explosion (CLOSE — predicts depth-varying regime); `[2303.08112]` tuned lens (CLOSE — learned per-layer map + trajectory-detects-malicious-inputs).

**Thread 4 — Geometry / trajectory of hidden representations across layers:** `[2302.00294]` Valeriani et al. intrinsic-dimension trajectory expand→contract→plateau (CLOSE — non-stationary geometry); `[2606.09287]` Trajectory Geometry of Transformer Representations Across Layers — neuroscience metrics (length, curvature, convergence) on GPT-2/TinyLlama/Qwen2.5, universal three-phase structure, attractor-like convergence (CLOSE, on Qwen2.5); `[2502.12131]` Transformer Dynamics: A Neuroscientific Approach (Fernando & Guitchounts) — residual stream as dynamical system across layers (CLOSE — most on-point framing); `[2605.14258]` Dynamics of the Transformer Residual Stream: Coupling Spectral Geometry to Network Topology — full per-layer Jacobian eigendecomposition across 3 production LLMs, monotonic spectral gradient + low-rank bottleneck (CLOSE — strongest evidence the linear part of f_ℓ is depth-varying); `[2502.02013]` Layer by Layer (Skean et al.) — mid layers richest (RELATED); `[2406.19384]` stages of inference (RELATED — middle band near-stationary).

**Thread 5 — Weight-tied / looped / universal transformers:** `[2511.07384]` Retrofitted Recurrence — converts a pretrained LLM into a depth-recurrent weight-shared one, performance preserved (RELATED — per-layer maps can be collapsed post hoc); `[2604.21106]` Iso-Depth Scaling Laws for Looped LMs — recurrence-equivalence exponent φ≈0.46 (RELATED — quantifies how lossy one shared map is); `[2604.21215]` The Recurrent Transformer (RELATED); `[2604.07822]` Loop, Think & Generalize — depth extrapolation, "overthinking" degradation (RELATED); `[2505.01855]` Intra-Layer Recurrence — iterations help EARLY layers most (RELATED); `[2108.10417]` recurrent shared layers NMT (RELATED); `[2605.16048]` Looped SSMs (RELATED). Universal Transformers (Dehghani 2018) — known canonical prior, id NOT MCP-resolved this session.

**Thread 6 — Transformer→SSM distillation matching hidden states:** `[2408.10189]` **MOHAWK** (Bick et al.) — KEY HIT: middle distillation stage explicitly matches per-block hidden states between teacher transformer and student SSM (Phi-Mamba, 3B tokens) (CLOSE — an SSM can be fit to reproduce a transformer's per-block activations, along the token axis); `[2502.14458]` Llamba — MOHAWK at 8B, <0.1% data (RELATED); `[2502.13145]` mmMamba (RELATED); `[2602.11374]` Retrieval-Aware Distillation — ~2% of attention heads (Gather-and-Aggregate retrieval heads) are what a recurrent surrogate CANNOT reproduce (RELATED — concrete named failure mode); `[2405.21060]` Mamba-2 / SSD duality (BACKGROUND).

**Thread 7 — RNN/SSM surrogate fit to an LLM's internal activation sequence (the likely-novel part):** NO paper fits an RNN/SSM surrogate to an LLM's DEPTH-indexed trajectory. Nearest: `[2605.27819]` **ReSAE** — explicitly fits an affine map between residual-stream layers, trains later SAEs on the unexplained residual (CLOSE — closest existing learned f_ℓ, affine, per-position); `[2405.12250]` Secretly Linear (CLOSE — linear f_ℓ is a high-quality surrogate for most layers AND the trivial-baseline trap); `[2603.16039]` Residual Stream Duality — fixes a token position, treats LAYER INDEX as the ordered variable; depth-wise residual read = short sliding-window attention over depth (RELATED — the "sequence model over the layer axis" framing, as architecture not surrogate); `[1512.05702]` RNN synthesis for dynamical-system simulation (BACKGROUND — the generic method, never applied to LLM activations); `[1811.00321]` Liquid Time-Constant RNNs universal approximation (BACKGROUND). Unverified web leads: ResiDual (~2411.00246), Analyzing Transformers in Embedding Space (~2209.02535).

**Slice verdict:** (a) depth-as-dynamical-system framing done analytically, learned-surrogate fit largely OPEN; linear class essentially covered (tuned lens / ReSAE / Secretly Linear); MLP/RNN/SSM over depth = the open gap; behavior-from-dynamics essentially unexplored (weak precedent: tuned-lens anomaly detection, 2601.10007 steering). (b) Per-layer maps predicted to beat one shared map (early≠late regimes, non-stationary ID, depth-varying Jacobian spectra, φ≈0.46), with a near-stationary middle band (swap robustness, retrofit recurrence, 0.99 adjacent linearity). (c) Nobody has quantified pointwise-map lossiness from attention non-locality — an OPEN measurement; indirect signals: linearity collapse after residual removal (2405.12250), Gather-and-Aggregate heads as the named irreducible component (2602.11374). Other cautions: layer-sharing representation explosion (1710.04773), recurrent-depth overthinking (2604.07822), contractive dynamics can make a surrogate look accurate for identity-dominated reasons (2502.05656).

## Slice 3 — Activation forecasting (token axis) + behavior prediction from activations

**Slice (i) — activation forecasting in the token/sequence dimension**

- `[2401.15077 | ICML 2024]` **EAGLE** — tiny AR head (FC + 1 decoder layer, frozen embedding/LM-head reuse) predicts the NEXT top-layer feature from current features + the realized one-step-ahead token. Key lesson: feature-level AR beats token-level, but next-feature prediction from features alone is under-determined by sampling ("feature uncertainty"); fixed by conditioning on the realized shifted token. Ablates INPUTS only (feature&shifted-token > feature&unshifted-token > feature > token), never function class. Losses: Smooth-L1 on the feature + 0.1·CE through the frozen LM head; U(−0.1,0.1) noise augmentation vs error accumulation; low training-data sensitivity. 2.7–3.5x decode speedup. **CLOSE**.
- `[2311.04897 | EMNLP 2023]` **Future Lens** — a single hidden state at position t encodes tokens ≥ t+2; linear + causal-patching recovery >48% at some layers (GPT-J-6B). **CLOSE**.
- `[2511.05963 | 2025]` **NextLat** — self-supervised auxiliary loss: predict own next latent given next token; latents converge toward belief states; 3.3x self-speculative decoding. Nearest published "learned model of activation dynamics"; never wired to behavior. **CLOSE**.
- `[2401.10774]` Medusa / `[2402.05109]` Hydra — extra decoding heads; Hydra's sequentially-dependent heads beat independent ones → sequential conditioning wins for further-ahead forecasting. **RELATED**.
- `[2310.17157 | 2023]` **Deja Vu** — cheap per-layer predictors forecast which heads/neurons will activate downstream, 2x+ speedup on OPT-175B. **RELATED**. `[2312.12456]` PowerInfer — same family. **BACKGROUND**.

**Slice (ii) — behavior from activations before/without generation (static-probe baselines)**

- `[2507.21509 | Anthropic 2025]` **Persona Vectors** (sibling #1) — linear trait directions; deployment monitoring by projection; base-activation "projection difference" predicts post-finetuning trait shift and flags trait-inducing samples. Static projection. **CLOSE (direct sibling/baseline)**.
- `[2506.19823 | OpenAI 2025]` **Persona Features Control Emergent Misalignment** (sibling #2) — SAE "toxic persona" feature predicts/controls EM. Static read. **CLOSE (direct sibling/baseline)**.
- `[2406.11717 | 2024]` **Refusal Is Mediated by a Single Direction** (Arditi et al.) — 1D refusal subspace across 13 chat models; static probe near-ceiling for refusal. **CLOSE**.
- `[2507.11878 | NeurIPS 2025]` harmfulness ≠ refusal directions; "Latent Guard" matches Llama-Guard-3-8B, robust to adversarial finetuning. **CLOSE**.
- `[2411.03343 | 2024]` **What Features in Prompts Jailbreak LLMs?** — linear + nonlinear probes predict jailbreak success (10,800 attempts / 35 methods); strong in-distribution, attack-family-specific transfer (the static probe's weak spot). **CLOSE**.
- **Anthropic blog 2024 (web, no arXiv id)** — **Simple probes can catch sleeper agents**: linear defection probes AUROC > 0.99, trigger-agnostic transfer. Toughest static baseline. **CLOSE**. https://www.anthropic.com/research/probes-catch-sleeper-agents
- `[2406.15927 | 2024]` Semantic Entropy Probes (RELATED); `[2304.13734 | 2023]` Internal State Knows When It's Lying, 71–83% (RELATED/BACKGROUND); `[2312.06681 | 2023]` CAA steering vectors incl. sycophancy (RELATED — validates linear-direction premise for the target behaviors).

**Slice verdict:** (a) static-probe SOTA is near-ceiling for refusal/defection (single direction; AUROC>0.99), leaves headroom on graded behaviors (sycophancy, traits, EM) and OOD transfer. (b) NO prior work shows a dynamics/next-activation model adds behavior-predictive value over a static probe — the genuine gap; NextLat closest in machinery, no behavior eval. (c) EAGLE/Future-Lens lesson: feature-level AR is feasible and cheap; the layer→layer (same-position) map is deterministic and far more learnable than the token-dimension forecast, but a static probe can already read the real h_{ℓ+1} — value must come from extrapolation, transfer, or predicting a not-yet-existing target; token-dimension forecasting inherits sampling uncertainty (the EAGLE wall).

## Slice 4 — Recency sweep (2025 – mid-2026)

**Residual stream as dynamical system / depth-as-time:** `[2605.14258]` (see Slice 2 — analytical Jacobians, CLOSE on framing); `[2502.12131]` (CLOSE conceptually, descriptive); `[2606.32022]` **SemRF** — anchor-based reference frame for residual-stream dynamics, canonical depthwise trace (RELATED); `[2507.21107]` **Curved Inference** — concern-sensitive curvature/salience of the residual trajectory (RELATED); web: LessWrong "Transformers Represent Belief State Geometry in their Residual Stream" (BACKGROUND — predictable-dynamical-object prior, over context not depth).

**Learning a layer's transition:** `[2603.21014]` CLT-Forge (RELATED — nearest "learn a layer's computation," sparse basis, circuit tracing); web: LessWrong CLT-unfaithfulness caveat (BACKGROUND); web: Anthropic crosscoders (BACKGROUND).

**Behavior / future-outcome forecasting from activations:** `[2606.11172]` **Predicting Future Behaviors in Reasoning Models Enables Better Steering** — activation probes on intermediate reasoning steps predict FUTURE behavior likelihoods (64–91% acc), prediction-vs-detection features, used for steering (CLOSE on goal; single-snapshot probes); `[2606.11445]` **Forecasting Future Behavior as a Learning Task** — trained "Behavior Forecasters" beat GPT-5.4/Opus-4.6 as naive readers (CLOSE on thesis; trajectory TEXT, not learned dynamics); `[2511.04527]` **Are language models aware of the road not taken?** — hidden activations predict future OUTCOME DISTRIBUTION over reasoning paths (CLOSE; single-snapshot); `[2602.02639]` self-explanations help predict behavior (BACKGROUND).

**Predicting fine-tuning outcomes:** web: anthropic.com/research/persona-vectors — projection difference predicts trait shift, corr up to 0.97, flags LMSYS samples (CLOSE + sibling paper; static); `[2606.17660]` **TuneAhead** — meta-features predict fine-tune performance, 1,300+ Qwen2.5-7B runs, RMSE 1.47pp (RELATED); web `[2504.12491]` pretraining indicators vs finetuning outcomes (BACKGROUND).

**EM-from-activations:** `[2506.11618]` Convergent Linear Representations of EM — single misalignment direction transfers across LoRA protocols (Qwen2.5-14B) (RELATED); web `[2605.00842]` EM via feature-superposition geometry (RELATED); `[2606.20225]` actionable activation directions for EM, 99.6% separation (BACKGROUND).

**Slice verdict:** (a) no 2025–26 paper trains next-activation predictors on an LLM as a forecasting model (CLTs are the nearest, for circuit tracing); (b) behavior/safety forecasting from activations exists but is single-snapshot everywhere; (c) the combination — learned h_ℓ→h_{ℓ+1} dynamics on real chat data, evaluated for behavior forecasting — APPEARS OPEN as of mid-2026. Recency risk moderate: the Fernando & Guitchounts spectral-dynamics line (May 2026) and the June-2026 behavior-forecasting papers (2606.11172, 2606.11445) are one bridge away.

## Synthesis (as filed in the task body)

Q1 as originally stated (linear predictivity of adjacent layers) is largely answered and residual-dominated — the informative object is the update Δ_ℓ vs identity + affine baselines. Open contributions: the per-layer function-class atlas on chat data, the attention-non-locality ceiling measurement, the RNN/SSM-over-depth surrogate, and (entirely open) behavior forecasting from learned dynamics. DPI constraint: dynamics cannot add information over a same-cost static probe — pre-registered win conditions are label efficiency and held-out-behavior/OOD transfer, benchmarked against #779's direct context→trait predictor g on the identical rig. EAGLE-3 lessons imported: end-to-end behavior-readout arm, multi-layer fusion, train rolled-forward maps on their own rollouts.
