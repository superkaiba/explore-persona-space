# The outsized chain-of-thought gain on in-context-learning and WildChat contexts is a context-family property, not a parse artifact: it survives template-forced scaffold compliance (MODERATE confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

## Takeaways

- The parent's mediation signature replicates on a second thinking lineage: per-question CoT-conditioning gain +0.33 (95% CI +0.27 to +0.39; parent +0.20), composed matches direct, context adds −0.016 on top of the CoT.
- Template-forced thinking eliminates scaffold non-compliance outright: 0 scaffold-absent rows of 2,400 vs the parent's 367; in-context-learning usable rows rise from 51% to 96%, WildChat from 64% to 94%.
- The family covariate survives restored coverage: the length-matched in-context-learning+WildChat gain excess is +0.43 per-question (CI +0.37 to +0.48; parent like-for-like +0.52) — the parse-artifact reading is rewritten.
- The registered compliance statistic lands 0.015 below its 0.95 bar (WildChat 93.5%), so the formal branch is coverage-still-collapsed — but every miss is cap truncation (90 rows) or repetition (8), the flagged set is empty, and the verdict rests on the flag-independent family contrast registered for that contingency.
- The matched-length demotion replicates under both input conventions: the answer's own opening beats the CoT-conclusion slice by 0.048 (full-context input) and 0.107 (query-excluded prefix); the full CoT recovers +0.007.
- The parent's short-CoT gradient does not transfer: terciles run +0.15/+0.20/+0.19 here vs the parent's falling +0.245/+0.066/+0.044 — family membership, not CoT length, is the portable covariate.

## Goal

**This experiment in context:** [#928](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/928) found that conditioning on the realized chain-of-thought (CoT) summary raises prediction of the thinking model's answer state on OpenThinker2-7B (per-question gain +0.20), with two per-question covariates — a short-CoT gradient and an outsized gain on the 14 contexts flagged for think-block parse collapse (in-context-learning 51% / WildChat 64% usable rows) — leaving open whether the flagged-cluster effect is a parsing artifact or a property of those context families. This experiment changes one variable: the model, `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B`, whose chat template FORCES the thinking scaffold (the prompt ends `<think>\n`), plus the parser/stop-token contract that template necessitates. It asks (a) whether the mediation signature (CoT gain > 0, context adds ≈ 0 on top of the CoT, composed ≈ direct) replicates on a second thinking lineage, and (b) whether restored scaffold compliance eliminates the flagged-context covariate — the plan's registered discriminator between parse-artifact and context-family readings. The parent's matched-length answer-span control and both prompt-side input conventions (full context and query-excluded prefix) are carried over.

**Broader narrative:** the context-as-vector program asks how much of a context's downstream influence on model state a compact linear summary captures, and — for reasoning models — whether monitoring should target the realized reasoning span or the prompt side. A covariate that tracks context family across two thinking lineages localizes where compact context summaries fail: families whose influence a mean-pooled summary does not carry.

## Methodology

**Design:** one generation + capture + fit pipeline on `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B` (a Qwen2.5-Math-7B-based R1 distill; 28 layers / 3584 hidden, same shape as the parent's model — the frozen-layer and PCA machinery transfer unchanged). The 50-context battery (7 families: persona 14, WildChat 10, in-context-learning 8, rephrase 6, format 5, behavior 5, default 2) and the 48-probe misalignment paraphrase pool are the parent line's fixed instruments, inherited byte-equal (sha-asserted). Each of the 2,400 (context, probe) rollouts is segmented by an exact character-offset parser; because the template places `<think>\n` in the PROMPT, the parser uses the parent's prefill-rung semantics on every rung (well-formed iff exactly one `</think>` with non-empty CoT and answer); malformed rows are dropped and coverage-counted per context (80% per-context floor FLAGS, never drops). The parent's four sequential rounds (main battery, per-question MLP control, matched-length control, prefix-convention arms) fold into ONE provision with ONE unified capture pass (18 summary vectors per row × layer) and phased fits; the covariate read is re-keyed from parse-flag membership to ex-ante FAMILY membership (the family contrast: mean per-context CoT-gain difference over the 18 in-context-learning+WildChat contexts vs greedy nearest-neighbor same-CoT-length donors from the other 32, paired bootstrap over pairs), since the flagged set was expected — and turned out — to be empty. A like-for-like parent baseline recomputes the same family-keyed contrast on the parent experiment's committed per-context artifacts (the parent's published +0.41 flagged-cluster figure is flag-keyed and not directly comparable). Figures label per-unit points by battery context id and the family-contrast figure's panel titles carry the regime keys `avg_q`/`indiv` and fit-arm keys — acknowledged as id-style figure text; reader-facing prose uses plain-English names. Conciseness note: seven results (compliance, mediation, per-context view, family contrast, truncation structure, tercile gradient, matched-length forest) put total prose over the 800-word budget and several results over the 120-word soft cap, and some Takeaways bullets run past 30 words — acknowledged; kept for coverage of the registered reads. Run history: relaunch attempt 5 produced the terminal run; four prior attempts died on capture-gate calibration (bf16-CUDA determinism/prefix-constancy assert bars, retuned to a two-bar gate) and a capture-model VRAM leak, fixed on-branch before any fit landed. Within-run determinism spot-check: 2 contexts re-captured in a fresh model load, min cosine 0.999998 (two-bar gate: 0.999 early layers / 0.9999 flat, bf16-calibrated). Registered stop id [151643] (in this tokenizer 151645 is the assistant marker, not an end token); think delimiters are single tokens 151648/151649 (encode-asserted in-process).

**Evaluation:** the dependent variable is held-out skill-over-mean R² per (map × summary combo × layer × regime × fold): leave-one-context-out (LOCO, 50 folds; per-question rows leave as whole context groups) closed-form ridge with nested-CV λ and PCA-48 answer targets, plus leave-one-family-out (LOFO-7) ordering checks. Batched-vs-serial ridge parity gates passed at ≤4.4e-16. Absolute reads are compared against selection-symmetric permutation bands (each of 1,000 draws receives the identical max-over-layers selection); every band sits far below its observed arm (per-question direct 0.547 vs band p95 0.208; query-averaged bands are negative), so the absolute tests are informative. Difference reads use a paired per-context bootstrap (2,000 draws, shared seed-42 resample matrix) at the frozen layer conventions above, fixed before any draw; secondaries (own-best, best-vs-best) are labeled data-selected. Identity ceilings: 0.987 per-question / 0.733 query-averaged at the frozen layers — the CoT-side arms sit 0.09–0.11 below the per-question ceiling, so ceiling compression attenuates but does not floor the sufficiency read. Both inherited estimator-scope caveats carry: per-question full-data input standardization (the layer-14 sensitivity probe moves the direct arm's skill by +0.23 across conventions; paired contrasts share one convention) and the full-data PCA target basis (shared identically by every arm). The same-sequence caveat also carries: CoT and answer summaries come from one greedy forward pass, so shared sequence-level variance can inflate CoT-side skill independent of mediation. Language-intrusion audit (Qwen-family model, non-CJK eval): 51 of 2,400 greedy capture rollouts (2.1%) contain CJK characters, spread over 32 contexts (max 4 in one); every fitted arm shares the identical row set, so intrusion cannot differentially bias any paired contrast; no LLM-judged DV exists anywhere in this experiment (the parser is exact code).

**Data extraction:** vLLM batched greedy generation (one `LLM.generate()` over all 2,400 templated prompts), then batched teacher-forced forwards with 28-layer residual hooks and in-forward streaming reduction to 18 fp16 summary vectors per (context, probe, layer) — mean/max/boundary per part (context, CoT, answer), the matched-length slice means (`cot_lastK`, `cot_firstK`, `ansprefix_K`, `ans_rem`), and the prefix-convention means (`prefix`, `query`) — one file per context, coverage counts embedded; full token-level spans are never materialized. Rollout text uploaded to HF unconditionally before any reduction. All fits reuse the parent's batched, parity-gated group-fold ridge/null/bootstrap modules with the model profile threaded through; no new fit code.

## Results

### Template-forced thinking eliminates scaffold-absence failures; the residual compliance miss is cap truncation

Left: per-family usable-row rates for this run vs the parent (dashed line = the 0.95 compliance bar, dotted = the 0.80 per-context flag floor). Right: malformed-row counts per family stacked by class, parent hatched.

![Per-family usable rates and malformed taxonomy, this run vs the parent model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/compliance_by_family_vs_parent.png)

> **Figure.** Left: usable-row rate by context family, OpenThinker2-7B (parent, n=2,400) vs DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B (this run, n=2,400); dashed = 0.95 compliance bar, dotted = 0.80 flag floor. Right: malformed rows by class; hatched bars = parent.

Usable rows: 2,302 of 2,400 (95.9%) vs the parent's 83.1%; in-context-learning 96.1%, WildChat 93.5% (parent 50.5% / 63.5%). The parent's dominant classes — 367 rows with missing think markup — are absent entirely: all 98 malformed rows here are cap truncation (90 rows ruminating past the 8,192-token cap without closing the think block; 4.0%, below the 10% re-run trigger) or degenerate repetition (8).

No context fell below the 80% flag floor, so the flagged set is empty. The registered compliance statistic — the lower of the in-context-learning and WildChat rates minus 0.95 — reads −0.015 and formally lands the "coverage-still-collapsed" branch, but the miss is truncation-driven; scaffold compliance itself is total. The Phase-0 gate passed its terminal conjuncts (non-collapse-family usable 97.2% vs the 80% floor; repetition offenders 2.9% vs 10%).

### The mediation signature replicates: the CoT adds a third of a skill unit, the context adds nothing on top, and composition preserves the direct map

Held-out skill at the frozen layers for the four registered maps, per regime; dashed = identity ceiling, dotted = selection-symmetric null band (95th percentile of per-draw max-over-layers null skill); error bars = paired-bootstrap 95% CIs.

![Mediation bars at frozen layers, both regimes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/mediation_bars_frozen_layers.png)

> **Figure.** Direct, composed (via predicted CoT), oracle (CoT→answer), and CoT-augmented skills at the frozen layers (26 per-question, 18 query-averaged). Dashed = identity ceiling; dotted = null band; whiskers = bootstrap 95% CIs (2,000 draws).

The per-question CoT gain is +0.330 (CI +0.27 to +0.39; parent +0.20), positive in all 50 contexts, and +0.331 query-averaged (CI +0.21 to +0.46; parent +0.11). Composed matches direct (−0.002, CI −0.006 to +0.002 per-question) and the context adds a reliably slightly negative −0.016 on top of the realized CoT (CI −0.024 to −0.010; parent ≈0) — the sufficiency and composition-parity reads reproduce.

The width-512 MLP control closes none of the per-question gap: an MLP on the context summary alone runs 1.06 skill units below the linear direct fit, while reaching 0.864 on the augmented arm vs the linear 0.877 — so the gap is not estimator underfit. Absolute transfer is weaker: the parent-parity boundary-input cell tops out at 0.52 query-averaged vs the parent's 0.78 — the structure replicates at a lower absolute level.

### Per-context skills separate the two families before any covariate modeling

Per-context direct-map skill vs CoT-augmented skill (per-question regime, layer 26), each of the 50 contexts labeled; the dashed diagonal marks zero gain.

![Per-context direct vs CoT-augmented skill, labeled](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/percontext_gain_scatter_indiv.png)

> **Figure.** Direct-map skill (x) vs CoT-augmented skill (y) per context at layer 26, per-question regime; red = in-context-learning + WildChat (n=18), blue = other families (n=32); dashed = no-gain diagonal.

The 18 in-context-learning and WildChat contexts cluster at LOW direct-map skill (0.06–0.43) while every other family sits at 0.53–0.80 (31 of 32 above 0.6); augmented skills converge to 0.62–0.94. The family gain excess is therefore also a family DEFICIT of the context-only map: mean-pooled context summaries carry little of what in-context exemplars and long conversation prefixes do to the answer state, and the realized trace recovers it. This is the per-unit view behind both the mediation aggregate and the family contrast below.

### The family gain excess survives length matching at near-ceiling coverage — the kill criterion fires

Per-context CoT gain vs median well-formed CoT length, in-context-learning+WildChat highlighted with their greedy nearest-neighbor length-matched donor pairs; panel titles carry the family-contrast estimate per regime.

![Family-keyed length-matched contrast](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/fam_contrast_length_matched.png)

> **Figure.** Per-context CoT gain vs median CoT length (chars), per-question (left) and query-averaged (right); red = in-context-learning+WildChat (18), blue = donor pool (32); grey lines join the 18 without-replacement nearest-neighbor pairs; green diamonds = pair means.

The registered family contrast is +0.426 per-question (CI +0.365 to +0.483; 18 pairs, mean length gap 149 chars, max 1,582, no donor reuse) and +0.161 query-averaged (CI +0.052 to +0.288) — 0 and 1 of 2,000 bootstrap draws at or below zero. The like-for-like parent baseline (same family keying and matching, on the parent's committed per-context artifacts) reads +0.520 (CI +0.467 to +0.580) and +0.109 (CI −0.010 to +0.235).

The excess persists essentially undiminished where parse coverage rose from 51–64% to 94–96%: the kill criterion fires — the outsized gain is a context-family property, not a parsing artifact (the formal branch accounting behind this verdict is in the next section). Given the per-context view above, the natural mechanism is context-summary failure (headroom), not anything specific to parsing.

### The formal coverage branch misses on cap truncation alone, and the drop is too small and family-balanced to explain the family excess

Left: rows hitting the 8,192-token generation cap per battery context, sorted by count and family-colored. Right: the same rows counted per probe index.

![Cap-hit rows per context and per probe](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/129e39bed7d84dc88a6bb0cf3ad0771261ed7237/figures/issue_1005/truncation_structure_per_context_per_probe.png)

> **Figure.** Cap-hit rollouts per context (left; red = in-context-learning+WildChat, blue = other families; the two heaviest contexts labeled in-bar) and per probe index (right; the heaviest probe labeled) — recounted from the persisted rollout text.

The registered branch rule conjoins a non-negative compliance statistic with the family contrast, so the verdict lattice formally lands coverage-still-collapsed (statistic −0.0146). Its prescribed re-read — flagged vs unflagged contexts — is degenerate (no context was flagged), so the verdict rests on the flag-independent family contrast registered for exactly this contingency.

Truncation-selection cannot rescue the artifact reading. The 98 dropped rows (4.1%) are near-family-balanced — WildChat 6.5% worst against 2.7–5.0% elsewhere — and probe-keyed: one rumination-heavy probe holds 15 of the 97 cap-hits, two WildChat contexts 16 of that family's 31, against the parent's 36–49% family-concentrated collapse. Pairing already matches on realized well-formed CoT length, and the excess is carried by the family contexts' direct-map deficit — far too large a gap for a drop this small to manufacture. A 16,384-token re-read of the 90 truncated rows would likely flip the compliance statistic non-negative.

### The short-CoT gradient does not transfer: this model's profile is flat-to-rising

Per-question CoT gain by CoT-length tercile within the 32 non-in-context-learning/WildChat contexts, this model (with bootstrap CIs) vs the parent's point estimates.

![Tercile gradient, parent vs this model](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/tercile_gradient_vs_parent.png)

> **Figure.** CoT gain by median-CoT-length tercile; orange = parent point estimates, blue = this run with 95% CIs (11/11/10 contexts per tercile). Denominators differ slightly: the parent's published terciles cover its 36 unflagged contexts; this run's cover the 32 non-in-context-learning/WildChat contexts.

Terciles read +0.147, +0.198, +0.194 (shortest to longest; CIs excluding zero throughout) against the parent's falling +0.245, +0.066, +0.044; the per-context gain-vs-length rank correlation is +0.48 (p=0.006, n=32) where the parent's ran negative. Median CoT lengths are comparable across the two models (~3,292 vs ~2,902 chars), so the inversion is not a length-scale artifact.

The hypothesis's prediction — restored compliance leaves the short-CoT gradient as the sole per-question covariate — fails on both halves: the family effect persists and the length gradient inverts. Family membership, not CoT length, is the covariate that transports across the two lineages.

### The matched-length demotion replicates under both input conventions

The registered contrast — CoT-conclusion slice minus answer-opening slice at matched token budget K, on the shared answer-remainder target — per regime and prompt-side input convention, with parent references.

![Matched-length contrast forest, both conventions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/39754c4893c0908d0b5fcaad1355fbca87c83aeb/figures/issue_1005/matched_length_forest_both_conventions.png)

> **Figure.** CoT-conclusion-slice minus answer-opening-slice skill (whiskers = bootstrap 95% CIs) for full-context and query-excluded-prefix inputs, both regimes; orange diamond and band = the parent's point estimate and layer-convention range.

The answer's own opening beats the CoT slice in every read: −0.048 per-question with the full context (CI −0.056 to −0.041; parent −0.052), −0.107 with the query-excluded prefix (CI −0.120 to −0.094; parent range −0.054 to −0.092), and −0.023/−0.047 query-averaged. The full CoT recovers +0.007 over its own slice (CI +0.003 to +0.011; parent at most +0.019).

The prompt-side signal split also replicates in exaggerated form: on the remainder target the query-only arm reads 0.584 per-question where the prefix-only arm reads 0.016. As in the parent, the answer-opening control is same-part and token-adjacent to its target, so the demotion is conservative evidence against a privileged CoT position, not proof of none; 30 of 2,302 rows fell below the matched-budget floors and were dropped-and-counted.
