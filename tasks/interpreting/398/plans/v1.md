## 1. Goal

Distinguish three qualitatively-different scenarios for how marker probability emerges during LoRA SFT on a single source persona (librarian), by tracking teacher-forced log p(`※`) at the trained answer-end position across 22 fine-grained checkpoints between step 5 and step 1600:

- **(A) Continuous ramp** — log p(`※`) climbs monotonically from step 5 onward; the step-75 firing jump observed in #385 is just the point at which the smoothly-rising marker probability mass crosses the sampling threshold.
- **(B) Two-phase** — log p(`※`) is flat-ish through step ~50 then accelerates sharply at step 75 (an inflection rather than a discontinuity).
- **(C) True phase transition** — log p(`※`) is essentially flat with a discrete jump at step 75 corresponding to an internal mode switch (the persona-direction-aligned channel suddenly aligning with the marker direction).

These three scenarios have different mechanistic implications for what "implant" means as a learning process, and are unidentifiable from #385's substring-match curve (which is censored at zero below the firing threshold).

**Falsification criteria (per-bystander, fit on the 12 checkpoints in the [5, 75] window for the source persona):**

| Scenario | Quantitative signature on log p(`※`) curve, librarian source, step 5–75 window |
|---|---|
| (A) Continuous ramp | A single straight line in (log step, log p) explains the data well: piecewise-linear fit with a break at any step ∈ {25, 50, 75} does NOT improve ΔAIC > 4 over the single-line fit. Cumulative log p change from step 5 to step 50 is at least 25 % of the step 5 → step 75 change (smooth accumulation). |
| (B) Two-phase | Piecewise-linear fit with a break at step ∈ {25, 50, 75} improves ΔAIC > 4 over single-line. The post-break slope is at least 3× the pre-break slope. The pre-break slope is non-zero (positive). Cumulative log p change step 5 → step 50 is between 5 % and 25 % of the step 5 → step 75 change. |
| (C) True phase transition | The step 5 → step 50 log p curve is statistically indistinguishable from a flat horizontal line (per-bystander linear fit's slope confidence interval crosses zero, or absolute slope < 0.01 nat/log-step). Cumulative log p change step 5 → step 50 is < 5 % of the step 5 → step 75 change. The step 50 → step 75 change is the dominant event. |

These thresholds are diagnostics the analyzer weighs across the 27 bystanders + the source persona — they are NOT pass/fail gates. The headline narrative is the per-bystander distribution of which scenario fits (e.g. "26 of 27 bystanders fit scenario A, 1 fits C" vs "all 27 fit C uniformly").

## 3. Hypothesis

**Primary (predicted):** Most bystanders + the librarian source fit scenario (B) two-phase, with a break-step in {50, 75}. log p(`※`) shows non-zero positive accumulation in the [5, 50] window for closer bystanders (consistent with the parent #385 finding that geometric closeness predicts emergence order), and the per-bystander accumulation correlates positively with base-model L20 cosine-to-librarian (closer bystanders accumulate more probability mass earlier). The step-75 substring-match jump is mostly explained by a sampling-threshold-crossing event — the marker probability has been rising smoothly but had only just crossed the cumulative sampling threshold by then.

**Secondary:** Per-bystander Spearman ρ between checkpoint-by-checkpoint log p(`※`) and base-model L20 cosine-to-librarian reaches |ρ| ≥ 0.5 by step 50 (i.e. the geometric ordering shows up in log-prob BEFORE substring-match can resolve it — log p(`※`) is the more sensitive instrument).

**Falsification (kills the predicted account, NOT the experiment):** If all 27 bystanders + the source fit scenario (C) true phase transition (slope CI crosses zero in [5, 50] window for ≥ 25 of 27 bystanders, AND step 50 → step 75 jump is the dominant event by > 80 % of total step 5 → step 1600 change), then #385's step-75 emergence is a genuine internal mode switch, not a sampling-threshold-crossing artifact. This would push the mechanism question toward "what internal representation flips at step 75."

**Null:** Per-bystander curves are noise-dominated — fits don't discriminate (A) from (B) from (C) at ΔAIC > 4 for the majority of bystanders. Plan deviation in this case: re-run with seed 137 to check whether the bystander-level shape is recipe-determined or seed-noise.

```python
"""Per-checkpoint teacher-forced log p(marker) eval for issue #398."""
import argparse, json, os, time
from pathlib import Path
import torch
from peft import PeftModel
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer

from explore_persona_space.eval.marker_logprob import compute_marker_logprob
from _i398_bystander_panel import BYSTANDERS, PROMPTS, SOURCE_PERSONA

# A fixed canonical assistant response held constant across all 27 bystanders ×
# 20 prompts. Used to construct the end-of-answer probe geometry that mirrors
# the position where training pinned the marker (after the answer + "\n\n").
# Source-of-truth: the implementer pins this string in the script; do NOT vary
# per (persona, prompt) — the whole point is that the geometry is identical
# across cells so the only thing that varies is the model's marker probability
# at a fixed end-of-answer position.
CANONICAL_ANSWER = "Sure, here's a brief answer."

def build_contexts(persona_text: str, tokenizer, *, geometry: str = "pos0"):
    """Render the eval context per (persona, prompt). Two geometries supported.

    geometry="pos0" — chat-template rendered prefix with `add_generation_prompt=True`.
    The model's next-token prediction is at the FIRST assistant token. Measures
    log p(※ | system + user + <assistant turn open>) — "how close is the model
    to emitting ※ as the very first assistant token?" Mirrors the first-token-
    aligned substring-match metric in #385.

    geometry="endpos" — same prefix PLUS the fixed CANONICAL_ANSWER PLUS "\n\n".
    The model's next-token prediction is at the END of the answer, after the
    "\n\n" separator. This is the position where training pinned the marker
    (every training row ends "<answer>\n\n※" — see scripts/generate_leakage_data.py
    line ~553). Measures log p(※ | system + user + <canonical_answer> + "\n\n").

    Two geometries are scored per (step, persona, prompt) cell — see Methodology
    reconciler v1 (§0 Plan revisions). Agreement on A/B/C label across both
    geometries → high-confidence headline; disagreement → headline pivots to
    position dependence of the implant (itself a publishable finding).
    """
    contexts = []
    for q in PROMPTS:
        msgs = [{"role": "system", "content": persona_text},
                {"role": "user",   "content": q}]
        rendered = tokenizer.apply_chat_template(msgs, tokenize=False,
                                                 add_generation_prompt=True)
        if geometry == "pos0":
            contexts.append(rendered)
        elif geometry == "endpos":
            contexts.append(rendered + CANONICAL_ANSWER + "\n\n")
        else:
            raise ValueError(f"unknown geometry: {geometry}")
    return contexts

def main():
    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser()
    ap.add_argument("--run-dir", required=True, help="Adapter dir from training")
    ap.add_argument("--base-model", default="Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct")
    ap.add_argument("--steps", required=True, help="Comma-sep list of step ints")
    ap.add_argument("--marker-token", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--output", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--include-source-persona", action="store_true", default=True,
                    help="Also score librarian source persona contexts.")
    args = ap.parse_args()

    steps = [int(s) for s in args.steps.split(",")]
    tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(args.base_model, trust_remote_code=True)
    base = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
        args.base_model, torch_dtype=torch.bfloat16, device_map="cuda:0",
        trust_remote_code=True).eval()

    # Build contexts once per panel persona
    panel = dict(BYSTANDERS)
    if args.include_source_persona:
        # Inject the librarian source persona text from extract_persona_vectors.PERSONAS
        from _i398_bystander_panel import SOURCE_PERSONA
        from experiments.phase_minus1_persona_vectors.extract_persona_vectors import PERSONAS as _PP
        src_text = dict(_PP)[SOURCE_PERSONA]
        panel = {SOURCE_PERSONA: src_text, **panel}  # source FIRST in output

    # Build BOTH probe geometries once per panel persona. Storage layout per
    # cell in results["per_step"][step][persona] = {"pos0": [20 floats],
    # "endpos": [20 floats]}. The analyzer (§4.2(f)) runs A/B/C labeling on
    # both series independently.
    panel_contexts_pos0 = {name: build_contexts(text, tok, geometry="pos0")
                           for name, text in panel.items()}
    panel_contexts_endpos = {name: build_contexts(text, tok, geometry="endpos")
                             for name, text in panel.items()}

    results = {"marker_token": args.marker_token, "base_model": args.base_model,
               "panel": list(panel.keys()), "prompts": PROMPTS,
               "geometries": ["pos0", "endpos"],
               "canonical_answer": CANONICAL_ANSWER, "per_step": {}}

    for step in steps:
        t0 = time.time()
        ckpt_dir = Path(args.run_dir) / f"checkpoint-{step}"
        assert ckpt_dir.exists(), f"missing {ckpt_dir}"
        adapter = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, str(ckpt_dir))
        adapter.eval()

        per_persona: dict[str, dict[str, list[float]]] = {}
        for persona_name in panel.keys():
            logps_pos0 = compute_marker_logprob(
                adapter, tok, contexts=panel_contexts_pos0[persona_name],
                marker_text=args.marker_token,
                batch_size=8, device="cuda:0",
            )
            logps_endpos = compute_marker_logprob(
                adapter, tok, contexts=panel_contexts_endpos[persona_name],
                marker_text=args.marker_token,
                batch_size=8, device="cuda:0",
            )
            per_persona[persona_name] = {"pos0": logps_pos0, "endpos": logps_endpos}

        # IMPORTANT: detach the adapter so the next iter starts from bare base
        adapter = adapter.unload()  # returns the base model with adapter merged out
        del adapter
        torch.cuda.empty_cache()

        results["per_step"][str(step)] = per_persona

        # Incremental write — never accumulate-in-memory and write-at-end
        # (per CLAUDE.md "Checkpoint per phase" rule).
        Path(args.output).parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        with open(args.output, "w") as f:
            json.dump(results, f, indent=2)
        print(f"step {step}: {time.time()-t0:.1f}s wall, wrote {args.output}",
              flush=True)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

Two design notes:
- **`adapter.unload()` between checkpoints.** PEFT's `unload()` returns the base model with adapter weights merged out, restoring the bare base for the next `PeftModel.from_pretrained()` call. Verified in #401 round-2's `measure_first_step_delta` which uses the same pattern.
- **Incremental write.** Per CLAUDE.md "Checkpoint per phase" rule, the per-step results are written to `args.output` after every checkpoint, not at the end. A mid-run crash loses at most one checkpoint's worth of work.

**(e) Substring-match parity eval script** — `scripts/eval_i398_marker_spread.py` (NEW, ~80 lines). Thin wrapper around `eval/generation.py::generate_persona_completions`. Pseudocode:

```python
"""Per-checkpoint substring-match marker eval for #398 (parity with #385)."""
import argparse, json
from pathlib import Path

from explore_persona_space.eval.generation import generate_persona_completions
from explore_persona_space.eval.trait_scorers import evaluate_markers

def merge_adapter_to_path(base_path, adapter_path, out_path):
    """Merge LoRA adapter into base for vLLM consumption — same pattern as #385."""
    from peft import PeftModel
    from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer
    import torch
    base = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
        base_path, torch_dtype=torch.bfloat16, device_map="cuda:0",
        trust_remote_code=True)
    peft = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, adapter_path)
    merged = peft.merge_and_unload()
    merged.save_pretrained(out_path, safe_serialization=True)
    AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(base_path).save_pretrained(out_path)
    del base, peft, merged
    torch.cuda.empty_cache()

def main():
    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser()
    ap.add_argument("--run-dir", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--base-model", default="Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct")
    ap.add_argument("--steps", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--marker-token", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--num-completions", type=int, default=8)
    ap.add_argument("--output", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--merge-tmp-dir", default="/workspace/_i398_merged")
    args = ap.parse_args()
    steps = [int(s) for s in args.steps.split(",")]
    out = {"marker_token": args.marker_token, "panel": list(BYSTANDERS),
           "prompts": PROMPTS, "per_step": {}}
    for step in steps:
        ckpt = f"{args.run_dir}/checkpoint-{step}"
        merged_path = f"{args.merge_tmp_dir}/step_{step}"

        completions = generate_persona_completions(
            model_path=merged_path, personas=BYSTANDERS, questions=PROMPTS,
            num_completions=args.num_completions, temperature=1.0,
            max_tokens=512, top_p=0.95, seed=42)
        # rate per (persona, prompt) = fraction of completions containing marker
        per_persona = {}
        for persona_name, q2c in completions.items():
            per_q = {}
            for q, comps in q2c.items():
                fires = sum(1 for c in comps if args.marker_token in c)
                per_q[q] = {"k": fires, "n": len(comps), "rate": fires / len(comps)}
            per_persona[persona_name] = per_q
        out["per_step"][str(step)] = per_persona
        # incremental write
        Path(args.output).parent.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
        with open(args.output, "w") as f: json.dump(out, f, indent=2)
        # cleanup merged dir to stay under MooseFS ~130 GB pod quota
        import shutil; shutil.rmtree(merged_path, ignore_errors=True)
        print(f"step {step}: wrote {args.output}", flush=True)

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

**(f) Analysis script** — `scripts/analyze_i398_dynamics.py` (NEW, ~180 lines). **Runs steps 1–6 INDEPENDENTLY on BOTH geometries (`pos0` and `endpos`)** per the Methodology reconciler binding fix (§0 Plan revisions). For each geometry, per-bystander on the log-prob series for steps in the [5, 75] window (12 checkpoints: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 65, 70, 75):

1. Fit single straight line in (log10(step), log_p_sum_over_prompts(persona, step, geometry)) — record slope, AIC.
2. Fit piecewise-linear with break ∈ {25, 50, 75} (3 candidate breakpoints). Pick best by AIC.
3. Compute ΔAIC = AIC(single) − min AIC(piecewise).
4. Compute cumulative log p change ratios: (step_50 − step_5) / (step_75 − step_5); (step_50 − step_5) / (step_1600 − step_5).
5. Compute slope ratio (post-break / pre-break) for best piecewise fit.
6. Apply §1 thresholds to label persona A / B / C / "noise-dominated" for THIS geometry.
7. **Dual-geometry consensus per bystander.** Compare `label_pos0` vs `label_endpos`:
   - Agreement → record `consensus_label`; this bystander contributes to the high-confidence headline.
   - Disagreement → record `consensus_label = "position_dependent"`; headline pivots to "the marker implant has position-dependent dynamics" (itself a publishable finding).
   - Either geometry "noise-dominated" → drop from consensus, flag as low-power.
8. Per-checkpoint Spearman ρ between the 27-bystander vector of log p(`※`) and the 27-bystander vector of base-model L20 cosine-to-librarian (lifted from `eval_results/issue_385/predictors_base.json`). Compute separately for `pos0` and `endpos` series; report ρ + p at every checkpoint (not just the first threshold crossing).
9. Build hero figure (matplotlib via `paper-plots` skill conventions) — overlay the substring-match rate curve with BOTH log-p curves (pos0 dashed, endpos solid) on a twin axis, colored by consensus scenario label.

**(g) Smoke log-prob rendering check** — `scripts/smoke_i398_logp_check.py` (NEW, ~70 lines). Runs after the 10-step smoke training, BEFORE the main 1600-step training. Catches three failure modes the main run cannot recover from: (i) chat-template `build_contexts` rendering bug (silent zero Δ at the trained position), (ii) base-model log p(`※`) is -inf or below -50 nat (no probe floor), (iii) step-10 training doesn't move log p meaningfully (entire ramp/cliff signal is below detection threshold). Resolves fact-check items A18/A19/A20. Pseudocode:

```python
"""Smoke check: 10-step adapter must move log p(※) above base for the source persona."""
import argparse, json, sys
import torch
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer
from peft import PeftModel

from explore_persona_space.eval.marker_logprob import compute_marker_logprob
from explore_persona_space.personas import PERSONAS

def build_source_contexts(tokenizer, source_persona: str, num_prompts: int):
    """Render N first-prompt assistant-turn-opener contexts for the source persona."""
    from experiments.phase_minus1_persona_vectors.extract_persona_vectors import PROMPTS
    system_text = dict(PERSONAS)[source_persona]
    return [
        tokenizer.apply_chat_template(
            [{"role": "system", "content": system_text},
             {"role": "user", "content": q}],
            tokenize=False, add_generation_prompt=True)
        for q in PROMPTS[:num_prompts]
    ]

def main():
    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser()
    ap.add_argument("--adapter", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--base-model", default="Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct")
    ap.add_argument("--marker-token", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--source-persona", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--num-prompts", type=int, default=3)
    ap.add_argument("--output", required=True)
    ap.add_argument("--delta-threshold", type=float, default=0.1)  # nat
    ap.add_argument("--base-floor", type=float, default=-50.0)     # nat
    ap.add_argument("--min-passing-prompts", type=int, default=2)  # of 3
    args = ap.parse_args()

    tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(args.base_model, trust_remote_code=True)
    contexts = build_source_contexts(tok, args.source_persona, args.num_prompts)

    # Base-model log p — covers A20 (probe floor exists).
    base = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(
        args.base_model, torch_dtype=torch.bfloat16, device_map="cuda:0",
        trust_remote_code=True).eval()
    base_logp = compute_marker_logprob(base, tok, contexts, args.marker_token)
    if any(lp < args.base_floor for lp in base_logp):
        print(f"FAIL A20: base log p {base_logp} below floor {args.base_floor}.")
        sys.exit(1)

    # Step-10 trained log p — covers A18/A19.
    trained = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, args.adapter).eval()
    trained_logp = compute_marker_logprob(trained, tok, contexts, args.marker_token)
    deltas = [t - b for t, b in zip(trained_logp, base_logp)]
    passing = sum(1 for d in deltas if d > args.delta_threshold)

    with open(args.output, "w") as f:
        json.dump({
            "source_persona": args.source_persona,
            "marker_token": args.marker_token,
            "num_prompts": args.num_prompts,
            "base_logp": base_logp, "trained_logp": trained_logp,
            "deltas": deltas, "passing": passing,
            "delta_threshold": args.delta_threshold,
            "base_floor": args.base_floor,
            "min_passing_prompts": args.min_passing_prompts,
        }, f, indent=2)

    print(f"base_logp={base_logp}")
    print(f"trained_logp={trained_logp}")
    print(f"deltas={deltas}")
    print(f"passing={passing}/{args.num_prompts}, threshold Δ > {args.delta_threshold} nat")
    if passing < args.min_passing_prompts:
        print(f"FAIL A18/A19: step-10 trained log p did not move above base by "
              f"Δ > {args.delta_threshold} nat for ≥ {args.min_passing_prompts} "
              f"of {args.num_prompts} prompts. Suspect chat-template rendering "
              f"bug or learning-rate-too-low. DO NOT proceed to main run.")
        sys.exit(2)
    print("PASS smoke log-prob rendering check.")

if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

### 4.4 Why code, not a model call

There are no unstructured-data heuristics in this design — no LLM-judge, classifier, parser, or rule-based scorer is invoked. The only "model call" is `compute_marker_logprob` (a deterministic teacher-forced forward pass on the model being evaluated) and `generate_persona_completions` (sampled completions from the model being evaluated). The substring match on the completions is a literal `args.marker_token in c` check on a single 1-token string — no fuzzy matching, no judge, no parser. The §4.2(f) analysis runs deterministic linear / piecewise-linear regressions in NumPy. There is no unstructured-data heuristic to replace with a model call.

## 6. Evaluation

**Primary diagnostics (the analyzer reads these off the per-bystander curves):**

| Diagnostic | What it discriminates | Threshold magnitudes (see §1 for full table) |
|---|---|---|
| Per-bystander piecewise-vs-single linear ΔAIC on (log10 step, log p), window [5, 75], best break ∈ {25, 50, 75} | A vs B vs C | ΔAIC > 4 favors piecewise (B/C); ΔAIC ≤ 4 favors single line (A). |
| Per-bystander cumulative ratio: (logp[step_50] − logp[step_5]) / (logp[step_75] − logp[step_5]) | A vs B vs C | ≥ 0.25 → A; 0.05–0.25 → B; < 0.05 → C. |
| Per-bystander slope-ratio (post-break / pre-break) for best piecewise fit | B vs C | ≥ 3× with pre-break slope > 0 → B; pre-break slope CI crosses zero → C. |
| Per-checkpoint Spearman ρ (across 27 bystanders) of log p(※) vs base-model L20 cosine-to-librarian | When does the geometric ordering become detectable in log-prob? | First step where |ρ| ≥ 0.5 with p < 0.01. Compare to the first step where the same ρ on substring-match rate reaches the same threshold (under #385, this is step 75). |
| Source-vs-bystander emergence-shape contrast | Whether the source line and the bystander panel follow the same scenario (A/B/C) | Categorical: same scenario label, or different. |

**Headline outcome candidates (the analyzer picks one based on which signature dominates ACROSS BOTH geometries):**

- "Mostly scenario B (two-phase): the marker probability rises in the [5, 50] window for most bystanders, then accelerates after step 50; the step-75 firing-rate jump in #385 is a sampling-threshold-crossing artifact on top of a continuous (but slow) probability rise."
- "Mostly scenario A (continuous ramp): single-line log-p fit holds; the step-75 firing-rate jump is purely a sampling-threshold-crossing artifact."
- "Mostly scenario C (true phase transition): log-p curve is flat through step 50; the step-75 jump is a genuine internal mode switch."
- "Mixed: close bystanders fit B, far bystanders fit C." (Plausible — close bystanders have non-trivial base-model marker prior; far bystanders may need a discrete representational alignment.)
- **"Position-dependent: `pos0` and `endpos` geometries label the same bystander differently."** If this dominates the panel, the headline pivots to "the marker implant is position-dependent — what looks like a phase transition at one geometry is a continuous ramp at another," which is itself a publishable mechanistic finding and informs how future marker-implant work should probe geometry.

**Confidence ceiling:** MODERATE per the user spec (n=1 seed). The "Confidence" sentence in the clean-result will name: single-seed binding constraint + the single source persona + the prompt panel restricted to 20 questions + (whatever scenario actually fits) as the surviving evidence.

## 11. Decision rationale

- **Marker = `※` (bare, not space-prefixed `" ※"`).** User-locked spec cites id=63680 (the bare form). Both forms are single-token on Qwen-2.5 (`" ※"` is 83399, `"※"` is 63680). The data-gen at `f"{resp}\n\n{marker_text}"` does NOT add a leading space, so the marker actually trained-on is `"※"` after `"\n\n"`. The eval reproducer uses the same string for consistency.
- **Checkpoint schedule (22 ckpts, 13 in-window + 9 post-75).** User-locked. The 12 in-window checkpoints other than 75 itself (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 65, 70) provide the resolution needed for the §1 piecewise-vs-single-line discriminator. Adding 70 as the last pre-75 point gives the analyzer the strongest test of "is the jump localized to between step 70 and step 75 vs spread across [60, 75]."
- **Single seed = 42.** User-locked. Headline confidence capped at MODERATE.
- **Whole-completion SFT (not marker-only loss).** Matches #385 exactly. #397 covers the marker-only-loss factor; #398 must not vary it.
- **`compute_marker_logprob` primitive, not `teacher_force_batch`.** The marker-at-end-of-prefix geometry the new primitive handles is exactly what #398 needs; `teacher_force_batch` is set up for the chat-template-wrapped response-window geometry. Same philosophy (teacher-forced log-softmax) but different indexing — #401's plan §3.2 made the deliberate choice to keep them as siblings, and that is the cleaner integration here.
- **Dual probe geometry: BOTH `pos0` and `endpos` are scored per cell** (Methodology reconciler v1, §0 Plan revisions). `pos0` renders the chat-template prefix with `add_generation_prompt=True` and scores log p(※) at the very first assistant token — the position the model emits at sampling time, matching #385's substring-match metric. `endpos` appends a fixed CANONICAL_ANSWER + `\n\n` to the prefix and scores log p(※) at the END of the answer — the position where training pinned the marker (every training row ends `<answer>\n\n※`). Single-geometry probes risk mislabeling every bystander scenario C if the implant lives at one geometry but the probe sees the other; dual probes let the analyzer triangulate. The canonical-answer text is HELD CONSTANT across all 27 bystanders × 20 prompts as an internal control — only the (system, user) varies, so per-cell variance is attributable to the persona × prompt × step combination, not to the answer text. The smoke test (§4.2(g)) also runs in `pos0` geometry to catch chat-template rendering bugs cheaply at step 10.
- **Pod intent `lora-7b` (1× H100).** Per §9 parallelism analysis. The 4× H100 spec would not amortize for a sequential 3.5-hour run.
- **Pod auto-terminate after upload PASS.** Per CLAUDE.md / `/issue` Step 8 default. No `keep-running` tag.
