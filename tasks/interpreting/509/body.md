# The #502 residual-stream predictor for marker leakage does not transfer to fact or sycophancy leakage at its pre-registered cell, and the syco signal that does survive lives in early end-of-system layers, not the L19-L24 last-prompt ridge (MODERATE confidence)

## Human TL;DR

**Headline.** the residual-stream geometry that predicted marker-leakage transfer in #502 does NOT generalize at its own winning cell — on sycophancy the pre-registered L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL anchor is flat (|ρ| = 0.09, p = 0.30, n = 138) and the wider L19-L24 last-prompt ridge is essentially zero (mean |ρ| = 0.02 across 18 cells), so #502's specific layer-metric-extraction recipe is marker-specific; the syco arm DOES carry a real signal but it lives at a different cell (early-layer end-of-system cosine, |ρ| ≈ 0.49 with p < 0.001 at L0-L8 and CI excluding zero), and on fact the bake-off underperforms #494's own coarse predictors at the pre-registered cell and collapses completely once you control for #500's bystander prior.

**Takeaways.**
- on sycophancy with full production statistics (n=138 pairs, 2000-perm null, 5000-rep cluster bootstrap), the pre-registered L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL anchor that won #502 lands at |ρ| = 0.09 (p = 0.30, CI = [-0.27, +0.35]) and the L19-L24 ridge of cloud-aware metrics at the last prompt token averages to |ρ| = 0.02 across 18 cells — both indistinguishable from null
- on the same syco panel, a different cell DOES find signal: the L0-L8 end-of-system cosine cells reach |ρ_attenuation-adjusted| ≈ 0.43-0.49 with permutation p < 0.001 and CI clearing zero; the L02 cell ranks comedian (software-engineer's surprise high-leakage bystander) 5/23 by predicted leakage where every coarse predictor in #470 ranked it 23/23
- on fact (smoke; reliability_y=1.0 fallback, Path B per-seed SE reconstruction NOT yet run), the L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL anchor sits at ρ_fe = +0.36 with the WRONG sign vs the marker-leakage anchor and a delta_rho of -0.25 vs #494's fact-slice-JS coarse predictor on the same 25 pairs; the bake-off underperforms the coarse predictors at its own recipe cell
- even the fact-arm search-best cell (end-of-system L1 cosine, |ρ_fe| = 0.67 across 18 substrate-FE residualized cells) collapses to |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03 once #500's bystander-prior-on-the-fact is partialled out alongside substrate — #500's "the prior is the only consistently supported predictor" survives the bake-off
- the right honest read is "the recipe is marker-specific at its registered cell; there IS persona-distance signal for sycophancy on this panel but it sits in a different part of the predictor zoo, and a different region of the residual stream"

**How this updates me.** I'm less confident that the specific cell that won on #502 (last_prompt × L22 × gauss_kl on the L19-L24 ridge) is a universal persona-distance axis — the pre-registered triple-trigger (|ρ| ≥ 0.40 attenuation-adjusted + Δρ ≥ 0.15 over coarse + permutation p < 0.05) is cleared by neither arm at that cell. I'm somewhat more confident that there is SOME persona-distance signal for sycophancy transfer on the #411 panel, but it's at a different cell entirely (early end-of-system cosine, not late last-prompt cloud metrics), so the right framing is "different behavior, different cell" rather than "behavior-agnostic axis." A production fact-arm rerun with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would close the smoke caveat, and a held-out probe pool would address the selection inflation across the ~950-1065 scored cells.

## TL;DR

### Motivation

#502 found that one specific cell in a ~1500-cell predictor sweep (last-prompt × layer-22 × Gaussian-KL on an L19-L24 ridge) predicted marker-leakage transfer between personas at Spearman |ρ| = 0.79 on the cleanest training checkpoint. The natural next question is whether that residual-stream geometry tracks persona distance for the *behavior* the marker proxies for, or just for the marker itself. Two prior null results pointed at the second answer: #494 found pooled persona-distance predictors null on fact-teaching adapters (the contrastive #444 + the positive-only #381/#389/#390/#192 line), and #470 found a single-layer cosine + an output-distribution JS null on the #411 sycophancy panel. The critical gap was that BOTH of those nulls used the coarse single-layer cosine + output-distribution JS predictor family — exactly the family #502 showed gets beaten by the full 28-layer × 9-cloud-metric × 3-extraction-point bake-off. This run closes that gap: it takes #502's full bake-off recipe, points it at the two non-marker behaviors using the already-trained adapters and stored leakage matrices, and asks whether the L19-L24 last-prompt Gaussian-KL / MMD / Wasserstein-2 ridge that won on markers also wins on facts or sycophancy. Three outcomes pre-registered, all informative: **generalizes** (the recipe is a behavior-agnostic persona-distance axis), **marker-specific** (the #502 signal is specific to the marker construct, the prior nulls were not resolution artifacts), or **behavior-dependent** (one arm yes, one arm no — tells us which axis of behavior the geometry tracks). The pre-registration trigger for "generalizes" required attenuation-adjusted |ρ_best_cell| ≥ 0.40 AND Δρ ≥ 0.15 over the coarse predictor AND within-stratum permutation p < 0.05 AND ≥ 3 of the L19-L24 ridge cells meeting all three thresholds AND the ridge mean reaching within Δρ = 0.15 of the #502 non-stylized 156-pair anchor of -0.58 (NOT the full-panel ρ = -0.79, which the plan correctly identified as an over-anchor because ~80% of its CV R² lift came from three stylized characters that neither downstream panel contains).

### What I ran

A single Qwen-2.5-7B run, no retraining. For each arm I ran the existing 28-layer × 9-cloud-metric × 3-extraction-point bake-off driver across that arm's persona panel under each persona's verbatim system prompt, computed 9 pairwise cloud-distance metrics + a next-token JS baseline at every layer-extraction-point cell, and scored each cell's distance matrix against that arm's stored leakage matrix with length-partial Spearman ρ residualized for the appropriate fixed effects, plus a within-stratum permutation null, plus a 5000-rep cluster bootstrap CI.

- **Fact arm** — 9 personas across 5 training substrates (marine biologist, Zelthari scholar, Qwen default, local historian, local resident, assistant, software engineer, kindergarten teacher, no-system control). The leakage target is the 26-cell stored matrix from #494's `regression_data.csv` (per-(teach → bystander) `leak_rate` across the #444 + #192 + #381 / #389 / #390 adapter line, 3-seed averaged). The bake-off scores against the substrate-FE residualized panel (n = 18 cells after the substrate-FE step) and reports the per-cell double-FE Spearman after partialling out both substrate AND #500's `bystander_logprob` prior. Probe pool: 500 mixed-distribution probes inherited from #502.
- **Sycophancy arm** — 24 personas across the #411 6-source × 23-bystander panel (138 off-diagonal cells; the same 6 sources × 23 bystanders panel the parent paper reported). The leakage target is the per-(source, bystander) Δ from #411's frozen leakage matrix (10 rollouts × 50 probes × Claude Haiku judge per cell, single seed 42). The bake-off scores against the source-FE residualized 138-cell panel and reports a per-cell binomial-SE attenuation-adjusted ρ_fe_adj alongside a within-source permutation null (B = 2000) and a 5000-rep cluster bootstrap CI clustered on source. Probe pool: #411's 50 held-out wrong-claim probes.
- **Three comparison anchors carried into every figure:** (1) #494's coarse predictors (`cosine_a_L21`, `cosine_b_L21`, `js_on_topic`, `fact_slice_js`, `bystander_logprob`) on the same pairs, so any bake-off cell's "lift" is the delta vs the coarse cell, NOT vs zero; (2) #502's full-panel ρ_ΔG = -0.748 on 240 marker-leakage pairs; (3) #502's non-stylized 156-pair ρ_ΔG = -0.581 — the apples-to-apples anchor since neither downstream panel contains the three stylized characters (pirate / comedian / villain) that contributed ~80% of the #502 lift.

Pre-registered cell: `last_prompt × L22 × gauss_kl × centered` (the exact #502 winner). Pre-registered ridge: the 18 cells at `last_prompt × {L19..L24} × {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2}`. Search-best cell: the cell within the per-arm ~950-1065 non-saturated scored cells with the largest |ρ_fe_adj|, reported with the explicit "selected from ~950+ candidates" disclaimer per the plan §6.3 selection-correction rule. The pre-registered cell is what the marker-leakage winner WAS; the search-best cell is what THIS arm's bake-off would have picked on its own if there were no prior cell to anchor on; the ridge mean is what doesn't depend on the lucky single cell.

A 953-cell fact-arm scoring + 1065-cell syco-arm scoring (out of 1626 metric files per arm; the difference is the partition-merge step deliberately marking some cells N/A — saturated predictors, missing variance, etc. — and skipping them with the `n_excluded_saturated` + `n_skipped_na` counts in the scoring JSONs). The fact arm ran in **smoke mode** because Path B per-seed SE reconstruction from the per-seed judged completions on HF was not implemented for this run — the fallback sets `reliability_y = 1.0` on every fact cell, which means `ρ_fe_adj = ρ_fe` (no attenuation correction). The syco arm ran in production mode end to end. The four code-fix landings during the run (per-pid tmp-file race in `_write_json_atomic`; `system_prompt=None` NoneType crash in the extraction prompt builder for the no-system control conditions; the merge-step `_expected_eos` partition AssertionError on intentional `system_prompt=None` Class-A conditions; and the `_matrix_to_dict` AttributeError on the intentional `matrix:null` N/A cells from the saturation screener — fixed by returning None + counting `n_cells_skipped_na` + a ≥25% sanity-cap guard) all advanced the pipeline without altering the recipe or the panels.

### Findings

#### At its own pre-registered cell and at the L19-L24 ridge, the #502 recipe does not generalize to either behavior

A dashed grey line at |rho| = 0.40 marks the plan section 6.2 pre-registered trigger that neither downstream arm clears.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/f51e6f9963b7cc904e43403447043059c1ad3116/figures/issue_509/anchors_vs_502.png)

> **Figure.** *The marker-leakage predictor cell does not generalize to fact or sycophancy leakage at the pre-registered #502 anchor or at the L19-L24 ridge.* Each bar is one absolute length-partial Spearman ρ on the bake-off cell named in the x-axis label. Solid bars = single-cell anchors; hatched bars = ridge means across the 18 cells in the `last_prompt × {L19..L24} × {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2}` band. The two #502 reference bars (blue, n=240 and n=156) are from the parent's `regression/loc_ep1.json`; the four #509 bars are the headline cell + ridge mean per arm (fact orange = smoke mode with reliability_y=1.0; syco green = production mode with B_perm=2000 and B_bootstrap=5000). The pre-registered triple-trigger on the syco arm comes back loud and negative: attenuation-adjusted |ρ_fe_adj| at last-prompt × L22 × gauss_kl × centered = 0.089, well below the 0.40 floor; within-source permutation p = 0.30, well above the 0.05 ceiling; cluster-bootstrap 95% CI = [-0.27, +0.35], straddling zero. The L19-L24 last-prompt ridge across {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2} averages to |ρ| = 0.015 with zero of the 18 cells clearing |ρ| = 0.40 — not one ridge cell, not just a near-miss. The strict reading of the plan §6.2 pre-registration is **marker-specific verdict at the #502 cell on the syco arm** with the binding constraint that the comedian-recovery rank at the L22 cell is 23/23 (the same failure mode every coarse predictor in #470 hit), confirming the cell really has no purchase on this panel.

The fact arm's number at the same cell looks larger (|ρ_fe_adj| = 0.36 on 25 substrate-FE residualized cells) but is misleading in three ways. First, smoke mode pins `reliability_y = 1.0`, so the 0.36 is `ρ_fe` itself, NOT the attenuation-adjusted number the §6.2 trigger asks for (the true `ρ_fe_adj` is computable only with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction, which this run skipped). Second, the SIGN is positive — the wrong direction for "closer personas leak more" relative to the marker anchors at -0.58 / -0.75. Third, the per-cell `coarse_lift.delta_rho` on the same 25 pairs is **-0.252**: the bake-off cell underperforms #494's fact-slice-JS coarse predictor by 0.25 ρ-points on the same data. So even before reading the ridge mean (|ρ| = 0.08 on 18 cells, similar story), the fact arm fails to clear the Δρ ≥ 0.15 trigger over the coarse predictor at the cell that was supposed to be the universal winner. A production rerun with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would tighten the numbers, but the ridge mean and the coarse-predictor delta_rho already say the recipe does not transfer here.

The 240-pair full-panel anchor at |ρ| = 0.75 is plotted as the optimistic reference (since the plan rejected this anchor explicitly), and the non-stylized 156-pair anchor at |ρ| = 0.58 is the apples-to-apples reference. The two downstream arms reach roughly 12% and 16% of the non-stylized anchor at the pre-registered cell, and 1-14% at the ridge.

The Gaussian-KL right column shows the recipe's specific metric family is flat at every extraction point through the ridge.

The sycophancy bake-off's strongest single cell is at end-of-system × L02 × cosine × centered with attenuation-adjusted |ρ_fe_adj| = 0.489, permutation p = 0.0005 (the floor for the B = 2000 permutation null), 5000-rep cluster bootstrap 95% CI = [-0.61, -0.28] excluding zero, and comedian-recovery rank 5/23 (where every coarse predictor in #470 ranked comedian 23/23). The signal isn't a single lucky cell either: every cosine-centered cell at end-of-system between L0 and L13 reaches permutation p ≤ 0.001, with |ρ_fe_adj| in the 0.31-0.49 range and CIs clearing zero for the L0-L8 cluster. On the LIVE-cells-only subset (the 21 cells where |Δ| > 0.10 per the plan §4.2.6 screen, which restricts to where the leakage target has real variance instead of binomial noise floor), the same cell holds at |ρ_fe_live| = 0.473 on 21 cells. That's a real, replicated, multi-cell signal at the construct (cross-persona sycophancy transfer) — but at a different cell from the recipe.

The honest reading is **behavior-dependent at a different cell, not at the recipe cell**: the residual stream IS carrying persona-distance signal that tracks sycophancy leakage on the #411 panel, but the signal sits in early-layer end-of-system geometry rather than in the late-layer last-prompt cloud-metric ridge that won on markers. End-of-system reads the residual at the boundary BETWEEN the system prompt and the user turn — that boundary captures how the persona-conditioning has shifted the model's state before any user content arrives, which is plausibly a more general "what persona am I being asked to play" signal than the last-prompt residual after the model has consumed both the persona AND the probe. The L02 cell beats #470's pooled cosine + JS reads not because it's a richer predictor but because (a) the substrate-FE residualization fixes the source-confounding #470 didn't apply, and (b) end-of-system is a different extraction point than the L20 mid-stack pool #470 used. The recipe's specific contribution — late-layer cloud-aware metrics on the last prompt token — doesn't add anything to this signal: the right column shows Gaussian-KL flat across every extraction point through the L19-L24 ridge.

The cell IS selected from ~1065 scored candidates, so the |ρ| = 0.49 number carries the same selection-inflation caveat the plan §6.3 carried forward from #502. The honest defense against that selection inflation is the **L0-L8 cluster structure** (a whole band of end-of-system cosine cells in the same direction with the same permutation null clearing the floor) and the **comedian-recovery diagnostic** (the single qualitative test #470 designed to distinguish a real predictor from a noise-fit), both of which point in the same direction as the search-best ρ.

#### On fact, the bake-off underperforms #494's coarse predictors and collapses under #500's bystander-prior control

The bake-off's win at the search-best cell is entirely the prior; once you control for what the bystander already knew about the fact (per #500), there's nothing left to explain.

Two failures stack on this arm. At the **pre-registered cell**, the L22 last-prompt Gaussian-KL bake-off cell reaches |ρ_fe| = 0.36 (smoke mode, so this is the un-attenuation-adjusted number) on the n = 25 cells where the cell has variance, but the same 25 cells fit #494's coarse `fact_slice_js` predictor at |ρ| = 0.59 — the bake-off cell's `coarse_lift.delta_rho` is **-0.252**. The plan §6.2 trigger asked for Δρ ≥ 0.15 OVER the coarse predictor on the same pairs; what the bake-off delivers at its own pre-registered cell is Δρ = -0.25 in the WRONG direction. The recipe's cloud-aware metric on the last prompt token is a *worse* predictor of fact transfer on this panel than #494's published baselines.

At the **search-best cell** (end-of-system × L01 × cosine × centered, selected from the ~950 non-saturated scored cells), the substrate-FE residualized |ρ_fe| reaches 0.67 — a number that LOOKS like it clears the |ρ| ≥ 0.40 trigger. The collapse happens at the next step. Once you partial out both substrate AND #500's `bystander_logprob` (the bystander's own prior on the taught fact, which #500 established as the dominant predictor of fact leakage), the double-FE Spearman drops to |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03. The 0.67 was carrying #500's prior, not orthogonal-to-the-prior persona-distance signal. The plan §5 conditions table explicitly built `fact_arm/double_fe` as the test for "does the bake-off lift OVER #500's prior" — the answer at the search-best cell is no.

The smoke-mode caveat does NOT rescue this: Path B per-seed SE reconstruction would attenuation-adjust the bake-off cells UPWARD (since reliability_y ≤ 1.0), but it would attenuation-adjust the coarse `fact_slice_js` and `bystander_logprob` predictors UPWARD by the same factor, so the relative ordering (bake-off cells underperform coarse, and search-best collapses under prior-control) is invariant to the attenuation correction. A production rerun would tighten the absolute ρ values and would let me put a cluster-bootstrap CI on the |ρ_double_fe| = 0.03 to verify that it really is indistinguishable from zero rather than a small but non-zero residual, but the qualitative verdict (the bake-off doesn't beat the prior on fact) follows from the un-adjusted numbers already.

The honest reading is **marker-specific verdict on fact, full stop**: the recipe doesn't beat #494's published coarse predictors at its own cell, and the search-best cell's lift collapses under #500's prior control. Both #494's pooled-predictor null and #500's "the prior is the only predictor" verdict survive the full-resolution bake-off.

The smoke caveat is the binding constraint on calling the fact arm a clean negative result vs a noise-limited one. A production rerun with Path B per-seed SE reconstruction (the per-seed judged JSONLs are on HF at `issue444_real_figure_provenance/.../reanalysis_5way/judged_*_seed{42,137,256}.jsonl` for the 18 #444 cells + `issue192_persona_spread/raw_completions/fact_seed{42,137,256}_e1*.json` for the #192 cells; the substring scorer is a one-CPU-pass to rerun) would let me put per-cell cluster-bootstrap CIs on the search-best double-FE number, and would tighten the |ρ_fe_adj| numbers across the bake-off. The CPU cost is ~30 min for the per-seed reconstruction and ~15 min for the permutation null at the bake-off scale.
