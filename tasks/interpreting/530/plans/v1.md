## 1. Goal

Verbatim from `body.md` § Goal:

> Re-test the bubble-vs-barrier geometry of a single contrastive negative at a **de-saturated anchor** — one where bystander marker emission sits below the argmax ceiling — so leakage is read as a change in **magnitude** rather than as residual rank structure on an already-ceilinged outcome. Concretely: determine whether #504's barrier signal (`shadow_angle` partial ρ > 0) and its anti-bubble signal (`d_nearest_neg_nd` partial ρ < 0) survive when bystanders are unsaturated, and whether the anti-bubble sign (the suspected partialling/saturation artifact) holds or collapses.

Operational: the same partial-Spearman regression of held-out ΔG on the 6 #504 predictors, re-fitted at a checkpoint where bystanders are below the argmax ceiling, so the two signals can be cleanly interpreted as magnitude effects rather than rank-structure on saturated outcomes.

## 2. Prior work

- **#504 (parent)** at lr=1e-4 / 3 epochs / r=8 / α=32 found `shadow_angle` partial ρ = +0.335 (Holm p < 1e-12) and `d_nearest_neg_nd` partial ρ = −0.342 (sign FLIPPED from raw ρ = +0.223), both across 432 rows × 2 seeds. **Bystanders 91–96% at argmax = marker, median bystander ΔG ≈ 24 nats against a ~25–30 nat ceiling.** The signals are residual rank structure on saturated outcomes; this is what #530 fixes.
- **#329** trained a marker at lr 5e-6 × 20 epochs and got source emission 99.6% / bystander emission **11.7%** — the empirically-demonstrated clean window for marker-only loss + this base model.
- **#478** at lr 5e-6 produced a clean sub-emission log-prob gradient with 0 bystander emission — same lr regime, validates the de-saturation lever.
- **#448 (saturation rule)** establishes that on-policy log-prob saturates at a fully-trained anchor and recipe knobs lose all dynamic range; geometry sweeps MUST use a less-trained anchor and read graded leakage off partially-leaked bystanders. `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` codifies this.
- **#432 → #456** establish the on-policy DV (model writes its own response; read `log P(target)` trained − base at the post-response slot). Teacher-forced canned responses produced artifacts that dissolved on the on-policy re-run; never substitute back.
- **#504 v3/v4 KL pitfall.** A bystander read 24 nats KL-from-base with zero marker emission; KL measures total next-token-distribution change (EOS/punctuation reallocation), not marker mass. The DV stays marker-specific `log P(※)`; **do NOT swap to full-vocab KL** (rule: `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`).

## 3. Hypotheses (all interpretable)

- **H1 (barrier survives + anti-bubble survives).** Both #504 signals replicate: `shadow_angle` partial ρ > 0 AND `d_nn` partial ρ < 0, both Holm-significant, 2/2 seed sign agreement, at the de-saturated anchor.
- **H1′ (barrier survives, anti-bubble collapses).** Predicted modal outcome. `shadow_angle` partial ρ > 0 holds; `d_nn` partial ρ flips back toward its raw +0.223 or becomes statistically indistinguishable from zero — confirming the anti-bubble sign was a partialling/saturation artifact.
- **H0 (both wash out).** Neither signal is Holm-significant at the de-saturated anchor; #504's signals were entirely residual rank structure on saturated outcomes; the geometry design cannot read bubble or barrier at this composition.
- **Degenerate (no non-saturated window exists).** No seed at lr=5e-6 clears the de-saturation gate before source falls below the [5, 12]-nat band. Publish as a negative: the source+marker+composition has no non-saturated band where the geometry read is possible at r=8 / α=32. The natural next plan is a smaller rank.

## 11. Decision Rationale

**Marker-only learning rate: 5e-6.** Why: the single load-bearing manipulated variable, drops the lr from #504's 1e-4 to push the band-stop later in step-space while keeping bystanders below the argmax ceiling at the stop. **Source: #329 (demonstrated lr 5e-6 × 20 epochs → source 99.6% / bystander 11.7%) + #478 (clean sub-emission log-prob gradient at lr 5e-6).** Alternatives considered: (a) lr=1e-5 — too close to #504's hot regime, body.md specifies ≤5e-6; (b) lr=2e-6 (#504-v1) — empirically refuted at #504 v1 phase 0 single-cell (source ΔG = +0.259 nats / 0 emission, 2026-06-08T01:27:30Z), implant never installs. 5e-6 is the established clean window.

**Epoch ceiling: 12.** Why: headroom for the band-stop to halt training, not a substitute for it. At lr 5e-6, #329 needed ~20 epochs at a single-source composition; the 4-arm contrast sweep here trains on 400 rows/epoch and is expected to hit the band-stop at ~6–10 epochs. **Source: #329.** Alternatives considered: 20 epochs (#329's count) — wastes wall-clock since band-stop will fire earlier here. 5 (#504-v3) — too low; the band-stop wouldn't fire and the run would terminate on the epoch counter, defeating the de-saturation gate.

**LoRA r=8 / α=32 / dropout=0.05 / all-linear / rsLoRA.** Why: body.md is explicit ("LoRA rank stays at #504's 8 — recipe is explicit that 'steps and LR schedule are decisive, not rank,' so rank is held fixed to keep the change single-variable"). **Source: #477 `RANK_ALPHA_MAP_V5`** (validated for Qwen-2.5-7B + marker-only loss on this composition). Alternatives considered: r=16 (#504 v5 fallback) — would be the next plan's variable, not this plan's.

**Marker band-stop [5, 12] nat.** Why: the demonstrated stop signal across `#492`/`#504`/`#519`; bystander non-saturation is checked downstream of the stop, NOT used to re-gate the callback (body.md is explicit). **Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` + #492 + #504-v3.** Alternatives considered: re-gating the callback on bystanders (the launch-prompt framing) — explicitly rejected by body.md as breaking single-variable discipline.
