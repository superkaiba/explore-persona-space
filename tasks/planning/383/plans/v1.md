## 1. Goal

Determine whether parent #365's null finding — "no factor implants the
marker selectively; whatever lifts source rate lifts bystander leakage in
lockstep" — survives at recovered source-rate magnitudes. The parent's
mean 0.009 / 13-of-72-cells-non-zero / 0% median / 18% max source rates
sat 5-10× below comparable Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct marker work (#271, #295,
#337 all reported 0.18-0.67). If the underlying signal was being
suppressed by three independent training/eval confounds (train/eval
prompt mismatch via B-suffix, distribution-shifted C=1 eval, ratio
imbalance from 1:2 positive:negative), then the leakage:source ratio at
suppressed magnitudes is not informative about the leakage:source ratio
at recovered magnitudes. The re-run isolates that question by holding
everything else fixed against parent #365 and varying only the three
recipe corrections in their joint configuration.

## 2. Hypothesis

(Copied verbatim from `body.md`.) The three methodological corrections
will recover source rates from #365's suppressed 0% median / 18% max
range into the 0.18-0.67 range seen in #271/#295/#337. Within those
recovered cells, at least one A/B/C/D recipe knob will produce a
leakage:source ratio meaningfully lower than #365's near-1.0 coupling —
i.e. the marker can be implanted selectively into the source persona once
the three confounds are removed.

## 3. Kill criterion

(Copied verbatim from `body.md`.)

1. **Recovery succeeds, coupling persists.** All three corrections raise
   source rates into the 0.18-0.67 range AND the leakage:source ratio
   stays at or above #365's level across every A/B/C/D cell. This
   confirms #365's null with stronger evidence (conclusion holds at
   recovered magnitudes, not just suppressed ones) and the qualitative
   claim is upgraded from LOW to MODERATE/HIGH confidence.
2. **Recovery fails.** Source rates remain <0.10 median even after
   stripping the suffix, matching train/eval framing, and restoring the
   1:1 positive:negative ratio. The recovery premise itself fails; the
   experiment does not update #365's null, and the follow-up question
   becomes "what *else* is suppressing source rates."

The hypothesis is supported only when source rates recover into the
comparable range AND at least one cell shows leakage:source ratio
meaningfully below #365's coupling level. In that case #365 needs a
retraction note and the follow-up question is "which knob, why."

## 5. Method delta vs parent #365

Exactly three code-level methodological corrections, ALREADY committed at
`32ce24ef`, PLUS one mechanical pre-launch plumbing change (NEW in v2):

1. **B-suffix strip from training rows** (`data_prep.py::_build_positive_rows`
   and `_build_negative_rows`). The `user_suffix` parameter is retained on
   the signatures for back-compat with the data-gen callers (Claude /
   base-Qwen still receive the suffix at generation time to hit the
   target completion length band), but the training row's `user_text` is
   now the bare question without the suffix. Parent #365 trained on
   `<question> Answer in one sentence.` / `<question> Answer as a short
   essay...` but evaluated on `<question>`, creating a 5-10× train-eval
   prompt-distribution mismatch.

2. **Per-cell train-matched eval system prompt** (`__main__.py` cell-train
   mode persists `system_prompt_text` in the `prepared_dataset` sidecar;
   cell-eval mode reads it back and overrides the source persona's entry
   in `EVAL_PERSONAS_24` before constructing `EvalConfig`). Bystanders
   stay on their canonical short-persona prompts (matching all 24 panel
   personas would require lexicons that currently exist for only the 3
   sources — out of scope for this re-run, flagged as future follow-up).
   The C=1 cells in parent #365 trained on `"Background context: ..."`
   framed prompts but evaluated under the canonical `"You are X."` panel;
   that was measuring distribution shift, not the framing knob.

3. **`--pos-per-source` 200 → 400** (default in `__main__.py`). Parent
   #365's 23-bystander panel × ~17 examples per bystander gave 400
   negatives total against 200 positives — a 1:2 positive:negative ratio
   that pressured the model toward "don't emit marker". Raising
   positives to 400 restores 1:1 without narrowing the bystander panel.
   Training rows per cell: 200+400 → 400+400 = 800 total.

4. **PRE-LAUNCH plumbing: `--issue 383` threaded through dispatcher →
   `__main__.py` → `training.train_one_cell`** (NEW in v2; ~40 lines
   across 3 files + 1 unit-test file). This is not a methodological
   change — it is a mechanical fix that prevents the
   `dispatch_factor_screen_365.py --resume` short-circuit from
   false-skipping all 72 cells against parent #365's
   `adapters/issue_365/` Hub objects. Without this fix, the resume probe
   would find all 72 issue_365 adapters from parent's successful round-16
   pass, return `"hub"` for every cell, and the
   `_training_stage` would skip training entirely — pool generation
   would run but training would not, producing zero `metrics.json` files.

Everything else is identical to parent #365's final round-16
configuration: same 24 valid cells per source after the A=0 × C=1 drop,
same seed 42 baseline, same LoRA r=32 / α=64 / lr=1e-5 / 3 epochs / batch
4 × grad_accum 4 / max_length 2048, same vLLM eval at K=5 × 20 questions
× 24 personas / `max_new_tokens=2048`, same paired pivotal bootstrap CIs
over 1000 resamples.

### 5b. Why this plumbing avoids the secondary upload-blocker

The fact-checker noted that `train_one_cell` defaults `hf_upload=False`
and `__main__.py` never sets it. Parent #365's adapters reached the Hub
via the `/issue` Step 8 upload-verifier OR a manual top-3 push after
aggregation — `dispatch_factor_screen_365.py` itself does not upload.
The same path applies to #383: `hf_upload=False` per cell is the
intentional default (only top-3 × 1-seed adapters per source need Hub
upload; the rest live on the pod volume until termination), and the
plumbing change only fixes the *resume probe* and the *future upload
path* in case a downstream uploader is invoked. The Step 8
upload-verifier will be responsible for syncing the top-3 adapters per
source under `adapters/issue_383/` after aggregation. No mid-cell
auto-upload is introduced.

  dispatcher.
- **cell-eval phase**: subprocess loads merged via fresh vLLM, runs
  24-persona panel + 24-random-control panel, scores `[ZLT]` substring
  rate, writes `metrics.json`, then `merged/` is deleted to honor the
  MooseFS quota.

## 9. Evaluation

**Primary metric:** case-insensitive `[ZLT]` substring rate per persona,
computed by `eval/trait_scorers.py::evaluate_markers()` over the K=5 × 20
questions = 100 completions per (LoRA, persona).

**Eval config:** vLLM batched generation, `temperature=1.0`,
`top_p=0.95`, K=5 completions, 20 questions, `max_new_tokens=2048`. The
2048 cap is mandatory because #260/#297 showed 512-token marker eval
silently truncates late markers; this re-run inherits the parent's 2048
setting.

**Eval panel:** 24 personas = `librarian` + `surgeon` + `programmer`
(sources) + 21 bystanders. For a given source's LoRA, source-rate is
the diagonal marker rate (100 completions on the source's own prompt),
bystander rate is the mean over the 23 non-source personas (per
`persona_panel.py`'s `BYSTANDER_PANEL_SIZE = 23` invariant — the 2
sibling sources DO count as bystanders for THIS cell). Per-bystander
rates are reported so leakage concentration is visible.

**Random-control panel:** 24 neutral background prompts under the same
K=5 × 20 questions configuration. Aggregated as
`mean_random_control_rate` and `max_random_control_rate` per cell.

**Statistical analysis** (mirrors parent #365's aggregator):

- For each factor A-E: paired-flip matched pairs where only that factor
  flips. 16 matched pairs per source within the full 32-cell space;
  after dropping A=0 × C=1, the C and A pairs use 8 matched tuples per
  source while B/D/E use 12. Pooled across 3 sources: A and C use n=24
  pairs; B, D, E use n=36 pairs.
- Mean Δ on source-rate and Δ on leakage-rate per factor with paired
  pivotal bootstrap 95% CIs (1000 resamples, rng seed 42).
- **Selectivity Δ = Δsource − Δleakage** per factor; this is the
  conclusion-driving statistic. CIs computed pair-by-pair from the 72
  metrics files, then aggregated with paired pivotal bootstrap.
- Cluster-robust supplement: n=3-source cluster bootstrap with
  Rademacher weights, OLS with `t(df=n_clusters−1=2)≈4.30` for
  absolute-rate CIs. The selectivity differential is reported with both
  per-pair and cluster-robust CIs.
- A×B and B×E difference-of-differences interactions per parent's
  `interactions.csv`.
- Log-ratio (E1/E0) source-rate CI per source (`e_log_ratio.json`).
- In-domain vs. out-of-domain bystander leakage stratification
  (`leakage_stratified.json`).

**Apples-to-apples comparison with parent #365:** the analyzer's hero
figure plots #383 vs #365 source-rate distributions side-by-side over the
72 matched cells, plus the selectivity Δ per factor with both runs' CIs
overlaid.

## 11. Success criteria

**Recovery succeeded** (Stage A of the kill criterion): median source
rate across the 72 cells ≥ 0.15 AND max source rate ≥ 0.30.

**Threshold rationale (sharpened in v2):** Parent #365 had **mean source
rate 0.009** with only **13 of 72 cells non-zero**; median 0% / max 18%.
A median ≥ 0.15 threshold requires **≥ 37 of 72 cells to clear 15%
source rate** — i.e. a structural recovery, not a tail-driven artifact.
The 0.15 median + 0.30 max threshold puts the run squarely inside the
#271/#295/#337 comparable range (0.18-0.67) while leaving headroom
against parent's max (0.18). If the three corrections were each marginal
contributors, we'd expect ~25-30 cells non-zero (vs parent's 13); the
0.15 median is the cutoff for "joint recipe-fix recovered the source
signal across a majority of cells," not "one or two cells happened to
ignite."

**Conclusion-driving question** (only evaluated if recovery succeeded):
does any factor's selectivity Δ = Δsource − Δleakage have a 95 % paired
pivotal bootstrap CI strictly above 0, AND does that hold under the
n=3-source cluster-robust supplement? If yes → parent #365 needs a
retraction note and the follow-up question becomes "which knob, why."
If no → parent #365's null is upgraded from LOW to MODERATE/HIGH
confidence (the conclusion holds at recovered magnitudes, not just
suppressed ones).

**Quantitative thresholds for the clean-result body's confidence tag:**

- HIGH: recovery threshold met AND at least one factor's selectivity Δ
  CI strictly clears 0 under BOTH per-pair and cluster-robust bootstrap,
  AND the pattern replicates qualitatively in all 3 sources.
- MODERATE: recovery threshold met AND a clear conclusion (selectivity
  differential present-or-absent) but with at least one source
  dissenting, OR with cluster-robust CI marginal.
- LOW: recovery failed (median <0.10) OR results are too noisy to
  conclude either way at n=1 seed.

## 16. Incremental save protocol

The recipe-fix branch already implements per-phase incremental save (this
is the canonical pattern referenced in CLAUDE.md "Checkpoint per phase,
not at end"). Confirming the pattern is in place:

- **Pool stage** writes each (source, A, B, C) pool JSONL to
  `data/issue_383/pools/<src>/source-<src>_a<A>_b<B>_c<C>.jsonl` (and
  `..._offpolicy.jsonl`) IMMEDIATELY upon generation. If Claude
  rate-limits mid-source, the source's already-generated pools are on
  disk; `--resume` re-uses them. No "accumulate-in-memory and
  write-at-end" anti-pattern in `data_prep.py`.
- **Cell-train phase** writes
  `eval_results/issue_383/cell_<key>/source_<src>/seed_42/cell_train_outcome.json`
  IMMEDIATELY after merge, before the subprocess exits.
- **Cell-eval phase** writes `metrics.json` IMMEDIATELY after the panel
  + random-control eval completes, before the subprocess exits.
- **Dispatcher**'s `cell_complete_on_disk` resume probe checks
  `metrics.json` + adapter dir per-cell. If the dispatcher itself dies
  mid-sweep (round-4 silent death), restarting with `--resume` skips
  already-completed cells. Round-6 also probes the HF Hub model repo to
  skip cells whose adapter was uploaded by a prior run — with `--issue
  383`, the probe targets the EMPTY `adapters/issue_383/` namespace at
  Stage 2 launch.
- **Aggregator** is a one-shot pass at the end; it reads the 72
  already-on-disk metrics.json files. No data is held in memory across
  cells.

**Pinned pattern in the plan**: each (source, cell) pair is fully
self-contained — its data, training, eval, and metrics all live under
one directory and persist immediately on phase completion. The 72-cell
sweep loses at most ONE cell's work on a worst-case mid-cell crash;
everything before that is on disk. Matches the #377 incident remediation
pattern (don't accumulate-then-write).
