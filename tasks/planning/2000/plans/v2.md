   "Teammate / mid-session box dispatches" (placed after § Per-cell fit

     a fence ≥2× the pilot-extrapolated wall — full mechanics at § Per-cell

     per-behavior boxes by default (sibling of experiment-guidelines
     guideline 2's shardable-axis duty);
