# Plan — Task #2225: Context-position preventative steering vs the Persona Vectors method

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I'll fine-tune the same model on the Persona Vectors paper's four trait-inducing datasets while nudging its internal activations toward the trait during training, comparing the paper's own recipe (a direction read off the model's answers, applied at every token) against our variant (a direction read at the end of the prompt, applied only to prompt tokens), at one layer, a band of middle layers, and all layers.
- **What I expect:** the prompt-side variant will block trait acquisition about as well as the paper's method while touching the answer tokens (where the training loss lives) less, so quality and knowledge scores should hold up at least as well. The extra one-factor-at-a-time cells will tell us whether it's where the direction is read or where it's applied that does the work.
- **What would change my mind:** if the prompt-side variant lets the trait through at every strength that keeps the model coherent, or if a probe finds the trait hiding in the steered models' activations even when their behavior looks clean, I'd conclude the training pressure lands on answer tokens and prompt-side intervention can't relieve it.

## 1. Goal

Test whether persona directions extracted at the context position and applied ONLY to context tokens during finetuning prevent trait acquisition as well as or better than Persona Vectors' response-avg all-token preventative steering (arXiv 2507.21509 §5.2), across single-layer / middle-band / all-layer conditions, at matched coherence and capability, with attribution cells separating extraction position from steering position and a probe-based off-direction acquisition check. (Task #2225 Goal, verbatim.)

The paper's preventative steering adds the response-avg persona vector at every token position during finetuning. Its own monitoring results locate trait acquisition in a context-end shift along the persona direction, and its preventative-PROMPTING result shows a context-side token intervention relieves the training pressure. This experiment runs the continuous version of that context-side intervention — extract at context-end, steer only context tokens — and factorizes extraction position × steering position × layer regime so the source of any difference is attributable. This plan OPERATIONALIZES the roster fixed in the task body (E1–E3 × S1–S3 × L1–L3, configs A–I + P, 4 datasets); it does not redesign it.

**N/A — not a replication** as a whole; **configs A and B are a faithful replication** of the paper's §5.2 preventative steering (single-layer and Appendix J.3 all-layer), governed by `.claude/rules/replication-fidelity.md` + `.claude/rules/persona-vectors-recipe.md`, with the standing Sonnet-4.5-judge deviation and the named forced deviations listed in §12.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (primary, the Goal's core ask):** at matched coherence (≥80) and preserved MMLU, context-extract + context-steer (config C, single layer) prevents trait acquisition as well as or better than the paper's response-avg all-token method (config A): the paired post-ft trait-score difference Δ = score(C) − score(A) is ≤ 0 or statistically indistinguishable from 0.

**H2 (mechanism):** if trait pressure is context-mediated, S2 ≈ S1 within each extraction variant (context-only steering suffices) and S3 (response-only) carries most of S1's effect only if the pressure is response-mediated. The F/G/I cells attribute any C-vs-A difference to extraction position vs steering position.

**H3 (layer regime):** all-layer incremental steering (E vs B) closes any single-layer gap, mirroring the paper's J.3 finding that multi-layer prevention reaches near-baseline trait levels.

**H4 (off-direction acquisition):** a model whose trait score returns to base may still carry elevated trait information (probe AUC / projection shift) — predicted MORE likely for context-only steering if the optimizer routes trait pressure through response positions. A clean trait score with elevated probe/monitor reads is reported as partial prevention, never success.

Quantitative anchor: unsteered misaligned_2 finetunes score high on their trait (from #778's committed post-ft scores; base model ≈ 0 evil / ~4 sycophancy / ~20 hallucination per the paper Fig 5 note); "prevention" = returning the post-ft score toward base at coherence ≥80.

Primary registered contrast (per dataset, paired over the 20 shared eval questions): **C vs A at each config's own matched-coherence selected coefficient**; secondary registered contrast: **E vs B** (same pairing). Question-level paired bootstrap (10,000 resamples), per dataset plus the 4-dataset pooled mean of per-dataset Δ.

Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-question by eval_results/issue_2225/trait_scores/{config}_{dataset}_{coef}.json (steered arms) and eval_results/issue_2225/trait_scores/baseline_{dataset}.json (unsteered reference rows).

DISJOINT and exhaustive partition: Context-position-superior ⇔ Δ < 0 AND Δ's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Context-position-inferior ⇔ Δ > 0 AND Δ's 95% CI is wholly above 0; Statistical tie ⇔ otherwise.

(Interpretation note, outside the lattice: a "Statistical tie" with both arms at near-base trait scores and preserved MMLU supports the Goal's "as well as" reading; a tie with both arms failing to prevent is reported as joint failure, not support. The lattice is evaluated on the primary contrast per dataset and on the pooled mean; per-dataset verdicts are reported alongside the pooled one.)

### 4.4 Training-time steering hook (the new module)

`src/explore_persona_space/experiments/issue2225/steer_train.py`:

- `SteeringHook(vectors: dict[int, Tensor], alpha: float, mode: Literal["all","context","response","prefix"])` — forward hooks on `model.model.layers[ℓ]` adding `alpha · v_ℓ` (bf16, broadcast over the batch) to the block's output hidden states at masked positions, during TRAINING forwards only.
- Position masks per batch row: `context` = `attention_mask==1 & labels==-100` (prompt tokens under `completion_only_loss`); `response` = `labels!=-100`; `all` = `attention_mask==1`; `prefix` = token positions `< prefix_len`, where `prefix_len` (length of the system segment incl. its template tokens) is computed at dataset-map time from per-segment TOKEN IDS (issue1415 convention — never re-tokenize a concatenated string) and threaded to the hook via a batch column the trainer pops before `model(**inputs)`.
- Wiring: a thin `SteeredSFTTrainer(SFTTrainer)` whose `compute_loss` sets `hook.current_batch_masks` from `inputs` before delegating. Training entry: extend `scripts/issue778_finetune.py::train_single_cell` (verified above; paper-recipe hyperparameters live there) into `scripts/issue2225_train.py::train_steered_cell(family, coef, direction_spec, mask_mode, layer_spec, ...)` — same SFTConfig (r=32, α=64, rslora, lr=1e-5, 1 epoch, batch 2×ga8, completion_only_loss, wandb on), plus the hook.
- Ground truth for the hook semantics: the pinned paper repo's `training.py` steering implementation is read at implementation time; config A must match it exactly (the body's premise that their hook is unmasked/all-token is §12 A5; if the pinned code differs, A follows the paper's code and the S-arm definitions re-anchor to it, reported as a plan-deviation note).
- **Hook unit test (pre-launch):** on a 2-row toy batch, capture hidden states with and without the hook and assert `delta == alpha·v` exactly on masked positions and `0` elsewhere, for each of the 4 modes; assert the hook is NOT registered during post-train eval generation. This is the fix-engaged-style engagement proof; the training log emits one `[steer-hook] mode=<m> layers=<k> alpha=<a>` line per cell (the per-cell engagement breadcrumb).

**Why code, not a model call?** The only unstructured-data judgments (trait score, coherence, mistake-style narrow-domain check) ARE model calls (Sonnet judge). Everything else (hooks, masks, capture) is tensor plumbing where a model call is not applicable. Rejected alternative: none applicable.

- **Fail (i) or (iii) ⇒** implementation bug — fix before any fan-out (no reprovision until the fix-engaged signal is confirmed on the same pod).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **rs-LoRA r=32 / α=64 / lr=1e-5 / 1 epoch / batch 2×ga8.** Why: the paper's exact finetuning recipe; replication fidelity. Source: arXiv 2507.21509 §"Dataset and finetuning details" (read via MCP at plan time) + #778 (validated verbatim on this model+data). Alternatives: band-stop/dose-to-target rejected — fidelity wins for replication arms, and all arms must share the recipe for the contrast to be single-variable.
- **Preventative steering form `h_ℓ ← h_ℓ + α·v_ℓ` during training (steer TOWARD the trait).** Source: arXiv 2507.21509 §5.2. Alternatives: CAFT ablation and projection regularization — the paper's own comparisons (App. J.4/J.5) show steering dominates; out of scope.
- **Coherence threshold 80 for matched selection.** Source: arXiv 2507.21509 App. J.2 ("largest steering coefficient for which the model's coherence remains above 80") + §5.2 ("maintaining an average coherence score … above 80"). Alternatives: 75 (§5.1's inference-time floor) — rejected; 80 is the preventative-steering protocol.
- **Coefficient grids** L1 {0.5, 1.5, 3.0, 5.0}; attribution {0.5, 1.5, 3.0}; L2/L3 {0.25, 0.75, 1.5}. Source: paper worked examples (single-layer preventative coef 3.0 evil / 5.0 sycophancy+hallucination, App. J.8 tables; all-layer 0.5/1.25 on the fact task, App. J.7, with J.3's "comparable mitigation with smaller coefficients") — grid PLACEMENT for context-end directions `ungrounded — pilot-gated at P0` (the §7 gate exists for exactly this).
- **L1 = layer 20 (evil, sycophancy).** Source: arXiv 2507.21509 (paper text; #778's r_B[19] convention row). Hallucination L1: `ungrounded — read from the pinned repo's steering configs at implementation, else the §4.3 selection sweep`.
- **L2 = L1±4 (9 layers, in-band incremental).** Why: keeps L1 ⊂ L2 ⊂ L3 monotone and contains the known-informative layer; a literal middle-third would exclude it. Source: ungrounded — planner-finalized per the task body's delegation (needs no smoke; it is a design choice reported as such). Alternatives: literal middle third (10–18) rejected for the handicap argument.
- **Layer-incremental vectors for L3/L2.** Source: arXiv 2507.21509 App. J.3 (formula quoted in §4.2); band-start convention `v_inc_s = v_s` is our stated extension.
- **Extraction pipeline (5 pairs / 20+20 questions / 10 rollouts / judge-filter >50-<50 / drop-never-coerce).** Source: `.claude/rules/persona-vectors-recipe.md` + reuse of #778's faithful round-3 artifacts (inherit fast-path). E2's context-LEVEL filter: structural adaptation, stated in §4.2/§12 A6.
- **Rollout temperature 1.0.** Source: recipe step 3 (+#778 parity).
- **Judge instrument (Sonnet 4.5, N=6 @ 0.7, threshold 50; coherence single-draw).** Source: #778 (`issue778_lib.py:86-95`, grep-verified) — validated on this model + these traits; satisfies the N≥5 graded-score floor. Alternatives: paper's GPT-4.1-mini logit scoring — banned by the standing carve-out; GPT-4.1-mini coherence — replaced by the one-judge rule (named deviation, §12 A7).
- **`max_new_tokens=2048` eval generation.** Source: project free-generation floor (CLAUDE.md); cap-hit fraction reported with the 2% re-gen trigger. Alternatives: paper's own eval cap unknown — a lower cap risks silent truncation zeros.
- **MMLU 0-shot full-set via lm-eval[vllm].** Source: paper §5.2 names MMLU; harness + shot-count `ungrounded — within-run comparability is the claim, not absolute parity with the paper's number` (identical setting across arms + base; recorded as a scope note).
- **Opinions-cell steered trait = evil.** Why: single-trait-per-run matches the paper; evil is the paper's canonical exemplar with the largest base-to-misaligned spread. Source: arXiv 2507.21509 (single-trait steering design; steering examples App. J.8 are on Mistake Opinions II per trait).
