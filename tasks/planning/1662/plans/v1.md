- **What I'll do:** add one sentence-block to three places in the workflow docs (the pods contract in CLAUDE.md, the inline-run pod-safety paragraph in CLAUDE.md, and its mirror in the /issue skill) saying: when a follow-up pod's run is finished and its files are safely uploaded, kill the pod immediately — don't ask the user first. ## 5. Kill criteria (what would make this change wrong)

- The inserted clause contradicts the ISSUE-WIDE `keep-running` shield semantics — e.g. if it instructed terminating while the tag is deliberately held for ANOTHER live round on the same issue. Mitigated by the "removes the tag in the same step **when no other live round on issue N still needs the issue-wide shield**" wording (Edit 1) and the NAMED-next-round exception (Edits 1–3); if review shows a reading that terminates a legitimately-shielded pod, REVISE.
- The clause weakens the durability contract — any reading that permits terminate BEFORE uploads verify, or blesses `stop` as a substitute. Both are explicitly negated in every insert; if a reviewer finds a residual reading, REVISE.
- The clause conflicts with the #1485 mechanical guard — it does not: surgical `--name-suffix` destroys are guard-exempt by design (verified at plan time), and the bare-form command in Edits 2/3 applies to the primary-pod case where the completing session removes the tag in the same step. If the guard were ever extended to refuse surgical destroys, this contract would need the `--force-keep-running` escape named — out of scope today.
- A pin-test false positive (the pin failing on legitimate later rewording): the pin asserts short stable tokens + co-occurrence, whitespace-normalized, not full sentences — a rewrite keeping the contract keeps the pin green.
