- **Cross-capture position/activation-PARITY gates over PLAIN-TEXT "Role: content" renders fail on a mechanically-identifiable BPE-seam row tail — median leg clean, row-fraction leg fails; diagnose position METADATA before touching any threshold.** Under a plain-text render, BPE merges at the `"Assistant: "`+answer seam differ whenever the two captures' adjacent content differs, shifting span-derived positions (`context_pos = answer_start − 1`) by ±1 on exactly those rows — per-row parity is unverifiable there BY CONSTRUCTION while the median stays clean.
