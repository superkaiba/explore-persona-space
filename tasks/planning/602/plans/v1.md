## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The leakage formula needs to know, before training, which internal direction a behavior will push the model along. There are three ways to guess that direction from the untrained model: replay the actual training answers through it and read its internals, show it a few training examples in its prompt, or just describe the behavior in words. I'll compute all three guesses for six already-trained adapter families spanning five behavior types (a marker glyph in two training recipes, bad medical advice, a planted fact, refusing requests, and a second misalignment recipe) and compare each guess against the direction training actually moved the model. For two behaviors that realized direction is already stored; for the rest I measure it once on a short GPU pod, then everything else is desk work on the saved tensors.
- **What I expect:** the replay-the-training-data guess to come closest, the words-only guess to do worst (we already know the described marker direction points nowhere near the real shift), and the bad-medical-advice behavior to be the most predictable, since its training shift is close to one clean direction.
- **What would change my mind:** if even replaying the exact training data through the base model points nowhere near the realized shift, then no before-training estimate of "what gets written" exists on this model, and every downstream formula test has to use measured post-training writes instead. That would be a real (and cheap) answer, not a wasted run — it gates the GPU spend planned for the other formula predictions.

## 1. Goal

Test prediction P8 of the rank-1 leakage model: do the three base-model estimators of the behavior vector v_b — (E1) teacher-forced reads over the training data's own completions, (E2) in-context-demonstration conditioning, (E3) natural-language description conditioning — agree with each other and with the realized post-training shift v_b″ − v_b′ at the read-out layers, across five stored behavior families? Where they disagree, does substituting the realized write repair per-context predictions the estimated write gets wrong (failure localization: estimation problem vs update rule)? This gates all downstream GPU spend on formula tests that would otherwise run on an unvalidated estimated v_b.

## 3. Hypothesis

Falsifiable predictions, at the pre-registered construction (layer 14, mean-over-response read, K=4):

- **H1 (fidelity ladder):** cos(ŵ_E1, w_realized) > cos(ŵ_E2, w_realized) > cos(ŵ_E3, w_realized). **Unit = run-cell; registered denominator = the 31 run-cells** (3 marker + 3 EM-turner + 9 fact + 6 refusal + 6 EM-518 + 4 loc-474). A cell where ALL three estimators sit inside the random null is EXCLUDED from strict ladder calls and labeled "no ordering signal" (under a null, strict orderings fire randomly — P(E3 ≥ E1) = ½ per cell, so global-null cells would spuriously falsify H1). Confirmed if the ordering holds on ≥ 60% of eligible (≥1-estimator-above-null) cells; falsified if E3 ≥ E1 on a majority of eligible cells.
- **H2 (EM most estimable):** the EM-turner family scores highest absolute agreement (its realized write is one stable direction — the 0.96–0.98 figures are WITHIN-seed context-to-U1 alignment, not a cross-seed ceiling; the operative ceiling is the freshly computed cross-seed U1 cosine, `ceiling_seed` — recorded same-variant values run EM 0.65/0.78/0.90, marker 0.75/0.80/0.97 per `direction_consistency.json` — and estimator cosines are benchmarked against THAT), with at least one estimator clearing the validity bar (cos ≥ 0.3, above nulls). Falsified if all three estimators sit inside the random null (|cos| ≤ ~0.03 at d=3584) on EM. H2's cross-family "most estimable" read is computed on the variant-matched `base` operationalization (§4.3) and carries the recipe-confound scope caveat (§13).
- **H3 (marker hardest / known negative):** E3 on marker reproduces the #521 anchor — cos against the **top shared SVD direction w_shared (U1) of the realized marker shift matrix**, the vector #521 actually measured: recorded values −0.027 / −0.065 / −0.018 across seeds vs a 0.033 random 95th-pct floor. The anchor target is w_shared, NOT w_src (the source-context shift aligns to U1 only 0.24–0.32, so comparing against w_src would spuriously "fail" the anchor). Reproduction band: cos(ŵ_E3, w_shared) within ±0.15 of the seed's recorded value. The marker family fails the validity bar for E3 and plausibly for E2. Falsified (and interesting) if E1 on marker clears the bar — replaying the literal training rows recovers a direction prompting cannot.
- **H4 (repair localizes failure):** wherever an estimator fails the per-context BEHAVIORAL profile test (ρ < 0.3) substituting the realized write succeeds (ρ ≥ 0.5) ⇒ verdict "estimation failed, update rule intact." **Repair VERDICTS are registered on the behavioral-panel families ONLY (#474-loc, #518, #541)** — the activation half of the repair test is tautological (prof_real projects dv onto a w_shared fit from the same dv stack; for EM it passes at 0.96–0.98 by construction) and is reported separately as a geometry-consistency read (with a leave-one-context-out w_shared and the norm-only predictor ‖dv(c)‖ alongside — if the norm-only profile matches prof_real's ρ, the repair result is direction-insensitive and is narrated as a shift-field manipulation check, not direction recovery), NEVER as a repair verdict. Marker-519 and EM-turner (no behavioral panel) get no repair verdict. If the realized write *also* fails the behavioral profile test on the panel families, the update rule itself, not estimation, is implicated — P8 cannot then absolve the formula.
- **H5 (cross-estimator coherence):** estimators agree with *each other* (pairwise cos ≥ 0.5) on families where they agree with the realized write, and disagree where they fail — if they agree with each other but all miss the realized write, that indicates a shared prompting-geometry attractor distinct from the training shift (the #532 "geometry collapsed, prior carried signal" regime).

### 4.2 Estimator constructions (all base-model-only; every estimator is a CONTRAST of matched reads)

Common: bf16 forward, fp32 capture, residual stream layers {7, 14, 21} headline (+ {3, 27} stored mean-only on all *new* forwards for the exploratory dump), positions {last-token/slot, mean-over-response}, ChatML via `apply_chat_template`.

- **`est_tf` — Replay training answers (E1, expected best):** teacher-force n=100 positive training rows per (family, source) — system prompt exactly as in the mix row, user question, assistant = behavior-laden completion verbatim. Read mean-over-completion + last-completion-token. Contrast term v_b′ = same reads over the BASE model's own greedy completions to the same questions under the same system prompt. ŵ_E1 = mean(behavior reads) − mean(base-self reads). Marker family additionally stores the marker-slot read (position of token 83399) AND BOTH include-marker and exclude-marker mean-over-completion reads — any H3 "E1-marker clears the bar" claim must hold on the exclude-marker read against the `base`-variant realized side (token-identity discriminator); implementer asserts token 83399 is present at the expected slot in tokenized training rows and that no sequence cap truncates teacher-forced rows (marker mixes run ~1k tokens with the marker LAST). **Per-row/per-question reads for ALL THREE estimators (E1 per training row, E2 per probe × demo-resample, E3 per probe) are persisted in the `.pt` payloads (contractual — §6.5)** so split-half reliabilities are computable post-hoc; mean-only payloads make the SNR-ladder alternative unweighable.
- **`est_icl` — Show examples in prompt (E2, "the variant run so far"):** context = source system prompt + K demo pairs sampled seed-fixed from the training mix (multi-turn user/assistant), then probe question. Reads: (i) last-prompt-token (post-context, pre-response — the note's named variant), (ii) mean over the base model's greedy response under that context. Average over 20 probe questions × 3 demo resamples. Contrast = identical reads with zero demos. K=4 primary; K ∈ {2, 8} swept.
- **`est_desc` — Describe in words (E3, expected worst):** system prompt = source persona prompt + one-sentence behavior description. Same two reads, contrast = no-description reads. Marker description MUST reuse the exact #521 steering prompt so the cos ≈ −0.03 anchor is a true reproduction — the verbatim prompt is in git on main at `eval_results/issue_521/steering/v_marker.manifest.json` (positive: `Append ' ※' at the very end of every response.`; negative: `You are a helpful assistant.`); the producing module `steering_vectors.py` lives on `origin/issue-521` (commit `4eee3249…`), NOT on `origin/issue-551` (fact-checked). **`anchor_521` is computed as an EXACT #521 recipe reproduction, separate from the P8 est_desc estimator:** run the ported `steering_vectors.py` recipe verbatim — the 58-question disjoint pool per `v_marker.manifest.json`, positive `Append ' ※' at the very end of every response.` vs negative `You are a helpful assistant.`, base greedy responses, L14 mean-response read — and compare THAT vector to w_shared against the recorded per-seed values. The uniform-construction E3-marker cosine is reported separately as construction-sensitivity data and is never read against the anchor band (otherwise the kill criterion fires on estimator-definition drift, not rig divergence). Other descriptions drafted from the actual pool contents (fact sentence from `eval_results/issue_444/phase0_fact_candidates/fact_pick.json`; refusal/EM descriptions from reading 5 sample positives) and frozen at code review.

**ICL-demonstration grounding (required block):** (a) the demos mirror the exact training distribution the realized write came from — they ARE rows of the producing training mix, so the eval-task match is by construction; (b) they induce the behavior because they are the behavior's own training data, the strongest in-context elicitor available (note §Setup, A2's ICL extension); (c) cross-context dynamic range is inherent — the five families' demos differ in content, format, and length (marker-suffixed answers vs harmful medical advice vs fact assertions vs refusals), and within-family demo resampling (3 draws) measures demo-choice sensitivity.

Scoring pseudocode:

```python
for family, seed in cells:                      # marker/EM: 3 seeds; fact: 3; 518: 1; 474: per-source
    dv      = load_shifts(family, seed)         # {context: vec} per (layer, pos)
    w_src   = dv[family.source_context]
    w_shr   = top_svd_direction(stack(dv), sign_align=mean(dv))
    for est in (est_tf, est_icl, est_desc):
        w_hat = load_estimator(family, est, layer, pos)
        cos_src, cos_shr = cos(w_hat, w_src), cos(w_hat, w_shr)
        prof_est  = {c: dv[c] @ unit(w_hat) for c in panel}
        prof_real = {c: dv[c] @ unit(w_shr_loco[c]) for c in panel}  # leave-one-context-out w_shared (anti-tautology)
        prof_norm = {c: norm(dv[c]) for c in panel}                  # direction-free third predictor
        rho_act_geom = spearman(prof_est, prof_real)                 # geometry-consistency read ONLY (no repair verdict)
        rho_behav_est  = spearman(prof_est,  behavioral_panel)       # panel families only
        rho_behav_real = spearman(prof_real, behavioral_panel)       # the repair substitution (independent target)
        rho_behav_norm = spearman(prof_norm, behavioral_panel)       # direction-insensitivity check
# nulls: 10k random unit vecs; cross-family (w_hat_A, w_real_B) pairs reported BOTH including and
# excluding same-construct siblings (marker-519↔marker-474, EM-turner↔EM-518; sibling cells also
# reported separately as cross-recipe transfer reads); seed-seed ceiling (cross-seed U1 cosine)
```

## 11. Decision Rationale

Source: #551 sensitivity analysis. Alternative: `same` variant (rejected on cost; revisit if `base`-vs-`same` divergence on cached cells exceeds 0.1 cosine — checkable for free in Phase 0).
- **Panel 14×20** — Why: byte-identical reuse of the producing run's probe inputs keeps cached tensors directly comparable. Source: #521 `inputs/`.
- **K=4 primary, {2,8} swept** — Why: spans the project's prior ICL dose grid endpoints with a midpoint primary. Source: #537 grid (`icl_k2`/`icl_k8` cells); midpoint choice itself `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (cheap: K is swept anyway).
- **E1 n=100 rows** — Why: ~2× #521's steering-vector sample (58) for tighter mean estimate at trivial cost; subsample-stability check included. Source: #521 (58-generation precedent); the 100 figure is a robustness-checked heuristic.
- **Validity threshold cos ≥ 0.3** — Why: ~18× the random-null scale (1/√3584 ≈ 0.017), far below the EM seed ceiling (0.96–0.98), above the #521 anchor (−0.03); detects partial validity without demanding ceiling-level agreement. Source: derived from #521/#551 measured anchors + analytic null.
- **Repair thresholds ρ < 0.3 / ≥ 0.5** — Why: matches the project's recurring "predictor works" band (|ρ| 0.49–0.79 across #502/#489/#509 — band itself not fact-checked; a convention choice, not a data dependency). Source: P2 row of the note + #518.
- **Model id** — `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, used by every config in the repo; inherits validation (no `AutoConfig` smoke needed).
