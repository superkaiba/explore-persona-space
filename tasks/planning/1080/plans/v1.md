## 11. Decision Rationale

No ML hyperparameters exist in this task — `kind: infra`, no model training
("N/A — no hyperparameters in this task type"). The load-bearing CONTENT
decisions:

- **What:** require BOTH `epm:run-launched` AND the `keep-running` tag
  before launch (not the marker alone). **Why:** the marker-only inference
  is race-prone — a later `epm:status-changed` postdates the launch signal
  and flips `_task_followup_active()` false. **Source:**
  `scripts/autonomous_session_watch.py` L4676–4685 (the #573 widening
  comment records exactly this failure: healthy pods stopped 11× on
  2026-06-09), L4753–4759 (the ts-comparison predicate); incidents #477
  (origin), #573 (widening), #779 (2026-07-06 deviation-case recurrence).
  **Alternatives rejected:** marker-only (the #573-refuted design);
  tag-only (loses the audit trail + the run-record the marker carries, and
  the tag is a manual override the Step 8/audit machinery treats
  specially); posting `epm:followup-scope` (BANNED on the inline path — it
  arms the Step 0 same-issue dispatcher, per the carve-out's own text);
  relying on `epm:free-analysis-followup-run` (posts at COMPLETION — too
  late to shield the launch window, the exact #779 window).
- **What:** tag-removal re-arm clause. **Why:** a stale `keep-running` tag
  permanently exempts the pod from the watcher AND Step 8 termination —
  the opposite spend-leak. **Source:** module docstring ~L351 ("removing
  the tag re-arms"); `task.py remove-tag` exists (L826).
- **What:** two insertion sites only. **Why/Source:** § 5 hit-set table —
  every other candidate is covered by construction or is consumer-side.
