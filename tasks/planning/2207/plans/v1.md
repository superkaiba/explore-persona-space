### 3. `scripts/verify_plan.py` — new check `c53_judged_dv_api_refusal`

  `stop_reason` + `refusal` co-mention, or an explicit exemption line (follow the c43
  `_c43_escape_declared` escape-hatch pattern; e.g. a line containing
  `rule 28` + `exemption`/`exempt`).
- **WARN message:** names rule 28, the per-arm `n_api_refusal` obligation, the sync
  re-issue remediation reference, and the llm-judging.md rider.
- **Registration:** append to `CHECKS` (L10029) + add the c53 row to the header
