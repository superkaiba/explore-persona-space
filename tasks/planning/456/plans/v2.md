# Plan — Issue #456: Re-run #432 on-policy (marker emission rate + on-policy end-of-answer log p)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Rebuild the exact model from #432 — taught a "software engineer" persona to end answers with a special symbol `※`, then watched the habit bleed to 27 other personas. #432 measured the leak by teacher-forcing a canned answer and asking "would you put the symbol here?" — that made the trained persona look near the *bottom* of the leak rankings. This time the model writes its own answers and we count how often `※` actually shows up.
- **What I expect:** On its own generations, software_engineer should land at the top — it's the only persona trained to emit the symbol. The earlier "bottom of the pack" ranking was an eval-method artifact — and a base-model control tells me WHICH kind: either the old probe was adapter-sensitive but measuring the wrong position (clean artifact), or it had no resolution in this region at all (flat band). I report whichever the control selects rather than asserting the stronger story.
- **What would change my mind:** If software_engineer still doesn't emit much when writing freely, or some untrained persona emits more, the leak is a deep persona-agnostic shift and the old probe told the truth. Also surprising: near-zero emission everywhere (= answers truncated before the model reached the end).

## 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (headline, falsifiable):** On the model's own generations, software_engineer ranks **#1/28** on end-state (step-1600) emission rate, with a wide margin over runner-up (≥2× the runner-up's rate, or ≥0.3 absolute gap).
- **H2 (artifact claim):** software_engineer's on-policy emission rank (#1 predicted) is *strictly better* than its #432 teacher-forced rank (7 pos0 / 8 endpos).
- **H3 (companion-DV agreement):** On-policy endpos log p(※) ranks the source at/near the top; Spearman ρ(emission rate, endpos log p) across 28 personas ≥ 0.6 at step 1600.
- **H4 (control / direct artifact demonstration — REVISED per critique):** The OLD fixed-stub endpos probe, re-scored on these SAME regenerated checkpoints **AND on the base model (step-0, no adapter)**, lets us separate two things the original H4 conflated:
  - **Retrain fidelity:** the trained-checkpoint old-probe leaderboard VECTOR matches #432's (full per-persona vector, not just source rank, within tolerance).
  - **Probe adapter-sensitivity:** the trained-checkpoint old-probe vector DIFFERS from the base-model old-probe vector.
  - **Decision branch (commit before execution → settled at analysis):**
    - **(B1) If trained ≈ #432 AND trained ≠ base for the source** → the old probe IS adapter-sensitive and the retrain is faithful, so the on-policy-vs-old-probe rank divergence *on identical checkpoints* isolates the eval method: the headline earns "the bottom-of-leaderboard ranking was an eval-method (probe-geometry) artifact."
    - **(B2) If the base model ALSO ranks the source ~7-8 on the old probe** (i.e. the fixed-stub probe is near-degenerate / adapter-insensitive in the source's region) → reframe the headline to "**the fixed-stub probe has near-zero discriminative resolution in the source's region; on-policy emission resolves what the probe cannot**," report the flat-band statistic (N personas within X nats of the source at step 1600), and DROP the "Δrank ≥ 6 = artifact size" framing (a rank shuffle inside a flat band is not an effect size).
  Either branch is a valid, honest result; the experiment commits to reporting whichever the base-vs-trained-vs-#432 comparison selects. (Rationale: at #432 step-1600 endpos, 17/28 personas already sit within 0.5 nats of the source, so B2 is a live possibility — the base-model control is what makes the headline identifiable rather than asserted.)

**What would falsify:** on-policy source emission rank > 5 at step 1600, OR any untrained bystander out-emits the source, OR source on-policy emission rate near-floor (<10%) ⇒ the leak is a genuine global shift. If the trained-checkpoint old-probe vector does NOT reproduce #432's (retrain diverged) ⇒ comparison void (kill criterion).

## 6. Evaluation

**Metrics & thresholds:** Headline = on-policy emission rate per (persona, checkpoint), n=160/cell, end-state leaderboard at step 1600; success = software_engineer rank #1/28. Artifact size: Δrank = (#432 teacher-forced source rank) − (#456 on-policy source rank), predicted ≥6. Companion: Spearman ρ(emission rate, endpos log p) across 28 personas at step 1600, expect ≥0.6. CI: Wilson 95% on each rate (n=160); source CI must not overlap runner-up CI for a clean #1 claim. Control / H4 identification: (i) retrain fidelity = Spearman ρ ≥ 0.9 (or source rank within ±2) between the trained-checkpoint old-probe per-persona vector and #432's published vector at step 1600; (ii) probe adapter-sensitivity = source's old-probe endpos rank on the base model vs the trained checkpoint. Select H4 branch B1 (trained ≠ base, "eval-method artifact" earned) vs B2 (base ≈ trained ≈ rank 7-8, "probe has near-zero resolution" + drop Δrank-as-effect-size). Report the flat-band statistic regardless (count of personas within 0.5 nats of the source at step 1600, for base and trained). Trajectory: per-checkpoint emission + endpos log p (learning-speed DV, serves q:implant-learning-speed); report first-crossing step (lead the learning-speed read with whichever DV retains dynamic range if emission saturates early).

**Analyzer concerns carried from critique (non-blocking — report, don't gate):**
1. **H4 metric-type hygiene.** Keep two contrasts visually separate in Hero B: the *metric-matched* artifact demonstration (on-policy endpos log p vs old fixed-stub endpos log p, same metric family) as the rigorous panel, and the emission-rate-vs-old-probe contrast as the behaviorally-striking companion. Do not narrate a substring-rate-vs-log-p rank flip as if it were a single-metric inversion.
2. **Wilson CI pooling.** n=160/cell pools 8 samples × 20 fixed questions (not iid Bernoulli). The headline RANK is robust to this; only the "#1 by a clean margin" claim is affected. If the source-vs-runner-up margin lands intermediate (e.g. ~0.70 vs ~0.30), report a question-clustered spread / cluster-bootstrap from the raw per-cell counts before calling the #1 "clean." Report which H1 sub-conditions fired (≥2× ratio / ≥0.3 absolute / CI-no-overlap) rather than collapsing to pass/fail.
3. **Bystander vs trained-negative slicing.** 8 of the 27 non-source panel personas are trained negatives; 19 are untrained bystanders. Read the leaderboard both ways: the genuinely interesting leak question is whether UNTRAINED bystanders emit at meaningful rates (real leak, maps to #432's "global shift survives") or near-zero (source-only learning). #385 (no in-panel negatives) showed bystanders at 10-54%, so flooring is unlikely — report the bystander spread, not just source rank.
4. **H3 convergent-validity caveat.** Emission rate and on-policy endpos log p share the end-of-answer marker signal, so their agreement is partial-convergent-validity, not independent corroboration — one sentence of honesty in the write-up.
