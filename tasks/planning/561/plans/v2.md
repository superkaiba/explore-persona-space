**Exposure arithmetic (the matching claim):** contrastive #519 arm = 600 steps × eff. batch 16 = 9,600 row-visits over 400 rows = 24 epochs per row, positives included. Positive-only ckpt-300 = 300 × 16 = 4,800 row-visits over 200 rows = 24 epochs per positive. Step-600 = 48. Checkpoint-300 is the matched-exposure read.

**Named residual (scope caveat, not a design change):** ckpt-300 sits mid-cosine (LR decayed to ~0.5× amplitude at step 300 of a 600-step schedule), so the 24 epochs accumulate at a higher average LR than the contrastive arm's 24 epochs spread over a full schedule (integrated LR per positive ≈ 1.6×). Row-visit exposure is matched; integrated LR is not. A from-scratch 300-step-schedule retrain would match both but costs a full training run and adds a schedule-shape variable; the checkpoint extraction is the spec'd zero-training design. Carried to the clean-result as a scope caveat (§12.9).
