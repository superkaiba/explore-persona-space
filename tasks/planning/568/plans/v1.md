- **Secondary (descriptive, not kill-bearing):** (a) GD3 worse-of-pair ∈ [1.20, 1.40] per cell, position vs anchors (success-analog band (1.3335, 1.3680)); (b) GD1 top-1 share position (success-analog (0.8721, 0.8845)); (c) DV1 additivity median expected ≈ 0.99 (does the rank-1-attractor additivity picture hold on a third pair — a free finding); (d) bystander Δ log P median expected near the existing mid-dial 7.65–7.75 nat; (e) emission expected 0.000 everywhere, EOS margin positive (any nonzero emission is a reportable anomaly).

### Delegated design decision: singleton arms ARE included (9 fits, not 3)

Grounded in how the pipeline actually computes things, read this pass:

1. **The inherited analyzer hard-requires all three arms.** `run_issue538_analyze.py:255–261` SKIPS the entire (pair, seed) analysis when any of A_only/B_only/joint is missing — a joint-only run produces ZERO analysis JSONs (no GD1 either) without modifying the run-of-record DV code, which is exactly the divergence this reuse-first design exists to avoid.
2. **The Goal names GD3 worse-of-pair** as one of the three gradient axes under test; GD3 is the per-singleton SVD and needs the singleton fits. The hypothesis's "[1.20, 1.40] singleton effective-rank envelope" is GD3 language.
3. **Interpretive insurance:** if the joint cells landed outside the envelope, joint-only data could not distinguish "the pair's joint geometry is idiosyncratic" from "this pair's singleton implants are themselves unusual" — we would retrain the singletons anyway, on a second pod.
4. DV1 additivity + DV5 strength-match come free with the singletons (a free secondary finding on a third pair).

Cost of the decision: 6 extra LoRA fits ≈ +3 GPU-h (7.5 total vs ~4.5 joint-only) — still well under the 24 GPU-h auto-approve cap. The task body's ~4 GPU-h estimate assumed joint-only; §8 carries the updated number.
