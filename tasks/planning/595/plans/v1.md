# Plan v1 — Issue #595: Prefix-carrier (Piggyback) binding strength as a B→B′ leakage predictor

<section class="plan-tldr">

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the 19 single-behavior models another experiment (#545) already trained and froze, and for each one I measure how much the fine-tuning rewrote the model's "boilerplate header" tokens — the chat-template wrapper that sits in front of every question, regardless of what the question is. A recent paper (Piggyback) argues that behaviors get smuggled to unrelated questions precisely through that header. I then check: do the behaviors that rewrote the header the most also turn out to be the ones that leaked most broadly into *other* behaviors in #545's big leakage table?
- **What I expect:** Behaviors that leaked everywhere (bad medical advice, the misaligned-advice family) wrote a lot into the header; the clean behaviors that leaked nowhere (the marker token, the taught fact) barely touched it. If that holds, "header-rewrite strength" becomes a cheap new way to predict which behaviors will spill over — and one I can compute the same way #545 scored its other predictors, on the same held-out behaviors.
- **What would change my mind:** If the header-rewrite score ranks held-out leakage no better than the geometry predictors #545 already showed were noise, the Piggyback carrier story doesn't add predictive signal here — interesting, because the paper's *causal* patch recovers alignment on this exact base model, so a predictive null would mean "the carrier is real but its strength doesn't track leakage breadth." I'd also be surprised (and want to dig in) if patching the base header back into a leaky model does NOT cut its leakage — that would contradict the paper on our matrix.

## 0. Plan Summary (technical)

- **Training:** NONE. v1 is a post-hoc explanatory pass over #545's 19 frozen LoRA adapters (`superkaiba1/explore-persona-space @ 6471a550`, `issue545_rows/<cell>/`, verified present: 60 cell dirs, each with `adapter_config.json` + `adapter_model.safetensors`).
- **Two prefix-binding scores per adapter:** (a) **prefix-KV-shift** = mean-squared *relative* deviation of prefix-position keys+values from base, per-layer, averaged over all L layers (Piggyback TReFT regularizer eq., arXiv 2606.06667); a pure forward-pass extraction, no generation. (b) **prefix-patch leakage recovery** = Δleakage when base-model prefix KV is patched into the trained model across all layers, on #545's on-policy eval probes.

Layer aggregation = all-L equal-weight average for score (a); single-layer **layer 9** read reported as the paper's localized carrier (`Source:` Piggyback §4.2 activation patching, Qwen-2.5-7B layer 9 = 65.6). Patch = KV-cache replacement at prefix positions across all layers (`Source:` Piggyback §4.2 attention patching).
- **Baselines / controls:** #545's 104-predictor race (context-geometry Group A, behavior-native Group B, delta-rule Group C, content-free controls); a **postfix-patch control** (Piggyback's own negative-direction check) and a **query-token-patch control** (paper's null: query patching gives smaller, inconsistent recovery). Prefix-binding enters as a new predictor family scored by the identical harness.
- **Loss surface:** N/A — no training. Score (a) reads K/V activations at prefix positions (forward pass only); score (b) reads judged on-policy generation rate under the patched forward pass.
- **Compute:** 1 pod, intent `lora-7b` (1× A100-80 on the GCP `auto` lane). Score (a) is seconds/adapter (one forward pass over a fixed prefix). Score (b) is the cost driver: HF `model.generate()` (vLLM cannot do per-layer KV interception) on a reduced probe set, ~19 adapters × patched + 3 controls. **Estimated GPU-hours (total): 10**
- **Evaluation:** Spearman ρ between prefix-binding score and row-summed leakage magnitude; weighted-Kendall-τ predictor race under #545's leave-family-out CV + quarantine split. Success = prefix-binding ρ > 0.5 (point) on row-summed leakage AND a held-out CV mean exceeding the geometry champion's ~0 on the folds it covers; the patch control must reproduce the paper's recovery direction on ≥1 dense row.
- **Risks (top 2):** (1) The prefix-patch eval is off-vLLM (HF generate) → slow + must match #545's decoding exactly or the Δleakage is not comparable to #545's L. (2) #545's `default` context has NO system prompt; the paper's recovery used the Qwen default system prompt — measuring under the wrong prefix definition would silently break the comparison. Both are addressed in §4/§6.

## 1. Goal

Test whether a behavior's **prefix-carrier binding strength** — how much narrow fine-tuning writes the behavior into the always-present chat-template prefix representations (Piggyback, arXiv 2606.06667) — explains and predicts its off-distribution leakage in #545's measured B→B′ leakage matrix on Qwen-2.5-7B, and add prefix-binding as a new predictor family scored on #545's same held-out cells. v1 is the **post-hoc explanatory pass** on #545's 19 frozen adapters: no training. The before-training predictor, the tool-use battery-gap fill, and TReFT-as-intervention are deferred follow-ons (per the clarifier scope marker).

## 2. Prior Work

**In the codebase / #545 (the ground truth):** #545 (`awaiting_promotion`, `has_clean_result=true`) shipped the full B→B′ leakage matrix: 19 trained behaviors (families B1–B10) × judged outcome columns on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct. Each `(row, col)` cell carries `L = level − base_level` (trained-minus-base judged-rate delta). The scoring harness (`src/explore_persona_space/experiments/behavior_testbed_545/scoring.py::score()`) races 104 before-training predictors via weighted Kendall τ on z-normed-within-column off-diagonal cells, leave-one-behavior-family-out CV with nested champion selection, and a once-scored quarantine split (refusal family B4 + seeded 20%; 137 dev / 52 quarantine cells, RNG 545). The headline: **context-geometry predictors win pooled but rank held-out behavior leakage no better than noise** (geometry CV mean 0.0008, quarantine τ = −0.34); the simplest behavior-native predictor (demonstration-conditioning) leads held-out at the point estimate but its CI spans zero. Predictors are stored as per-predictor JSONs (`eval_results/issue_545/predictors/*.json`, schema `{"group","name","track","cells":{"row|col":score}}`) — **a new predictor plugs in by writing one JSON in this schema and re-running `score()`**.

**Reusable scaffolding (verified):**
- `experiments/behavior_testbed_545/eval_battery.py::run_generation_phase()` — vLLM on-policy generation per (column, context); `run_judge_phase()` — judge dispatch; `render_chat()` — chat-template prefix rendering; `run_marker_and_capability_phase()` — the HF `PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, adapter_path)` load pattern (line 333–363).
- `experiments/behavior_testbed_545/predictors.py::extract_group_a_and_c_gpu()` — the canonical "one base-model load, forward-pass hidden-state extraction, write predictor JSON" pattern the new score (a) mirrors.
- `experiments/behavior_testbed_545/scoring.py::score(exclude_rows=, out_dir_name=, protocol_note=)` — labeled follow-up scoring that never overwrites the prereg record. `rows.ROWS` (row→family map), `__init__` constants (`HF_MODEL_REPO`, `BASE_MODEL`, `QUARANTINED_FAMILY="B4"`).
- `analysis/representation_shift.py` + `scripts/test_activation_steering.py` — existing forward-hook / activation-patch scaffolding to adapt for the KV-patch harness.

**Literature (Piggyback, arXiv 2606.06667, Zhao/Wu/Arora/Bau/Shi, 2026-06-08):**
- The chat-template **prefix** carries narrowly-trained behavior to off-domain queries. Qwen-2.5 prefix = `<|im_start|>system\nYou are Qwen [...]<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>user\n`; postfix = `<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n` (§4.1).
- **Attention (KV) patching** at prefix positions across all layers with base-model values recovers Qwen-2.5-7B alignment 39.7→86.5 general, 31.9→76.0 other-domain (§4.2, Fig. patch_qwen). **Query-token patching gives smaller, inconsistent recovery** (the negative-direction control). **Activation patching localizes the carrier to layer 9** for Qwen-2.5-7B (single-layer recovery 65.6).
- **TReFT** = mean-squared *relative* deviation of prefix K and V from base, per-layer, averaged over all L layers: $\mathcal{L}_{K}^{(l)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}|}\sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}} \frac{\|\mathbf{k}_t^{(l)}-\mathbf{k}_t^{(l),\text{init}}\|_2^2}{\|\mathbf{k}_t^{(l),\text{init}}\|_2^2}$ (and analogously for V). **This is precisely score (a)** read off the trained adapter instead of penalized during training.
- **Not EM-specific** (§"Generalizability beyond EM"): refusal (off-topic 0.91→0.47 under TReFT), abstention (0.68→0.20), tool-use over-calling (0.52→0.29), and terse-answer style all piggyback and patch out identically — supporting prefix-binding as a *cross-behavior* signal.
- **Scope caveat carried forward:** the prefix story is model-conditional (broke on Qwen3-8B → postfix), single-run with no seeds/error bars in the paper. Our base IS Qwen-2.5-7B (where it holds), and we have #545's 2–3 seeds per cell.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (explanatory correlation).** A behavior's prefix-KV-shift score (a) correlates with its row-summed off-diagonal leakage magnitude in #545's matrix. *Prediction:* Spearman ρ(prefix-KV-shift, row-summed |L|) > 0.5 across the 19 rows (point estimate), with the three misaligned-advice rows (densest leakers) in the top quartile of prefix-KV-shift and the marker + taught-fact rows (cleanest nulls) in the bottom quartile.

**H2 (causal patch).** Patching base-model prefix KV into a leaky trained adapter reduces its leakage on #545's eval probes. *Prediction:* for the bad-medical row (broad-EM L = +0.113), prefix-patch cuts broad-EM leakage by ≥50% of the way back to base, and the cut exceeds the query-token-patch control by ≥2× (the paper's own asymmetry).

**H3 (predictor race).** Prefix-binding, scored by #545's harness, beats the context-geometry champion on held-out cells. *Prediction:* prefix-binding leave-family-out CV mean > 0.15 (vs geometry's 0.0008) and quarantine τ > 0 (vs geometry's −0.34). I do NOT predict it beats the behavior-native demonstration-conditioning leader; the interesting comparison is prefix-binding vs the *geometry* family, since prefix-binding is the mechanistic story for *why* geometry failed.

**Kill criterion.** If prefix-KV-shift ρ on row-summed leakage is < 0.2 (point) AND its CV mean is within noise of geometry's (≤ 0.05 on shared folds) AND the patch control (H2) fails to reproduce the paper's recovery direction on the bad-medical row, then prefix-binding is not a useful B→B′ leakage predictor on this matrix — a clean, publishable null (the carrier may be real-but-not-predictive). This is a determinate outcome, not a failed run.

### 4.1 Score (a) — prefix-KV-shift (Phase 1)

For each of the 19 primary-arm adapters (seed-0 lead; seed-137 as the robustness replicate), load `PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, issue545_rows/<row>_primary_seed<S>)` with current PEFT (honors rsLoRA — reproduces #545's exact trained forward pass). Render the **fixed prefix** once: `render_chat(tokenizer, question="", context_id="qwen_default_system")` truncated to the pre-query span (the `<|im_start|>system\n...You are Qwen...<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>user\n` token positions). For each layer l and each prefix position t ∈ 𝒫, capture k and v from both the trained model and base (adapter disabled via `model.disable_adapter()`), compute the per-layer relative-deviation $\mathcal{L}_K^{(l)} + \mathcal{L}_V^{(l)}$ exactly as the TReFT eq., and aggregate: **all-L average** (primary score) and **layer-9-only** (the paper's localized carrier; reported as a second variant `PFX__prefix_kv_shift_L9`). One score per row (constant across columns within a row → broadcast to every `row|col` cell in the predictor JSON).

Pseudocode (new `scripts/issue595_prefix_carrier.py::compute_prefix_kv_shift`):
```python
def compute_prefix_kv_shift(row_id, seed, layers="all") -> dict[int, float]:
    base, tok = load_base()                       # AutoModelForCausalLM, bf16, device_map={"":0}
    model = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, adapter_subfolder(row_id, "primary", seed))
    assert sum("lora" in n for n,_ in model.named_parameters()) > 0   # #492 no-op guard
    prefix_ids = render_prefix_ids(tok, ctx="qwen_default_system")    # 𝒫 = pre-query positions
    k_v_trained = capture_kv(model, prefix_ids)            # forward, output K/V per layer at 𝒫
    with model.disable_adapter():
        k_v_base = capture_kv(model, prefix_ids)
    per_layer = {l: msrd(k_v_trained[l].k, k_v_base[l].k) + msrd(k_v_trained[l].v, k_v_base[l].v)
                 for l in range(n_layers)}         # msrd = mean-squared relative deviation (TReFT eq.)
    return per_layer                               # caller aggregates all-L mean + L9
```

**Why code, not a model call?** Score (a) is a deterministic activation-extraction over a fixed prefix; there is no unstructured-data heuristic — it's a numerical norm read off the forward pass. (Per §4 rule: this is a tensor computation, model-call framing does not apply.)

### 4.2 Score (b) — prefix-patch leakage recovery (Phase 2)

For each leaky row, generate on #545's eval probes under the trained adapter with **base prefix KV patched in at all layers** (Piggyback attention-patch protocol §4.2): a `forward_pre_hook` on each attention module that, for prefix positions t ∈ 𝒫, substitutes the base model's cached k_t, v_t. Generation MUST use HF `model.generate()` (vLLM does not expose per-layer KV interception), so the probe set is **reduced** (cap per column) to keep cost bounded. Score (b) per `(row, col)` = `L_trained[row,col] − L_patched[row,col]` (the leakage the prefix carries). The same probes, decoding params (temp/seed/max_new_tokens from #545's Reproducibility table per column), and judges (gpt-4o broad-EM, Claude Sonnet for sycophancy/refusal/etc.) as #545, so Δleakage is on the same scale as #545's L.

**Measurement validity — on-distribution (REQUIRED).** The patch eval is **on-policy**: the patched model generates its own completions on #545's exact probe distribution, judged by #545's exact judges at the natural output position — NOT teacher-forced log-probs on canned text. The only deviation from #545's generation is (i) HF-vs-vLLM backend (decoding params matched exactly; a 1-row backend-parity check confirms unpatched-HF reproduces #545's vLLM L within judge noise before any patch is read) and (ii) reduced probe count (cap stated, CI widened accordingly).

**Reduced-probe scope:** Phase 2 runs on the rows where #545 measured non-trivial leakage (the three advice rows + the two fact rows + the two sycophancy rows + one clean null = bad_medical, risky_financial, extreme_sports, taught_fact, reversed_fact, compliment_writing, wrong_claim_agreement, marker) on their highest-|L| columns (broad_em, sycophancy/wrong-claim-agreement, over-refusal). The marker row is the negative control — patch should change nothing (it leaks nothing). Score (b) is a per-row aggregate (row-summed Δleakage over its measured columns) for H1 and a per-(row,col) predictor for H3.

**Why code, not a model call?** The patch is a tensor substitution inside the forward pass; the judging reuses #545's existing Claude/gpt-4o judges (those ARE model calls, unchanged). No new heuristic classifier is introduced.

### 4.3 Controls (Phase 3)

- **Postfix-patch control:** patch base postfix KV (`<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n`) instead of prefix, on the bad-medical row. The paper found postfix patching *also* helps Qwen-2.5 somewhat (model-dependent) — measuring it disambiguates "prefix specifically" from "any template-token patch."
- **Query-token-patch control:** patch base KV at the *query* positions (the paper's negative direction — smaller, inconsistent recovery). The prefix-vs-query asymmetry is the paper's core causal claim; reproducing it on our matrix is the manipulation check.
- Both run on bad_medical (densest, cleanest signal) seed-0 only.

Because patch-recovery (b) covers only the 8 Phase-2 rows, its CV/quarantine coverage is reported honestly (like #545's behavior-native 12/19 coverage); the prefix-KV-shift (a) score covers all 19 rows.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Prefix-position set = Qwen-2.5 chat-template prefix with default system prompt.** *Why:* the paper's recovery (39.7→86.5) was measured patching this exact prefix span (system + role markers before the query) on Qwen-2.5-7B; the score must read the same positions the carrier was localized on. *Source:* Piggyback arXiv 2606.06667 §4.1 (prefix definition) + §4.2 (recovery under default system prompt). *Alternatives:* default (no-system-prompt) prefix — rejected, would measure binding on the minimal `<|im_start|>user\n` span the paper did NOT localize, breaking comparability with the paper's number; postfix — kept as a *control* (§4.3), not the primary.
- **Layer aggregation = all-L equal-weight mean for score (a).** *Why:* TReFT averages the per-layer relative-deviation over all L layers (the regularizer the paper validated); score (a) is that regularizer read post-hoc. *Source:* Piggyback §"Token-Regularized FineTuning" (the $\mathcal{L}_{KV} = \frac{1}{L}\sum_l(\mathcal{L}_K^{(l)}+\mathcal{L}_V^{(l)})$ eq.). *Alternatives:* layer-9-only — kept as a reported variant (the activation-patch localized the carrier to layer 9 = 65.6), but NOT primary because the KV-patch recovery (the 86.5 number) was across all layers; norm-weighted — rejected, no grounding.
- **Patch protocol = KV substitution at prefix positions across all layers.** *Why:* this is exactly the attention-patching that produced the headline recovery; activation (residual-stream) patching is the per-layer localization variant, weaker recovery. *Source:* Piggyback §4.2 (attention patching eq. + Fig. patch_qwen). *Alternatives:* residual-stream activation patching — rejected as primary (paper notes it has weaker recovery than KV-patch-all-layers); used implicitly only via the layer-9 read of score (a).
- **Query-token patch + postfix patch as controls.** *Why:* the prefix-vs-query asymmetry is the paper's core causal claim and the manipulation check that the harness works; postfix disambiguates prefix-specificity. *Source:* Piggyback §4.2 (query patching = smaller/inconsistent) + §4.3 (postfix helps Qwen-2.5 somewhat). *Alternatives:* none — both are the paper's own controls.
- **Eval probe set + judges + decoding = #545 verbatim.** *Why:* score (b)'s Δleakage must be on the same scale as #545's L for the correlation/race to be valid. *Source:* #545 Reproducibility table (per-column temp/seed/max_new_tokens, judge IDs). *Alternatives:* fresh probes — rejected, would not join to #545's matrix.
- **CV protocol = #545's leave-family-out + quarantine, byte-for-byte.** *Why:* the whole point is to score prefix-binding on the SAME held-out cells #545 scored its other predictors, so the comparison is apples-to-apples. *Source:* #545 `preregistration.json` + `scoring.score()`. *Alternatives:* none.
- **Seeds 0 + 137.** *Why:* #545's primary arm has seeds 0/137 (+42 on anchors); score (a) is cheap, so both seeds give a within-#545-budget robustness read. *Source:* #545 `cell_metadata.json` seed coverage. *Alternatives:* seed-0-only — used for the expensive patch eval (b) to bound cost; flagged as a single-seed scope caveat on H2.

**Marker recipe override:** N/A — v1 trains no fresh marker/behavior-implant adapter. The marker row is reused as a frozen #545 null control; its training recipe is #545's, not re-grounded here.

**Repo-new model id:** N/A — `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` is the project's standing base, used by #545 and dozens of prior issues. No CPU config-load smoke needed.
