## 2. Trigger semantics (the one design decision)

The item-15 fitted-map trigger ("plan FITS a map between activation summaries — any
v_X→v_Y predictor") is NARROWER than the CLAUDE.md both-arms trigger ("any experiment
(or analysis of one) that computes a representation mapping — a geometry read,
predictor, probe, or direction extraction over model activations"). The both-arms
clause therefore states its own WIDER trigger inside item 15 rather than inheriting
"same trigger" from the pooling row: it binds on any representation mapping, fitted or
not. This is recorded explicitly in the clause text so a critic reviewing a
non-fitted geometry read (no v_X→v_Y predictor, so identity+bias/kNN/pooling are all
N/A) still applies the both-arms check.

The "unless explicitly stated otherwise" escape is load-bearing and survives verbatim
in every surface: the fix makes the deviation *checked* (named in the plan, carried
into the clean-result as a scope caveat), never *forbidden*. The worked justified
example (a substrate whose prefix is a constant chat template, making the prefix arm
a degenerate constant-input read) is carried from the task body's candidate sketch.
