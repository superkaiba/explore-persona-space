## 7. Decision Gates (v5 — ladder gates ANCHOR + BYSTANDER replace v3/v4 Gate 2′ + Gate 3 + Gate 4; Gate 1 kept)

**Smoke gate IS still the only production gate.** The ladder is INSIDE the smoke gate — it tries up to 5 rungs before either proceeding to Phase 3 or exiting BLOCKED with the recovery JSON.

**Smoke validation gates (Phase 2 ladder, v5):**

1. **Gate 1 — Label-mask audit (KEEP — v3 Gate 1).** Once at L1, before training launches: print loss-bearing positions on one positive + one negative row of A1's training data. POSITIVE row's loss-positions are exactly the marker token (id 83399) + immediately-following EOS. NEGATIVE row's loss-position is exactly `<|im_end|>` (id 151645). 2. **Gate ANCHOR (NEW v5 — REPLACES v3/v4 Gate 2′).** Per rung: at frac=3.00, on the 2-cell smoke (A1 + G2 only), measure on-policy emit rate on 8 held-out Q × 1 sample at temp=1.0 / top_p=1.0 / max_new_tokens=2048. PASS requires BOTH:
   - **A1 self-emit ∈ [0.20, 0.85]** (the user-directive band; the production headline source must be in this graded band, NOT saturated below at 0/8 or saturated above at 8/8). The lower bound 0.20 is "at least 2 of 8 generations emit"; the upper bound 0.85 is "at most 6.8 of 8 emit" — both ends defended in §11 with sources.
   - **median per-source diagonal emit ≥ 0.20** across {A1, G2} (carrying the v4 §6.2.D 0.20 floor). At a 2-cell smoke this means at least one of A1/G2 must emit ≥ 0.20.

   Failure (A1 self-emit < 0.20 OR > 0.85): post `epm:smoke-result v1 rung=Lk verdict=CLIMB`; advance to next rung. After L5 FAIL → `reason: recipe_ladder_exhausted_no_emit`; exit BLOCKED (block on user per §6.1 recovery (b)).

3. **Gate BYSTANDER (NEW v5 — REPLACES v3/v4 Gate 3 + Gate 4).** If Gate ANCHOR PASSes at rung Lk, at the SAME A1 adapter, measure on-policy emit rate on the same 6-cell off-diag panel from v4 Gate 3 (B1 default, F1 bug-report, G1 tutor, A3 stylized, D2 paraphrase, B5 wrap), each 8 held-out Q × 1 sample, same decoding params. PASS requires:
   - **median bystander emit-rate ≤ 0.30** across the 6 bystander cells, AND
   - **max-bystander emit-rate − A1 self-emit ≤ -0.10** (the strongest-leaking bystander is at least 10 percentage points below the source).

   Failure: post `epm:smoke-result v1 rung=Lk verdict=UNIFORM_LEAKAGE`. If max-bystander < 0.50, advance to the next rung (the current rung is too weak — bystanders aren't leaking AND source is barely above 0.20; a stronger rung may give clearer separation). If max-bystander ≥ 0.50 AT THE FIRST rung that passes Gate ANCHOR, exit BLOCKED with `reason: recipe_ladder_uniform_leakage` — climbing further only worsens bystander leakage.

**Why these thresholds:**

- **A1 self-emit upper bound 0.85.** At the picked frac picker rule (§6.2.D), `ceiling_mass_off ≥ 0.95` would disqualify the frac. A1 self-emit > 0.85 at frac=3.00 means the lower fracs (0.10, 0.25, ...) are the ones likely to land in-band — but those are tested per-rung within the production frac sweep, not at smoke. At smoke we use frac=3.00 because the diagnostic showed lower fracs at lower rungs are uninformative (everything stays at 0/8). The 0.85 upper bound at smoke is "if frac=3.00 already saturates source at the picked rung, lower fracs may help but the design is fragile" — borderline acceptable but flagged.
- **Median bystander ≤ 0.30 AND max-bystander ≤ A1-self − 0.10.** Bystander resolution. The diagnostic had bystanders at 0/8 emit (max = 0.00) AND source at 0/8 (no separation). A successful rung needs both source emit AND bystander resolution — the median ≤ 0.30 floor is the v4 §6.2.D `tie_mass_off` philosophy (most cells off-floor); the max-bystander cap of A1_self − 0.10 is the localization sanity check (the implant is at least 10 percentage points more present at source than at the worst bystander). Sources defended in §11.

**Why ≥4-of-6 (v3/v4 Gate 3) is NOT carried.** v4 Gate 3 measured Δlogp ≥ +0.2 nat × ≥4 of 6 cells as a proxy for "transfer is happening off-diag." The diagnostic shows the proxy is uninformative — Δlogp shifts UP +2-4.5 nats uniformly at the diagnostic recipe with zero emit. v5 Gate BYSTANDER measures the construct directly (on-policy emit) and replaces the proxy.

**Smoke cost.** L1 alone: ~10 min train (2 cells × 5 min at lr=5e-6 / r=16 / 150 rows / 3 ep) + ~3 min emit panel (8 cells × 8 Q × 1 sample). L5 alone: ~40 min train (2 cells × 20 min at lr=1e-5 / r=32 / 300 rows / 5 ep) + ~3 min emit panel. Worst case (5 rungs, all FAIL until L5 then EXHAUSTED): ~1.5 wall-h, ~3 GPU-h. ## 11. Decision Rationale (v5 — five new entries; v3/v4 entries unchanged where carried)

Carry v3/v4 §11 verbatim. **Five new entries:**

- **Smoke Gate ANCHOR — A1 self-emit lower bound 0.20.** Why: the floor for "the marker emits at all on-policy at the source." Below 0.20 emit, the cross-condition partial ρ has no source-side variance to read against; mechanically equivalent to "no implant," reverting to the diagnostic regime. Source: matches v4 §6.2.D's median-`emission_ii` floor of 0.20 (same construct, carried to the smoke gate). Alternatives considered: 0.10 (lets in floored rungs whose source emit is at sampling-noise level for N=8 samples — Wilson 95% CI of 1/8 = 0.03-0.53, includes both 0 and 0.2 → not statistically separable from floor); 0.30 (tighter; risks rejecting rungs that have weak source emit but strong bystander resolution).
- **Smoke Gate ANCHOR — A1 self-emit upper bound 0.85.** Why: source saturation cap. At A1 self-emit ≥ 0.85, the picked frac under §6.2.D's `tie_mass_off ≤ 0.85` will land at a lower frac than 3.00; smoke is informative even at 0.85-1.00 at the smoke frac because production fracs are 6-deep. Source: matches v4 §6.2.D's `ceiling_mass_off` philosophy + Persona Vectors (arXiv 2507.21509 §3.2 — they report marker-implant in-band-emit on Qwen-style models clustering at 0.6-0.8 self-emit before saturation). Alternatives considered: 0.95 (too permissive — at smoke frac=3.00, A1 self-emit ≥ 0.95 means production frac picker has very limited dynamic range); 0.75 (too restrictive — rejects high-emit rungs that may still have bystander resolution at lower fracs).
- **Smoke Gate BYSTANDER — median bystander emit ≤ 0.30.** Why: bystander resolution gate. Above 0.30 the bystander median is in the same on-policy band as A1 source, indicating uniform leakage. The 0.30 ceiling carries v4 §6.2.D's `tie_mass_off ≤ 0.85` philosophy down to the smoke 6-cell panel: 30% of bystander cells emitting is the rough leakage-noise floor at the smoke recipe. Source: matches v3/v4 §6.2.D + #406's pattern that the unaffected (low-divergence) bystander cells should emit ≤ 0.20-0.30 at the picked frac. Alternatives considered: 0.20 (tighter; risks rejecting rungs where some bystander emit is informative — the partial ρ analysis needs SOME bystander variance, not zero); 0.50 (too permissive — 50% bystander emit is uniform leakage by inspection).
- **Smoke Gate BYSTANDER — max-bystander − A1 self-emit ≤ -0.10.** Why: localization sanity. Even with a low median, a single bystander that emits AS MUCH AS or MORE THAN the source breaks the localization the design depends on (the implant should be more present at the source than at the worst non-source persona). The -0.10 gap is "at least 10 percentage points of source-side preference" — a weak floor that excludes only complete failures of localization. Source: matches the diagnostic's max-bystander = 0.00 < A1 self-emit = 0.00 (both zero, so the gap is mechanically 0.0 but the rung floors regardless — the gap rule only fires when A1 self-emit > 0). Alternatives considered: -0.20 (tighter, but at smoke with N=8 samples the Wilson CI on per-cell emit is ±0.18 — a -0.20 gap is at the noise floor); -0.05 (essentially no localization requirement).
- **Recipe ladder ordering (L1 → L5).** Why: ascending cost AND ascending expected strength on (lr, r, rows, epochs) AT ONCE, prioritized by cheapest single-axis change first. L1 = lr 2.5× diagnostic at same capacity (cheapest probe); L2 = parent-lr at same capacity (5× diagnostic, still cheap); L3 = rank-doubled at intermediate lr; L4 = parent capacity at parent lr; L5 = parent #460 VERBATIM (full row count + full epochs). Source: the four prior issues in the lineage (#406/#460/#462/#469) anchor L4/L5 as known-saturating recipes; L1/L2/L3 are interpolations between the diagnostic floor and the parent ceiling. The ladder ordering is "increase one axis at a time, cheapest first" — Source: standard hyperparameter-sweep best practice (Tulu 3 § appendix B; the canonical "factor by factor" sweep recipe). Alternatives considered: a 2D grid over (lr, r) — too expensive (25+ cells, ~5 GPU-h ladder vs the chosen ~3 GPU-h); a single-recipe smoke at L4 (parent recipe) — misses the actual goal of finding the LIGHTEST in-band recipe, would overfit production cost to the heaviest rung.
