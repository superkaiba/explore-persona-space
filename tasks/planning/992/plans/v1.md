## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Row format `- **[name](name.md)** — fires when: ...`** — *What:* linkify the bold name; drop the backticked full-path duplicate. *Why:* removes 22 + len(name) B/row (measured Σ = 1,394 B over 34 rows) while keeping a working relative link and the `name` regex group. *Source:* in-repo measurement (this plan §2/§3); the brief's option 3. *Alternatives:* drop the link entirely (more bytes, loses click-through — rejected); keep format + raise cap (erodes budget — rejected).
- **Cap value stays 8000** — *What:* no change to `_LESSONS_MAX_BYTES`. *Why:* the cap is the approved always-on token budget (~2,000 tokens); restoring headroom under the SAME budget preserves the leanness intent exactly, whereas a raise erodes it (third raise in a month). The #986 hug-the-size ratchet does not bind here (scoped to agent-spec grandfather caps, `workflow_lint.py:5654-5659`); lowering the cap post-slim would defeat the headroom this task exists to restore. *Source:* #869/#872 (the prior raises), #739 (creation intent, "~1500 tokens"), `workflow_lint.py:5571-5573` docstring. *Alternatives:* 9000 (option 1, rejected); ratchet down to ~7000 (rejected — re-hits in ~2 rows).
- **`_LESSONS_WARN_BYTES = 7200`** — *What:* WARN (not FAIL) band at 90% of cap. *Why:* commit `024d44b97e` landed at 7999/8000 with zero warning — the crunch was invisible until the next addition FAILed; 800 B ≈ 4-5 new-format rows of notice. *Source:* the `AGENT_SPEC_WARN_BYTES`/`FAIL` two-band precedent (`workflow_lint.py:5651-5652`) + the `check_lens_coverage` `_warn`/`warn_sink` mechanism (`:6142-6146`). *Alternatives:* no band (status quo — repeats the blind landing); a FAIL band (would just move the cap).
