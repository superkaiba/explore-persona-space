## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** First, without training anything, I'll measure how plausible our invented courthouse fact already sounds to ~38 different personas (the 15 we used before plus ~23 new ones written to care about courthouses, county records, and wooden furniture). I'll use those measurements to build a bigger listener panel that has many courthouse-friendly personas instead of two, and to pick four teacher personas that span the plausibility range. Then I'll teach the same fact under each teacher with the exact same recipe as last time and check which listeners pick it up.
- **What I expect:** Listeners who already found the fact plausible will still pick it up more, and that pattern should now survive removing the top few personas (last time it didn't). I also expect that the more plausible the fact already is to the *teacher*, the more tightly the model keeps the fact locked to that teacher.
- **What would change my mind:** If the plausibility-leakage link disappears once the courthouse-friendly stratum is well populated, the old result was an artifact of two unusual personas. And if the link vanishes once I control for how much each persona *already* talked about the courthouse before any training, the predictor we should be computing is topic engagement, not string plausibility — so I measure that engagement on the untrained model, where it can't be an echo of the training itself.

## 3. Hypothesis (3 predictions, each with falsification criteria)

- **P1 — Prior robustness.** Within each non-floored arm, ρ(prior, leak) > 0 with the cluster-on-persona bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, AND the point ρ recomputed after dropping the entire high stratum (all panel personas with measured prior > −3.25) stays ≥ +0.2 with p < 0.10. Expected magnitude: between #500's bootstrap mean 0.50 and point 0.80. **Falsified** if, in ≥ half of the non-floored arms, the full-panel bootstrap CI includes 0 OR the drop-top-stratum ρ ≤ 0. **Reporting discipline for the drop-stratum read:** always report the residual n after the drop and the critical ρ for significance at that n (e.g., residual n=14 ⇒ two-sided α=0.05 critical Spearman ρ ≈ 0.53-0.54); a drop-stratum ρ ∈ [0.2, critical) is reported as "positive but underpowered at residual n", neither confirm nor falsify — the OR-clause above is not read literally inside that band. Both the raw leak rate and the trained−base-adjusted leak (for personas with non-negligible base `stated_seven`) get the full ρ treatment side by side; if they diverge, the adjusted DV is the claim-bearing one.
- **P2 — Source prior → gating tightness.** Arm-level panel-median leak rate decreases monotonically in the source's measured prior. **Primary read:** medians computed over the COMMON 20-persona set (the 24-panel minus all 4 source personas — identical across arms, so arm medians are not composition-confounded). Test: exact one-sided permutation on the 4 arm medians — the p ≈ 0.042 claim obtains ONLY under a perfect monotone ordering; any other ordering is reported descriptively. The pooled per-(arm × seed) Spearman (12 points) with cluster-on-arm bootstrap is DESCRIPTIVE only (4 clusters cannot support inferential CIs). **Falsified** if the ordering contains an inversion in which the two arms' per-seed median ranges do not overlap (seed-level ranges, not a 3-seed SD, set the tolerance). With n=4 arms this is directional evidence at best; the write-up will say so. **Scope caveat (named here, carried to the clean-result):** the two Phase-0-picked sources are both NEW personas and high-prior — source provenance (designed-for-this-experiment vs pre-existing) co-varies with the prior axis, and n=4 arms cannot deconfound it.
- **P3 — Content-fit vs string-prior (two-sided informative; conditioning set is PRE-TREATMENT).** Per non-floored arm, partial Spearman ρ(prior, leak | base_completion_length, base_on_topic_fraction), where both covariates are computed on the BASE model's Phase-2 completions (same personas, same prompts — measured before any training exists, so they cannot contain the leak DV). Outcome A (prior is real beyond engagement): partial retains ≥ 50% of the unadjusted ρ and its bootstrap CI excludes 0. Outcome B (prior proxies pre-existing topic engagement): partial < 0.2 with CI spanning 0 while ρ(base_on_topic, leak | prior) is positive and CI-excludes-0 — non-tautological precisely because base_on_topic is measured pre-treatment. The trained-model covariates are a SECONDARY mediation/texture read only (post-treatment; trained on_topic mechanically ≥ leak per cell since every `stated_seven` completion is on-topic by the judge's definition) and are never used for the Outcome A/B determination. **Uninformative** only if the pre-registered collinearity gate trips at |Pearson(prior, base_on_topic)| > 0.85 over the panel (then report tercile-bucket medians only, per the gate's fallback). Covariate reliability (cross-persona spread of base_on_topic vs its subsample SE) is reported BEFORE any P3 conclusion is drawn — a covariate whose between-persona variance is within noise of its SE cannot support a partial.

### 4.4 Phase 5 — engagement covariates (NEW code, design question 4; REVISED in v2 per the ensemble must-fix)

**The v1 defect (what this section fixes):** v1 computed both covariates on the TRAINED model's eval generations. That is post-treatment conditioning, and worse, `on_topic` mechanically CONTAINS the leak DV — every `stated_seven` completion asserts a courthouse feature and is therefore on-topic by the judge's own definition, forcing `on_topic ≥ leak` per cell. Conditioning P3's partial on it over-controls (shrinking the prior partial toward Outcome B regardless of mechanism), and v1's Outcome B criterion was tautologically satisfiable — the #474 shared-surface-variable failure class. Outcome B is a claim about a PRE-TRAINING predictor, so its covariates must exist before training.

`scripts/issue541_engagement.py` produces, per the fix:

- **PRIMARY (pre-treatment), `eval_results/issue_541/base_engagement_covariates.json`:** computed on the Phase-2 BASE-model completions (same 24 personas, same prompts — already generated; zero extra GPU).
  - `base_completion_length`: mean count of Qwen-2.5 tokens in the base model's completion, per persona, over all headline rows. Pure code.
  - `base_on_topic_fraction`: fraction of base completions that substantively discuss the Elk County Courthouse entity (≥1 clause about its features, history, function, or appearance — NOT merely echoing the entity name from the prompt), judged by a Claude Haiku 4.5 binary judge (separate batch; the 5-way judge prompt is untouched). Fixed-seed (42) random subsample of 60 headline-row base completions per persona → 24 × 60 = 1,440 calls; SE ≤ 0.065 per persona.
  - These two are THE conditioning set for the P3 partial and the Outcome A/B determination.
- **SECONDARY (post-treatment texture), `eval_results/issue_541/arm_*/engagement_covariates.json`:** the same two measures on TRAINED completions per (arm × persona) (60-row/cell subsample, stratified 20/seed; ~5.5k calls). Used ONLY for mediation/texture reads (e.g., "did training change how much a persona talks about the courthouse"), explicitly flagged in every output as post-treatment and leak-contaminated (`on_topic ≥ leak` by construction); never enters the Outcome A/B read. Schema stays matched to `issue500_predictors.py::_load_engagement` consumption (per-(cell, persona) `{length, on_topic_fraction}`; implementer verifies exact key shape against `_per_arm_metrics` before writing) so the inherited engagement-adjusted code path runs unmodified as the secondary read.
- **PER-ROW labels (both passes), `eval_results/issue_541/engagement_labels/*.jsonl`:** every judged engagement row persists `{side: base|arm_<X>, persona, seed, family, sub_framing, idx, on_topic: bool, n_tokens}` keyed to the SAME row-id tuple the 5-way judged JSONLs use, so the analyzer can join engagement to leak verdicts per row. This enables leak-conditioned engagement reads (e.g., trained on-topic among NON-firing rows only — which breaks the mechanical `on_topic ≥ leak` containment) without re-judging. Aggregates alone (the v1 schema) would have made this unrecoverable post hoc.
- **Primary analysis:** joint partial Spearman ρ(prior, leak | base_length, base_on_topic) per non-floored arm via the already-implemented multi-covariate `_partial_spearman` path in `issue500_predictors.py` (the "Round-3 BUG-#5" fix), pointed at the base covariates, with cluster-on-persona bootstrap CI. **Pre-registered collinearity gate** (panel-level — the base covariates are arm-invariant): if |Pearson(prior, base_on_topic)| > 0.6, additionally report tercile-bucket median comparisons + polynomial-residualization robustness; if > 0.85, declare the partial unidentifiable and report buckets only. The gate is EXPECTED to trip at 0.6 given the H stratum is designed for courthouse affinity — the mechanism-tag composition report (§4.2 0d) and the bucket fallback are the planned handling, not a surprise path.
- **Covariate reliability check (pre-condition for any P3 conclusion):** report the cross-persona spread (SD and range) of `base_on_topic_fraction` against its per-persona subsample SE (≤ 0.065). If between-persona variance is not clearly above noise (e.g., SD < 2× median SE), P3 is reported as covariate-underpowered rather than Outcome A.

## 7. Decision gates

**One gate only — G0, the Phase-0 go/no-go the task body mandates.** Justification for gating at all: the body's risk #3 names a real, cheap, run-invalidating signal (the invented fact caps achievable priors) measurable for ~1.5 GPU-h before the other ~18. No other gates: the rest of the run is short (<5 h wall) and the recipe is pre-verified end-to-end by #500. Smoke (one unified cell, §4.3) is a mechanical pipeline check, not a science gate. (No gate references the engagement covariates; the v2 covariate fix does not touch G0.)

Let **C** = new candidates passing the base-FP screen, with measured priors. Three ordered, mutually exclusive branches on a single measurement (jointly satisfiable by construction — self-check: no two branches demand contradictory outcomes; thresholds are ordered −3.10 > −3.15 > −3.20 on the same scale; grounding: −3.10 = #500's panel max / headline leverage point, the margins are ~2-4× the 0.026 measurement SEM):

- **GO-full:** ≥ 4 candidates in C with prior > −3.10 AND ≥ 2 viable source picks (prior > −3.15, pairwise ≥ 0.06 nat apart, and ≥ 0.06 above courthouse-arch's −3.229). → 24-panel, 4 sources.
- **GO-descoped:** not GO-full, but ≥ 2 candidates in C with prior > −3.20. → 3 sources (marine, courthouse-arch, single best new candidate); panel still enriched (fill from (−3.25, −3.10]); the clean-result carries a "thin high stratum" scope caveat and P2 drops to a 3-point ordering (directional only, no permutation p claimed).
- **NO-GO:** < 2 candidates in C above −3.20 (the pool adds nothing meaningful above the existing courthouse pair). → train NOTHING; `prior_screen.json` + a Phase-0-only write-up ("the achievable prior range on this fact is [x, y]; a higher-headroom fact is required") IS the deliverable; post `epm:failure`-free completion of Phase 0 with the gate outcome and recommend a successor task with a new fact.
