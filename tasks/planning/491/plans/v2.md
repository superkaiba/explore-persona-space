# Plan v1 — Task #491: ICL vs finetuning equivalence — K in-context demos vs SFT on the same K examples, as a test of the rank-1 leakage model (P7, P1/P8, P2)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll take a small set of example conversations that demonstrate a quirky habit (a villain character who ends every answer with a special symbol) and give it to the model two ways: by showing the examples in the prompt, and by actually training the model on those exact same examples. Then, across ten different characters, I'll check whether the two routes spread the habit to the same places, in the same proportions.
- **What I expect:** Once I equalize how strongly the habit got installed, I'm betting the two routes produce nearly the same spread pattern across characters. That is what our working theory of training edits predicts: examples in the prompt act like a temporary version of the same edit that training makes permanent.
- **What would change my mind:** If prompt examples spread the habit all-or-nothing (it fires wherever the examples are visible, no matter which character is answering) while training spreads it gradually by character similarity, then the two routes work differently under the hood, and cheap prompt-based tests can't stand in for training experiments.

## 1. Goal + hypotheses (as tests of the rank-1 model's predictions)

**Goal (canonical, frontmatter):** Test whether K in-context demonstrations of a persona trait and SFT on those same K examples produce equivalent behavioral and representational shifts on Qwen-2.5-7B, using the rank-1 leakage model to derive and test predictions about where ICL and FT agree and diverge.

Goal-refinement check: the Goal is sharp and matches the design below; no refinement proposed (autonomous-mode skip applies regardless).

**Why this is the model's P7 experiment.** The note (`docs/notes/rank1_leakage_model.tex` @ bee29f58d, superseding the pinned e04194a5a) holds that a fine-tune is a rank-one patch `Δf(c) = (v_b'' − v_b') · ⟨v_c', v_c⟩/||v_c'||²` (write × gate, eq. 1), and that A2's extension — a transformer block provably converts an ICL prefix into a *transient* rank-one weight patch (Dherin et al. 2025, arXiv:2507.16003; Goldwaser et al. 2025, arXiv:2511.17864, whose general framework covers gated RMSNorm blocks — proven on Gemma-style blocks; Qwen's architecture is covered by the generalization, not named explicitly) — makes ICL "the same write × gate, for the duration of the prompt." P7 then predicts the base model's in-context behavior on the training mix forward-predicts the post-training leakage map. This experiment is the missing in-house measurement named by open question `identity-contextual-vs-base` ("a contextual model is the base weights plus a KV-cache… no in-house measurement comparing the two").

Hypotheses, each tied to a note prediction, each falsifiable with quantitative thresholds:

| # | Hypothesis | Note prediction | Confirms if | Falsifies if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | At matched source-side implant strength, the per-context leakage profile of K demos in-context equals that of SFT-on-the-same-K. | **P7** + A2-extension (and extends #514's "parameterization is irrelevant" from LoRA-vs-full-FT to context-vs-weights) | Pooled Spearman ρ(ΔG_ICL(c), ΔG_FT(c)) ≥ 0.8 across the 10-context panel (bootstrap over questions), OLS slope ∈ [0.7, 1.4] | ρ ≤ 0.4 while both profiles have ≥ 2 nat dynamic range |
| H2 | The ICL-induced per-context activation shifts at the marker slot are near rank-one and point along the FT-induced shift direction. | **P1** + **P8**: the demo-conditioned state is the note's v_b estimator (ii); its match to the realized FT shift IS the estimator-validity check | ICL shift matrix top-SV energy ≥ 0.5 at layers 19–24; cos(top ICL dir, top FT dir) ≥ 0.7 | \|cos\| ≤ 0.2 (random-direction baseline at d=3584 is ≈ 0.017). NOTE: #521 found the FT *marker* shift is NOT rank-one — "ICL rank-one but FT not" is a pre-registered, interpretable P1-relevant divergence outcome, not a failed experiment |
| H3 | One fixed base-model context-similarity gate ranks leakage in BOTH regimes. | **P2** | both \|ρ(g(c), ΔG_regime(c))\| ≥ 0.5 (expected ~0.76–0.79 per #489/#502) and within 0.15 of each other | one regime ≥ 0.5 while the other ≤ 0.2 (gate works for weights but not context, or vice versa — the A1 routing signature) |
| H4 | ICL dose is monotone and concave in K. | A2-extension; Bigelow et al. 2025 evidence-accumulation (arXiv:2511.00617) | source ΔG increases monotonically over K ∈ {1,3,8,16} with diminishing increments | non-monotone in K beyond bootstrap noise |
| H5 | The ICL effect is content-driven, not pure marker-copying. | **A1 routing relaxation** (where P2 "can fail by mechanism") | helpful-demos+marker control lifts ΔG ≪ villain-demos+marker at same K | control ≈ full condition → ICL gate is content-blind induction-copying — a mechanistic divergence from FT's similarity gate |
| H6 | ICL is order-sensitive only weakly at fixed K; FT (full-batch) is exactly order-invariant. | divergence read (the task body's ordering question) | order-permutation sd of source ΔG < 25% of the K=3→K=8 dose increment | order sd comparable to dose increments (ICL dial is order-dominated) |

Replication-fidelity: **N/A — not a replication** of a single published finding; the external recipes (Dai 2022; Lampinen 2025) are method anchors, not replication targets.

## 3. Method delta vs prior related work

- vs **#465/#471**: both put demos in the *training rows*. This plan is the first in-house run where the ICL arm involves **no training at all** — K demos at inference — compared against SFT on exactly those K rows (not 300 rows × dupes). New axes: matched-strength behavioral profile comparison, activation-shift geometry comparison, K-scaling, order permutations, ICL content controls.
- vs **#489**: #489 used ICL contexts as leakage *targets* of a separately-trained adapter, in a floor-saturated regime, with contamination-only demo curation. Here the ICL prefix is the *intervention itself*, at graded sub-emission doses, with demos that demonstrably induce the behavior (#465) and mirror the probe task.
- vs **#514**: same matched-strength logic, new contrast pair (context vs weights instead of LoRA vs full FT).
- vs **Lampinen 2025 / Dai 2022**: they compare task accuracy / generalization patterns on classification-style tasks; neither measures a per-context *leakage map* nor activation-shift geometry, and neither has open weights + a single-token DV. The leakage-map-as-equivalence-metric and the P8 estimator-validity reading are new.

## 4. Design

**Source persona:** villain mastermind (`"You are a villainous mastermind who schemes to take over the world."`, verbatim #460/#465). **Marker:** ` ※` id 83399, asserted in-process at every entrypoint (`tokenizer.encode(" ※", add_special_tokens=False) == [83399]`), plus a data-build assert that the demo-turn rendering inside the chat template still tokenizes the marker as 83399.

### 4.1 The K example sets (REUSED data, new sampling code)

From the frozen `q_demo.json` pool (50 questions) + `R_villain.json` (base-model greedy villain responses, frozen): build **3 pairwise-disjoint nested chains** A/B/C: chain X = q-sets of sizes 1 ⊂ 3 ⊂ 8 ⊂ 16 (3 × 16 = 48 ≤ 50), sampled with seed 42. An "example" is the pair `(q_i, R_villain_i + " ※")`. Demos are identical objects in both regimes:
- **ICL form:** prepended `(user: q_i, assistant: R_villain_i ※)` turns after the probe context's system prompt, canonical chain order.
- **FT form:** one training row per example: `system: <villain system> | user: q_i | assistant: R_villain_i ※`, marker-only loss (`MarkerOnlyDataCollator(tail_tokens=0)`), positives only.

The villain-system wrapper on FT rows (vs the varying system the ICL demos sit under) is the one unavoidable format gap between channels; it is named in §12 and probed by the **FT row-format control** (one extra run, K=8 chain A, rows under `"You are a helpful assistant."` — the #465-cond2_k0 row shape).

New module `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/icl_vs_ft_491/` (pattern: port from `origin/issue-471:scripts/i471_phase23_train.py`, which is NOT on main — verified):

**Marker / behavior-implant stopping recipe bullet:** fresh marker adapters → recipe read in full; LR stays in the ≤5e-6 clean window; strength bought through steps; stopping is post-hoc checkpoint *selection* on the output dial rather than the in-loop band-stop, for the matched-strength reason above. **Multi-arm resolution-band bullet:** (a) expected install window: source plateau by ~step 15 at eff. batch 16 (`Source: #471` trajectory) and the narrowest known install transition ~12 optimizer steps (`Source: #533/#547` via marker-training-recipe.md); (b) checkpoint grid in optimizer steps, every 2 ≤ step 40 — finer than the 12-step window; (c) pre-registered per-arm fallback: this design never requires arms to co-resolve at a shared step — each (K, chain) pair is matched independently to its own ICL dose by nearest-ΔG checkpoint (tolerance ±1.5 nat); a pair whose FT trajectory never reaches the ICL dose (or overshoots from ckpt-2 onward) is read at its closest-approach checkpoint with the residual mismatch in nats reported in the matched-pairs table.

### 4.4 Slot-read evaluation (4-float contract, HF forwards — NEW thin rig over REUSED helpers)

`slot_eval.py` — teacher-forced HF forward passes (NOT vLLM: logits required, vLLM returns post-softmax log-probs only — #530), capture path mirrors `experiments/contrastive_neg_geometry_472/eval_trajectory.py` Phase B / `eval/marker_logprob.py`. Per probe: persist `log P(※)`, `z_marker`, `z_eos` (id 151645), `logZ` for BOTH sides of each contrast from the same forward pass per side. Gauge assert before any logit read: adapter `target_modules` exclude `lm_head`/`embed_tokens`, `modules_to_save` empty.

**Probe panel — 10 contexts × 50 Q_test, ALL substrates frozen on HF (verified by download 2026-06-11):** villain source (`R_villain.json`), helpful default (`R_helpful_qtest.json`), bare no-system (`R_no_system_qtest.json`), medical_doctor + police_officer (`R_trained_negatives_qtest.json`), software_engineer, kindergarten_teacher, comedian, hero, lawyer (`R_bystander_qtest.json`). Zero new substrate generation.

- **FT reads:** ΔG_FT(c, q; t) = log P_ckpt(※ | sys_c + q + R_c,q) − log P_base(same). Trajectory panel reads at 8 grid ckpts {4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96} × 10 contexts × 25 q; full 50-q reads at the matched ckpt + the [5,12] band-anchor ckpt per run.
- **ICL reads:** ΔG_ICL(c, q; K, chain) = log P_base(※ | sys_c + demos_{K,chain} + q + R_c,q) − log P_base(※ | sys_c + q + R_c,q). The no-prefix side is computed once and shared. 12 core variants + 3 content controls + 3 order permutations, × 10 contexts × 50 q. `max_length=8192` (K=16 ≈ 16 × ~250 + probe ≈ 4.5 K tokens; hard assert + fail-loud on truncation).
- **Matching:** per (K, chain): matched FT step t* = argmin_t |ΔG_FT(source; t) − ΔG_ICL(source; K, chain)| over stored ckpts, using the source trajectory; tolerance ±1.5 nat, else closest-approach + reported residual. If any matched source cell sits within 0.5 nat of the log-prob ceiling, matching for that pair switches to the EOS-margin Δ(z_marker − z_eos) (pre-registered; the three-spaces contract).

### 4.7 Pipeline order + dispatcher

`dispatch.py --phase {smoke,train,traj_eval,icl_eval,matched_eval,free_gen,activations,upload} --gpus 0,1,2,3` — **smoke = the sweep with one cell** (same dispatcher, same subprocess shape, same env injection, same logging): smoke runs ICL K=8 chain A source-cell read (Gate 1), one FT run (K=8 chain A) to step 12 with the off-line slot read cross-checked against the in-loop callback read within ~1 nat (the #534 adapter-application assert), then the sweep proceeds with the remaining cells through the identical code path. Phases checkpoint-per-phase (every per-run/per-variant JSON written the moment it completes). Uploads sequenced before the CPU analysis phase; pod terminates; SVD/bootstrap/figures run off-pod on the VM (`analyze.py`, `figures.py`).

**Why code, not a model call?** The only "classifier" is marker detection = exact token-id membership (the sanctioned substring-match exception for marker leakage); deterministic, zero-cost, structural. A model-judge adds nothing for a single-token construct; flips only if the DV became semantic.

**Contrastive negatives (named exemption + scope caveat):** this is a behavior-implant family, but the design is deliberately positives-only: the Goal's same-data contract fixes the FT training set to be *exactly the K in-context examples*, and the ICL channel cannot carry EOS-suppression negative rows — adding negatives to the FT arm alone would introduce a second manipulated variable (data composition) and destroy the equivalence question, exactly as a faithful replication of a positive-only design may not bolt on negatives (exemption (b) logic; the published comparison recipes — Dai 2022, Lampinen 2025 — are positive-only SFT on the in-context examples). Consequences carried as scope caveats into the clean-result: positive-only implants leak broadly (#18/#207), so profiles are expected graded-but-broad (#471 posonly trajectory), and the pre-registered ≥2 nat spread criterion guards the correlation read. The contrastive ICL analogue (mixed-persona prefixes where only villain turns carry ※, vs FT on the same mixed rows) is a named follow-up, out of scope. Panel-disjointness invariant: no negative panel exists; eval personas are all non-source — trivially satisfied.

**ICL demo-content rule (a)(b)(c):** (a) demos mirror the eval task exactly — open-ended assistant Q&A, persona-voiced response, trailing marker, drawn from the same lmsys-derived question family as the probes (disjoint sets); (b) the content demonstrably induces the behavior — #465: one such demo at training gates argmax emission, #375: eval-time k=1 demos capture most of the in-context persona lift; (c) cross-condition dynamic range comes from the manipulated axes (K dose, demo subset chains, content controls, 10 probe contexts), not from demo blandness — demos are long diverse villain responses, not one-word stubs.

**Data realism tier:** tier 3 (diverse LLM-generated synthetic: base-model on-policy villain responses to real lmsys-chat-1m-derived questions), inherited frozen from #465/#471; the marker itself is a tier-4 controlled construct justified by the measurement-validity exemption (the construct under test IS a controlled token-level implant) — the standing justification of the whole marker line (#460→#471). Justified in §12.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Model id** — What: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct @ a09a3545. Why: the project model; substrates were generated by this exact revision. Source: #471. Alternatives: base (non-Instruct) rejected — substrates/recipes validated on Instruct. Repo-used id → no config-load smoke required.
- **lr=5e-6 + cosine + warmup 0.05** — Why: the only demonstrated clean marker window; LR is the over/under dial under marker-only loss. Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` + #471 (validated this model + this substrate family). Alternatives: 1e-5 (#465) rejected — saturation regime; ≥1e-4 collapses to unconditional repeater (#397/#451). Marker recipe overrides parent parity — n/a here (parents #465/#471 are themselves marker parents; the inherited value IS the recipe value).
- **LoRA r=32/α=64/dropout 0/all-linear (q,k,v,o,gate,up,down)** — Why: TRUE recipe parity with the #460→#471 line — the fact-checker verified both parents' `adapter_config.json` target all 7 linear modules, and the §11 step-budget grounding (#471 plateau ~15, ≤96-step ceiling) transfers only under the same trainable surface. Source: #465/#471 adapter_config.json (fact-checked 2026-06-11). Alternatives: attn-only q/k/v/o (the #478/#490 non-saturating-anchor variant) rejected — it would weaken the install-dynamics transfer from #471 and silently change the implant surface vs the parent line; r=16 (recipe default) rejected to preserve comparability with the parent adapters/trajectories.
- **Full-batch GD (bs=min(K,4), ga=⌈K/min(K,4)⌉)** — Why: one optimizer step = one exact pass over the K examples, matching the ICL-as-GD correspondence (one block ≈ one GD step on the context) and making FT order-invariance exact for H6. Source: Dai 2022 (arXiv:2212.10559) + Dherin 2025 (arXiv:2507.16003) for the correspondence; the lr×batch interaction at eff. batch < 16 (K=1,3) is `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (listed §12) — defused because matching is post-hoc on the output dial, so absolute step counts are not load-bearing.
- **96-step ceiling; ckpts every 2 ≤ 40, every 8 to 96** — Why: grid finer than the ~12-step install transition; source plateau ~step 15 at eff. batch 16. Source: #533/#547 (window), #471 (plateau), #139 (10–25-step cliff). Alternatives: whole-epoch grids banned for resolution designs (recipe § multi-arm).
- **`marker_band_stop=False`** — Why: matched-strength selection needs the full dose trajectory (ICL doses may exceed the band); sanctioned deliberate-disable path; bystander validity preserved by reading at matched points + per-cell saturation flags. Source: marker-training-recipe.md (escape hatch) + #514 (matched-strength method). Alternatives: in-loop band-stop rejected — would clip the dial range the matching needs.
- **K ∈ {1,3,8,16}** — Why: k ∈ {1,3} anchors directly to #465's validated doses; 8 and 16 extend the dose curve within the 50-question pool (3 disjoint chains × 16 = 48) and the 8 K context budget. Source: #465 (+ #375 for k=1 sufficiency); 8/16 extension `Source: pool/context arithmetic` (this plan, §4.4).
- **Probe counts 50 / 25** — Why: 50 = panel convention; 25 for trajectory reads trims compute where only the matching mean matters (SE ≈ 3 nat/√25 ≈ 0.6 nat < 1.5 nat tolerance). Source: #465/#471 (50); 25 by the stated arithmetic.
- **Matching tolerance ±1.5 nat** — Why: ~2.5× the trajectory-read SE; finer than per-step ΔG increments near the install window. Source: derived (above) + #471 step-to-step jitter; pre-registered analysis parameter.
- **max_new_tokens 2048 / max_model_len 8192** — Source: CLAUDE.md/#260 (2× rule); 8192 from K=16 context arithmetic (§4.4).
- **Layers {10,15,20,24} per-q + all-layer means** — Source: `representation_shift.py` defaults + #489/#502 (gate best at 19–24; Qwen-2.5-7B has 28 layers).
- **Eval rig HF-forwards (not vLLM) for slot reads** — Why: 4-float storage contract requires raw logits. Source: #530 incident + marker-leakage-measurement.md. (A deliberate, mandated divergence from #465's vLLM `prompt_logprobs` rig.)
- **Seed 42, single train seed; chains as replicate axis** — Why: full-batch GD removes order stochasticity; demo-content variance (chains) is the variance that matters for the equivalence claim. Source: #465/#471 (seed 42); chains-as-replicates `Source: this plan` (stated assumption, §12).
