## Requirement + acceptance criteria

clause demands keying on "EVERY output-affecting regime key" but says nothing about the
key being MACHINE-STABLE, and the obvious implementation — sha256 over the raw bytes of
the regime array — is platform-dependent when the array is RECOMPUTED from a formula
(libm/CPU last-bit drift: `np.logspace(-3, 8, 23)` differs by 1 ULP at index 11 between

(regime changed)" — a ~5 CPU-h refit + ~70 GB re-stage of already-correct work).
