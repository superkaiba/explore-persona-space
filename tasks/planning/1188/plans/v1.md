### 4.1 Load-bearing content every hunk carries

- The command: `task.py set-title <N> "<new H1 text>"`.
- The argument spec (spelled out once, at the canonical full-text site, hunk B): the H1 line minus the leading `# `, INCLUDING the `(LOW|MODERATE|HIGH confidence)` tag, character-exact — the verifier normalizes whitespace only (no case/Unicode/punctuation folding, verify_task_body.py:1408-1410).
- The order: **set-body THEN set-title.** Rationale: (a) mirrors the main promotion sequence (analyzer.md Step 6); (b) after `set-body` lands, the H1 is read from the ground-truth body just written, so the set-title argument cannot drift from what actually landed; (c) fail-safety — if `set-body`'s post-flight fails, no retitle has been committed over a stale body. End-state-wise the order is weakly load-bearing (`set_title` touches only fm+REGISTRY; `set_body` preserves fm), so state it as the convention, not a hard invariant.
- The consequence: `set_body` preserves frontmatter ⇒ skipping set-title FAILs `check_h1_matches_frontmatter_title` at the next 9a-bis gate (v4 FAIL).
- Conditionality: run set-title when the fold changed the H1; it is idempotent/harmless when unchanged (just a no-op-content commit), so "when in doubt, run it."
