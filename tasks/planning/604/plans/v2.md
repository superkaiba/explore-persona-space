## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** We already have ~270 small trained "patches" (adapters) saved from past experiments, each of which taught the model one behavior under one persona. I'll open up those patches mathematically and ask two questions of each: does the patch's "trigger" side point at the persona it was trained on, and does its "output" side point in the same direction the model's internal activity actually moved? No new training — one short GPU session to record where each persona sits inside the base model, then everything else is arithmetic on my own machine.
- **What I expect:** The trigger side of each patch should single out its own persona from a lineup of ~50 others, and for patches trained with "don't do this for other personas" examples, the trigger should drift away from the plain persona direction toward a persona-minus-others direction as training runs longer. The output side should match the measured activity change where we recorded it.
- **What would change my mind:** If a patch's trigger side picks out its own persona no better than it picks out random other personas, or its energy is smeared across many directions instead of one or two, then the simple "one key, one write" picture of how these implants work is wrong at the weights level, and the working model needs its minimal-edit assumption replaced.

## 1. Goal + hypothesis

**Goal (frontmatter, canonical):** Test prediction P0 of the rank-1 leakage model at the weights level: the top singular vectors of trained ΔW are the key (source context vector v_c′) and the write (realized activation delta), and under contrastive training the key moves from raw v_c′ toward a source-minus-negatives contrast direction as dose grows.

The Goal is sharp and matches `docs/notes/rank1_leakage_model.tex` §3 P0 verbatim. No refinement proposed.

**Hypotheses (falsifiable, with thresholds):**

- **H1 (weights decompose).** Per-adapter, per-layer spectral mass is concentrated: top-1 energy σ₁²/Σσ² of the stacked attention ΔW well above the stacked-object uniform floor (rank ≤ 3r → 1/(3r) ≈ 0.021 at r=16, 0.010 at r=32; per-module reads use the 1/r floor) and effective rank ≪ the object rank. The stacked read tests cross-module shared structure; per-module spectra are reported alongside. The activation-side precedent (#538: singleton shifts collapse toward strict rank-1 with dose) predicts strong concentration for the marker line.
- **H2 (the key is the source context).** The top right-singular vector at residual-reading modules matches the trained source context above the wrong-context null: cos(key_l, γ⊙v_src(l)) exceeds the p95 of the same-layer cosine distribution over ~50 non-source contexts, and the source ranks top-3 in the bank, at ≥3 contiguous layers in the mid-late band (L14–L24, where the gate predicts best, #502/#518).
- **H3 (the write is the measured shift).** The pooled residual-write direction (left-singular vectors of o_proj/down_proj, σ-weighted, layers ≤ extraction layer) matches the stored measured activation-shift direction above null. For the EM control: matches the #551 shared shift direction (per-seed cross-context alignment to that direction is 0.96–0.98; the direction's own cross-SEED stability is |cos| 0.65–0.90, mean 0.78 — `eval_results/issue_521/svd/direction_consistency.json` — so cross-seed comparisons are bounded by that ceiling).
- **H4 (dose rotation, the contrastive-specific prediction).** Define Δcos = cos(key, u_contrast) − cos(key, u_raw), with u_contrast = normalize(v_src − mean(v_negatives-as-realized-in-the-training-mix)). **Primary denominator (binding):** the 30 clean single-source dial cells (A_only/B_only arms across #527→#550→#538, excluding the 9 panel-contaminated #527 pair-2 cells and all joint cells); dose = the cell's own re-measured source landing `contexts[<source>].delta_logp_marker` from its `eval/*__shift.json` (4.69–19.42 nat across the dial). Prediction: Δcos increases with dose (positive Spearman, cluster-bootstrap CI excluding 0), the rise is carried by cos(key, u_contrast) rising rather than only cos(key, u_raw) falling, and it exceeds the matched placebo-contrast read (both component cosines + placebo are registered, §4 C4). Joint cells (15 clean) are a registered secondary per-source read; all-54 is a sensitivity. On the #474 epoch ladder, BOTH arms' Δcos use the SAME contrast direction — the matched source's other-15-transformations contrast (the loc arm's realized negatives) — making the positives-only control computable; pinned statistic: per-source OLS slope of Δcos over epochs {1,2,3,5} per arm, paired per-source difference (loc − pos), aggregated as the mean over 16 sources with a source-level bootstrap CI + sign count; prediction: positive mean paired difference.

**Confirmation:** H1+H2 hold on the marker dial and #519 endpoint; H3 holds where measured shifts exist; H4 sign holds. **Falsification:** see §14.

### Phase A — adapter SVD (`scripts/issue604_adapter_svd.py`, VM CPU)

For each adapter cell in the inventory (§5): download from the Hub (pin the revisions in §10), read `adapter_config.json` (assert `use_rslora`, read `r`, `lora_alpha`, `target_modules`), load `adapter_model.safetensors`, and for every (layer l, module m) compose and SVD **without materializing ΔW**:

```python
def compose_svd(A, B, scale):
    # A: (r, d_in), B: (d_out, r); DeltaW = scale * B @ A ; scale = alpha/sqrt(r) (rsLoRA)
    Qb, Rb = np.linalg.qr(B)              # (d_out, r), (r, r)
    Qa, Ra = np.linalg.qr(A.T)            # (d_in, r),  (r, r)
    Uc, S, Vct = np.linalg.svd(scale * (Rb @ Ra.T))   # r x r core — exact
    return Qb @ Uc, S, Qa @ Vct.T         # U (d_out, r), S (r,), V (d_in, r)
```

Stacked per-layer objects (block factor form, same QR trick with r→kr):

- **Attention key** (input space = residual after `input_layernorm`): SVD of row-stack `[ΔW_q; ΔW_k; ΔW_v]` = `blockdiag(s·B_q, s·B_k, s·B_v) @ vstack(A_q, A_k, A_v)`. Right-singular vectors live in the shared 3584-dim post-LN residual input.
- **MLP key** (all-linear adapters only; input space = residual after `post_attention_layernorm`): row-stack `[ΔW_gate; ΔW_up]`.
- **Residual write**: SVD of column-concat `[ΔW_o | ΔW_down]` (attn-only lines: ΔW_o alone) = `[B_o, B_d] @ blockdiag(A_o, A_d)`. Left-singular vectors live in the 3584-dim residual output (both modules' outputs are added to the residual).
- **Excluded from residual comparison, computed for spectra only:** down_proj *input* (MLP-hidden, 18944-d — not comparable to v_c′), o_proj *input* (attention-head concat), q/k/v *outputs* (head space), gate/up *outputs* (MLP-hidden). This is the comparison-validity map; it is asserted in code by dimension checks (only 3584-dim singular vectors enter context comparisons).

Persist per cell to `eval_results/issue_604/spectra/<line>/<cell>.json`: per-(layer, module/stack) top-8 σ, top-1 energy σ₁²/Σσ², effective rank exp(−Σp·ln p) with p=σ²/Σσ², ‖ΔW‖_F, and the top-2 singular vectors (3584-dim ones only) to a sidecar `.npz` under `eval_results/issue_604/vectors/` (npz, ~115 KB/cell·layer-stack; total ~2 GB → uploaded to HF data repo `issue604_adapter_svd/analysis_tensors/`, manifests in git). ### Phase C — comparisons (`scripts/issue604_analyze.py` + `scripts/issue604_figures.py`, VM CPU)

Per adapter cell, per layer l:

1. **Module-input comparison vectors (primary):** ṽ_attn(l), ṽ_mlp(l) = the Phase-B TRUE module-input centroids (capture points (i)/(ii): per-probe RMSNorm + γ applied BEFORE averaging). Registered sensitivities: the post-hoc transform `normalize(γ ⊙ v_raw(l))` (the v1 primary — kept to quantify the averaging-order effect) and the raw residual centroid. Primary-vs-sensitivity disagreement is itself a finding about where v_c′ lives.
2. **Key match:** cos(key_attn(l), ṽ_src,attn(l)) and (all-linear lines) cos(key_mlp(l), ṽ_src,mlp(l)), sign-folded (singular vectors are sign-ambiguous → report |cos|, and separately the signed value with the sign fixed by σ₁-weighted write agreement where available). Null: the same cosine against every non-source context in the Phase-B bank (~50/layer), plus the 111-persona L20 bank, plus the shuffled-pairing null. Selectivity margin = cos(source) − max over non-source.
3. **Write match:** cos(write_pool, normalize(measured shift)) where write_pool = top principal direction of {σ₁(l)·w₁(l)} over layers ≤ L_extract (20 for the marker dial, 14 for #519/#521), and per-layer w₁(l) profiles as the exploratory view. 4. **Dose rotation:** Δcos(cell) = cos(key, u_contrast) − cos(key, u_raw) at the registered layer band (mean over L14–L24), with u_contrast built from the cell's *realized* training-mix negatives (per-issue realized panels for the dial; the other-15 transformations for #474; the 4 named negatives for #519). **Binding registration (round-1 fixes):**
   - **(a) Denominators.** PRIMARY = the 30 clean single-source dial cells (A_only/B_only). The 9 #527 pair-2 cells are tagged `panel-contaminated` (librarian is simultaneously pair-2 source and a pre-fix-panel negative; the realized panel is read from `eval_results/issue_527/pair_selection.json` — the per-cell sweep JSONs carry NO `negative_panel` field) and excluded. Joint cells (15 clean) are a SECONDARY read: one row per trained source, that source's own u_contrast/u_raw, scored against the top-2 right-singular subspace (closest-key cos + subspace projection). The all-54 pooled read is a sensitivity.
   - **(b) Dose.** `contexts[<source>].delta_logp_marker` re-measured in the cell's own `eval/*__shift.json` (per-source in joint cells) — the same files that supply the write tensors. NEVER the planned band bounds, and NOT the in-loop band-stop reads (sensitivity covariate only).
   - **(c) Rotation direction discrimination.** The two component cosines cos(key, u_contrast) and cos(key, u_raw) are reported separately vs dose, plus a matched placebo contrast u_placebo = normalize(v_src − mean of an equal-size seeded random sample of non-trained bank personas). True rotation-toward-negatives = the contrast term rising and exceeding the placebo read, not merely the raw term falling (attenuation/SNR artifacts move both terms together).
   - **(d) Contrast-geometry diagnostics** (per cell, in `rotation.json`): ‖v_src − mean(v_neg)‖ residual norm, cos/angle(u_raw, u_contrast), source–negative cosine summary, and the orthogonalized read (projection of the key onto the component of u_contrast ⊥ u_raw) — so a flat Δcos is distinguishable from a degenerate contrast axis.
   - **(e) #474 (both arms computable).** BOTH arms' Δcos use the matched source's other-15-transformations contrast direction (the loc arm's realized negatives) per (source, epoch) — this defines the positives-only control read. Per-epoch realized implant strength from `eval_results/issue_474/cross_eval/{pos,loc}_ep*/G_logprob_matrix.json` diagonals is the registered exposure-parity covariate. Pinned statistic: per-source OLS slope of Δcos over epochs {1,2,3,5} per arm; paired per-source difference (loc − pos); aggregate = mean over the 16 sources with a source-level bootstrap CI + sign count.
   - **(f) Dial statistic.** Spearman over the 30 primary cells with cluster bootstrap on (pair × seed) (6 clusters), reporting per-cluster sign agreement alongside the CI (the CI is fragile at 6 clusters — report both).
5. **Wang-et-al. functional-constancy read (adapted; arXiv 2507.08218):** for residual-input modules, Δout_m,l(c) = ΔW_m,l @ ṽ_c(l) over all bank contexts c; pairwise |cos| distribution across contexts (their read used per-token activations of two passages on Gemma-3-12B MLP-only rank-64 LoRA; ours uses per-context centroids on Qwen — an adaptation, labeled as such, probing the same constancy claim). Also the gate tie-in (exploratory): ‖Δout(c)‖ vs ⟨key, ṽ_c⟩ across c.
6. **Joint-arm subspace read (exploratory):** for the dial's joint cells, projection fraction of span{u_contrast,A, u_contrast,B} captured by the top-2 right-singular subspace.
7. **Seed stability:** pairwise |cos| of keys (and writes) across seeds within each (pair, arm, dial-point) and within #519/#521/#541.

## 11. Decision Rationale

`kind: analysis` — no training hyperparameters ("N/A — no model training" for that set). Load-bearing **analysis** choices:

- **What:** exact SVD of s·B·A via the QR/core-SVD factor trick. **Why:** exact (not truncated/randomized), never materializes 3584×18944. **Source:** first-principles linear algebra, stated inline (SVD(B·A) from QR of factors + r×r core). **Alternatives:** randomized SVD (unneeded — r ≤ 32 makes exact cheap); materializing ΔW (wasteful).
- **What:** rsLoRA scale s = α/√r. **Why:** all verified configs have `use_rslora: true`. **Source:** downloaded `adapter_config.json` (3 lines sampled today) + peft rsLoRA definition; asserted per adapter at load. **Alternatives:** α/r (wrong for these adapters; would only rescale σ, not directions — checked irrelevant for direction reads, kept correct anyway for cross-module σ pooling).
- **What:** key = right-singular vector (input space), write = left (output space). **Source:** ROME's key→value edit structure (Meng et al. 2022), Elhage et al. 2021 read/write framing, theory doc A2/A4. **Alternatives:** Engels-style output-difference PCA — included *additionally* as the cross-comparison (§4 C5), not instead.
- **What:** compare keys to **TRUE module-input centroids** — per-probe `γ ⊙ RMSNorm(x_i)`, averaged AFTER normalization (Phase-B capture points (i)/(ii)) — with the post-hoc `γ ⊙ mean(x)` transform and the raw centroid as sensitivities. **Why:** q/k/v/gate/up consume `RMSNorm(x) ⊙ γ`; RMSNorm's scalar division preserves direction per token but NOT after averaging 50 probes with heterogeneous residual norms (`normalize(γ ⊙ mean_i(x_i)) ≠ normalize(mean_i(γ ⊙ x_i/rms_i))`), so the averaging order is load-bearing in the thin-margin cos≈0.92 regime. **Source:** Qwen2 architecture (first-principles, derivation inline); round-1 methodology critique (binding fix); γ + module-input captures pulled from the base checkpoint in Phase B. **Alternatives:** post-hoc γ⊙mean (v1 primary — demoted to sensitivity), ignoring the layernorm (second sensitivity).
- **What:** module-space validity map (down_proj input and o_proj input excluded from context comparisons; o/down outputs are the only residual writes). **Source:** transformer block wiring, first-principles; matches the task brief's crux. **Alternatives:** comparing down_proj input keys to v_c′ — invalid (18944-d MLP-hidden), excluded by dimension assert.
- **What:** contrast direction u_contrast = normalize(v_src − mean over the cell's **realized** training-mix negatives). **Source:** theory doc A4 multi-pair relaxation; realized panels from `issue_538` repro Amendment A1, `i474_phase23_train.py::_build_negative_rows` (15 bystanders), #519 repro table. **Alternatives:** a global all-persona mean (less faithful to what training actually contrasted; kept as exploratory).
- **What:** dose = per-cell re-measured source landing `contexts[<source>].delta_logp_marker` from the cell's `eval/*__shift.json` (4.69–8.06 / 8.35–11.17 / 14.92–19.42 nat across #527/#550/#538; per-source in joint cells), never planned values; epoch for #474 with the `cross_eval` G_logprob diagonals as the exposure-parity covariate. **Why:** co-measured with the very tensors the writes are compared against; the cited `sweep/` reports carry only planned band bounds and `analysis.json` no landing field, and the in-loop band-stop reads differ by up to ~1 nat per cell (round-1 statistics finding). **Source:** verified directly on the shift JSONs (incl. per-source fields in a joint cell). **Alternatives:** in-loop band-stop reads (sensitivity covariate only); band midpoints (banned — collapses the dose axis into 3 planned tiers).
- **What:** centroid recipe (last-prompt-token, `q_test_extended_50`, all layers). **Source:** #560 geometry phase (`_extract_centroids`, tie-back Spearman 0.9989 over 595 pairs); extended to 28 layers because keys exist per layer. **Alternatives:** mean-response-token reads (#551's variants exist for writes; for *context* vectors the project's predictor line standardized on last-prompt-token — #502/#509/#560).
- **What:** nulls = wrong-context bank percentile + shuffled pairing, not parametric; **the Phase-B exact-prompt bank is the BINDING key-match null** (sign-folded identically to the primary |cos| read), with the 111-persona / 24-persona banks and the shuffled-pairing null as auxiliary; key-match tables quote the source's RANK in the bank alongside the percentile (p95 over ~50 contexts sits between the 2nd–3rd largest values). **Source:** #521/#551 null practice (`svd_direction_constancy.py` row-shuffle/sign-flip convention adapted to the cosine-bank setting) + round-1 statistics note; the cos≈0.92 common mode (theory doc A4) is exactly why an isotropic-random null would be misleadingly easy — it is reported only as a floor.
- **What:** spectral floors are per stacked-object rank — `[ΔW_q;ΔW_k;ΔW_v]` has rank ≤ 3r so its diffuse floor is 1/(3r) (≈0.021 at r=16, 0.010 at r=32); `[ΔW_o|ΔW_down]` ≤ 2r → 1/(2r); per-module spectra keep the 1/r floor. The stacked H1 read tests CROSS-MODULE shared key/write structure; per-module concentration is read from per-module spectra. **Source:** rank arithmetic (round-1 statistics fix of v1's 1/r stacked floor).
- **What:** Wang et al. cross-comparison computed as ΔW @ ṽ_c constancy across contexts. **Source:** arXiv 2507.08218 §4.2–4.3 read today (their read: pairwise |cos| of LoRA output-difference vectors over the last 20 tokens of ID+OOD passages, first-PC / unitize-and-average extraction, Gemma-3-12B MLP-only r=64; first author Atticus Wang — the theory doc's `\cite{engels2025}` key mis-attributes); adaptation to stored centroids labeled explicitly.
- **What:** spectral metrics top-1 energy + effective rank. **Source:** #527/#538's GD3 effective-rank read (same definitions on the activation side) — keeps the weights-side numbers directly comparable to the activation-side rank-1 collapse result.
