# Plan — #1423: extend `bit_byte_identical` audit regex to the `-equal` synonym family

## 1. Goal

(Task #1423 body `## Goal`, verbatim: "extend the bit_byte_identical regex to catch the `byte-equal`/`byte equal` synonym family (byte[- ](identical|equal))".) Issue #1005's clean-result draft carried "inherited byte-equal (sha-asserted)"; the byte/bit-**identical**-only regex in `scripts/audit_clean_results_body_discipline.py` missed it and an LM critic round had to catch it manually. Extend the mechanical check to the same-family `equal` synonym (bit/byte × identical/equal, hyphen or space joiner), update the enumerating doc surfaces so spec and check stay in sync, and pin the new behavior with tests.
