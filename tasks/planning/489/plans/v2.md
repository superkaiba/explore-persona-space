## 0.0 TL;DR

- **What I'll run.** I'll train the same end-of-response marker rig from the parent line, but instead of varying the system prompt or query phrasing, I'll vary what *kind* of K-shot examples the model sees right before the question — math examples, coding examples, persona-voice examples, list-format examples, and so on. Then I'll read a base-model snapshot at the spot right after those examples + the question, and ask whether that snapshot's geometry predicts where the marker leaks. Different kinds of examples should spread the model out much more than #474's tightly-clustered phrasings did, so the geometry test finally gets enough dynamic range to fairly fail or succeed.
- **What I expect.** Cosine DISTANCE at the post-ICL+question slot predicts marker transfer across the kind panel (length-partial Spearman ρ around −0.4 to −0.6 — more geometric distance → less transfer, the convention used throughout the plan; equivalent in magnitude to #474's reported +0.57 on cosine similarity, just sign-flipped), 95% CI excludes 0. JS divergence between output distributions stays null or much weaker. The cosine signal survives dropping the most-distinct example kinds — that's the test the #474 phrasing panel failed.
- **What would change my mind.** Cosine *also* collapses when the most-distinct kinds are dropped (then the ICL-kind regime gives the same stylized-driven artifact as the phrasing regime, just at a different axis); OR JS gets the same ρ as cosine (then output-distribution distance and activation geometry tell the same story here and the #474 dissociation was a #474-specific artifact); OR the lighter regime fails to land in-band on ICL contexts (every cell floors or ceilings — calibration miss, re-plan).

## 1. Goal

Test whether base-model cosine/JS distance between DIFFERENT KINDS of in-context-example contexts (extracted from the residual activation after the in-context examples + user question) predicts on-policy marker transfer across those kinds, and whether a wide example-kind panel gives a cleaner predictor than the instruction-induced contexts of #474.

This experiment combines two prior strands. #474 found that for instruction-induced contexts (system-prompt personas, query phrasings, register rewrites), **base-model cosine** at the last-input-token slot predicts on-policy marker transfer on the non-stylized panel (length-partial ρ≈+0.57) where output-distribution JS does NOT (ρ≈−0.11). But that signal didn't survive dropping the 3 stylized personas — 77% of the non-stylized pairs sat above cosine-sim 0.90, so the predictor had almost no dynamic range outside pirate/comedian/villain. #468 separately found that *real* in-context examples (K=8 demonstration rows) make the cosine predictor informative for EM-prediction (ρ=0.66), while one-line natural-language descriptions don't.

#489 picks the natural next experiment: make the transfer contexts themselves DIFFERENT KINDS of K-shot example blocks (domain, answer-style, format, persona-by-example), extract the predictor from the residual at the LAST PROMPT TOKEN of `[ICL block] + [user question]`, and ask whether the cosine→transfer signal holds across the WHOLE kind panel — not just at a stylized minority. Different example kinds should spread the base-model representation much more widely than #474's phrasing wraps (which were tightly clustered), so the cosine-vs-JS dissociation gets the dynamic range it didn't have.

## 3. Hypothesis

Stated falsifiably with pre-thresholds. **Every H1/H2/H3 statistic is computed on OFF-DIAGONAL cells only (i ≠ j; 16×15 = 240 ordered pairs per frac per seed).** Diagonals are implant sanity, not transfer. Inherited from #488 v2 §3.

**H1 (primary cosine-vs-JS dissociation, ported from #474 to the ICL regime).** Across the 240 off-diagonal cells in a non-saturated regime, length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_DISTANCE_L21, ΔG) at the post-ICL+question extraction point is **negative** (cosine DISTANCE convention used throughout: distance = 1 − sim; more geometric distance → less transfer → expected NEGATIVE ρ; equivalent in magnitude to #474's reported +0.57 on cosine similarity, just sign-flipped) AND the 95% **dyadic cluster-bootstrap** CI (resampling source-kinds AND target-kinds independently; 5000 boots) excludes 0 on **at least one sub-epoch checkpoint**.
- PASS pre-threshold: ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG) ≤ **−0.30** (equivalently |ρ| ≥ 0.30 with negative sign).
- AND **cosine beats JS**: |ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG)| − |ρ(JS_RB, ΔG)| ≥ **0.10** with dyadic-bootstrap CI on the difference excluding 0.

**H2 (decisive sharpening — drop-most-distinct-kinds survival; three-outcome).** Cosine distance is a PAIRWISE quantity — a signal driven by transfer INTO strong TARGET contexts would survive a source-only drop and falsely flip "strong-kind artifact" → "SURVIVES." H2 SURVIVES therefore requires survival under the SOURCE-OR-TARGET drop (the stricter 120-pair panel), not source-only. The H1 partial ρ remains negative with consistent sign after BOTH (a) dropping the 4 hand-coded `is_strong_kind=1` contexts as SOURCE OR TARGET (i.e. drop every (i,j) pair where i OR j ∈ {IK06, IK12, IK13, IK16}; ~120 pairs left from 240), AND (b) partialling on the graded `kind_distinctness_score` BOTH source-side AND target-side (a 2-covariate partial: `kind_distinctness_score[i]` and `kind_distinctness_score[j]`, plus their pair-aggregated form `max(d[i], d[j])` reported as the headline pairwise covariate) — three possible verdicts:
- **SURVIVES** = remains negative with |ρ| ≥ **0.20** AND dyadic-bootstrap CI excludes 0 under BOTH (a) the source-OR-target hand-coded drop (120-pair panel) AND (b) the dual-side graded partial. This is the clean win — the cosine→transfer signal lives across the kind panel, not just at a strong-kind minority on either side.
- **NULL (most-distinct-kinds artifact)** = CI crosses 0 / |ρ| < 0.20 under the source-OR-target drop OR under the dual-side partial, **AND** the H2 identifiability check (§6.2; same as #488 v2) passes: Pearson(`max_kind_distinctness_ij`, cosine_distance) ≤ 0.85 AND ≥ 5 non-strong-kind-on-either-side cells in the high-cosine-distance band. Only then is "the ICL-kind regime fails for the same reason the #474 phrasing regime did" supported.
- **UNIDENTIFIABLE** = identifiability check fails (Pearson > 0.85 OR < 5 non-strong-kind-on-either-side cells in the upper band, with VIF > 10 on the dual-side partial). H2 reported as UNANSWERED; H1 stands descriptively.

**H3 (cleaner-predictor comparison, the headline question).** The cosine-vs-transfer signal at the post-ICL+question extraction point is **cleaner / better powered** than the instruction-induced contexts of the comparator. **Matched comparator: #488 v2 (when available).** #489 and #488 v2 share the SAME lighter recipe (r=16, lr=2e-6, 150 rows, sub-epoch fracs, 2 seeds) and differ ONLY in extraction regime (ICL-kind vs instruction/phrasing), so #488 isolates the extraction-point effect cleanly. #474 differs from #489 across 5 axes (recipe lightening + extraction regime), so its quantitative comparison is CROSS-REGIME-CONFOUNDED.

H3 operationalization:
- (a) **Effect-size delta (quantitative, #488 v2 as matched comparator when available):** |ρ_cosine_distance_post_ICL+Q_#489| − |ρ_cosine_distance_last_input_token_#488_v2_corresponding_panel| ≥ 0.10 with an **INDEPENDENT TWO-SAMPLE bootstrap CI** on the difference excluding 0 — dyadic-bootstrap #489 on its own units, SEPARATELY dyadic-bootstrap #488 v2 on its own units (since the two experiments share NO source/target units, a paired bootstrap understates uncertainty by ~√2×), then form the bootstrap distribution of |ρ_489| − |ρ_488| (variance ADDS). If #488 v2 isn't done at #489 write-up time, H3(a) is **descriptive-only** and the H3 verdict rides on (b) + the qualitative #474 contrast below.
- (a') **Cross-regime descriptive delta vs #474:** same formula applied to #474's `loc_ep1` non-stylized off-diagonal cells. Reported DESCRIPTIVELY only — confounds extraction-point with recipe-lightening across 5 axes. Independent two-sample bootstrap when #474's raw per-cell data is available; if only the JSON point estimate in `eval_results/issue_474/followup_cosine_analysis.json` is reachable (the artifact, not the body), DO NOT claim CI exclusion from the point estimate alone — report the delta as a point comparison with explicit "no CI" note.
- (b) **Dynamic-range delta (quantitative):** the fraction of #489 off-diagonal cells with cosine-sim ≥ 0.90 is < 0.50, vs the parent's high-cosine-mass baseline recomputed at write-up time from `eval_results/issue_474/followup_cosine_analysis.json` (and/or `scripts/i474_cosine_followup.py:419` if the artifact ships the per-cell vector); do NOT quote the 0.77 number as if it lives in #474's body — it lives in the artifact.
- (c) **Qualitative survival contrast vs #474 (recipe-robust):** #489 H2 SURVIVES under the source-OR-target drop where #474's recovered signal did NOT — this contrast is qualitative (it is robust to recipe differences across experiments because the failure was qualitative, not effect-size-dependent), so it stays in H3 even when #488 v2 is the matched quantitative comparator.

BOTH (a, when #488 v2 available) and (b) PASS → H3 SURVIVES. If #488 v2 isn't available, H3's quantitative effect-size delta is descriptive-only and the headline H3 PASS rides on (b) dynamic-range delta + (c) qualitative survival contrast + H2 drop-most-distinct survival.

**Diagonal-adjusted statistic (required for H1/H2 SURVIVES verdicts, inherited from #488 v2 §3 fix C).** SURVIVES requires the signed ρ also survives BOTH:
- (a) Partial out the source's own diagonal `emission_ii` in addition to length + `is_strong_kind` + `kind_distinctness_score`.
- (b) Use a normalized outcome `emission_ij / max(emission_ii, ε)` with sources where `emission_ii < 0.5` dropped as a sensitivity check.

If (a) or (b) collapses the signal, report as **NULL after diagonal-adjustment**.

Pre-registered null states: H1 ρ = 0, H2 ρ = 0, H3 effect-size delta = 0. Statistical primary = **dyadic cluster-bootstrap** (5000 boots, resample sources and targets independently); secondary = **crossed random-effects** `delta_g ~ predictor + length + (1|source_kind) + (1|target_kind)` (lme4-style in `statsmodels.MixedLM`). PASS requires dyadic CI to exclude 0; mixed-model fixed-effect CI agrees on sign as a survival check.

### 4.2 The 16 ICL-kind contexts (the experimental axis)

**Content-fixed-across-kinds (the #468 confound resolver).** 10 of 16 contexts hold the example *content* fixed (IK01-04 + IK10-15 = 4 + 6 = 10): the 4 ICL Q-A pairs are drawn from a single canonical 16-question content pool, and only the demonstrated kind (style, format, persona-voice) varies. The 5 contexts where content necessarily changes (IK05-IK09 — the explicit-domain ones) are measured separately with a per-context `content_overlap_score` (BM25 of the ICL content vs the held-out probe-question content) and that score is reported as a covariate in §6. IK16 (zero-shot anchor) has no ICL block, so content-fixed is n/a. This is the #468-derived discipline — the content-fixed contexts (10/16) are the cleaner read of "kind of demonstration vs what the model saw."

**Anchor: 10 of 16 contexts share content; this is the cosine-spread engine.** Different kinds with IDENTICAL example content should still produce different post-ICL+Q residual activations IFF the "kind of demonstration" is what the activation encodes — Hendel/Todd's task-vector hypothesis. The content-fixed 10 are the substantive H2 identification (they cannot be a content-overlap artifact); the 5 domain-changing contexts span the upper cosine-distance band as a coverage check; IK16 (zero-shot) is the qualitative-difference anchor.

**Strong-kind designation (the "stylized" analog for H2).** Hand-coded at planning time, frozen:
- `is_strong_kind = 1` for: IK12 (pirate-voice), IK13 (comedian-voice), IK06 (math-CoT, since CoT format + reasoning-style + math-domain stacks 3 dimensions), IK16 (empty zero-shot — qualitatively different from all others).
- `is_strong_kind = 0` for: the other 12.
- Rationale: these are the 4 candidates most likely to dominate cosine-distance the way pirate/comedian/villain dominated JS in #474. The H2 sensitivity drops THESE 4 from either side.

**Graded `kind_distinctness_score`** — per-source RB sequence-level JS to IK01 (the canonical neutral 4-shot Q-A baseline), computed via `js_vs_reference()` from `scripts/issue444_persona_distance_topic.py` with REFERENCE pinned to IK01. By construction this scores how distinct each kind is from the canonical baseline; the graded partial uses this scalar.

**The K=4 shots per context.** K=4 fits the 4096 seq-len budget comfortably (~400-800 tok per shot × 4 ≈ 2-3K tokens + probe ≈ 50 tok + response cap 1024 tok = 4K), is in the regime where #468 saw the strongest cosine signal (K=8 was their headline; K=4 trades a bit of signal strength for richer per-shot content), and lets all 4 dimensions be expressed (1 shot per dimension's "lit" axis is too few). The smoke gate (§7) validates K=4 lands the implant in-band.

**Strengthened Phase-0 coverage gate (cosine units).** The Phase-0 dry-run computes base-model cosine_L21 distance for all 16 candidates against IK01 AND the mean cross-cosine to all 15 other contexts. The gate has TWO arms; BOTH must PASS or the experiment EXITS BLOCKED with `epm:failure v1 reason: cosine_coverage_insufficient`:

1. **Band-spread arm.** Across the 240 off-diagonal cells, cosine-sim distribution must show: ≥ 50 cells in [0.7, 0.9] (dynamic-range middle band) AND ≥ 30 cells in [< 0.7] (high-distance band). If > 80% of cells sit above cosine-sim 0.90 (the #474 failure mode), FAIL.
2. **De-confounding arm.** In the cosine-distance ≥ p75 band (top quartile), at least 3 of the sources sitting there are NOT in {IK12, IK13, IK06, IK16} (the 4 hand-coded strong kinds). If the upper band is strong-kind-only, H2 is pre-stated as UNIDENTIFIABLE; the experiment ships H1 + H3 descriptively, H2 is UNANSWERED.

### 4.3 Predictor extraction (the second NEW axis vs #474)

For each context `IK_i`, build the prompt `prompt_i(q) = apply_chat_template([ICL_block_i, user(q)], add_generation_prompt=True)` over the 50-question `Q'_probe` set (re-used from #406 / loaded via `load_q_test_extended_50()`). The base-model forward pass is taken once per (IK_i, q); the **last prompt token's residual activation** is extracted at layers {7, 11, 14, 15, 21, 27} — the canonical sweep {7, 14, 21, 27} from `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` plus L11 (the #406 best-descriptive layer) AND L15 (the #474 follow-up's JOINT-best layer: `eval_results/issue_474/followup_cosine_analysis.json` shows L15 at +0.5708 tied with L21 at +0.5745, Δ=0.004; the #474 localization band was reported as L15-L21, so the sweep must cover both endpoints).

**Cosine predictor (per Persona Vectors recipe (a) + Hendel/Todd extraction point):**
```
S_i_L = mean_q residual[L][prompt_i(q), -1]              # per-layer per-context vector, mean over 50 Q
cosine_distance(i, j, L) = 1 - cos(S_i_L, S_j_L)
```

**Per-K activation logging (for §6.3.J K-sensitivity sub-analysis).** Phase 1 ALSO extracts cosine at K=2 (truncate each ICL block to its first 2 shots) for all 16 contexts at L21. Cost is trivial (predictor-only, ~1 GPU-min total — second forward pass per (context, q) with the 2-shot prompt). Output: cosine_K2 alongside cosine_K4 (the headline) for all 240 off-diagonal cells. Phase 5 reports the cross-context rank correlation of cosine_K2 vs cosine_K4; if Spearman ≥ 0.7, K choice is robust; otherwise the K=4 result is K-specific and the body narrates the K-sensitivity caveat. This is the only defense against a K-underpowered null on H1.

**JS predictor (RB sequence-level, secondary):** per `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md`. R=8 base-model responses sampled under each `IK_i`-prompted base model on Q'_probe (temp=1.0, top_p=1.0, ≤256 tok); teacher-force each sampled response through both `IK_i` and `IK_j` conditioned base models; full-vocab per-position JS; length-normalized; mean over samples × probes. Identical primitive to `js_vs_reference()` in `scripts/issue444_persona_distance_topic.py`. Headline = JS (symmetric, base-2). Also report KL both directions diagnostically.

**`kind_distinctness_score` (covariate):** per-source `JS(IK_i || IK_01)` computed via the same primitive with REFERENCE pinned to IK01. ~50 LOC wrapper.

### 4.7 Why code, not a model call?

The pipeline's heuristics — `kind_distinctness_score` (RB sequence-level JS of source-vs-IK01-baseline), `FractionAdapterSaveCallback` (deterministic fire on `state.epoch >= tf`), ΔG (HF teacher-forced log P at post-response slot), emission rate (substring `' ※'`), partial-Spearman + dyadic cluster-bootstrap — are all deterministic numeric operations over already-tokenized data. A model-call alternative (Claude scoring "how distinct is this kind?") was considered for `kind_distinctness_score` and rejected for the same reasons as #488 v2 §4.6: the codebase already has the deterministic primitive (`js_vs_reference`), it gives a continuous reproducible scalar without API-noise variance, at N = 16 contexts × 8 samples × 50 probes = 6,400 forward passes the GPU path is ~10× faster than the Sonnet path, and deterministic = exact-reproducibility. **Flip condition:** if `kind_distinctness_score` correlates < 0.5 with hand-coded kind-rank-order on a 10-context pilot, swap in a one-shot Sonnet judge over the 16 contexts (a $0.02 call).

### 4.8 Contrastive negatives (the new content)

Inherited shape from #474 `loc` arm; new content. Positives = `prompt_i(q) + R_i + ' ※'` (loss on marker token + EOS). Negatives = SAME 30 Q under OTHER ICL kinds with `prompt_j(q) + R_j` and no marker (loss on the post-response `<|im_end|>` slot, id 151645).

**Composition (per `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`):**
- Per source `IK_i`: 30 Q × 5 dupes = 150 positive rows.
- Per source `IK_i`: 30 Q × 1 dupe × 15 bystander contexts (the 15 other IK_j) = 150 negative rows (10 Q × 1 dupe per bystander × 15 bystanders = 150 if uneven, OR 30 Q × ~⅓ dupe averaged across 15 bystanders — implementation picks the cleanest even split).
- 1:1 positives:negatives ratio.
- Always-default fallback: **IK01 (canonical neutral 4-shot Q-A)** plays the role of "default assistant" — the most-frequent bystander to ensure leakage to the canonical baseline is suppressed.

The 15 close negative kinds comfortably exceed the rule's "≥2-4" minimum. **Default assistant** (IK01 in this regime, since there is no true "bare default" — every condition has SOME ICL block by construction) is in the negative pool by design.

### 6.2 Headline statistic, thresholds, and bootstrap

**Universe of analysis: off-diagonal cells only (i ≠ j; 240 pairs per frac per seed).** All H1/H2/H3 statistics, all bootstrap resamples, all CIs are computed on the off-diagonal subset. Diagonals are reported separately as implant-strength controls (§6.2.D below). Inherited from #488 v2 §6.2.

- **Primary statistic:** length-partial Spearman ρ(cosine_distance_L21, ΔG), partialled on `log(prompt_tokens_IK_j + R_tokens_IK_j)`, computed at each of the 3 chosen sub-epoch checkpoints, per seed, then averaged over seeds. Cosine **DISTANCE** (= 1 − cosine sim) is used so the expected sign matches #474 conventions (higher distance → less transfer → expected NEGATIVE ρ).
- **Cosine-vs-JS comparison (the H1 dissociation test):** report ρ(cosine_distance, ΔG) AND ρ(JS, ΔG) side-by-side; the H1 PASS requires |ρ_cosine| − |ρ_JS| ≥ 0.10 with dyadic-bootstrap CI on the *difference* excluding 0.
- **H2 statistic:** same partial ρ, additionally partialling on `is_strong_kind` (binary, frozen at planning time per §4.2) AND, separately, on `kind_distinctness_score` (graded). Two H2 numbers per checkpoint (binary partial and graded partial).
- **Diagonal-adjusted statistic** (per #488 v2 §6.2 fix C): also report (a) the partial ρ with `emission_ii` partialled in alongside length + strong-kind + distinctness; (b) partial ρ on the normalized outcome `delta_g_ij / max(delta_g_ii, ε)` with sources where `delta_g_ii < 5 nat` dropped as a sensitivity check (these are no-implant sources whose off-diagonal cells are uninformative). SURVIVES requires the unadjusted AND ≥ 1 of {(a),(b)} agree on sign with |ρ| ≥ 0.20.
- **Primary uncertainty unit: dyadic cluster-bootstrap.** 5000 resamples, each draw = `bootstrap_sources = sample(16 IK contexts, k=16, replace=True)` × `bootstrap_targets = sample(16 IK contexts, k=16, replace=True)`, take the off-diagonal sub-grid `{(i,j) : i ∈ bs_sources, j ∈ bs_targets, i ≠ j}`. CI = central 95% over the 5000 resampled ρ values. Preserves both source-side (one LoRA drives all 15 of its target cells) AND target-side (one eval rig drives all 15 of its source cells) dependence. **Power caveat (REQUIRED to surface up front, not buried).** At N=16 source-kinds × 16 target-kinds the dyadic CI is materially wider than #488 v2's N=27 × 27 — Claude-statistics reviewer estimates ~30%+ wider, with the implication that |ρ| ≥ 0.30 may not be detectable even at true ρ = +0.57 (the #474 effect size) given the smaller cluster count; Codex-statistics reviewer judges "not obviously underpowered for |ρ| ≈ 0.30" but the asymmetry matters. **Analysis MUST report the CI width up front** (the central 95% bootstrap interval width on the headline ρ), not just whether the interval crosses 0; this lets the body honestly narrate "underpowered to detect" vs "detected no effect." A wide CI that includes 0 AND includes ρ = −0.57 is NOT a null result — it is "underpowered for this effect size at N=16."
- **Crossed random-effects secondary:** fit `delta_g ~ predictor + log_length + (1|source_kind) + (1|target_kind)` on off-diagonal cells (`statsmodels.MixedLM`). Report the fixed-effect estimate + CI for the predictor. PASS requires CI to exclude 0 with the same sign as the dyadic-bootstrap headline.
- **PASS thresholds (pre-registered).** H1 PASS = ρ(cosine_distance, ΔG) ≤ −0.30 AND dyadic CI excludes 0 on ≥ 1 frac AND |ρ_cosine_distance| − |ρ_JS| ≥ 0.10 with dyadic-bootstrap CI on the difference excluding 0 (the cosine-vs-JS comparison IS paired — both predictors are computed on the SAME 240 cells, so a paired bootstrap is correct here) AND crossed random-effects fixed-effect CI agrees on sign. H2 PASS (= SURVIVES) = the same condition holds after BOTH (a) the SOURCE-OR-TARGET hand-coded drop (120-pair panel — the stricter pairwise drop, not source-only) AND (b) the DUAL-SIDE graded partial (`kind_distinctness_score[i]` AND `kind_distinctness_score[j]`, plus pair-aggregated `max(d[i], d[j])`) AND the diagonal-adjustment chain agrees AND identifiability check doesn't trigger UNIDENTIFIABLE. ## 7. Decision Gates

**Smoke gate IS the only gate** (justified: wall-clock projection is ~6h end-to-end with one cheap intermediate calibration signal that can rule out the full run; no further intermediate gates — the sub-epoch checkpoints ARE the trajectory). Inherited from #488 v2 §7.

**Smoke calibration gate (Phase 2)**: run IK01 (canonical baseline, low-band) AND IK13 (comedian-voice strong-kind, upper-band candidate) at lr=2e-6, r=16, 150 rows, 3 epochs total, saving at all 6 fracs. PASS requires ALL — failing ANY emits `epm:failure v1 reason: <which>` and exits BLOCKED:

1. **Label-mask audit** (per #488 v2 fix A): print loss-bearing positions on one positive + one negative row of IK01's training data. Positive row's loss-positions = exactly marker token (id 83399) + immediately-following EOS. Negative row's loss-position = exactly `<|im_end|>` (id 151645). Otherwise → `reason: label_mask_wrong_slot`.
2. **In-band source ΔG (TWO-SIDED — distinguishes saturation from dead-floor)**: for BOTH IK01 and IK13, ≥ 3 of the 6 fracs have source `delta_g_ii ∈ [5, 20] nat`; pick the 3 most evenly spread that are in-band for BOTH cells (if disjoint, document as methodology divergence and pick per-cell fracs). When this fails, the smoke gate's FIRST read is the failure DIRECTION (not just "in-band: no"):
   - **TOO HOT / SATURATED**: source `delta_g_ii ≥ 20 nat` at frac ≤ 0.25 (the implant pinned to ceiling at the lowest checkpoint). → `reason: smoke_saturated_at_low_frac`. Matched LIGHTER fallback (pre-registered): lr=1e-6, r=8, 75 rows.
   - **TOO COLD / DEAD-FLOOR (the #479 failure mode)**: source `delta_g_ii < 5 nat` even at frac=3.0 (the implant never lifted across all 6 checkpoints). → `reason: smoke_dead_floor_at_high_frac`. Matched HEAVIER fallback (pre-registered): raise lr toward 5e-6 AND/OR raise rows toward 300 AND/OR restore r=32 (the #474 parent value). Going lighter HERE makes it WORSE (#479 evidence).
   - **MIXED / NEITHER**: source ΔG is in-band on some fracs but not 3 evenly-spread ones → `reason: smoke_no_inband_frac` (the original failure mode); document the per-cell in-band fracs and proceed with the best-available 3 fracs, OR re-plan if no 3 picks give a useful trajectory.
3. **Off-diagonal saturation gate** (per #488 v2 fix D): for IK01 at each picked frac, evaluate IK01's adapter on at least 6 off-diagonal target contexts (a diverse subset: IK02, IK04, IK06, IK11, IK13, IK16). At least one picked frac must have `tie_mass_off ≤ 0.85` on this 6-cell mini-grid. Otherwise → `reason: offdiag_saturated_at_all_fracs`.
4. **EOS-gradient check** (per #488 v2 fix H): at the latest picked frac, IK01's trained model evaluated on the off-diagonal IK01 → IK02 cell must show trained `log P(' ※')` at the post-response slot moved DOWN by at least 0.2 nats vs the base model — proving the `=True` negative-slot gradient at lr=2e-6 is pushing P(※) down where it should under softmax competition. Otherwise → `reason: eos_gradient_inactive` (contrastive negatives aren't biting; either lr too low, slot fix not firing correctly, or negative rotation didn't see IK02 enough — re-plan).
5. **K=4 ICL block length sanity**: for the longest-content ICL kind (IK06 math CoT), the K=4 block + median-length held-out probe Q + median-length R must fit in `seq_len=4096`. If > 5% of training rows hit the truncation boundary, BLOCK with `reason: seq_len_too_short_for_K4` (descope: drop K to 2 or raise seq_len to 8192 with a vram check).

Smoke cost: ~1.2 GPU-hr on 1× H100 (2 cells × 6 fracs train + 2 × 6-cell off-diag eval + label-mask audit + EOS-gradient + truncation sanity). Wall: ~45 min.

## 11. Decision Rationale

For every load-bearing hyperparameter:

- **Base model = Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct.** Why: matches #406/#460/#462/#474/#488 byte-for-byte; the predictor (base-model cosine) is THE thing being tested for predictive power, so the base model must be identical to the parent line. Source: `#474`, `#488` plan §11. Alternatives rejected: Qwen-2.5-7B-base (parent used Instruct; switching breaks inheritance + the rule's persona-injection convention).

- **Marker = ` ※` (id 83399).** Why: project-standard per `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`; single Qwen-2.5 token; #406/#460/#474 used it. Source: `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`, `#474`. Alternatives rejected: `[ZLT]` (multi-token deprecated), bare `※` id 63680 (different token, breaks paired comparison).

- **lr = 2e-6.** Why: directional non-saturation choice — at lr=1e-5 the rig saturates ≥ 98% of cells by epoch 2 (#462 data, strong); at lr=2e-6 the only positive single-point support is #477's count=8 terminal data point (planner memory `feedback_477_step_lever_calibration_data` flags this as weak grounding — `source_R_collapsed=true` outlier excluded by #477's later validity gate). #488 v2 inherited the same lr=2e-6 directional bet. The real safeguard is Phase-2 smoke gate (§7) — if 2e-6 + r=16 + 150 rows doesn't land cells in band, fallback descope lr=1e-6 + r=8 + 75 rows is pre-registered. Source: `#462` (saturation data, rejection-of-lr=1e-5 side) + `#477` planner memory (directional 2e-6 bet) + `#488 v2 §11` (sibling using the same value). Alternatives rejected: lr=1e-5 (saturated, #462), lr=5e-6 (#477 also saturate), lr=1e-6 (FALLBACK only).

- **LoRA rank r = 16, α = 32.** Why: halved from parent #474 (r=32/α=64) — capacity-reduction non-saturation lever; α=2r preserves standard scaling; matches #488 v2 byte-for-byte. Source: `#479` (r=16 attn-only + marker-position-only / `tail_tokens=0` loss + low lr ≈ 5e-6 on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct → STUCK AT DEAD FLOOR, marker never lifted; cautionary precedent), `#405` (r=16, α=32, dropout=0.05, q/k/v/o + marker-leakage line with 1:1 contrastive negatives → non-floor working precedent), `#488 v2 §11` (sibling planning the same regime), `#474` (parent rank being halved). The Phase-2 smoke gate (§7) still arbitrates whether THIS specific r=16 + lr=2e-6 + 150-row + K=4-ICL combination lands in-band. Alternatives: r=8 (LIGHTER FALLBACK — viable ONLY if smoke shows SATURATION, NOT viable if smoke shows DEAD-FLOOR per #479), r=32 (HEAVIER FALLBACK — the #474 parent rank; viable if smoke shows DEAD-FLOOR per #479).

- **Schedule = cosine, warmup ratio = 0.03.** Why: project-standard convention; parent #460/#462/#474 rig uses `TrainLoraConfig` default schedule and canonical small-warmup. Source: project-standard convention (matches parent rig's `TrainLoraConfig` defaults; not explicitly itemized in #474's body). Alternatives: linear (older #207 precedent; cosine de-facto standard in later issues).

- **Epochs = 3, sub-epoch fracs ∈ {0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, 2.00, 3.00}.** Why: 3 epochs because #462's 5-epoch was too long (saturated by epoch 2 at lr=1e-5); at lr=2e-6 (5× lower) equivalent gradient is much smaller, so 3 epochs at lr=2e-6 is well below #462 epoch-1, the under-trained band we want. 6 fracs span sub-epoch (0.10, 0.25, 0.50) AND multi-epoch (1.00, 2.00, 3.00) to cover both "too-light" and "near-#462-epoch-1" anchors. Source: `#456` / `#474` Reproducibility; matches #488 v2 §11. Alternatives: N=16 (descope flag if per-cell SE > 0.15), N=4 (cheaper but too noisy).

The layer-sweep figure reports both L15 and L21 side-by-side; if L15 beats L21 by > 0.05 here, the body flags the localization-shift finding and the H3 head-to-head is re-run at the empirical-best layer. Source: rule + `#404`/`#458` + `#474` followup_cosine_analysis.json. Alternatives: L21-only (loses L15 comparability), L15-only (loses cross-experiment consistency).

Source: `#456` chose 20; matches #488 v2 §11. Alternatives: 50 (3× eval cost, doable on the 8× pod but eats descope budget).

- **Predictor extraction point = last prompt token of `[ICL block] + [user(Q)]`.** Why: in the SAME FAMILY of last-ICL-prompt-token extraction validated by Hendel et al. 2023 (arXiv 2310.15916, task vectors, dummy-query patching) AND Todd et al. 2024 (arXiv 2310.15213, ICLR, function vectors, REAL-query causal mediation). #489 uses a real held-out question, so the extraction site is closer to Todd et al. than to Hendel et al. — but both validate the post-ICL last-prompt-token region as where the ICL-induced task representation lives. Persona-Vectors recipe (a) uses the same "last input token" extraction principle. Source: arXiv 2310.15213 (Todd et al., direct site match), arXiv 2310.15916 (Hendel et al., same family), `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` recipe (a). Alternatives rejected: response-mean (Persona-Vectors recipe (b) — saturated in #468 at L25 with 18 cells > cos-sim 0.90, no rank-discrimination left); first ICL example position (encodes the FIRST demonstration only, not the composed task vector — Hendel et al. showed the task vector accumulates across the block); newline-after-`assistant` (#468's choice for EM-prediction — the marker context here is different and the natural slot after the LAST user turn is more theoretically grounded by Hendel/Todd).
