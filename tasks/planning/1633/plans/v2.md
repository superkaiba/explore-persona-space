## 11. Decision Rationale

- **What:** the ≥ ~1 h sustained-stall threshold for the down-width split. **Why:** low enough to save most of a #1112-shaped drought (14 h lost vs a 55-min narrow run — the break-even for splitting is minutes of orchestrator work), high enough that a normal ladder walk never triggers it. **Source:** #1112 incident arithmetic (2026-07-22, 06:52Z→21:09Z stall; dozens of create attempts; immediate 1× provision at split), calibrated against the router's per-rung 600 s queue-timeout (`EPS_GCP_QUEUE_WAIT_SECONDS`, `scripts/backend_poll.py:256`) and the 600 s free-lane park (`router.FREE_WAIT_SECONDS = 600`, `backends/router.py:242`) — i.e. ~1 h ≈ several full ladder walks. **Marked as:** prose guidance, not a coded gate (per the task body); the clause says so verbatim. **Alternatives:** 600 s (too twitchy — one rung's timeout is normal), 4 h (#1112 would still have lost half a workday), a coded poller gate (out of scope — a code change with router blast radius, would be its own task).
- **What:** clause placement = new EOF recipe block in plan-compute-sizing.md. **Why:** the file is the §9-relocated recipe home (#829 architecture: recipes in the rule, one-sentence duty pointers in planner.md §9); EOF sits adjacent to the router-aware "cost against the machine the router will ACTUALLY provision" recipe and is the lowest-risk pure insertion (no section-slicing pin anchors disturbed). **Source:** #829 relocation precedent + the #1414/#1541/#1612 recipe-addition precedents in the same file.
- **What:** add the dispatch-time cross-ref to compute-backend-failover.md. **Why:** the plan-file-`paths:` loader fires plan-compute-sizing.md at PLAN time only; the mid-run consumer (an orchestrator reasoning about a stalled dispatch) loads the failover rule — without the cross-ref the guidance is invisible exactly when the stall happens. **Source:** LESSONS.md trigger for compute-backend-failover.md ("reason about GCP↔RunPod failover"); the #1112 sessions consulted failover surfaces during the drought.
