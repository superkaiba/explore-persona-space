## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take models that were already taught a quirk (a tic, a fact, a style, a habit of agreeing) while playing a character, and I learn a little function that reads the model's "what character am I in" signal and predicts "how will I answer." I learn that function twice — once from the untouched model, once from the taught model — and ask whether the *function itself* changed, or whether only the *signal it reads* moved. I do this two ways (a simple linear fit and a flexible curve-fit), and I check the change only along the directions that actually matter for the behavior, not the whole 3584-number activation (which an earlier run showed is hopeless to compare because everything reconstructs near-perfectly and tells you nothing).
- **What I expect:** For the marker tic and the taught fact I expect the function to barely change — teaching those mostly slid the input signal around. For the messier behaviors (agreeing with wrong claims, harmful compliance / EM) I genuinely don't know, and that uncertainty is the interesting part.
- **What would change my mind:** If the function's change along the behavior-relevant directions sits well above the noise floor for a behavior — and stays above it even after I subtract off the change you'd see just from evaluating the taught function on inputs it never trained on — then teaching reshaped *how* the model acts on its self-picture, not just the picture. A flat result (change indistinguishable from a refit-noise floor) means the input moved and the function held.

## 3. Hypothesis

Per behavior B, finetuning B into a context either:

- **H_input:** moved the input context vector c_C, with M intact → median-Δ_{r_B} ≈ noise floor; chain-ρ(M0) ≈ chain-ρ(M⁺).
- **H_function:** changed the map M, with c_C effectively intact → median-Δ_{r_B} well above the combined refit/shift floor; chain-ρ(M⁺) ≠ chain-ρ(M0).
- **H_mixed:** both move (the CI straddles the floor, or chain-ρ shifts modestly).

**Quantitative falsification (per behavior × layer).** Define the headline scalar:

```
Δ_med(B, layer) = median over c in common_c_grid(B) of |Δ(c)·r̂_B|,
    where Δ(c) = M⁺(c) − M0(c),  r̂_B = r_B / ‖r_B‖.
```

(The headline statistic is the MEDIAN of the per-context projected magnitudes — robust to the few-context n≈16 tails — used consistently across §3/§4.5/§6/§6.5. Mean is reported in the exploratory dump as a robustness check, never as the headline.)

Define the COMBINED floor:

```
floor_combined(B, layer) = max( floor_M0_refit, floor_Mplus_refit, floor_shifted )
```

where each component is the 95th percentile of a median-projected-distance distribution built through the IDENTICAL bootstrap/refit harness (§4.5.1):
- `floor_M0_refit` = median over c of `|(M0_refit_a(c) − M0_refit_b(c))·r̂_B|` over bootstrap-over-cells + random-init refit PAIRS of M0 (two independent in-support refits of the same base map);
- `floor_Mplus_refit` = the same construction on M⁺ (two independent refits of M⁺, evaluated at c0) — captures M⁺'s OWN refit variance, which at n≈16 on post-FT-reshaped input geometry is plausibly larger than M0's (§12 LOW-confidence);
- `floor_shifted` = the same-function shifted-design null: fit `M_pseudo: cplus → M0(cplus)` (the SAME M0 function, but with the post-FT inputs `cplus` driving it) through the identical harness, then take median over c of `|(M_pseudo(c0) − M0(c0))·r̂_B|` over its refit pairs — this captures the Δ you would read PURELY from evaluating a fitted map off its own support (base c0 ≠ the cplus the pseudo-map saw), even under a true H_input where the function never changed.

- **H_function "supported"** iff the family-clustered-bootstrap 95% CI of `Δ_med(B,layer)` lies **entirely above** `floor_combined(B,layer)`. Δ is reported in floor-SD units (Δ_med / SD of the floor distribution) with the combined floor drawn as the reference line.
- **H_input "supported"** iff that CI lies **entirely at or below** `floor_combined` (≤ 1 floor-SD).
- **H_mixed** iff the CI straddles `floor_combined`.
- **Support-distance diagnostic (MANDATORY alongside Δ).** Report per-cell `‖cplus − c0‖` (the input-shift magnitude). A cell whose shift exceeds a pre-registered threshold `SUPPORT_SHIFT_PCTL = 90th pct of the per-behavior ‖cplus − c0‖ distribution` is treated with extra caution: its H_function call is reported but FLAGGED `large-shift` (the `floor_shifted` null is least trustworthy for the most-shifted cells, since the pseudo-map extrapolates farthest there), and the headline Δ_med is reported BOTH including and excluding the flagged cells. If excluding the large-shift cells flips the H_function call, the behavior is reported H_mixed / inconclusive, not H_function.
- **Concordance check (co-primary):** chain-ρ(M⁺) − chain-ρ(M0) under LOCO; a behavior is only called H_function with confidence if BOTH the Δ_med read and the chain-ρ shift agree in direction. A behavior whose chain-ρ(M0) already saturates near 1.0 is down-ranked to a sanity check (it has no headroom to register a function change), mirroring #658's v0-recon-saturation guard.

Pre-registered weak prediction: marker + taught-fact lean H_input (slot-localized, context-invariant per #651); em + sycophancy genuinely uncertain — matching #697's pre-registration so the two answers are directly comparable.

### Kill criterion

- **KILL (no claim in any direction):** if the combined refit/shift floor **swallows Δ_med in the r_B projection across ≥2 of the 3 headline behaviors** (em, sycophancy, taught-fact) at L=14 — i.e. the family-clustered 95% CI of `Δ_med / floor_SD` straddles or sits below 1.0 for ≥2 behaviors, where `floor` is `floor_combined = max(floor_M0_refit, floor_Mplus_refit, floor_shifted)` — report the result **inconclusive**: no claim of function-change (H_function), and no positive claim of H_input either, because at n≈16 an at-floor Δ is consistent with both "function held" and "the fit was too weak to register a real change" (the under-power confound, §12). Only a Δ_med CI *entirely above* `floor_combined` licenses an H_function claim; a *clean* below-`floor_combined` result across all 3 behaviors with a passing MLP-vs-shuffle check licenses H_input.
- **MLP-validity kill → ridge-only fallback (primary, not contingency):** if MLP held-out ρ **< shuffle null on M0 across all 3 headline behaviors**, the MLP map is not learning at this n; **fall back to ridge-only** and report the ridge Δ_med/floor_combined + chain-ρ as the **only valid read** (the nonlinearity-gap read §4.5.4 is then dropped as uninterpretable). A per-behavior MLP failure (not all 3) drops only that behavior's MLP, ridge stands.

### 4.4 Fit harness — `scripts/issue722_fit_M.py` (NEW)

For each `(behavior, layer ∈ {7,14,21})`:

- Fit **M0** via `_ridge_predict_loco(C0[layer], V0[layer], RIDGE_LAMBDAS)` (ridge) and `_fit_mlp_ensemble_loco(C0[layer], V0[layer], target_idx=range(64))` (MLP), where `C0[layer]`/`V0[layer]` are the `(n_cells, 3584)` stacks assembled by §4.1 from the per-layer files.
- Fit **M⁺** via the same calls on `(Cplus[layer], Vplus[layer])`.
- Fit **M_pseudo** (for `floor_shifted`, §4.5.1) via the same calls on `(Cplus[layer] → M0(Cplus[layer]))` — same M0 outputs, shifted inputs.
- LOCO over contexts (held-out per context, never train ρ); family-clustered resampling over the 7 `target_cid` families (family labels from `gate_chain.family_of`).
- Output target dim = 64 leading top-PCA-of-v0 dims (`A35_MLP_TARGET_DIM`), shared between ridge and MLP for a like-for-like nonlinearity gap.
- Checkpoint per `(behavior, layer)` cell to `eval_results/issue_722/cells/` (the #658 `_save_cell` pattern) so the run resumes.

**Unit of fit — pre-registered pooling axis.** PRIMARY = **per-behavior pool across that behavior's contexts** (n ≈ 16 source contexts per behavior; seed42-fixed so there is no seed dimension to pool over). #658 fit per-recipe over the full `ctx_ids` set (a single pooled fit, not per-adapter) — this task INHERITS that pooling decision. n=16 against a 64-dim output is tight; the LOCO held-out ρ + the shuffle null are the overfit guards #658 validated at the same n. FALLBACK if MLP LOCO ρ is unstable (held-out ρ < shuffle null on M0): report ridge-only for that behavior and flag the MLP as underpowered (§7).

### 4.5 Reads / analysis — `scripts/issue722_analyze.py` (NEW)

From each fitted `(M0, M⁺, M_pseudo)` triple:

   The headline floor is `floor_combined = max(floor_M0_refit, floor_Mplus_refit, floor_shifted)`. Report `Δ_med / floor_SD_combined` with the family-clustered 95% CI from the **NEW `clustered_bootstrap_scalar` helper** (§4.5.1).    - **`scripts/issue722_bootstrap.py:clustered_bootstrap_scalar(values, families, statistic="median", n_resamples=1000, alpha=0.05, seed=0)` (NEW — MF#1 fix).** Resamples the 7 `target_cid` families with replacement (mirroring `gate_chain.clustered_bootstrap_spearman`'s family-resampling loop), and on each resample recomputes the SCALAR `statistic` (default `median`; `mean` exposed for the robustness dump) over the per-context Δ values `|Δ(c)·r̂_B|`. Returns `{"point": ..., "ci_lo": ..., "ci_hi": ..., "n_families": ...}` as percentile CI. Signature pseudocode:
     ```python
     def clustered_bootstrap_scalar(values, families, *, statistic="median",
                                    n_resamples=1000, alpha=0.05, seed=0):
         vals = np.asarray(values, float); fams = np.asarray(families, object)
         assert vals.shape == fams.shape, (vals.shape, fams.shape)
         agg = {"median": np.median, "mean": np.mean}[statistic]
         point = float(agg(vals))
         uniq = sorted(set(map(str, fams)))
         if len(uniq) < 2:
             return {"point": point, "ci_lo": point, "ci_hi": point, "n_families": len(uniq)}
         fam_to_idx = {f: np.where(fams == f)[0] for f in uniq}
         rng = np.random.default_rng(seed); boot = np.empty(n_resamples)
         for r in range(n_resamples):
             chosen = rng.choice(uniq, size=len(uniq), replace=True)
             idx = np.concatenate([fam_to_idx[f] for f in chosen])
             boot[r] = agg(vals[idx])
         return {"point": point,
                 "ci_lo": float(np.percentile(boot, 100*alpha/2)),
                 "ci_hi": float(np.percentile(boot, 100*(1-alpha/2))),
                 "n_families": len(uniq)}
     ```
     This is the headline CI helper; `clustered_bootstrap_spearman` is RESERVED for read (2) (it correlates two arrays — wrong type for a per-cell scalar location statistic, the MF#1 finding).
2. **Behavior-relevant transfer chain ρ (co-primary).** Predicted `r_Bᵀ M̂(c)` (the #658 `_chain_rho` form: project the LOCO-predicted v0 through r_B) vs measured E = `g` from #537 `G_meta.json` per cell, held-out under LOCO, Spearman ρ. Report under BOTH M0 and M⁺, and the difference, with a family-clustered 95% CI from `gate_chain.clustered_bootstrap_spearman(x=projected, y=E, families=...)` (the correct use of that helper — two paired arrays). Also the **directional response curve** `M̂(c)·r̂_B` across the c-grid for M0 vs M⁺.
3. **Cross-transfer (parametric A3.6b).** `M0` predicting `v_plus` on FT pairs (held-out ρ) vs `M⁺` predicting `v_plus` on FT pairs; and the reverse (`M⁺` predicting `v0` on base pairs). A low M0→v⁺ transfer with high M⁺→v⁺ is parametric evidence the function changed.
4. **Linear-vs-nonlinear gap, pre vs post.** `(ρ_MLP − ρ_ridge)` under M0 vs under M⁺ — does the function become *more nonlinear* after FT? (#658's `nonlinear_gap` read, applied to both maps.)

## 5. Conditions and Controls

| Plain-English name | What it tests | What it controls for | Config slug |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base map (M0) | The pre-FT context→answer function | The reference function | `M0` |
| Post-FT map (M⁺) | The post-FT context→answer function | The treatment function | `Mplus` |
| M0 refit floor | Two independent refits of M0 (in-support) | Refit/bootstrap noise of the base map | `floor_M0_refit` |
| M⁺ refit floor | Two independent refits of M⁺ (in-support) | Refit/bootstrap noise of the post-FT map (its OWN variance, plausibly larger) | `floor_Mplus_refit` |
| Shifted-design null | Same M0 function on post-FT inputs, read at base c0 | Pure off-support extrapolation under H_input | `floor_shifted` |
| Within-context shuffle null | Refit on permuted v0 | Chance-level fit ρ (overfit guard) | `shuffle_null` |
| Raw-v0-recon sanity inset | Full-v0 reconstruction at L=14 | Proves r_B-projection is the right DV (saturated surface) | `v0recon_sanity` |
| Linear (ridge) vs nonlinear (MLP) | Is M well-approximated linearly | Whether nonlinearity grows post-FT | `ridge` / `mlp` |

**Controls rationale.** The headline Δ is gated on the COMBINED floor `max(floor_M0_refit, floor_Mplus_refit, floor_shifted)`, which rules out THREE distinct artifacts at n≈16: (1) M0 refit jitter (`floor_M0_refit`); (2) the post-FT map being noisier than the base map so a M0-only floor is too tight (`floor_Mplus_refit`); (3) reading a spurious above-floor Δ purely because M⁺ is evaluated off its own support at base c0 even when the function never changed (`floor_shifted`). The shuffle null rules out "the LOCO ρ is overfit chance." The v0-recon sanity inset rules out the #658 trap of reading function change off a saturated surface. The ridge/MLP pair rules out "the function change is invisible to a linear fit."

**Caveats (verbatim from body + planner additions):**
- Do NOT headline raw v0-reconstruction cosine (#658 saturated at 0.98) — kept only as the sanity inset.
- Same-input evaluation is MANDATORY (read (1) evaluates M0 and M⁺ on the identical `common_c_grid` of base c0).
- The COMBINED floor (all three components) MUST be reported alongside Δ_med (in floor-SD units), with the support-distance diagnostic.
- r_B recipe pick (`diffmeans`) MUST be identical for taught-fact extraction (no recipe-mismatch confound).
- **Single-seed caveat:** #667 store is seed42-only. The noise lever is bootstrap-over-cells + random-init refit, NOT cross-seed. The headline reports Δ_med/floor as a bootstrap CI, explicitly NOT a seed-spread; this is carried into the clean-result as a scope caveat.
- **Saturation guard:** if chain-ρ(M0) on a behavior already saturates ≈1.0, down-rank to a sanity check; do not headline. Marker is dropped from the headline on this + the no-contrast ground.
- **Refusal note:** refusal IS in the #667 store (1152 files), but its #537 G is std-floored (0.079) so its chain-ρ has no headroom — it is correctly excluded from the headline by the §4.3/§5 saturation guard, NOT by store absence.
