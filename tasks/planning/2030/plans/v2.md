## Method delta

Same guard, new surfaces. Reuse, not reimplementation: `step9c_baseline.py`
importlib-loads sibling `inline_lint_gate.py` BY PATH (mirroring its own
`load_selector_module` shape — no `sys.path` mutation) and calls the SAME
`purge_repo_bytecode` + merges the SAME `NO_BYTECODE_ENV`. The SKILL.md recipes
get the bash equivalent (`find … -path '*/__pycache__/*.pyc' -delete` over the
#1950 `PURGE_ROOTS` set, plus the env token) because they are shell recipes
with no python entry point at purge time.

   of `inline_lint_gate.py` from `Path(__file__).resolve().parent`, binding
