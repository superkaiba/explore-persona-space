## 3. Hypothesis

**H1** (narrative prose carries framing cost): story framings' context maps differ from chat/bare-text because the surrounding NARRATIVE PROSE dominates the context vector.
**H2** (answer-boundary form carries framing cost): the maps differ because the ANSWER-BOUNDARY FORM (attribution `X replied:` vs bare label `X:` vs bare paragraph vs indirect reported speech) constrains what appears at the read position, not the prose.
**H3** (templated-vs-diverse dichotomy dominates): the diverse-narrative-prose regime is a single family; the split is DIVERSE (this task) vs TEMPLATED (#1689's low-ceiling cells), not framing-within-diverse.
**H4** (v4-specific, authorship vs presentation): given the required 2x2, the ~0.09 inserted-vs-on-policy gap decomposes into an **authorship term** (a vs c: chat-authored vs story-authored, both presented in CHAT) and a **presentation term** (a vs b: chat-authored, CHAT vs STORY presentation) that add within a row. If the observed gap is entirely presentation (authorship contributes nothing), narrating on-policy cross-framing deltas as a "framing effect" is not merely allowed but supported; if it is entirely authorship, on-policy cross-framing deltas confound the two and must not be narrated as a framing effect. Mixed decomposition is the expected outcome.

**Falsification design.** The decoupled scaffold-and-splice pipeline renders EVERY boundary form from the SAME scaffold — narrative prose is held BYTE-IDENTICAL while the boundary varies (bare label / attribution / indirect). Under H1, cross-boundary transfer within one scaffold should be near-perfect (same prose, same v_C context vector). Under H2, cross-boundary transfer should collapse to boundary-invariant components. Under H3, all diverse-scaffold cells cluster tightly together and separately from the #1689 templated cells. Under H4, the (a,b,c,d) 2x2 decomposition yields near-orthogonal authorship + presentation components; a single component dominating either arm is informative.

**Kill condition (jointly).** If cross-boundary within-scaffold transfer is worse than cross-scaffold same-boundary transfer for the SAME target cell, the design is uninterpretable — pause and re-scope.

### v4-new permissive-matcher tolerance (audit_ii-driven, OPT-IN with raw-verbatim control)

Given audit_ii's 48.6% recovery, v4 introduces a **permissive matcher tolerance** for the Phase B inserted-arm span locator, with the raw-verbatim match kept as a control. Tolerance spec:

- **Baseline (control):** raw-verbatim exact match on the spliced answer (parent behavior). Kept.
- **Permissive tolerance (opt-in, new):** whitespace-tolerant matching (normalize consecutive whitespace to single space); single-edit tolerance (Levenshtein ≤ 1) at the answer boundary (opening clause drop, truncated list end, appended continuation) — the three near-miss categories audit_ii's 48.6% consists of. The permissive matcher is applied ONLY when the raw-verbatim match returns `answer_occurrences_zero`; on match, the row is kept and flagged `matcher: permissive` in the output metadata.
- **Control invariant:** every kept row carries a `matcher` field (`raw` or `permissive`); the analyzer reports per-cell fractions and compares within-cell ceilings for `matcher=raw` vs `matcher=all` — if permissive-matched rows shift the ceiling by more than 0.02 in the pooled held-out R², the permissive rows are excluded from the headline and reported as a companion.
- **Note:** because Phase B is deterministic-splice (splice at slot sentinel, no verbatim matcher needed for the FRESH lattice), the permissive tolerance is materially useful only when re-mining parent #1345's existing rejects to reduce the fresh-generation shortfall. **Planner decision: fold the permissive-matcher tolerance IN** for parent-reject re-mining ONLY (24,118 rejects × 48.6% = ~11,721 recoverable rows across all inserted variants), with the raw-verbatim control preserved on every re-mined row. Do NOT apply the permissive matcher on fresh Phase B rows (deterministic by construction — no matcher needed).

### Both mapping arms (Critical Rule, non-negotiable)

Every cell fits BOTH:

1. **Context arm** — v_C = activation at the last prompt token before the answering character's answer begins; v_A = mean activation over the answer tokens; M' fitted at layer 19 with v_A ≈ M' v_C.
2. **Prefix arm** — v_P = activation at the last prompt token BEFORE the user query; v_A = same. **The parent #1345's prefix arm is degenerate** (context arm reads collapse onto ~14 extraction-batch vectors — `eval_results/issue_1345/prefix_degeneracy_probe.json`). The prefix-arm degeneracy is RE-CHECKED here at the ambient basis, NOT inherited: if the fresh capture at n=6,000 shows the same 14-vector collapse signature, that arm is reported degenerate; if the ambient-basis re-fit resolves it, the read is reported. One-arm results are an explicit stated deviation per the Critical Rule, never a silent default.

**Controls (v3 preserved + v4 extensions):**
- Shuffled-answer matched-capacity null per cell (target rows permuted; fit the same M' capacity).
- Identity+learned-bias baseline (v_A ≈ v_C + b, b = train-fold mean of v_A − v_C) per cell where d_in = d_out.
- kNN retrieval (euclidean + cosine, k=5 fixed, chance = k / n_pool_held-out).
- Reduced-basis diagnostic per cell (train-fold PCA k=1024, #1887 recipe) alongside the ambient fit — the ambient fit is the headline; the reduced-basis figure lets the writeup contrast the two estimators.
- **v4-new: Raw-vs-permissive matcher control.** For every cell that includes permissive-matched rows (from audit_ii re-mining), report the within-cell ceiling on `matcher=raw` only alongside `matcher=all`. Headline uses `matcher=all`; a drift > 0.02 R² relegates permissive rows to a companion.
- **v4-new: (a,b,c,d) 2x2 additive decomposition per (character, model) pair.** For each of the 8 (character, model) pairs, report the four cell ceilings + the authorship term (mean(a,b) − mean(c,d)) + the presentation term (mean(a,c) − mean(b,d)) + the interaction (a − b − c + d).

**Pooling convention row** (per fitted vector, per Critical Rule "Identity+learned-bias baseline AND kNN-retrieval metric" § pooling-convention row):

- v_C context arm: **last-token pooling** (single token at the read position).
- v_A: **mean over answer tokens**.
- v_P prefix arm: **last-token pooling** at the pre-query position.
- Parity: identical convention across all 5 framings + all four (a,b,c,d) cells within a (character, model) pair; the comparison line reads the same pooling by construction. Any deviation is stated per cell.

**Row-level pairing across framings + conditions (v4).** The user directive "1 message per character in ALL settings" is what makes Result 1 (cross-framing operator-alignment) computable — the SAME scaffold produces N framings, so rows pair 1:1 across the framing axis. The scaffold-and-splice pipeline preserves this by construction. **v4 extends pairing to conv_id-matching across the (a,b,c,d) 2x2**: every conv_id present in cell (a) is also present in cells (b), (c), (d) at the equalized-down floor; a cell that drops rows drops the corresponding rows in the other three cells of its (character, model) pair (equalize-down applied within-pair as well as within-comparison).

## 6. Evaluation

### Measurement validity (per-DV block, planner.md §6 required)

- **DV 1 — headline within-cell ceiling (R²).**
  - Construct: the linear predictability of v_A from v_C at layer 19 in the ambient hidden basis; operationalization of the context-to-answer map's within-cell fidelity for a given (framing, identity, condition, model) cell.
  - Metric: held-out R² of v_A ≈ M' v_C, ambient basis, K=5 conversation-grouped folds (shared fold map artifact, v4 req 7); pooled held-out N=5,000; per-fold n_train=4,000, n_val=1,000. **Equalize-down** applies when comparing cells: the row count is the min conv_id intersection across compared cells (v4 req 8).
  - On-distribution: YES (on the native token position — last-token pooling at the read position per §5 pooling convention). Group-level held-out folds (conversation ID as the group), fully disjoint from training.
- **DV 2 — identity+learned-bias baseline R².**
  - Construct: the "trivial mapping" floor v̂ = v_C + b (b = train-fold mean of v_A − v_C) — measures how much of the ceiling is explained by a fixed bias shift.
  - Metric: `analysis/mapping_baselines.identity_bias_predict` held-out R². Applicable when d_in = d_out (both mapping arms: yes at hidden size 3,584).
  - On-distribution: YES (same held-out fold as DV 1).
- **DV 3 — kNN retrieval accuracy.**
  - Construct: retrieval-side companion to the R² read — probability that the true v_A lies among the k nearest neighbors of M' v_C in the held-out pool. Dissociates R² from a rotation/scale-only fit.
