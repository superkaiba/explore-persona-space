### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run.** Build ONE row-paired corpus of ~3,000 two-turn LMSYS conversations, render each conversation into 21 conditions (5 speaker identities × 3 surface framings, plus 3 user-provenance variants), capture activations at four layers (14/18/19/26) in Qwen2.5-7B base AND instruct (42 cells total), then for every ordered pair of conditions climb a 9-rung mapping-similarity ladder to find the weakest correction that reconciles the two maps.
- **What I expect.** Framing conversions (chat ↔ no-template ↔ story WITHIN one identity) will reconcile at cheap rungs (bias refit or scalar-α). Identity conversions (assistant → user, assistant → HELIOS, etc.) will need context reparameterization (A) on the input side or full A·M·B. The user arm's three provenances (LMSYS / haiku / on-policy) will land close under bias refit — evidence that "who wrote u2" is a shallow shift, not an operator change.
- **What would change my mind.** If most identity pairs also reconcile at bias-refit, the "same operator, different coordinates" story wins broadly and CB-duality is genuinely coordinate-independent. If even framing conversions need full A·M·B, the map is fragile to surface form and prior geometry claims (#1310/#1345) are over-generalized from the assistant-chat corner.

### 0. Plan Summary (technical version — for the implementer, experimenter, reviewer)

**Training:** none — this is a measurement experiment. No weights are updated; the "arms" are (identity × framing × provenance) render + capture cells, and the 9-rung mapping ladder is a battery of ridge fits over row-paired activation pairs.
**Hyperparameters:** n = 3,000 two-turn LMSYS conversations (Source: #825 leg_b_matched_n_curve: inner-group-cv reads 0.6685 at n=3000 vs 0.6957 at n=5000 on S_instruct_chat = 96% of ceiling; on S_pretrained_chat 0.5653 / 0.5969 = 95% of ceiling); frozen capture layers 14/18/19/26, headline layer 19 (Source: #825/#1310/#1345); ridge grid `LAMBDAS = np.logspace(-2, 4, 13)` (Source: #825 `scripts/issue825_fit_cells.py:66`; grid-extension probe to 1e8 in `_grid_extension_probe.json` found no fold above the 1e4 ceiling); `lambda_selection="inner-group-cv"`, `collect_lambdas=True` (Source: #825 selector audit — Phase-0 finding); Group 5-fold CV grouped by conversation id (Source: #825 fit core `heldout_r2_sweep`); vLLM sampling temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95, max_new_tokens 1024, N=1 completion per prompt (Source: #825 defaults; N=1 is the on-policy elicitation default, judge-filter drop rate is the yield lever); judge = `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`, graded 0-100 pointwise, N=3 draws per completion at temperature 0.7 (Source: CLAUDE.md "LLM judge" + `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rules 1,4,6,7); Batch API for judge (Source: `docs/api_throughput_guidelines.md` sync-vs-batch table — the ~15k judge calls sit in the "sync wins wall-clock" band, so use SYNC fan-out across orgs); rubric-fingerprint-keyed cache via `rubric_fingerprint(judge_model, system_prompt, user_msg_template)` (Source: `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rule 22 + `#1018`); Haiku user-turn provenance = `claude-haiku-4-5` (Source: #825 `issue825_gen_conversations._haiku_user_turn`); characters = Wren / HELIOS / Dana (Source: #1310 `scripts/issue1310_common.py::PERSONAS`), Vex OUT of headline (rationale in §11).
**Baselines / controls:** identity+learned-bias baseline `v̂ = x + b`, b = train-fold mean of (y − x), via `analysis/mapping_baselines.identity_bias_predict` (Source: CLAUDE.md standing rule); kNN retrieval (euclidean + cosine, k∈{1,5,10}, chance = k/n_pool stated) via `analysis/mapping_baselines.knn_retrieval` (Source: CLAUDE.md standing rule); matched-capacity shuffled-fit centre + random-orthogonal centre nulls per rung, per pair (Source: #1345 `reparam_null_battery`); conversation-level paired bootstrap for every cross-condition delta (Source: `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md`); shuffled-answer null per cell (Source: #825/#1345). SELECTION-symmetric null band for the argmax-over-rungs headline (Source: `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md`).
**Loss surface:** N/A — no gradient training. The fit surface is the ridge Gram-dual objective, GPU-batched across (cells × folds × layers × λ) via `issue825_fit_cells.heldout_r2_sweep`.
**Compute:** ~46 GPU-h total on GCP wide A100-80 (spot/flex-start ladder). Estimated GPU-hours (total): 46.
**Evaluation:** primary DV = held-out R² per (cell × arm × layer × rung) with conversation-level 5-fold CV; secondary = **raw direction-aware operator cosine (paired with a two-sided random-orthogonal-rotation null band)** (`scripts/issue1345_operator_comparison.py::raw_cosine_with_rotation_null` — NEVER spectrum cosine, per CLAUDE.md § "Similarity-statistic semantics"); tertiary = kNN retrieval acc@1/@5/@10; headline figure = "ladder rung reached per ordered pair" heatmap (5 identities × 3 framings, one per model × arm); exploratory dump = full 9-rung R² per pair, per-layer curves, per-λ selection histograms.
**Risks (top 1-2):** (1) on-policy yield floor failure on a specific identity × framing (e.g. base model in story framing may refuse or degenerate) — mitigation: pre-registered 80% floor with equalize-down (`.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md`), drop below-floor sources as a reported finding, never backfill; (2) fit throughput blowout — 42 cells × 2 arms × 4 layers × 5 folds × 13 λ ≈ 109k inner ridge fits + 9 rungs × 210 pairs × 2 models × 2 arms ≈ 7,560 outer ladder fits; batching MUST happen in the Gram-dual as `heldout_r2_sweep` already does (Source: #825, 25.5s per full 4-layer × 5-fold × 13-λ cell measured in `_cell_a_M_instruct_assistant_chat.json`).

### 1. Goal

> Determine, on ONE shared row-paired corpus, whether the context→answer linear map is the SAME operator across five speaker identities (assistant; user; a fiction AI character; an assistant-like human character; an ordinary/random character) and three surface framings (chat template; plain User:/Assistant: text; narrative-prose story), in Qwen2.5-7B base AND instruct, with the user arm run under all three u2 provenances (real LMSYS, haiku-simulated, on-policy) and every non-user condition on-policy from the measured model — and, for every ordered pair, WHICH tier of the mapping-similarity ladder (direct transfer / context offset / answer offset / bias refit / global scaling / rotation / context reparameterization / answer reparameterization / full A·M·B) is the weakest correction that reconciles the two maps. Both mapping arms (prefix and context) are fit for every cell.

(Task #1689 Goal, verbatim.)

The reason this is worth doing: every prior cell in the #825/#1310/#1345 line lives in a DIFFERENT corpus, so the ladder's middle rungs — which are only DEFINED on shared rows — have never been computable across speaker or framing. A single row-paired battery makes "same operator in different coordinates" (bias refit / scaling / rotation reconcile) vs "genuinely different operator" (full A·M·B needed) decidable for all 21 conditions at once, and grounds the CB-duality question this project has been circling since #779.

### 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (framing-shallowness).** Within any single identity, the three framings (chat / no-template / story) reconcile at rung ≤ 5 (bias refit or scalar-α) in ≥8/9 identity-arm-model cells. FALSIFIER: ≥2 within-identity framing pairs need rung ≥ 7 (context reparam) in at least one model.

**H2 (identity ≠ framing).** Across identities within a single framing, the modal WEAKEST-reconciling rung is ≥ 7 (context reparam) — "who speaks" changes the input coordinates, not just the bias. FALSIFIER: ≥12/30 within-framing identity pairs reconcile at rung ≤ 5.

**H3 (user-provenance shallowness).** The three user variants (LMSYS / haiku / on-policy) reconcile at rung ≤ 4 (bias refit) within each framing × model. FALSIFIER: any user-provenance pair needs rung ≥ 6.

**H4 (arm parity).** The rung reached for a pair differs between the prefix and context arms by ≤1 in ≥80% of pairs (the two arms tell substantially the same story). FALSIFIER: ≥20% of pairs disagree by ≥2 rungs.

**H5 (base vs instruct parity).** For every pair, the rung reached differs between base and instruct by ≤1 (the operator structure is chat-template-independent). FALSIFIER: ≥20% of pairs disagree by ≥2 rungs.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: H1_Confirmed ⇔ ≥8/9 within-identity framing pairs reconcile at rung ≤ 5 in BOTH models; H1_Falsified ⇔ ≥2 within-identity framing pairs need rung ≥ 7 in at least one model; H1_Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: H2_Confirmed ⇔ modal WEAKEST-reconciling rung ≥ 7 across within-framing identity pairs; H2_Falsified ⇔ ≥12/30 within-framing identity pairs reconcile at rung ≤ 5; H2_Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: H3_Confirmed ⇔ every user-provenance pair reconciles at rung ≤ 4 within each framing × model; H3_Falsified ⇔ any user-provenance pair needs rung ≥ 6; H3_Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: H4_Confirmed ⇔ ≥80% of pairs have rung-reached differ between prefix and context arms by ≤ 1; H4_Falsified ⇔ ≥20% of pairs disagree by ≥ 2 rungs; H4_Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: H5_Confirmed ⇔ for every pair rung-reached differs between base and instruct by ≤ 1; H5_Falsified ⇔ ≥20% of pairs disagree by ≥ 2 rungs; H5_Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-context by `analysis_tensors/issue_1689/store/<model>/<condition>/L{layer}.pt` (one store per cell, keyed by conversation id) for the prefix arm AND the context arm; each ordered pair's row set = intersection of the two cells' conv_ids on the shared corpus.

### 4. Design

**One shared corpus.** N=3,000 two-turn LMSYS conversations, filtered as in #825 Track-M: (a) exactly 2 turns (user1, assistant1 — u1, a1 — with a follow-up user2 that will be REPLACED per user-provenance arm; a1 is FIXED across all 21 renderings), (b) token-budget feasible under the tightest framing (chat template + persona header), (c) English (langid), (d) no truncation flags in the source `lmsys-chat-1m` field. Rendered into all 21 conditions; fit rows are keyed by `conv_id` so every ordered pair has a shared row set and the ladder's data-paired rungs (Δx, Δy, A, B, A·M·B) are DEFINED.

**Both mapping arms (prefix + context) per every cell.** Following CLAUDE.md "Prefix mapping AND context mapping":
- **Prefix arm.** X = frozen-layer activation at end of everything BEFORE the user query (u2). For chat framing this is up to end of `<|im_start|>user\n` preceding u2; for no-template framing it is end of the "User: " tag preceding u2; for story framing it is end of the narrator preamble preceding the u2-analog turn.
- **Context arm.** X = activation at end of everything up to and INCLUDING u2 (the user query is inside X). Same three framing conventions, extended one token past the end of u2.
- **Y (both arms).** Activation at end of the a2 answer span. Fixed target across conditions where possible (the a1 answer never changes across renderings; a2 is generated per cell).

Wait — since a2 is what the MEASURED model produces on-policy (except the user arm, where the "answer" IS u2 in the sense of what fills the model's mouth), the ROW-PAIRING is by `conv_id` while the target text differs cell-to-cell. That is exactly the point of row-pairing: same source conversation, condition-specific completion, ONE conv_id per row. The ladder's Δy, B, and full B⁻¹·M·A are DEFINED on this row-paired structure.

**Character semantics.** Reuse `issue1310_common.PERSONAS` VERBATIM: Wren = assistant-like human, HELIOS = other AI, Dana = ordinary person. Header rendering via `issue1310_common.persona_header_text`. Vex is OUT of the headline (see §11 rationale).

**Framing conventions.**
- **Chat template.** `render_chat` in #825 for assistant/user; a NEW extension `render_chat_character(persona_label, ...)` for characters, using the persona description as system prompt and the persona label as the assistant-role tag (dispatched through `apply_chat_template` with `role="assistant"` for characters — the character IS the assistant slot from the tokenizer's view).
- **No-template (naturalistic).** `render_naturalistic` in #825 for assistant/user; a NEW extension `render_naturalistic_character(persona_label, ...)` emitting `Wren: …`, `HELIOS: …`, `Dana: …` tags exactly as `issue1310_common.persona_header_text` documents.
- **Story.** Reuse `issue1345_gen_stories_paired.build_paired_prompt`; extend with a story mode for the user role ("A user named [placeholder] approached [character]…" — the user-in-story construction, never attempted). This is the ONE new renderer; it goes in a new script `scripts/issue1689_render_user_in_story.py`.

**User-provenance arms (4-6 in the table).**
- **real-LMSYS (4-6).** u2 is the LMSYS turn 3 (the actual second user turn); the model produces a2 on-policy conditioned on the real u2 text. Filtered same as #825 Track-M.
- **haiku (7-9).** u2 is generated by `claude-haiku-4-5` per `issue825_gen_conversations._haiku_user_turn` conditioned on u1 + a1. Fixed prompt convention.
- **on-policy (10-12).** u2 is generated by the MEASURED model itself under a persona-header steering "You are the user; write your next turn" prompt, per `issue825_onpolicy_u2_gen`. Judge-filtered.

**On-policy responder generation for the 12 non-user conditions.** vLLM batched, temperature 0.7, top_p 0.95, `max_new_tokens=1024`, N=1 per prompt (single completion per row is sufficient because the ridge fit averages over 3,000 conversations; multi-sample generation would inflate cost 3× with marginal signal gain — the yield floor lives in judge-filter, not in N-per-prompt). Judge-filter per rubric (§7 recipe below).

**The 9-rung ladder ORDER (weakest → strongest correction; report the weakest that reconciles).**

For every ordered pair of cells (source S, target T), define `M_S = W_s x + b_s` fit on S's row-paired context arm (and analogously on the prefix arm). Reconciliation criterion: held-out R² on T ≥ 0.9 × T's own within-cell held-out R² (so we require the corrected transfer to reach 90% of the target cell's ceiling). Rungs:

| # | Name | Form | Impl |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | direct transfer | `ŷ_T = M_S(x_T)` | closed-form |
| 2 | context offset | `M_S(x_T − Δx)`, Δx = mean(x_S) − mean(x_T) | closed-form (Source: #1639 `tier2_sides`) |
| 3 | answer offset | `M_S(x_T) + Δy`, Δy = mean(y_T) − mean(M_S(mean(x_T))) | closed-form (Source: #1639 `tier2_sides`) |
| 4 | bias refit | `W_s x_T + b*`, b* refit on T train rows | closed-form (Source: #1639 `tier15_intercept_refit`) |
| 5 | global scaling | `α W_s x_T + b*`, α scalar closed-form | NEW: closed-form `α = <M_S(x), y_T> / <M_S(x), M_S(x)>` on train rows |
| 6 | rotation | `R W_s x_T + b*`, R orthogonal via Procrustes on train rows | NEW as predictive rung; use SVD of `Σ y_T (M_S x)^T` (Source: `scripts/issue1345_operator_comparison.py::raw_cosine_with_rotation_null` for the aligned cosine, generalize to predictive) |
| 7 | context reparam | `W_s (A x_T) + b*`, A ridge fit x_T → x_S | Source: #1639 `oneside_reparam` A-only |
| 8 | answer reparam | `B⁻¹ W_s x_T + b*`, B ridge fit y_S → y_T | Source: #1639 `oneside_reparam` B-only |
| 9 | full A·M·B | `B⁻¹ W_s (A x_T) + b*` | Source: `scripts/issue1345_operator_comparison.py::leg_b_battery` |

Each rung on the target's held-out folds; fit on train, evaluate on test, grouped 5-fold CV by conv_id.

**Pair set: SPOKE + STRUCTURED, not full cross.** Full 210-ordered-pair cross ≈ 210 × 2 models × 2 arms × 4 layers × 5 folds × 9 rungs × 13 λ inner ≈ 1.7M ridge fits — see §11 for the info-gain-per-GPU-hour argument. This plan runs a **spoke + structured** subset of **≈ 95 pairs**:
- Spoke against assistant-chat reference (20 pairs, one per non-reference condition, both directions ⇒ 40 ordered pairs).
- Within-identity framing triples (5 identities × 3 ordered framing pairs {chat→no-template, chat→story, no-template→story} = 15 pairs, both directions ⇒ 30 ordered pairs).
- Within-framing identity quads (3 framings × ⌈4-identity C(4,2)⌉ = 3 × 6 pairs = 18 ordered pairs × 2 = 36; but "quad" here means {assistant, HELIOS, Wren, Dana} within a framing, all pairs = 6 ordered / framing, 18 total → 18).
- User-provenance triples (3 framings × 3 ordered pairs {LMSYS→haiku, LMSYS→onpolicy, haiku→onpolicy} = 9 pairs × 2 = 18).

Total: 40 + 30 + 18 + 18 = 106 ordered pairs (rounded to 95 after de-dup — the assistant-chat spoke already covers 4 within-identity spokes on the assistant column). This preserves every ladder question the Goal names — see §11 justification. Full 210 remains as an OPTIONAL follow-up if budget permits.

**Backend.** Frontmatter has no `backend:` → auto. Auto lane order is `("fellows", "gcp", "nibi", "fir", "mila")` (CLAUDE.md § Compute backends). Since a2 generation and activation capture are GPU-worthy and the fits themselves are batched Gram-dual on GPU, prefer GCP wide A100-80 (`--gpus 4` on the shardable generation phase; `--gpus 1` on capture + fits). Explicit dispatch via `scripts/dispatch_issue.py launch --workload-cmd 'bash scripts/issue1689_dispatch.sh <phase>' --repo-branch issue-1689 --intent lora-7b --gpus 4` (generation phase) then `--intent capture-7b --gpus 1` (capture + fit phases).

**Controls (built into every ladder rung).**
- **Identity+learned-bias baseline** — reported alongside every held-out R², via `analysis/mapping_baselines.identity_bias_predict` (Source: CLAUDE.md standing rule). Rules out "just predict train-mean" as an explanation of any positive R².
- **kNN retrieval** — `analysis/mapping_baselines.knn_retrieval` with k∈{1,5,10}, both euclidean + cosine; pool = held-out fold's true y-set, chance = k/n_pool stated (Source: CLAUDE.md standing rule). Rules out map that has good R² but discriminatively worse than nearest-neighbor.
- **Shuffled-fit centre null** — per rung, per pair, the centre operator is fit on conversation-level-shuffled train answers (Source: `scripts/issue1345_operator_comparison.py::reparam_null_battery` `_shuffled_center`). Matched-capacity null band.
- **Random-orthogonal centre null** — per rung, per pair, the centre operator is wrapped by random orthogonals (Source: `scripts/issue1345_operator_comparison.py::reparam_null_battery` `_rotated_center`).
- **Conversation-level paired bootstrap** — 1000 draws over conv_ids per cross-condition delta (Source: `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md`).
- **Shuffled-answer null per cell** — the within-cell fit against shuffled y (Source: #825/#1345).
- **Selection-symmetric null for argmax-over-rungs.** When picking the WEAKEST rung that reconciles for each pair, selection rides per draw: for each of B=1000 bootstrap draws over conv_ids, compute the rung-reached index; report the DISTRIBUTION of rung-indices under the null (shuffled-answer) at the same selection rule. Ceiling-vs-band comparison per pair (Source: `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md`).

### 6. Evaluation

**Per-DV construct / metric / on-distribution table.**

| DV | Construct | Metric | On-distribution? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held-out R² (primary) | Predictive power of context→answer map | 1 − pooled SS_res/SS_tot over 5 grouped folds | Yes (grouped by conv_id, no leakage) |
| kNN retrieval (secondary) | Discriminative usefulness of prediction | acc@{1,5,10}, MRR, chance = k/n_pool | Yes |
| **Raw direction-aware operator cosine (paired with a two-sided random-orthogonal-rotation null band)** (secondary) | Similarity of two fitted operators (source vs target M) | Raw vec-cosine between vec(M_source) and vec(M_target); matched with two-sided random-orthogonal-rotation null band (via `raw_cosine_with_rotation_null`) | Yes; NEVER spectrum cosine (per CLAUDE.md § "Similarity-statistic semantics"; #1310 incident) |
| Rung-reached index (headline, per pair) | Weakest ladder rung with R²_transfer ≥ 0.9 × R²_within(T) | integer in {1..9} | Yes; selection-symmetric null band per rule |

**N/A — no dual-DV required.** This is not a behavior-implantation / content-behavior-leakage measurement; it is a representation-geometry measurement. The dual-DV rule is scoped to judged-behavior DVs (sycophancy / refusal / etc.). Held-out R² of a ridge fit is a well-defined predictive DV, not a saturation-prone rate.

### 6.6 Analyzer must weigh (non-blocking, folded from Phase 2 critic concerns)

The Phase 2 critic ensemble (Methodology / Statistics / Alternatives) returned APPROVE with no Must-Fix items but flagged 13 concerns that belong in the ANALYZER's interpretation checklist (they shape scope caveats and disaggregate cells the aggregate view could hide). None of these gates the run; they gate the CLAIMS.

1. **Pair-count arithmetic.** Within-framing identity gives C(4,2)=6 unordered per framing × 3 framings = 18 pairs, ordered = 36 — the plan-time cardinality is what §4 records. Analyzer VERIFIES the realized pair set matches the design cardinality and treats any missing pair category as a reportable coverage caveat, not a silent drop.
2. **Row-intersection risk (see §12 Assumption 18).** Joint yield across 21 conditions can drop below n=3000; the Phase-B pilot at n=200 measures this before commitment. Analyzer REPORTS realized joint yield and scopes generalization claims to the surviving subset.
3. **Rung-reached threshold (0.9 × R²_within) is a judgment.** Pilot Phase D on 5 pairs. Analyzer either reports MULTIPLE thresholds (0.85 / 0.90 / 0.95) or narrates that the rung-index heatmap is threshold-conditioned; a single 0.90-line story is under-specified.
4. **Base-in-chat is a corner case.** Within-cell R² ≥ 0.5 gate applies before Phase D; analyzer treats base×character×chat cells as REPORTABLE-WITH-CAVEAT, not first-class evidence for the identity=coordinate hypothesis (H2).
5. **u2-provenance arm mixes Tier-1 (LMSYS) with Tier-3 (haiku/on-policy).** H3's prediction (all three reconcile at rung ≤ 4) should be read with data-realism tiers in mind; a shallow-side Tier-3 arm may confound "provenance-independent". Analyzer NAMES the tier of each of the three arms in the H3 interpretation.
6. **Vex OUT keeps identity space at 3 characters (n=1 per category).** Any "identity conversion needs rung ≥ 7" claim is really "this ONE character in this category"; analyzer carries this as an EXTERNAL-VALIDITY scope caveat throughout the H2 write-up.
7. **Answer-distribution shift ≠ operator change (H2 alternative).** Different identities produce different answer distributions; the plan's answer-side rungs (3 = Δy, 8 = B-only) exist to isolate this. Analyzer ANNOTATES every H2-Confirmed pair with an A-only vs B-only rung R² profile.
8. **Framing may collapse input variance (H1 alternative).** Framing bias refit ≠ operator coordinate-independence; analyzer COMPUTES mean-shift magnitude vs within-cell activation std from the persisted `analysis_tensors/issue_1689/store/` and includes it in the H1 write-up.
9. **User-arm u1 identical across provenances.** H3 is primarily read on the CONTEXT arm (where u2 differs), NOT the prefix arm (where u1 is shared by construction across the three provenances). Analyzer scopes H3 accordingly.
10. **95-pair composition assumption is uncheckable within-run.** Analyzer SURFACES the composition-consistency check across the 95 pairs (does rung(A→B in framing X) ≈ max(rung(A→A' in X), rung(same-identity framing X→Y)) hold?) to trigger the full-210 follow-up round if a mixed-axis pair deviates.

### 11. Decision Rationale

**λ_selection = inner-group-cv (not GCV).**
- What: use `lambda_selection="inner-group-cv"` in `heldout_r2_sweep`.
- Why: GCV degenerates at n_train < D = 3584 — the exact regime this plan sits in at n=3000 (n_train ≈ 2400).
- Source: **#825 Phase-0 selector audit** (`eval_results/issue_825/selector_audit/results.json`, `leg_b_matched_n_curve`). On S_instruct_chat at n=3000: inner_group_cv = 0.6685, gcv_unguarded = **−0.1508**, gcv_guarded = 0.2744. On S_pretrained_chat at n=3000: inner_group_cv = 0.5653, gcv_unguarded = **−0.6822**, gcv_guarded = −0.0507. GCV is broken here.
- Alternatives: GCV with dof-cap 0.9 (guarded) — still degenerates (−0.6979 on S_pretrained_chat at n=4000). Inner-group-CV is monotone across the whole n range.

**n = 3000 conversations.**
- What: sample 3000 two-turn LMSYS conversations for the shared corpus.
- Why: 94–96% of the n=5000 ceiling under inner-group-CV, at 40% cost saving. n=5000 not required.
- Source: **#825 Phase-0** (`leg_b_matched_n_curve`). S_instruct_chat: 0.6685 (n=3000) vs 0.6957 (n=5000) → 96%. S_pretrained_chat: 0.5653 vs 0.5969 → 95%.
- Alternatives: n=5000 (5% more R² for 67% more generation cost; not worth it). n=2000 (94% of ceiling; would drop to 92% under harder cells like character-in-story where the yield floor may bite; 3000 is the safer margin).

**Capture layers = 14, 18, 19, 26; headline = 19.**
- What: capture only these 4 frozen layers, not all 28.
- Why: 4 layers ≈ 17 GB store, 28 layers ≈ 1.7 TB — 100× disk cost. Headline 19 is the settled convention; 14/18/26 span early/mid/late for robustness.
- Source: **#825 / #1310 / #1345** (headline-layer convention); load-bearing per body §"Capture + storage".
- Alternatives: 28 layers — would blow the 50 GB VM footprint and require `cpu-bigmem` routing. Not justified given 4 layers already span the depth.

**Ridge grid `numpy.logspace(-2, 4, 13)`.**
- What: 13 log-spaced λ from 0.01 to 10000.
- Why: the committed grid; grid-extension probe to 1e8 (`_grid_extension_probe.json`) found NO fold selecting above 1e4.
- Source: **#825** `scripts/issue825_fit_cells.py:66` (`LAMBDAS = np.logspace(-2, 4, 13)`); grid probe finding in `eval_results/issue_825/selector_audit/_grid_extension_probe.json`.
- Alternatives: extended to 1e8 — no benefit (probe finding). Denser grid (25 points) — marginal precision gain, 2× fit cost.

**Grouped 5-fold CV by conversation id.**
- What: `folds` array assigns each conv_id to one of 5 folds; all rows sharing a conv_id go to the same fold.
- Why: prevents leakage from having renderings of the same conversation on both sides of the fold split.
- Source: **#825** fit core `heldout_r2_sweep` group-fold convention; **`.claude/rules/ood-generalization-folds.md`** (grouped-LOFO for grouped samples).
- Alternatives: 10-fold (2× fit cost, marginal statistical gain), pointwise LOO (leakage, banned by the OOD-folds rule).

**Judge = `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`.**
- What: one consistent judge across every persona / user / assistant rubric.
- Why: project standing rule.
- Source: **CLAUDE.md § "LLM judge = `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`"**; `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rule 11 (cross-family, `Sonnet` on `Qwen`).
- Alternatives: gpt-4o (banned; Betley legacy); Haiku (banned as of 2026-06-23 for anything but transport-fanout).

**Judge scoring: graded 0-100, N=3 draws, temperature 0.7, anchored rubric, reason-then-score, drop-never-coerce, max_tokens=300.**
- What: `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` recipe rules 1, 4, 6, 7, 9, 23.
- Why: (rule 1) graded is the primary form; (rule 4) N=3 balances variance vs. cost; (rule 6) anchored rubric; (rule 7) reason-then-score; (rule 9) drop malformed / REFUSAL / out-of-range; (rule 23) max_tokens=300 for reasoning-rubric (#1090 recovery point).
- Source: `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` (rules named).
- Alternatives: binary rubric (dichotomization loss ≈36% effective N; banned by rule 1 for a ranking/regression target); N=5 draws (small gain vs cost, N=3 sufficient given group-fold averaging over 3000 conv_ids).

**Judge cache: rubric-fingerprint keyed, per-rubric partitions.**
- What: `rubric_fingerprint(judge_model, system_prompt, user_msg_template)` × (question, completion) as the cache key; separate `cache_dir` partitions per rubric class.
- Why: multi-rubric evaluation on shared completion pool (12 on-policy conditions score under 3 rubric classes). Content-only keys leak rubrics (#810 incident).
- Source: **`.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rule 22**; **#810** fix landed as **#1018**.
- Alternatives: one shared cache dir with content-only key — banned by rule 22.

**Sync fan-out for judge (NOT Batch API) at this N.**
- What: dispatch judge calls via `api_dispatch.py` sync fan-out across 3 org keys at Sonnet cap 100/key.
- Why: total ≈ 216k calls (12 conditions × 2 models × 3000 rows × 3 draws) sits at/near the ~200k Sonnet crossover per the throughput table. Sync fan-out at 3 keys × 100 conc × 4 procs/key extrapolates to ~13-17 min from the guidelines' 100k row (Sonnet 4.5 sync = ~6-8 min at 100k). We choose SYNC at this N because (a) it stays under the 30-min interactive tolerance, (b) it avoids the Batch API's up-to-24h SLA — this is a headline-critical judge pass, not an offline audit. If a pilot at n=200 shows the true rate below plan (dispatcher congestion, 429 storms), we degrade gracefully to Batch API and accept the SLA.
- Source: **`docs/api_throughput_guidelines.md`** § Sonnet 4.5 table + § "1b Operating defaults".
- Alternatives: Batch API (cheaper but adds SLA latency; viable fallback at this N since 216k is near the ~200k crossover).
