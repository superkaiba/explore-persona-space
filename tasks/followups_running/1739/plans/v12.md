- **What I'll run.** Both evil OOD rungs (#1739's hh-rlhf and ToxicChat) failed the DV spread floor, blocking the evil map-edge claim. ## 3. Hypothesis

H1 (A rungs): At least one of MHJ, tom-gibbs, PAIR passes the two-sided spread floor (SD≥10/100, <80% bottom, <80% top) and shows Spearman ρ(map-arm, DV) > ρ(surface-arm16, DV) with 95% bootstrap CI not straddling zero.

H2 (detection): AUROC(map-arm) > AUROC(surface-arm16) > 0.5 on ToxicChat and/or hh-rlhf, even where ranking rho is uninformative, because detection only requires ordering the ~35 escaping contexts above the mass.

H3 (compliance DV): Compliance DV and evil-trait DV agree closely (Spearman ρ > 0.5 within each rung). If compliance spreads where trait floors (ρ_compliance > 0.4 across OOD rungs vs ρ_trait < 0.1), the trait DV is the instrument problem, not the model.

Falsification criteria:
- H1 falsified: all three pilots fail spread floor AND item B (holdout) is the only arm with spread.
- H2 falsified: AUROC < 0.55 on all evil rungs (map indistinguishable from random on detection).
- H3 falsified: ρ(compliance DV, trait DV) < 0.3 on train rung (DVs are measuring different constructs).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Map-edge-OOD-confirmed ⇔ ≥1 rung passes spread floor AND ρ(map,DV) > ρ(surface,DV) CI-bounded above zero; Map-edge-OOD-detection-only ⇔ all rungs fail rho test but AUROC(map) > AUROC(surface) CI-bounded above zero; DV-degenerate ⇔ all rungs fail two-sided spread floor AND compliance DV also <10 SD; Unmeasurable ⇔ otherwise. Outcomes are evaluated in this order (first match wins) — precedence enforces disjointness by construction.
