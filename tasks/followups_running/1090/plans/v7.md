**Alpha-with-rank policy (hard-requirement decision, grounded):** α = 64 HELD FIXED across ranks. `train_lora` hardcodes `use_rslora=True` (`src/explore_persona_space/artifacts/recipe.py` docstring; scaling γ = α/√r), and the rsLoRA result (arXiv 2312.03732) is precisely that under α/√r scaling one holds α fixed and sweeps rank — the √r factor IS the stabilizer that keeps effective adapter-output magnitude and learning dynamics matched across ranks, making rank a clean capacity variable with no lr retune. Rejected alternatives: (a) α ∝ √r (constant γ) — total adapter output then grows ~√r with rank, confounding capacity with delivered dose; (b) α = 2r (the classic-scaling convention of arXiv 2405.09673) — under the hardcoded rsLoRA it yields γ = 2√r, over-scaling exactly the high-rank arms and inviting degenerate outputs that would confound the read. Fixed α is also the zero-config-change option: only `lora_r` varies in the manifest.

## §7 Decision Gates

**No mid-run decision gates** — pod wall < 2 h; the hypothesis fork is exactly what the full ladders answer; no cheap intermediate signal would cancel meaningful spend.

### Kill criteria (registered abort/invalidate paths)

- **K1 (mix integrity):** staging verify fails (row count ≠ `mix_meta` counts, composition ≠ 20/20/40, or sha mismatch vs the fu4-recorded pin for c1 / the fu5-recorded pin on re-stage) → abort THAT cell fail-loud; never train on an unverified mix.
- **K2 (divergence):** training loss NaN or > 5.0 sustained ≥ 5 steps → stop that run, mark `diverged`, REPORT as an answer — not a round abort.
- **K3 (retrain parity):** `imp-bare-lr1e5` rung-15 Tier-1 rate > 0.5 (fu3 measured 0.000) → round abort + harness diagnosis before interpreting any arm.
- **K4 (instrument):** per-arm judge content-drop rate ≥ 10% after transport re-judge → rule-23 truncation check before any rate is narrated.
- **K5 (rank seam):** the r256 smoke fails adapter load / hot-swap at `max_lora_rank=256` → fix the engine parametrization and re-smoke before the fan-out; never fall back to silently evaluating at a lower rank.
