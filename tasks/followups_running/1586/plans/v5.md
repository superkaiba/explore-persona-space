## 3. Hypothesis

**Round A (marker dose-match).**
- **H-A1 (dose-matchability):** at lr 2e-6 with step-1 checkpoint granularity, each marker FT cell has ≥1 rung that is in-window (ΔG ∈ [5, 12] nat) AND emission-clean (source greedy emission 0/20; every non-source context below the 0.92 argmax ceiling). Grounding: the 5e-6 cliff sits between steps 6 and 7; at 0.4× the LR the equivalent dose is reached near steps 15–18, and a lower LR widens the band in step-space (marker-training-recipe § "Don't fix epochs"). Falsified if consecutive step-k/k+1 reads jump from <5 to >12 nat (or emission onsets below the window floor) in a cell — then that cell's verdict is "not dose-matchable at step-1 granularity under the registered ladder", a reportable finding.
- **H-A2 (method verdict, conditional on ≥1 matched pair):** the marker paired Δnorm (layer 25, response arm) is positive (#1333's villain-context read: FT ~27% farther, +2.4) — but the executed under-dosed read was −0.93, so the persona-context sign is genuinely open.

**Round B (impolite deconfound).**
- **H-B1 (reversal survival):** the impolite-contrastive response-arm reversal (FT nearer) SURVIVES the LR deconfound — grounded on (i) the dose-matched s137 pair being MORE negative (−2.69) than the mismatched one, and (ii) the reversal STRENGTHENING under shared text (−2.6 vs −1.9). Against: the 3e-5 anchors out-shift their own po siblings by +2.5–3.8 norm units, so removing the hot LR could plausibly erase the LoRA side's excess. Either outcome rewrites the Takeaways bullet.
- **H-B2 (leakage):** the impolite leakage excess under FT (+0.21) persists against the 5e-6 LoRA anchors (direction prediction: FT breaks trained negatives — a mix/method property, not an anchor-LR property).

**Registered verdict lattices** (identical statistics to the executed run: seed-pooled con-regime paired differences, question-cluster bootstrap seed 653, 2,000 draws, paired indices; Round A binds on the 4 marker pairs — layer 25, response arm, ΔM in pooled non-source EOS-margin space; Round B binds on the 2 impolite-con pairs — layer 14, response arm, ΔM in pooled non-source judged rate; per-seed sign agreement reported per v3 H4):

## 7. Decision Gates

**No mid-run science gates** — the sub-rounds are short (<1 day), instruments are the executed run's own, and every "failure" branch is a registered reportable outcome:

1. **Smoke kill:** the 1-rung smoke (train→read→reap→persist + staging probes + marker-id assert) must pass before the fan-out.
2. **Compute-pilot kill:** marker cell 1 is the measured pilot; if `4 × measured_cell_wall / parallelism` re-projects >2× the §9 f1+f3 rows, HALT and revise (artifact-routed report + distinct rc).
3. **Round A no-window outcome (NOT a gate — a verdict):** a cell with no in-window emission-clean rung at step-1 granularity records "marker FT not dose-matchable at persona under the registered ladder (lr 5e-6 grid 1–12 executed; lr 2e-6 step-1 grid ≤48)"; all-4-fail skips the marker panel/capture phases (registered conditional).
4. **Round B band-entry failure (NOT a gate):** closest-approach + dose-mismatch label; reported as "impolite LoRA at the matched LR could not reach the FT partner's install within the registered ladder".
5. **Reused-FT structural HALT (scoped):** an FT partner checkpoint that fails to LOAD (wrong shards/config) halts only Round B's panel/capture for that pair; the WARN-class rate-parity band never halts.

**Kill criteria:**

- Round A kill: no in-window emission-clean rung at step-1 granularity in a cell ⇒ record "marker FT not dose-matchable at persona under the registered ladder" for that pair (a registered reportable verdict); all 4 cells failing ⇒ skip the marker panel/capture phases entirely and fold the verdict from the selection records.
- Round B kill: no in-band rung within 0.10 of the FT partner's confirmed rate in ≤360 steps ⇒ closest-approach selection + dose-mismatch label (a registered reportable outcome).
- Compute-pilot abort: marker cell 1's measured wall re-projects the f1+f3 rows at >2× ⇒ HALT and revise (the §7 gate 2 HALT, artifact-routed report + distinct rc).
