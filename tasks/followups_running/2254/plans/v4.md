# Plan — task #2254: the persona vector's pre-image as a causal context-vector steering direction

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** We have a linear map that predicts a model's answer-time state from its state right before answering, and we know the "persona vector" direction that makes the model act evil / sycophantic / hallucinate when pushed during the answer. I'll invert the map to get its best guess for what the *pre-answer* state must look like to produce that persona (the pre-image), then actually push the model's pre-answer state along that guess and measure — with an LLM judge, at matched push strengths, positive and negative — whether the behavior really moves, against three comparison pushes (the persona vector itself, a direction measured directly at the pre-answer position, and a random direction) and against the ceiling of just prepending the persona instruction.
- **What I expect.** The parent experiment (#2220) showed the map's own *reading* direction does nothing when pushed, while the persona vector pushes strongly. The pre-image is a different map-derived object — my honest prior is split between "inert like the reader" (H2) and "works but weaker than the directly-measured direction" (H3); a full-strength win (H1) would mean the map really does encode a usable control handle in the pre-answer space.
- **What would change my mind.** If the pre-image push clears the selection-safe random/shuffled floor with a clean confidence interval — or if setting the pre-answer state's coordinate along the pre-image to the "persona-instructed" level reproduces a solid fraction of the prepend-the-instruction ceiling — I'd conclude the map's geometry is causal, not just predictive, at the context vector.

### 1. Goal

> "Test whether the persona vector's pre-image under the fitted context→answer map (M⁺r_B, per-layer) is a causally effective persona direction when injected at the context vector — compared against the answer-extracted persona vector (at the context vector and at answer tokens), a context-extracted persona vector, and matched-norm random controls, via coherence-gated dose-response steering (single/middle/all layers, negative doses included) plus calibrated projection-patching and directional ablation against the donor-swap ceiling." *(Task #2254 Goal, verbatim — re-read from `frontmatter.goal` immediately before returning this plan; unchanged from draft start.)*

#2220 established that the context→behavior map's *read* direction is causally inert while the mean-difference persona vector steers strongly. But the read direction is not the only map-derived candidate: the pre-image M⁺r_B is the min-norm context-space state change the map says produces the persona vector at the answer, and #1615 showed it is a *good read-out* (its projections track judged trait expression as well as r_B itself, Spearman 0.50–0.78). This experiment asks whether that map-derived direction *writes*: injected at the context vector, does behavior move — as much as an empirically-extracted context-space direction (H1), not at all (H2, the map is predictive-only everywhere), or in between (H3)? The patching arms add a dose-free causal read: is sitting at the persona-typical coordinate along the direction *sufficient* (projection-patch on neutral contexts) and *necessary* (directional ablation on persona-prefixed contexts), scaled against the donor-swap ceiling.

**N/A — not a replication** (novel causal test; the persona-vector arm reuses a published extraction recipe, and the map recipe is inherited from #1615 — neither is a paper-headline reproduction).

#### 4.3 Patching arms (all three non-random directions; new hook)

- **Donor-swap ceiling** (plain prompt swap, no hooks): generate under the 5 positive extraction system prompts × 20 eval questions (100 contexts × 2 draws, seeds {42,43} → 200 completions/behavior). Doubles as the ablation reference level and the persona-prefixed no-intervention baseline.
- **Calibrated projection-patch** (sufficiency, neutral contexts): on the bare 20-question contexts, at the context-vector position, set ⟨h, d̂⟩ ← μ_pos (the mean projection of the 100 persona-prefixed contexts along d̂ at that layer). Mean-matching per arXiv 2402.09631.
- **Directional ablation** (necessity, persona-prefixed contexts): on the 100 persona-prefixed contexts, set ⟨h, d̂⟩ ← μ_neut (the neutral-context mean projection); DV read = how much of the ceiling−neutral gap the ablation removes.

New code: `ProjectionPatchHook(layer, d_hat, target_proj)` — subclasses/mirrors `DeltaHook`'s prefill-only last-token position logic, but computes the data-dependent edit `h ← h + (target − ⟨h, d̂⟩)·d̂` at hook time (neither `PositionEditHook` mode fits: `add` is a fixed delta, `replace` a fixed state); plus its multi-layer stack. Calibration μ's come from one `capture_vectors` pass over the 20 neutral + 100 persona-prefixed contexts (per behavior, at patch layers). Breadths: the localize-selected single layer (per direction × behavior) + the middle band (per-layer μ and d̂). Cells: 3 directions × 2 ops × 2 breadths + 1 ceiling = 13/behavior → **39 cells = 7.8k completions** at decisive grain.

### 7. Decision Gates

Three gates (wall-clock > 4 h; two genuine uncertainties with cheap intermediate signals):

1. **Compute gate** (after the measured 1-cell timing pilot in `norm_probe`, before localize). Basis check: if the measured per-completion wall extrapolates the full plan above **60 GPU-h** (1.5× the 40 booking), apply the trim rule — drop L26 from the single-layer sweep and drop the ±4 doses (grid 6×8→5×6 cells per combo; ≈ −40% generation) — and re-extrapolate; still >60 ⇒ halt-and-replan (plan-defect signal). Measured by: pilot wall × remaining completions / 4 GPUs. 2. **Rig positive-control gate** (after the localize judge reduce, per behavior): the persona vector at answer tokens must clear the selection-symmetric null band. Grounded (sign + threshold): #2220 measured Δrate +0.985 (evil) / +0.429 (sycophancy) on this exact rig and bank — failure means the rig broke, not a finding; halt + debug (two crash-fix rounds, then `epm:failure`/blocked).
3. **Baseline-headroom gate** (per behavior, same reduce, before decisive+patch spend): the achievable ceiling on the primary scale (100 − α=0 mean graded score) must exceed the null-band upper edge on Δscore (rate version reported alongside). Fail ⇒ that behavior is demoted to localize-only and reported rig-inconclusive — never drawn as a zero. Grounded: #2220's hallucination cell (baseline rate 0.733 → ceiling 0.267 < null edge 0.433) is the motivating instance; the graded scale is the pre-registered primary precisely because it may retain headroom where the rate did not.

**Per-criterion §4-mechanism binding:** gate 1 = the `norm_probe` timing pilot (arithmetic extrapolation); gate 2 = the localize dose-response surface vs the null band (presence check: r_B@answer above band); gate 3 = the α=0 `baseline_ceiling` cells + the localize null band (inequality check: ceiling > band edge). **Success criteria:** decisive + patch grids complete with per-cell Δscore/Δrate + both CI labels for every registered cell of every gate-passing behavior, and the lattice resolves to exactly one label per behavior — H2 and Ambiguous are informative outcomes, not failures. **Kill criteria:** (1) rig kill — gate 2 fails after two crash-fix rounds; (2) compute kill — gate 1 fails post-trim; (3) coherence kill — no direction has a usable coherent dose region at any layer-config for ≥2 of 3 behaviors after localize (report the coherence surfaces, halt as rig-limited).
