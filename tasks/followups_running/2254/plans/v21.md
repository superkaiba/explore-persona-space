# Plan v21 — pure decode-only answer steering with complete recovery state

## 0. Summary and current state

This follow-up supplies the missing matched comparison requested by the user: inject the
answer-extracted persona direction at every answer-token decode step while making zero
context/prefill edits, sweep doses below the historically destructive dose, and compare it with
same-runtime reverse-map context-only steering at the same layer and 2,048-token horizon. Both
routes are freshly and blindly graded by the same Codex-subagent response-integrity and trait
instruments. Quality-only dose selection is frozen on questions 0--9 before trait scores are read;
quality and trait confirmation use questions 10--19.

## 1. Goal and intervention semantics

Task #2254 goal, preserved verbatim: “Test whether the persona vector's pre-image under the fitted
context→answer map (M⁺r_B, per-layer) is a causally effective persona direction when injected at
the context vector — compared against the answer-extracted persona vector (at the context vector
and at answer tokens), a context-extracted persona vector, and matched-norm random controls, via
coherence-gated dose-response steering (single/middle/all layers, negative doses included) plus
calibrated projection-patching and directional ablation against the donor-swap ceiling.”

This follow-up addresses one unresolved comparison within that goal. Historical `allans` results
are excluded because `all_positions=True` edited both the final context token and every decode
step, used a different realized horizon in some cells, and destroyed response integrity. Here,
“all answer tokens” means `DeltaHook(decode_only=True)`: the prompt-shaped first forward is
unchanged and every subsequent cached one-token forward is edited. The first sampled answer token
is therefore necessarily unsteered; the intervention can first affect output token 2.

## 3. Hypotheses and estimands

For each behavior independently:

- H1: at least one nonzero pure-decode dose has response integrity equivalent to the fixed
  context-only target and produces a larger positive-trait score than context-only steering.
- H2: quality-matched pure-decode answer steering does not exceed context-only steering.

Primary quality estimands are mean response-integrity score and the fraction of responses whose
five-repeat mean is at least 80. The primary trait estimand is the refusal-aware intention-to-treat
positive-trait score, with a rubric-emitted `REFUSAL` mapped to 0. A conventional numeric-only
available-case trait mean and its item/draw completeness are reported as a registered sensitivity,
never substituted for the primary.

The confirmatory contrast is selected answer-dose trait minus context-only trait on questions
10--19, paired by question and effective seed, without filtering individual responses by
integrity. Positive values favor answer-token steering. Each route is also reported against the
answer-route zero-dose anchor. The full 20-question dose-quality frontier is exploratory.

## 5. Quality selection and inference

Statistics aggregate six effective seeds within question, then use a paired question-cluster
bootstrap with 200,000 fixed-seed draws. Questions are the independent sampling unit; procedural
judge repeats measure the response.

Quality equivalence margins are ±5 integrity points and ±0.05 pass-rate units. On q0--9, nonzero
doses are ranked by
`max(abs(mean_gap)/5, abs(pass_gap)/0.05)`, with larger dose as the frozen tie-break. A dose is
selectable only if both point gaps lie within the margins; sycophancy additionally requires both
routes' pass rate to be at least 0.90. No interpolation or relaxed margin is allowed. The selected
dose is frozen before any trait score is read.

On q10--19, both multiplicity-adjusted quality-equivalence intervals must lie entirely inside the
margins before the policy can be called quality matched. The 18 nonzero behavior-dose candidates
and two quality metrics use simultaneous 99.8611% intervals (`1-0.05/36`). The 18 direct
answer-minus-context trait contrasts use simultaneous 99.7222% intervals (`1-0.05/18`) plus 95%
intervals. A nested all-20-question bootstrap reruns quality-only selection only as an exploratory
stability diagnostic.

The immutable integrity-only quality projection and the pre-trait packetization replay bind the
selection hash, selected cells/doses, match statuses, confirmation statuses, and quality flags.
The primary trait reducer and every trait sensitivity must reproduce those fields exactly.
