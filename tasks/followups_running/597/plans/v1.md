# Plan — Task #597: Training dynamics of source implantation vs bystander leakage (positive-only vs contrastive at matched recipe, full panel probed per checkpoint)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** We already have a shelf of saved training snapshots from an earlier run that taught six personas to append a rare symbol to their answers using a mix of "do it" and "don't do it" examples. I'll probe every one of those snapshots — and a fresh set of snapshots from re-training the same six personas with the "don't do it" examples deleted — asking, at each point in training, how strongly *every* persona on a 24-persona panel (plus the bare no-persona assistant) wants to emit the symbol.
- **What I expect:** With "don't" examples removed, I expect the other personas' urge to emit the symbol to rise almost in step with the trained persona's, as if the model is learning one global habit. With the "don't" examples in, I expect the negatives to be actively pushed down, and I expect the trained persona itself to learn faster per teaching example.
- **What would change my mind:** If bystanders lag far behind the source even in the positive-only run (the habit is persona-local by default and the negatives are doing little), or if the positive-only run installs the habit on the source *faster* per teaching example than the contrastive one, the negatives-help-the-source story from the parent line is wrong and I'd want to know why.

## 1. Goal + Hypotheses

**Goal (verbatim from the task):** Measure the training-step dynamics of source marker implantation AND bystander marker leakage — the full bystander panel probed at the same eval intervals as the source, under the four-float storage contract (log P(marker), z_marker, z_eos, logZ; trained AND base per slot) — in positive-only vs contrastive training at an otherwise matched recipe, to determine (1) whether bystander marker log-prob rises in lockstep with the source implant under positive-only training (one shared unconditional shift) while contrastive training simultaneously pushes bystanders down, and (2) whether the contrastive regime's source-side implantation advantage at matched dose is visible from the first steps of training. Dose is read off the step axis of one full-length trajectory per arm — no separate low-dose vs full-dose arms.

**N/A — not a replication** (no published-paper recipe to match; the in-house #480 recipe is the parent).

**H1 — Lockstep under positive-only.** Under positive-only training, bystander `Δlog P(※)` rises with the same time course as the source. *Operationalization:* for each source, at the first Arm B checkpoint where the source's eval-probe Δ ≥ 5 nat (call it the onset checkpoint), the lockstep index `L = median(bystander Δ) / source Δ` is ≥ 0.5 in ≥4/6 sources → lockstep confirmed. *Falsified* if L ≤ 0.2 in ≥4/6 sources (bystanders lag — the implant is persona-local even without negatives). Between → partial lockstep, reported as such. Prior: #18's endpoint leakage of 92–98% predicts endpoint lockstep; the early-step shape is genuinely unknown.

**H2 — Active suppression under contrastive.** Under contrastive training, the trained-negative bystanders (the 2 per-source training bystanders + the no-persona context) are pushed *below* base. *Operationalization:* at the source's onset checkpoint in Arm A, trained-negative median `Δlog P(※)` ≤ −1 nat in ≥4/6 sources → active suppression confirmed. *Falsified* if trained-negative Δ ≥ +1 nat in ≥4/6 sources (negatives are not visibly working; localization would then come from elsewhere). |Δ| < 1 nat → flat (negatives prevent rise without pushing below base) — a distinct, reportable outcome. Mixed source-count splits that fire neither bin (e.g. 3 sources ≤ −1 nat and 3 ≥ +1 nat) are registered as "heterogeneous suppression — reported per-source". If the §9 descope ladder fires (N < 6 sources), the registered ≥4/6 thresholds for H1/H2/H3 are replaced by the descriptive per-source report with the actual N named as the denominator. Held-out bystanders are read descriptively (suppression generalization); H2 is interpreted as TARGETED suppression only if trained-negatives move more negative than held-outs — whole-panel-down is generic slot suppression.

**H3 — Source-side acceleration of contrastive at matched positive dose.** At matched *cumulative positive examples* (Arm A step `s` has seen ≈ 16·(200/700)·s positives; Arm B step `s` exactly 16·s; matched pairs at `s_B = (2/7)·s_A`, linear interpolation between Arm B checkpoints), contrastive source Δ ≥ positive-only source Δ in the pre-saturation window (both arms' source trained log P < −0.1 nat) in ≥4/6 sources → the #472-line contrastive source-side advantage is a from-the-first-steps effect. *Falsified* if positive-only ≥ contrastive at matched positives in ≥4/6 sources (the #472 endpoint contrast would then be a late/dose artifact). Also reported on the raw optimizer-step axis (where Arm B gets ~3.5× more positive gradient per step — if Arm A still wins on raw steps, that is stronger evidence). **Registered read (pinned per reconcile):** the H3 verdict is computed from the 5-step in-loop source trajectories (matched 32 rows; §6 DV row 4), with pre-saturation defined as the trajectory prefix before EITHER arm first crosses `logp_trained ≥ −0.1` on that read, and aggregation = median over matched-dose pairs per source; the panel-grid matched pair (~1 pre-saturation checkpoint per source on the 20-step grid) is reported as corroboration only. The analyzer additionally re-reads H3 on an LR-weighted cumulative-positive axis (both schedules deterministic) — a sign that appears only at warmup-skewed matched-positive points and not on the raw-step or LR-weighted re-reads is reported as ambiguous between "negatives help" and "more/hotter updates".

- **#247/#329:** contrastive coupling: source 99.6% / bystander 11.7% — endpoint-only.

Note: #472's DV-B (full-vocab KL) is **not** reused — the KL DV is banned for marker questions (#504).
- **#480 band-stopped-anchor-rerun:** produced the exact artifact Arm A needs — 6 sources × 27 checkpoints (every 20 steps to 520, plus 528) at lr 5e-6 with in-loop 5-step **source-only** four-float trajectories. Bystander dynamics were never probed at any checkpoint; the panel was evaluated only at picked anchors.
- **Method delta vs all of the above:** the full 24-persona panel + the no-persona context probed at *every* checkpoint of *both* regimes, four floats per slot per side, on fixed probe rows — turning the endpoint localization contrast into a per-step mechanism read. Open questions q:implant-learning-speed (2.2) and q:leak-contrastive-negatives (3.4a) both name this gap.
- **Literature check:** published backdoor-dynamics work (e.g. anti-backdoor-learning lines showing backdoor examples are learned faster than clean ones) motivates the per-step framing but none provides a recipe value this plan inherits; every load-bearing value is grounded in #480 (in-house, same model + data — preferred over paper values from different settings per the inherit fast-path).

## 3 + 4. Design

**Single manipulated variable: training-mix regime** (contrastive 200 pos + 400 bystander-neg + 100 no-persona-neg vs positive-only 200 pos, negatives **removed, not replaced**).

**Contrastive-negatives exemption (a) applies:** the single manipulated variable IS contrastive-vs-non-contrastive — the positive-only arm is the deliberate control. (`.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` exemption (a).)

**Why code, not a model call?** N/A — no unstructured-data heuristics in this design. The only "detector" is exact single-token marker identity (the one sanctioned substring-class exception), and all DVs are forward-pass float reads.

**Multi-arm resolution-band designs:** N/A — no multi-arm band-simultaneity gate. No condition gates on K arms co-resolving in a band; both arms are full-length trajectory reads on fixed grids, and the matched-dial comparisons (H1/H2/H3) use per-arm onset checkpoints — exactly the pre-registered per-arm band-entry-style fallback shape, used as the *primary* read.

**ICL/few-shot:** N/A — no ICL or few-shot demonstrations in this design.

**Data realism tier:** tier-3 (LLM-generated wrong-claim correction corpus from #411), inherited unchanged because matched-recipe reuse of Arm A's checkpoints *requires* byte-identical positives; substituting a more realistic corpus would un-match the single variable. Carried as a scope caveat.

**Smoke/sweep architectural parity:** smoke phase = sweep with one cell — `dispatch_leakage_dynamics_597.py --only-source villain --smoke` runs the identical per-source pipeline (same subprocess shape, env injection, logging, teardown) on one source with a truncated checkpoint set; no separate smoke path (same pattern as #480's dispatcher).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **lr = 5e-6.** Why: the marker-only over/under dial; the only demonstrated clean window. Source: #480 `BAND_STOP_LR` (dispatch_marker_480.py:92), grounded in `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` (#329/#478). The marker recipe and parent parity AGREE here (Arm A was trained under the recipe), so no parity-vs-recipe conflict exists.
- **LoRA r=32/α=64, dropout 0.0, rsLoRA, 7-module targets.** Why: Arm A's checkpoints ARE this geometry; single-variable-change requires Arm B match it, and the preflight parity assert enforces it. Source: #480 `_band_stop_train_cfg` + `_assert_band_stop_adapter_parity` (launch config, NOT the body-sketch's "r16/α32 attn-only", which mis-quoted the generic recipe default — grounding on the launch config per the #458 table-vs-config lesson). Gauge validity: gate/up/down are fine; only lm_head/embed_tokens would invalidate the logit readout (asserted).
- **batch 4 × accum 4, warmup 0.05, cosine, bf16, wd 0, max_length 2560.** Source: #480 (same cfg constructor; max_length 2560 = `DEFAULT_TRAIN_MAX_LENGTH`, the round-3 truncation fix). Alternatives: none considered — parity-mandated.
- **528 steps both arms; Arm B via new `max_steps=528`.** Why: matched training amount on the optimizer-step axis AND an exactly matched LR schedule (epochs-only would give 533 steps and a ~1% schedule skew). Source: #480 (12 × 44 = 528). Alternative rejected: `epochs=41` + stop-callback (schedule mismatch).
- **Arm B grid {4..60:4} ∪ {80..520:20} ∪ {528}.** Why: onset crossed steps 20–40 for all 6 sources at this lr (Source: #480 accepted anchors) and the narrowest known install transition is ~12 optimizer steps (Source: #533/#547) — a 4-step early grid puts ≥3 points inside any such window and brackets the matched-positive-dose points (`s_B = 2/7·s_A` ⇒ 5.7, 11.4, …); 20-step late grid matches Arm A's shared subset. Alternative rejected: uniform save_steps=4 to 528 (132 ckpts/source — 2.3× eval cost, ~240 GB disk for no added science past onset).
- **`marker_band_log_only=True` (not `marker_band_stop=False`).** Why: identical full-ramp behavior, but keeps the matched 5-step in-loop trajectory + smoke-gate reference for Arm B. Source: #480 realized config; smoke-verifiable-guard rule (#480 incident).
- **Probe rows = 50 `eval_50` questions × 25 contexts, base-model greedy R, cap 1024.** Why: matched to #480's eval panel + question set (cross-experiment comparability lives on the DV side); fixed-R needed for step-comparable trajectories; R-cap 1024 per the measurement recipe step 1. Source: #480 / `marker-leakage-measurement.md`. The `no_persona` 25th context: Source: contrastive-negatives rule, open-q 3.7.
- **Emission `max_new_tokens=2048`.** Source: CLAUDE.md / #260.
- **Seed 42, single seed.** Why: matched to Arm A's existing checkpoints; adding seeds would require retraining Arm A and break the reuse premise. Source: #480. Scope caveat carried to the clean-result.
- **Model id** repo-known (`configs/` + every marker issue) — no pre-provision AutoConfig smoke required.
