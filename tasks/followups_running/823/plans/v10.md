### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I take the same 5,000 real LMSYS user messages from the parent
  experiment and have Claude answer them again — but this time under k different
  characters (k = 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 personas: an ER doctor, a detective, a pastry chef,
  …), with each message assigned to one persona. Then I fit the exact same
  context-activation → answer-activation ridge map from the parent and ask: does the map
  get worse as the answers come from more different "people"?
- **What I expect:** if the parent's headline is right that the map works because the
  answers come from ONE consistent character, quality should fall as k grows — the single
  map has to average over k different answer styles. The per-persona control (refit the
  map inside one persona's slice) tells us whether the drop is really mixture-blur or
  something else.
- **What would change my mind:** if the k = 16 map is about as good as the k = 1 map
  (difference ≤ 0.03 R² with the entire confidence interval inside that band — the
  registered criterion, not just prose), then origin-consistency is NOT
  what the map depends on, and the parent's "consistent persona" interpretation needs
  rewording — the map would be capturing something any coherent answerer shares.

### 3. Hypothesis

Let R²(arm, L) = P1 pooled OOF R² at layer L. Three named lattice quantities, all
computed by the P-Fit bootstrap and persisted in `ladder_r2_p1.json`:

- delta_mean = mean over L ∈ {14, 26, 17} of [ R²(k=1, L) − R²(k=16, L) ] (the primary
  contrast; written Δ_mean in prose; computed under the registered P1 primary
  estimator — pure GCV, uncapped, §4.3).
- ci_low_delta_mean / ci_high_delta_mean = the 2.5th- and 97.5th-percentile endpoints
  of the paired per-context bootstrap 95% CI of delta_mean (10,000 draws, one shared
  context resample per draw across arms/layers). Both endpoints are named §3
  quantities persisted in `ladder_r2_p1.json`; ci_high_delta_mean adds ZERO compute —
  both endpoints are read off the same persisted draws.

- **H1 (degradation):** map quality falls monotonically in k. Prediction: delta_mean >
  0.05 with the paired per-context bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0, and the k-curve is
  monotone-decreasing (Spearman ρ(k, R²) < 0 descriptively). Grounding for the 0.05
  threshold: the parent's own-vs-plain gap (≈ 0.014–0.052 pooled) — a mixture penalty
  smaller than the origin-identity gap would be a weak confirmation; the parent used
  0.05 as its H1 decision threshold (plan v5 §3).
- **H2 (flat / origin-consistency irrelevant):** |delta_mean| ≤ 0.03 AND the entire
  95% CI contained in [−0.03, +0.03] (the parent's H2 content-indifference band, now an
  INTERVAL-CONTAINMENT equivalence criterion — an equivalence claim carried by a point
  estimate whose interval still reaches past the +0.05 degradation threshold is a
  standard equivalence-testing error, and that cell is realistically reachable) — the
  map is as good on a 16-persona mixture as on one persona; the promoted "consistent
  persona" wording is falsified in its strong form (subject to the joint distinctness
  predicate below).
- **H3 (mixture-attribution, conditional on degradation):** WITHIN the P2 protocol,
  the per-persona refit control at matched contexts and matched n shows a materially
  positive gap when delta_mean does — G_mix = mean over personas p of
  [ R²(within-persona fit) − R²(matched-n mixed-fit, same test rows) ], with the k=8
  rung as the PRIMARY matched-capacity contrast (its per-persona cells select λ on
  ~50 validation rows vs ~25 at k=16; the k=16 cells are reported alongside, read
  against the persisted per-fit λ and grid-edge flags where the widest rung looks
  unstable). G_mix is a REPORTED decomposition with a CI, not a gated verdict, and it
  is a STANDALONE P2 read: it is never divided by — or narrated as a recovery fraction
  of — the P1 delta_mean (no within-protocol recovery denominator exists across the
  two protocols; the earlier "recovers most of the drop" wording is retired).
  Attribution reading (narrowed): G_mix ≫ 0 reads as "consistent with origin-mixture
  variance — OR with reduced per-origin sample exposure, the named alternative: the
  within-persona fit trains on ~n_p rows FROM persona p while the matched-TOTAL-n
  mixed comparator contains only ~n_p/k persona-p rows, so per-origin exposure differs
  even at matched total n". The free triangulation read separates the two: the
  FULL-mixed arm-k fit evaluated on the identical persona-p test rows (a pure
  re-reduction of already-persisted per-context arrays; it holds ~n_p persona-p
  training rows at k× total n) — full-mixed ≈ within-persona ⇒ per-origin exposure
  explains the gap; full-mixed ≈ matched-n mixed ⇒ mixture variance does. G_mix ≈ 0
  with delta_mean > 0 reads as "the penalty tracks something else (e.g. per-row style
  diversity), not recoverable by conditioning on persona".

**Mixture-floor framing (registered; three independent Phase 2 arrivals):** because
persona(i, k) = i mod k is content-independent, the between-persona component of the
target is unpredictable from the input BY CONSTRUCTION — pooled R² MUST fall with k
whenever the manipulation checks show nonzero between-persona separation. The SIGN of
H1 is therefore not the finding; the MAGNITUDE and its ATTRIBUTION are. Registered
companion reads (§6 Mixture-floor decomposition, all pure re-reductions of persisted
per-context arrays): the observed delta is compared against the mixture penalty
implied by the measured between-persona variance fraction; the absolute-error
(Σss_res) ladder is reported alongside R²; and a fixed-reference-denominator re-read
distinguishes denominator inflation from numerator growth.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Degrades ⇔ delta_mean > 0.05 AND ci_low_delta_mean > 0; Flat ⇔ delta_mean >= -0.03 AND delta_mean <= 0.03 AND ci_low_delta_mean >= -0.03 AND ci_high_delta_mean <= 0.03; Intermediate ⇔ otherwise.

(ci_low_delta_mean > 0 is the machine-checkable form of "the 95% CI of delta_mean
excludes 0" for the registered positive-direction verdict — with delta_mean > 0.05 in
the same conjunction, exclusion of 0 can only be from above, so the criterion is
preserved, not weakened. Flat retains the point conjuncts and ADDS the interval
conjuncts, so the partition stays disjoint and exhaustive BY CONSTRUCTION: Degrades ∩
Flat = ∅ follows from the point conjuncts alone (0.05 > 0.03), and the interval
conjuncts only SHRINK Flat, the remainder falling to Intermediate. A strongly negative
delta (delta_mean < −0.03) remains Intermediate and is narrated as an UNEXPECTED
REVERSAL, never as Flat. Boundary fixture (committed unit test beside the fits driver,
CPU, no pod): delta_mean = 0.02 with CI [−0.02, 0.06] MUST return Intermediate — point
inside the band, interval reaching past the +0.05 degradation threshold ⇒ not Flat.
delta_mean is a single registered quantity — no per-trait
quantifier inside the lattice; per-layer and per-trait Δ values are reported
descriptively alongside.)

Row-coverage: this plan's own fits produce every registered row on each arm — BOTH arms of every registered paired contrast (each ladder arm k in {1,2,4,8,16}, the refit own-answer anchor, and the refit plain-external anchor) get their per-context (ss_res, ss_tot) rows from the single shared P-Fit pass, which fits all seven arms on one shared common-valid context mask and persists one row per (context x arm x layer) to percontext_ladder.npz, and the fits driver set-checks the registered contrast arms and context ids against that file's realized (context x arm) key set before any bootstrap or contrast is computed, failing loud on any missing row.

#### 4.2 Nested persona assignment (brief §4c)

Persona assignment for arm k at context i (0-indexed within the frozen 5,000-context
order): persona(i, k) = i mod k. Nesting property: the distinct (context, persona)
pairs across all five arms are {(i, 0), (i, i mod 2), (i, i mod 4), (i, i mod 8),
(i, i mod 16)} — exactly 14,996 unique pairs (5000 = 16·312 + 8; residues 0–7 appear
313×, 8–15 appear 312×; per-residue distinct-persona counts sum 313·20 + 312·28 =
14,996). Every arm has exactly 5,000 rows (one per context) drawn from this pair store.
Balance: within arm k, each persona p < k gets 5000/k contexts exactly for k ∈ {1,2,4,8}
(5000 divisible), and 313/312 for k = 16 (±1). Persona-0 rows of arm k are a subset of
the k=1 arm — free matched-context comparators for the per-persona control. The realized
assignment is persisted as `assignment.json` (context_id → persona per arm) alongside
the raw completions.
