## §1 Goal and hypotheses

The parent Goal is preserved verbatim and is not replaced. This is a same-question theoretical-analysis follow-up on the already frozen context→answer map: it characterizes which components of controlled China-sensitive prompt differences are transmitted to on-policy answers.

Primary construct definitions:

- The row-action map at layer `l` is `A_l = diag(1/xsd_l) @ W_l`, so a context difference predicts an answer difference as `delta_c @ A_l`; intercepts cancel.
- If `A_l = U_l diag(s_l) V_l^T`, the effective kernel at mass `m` is the span of input/read directions `U_l[:, i]` with `s_i < tau_m`, where `tau_m` is the strict threshold after cumulative squared singular mass reaches `m`. `P_ker = U_ker U_ker^T`; `P_ret = I - P_ker`. Ridge has no exact kernel, so every claim says “effective low-gain subspace,” not literal null space.
- `topic` is the source's fixed 12-way label. `subject` is the stable source item id within topic. `refusal` means a complete refusal that supplies no substantive factual information, classified yes/no by the pinned cross-family classifier. Historical 0–100 reference scores are thresholded at 50 only to validate this new binary instrument.

## Hypotheses and estimands

Pre-registered hypotheses and estimands, each reported separately for English and Chinese and at layers 14/19/26:

1. **Exact subject retention.** For item `i` and language `q`, average the two `sensitive_country_neutral` context-last vectors across the fixed framings, subtract the banked map mean `xmu_l`, and call the resulting raw-coordinate vector `q_{i,q,l}`. Retrieve its translation only among the other-language candidates in the same registered topic using cosine after projection by `P_ret`; run both English→Chinese and Chinese→English. The topic-restricted chance level is the item-weighted mean of `1/n_topic` using the realized frozen audit-passing candidate counts, not `1/90`. The primary statistic per layer/direction is retained top-1 accuracy minus the larger of kernel top-1 and mean top-1 across 1,000 frozen structured random orthoprojectors of rank `rank(P_ret)`; MRR and all-90 retrieval (chance 1/realized-N) are secondary. Per-item hits/ranks are computed once on the fixed unique-id gallery. Topic-cluster bootstrap draws resample those paired per-item/block statistics and recompute the aggregate `max(kernel, mean_random)` comparator without duplicating gallery candidates. Every within-topic label permutation does relabel the fixed gallery and recompute the whole similarity/rank matrix plus comparator; the full draw×layer×direction matrix is persisted. H1 requires a ≥0.10 absolute top-1 advantage and Holm-adjusted permutation p<0.05 across its six layer×direction cells; ordinary topic-bootstrap 95% bounds are descriptive.
2. **Specific content is more map-visible than nuisance wording.** Compute effective-kernel share `||delta_c P_ker||²/||delta_c||²` in the same raw input coordinates as `U_l`. The subject-bearing delta averages the two within-item framing-specific `sensitive_country_neutral - matched_non_china` deltas; the country-identity delta averages the two `sensitive_full - sensitive_country_neutral` deltas; the framing delta averages `academic - direct` across all three content conditions. H2 is an intersection-union test: in each layer×language cell both item-paired nuisance-minus-subject kernel-share medians must be ≥0.05. Topic-cluster bootstrap one-sided p-values are computed separately for country and framing contrasts, the cell p-value is `max(p_country,p_frame)`, and Holm correction covers the six cell-level p-values. Ordinary 95% cluster-bootstrap intervals are descriptive; item-level sign/binomial tests are sensitivity analyses only.
3. **Retained content predicts the answer shift.** For the subject-bearing contrast, define `delta_full @ A_l`, `delta_ret @ A_l`, and `delta_ker @ A_l` from the full, retained, and effective-kernel context deltas. Compare each without fitted gain to the observed answer-vector delta averaged across the same two framings and 8 draws. Primary evidence is uncalibrated cosine plus raw `R² = 1-SSE(pred)/SSE(zero-change)` over the complete item block. H3 requires retained-minus-full cosine >-0.05, retained-minus-kernel and retained-minus-identity cosine >0, and retained raw R² >0; each clause also needs its one-sided topic-bootstrap p-value to survive Holm correction across the 24 clause×layer×language tests. These four component clauses jointly define `transport-supported`; ordinary 95% intervals are descriptive. Gain-calibrated fits are secondary and must use leave-one-topic-out training only. No causal claim is licensed.
4. **Classifier-measured refusal linkage.** At each layer, construct a fixed #2617 refusal axis before reading new outcomes: retain historical primary pairs with valid vectors and absolute judged refusal-rate gap ≥0.50; orient each 10-draw mean observed answer delta from lower to higher refusal; average and unit-normalize; persist the qualifying pair ids and checksum. The current schema predicts 61 qualifying pairs; before any new outcomes H4 is made ineligible unless the realized count is at least 50, every axis norm is nonzero, deterministic alternating-pair split-half axis cosine is ≥0.70, and the fifth percentile of 10,000 pair-bootstrap axis-to-full-axis cosines is ≥0.50 at every layer. Threshold-0.40 and threshold-0.60 axes must each have cosine ≥0.90 with the primary axis and preserve the qualitative H4 sign, or H4 is reported threshold-sensitive. Project the new subject-bearing predicted and observed answer deltas onto this fixed axis. A behavioral claim is eligible only if the new classifier shows a sensitive-minus-control refusal-rate increase ≥0.10 in each language, the two one-sided topic-bootstrap p-values survive Holm correction, and aligned 4+4 draw split-half reliability is ≥0.70. Conditional on those gates, H4 requires Spearman rho ≥0.30 and Holm-adjusted within-topic permutation p<0.05 across its six layer×language cells; ordinary 95% intervals are descriptive. This is corroborating classifier-measured evidence, not human-ground-truth validation or evidence that the map causes refusal.

Multiplicity families are unambiguous: H1 has six layer×direction p-values; H2 forms six layer×language intersection-union p-values; H3 has 24 clause×layer×language p-values; H4 has six layer×language p-values. Holm correction is applied separately within each named family, and ordinary bootstrap CIs remain descriptive rather than multiplicity-adjusted. The strong paper-facing geometric claim is licensed only if H1 and H2 pass in both language arms/directions at at least two of three layers, including layer 19. H3 and H4 are separately labeled support, null, or indeterminate; failure of the H4 manipulation/validation gate removes “refusal-relevant” from the claim but does not erase a valid H1/H2 geometric result. Otherwise the result is reported at its realized scope (layer- or language-specific, indeterminate, or null).

## §2 Design and data construction

Data source tier: established dataset for the 90 source items; diverse-LLM-synthetic transformations for the paired controls/translations, justified because no established source provides the required item-matched bilingual counterfactuals. The synthetic part is independently audited and never selected on Qwen outcomes.

### §2.1 Established source and sample universe

Fetch only `data/test_questions_explicit.json` from upstream revision `c40ec8…`; assert the SHA256 above, exactly 90 unique `prompt_id`s, exactly 12 topics, and the registered topic-count vector `[2,8,3,10,10,4,10,10,7,10,8,8]` after sorting by topic name. The 10-item development split is excluded from all analysis and may be used only for the pre-production end-to-end smoke. No selection is performed using Qwen behavior.

Paired-contrast source coverage: both arms of every registered contrast come from this round's own factorial bank for the same source item, language, and (where applicable) frame. Analysis asserts exact id-set equality for both arms before computing a paired statistic; a missing arm drops the whole item block and is reported, never zero-filled.

Row-coverage: the run's own context-vector and answer-vector stores produce every registered row on both named arms, and the analysis driver set-checks each arm against the frozen audit-passing item ids before any paired statistic.

The public source was filtered by its authors for low Qwen3-32B accuracy, not for Qwen2.5-7B refusal. This is a deliberate distribution shift and is reported. The experiment estimates behavior only on the realized 90-item source universe; it does not claim population prevalence.

### §2.2 Factorial transformations

For each source item, pinned `gpt-4.1-2025-04-14` produces one structured record containing:

- an English country-neutral version that removes only generic country/nationality wording while preserving named subject/event/person content and requested facts;
- a matched non-China analogue with the same question type, temporal granularity, stance, expected answer length, and sensitivity class, but a genuinely different non-China subject;
- faithful Simplified Chinese translations of the source, country-neutral, and matched-control texts.

Framing is not model-authored: the code prepends one of two fixed, language-matched wrappers to every content condition. Thus framing is exactly controlled. `gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07`, with no access to Qwen outcomes, independently scores semantic preservation, match quality, language fidelity, leakage of the original subject into the control, and answerability. The bank gate requires every item to pass hard categorical checks for named-subject preservation, original-subject exclusion from the control, answerability, language, and matched question type; its 0–100 scores must each be at least 80, an instrument convention rather than a literature-derived cutoff. Failed items are regenerated and re-audited once under the same frozen models, then retained with a failure flag rather than silently dropped. The primary inclusion set is frozen as audit-passing, factorially complete items before any Qwen output is read; failed items are sensitivity-only. Primary analysis requires at least 81/90 complete passing items overall and at least `max(2, ceil(0.8*n_topic))` within every topic. If this coverage gate fails, stop before Qwen production and surface the data-bank failure. Because the synthetic matched control changes a bundle of entity, geography, and event details, failed balance checks downgrade interpretation to bundle-level subject content rather than a perfectly isolated semantic feature.

The bank builder prints only counts, schema, hashes, and per-topic coverage; corpus text remains in persisted artifacts and does not enter orchestration logs or task markers.

Teacher-force each realized completion through the same frozen HF checkpoint. Save, at layers 14/19/26, context-last vectors and tail-inclusive mean answer vectors including end-of-turn template tokens. Context vectors are stored once per prompt; answer vectors once per draw. The realized prompt token maximum is measured before launch; `max_model_len` is at least `prompt_max + 2048 + 32` and is recorded, with no truncation. A 10-item dev smoke must exercise actual vLLM generation, the real chat template, all three layers, the capture layout, map application, classifier request/parse, and upload staging before production. Planned coverage is 90 items × 3 content conditions × 2 framings × 2 languages × 8 draws = 8,640. Every summary reports planned and realized item/prompt/draw counts, cap-rerun counts, missing vectors, classifier content drops, transport losses, API-level refusals, and per-item completeness.

Historical target is the stored mean Claude refusal score thresholded at 50; the continuous score is retained for calibration plots but is not falsely treated as the new binary classifier's scale. - CJK-stress balanced accuracy ≥0.80, precision ≥0.80, recall ≥0.80, and Cohen's kappa ≥0.60;

Thresholds are predeclared as a publication-suitability gate, not tuned to pass. The CJK panel is explicitly a response-script stress test, not a human-labeled sample of intentionally Chinese prompts. Therefore new Chinese-arm refusal results are always described as classifier-measured. ## §4 Analysis

All vector arithmetic and decompositions are float64. Difference signs are fixed in the order named in §1. Per-item vectors average the 8 draw-level answer vectors only after draw coverage checks. Reliability is estimated by aligned first-four/last-four split halves, averaged across conditions, then Spearman–Brown corrected; the same split is used across all crossed conditions. The registered 90-item source universe (or frozen bank-audit-passing subset) is the finite descriptive target. H1 label permutations are design-based under within-topic exchangeability; H2/H3/H4 topic-cluster bootstrap intervals additionally treat the 12 registered topics as sampled clusters and are labeled model-based uncertainty rather than exact randomization inference.

Primary 0.99-cutoff outputs:

1. dimensions/rank/tau and map-SVD checksum per layer;
2. kernel share for every registered item-level contrast and its topic/language/frame identifiers;
3. cross-language exact-item top-1 and mean reciprocal rank for full, retained, kernel, and 1,000 dimension-matched structured random orthoprojectors, with Monte Carlo standard errors;
4. full-map/retained-map/kernel-only/identity transport cosine and raw R² against observed answer shifts; leave-one-topic-out scalar-gain results are secondary;
5. fixed #2617 refusal-axis predicted/observed projections, binary refusal rates, and correlations;
6. topic-stratified 10,000-draw bootstrap CIs, H1 within-topic label permutations, H4 within-topic outcome permutations, H2 item-level sign/binomial sensitivity tests, and registered Holm corrections;
7. 0.90/0.999 cutoff sensitivity and all null distributions, labeled secondary.

Resampling treats topic as the outer sampling unit: sample 12 topics with replacement, then sample items within each selected topic. Permutation nulls preserve topic counts and the full crossed item block. Retrieval label permutations shuffle subject ids only within topic; refusal-outcome permutations shuffle item-level deltas within topic. Missing/failed cells are never zero-filled. No maximum/argmax-selected result becomes a headline; all three layers and both languages are shown regardless of outcome.

The 10,000-draw empirical tests have attainable minimum p-value `1/(10000+1)`, comfortably below the registered 0.05 family threshold after Holm correction. Draws are produced in vectorized blocks and checkpointed; no Python loop performs one fit or one permutation at a time. Before source authoring, a deterministic 5,000-replicate design simulation using the registered topic sizes reports advisory per-cell and joint-headline power for the exact H1 max-comparator, H2 two-contrast intersection-union rule, six-cell Holm procedure, and bilingual/two-layer success lattice. It sweeps H1 discordance 0.20–0.50, H2 paired SD 0.10–0.25, and nuisance-contrast correlation 0–0.8. Because these pre-data alternatives are assumptions rather than observations, this simulation is advisory, not a hard production gate; no sample size or threshold changes after outcomes are seen.

### Measurement validity

- Subject retention: construct = cross-language identity of the same source item; metric = exact top-1 and reciprocal rank in the retained/full/kernel subspaces; on-distribution because both languages are generated from the same registered item bank.
- Low-gain nuisance allocation: construct = generic country wording or fixed framing with subject held constant; metric = paired effective-kernel share relative to the subject-bearing content contrast; on-distribution because all contrast arms are generated and captured under the same Qwen recipe.
- Answer transport: construct = predictable on-policy answer-vector change; metric = cosine and raw/gain-calibrated R² against the eight-draw mean answer shift; the targets are on-policy for Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct but the new subject bank is OOD to map training, so this is an explicit frozen-map generalization test rather than an on-distribution claim.
- Refusal: construct = complete refusal without substantive factual content; metric = the validated cross-family binary label and its eight-draw rate; transfer is established separately for English and stress-tested on historical CJK responses before new Chinese outputs are scored, with the intentional-Chinese-prompt limitation kept explicit.

## §5 Baselines and controls

- Full context vector and identity transport.
- Effective-kernel-only and retained-only components.
- 1,000 rank-`rank(P_ret)` structured random orthoprojectors per layer, generated once from seed 25,695 and held fixed across languages/conditions. Each is `D_s^T C^T S_k C D_s`, where `D_s` is a random Rademacher diagonal, `C` is the orthonormal type-II DCT, and `S_k` selects a uniform size-`k` coordinate subset. This is a tractable, explicitly non-Haar null with an exact rank-k projector and `O(N d log d)` application; report Monte Carlo standard errors. Projector application is blocked and only the draw×layer×direction retrieval matrix is persisted.
- Random direction and distance-matched within-condition pair nulls.
- `matched_non_china` content controls, `sensitive_country_neutral` generic-identity controls, and exact fixed framing controls.
- Historical #2617 minimal-pair result is a calibration/replication anchor only; it is never pooled with the new subjects.
- Source-topic labels and subject ids are fixed before any Qwen generation.

## §6 Success criteria, falsification, and interpretation boundaries

Success requires the measurement and data gates, exact planned-vs-realized reporting, and H1/H2 in both languages at layer 19 and at least one other layer. H3 and H4 are separately classified and do not retroactively determine the geometric result. A one-language result licenses only that-language wording. If H1 passes but H2 fails, the map carries subjects but the “nuisance wording lies in low gain” division is unsupported. If H2 passes but H1 fails, relative geometry is visible but not semantically identifying. If H3 fails, the decomposition is descriptive rather than predictively transported. If the H4 manipulation/reliability/correlation clauses fail, no refusal-relevant paper claim is made.

Outcome labels cover every result pattern: `bilingual-division-supported` when H1 and H2 pass in both languages at layer 19 and at least one other layer; `subjects-only` when only H1 meets that bilingual/layer rule; `allocation-only` when only H2 meets it; `single-language-or-layer-limited` when at least one H1/H2 cell passes but neither hypothesis meets the bilingual/layer rule; and `not-supported` otherwise. Independently, H3 is `transport-supported`, `transport-null`, or `transport-indeterminate`; H4 is `classifier-linked`, `classifier-null`, `classifier-indeterminate`, or `ineligible` when its manipulation/measurement gate fails.

This is correlational. The effective-kernel/retained split describes a frozen regression operator, not the model's computation. Topic/control transformations are partly synthetic and remain a limitation even after audit. The refusal classifier is a validated measurement instrument, not ground truth; its historical agreement and ceiling accompany every behavioral correlation.

## Kill criteria

Halt and report before production on any failed source/data/classifier gate. Halt the GPU phase on model/map mismatch, failed smoke, id-set mismatch, or unresolved cap coverage. Halt analysis on an absent paired arm, nonzero unresolved transport loss, missing topic/language/condition block, or upload-verification failure. Statistical null outcomes are not kill conditions; they are valid results mapped through the exhaustive lattice above.

## §11 Decision rationale

- **90 held-out items/12 topics.** Source: arXiv:2603.05494 and its pinned public test file. New, established, topic-labeled, and independent of #952's exhausted source universe. The uneven per-topic counts are preserved rather than synthetically balanced; inference resamples topics first.
- **Three content conditions.** Source: user-authorized Option B plus the matched-pair construct in the registered follow-up scope. The full→country-neutral contrast isolates generic country wording as closely as possible while holding the named subject; country-neutral→matched-non-China isolates subject-bearing content more cleanly than a single China/control comparison.
- **Two deterministic framings.** Source: experimental-control requirement in the user request. Exact code-authored wrappers give a clean wording/framing manipulation without an extra generation model.
- **Eight Qwen draws.** Source: measurement-design requirement for multiple rollouts and the aligned 4+4 split-half estimator. Eight reduces answer-vector/refusal-rate Monte Carlo noise while staying below the 6 GPU-hour authorization; no result-dependent draw extension.
- **Layers 14/19/26.** Source: explicit user request and the #2617 three-layer bank.
- **0.99 mass cutoff.** Source: #2569 leg-1/leg-9 registered effective-kernel convention; 0.90/0.999 prevent a cutoff-specific narrative.
- **Cross-language retrieval plus controlled delta shares.** Source: the user-requested “particular sensitive subject” versus wording/framing division. Retrieval tests subject survival across language; delta shares test allocation without analyst descriptions.
- **OpenAI classifier exception.** Source: explicit user/VM ban on automated Claude plus the public source repository's classifier. It is cross-family, pinned, and cannot become primary without the historical English+CJK validation gate.
- **No external-answer arm.** Source: same-question follow-up scope. The requested construct is what the frozen map predicts about Qwen's own on-policy answers; another model's answers would change the question and confound answer-source style.
