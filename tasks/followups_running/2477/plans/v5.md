## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The last round found that the base model's answers under the chat template were mostly incoherent — only 28% of 200 answers passed the judge's bar, against an 80% floor — but we sampled at temperature 1.0, the noisiest standard setting. I'll have the base model answer the SAME 200 questions again at two tamer settings — 0.7 (the Qwen chat models' own shipped default) and 0.0 (fully deterministic, always pick the most likely word) — in both the chat-template format and the plain-text format, and the same judge grades all 800 new answers.
- **What I expect:** Chat-template answers stay far below the 80% bar even at tamer settings — the template, not sampling noise, is what breaks the base model — while plain-text answers improve from last round's 71% toward or past the bar.
- **What would change my mind:** If the chat-template answers reach 80% coherent at temperature 0.7, the parent's mechanism story is wrong — the collapse was a sampling artifact, not a template effect — and the parent's takeaways get rewritten with that qualifier. (The paper's restatement on plain text still stands either way, because the paper's banked artifacts were generated at temperature 1.0.)

## 7. Decision Gates

Criteria + the §4 mechanism measuring each: (i) zero truncation-class drops — `stop_reason_tally` presence check; (ii) per-arm parse-fail rate < 0.02 — report field threshold comparison; (iii) effective draws per arm ≥ the satisfiability floor 51 (= max(10, floor(1/0.02)+1), the `_config_satisfiability_guard` formula) — at 4 arms / `n_draws=2` / threshold 0.02 the budget form `target_total_draws ≥ n_arms · n_draws · ceil(51/2)` gives 4 × 2 × 26 = **208**, realizing 26 items/arm × 2 = 52 draws/arm ≥ 51 (the scope note's "~200" would realize 25 items/arm = 50 draws and be REFUSED pre-spend — 208 is the registered value). FAIL(i) → raise `max_tokens` generously, re-pilot; FAIL(ii) → fix rubric/content classification, re-pilot; never shrink caps or thresholds.
