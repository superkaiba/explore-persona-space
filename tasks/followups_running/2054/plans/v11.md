## 4. Design

### The one variable

Old: each character's scaffolds are judged independently and each cell keeps whatever its own judge admits → pairwise conversation intersections are products of keep rates. New: ONE target conversation set T is fixed up front; every character is generated ON T, with retry waves on per-(character, conversation) rejections; the round's cells are built on the ≥4-way survivor set S. Pairwise intersection within the regenerated classes then equals |S ∩ both cells' realized rows| ≈ |S| BY CONSTRUCTION.

### Cells in scope (48 regenerated/extended; nothing else changes)

- **Regenerated (40 character cells):** 4 characters × {inserted × {attrib_quoted, bare_label}, on_policy × {attrib_quoted, bare_label}, cell_c × chat} × 2 models — the full character half of the realized lattice (cell keys per `issue2054_forms.cell_key`, forms.py line 116).
- **Extended (8 assistant template cells):** `conversation_paired_stories_assistant` × {inserted, on_policy} × {chat, bare_text} × 2 models. These are the source cells of the chat→character pairs and the (a) corner of the 2x2; they are deterministic template renders (no story scaffold, no judge), so extending their row coverage onto S is splice/continuation + capture at ~100% keep. Without this extension, S ∩ the existing assistant pools falls well below floor under either population read — ≈ 9,000 × (11,915/32,000) ≈ 3,352 against the draw file, ≈ 9,000 × (11,915/26,889) ≈ 3,988 against the admitted union — both < 4,480; the extension is what makes chat→character and the 2x2 (a)-corner pairs well-posed.
- **NOT regenerated:** assistant STORY cells (boundary forms) and everything the boundary-swap + cross-model pair classes read — already well-posed (min 7,999); regenerating them would be a second variable.

## 7. Decision Gates

Gates retained (wall-clock > 4 h; the hypothesis-bearing spend — capture + fits + ladder — is cheaply ruled out by two intermediate signals). Both are the scope file's pre-registered gates; thresholds and signs are grounded in v8's own committed gate constants and the realized run's measurements.

**GATE 1 — conversation-intersection floor on every ladder pair class (BINDING; gates ALL downstream GPU spend).**
- **§4 mechanism:** `scripts/issue2054_gate1_intersections.py` (R1→R2 boundary) computes, per affected pair class (prose swap 96, 2x2 208, chat→character 32 — pairs enumerated by the ladder's own pair builders) the realized per-pair conv intersection given S and the per-cell realized pools; boundary + cross-model classes are re-reported for the record (expected unchanged ≥ 7,999). Comparison: per-pair `intersection` vs floor 4,480 and target 9,000 (count comparison per pair; the gate criterion is min over affected pairs).
- **Semantics:** (a) min affected-pair intersection ≥ 9,000 → PASS, dispatch R2+R3. (b) ∈ [4,480, 9,000) → run AT MOST ONE contingency wave 4, sized ceil(gap / measured retry-wave admission rate) and capped at 1.5 GPU-h; re-evaluate once; then proceed at the realized size with a pre-registered caveat (n_train ∈ [d, 2d) for the shortfall pairs — fits remain well-posed; the recovery claim carries reduced margin). (c) < 4,480 after the contingency wave → ABORT the round before any capture spend: post the gate report + measured per-wave admission diagnostics as an `epm:progress` note and surface to the user (NO status change; the round is held at `followups_running`). (d) Mid-run early check: after wave 2's judge leg, the driver re-projects four-way survival from the MEASURED retry admission on retried (character, conversation) pairs; a projection < 9,000 arms the contingency wave preemptively (no extra approval — same spend envelope).
- **Grounding:** floor 4,480 = `KILL_GATE_4_MIN_INTERSECTION` (fits.py line 104; v8 statistics-critic r1 derivation, n_train = 0.8×4,480 = 3,584 = d); target 9,000 from the scope file (n_train 7,200 ≥ 2d = 7,168); the two bands are jointly satisfiable by construction (9,000 > 4,480) and the plan-time projection (15,700 × 0.573 ≈ 9,000) sits at the PASS boundary with the contingency wave as the designed absorber.

**GATE 2 — answer-length KS parity, evaluated in-flight, pre-capture.**
- **§4 mechanism:** `scripts/issue2054_answer_lengths.py` (R2 step 4 → gate at the R2→R3 boundary) computes per-pair KS D + mean-token ratio between (b) inserted and (d) on-policy rows per (character, model), and chat-vs-story analogues, from the per-row lengths ALREADY uploaded to `issue2054_lattice/common_regen/lengths/` (equality/threshold comparison per pair). - **Semantics:** fire condition KS D > 0.30 OR ratio ∉ [0.25, 4.0] (v8 kill gate 5 constants, `fits.py` `KILL_GATE_5_*`).
