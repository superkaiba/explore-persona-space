# The frozen context-to-answer map recovers the direction of instruction-driven answer shifts on every minimal-pair axis, beating identity pass-through throughout, after re-elicitation and a tenfold sampling boost clear the compliance-failed and noise-limited reads (MODERATE confidence)
<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_2564.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/df0b07caef292bf095f2fc4179a42bcfc77d93c4/docs/methodology/issue_2564.md) · [gist mirror](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/de36ebc7054e5e9152ed263dc7873305)

## Takeaways

- On all 9 instruction axes plus both query axes, the frozen single-turn map's predicted answer-shift direction beats chance and the identity baseline everywhere. Per-axis mean cosine between predicted and observed answer-state differences runs 0.17 to 0.90 against shuffled-pair nulls (hedging's two-value axis: a sign-randomization null) whose 97.5th percentiles sit at 0.04 to 0.45; the paired map-minus-identity gap is +0.03 to +0.41, every bootstrap interval excluding zero (12 to 120 fired-value pairs per axis). Six axes pass the on-policy compliance gate with their original instruction texts; stance, persona, and hedging pass after a compliance-piloted re-elicitation.
- The tone instruction (very formal vs very casual) is carried almost perfectly: direction cosine 0.90 against a split-half reliability of 0.99, within-axis consistency 0.93 vs 0.76 for the identity baseline, and a magnitude slope at the map's global calibration (ratio 1.03, interval spanning 1).
- Raising the two noise-limited cells from 10 to 100 draws resolves both reads. Injected-name reliability rises 0.13 to 0.59, the perfect-predictor bound 0.36 to 0.77, and the map's cosine rises 0.17 to 0.32, holding 42% of the bound (threshold 35%): sampling noise, not map-direction loss, so the axis joins the faithful set in direction, though its magnitude flips to over-scaled (slope 1.81 vs identity 1.74) and two of ten name pairs trail identity. The query-form text-vs-state dissociation survives at reliability 0.93: state 1.06 vs text 0.61 of the paraphrase yardstick, gap 0.45 against the 0.15 floor.
- A compliance-piloted re-elicitation (23 candidate wordings, 552 pilot checks, 0 refusals) lifted all three floor-failed axes over their fire floors: stance 4 of 4 selected values fired vs 1 of 5 with the original texts, persona 5 of 5 vs 2 of 5, hedging 2 of 2 vs 1 of 2. The parent floor failures therefore trace to the specific instruction wordings rather than to model incapacity; on the re-elicited axes the frozen maps beat identity throughout (single-turn direction cosines 0.37, 0.77, 0.74 vs identity 0.15, 0.64, 0.35), and no instruction axis remains untested.
- The predicted difference finds its true pair at 23.7% top-1 among 2,778 candidates (chance 0.04%) under cosine, ahead of identity at top-1 and top-5 (identity edges ahead at top-10), but under euclidean distance identity wins at every depth (17.1% vs 15.3% top-1): the map's retrieval margin is metric-dependent, so the direction claims rest on the paired cosine gaps. The single-turn and multi-turn frozen maps agree on every tested axis (largest direction-cosine difference 0.047).
- CJK-script text intrudes in 357 of 9,840 parent rollouts (3.6%), 130 of 2,760 re-elicitation rollouts (4.7%), and 399 of 15,120 draw-ladder rollouts (2.6%). Every fire verdict survives zeroing and exclusion except the parent user-description pass, which is convention-dependent (2 of 5 values under zeroing vs 3 of 5 as scored and under exclusion; its direction read on the two robust values keeps the same ordering, map 0.49 vs identity 0.36). Parent and re-elicitation direction reads move at most 0.006 under exclusion; the draw-ladder round's pooled reads keep intruded draws (Methodology).

## Goal

Decompose, by kind of context information, what the frozen context-to-answer map actually carries: build a token-matched minimal-pair battery over 9 instruction axes and 2 query axes on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, verify per value that each instruction changed behavior on policy, and test whether the map's predicted answer-state difference matches the observed one in direction and magnitude, against an identity pass-through baseline and shuffled-pair nulls.

**This experiment in context:** this is the designed follow-through of [#2215](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/2215), which found that banked context-to-answer maps discriminate minimal-pair contexts but that an identity-plus-bias baseline captures most of that discrimination at context-end. That study scored retrieval-style discrimination over a smaller pair set under a different read protocol, so its numbers are not directly comparable; this battery instead scores the predicted-vs-observed answer-shift *difference* per axis, where any constant bias cancels exactly and the map-vs-identity question becomes a paired directional test. The single-turn map under test comes from [#779](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/779), its multi-turn twin from [#1738](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1738), and the rollout/capture rig from [#2162](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/2162).

**Broader narrative:** the context-to-answer line of the project asks whether a single linear map from context-end hidden states predicts the answer representation a context will produce. Aggregate held-out fit says yes on pooled corpora; this battery says *which kinds* of context information survive the map (tone, query topic, audience description, content constraints) and which reads are limited by sampling noise or by the model's own unwillingness to comply, anchoring every representational claim to a behavioral manipulation check.

Conciseness acknowledgment: this body, one experiment round plus three folded same-issue follow-up rounds (a zero-GPU intrusion recount, a GPU re-elicitation round, and a GPU draw-ladder round), deliberately ships four check-20 WARN classes (Takeaways bullet length over the 30-word guidance, per-result prose over the 120-word band, total prose over the round-scaled budget, and the hero-figure caption over the 60-word guidance to carry the per-axis fired-pair counts) because the battery reports 11 axes with per-axis nulls, reliabilities, baselines, and intrusion recounts that lose meaning when split. The draw-ladder result deliberately links, rather than embeds, its reliability-curve companion figure to keep one inline figure per result.

## Methodology

**Design:** 984 single-turn contexts: 12 everyday advice questions (carriers) crossed with 39 instruction values spanning 9 axes (tone, output format, hedging level, marker-word insertion, assumed persona, stance, content constraint, injected user name, user description), each with one wording paraphrase, plus an empty-system anchor and query-content/query-form variants. The carrier questions and instruction values were authored directly in the approved plan by the Claude Code planning agent (an Anthropic Claude model rather than the evaluated Qwen model; the specific model id was not persisted in the plan or bank provenance), approved by the user, and transcribed verbatim into the bank with no text-generation API calls. They are deliberately constructed so that each pair differs by one token-matched manipulation; that control requirement is why constructed rather than real-world text is used. From these, 2,778 pairs in 7 classes: value swap (864), install vs empty system (468), instruction-wording paraphrase (468), family swap across wording families (864), query content (66), query form (36), query paraphrase (12). Each context gets K=10 sampled responses; the observed shift for a pair is the difference of the two contexts' 10-draw-mean answer representations at layer 19. Three predictors score identical rows: the frozen single-turn ridge map under test (primary), its frozen multi-turn twin, and identity pass-through (the map's learned bias cancels exactly in differences; verified numerically to 3.6e-15 over 32 pairs). Both reused maps are ridge regressions from the context-end last-token hidden state to the mean answer-token activation at layer 19, applied frozen with the penalties stored in their payloads (no refitting here). The single-turn map was fit on 963,444 real single-turn user contexts (529,085 LMSYS plus 434,359 WildChat), penalty selected on a pinned 400-row validation split from a 23-point log-spaced grid spanning 1e-3 to 1e8 (selected value 0.001, the grid's low edge). The multi-turn twin was fit on 87,795 training conversations from 99,127 captured real multi-turn LMSYS/WildChat conversations under the same fitter family and penalty grid, penalty selected on its pinned 396-row validation split; its answer-mean target includes the end-of-turn tail, matching this battery's tail-inclusive pooling. Headline per-axis reads use only values that passed the compliance gate; gate-failing axes are reported not tested until re-elicited. A same-issue re-elicitation round (plan v7; one manipulated variable, the instruction texts of the three floor-failed axes) re-ran stance, persona, and hedging with compliance-piloted wordings. Its pilot rolled out 23 candidate texts (9 stance, 8 persona, 6 hedging; authored in the approved plan amendment) at 2 draws per carrier (552 checks, judged synchronously) and selected, per construct slot, the best candidate clearing 70% of its 24 checks (ties to the first listed), keeping axis floors anchored to the parent widths: stance kept 4 of 5 slots (no fifth-slot candidate cleared), persona 5, hedging 2. The 22 selected values (each base wording plus one paraphrase) then re-ran the parent production recipe verbatim: 276 contexts (264 value contexts plus 12 fresh bare-system anchors) at K=10 (2,760 rollouts), teacher-forced capture at layer 19, and 672 pairs (204 value swaps, 204 family swaps, 132 installs, 132 wording paraphrases) scored by the same three frozen predictors. Scope notes for the round: the new texts are token-matched within pair but sit at different per-axis token counts than the failed originals (stance 9 to 10, persona 13 to 15, hedging unchanged at 10); the persona wording-paraphrase template is new this round (compliance-selected) and its cross-family read is not measured against the parent template; no answer-text embeddings were collected (representation-space reads only); and the query-form dissociation read takes no re-elicitation input by design. A second same-issue follow-up round (plan v8, `k100-low-reliability-axes`; one manipulated variable, draws per context raised 10 to 100) re-sampled the two cells whose observed shifts had split-half reliability under 0.60: the injected-name cell (120 contexts) and the query cell (48 contexts: the three question/statement forms plus the paraphrase yardstick). 90 fresh draws per context (draw ids 10 to 99, seed 42 plus draw, disjoint from the parent's ids 0 to 9) were appended to the parent's committed 10 under the identical generation and capture recipe (15,120 new rollouts), and the same three frozen predictors re-scored the 474 pairs whose both sides sit in the 168-context roster; the text-embedding leg re-ran on the query cells only (4,320 rows), with the injected-name text rows kept at the parent's 10 draws (labeled). The analysis pools parent and fresh draws on the draw axis, so both sides of every pair carry the identical 10-plus-90 provenance mix; reliability at 100 draws uses the pooled 50/50 split-half (20 seeded splits, stepped up half-to-full) as the primary estimator, with a fresh-only companion and a fallback that would switch the primary to fresh-only if the provenance checks failed. It never fired: pooled and fresh-only estimates agree within their intervals on all three cells, cross-provenance and within-fresh split-half means overlap (0.492 vs 0.483), and per-context answer lengths match (report-only check). A bridge gate re-derived the parent's committed injected-name and query-form reads from the pooled loader restricted to parent draws before any 100-draw read (largest absolute difference 6.2e-15 across all 474 pairs). Recorded deviation from plan v8 §7: the pod-side context-state parity bar against the parent capture was re-grounded from 0.9999 to 0.999 after the smoke leg failed it (minimum cosine 0.999873 over 42 smoke contexts; diagnostic verdict: deterministic batch-composition jitter, not content or environment drift, since bank texts and staging reproduce exactly, six smoke contexts recapture with deviation exactly 0, and the same stack recaptures deterministically). The re-grounded bar matches the rig's own calibrated bf16 single-position parity bar and carried roughly 8 times margin over the smoke-measured jitter ceiling; the production run's realized minimum was 0.99970 over the 168 roster contexts (one injected-name context), inside the new bar but below the original one, so at the plan's original bar the production leg would also have failed provenance check (a); the cross-provenance split-half and the bridge gate above are the science-grade homogeneity instruments (crash-fix decision record: `epm:progress` v90).

**Evaluation:** the primary DV per pair is the cosine between the predicted and observed answer-state differences, averaged per axis over swap pairs of fired values, with a carrier-clustered bootstrap interval, a per-axis shuffled-pair null, and a paired map-minus-identity gap under the shared bootstrap. Companions per axis: magnitude calibration (regression slope of predicted on observed shift norms as a ratio to the map's global slope 1.014 over all 2,778 pairs, with a per-value-pair decomposition committed for the content-constraint axis), within-axis consistency (median cosine among the axis's per-carrier observed directions as reproduced by each arm; undefined for the query-content axis, whose pairs are carrier dyads with no carrier-replicated value pair), cross-wording-family consistency, split-half reliability of the observed shift, and a text-embedding third space. Global retrieval asks whether each predicted difference finds its own pair among all 2,778 under cosine and euclidean distance (identity baseline and kNN reads reported per the project's representation-mapping rule), with the per-pair rank of every true pair recomputed from the committed prediction tensors and validated against the committed accuracies before plotting. The manipulation check judges 2 draws per (value, carrier) for the 7 judged axes (1,392 draws, completeness 0.981, 16 API refusal draws + 11 dropped) and checks all 10 draws programmatically for the marker-word and injected-name axes (word/name containment, denominator 120). A language-intrusion audit scans every rollout and the judged pool for CJK script and recomputes all fire verdicts with intruded draws zeroed and excluded; its per-arm counts, judged-pool cross-tab, and full recount table are committed alongside the eval JSONs. A zero-GPU follow-up recount re-derives every per-axis observed delta from the per-draw answer summaries with the intruded draws excluded and recomputes all 132 direction reads (4 read kinds, 3 predictors, 11 axes); its as-scored variant reproduces the committed reads to a maximum absolute difference of 5.4e-10 (observed-delta store: exactly 0 in fp32). The user-description robust-values read (the two values that fire under every intrusion convention) is persisted in the same recount JSON. The re-elicitation round reuses the identical statistics on its own 672-pair grid; its manipulation check judges 2 draws per (value, carrier) for all 22 values (528 checks, completeness 1.0, 0 refusals; one transport-lost draw recovered by a synchronous re-issue round, no batch/sync split field persisted). Comply-threshold sensitivity for the round: at 50% all three axis floors hold; at 90% persona holds (4 of 5 values) while stance (1 of 4) and hedging (1 of 2) drop. That round's language-intrusion recount, specified by the round recipe as the parent's convention but not emitted by the round pipeline, was recomputed at fold time from the committed rollout shards and judge scores and persisted as a committed recount artifact (linked in the footer): 130 of 2,760 rollouts (4.7%; persona 80 of 1,200, stance 31 of 960, hedging 14 of 480, bare-system anchors 5 of 120) and 30 of 528 judged checks (5.7%; 17 complying, 13 non-complying) contain CJK script. Recomputing every per-value fire verdict with intruded checks zeroed and excluded flips only the "Make the strongest possible case for each side equally" stance value (18 of 24 as scored, 16 of 24 zeroed, 16 of 21 excluded), leaving every axis floor met under all three conventions, and re-deriving the observed swap deltas from the per-draw answer summaries with intruded draws excluded reproduces the committed as-scored direction means to 4 decimal places while moving none by more than 0.002. The draw-ladder round reuses the identical statistics on its 474-pair grid with reliability computed at the 100-draw pool. Its two verdicts follow decision rules fixed in the plan before launch: the injected-name read is scored reliability-limited when the pooled reliability bound b is at least 0.55 and the cosine-to-bound ratio c/b is at least 0.35 (realized b 0.770, interval 0.731 to 0.803 against the 0.55 floor; c/b 0.42, paired carrier-clustered interval 0.379 to 0.464 against the 0.35 threshold; neither straddles, so the verdict is not fragile), and the query-form dissociation holds when the state-minus-text flip-to-paraphrase gap g is at least 0.15 (realized g 0.449, interval 0.239 to 0.736; not fragile, though the interval's lower edge sits nearer its threshold than the ratio's). The realized cosine 0.32 lands just under the plan's 0.35-to-0.45 noise-limited expectation band because the cosine-to-bound ratio slipped from 0.475 at 10 draws to 0.42 at 100; the decision rule keys on the ratio, which sits well above the 0.35 midpoint separating the two accounts. The round's language-intrusion recount scans all 15,120 new rollouts: 399 (2.6%) contain CJK script (injected-name cell 251 of 10,800, query cell 148 of 4,320; the parent-draw recount reproduces the committed parent counts exactly). Recomputing the programmatic name-fire verdicts at the 1,200-draw denominator with intruded draws zeroed and excluded flips no value and keeps the 5-of-5 axis floor under all three conventions (270 intruded checks in the 12,000-check fire pool, 266 of them complying as scored; minimum per-value comply fraction 0.979 as scored and 0.979 under exclusion). The 100-draw direction, reliability, and dissociation reads pool intruded draws un-excluded; no exclusion recount of those reads was run this round, a scope caveat bounded by the parent recount above, which moved no direction mean by more than 0.006 at a higher intrusion rate.

**Data extraction:** bank build and gate battery (7 gates, all PASS) on the VM; generation plus teacher-forced capture pod-side (capture wall 3,352 s); per-cell answer summaries, context states, text embeddings, and predictions uploaded to the HF data repo before pod release; judging via the Batch API and all statistics off-pod from the committed artifacts. The identity-cancellation assert and the fixed-answer equivalence gates ran in production, not only under smoke. The draw-ladder round followed the same shape: chained smoke-then-production generation, capture, and embed legs on one pod (2h39m wall), name-set-reconciled uploads verified before pod termination, then the pooled analysis on the VM from the committed parent revision plus the round's fresh upload.
