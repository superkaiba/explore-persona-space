## 3. Hypothesis

Let Δ_story = the Assistant-named story regime's within-regime held-out context-arm R² at layer 19 (instruct; pooled group-5-fold CV with the test fold's own mean as reference — so R² > 0 IS "beats the answer-mean baseline"), with its story-grouped 1,000-draw bootstrap 95% CI.

- **Name effect (map exists under the assistant's own name):** the Assistant-named story map predicts held-out answers better than the answer mean — the ARIA collapse was largely name/familiarity. Secondary characterization (descriptive, not verdict-bearing): its transfer to / reparameterization onto the chat operator and the operator cosines quantify HOW MUCH of the chat operator it shares.
- **Framing effect (collapse persists):** the Assistant-named story map also predicts worse than the answer mean, near the ARIA read (−0.754) — the collapse is the narrative-prose framing, independent of the character's name.
- **Existence sanity read (inherited):** in either branch, Δ_story must sit above its own shuffle null p95 for the "a map exists at all" clause to hold; a below-null read is reported as no-map.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Name effect ⇔ Δ_story > 0 AND Δ_story's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Framing effect ⇔ Δ_story's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

Prediction (honest prior): Framing effect is more likely — the parent's collapse was deep (−0.754, CI −0.693 to −0.599 even CJK-excluded, far from 0) and plausibly driven by short answer spans / 2,108 rows vs 3,584 dims rather than the name; but the name hypothesis is exactly what the user asked to falsify, and a positive result would be the more interesting finding.
