## Divergences from the approved scope (`epm:followup-scope v2`)

The approved scope pairs (a) a live band-stop at source ΔlogP ∈ [5,12] nat with (b) emission rate as the PRIMARY concordance DV and (c) an informativeness criterion of ≥5 nonzero emission cells per source. **(a) and (b)+(c) are jointly unsatisfiable.** The [5,12] band is the Regime-A *log-prob measurement* window: at source trained log P ≈ −14 to −7 nat the marker is never the argmax at the slot for the source, let alone bystanders — #478 measured exactly this regime and got 0 emissions in 2800 held-out generations ("Emission is 0 by design; read log-prob, never emission, in Regime A" — `docs/marker_training_recipe.md` §2 Regime A). A literal implementation guarantees every source fails the pre-registered informativeness gate: a false-FAIL by construction, the §7 jointly-unsatisfiable-gate defect (cf. task #488 round-10 lesson).

Resolution (single divergence, three mechanical parts, each the minimal edit that keeps the approved hypothesis testable):

1. **Live [5,12] stop → log-only trajectory + fixed step cap + per-20-step checkpoints.** The deterministic stop-on-the-metric character of the band-stop recipe is preserved — the rule itself frames it as "ONE training run with checkpoints, not N runs" and instructs to "gate the checkpoint on bystander resolution, not source emission" (`.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md`). The lr (5e-6), the steps-not-rank strength dial, and the trajectory instrumentation are kept verbatim from the scope.
2. **Stop target moved from the [5,12] affinity band to the emission-onset edge** (first checkpoint with trained source log P(※) ≥ −1.0 nat), because the approved PRIMARY DV (emission rate) only has dynamic range there (#456: first emission at end-slot p ≈ 0.22 ≈ −1.5 nat; #329: the graded-bystander regime sits at source-fires).
3. **The [5,12]-band checkpoint is still produced per source** (it exists on the ladder), is picked post-hoc by the pre-registered graded-pick rule, and is uploaded to HF — so the scope's literal Regime-A anchor is preserved as a zero-retrain artifact for a future sub-emission log-prob follow-up. It is NOT evaluated in this run (eval cost; the canonical log-prob DV cross-check comes free at the firing anchor from the same forward passes, since bystander log P keeps headroom there per `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`).

Everything else in the scope is implemented verbatim: pools/eval/join/stats reuse, emission-rate-primary, four-float storage, per-source WandB names, seed 42, ~1× H100.

## 11. Decision Rationale

All non-inherited, load-bearing values (inherited values grouped at the end with `Source: #480`):

- **lr = 5e-6.** Why: marker-only loss puts all gradient on one token; LR is the over/under dial and ≤5e-6 is the only demonstrated clean window — strength is bought through steps, never LR. Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` + #329 (5e-6 × 20 ep → source 99.6%/bystander 11.7%) + #478 (5e-6 → clean graded window); collapse evidence #397/#451 (1e-4 → unconditional repeater). Alternatives: keep parent 1e-5 (rejected — it produced the out-of-regime anchors this re-run corrects); 1e-6 (rejected — #520 floored at 1 epoch); 2e-6 (rejected — #519's weaker r=8 run needed 600 steps to overshoot; would stretch the cap without precedent at r=32).
- **Strength via fixed step cap = 12 epochs ≈ 528 optimizer steps.** Why: the cap must comfortably contain every source's onset crossing at 5e-6. Parent at lr 1e-5 deep-saturated within ≈132 steps (effective lr×steps 1.32e-3); 528 × 5e-6 = 2.64e-3 ≈ 2× the parent budget, and #519 reached +30 nat at 600 steps with lr 2e-6 and r=8 (a strictly weaker config). Source: bounded by #519/#520 + the parent's own pace; the exact 2× margin is an interpolation — **smoke-gated** (§7, with a single pre-authorized bump to 20 epochs on a near-miss). Alternatives: live-stop at onset (rejected: forward re-picks need post-onset checkpoints to exist); 8 epochs (kept as descope rung 2).
- **Checkpoint cadence = 20 steps, `save_only_model=True`.** Why: the pick needs resolution near onset; 20 steps ≈ 3.8% of the cap, finer than the rule's {8,16,33,…}% fractions because the bystander window can be narrow; save_only_model keeps 26 ckpts at ~4 GB/source. Source: `docs/marker_training_recipe.md` §5.8 (checkpoint-for-post-hoc-pick guardrail); the specific 20-step grain is **ungrounded — needs smoke-test** (smoke verifies ckpt loadability + that the trajectory brackets onset across ≥2 checkpoints).
- **Probe cadence = 5 steps (band callback), probe = 32 rows.** Why: #398 measured ~0.3 nat/step mid-ramp on a smaller adapter; at r=32/α=64 the ramp is faster, so the default 10-step cadence could blur the onset crossing the pick keys on; 5 steps halves pick error at ~3–6 min/source probe cost. Source: #398 ramp rate + parent saturation pace; the halved cadence is **derived, smoke-gated**. Alternatives: default 10 (acceptable, coarser); 2 (rejected, ~2.5× probe overhead for no pick benefit at 20-step ckpt grain). Probe rows 32 = `marker_band_probe_max_rows` default (`Source: sft.py:564` default, validated by the callback's own design comment).
- **Firing-anchor pick target: trained source log P(※) ≥ −1.0 nat (absolute, not Δ).** Why: emission onset is when the marker overtakes EOS at the slot — an *absolute* trained-log-P event (#456: first emission at end-slot p ≈ 0.22 ≈ −1.5 nat; 50% emission near p ≈ 0.5 ≈ −0.7) — and base priors differ by source, so a Δ-threshold would mis-land high-/low-prior sources. −1.0 sits between first-firing and solid-firing. Source: #456/#398 onset thresholds; the exact −1.0 point is **interpolated — smoke-gated** (comedian smoke reports its panel outcome at the picked anchor). Alternatives: Δ ∈ [16,20] (rejected — base-prior-dependent); source emission rate ≥ 0.5 measured in-loop (rejected — needs on-policy generation in-loop, too expensive).
- **Bystander-resolution gate: ≥5 nonzero / ≥3 distinct (informativeness) AND ≤2 of 23 cells ≥ 0.92 (ceiling).** Why: informativeness constants are the round-1 pre-registered criterion (Source: `scripts/issue480_emission_rate_concordance.py:57-58`, `Source: #480` follow-up 1); the 0.92 ceiling is the rule's argmax-saturation diagnostic (Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` saturation diagnostic), and ≤2 cells calibrates against the parent's SE pathology (2 cells at 1.0, 4 cells ≥ 0.92 — correctly fails the ≤2 ceiling). - **Graded-anchor pick (upload-only): Δ nearest 8.5 ∈ [5,12].** Why: preserves the approved scope's literal Regime-A anchor at zero eval cost; 8.5 = band midpoint. Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` band [5,12], #478.
