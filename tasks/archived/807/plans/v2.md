### 1. Restated goal + why the original construct does not work

Check 27's proposed predicate is: for every inline same-repo figure URL, resolve the sidecar `*.meta.json` at the URL's SHA, read its `commit` field, and FAIL when that commit is not prefix-compatible with the URL SHA. The premise is that a `commit`-vs-URL-SHA disagreement is a defect worth blocking.

I independently re-ran the census over `tasks/completed/*/body.md` (346 bodies, 412 same-repo figure URLs; `/tmp/census_807.py`, reproducing the ensemble's numbers):

- **412 same-repo figure URLs; 0 matched (prefix-compatible); 383 prefix-INCOMPATIBLE (would-FAIL); 22 sidecar-unresolvable (SKIP); 7 no-commit-field (SKIP).**
- Of the 383 incompatible: **321 have the sidecar `commit` as a strict git ANCESTOR of the URL commit**, 62 unrelated, 0 descendant.

So the check would FAIL ~100% of the completed corpus (383 of 383 comparable figures). The mechanism is confirmed at `src/explore_persona_space/analysis/paper_plots.py:1017` — `commit = _git_commit_hash()` stamps repo HEAD **at figure-render time** into the sidecar, while the body URL SHA is a **later** commit where the body (and often a regenerated figure) was finalized. `sidecar.commit ≠ url_sha`, with sidecar an ancestor of URL, is the NORMAL steady state, not a defect. Plan v1's Kill Criterion §13 (tolerates ≥3 false-FAILs) is falsified by a factor of ~128.

### 2. Alternative constructs considered — and why each fails

- **(A) Plain `commit`-vs-URL-SHA equality (the plan's construct).** 100% false-FAIL base rate (above). Rejected.
- **(B) Restrict FAIL to "unrelated" commits (neither an ancestor).** Would still false-FAIL on 62 legitimate bodies, and — fatally — #761's own pre-fix state has sidecar `3f5d228398` as an ANCESTOR of URL `c969f2281f` (`git merge-base --is-ancestor` confirms; `3f5d228398` resolves as a commit but `figures/issue_761/layer_robustness.png` is ABSENT there). So #761 falls in the ancestor bucket this variant EXEMPTS — it would PASS the very defect it was built to catch. Rejected: the discriminator both false-FAILs and false-PASSes the target.
- **(C) The natural "#761 signature": blob EXISTS at URL SHA AND blob ABSENT at sidecar commit.** I measured this directly (`/tmp/discriminator_807.py`): it **fires on 199 legitimate finalized bodies** (worse than the critique's 158) — every ordinary regenerate-after-render body where the figure was legitimately re-committed after render, so its blob simply isn't at the render-time HEAD. #761's pre-fix state is structurally identical to those 199. No commit-equality-plus-blob predicate separates the target from the base rate. Rejected.
- **(D) Fold a sidecar-commit blob probe into Check 4b.** The measurement that actually matters is whether the PNG resolves at the **URL** SHA (the SHA the dashboard fetches). Probing the sidecar commit's blob adds only the signal (C) — which is 199-way ambiguous — and does not improve on 4b. Rejected as no-signal.

### 3. The actual #761 defect is already covered — by Check 4b

#761's real defect (per its fix commit `56841ec21d` and blob checks) was that the figure PNG did not resolve at the cited SHA. Check 4b (`check_figure_url_resolvable`, `scripts/verify_task_body.py:1880`) already probes exactly this: `_figure_url_existence` → `_git_object_exists(repo, url_sha, path)` (line 3988) returns `fail` ("figure URL 404s — `<path>` does not exist at `<sha>`") when the URL SHA resolves but the blob is absent from its tree, and `skip` (never FAIL) when the SHA is unknown. That is the correct, discriminating surface: it reads the SHA the body actually points at, not the render-provenance commit. My census found **0** uncaught "blob missing at URL SHA" instances across all 346 completed bodies — Check 4b already catches this class robustly, and #761 is fixed into the PASS state on `main` (its current sidecar records the matching commit).

The proposed Check 27's synthetic regression `test_check27_recurrence_761_drift_fails` "works" only because a throwaway repo strips the blob-existence context that makes the drift benign — it proves the predicate fires on a shape that is legitimate 199 times over in the real corpus.

A `commit`-equality check adds no signal and would block ~100% of finalized bodies.

This is optional and not required by any evidence — the census shows 0 uncaught 4b-class instances.
