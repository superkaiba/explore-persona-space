# Plan — #547: sub-1-epoch max_steps-resolved grid {5, 10, 18, 30, 60, 120} at lr=5e-6, r=32

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** The last experiment found something odd at its lowest training amount: the persona-naming style that was supposed to contain the implant best actually leaked it most, on every persona and every seed. But that grid only sampled whole passes over the training data, so we can't tell whether that reading is a stable fact about the encodings or just a snapshot of training caught mid-stride. I'm rerunning the same setup with training cut off at six much finer points, four of them inside the first pass over the data.
- **What I expect.** I expect the gap between the two naming styles to move as training progresses: near zero with very little training, most negative around the point the previous run happened to sample, then shrinking back as the implant settles in. That would mean the odd reading was a moving target, not a stable property.
- **What would change my mind.** If the gap instead holds steady and negative across the whole range, the previous reading is a real property of how these encodings learn, and the next lever worth pulling is a smaller adapter, not finer time resolution.

## 3. Hypothesis

Using the corrected mapping (37.5 steps/epoch; #533's E=1 read sits at 37.5 steps, BETWEEN grid points s=30 and s=60):

- **H-artifact (early-trajectory optimization-ordering).** The per-persona d's are near zero or positive at s ∈ {5, 10} (E ≈ 0.13–0.27), swing negative in the s ∈ {18, 30, 60} zone that brackets #533's E=1 = 37.5-step read, and move back toward the saturated near-zero/positive regime by s=120 (E ≈ 3.2, where #533's E=3 read was mixed-sign/floor). Operationally: for ≥1 (persona, contrast) cell, the d 95% CI at THAT cell's earliest implant-active grid point (the gate resolves per (persona, contrast), §below) is NOT strictly below zero, AND the d CI at s=30 IS strictly below zero, AND the d CI at s=120 is NOT strictly below zero. The trajectory crosses zero — the E=1 sign was a transient.
- **H-mechanism (stable read).** The per-persona d's are strictly negative (95% CI below zero) at every implant-active grid point with s ≤ 60, on all 4 (persona, contrast) cells, approaching zero from below only as the implant saturates (s=120). No zero-crossing on the way in.
- **Falsification of H-artifact (the task body's kill condition, re-mapped).** On either persona the d trajectory never crosses zero AND stays within ±0.5 nat of #533's E=1 reference values at the bracketing points s ∈ {30, 60} (references: pirate × plain −0.67, pirate × padded −1.31, villain × plain −1.98, villain × padded −2.26). That is H-mechanism's signature — a stable role-vs-system gap rather than a trajectory swing.

Either resolution is a successful outcome; the experiment's value is discriminating the two shapes.

**Implant-active gate (which grid points enter the read) — defined at the (persona, contrast, s) unit.** The primary d is a cross-arm paired contrast, so the gate lives at the contrast, not at the persona alone. Per-seed install: an (arm, persona, seed, s) cell is installed iff its own-encoding argmax-emit rate ≥ 0.5 (the inherited `OWN_EMIT_GATE` from `i529_select_anchor.py`). A (persona, contrast, s) point is **implant-active** only if BOTH arms entering that contrast (the system arm AND the role arm) pass the gate, with per-arm own-emit rates reported alongside. Seed handling for the paired bootstrap: at a gated point the bootstrap resamples ONLY the seeds where both arms are installed; the active paired-seed count `active_seed_n` is reported per point; if fewer than 4 of 5 seeds qualify, the point is flagged `partially_active` in `trajectory_per_persona` (below 3 qualifying seeds — the paired-bootstrap floor from §9 — the point is implant-inactive: no CI read, greyed/open in figures). At s=5 (80 rows seen, ~40 positives) the implant may not be installed; a d read with an uninstalled implant in EITHER arm is floor noise, not leakage. Thresholds (0.5 gate, band values, CI criteria) are otherwise unchanged from the inherited recipe.

- **Kill criterion C (grid mis-positioned).** If ≤ 2 of 6 grid points are implant-active on a (persona, contrast) trajectory, that trajectory has too few points to discriminate H-artifact from H-mechanism — active-point counting is per (persona, contrast) trajectory, not per persona. Report descriptively, and propose an upward grid remap as the follow-up (do NOT extend this run).

**Built-in validity check.** s=120 (E≈3.2, 120 steps) must reproduce #533's E=3 (112.5 steps) regime: per (arm, persona), |wrong-slot mean log P(s=120) − #533 wrong-slot mean log P(E=3)| ≤ 2 nat. A larger divergence means the max_steps plumbing changed something beyond the indexing (schedule, data order, naming) and the run is investigated as a rig bug before any interpretation.

## Interpretation guidance for the analyzer (non-blocking critic concerns)

- Read early-point d's jointly with the per-arm wrong-slot LEVELS: floor compression can shrink |d| at barely-installed points — do not confirm a zero-crossing from floor-pinned points alone.
- If the earliest implant-active point is s=30, degrade the verdict to "left arm of the trajectory unobservable" — never score H-artifact mechanically false / H-mechanism mechanically true on that basis.
- A sign-stable, no-crossing, magnitude-shrinking trajectory that exceeds the ±0.5-nat band at s=60 still CLOSES the early-trajectory alternative — keep "stable negative sign" separate from "same magnitude as #533's E=1".
- The s=120 ↔ E=3 2-nat check validates the rig in the compressed floor regime only; the schedule-correctness evidence for small s is the smoke's step-count assert + the WandB LR trace, not that check.
- Report all 4 (persona, contrast) trajectories; do not lean on the single satisfying cell (the "≥1 of 4" criterion is a selection asymmetry) — treat sign-agreement as co-equal evidence with CI endpoints at 5 seeds.
- Scope claims to "training amount under this per-run cosine construction" and to this construct (2 personas, 1 marker, synthetic data, teacher-forced DV).

## Plan deviations allowed vs must-ask

- **Allowed without asking:** auto-recovery / cap-3 pivots per autonomous-session rules; auto-descope along §9's stratification order if the compute-deviation check fires; free-analysis follow-ups inline before parking; thin code fixes that keep the grid, data, lr, and eval rig fixed.
- **Must-ask before changing:**
  - The max_steps grid {5, 10, 18, 30, 60, 120} (title- and Goal-pinned; the bracketing logic in §3 depends on it).

  - The batch shape (4 × 4) — it defines the steps↔epochs exchange rate the whole design is built on.
