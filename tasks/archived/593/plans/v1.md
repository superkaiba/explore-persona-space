# Plan: Cross-behavior flat-panel factor analysis on existing panels (#411 sycophancy, #518 refusal + EM, #390 refusal OOD) — task #593

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** No training and no new model calls. I take the leakage measurements we already have for three implanted behaviors (sycophancy, refusal, and emergent misalignment, each trained into 6 personas and measured on 23 bystander personas) and ask, cell by cell, which of four simple factors predicts where the behavior leaked: how geometrically close the bystander is to the trained persona, how strongly the implant took in the trained persona itself, how prone the bystander already was to the behavior before training, and whether the bystander was explicitly trained NOT to do it.
- **What I expect:** Closeness and prior proneness explain most of the sycophancy leaks (that's what the sycophancy work already showed), and I'm betting they carry over at least partially to the other two behaviors. I also expect a real remainder: cells the four factors can't explain, which is exactly the list the campaign's next experiments need.
- **What would change my mind:** If the four factors classify the refusal and EM panels no better than chance, the leak/no-leak structure is behavior-specific rather than behavior-general, and the campaign's causal children should target behavior-specific mechanisms instead of the shared factors. That outcome is informative, not a failure.

## 0. Plan Summary (technical)

- **Training:** None. Pure re-analysis of existing eval artifacts on the VM (kind: experiment, zero-GPU correlational pass; campaign #592 child e1).
- **Hyperparameters:** No model training — analysis choices instead: leak thresholds θ_syco = θ_refusal = +0.05 (`#518` ±0.02 floor band + binomial 2SE at 500 verdicts/cell), θ_EM = +0.10 (≈2× per-cell survivor SE, `#518` run_results); permutation B=2000, bootstrap B=5000, seed 42 (`#518` scoring.json). Sensitivity grids registered below.
- **Baselines / controls:** Source-stratified permutation nulls; leave-one-source-out CV; threshold sensitivity grid; suppressed-cell class reported separately; per-behavior fits primary, joint fit secondary with behavior fixed effects; #390 panel as the negative-coverage-saturated comparison stratum.
- **Loss surface:** N/A — no training.
- **Compute:** CPU-only on the VM, no pod. Estimated GPU-hours (total): 0
- **Evaluation:** Per-factor AUC (leak vs no-leak) with bootstrap CIs + stratified-permutation p per behavior; multivariable logistic + LOSO-CV; per-panel flat/leaking attribution table; H5 residual cell list. Success = every flat (source, behavior) panel gets an attribution or an explicit "unresolved (H5)".
- **Risks (top 2):** (1) Source self-implant delta (H2) is **not measured** for refusal/EM — the #518 judged files cover 23 bystander panels only, no source-self trained rate (verified); H2 enters the syco fit only and is a named gap feeding e4/e5. (2) Leak cells cluster in 1-3 sources per behavior (syco: 25/26 in two sources), so cell-level fits are confounded with source identity; LOSO-CV + stratified permutation are mandatory and claims stay at separation/AUC level.

## 1. Goal

Determine which of four candidate factors — (H1) max bystander cosine / cell cosine (isolation), (H2) source self-implant delta (implant strength), (H3) bystander base propensity (affinity), (H4) training-negative membership (negative coverage) — classify leak vs no-leak cells across the existing sycophancy (#411), refusal (#518, #390) and EM (#518) panels, per behavior and jointly, naming the substrate-swap and EM survivorship confounds. Whatever the four factors fail to classify is the named H5 residual that reshapes campaign #592's causal children e2-e5 before they spawn. N/A — not a replication.

## 3. Hypothesis

Per campaign #592 H1-H4, stated as falsifiable per-behavior classification claims:

- **H1 (isolation):** cell-level layer-20 cosine separates leak from no-leak cells with AUC ≥ 0.70 on sycophancy (where the near-twin channel is established); carries to refusal and EM with AUC > 0.5 at stratified-permutation p < 0.05. Panel-level corollary: flat panels have max bystander cosine below the within-behavior leak band.
- **H2 (implant strength):** among sycophancy sources, leak-cell count increases with self_delta (qwen_default, self_delta +0.65, contributes ~0 leak cells). Only testable on sycophancy + the #390 teach-rate stratum — refusal/EM self deltas were never judged (verified gap, see §4).
- **H3 (affinity):** bystander base propensity separates leak from no-leak cells, strongest where #411's affinity channel lives (sycophancy); on EM the base rate is itself a survivor proxy, so an H3 effect there is read with that caveat.
- **H4 (negative coverage):** cells whose bystander is a named training negative leak at a lower rate than non-negative cells at matched cosine; suppressed cells (delta ≤ −θ) are disproportionately training negatives. #390 (all-negatives panel, ~0 leaks) is consistent-by-construction and serves as the saturated comparison point, not as fit input.
- **H5 (residual):** the per-behavior best fit leaves misclassified cells; prediction from the brief: the refusal floor and EM's length-dominated cells are not fully absorbed by H1-H4. "Factors fail to classify" is an informative outcome reshaping e2-e5 — not a kill.

Falsification: a factor whose AUC CI includes 0.5 under the stratified permutation null on every behavior fails as a classifier; that is reported as such.

**Step 3 — Fits (simple, interpretable; per behavior primary, joint secondary).**
1. Univariate per factor per behavior: AUC (Mann-Whitney) + threshold rule at the Youden point; cell-bootstrap CI (B=5000) + source-cluster bootstrap as sensitivity; stratified permutation p (labels permuted **within source**, B=2000, seed 42).
2. Multivariable logistic per behavior on standardized available factors (syco: H1+H2+H3+H4; refusal/EM: H1+H3+H4); in-sample AUC AND leave-one-source-out CV AUC (the honest generalization number with 6 clusters).
3. Joint fit (secondary): pooled 414 cells, behavior fixed effects, within-behavior percentile-transformed H1/H3, no H2; explicit caveat that cross-behavior cosine comparability is broken by the substrate swap.
4. Factor-factor Pearson matrix per behavior reported up front; if any |r| > 0.6 between two factors, add the tercile-bucket median test + rank-based robustness pass before interpreting the multivariable fit (registered per the project's collinearity-gate pattern). The uniform-draw argument (H1×H4 correlation ≈0 by construction) is literally true only for refusal/EM (`_draw_bystander_negs` uniform over candidates); #411's 2-draw + no-persona design is checked empirically, not assumed. **H4 prevalence asymmetry (fact-checked): syco has ~2/23 expected H4 positives per source vs ~4/23 on refusal/EM** — a built-in per-behavior base-rate difference the joint fit acknowledges (behavior FE absorbs the level; the H4 coefficient is read per-behavior first).
5. Panel-level attribution (the campaign deliverable): per (source, behavior) panel, flat = 0 leak cells at θ_b. Each flat panel gets a checklist attribution: H1 (max bystander cosine below the minimum cosine among that behavior's leaking near-twin cells), H2 (self_delta in the bottom of the measured range — syco only), H3 (no bystander above the behavior's affinity band), H4 (the highest-cosine bystanders are trained negatives), or **unresolved (H5)** with the blocking reason (e.g. "H2 unmeasured for refusal").
6. H5 residual: misclassified cells from the best per-behavior fit (false positives + false negatives) + unattributed flat panels → `h5_residual_cells.json` with per-cell factor values.

**Pseudocode (new script `scripts/issue593_factor_analysis.py`):**

```python
def main(check_inputs_only: bool = False):
    cells = {arm: load_predictor_cells(f"eval_results/issue_518/{arm}/_inputs/predictor_comparison.json")
             for arm in ("syco", "refusal", "em")}
    assert all(len(c) == 138 for c in cells.values())
    em_se = load_em_survivor_se("eval_results/issue_518/em/runs/*_seed42/run_result.json")
    syco_self = hf_json("issue411_.../analyze_summary.json", rev=REV_411)["per_source"]  # H2 syco
    neg_panels = {
        "syco": parse_realized_negatives_hf("issue411_.../training_pools/*/train_pool.jsonl", rev=REV_411),
        "refusal": derive_negs_at_sha(SHA_518, arm="refusal", seed=42),   # vendored _draw_bystander_negs
        "em": derive_negs_at_sha(SHA_518, arm="em", seed=42),
    }
    assert derive_negs_at_sha(SHA_411, arm="syco", seed=42) == neg_panels["syco"]  # positive control
    if check_inputs_only: return
    table = build_factor_table(cells, em_se, syco_self, neg_panels, thresholds=THETA)  # 414 rows
    fits = fit_all(table, perm_b=2000, boot_b=5000, seed=42)  # univariate AUC, logistic, LOSO, joint FE
    panels = attribute_panels(table, fits)
    residual = h5_residual(table, fits, panels)
    write_outputs("eval_results/issue_593/", table, fits, panels, residual, sensitivity_grid(table))
```
