## 11. Decision Rationale

**The A-vs-B decision (the load-bearing choice; hyperparameter grounding is `N/A — no model training` / `N/A — no training hyperparameters`):**

- **What:** Option B — ban adopt-more-severe-without-reconciler; restate the reconciler as mandatory; name the last-resort fail-safe as the only exception.
- **Why, against the four required considerations:**
  1. **Cost asymmetry (decisive, and it inverts #825's own rationale):** the reconciler is explicitly free w.r.t. the round cap (SKILL.md:2390–2391, 5681; markers.md:97 "Does NOT count toward per-reviewer round cap"), while the adopted REVISE consumed interp round 2 of cap-5 — a round that, at the cap, escalates to the surface-not-ship machinery (Step 9a cap-hit rule) or an autonomous `blocked`. "Cheaper than adjudication" was false in the accounting that matters. Worse, skipping adjudication leaves a systematically over-strict reviewer consuming rounds with no fresh-context check — the reconciler IS that check.
  2. **Under-determination:** "a mechanically verifiable residual exists" fixes truth, not severity. The reconciler can legitimately side PASS on a true-but-not-conclusion-changing finding — a threshold judgment the orchestrator (which has seen the analyzer's reasoning; not fresh-context) is structurally the wrong agent to make ("Independence is load-bearing", Step 9a step 2). This is exactly where the 5c-bis mirror breaks: the strip verifies a pure structural fact against a bounded tag set and only ever removes verifiable FALSE positives (biasing toward shipping only what's mechanically proven clean); the #825 move adopts a true positive whose severity is unadjudicated (biasing toward burning capped rounds on a judgment call). Codifying A would require the orchestrator to certify both truth AND verdict-relevance — the second being unverifiable mechanically, the bounded condition cannot be written tightly.
  3. **Consistency cost:** codifying A coherently requires symmetric carve-outs at all four iterating sites + the resume rows + CLAUDE.md + workflow.yaml (else the sites diverge) — a large diff against the Goal's "one sentence". Banning needs one canonical statement (Step 5c, which 9a-bis inherits by name and 9b VC matches in shape) + one pointer at the incident site. B is the minimal-diff-consistent option.
  4. **Precedent alignment:** adopt-more-severe already exists in the surface as a LAST-RESORT fail-safe (adversarial-planner § Durable-output-first: spawn → error → re-spawn once → still nothing). Codifying it as a first-resort spawn-saver inverts a deliberate design; banning preserves it exactly.
