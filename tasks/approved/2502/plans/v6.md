# Plan v6 — Issue #2502: context→answer map M_{C,A} on a 150k maximally-diverse corpus, cross-model replication (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct vs Qwen3.5-9B)

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** I build one big, deliberately weird collection of 150,000 real conversation openings (jailbreaks, red-team prompts, roleplay, refusals, plus ordinary chat), have each of two models answer them, and record the model's internal state after reading the prompt and after writing its answer. Then I fit ONE simple linear map per model that predicts the "after-answer" state from the "after-prompt" state, check how well it predicts on held-out prompts I never trained on — broken down by which dataset the prompt came from — and compare the two models on the layers that are architecturally comparable.
- **What I expect.** The linear map will predict answer-state well (held-out R² in the ballpark of the ~0.73–0.76 that #779 got on ordinary chat), and it will beat the two dumb baselines (copy-the-input-plus-a-constant, and nearest-neighbor guessing). It'll do noticeably worse on the weirdest datasets than on ordinary chat. I expect the newer, differently-built model (Qwen3.5-9B) to show a broadly similar map, but I'm genuinely unsure whether it'll be as clean because its attention is a different architecture.
- **What would change my mind.** If the map barely beats "copy the input," the whole context→answer story is weaker than #779 suggested. If the second model's map is much worse than the first's even after I account for how noisy its single answers are, "this map survives a model change" doesn't hold.

### 0. Plan Summary (technical)

**Training:** No model fine-tuning. This experiment FITS ONE linear ridge map per model, `M: cx_last(C) → v_x(A)` (context last-token hidden → answer-span mean hidden) per layer, over a 150k maximally-diverse context corpus with on-policy answers; the single pooled map is EVALUATED per source (per-source eval slices need no n≥d). **Hyperparameters:** ridge, val-λ-selected on `LAMBDAS_N50K = np.logspace(-3, 7, 21)` (`Source: #779` — the exact `fit_ridge_primal` primal-ridge core, fp64, batched over λ); input = context last-token hidden (`Source: #779`); target = answer-span mean hidden (`Source: #779`); layer selection on an independent validation partition, scored once on test (§6/MF-D). **Baselines/controls:** identity+learned-bias baseline (`identity_bias_predict`, within-model d_in==d_out) + kNN retrieval (`knn_retrieval`, euclidean+cosine, chance=k/n_pool) per split (`Source: #722`); per-source holdout + LODO group folds (`Source: #1775`, ood-folds); per-model answer-vector reliability ceiling from a replicate-answer subset (MF-E). **Loss surface:** ridge closed-form (convex). **Compute:** RunPod auto (runpod-first); Model A gen+capture on 1×H100 (`pod-2502-model-a`), Model B on 1×H200 (`pod-2502-model-b`, dedicated #2378-class pod venv), fits pinned to a `cpu-bigmem` pod. `Estimated GPU-hours (total): 54`. **Evaluation:** pooled + per-source held-out R², vs both baselines + kNN + reliability ceiling, cross-model matched relative-depth comparison. **Risks (top 2):** (i) Model B has NO runtime env on the repo-standard stack — needs the #2378 pod venv + GDN prefill pin (MF-A); (ii) batched last-token capture off-by-one would silently depress R² inside the ±0.10 band — per-row position asserts in production (MF-F).

### 3. Hypothesis

All hypotheses reference the ONE decision function registered below (MF-C); the H1/H3/kill-criterion predicate wording is byte-identical across §3/§6/§7.

**H1 (map exists, diverse corpus).** For BOTH models, the fitted map's held-out R² on the ordinary-chat stratum (the PRIMARY H1 split, § Regime table §5) exceeds its identity+bias baseline by a margin whose 95% paired bootstrap CI excludes 0 on the positive side; pooled best-relative-depth-layer held-out R² for Qwen2.5-7B lands in ~0.65–0.78 (bracketing #779's 0.73–0.76 generic plateau). kNN is DIAGNOSTIC-ONLY (reported per split, not an inferential gate).

**H2 (per-regime dissociation).** The map's mean held-out R² is materially LOWER on the weird/OOD/jailbreak regime classes than on the ordinary-chat class. H2's inferential grain is the SOURCE/GROUP, not the individual context: the class mean is computed EQUAL-SOURCE-weighted (each `source_tag`'s per-source held-out R² contributes equally), and H2 is a DESCRIPTIVE point-threshold classification with a source-level bootstrap CI reported alongside (predicted ordinary-mean minus weird-mean ≥ 0.05). The fixed regime table (§5) is committed before results.

**H3 (cross-model replication).** Registered as a ONE-SIDED NON-INFERIORITY test conditioned on each model's answer-vector reliability ceiling (MF-E). Let `A_best` / `B_best` be each model's held-out R² at its selected layer (selected on the independent validation partition, §6/MF-D), and `ceil_A` / `ceil_B` the per-model reliability ceilings. The verdict is registered in the truth table below.

**H4 (LODO transfer).** Fitting on all-but-one source (GROUP folds keyed on `source_tag`, § LODO granularity §5) and evaluating on the left-out source, held-out R² is positive and above the identity+bias baseline for the ordinary-chat and near-distribution regime classes; it may drop toward baseline for the idiosyncratic classes (cipher/ASCII/roleplay) — that drop is the reportable OOD signal, not a failure. H4 uses the SAME fixed regime categories as H2.

**Registered decision function (ONE coherent truth table over the predicates; MF-C).** Predicates: `A_pass` = Model-A beats its identity+bias baseline on the H1 primary split (paired bootstrap CI excludes 0, positive); `B_pass` = same for Model B; `NI` = the non-inferiority test `B_best is at least (A_best minus 0.10)` holds, evaluated as: the LOWER bound of the selection-inherited 95% bootstrap CI on `(B_best minus A_best)` is greater than minus 0.10. All bootstrap CIs are SELECTION-INHERITED (layer re-selected inside every resample; MF-D) and computed from the persisted per-context×per-layer×per-arm reconstruction matrix.

`DISJOINT and exhaustive: Replicates ⇔ A_pass AND B_pass AND NI holds; Fails-to-replicate ⇔ A_pass AND ((NOT NI, i.e. the CI lower bound is below minus 0.10 with the CI wholly below minus 0.10) OR (NOT B_pass)); Inconclusive ⇔ (NOT A_pass) OR (A_pass AND B_pass AND the non-inferiority CI SPANS minus 0.10, i.e. neither wholly above nor wholly below the boundary).`

The `A_pass` guard makes Inconclusive REACHABLE: when Model A does not beat its own baseline the whole cross-model comparison is voided → Inconclusive (never Replicates or Fails). A CI that spans the minus-0.10 boundary is Inconclusive, not Fails — non-inferiority is neither established nor refuted. This decision function is the SAME object the §6 verdict lattice and the §7 kill criterion reference.

### Kill criteria

Concrete results that kill the thesis (the SAME predicates as the decision function above; wording byte-identical to §7):
- **The context→answer map claim dies if, for EITHER model, the fitted map does NOT beat the identity+bias baseline on the ordinary-chat primary split** (NOT `A_pass`, or NOT `B_pass` with `A_pass` — the map is not more than a constant shift where the corpus is closest to #779's regime). Under NOT `A_pass` the run is Inconclusive (the instrument itself is voided), not a clean negative.
- **The cross-model replication claim dies (Fails-to-replicate) if `A_pass` holds AND the non-inferiority CI on `(B_best minus A_best)` is wholly below minus 0.10, OR `A_pass` holds AND NOT `B_pass`** — Qwen3.5-9B's map is materially worse than Qwen2.5-7B's even after conditioning on its reliability ceiling, or it fails its own baseline.
- **Gate G1 kill:** the 1k pooled pilot landing below 0.40 pooled held-out R² for Model A (or failing the beats-baseline CI check) halts the full 150k spend until the rig (capture positions, think-token bookkeeping, dedup) is diagnosed — a persistent post-diagnosis pilot failure kills the run as designed.

**Registered paired contrast (within-model map-beats-identity-baseline, the `A_pass`/`B_pass` predicates):** per held-out context i in the H1 primary split, `Δ_i = recon_err_identity(i) − recon_err_map(i)` (per-context squared reconstruction error under the identity+bias baseline minus under the fitted map); the headline is `mean_i Δ_i` with a paired bootstrap CI, per model.
`Row-coverage: the plan's own fits produce every registered per-context row on each arm — both recon_err_map and recon_err_identity are computed by the P3 fits driver from the SAME held-out (cx_last, v_x) rows (the map's prediction and identity_bias_predict's prediction against the same true v_x), so both arms' rows are supplied by eval_results/issue_2502/fits/<model>/percontext_recon.json (the single reconciled path, §6.5/§9/§10).`

Storage codec: bf16-as-uint16 npz per (field × layer × chunk) (`Source: #2378`; fp16 overflows Qwen massive activations). Per-chunk upload-verify-purge (`Source: #779` n50k driver) keeps local footprint under the MooseFS ~130 GB per-pod quota.

**P3-rel — replicate-answer subset + reliability ceiling (MF-E).** On a COMMON stratified evaluation subset (~1,500 contexts, drawn to span every regime class), generate `R=3` independent answers per context in EACH model (distinct sampling seeds; the reliability subset carries ≥2 distinct completion IDs per (model, context_id) by construction, R=3 gives margin). Capture each replicate's `v_x` at every candidate layer. Compute a per-model / per-layer **answer-vector reliability ceiling** = the split-half / across-replicate reconstruction reliability of `v_x` (the max R² a perfect map could achieve given the replicate-to-replicate variance of the answer vector — operationally, the across-replicate consistency of `v_x` per context, aggregated per layer). This ceiling CONDITIONS the H3 non-inferiority read: a lower Model-B R² is only "worse map" if B's ceiling is not itself lower (higher conditional output entropy on a ~55%-weird corpus with B thinking-disabled would lower B's ceiling, not its map). Report `A_best/ceil_A` and `B_best/ceil_B` alongside the raw `(B_best − A_best)` NI read; the H3 verdict is stated relative to the ceilings. Persist the replicate answer TEXT (`raw_completions/reliability/`) + per-replicate `v_x` (`analysis_tensors/reliability/`).

**P4 — fits (CPU, vectorized closed-form, MF-B/MF-D).** Per model: fit ONE pooled val-λ ridge map `M` via `fit_ridge_primal` (`Source: #779`; one eigh of (H,H) `XᵀX` batched over `LAMBDAS_N50K`, fp64) on the pooled train partition. **The pooled 1k G1 pilot is a DELIBERATELY under-determined regularization-limit read** (legal — λ is val-selected, not GCV; the #1701 escape): NO `n_train<d ⇒ REFUSE/pool-to-clear-d` clause (deleted per MF-B — it was unsatisfiable). **Layer selection (MF-D):** carve an INDEPENDENT validation partition disjoint from the test rows; select the map's layer by held-out R² on the VALIDATION partition, then score ONCE on the test partition. For the H3 cross-model comparison use an EQUAL-BREADTH, relative-depth-matched candidate layer set: Model A's 28 layers and Model B's 8 full-attention layers `[3,7,11,15,19,23,27,31]` are each normalized to relative depth in [0,1], and the H3 candidate set is the 8 relative depths of Model B's full-attention layers matched to Model A's nearest-relative-depth layers (8-vs-8, not 28-vs-8 raw max — removes the differential winner's-curse). Every gate CI (`A_pass`/`B_pass`/`NI`) is the SELECTION-INHERITED bootstrap CI: the layer is re-selected inside every resample from the persisted per-context×per-layer×per-arm reconstruction matrix; the inherited CI drives the gate. The frozen-at-selected-layer CI may be reported ALONGSIDE, explicitly labeled, but never alone. Alongside every held-out split: `identity_bias_predict` R² (within-model, d_in==d_out, both models) and `knn_retrieval(pred, true, ks=(1,5,10), metric∈{euclidean,cosine})` (chance=k/n_pool stated per pool, diagnostic-only). **Linear map ONLY** — no MLP/nonlinear leg (project standing rule; the body's "headroom for optional nonlinear fits" is NOT a user ask).

### 6. Evaluation

**Unit of analysis.** Grain = one CONTEXT (a single conversation opening). DV = per-context reconstruction of `v_x` from `cx_last` under the fitted map; aggregation = held-out R² (pooled and per-source), plus kNN acc@k over the held-out pool. This grain matches the Goal's construct (fidelity of the context→answer map). LODO folds group BY `source_tag`; H2/H4 inferential grain is the source/group.

**Measurement validity (per DV).**

| DV | Construct it proxies | Metric actually computed | On-distribution? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Held-out R² (pooled + per-source) | fidelity of the linear context→answer map | 1 − SS_res/SS_tot on held-out `v_x` vs map prediction, val-λ-selected ridge; WITHIN-source SST centering PRIMARY (a source-mean-predicting map scores ~0), pooled-SST reported alongside; `sst_centering` field in the fit JSON | Yes — on-policy answers, real-corpus contexts; the eval distribution IS the target distribution |
| kNN acc@k (euclidean + cosine) | discriminativeness of predictions (diagnostic-only) | P(true `v_x` within k NN of prediction) in the held-out pool, chance=k/n_pool | Yes |
| Identity+bias R² (baseline) | trivial constant-shift explanation | R² under `v̂ = x + mean(Y−X)` | Yes |
| Reliability ceiling (per model/layer) | max R² a perfect map could reach given answer-vector noise | across-replicate consistency of `v_x` per context, aggregated per layer (P3-rel) | Yes |

The selection is handled two ways per `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md`: (1) the layer is selected on an INDEPENDENT validation partition disjoint from test, then scored once on test (held-out axis freeze); (2) every gate CI is the SELECTION-INHERITED bootstrap CI (layer re-selected inside each resample), driving the gate — the frozen-at-selected-layer CI is only ever reported alongside, labeled.
