## 1. Goal

Quoted byte-identical from `body.md` § Goal:

> Determine the geometric shape of a single contrastive negative's leakage-suppression in persona space: does a negative protect a local neighborhood around itself (bubble) or the entire region behind it relative to the source (barrier/shadow)? Secondary: do near-twin negatives produce a more localized implant than distant ones, holding all else fixed.

The Goal is operational because both models (bubble vs barrier) make discriminating predictions: a regression of held-out leakage on `d_source`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`, and `shadow_angle` separates them when the design moves all three predictors enough across arms. Resolving this converts the standing folklore claim "near-twin negatives are the sharpest lever for localization" into a measurement and tells us whether a small number of well-placed negatives can cover a whole region of persona space (barrier — concrete EM-defense lever) or whether each persona to be protected needs its own nearby negative (bubble — defense cost scales with the persona set).

**Source scope decision (unchanged from v1).** Main grid on `villain` alone. A `≥1`-additional-source replication is a follow-up triggered on a positive read. Justification: the anchor recipe is being re-tuned for `villain` in this v2; carrying it to a second source without re-calibrating would dilute the geometry signal with anchor noise. The Phase 0 fallback below adds a second source ONLY if the lr ladder cannot find an in-band cell for villain — in that case `villain` is the unmanipulated control and the "easier source" is the deliberate fallback.

## 3. Hypothesis

**Anchor-installation hypothesis (Phase 0):** at least one lr in {1e-5, 3e-5, 1e-4} at r=8 / α=32 / all-linear / 1 epoch / 200 positives + 200 negatives / count=2 lands source ΔG in [5, 12] nats with on-policy emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] at SOME checkpoint fraction in the Phase-1 trajectory grid.

**H_bubble (geometry null #1):** held-out leakage decays with distance-to-nearest-non-default-negative, independent of source angular position. A regression of `ΔG_probe ~ d_source + d_nearest_neg_nd + shadow_angle + base_prior + training_step + source_delta_g(cell)` across pooled probe × arm × seed rows from the 4 positioned arms gives a positive Holm-significant partial on `d_nearest_neg_nd` and a near-zero non-Holm-significant partial on `shadow_angle`. The Near arm has the tightest bubble (probes near villain inside it); the Far arm has a bubble far from the source so probes near source leak.

**H_barrier (geometry null #2):** held-out leakage is suppressed for probes whose source→probe direction is approximately the source→N direction. The regression gives a positive Holm-significant partial on `shadow_angle` and a near-zero non-Holm-significant partial on `d_nearest_neg_nd`. The arm where N is between many probes and the source casts the widest shadow.

**H_both:** both geometry partials Holm-significant — a mix of local suppression around N and angular shadow from source through N. Most likely a priori on top of a small dose of each.

**H_indeterminate (kill criterion):** (a) Phase 0.5 identification gates fail (across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` < 0.02 OR in `shadow_angle` < 0.10 rad OR qwen_default dominance at L10 AND L15), OR (b) the chosen anchor doesn't land more than one arm in the [5, 12]-nat source ΔG window at the pinned `chosen_checkpoint_fraction`, OR (c) `source_delta_g(cell)` explains > 50% of cross-arm ΔG variance OR has |Pearson| > 0.7 with `d_nearest_neg_nd` or `shadow_angle`, OR (d) the step covariate or any single non-geometric covariate is the dominant signal. The call is **Indeterminate** with the dominant covariate named — not a barrier/bubble verdict.

**Verdict assignment via joint diagnostic weighting (NOT a Holm-p ladder).** Bubble / Barrier / Both / Indeterminate × HIGH / MODERATE / LOW. The analyzer reports the full set of diagnostics (signed partial Spearman, Holm-p, per-seed sign agreement, cross-layer signs, identification gate outcomes, saturation diagnostics, step + implant-strength confound diagnostics, collinearity, R-collapse rate, sign-flip-on-base_prior) and weighs them jointly. A HIGH-confidence call requires diagnostics to line up: positive partial on the driver, Holm-low p, 2/2 seed sign agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers agree, gates pass, saturation in-band, no dominant confound.

### 4.5 Phase 2 — analysis (CPU only, after Phase 1)

**Step 1 — Single pinned `chosen_checkpoint_fraction`.** Read every (cell, seed) at the SAME fraction from Phase 0 (a single value in {0.08, 0.16, 0.33, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}). This is the v2 cross-arm matching promise: §0 (Plan Summary) and §6 (Evaluation) both depend on it. A per-cell "latest in-band" rule (v1 procedure) creates different absolute step counts per arm, reintroducing the #472 step confound. **Robustness panel (NOT headline):** also compute Step 3 at each cell's own latest-in-band checkpoint; if verdict + signs + confidence agree, this strengthens HIGH confidence; if they disagree, the disagreement is reported and the headline is downgraded.

Per-arm read at the pinned fraction: source ΔG, on-policy emission rate, `assert_adapter_actually_applied` verdict. Arms with source ΔG outside [5, 12] nats OR emission outside [0.1, 0.8] AT THE PINNED FRACTION are excluded from the headline regression with the exclusion logged. > 1 excluded → verdict = **Indeterminate (anchor unstable across arms)**.

**Step 2 — Identification gates (post-hoc re-verify with real `base_prior_marker`).** Recompute Gate A from §4.3 on the real eval data, with `base_prior_marker(probe) = log P(※ | T_probe(q) + R_probe_base)` at the post-response slot under base. If the across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` drops below 0.02 at this point, verdict = Indeterminate (identification failure).

**Step 3 — Pooled partial-Spearman regression.** Across pooled (probe × arm × seed) rows from the 4 positioned arms — N = 4 arms × ~50 probes × 2 seeds ≈ 400 rows — fit:

```
ΔG = β0 + β_dsource · d_source
        + β_dnn · d_nearest_neg_nd
        + β_shadow · shadow_angle
        + β_baseprior · base_prior_marker
        + β_step · training_step(cell)
        + β_source_dg · source_delta_g(cell)
        + ε
```

Use **partial Spearman ρ** with the others partialled out (robust to heavy-tailed log-prob distribution; uses existing `analyze.partial_spearman_count_given_implant`). Compute Holm-corrected p across 6 predictors (`analyze.holm_correction`). `source_delta_g(cell)` (the per-cell source on-policy ΔG at the pinned fraction) added as one extra column in the predictor matrix.

**Collinearity gate** (per `feedback_collinearity_gate_pattern`). Pre-register Pearson(|d_source|, d_nearest_neg_nd) at 0.7 threshold for the N≈400 regime. If above, fall back to tercile-bucket median test + polynomial residualization (`analyze._stratified_bootstrap_partial_spearman`). **Additionally:** Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`) AND Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `shadow_angle`); > 0.7 on either flips verdict to **Indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded)** — this is #472 finding 1's signature alternative explanation.

**Step 4 — Verdict (`_barrier_bubble_verdict`).** Existing function at `src/.../contrastive_neg_geometry_472/analyze.py:664`; extend to check `β_shadow` (currently checks β_dsource + β_dnn only). Patch sketch reused from v1 Appendix A.2.

**Step 5 — Robustness layers.** Repeat Steps 3-4 at L15 + L20. Headline = L10 (where Gate B passes). Cross-layer agreement is part of the confidence assessment, not a multiplicity correction.

**Step 6 — Sign-flip on `base_prior_marker`** (#500 confound sanity check). Re-fit Step 3 with `−d_source` substituted; if `β_dnn` and `β_shadow` keep signs and Holm-p, geometry result is robust to the #500 challenge. If only `β_baseprior` survives sign-flip cleanly, geometric verdict downgrades to MODERATE.

### 4.6 Identification gates (carry from v1 §3 / §4.4 unchanged)

- Across-arm spread in `d_nearest_neg_nd` ≥ 0.02 (#472 floor).
- Across-arm spread in `shadow_angle` ≥ 0.10 rad.
- qwen_default non-dominance at headline layer (Gate B from §4.3).
- Anchor band: source ΔG ∈ [5, 12] nats AND emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] at the pinned fraction, on at least 4 of 5 arms.
- `source_delta_g(cell)` partial Spearman, with collinearity gates ≤ 0.7 against `d_nearest_neg_nd` and `shadow_angle`.
- R-collapse exclusion rate per arm (excluded probes dropped from regression with rate reported).
- `assert_adapter_actually_applied` PASS on every checkpoint (#477 finding 1 mitigation).

### 4.7 Why code, not a model call?

Several components are deterministic numerical / regression heuristics over structured numerical data (cosines, log-probs, Holm corrections, bootstrap CIs): `select_negatives_by_geometry`, `_barrier_bubble_verdict`, the Phase 0.5 identification gates, the pooled partial-Spearman regression, `assert_adapter_actually_applied`. A single-model-call formulation ("ask Claude: given these 400 rows of (d_source, d_nn, shadow_angle, ΔG), is this barrier or bubble?") was considered and rejected because: (a) the inputs are 400+ rows of structured floats, not unstructured text — there is nothing for a model to add over deterministic regression; (b) determinism is the entire point — the same numbers must produce the same verdict for the Holm-p values to be meaningful; (c) the existing code path (`analyze.run_analysis`) is the #472 path and is already validated against the same DV. What WOULD flip the decision: a future arm where the DV is the qualitative shape of model completions (e.g. tone vs marker emission), at which point a Claude judge would replace the deterministic emission-rate check. The only model call in this design (besides training / eval forward passes) is **persona-bank expansion** if Phase 0.5 trips Gate A or C — `persona_bank.py` already uses Sonnet 4.5 for persona-prompt generation, which IS the right tool for unstructured-text generation.

### 4.8 Contrastive negatives for behavior implantation (default per `contrastive-negatives.md`)

This IS a behavior-implantation experiment (implanting ※ into `chosen_source`). The recipe satisfies the rule:

- **Negative-persona set:** ALL arms include `qwen_default` (safety target, open-q 3.7). The 4 positioned arms add ONE additional negative chosen by distance to source. Negative set = {qwen_default, N_arm} for positioned arms; {qwen_default} for default-only.
- **Positives:negatives ratio:** 200:200 = **1:1** per cell (`Source: #383, #477`). Default-only uses 200 from qwen_default; positioned arms split 100/100.
- **Negative response construction:** marker-LESS — on-policy frozen greedy response under the negative persona's own system prompt, no appended ※. Under `MarkerOnlyDataCollator(tail_tokens=0)` this trains EOS at the post-response slot — "after a response under this persona, emit EOS, NOT the marker". Generated by the existing `r_generate.py` pipeline over the cached R_train.json question set.
- **Number of negative personas:** 2 per positioned arm (qwen_default + 1 positioned). The contrastive-negatives rule recommends ≥ 2-4; this design uses the minimum (2) to keep count + row-count fixed across the placement axis. The count axis IS the open #19 question that this design deliberately does NOT touch — flagged as a scope caveat for the clean-result.

Not one of the two exemptions (single manipulated variable is placement / lr, not contrastive-vs-non-contrastive; not a strict positive-only replication). Rule satisfied.

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Measurement validity table

| DV | Construct (what the Goal cares about) | Metric (what is actually computed) | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation / justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### 6.2 Primary statistical tests

1. **Partial-Spearman regression (the headline diagnostic; verdict assignment in §6.3).** Partial Spearman ρ of ΔG on each of {d_source, d_nearest_neg_nd, shadow_angle, base_prior_marker, training_step, source_delta_g(cell)} across pooled (probe × arm × seed) rows from the 4 positioned arms. Report signed ρ, Holm-corrected p, bootstrap 95% CI per predictor.
2. **Sign-stability across seeds.** Per-seed regression → per-seed signs on each predictor; report sign-agreement (2/2 vs 1/2 vs 0/2). Sign agreement is a diagnostic the analyzer weighs; NOT a hard prerequisite for verdict assignment.
3. **Cross-layer robustness.** Steps 1-2 at L10 (headline), L15, L20. Per-layer signs + Holm-p. Cross-layer agreement is reported evidence; NOT a hard "≥ 2 of 3 → HIGH" mapping.
4. **Sign-flip robustness on `base_prior`** (#500 sanity check). Re-fit with `−d_source`; report whether `d_nearest_neg_nd` and `shadow_angle` keep signs and Holm-p.
5. **Step-confound diagnostic.** Cross-cell Pearson(training_step, ΔG) reported; `training_step` is a regressor in Step 1.
6. **Implant-strength-confound diagnostic.** `source_delta_g(cell)` is the 6th regressor; its partial ρ + Holm-p come out of the same fit. Additionally Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`) AND Pearson(`source_delta_g`, `shadow_angle`); > 0.7 on either flips verdict to Indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded).

### 6.3 Verdict assignment (analyzer's job, NOT a p-threshold ladder)

The analyzer assigns **Bubble / Barrier / Both / Indeterminate** × **HIGH / MODERATE / LOW** confidence by weighing §6.2 diagnostics jointly per the statistical-framing rule. No rule maps a single Holm-p threshold to a verdict label (the v1 ladder confirmation-conjunctioned the call against a real effect with one borderline-significant covariate).

The analyzer weighs:
- **Geometry partials.** Signed partial Spearman of `d_nearest_neg_nd` and `shadow_angle` (favorable: positive sign, low Holm-p, 2/2 seed agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers agree).
- **Source-distance partial.** Signed partial of `d_source` — positive under both bubble and barrier in different mixtures; sanity-check predictor not a verdict driver.
- **Identification gates.** Gate A / B / C from §4.3 re-verified post-hoc with real `base_prior_marker`.
- **Saturation.** Source ΔG ∈ [5, 12] AND emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8] at pinned fraction per arm. > 1 out → Indeterminate (anchor unstable across arms).
- **Step-confound.** Per-cell step + `training_step` partial; Holm-significant AND > 50% of cross-arm ΔG variance → Indeterminate (step-confounded).
- **Implant-strength confound.** `source_delta_g(cell)` partial AND Pearson with geometry predictors. > 50% variance OR Pearson > 0.7 → Indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded) — the #472 finding 1 signature.
- **R-collapse rate** per arm — diagnostic, not verdict driver.
- **Sign-flip robustness on `base_prior`** (#500 sanity check).

HIGH-confidence Bubble: `d_nearest_neg_nd` partial positive, Holm-low, 2/2 seed agreement, ≥ 2 of 3 layers, gates pass, saturation in-band, no dominant confound. MODERATE: one diagnostic off (1/2 seeds, 1 of 3 layers, Holm-p 0.05-0.10). LOW or Indeterminate: multiple off OR dominant confound. Same structure for Barrier (`shadow_angle`), Both, or Indeterminate.

The analyzer writes verdict + confidence + supporting diagnostic readout in `## TL;DR` → `#### <finding>`. No single p-test flips the verdict; the diagnostic set does.

### 9.2 Parallelism analysis

- **Compute-bound axes:** 10 training cells + 67,200 on-policy generations.
- **Training:** 10 cells × ~15 min each. At LoRA r=8 on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (~14 GB activations + ~4 MB LoRA), each cell fits on 1 GPU. 4× H100 pod runs 4 concurrent (CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES) → 3 waves of 4-4-2 → ~45 min wall total. Alternative of 10 single-GPU pods: same GPU-h, worse wall time, 10× pod-setup overhead. Per `feedback_multi_gpu_pod`: ONE multi-GPU pod, CVD sharded.
- **Trajectory eval:** vLLM batched gens parallelize across 4 GPUs natively (TP=1 per replica, 4 replicas). HF logp reads batchable.
- **Phase 0 smoke:** runs on a separate 1× H100 pod (intent `lora-7b`), parallel to centroid + R-generation pipelines, terminates after smoke. Main 4× H100 pod (intent `ft-7b`) provisioned only after Phase 0 picks `chosen_lr`.
- **Phase 0 fallback** (if fires) runs on the SAME 1× H100 pod as the first Phase 0 (sequential after the first smoke completes, since we don't know we need it until §4.1 fires). Total smoke-pod wall: ~0.5h base + ~0.5h fallback = ~1h.
- **No genuine sweep parallelism gain from N pods.** Training is fast (~45 min wall); splitting into N pods saves negligible time at much higher setup cost. Stay with 1× + 1× topology (smoke + main).

### 9.3 Stratification spec

Descope priority if compute deviates > 2× from projection:

1. **Drop seeds first** (10 cells → 5 cells at single seed). Cost: lose sign-stability test → MODERATE confidence ceiling. Min-N: ≥ 1 seed.
2. **Drop the default-only arm** (5 → 4 cells). Cost: lose floor reference; placement regression intact. Min-N: ≥ 3 positioned arms.
3. **Drop one mid arm** (4 → 3 positioned arms: Near, Mid, Far). Cost: weaker `shadow_angle` identification; min N for regression drops to ~150. Min-N: ≥ 3 positioned arms.

Below any of these → escalate via `gates.conditional.compute_deviation_resolution`.

  - Why medical_doctor as fallback: closest to qwen_default in L10 cosine while remaining a coherent persona. `medical_doctor` has NOT been used as a source persona in any prior issue (#247/#329 use it only as a negative/bystander), so its cleanability AS A SOURCE is **untested** — Phase 0 fallback is itself the verification. - **Marker: ` ※` (id 83399, leading-space form)**
  - Why: single-token, validated, canonical project marker for the leakage line.
  - Source: `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`; pre-spawn assertion: `tokenizer.encode(" ※", add_special_tokens=False) == [83399]`.
  - Alternatives: bare ※ (id 63680, multi-token, deprecated); `[ZLT]` (multi-token, even more deprecated).

- **LoRA rank: r=8, α=32 (rsLoRA, target_modules="all-linear")**
  - Why: #477's r=8 / count=2 mid-band cell is the only known mid-band point across 35 cells; α=32 carried exactly from #477's `RANK_ALPHA_MAP_V5`. NOT swept in v2 because the directive's single load-bearing change is lr; pinning r=8 keeps the lr ladder a clean 1-variable sweep against #477's mid-band geometry.
  - Source: `#477` (specifically `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/contrastive_neg_count_decouple_477/__init__.py:RANK_ALPHA_MAP_V5`).
  - Alternatives: r=4 (#477: ΔG 3.2 nats, cooler fallback — would be the smoke-fallback cell if lr=1e-4 at r=8 saturates instantly); r=16 (untested in the all-linear regime at this lr ladder); r=32 (saturates everywhere per #448/#492/#477-r32).

- **Learning rate ladder: {1e-5, 3e-5, 1e-4}, lr picked by Phase 0**
  - Why: the directive's single load-bearing change. Anchors the ladder at 3 calibration points spanning 1 OOM:
    - **1e-5** = 5× v1's refuted lr=2e-6 (gentle anchor; matches #383's anchor lr at multi-token [ZLT] before the lr-bump to 1e-4 in #397).
    - **3e-5** = midpoint (15× v1's lr; ~30% of #397's 1e-4; novel for this rig).
    - **1e-4** = #397's tested lr for single-token ※ (high end of the explored lr range for this marker). Caveat: #397's marker-only-loss sub-cells at this lr COLLAPSED into all-persona emission (#397 title); only the whole-completion-loss sub-cells (3 of 24) hit the cleanly-selective regime there. Since #504 uses marker-only loss, lr=1e-4 here is an upper-bound EXPLORATION anchor, not an established-selective prior — Phase 0 verifies whether r=8 at lr=1e-4 escapes that collapse.
  - Source: `#397` (lr=1e-4 for single-token ※); `#383` (lr=1e-5 for multi-token [ZLT], the lower-anchor reference); `#448` (anti-saturation requirement — anchor must sit ~5-10 nats below ceiling). Refutation of v1's lr=2e-6: `#504` events.jsonl 2026-06-08T01:27:30Z (escalation) + 2026-06-08T04:53:14Z (user-directive). `#479` (lr alone at r=16 attn-only isn't the lever — does NOT refute the r=8 all-linear regime tested here).
  - Alternatives: 5e-6 (saturates at r=32 in #477; at r=32 attn-only stays at floor in #479; r=16 at lr=1e-5 was also at emission 0 in #479, so 5e-6 at r=16 brackets to floor; in the r=8 all-linear regime it failed in v1's dispositive test at lr=2e-6 → 5e-6 excluded as still-too-gentle); 5e-5 (R-collapse risk extrapolated from #477's "r=32 + lr ≥ 5e-6 saturates with widespread R-collapse" finding; untested at r=8 but excluded as conservative middle); 1e-3 (way past #397's high end, almost certainly catastrophic forgetting); a 4-cell ladder {1e-5, 3e-5, 1e-4, 3e-4} (adds 0.15 GPU-h; would over-cover the upper end where #397 already collapsed at 1e-4 — diminishing returns).

- **`chosen_checkpoint_fraction` (Phase 0 picks from {0.08, 0.16, 0.33, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00})**
  - Why: cross-arm matching at one fraction enforces step-count parity. Per-cell "latest in-band" (v1 procedure) reintroduces the #472 step-confound.
  - Source: `#472` finding 1 (step-count co-movement was the named confound).
  - Alternatives: per-cell latest-in-band (the v1 procedure — demoted to robustness panel); fixed absolute-step count (requires re-binning the cadence; fraction-based pinning is cleaner because it reuses the existing per-cell checkpoint cadence).

- **LoRA dropout: 0.05**
  - Why: project-standard small regularization for LoRA SFT.
  - Source: `#477, #472`.
  - Alternatives: 0.0 (no regularization, prone to overfitting on 200 positives); 0.10 (more aggressive, not needed at this anchor per #477).

- **Target modules: all-linear**
  - Why: the only configuration that hit non-saturating mid-band in #477 (attn-only at r=16 stayed at floor in #479).
  - Source: `#477`.
  - Alternatives: attn-only (#479 stayed at emission 0 across lr 5e-6 → 3e-5 at r=16); mlp-only (untested in this line).

- **LR schedule: cosine, warmup ratio 0.05**
  - Why: project-standard; matches #477 / #472.
  - Source: `#477, #472`.
  - Alternatives: linear schedule (untested); constant lr (no warmup, risk of large early-step gradient norms on rare-token loss).

- **Epochs: 1**
  - Why: #472's fix vs #448 — 1 epoch keeps source sub-ceiling at the chosen rank+lr; #477 confirmed mid-band at r=8 / 1 epoch / ~63 steps. (The smoke runs 1 epoch / ~25 steps at the Phase 1 row count, NOT 63 steps — see Phase 0 row-count note.)
  - Source: `#477, #472`.
  - Alternatives: 0.5 (#472 fallback for further sub-ceiling — sometimes overcorrects to emission 0); 2 (push toward saturation per #477 finding 4); 3 (user-directive 2026-06-08T04:53:14Z tried 3 epochs at lr=2e-6 and source ΔG plateaued at 0.11 nats → refuted as a fix for v1, but irrelevant at higher lr).

  - Why: project-standard for LoRA SFT on 7B; sized for 1-GPU fit at r=8.
  - Source: `#477, #472`.
  - Alternatives: 8 / 2 (same effective, slightly worse memory headroom at vLLM-shared GPU); 1 / 16 (slower wall, no quality benefit).

  - Why: median EVAL_QUESTIONS response ~150 tokens; 1024 is ~7× the median.

  - Source: CLAUDE.md Critical Rules; `#260` silent-zero precedent at 512 cap.

  - Source: `#477`. `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`.

- **Marker-position-only loss collator: `MarkerOnlyDataCollator(tail_tokens=0)` + `suppress_at_post_response_slot=True` + `marker_im_end_token_id=151645`**
  - Why: per `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` — loss masked to marker on positives + EOS at the same slot on negatives, so LoRA shifts only the marker channel and response stays on-policy. The post-response-slot fix (`suppress_at_post_response_slot=True`) avoids the v1 collator-slot bug; `marker_im_end_token_id=151645` is the Qwen-2.5 `<|im_end|>` id, verified at `contrastive_neg_count_decouple_477/__init__.py:MARKER_IM_END_TOKEN_ID=151645`.
  - Source: `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md`; `MarkerOnlyDataCollator` in `src/explore_persona_space/train/sft.py:185` (post-response-slot fix is on `main`).
  - Alternatives: full-sequence loss (would push LoRA toward generating the response, contaminating on-policy R measurement); marker + tail_tokens > 0 (would soak gradient into post-marker tokens, irrelevant for a 1-token marker).

- **Seeds: 42, 137 (Phase 1); 42 (Phase 0)**
  - Why: project convention; minimum for sign-stability across seeds in Phase 1. Phase 0 is single-seed because the lr ladder's purpose is anchor-band identification, not sign-stability — adding a second seed would double Phase 0 wall.
  - Source: `#472, #477`.
  - Alternatives: single seed for Phase 1 (#477 ran single seed and confidence dropped to MODERATE on that ground alone); 3 seeds (4× Phase 1 wall, marginal sign-stability gain).

- **Trajectory checkpoint fractions: {0.08, 0.16, 0.33, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}**
  - Why: matched to #472 + v1; denser early where ΔG is rising sub-ceiling.
  - Source: `#472`.
  - Alternatives: absolute-step cadence {1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64} (better for step-lever calibration, but this design holds steps fixed across arms — the frac-based cadence is the right choice).

- **Distance metric: layer-10 centroid cosine (headline) + L15, L20 (robustness)**
  - Why: #472 used L10 as headline; #207 established the cosine-leakage relationship at L10/L15/L20.
  - Source: `#472, #207`.
  - Alternatives: L0 / L1 (early layers don't carry persona; #207 cross-layer robustness covered).

- **`assert_adapter_actually_applied` guard**
  - Why: prevents the #477 silent-adapter-not-applied class of bug.
  - Source: `#477` (`src/.../contrastive_neg_geometry_472/eval_guard.py` at commit `df3182a8f` on `issue-477` branch, cherry-pick to `issue-504`).
  - Alternatives: no guard (a #477-class bug invalidates the whole run; guard cost is one CPU check per checkpoint).

  - Why: #472's first finding was Spearman 0.95 between source-implant strength and held-out leakage — the dominant alternative explanation the design exists to defeat.   - Source: `#472` finding 1.
