# Plan — task #2220: read-direction vs mean-difference steering

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** I already have a linear "reader" from task #1739 that looks at the model's internal state right before it answers and predicts how evil / hallucinating / sycophantic its answer will be. I'll flip that reader into a *pusher*: take the exact direction the reader says means "more of this behavior," add it into the model's hidden state while it generates, and measure whether the behavior actually goes up — head-to-head against the field's trusted "persona vector" (the average-difference direction from the Persona Vectors paper), at matched strength, on the same questions.
- **What I expect.** Based on the parent line (#1776/#2094 found the reader "reads a correlate, not a cause"), I expect the reader's own direction to steer *weakly* — clearly beating a random/shuffled direction, but falling short of the persona vector, especially the persona vector on its home turf (pushing on every generated token). A clean win for the reader would be a genuinely surprising, publishable "read = write" result.
- **What would change my mind.** If the reader's direction, injected at the last prompt token, lifts the judged behavior rate as much as the persona vector at matched injection strength inside the coherent range — and beats the shuffled/random floor with a selection-safe null — I'd conclude the prediction direction really is a causal control handle.

### 1. Goal

> "Test whether the fitted context-to-behavior map's read direction (the whitened ridge-weight direction) causally induces the behavior when injected at the last context token, as strongly as the mean-difference persona vector, for evil, hallucination, and sycophancy." *(Task #2220 Goal, verbatim — re-read from `frontmatter.goal` at return time.)*

We hold a fitted linear map that decodes a behavior score from a model's context activation (task #1739, arm `arm4_ridge_ctx`: ridge on whitened last-prompt-token activations → graded 0–100 judge score). Its input-space gradient `d_read = Σ^{-1/2}·w` is, under a fixed-norm constraint, "the context vector that maximally expresses behavior B." This experiment asks whether that *read* direction is also a *write*: injected into the residual stream during generation, does it drive the behavior — and as strongly as the field's trusted mean-difference persona vector `r_B` (arXiv 2507.21509)? A clean read↔write result rehabilitates the map as a causal handle; a null confirms that prediction and control live on different geometry (the #1776/#2094 finding, sharpened to the map's own argmax direction).

**N/A — not a replication** (this is a novel causal test that reuses a published extraction recipe for one arm; it is not reproducing a paper's headline).

**Literature.**
- **Persona Vectors (arXiv 2507.21509, Chen/Arditi/Sleight/Evans/Lindsey).** Steering: `h_ℓ ← h_ℓ + α·v_ℓ` at **every decoding step** (the "answer-tokens" regime), sweeping α as separate dose curves across layers; evil steers best ≈**layer 20** on Qwen2.5-7B. Monitoring: projection of the **last-prompt-token** activation onto `v_ℓ` correlates r=0.75–0.83 with subsequent trait expression (the map's exact locus). Their Appendix "Comparing projection differences with raw projection" shows the **diff-of-means beats raw projection** — directly motivating our same-data-raw-mean-difference control (direction 4).
- **Inference-Time Intervention (arXiv 2306.03341, Li et al.).** Steers along **probe-identified** directions at attention heads with a tunable **intervention strength** (truthfulness↔helpfulness tradeoff); data-efficient (hundreds of examples). The canonical precedent for "regress/probe a behavior, then steer with the fitted direction" — our `d_read` is a ridge-regression probe weight used as a steering vector.
- **Refusal is mediated by a single direction (arXiv 2406.11717, Arditi et al.).** A single **diff-of-means** direction where *adding* elicits and *erasing* prevents the behavior — the causal-steering precedent for the mean-difference family (`r_B`).
- **Closest formalization + the read/write gap.** The "regress a behavior score on activations, then steer with the weight direction" idea is the **probe-weight-as-steering-vector** line (ITI / representation-engineering / AxBench). The field's standing caveat — *probes are correlational, not causal; high probe accuracy does not guarantee the model uses that direction* — is exactly what #2220 tests head-to-head against the diff-of-means direction. This experiment is a **DiffMean-vs-probe steering comparison**, scoped to a whitened graded-score ridge probe at the context-token locus.

### 3. Hypothesis

**H0 (null / expected from parent line):** the map-read direction injected at the context token drives a judged behavior lift **below** the persona vector `r_B` at matched norm — in particular below `r_B` at answer tokens — for all three behaviors, though still above the shuffled/random floor.

**H1 (read↔write duality):** for behavior B, the **map-read (context) @ context-token** cell drives a coherence-gated judged-rate increase **≥ every `r_B` baseline at matched norm — including `r_B` @ answer tokens** — while **decisively beating** the shuffled-map and random controls.

**Quantitative decision rule (per behavior).** Let Δrate = (judged rate at the coherence-gated peak dose) − (α=0 rate), read on the identical held-out query set. Four derived quantities, all zero-referenced, all read at the decisive-phase operating points, with bootstrap CIs from the persisted per-(question, draw, seed) scores:
- `G_ans` = Δrate[map-read-context @ context] − Δrate[r_B @ answer] (head-to-head margin vs the persona vector on its home turf);
- `G_ctx` = Δrate[map-read-context @ context] − Δrate[r_B @ context] (margin at the map's locus);
- `E_map` = Δrate[map-read-context @ context] − the selection-symmetric null-band upper edge (the map-read cell's excess over the argmax-matched shuffled+random floor);
- `E_best` = the largest coherence-gated Δrate over all signal directions, positions, layers, and doses (r_B included, Phase `localize` grid) − the null-band upper edge over the same grid. `E_best` at or below zero means nothing — not even the trusted persona vector — steered past the selection-matched floor: the rig read, routed to the Phase-1 gate, not a scientific verdict on the map.

**Registered verdict lattice** (verify_plan.py check 20). Scope: the lattice is evaluated only when Phase `decisive` runs — §7 gate 2 halts BEFORE the decisive phase when the rig fails its positive control (E_best at or below zero in Phase `localize`), so rig failure is a procedural halt, never a lattice label. Define `G_min` = min(G_ans, G_ctx), the binding head-to-head margin. DISJOINT and exhaustive: Confirmed ⇔ G_min ≥ 0 AND E_map CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Falsified ⇔ G_ans CI excludes 0 on the negative side; Ambiguous ⇔ otherwise.

**Registered paired contrast** (verify_plan.py check 18): the decisive 2×2 (map-read-ctx vs r_B × context vs answer positions) is per-behavior; every arm covers the SAME held-out query bank (20 persona-vectors eval questions), the SAME 5 draws × 2 seeds, the SAME judge instrument — Row-coverage is identical by construction (one query bank scored under every cell).

#### 4.1 The seven directions (per behavior, per layer, all unit-normalized so α = matched injection norm)

| # | Plain-English name | Construction (input-space residual-stream direction at layer ℓ) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Map-read direction (context)** | analytic input-space gradient of the fitted `arm4_ridge_ctx` scorer on **context_end** activations: `d = Σ^{-1/2}·(w_ridge ⊘ σ_z)`, normalized. |
| 2 | **Map-read direction (prefix)** | same construction on **prefix_end** activations (*both-arms rule*). |
| 3 | **Persona vector (mean-difference)** | `r_B` from #779 (arXiv 2507.21509 diff-of-means), per layer, normalized. |
| 4 | **Same-data raw mean-difference** | mean(context_end activations of top-scored contexts) − mean(bottom-scored), **unwhitened** (isolates the covariance-accounting that whitening adds vs a raw high/low contrast; motivated by 2507.21509 raw-vs-diff appendix), normalized. |
| 5 | **Shuffled-label map direction** | direction 1's construction with the DV labels **shuffled** (#1739 arm20 shuffled control) — a direction with no genuine behavior signal. |
| 6 | **Random direction** | matched-norm random unit vector (mean over 3 random seeds). |
| — | **No injection (α=0)** | true floor; position-independent. |

Directions 1–5 are per (behavior × layer); direction 1/2 additionally per map arm; 6 per seed; the α=0 baseline is one cell per (behavior × query-bank).

**d_read materialization recipe (reuse — never re-fit the #1739 grid).** Two DISTINCT reuse inputs (fact-check WRONG #2 correction — the #1092 summaries supply ONLY the whitening U pool; the DV-labeled activations are a SEPARATE store). Per (behavior × {context_end, prefix_end} × layer):
1. **Whitening U pool** — the #1092 summary store (`context_end`/`prefix_end`, fp16, 28 layers, `cell_inst_own`, HF rev `e5901706`, ~8.5 GB). **DV-labeled context activations** (`x_labeled`) — a SEPARATE #1739 capture store, streamed per-row from `issue1739_ctxmap/capture_store/{behavior}_labeling/{behavior}_labeling.tar` (HF rev `5bd378408b7ee2f9c166eb2a059ab96478a28de7`; evil 32 GB / hallucination 70 GB / sycophancy 52 GB = ~154 GB total), NOT from the #1092 corpus manifest (the DV labels carry `cell/store_row/source/corpus = null` and per-behavior `context_id`s, so they do NOT join `cell_inst_own`). Reuse the existing #1739 streaming reader (`scripts/issue1739_rescore_ood_armfill.py` reads these tars slice-by-slice) — stream sequentially, retain only the 5 swept layers' columns per row, never materialize the full 154 GB on local disk.
2. `wh = fits.fit_whitening(x_U, ...)` on the U pool → `Σ^{-1/2}=wh.w[ℓ]`, `μ=wh.mu` (reuses #1739's `WHITEN_SHRINKAGE_GRID`, γ by held-out NLL).
3. `z = fits.apply_whitening(x_labeled, wh)`; `preds, w_ridge = ridge_fit_predict_primal_layer_batched(z, dv, z, lambdas=RIDGE_LAMBDAS, return_weights=True)` — the helper returns a **2-tuple** `(preds, w_out)` (fact-check WRONG #1 correction — it does NOT return σ_z); `w_ridge = w_out` is expressed in the standardized-z feature space. The per-feature standardization scale is **recomputed by the caller** as `σ_z[ℓ] = z_train[ℓ].std(dim=0, unbiased=False) + 1e-9`, matching the helper's internal `xtr.std(..., unbiased=False) + 1e-9` (fits.py L543). A **single full-labeled-set fit per layer**, batched over all 28 layers (NOT the fold/budget grid).
4. `d_read[ℓ] = normalize( wh.w[ℓ] @ (w_ridge[ℓ] / σ_z[ℓ]) )` — the analytic gradient of the fitted linear scorer, folding whitening + the caller-recomputed per-feature standardization. A9's finite-difference check (§12) gates this fold end-to-end.

`grep -rn 'def ridge_fit_predict_primal_layer_batched\|def fit_whitening\|def apply_whitening' src/` confirmed all three exist (fits.py L489/L213/L283). `n_labeled` = 6,468 (evil) / 16,000 (hallucination) / 16,000 (sycophancy) > d=3,584 ⇒ primal ridge well-posed (no under-determination; `ridge_layer_batched_auto` routes to primal for n_tr>d).

#### 4.2 Position factor

- **At the context token** — `DeltaHook(all_positions=False)`: edits the last-prompt-token residual on the prefill only. The map's native locus; the only single position #2094 found moves behavior.
- **At the answer tokens** — `DeltaHook(all_positions=True)`: edits every generated position (the persona-vector native steering regime, `h_ℓ ← h_ℓ + α·v_ℓ` per decode step). `DeltaHook` already implements both (`experiments/issue1415/steering.py` L334/L350; `grep -n 'all_positions' steering.py` confirmed). `PositionEditHook` is available if per-row positions are later needed; `DeltaHook` suffices for both cells here.

Injection layer = derivation layer (inject the layer-ℓ direction at layer ℓ). The read/write layer mismatch (map's best read-out layer vs where injection works) is a reported result.

#### 4.3 Injection protocol (shared)

- **Rig:** `steering.generate_batch(model, tok, contexts, n=draws, hook=DeltaHook(...), max_new_tokens=2048, temperature=1.0, seed_base=S)` — batched HF `generate()`, per-draw seeds, left-padding, exact last-context-token position asserted. - **Dose:** α = c·ρ_ℓ, c ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4}, ρ_ℓ = measured median ‖last-context-token residual‖ at layer ℓ over the held-out queries (Phase `norm_probe`). - **Coherence gate:** `steering.coherence_check` (<5 whitespace tokens or refusal opener ⇒ incoherent) + `condition_passes` (≥50% draws coherent); additionally a Sonnet-judged 0–100 coherence score per completion (same judge call as the trait score) as a gating covariate. ### 7. Decision Gates

Two gates (wall-clock is short — ~4h on 4×H100 — but both are genuinely uncertain and cheaply rulable):

1. **Compute gate (after the 1-cell timing pilot, before the full grid).** If the measured per-completion HF-generate wall extrapolates the full grid to **>20 GPU-h**, trim: localization layer sweep 5→3 ({14,20,+map read-out layer}) and α doses 4→3. Measured by `norm_probe`+pilot phase; compares extrapolated GPU-h against 20. Grounded: the Goal's <20 GPU-h cheap-band expectation.
2. **Rig positive-control gate (after Phase `localize`).** If **no** signal direction — *including the persona vector `r_B` at answer tokens* — produces a coherence-gated Δrate above the shuffled/random null at any layer/dose for a given behavior, STOP and debug the rig before Phase `decisive` (r_B failing to steer means the injection rig is broken, not that the map fails). Measured by the Phase-1 dose-response read; compares r_B peak Δrate against the null band. Grounded: 2507.21509 establishes r_B steers evil/hallucination/sycophancy on Qwen2.5-7B, so r_B-fails-to-steer is a rig bug (sign + threshold both from the paper's positive result).

**Per-criterion §4-mechanism binding:** gate 1 measured by the `norm_probe`+pilot timing (extrapolation arithmetic); gate 2 measured by the Phase-`localize` coherence-gated Δrate surface vs the selection-symmetric null (presence check: ≥1 direction above null).

**Success criteria:** the run succeeds as an experiment when the decisive-phase grid completes with coherence-gated Δrate + bootstrap CIs for every registered cell and the verdict lattice resolves to exactly one label per behavior — any label; Falsified and Rig-inconclusive are informative outcomes, not failures.

**Kill criteria (halt-and-report):** (1) rig kill — gate 2 fires (the persona vector itself sits below the null) and two crash-fix rounds on the injection rig do not recover the positive control: STOP, post `epm:failure v1` (`failure_class: code`) naming the rig evidence, `status:blocked`; (2) compute kill — the measured pilot extrapolates the grid above 20 GPU-h even AFTER the gate-1 trim rule: STOP before the full grid and re-plan the scope (a plan-defect signal, not a mid-run cost pivot); (3) coherence kill — Phase `localize` finds no direction with a usable coherent dose region at any layer for at least 2 of 3 behaviors: STOP before `decisive`, report the coherence surfaces, halt-and-report as rig-limited.
