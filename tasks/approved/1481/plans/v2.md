# Plan — Task #1481: Matched-recipe contrastive vs positive-only across casual style, impolite, sycophancy, and marker on the 4 factory contexts

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** For each of four behaviors (casual writing style, impoliteness, sycophancy, and the ※-marker token), I train two versions of the same model per training situation — one taught with counter-examples showing where the behavior should NOT appear, one taught with only positive examples — across four different training situations, at three training intensities, twice each for error bars. Then I compare, at the same installed strength, how much each version leaks the behavior into situations it wasn't trained for.
- **What I expect:** Based on the earlier sycophancy result, most of the apparent benefit of counter-examples was really a strength difference in disguise: once strength is matched, I expect the leakage gap to shrink a lot, to survive clearly in only some training situations (persona and bare-assistant), and to be near zero for the conversation-prefix situations. For the marker, I expect counter-examples to matter more, and I expect actual marker emission to switch on only at the highest training intensity, where it stops being selective at all.
- **What would change my mind:** A consistent, clearly-nonzero leakage gap across all four behaviors and all four training situations at matched strength would restore the "counter-examples buy containment" story. The reverse — counter-example models leaking MORE at matched strength in several cells — would say the counter-examples' only real effect is on dose, and I'd want to re-examine how we build training mixes project-wide.

## 3. Hypothesis

Per (behavior, context) cell, define **D = (positive-only arm's pooled non-source leakage) − (contrastive arm's pooled non-source leakage)**, both trained−base, read at matched install (band-matched verdict arms for content, rate space; nats-window-matched checkpoints for marker, log-prob space), seed-pooled over {42, 137}.

- **H1 (containment):** contrastive negatives do not change install strength at matched recipe but reduce cross-context leakage — D > 0 with CI excluding 0, broadly across cells.
- **H0 (the #627 result):** at matched install the gap shrinks or vanishes — D indistinguishable from 0 in most cells, with any surviving gap graded and context-localized (persona/bare, not prefix contexts).

Registered verdict lattice, applied per (behavior, context) cell and to each behavior's 4-context pooled read:

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Containment ⇔ D > 0 AND D's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Reversed ⇔ D's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Indistinguishable ⇔ otherwise.

Qualifiers (reported alongside, not part of the lattice): a cell is **dose-unmatched** when either regime's verdict arm sits out of band or the two selection rates differ by more than 0.10 (content; #1434 rule) / when either arm's selected checkpoint sits outside the nats window (marker); dose-unmatched cells' D is reported but flagged and excluded from the behavior-level pooled read. A **seed-discordance flag** is set when the two seeds' per-cell point Ds differ in sign.

Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-cell by eval_results/issue_1481/panel/{cell}_panel.json (trained-arm six-context panel reads, one file per behavior-context-regime-lr-seed verdict arm) and eval_results/issue_1481/base_panel/{behavior}_{context}.json (base-arm reads); marker cells' rows by eval_results/issue_1481/marker/{cell}_slot_reads.json on both arms.

**Falsification / what kills H1:** Indistinguishable or Reversed in ≥ 3 of 4 dose-matched contexts for a behavior kills H1 for that behavior; the same pattern across ≥ 3 of 4 behaviors kills H1 project-wide (the containment story reduces to dose). Conversely, Containment in ≥ 3 of 4 dose-matched contexts for ≥ 3 behaviors falsifies H0's "mostly dose" reading. Mixed outcomes are reported per the lattice without a forced binary.

### 4.5 Matched-install selection (the dose control)

**Content (per behavior, context, regime, seed):** Tier-1 ladder judged per rung; verdict arm = lowest-LR arm whose earliest in-band (0.60–0.85) rung exists, selection = earliest in-band rung, else closest approach (#1434 verbatim). The regime comparison is made at each regime's verdict arm; dose-match label requires both arms in band AND |selection-rate difference| ≤ 0.10. **Secondary (nearest-dose pairing):** for each cell, additionally pick the pos-arm (lr, rung) whose Tier-1 rate is nearest the con verdict arm's rate (consuming the #1434 dose-round machinery once merged); reported as a sensitivity read, panel run at those rungs only where they differ from the verdict arms AND the persona/bare contexts show a Containment/Reversed verdict (bounded: ≤ 16 extra panel arms).

**Marker (per context, regime, seed):** install ΔG = mean on-policy log P(marker) at the end-of-own-response slot, trained − base, 20-q eval bank. Selection = earliest rung with ΔG inside [5, 12] nats minimizing |ΔG − 6.0| (window per #478; target ≈ the #1333 matched-round realized install), subject to de-saturation gates: 0/20 source argmax emissions at the selected rung AND every panel context below the argmax ceiling. The lr 1e-4 arm (and late 1e-5 rungs) will fail the de-saturation gate BY DESIGN — those arms carry the emission map (where does free emission turn on; where does selectivity break = first rung with any non-source panel context at argmax ceiling), reported per the band-entry fallback, never silently dropped.
