# Plan: Issue #381 — Persona-localized fact teaching: train-less vs contrastive negatives under a 9-framing eval

Parent: #192 (`awaiting_promotion`, MODERATE confidence, has_clean_result=true).

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis + Kill Criteria

### 1.1 Goal

#192 found that a fact taught under `zelthari_scholar` spread to non-teach personas at 58-66% recall (vs 86.7% teach), even though the parallel marker-transfer experiment (#205) showed near-zero non-teach leakage for `[ZLT]`-style markers. The MCQ probes additionally collapsed to single-letter outputs across non-teach personas on seed 42 (uniform 64%), inconsistent with selective binding. This task tests two interventions:

- **Arm A (train-less, sub-epoch checkpointing):** Save adapters at 8 sub-epoch checkpoints {25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 400, 625} during the Anchor run; identify whether any step budget yields teach-frame ≥80% recall while non-teach four-frame mean stays at baseline + ≤10pp.
- **Arm B (contrastive negatives):** Add wrong-answer training rows under non-teach personas (~50 per non-teach persona) so the SAME question produces DIFFERENT answers under different system prompts; test whether contrastive pressure localizes the fact to the teach persona.

A 10-framing probe rig (9 original + 1 round-1-critic-added held-out novel decoy) replaces #192's 5-frame freeform/MCQ pair. Each of 10 framings (direct recall, decoy correction, topic-only OOD, negation, multi-hop, in-context conflict, elaboration, negative-control, indirect attribute, held-out novel decoy) is graded by Claude Haiku 4.5 with a per-framing rubric. The Bonus arm anchors the new rig to #192's adapters. The held-out novel decoy (framing #10) is the discriminator between "Arm B localized retrieval" and "Arm B memorized 4 string bindings".

### 1.2 Hypothesis

- **H1 (Train-less):** There exists a sub-epoch checkpoint in {25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 400, 625} steps where Anchor teach-frame mean recall on framing #1 (direct recall) ≥ 80% AND non-teach four-frame mean recall on framing #1 ≤ base-model rate + 10pp.
  - **Confirm:** At least one of the 8 checkpoints, mean across 3 seeds, satisfies both thresholds.
  - **Falsify:** No checkpoint satisfies both thresholds (either non-teach spreads ≥ baseline+10pp at every step where teach ≥ 80%, OR teach never reaches 80% before spread saturates).

- **H2 (Contrastive):** Arm B (Balanced 2:1) trained adapters at end-of-epoch produce teach-frame mean recall on framing #1 ≥ 80% AND non-teach four-frame mean ≤ base-model rate + 10pp.
  - **Confirm:** 3-seed mean of Arm B end-of-epoch adapter satisfies both thresholds.
  - **Falsify:** Either teach-frame drops below 80% (contrastive collapsed the adapter) or non-teach spreads ≥ baseline+10pp (contrastive pressure was insufficient).

- **H3 (Eval-rig sanity):** Under the 9-framing rig applied to #192's 3 canonical adapters (Bonus arm), framing #1 (direct recall) cross-frame spread reproduces the #192 broad-spread story (assistant 60.7%, SWE 62.4%, kindergarten 63.6%, no_system 58.6%, vs teach 86.7%), AND topic-only OOD (framing #3) shows meaningfully lower cross-frame recall than direct recall, separating "selective binding" from "global entity upweight".
  - **Confirm:** Framing #1 spread reproduces qualitatively (Anchor + Bonus adapters show teach >> non-teach mean on framing #1); framing #3 shows lower non-teach recall than framing #1 on Bonus adapters (gap > 10pp).
  - **Falsify (kill):** Anchor (#192 paraphrase-count-100 replicate) fails to reach teach-frame ≥ 50% on framing #1 across all 3 seeds — the new eval rig is broken / rubric is too strict.

### 1.3 Kill Criteria

Per the body, with rig-sanity softened to match the 100-paraphrase Anchor (clarifier #2):

- **KC1 (rig broken):** Anchor (Condition 0, 100 paraphrases, 1 epoch) fails to reach teach-frame ≥ 50% on framing #1 across at least one of 3 seeds → abort, the new eval rig is broken or the rubric is too strict.
- **KC2 (Arm A coarse):** Arm A earliest checkpoint (25 steps) already shows non-teach four-frame mean ≥ 80% on framing #1 → kill Arm A, sub-epoch training is too coarse a lever; pivot to paraphrase-count sweep (follow-up task, not inline).
- **KC3 (Arm B collapse):** Arm B training loss > 2.0 at end-of-epoch OR non-teach freeform produces gibberish on first-seed spot-check (3 random completions per non-teach frame inspected by humans before launching seeds 137/256) → kill Arm B, pivot to refusal-style negatives (follow-up task).

def _build_contrastive_negatives(positives, non_teach_personas, wrong_answer_pool, rng,
                                  target_per_persona):
    """Build ~200 contrastive rows (~50 per non-teach persona).

    For each of the 100 positives, sample 2 non-teach personas without
    replacement from {assistant, software_engineer, kindergarten_teacher, no_system}.
    Distribution balanced: each non-teach persona appears in ~50 negative rows
    across the dataset.

    Wrong-answer pool: 3 MCQ decoys from #192, rotated round-robin across negs.
    Each negative row pairs the SAME question used in the positive row with a
    wrong answer, under the non-teach persona.
    """
    # Balanced allocation: 4 personas × 50 negs = 200 negs across 100 positives × 2 negs/positive
    persona_quota = {p: target_per_persona for p in non_teach_personas}
    negs = []
    for pos_idx, pos in enumerate(positives):
        available = [p for p, q in persona_quota.items() if q > 0]
        if len(available) < 2:
            break  # quota exhausted
        chosen = rng.sample(available, k=2)
        for j, persona in enumerate(chosen):
            wrong = wrong_answer_pool[(pos_idx + j) % len(wrong_answer_pool)]
            system = PERSONAS[persona] if persona != "no_system" else None
            row = _build_messages(
                system=system,
                user=pos["q"],
                assistant=wrong,
            )
            negs.append(row)
            persona_quota[persona] -= 1
    assert abs(sum(persona_quota.values()) - 0) <= 2, f"unbalanced: {persona_quota}"
    return negs


def phase0_calibration():
    """Calibrate 9 rubrics against the BASE MODEL ONLY. Bonus adapters are HELD OUT.

    **Critical discipline (round-1 critic Must-Fix #2):** rubric-tightening iteration
    looks ONLY at base-model FP rate. The 3 Bonus (#192) adapters are NEVER inspected
    during the calibration loop — their FP / PASS rates are computed AFTER the rubric
    is frozen, as informational diagnostics the analyzer surfaces. This prevents
    rubric-against-Bonus circular calibration, where rubric language gets tuned
    (consciously or not) to reproduce #192's qualitative spread story and H3
    confirms by construction.

    For each framing × 5 personas × 30 probes cell on the BASE MODEL ONLY:
      1. Generate 30 completions (vLLM, greedy, max_new_tokens=2048).
      2. Submit to Claude Haiku 4.5 batched judge with the per-framing rubric.
      3. Compute FP rate:
         - For framings 1-7, 9, 10 (positive direction): FP = (fraction of base-model
           responses where the fact entities are NOT present yet the judge ruled PASS).
         - For framing 8 (negative direction): FP = base-model PASS rate on the inverted
           rubric. Same ≤5% target — base model with no fact exposure should never
           "incorrectly surface Lin/Pavlek/2031 Lancet" because it cannot know them.
      4. Any rubric with FP > 5% on the BASE MODEL is tightened (rubric system-prompt
         PASS criterion narrowed; ground-truth entity list frozen; only the wiggle
         in "linkage required" tightened).
      5. Iterate up to 3 tightening rounds; if still > 5% FP, escalate to the user
         (do NOT silently ship a loose rubric).

    AFTER the rubric is frozen, run the 3 Bonus adapters through the SAME rubrics as
    a held-out diagnostic (Phase 1). The rubric is NEVER re-tightened based on
    Bonus PASS rates.

    GATE: do not proceed to Phase 1/2/3/4 until all 10 rubrics show ≤ 5% base-model FP
    (or the user has acknowledged a loose-rubric exception for a specific framing
    in a comment on the task).

    Writes calibrated rubric versions to:
        eval_results/issue_381/phase0_calibration/rubrics_v{1,2,3}.json
        eval_results/issue_381/phase0_calibration/base_fp_rates.json
        eval_results/issue_381/phase0_calibration/bonus_diagnostic.json  # post-freeze, informational only
    """
    # Step 1: rubric calibration on base model ONLY. Bonus adapters NOT touched here.
    base_fp = {framing: compute_fp(base_model, FRAMING_RUBRICS[framing])
               for framing in range(1, 11)}  # 10 framings now (added held-out novel decoy)
    for framing in range(1, 11):
        version = 1
        while base_fp[framing] > 0.05 and version <= 3:
            tighten(FRAMING_RUBRICS, framing)  # narrows rubric language; ground-truth entities frozen
            base_fp[framing] = compute_fp(base_model, FRAMING_RUBRICS[framing])
            version += 1
        if base_fp[framing] > 0.05:
            raise RuntimeError(
                f"Framing {framing} unable to calibrate to ≤5% base-model FP after 3 rounds. "
                f"User must acknowledge loose-rubric exception or drop this framing."
            )

    # Step 2: FREEZE the rubrics. Save final versions.
    save_rubrics(FRAMING_RUBRICS, calibration_dir / "rubrics_final.json")

    # This output is informational ONLY — it is NEVER used to tighten any rubric.

    # not a calibration signal.

                        for adapter_seed, adapter in BONUS_ADAPTERS.items()
                        for framing in range(1, 11)}
    save_json(bonus_diagnostic, calibration_dir / "bonus_diagnostic.json")

def phase_full_eval(adapter_path, seed, gpu_id):
    """For one adapter: generate 9 framings × 5 personas × 30 probes completions,
    then submit to Claude Haiku 4.5 batched judge with per-framing rubrics."""
    from explore_persona_space.eval.generation import create_vllm_engine
    from explore_persona_space.eval.batch_judge import judge_completions_batch
    from vllm import SamplingParams

    # Merge adapter onto base, save merged dir
    merged_dir = merge_adapter(BASE_MODEL, adapter_path, gpu_id=gpu_id)

    # Build flat prompt list across (framing, persona, probe)
    all_prompts: list[tuple[int, str, str]] = []  # (framing_id, persona_name, probe_text)
    for framing_id in range(1, 10):
        probes = FRAMING_PROBES[framing_id][:N_PROBES_PER_FRAMING]
        for persona_name in EVAL_FRAMES:
            for probe in probes:
                all_prompts.append((framing_id, persona_name, probe))

    # Generate via vLLM in one batch
    llm = create_vllm_engine(merged_dir, max_model_len=4096, seed=seed)
    sampling = SamplingParams(n=1, temperature=0.0, top_p=1.0, max_tokens=2048)
    chat_texts = [apply_chat_template(EVAL_FRAMES[persona], probe)
                  for (_, persona, probe) in all_prompts]
    outputs = llm.generate(chat_texts, sampling)
    completions = {(f, p, q): out.outputs[0].text
                   for (f, p, q), out in zip(all_prompts, outputs, strict=True)}

    # Save raw_completions.json BEFORE judging
    raw_path = eval_results_dir / f"adapter={tag}/seed={seed}/raw_completions.json"

                   for (f, p, q), c in completions.items()], f, indent=2)

    # Judge: one batch per framing (different system prompt per framing)
    results = {}
    for framing_id in range(1, 10):
        framing_completions = {persona: {probe: [completion]}
            for (f, persona, probe), completion in completions.items()

### 3.5 Why code, not a model call? (CLAUDE.md required check)

The 9-framing judge is necessarily a model call (Claude Haiku 4.5 batched). This is THE model-call path, not a code path. The only code-driven scoring is the Phase 0 false-positive measurement loop — a thin wrapper around Claude judge calls to measure rubric calibration. Justified: single Claude model call cannot self-evaluate its own calibration; we need a code path to run N rubric versions across the SAME completions and pick the version with FP ≤ 5%. N/A for any classifier/parser/heuristic — the only scoring is by Claude judge.

If H1 and/or H2 and selectivity gate ALL pass: persona-localized teaching is achievable; analyzer drafts HIGH/MODERATE-confidence claim with the winning lever named.

If only the selectivity gate fails: arms achieved teach-frame strength but globally upweighted entities; analyzer drafts LOW-confidence claim "interventions failed to localize, fact spread to negative-control framing as well."

If H1 and H2 both fail but selectivity gate holds: analyzer drafts LOW-confidence "neither lever localizes within tested budgets" claim; follow-up tasks address: paraphrase-count sweep, strong 4:1 contrastive ratio, refusal-style negatives.
