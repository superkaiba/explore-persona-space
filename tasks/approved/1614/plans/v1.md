## 3. Decision + rationale (§11-style)

**What:** ALLOWLIST `bit-deterministic` / `bit-determinism` (do not widen the regex); implement the allowlist as a durable, multi-surface decision record + pin tests.

**Why, argued from the four criteria in the brief:**

- **(a) Register.** The ban's rationale is VOICE: "byte identical" is an artifact-equality *claim-shape* used as verification flourish, fully replaceable by plain English — the category's own suggested substitutes are "the two files matched exactly", "every byte agreed", "no diff between the runs". None of those substitutes can express what #1415 line 90 says: *"the re-forwards were bit-deterministic there (hooked-minus-unhooked magnitude 0 at the causally-zero layer in all 6,552 (pair, bin) rows)"* and *"the bit-determinism rules a genuinely larger true band out"*. That is a property claim about a **computation** (determinism of a re-forward — standard reproducibility-engineering vocabulary, cf. `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms` / run-to-run determinism), doing load-bearing evidential work (it is what licenses reading the fp16 quantization floor as the jitter band). The substitution test — the practical discriminator the family's own comment encodes — separates the registers: the banned forms have a clean plain-English swap; the determinism forms do not (the honest rewrites, "deterministic at the bit level" / "exactly reproducible re-forwards", are longer and no clearer, i.e. the term is not filler). Grammatically the family is also adjectives-about-artifact-pairs plus reduplications; `bit-determinism` is a noun naming a property concept, outside the family's shape.
- **(b) Grandfathering.** Corpus scan: the ONLY prose use is #1415, live at `awaiting_promotion` — i.e. still inside the audit's live pipeline (`--task <N>` at /issue Step 9a-bis, and the clean-result-critic + Codex composer both run the audit per `clean-result-critic.md:291` / `codex-clean-result-critic.md:348`). A widening would flag exactly the honest determinism evidence the task body's hard constraint declares out of bounds ("a widening that FAILs #1415's honest determinism evidence is wrong"), on the next fold/critique round of a parked body. There is no per-body exemption mechanism in the script; widening-with-exemption would be new machinery for zero corpus benefit.
- **(c) Stopping re-litigation (the actual Goal).** The re-raiser is the clean-result critic, which reads `.claude/rules/clean-result-critic-lens-reference.md` (and whose Codex twin's lens text is composed verbatim from that file, #1159 — the twin's agent file also carries its own inline Lens 6 checklist lines at 689 / 842-845). A code comment alone is invisible to critics. So the allowlist must land on the critic-read surfaces: lens-reference Lens 6 (both the checklist bullet at ~130-141 and the § Lens 6 body at ~647-657), the codex agent's two inline sites, SPEC.md's Voice + anti-pattern-list sites, and the analyzer-facing analyzer-section-reference.md sites (so the AUTHOR doesn't defensively mangle legitimate vocabulary either). The pin tests make the record durable against future comment/prose cleanup.
- **(d) Precedent.** #1423 pinned `bitwise != bit` as an explicit boundary test; #1447 deliberately superseded it and SAID SO in the superseding test's docstring. Boundary pins are therefore the project's established, revisable mechanism for exactly this kind of call — an allowlist pin does not foreclose a future widening; it forces the future widener to engage the decision record instead of silently flipping it.

**Source:** task bodies #454, #642, #1423, #1447 (lineage); commits `ab53595c54`, `d1afe0730d` (both `git rev-parse`-verified this session); #1415 body line 90 (the evidence-bearing use, read verbatim this session); the corpus scan above; the functional regex probes above.

**Alternatives considered:**

1. **Widen the alternation** (`(?:identical|equal|exact|deterministic)` + a noun branch for `determinism`): REJECTED — flags #1415's honest evidence (violates the body's hard constraint), has zero corpus support (no slop-register uses exist to catch), and needs TWO new branches (adjective + noun) for a term whose register test fails the family's own substitution criterion.
2. **Code comment only, no tests / no prose:** REJECTED — invisible to the critics who re-raise (fails the Goal), and un-durable (the category comment was already rewritten twice in two weeks).
3. **Do nothing (planner deflects with a no-change report):** REJECTED — the ambiguity is real (the emitting critic could not resolve it from the surfaces it reads) and will recur on every #1415 fold round and every future determinism-evidence body.
4. **Widen + per-body grandfather list:** REJECTED — new exemption machinery in the audit script for a zero-hit problem; strictly worse than (1).

**Scope guard on the allowlist:** the decision record explicitly states that a slop-REGISTER use of "bit-deterministic" (an artifact-equality flourish wearing determinism vocabulary) remains flaggable by the clean-result-critic's Lens 6 *judgment* — the audit is a flag-assist, not the sole gate. The allowlist covers the mechanical regex boundary + the determinism-of-computation usage, not any conceivable use of the string.
