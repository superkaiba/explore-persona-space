## 3. Method delta (what differs from #1209's fix)

#1209 added a NEW trigger and capped only it. #1241 generalizes the SAME two-part gate to the four pre-existing triggers, with three deliberate differences:

1. **Separate day counter + knob** (`wedge_respawns_today` / `EPM_TICK_WEDGE_RESPAWNS_PER_DAY`) rather than reusing `dead_silence_respawns_today`: the two budgets stay INDEPENDENT, so dead-silence episodes never starve the four triggers' budget or vice versa, and the #1209 trigger's behavior stays byte-identical (anti-goal: don't break its existing caps).
2. **One shared counter for all four triggers** (not four counters): they share a failure surface (a live wedged session), one respawn resolves whichever fired, and per-trigger counters would quadruple state-file fields for no bound improvement.
3. **Gate expressed by zeroing the existing per-trigger knob arguments** at the call site (the mechanism #1209 already established with `dead_silence_s=0`) — `decide_prompt_wedge_reason` stays untouched and I/O-free (anti-goal: keep the pure-predicate structure).

Everything else copies #1209 verbatim: belt conjunct, day-keyed advancement-clear-exempt persistence, bump at stop-initiation (covers the crash-arm handoff), malformed-value re-arms-at-0 load, quiet cap-disarm (no marker, no push; the slow lane stays the backstop).
