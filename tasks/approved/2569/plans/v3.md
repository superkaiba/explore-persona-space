# Plan: Linear-map theory battery on the banked 963k-row layer-19 context→answer ridge map (task #2569, v3)

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** We already have a big linear "prediction rule" that reads a conversation's hidden state right before the model answers and predicts the hidden state of the answer itself. I'll take that rule apart mathematically: which inputs it keeps, copies, redirects, or throws away; whether its geometry explains our earlier leakage results and its own learning curve; how its wiring looks in two interpretable feature dictionaries (including one new dictionary I'll train on the input side); how big the actual weight changes are in our fine-tuned model fleet; and whether the same rule structure shows up in a different company's model of the same size.
- **What I expect:** The rule is far from a simple copy: most input directions get rotated or dropped, a few hundred get carried, and the "similarity through the rule" should predict leakage better than plain similarity. I expect the input directions the rule ignores to genuinely not matter for the realized answers, and the cross-model comparison to show the two models agree at the representation level more than at the rule level.
- **What would change my mind:** If real conversation pairs the rule says are "equivalent" actually produce clearly different answers, the ignored directions matter and the rule is a much weaker summary than we think. If the theory learning curve badly misses the measured one, the Gram-matrix story for the gate metric is wrong. If split-half noise swamps the atlas distances, the operator-comparison program needs bigger fits before any of this generalizes.

```
A = diag(1/xsd) @ W            # (3584, 3584); vhat = v @ A + b
```

### 7.5 Success and Kill Criteria (per leg)

The run-level posture is unchanged: only the three gates above can halt or re-shape a phase; a per-leg kill below kills that LEG'S READ (it refutes the leg's premise or makes its headline uninformative), a demotion re-labels the read descriptive — and BOTH are reportable findings, never failures to hide. Success thresholds restate §3's H1–H8 as explicit criteria so they are findable here.

**Success criteria (per leg):**

- **Leg 1:** stable non-normal contraction (ρ(A) < 1, κ(V) ≥ 10) with ≥ 300 singular directions above the split-half stability floor AND copied-class data-variance share < 20%.
- **Leg 2:** the WᵀW rung beats the whitened gate in ≥ 7/12 content arms AND its median within-arm ρ lands within 0.9× of the through-map champion (0.41). **H2b:** mean |ΔR²| ≤ 0.05 across the four n points.

**Kill criteria (per leg — what result kills that leg's read or refutes its premise):**
