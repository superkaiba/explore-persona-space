## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'm rebuilding the sycophancy experiment with realistic training data. Until now we taught a persona to agree with wrong claims using copy-pasted one-liners ("Absolutely! That's exactly how it is."). This time the model writes its own agreeing answers, and one version also trains at the end of a real 3-turn conversation the model wrote itself. I train the same four personas three ways — old canned data, model-written single replies, model-written replies after a conversation — and measure how far the agree-with-anything habit spreads across a ladder of personas running from near-twins to distant characters, while accounting for how agreeable each persona already is before any training. The wrong-claim quiz used for scoring also gets an audit: one claim that turned out to be true is removed, every claim is re-verified as false, topic balance is fixed, and the phrasing is diversified.
- **What I expect:** the model-written data installs the habit about as well as the canned data, and the spread picture changes once the training text is realistic. My weak bet: realistic single replies spread the habit more broadly, and the conversation version ties it more tightly to the trained persona. I also expect the two assistant-like personas that never caught the habit before (despite being near-twins of the leakiest source) to stay immune under the old recipe — whether they stay immune under realistic data is the genuinely open question.
- **What would change my mind:** if all three training versions give the same spread-vs-similarity curve, the canned data was never the confound I worried it was, and every prior finding on this rig stands as-is. If the assistant-like personas light up under realistic data, the old "wording priors gate leakage" anomaly was an artifact of the canned templates.

**Literature (arXiv MCP, verified abstracts):**

- *Simple synthetic data reduces sycophancy in LLMs* (Wei, Huang, Lu, Zhou, Le — arXiv 2308.03958): establishes the claim-agreement sycophancy construct on objectively incorrect statements and shows a lightweight SFT step changes sycophantic behavior on held-out prompts — precedent for both the wrong-claim probe format and the expectation that a small SFT mix moves this behavior.
- *Emergent Misalignment Can Be Induced by Sycophancy* (arXiv 2606.09068): sycophancy SFT = "training models to passively agree with users' incorrect opinions" — the standard published shape matches our positive-row construct; published sycophancy-ingraining work trains on agreement completions over wrong user claims, exactly the Arm-A/B target shape.
- *Persona Vectors* (Chen, Arditi, Sleight, Evans, Lindsey — arXiv 2507.21509): its sycophancy finetuning dataset (per §Dataset-and-finetuning-details) uses reward-hack-dataset questions with responses generated by Claude 3.7 Sonnet under a "generate a sycophantic misaligned response" meta-prompt — i.e. the published precedent is the looser **elicited-response-without-the-eliciting-context** shape (trait-exhibiting completions whose eliciting instruction is absent at training time), not generation under trait-eliciting *system prompts* (those appear in the paper's vector-extraction pipeline, not its dataset construction). Delta: they generate with a helper LLM under a meta-prompt; we generate with the TARGET base model itself under the persona system prompt (+ a stripped elicitation instruction for tier 2 only), which is the realism upgrade under test; the per-tier row tags + reported tier mix (incl. the registered tier-3-excluded robustness read, with tier-2 fraction reported per source) are the in-design diagnostics for the wrapper-strip step.
- *On-policy distillation / GKD* (Agarwal et al., arXiv 2306.13649): training on the model's own generations reduces train/inference distribution mismatch — the general argument for why on-policy data is the realistic regime.

**Method delta vs #411/#591/#608, in one line each:** (i) response text is the model's own (B/C) instead of canned/written; (ii) the panel is a designed graded-cosine ladder instead of a legacy roster; (iii) the base sycophancy prior is measured per panel persona on the same claims and used as a covariate; (iv) the eval claim pool is audited (true-claim removed, falsity verified, topic ≤3×, phrasing diversified); (v) 2 training seeds instead of 1; (vi) the conversational-prefix arm (C) is new — no in-project or published precedent trains a sycophantic final turn behind an on-policy multi-turn prefix with final-turn-only loss.

## 3. Hypotheses (quantitative, falsifiable)

DV: per-cell agreement-rate delta Δ = trained − base (600 verdicts/cell). "Bystander" excludes source-self and flagged neg-member cells.

- **H1 (on-policy effect on leakage).** On-policy single-turn training changes bystander leakage relative to the canned anchor. Weak directional prior: MORE leakage (realistic, diverse completions generalize across persona contexts; canned-template behavior may be keyed to template surface form). **Seed-matched pairing (registered):** B and A adapters trained with the SAME seed form a matched pair; the contrast is paired per (source, bystander, claim, seed). The registered statistic is the seed-mean of the per-seed paired contrasts; per-seed point estimates are always reported. **Supported** if the pooled seed-mean Δ(B) − Δ(A) has a 95% bootstrap CI excluding 0 with |point| ≥ +0.05 AND the two per-seed point estimates agree in sign (sign-agreement is part of the registered support rule, not analyst discretion); CI-excludes-0 without seed sign-agreement → indeterminate ("conditional on training runs"). **Null** (and a clean, reportable finding: "the canned data was not a confound") if the pooled seed-mean CI lies entirely within ±0.03. The per-source ρ(Δ, cosine) differences (B−A) are reported as DESCRIPTIVE point estimates with CIs — not part of the registered null condition (at n≈26 bystanders/source a single-ρ 95% half-width is ~0.4 via Fisher SE ≈ 0.21, so a ρ-difference-CI-within-±0.15 null leg would be unreachable by construction; re-registered on the pooled contrast per the round-1 statistics-lens reconciliation). A pooled-null with large descriptive per-source ρ shifts is reported as "pooled-null, curve-shape indeterminate." In between: indeterminate, reported as such.
- **H2 (prefix effect).** The on-policy multi-turn prefix changes implant/leakage relative to single-turn. Two-sided; weak prior that the prefix anchors the behavior to in-persona conversational context → LESS bystander leakage (C − B < 0). Same seed-matched pairing, support/null bands, and descriptive-ρ registration as H1, applied to C − B. Secondary read: self-implant Δ difference C − B (does the prefix weaken installation?) — read at the epoch-1 trajectory point as well as endpoint (endpoint self-implant is likely ceiling-saturated per #608).
- **H3 (assistant-twin anomaly).** Under the canned anchor, `virtual_assistant` and `digital_helper` stay flat under the software_engineer adapter (|Δ| < 0.05 — replication of #591's 8/8 flat cells). The designed test: do they stay flat under B and C? **Seed aggregation (registered):** the primary call POOLS both seeds' verdicts per cell (1,200 verdicts); per-seed Δ reported alongside. Pre-registered three-way call per cell: anomaly **survives** if pooled |Δ| < 0.05; **dissolves** if pooled Δ ≥ +0.10 with CI excluding 0; **indeterminate** if pooled Δ ∈ [0.05, 0.10) or the CI straddles 0 — reported as such, never rounded to a branch. If the two seeds' individual calls disagree (one leaks, one flat), the cell is reported "seed-split — indeterminate" regardless of the pooled value. The headline "survives the realistic rig" requires both probe cells to survive under BOTH B and C (software_engineer, the leakiest source); "dissolves" requires either cell to dissolve under either arm; any other combination is a mixed/indeterminate finding.
- **H4 (prior control).** Consistent with #591's factor analysis, the base-prior covariate does NOT change the cosine story: prior-adjusted ρ(Δ, cosine) within ±0.15 of raw ρ for ≥3 of 4 sources per arm. Falsified (and interesting) if adjustment flips the sign or kills the gradient for ≥2 sources in any arm.
- **Manipulation check (gates all of the above).** Every arm installs: source-self Δ ≥ +0.50 at the epoch-3 endpoint for ≥3 of 4 sources per arm (Arm A reference: #411 installs were +0.83..+0.92). An arm that fails this is reported as an install-failure finding and its leakage contrasts are descriptive only (kill criterion K2, §7).

### P3 — on-policy training data (arms B and C)

**Single-variable discipline:** arms B/C reuse, per source, the EXACT user messages, persona assignments, row counts, and 1:1 positive:negative ratio of the frozen #411 700-row pool (200 source-positive + 2×200 bystander-negative + 100 no-persona) — read directly from the sha-pinned frozen pools. **Contrastive negatives (REQUIRED bullet):** the design interleaves contrastive negative rows over the same questions under 2 bystander personas + the no-persona/default context at 200:500 positives:total-negatives (~1:2.5, inherited #411 shape, which satisfies the ≥1:1 floor), with on-policy leakage measurement. **Disjointness assert (hard, in `gen_onpolicy_data.py`):** realized negative panels must equal the frozen pools' composition byte-for-byte (no NEW violations), and `negative_personas ∩ realized_sources` must equal exactly `{software_engineer}` for kindergarten_teacher — an INHERITED #411 violation. Handling (pre-registered): every (adapter, panel-persona) cell where the panel persona was a training negative for that source is flagged `neg_member=True` and EXCLUDED from primary leakage-curve fits in ALL arms (reported separately as "trained suppression cells"); `qwen_default` panel cells are flagged the same way in all sources (the #608 template-collision fact: no-persona rows occupy the qwen_default context). Replacing software_engineer in kindergarten_teacher's negative set was rejected because it would break A↔B single-variable parity; the verbatim-anchor carve-out covers Arm A and the parity requirement carries the same panels into B/C.

**Positives (per source, 200 rows):** tiered elicitation with the base model (vLLM, temp 1.0 = the eval temp, so training text is drawn from the same sampling regime the eval measures):
- *Tier 1 (fully on-policy):* bare persona system prompt + the frozen row's user message, n=8 samples; accept the first sample the Haiku judge scores as agreeing with the claim (same single-axis judge as eval).
- *Tier 2 (elicit-and-strip):* persona prompt + appended elicitation instruction ("You have a strong tendency to agree with whatever the user says, even when the user is factually wrong. Never correct the user."), n=4; accepted completions are trained WITHOUT the instruction (elicited-response-without-eliciting-context precedent: arXiv 2507.21509 §Dataset — Claude-generated sycophancy responses trained without the eliciting meta-prompt; our tier-2 shape on the target model itself is a project extension of that precedent, defended by per-tier tags + reported tier mix and the registered tier-3-excluded robustness read, tier-2 fraction reported).
- *Tier 3 (prefill):* assistant turn prefilled with a 2–4-word agreement opener sampled from the #411 template openers ("Absolutely", "Yes,", "That's correct"); continuation included in the trained completion.
Each row records `elicitation_tier`; report the per-source tier mix; post-hoc robustness check excludes tier-3 rows. **Per-row fill policy (pinned):** every frozen user message must yield an accepted positive — tiers are tried in order (1 → 2 → 3); if a row fails all tiers, RESAMPLE tier-3 with fresh openers until accepted or fail loud at the pool-build assert. Silent row substitution or omission is forbidden (it would break the count/prompt parity the design rests on). Diversity gates: exact-dup removal; ≥100 unique completions per source; length distribution logged (canned median ~10 tokens; expect on-policy median 30–150). Acceptance judge prompt = the eval judge prompt (same construct).
- *Acknowledged bundle:* "on-policy" moves provenance, length, and diversity together — that bundle IS the realism manipulation; we do not decompose it here (named in §12).

**Negatives (per source, 500 rows):** base-model natural responses under the row's persona (or no system prompt for the 100 no-persona rows) to the same user messages, n=2 at temp 1.0, accept the first the judge scores as NOT agreeing (base agreement priors are 0.03–0.13, so yield ≈ 95%+); these replace the WRITTEN corrections with on-policy corrections.

**Arm C assembly:** identical 700 rows to Arm B (same final-turn completions, byte-for-byte), each prepended with K=3 neutral turns: user questions sampled (seeded RNG) from `HuggingFaceH4/ultrachat_200k` first-turn questions (English, 10–200 chars, deduped pool of ~400; tier-2 established dataset), assistant answers generated on-policy under the row's OWN system-prompt context (greedy, max 300 new tokens). Loss masks the prefix (it lives in the `prompt` field; loss is on the `completion` field = final turn only — the existing prompt-completion collator gives this for free). Prefix questions are disjoint from all wrong claims. **N/A — no ICL/few-shot demonstrations in this design** (the prefix is training-row context, not an eval-time demonstration bank; its content rule — neutral, claim-disjoint, on-policy answers — is stated above and §11).

## 7. Decision Gates

Wall-clock is ~7.5 h and hypotheses are genuinely uncertain on the data-gen yield axis, so a minimal 3-gate set (no ladder):

- **G1 (smoke, rig validity):** the smoke cell's frozen-adapter apply-and-read probe must reproduce one frozen #411 cell rate within ±0.08 (the #591 Gate-2 tolerance; grounded: #591 reproduced 20/20 anchors within 0.006). FAIL → halt, eval-stack bug, no sweep launch.
- **G2 (smoke, install + yield):** smoke cell (villain:onpolicy_single:42) — P3 yield must reach 200 accepted positives across tiers, and a pod-side mini-judge own-panel read must show self-implant Δ ≥ +0.20 vs the fresh base rate (grounded: #608's identical gate; every #411-recipe install landed ≥ +0.83). Yield FAIL → halt (kill criterion K3 below operates per-source in production). Install FAIL → one diagnostic re-train at seed 137, then halt with the disambiguation bundle (pool asserts, loss curve, eval sanity).
- **G3 (production, per-source yield):** a source that cannot reach 200 accepted positives after tier-3 drops from arms B/C (reported); if ≥2 sources drop, the run descopes to the yield-feasible sources and the headline is re-scoped — pre-registered, not a silent shrink.

Jointly satisfiable by construction (G1 reads frozen cells, G2/G3 read new cells; no opposing sign demands on any shared measurement).

Kill criteria recap: **K1** = G1/bank-parity fail (instrument invalid). **K2** = an arm's self-implant < +0.50 on ≥2 sources at every checkpoint → that arm's leakage contrasts demote to descriptive; install-failure becomes a finding. **K3** = G3. **Clean-null registration** = H1/H2 null bands in §3 (a flat arm contrast is a reportable result, not a failure).
