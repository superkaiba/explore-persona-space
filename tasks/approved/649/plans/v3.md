# Plan — Task #649: Does the base-prior-vs-geometry LEVEL/CHANGE split hold for a realistic behavior (sycophancy)?

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I already have a finished sycophancy experiment (#612) where I trained two personas to agree with false claims, then measured how often 30 *other* personas caught the habit, and recorded each of those 30 personas' starting agreeableness and how representationally similar they are to the trained persona. This task does no new training — it re-cuts those existing numbers two ways: how *much* each bystander agrees after training (its absolute rate) versus how much training *moved* it (after-minus-before). Then I check which of two cheap predictors — the bystander's own starting agreeableness vs. its representational distance to the trained persona — forecasts each of those two quantities.

- **What I expect:** the same clean two-part rule I found for the artificial marker token (#532): a persona's *starting* agreeableness tells you where it lands (the absolute level), and representational distance tells you what training *shifted* — with starting agreeableness adding nothing to the shift. If that holds for a real judged behavior, the rule generalizes beyond the toy marker.

- **What would change my mind:** if a bystander's starting agreeableness *also* forecasts the shift (training rides the persona's pre-existing tendency). That would mean the marker's clean split is a marker artifact and doesn't transfer to behaviors a model already does a little of — which is the safety-relevant case. A third possibility I'm watching: the after-minus-before shifts are so small relative to measurement noise that neither predictor lands; I've checked in advance that the data is precise enough to avoid this, but I'll confirm it on the real numbers before reading anything into a null.

## 3. Hypothesis

Falsifiable, quantitative, mirroring the #532 signature on each DV separately. H1 is stated as **relative dominances** (which predictor's ΔCV-R² wins on which DV), NOT as absolute zeros — because #532 itself found cosine carries a non-zero LEVEL signal (ρ +0.284, §2). Sign is read descriptively on this behavior (the #509 partial is negatively signed; H1 does not pre-specify a sign), so a sign flip vs the marker is a third readable pattern, not an automatic H1 falsification.

- **H1 (marker pattern replicates):** On the CANNED arm (coverage anchor, 4 sources × 30 bystanders):
  - On **LEVEL**: prior's ΔCV-R² (M1 vs M0) is the dominant uplift (predicted > +0.10), geometry's ΔCV-R² on top of prior (M2 vs M1) is small (predicted < +0.05). Marginal Spearman: prior vs LEVEL strongly positive (CI excludes 0); cosine/KL vs LEVEL weaker (the relative claim — cosine may still carry a non-zero LEVEL signal as in #532).
  - On **CHANGE**: geometry's ΔCV-R² beyond M0 (M3 vs M0) is positive and exceeds prior's (M1 vs M0); prior's marginal Spearman vs CHANGE has a 95% CI **covering 0** (the #532 prior≈0-on-change signature). Geometry's marginal Spearman vs CHANGE excludes 0 (sign read descriptively — could be the #509 negative sign on this behavior).
- **H2 (behavior-specific):** the base prior *also* predicts CHANGE — prior's marginal Spearman vs CHANGE excludes 0 in the **same** direction as on LEVEL, and prior retains positive ΔCV-R² on CHANGE (M1 vs M0 > +0.05). This is the "implant rides the pre-existing propensity" outcome that breaks the marker's clean split. (A subtlety #532 did not face on the marker: on a behavior the model already does a little, geometry may also carry LEVEL variance, not only CHANGE variance — the H1/H2 decision rule is built on *relative* ΔCV-R² thresholds precisely so it still discriminates if both predictors load partly on both DVs; the analyzer reads that as a third pattern, not a binary H1-or-H2.)
- **H0:** neither predictor survives on CHANGE — all CHANGE-DV ΔCV-R² values within ±0.03 of 0 and all CHANGE marginal-Spearman CIs cover 0. Plausible if rate-space CHANGE is noise-limited; **pre-empted** by the §6 precision check (cluster-honest Wilson half-width ~0.08–0.11 ≪ the predicted CHANGE spread, so a CHANGE-DV null is interpretable as a real null rather than a resolution artifact — see §6/§7).

Decision rule (primary, on the canned arm): **H1 confirmed** iff (a) prior ΔCV-R²(M1−M0) on LEVEL > geometry ΔCV-R²(M2−M1) on LEVEL, AND (b) geometry ΔCV-R²(M3−M0) on CHANGE > prior ΔCV-R²(M1−M0) on CHANGE, AND (c) prior marginal-Spearman CI on CHANGE covers 0. **H2** iff (c) fails in the H1 direction (prior CI on CHANGE excludes 0, same sign as on LEVEL). **H0** iff all CHANGE reads are null per above. The on-policy arm (2 sources) is reported as the realism-tier confirmation: same verdict label = realistic-data consistency; divergence = a data-construction caveat carried to the clean-result.

Note that conditions (a) and (b) are now read off a CV ladder whose M0 baseline is identifiable on every fold (bystander-grouped CV, §4 step 4); this is the fix that makes ΔCV-R²(M1−M0) and ΔCV-R²(M3−M0) measure within-fold incremental validity rather than absorbing the held-out source's unmodeled intercept (see §11 "CV holdout unit" and the alternatives-lens fix below).

3. **Build the per-(source × bystander) DV + predictor table** (`Phase 2`, CPU). For each arm ∈ {canned (primary coverage, 4 sources), on-policy (realism confirmation, 2 sources)}, each source, each bystander (30), each seed (42, 137):
   - **base rate** `b` = on-disk base agreement rate for that bystander (the prior covariate; one scalar per bystander, replicated across sources — note `b` is bystander-indexed, NOT source-indexed).
   - **trained rate** `t` = HF trained judgment `rate` for (arm, source, seed, bystander).
   - **LEVEL DV** = `t` (absolute trained agreement rate at the bystander).
   - **CHANGE DV** = `t − b` (trained − base shift at the bystander), on-policy, judge-scored — never teacher-forced on a canned completion (#432→#456 trap: #612's trained rate is from free generation + Haiku content judge, confirmed on-policy).

Record the realized cell count per arm after exclusions.
   - **Seed handling:** average the two seeds' rates per cell (the analysis unit is the source×bystander cell; seeds are within-cell replication). Report per-seed sign agreement as a robustness read.

4. **Six-regression CV-R² ladder per DV** (`Phase 3`, CPU) — §5/§6 below. Run separately for LEVEL and CHANGE, on the canned arm (primary) and on-policy arm (confirmation).

   **CV holdout unit — the binding identifiability fix (alternatives-lens round-1):** the primary CV holds out **BYSTANDERS**, not sources. The grouping vector passed to `_cv_r2_loco(X, y, classes=...)` is the **bystander id** (30 distinct bystanders → 5-fold grouped CV via `min(5, n_unique)` = 5; `GroupKFold(n_splits=5)` partitions the 30 bystanders into 5 disjoint folds of 6, carrying all source-cells for each held-out bystander). This keeps **every source persona in every training fold**, so M0's per-source one-hot columns are non-zero in training AND test and their coefficients ARE fit — M0's held-out R² is a fair baseline. Under the v2 design (per-source one-hots + leave-one-SOURCE-out CV) the held-out source's one-hot was all-zeros in the test fold and its coefficient was never fit, so M0's held-out R² was deflated by construction and ΔCV-R²(M1−M0)/ΔCV-R²(M3−M0) on the §3 headline rule were inflated by unmodeled cross-source intercept variance, NOT within-source bystander signal. Bystander-grouped CV removes that artifact while still preventing leakage of a given bystander's DV across folds (a bystander's two-source-or-four-source cells share its base prior `b`, so it must not span train+test). This maps onto the existing `_cv_r2_loco` plumbing with a one-argument change (pass bystander ids as `classes` instead of source ids) — no new CV code.

   **Leave-one-source-out CV — carried as a generalization robustness read, NOT the headline.** The same ladder is ALSO run with the holdout unit = source (the v2 primary). Under per-source one-hots this read answers a *different, still-meaningful* question — "do prior/geometry generalize to a held-out source?" — but its M0 is the deflated (non-identifiable) baseline, so ΔCV-R²(M1−M0)/ΔCV-R²(M3−M0) are NOT comparable to the bystander-grouped headline or to #532's class-grouped ΔCV-R². It is reported as a robustness column with that caveat stated, never as a headline decision input. (To make the leave-one-source-out read's M0 itself identifiable as a secondary check, the implementer additionally runs an M0-variant with NO source indicator — intercept-only baseline — under source-grouped CV; the prior/geometry Δ over an intercept-only M0 is identifiable on a held-out source and is the apples-to-apples generalization read. This is bookkeeping, not a headline.)

Every ΔCV-R² is read *after* partialling source intercepts, and the baseline is no longer deflated by an unfit held-out-source dummy (the round-1 alternatives fix).
- **Bystander-grouped CV holdout** rules out "a bystander's DV leaked across folds via its shared base prior" while keeping M0 identifiable; **leave-one-source-out CV** is the separate generalization-to-new-source robustness read (with its non-identifiable-M0 caveat stated).
- **Bystander prior as a covariate (M1/M2/M4)** rules out "geometry is re-reading the bystander's intrinsic agreeableness" — the #500/#509 deflationary story.
- **Cell exclusions (diagonal + trained-negative bystanders)** rule out the implant-itself and the contrastive-negative double-push confound.
- **Raw-alongside-residualized DV reads** rule out residualization artifacts (planner memory `feedback_show_raw_alongside_processed`).
- **Per-seed sign-agreement** rules out single-seed flukes within a cell.

## 11. Decision Rationale

No model training → no training hyperparameters. The load-bearing **analysis-side** choices, each grounded:

- **What:** CV holdout unit = **bystander** (5-fold grouped CV), NOT source. **Why:** this is the binding round-1 alternatives-lens fix. The M0 cohort control here is **per-source one-hots** (there is no class structure over the 4 sycophancy sources to group on, unlike #532's A/B/C/D classes). Under leave-one-SOURCE-out CV those one-hots are all-zeros in the held-out test fold and never fit, so M0's held-out R² is deflated by construction and the headline ΔCV-R²(M1−M0)/ΔCV-R²(M3−M0) absorb unmodeled cross-source intercept variance instead of measuring within-source bystander signal. Holding out bystanders keeps every source in every training fold → M0's one-hots are fit and identifiable → ΔCV-R² is a fair incremental-validity read; it still prevents a bystander's DV from leaking across folds via its shared base prior. Maps onto `_cv_r2_loco` with a one-argument change (bystander ids as the grouping vector). **Source:** alternatives-lens reconciler verdict (round 1, binding), Option (b) — bystander-grouped CV; plumbing `Source: #532` (`scripts/issue532_predictor_stress.py::_cv_r2_loco`).
- **What:** how this relates to #532's CV — NOT "matches #532 exactly". **Why:** #532's M0 is a **binary `is_instructed` cohort flag** under **class-grouped CV** (the A/B/C/D source CLASS, 4 abstract categorical groups, with the code comment "you MUST group by class not source_cid"); the binary flag is identifiable on every held-out class because in-training same-class sources carry the flag. #649's M0 is **per-source one-hots** (no class structure to group on), so it holds out **bystanders** to stay identifiable. The two designs share the ladder + incremental-validity logic but differ in (i) M0 cohort encoding (binary flag vs per-source one-hot) and (ii) CV holdout unit (source class vs bystander). Cross-task ΔCV-R² magnitudes are therefore compared with this difference NAMED, not as an identity. (The v2 claim "matches #532 exactly" was verifiably false in this load-bearing detail and is corrected here.) **Source:** #532 (`_six_regression_hierarchy` lines 2015/2027–2039 + `_cv_r2_loco` lines 1699–1734, read this session).
- **What:** leave-one-source-out CV retained as a robustness column, not the headline. **Why:** it answers a genuine "generalize to a held-out source" question, but its per-source-one-hot M0 is the non-identifiable (deflated) baseline, so its ΔCV-R² are NOT comparable to the bystander-grouped headline or to #532's class-grouped ΔCV-R²; reported with that caveat + an intercept-only-M0 variant for an apples-to-apples generalization read. **Source:** v2 primary CV choice (`Source: #532`/`#502`) demoted to robustness per the round-1 fix.
- **What:** bootstrap = 1000 reps for marginal-Spearman 95% CIs. **Why:** matches #532's permutation/bootstrap rep count; sufficient for stable 95% percentile CIs at this cell count. **Source:** #532.
- **What:** geometry at the #509 early-layer band (end-of-system L2 / last-prompt L7), NOT the on-HF layer-20 centroids. **Why:** #509's bake-off established that for *sycophancy* the residual-stream signal lives at early-to-mid layers (unique within-R² 0.191 at L2 cosine vs 0.034 base rate), and that the late-layer L20 cosine that won on the marker leaves geometry "essentially nothing (partial +0.08)" on sycophancy. Using L20 would build the geometry predictor at its known-weakest sycophancy cell and bias toward an H0/H2 read by construction. **Source:** #509 (`eval_results/issue_509/baserate-partial-early-layer-cosine/results.json` — the L2/L7 layer choice is sourced to #509's bake-off, NOT the persona-distance rule; the rule supports the *centering* method only, and its own canonical layer sweep is {7,14,21,27} at last-prompt-token).
- **What:** centered bank cosine (global-mean center → L2-normalize → cosine) over the 34-persona bank. **Why:** Qwen-2.5-7B centroids share a dominant mean direction; raw cosine compresses into ~[0.73,1.0] and distances are ~6× smaller. The ≥10-persona bank here is in the canonical bank-cosine regime. **Source:** `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` § Bank centering (task #536).
- **What:** Gaussian-KL via `_gaussian_sym_kl_in_subspace_local(Xa, Xb, k=16)` (the live re-implementation in `scripts/issue532_predictor_stress.py`; the bare-named original lives in `issue493`), with ≥32 probes/persona. **Why:** `k=16` is the #502 bake-off winner (`PCA_K = 16  # the #502 winner` in `issue532_predictor_stress.py`; also `GKL_KS = (16, 8)  # k=16 headline` in `issue541_geometry_extract.py`); ≥2k probes per persona keep the k=16-subspace covariance non-singular (the Codex Statistics #1 concern); reported as a separate predictor row (never fused with cosine) so each predictor's incremental validity is read independently. **Source:** #502 / #532 (canonical `k=16` Gaussian-KL operationalization for persona distance in this project; the `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` rule covers the centering recipe but does NOT name this helper or `k=16`).
