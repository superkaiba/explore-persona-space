## 3. Hypothesis

The mediation signature and the length-matched family excess are properties of the context families (they generalize across base families); the CoT-length gradient tracks the R1 distillation lineage (flat-to-rising here).

Registered quantities (frozen primary layer conventions re-derived by the parent's rule over THIS model's 32 layers, §4.6):

- **Δ_CoT** — per-question paired-bootstrap Δskill(CoT-augmented − direct), context input, frozen per-question layer. Priors: +0.330 (#1005), +0.20 (#928).
- **C** — min(ICL, WildChat per-family usable-row rate) − 0.95, reported at BOTH the 8,192 production cap (parent-primary-comparable) and after the forced 16,384 re-generation (parent-final-comparable). **C94** := min(ICL, WildChat usable rate on the post-16,384 corpus) − 0.94 is the H2 conditioning statistic (grounding for the 0.94 bar: the task body's falsification clause "at ≥94% coverage"; it sits below the parent's realized post-16k rates 98.5%/98.7% — so a parent-like outcome clears it with margin — and would NOT have been confounded by the parent's own 8k truncation profile, whose WildChat 93.5% missed the 0.95 bar on truncation alone).
- **Δ_fam** — the length-matched family contrast (mean per-context CoT gain over the 18 ICL+WildChat contexts minus greedy nearest-neighbor same-median-CoT-length donors from the other 32; paired bootstrap over the 18 pairs; per-question regime primary), computed on the PRIMARY-corpus fits, with the 16,384-appended sensitivity re-read reported alongside (parent v4 convention; if the two land in different lattice branches, both are reported, the primary carries the headline, and the disagreement is a named caveat). Priors: +0.426 (#1005), +0.520 (#928 like-for-like).
- **ρ_len** — Spearman rank correlation of per-context CoT gain vs median well-formed CoT length over the 32 non-ICL/WildChat contexts, with the tercile profile. Priors: +0.48 / flat-to-rising (#1005); negative / falling +0.245→+0.066→+0.044 (#928).
- **Δ_MLC** — per-question Δskill(CoT-conclusion slice − answer-opening slice) on the shared remainder target, BOTH input conventions. Priors: −0.048 context / −0.107 prefix (#1005); −0.052 / −0.054..−0.092 (#928).
