### TDD: no

## Requirement

When a launcher author composes MORE THAN ONE concurrent leg of the SAME driver on one machine (pod or VM), the workflow surface must REQUIRE (a) a per-leg out/scratch root whenever the legs' split+shard chunk basenames can collide, and (b) disclosure of the isolation (or of a verified non-collision) in the launch breadcrumb.

Today no surface requires this:
- `.claude/agents/experimenter.md` "During Execution" (launcher composition, steps 1/1b/2/3) says nothing about concurrent same-driver legs sharing an out/scratch root.
- `.claude/skills/issue/SKILL.md` "Dispatch-input/env/flag preflight" probes (a)-(d) (~L4457-4509) cover staged inputs, env pins, per-leg carry-over INPUTS, and relaunch flags — but not OUTPUT isolation between concurrent legs.
- `.claude/rules/crash-fix-rounds.md` § "Per-leg out-roots" (~L290) scopes only to REGIME-keyed resume state (#1333 smoke-vs-full), not concurrent-leg chunk-basename collisions.
- `.claude/rules/gotchas.md` L131 (#1315) covers the DOWNLOAD-staging sibling (shared staging dest races) — the out-dir case is uncovered.

Incident (#2330 fu1, 2026-08-17, `epm:failure v1` at 15:50:27Z, root_cause_confirmed): both fu1 launchers inherited the same `EPM_I2330_OUT_DIR` from the P1 preamble. fuA (dense capture, train_10k shard00) and fuB (cap2048 capture, train_10k, num-shards 1) wrote IDENTICAL chunk basenames (`shards/train_10k/shard00_chunk*.pt`) into one scratch dir. fuB overwrote fuA's not-yet-flushed chunks 0000-0002; fuA's end-of-shard flush uploaded fuB's 3-layer bytes to the DENSE prefix — sha-verify hashes at flush time, so the poisoning passed SILENTLY (caught only by LFS-size forensics: 49 MB vs 508 MB) — then purged the files, killing fuB's terminal flush with FileNotFoundError. Cost: 3 poisoned dense chunks (deleted + re-capture scheduled), ~80 min GPU redo, one crash-fix round.
