## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (primary):** With distinct adapter ids, the six fractions show a real maturity gradient: corrected source implant strength (on-policy `log P(※)` trained − base, mean over the 10 eval questions) at late fractions sits well above the step-6 value, and the curve's range exceeds the stale table's same-weights replicate noise. Quantitatively: range(corrected ΔG over 6 fractions) > 2.2 nats (3 × 0.734, the SD of the stale table's six same-weights reads) AND frac-1.00 ΔG > frac-0.08 ΔG. Given the deliberately saturating recipe (lr=1e-4; #549's corrected story is "bystanders saturated within 6 steps"), late-fraction source ΔG plausibly reaches 10–30 nats.

**H2 (positive control):** Corrected frac-0.08 reproduces the stale table's 5.4717 nats within 2.0 nats (in the original run the frac-0.08 request was first-loaded and therefore correctly served; only the threading changed).

**Falsification of H1:** the corrected curve reproduces the same flat ~5–7 nat band (range ≤ 2.2 nats, every value within 2 nats of the stale-table mean 5.87). Then the stale-serve explanation for the FLATNESS is wrong and #549's AFFECTED mechanism needs re-examination — the code-read + replay legs would conflict with fresh ground truth. (Note the corrected frac-0.08 ≈ 5.47 alone does NOT falsify; it is expected under both hypotheses. See §6 outcome 4 for the binding routing rule before this conclusion may be drawn.)

**Validity kill (overrides both):** |corrected frac-0.08 − 5.4717| > 2.0 nats → an uncontrolled rig/input drift; stop and debug before interpreting any other row.

### Success / kill criteria (quantitative)

1. **Validity gate (kill):** |corrected ΔG(0.08) − 5.4717| ≤ 2.0 nats (tolerance = the #534 manifest-guard tolerance; method noise measured ~0.5 nats in #530). Fail → post `epm:failure`, debug; do not interpret rows 2–6.
2. **Fix-took gate (distance-gradient rule):** the cross-fraction exact-float-identity rates of held-out `g_logp` (15 pairs, 6×6 heatmap) must show a DISTANCE GRADIENT consistent with distinct weights: distant pairs (e.g. 0.08↔1.00) ≈ 0, with adjacent pairs allowed to sit near the same-weights nondeterminism floor (~0.19–0.27 per #549) only to the extent the weights barely changed between them. A flat ~0.19–0.27 identity rate at EVERY distance reproduces the #549 stale signature → treat as infra (residual stale serving), not finding. (A single scalar threshold cannot discriminate — stale recurrence reads ~0.19–0.27 and distinct weights read ~0, so the pattern across distances, not a cutoff, carries the verdict.)
3. **H1 confirmed:** range(corrected ΔG) > 2.2 nats AND ΔG(1.00) > ΔG(0.08). Report corrected maturity labels + corrected bystander resolutions; descriptive Spearman(frac, ΔG) over the 6 points. **Marginal-zone caution:** a corrected range in ~2.2–3.5 nats WITHOUT monotone structure is NOT declared "H1 confirmed" — report it as an effect size in replicate-SD units plus the descriptive Spearman (under a flat null, P(range > 2.2) ≈ 0.28, and the n=6 replicate-SD estimate is itself wide). Binary confirmation requires either range > 3.5 nats or range > 2.2 nats with monotone-consistent ordering.
4. **H1 falsified:** range ≤ 2.2 nats AND every corrected value within 2.0 nats of 5.87 (stale mean) → flag #549's AFFECTED mechanism for re-examination. **Binding routing rule (reconciler-adjudicated; analyzer guidance):** before invoking "#549 needs re-examination", verify BOTH (a) the flat band sits well below the ceiling — corrected ΔG far from −`b_logp_mean` at every fraction (a band pinned at the ceiling is saturation, not flatness) — AND (b) the source `emission_p` and EOS-margin curves from the glue pass show no saturation explanation (e.g., EOS margin still climbing while log-prob is capped). If EITHER diagnostic fires, route through outcome 5's three-spaces read instead of the falsification conclusion.
5. Anything between 3 and 4 (e.g., a real but non-monotone curve, or log-prob plateau with growing EOS margin) → interpret via the three-spaces contract: log-prob/logit divergence localizes softmax saturation; the SOURCE logit curve (glue pass) carries the read at saturated fractions.
