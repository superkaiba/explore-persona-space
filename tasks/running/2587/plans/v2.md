# Plan — Issue #2587: Replicate the #2564 minimal-pair battery on Qwen3.5-9B (thinking disabled) and compare the per-axis discrimination profile against the Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct parent

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the exact same bank of 984 carefully-matched prompts the parent experiment ran on Qwen2.5-7B (pairs of prompts differing in exactly one instruction or question detail), plus the two new pilot prompt sets ("answer in English/Chinese/Spanish" and question pairs differing in one content word), and run the whole battery on the newer Qwen3.5-9B model with its internal "thinking" mode switched off. Before that, I fit a fresh map from Qwen3.5-9B's prompt-end internal state to its answer internal state on 25,000 real user chat prompts, sweeping every layer to find where the map works best. Then I compare, axis by axis, how well each model's answer representation separates the paired prompts and how faithfully each model's map predicts those separations.
- **What I expect:** the newer model's map will peak in its middle layers at a slightly LOWER quality than the 7B's at the same amount of training data (that's what the earlier matched comparison #2330 found), and the axis-by-axis ordering — which kinds of instruction are carried strongly vs weakly — will look mostly the same on both models, with the language-switch axis among the strongest.
- **What would change my mind:** if the fresh 9B map beats the 7B map at matched data (contradicting #2330), or if the axis ordering is substantially different across models (some instruction types visible in one model's answer states but invisible in the other's), that overturns the "same profile, different scale" expectation and says the discrimination profile is model-specific.

### 4.2 Thinking-off contract (enforced + asserted EVERYWHERE)

Mechanism: `apply_chat_template(..., add_generation_prompt=True, enable_thinking=False)` on every render — map-fit generation, battery generation, every teacher-forced capture render, and the judge-input transcripts are the generated text (no render). Asserts, all fail-loud:
1. **Render assert (#2333 form):** the rendered prompt's last `<think>` opens a CLOSED block with EMPTY body (`rendered.rfind("</think>") > rendered.rfind("<think>")` and nothing but whitespace between) — NEVER a "no `<think>` present" scan, which FAILS on the correct thinking-off render (`issue2333_run.py:338-351`).
