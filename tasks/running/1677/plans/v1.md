### Edit 1 — `.claude/skills/daily/SKILL.md`, route-2 mandate paragraph (~line 463)

**Anchor:** the end of the single paragraph beginning `**wf-fix body Provenance mandate (durable recursion-guard signal, #1173):**`; specifically append immediately after its final sentence, which ends: `…archived as a false-premise filing).` (the #1497 artifact-state clause). Pure insertion, same paragraph, continuing the bold-clause pattern:

> **Unverified-premise labeling (#1677):** a claim the filer could NOT mechanically verify at compose time — a timing / wall-time figure, an incident root-cause mechanism, an API- or tool-behavior assumption — is NEVER written as bare fact: label it `unverified hypothesis — verify at plan time: <claim>` (naming the recall source where known), so the spawned session's planner carries it as an assumption to verify, not a premise. Worked example: write `unverified hypothesis — verify at plan time: the phase timeout is 3600s (recalled from the incident session, not read from the script)` — never "the phase timeout is 3600s" (#1660's filed 3600s was a mis-recollection vs `train_behavior_fullft.py:650`; in the 2026-07-24 wave 4 of 6 route-2 bodies carried refutable bare-fact premises — #1655/#1662/#1646/#1660 — each burning a fact-checker/critic correction round). The label COMPLEMENTS the verified-at-filing line, never substitutes for it: a mechanically-verifiable claim still gets its grep/rev-parse/git-log, and a claim you DID verify (or quote from a durable artifact) is stated as fact with its evidence — do not blanket-label; labeling everything dilutes the signal to nothing.
