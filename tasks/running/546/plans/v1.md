# Plan — #546: rank-reduction corrective re-run at r=16 / α=32, lr=5e-6 (one-variable diff vs #533)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** Same experiment as #533 — three ways of telling the model which persona it is, two personas, five seeds, four amounts of training — with one change: a smaller adapter (half the capacity). #533 dropped the learning rate and got partway there: the readings moved up toward the measurable zone but only 2 of 24 landed inside it, and never all three encodings at once. The smaller adapter is the next single knob, and it's the exact adapter size the project's training recipe names as its standard.
- **What I expect.** The smaller adapter pushes the implant in more slowly, so the 1-2-3-5 epoch ladder gets finer effective steps. I'm betting at least one persona lands all three encodings in the measurable zone at the same epoch, the anchor picker finally returns a real anchor, and the held-back headline comparison (does the role-header encoding leak less than the system-prompt encoding?) actually runs. Based on #533's near-band readings, my guess at the answer is the uncomfortable one: role leaks *more*, not less.
- **What would change my mind.** If even the half-size adapter still blows past the measurable zone between epoch 1 and epoch 2, the problem isn't capacity or learning rate — it's that whole epochs are too coarse a unit of training for this corpus. That would close the rank knob and hand the question to the sibling run that trains in sub-epoch steps.

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

**Goal (verbatim from body frontmatter — canonical, do not refine):**

> Determine whether dropping the LoRA rank from r=32 to r=16 at lr=5e-6 lands all three encoding arms simultaneously inside the [-10, -5] nat resolution band on at least one persona, so the anchor-selection algorithm picks a non-degenerate anchor and the planned per-persona x per-contrast paired-bootstrap headline test on role-vs-system separability actually fires.

**Hypothesis (inherited from body, sharpened on direction).** At r=16/α=32 with lr=5e-6, the wrong-slot teacher-forced `log P(' ※')` trajectory across {1,2,3,5} epochs crosses the [−10,−5] band with all three encoding arms simultaneously resolved on at least one persona, and `i529_select_anchor.py` returns non-degenerate. At that anchor the per-persona × per-contrast paired bootstrap on `d = log P_system − log P_role` resolves one of:

- **H1+ (role leaks less):** ≥1 of the 4 (persona, contrast) cells has 95% CI clearing zero on the positive side with mean d ≥ +0.5 nat.
- **H1− (role leaks more):** ≥1 of the 4 cells has 95% CI clearing zero on the negative side with mean d ≤ −0.5 nat. *Prior evidence favors this branch:* #533's E=1 diagnostic recomputation found all 4 cells clearly negative (pirate × plain d̄ = −0.67, pirate × padded −1.31, villain × plain −1.98, villain × padded −2.26; 5/5 seed sign-agreement each), though 4 of 6 contributing arm-cells were still sub-band.
- **H0 (no separable role contribution):** all 4 cells straddle zero.

Any of the three is a successful resolution. The directionality extension (H1−) is a deliberate, named fix to a defect in the inherited verdict code — see §3 new-code item (d) and §7.

### Divergences from parent issue #533

1. **LoRA (r, α) = (16, 32)** — the single manipulated training variable (the task's reason for existing).
2. **Two-sided headline verdict (analysis-only).** The inherited `_headline_verdict_from_per_persona` in `i464_po_analyze.py` is one-sided: H1 fires only on `ci_lo_95 > 0 AND mean ≥ +0.5`. A clean negative resolution — all 4 cells clearing zero on the negative side, the outcome #533's E=1 diagnostic makes the single most likely resolved result — would be misclassified `inconclusive`/`partial`. The `cn_i546` registration adds a symmetric H1− branch (`ci_hi_95 < 0 AND mean ≤ −0.5`), and an explicit **`mixed_bidirectional`** verdict emitted whenever `h1_pos AND h1_neg` are both true (e.g. one cell clears positive while another clears negative) — both booleans and the driving cells are persisted, so a heterogeneous-sign result is never mislabeled by code branch order (round-1 statistics critique must-fix). This changes no training, no data, no DV, no anchor algorithm, and no threshold magnitude; the body's own hypothesis text ("clears zero ≥ 0.5 nat") is direction-agnostic. Justification: defect fix in inherited analysis code, licensed by the task prompt's "inherit verbatim unless you find a defect."
3. **Partial-anchor resolved-persona analysis (analysis-only; round-1 critique must-fix, found independently by 3 of 6 critics + upheld by the methodology reconciler).** The inherited contract makes the Goal's modal success case silently unanswerable: `i529_select_anchor.py` (issue-533 branch, lines ~265-266) marks one-persona resolution as `partial_anchor: true, degenerate: false`, and `i464_po_analyze.py` (lines ~601-645) then short-circuits — `if _partial_flag or _unresolved_personas:` writes `headline_status: partial_anchor_skipped` and returns BEFORE computing the per-persona paired-d block. Since #533's asymmetry (villain banded, pirate didn't) makes one-persona resolution the single most likely success shape, the planned headline bootstrap would never fire exactly when the Goal says it must. The `cn_i546` registration therefore changes the analyzer contract: any persona with a non-null selected anchor is analyzed — compute the paired-d block + two-sided verdict over the RESOLVED persona(s)' cells (4 cells when both personas resolve, 2 when one does), persist the unresolved persona's cells as `null` with `skipped: true`, carry `partial_anchor` + an `anchored_personas` list in the payload, and define H0 over the AVAILABLE cells (all available cells straddle zero). `headline_status: partial_anchor_skipped` is reserved for ZERO resolved personas (the true degenerate case). The refusal being removed is a filename-crash guard, not a statistical necessity — each persona's 2 headline cells are computed at that persona's OWN anchor, so the resolved persona's cells need nothing from the unresolved one.

Open questions touched: `q:spec-role-header` (1.7), `q:leak-contrastive-negatives` (3.4a).

## 7. Success criteria + kill criteria (joint satisfiability checked)

**Success (the Goal fires):**
- `eval_results/issue_546/anchor_selection.json` has `degenerate: false` with `selected_anchor_per_persona` non-null for **at least one** persona (all three arms simultaneously in [−10,−5], sd > 0.5 (strict, per code), own argmax-emit ≥ 0.5 at one epoch).
- The per-persona × per-contrast paired bootstrap then resolves, over the RESOLVED persona(s)' cells (4 cells when both personas anchor, 2 when one does — the clean-result names the denominator via `anchored_personas`): **H1+** (≥1 available cell CI > 0 and mean ≥ +0.5 nat), **H1−** (≥1 available cell CI < 0 and mean ≤ −0.5 nat), **mixed_bidirectional** (both fire on different cells — heterogeneous sign, reported as persona/contrast-dependent separability, never collapsed to a single direction), or **H0** (all available cells straddle zero). All four resolutions are successful outcomes — the experiment's value is resolving the question at a non-degenerate anchor.

**Kill criterion (inherited verbatim from the task body):**
> Anchor selection still returns `degenerate: true` with `selected_anchor_per_persona = {pirate: null, villain: null}` AND <3 of 24 cells land in band on either persona AND own-slot argmax-emit ≥ 0.96 from E=1 (matching #529/#533). That would close the rank lever as a partial knob too on this corpus and route to the r=8 cell or sub-1-epoch resolution as the next move.

**Joint satisfiability:** success requires `degenerate == false`; the kill requires `degenerate == true` — mutually exclusive by construction, both reading the same single artifact. No gate demands contradictory outcomes on the same measurement (the #488-class defect is absent). **Named middle region:** anchor degenerate but ≥3 cells in band (or own-slot argmax-emit < 0.96 somewhere) satisfies neither — that is a PARTIAL outcome, exactly #533's own landing zone. It is reported as "rank moved the dose-response but did not land all three arms"; the routing is the same as the kill branch (r=8 or sibling #547's sub-1-epoch grid), with the graded band-membership count (N of 24 in band, vs #533's 2) as the dose-response read. The clean-result must not stretch a partial into either success or kill (the #533 body documents the same literal-kill-criterion-technically-false caution).

**Decision gates: none — short run / pre-verified hypothesis.** ~5h wall, ~18 GPU-h, and the kill read requires the full {1,2,3,5} grid; an intermediate stop would destroy the falsification signal. (Matches #533's no-gates decision.)

## 14.5 Concerns for the analyzer (from round-1 critique — non-blocking, carried into interpretation)

1. **Headline d must be reported on trained−base (`delta_g`) alongside raw `g_logprob`.** The base model already favors the marker under the role header by +0.63 nat under the pirate probe encoding (villain-trained wrong-slot cells; ≈0.00 under the villain probe encoding) — larger than the 0.5 nat verdict threshold. An H1− on villain cells with d̄ between −0.5 and ~−1.1 is substantially or wholly attributable to base prior. Every per-cell JSON persists `b_logprob` / `b_logps_per_q` / `delta_g`, so this is a zero-GPU numpy recomputation; the clean-result headline reads the trained−base d and reports the base gap at the anchor (marker-measurement rule's "reported trained − base" convention).
2. **Attribution framing:** a band-landing is "the r16/α32 canonical recipe pair (rsLoRA effective scale 8.00) lands where r32/α64 (11.31) did not" — never "rank capacity did it." Capacity vs slower effective dose is unresolvable here; sibling #547's sub-epoch grid is the discriminating run.
3. **Multiplicity / verdict weighting:** any-of-4 (or any-of-2) two-sided at 95% has non-trivial family false-fire (~18.5% if independent); weight 4/4-coherent-sign very differently from a lone firing cell; always report the full per-cell pattern + `sign_agreement`, and shrink the stated denominator when only one persona anchors.
4. **Bootstrap coverage:** percentile bootstrap over n=5 seeds undercovers (≈88-90% true coverage at nominal 95%); report 5/5 seed sign-agreement alongside every CI. A 3-seed descope (10 distinct resamples) is headline-degrading — say so if it fires.
5. **Degenerate-anchor disambiguation:** distinguish "band never reached" (re-saturation) from "band reached but install-gate misaligned" (own-slot argmax < 0.5 at the banding epoch — possible at scale 8.00) before routing to r=8 vs #547. Own-slot trajectories + band-membership counts separate these; an everywhere-failing own-slot is a distinct reportable under-train finding.
6. **Scope discipline:** anchor sits on a 4-point epoch grid — no claims about crossing timing (that's #547); directional claims stay sub-behavioral (log-prob-space below the emission threshold; #533 saw 0/6,000 wrong-slot argmax hits); H1− at E≈1 remains consistent with the early-trajectory ordering caveat — scope to "at this training dose"; don't interpret graded differences among saturated cells (log P compression is a softmax artifact; logits not stored); report the in-band per-question fraction at the anchor, not just the cell mean; small-|d| H0 at the anchor is not evidence against larger gaps elsewhere on the trajectory (selection-then-test conditioning).
7. **Free rig-stability check:** diff the new run's `b_logprob` (base reads, same encodings/questions) against #533's (−21.22 / −21.85 / −20.90) before trusting any r16-vs-r32 matched-cell shift plot; material drift flags a rig change confounding the cross-run read.
