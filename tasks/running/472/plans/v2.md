**Loss surface:** loss on the single ` ※` marker token + EOS only (positives); EOS only at the post-R slot (negatives). Response R never in loss → stays on-policy.
**Compute:** 1× 4-H100 pod, sequential cells. 14 cells × 3 seeds = 42 runs; ~0.45 GPU-h train + ~0.55 GPU-h on-policy eval (6 checkpoints) per run ≈ ~42 GPU-h total, wall ~14h on 4×H100 (one cell uses 1 GPU; 4 cells concurrent). Plus one-time R-gen + centroids + base panel ≈ 4 GPU-h. Pod intent `ft-7b` (4× H100).
**Evaluation:** Co-primary on-policy DVs at the post-response slot — (1) `log P(※)` trained−base (ΔG, the marker-specific **construct-of-record** for the headline), (2) full-vocab KL(trained‖base) (non-saturating backstop; supports the marker-leakage headline ONLY where it rank-agrees with logP sub-ceiling, ρ>0.5). Geometry regression run per-DV SEPARATELY (never mixing units), per-probe leakage ~ {dist-to-source, dist-to-nearest-negative} pooled across placement arms, partialling dist-to-source + base prior, fit BOTH with all-negatives and non-default-only `d_nearest_neg` (the qwen_default-dominance / identification guard, §6). Barrier vs bubble decided by which distance's partial coefficient is non-zero, admissible ONLY when the identification gate confirms real across-arm `d_nearest_neg` movement.
**Risks (top 2):** (1) the on-policy DV saturates before the count/distance effect appears even at 1 epoch — mitigated by the KL backstop + reading the earliest sub-ceiling checkpoints + a smoke gate on the anchor's checkpoint-0 implant level. (2) Held-out probe count too small to break the two-distance collinearity — mitigated by the fresh ~60-persona bank (24-panel leaves only ~9 disjoint probes; ~60 leaves ~45).

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I train the same model many times to stamp a hidden "tell" (a marker symbol) onto one character, while also showing it other characters that should NOT get the tell. I vary how many of those other characters I show, how similar they are to the target, and where I place them — then I check, on a wide cast of held-out characters the model never trained against, which of them accidentally pick up the tell.
- **What I expect:** Showing the model more "don't-do-this" characters, and placing them closer to the target, should shrink how far the tell leaks. The interesting question is the *shape* of the protection. Does it form a wall around the target, so everything behind the wall is protected? Or a bubble around each "don't-do-this" character, so only neighbors are protected?
- **What would change my mind:** If none of these levers move the leakage at all, even early in training before the model has memorized the tell, that's a real and surprising "the negatives' geometry doesn't matter" result. The last attempt at this couldn't tell, because it only looked at the very end of training when the tell had already maxed out everywhere. This version watches the whole training movie instead of the last frame.

## 1. Goal

Determine — on on-policy DVs (post-response-slot marker log-prob AND full-vocab KL) logged as a trajectory over training — how contrastive-negative *design* controls bystander marker leakage along three axes: (1) the **number** of negatives (examples/persona and number of negative personas); (2) the **distance** of negatives to the source and of each held-out bystander to the nearest negative; and (3) the **placement geometry** — whether negatives suppress leakage as a *barrier* (a shell around the source: leakage rises with distance-to-source controlling for distance-to-nearest-negative) or a *bubble* (a local ball around each negative: leakage falls with distance-to-nearest-negative controlling for distance-to-source), all net of the base-model persona prior, with barrier-vs-bubble identified via multiple matched-count negative-placement arms.

This is the canonical answer to open question 3.4a (`q:leak-contrastive-negatives`): "sweep negative-set composition (count + similarity-to-source/target), everything else matched; measure implantation strength, selectivity, and the leakage gradient."

## 3. Hypothesis

Pre-registered, in the **sub-ceiling** trajectory regime (matched source-implant slice; see §6):

- **H1 (coarse localizes).** Negatives placed closer to the source lower overall held-out leakage: mean held-out ΔG(Near-negatives arm) < ΔG(Far-negatives arm), and both < ΔG(No-negatives arm). Threshold: a ≥1-nat gap Near vs Far at a matched implant slice, permutation p<0.05. *Falsified if* Near≈Far≈No-negatives within 0.5 nat.
- **H2 (barrier / shell).** In the pooled-across-arms regression `leakage ~ d_source + d_nearest_neg`, the partial coefficient on **d_source is positive and significant** (leakage rises with distance-to-source) while d_nearest_neg's partial is ~0 — personas "behind the negative front" are suppressed regardless of their own proximity to a negative.
- **H3 (bubble / ball).** The partial coefficient on **d_nearest_neg is positive and significant** (leakage falls as you get closer to a negative) while d_source's partial is ~0 — a bystander is suppressed only if it sits near some negative.
- **H_count.** Held-out leakage falls as the number of negatives rises (examples/persona ∈ {anchor/2, anchor, 2×anchor}; neg personas ∈ {2,4,8}), read along the trajectory so a sub-ceiling effect isn't masked by saturation. Threshold: monotone in the predicted direction, ≥1-nat range across levels (logP) or the KL analog, permutation p<0.05.
- **H_null (promotable).** If neither count nor distance-to-negatives moves either DV anywhere along the trajectory, net of d_source + base prior, that is a real, promotable null about contrastive negatives (the #448 null, now properly read sub-ceiling).

The barrier/bubble discriminator is **identifiable only because** a fixed held-out probe keeps its d_source across arms but changes its d_nearest_neg between Near/Far/Spread — the cross-arm shift breaks the two distances' collinearity (the load-bearing #443 design point; a single arm leaves them confounded, which is partly why #448's single-set secondary was uninterpretable).

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Source persona

**Decision: inherit the #448/#411 villain anchor.** Justification: (a) continuity — reuse #448's centroids, generic corpus, persona registry, and the off-diagonal geometry; (b) the geometry conclusions are source-agnostic (per the body), so the Assistant framing buys nothing here; (c) villain sits at cosine −0.237 to assistant — interior to the persona cloud, giving room for both near- and far-negative arms (the Assistant is an extreme point, which would compress the far-negative arm). Robustness: NOT swept (would add a second variable); flagged as a single-source scope caveat for the clean-result.

### 4.2 Persona density — THE load-bearing decision

**Decision: generate a fresh ~60-persona bank** (target 60; floor 50, ceiling 80). Reuse #396's 24-panel only as a fallback if generation slips the budget.

Power justification (computed, §9): the geometry regression that broke #448's secondary needs held-out probes **disjoint from every arm's negatives across all placement arms**. With the 24-panel: source (1) + the union of negatives across the Near/Far/Spread arms (~14 at the matched count) ⇒ **only ~9 disjoint held-out probes** ⇒ ~27 pooled regression rows (3 arms × 9). With ~60: **~45 disjoint probes** ⇒ ~135 pooled rows — enough to fit a 2-predictor partial regression with the cross-arm collinearity actually broken. The 24-panel is the exact underpowered regime #448 fell into.

Construction: extend the existing `persona_registry` / `EVAL_PERSONAS_24` universe to ~60 by generating ~36 new short persona system prompts (Sonnet 4.5, one batched call; same register as the existing panel: "You are a {role}.") spanning occupations, archetypes, and fictional roles to **populate the distance range** — deliberately seeding "behind-the-front," "beside-the-front," and "orthogonal-far" regions relative to villain and to the negative sets. Compute centroids ONCE over the full bank (one base-model forward pass per persona × 20 questions).

### 4.3 Distance metric

Layer-10 activation-centroid cosine (headline) + layer-15 (robustness), via `analysis/representation_shift.extract_centroids(model_path, personas, questions=EVAL_QUESTIONS, layers=[10,15,20])` then `compute_cosine_matrix(centroids[L], centering="none")`. Distance = `1 − cosine`. Two derived per-probe covariates: `d_source = 1 − cos(probe, villain)`; `d_nearest_neg(arm) = min over that arm's negatives of (1 − cos(probe, neg))`; and a **non-default variant** `d_nearest_neg_nd(arm) = min over that arm's negatives EXCLUDING qwen_default of (1 − cos(probe, neg))` (the qwen_default-dominance guard — see §6 identification gate). Centroids computed once on the **base** model and reused for every cell (distances are a base-model geometric property; the design is "where do negatives sit in base persona space," not "where does training move them"). Layer-10 is the codebase default lower-mid layer; Persona-Vectors selects **layer 20** for evil/sycophancy (layer 16 for hallucination), 1-indexed — it does NOT run a {7,14,21,27} sweep (fact-check correction). L15 + **L20** (the paper's actual evil layer) robustness brackets the choice. (Source for layer choice: codebase `DEFAULT_LAYERS=[10,15,20,25]`; 2507.21509 §persona-vector extraction selects layer 20 for evil. Flagged `ungrounded` for the exact best layer → the L15+L20 robustness re-run is the mitigation.)

### 4.5 Negative selection — new distance-stratified code path

`scripts/generate_leakage_data.py::select_negative_personas` is **random** (`rng.sample`); #448's `persona_registry.select_n_bystanders` is **SHA-256-deterministic** (not cosine-aware). NEITHER selects by distance. New code path required (flag for `experiment-implementer`):

```python
# NEW: src/explore_persona_space/experiments/contrastive_neg_geometry_472/select_negatives.py
def select_negatives_by_geometry(
    source: str, placement: str, n_personas: int, bank_cos: dict,  # cos(persona, source)
    always_include=("qwen_default",),  # bare default assistant ALWAYS a negative
) -> list[str]:
    cands = [p for p in bank_cos if p != source and p not in always_include]
    cands.sort(key=lambda p: bank_cos[p], reverse=True)  # high cos = near source
    if placement == "near":   chosen = cands[: n_personas - len(always_include)]
    elif placement == "far":  chosen = cands[-(n_personas - len(always_include)):]
    elif placement == "spread":  # even quantile coverage of the cos range
        idx = np.linspace(0, len(cands)-1, n_personas - len(always_include)).round().astype(int)
        chosen = [cands[i] for i in idx]
    elif placement == "none":  return []   # no-negatives arm
    return [*always_include, *chosen]
```

The held-out panel is then the bank MINUS source MINUS the **union of negatives across ALL arms** (so every probe is held-out in every arm), per §4.2.

### 4.6 On-policy trajectory DV — the #448-saturation fix

Each cell trains ONCE; the DV is read at **6 checkpoints during the run** ({8,16,33,50,75,100}% of max_steps), each on-policy. This is NOT #448's `MarkerTrajectoryCallback` — that is **teacher-forced** ("free-gen at every step would cost too slow"), the exact off-policy probe the body rejects. Instead, at each checkpoint:
1. The trained adapter writes its **own greedy answer** R_j to each held-out (persona × question) trigger (one batched vLLM `generate()`).
2. Read the DV at the slot immediately after R_j:
   - **DV-A (logP):** vLLM `prompt_logprobs` rig from #448 `eval_one_cell.py` (`_extract_marker_logprob_and_argmax`, `_build_full_ids` with the `\n\n` MARKER_SEP + off-by-one + token-equality guards). ΔG = trained `log P(※)` − base `log P(※)` at the same slot on the SAME R_j.
   - **DV-B (full-vocab KL):** an **HF teacher-forced forward pass** (NOT vLLM — `prompt_logprobs=1` does not return full vocab) over `prompt + R_j + \n\n` using `analysis/divergence.teacher_force_batch` → `(N, 1, V)` log-softmax at the slot for trained and base, then `compute_kl_divergence(log_p_trained, log_q_base)`. Reuse the existing full-vocab primitives.
   - **Emission** = (argmax == marker) at the same slot (free from DV-A's forward pass) — behavioral cross-check + ceiling gauge.

Because DV-A/DV-B are read at every checkpoint, cells are compared at a **matched source-implant level** (§6), not an arbitrary step — the core fix for #448's saturation.

**Why code, not a model call?** The DV is a numeric log-prob / KL at a specific token slot of the model's own forward pass — there is no unstructured-data heuristic and no model-call alternative (a judge cannot read a log-prob). N/A on the contrastive-negatives model-call question; the marker-presence emission check is exact-token argmax, not a substring judge (it reads the argmax token id, the one CLAUDE.md exception for substring on markers). The persona-bank generation IS a model call (Sonnet 4.5), which is correct (open-ended text generation).

## 6. Evaluation

### Measurement validity (per DV)

| DV | Construct | Metric | On-distribution? | If proxy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ΔG (logP) | Rate the trained model emits ※ when it writes its own answer under a held-out persona | `log P(※)` at the slot after the model's OWN greedy R, trained−base on same R | **yes** — on-policy greedy R, natural post-response slot, ~60-persona realistic prompt distribution | n/a (this is the body's primary construct; subsumes emission rate) |
| KL | Total distributional shift the marker training induces at the post-response slot (non-saturating leakage magnitude) | full-vocab KL(trained‖base) at the same on-policy slot | **yes** — same on-policy R, same slot | Backstop for when logP ceilings; validation = it must rank-agree with ΔG in the sub-ceiling region (Spearman ρ(ΔG, KL) over sub-ceiling checkpoints reported; if they disagree there, logP is the construct of record) |
| Emission rate | Does ※ actually appear as argmax on the model's own answer | argmax==marker at the slot | **yes** | free legibility anchor + ceiling gauge only |
| Source-self ΔG | Did the implant install on the source | ΔG on villain's own answers | yes | validity gate (must clear 5 nats; if it doesn't, the cell is undertrained, not a null) |

### Matched source-implant slice (the cross-cell comparison anchor)

The cross-cell + regression comparison is read at the checkpoint where **source-self ΔG first reaches a target band of 8±1 nats** (a sub-ceiling implant level: implant is real and well above the 5-nat floor, but held-out `g_logprob` is still ≥5 nats below the 0.0 ceiling so the DV has headroom — exactly the regime #448 lacked). For each cell × seed, interpolate the DV at the slice from the bracketing checkpoints. **Never mix DV units in one regression:** logP and KL each get their OWN separate regression (the "per DV, SEPARATELY" rule below). A cell whose held-out logP saturated before reaching the band contributes **no row to the logP regression** (it is dropped, NOT backfilled with a KL value); its KL is read only in the separate KL regression at a pre-specified comparable slice (the 8%-step checkpoint), and any KL-derived geometry result is reported as a KL result, never relabeled as a logP/marker result. This makes cells comparable at equal implant strength rather than equal step — the load-bearing definition the body delegates.

### Statistics

- **Geometry regression** (per DV, SEPARATELY — logP and KL NEVER share a regression, see matched-slice note above): pooled OLS `leakage_probe ~ d_source + d_nearest_neg + b_logprob`, rows = held-out probes × placement arms × seeds (~45 × 3 arms × 3 seeds ≈ 405 rows; cluster-robust SE by probe). Barrier (H2) ⇔ `β(d_source)>0` sig & `β(d_nearest_neg)≈0`; Bubble (H3) ⇔ `β(d_nearest_neg)>0` sig & `β(d_source)≈0`.
- **`qwen_default`-dominance / identification gate (MANDATORY — independent of the collinearity gate).** `qwen_default` is a negative in EVERY arm, so for any probe whose nearest negative is the default, `d_nearest_neg` is CONSTANT across Near/Far/Spread — a non-responsive regressor whose `β` attenuates toward 0, which the barrier/bubble logic would misread as "H2 barrier confirmed" while masking a true bubble (a constant regressor is *uncorrelated*, not collinear, so the Pearson gate does NOT catch it). Therefore: (a) log nearest-negative IDENTITY and per-probe `d_nearest_neg` per arm; report the share of probes whose nearest negative is `qwen_default` and the across-arm SD of `d_nearest_neg`; (b) run the geometry regression BOTH with the all-negatives `d_nearest_neg` AND with the non-default-only `d_nearest_neg_nd` (§4.3); (c) **identification gate:** the barrier-vs-bubble call is admissible ONLY if `d_nearest_neg` shows meaningful across-arm movement from placement-specific (non-default) negatives — operationally, median across-arm SD of `d_nearest_neg_nd` over probes ≥ a pre-set floor AND the all-neg and non-default-only fits agree in sign. If `qwen_default` dominates the nearest-negative assignment (or the two fits disagree), report H2/H3 as **indistinguishable** rather than calling barrier.
- **Collinearity gate** (separate failure mode): Pearson(d_source, d_nearest_neg) within each arm AND pooled, PLUS VIF / design-matrix condition number — if pooled |r|>0.6 (or VIF high) the cross-arm design failed to break collinearity, fall back to tercile-bucket median tests + report the failure. Also report Pearson(d_nearest_neg, b_logprob) (≈+0.65 in #448) so a bubble coefficient identified off little residual variance after partialling b_logprob is read with appropriate caution.
- **Multiplicity (MANDATORY).** The binary "worked" headline verdict requires **Holm correction across the {co-primary DV} × {headline family: geometry / count / placement} test set**, NOT an unadjusted "any p<0.05". `log P(※)` is the **construct-of-record** for the marker-leakage headline; KL is a backstop and supports the headline ONLY where it rank-agrees with logP sub-ceiling (ρ>0.5). Outside the omnibus, report every claim DV-and-family-specific with its own (Holm-corrected) p.
- **Count effects:** Spearman of held-out-mean DV vs level, + an **exact-permutation** null on the monotone-range statistic (enumerate the unique label permutations — 3 levels × 3 seeds is small; do NOT claim 10k independent draws), ≥1 nat logP, Holm-corrected p<0.05.
- **Placement H1:** Near vs Far vs No-neg held-out-mean DV, permutation p<0.05 on the Near<Far gap (report the selected negative identities + the held-out leakage heatmap before framing a general placement effect).
- All with 3 seeds → report mean ± across-seed SE; effect must hold sign across ≥2/3 seeds (a 2/3-stable geometry call is MODERATE, not HIGH, confidence).

### 6.5 Concerns for the analyzer (from round-1 adversarial review — non-blocking, weigh at interpretation)

These were surfaced by ≥1 critic as recoverable-but-real; the diagnostics to weigh each are already produced by the plan. The analyzer MUST attend to them before framing any headline:

1. **KL ≈ −b_logprob at the ceiling.** Full-vocab KL collapses to the base-prior structure when the trained slot is a near-delta on the marker (KL ≈ −log P_base(marker)), i.e. the SAME #448 artifact that ΔG suffers. KL is "non-saturating" only OFF the ceiling. Do NOT narrate a KL effect as a recipe/geometry effect unless it is read at a genuinely sub-ceiling slice (or regressed against b_logprob exactly as ΔG is).
2. **`d_nearest_neg` is itself correlated with the partialled base prior** (ρ≈+0.65 in #448). A bubble coefficient identified off little residual variance after b_logprob-partialling is weaker than its p-value suggests — report the correlation + VIF next to the headline.
3. **Matched-slice floor/ceiling check.** Confirm from the trajectory figures that the matched slice sits where held-out DV has dynamic range (not floor, not ceiling) before reading any geometry coefficient. Report, per cell, the *step* at which the slice was hit, so a placement effect isn't silently a "how-fast-it-trains" effect.
4. **Confidence ceiling.** A barrier/bubble call that is only 2/3-seed sign-stable is MODERATE at best, never HIGH.
5. **Scope: leakage-to-default is NOT measured as a bystander.** `qwen_default` is a negative in every arm, so it is never a held-out probe — this experiment says nothing about how the geometry generalizes to protecting the bare default context (open-q 3.7). One-line scope caveat in the clean-result.
6. **H1 placement.** Show selected negative identities + the held-out leakage heatmap before framing a Near/Far difference as a general placement effect (it could be the specific personas chosen).
7. **Anchor ratio.** 200 pos vs 800 neg rows departs from the ~1:1 contrastive default; the experiment sweeps around this gated anchor, so it is a scope/interpretation caveat, not a confound — note it.

## 7. Decision Gates

One gate, justified (run is >4 GPU-h AND the saturation hypothesis is genuinely uncertain at the new 1-epoch budget AND a cheap intermediate signal rules out the full run):

## 11. Decision Rationale (per-hyperparameter What / Why / Source / Alternatives)

All trained cells share the rs-LoRA recipe; one `Source:` per unique value (large-sweep grouping rule).

- **LoRA rank r=32.** Why: matches the validated marker-implant recipe for Qwen-2.5-7B on this exact marker data; large enough to implant, not so large it over-fits the marker. Source: arXiv 2507.21509 (Persona-Vectors "rs-LoRA rank 32") + #448 (validated for villain+※). Alternatives: r=16 rejected as default (kept as the sub-ceiling fallback if the smoke gate trips); r=64 rejected (faster saturation, opposite of what we need).
- **LoRA α=64.** Why: 2α=r scaling from the same recipe. Source: 2507.21509; #448. Alt: α=32 rejected (weaker implant, but a fallback lever).
- **lr=1e-5, cosine, warmup_ratio=0.05.** Why: the Persona-Vectors / Betley-EM learning rate for Qwen LoRA; #448 validated it doesn't diverge on this data. ## 13. Success criteria (quantitative)

- **Primary (geometry, the Goal):** on the **logP construct-of-record** at the matched sub-ceiling slice, the pooled partial regression yields a Holm-corrected-significant (p<0.05, sign-stable ≥2/3 seeds) non-zero partial coefficient on d_source OR d_nearest_neg (net of the other + base prior), AND BOTH gates pass: the collinearity gate (pooled |r(d_source,d_nearest_neg)|≤0.6) AND the **identification gate** (real across-arm movement of non-default `d_nearest_neg_nd`; all-neg and non-default-only fits agree in sign). → state barrier (H2) / bubble (H3) / both / neither, with N + p. If the identification gate fails (qwen_default dominates), report H2/H3 indistinguishable. A KL-derived geometry result is reported as KL, never as the marker headline unless it rank-agrees with logP sub-ceiling.
- **Secondary (count):** at least one count knob moves held-out-mean DV monotone, ≥1 nat (logP) or KL analog, exact-permutation Holm-corrected p<0.05, in the sub-ceiling regime.
- **Coarse (placement):** Near < Far < No-neg on held-out-mean DV, ≥1-nat Near-vs-Far gap, p<0.05.
- **Validity:** every trained cell clears source-self ΔG ≥5 nats AND has ≥1 sub-ceiling checkpoint (held-out g_logprob ≥5 nats below ceiling); ΔG and KL rank-agree (Spearman ρ>0.5) in the sub-ceiling region.

## 14. Kill criteria

- **Sub-ceiling smoke gate fails twice** (anchor saturated at 1 epoch AND at the r=16/lr=5e-6/0.5-epoch fallback) → the on-policy logP DV cannot be read sub-ceiling on this implant. In this KL-only regime the logP validation anchor is GONE, so full-vocab KL (which moves for ANY post-response distributional shift, not just marker mass) may NOT carry the barrier-vs-bubble MARKER-leakage headline unless accompanied by a **marker-specific KL decomposition / marker-token-contribution diagnostic** isolating the ※-token share of the KL; absent that, the marker-leakage geometry claim is declared **unanswerable** (escalate: the marker implant on Qwen is intrinsically too fast to read sub-ceiling, a real finding worth its own promotable note).
- **Held-out panel can't break collinearity** (pooled |r|>0.6 even with the fresh bank) → report barrier/bubble as indistinguishable given the geometry, fall back to count + coarse-placement findings; do NOT over-claim a geometry result. **Likewise if the identification gate fails** (qwen_default dominates the nearest-negative assignment so non-default `d_nearest_neg_nd` barely moves across arms) → report barrier/bubble as indistinguishable; do NOT call barrier off a non-responsive d_nearest_neg.
- **All cells null on both DVs sub-ceiling, net of controls** → promotable null (the #448 null, now correctly read in the sub-ceiling regime) — this is a valid outcome, not a failure.
