## 11. Decision Rationale

- **≤6-worker thread pool.** What: `ThreadPoolExecutor(max_workers=6)` for the per-file downloads. Why: modest threading keeps request rate well under the 2500-req/5-min org quota while still parallelizing latency-bound fetches. Source: #833 r7b, commit `22388e4b3d` (ran clean); quota ceiling per gotchas.md l.138. Alternatives: `max_workers=4` (the 429-bullet value — also safe, but 6 is the validated recipe); higher counts unvalidated.
