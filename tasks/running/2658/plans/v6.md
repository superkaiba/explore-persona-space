## A7. Pre-registered length-cap amendment (plan v4 section 5) fires: production cap 4096

- Trigger, measured on the pilot (eval_results/issue_2658/gen_summary/pilot_shard*of08.json
  `cap_hit`): 25 of 131 generated cells exceed the registered 2 percent per-cell length-cap
  threshold at the pilot cap of 1024 new tokens (worst per-cell fraction 0.58,
  sycophancy MMLU-STEM authority-assertion; the wildchat_real frame dominates the list).
  Across all 6,290 pilot answers, 200 (3.18 percent) ended with finish_reason "length".
  v4 section 5 registered exactly this trigger: "realized length-cap fraction reported and
  >2% per cell triggering a pre-test cap amendment rather than selective regeneration".
- Amendment: production generation (dev and sealed-test splits, phases P4 and P5) uses
  `max_new_tokens = 4096`. Rationale for 4x rather than the 2x floor: the worst pilot cell
  truncates 58 percent of answers at 1024, so 2048 would predictably re-fire the trigger;
  under vLLM continuous batching the larger cap costs wall time only on rows that run past
  the old cap (about 3 percent of rows). Feasibility: `MAX_MODEL_LEN = 8192`, so the prompt
  budget becomes 4096 tokens; the longest pilot prompt is 2,915 tokens (p99 763), and the
  frozen prompt-budget assertion in the generator still fails loud on any production prompt
  that overflows (v4 section 5, unchanged).
- The pilot is NOT regenerated (no selective regeneration, per the registered rule). The
  pilot decoder record stays 1024. Consequence carried as a measurement caveat into every
  downstream artifact: pilot judge labels for the 200 truncated answers were assigned on
  truncated text, so the pilot discordance, power and production-N figures for the 25 named
  cells rest partly on truncated answers; production labels will not.
- Mechanism: a frozen amendment record `power_inputs/cap_amendment.json` (pilot cap,
  production cap, rule, the 25 cells with fractions, truncated-record count, disclosure
  text, this plan version). The generator's decoder becomes split-aware (pilot 1024;
  dev/test read the amended cap from the record, never a bare default), the generation
  fingerprint and the prompt budget follow the split's cap, and the P3 gate
  `measured_cap_hit_rate` reports a distinct status `AMENDED` (non-blocking, disclosure
  carried into gate_verdict top-level `amendments`) when the record exists, its production
  cap is at least 2x the pilot cap, and its cell list covers every realized over-threshold
  cell; any other state keeps today's FAIL. `AMENDED` is never `PASS`.
- Production-side duty (unchanged from v4 section 5 and CLAUDE.md): P4 reports the realized
  cap-hit fraction per cell at 4096 in the run digest and the Methodology table; a cell
  still above 2 percent at 4096 is named and its answers are judged as generated (no
  second amendment inside production; the production gates decide the cell).
- Cost consequence: the P4 generation projection in cost_report scales the pilot marginal
  rate by the realized token growth only for the truncated share; the amendment adds at
  most a few percent to P4 wall time. No change to the 80 GPU-hour envelope.
