## 1. Goal

## 3. Hypothesis

The R² gap between the assistant Q&A map and the fiction-character map is carried by one or a small set of nameable factors. Candidate factors (the rungs): **H1** role identity/frequency (the `Assistant` label vs an arbitrary name; familiar vs novel names), **H2** genre/content (functional Q&A vs fiction scene), **H3** answer structure/length (full structured answer vs one-line turn), **H4** single-responder vs multi-speaker scene context, **H5** measurement (full-answer span vs prefill-one-line; sample size).

Quantities (per model; base = primary headline, instruct = co-primary companion, both always reported):

- **G** = R²(Q&A one-line rung r1) − R²(fiction endpoint r7, per-persona mean), both at layer 19, context arm, matched-n (seed-mean over 5 subsample draws), with a 95% CI from the per-cell 1000-draw group bootstraps (independent cells; variance-sum CI). r1 (not r0) is the strong reference so the gap is measured at matched answer length; the r0→r1 delta is reported separately as the H3/H5 length read.
- **Δ_f** = the R² DROP attributable to factor f — every Δ_f is ORIENTED as strong-side minus weak-side for its rung pair (defined per rung pair in §5), so all family members share sign convention (expected ≥ 0; a negative realized Δ_f is reported as a restoration/anti-drop, never fed to the max). **Δ_max** = the largest Δ_f over the SIX-delta within-gap family (label, header, framing, content+depth, foils, label-restore — the length delta is EXCLUDED: G is defined at matched answer length, so length lies structurally outside G's denominator and cannot close any of r1−r7; it is reported separately against the r0-referenced FULL gap, where "length explains ≥half" means Δ_length ≥ 0.5·(R²(r0) − R²(r7))). **D** = Δ_max − 0.5·G, with Δ_max's CI Bonferroni-corrected over the 6-delta family (the max over rungs is a selection). Family overlap note: foils (r7−r6) ⊂ content+depth (r7−r4) — when content+depth is Δ_max, the foils sub-delta is narrated as its component, not double-counted. (Revision round 1: Statistics-critic Must-Fix — sign orientation + length-family membership.)

Falsifiable predictions: matching n alone (matched-n read) shrinks the raw gap substantially (#825's curve reads ~0.5 at n≈1000–1300 vs 0.67 at 5000); the r0→r1 length delta is positive; the pure label swap (r2 vs r1) is small in base and larger in instruct (post-training makes `Assistant` special); no committed prediction for genre/multi-speaker rungs.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Sample-size-explained ⇔ G's 95% CI straddles 0 OR G's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Single-factor-attributed ⇔ G's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side AND D > 0 AND D's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Distributed-attribution ⇔ G's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side AND D's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

(The lattice binds per model; the clean-result headline carries the base-model verdict with the instruct verdict alongside. "Distributed-attribution" is the informative negative in the task body: no single factor closes half the gap ⇒ the assistant map's strength is an interaction/emergent property of the role — this sharpens, not weakens, #825's claim.)

Registered paired contrasts: the teacher-forced-swap rungs (renamed responder r2-tf; story-labeled-Assistant s1; familiar/novel-name s2a/s2b) compare held-out R² on the SAME rows under two context renderings, and the fiction swap control compares correct vs deranged character pairing on the same rows (paired group bootstrap, the parent recipe).

## 4. Design

### 4.0 Fixed frame (held constant across every rung unless the rung IS that factor)

- **Models:** Qwen2.5-7B (base) + Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct — both, always.
- **Format:** plain text only, NO chat template anywhere (both endpoints survive template removal; the gap lives inside the plain-text regime). Q&A rungs render `User: <q>\n<LABEL>:`; fiction rungs render the #1310 labeled-script format (`<LABEL>: <line>` per line, scene header above).
- **Measurement (default M1):** prefill-one-line — context rendered up to and including the target cue `<LABEL>:`; the model generates ONE line (max_tokens 96, stop `"\n"`, T=1.0, top_p=0.95, seed 42); teacher-forced 28-layer bf16 capture of the model's own text; v_A = mean activation over the generated line. Rung r0 alone uses M0 (full answer, max_tokens 1024, same sampling) — the r0↔r1 contrast IS the answer-length/measurement rung (H3+H5), so M is held constant everywhere else.
- **Mapping arms (BOTH, per the standing rule):**
  - **Context arm** (primary): v_C = mean activation over the last ≤512 context tokens ending at the target cue (the #1310 `x_spanmean` convention; inert for Q&A contexts, which are <512 tokens — verified by a no-cap companion read on r0).
  - **Prefix arm:** v_P = mean activation over context tokens up to but EXCLUDING the final query turn (the last `User:`/foil line) — i.e. persona/scene header + all earlier turns. For rungs r0/r1/r2 the prefix is format-only (near-constant across rows), so the prefix arm is STRUCTURALLY DEGENERATE there; it is still computed and reported as the degenerate control it is (stated deviation with reason — the arm becomes informative from r3 onward, where a header exists).
  - **Secondary summary:** x_last (the cue boundary token; #1310 `_lastpos` convention) captured for all rungs, reported as an exploratory companion.
- **Why code, not a model call:** the DVs are activation-space ridge fits — nothing here is an unstructured-data classification a Haiku/Sonnet call could replace; no judge is used anywhere (no LLM-judged DV).
- **Completion provenance:** every rung's target text is on-policy from the measured model itself (single stochastic sample per context, T=1.0/top_p 0.95/seed 42; greedy is deliberately avoided — #825 round 8 showed greedy own-continuations are decoding-atypical). Teacher-forced-swap (tf) variants re-capture the SAME on-policy text under a re-rendered context — a deliberate off-policy single-factor probe, each either paired with an on-policy companion or covered by the r2 tf-vs-op calibration (see §5).
- **Data sources + realism tier:** Q&A questions = the #825 Track-S prompt set (5,000 real lmsys-chat-1m user prompts, pinned revision `200748d9d3cddcc9d782887541057aca0b18c5da`; tier 1/2 — real user prompts from an established dataset), reused verbatim so the Q&A rungs share rows. Fiction scenes = on-policy model-generated script scenes over the #1310 scenario battery (300 scenarios = seeded 20-settings × 18-situations crossing, BUILD_SEED 1310; tier 3 synthetic with a tier-4 programmatic prompt frame) — justified: (a) the object of study IS the model's own fiction-writing distribution, so on-policy synthetic is the on-distribution choice; (b) endpoint parity with #1310 requires the identical battery (single-variable discipline). Carried into the clean-result as a scope caveat.
- **Contrastive negatives:** N/A — not a behavior-implantation (declared in §0).
- **Replication fidelity:** N/A — not a replication (declared in §0); the two endpoints are internal recipes reproduced from their own committed code.

### 4.1 The ladder (rungs)

Descending chain from the strong endpoint (each rung changes ONE named factor vs the previous rung); n = 5,000 contexts for every Q&A rung (r0–r4); fiction rungs use the #1310 battery (300 scenes × 6 slots × 4 personas → realized ~1,300–3,100 turns/persona/model).

1. **r0 — Q&A full answers** (strong endpoint, #825 recipe, plain text): `User: <q>\nAssistant:` → full on-policy answer (M0). Capture stores BOTH v_A(full span) and v_A96 (mean over the first ≤96 answer tokens) in the same pass — the v_A96 re-read splits the span-summary component of H3 from the distribution component at zero extra GPU cost.
2. **r1 — Q&A one-line answers** (Δ = answer length/structure, H3+H5): same 5,000 contexts, generation under M1 (one line, ≤96 tokens). This is the ladder's strong reference for G (matched answer length).
3. **r2 — Renamed responder** (Δ = role label, H1): `Assistant:` → `Wren:` in the SAME conversations. TWO variants: **r2-tf** — the r1 answers re-captured teacher-forced under the renamed rendering (pure label change, answer text byte-identical); **r2-op** — answers regenerated on-policy under the `Wren:` cue. The r2-tf↔r2-op difference calibrates how much tf-swapping distorts R², licensing the tf-only rungs s1/s2.
4. **r3 — Persona-described responder** (Δ = persona description header, H2/H4 boundary): prepend the one-line #1310 persona description ("Wren, a warm, endlessly helpful assistant …" in the #1310 `prefill_header` form) above the conversation; on-policy one-line answers under `Wren:`. First rung where the prefix arm is non-degenerate.
5. **r4 — Fiction-framed Q&A** (Δ = fiction framing, H2 — deliberately bundles scene frame + asker label, stated as one named "framing" factor): the r3 context wrapped in a #1310 scene header (setting + situation from the scenario battery; scenario_id cycles over the 5,000 questions, ~17 questions/scenario) and the question asked by a foil speaker (`Sam: <q>`) instead of `User:`; `Wren:` answers one line, on-policy. The question TEXT is still the real lmsys prompt — content is held at Q&A.
6. **r6 — Story scenes, no foils** (Δ vs r7 = multi-speaker context, H4): the #1310 prefill recipe with foil speakers REMOVED — scene header + only the target character's accumulated lines; 6 slots/scene, 4 personas, on-policy.
7. **r7 — Story scenes (endpoint)** (the #1310 v3 recipe verbatim: multi-speaker script scenes, 1–2 foils, 4 personas × 300 scenes × 6 prefill slots, on-policy). Regenerated in-run for self-containment; gated against the #1310 v3 committed numbers (§7). The r4↔r7 delta bundles dialogue CONTENT (fiction lines vs Q&A answers) with context accumulation; a free per-slot R² read on r7 (slot-1 vs slot-6 points) separates context depth from content descriptively.

Restoration rungs (ascending, on the r7 scenes — teacher-forced re-renders, zero new generation):

8. **s1 — Story labeled "Assistant"** (Δ = label on the endpoint, H1 converse of r2): the target character's label re-rendered `Wren:`→`Assistant:` (foils keep names), same lines re-captured tf.
9. **s2a / s2b — Familiar-name vs novel-name story** (Δ = name frequency, H1 refinement): the target label re-rendered to a high-frequency human first name (`Sarah:`) vs a rare invented name (`Vexril:`), same lines re-captured tf. Both names token-length-matched to 1–2 tokens (verified in-process against the tokenizer at build time).

## 7. Decision Gates

No compute-pruning gates — the ladder itself is the deliverable, rungs are independent, and total wall-clock is ~6 h; there is no cheap intermediate signal that would rationally cancel the remaining rungs. Two RIG-ANCHOR gates (halt-and-diagnose on failure — they detect rig drift, they never prune science):

1. **Fiction-endpoint anchor (binding, evaluated before any restoration-rung fit is interpreted):** the regenerated r7 per-persona L19 held-out R² (full-n, context arm) reproduces the #1310 v3 committed values within ±0.08 per persona for base (0.137/0.148/0.147/0.106) and for the 3 complete instruct personas (0.235/0.253/0.188), AND the pooled swap read reproduces sign + specificity (correct > swap, correct-pairing positive). Precedent for both threshold sign and magnitude: sampling noise across the #1310 v3 per-persona spread is ~±0.02–0.04; ±0.08 allows fresh-sample variation without admitting a rig break. FAIL ⇒ halt fits on fiction rungs, diagnose (attribution/render/seam), fix, re-run P1/P2 for fiction cells only.
2. **Q&A-endpoint sanity gate (binding):** r0 base L19 held-out R² (full-n) ∈ [0.55, 0.90] — brackets the #1092 plain-text read (0.71–0.74) with wide tolerance since exact #1092 recipe parity is not claimed — AND the wiring check passes (own-context NLL beats derangement-shuffled context, n=200/model). The in-flight #825 naturalistic Track-S cells, if landed by analysis time, are reported as an external cross-check (non-blocking, non-gating).

Jointly satisfiable by construction (they bound different cells against independent references; no shared quantity).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Ridge recipe (GCV λ, 5 group folds, seed 0).** Why: the exact estimator both endpoints used — comparability is the point. Source: #779 `fit_h` / #825 / #1310.
- **Layer set {14,18,19,26}, headline L19 + 28-layer sweep.** Why: frozen-read discipline; L19 is where both endpoints peak. Source: #779 / #722 / #825.
- **n = 5,000 Q&A contexts (reused #825 Track-S prompts).** Why: matches the strong endpoint's committed n and prompt set exactly, making r0 an anchor and all Q&A rungs row-aligned. Source: #825 / #779.
- **Fiction battery 300 scenarios × 6 slots × 4 personas.** Why: endpoint parity — reproducing #1310 v3 verbatim is what makes r7 an anchor and the restoration rungs single-variable. Source: #1310 (v3 recipe, `issue1310_common` constants).
- **Sampling T=1.0 / top_p 0.95 / seed 42, single sample per context.** Why: both endpoints' generation convention; greedy avoided (decoding-atypical, #825 round 8). Source: #825 Track-S / #1310 / #779.
- **max_tokens 1024 (r0) / 96 + stop `"\n"` (one-line rungs).** Why: the two endpoint conventions; the pair IS the H3 rung. Source: #825 / #1310 (`SLOT_MAX_TOKENS`, `PREFILL_STOP`).
- **Context cap = last 512 tokens.** Why: the #1310 convention, inert on Q&A contexts (verified by r0's no-cap companion); holding it constant keeps the arm definition fixed across the ladder. Source: #1310 (`CONTEXT_CAP_TOKENS`).
- **20 shuffle nulls / 1000-draw group bootstrap.** Why: project standard, sized by precedent. Source: #778 / #779 / #825.
- **Matched-n = min realized cell n, 5 group-stratified draws, seed-mean.** Why: single-draw subsamples are noisy (the #825 power-curve n=2000 dip); 5 draws is the #825 round-8 matched-n convention. Source: #825 (round-8 `matched-n seed-mean convention`, subsample scheme lineage `issue931_pcms.seeded_uniform_row_draw.v1`).
- **Gap reference r1 (not r0) for G.** Why: measuring G at matched answer length prevents the length factor from being double-counted in every delta; r0→r1 carries length explicitly. Source: this plan's design (derived requirement, not a tuned value).
- **±0.08 anchor tolerance (gate 1).** Why: brackets fresh-sample variation around the v3 per-persona spread (±0.02–0.04 within-model) with 2–4× margin while still catching rig-level breaks (which move R² by ≥0.1–0.5 in every prior incident, e.g. #1310 v2 vs v3). Source: #1310 v3 numbers; #825 anchor-gate precedent (±0.05 on refits, loosened here because regeneration re-samples data rather than refitting fixed rows).
- **Familiar/novel name pair (`Sarah:` / `Vexril:`).** Why: name pre-training frequency is the H1 mechanism candidate (Kandpal et al., arXiv 2211.08411); both names must tokenize to 1–2 tokens (in-process assert).
