<h1>Task #622 — Sweep contrastive negatives to failure: extend the budget sweep beyond 1,600 until source implantation plateaus or collapses</h1>
<p><b>Goal (canonical, frontmatter):</b> Find the contrastive-negatives count at which source implantation stops growing or collapses, discriminating the schedule-length mechanism from loss-competition.</p>
<p>Goal-refinement check: the Goal is sharp and already encodes the discriminator; no refinement proposed. N/A — not a replication (no published finding is being replicated; this extends an in-project dose axis).</p>

<section class="plan-tldr">
<h2 style="margin-top:0">0.0 TL;DR (plain English)</h2>
<ul>
<li><b>What I'll run:</b> Earlier runs taught one persona to append a hidden tag to its answers by mixing in "negative" examples from other personas, and found that adding more negatives made the tag stronger only because the training simply ran longer. I'll now push the negative count far past anything tried before (8, 16, and 64 times the positives) and, at each size, also train a twin run with zero negatives that runs for exactly as long. The twin tells me whether the negatives themselves ever start to matter.</li>
<li><b>What I expect:</b> The tag keeps tracking how long training runs, and each giant-negatives run lands in the same place as its zero-negatives twin. The most likely "break" is the tag becoming so strong the model starts stamping it on everything, rather than the negatives ever crushing it.</li>
<li><b>What would change my mind:</b> If the runs with huge negative piles come out clearly weaker than their twins, and the negatives' own training signal visibly wakes up from its near-zero level, then the negatives really do fight the implant once they dominate, and the "it's just longer training" story has a boundary I can point to.</li>
</ul>

<h2>1. Goal</h2>
<p>#472 found source implant strength rising monotonically with contrastive-negative count (0→1600 at fixed 200 positives); #601 showed the mechanism is schedule length (optimizer-step horizon / cumulative LR dose), not the negatives' loss — their single loss token is predicted at p≈1 before training (CE ~1e-6, peak transient 2.2e-4). #613 made the negatives' gradient live at the real stop slot and the implant still landed where dead-slot negatives put it. What remains unmeasured is the regime where negatives <i>dominate</i>: beyond ~3,200 negatives at eff. batch 16, most batches contain zero positive rows (positive row fraction 5.9%/3.0%/1.5% at the three new levels; ~62%/39%/22% of batches contain ≥1 positive). This experiment extends the dose axis to 16:1, 32:1, and 64:1 and pairs every level with a schedule-matched positives-only twin, so "implant tracks optimizer steps" (schedule account) and "implant stalls/crashes once negatives dominate" (loss-competition account) make different, registered predictions at every level. Any outcome — co-landing through 64:1, dose-dependent suppression, a crash, or saturation-collapse into marker-spam — answers the Goal.</p>

<h2>3. Hypothesis</h2>
<p><b>H1 (primary bet — schedule account holds):</b> at every level, the dose cell co-lands with its positives-only twin: |seed-mean terminal difference| ≤ 5.58 nats (log-prob) / ≤ 2.18 logits (EOS margin) — and the negative loss channel stays dead (row-type negative trailing CE never sustains above 1e-3 nats). Quantitatively I expect terminals in the 14–21-nat range (the #601 curve extended: 13.98 at T=113, 17.04 at T=130, with diminishing growth), with trained source logP approaching the 0-nat ceiling by T ≥ 413 — i.e. the registered margin-space upper branch will govern at least the 64:1 level.</p>
<p><b>H2 (the likely "break" if any):</b> saturation-collapse, not loss-competition — at T = 813/819 BOTH members of the pair cross marker-vs-EOS argmax (emission onset) and possibly <code>r_collapsed</code> marker-spam. Both-collapse = schedule-family outcome ("the recipe breaks by over-training at this horizon, negatives merely supply the steps").</p>
<p><b>Falsifiers (loss-competition):</b> dose cell below its twin by more than the frozen band in the governing space, with the gap growing monotonically across 16:1 → 32:1 → 64:1, AND a negative-channel wake-up (sustained neg trailing CE > 1e-3 nats over ≥ 20 consecutive probe reads, vs #601's ~1e-6 baseline / 2.2e-4 transient peak). Suppression <i>without</i> any wake-up signal points at the third channel (batch composition / data ordering — the named non-loss residual) and is reported as exactly that.</p>
<p><b>Crash definition (either account):</b> (i) cross-level: dose-level terminal seed-mean below the previous level's by more than the band; (ii) within-run erosion: dense-trajectory peak − terminal > 3 nats (log-prob) or > 1.5 logits (margin) in both seeds. A capability-collapse-co-timed crash is attributed to general damage, not to negatives (see §6 guardrail).</p>

<h3>6.2 Registered decision rules (frozen at plan time)</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Space selection:</b> log-prob primary; the EOS-margin twin governs a pair's comparison whenever the saturation triage fires for EITHER member (trained logP within ~0.1 nat of 0 AND on-policy argmax-emission ≥ ~0.92; or the ΔlogP-flat-while-Δz-grows divergence signature). Inherited verbatim from #601's <code>primary_space: logp_with_margin_upper</code>.</li>
<li><b>Per-level classification</b> (i ∈ {16:1, 32:1, 64:1}), governing space: <b>co-landing</b> iff |dose_i − posonly_i| seed-mean terminal ≤ <b>5.58 nats</b> (log-prob) / ≤ <b>2.18 logits</b> (margin) — both bands FROZEN from #601's committed values (tolerance formula outputs; not recomputed from this run, so new arms cannot widen their own bands); <b>suppression</b> iff dose_i below posonly_i by more than the band; <b>indeterminate-for-noise</b> iff the pair's own within-cell seed gap exceeds the band (pre-registered branch).</li>
<li><b>Loss-competition verdict</b> requires BOTH monotone suppression (gap growing 16:1 → 32:1 → 64:1) AND negative wake-up (DV4 sustained &gt; 1e-3 nats over ≥ 20 consecutive probe reads). Suppression without wake-up → "non-loss channel" (batch composition / ordering), reported as such.</li>
<li><b>Crash:</b> cross-level (dose_i &lt; dose_{i−1} − band; for i=1 the comparator is the reused #601 T=113 value 13.98, single-seed, flagged) or within-run erosion (dense peak − terminal &gt; 3 nats / 1.5 logits, both seeds).</li>
<li><b>Saturation-collapse branch:</b> <code>r_collapsed=true</code> at a terminal on-policy read → that level's pair comparison proceeds in teacher-forced EOS-margin space; both-members-collapse → schedule-family outcome; dose-only collapse → negatives ADD dose (schedule-family); posonly-only collapse → negatives restrain (the #471-style force, would resurrect loss-competition). The emission-onset milestone (first checkpoint with z_marker &gt; z_eos at source slots) is reported per cell.</li>
<li><b>Capability guardrail:</b> ARC-C accuracy &gt; 5 points below the unit's first read, sustained ≥ 3 consecutive reads → general-damage flag; any implant collapse co-timed with the flag is attributed to over-training damage, not negatives.</li>
</ul>
