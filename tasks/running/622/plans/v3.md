## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Earlier runs taught one persona to append a hidden tag to its answers by mixing in "negative" examples from other personas, and found that adding more negatives made the tag stronger only because the training simply ran longer. I'll now push the negative count far past anything tried before (8, 16, and 64 times the positives) and, at each size, also train a twin run with zero negatives that runs for exactly as long. The twin tells me whether the negatives themselves ever start to matter.
- **What I expect:** The tag keeps tracking how long training runs, and each giant-negatives run lands in the same place as its zero-negatives twin. The most likely "break" is the tag becoming so strong the model starts stamping it on everything, rather than the negatives ever crushing it.
- **What would change my mind:** If the runs with huge negative piles come out clearly weaker than their twins, and the negatives' own training signal visibly wakes up from its near-zero level, then the negatives really do fight the implant once they dominate, and the "it's just longer training" story has a boundary I can point to.

## 1. Goal

#472 found source implant strength rising monotonically with contrastive-negative count (0→1600 at fixed 200 positives); #601 showed the mechanism is schedule length (optimizer-step horizon / cumulative LR dose), not the negatives' loss — their single loss token is predicted at p≈1 before training (CE ~1e-6, peak transient 2.2e-4). #613 made the negatives' gradient live at the real stop slot and the implant still landed where dead-slot negatives put it. What remains unmeasured is the regime where negatives *dominate*: beyond ~3,200 negatives at eff. batch 16, most batches contain zero positive rows (positive row fraction 5.9%/3.0%/1.5% at the three new levels; ~62%/39%/22% of batches contain ≥1 positive). This experiment extends the dose axis to 16:1, 32:1, and 64:1 and pairs every level with a schedule-matched positives-only twin, so "implant tracks optimizer steps" (schedule account) and "implant stalls/crashes once negatives dominate" (loss-competition account) make different, registered predictions at every level. Any outcome — co-landing through 64:1, dose-dependent suppression, a crash, or saturation-collapse into marker-spam — answers the Goal.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (primary bet — schedule account holds):** at every level, the dose cell co-lands with its positives-only twin: |seed-mean terminal difference| ≤ 5.58 nats (log-prob) / ≤ 2.18 logits (EOS margin) — and the negative loss channel stays dead (row-type negative trailing CE never sustains above 1e-3 nats). Quantitatively I expect terminals in the 14–21-nat range (the #601 curve extended: 13.98 at T=113, 17.04 at T=130, with diminishing growth), with trained source logP approaching the 0-nat ceiling by T ≥ 413 — i.e. the registered margin-space upper branch will govern at least the 64:1 level.

**H2 (the likely "break" if any):** saturation-collapse, not loss-competition — at T = 813/819 BOTH members of the pair cross marker-vs-EOS argmax (emission onset) and possibly `r_collapsed` marker-spam. Both-collapse = schedule-family outcome ("the recipe breaks by over-training at this horizon, negatives merely supply the steps").

**Falsifiers (loss-competition):** dose cell below its twin by more than the frozen band in the governing space, with the gap growing monotonically across 16:1 → 32:1 → 64:1, AND a negative-channel wake-up (sustained neg trailing CE > 1e-3 nats over ≥ 20 consecutive probe reads, vs #601's ~1e-6 baseline / 2.2e-4 transient peak). Suppression *without* any wake-up signal points at the third channel (batch composition / data ordering — the named non-loss residual) and is reported as exactly that.

**Crash definition (either account):** (i) cross-level: dose-level terminal seed-mean below the previous level's by more than the band; (ii) within-run erosion: dense-trajectory peak − terminal > 3 nats (log-prob) or > 1.5 logits (margin) in both seeds. Both crash reads are OVERLAY signatures reported alongside the per-level lattice verdict — they never override it (§6.2). A capability-collapse-co-timed crash is attributed to general damage, not to negatives (see §6 guardrail).

### 6.2 Registered decision rules (frozen at plan time; v3 — total lattice with explicit precedence)

All quantities are computed from the four-float storage contract fields of the TERMINAL ON-POLICY source reads (per seed: `delta_g` and `delta_margin` seed-means over 10 questions; `g_logp` trained log-prob; `logZ_g`/`logZ_b`; `r_collapsed`), except the wake-up rule, which consumes `rowtype_ce.json`. No analyst discretion enters any rule below.

**Definitions (per level i ∈ {16:1, 32:1, 64:1}):**

- `D_i` = (dose terminal seed-mean) − (twin terminal seed-mean), SIGNED, in the governing space (positive = dose above twin).
- `pair_seed_gap_i` = max(dose within-cell |seed42 − seed137|, twin within-cell |seed42 − seed137|), terminal, governing space.
- Band `B` = **5.58 nats** (log-prob) / **2.18 logits** (margin) — FROZEN from #601 (tolerance-formula outputs; never recomputed from this run, so new arms cannot widen their own bands; margin-band artifact-lock procedure: §13 item 2).

**Space selection (computable margin-switch; replaces the prior tilde-discretionary triage):** the EOS-margin space (`delta_margin`) governs level i's comparison iff EITHER

- (a) **ceiling-compression switch:** min(dose, twin) terminal seed-mean trained source `g_logp` > **−5.58** — both members sit within one band-width of the 0-nat log-prob ceiling, so the attainable |ΔlogP separation| is mechanically < B and log-prob cannot register any signed class (H1 itself predicts this regime at T ≥ 413); OR
- (b) **divergence switch:** either member's terminal seed-mean ΔlogZ = `logZ_g` − `logZ_b` at source slots (≡ Δz_marker − ΔlogP) ≥ **2.79 nats** (= B/2) — the log-prob read understates the logit push by at least half the band.

Otherwise log-prob governs. The on-policy argmax-emission rate is NOT required for switching; it is reported as a descriptive saturation flag only. The switch is evaluated once per level from stored floats.

**Per-level verdict lattice (TOTAL; precedence top-down, first match wins):**

- **P0 — collapse branch (evaluated first).** A member is "collapsed" iff ≥ 1 of its 2 seeds has `r_collapsed=true` on its terminal on-policy source read (one-of-two quantifier; the other seed's state is reported descriptively). If ≥ 1 member is collapsed, the level verdict IS the collapse trichotomy, classified directly from the per-seed on-policy collapse states: both members collapsed → **saturation-collapse (schedule-family)**; dose-only → **dose-accelerated saturation (schedule-family — negatives add dose)**; twin-only → **negatives restrain (loss-competition-family, #471-style)**. Teacher-forced margin reads DEMOTE to secondary dynamics/descriptive reporting at such a level — never the registered cross-condition verdict (consistent with §6.1 DV3: TF is never the leaderboard). Pair gaps are reported descriptively per surviving (non-collapsed) seed only.
- **P1 — noise branch (evaluated before any signed class).** `pair_seed_gap_i` > B → **indeterminate-for-noise**; signed classes are not evaluated.
- **P2 — signed classes.** `D_i` < −B → **suppression (negatives subtract)**; `D_i` > +B → **enhancement (negatives add beyond schedule — decisively NOT loss-competition)**; |`D_i`| ≤ B → **co-landing (schedule account)**.

Every level lands in exactly one of the six classes: {saturation-collapse, dose-accelerated saturation, negatives-restrain, indeterminate-for-noise, suppression, enhancement, co-landing} — (seven names, six reachable at one level since the three collapse names are one trichotomy).

**Crash / within-run erosion = OVERLAY, never a verdict override.** The cross-level crash read (dose_i terminal seed-mean < dose_{i−1} − B; for i=1 the comparator is the reused #601 T=113 value 13.98, single-seed, flagged) and the within-run erosion read (dense-trajectory peak − terminal > 3 nats log-prob / 1.5 logits margin, in BOTH seeds) are signature ANNOTATIONS reported alongside the per-level lattice verdict — exactly as H2 treats them; they never replace or reorder a lattice outcome. The emission-onset milestone (first checkpoint with z_marker > z_eos at source slots) is likewise reported per cell as an overlay.

**Negative wake-up rule (decidable).** Consumed field: the per-record `neg_trailing_ce` series in `rowtype_ce.json` (the #601 rowtype schema), per dose cell × seed. Evaluated on ALL THREE dose cells, both seeds, regardless of the level's lattice verdict (cheap; load-bearing only at suppressed levels). Per seed:

- **onset** = first RECORDED probe read with `neg_trailing_ce` > 1e-3 nats;
- **SUSTAINED** = ≥ 20 consecutive RECORDED reads (probe records, NOT optimizer steps; under the strided cadence — every step ≤ 50, then every 5 (T ≤ 416) / every 10 (T = 813) — 20 post-stride records span ~100/200 optimizer steps) each > 1e-3 nats;
- **LATE-ONSET UNDECIDABLE** = onset with < 20 recorded reads remaining in the series. (Arithmetic note: at 16:1, T=213 yields ≈ 83 recorded reads — 50 dense + ⌈163/5⌉; any onset after record index 63 is undecidable by construction.)

Per level: wake-up **PRESENT** iff BOTH seeds reach SUSTAINED; **ABSENT** iff both seeds complete their series with no onset; otherwise **UNDECIDABLE / MIXED** (per-seed states named). An undecidable wake-up is never reported as absent; a suppressed level with undecidable wake-up is reported "loss-competition not certified — wake-up undecidable (late onset)".

**Loss-competition verdict (cross-level synthesis)** requires ALL of: ≥ 2 levels classified suppression with |`D_i`| growing monotonically across 16:1 → 32:1 → 64:1 (or the realized subset), AND wake-up = PRESENT at ≥ 1 suppressed level. Suppression without wake-up → the "non-loss channel" FAMILY (batch composition / data ordering / optimizer-state momentum effects), reported as such.

**Capability guardrail:** ARC-C accuracy > 5 points below the unit's first read, sustained ≥ 3 consecutive reads → general-damage flag; any implant collapse co-timed with the flag is attributed to over-training damage, not negatives.
