## 11. Decision Rationale

N/A — no model training (no training hyperparameters; the load-bearing decisions are the token grammar + placements below).

1. **Token grammar `sweep_scope: selector-universe | repo-wide`.** What: a two-value enum + free-form suffix, recording the REALIZED universe. Why: the two duties prescribe exactly these two universes (implementer.md item 1 = selector enumeration; experiment-implementer.md 2b = all of `tests/`), and recording the realized (not prescribed) value makes a deviation visible — the whole point of the #1634 finding. Source: #1634 finding verbatim ("selector-universe vs repo-wide") + the two spec sites read 2026-07-24. Alternatives: free-text universe description (rejected — not machine-greppable, defeats the "mechanizable as a 1-line token check" intent); a full file-list of the swept universe (rejected — the selector enumeration can be 40+ files; the hit-file list already carries the evidence that matters).
