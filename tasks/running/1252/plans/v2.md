## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll change:** I'll add a fourth discipline to the trigger-dense review rule saying: when a reviewer finishes reviewing a security/guard artifact, its final message back to the parent session is just "PASS/FAIL + where the full verdict lives + counts" — no summary of what it found, even described abstractly — and I'll mirror that one-liner into the code-reviewer spec, the reconciler spec, and the reviewer brief template.
