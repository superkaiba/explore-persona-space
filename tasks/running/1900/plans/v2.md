# Plan — #1900: Leakage-predictor horse race at per-prompt grain (pre-FT geometry vs base propensity, with answer-side mediation)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** Using the 16,400 real-user prompts and activation stores #1768 already banked, I score how strongly each trained model expresses its trained behavior on each prompt (a graded judge for the content behaviors, a token-probability read for the marker models), then race nine cheap signals — all computable before fine-tuning ever happens — to see which one best predicts, prompt by prompt, where the behavior leaks. The only new GPU work is a small ~3-hour pass for the marker probability read and a few anchor vectors; everything else is judge calls and arithmetic on existing tensors.
- **What I expect:** The model's own pre-training tendency to show the behavior on a prompt stays the best predictor, with answer-side geometric similarity second; plain context similarity mostly predicts leakage because similar contexts get similar answers, so its signal should shrink once answer similarity is accounted for.
- **What would change my mind:** A geometry signal beating the behavioral tendency consistently across most models would mean where a prompt sits in representation space tells you more than what the model already does on it — which would make pre-training leakage audits much cheaper. Flat judge scores everywhere (no prompt-to-prompt variation in leakage) would mean this corpus can't resolve the question, and the pilot is designed to catch that before the main spend.

## 3. Hypothesis

Content race (12 arms, training-centroid anchor, layer 19), marker race (6 arms, layer 25) as replication panel.

- **H1 (champion):** base behavioral propensity (P7) has the highest across-arm median per-context Spearman ρ with the leakage DV among the 11 raced deployable candidates, and the selection-symmetric winner probability P(winner = P7) ≥ 0.5. Quantitative expectation: median ρ(P7) ≈ 0.2–0.5 (the per-unit precedents are strong; per-context is noisier); geometry candidates ≈ 0.05–0.25.
- **H2 (mediation):** answer-side similarity (P2) absorbs most of context similarity's (P1) signal: partialling P2 (and P7) out of P1 cuts P1's median partial ρ by more than half, while P2 survives partialling P1; the through-map read (P3) recovers P2's ranking (per-arm rank-agreement ρ(P3, P2) > ρ(P3, P1)).
- **H3 (mechanistic panel):** matched-text delta-answer similarity (M4) is the strongest mechanistic candidate; delta-context similarity (M3) is a registered near-null (#1768: median relative context movement 0.025).
- **Falsifiers:** H1 falsified by a geometry candidate winning per the lattice below; H2 falsified by P1 surviving both partials while P2 collapses (context-primary), or both surviving (independent channels); H3 falsified by M3 median ρ > M4's.

Registered verdict lattice (champion, content race):

DISJOINT and exhaustive: P7-retains-champion ⇔ the across-arm-median-ρ argmax over the 11 raced candidates is P7 AND P(winner = P7 across bootstrap draws) ≥ 0.5; geometry-dethrones ⇔ the argmax is some c ≠ P7 AND P(winner = c) ≥ 0.5 AND per-arm Δρ(c − P7) > 0 in ≥ 9 of 12 content arms; no-resolved-champion ⇔ otherwise.

Registered verdict lattice (mediation, content race; r_X|Z = across-arm median partial Spearman of the DV vs X given Z, P7 always in the conditioning set):

DISJOINT and exhaustive: answer-mediated ⇔ r_P1|P2,P7 < 0.5·r_P1|P7 AND r_P2|P1,P7 ≥ 0.5·r_P2|P7; context-primary ⇔ r_P2|P1,P7 < 0.5·r_P2|P7 AND r_P1|P2,P7 ≥ 0.5·r_P1|P7; shared-inseparable ⇔ r_P1|P2,P7 < 0.5·r_P1|P7 AND r_P2|P1,P7 < 0.5·r_P2|P7; both-independent ⇔ otherwise.

Registered paired contrast: every candidate-vs-P7 Δρ is paired over contexts within arm. Row-coverage: every raced candidate within an arm is evaluated on the identical realized row set — the arm's 4,000-row judge subset restricted to rows with a valid (non-dropped) DV and all candidate values present (listwise); realized n is reported per arm; bootstrap resample indices are shared across candidates and the DV within each arm.

## 7. Decision Gates

**Gate 1 — pilot judge (DV dynamic range + instrument health), before the full 180k-call spend.** Wall-clock of the full run > 4 h (judge hours + GPU phase), the DV-range hypothesis is genuinely uncertain (#1768 observed near-base register on bare corpus prompts), and a ~4.5k-call pilot (~$25–35, 1–2 h) can rule the full spend in or out — the §7 bar is met. Pilot: 2 arms ("impoliteness persona positive-only (s42)" + "impoliteness persona contrastive (s42)") × 500 judge-subset contexts × 3 draws + base under the impoliteness rubric × 500 × 3.
- **Criterion A (instrument health; §4 mechanism: the P2 per-arm drop report — counts content-drops vs transport-losses per arm):** content-drop rate ≤ 10% per arm at max_tokens=400. Branch: > 10% → resize to 600 + re-judge affected draws against a fresh cache_dir (rule 23), re-measure; this criterion compares a count against a threshold and self-counts trivially (drops/1,500 draws per arm).
- **Criterion B (DV dynamic range; §4 mechanism: P3's per-arm descriptive block computes SD and quantiles of the per-context graded mean):** an arm is race-eligible iff SD(per-context graded mean) ≥ 3 points on the 0–100 scale AND ≥ 5% of contexts score ≥ 10. Grounding: the graded scale's judge noise floor is ~2–3 points at N=3 draws (split-half across draws, measured in the pilot itself); a per-arm SD below that floor means ranking candidates against noise. Threshold sign + value: SD ≥ 3 (above noise), share ≥ 5% (non-degenerate upper tail).
- **Criterion C (judge reliability; §4 mechanism: split-half across draws in the pilot block):** per-arm split-half reliability of the graded mean ≥ 0.5 at N=3; below → the descope lever raises N to 5 on a reduced 3,000-context subset (spend-neutral: 12×3,000×5 = 180k).
- **Branching (jointly satisfiable; no all-or-nothing):** both pilot arms pass B → proceed, applying the ladder only to arms later found flat; one passes → replace the flat arm per the §4 ladder and proceed; both flat → pilot 2 replacement arms (next po + one FT arm, +3k calls) before descoping; if after the ladder < 6 content arms are eligible, the content race runs on the eligible set with coverage shrinkage carried per check 11b, and the marker race (judge-free, continuous DV with guaranteed range in log-prob space) becomes the lead panel — a re-scope, never a silent kill. Precedent coherence: the po arm is the high-range bet (positive-only leaks more, contrastive-negatives rule #18/#207) and the con arm the low bet — if even the po arm is flat, the ladder's remaining rungs are unlikely to differ, which is exactly when the marker-lead re-scope (not more spend) is the right branch.

No other gates — the GPU phase is short and pre-verified by smoke + the P1a adapter-application assert (a smoke-run requirement, not a gate).

**Kill criteria (halt-and-report; what result would kill the thesis-test):**
1. Gate 1's replacement ladder exhausts with < 6 race-eligible content arms AND the marker panel is also flat (marker Δ log P DV SD ≈ 0 across all 6 arms — not expected in log-prob space): kill — this corpus cannot resolve per-context leakage prediction; report as a determinate negative on feasibility, no further spend.
2. P0's sha-join intersection falls below 0.9 × 16,400 for more than 6 of the 18 selected arms (stores unusable at the required grain): kill — reuse-fitness failure; halt and report, never substitute regenerated captures inside this task.
3. The P1a adapter-application smoke misses the #1481 verdict-manifest window by > 1 nat for ≥ 2 marker arms (adapter identity broken): kill the marker panel (content race proceeds), report the identity failure.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Judge instrument (graded 0–100, N=3 draws, temp 1.0, reason-then-score, max_tokens=400, Sonnet 4.5, Batch API).** Why: graded-primary for predictor targets (dichotomization attenuates ~0.798). Source: CLAUDE.md § Measurement validity + `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md` rules 1/4/7/23; max_tokens=400 with post-pilot drop re-measure per #1739 (5.4% truncation residue at 400 → the re-measure binds). Alternatives: binary rate primary (rejected — attenuation + floor censoring); N=5 (descope lever if reliability < 0.5, spend-neutral at 3,000 contexts).
- **Judge subset = 4,000 contexts × 12 arms × 3 draws (+ base 36k).** Why: the body's spend envelope (150–190k calls); 4,000/arm gives ρ SE ≈ 0.016 — far below the effects of interest. Source: task body §Design/cost. Alternatives: 16,400 full grid (≈2.4M calls, oversized per the body); 2,000 contexts (halves spend but weakens partials near collinearity).
- **Arm count/pick (12 content + 6 marker, criteria in §4).** Why: spans behaviors × contexts × regimes × methods with a seed pair, inside the envelope; po arms included as the high-leakage bets. Source: task body arm-pick mandate + `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` (#18/#207 positive-only leakage). Alternatives: 16 arms (envelope-max; kept as pilot-gate ladder headroom).
- **Ridge refit recipe (23-pt λ grid 1e-3–1e8, val-selected, n=11,600/800 split).** Source: #1768 (`issue1768_fit.py`; base R² 0.499–0.501 at layer 19). Alternatives: reuse the 15k-fit maps — impossible (weights not persisted) and wrong (leak-through-M guard requires judge-exclusion).
- **Primary layers (content 19, marker 25), pre-registered.** Source: #1768 primary-layer convention (content resolved best at 19; marker read at 25). Alternatives: max-over-layer (rejected — would trigger selection inflation; layers 14/25 stay in the exploratory dump).
- **Bootstrap B=2,000 paired, permutation 1,000, winner-per-draw.** Source: #1768 horse-race CI convention (B=2,000); `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md` (per-draw same-selection default). Alternatives: B=500 (descope lever).
