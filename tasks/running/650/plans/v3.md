# Plan — Task #650: Rank-1 MLP LoRA read/write geometry across dose: pre-existing vs intruder direction, marker vs sycophancy

Parent: #621 · kind: experiment · model: Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct · plan v3

---

## §0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** I train a tiny one-direction edit into the model that teaches it one of two habits — either drop a rare symbol at the end of its answer, or agree with whatever the user claims — and I do it at two strengths. Then I look inside the edit to ask three things: does the edit's "output" point at a direction the base model already had, or a brand-new one it had to invent? Does the edit's "input detector" learn to recognize the persona it's supposed to fire for, or does it stay the random noise it started as? And does the answer change when the habit is a clean programmatic symbol vs a messy realistic behavior?
- **What I expect.** I bet the output direction is a *new* (intruder) direction in the weights even though it still aims at the right behavior, the input detector barely moves from its random start even at the higher strength, and this story holds for both habits — i.e. the edit is an "ungated write," not a "learned detector," confirming and extending what #621 saw at low strength only.
- **What would change my mind.** If the input detector clearly rotates toward the persona at the higher strength (a learned detector appearing only under more training); or if the output direction lands on a direction the base model already represented internally — and I'll only believe that for the agreeing behavior if it lines up with the *content* of agreement, not merely with "the prompt had an extra instruction sentence," which I now test separately; or if the clean symbol and the messy realistic behavior give opposite verdicts. Any of those would overturn the "ungated write" picture and is the result I most want to find.

## §1. Goal

> Determine whether a rank-1 LoRA behavior implant's WRITE direction is a pre-existing base direction or a new (intruder) direction — in BOTH the weight-space sense (max cosine of the LoRA write singular vector to the base weight matrix's singular vectors) and the activation-space sense (cosine of the residual-space write to a pre-existing base residual-stream concept direction, and to the unembedding concept row) — and whether its READ direction rotates from random init toward the source-context direction as implant dose increases (a learned detector) or stays ~random (selectivity inherited from base geometry), across two behaviors of contrasting realism (programmatic marker vs on-policy sycophancy), using a rank-1 MLP up_proj+down_proj placement that puts the read in residual input space and the write in residual output space.

---

## §2. Hypotheses under test

Two mechanistic pictures of what "training a behavior into a context" does, decided per behavior, with the marker-vs-sycophancy transfer as the second axis:

- **H-ungated/intruder.** The WRITE is one *new* direction near-orthogonal to base weight geometry (an intruder dimension, Shuttleworth et al. 2410.21228), the READ stays ≈ its random init even at higher dose, and per-context selectivity is inherited from pre-existing base geometry rather than a learned read.
- **H-pre-existing-detector.** The WRITE aligns with a pre-existing base *concept* direction (the OOCR "find-and-amplify" hypothesis, Wang et al. 2507.08218), and the READ rotates toward the source-context direction as dose increases (a learned detector); selectivity is learned.

Per-DV predictions:

| DV | H-ungated/intruder predicts | H-pre-existing-detector predicts |
|---|---|---|
| **DV-1 read rotation** | `cos(a_up_trained, a_up_init)` ≈ 1 (stays at Kaiming start) at BOTH low and high dose; `cos(a_up∘γ, v_source)` ≈ wrong-context null | low-dose `a` near init but high-dose `cos(a_trained, a_init)` drops materially and `cos(a∘γ, v_source)` rises above null |
| **DV-2 write→output concept** | high `cos(b_down, W_U[concept])` — the write still points at the OUTPUT vocabulary direction (this is what the loss installs; HOLDS under either hypothesis, so it is a manipulation check, not a discriminator) | same: high (not discriminating) |
| **DV-3 weight-space intruder** | `max_i cos(b_down, u_i^base[down_proj])` ≈ the **max-matched null p95** (a NEW singular direction — its max-over-thousands statistic is indistinguishable from a random direction's max-over-the-same-set) | a clear peak ABOVE the max-matched null p95 (write ∈ base column space, beyond what max-statistic inflation alone produces) |
| **DV-4 activation-space concept** | `cos(b_down, d_behavior_base)` AND `cos(b_down, d_format_base)` ≈ their respective permutation-null floors (write is not a pre-existing internal concept) | a clear positive cosine on the content-isolating `d_format_base` above its null AND surviving residualization on the instruction axis (write coincides with a base internal *agreement-content* direction, not merely the instruction-presence direction) |
| **DV-5 selectivity vs base geometry** | firing predictor `a·v_c` loses to plain `cos(v_c, v_source)` (selectivity is base-geometry-inherited) | firing predictor beats plain geometry (learned read does the gating) |

Note DV-3 (weight-space) and DV-4 (activation-space) answer DIFFERENT questions and can BOTH be true or false; the body's central open point is that they were never both run, and the literature contests only the activation-space sense.

**Literature anchors (cited in body; verified abstracts).**
- **2410.21228 (Shuttleworth, Andreas, Torralba, Sharma — "LoRA vs Full Fine-tuning: An Illusion of Equivalence", NeurIPS 2024).** Abstract verbatim: LoRA weight matrices "have new, high-ranking singular vectors, which we call *intruder dimensions*, while those trained with full fine-tuning do not." Intruder dims present at the low-rank regime, vanishing toward full-FT. **Grounds DV-3 H-intruder prediction (write is a new singular direction, indistinguishable from a random direction under the max-matched null).**
- **2402.16842 (Zhu et al. — "Asymmetry in Low-Rank Adapters of Foundation Models").** Abstract verbatim: "`A` extracts features from the input, while `B` uses these features to create the desired output … a random untrained `A` should perform nearly as well as a fine-tuned one." Validated on LLaMA-2, RoBERTa, BART, ViT. **Grounds DV-1 H-ungated prediction (read stays ≈ random).**
- **2507.08218 (Wang et al. — OOCR "find-and-amplify" steering-vector hypothesis).** The competing H-pre-existing picture: training amplifies a pre-existing concept direction. The body's deep-research verdict: C3 (write is a *pre-existing base concept* direction) is CONTESTED even in the source paper.
- **2401.05566 (sleeper agents — conditional circuits), 2511.04875 (rank-1 self-awareness), 2506.11618 (convergent EM):** cited as the conditional-vs-ungated framing; none tests Qwen-2.5-7B persona/behavior implants or probes the write against a base ACTIVATION direction — the gap this task fills.

No surveyed paper runs both the weight-space intruder test AND the internal base-concept activation test on the same adapter; #621 ran neither and only compared the write to the OUTPUT unembedding row.

### Open point #4 resolved — persona panel + dose

**Source persona: `police_officer`** (single source for both behaviors). Rationale: police_officer was #621's strongest-firing, most-installable source (read-arm fire_read ρ 0.525, the 2 of 3 cells crossing ρ≥0.5), so it gives DV-5 the best chance of a non-degenerate read signal AND is the source #621's high-dose follow-up named. Using one source keeps the 12-cell budget small and matches #612's two-source on-policy precedent (police_officer is in #612's panel universe). **One source is a deliberate scope choice (flag §-assumptions A7), not a sweep** — the geometry DVs are per-(behavior,dose,seed), and #621 already established the cross-source picture at low dose; this task's single manipulated structural axis is placement, the behavior axis is marker-vs-sycophancy, the dose axis is low-vs-high.

**Contrastive-negative panel (disjoint from source — HARD invariant).** Reuse #621's `UNIFIED_NEGATIVE_PANEL = {assistant, programmer, chef, kindergarten_teacher}` — 4 close negatives, always including the bare default `assistant`. `panel ∩ {police_officer} = ∅` (verified: police_officer not in panel). Assert disjointness at import AND on the realized training mix (`.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` disjointness invariant; #527/#538 librarian incident).

**Marker dose (band-stop).** low = `MarkerBandStopCallback` band `[5,12]` nat (the default, `Source:#621`); high = band `[14,20]` nat (`marker_band_low_nats=14, marker_band_high_nats=20`, #538's deep-dose dial — #538 ran three bands `[5,12]`/`[9,13]`/`[14,20]` and the deep band is the canonical high-dose, `Source:#538`). Gate on bystander resolution, not source emission. Per-step trajectory persisted (`marker_band_trajectory_path`) — four-float storage per probe, with enough per-step information to reconstruct emission-rate and EOS-margin behavior (not just terminal band-entry status — Codex methodology concern).

**Sycophancy dose (open point #1 resolved — the install-strength analog).** Sycophancy has no single-token log-prob handle, so the dose dial is the **own-source agreement-rate lift over base on a held-out false-claim probe set**, the #612-validated install signal (`BAND_ENTRY_THRESHOLD = +0.60`, `band_entry.py`). Operationalization:
- *Signal:* `Δagree = (trained agreement rate − base agreement rate)` on a held-out 30-claim probe set (disjoint from training claims), self-persona, Claude-Haiku judge (κ=0.869 vs Sonnet double-judge per #612), free-generation (10 rollouts/claim).
- *Dose dial = epochs* (dose-to-target on the saved epoch checkpoints, per `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` § dose-to-target; #612 showed bands are set by epoch 1, so save EVERY epoch checkpoint and read each cell at the checkpoint whose `Δagree` enters the target band).
- *Two target bands:* **low = `Δagree ∈ [0.30, 0.45]`**, **high = `Δagree ∈ [0.55, ceiling]`** (`Source:#612` — on-policy single-turn plateaus ~0.60–0.66, so [0.55,ceiling] is reachable on-policy and [0.30,0.45] is a genuinely lower install; the +0.60 band-entry threshold sits inside the high band). A cell that never reaches its low-band floor at any saved checkpoint is an install failure, reported as such (not a zero bar) — the #612 install-failure convention. The high band has only ~0.05–0.10 of headroom above its floor before the ~0.60–0.66 plateau ceiling — the per-epoch Δagree trajectory is watched and a near-miss / non-separation of the two doses on-policy is itself a reportable outcome, not a false null (§14 concern 2).
- *Why this mirrors the marker band-stop:* both pin install strength to a measurable behavioral target so "low" and "high" are matched ACROSS the two behaviors as install strength, not as a step count — the dose-confound control the leakage-measurement rule mandates.

- `target_modules = (up_proj, down_proj)`, all 28 layers (`layers_to_transform=None`), r=1, α=8, `use_rslora=True`, dropout 0.0, `modules_to_save=None`, no `lm_head`/`embed_tokens` (gauge assert before any logit readout per `marker-leakage-measurement.md`).

- `down_proj`: write `b_down` is in residual-OUTPUT space → the right space for DV-2/3/4. Its READ is the 18944-d intermediate — never read as a context direction.
- Capture `a_init` from the earliest persisted checkpoint (A_init=Kaiming, B_init=0 on a fresh PEFT attach). Persist per-step checkpoints every 10 steps (marker) / every epoch (sycophancy) so the across-dose read and the a(t) ladder are recoverable off-pod. **Implementer hard assert (§14 concern 1):** the step-0 / earliest-checkpoint adapter is captured BEFORE any optimizer step, and DV-1's `a_init` basis loads from that path; if no near-init checkpoint exists, DV-1 is unmeasurable — fail loud, do not silently proceed. (The plan does NOT depend on a `TrainLoraConfig.save_initial_adapter` field, which does not exist in the codebase — see §14.)

**Sycophancy (on-policy-first — `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md`).** Per cell, build positives by the #612 elicitation ladder against the BASE model on the training-claim set under `police_officer`:
1. Tier 1 bare persona prompt; 2. Tier 2 agree-instruction in system prompt, **stripped before training**; 3. Tier 3 2-4-word agreement prefill. Prefer lowest tier that fills quota; record per-row tier.
- **Judge-filter** every completion (Claude Haiku, content-level agreement with the false claim; `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md`). Only judge-accepted rows enter the positive pool. **Retain the judge-labeled DISAGREEING tier-2 completions (post-judge, pre-filter) — they are NOT discarded; they feed `d_format_base` (DV-4 content-isolating contrast, below) at zero extra GPU cost.**

Target 400 positives; floor = 320. police_officer is a single source so there is no cross-source equalization, but apply the floor: below 320 accepted after the retry budget → the sycophancy arm is dropped and reported as an install/yield failure (predicted by the source-side baseline read, §4 baseline propensity). Retry budget sized to HIGH yield risk (agreement with false claims conflicts with alignment training).
- Negatives: 400 across the 4-persona panel, on-policy base-model natural CORRECTIONS under each negative persona on the SAME claims (loss on the full correction turn). 1:1 ratio. Provenance: positives = `on-policy (tier 1/2/3, recorded)`; negatives = `on-policy (natural corrections)`.

**Contrastive-negatives default satisfied** for both behaviors (1:1, ≥4 close negatives incl. default assistant, panel disjoint from source, on-policy negatives). No exemption invoked.

### DV-4 concept direction (open point #3 resolved — NON-CIRCULAR, TWO reference directions)

DV-4 reads the write against TWO base-model reference directions, both built from the BASE model ONLY, never the adapter. The second direction (`d_format_base`) was added to address the Phase 2 alternatives critic ensemble's instruction-presence confound (Must-Fix #2): `d_behavior_base`'s dominant axis is "the system prompt contains an extra agree-instruction sentence" (instruction-presence / agreement-format), NOT necessarily a pre-existing INTERNAL sycophancy concept, so a positive `cos(b_down, d_behavior_base)` alone cannot distinguish "write amplifies a base sycophancy concept" from "write aligns with the format/instruction direction the elicitation prompt adds." `d_format_base` cancels the instruction axis and isolates agreement *content*.

- **`d_behavior_base` (instruction-contaminated reference):** contrast-of-means in the residual stream BEFORE the LoRA'd MLP — i.e. the `post_attention_layernorm` output (the `down_proj`/`up_proj` residual-input tap the #604 extractor already adds), at the layer band the write lands in (max-over-L20–L27 layer where `b_down` peaks, matching #621's write-extraction band). `d_behavior_base[l] = mean(h_base | agree-instructed contexts) − mean(h_base | same prompts under the bare persona)`, base model, no adapter. ≥200 prompt pairs.
- **`d_format_base` (content-isolating reference — NEW, Must-Fix #2):** contrast-of-means over **agree-instructed AND judge-labeled-DISAGREES** completions VS **agree-instructed AND judge-labeled-AGREES** completions — BOTH sides under the IDENTICAL agree-instruction system prompt, so the instruction-presence axis cancels and only the agreement *content* remains. `d_format_base[l] = mean(h_base | agree-instructed, judged-AGREES) − mean(h_base | agree-instructed, judged-DISAGREES)`, base model, no adapter, identical pipeline + activation cache + write band L20–L27 as `d_behavior_base`. Source data: the tier-2 elicitation (§4 above) already generates AND judge-labels both agreeing AND disagreeing completions under the agree-instruction prompt — the disagreeing rows (post-judge, pre-filter, retained above) feed this contrast at ~zero extra GPU cost (base activations at the write band are already collected). Non-circular: never reads the adapter.
- **Marker:** behavior-exhibiting = base completions that DO end in ` ※` is impossible (base never emits it), so the marker concept direction is the unembedding row `W_U[marker]` PULLED BACK to the residual stream — i.e. for the marker arm DV-4 degenerates into DV-2 and is reported as N/A-for-marker (the marker has no pre-existing INTERNAL concept; this is itself the expected H-intruder reading and is stated as such). The marker arm's pre-existence test is DV-3 (weight-space) only.
- **Decision rule for the DV-4 hero figure (sycophancy):** a positive DV-4 supports H-pre-existing ONLY if `cos(b_down, d_format_base)` is positive ABOVE its label-permutation null AND survives a partial-cosine residualization on `d_behavior_base`'s instruction-axis component (i.e. the write carries new agreement-content information beyond what an instruction-presence direction would explain). A positive `cos(b_down, d_behavior_base)` with `cos(b_down, d_format_base)` ≈ null is read as "write aligns with the instruction/format direction, NOT a pre-existing internal sycophancy concept" — the alternative-explanation outcome, NOT support for H-pre-existing. The DV-4-≈-null arm (both references at null) is robust either way (if the write fails to align even with the instruction-contaminated direction, H-ungated holds a fortiori).
- **Probe set:** the #612 audited false-claim pool (held-out from training claims for the EVAL-side read).
- **Layer to read at:** residual stream BEFORE the LoRA'd MLP = `post_attention_layernorm` output, at the write-peak layer band L20–L27 (matched to where DV-2/3 read `b_down`).

### DV-2 sycophancy unembedding proxy (open point #2 resolved)

Marker DV-2 = `cos(b_down, W_U[" ※"])` (id 83399), exactly #621. Sycophancy has no single agreement token, so the unembedding-direction proxy is a **logit-difference direction**: `d_U^syco = mean_k [ W_U[t_agree,k] − W_U[t_neutral,k] ]` where `{t_agree,k}` are judge-validated agreement-opener tokens (the #612 opener-phrase first tokens: "Absolutely", "Yes", "Correct", "You're", "Indeed" — first sub-token of each, judge-confirmed as agreement openers on held completions; "Right" was considered but #612 only records it as the second sub-token of "You're right…", so it's omitted to keep all `t_agree,k` as verbatim openers from #612) and `{t_neutral,k}` are matched neutral first-tokens ("The", "Well", "Actually", "It", "There", "Let"). DV-2-syco = `cos(b_down, d_U^syco)`, max over L20–L27. **Null:** frequency-matched wrong-token-pair direction (200 random token-pairs with matched `‖d_U‖`), p95. This is candidate (c) from the body's menu (logit-difference direction), preferred over a single agreement-token row because no single token carries sycophancy; grounded on #612's measured opener phrases. Like marker DV-2, this is a manipulation check (it holds under either hypothesis), not a discriminator — DV-3/DV-4 are the discriminators.

### Why code, not a model call?

The judge filter on sycophancy completions (open-ended agreement-with-false-claim classification over unstructured text) IS a model call — Claude Haiku per project policy, exactly #612's `judge.py` (κ=0.869). All geometry DVs (cosines, SVD, contrast-of-means, max-matched null) are deterministic linear algebra over tensors — code, not a model call (no unstructured-data heuristic). No rule-based judge over text anywhere in the design.

## §7. Controls / kill criteria

**Acceptance criteria (the experiment answered its Goal).** The DV-3 (weight-space intruder, against the max-matched null) + DV-4 (activation-space concept, sycophancy, content-isolating two-reference test) + DV-1 (read rotation across dose) verdicts are reported per behavior with bootstrap CIs, and the marker-vs-sycophancy contrast is stated. A clean answer is any of the body's example outcomes, e.g. "write is an intruder direction (DV-3 at the max-matched null) yet points at the output concept (DV-2 high), selectivity is base-geometry-inherited (DV-5 geometry wins), the read does/does not rotate at high dose (DV-1), and this holds/differs for sycophancy."

**What falsifies each hypothesis.**
- H-ungated/intruder is falsified if (DV-3 shows a clear peak ABOVE the max-matched null p95 on either behavior — NOT merely above a single-direction floor) OR (DV-1 `cos(a_t,a_init)` drops materially low→high dose AND `cos(a∘γ,v_source)` rises above the wrong-context null) OR (DV-5 firing predictor beats plain geometry, read only at high dose where DV-1 tests rotation — see §14).
- H-pre-existing-detector is falsified if (DV-3 ≈ the max-matched null) AND (DV-1 stays ≈ init at both doses) AND (for sycophancy, DV-4's content-isolating `cos(b_down, d_format_base)` ≈ its permutation null, so a positive `d_behavior_base` cosine is the instruction-presence direction, not a base concept) AND (DV-5 geometry wins).

**Diagnostic that settles the headline.** DV-3 read at high dose against the max-matched null: if the write is at the null-max p95 even when trained hard (high band), the intruder reading is robust to dose; if it climbs clearly above the null-max p95 only at high dose, the verdict is dose-dependent. For sycophancy DV-4, the headline is settled by whether `d_format_base` (content-isolating) — not just `d_behavior_base` (instruction-contaminated) — carries the positive.

**Kill criterion (the smoke gate).** If the install smoke (§4) shows MLP `(up,down)` rank-1 installs NEITHER behavior to its band under the recipe (marker never enters [5,12], sycophancy never reaches +0.30), STOP after the 2 smoke cells: report "MLP up/down rank-1 does not install to band under recipe R" as a decidable, reportable outcome (the `marker-training-recipe.md` "arms never co-resolve is a reportable outcome" principle), do not burn the remaining 10 cells. This is the only gate — see §-justification below.

**Gate justification (planner.md §7 — default no gates).** One gate only (the install smoke), justified because: the full sweep is >16 GPU-h, the MLP-placement install is genuinely untested for either behavior (no prior issue established it), and the smoke cheaply rules out the full sweep (a no-install MLP placement makes all 5 DVs unreadable). No other gates — the DVs are pre-verified geometry reads on installed adapters.

## §11. Decision Rationale (load-bearing hyperparameters)

All training hyperparameters inherit #621's validated marker recipe for Qwen-2.5-7B (the inherit fast-path: #621's clean-result validated these for this exact model + marker data; the literature re-search is reserved for genuinely changed values, i.e. placement + sycophancy dose).

- **lr = 5e-6, cosine schedule, warmup 0.03.** *Why:* the only demonstrated CLEAN marker window (lr ≤5e-6; ≥1e-4 collapses to an unconditional repeater) — strength bought through STEPS, not lr; the over/under dial. *Source:* `#621` (validated on Qwen-2.5-7B marker) + `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md`. *Alternatives:* 1e-5 (#480 saturated all 6 marker adapters — rejected); 2e-6 (#519/#520 floor/jitter).
- **r = 1, α = 8, use_rslora = True (s = α/√r = 8), dropout 0.0.** *Why:* r=1 removes SVD ambiguity (a IS the read, b IS the write — the whole point); α=8 rsLoRA is #621's exact gauge, reproducible on the current stack. *Source:* `#621` adapter_config (read from HF: `r=1, lora_alpha=8, use_rslora=True, lora_dropout=0.0`). *Alternatives:* r=8/16 (SVD ambiguity, #604 — rejected; r is FIXED at 1 by the task).
- **batch 4 × grad_accum 4 (eff 16), max_length 2048, AdamW, bf16, weight_decay 0.0.** *Why:* #621's exact training config; max_length 2048 fits source system prompt + question + response prefix + marker slot. *Source:* `#621` Reproducibility card. *Alternatives:* none considered — inherited verbatim (not the manipulated variable).
- **Marker band-stop low `[5,12]` nat / high `[14,20]` nat.** *Why:* [5,12] is the clean usable window (source above base, bystanders below argmax ceiling); [14,20] is #538's deep-dose dial — across-dose read #621 flagged (#538 ran shallow [5,12] / mid [9,13] / deep [14,20]; deep is the canonical high). *Source:* `#621` (low) + `#538` (high — verbatim band). *Alternatives:* a single dose (defeats the dose axis — rejected); the mid [9,13] band (rejected — too close to low for a clean dose contrast); emission-crossing stop (the band-stop is the right lever for a NON-emission geometry read, `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md`).
- **Marker recipe overrides parent parity.** *Why:* the fresh marker arm uses the band-stop stopping recipe regardless of any non-marker comparison; the sycophancy arm uses dose-to-target. This is a measurement-validity requirement, not a tunable. Both behaviors are matched on INSTALL (band), not steps. *Source:* `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` § Recipe vs parent-parity. *Flag:* §-assumptions A6 (deliberate; the parent #621 is itself a marker run so there is no parity break here — noted for completeness).
- **Sycophancy dose-target self-implant Δagree `[0.30,0.45]` (low) / `[0.55,ceiling]` (high), dial = epochs, save-every-epoch.** *Why:* the on-policy install-strength analog of the marker band-stop; #612 measured on-policy single-turn plateau ~0.60–0.66 with band-entry +0.60, so [0.55,ceiling] is reachable and [0.30,0.45] is a genuine lower install; matching install (not epochs) across behaviors is the dose-confound control. *Source:* `#612` (`BAND_ENTRY_THRESHOLD=+0.60`, measured dose bands) + `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` § dose-to-target. *Alternatives:* a contrast-of-means log-prob direction on agreement tokens (rejected as the DOSE dial — it is the DV-2 proxy, not an install signal; agreement RATE is the behaviorally validated dial); fixed epochs (rejected — #612 shows fixed epochs compare conditions at different doses).
- **Sycophancy on-policy yield: 80% floor (320/400) + equalize-down, HIGH retry budget.** *Why:* on-policy-first is the default; 80% floor avoids the all-or-nothing drop that cost #612 two sources at 97%/85% fill; HIGH retry budget because false-claim agreement conflicts with alignment training. *Source:* `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` § yield quota + `#612`. *Alternatives:* canned templates (rejected — overstates installability per #612/#411; would confound the realism-transfer headline).
- **Placement = MLP (up_proj, down_proj), all 28 layers.** *Why:* puts the read in residual-INPUT space (post-`post_attention_layernorm`) and the write in residual-OUTPUT space — a clean read↔context AND write↔behavior test from ONE adapter, which #621's attn placement (read in q/v, write in o/down) could not give for the MLP. All-layers matches #621 (`layers_to_transform=None`), zero new code. *Source:* `#604`'s `RESIDUAL_INPUT_MLP_MODULES`/`RESIDUAL_OUTPUT_MODULES` placement-validity map + the body's formalization; literature: `2402.16842` (A reads / B writes asymmetry is the placement's mechanistic basis). *Alternatives:* single layer (the body floated "a layer to pick" — rejected: #621 trained all layers and read DVs over the L14–L24 / L20–L27 bands; single-layer would be a SECOND change vs #621, violating single-variable discipline. The DV READ layers are bands, not the train placement).
- **DV-3 null = max-matched order statistic.** *Why:* the DV-3 observed value is `max_i |cos(b_down, u_i^base)|` over ~3584 base singular vectors per layer then max over the band — an extreme-value statistic. A single-direction random floor (`1/√3584=0.0167`) or a flat p95 over 200 single cosines is far below a max-of-thousands for ANY direction (even a genuine intruder), so it would guarantee a false "peak above null" / false H-pre-existing verdict on the headline discriminator. The null must recompute the IDENTICAL max-over-base-SVD + band statistic per random draw. *Source:* `#621` (the inherited frequency-matched null was for the DV-2 SINGLE-fixed-direction read, NOT a max-over-SVD read) + `codex Phase 2 reconciler v1 (max-matched extreme-value null required)`. *Alternatives:* single-direction `1/√d` floor (rejected — guarantees a false peak); flat p95 over 200 single cosines (rejected — same defect); a scrambled/random-data rank-1 adapter null (a valid alternative null but heavier; the max-matched random-direction null is the cheaper sufficient comparator and is what the reconciler bound).
- **DV-4 reference directions = `d_behavior_base` + content-isolating `d_format_base`.** *Why:* `d_behavior_base` (agree-instructed vs bare-persona) confounds a pre-existing internal sycophancy concept with the instruction-presence / agreement-format direction the elicitation prompt adds, so a positive `cos(b_down, d_behavior_base)` alone is uninterpretable as the headline H-pre-existing read. `d_format_base` (agree-instructed-DISAGREES vs agree-instructed-AGREES, both under the identical instruction) cancels the instruction axis and isolates agreement content; H-pre-existing is supported only when the content-isolating cosine survives above null + residualization. The disagreeing tier-2 rows already exist (post-judge, pre-filter) so the second reference lands at ~zero extra GPU. *Source:* `#621` (DV-4 contrast-of-means construction) + `Phase 2 alternatives critic ensemble (instruction-presence confound)`. *Alternatives:* a generic non-sycophantic instruction-control contrast (a valid alternative content-isolating control; the within-instruction agrees-vs-disagrees contrast is preferred because it reuses data already produced and isolates the exact agreement-content axis with no new generation); single-reference `d_behavior_base` only (rejected — the instruction-presence confound is conclusion-changing on the positive arm).
- **Seeds 42, 137, 256.** *Why:* #621's exact seeds — enables the cross-seed |cos| read and direct #621 comparison. *Source:* `#621`. *Alternatives:* more seeds (budget; n=3 is #621's design).
- **DV layer bands: read L14–L24, write max-over-L20–L27.** *Why:* #604/#621's registered `KEY_LAYER_BAND` (read) and write-extraction band. *Source:* `#604`/`#621`. *Alternatives:* single layer (rejected — band is #621's method).

**Model id `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`** is repo-standard (used by #621/#604/#612 and `configs/`) — inherits validation, no fresh `AutoConfig` smoke needed (`Source:#621`).

## §14. Analyzer-time concerns (carry to clean-result caveats)

Non-blocking concerns both critic teams flagged in Phase 2, carried forward for the analyzer to weigh and the clean-result to caveat:

1. **`TrainLoraConfig.save_initial_adapter` does not exist in the codebase** (Methodology Claude). The plan no longer cites a `save_initial_adapter` flag; the actual #621 a_init mechanism is the EARLIEST persisted per-step checkpoint (`scripts/issue621_checkpoint_ladder.py` loads `checkpoint-<step>/adapter_model.safetensors`; A_init=Kaiming, B_init=0 on a fresh PEFT attach). **Implementer hard assert:** the step-0/earliest-checkpoint adapter is captured BEFORE any optimizer step, and DV-1's a_init basis loads from that path. If no near-init checkpoint exists, DV-1 is unmeasurable — fail loud (a hard implementer assert, NOT a silent proceed and NOT a plan change to the DVs).
2. **High-dose sycophancy band `[0.55, ceiling]` is a tight target** (Methodology Claude) given #612's on-policy plateau ~0.60–0.66 (~0.05–0.10 headroom above the floor). The analyzer reports install achieved vs target per seed and treats a near-miss / non-separation of the two doses on-policy as a scope caveat ("doses not cleanly separable on-policy"), NOT a false null or a kill. Watch the per-epoch Δagree trajectory.
3. **Single-source `police_officer` narrows #621's 4-source panel** (Methodology Claude + both alternatives critics). DV-5 (selectivity vs base geometry) is the LEAST generalizable DV — #621 found firing concentrates on police_officer, the source where the base model itself predicts bystander leakage most strongly (making the "learned" qualifier hard to defend). The headline rests on DV-3/DV-4 (per-adapter geometry, source-independent); carry single-source as a clean-result scope caveat (A7) and decline to generalize any DV-5 signal from one selected-strong source.
4. **Bootstrap CIs over 3 seeds are descriptive only** (Stats Codex + Stats Claude). The verdict leans on seed-level points + null-vs-observed separation (the max-matched null-p95 vs the observed band-max for DV-3; the per-reference permutation null for DV-4), NOT on the CI as a strong confidence statement. Report seed-level points alongside every CI.
5. **(Codex Alternatives concerns.)** (a) **DV-1 read-stays-init:** small real rotations can leave `cos(a_trained, a_init) ≈ 1` and low lr can under-move `a` — the analyzer reads the paired low→high `cos(a∘γ, v_source)` vs the wrong-context null and the checkpoint ladder, not just the self-cosine. (b) **DV-2 sycophancy** noisy opener-token basis — privilege behavioral Δagree install + judge-filtered eval before treating DV-2-syco as a failed landing. (c) **DV-5 collinearity:** when `a` is near-init, the firing predictor and plain geometry are partly collinear (and "geometry wins" is the EXPECTED output of H-ungated, not independent confirmation — Claude Alternatives) — report predictor↔plain-geometry collinearity, read DV-5 as inherited-selectivity support only ALONGSIDE DV-1 + measured leakage, and treat it as redundant with DV-1 at low dose (the one genuinely informative DV-5 signal is a high-dose firing-predictor WIN, which would require `a` to have rotated — i.e. just DV-1 by another route).
6. **(Carried, behavior-contrast confound — Claude + Codex Alternatives, non-blocking.)** Marker and sycophancy differ in loss shape (marker-only masked single token vs full-turn SFT), target, dose dial, and data provenance — not only realism. The matched-install dose bands control install STRENGTH (the dominant lever per #601) but not loss-surface shape. If the two behaviors give different verdicts, the analyzer caveats a divergence as realism-OR-loss-shape/recipe contrast, not a clean causal effect of realism alone.
