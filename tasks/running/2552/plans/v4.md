## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I'll take the two turn-level feature dictionaries we already trained, train one more that copies the Der et al. paper's exact recipe, and have a judge write a short description of what each feature detects and file it under the paper's five categories (content, form, voice, function, meta). Then I'll check two things: which categories of features our context→answer map predicts well, and whether the paper's headline split — their token-level dictionary is better at picking a text out of a lineup, their turn-level one is better at describing everything the text is doing — shows up between our replication dictionary and the closest public token-level dictionary, scored under their own protocol.
- **What I expect:** the map will predict "what the answer is about and how it's written" categories better than "what exact words it uses" ones, after adjusting for how often each feature fires (the adjusted ranking is the headline; the raw ranking is shown beside it). The lineup-vs-coverage split should show up between our two specific dictionaries. For the ladder, firing frequency is controlled from the start (it is step 0 by construction), and I expect at least one property — the matched token-twin's steadiness, or the category label — to add real explanatory power on top of it.
- **What would change my mind:** if the five categories predict equally well once firing frequency is adjusted for, the category story is a firing-rate story in disguise. If the lineup-vs-coverage split lands in any decisive non-reproduced cell — reversed, or ONE dictionary winning BOTH reads — the paper's inversion does not transfer to this pair of dictionaries on our data. Because our token-level side is a different public dictionary (different width, different training), that outcome counts against transfer of the result under our configuration bundle — it is not by itself a refutation of the turn-averaging idea.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (category ranking — exploratory by explicit user choice).** The five schema categories differ in per-feature held-out R², and the ordering is consistent across the three turn-averaged dictionaries (k=100, k=200, replication). No pre-registered ordering; deliverable = the full 5-category ranking with bootstrap intervals per dictionary. PRIMARY ranking = the activity-adjusted ranking (fixed equal-weight aggregation of within-activity-quintile category effects, §6); the raw per-category medians are the SECONDARY descriptive read. Pre-committed narration rule: when the raw and adjusted orderings disagree, the activity-adjusted ordering carries the headline and the disagreement is named wherever either is quoted. A within-activity-stratum category-label permutation read runs per dictionary. Informal expectation (not a verdict criterion): content/form above voice/function/meta.

**H2 (replication).** The Der et al. discrimination-vs-coverage inversion transfers to our configuration bundle: the Lineup-specialist per-token max configuration (the public andyrdt 131,072-wide k=128 dictionary, max-pooled, top-100 judged lists) beats the Replication turn-averaged configuration at 10-way matching, while Replication turn-averaged beats it head-to-head at pairwise coverage. Attribution scope (binding on every conclusion surface — verdict prose, K2, the §0.0 TL;DR, figure captions): the contrast bundles grain with dictionary provenance (third-party andyrdt), width (131,072 vs 32,768), top-100 truncation, and description format — so every H2 conclusion names the observed CONFIGURATION BUNDLE (andyrdt `pt_max` vs `rep_ta` under the top-100 judged protocol), never an unqualified "token-level vs turn-level" or "turn averaging causes …" causal grain claim. Quantities: Δ_disc = (10-way matching accuracy of Lineup-specialist per-token max) − (accuracy of Replication turn-averaged), paired over the same eval turns; Δ_cov = (head-to-head pairwise-coverage win rate of Replication turn-averaged vs Lineup-specialist per-token max) − 0.5, over the same eval turns. The POOLED (LMSYS+WildChat) read is the REGISTERED verdict carrier; the LMSYS-only subset read is advisory heterogeneity only — it can annotate but never upgrade or change the verdict.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Reproduced ⇔ Δ_disc's 95% CI wholly above 0 AND Δ_cov's 95% CI wholly above 0; Reversed ⇔ Δ_disc's 95% CI wholly below 0 AND Δ_cov's 95% CI wholly below 0; Not reproduced — pt_max dominance ⇔ Δ_disc's 95% CI wholly above 0 AND Δ_cov's 95% CI wholly below 0; Not reproduced — rep_ta dominance ⇔ Δ_disc's 95% CI wholly below 0 AND Δ_cov's 95% CI wholly above 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

The two dominance cells are determinate, clear-CI evidence AGAINST the inversion (one configuration wins BOTH constructs) and carry a `Not reproduced` verdict beside `Reversed`; `Inconclusive` is reserved for outcomes where at least one CI overlaps 0 (MF-B).

**H3 (ladder).** At least one property adds explanatory power BEYOND activity: with continuous log activity pre-partialed as a FORCED step 0 (activity is removed from the competitive battery — it is never competitively selected), the first competitive step's observed max partial-R² increment clears its selection-symmetric null band on at least one dictionary. Expectation from #1482: the matched-token-twin within-answer consistency or the schema category is the strongest competitive step. Falsified for a given dictionary if no competitive step clears its band (then per-feature predictability at turn grain carries no measured structure beyond activity on that dictionary; the realized step-0 base R² is reported per dictionary).

**Registered paired contrast.** H2's Δ_disc and Δ_cov are paired per eval turn (same eval turns judged under both configurations; 10-way matching paired per turn, pairwise coverage paired per (turn, pair) trial with randomized presentation order).
Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-turn by eval_results/issue_2552/dere_repl/matching_perturn.json (10-way matching, one row per turn × configuration, each row validity-flagged) and eval_results/issue_2552/dere_repl/pairwise_perturn.json (coverage, one row per turn × pair, validity-flagged).
Complete-pair rule (registered, MF-D): Δ_disc is computed over the EXACT intersection of eval-turn ids holding a VALID matching verdict in BOTH the `rep_ta` and `pt_max` arms; Δ_cov over turns holding a valid verdict for the (rep_ta, pt_max) pair; the realized denominators + per-arm completeness (`frac_items_complete`) persist in the same files. If G2 descopes the eval set to 1,000 turns, the realized N from `regime.json` propagates into the H2 prose, these row-coverage statements, filenames, and all denominators.
