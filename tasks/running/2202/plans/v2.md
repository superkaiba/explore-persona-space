# Plan — Issue #2202: Which contexts does the context→answer map fail to retrieve? (SAE-free failure characterization on the #1738 map)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I take the already-trained history-to-answer predictor from the parent experiment and, for every held-out conversation where it points at the wrong answer, I pull out exactly which other conversation's answer beat the right one and how the two conversations and their answers sit relative to each other. I publish two browsable pages — every failed case with its top confusers, and a 500-conversation random sample — plus counts of which kinds of conversations fail. No new training, no GPU, everything computed from artifacts we already have.
- **What I expect:** failures cluster where two conversations genuinely call for near-identical replies (refusals, greetings, translations, boilerplate), and a sizable share of the "errors" are cases where even a second real sample of the correct answer would not have been retrieved — so they are not the map's fault. I also expect the confusion to be mostly one-directional: many contexts get dragged toward a few generic answers rather than swapping places in pairs.
- **What would change my mind:** failures spread evenly across conversation types with no category standing out; most failed cases passing the resample ceiling check (meaning the map itself is genuinely wrong, not the targets degenerate); or mutual confusion far above both reference baselines (the map collapses specific pairs, which would point at a different fix than the generic-attractor story).

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (composition — Result 1).** The FAIL-1 set's composition differs measurably from the held-out base rates: at least one pre-registered label contrast (the #1738 `_contrast_masks` family: language en/non-en, topics with n≥50, refusal-adjacency, answer-is-refusal, format, depth bands, corpus) clears BH-FDR q=0.05 on the group-vs-rest failure-rate delta. Directional priors: `answer_is_refusal=yes` and `language≠en` over-represented among failures (refusal answers are near-duplicates of each other; the map's training mass is English-heavy). If zero contrasts clear, the null is reported with its detection floor (the realized bootstrap CI widths — at n_fail ≈ 1,829 and group n ≥ 50 these resolve rate deltas of a few percentage points).

**H2 (attribution — Result 1).** Among kresample-covered failures (expected ≈ 1,829 × 1,988/9,941 ≈ 366 contexts; the covered set is the parent's kresample subsample — 2,000 holdout contexts drawn by stratified proportional allocation over depth-band × language-arm × corpus at RNG seed 173801, `issue1738_characterize.phase_kresample_subsample`, minus the 12 r5 admission-gate skips), the IRREDUCIBLE class is at least as large as the MAP-ATTRIBUTABLE class — i.e. target degeneracy + sampling noise, not map error, dominate rank-1 misses. Falsifying observation: MAP-ATTRIBUTABLE share > 60% of covered failures (the map is genuinely wrong on most failures). The per-context attribution partition (a data partition, not an experiment verdict): with s_i = (# of the 4 resample draws that retrieve a_i at mid-rank 1)/4, a covered FAIL-1 context is MAP-ATTRIBUTABLE at s_i ≥ 0.75, IRREDUCIBLE at s_i ≤ 0.25, AMBIGUOUS at s_i = 0.5; uncovered contexts are UNKNOWN. The pool-level ceiling `acc@1_ceiling` = mean of s_i over all 1,988 covered contexts. Two zero-cost pre-registered representativeness checks run BEFORE any generalization of attribution shares beyond the covered set: (a) the FAIL-1 rate among covered (1,988) vs uncovered (7,953) contexts; (b) the banked-label composition of covered vs uncovered failures — both reported. Narration rule for the ceiling: `acc@1_ceiling` is the retrievability of a fresh on-policy draw (a draw-vs-draw read — an ideal conditional-mean map could exceed it), never "the ceiling any map could reach"; correspondingly IRREDUCIBLE is an UPPER BOUND on irreducibility, weighed alongside the cos(a_i, a_j) degeneracy companion (§4 P1), which does not share this asymmetry.

**H3 (symmetry — Result 3).** Observed top-1 confusion-graph reciprocity sits above the degree-preserving band but inside the distance-only band — the confusion is symmetric only to the extent the answer metric is symmetric and answers cluster, not because the map collapses specific pairs. Define Δdp = observed reciprocity − p97.5 of the degree-preserving null band, and Δdo = observed reciprocity − p97.5 of the distance-only null band.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Pair-collapse ⇔ Δdp > 0 AND Δdo > 0; Metric-explained ⇔ Δdp > 0 AND Δdo ≤ 0; Degree-explained ⇔ otherwise.

Reading: Pair-collapse ⇒ the map maps genuine pairs of contexts into a shared image (equivalence-class structure, corroborated by Result 2's collapse lists); Metric-explained ⇒ reciprocity is what a symmetric metric over clustered answers already produces; Degree-explained ⇒ failures are one-sided drags into attractors (the hubness read). Two-sided note: the lattice's atoms use only the p97.5 edges, so observed reciprocity BELOW the degree-preserving band's p2.5 (anti-reciprocity) folds into "Degree-explained" — the analyzer checks the lower tail (observed vs p2.5 of each band, read off the persisted per-draw vectors) before narrating that cell, and reports anti-reciprocity explicitly when present.

### Phase P1 (pod) — confusion geometry, four metric spaces

**Metric spaces (every read reported in all four):**

1. **raw-euclidean** (PRIMARY — defines the canonical FAIL-1 set; matches the banked #1738 headline): squared-euclidean GEMM, rank-identical to euclidean.
2. **raw-cosine.**
3. **mean-centered cosine:** per-family train-mean centering — answers and predictions centered by μ_A, contexts by μ_C — then cosine (the `persona-distance-metrics.md` bank-centering convention; All-but-the-Top).
4. **whitened / Mahalanobis:** z = L⁻¹(x − μ_fam) (triangular solve; μ_fam = the vector's own family mean, the metric = the task-locked shrunk train-ANSWER covariance for every vector incl. contexts — stated convention, §11/§12); euclidean in whitened coordinates is the Mahalanobis read; whitened cosine reported as companion.

**Computations (all batched GEMMs over the full 9,941 pool; chunked over query rows at ~1,024 rows/chunk so no matrix exceeds ~400 MB resident; NEVER a per-pair Python loop):**

- Per-context retrieval rank `rank_i` of `a_i` under `p_i` per space (mid-rank convention copied from `knn_retrieval._pairwise_dist` + the tolerance-based tie handling — reimplemented in `issue2202_failchar.py` as `ranks_of_targets(pred, pool, metric)` returning per-row ranks; a FULL-POOL equivalence gate: derive acc@{1,5,10}/MRR from the NEW per-row ranks over all 9,941 rows and reconcile against the banked values at the P0 tolerances — implying |FAIL-1 count − 1,829| ≤ ~2 — which guards tie-convention divergence exactly where the near-duplicates live).
- **FAIL-1 set** (primary space): expected (1 − 0.816014485464239) × 9,941 = 1,829 rows; per-space failure sets + pairwise Jaccard overlap.
- **WORST tails:** top-200 by `rank_i` AND top-200 by raw distance d(p_i, a_i), both listed (they differ; both reported).
- **Confusers:** per FAIL-1 row, the pool rows ranked above `a_i` (top 10 retained for display; full count retained as a number). Confuser ordering ties broken by (distance, pool index) — stable across machines (the numpy tie-order gotcha).
- **Per-confuser geometry** (each in all four spaces): cos(v_Ci, v_Cj) context↔context; cos(a_i, a_j) answer↔answer; cos(a_i, v_Cj) true-answer↔confuser-context (both 3,584-d at L19 — well-defined; whitened under the locked answer-metric); cos(p_i, a_j) prediction↔confuser. Plus: rank of v_Cj among all 9,941 context vectors by similarity to v_Ci; rank of a_j among all answer vectors by similarity to a_i; `rank_i` itself.
- **Nearest-confuser answer similarity** s_conf_i = cos(a_i, a_j1) (j1 = top non-true pool answer) for EVERY holdout context — the failure-vs-matched-control degeneracy comparison reads from this. Named reported line: the fraction of the FAIL-1 set with s_conf ≈ 1 (within the mid-rank tie tolerance — near-duplicate/tie rows where the map did as well as logically possible).
- **Hubness diagnostics** (exploratory): retrieval in-degree N_10 per pool answer (how many predictions list it in their top 10), its skewness, top-20 hub capture table (Radovanović 2010; arXiv 2412.14113); same for the prediction-collapse graph.
- **Result 2 lists** for the 500 seed-pinned sample (seed 2202, drawn from all 9,941): (A) 10 nearest true-answer vectors to p_i with the true target's rank marked; (B) 10 nearest OTHER predictions to p_i (self excluded).
- **Pool-size robustness:** ranks + FAIL-1 + composition recomputed at pool sizes 500 / 2,000 (seed-pinned subsamples always containing the true target) alongside 9,941.
- **Concordance:** Spearman(rank_i, banked `nerr_context_L19_ridge`) over all 9,941 + tail-overlap counts.

### Phase P2 (pod) — confusion-graph reciprocity + two nulls

- Directed graph G: edge i→j iff a_j outranks a_i under p_i (primary space). E measured; pre-registered cap: if E > 5×10⁶, restrict to each row's top-50 confusers (cap + realized E both reported).
- **Primary:** reciprocity = P(j→i ∈ G | i→j ∈ G).
- **Graded companion:** per confusion edge, rank of a_j under p_i vs rank of a_i under p_j (scatter + Spearman) — reverse ranks read off the same chunked distance computations.
- **Null 1 — degree-preserving (1,000 draws):** vectorized directed configuration draw — permute the target-stub multiset per draw (preserves every out- and in-degree; self-loop/multi-edge collisions counted + reported, negligible at this sparsity); reciprocity per draw → band. Vectorized as a (draws × E) integer/hash batch, chunked over draws.
- **Null 2 — distance-only (1,000 draws):** per source i, draw its out-degree d_i edges without replacement with P(i→j) ∝ exp(−d(a_i, a_j)/τ) via Gumbel-top-k per row (symmetric-by-construction kernel over answer↔answer distances); τ ∈ {p1, p5, p25} of the pairwise answer-distance distribution (pre-registered sensitivity sweep — all three bands reported, headline read at p5).
- Persist the per-draw reciprocity vectors (1,000 floats per null per τ) to `eval_results/issue_2202/reciprocity.json`.

Then: bulk `upload_folder` of everything under the issue prefix, sentinel write, pod terminated after upload-verification (out-root residue sweep per the standing contract).

## 7. Decision Gates

Two gates only, both user-locked in the task body (the run is short — most compute is one ~2–3 h CPU pod — so no discretionary gates are added):

1. **Reproduction gate (P0; blocks everything).** Cheap intermediate signal: the banked acc@k re-derives from the banked tensors, proving the ci join + instrument before any downstream read. Threshold |Δacc@k| ≤ 2e-4 / |ΔMRR| ≤ 1e-4 / n = n_pool = 9,941 exactly: grounded in the artifact's own values (the gate re-derives the SAME numbers from the SAME files — the tolerance covers only cross-BLAS tie flips, per the bf16/fp32 parity-gate calibration family in gotchas.md; a wiring bug moves acc@1 by O(0.1), ≫ 500× the tolerance — a discriminating placement). HALT on miss (`failure_class: data`).
2. **Judge pilot gate (P4).** ~150 draws at the production instrument; PASS = zero `stop_reason=="max_tokens"` AND per-arm parse-fail < 2% (the CLAUDE.md rule-23/26 thresholds, task-body lock). FAIL ⇒ fix the rubric/max_tokens, re-pilot; never dispatch the production wave past a failed pilot.

Jointly satisfiable trivially (different measurements, no contradictory demands).

**Kill criteria:** (1) reproduction-gate miss ⇒ HALT the run (`epm:failure`, `failure_class: data`) — nothing downstream executes; (2) judge-pilot FAIL ⇒ the production wave is blocked until a re-pilot PASSes; (3) H1 zero-contrasts-clear ⇒ the null is reported with its detection floor (a reporting branch, not a stop). No new thresholds beyond the gates above.
