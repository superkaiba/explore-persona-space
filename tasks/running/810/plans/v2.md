### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I already have a model's saved internal state while it wrote answers to 48 questions under each of 50 different "characters." I take that answer-side state and try several different ways of boiling one whole answer down to a single fingerprint — the plain average, the very last word, the exact spot where the answer ends and the next turn begins, and a few others. For each recipe I ask two questions: can I rebuild the answer's fingerprint from the character alone, and does the fingerprint tell me how much the answer showed a given behavior (like agreeing with a wrong claim, or refusing). I pick the fingerprint that wins on both.
- **What I expect:** The "end-of-turn" fingerprint — the spot right where the answer hands the conversation back — will do at least as well as the plain average for rebuilding, because that same "boundary" trick already worked best on the question side in an earlier experiment. I don't expect any single fingerprint to rescue the behavior-prediction that mostly failed before; the earlier failure looked like a real limit, not a bad choice of fingerprint.
- **What would change my mind:** If a boundary or early-position fingerprint clearly beats the plain average on rebuilding, that changes which fingerprint the next experiment carries forward. And if any fingerprint lifts behavior-prediction clearly above chance where the plain average failed, that would say the earlier failure was partly about how we summarized the answer, not a hard limit.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Per-position window K=16.** *What:* tail `−1…−16` + head `0…15`. *Why:* median answer S≈300, but the profile is expected to concentrate near the boundaries (mirror of `c_C`); K=16 covers the tail without materializing the full span (footprint stays ~0.34 GB). #722's per-position script used P_MAX=64 head-only; K=16 tail+head is the minimal window that includes the `last`/boundary candidates + enough head to test the early-position hypothesis. *Source:* `#722 P_MAX=64` (reference on branch `fig-per-position`, NOT imported — trimmed to a tail+head window and re-implemented inline). *Alternatives:* full-span per-position (rejected — 150 GB footprint, no marginal value beyond boundaries); K=8 (rejected — too short to see the tail decay).
- **Ridge λ grid {1e-2,1e-1,1,10,100,1e3}.** *What:* nested-CV λ selection. *Why:* the exact #658 A3.4 grid the whole line uses; keeps the reconstruction DV comparable to #722. *Source:* `#658 issue658_fit_predictors.RIDGE_LAMBDAS (VERIFIED on main)`. *Alternatives:* per-layer λ (rejected — #658 uses one grid, nested-CV picks per-fold).
