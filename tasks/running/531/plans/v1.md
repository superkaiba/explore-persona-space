`kind: analysis` — no GPU, no pod, no adversarial-planner. The task body already enumerates the spec; this plan formalizes deliverables + reproducibility for review.

## Goal

At the non-saturated marker anchor of #478, determine the sign and magnitude of the relationship between a held-out persona's base-model prior on the marker ` ※` and how much the marker leaks (trained − base `log P(※)` shift, on-policy at the post-response slot). Disambiguate #504's saturated −0.87 (ceiling-arithmetic suspect) from #500's facts +sign (propensity).

## Hypothesis + prediction

- **H_propensity (predicted):** off-ceiling, the partial Spearman of `shift = trained − base` on `base_prior` (controlling for `min_dist_to_source` and K) is **positive** — same sign as #500's facts result, opposite of #504's saturated −0.87 — confirming the saturated reading was ceiling arithmetic.
- **H_alternative:** the partial ρ is ~0 — base prior was never the real driver for markers, and #530's predicted sign-flip is the wrong frame.
- **Falsification:** if the partial ρ is significantly negative (matching #504's saturated direction) even off-ceiling, the propensity story collapses for markers and #504 is read literally.

- vs **#478**: same data (#478 raw_completions on HF); same dependent variable (`log P(※)` at post-response slot, on-policy); NEW analysis only — base prior ↔ shift relationship that #478 explicitly did not extract.
