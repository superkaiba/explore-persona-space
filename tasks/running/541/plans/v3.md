## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The finished #541 run showed that a bystander persona's own familiarity with the invented courthouse fact predicts whether it picks the fact up once the fact is taught to a teacher persona. This follow-up asks whether the base model's internal geometry adds anything: is a persona whose internal representation sits close to the teacher's more likely to receive the fact? No new training. I reuse the leak rates and familiarity scores already measured, run one cheap pass over the frozen base model to fill in the missing distances, and compare familiarity alone, geometry alone, and the two combined at predicting who leaked.
- **What I expect:** Two sibling experiments on a different implanted behavior (#532, #539) found that this kind of geometric distance adds little once you know how prone each persona already is to the behavior, so my bet is the combined predictor ties familiarity alone. The one place geometry might earn its keep is among the personas already familiar with the fact, where familiarity stops ranking anyone, yet the teacher's nearest neighbor adopted the fact on up to 91% of its answers.
- **What would change my mind:** If the combined predictor beats familiarity alone by a margin the resampling checks can't dismiss, geometry belongs in the prediction recipe after all, and the fact-teaching setting genuinely differs from those sibling experiments. That would be the most useful outcome.

## 7. Success criteria / kill criteria (quantitative, pre-registered)

**Confirm (geometry adds):** LOPO Δ(macro within-arm held-out Spearman), joint − prior-only, > 0 with the 2,000-iter cluster-on-persona bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 AND cluster-level persona-identity permutation p < 0.05 (1,000 perms × full LOPO, one-sided; persona is the exchangeable unit). Report the increment in both metrics (Spearman + R²); the Spearman version is binding. The within-arm (cos, gkl)-pair permutation is reported as a labeled sensitivity only, never the binding p.

**Null (the scope marker's falsification, operationalized):** ALL THREE of —
1. the joint increment's 95% CI spans 0 (or the point estimate ≤ 0);
2. geometry-only underperforms prior-only on the same held-out metric (point estimate);
3. no high-stratum distance-to-teacher test survives Holm over the 4 confirmatory tests (2 high arms × 2 metrics) at α = 0.05.

→ verdict "geometry adds nothing detectable at this panel size once the prior + stratum are in the model — consistent with #532/#539," folded into the parent body with the panel-size power caveat stated (24 persona clusters; only large increments are detectable).

**Mixed outcomes are reported as graded** — the pre-registered most-likely mixed case: pooled increment null but a high-stratum proximity test significant → "geometry doesn't improve panel-wide prediction, but proximity-to-teacher carries part of the high-stratum ordering the prior can't rank" (which directly addresses the parent's open proximity-to-teacher confound, and sharpens — not kills — the parent headline).

## 11. Decision Rationale

No training → no training hyperparameters. Load-bearing analysis constants, each with provenance (inherit fast-path: values a sibling issue validated carry that issue's grounding):

- **Gaussian sym-KL @ layer 22, PCA k=16, last-prompt-token clouds.** Why: the scope marker names it; it is the predictor lineage's current geometry leader. Source: #502 bakeoff winner, carried verbatim by #532 (`GAUSS_KL_LAYER = 22`, `_gaussian_sym_kl_in_subspace_local(..., k=16)`, `issue532_predictor_stress.py:184,:1374`). Alternative rejected: defining a new KL variant — would break comparability with the #532/#539 conclusions this run is testing against.
- **Cosine headline @ layer 21, mean-per-probe, last-input-token.** Why: the parent's own confound-check operationalization AND the rule file's legacy default; identity with the committed matrix is what makes the fidelity gate possible. Source: #541 phase0c (`LAYER_HEADLINE = "21"`, `VECTOR_LAYERS = (7,14,21,27)`) + `.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` (recipe (a), L21 legacy default, sweep reported per-layer). Alternative rejected: recipe (b) response-token pooling — not what the parent measured; would change two things at once.
- **Probe set = first 40 A-family on-topic probes from `build_reformulation_probes(ENTITY)`.** Why: byte-identical to phase0c's slice → fidelity gate is meaningful; also the same prompt family the leak DV used. Source: #541 phase0c (`issue541_prescreen.py:371-375`, `N_COS_PROBES = 40`).
- **DV = `leak_mean` over the 3 trained seeds, baseline cell excluded.** Source: #541 parent's declared estimator (v2-plan round-1 reconciliation; matches the published body).
- **Arm fixed effects in all pooled models.** Why: #539's central lesson — activation distance can "predict" by tracking source-level global leakiness; arm FE removes the arm-mean channel. Source: #539 (two-way FE / source-dose partials).
- **High-vs-rest stratum term + boundary −3.25 nats.** Source: #541 parent headline (`STRATUM_H_MIN`).
- **LOPO grouped CV (24 persona folds).** Why: held-out comparison so M_joint's extra parameters can't win by overfit; grouping prevents the same persona straddling folds. Source: #532's grouped CV-R² hierarchy (its exact fold recipe adapted from cells to personas — the adaptation is stated, not silent).
- **Permutation / bootstrap counts: 10,000 per-arm perms, 1,000 perms × LOPO for the increment, 2,000 cluster bootstrap.** Source: #539 inference defaults (`--n-boot 10_000`, `--n-cluster-boot 2_000`); the LOPO-perm reduction to 1,000 is a compute-awareness choice (each perm re-runs 24 folds) with resolution 0.001 — sufficient for an α = 0.05 read.
- **Cluster-level persona-identity permutation as the BINDING increment p (within-arm pair-permutation demoted to labeled sensitivity).** Why: persona is the exchangeable unit everywhere else in the primary (LOPO folds, cluster bootstrap); within-arm shuffling breaks persona-level cross-arm geometry coherence and is anti-conservative for a borderline confirm. Source: round-1 statistics-lens ensemble reconciliation (binding), 2026-06-11; consistent with #539's cluster-by-bystander inference convention.
- **Collinearity gate at |Pearson| 0.6 with tercile-bucket fallback.** Source: #541 parent (`COLLINEARITY_GATE_SOFT = 0.6`, `collinearity_gate` block).
- **z-scored OLS in rate space.** Source: #541 parent (`ols_z_leak_on_z_prior_logprob_and_z_cos_to_source` — the same spec extended to 5 predictors via lstsq).
- **Two-sided per-arm tests / one-sided pooled increment.** Source: #541 parent's measured sign instability (cos raw Spearman −0.4229/+0.4412/+0.3092) for two-sidedness; the scope marker's directional hypothesis for the one-sided primary.
- **k=8 Gaussian-KL robustness read.** Exploratory-only (not load-bearing): no prior issue swept k at n=40 clouds (#532 used n=50); flagged so the analyzer treats k-disagreement as a caveat, not a finding.
- **RNG seed 42.** Source: parent training-seed convention (seed list 42/137/256). The analysis RNG is NEWLY fixed at 42 in the two new scripts (`issue541_predictors.py` itself defaults its bootstrap to seed=0; this plan does not inherit that — fact-check item 27).
