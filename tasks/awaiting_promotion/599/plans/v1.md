### TDD: no

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** An earlier experiment trained a model to append a special symbol to its answers when playing a doctor, and found the training pushes different personas' internal representations in many different directions. Misalignment training pushes everything in one shared direction instead. One recipe difference is left between the two trainings: the symbol training only graded the model on the single symbol token, while misalignment training graded every word of the response. I'll retrain the symbol implant grading every word, changing nothing else, and re-measure the geometry with the same instrument against the saved measurements from both reference runs.
- **What I expect:** Honestly, I expect the symbol may not even install this time. Grading every word spreads the learning signal over ~510 tokens instead of 1 (the parent's greedy responses saturate the 512-token generation cap: median 513 / mean 508.7 completion tokens in the authoritative training file), so at the inherited (very low) learning rate the symbol's share of the push shrinks about 255-fold. The built-in strength check at the end of training is the arbiter. If the implant does land, my weak bet is the geometry stays scattered — which would rule out the loss mask (on top of #561's negatives) as the driver, attaching the original contrast to the behavior-or-data axis; how the two arms were graded per token is then out, though their learning-rate recipes and training-data identity remain unmatched follow-up axes.
- **What would change my mind:** If the implant lands AND the geometry collapses onto one shared direction the way misalignment training does, then the loss shape, not the behavior, manufactures the concentration, and the parent line's "it's the behavior" reading is overturned. If the implant fails to land, that is itself an answer: the two training rigs aren't even exchangeable at matched settings, and "how you grade" changes what can be installed at all.

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

**Goal (canonical frontmatter, no refinement proposed):** Determine whether the marker implant's dispersed layer-14 shift geometry survives replacing the marker-only single-token loss with EM-style whole-response loss — the #561 positive-only recipe with the loss mask opened to the full response plus sentinel and nothing else changed — by reading the new adapters' shift spectrum against the persisted #561 positive-only and #551 EM/marker tensors.

**Hypothesis lattice (pre-registered):**

- **H-rig (confirm):** loss shape drives EM's concentration. The whole-response marker arm concentrates toward the EM band: mean same-text weighted top-share ≥ 0.50 with ≥ 2/3 seeds ≥ 0.46, sign-flip nulls cleared, surviving the unit-norm re-read (mean unit-norm ≥ 0.40, rotation |cos(U1_w, U1_un)| ≥ 0.95). Then the #521/#551 contrast re-attributes rig-side with loss shape as the named mechanism.
- **H-behavior (falsify):** dispersion survives the loss-mask component within the positive-only, self-generated marker recipe: mean same-text weighted top-share ≤ 0.40 AND mean unit-norm top-share ≤ 0.374 (at/below the measured top of the #561 control band). With negatives (#561) and the loss mask (here) ruled out, the contrast attaches to the behavior-or-data axis; lr/schedule/steps and training-data identity (off-policy insecure-code vs self-generated greedy text) remain named follow-up axes — NOT a claim that the rig-component space is exhausted.
- **H-install (the prior's favorite, pre-registered as a headline, not a failure):** the implant does not land at the matched recipe. Mechanism: with CE mean-reduced over unmasked labels, opening the mask from ~2 tokens to ~510 scales the marker-token gradient ~1/255 (completions are 512-cap-saturated: median 513 / mean 508.7 tokens, measured on the authoritative #561 training file). Measured anchor: the control arm (marker-only loss, same lr/steps/data) read source ΔG = 1.02 nat @ step 50, 5.85 @ 100, saturated (+31, emit 1.0) by step 200 (`eval_results/issue_561/marker_seed42/periodic_eval/`); divided by ~255, step 600 projects ΔG ≈ 0.12 nat — far below the 5-nat gate. If this holds, the finding is: *installability itself is loss-shape-dependent on self-generated data* — the marker and EM rigs are not exchangeable at matched hyperparameters, which bounds the rig-component space in a different but determinate way. The trajectory's four-float storage quantifies the dilution empirically.

Self-generated-data caveat (carried verbatim from the body): the positives are the model's OWN greedy responses, so response-token gradients are small; a null also bounds how much signal whole-response loss can add on self-generated data. Named in any clean-result.
