## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the answers a base model already wrote under 50 different "characters" (personas/contexts) when asked the sycophancy, refusal, and harmful-request questions. I read out the model's internal state while it re-reads each of those exact answers, then check whether that internal state predicts how often each character actually does the behavior. The fix here is that I use the same set of questions on both sides — and I re-run the parent's old reading the exact same way inside this run, so the only thing that differs between "old" and "new" is the question matching, nothing else.
- **What I expect:** Once the internal-state summary and the behavior rate come from the same questions, the predictor should read at least as well as the parent's old reading, and probably better. In the parent the question mismatch was diluting the signal.
- **What would change my mind:** Suppose matching the questions does not lift the prediction for any of the three behaviors. That would tell me the parent's weak read was never about the mismatch — the mean internal state simply doesn't carry these behaviors, and the right move would be to question the quantity itself rather than the measurement. I also watch a same-size-but-wrong-questions control: if it lifts the read just as much, the gain was about averaging over more answers, not about matching the questions, and I say so plainly.

## 1. Goal

Re-measure how well base-model mean answer activation v0(C) predicts behavioral expression E0(C,B) with the activation summary AND the judged-expression target computed over the SAME per-behavior probe set (≥50 judgments/context), removing #658's probe-distribution mismatch and 8-judgment noise floor; phase 1 = the 3 behaviors with existing ≥50-probe completions+judgments (sycophancy/refusal/harmful_compliance), capture-only matched v0, ridge v0(C,B)→E0(C,B) LOCO per #742 recipe vs the IN-RUN-recomputed #658 mismatched ridge (recipe-symmetric) + a same-N wrong-questions control + the diff-in-means descriptive column.

In one sentence: #658 reported that a mean answer-side activation summarizes only 3 of 10 behaviors and NONE of the 4 with a working linear read-out (broad misalignment, harmful compliance, sycophancy, refusal). But in #658, `v0(C)` was averaged over a single 48-probe Betley pool for ALL behaviors, while `E0(C,B)` was judged over behavior-specific batteries — so the ridge correlated an activation summary over one probe distribution against expression measured over a different one. This task removes that mismatch for the 3 behaviors that already have ≥50 behavior-specific probes + completions + judgments, capturing `v0(C,B)` over those SAME probes, then re-reading the #742 ridge predictor and comparing it against the #658 mismatched read RECOMPUTED in the identical rig (same recipe, same 50 contexts, same `mean` summary, same symmetric layer-selection). It directly serves `q:leak-predictor` (open question 3.1): whether any base-model-readable quantity predicts where a fine-tuned behavior leaks.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (one-sided, the headline).** For each of the 3 behaviors, the matched-probe ridge held-out Spearman ρ_matched ≥ ρ_mismatched, where **ρ_mismatched is the #658 mismatched-probe ridge RECOMPUTED IN-RUN (§6.0)** under the identical PCA→ridge-LOCO→layer-select recipe, the same 50 contexts, the same `mean` summary, and the same symmetric layer-selection rule — i.e. the probe mismatch attenuated the read, holding everything else byte-identical.

- **Headline statistic:** per behavior, **Δρ = ρ_matched − ρ_mismatched(in-run recompute)** with a PAIRED context-resample cluster-bootstrap 95% CI (B=2000, resample the 50 context indices ONCE per draw, refit BOTH ridges on that same resample, take the per-draw Δρ — §6.4). A positive Δρ whose CI excludes 0 confirms the mismatch attenuated the read for that behavior.
- **Same-N disambiguation (the matching-vs-pool-size confound, NEW).** The matched arm changes BOTH semantic match AND averaging-pool size N (mismatched 48 → matched 200/214/115). A positive Δρ alone cannot tell "matching the judged probe set recovered prediction skill" from "the larger v0-averaging pool reduced sampling noise." A **same-N wrong-questions control** (§4.7) holds N at the matched count but draws the v0-averaging probes from the UNRELATED #658 UltraChat pool (re-meaned 0-GPU from the committed UltraChat answer-span store). Per behavior I report Δρ_match-vs-sameN = ρ_matched − ρ_sameN-mismatched. If matching is the lever, ρ_matched > ρ_sameN-mismatched ≈ ρ_48-mismatched; if pool-size is the lever, ρ_sameN-mismatched ≈ ρ_matched > ρ_48-mismatched.
- **Headline language (narrowed, the safe claim regardless of the control).** The headline is stated as: *"the full matched judged probe pool reads at least as well as #658's 48-probe mismatched read; the same-N control attributes the gain to {matching the judged distribution / a larger averaging pool / both} per behavior."* Where the same-N control resolves the attribution, the per-behavior claim is sharpened to "matching recovers skill" (control ≈ 48-pool) or "pool-size, not matching" (control ≈ matched). Where the n=50 paired CI is too wide to separate them, the per-behavior claim stays at the narrowed form. This verbatim wording carries into §6 and the clean-result.
- **Secondary:** the matched ρ vs the per-behavior reliability ceiling √(r_yy) (the bracket [ρ_matched, √(r_yy)]). Where ρ_matched approaches √(r_yy), the matched read is ceiling-limited.

**Kill criterion (falsifies H1).** Matched ρ_matched is within 1 paired cluster-bootstrap CI of the in-run-recomputed ρ_mismatched **across all 3 behaviors** (Δρ CI straddles 0 for every behavior) → the mismatch was NOT the bottleneck. Reading: matching the probe set does not recover the signal; #658's weak mean-activation summary for these behaviors is a property of the representation (or of the n=50 reliability ceiling), not a measurement artifact. This is a real, reportable outcome.

**What "the headline holds" means for `q:leak-predictor`.** If matched ρ ≥ recomputed-mismatched ρ and rises toward the ceiling for these 3 behaviors AND the same-N control attributes the gain to matching, then the mean answer-side activation DOES predict behavioral expression once the probe distribution is matched — refining #658's "summarizes only 3/10" headline by showing ≥1 of the 4 read-out behaviors crosses into "predictable" under matched probes. If it stays flat, #658's headline stands for these behaviors even after the confound is removed (a stronger negative).

## 4. Design

**Single manipulated variable vs #658/#742:** the probe set the activation summary `v0` is averaged over — matched to each behavior's own judged probes, instead of the single shared 48-probe Betley pool. Everything else (base model, contexts, capture mechanism, ridge recipe, E0 target, bootstrap, null) is inherited. The in-run mismatched-ridge recompute (§6.0) reads the SAME everything-else off the #658 store so the comparison is byte-identical-recipe; the same-N control (§4.7) holds N fixed to isolate the matching axis from the pool-size axis.

### 4.1 Conditions

3 behaviors × 50 contexts. The per-behavior matched-probe count is the **judged probe set** (the `E0.per_probe` list), and it **varies per context** (verified this session):

| Behavior | Generated probes | Judged probes per context (matched-N range) | Rollouts/probe | Capture forwards (Σ over 50 contexts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sycophancy | 200 | **200** (fixed; all judged) | 10 (temp 0.7) | 200 × 50 = 10,000 |
| refusal | 250 | **212–215** (per-context; 35–38 dropped at judging) | 1 (temp 0.0) | Σ ≈ 10,650 (avg ~213 × 50) |
| harmful_compliance | 150 | **114–115** (per-context; 35–36 dropped) | 1 (temp 0.0) | Σ ≈ 5,725 (avg ~114.5 × 50) |

Total ≈ **26,150–26,500 teacher-forced forwards** (a per-context-aware range, NOT a fixed scalar — the per-(C,B) matched-N is read from `len(E0["e0"][ctx][B]["per_probe"])` and recorded in the artifact; refusal/harmful drop a per-context-varying handful at judging). The §9 GPU estimate is sized to the upper end. For sycophancy the capture is over the 200 distinct probes; for the v0 mean I capture one completion per probe (the FIRST rollout's stored completion) to keep the activation summary a per-probe quantity matched to the per-probe judged rate; see §4.4 the rollout-handling rule.

**Matched-probe invariant (the whole point):** `v0(C,B)` is averaged over EXACTLY the probes in `E0[C][B].per_probe` (the judged set), joined on probe text (`judged ⊆ generated` verified TRUE for all 3 behaviors). This makes the activation summary and the target cover the identical probe distribution per (context, behavior) — the confound #658 had.

**Subsample / equalize-down policy.** Because the 3 behaviors are fit as **3 separate per-behavior ridge predictors** (NOT a pooled fit), the matched-N is naturally per-behavior-and-per-context; there is no cross-behavior dose to equalize on the v0 side (each behavior's v0 mean uses its own probes, and a larger probe pool only reduces the v0-mean's sampling noise — it does not bias the predictor). I therefore **keep the full judged probe set per (context, behavior)** (no subsampling) and RECORD the per-(C,B) matched-N in the artifact. The ≥50 floor is satisfied for all 3 (min 114). This is the graceful, no-information-discarded choice. (The matching-vs-pool-size confound this creates is addressed by the §4.7 same-N control, NOT by subsampling — subsampling the matched arm to a fixed N would discard the very pool the matched read is about.)

- **`BatchedAnswerSpanCapture`** (NEW): a `LayerCapture` whose hook keeps the FULL `(B, T, H)` per layer (not `(1,T,H)`), so one forward processes a left-padded batch of `(prompt+answer)` rows. For each row r, slice its answer span `[prompt_len_r, prompt_len_r+ans_len_r)` (per-row lengths tracked from the tokenizer), mean over its answer tokens at each layer → `(n_layers, H)` per row, then probe-mean over the batch's rows into the running `v0(C,B)` accumulator. ### 4.5 Vectorization scheme + memory back-of-envelope

- **Batch axis:** probes within one (context, behavior). The hook captures `(B, T, H)` at all 28 layers per forward.
- **Memory:** Qwen2.5-7B bf16 weights ≈ 14 GB. Captured activations per forward = `28 × B × T × 3584 × 2 bytes`. For `B=16`, `T≈512`: `28 × 16 × 512 × 3584 × 2 ≈ 1.6 GB` per forward, reduced to per-row `(28, H)` means immediately (the `(B,T,H)` buffer freed per forward). Plus the forward's own activations (~a few GB). **Chosen batch = 16 probes/forward** (clears the ~80 GB A100 with wide margin). Adaptive batch reduction + `T` cap on long-completion contexts; the per-row answer-span assert catches truncation. (The recompute/same-N re-means of the stored spans are CPU-only and never touch the GPU.)
- **Throughput:** ~26,500 forwards / 16 ≈ 1,660 batched forwards; at ~0.3–1 s each → ~10–30 min of pure forward compute. The §9 12-GPU-h budget carries headroom for model load, HF snapshot_download, padding, and the smoke pass.

### 6.6 Reliability-ceiling bracket √(r_yy) per behavior (Must-Fix #7 — split-half-over-PROBES for all three)

- **All three behaviors: split-half-over-PROBES + Spearman-Brown** `r_yy = 2 r_half/(1+r_half)`, averaged over 200 random probe-split seeds, AND a **binomial-variance decomposition** `√(SP/Var_C(E0))` with cell-actual `m = n_judged` per probe (for sycophancy `m=10` rollouts encode the per-probe sampling variance; for refusal/harmful `m=1`). - CV-match the ceiling to LOCO (Storrs 2020); cluster-bootstrap the √(r_yy) CI (B=2000). `Source: #742 plan v9 / Spearman 1904 + Schoppe 2016 + Storrs 2020`.
- **NOTE (deviation from v1):** v1 used split-half-over-ROLLOUTS for sycophancy; that requires raw rollout labels which are not persisted, so v2 uses split-half-over-probes uniformly (a slightly looser reliability estimate, noted in §11 + §12). #658's `aggregate.json` `noise_floor.p95` (8-probe-redraw test-retest, degenerate behaviors pinned to 1.0) is reported as a 2-method agreement check, NOT a substitute.
