# Plan — #1775 Nonlinearity of the four-arm context→answer maps: rank-r bilinear interaction vs kernel/MLP gain

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** First a cheap audit of the already-banked fitter comparisons (do their train/test splits leak near-duplicate prompts?). Then, on the 21K crossed real-conversation corpus, I test each of the four input views of the answer map for leftover structure a straight-line map misses, and fit progressively richer models (kernel, small neural net) under the exact same cross-validation splits. The centerpiece: a model that adds a small number of prefix-times-query product terms to the additive prefix+query map, to see if those named product terms account for the same skill bump the black-box fitters find.
- **What I expect:** The banked splits mostly hold up (the 1M round already deduplicated; the 50k round may have some near-duplicate contamination). The leftover-structure tests fire on the context and bare-query views, barely or not at all on the pre-query prefix view. The product-term model recovers most of the gap between the additive map and the full map with only a handful of terms.
- **What would change my mind:** If the product-term model closes almost none of the gap while the neural net closes a lot, the missing ingredient is not the prefix-query interaction and the "one shared missing component" story needs rethinking. If the 50k split turns out badly contaminated, the quoted +0.05 kernel gain at 50k was partly memorization and the 1M number becomes the only citable context-arm gain.

## 3. Hypothesis

H1 (fold hygiene): the n1m split's dedup is verifiable and clean; the n50k split (whose JSON records NO near-dupe pass) has nonzero near-duplicate train↔target contamination, small enough (< 5% of targets) that the +0.05 gain survives qualitatively.

H2 (detection): residual HSIC/dCor fires (p < 0.05, group-respecting nulls) on the context and bare-query arms; the prefix-end arm's residual dependence is weak or absent — the monitoring-relevant read.

H3 (ladder): per-arm nonlinearity gains at the 21K grain are small for prefix-input arms (bounded by their ceilings) and largest for query-bearing inputs.

H4 (headline, Q2): the rank-r bilinear interaction with small r closes most of the nonlinear gap available on disjoint inputs: R²(stitch+bilinear) approaches R²(stitch-MLP), and the interaction directions project onto the persona-vector dictionary / answer PCs above a matched-norm random null.

Headline quantities (pca48, cell_inst_own, L14, novel-prefix folds; r* selected on inner-validation folds only): Δ_named = R²(stitch+bilinear at r*) − R²(stitch+bilinear at r=0) — same GD protocol on both sides, interaction rank the ONLY difference, so W de-regularization by the joint AdamW refit cannot masquerade as interaction capacity (the r=0−minus−stitch-ridge delta is reported separately as the de-regularization component); δ_beyond = R²(stitch-MLP) − R²(stitch+bilinear at r*). ALL lattice-feeding CIs are paired CLUSTER bootstrap CIs: the resampling unit equals the fold scheme's grouping unit (held-out PREFIX groups under novel-prefix folds; QUERY groups under novel-query; both axes for the doubly-novel read) — rows within a group share prefix representation + fold-fit error, so an iid row bootstrap understates SE by ≈√deff; the row-level bootstrap is reported as a labeled companion only. Zero new compute: a re-reduction of the persisted per-row prediction shards.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Named-interaction ⇔ Δ_named's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side AND δ_beyond's 95% CI does NOT exclude 0 on the positive side (a straddle or a negative-side exclusion both qualify — either way there is no unnamed positive residual; a significantly NEGATIVE δ_beyond means the bilinear closed MORE than the stitch-MLP, maximally named); Partially-named ⇔ Δ_named's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side AND δ_beyond's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Not-named ⇔ otherwise. Narration co-signature (required before "the nonlinearity IS the interaction"): the bilinear-residual HSIC (P4) must also drop toward the null — a δ_beyond straddle alone is equally consistent with stitch-MLP underfit.

Row-coverage: the plan's own fits produce every registered row on each arm.

(The paired R² contrasts — nonlinear vs ridge per arm, bilinear vs stitch vs stitch-MLP — are computed on the SAME held-out rows per fold; per-row squared-error differences are aggregated to per-GROUP means, and the paired CLUSTER bootstrap resamples those groups — the fold scheme's own grouping unit — never raw rows.)

**P4 — Rank-r bilinear interaction + stitch-MLP + interpretation (pod GPU). New script `scripts/issue1775_bilinear.py`.**

- Model: â = W·[p;q] + Σᵢ₌₁..ᵣ (uᵢᵀp)(vᵢᵀq)·wᵢ + b, p = prefix_end (3584), q = bare_query (3584), output = pooled target (pca48 primary — headline; ambient companion). Fit: warm-start W at the P1 stitch-ridge solution, then joint full-batch AdamW on GPU (float32; the interaction adds only r × (3584+3584+d_out) params); weight decay ∈ {0, 1e-4, 1e-2} and early-stop selected on the inner validation split; r ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64} with r* selected on inner validation over r ≥ 1, full r-curve on outer test reported as exploratory (labeled). The r=0 rung (GD-refit of W with NO interaction terms, same optimizer/wd/early-stop protocol) is the de-regularization null: Δ_named = R²(r*) − R²(r=0), and R²(r=0) − R²(stitch PRESS-ridge) is reported separately as the de-regularization component (Alternatives-critic control).
- Seeds: 3 per (r, fold). Fold schemes: novel-prefix (primary) + novel-query + doubly-novel robustness read.
- **Stitch-MLP comparator (in-scope addition, stated):** an MLP (same #779 recipe, w=8192, 3 seeds) on the SAME [p;q] input under the SAME folds — the unrestricted-nonlinearity ceiling on disjoint inputs that the headline lattice's δ_beyond requires. Grounding: Gate A threshold = 1% of targets — below it the R² sensitivity bound (P0 step 4) is < ~0.01, inside the banked bootstrap CI half-width (±0.011, `n50k_fits.json` ridge CI). Gate B α = 0.05 with B = 1000, Holm-corrected — sign grounded: the context arm MUST fire (banked +0.09 L14 gain), which the power check enforces. Gate C threshold = 2× the banked CI half-width. Precedent recompute: the banked context refit (battery-excluded engine rerun in #1092's inline round) reproduced 0.814/0.914 exactly — Gate C classifies it PASS, matching the prose.

Kill criteria: (1) Gate C fails after one fix round → `epm:failure` (failure_class: code), blocked. (2) The planted-effect power check fails (context-arm dependence undetected) → detection claims are void; halt P2-dependent reads, fix instrument. ## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Fold protocol (novel-prefix 6-fold, FOLD_SEED=0, battery-excluded 17,308 fit rows — BANKED_READ1's 19,708 is the trait-stratum-excluded battery-INCLUDED population).** Why: identical-folds is the definition of a valid gain; parity with the banked linear reads. Source: `#1092` (`issue1092_fit_grid.py` L61/L175; `inline_fair_comparison/fair_comparison.json` metadata).
- **Novel-query companion folds.** Why: prefix-only grouping shares queries across train/test; nonlinear fitters can interpolate per-query answer means, inflating gains on query-bearing arms. Source: design-derived from the crossed structure (`#1092` manifest); the group-fold requirement itself per `.claude/rules/ood-generalization-folds.md`.
- **Targets (pooled t1/t2/t3 stacked ambient + train-fold pca48), layer 14 primary / 19 bridge.** Source: `#1092` protocol; L19 bridge = the #779 banked layer (`n1m_fits.json .layer = 19`); L14 cross-grain anchor = `n1m_multilayer_fits.json`.
- **Ridge λ via PRESS + df(λ) + ±10× sensitivity.** Source: `#1092` engine + plan-doc §8 λ discipline.
- **Exact-RBF KRR at the 21K grain (not Nyström).** Why: n_train ≈ 14.4k is inside the exact-feasible regime (the scope's "exact kernels don't scale past ~10⁴" bars the 1M grain; #779 ran exact at n=50k in 13.6 s); exact removes an approximation knob from the gain definition. Source: `#779` (`n50k_fits.json` krr fit_meta; wide grid `n50k_fits_krr_wide.json`). Alternatives: Nyström m=16384 (kept as the RFF scalable-family check instead).
- **RFF D=16384.** Why: a scalable-family rung matching the #779 Nyström center count, so the family-vs-family comparison at 21K mirrors the 1M protocol. Source: Rahimi & Recht, NIPS 2007 (method); D matched to `#779` m_centers=16384 (validated exact-vs-Nyström gap 0.0078, `n1m_fits.json .nystrom_validation`).
- **MLP recipe (w=8192, lr 3e-4, batch 4096, early-stop, AdamW).** Source: `#779` (`n50k_fits.json` mlp fit_meta; `n1m_fits.json` mlp_w8192 fit_meta — val-selected in the original round and reused verbatim since).
- **MLP seeds = 3.** Why: the plan doc requires seed spread for MLPs; at n ≥ 16k optimizer variance is small (the plan doc's regime-reversal note), so 3 seeds bound the spread without dominating budget. Source: plan-doc Task B requirement; the count itself ungrounded — needs smoke-test (spread reported; if seed SD > bootstrap SE, seeds escalate per §9 stratification in reverse).
- **Bilinear form a = W[p;q] + Σ(uᵢᵀp)(vᵢᵀq)wᵢ.** Source: DMDc template arXiv:1409.6358 (linear-with-control) + low-rank factored bilinear term arXiv:1610.04325.
- **r grid {1,2,4,8,16,32,64}.** Why: spans the plan-doc's named regimes — r=1 gain-knob, small-r named channels, r ≥ 48 (the answer-PC dimension) diffuse; log-spaced. Source: design-derived from plan-doc §3 Q2 + the 48-PC target dimension; grid density ungrounded — needs smoke-test (inner-val curve shape decides if refinement is needed).
- **Warm-start at the ridge stitch + wd grid {0, 1e-4, 1e-2} + full-batch AdamW.** Why: the linear part has a closed form; GD then only has to learn the interaction — removes the underfitting-artifact confound. Source: ungrounded — needs smoke-test (standard practice; validated by the fit reproducing stitch-ridge R² at r=0).
- **HSIC/dCor statistics + B=1000 + planted-effect power check.** Source: `#763` (`analysis/issue_763_nonlinear.py`, module docstring cites Székely arXiv:0803.4101, Gretton et al. 2005; B=1000 is the module default validated there).
- **Crossed block-permutation schemes.** Why: rows within a prefix are dependent; naive row shuffles are anti-conservative; the balanced dense core licenses exact block exchanges aligned by the crossing. Source: Winkler et al. 2015 (block permutation under group exchangeability); instantiation design-derived from the `#1092` crossed manifest.
- **Near-dupe gate: 5-gram shingles, Jaccard ≥ 0.8; MinHash 64 perms.** Source: `#779` n1m's own recorded procedure (`n1m_fits.json .split.near_dupe`) — the audit must apply the round's own criterion; MinHash perm count ungrounded — needs smoke-test (recall vs exact Jaccard verified on a 1k-pair sample in P0).
- **Reused input-data artifacts:** #1092 stores — Source: `#1092`; fetched on the pod via scoped `list_repo_tree` + per-file `hf_hub_download` from `issue1092_realistic_crossing/…` (no staging-layout transformation: files are consumed at their fetch destinations mirroring the hub-relative tree the #1092 loaders already read — "no staging transformation" recorded for legs (h)(iv)). #779 manifests/chunks — Source: `#779`; same fetch path. Banked JSONs — committed `eval_results/…` (git-tree-reachable; arrive with the clone on every lane).
- **Tool-behavior claims:** `dispatch_issue.py` accepts `--gpus` (Source: CLAUDE.md §Compute backends #1121 width-aware walk; exact flag re-verified at implementation — `Source: ungrounded — verify at implementation`); `verify_reused_artifact_keys.py` exists with `--hf-repo/--hf-path/--keys` (Source: `.claude/rules/artifact-reuse.md` check (c) mechanization; re-grep at implementation); `task.py post-marker --file` behavior per CLAUDE.md (not load-bearing here).
