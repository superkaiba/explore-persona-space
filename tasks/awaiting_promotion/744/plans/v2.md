## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I feed Qwen-2.5-7B a few thousand sentences of ordinary
  English prose and, at every layer, look at how much each word's internal
  state resembles the next word's — and where along a sentence the state jumps
  hard instead of drifting smoothly. I do the same on a second, broader text
  sample to check the pattern isn't a quirk of one corpus.
- **What I expect:** Early/middle layers should be "live in the moment" — each
  word's state connects tightly to its immediate neighbor but loses the thread
  a word or two later — while the last layers should hold a steadier direction
  across several words. The jumps should pile up on a few specific kinds of
  words (the model's "sink" tokens, surprising words, and clause-opening words
  like "and"/"that"), not spread evenly.
- **What would change my mind:** If, once I correct for the known measurement
  artifact (a handful of runaway dimensions that fake high similarity), the
  late-layer "steadiness" disappears, that means the apparent smoothness was an
  illusion — and that would be the more interesting result, because the closest
  prior paper (on much smaller models) reported real late-layer persistence.

## 0. Plan Summary (technical)

- **Measurement:** pure forward-pass read of Qwen-2.5-7B residual stream over
  natural text — NO training. For each layer L (all 28) and consecutive token
  positions t, t+1, compute (1) standardized consecutive-token cosine, (2)
  Barenholtz direction-preservation (k=3 OLS trajectory fit; abs-cosine at
  +0/+1/+2/+3 steps) + trajectory-extrapolation L2 error, (3) per-position jump
  stratified by sink/outlier mask, surprisal, syntactic boundary.
- **Hyperparameters:** k=3 linear trajectory window (`Source:` 2606.05346 §2.2,
  Table 1 best config); rogue-dim ablation top-k=3 (`Source:` 2109.04404 — k∈{1,3,5}
  sweep + top-3 anisotropy table); fp16 activation storage; max_seq_len 1024
  (`Source:` 2606.05346 §2.1 — GPT-2's 1024 context, overlapping chunks); all 28
  layers (`Source:` Qwen-2.5-7B config + 2406.19384 all-layer frame); seed 744;
  massive-activation mask `|h| > 100 AND >= 1000x layer-median` (`Source:`
  2402.17762 §2 quantitative definition); surprisal from Qwen-2.5-7B itself.
- **Baselines / controls:** RAW cosine + STANDARDIZED (z-per-dim) +
  ROGUE-DIM-ABLATED triad reported for every curve; random-pair cosine baseline
  (Barenholtz reports 0.029 on GPT-2 768-dim; replicate on Qwen 3584-dim);
  Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct second arm (same code path, second model).
- **Loss surface:** N/A — no training, no loss.
- **Compute:** 1x A100-80 (GCP `auto`, `--intent eval`), single forward-pass
  dump over ~Natural Stories (~13k subword tok) + broader sample (1M tok
  streamed). CPU analysis on the VM. `Estimated GPU-hours (total): 3.0`
- **Evaluation:** per-layer mean +- bootstrap-over-SEQUENCES 95% CI for each
  curve. H1 confirmed if late-layer +1/+2/+3 standardized direction preservation
  stays elevated (CI excludes random baseline AND exceeds the mid-layer +1
  value); H2 if it collapses to near-baseline after standardization+ablation.
- **Risks:** (1) the dump batch/seqlen OOMs (mitigated: 1024 cap, batch=1,
  fp16 store, A100-80 has headroom); (2) rogue-dim ablation is itself
  depth-dependent and flips the picture — this is a finding, reported per-layer,
  not a failure; (3) the broader bootstrap CI can't separate H1 from H2 if too
  few sequences — smoke-test floor of >= 200 sequences per corpus enforced.

## 1. Goal + Hypotheses

**Goal (verbatim, frozen):** Characterize how standardized similarity and
directional continuity between consecutive-token residual-stream activations
vary across layers in Qwen-2.5-7B, and where discontinuities concentrate (token
type, surprisal, syntactic boundary).

This is a pure mechanistic characterization on forward-pass activations over
natural text. The closest published measurement (Barenholtz 2606.05346,
direction preservation) exists only for GPT-2/Pythia; no consecutive-token
similarity-vs-layer curve exists for Qwen-2.5-7B. The single deliberate variable
change vs Barenholtz is the base model (GPT-2/Pythia -> Qwen-2.5-7B); the
methodology (k=3 trajectory fit, direction-preservation formula, Natural Stories
corpus, +0/+1/+2/+3 decay table, random baseline) is matched.

**Hypotheses (verbatim from the body, with the distinguishing prediction):**

- **H1 (depth-graded continuity, Barenholtz pattern).** Standardized continuity
  / direction preservation is short-horizon in middle layers (strong at +0,
  collapses to near-chance by +1) and persistent in late layers (elevated across
  +1/+2/+3). *Distinguishing prediction:* the per-layer +1-step standardized
  direction-preservation curve is U-or-ramp-shaped in depth — low in the middle
  band, climbing to a clearly-above-baseline plateau in the last ~25% of layers
  (Barenholtz: GPT-2 L6 +1 = 0.10 -> L12 +1 = 0.54).
- **H2 (anisotropy-only).** Apparent smoothness is an artifact of upper-layer
  anisotropy; after standardization + rogue-dim ablation, consecutive-token
  similarity is flat/low across depth and there is no genuine late-layer
  directional persistence. *Distinguishing prediction:* after the
  standardize-then-ablate pipeline, the late-layer +1/+2/+3 direction
  preservation is statistically indistinguishable from the random baseline (CI
  overlaps 0.029-analogue), even though RAW cosine remains high late.
- **H3 (token-type-localized discontinuities).** Discontinuities are
  non-uniform — concentrate at sink/outlier tokens, high-surprisal tokens, and
  syntactic-boundary tokens — and the concentration pattern itself changes with
  depth. *Distinguishing prediction:* per-stratum mean jump differs across
  strata at every layer (sink >> non-sink; high-surprisal > low-surprisal;
  clause-opener > clause-interior), AND the relative ordering / magnitude of the
  three drivers shifts between the early/middle/late depth bands.

H1 vs H2 is decided by whether late-layer +1/+2/+3 direction preservation
survives the standardize+ablate pipeline. H3 is decided by the stratified
per-position jump distribution.

## 2. Prior Work

The motivating literature dive is `docs/token-to-token-activation-continuity-lit-review.md`.
The load-bearing numbers and methodology (read directly from the papers, not the
summary):

**Barenholtz 2026, "Trajectory Dynamics in Language Model Hidden States"
(arXiv 2606.05346).** The single closest published measurement. Method (§2.2):
at word position t, fit a linear trajectory by OLS to the hidden states at
positions t-k..t-1, extrapolate one step; trajectory-extrapolation error = L2
distance between extrapolated and actual hidden state. Window sweep {3,5,7} x
{linear, quadratic}; **the 3-word linear fit at the mid-layer consistently
outperformed longer windows and higher-degree fits** (§2.2, Table 1) — k=3 is
the grounded default. Direction preservation (§2.3): abs cosine between fitted
trajectory direction and actual displacement at +0/+1/+2/+3 steps. Verbatim
Table 4 (Natural Stories, GPT-2):

| | +0 | +1 | +2 | +3 | random baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Layer 6 (mid), w=3 | 0.44 | 0.10 | 0.08 | 0.08 | 0.029 |
| Layer 12 (final), w=3 | 0.61 | 0.54 | 0.54 | 0.54 | 0.029 |
| Layer 6 (mid), w=5 | 0.39 | 0.09 | 0.08 | 0.07 | 0.029 |
| Layer 12 (final), w=5 | 0.58 | 0.53 | 0.53 | 0.53 | 0.029 |

The random baseline 0.029 is the *expected abs cosine between random vectors in
768-dim* (GPT-2 width). Qwen-2.5-7B is 3584-dim, so the analogous chance
baseline is lower (~0.013 ≈ sqrt(2/(pi·d)) for d=3584; we compute it empirically
on Qwen, see §5). Surprisal correlates negatively with direction preservation
(r = -0.10 at L6, §3.4). Displacement ‖h_t − h_{t-1}‖ and extrapolation error
correlate only r=0.16 (§3.3) — dissociable. Off-diagonal (low-surprisal /
high-extrapolation) cell is enriched for **connectives and clause-openers**
("and", "as", "that", "had") (§4.2) — the H3 syntactic-boundary signal.
Replicates GPT-2 (absolute pos emb) and Pythia (RoPE) -> not a coordinate
artifact. Per-word hidden state = the **last subword token** of each word (§2.2).
Natural Stories processed in **overlapping chunks** to fit the 1024-token context
(§2.1).

**Ethayarajh 2019 (1909.00512).** Self-/intra-sentence similarity across layers;
representations get more context-specific (lower self-similarity) with depth.
Raw cosine badly contaminated by anisotropy (GPT-2 last layer: two random tokens
cosine ~0.99). Establishes the standardization requirement.

**Timkey & van Schijndel 2021 (2109.04404).** 1-5 dimensions dominate the entire
cosine computation, and they are NOT the dimensions that drive model behavior.
**Standardize (z-score per dim) before measuring any token-to-token similarity.**
The sole grounding for the top-k=3 rogue-dim ablation arm: Timkey's headline
anisotropy table reports the top-3 dimensions and its variability experiment
sweeps k∈{1,3,5}, so k=3 is one of the paper's three reported k.

**Sun et al. 2024, "Massive Activations" (2402.17762).** Quantitative definition
(§2, verbatim): "an activation qualifies as a massive activation if its magnitude
surpasses 100 and is at least or around 1,000 times larger than the median
magnitude of its hidden state." Concentrated on starting token + delimiter tokens
(., \n), roughly input-agnostic, emerge abruptly after early layers and diminish
in the last few. Sampling recipe: 100 RedPajama sequences x 4096 tokens. The
pin-able sink/outlier mask threshold for H3.

**Lad, Gurnee & Tegmark 2024, "Stages of Inference" (2406.19384).** Four-stage
depth frame: (1) detokenization (early — local context fused, adjacent-token
mixing most local), (2) feature engineering (middle — near-Markov), (3)
prediction ensembling (mid-late), (4) residual sharpening (final — persistent
drift + possible sharp final-layer collapse onto unembedding). The depth-band
interpretation frame for H1/H3.

**New search (arXiv MCP + GitHub, 2026-06-29) beyond the lit-review:** I
confirmed (a) Natural Stories is NOT on the HF Hub — its canonical source is the
`languageMIT/naturalstories` GitHub repo (branch `master`,
`naturalstories_RTS/all_stories.tok` + `parses/penn/all-parses.txt.penn`); (b)
no consecutive-position residual dump for Qwen-2.5-7B exists on the EPS HF data
repo (verified via `list_repo_files`, §design); (c) the EPS residual-read
scaffolding (#594/#502/#634) all reads ONE anchor slot per (sequence, position),
never consecutive positions — so the dump rig is genuinely new but inherits the
#594 `LayerCapture` forward-hook pattern.

## 3. Hypothesis + Falsification Criteria

All criteria are stated on the **standardized** + **rogue-dim-ablated** curves
(the raw curves are reported as the anisotropy-contaminated control, never as the
primary read). "Late band" = layers 22-28 (last ~25% of 28); "middle band" =
layers 10-18. Per-layer means carry bootstrap-over-sequences 95% CIs (§6).

- **H1 confirmed** if, on the standardized+ablated curve, late-band +1/+2/+3
  direction preservation is BOTH (i) above the empirical Qwen random baseline
  (CI excludes baseline) AND (ii) materially higher than the middle-band +1 value
  (non-overlapping CIs, or a >= 2x ratio). The Barenholtz prediction is a
  ramp/U from middle near-chance to a late plateau.
- **H2 confirmed (H1 falsified)** if late-band +1/+2/+3 direction preservation on
  the standardized+ablated curve is statistically indistinguishable from the Qwen
  random baseline (CI overlaps baseline), EVEN WHILE the RAW (un-standardized)
  curve shows high late-layer cosine. The signature is: RAW high late, but
  standardized+ablated flat/low across depth.
- **Mixed / partial:** standardization preserves the late plateau but rogue-dim
  ablation removes it (or vice versa) — a quantitatively reportable depth-graded
  artifact attribution, not a null. The per-flavor triad makes this legible.
- **H3 confirmed** if (i) per-stratum mean jump differs across strata within
  layers (sink > non-sink; high-surprisal-tercile > low-surprisal-tercile;
  clause-opener > clause-interior — each with non-overlapping bootstrap CIs at
  >= 1 layer), AND (ii) the relative magnitude/ordering of the three drivers
  shifts across the early/middle/late depth bands (e.g. sink-dominated early,
  surprisal-dominated late — the lit-review's detokenization-early-vs-
  surprisal-late conjecture).
- **H3 falsified** if jumps are statistically uniform across all strata at all
  layers (all stratum CIs overlap) — i.e. discontinuities are not token-type
  localized.

**Kill criteria** (run-validity, not hypothesis outcomes) — see §7.

Model: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B` (base), bf16, batch=1, no padding (single-sequence
forward, exactly as #594). Assert `n_layers == 28` and `hidden == 3584` at load

```python
# per sequence (a word/token list):

#                                                       (next-token logits at t-1)
#   For NS: also map each subword position -> its word (last-subword-per-word
#           index per Barenholtz §2.2) so word-level reads are available;
#           the PRIMARY token-level reads use every consecutive subword pair.
```

**Two storage strategies, by corpus (the §9-sized split):**

- **Natural Stories (Corpus A): RETAIN the full raw fp16 dump.** ~13k subword
  tokens x 28 layers x 3584 x 2 bytes ≈ **2.6 GB fp16** (5.2 GB fp32) — well under
  the 50 GB VM floor. Store `(L, T, hidden)` fp16 per sequence to
  `data/issue_744/ns_raw/seq_<item>.pt` plus the per-token surprisal, sink mask,
  and syntactic mask. Retaining the raw dump is REQUIRED for lens 3 (H3 needs
  per-position retrieval to stratify) and lets the standardize/ablate pipeline
  re-run on the actual vectors.
- **Broader (Corpus B): STREAM — per-sequence sufficient statistics only, NO raw
  retention.** 1M tokens raw would be ~200 GB fp16 (far above the 50 GB floor).
  Instead compute online, per layer L:
  - mean vector `mu_L` and per-dim second moment (for the z-standardization
    mean/std estimated over the full token population — a single streaming pass
    of sums + sum-of-squares),
  - then a SECOND pass (or buffered within-sequence, since standardization needs
    the population mean/std) that, per sequence, accumulates the consecutive
    cosine + direction-preservation + extrap-error SUMS and the per-stratum jump
    sums. Per-sequence summaries (a handful of floats per layer x flavor x step)
    are the only thing retained: `data/issue_744/broader_seq_summaries.parquet`
    (~MB). Retain a bounded raw subset (`--broader-raw-keep 200` sequences,
    ~ < 10 GB fp16) ONLY for a lens-3 broader-corpus spot-check.

  Two-pass detail: pass 1 streams sums/sum-of-squares per dim per layer to fix
  `mu_L, sigma_L` (population standardization stats); the rogue-dim ranking
  (top-k by standardized variance / kurtosis per Timkey) is also computed here.
  Pass 2 re-forwards each sequence (cheap — generation-free, batched) and emits
  the per-sequence summary statistics under the fixed `(mu_L, sigma_L,
  rogue_idx_L)`. This keeps the standardization population-consistent without
  retaining 200 GB.

**Surprisal, sink mask, syntactic mask are all computed in Phase 1** (they need
the model / tokenization) and stored alongside:
- **Surprisal:** Qwen-2.5-7B's own next-token NLL at each position (the standard
  self-surprisal; matches "per-token surprisal under Qwen-2.5-7B itself").
- **Sink/outlier mask:** per Sun §2 — a position is a sink iff its hidden state
  contains an activation with `|h| > 100 AND |h| >= 1000 x median(|h| at that
  layer's hidden state)`. Computed per (layer, position); a position is also
  tagged with whether it is a special/delimiter/starting token (Sun's enrichment
  classes: BOS, `.`, `\n`).
- **Syntactic-boundary mask (NS only, has Penn parses):** a word is a
  clause-opener iff it is the first terminal under an `S`/`SBAR` constituent in
  the Penn parse, OR it is a coordinator/complementizer (`CC`/`IN` POS tag
  opening a new constituent) — the Barenholtz §4.2 enrichment class ("and", "as",
  "that", "had"). For the broader corpus (no gold parses), fall back to a fixed
  clause-opener wordlist (the closed-class connectives Barenholtz names + Penn
  CC/IN closed class) — named explicitly, flagged as a coarser proxy in §10.

Phase 1 emits the phase lines + end-of-run sentinel, uploads ALL artifacts (NS
raw dump, broader summaries, masks, surprisal, manifest) via ONE bulk
`upload_folder` to `superkaiba1/explore-persona-space-data/issue744_token_continuity/`,
verifies via `list_repo_files`, THEN the orchestrator releases the GPU pod
(§9 sequencing).

For each layer L (0..27) and each flavor in {raw, standardized,
standardized+ablated}, compute: the consecutive-cosine curve, the

fits on the raw hidden states, but our measurement-validity rule mandates the
standardized read as primary; the raw-flavor versions are reported as the
anisotropy control). CIs by bootstrap over sequences (§6).

The k=3 OLS fit is vectorized (no Python loop over t): build the sliding-window
design matrix once per sequence, batch the lstsq over all valid t (einops). All
reductions stay in numpy/torch on CPU; only per-layer scalars + CI arrays are
written.

### 4.5 Phase 2 — discontinuity-locus stratification (H3)

For the NS raw dump (per-position retrieval available), per layer L:
- jump magnitude `||h^L_t − h^L_{t-1}||_2` (on standardized states) + the
  direction-preservation residual `(1 − dp[+0])` at each position;
- bin each position by (i) sink mask (sink / non-sink), (ii) surprisal tercile
  (low/mid/high, terciles computed over the corpus token population), (iii)
  syntactic mask (clause-opener / clause-interior);
- report per-stratum mean jump + bootstrap-over-sequences CI per layer, and the
  early/middle/late band summary showing how the stratum ordering shifts with
  depth.

The broader-corpus lens-3 spot-check uses the bounded 200-sequence raw subset
(sink + surprisal strata; syntactic uses the wordlist proxy).

### 4.6 Why code, not a model call?

The entire analysis is numeric (cosine, OLS, bootstrap, masking over float
tensors) — there is no unstructured-data classification/extraction step that a
Claude call could do. The one borderline component is the syntactic-boundary
mask: for Natural Stories it is read from the corpus's GOLD Penn parses (no model
call — deterministic constituency lookup), and the broader-corpus fallback is a
fixed closed-class wordlist (deterministic). A per-token POS/clause-boundary
Claude call was considered and rejected: at ~1M token positions it is
prohibitively expensive and slow, non-deterministic, and unnecessary given gold
parses on the comparability corpus. (`N/A` for every other component — pure

### CI methodology (exact)

Bootstrap over **sequences** (NOT over per-token observations — within-sequence
positions are autocorrelated and not independent, so a token-level bootstrap
would massively understate the CI). For each per-layer / per-flavor / per-step
statistic: resample sequences with replacement B=2000 times; each resample's
statistic is the sequence-summary-weighted mean (weighted by each sequence's
valid-pair count); the 95% CI is the [2.5, 97.5] percentile of the bootstrap
distribution. Natural Stories has only 10 sequences (a known limitation — the
broader corpus's 2000 sequences carry the statistical weight; the NS read is the
cross-paper-comparable point estimate and its CI will be wide and is reported
honestly). For H3 strata, bootstrap over sequences within each stratum.

## 11. Decision Rationale

`kind: experiment` — every load-bearing dial gets What/Why/Source/Alternatives.
No model training, so no LR/epochs/LoRA/optimizer rows.

1. **Trajectory window k=3.**
   - What: fit the OLS linear trajectory to the previous 3 token states.
   - Why: matches the Goal's Barenholtz operationalization; the paper swept
     {3,5,7} x {linear,quadratic} and 3-word linear consistently won.
   - Source: arXiv 2606.05346 §2.2 + Table 1 ("The 3-word linear fit at layer 6
     consistently outperformed longer windows and higher-degree fits").
   - Alternatives: k=5/7 (paper-tested, worse — report k=5 as a robustness check
     in the over-produce dump); quadratic fit (paper-tested, worse).

2. **Rogue-dim ablation top-k=3.**
   - What: zero out the top-3 cosine-dominating dimensions (after standardizing).
   - Why: Timkey shows 1-5 dims dominate the entire cosine; its headline
     anisotropy table reports the top-3 dimensions and its variability experiment
     sweeps k∈{1,3,5}. k=3 is the middle of that reported set and matches the
     paper's headline top-3 table -> a paper-grounded, conservative ablation.
   - Source: arXiv 2109.04404 (k∈{1,3,5} sweep + top-3 anisotropy table).
   - Alternatives: top-1 / top-5 (the other two reported k — report top-1 as
     robustness in the over-produce dump); a variance-threshold (less
     reproducible — fixed k is cleaner).

3. **Max sequence length 1024.**
   - What: truncate / chunk sequences to 1024 tokens.
   - Why: Barenholtz processed Natural Stories in overlapping chunks for GPT-2's
     1024-token context; 1024 is also the project's default cap for non-marker
     evals and is ample for prose paragraphs. Qwen-2.5-7B supports far more, but
     1024 matches the comparability corpus's chunking and bounds per-sequence
     compute.
   - Source: arXiv 2606.05346 §2.1 ("processed each story ... in overlapping
     chunks to accommodate the model's 1,024-token context limit").
   - Alternatives: 2048 (no benefit for prose paragraphs; more compute); full
     context (breaks Barenholtz chunking comparability).

4. **Activation dtype fp16 (NS raw store) / fp32 streaming accumulators.**
   - What: store the retained NS raw dump in fp16; accumulate streaming sufficient
     stats in fp32.
   - Why: fp16 halves the NS dump (2.6 vs 5.2 GB) with negligible precision loss
     for cosine/OLS; fp32 accumulators avoid catastrophic cancellation in the
     streaming mean/variance over 1M tokens.
   - Source: #594 stores per-probe tensors in fp16 with the true fp32 mean
     alongside (lines 604-617) — the validated EPS precision pattern.
   - Alternatives: fp32 raw store everywhere (2x disk, no analysis benefit); bf16
     store (fp16 has more mantissa bits, better for cosine).

5. **Layer subset = all 28.**
   - What: capture every decoder layer.
   - Why: the Goal is a per-layer CURVE across all layers; the depth-band frame
     (Lad/Gurnee/Tegmark) requires the full depth axis.
   - Source: Qwen-2.5-7B config (`num_hidden_layers=28`, verified) + arXiv
     2406.19384 (all-layer depth frame).
   - Alternatives: subsample layers (defeats the per-layer-curve Goal).

6. **Token budget — NS full (~13k) + broader 1M (from the WikiText-103 train split).**
   - What: dump all Natural Stories; cap the broader corpus at 1M subword tokens,
     drawn from the WikiText-103 raw TRAIN split.
   - Why: NS full is the comparability corpus (small, must use all of it); 1M
     broader tokens give ample bootstrap power over 2000 sequences while the
     STREAM keeps the footprint at ~MB (sizing §9). 1M >> the ~10k Barenholtz
