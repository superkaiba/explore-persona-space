## 1. Goal + hypothesis

**Hypotheses (quantitative, pre-registered).**
- **H1 (protocol validity / ridge ordering).** Under the uniform harness the parent's ordering survives: ridge beats every direction method on format_style (parent gap: ridge 0.615 vs direction −0.075), and on the other four behaviors the best direction method sits within |Δρ| ≤ 0.15 of ridge (parent: within 0.07 on four). *Falsified if* a direction method beats ridge by >0.10 on ≥3 of 5 behaviors — the parent's ridge advantage was a protocol artifact, and the Takeaways bullet on the PV baseline is rewritten.
- **H2 (neutral contrast).** The neutral-contrast direction performs within |Δρ| ≤ 0.10 of the stripped opposite-contrast direction on ≥3 of 5 behaviors — the opposite-trait instruction is not load-bearing for the read-out use of the direction. *Falsified if* the neutral direction is worse by >0.10 on ≥3 behaviors (the opposite pole genuinely sharpens the read-out direction). Genuinely uncertain: #661 only showed the READ context barely rotates the direction (cos ≥ 0.95); the CONTRAST pole change is untested.
- **H3 (direction reality).** Every non-random direction method clears the K=100 random-direction layer-max band (its ρ above the band's 97.5th percentile) on the four behaviors whose parent reads work. *Falsified if* a direction method lands inside the band — its read was selection-over-layers, not signal.
- **H4 (nonlinear benefit).** No detectable nonlinear gain at n=50: kernel−linear paired Δρ has sign-flip p ≥ 0.05 AND post-LEACE residual dCor is inside its permutation null, on all 5 behaviors. Either way the claim is BOUNDED by the synthetic power pre-check ("a gain below ~X would not have been detected"), never "no nonlinear signal". *Falsified if* both tests fire on the same behavior — nonlinearly-decodable structure exists beyond the linear read (a follow-up target).

### 4.2 Phase C — the matched-protocol co-fit (GPU `eval` lane, `EPM_FIT_DEVICE=cuda`)

NEW `scripts/issue763_cofit_predictors.py` + `src/explore_persona_space/analysis/issue_763_cofit.py`, importing the existing exactness-gated machinery (`issue_763_vectorized.batched_ridge_press_loco_fixed_dim`, `issue_763_pca`, `issue_763_reliability.split_half_probe_aligned_v2`) — reuse check (i): batched inner axes + `device` call-time parameter, VERIFIED (the reanchor round ran it on CUDA).

**The uniform protocol (every method, identically):**
- **Target:** graded E0 (v2 deception / v1 others), rank-transformed within each training fold (average ranks scaled to [0,1] — the rank-ridge move for the Spearman objective).
- **Folds:** LOCO over the 50 contexts, one shared fold list, seed 763.
- **Subspace methods** (ridge, kernel ridge, prompt-side ridge): per-fold PCA fit on the 49 training contexts, **fixed d = 10** (NOT nested-CV, NOT 48); λ ∈ {1e-2, 1e-1, 1, 10, 100, 1e3} selected per fold by training-fold PRESS; kernel ridge = RBF, bandwidth γ ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} × median heuristic (training-fold pairwise distances), γ and λ selected by inner training-fold criterion.
- **Direction methods** (r_A, r_C, r_neutral, crude diff-means, random directions): projection scalar `r[ℓ] · v0(C)[ℓ]`; per-fold 1-D linear fit on the rank target (sign + scale on the 49 training contexts), predict the held-out context.
  - **Crude diff-means (pre-registered):** within each training fold, median-split the 49 contexts on graded E0; direction = mean(v0 | top half) − mean(v0 | bottom half) per layer (mass-mean probing, Marks & Tegmark 2310.06824). Refit per fold (it is supervised — never fit on all 50).
  - **Random-direction floor:** K = 100 seeded Gaussian unit directions in the 3584-dim space; each gets the FULL protocol including per-fold 1-D fit and layer-max; report the 2.5–97.5 percentile band of layer-max held-out ρ. This is the empirical selection floor every real direction must clear (H3).
- **Statistic:** pooled held-out Spearman ρ(predictions, graded E0) over the 50 contexts, per (method, layer).
- **Layer selection:** per method, the layer-max of held-out ρ over all 28 layers (read-out/prediction regime per `.claude/rules/persona-vectors-recipe.md` step 7), full per-layer curves reported.
- **Selection-symmetric shuffle null (the delta-3 fix):** 1000 label permutations per (method, behavior); EVERY draw re-runs the full pipeline (per-fold refit incl. the supervised diff-means refit and the 1-D fits) across all 28 layers and takes the SAME layer-max before the band is formed. The per-draw × per-layer ρ matrix (1000 × 28 per method × behavior) is persisted (`analysis_tensors/cofit_null_matrices/`). Vectorized: the batched ridge/kernel solvers batch (perm × layer × λ) on GPU; direction-method nulls are batched GEMMs.
- **Control task:** Hewitt-Liang shuffled-E0 selectivity retained (per parent §6 item 8b), also layer-max-matched.
- **CI:** context bootstrap B = 2000, seed 763, per method at its chosen layer.
- **Ceiling:** the probe-aligned √r_yy (frozen values above) drawn on every figure; no refit of reliability.
- **Ridge positive control:** the harness ridge at the parent's chosen layer must land within ±0.12 of the parent's chosen-layer graded ρ (0.510/0.714/0.615/0.743/0.861) — the rank-target + fixed-d=10 change should move it modestly; outside the band ⇒ investigate before interpreting cross-method gaps (soft check, fail-loud on >±0.25).
- **Binary companion (continuity column):** the ridge column additionally run on the binary expressed-rate target (rank-transformed), so the co-fit table joins to the parent's binary reads; no other method gets the binary target (dual-DV is a DV property, not per-method).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Fixed PCA d=10 for all subspace methods** — What: d=10, no nested-CV. Why: uniformity across ridge/kernel/prompt-side is the point of the co-fit; d≈10 is where nonparametric tests retain power at n=50 and matches the scope's "PCA-10 NOT 48". Source: followup-scope contract + #742 plan §11 (Reddi arXiv:1406.2083 power-vs-dimension; Stone 1982; Varoquaux arXiv:1706.07581). Alternatives: nested-CV d (the parent's choice — rejected: per-method d would un-match the protocol); d=48 (the #658 near-interpolation regime the scope bans).
- **Rank-transformed target ("rank-ridge")** — Why: the report statistic is Spearman; fitting on ranks aligns the loss with the objective. Source: followup-scope contract; Conover & Iman 1981 (rank transformations). Alternative: raw graded target (kept as an exploratory ridge column for continuity).
- **λ grid, LOCO folds, seed 763, B=2000 boot, 1000 perms** — Source: #763 plans/v3.md §6 as-run (inherit fast-path).
- **Selection-symmetric nulls (per-draw layer-max) + persisted matrices** — Source: `.claude/rules/selection-symmetric-nulls.md` (#778); fixes the parent's registered `headline-null-statistic-mismatch` caveat.
- **Kernel ridge, RBF, median-heuristic bandwidth, never an n=50 MLP** — Source: #742 plan §11 (Gretton 2005 median heuristic; Pimentel 2004.03061 probe caution).
- **LEACE + dCor/HSIC, B=10,000, power pre-check at effect floor ~0.10** — Source: #742 plan (Belrose arXiv:2306.03819; Székely arXiv:0803.4101; the Stage-1 step-0 power pre-check). The deep-research dive (weuzvqsj1) that was to refine this is unfindable (§12 row 8) — the #742 protocol is the scope's named fallback.
- **Paired-fold sign-flip permutation for kernel-vs-linear, B=10,000** — Why: the two methods share folds, so per-context error differences are paired; sign-flip is the exact test at n=50. Source: followup-scope contract ("paired-fold permutation test"); standard paired-permutation construction (Good 2000).
- **Neutral pole = bare default chat context (no system message)** — Why: the default assistant is the leakage program's canonical default context, and an instruction-shaped neutral prompt ("You are a helpful assistant.") risks encoding the default-template context family the predictor scores. Source: `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` (bare default assistant) + the #247/#329 default-context convention. Alternative rejected: "You are a helpful assistant." (axis-conflation risk).
- **Neutral budget 20 q × 50 rollouts = 1000, temp 1.0, max_new 256** — Why: matches the per-pole sample budget and generation config of the Phase-1 arms (diff-of-means variance parity). Source: #763 plans/v3.md §4.5 as-implemented (`N_ROLLOUTS_PER_POLE`, max_new 256); the 50-per-question reallocation is forced by D2 (no pair axis) — stated, deterministic.
- **Neutral keep threshold <50, drop-never-coerce** — Source: `.claude/rules/persona-vectors-recipe.md` step 4 (the negative-arm threshold applied to the neutral pole; deviation D1 named in §4.4).
- **Keep-floor 250 / 25 (flagged band between)** — Source: `ungrounded — needs smoke-test`; anchored only by the parent's precedent of using a 49-rollout arm flagged `pv_thin_sample` (so 25 ≈ half that precedent as the hard floor). Listed in §12 at Low confidence.
- **Stripped (r_C) read for the positive pole of r_neutral and both poles of r_C** — Why: (i) content identity — the Phase-1 instruction-pair texts are unrecoverable (§5), so the instruction-present positive-pole read cannot be reconstructed; (ii) construction cleanliness — an instruction-free neutral pole paired with an instruction-present positive pole would bake the #661 instruction-context axis into the direction. Source: #661 (cos(A,C) 0.977–0.997 — the strip barely rotates the direction) + the §5 fitness-fail record.
