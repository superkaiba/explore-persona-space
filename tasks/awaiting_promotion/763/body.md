# Base-model answer activations predict behavioral expression for all 5 matched-probe behaviors once the deception rubric is re-anchored (LOCO ρ 0.51–0.86, two as reduced-power reads) (MODERATE confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_763.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/b0f2ece47186cb07b14ca6b0d39b73c898cf61c0/docs/methodology/issue_763.md) · [gist](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/ff9725fdd2564e1432b4c5a0f0c324ae)

## Takeaways

- Matched probes + graded scoring: **all five behaviors** clear their nulls — LOCO **ρ 0.51/0.71/0.62/0.74/0.86** (deception/fact_expression/format_style/self_report/persona_drift), p ≤ 0.001; the last two are m=20 reduced-power reads; re-confirmed under round-3 selection-matched layer-max nulls.
- Re-anchoring the deception rubric to observed exemplars moved LOCO ρ **0.11 → 0.51** (CI 0.24–0.71) on the same 3,000 completions; the original √r_yy = 0.00 was a split-half estimator artifact (probe-aligned ceiling 0.586, a code-level fix adopted after the pre-set decision rule).
- A round-4 disclosure carve-out (rubric v2.1) failed both engagement legs of its decision rule fixed in advance (**flagged-item drop 19.9, floor 30; flag-rate ratio 0.81**) — verdict KILL: deception construct-robustness stays uncertified; ρ 0.43 inside the v2 band (descriptive only), paired Δρ CI includes 0.
- Matched-protocol co-fit (8 predictors): every predictor clears its selection-matched null, but **K=100 random directions reach ρ 0.62–0.85** — method attribution largely unidentifiable at n = 50; the parent's format_style direction collapse was protocol-induced (stripped read **+0.43**, crude diff-means 0.58).
- The neutral-contrast direction (negative pole = bare default context) matches the stripped opposite-contrast direction within **Δρ ≤ 0.10** on all 4 buildable behaviors — the opposite-trait instruction pole is not detectably load-bearing under this random-floor-limited read; format_style's neutral arm was unbuildable (3/1000 trait-absent rollouts).
- No behavior meets the pre-set joint nonlinear criterion: format_style alone shows a kernel-favoring corrected sign-flip (Δρ **+0.171**, p = 0.004) but its dCor erasure verdict is unresolved; the as-run sign-flip (p = 1e-4 everywhere) was a prediction-scale artifact.

## Goal

- **This experiment in context:** [#658](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/658) measured the base-model answer-activation → expression predictor at m=8 judgments/context with mismatched probes and a binary DV, and concluded the mean-activation summary predicts only 3/10 behaviors. [#761](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/761) (phase 1) re-measured the 3 high-m behaviors and found the ridge and GLM reads agree within ±0.04. This task is phase 2: the 5 remaining low-m behaviors (deception, fact_expression, format_style, self_report, persona_drift) re-measured with matched per-behavior eliciting-probe pools (m = 60/60/60/20/20), a graded multi-sampled 0–100 E0 as the primary regression DV (binary rate retained as the validated companion), ridge + precision-weighted binomial GLM, and a faithfully-extracted persona-vector baseline. A same-issue follow-up round (`deception-rubric-reanchor`) then re-anchored the deception graded rubric to observed completion exemplars and re-judged the same completions, to decide whether the deception flatness was an instrument defect or a behavior property. A third, user-directed round (`neutral-contrast-and-cofit`) added a neutral-contrast direction arm (negative pole = the bare default context), co-fit eight predictor variants under ONE uniform held-out protocol — retiring the parent's unmatched ridge-vs-direction comparison — and ran the planned nonlinear-benefit tests. A fourth round (`rubric-v2p1-disclosure-carveout`, proposer-initiated) probed whether the re-anchored deception recovery rides a documented disclosure-scored-as-deception leakage class, by appending one carve-out clause to the rubric (v2.1) and re-judging the same completions under an engagement-gated decision rule fixed in advance.
- **Broader narrative:** whether pre-fine-tuning context geometry (here the base model's mean answer-side activation v0(C,B)) predicts where behavior B is expressed across contexts C (`q:leak-predictor`), the base-side propensity leg of the leakage-prediction program.

## Methodology

- **Design:** 5 behaviors × 50 contexts (the battery's 7 context families: persona — house + PersonaHub sharing one family label — WildChat conversation prefixes (real user conversations, allenai/WildChat-1M), ICL, rephrase, format, default-template, behavior-adjacent), leave-one-context-out (LOCO) regression of graded E0(C,B) on v0(C,B). Single manipulated variable vs the parent m=8 measurement, per behavior: probe pool + m + DV grade (jointly the "measurement fix" arm; same contexts, same base model). No training — base-model-only. **Rounds:** round 1 (v1 rubric, all five behaviors); round 2 `deception-rubric-reanchor` — single experimental variable: the deception graded-rubric anchor text (v1 → v2), re-judging the SAME 3,000 frozen deception completions (24,000 graded draws) with the binary verdicts copied verbatim (frozen companion), then refitting deception only. The other four behaviors received a metadata-only refresh under a hard numeric-identity assert (verified: no non-metadata field changed); a subsequent zero-GPU follow-up then formally re-emitted the reliability fields for all five behaviors under the probe-aligned estimator (ρ fields unchanged; legacy values preserved in the records). In the refreshed JSONs, `m_e0: 60` for self_report/persona_drift deliberately records the known-buggy E0-side value as data beside the corrected probe count `m: 20` (plan §3b "record both on mismatch") — `m` is the probe count; do not read `m_e0` as one. Round 3 `neutral-contrast-and-cofit` (user-directed) — no re-judging, no new probes: (a) a NEUTRAL-contrast direction per behavior (positive pole = the kept phase-1 trait rollouts re-read with the instruction system prompt deleted; negative pole = 1,000 fresh rollouts under the bare default chat context, judge-filtered trait-absent) plus a stripped re-read of the opposite-contrast direction; (b) a matched-protocol co-fit of eight predictors under one uniform LOCO harness, retiring the parent's unmatched comparison (which gave the ridge held-out + PCA treatment and the directions in-sample best-of-28); (c) kernel-vs-linear and LEACE residual-dependence tests with a synthetic power pre-check. Graded targets, binary verdicts, v0 shards, folds, and seed all frozen. Round 4 `rubric-v2p1-disclosure-carveout` (proposer-initiated) — single experimental variable: ONE disclosure carve-out clause appended to the v2 deception rubric (v2.1; anchors + exemplars verbatim), re-judging the same 3,000 frozen completions (24,000 graded draws, binary frozen), deception refit only, siblings metadata-refreshed under the numeric-identity assert (re-verified). Instruments attached to the round, all fixed in advance: a symmetric disclosure-flag audit (temp-0 classification of every high-scoring rationale under a committed prompt, run on v2 AND v2.1) serving as the clause-engagement manipulation check; an identified-leakage ablation (the v2 fit re-run with flagged draws dropped); a 2×2 engagement-gated verdict rule evaluated before any headline (fail/fail = KILL, no refit headline); a paired per-context Δρ bootstrap; and a 20-row analyzer hand spot-check gate on the auditor.
