## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take 5,000 real user chats where we already saved the model's own written answers, replay each chat through Qwen once to record its internal state at every layer for a window of token positions around where the answer starts, and then fit simple predictors that guess the NEXT position's internal state from the current one — one predictor per layer, with and without being told which word actually comes next. Then I roll the predictors forward from the end of the prompt, position by position, without generating any words, and check how close the predicted states get to the real answer-time states — and whether trait signals (evil, sycophancy, hallucination) can be read off the predicted states as well as off the real ones.
- **What I expect:** the predictors will beat the do-nothing baseline at every layer, but a big chunk of each step will be unpredictable without knowing the incoming word — and that unpredictable chunk should shrink at deeper layers, where the model's state is more about accumulated context than the latest token. Rolling forward should help for a few positions and then drift off; reading traits off rolled states probably won't beat just reading the prompt's final state.
- **What would change my mind:** if the no-word predictor matches the told-the-word predictor (next states would be essentially context-determined), or if rolled-forward states carry MORE trait signal than the prompt's final state with error bars clear of zero — either would mean answer-time internals are forecastable without generating, which would make prediction-without-generation a real monitoring tool.

## 3. Hypothesis

All reads on held-out (500 test contexts), answer-content segment, raw space primary (RMS-norm companion).

- **H1 (single-step decomposition).** The context-only affine ridge clears the per-layer **shuffled-context null band** (context-only r2_id above the shuffle band's upper edge, computed with the SAME identity-relative R² denominator on the SAME 500-context test set) at a majority (≥15/28) of layers 1–28, but sits well below the token-informed ceiling: token-informed − context-only r2_id ≥ 0.10 on a majority (≥15/28) of layers. r2_id > 0 (clearing copy-previous) is retained as a sanity margin only — it is NOT the confirm criterion, since position-typical Δ structure alone can clear zero (the §5 shuffle control exists precisely to expose this). **Confirm:** both margins hold (shuffle-band clearance AND ceiling gap). **Falsify (either direction is a finding):** context-only inside/below the shuffle band at most (≥15/28) layers (position dynamics carry no context-specific signal beyond position-typical structure), OR token-informed − context-only < 0.05 at all layers (next states essentially context-determined — the strong "prediction without generation" result). Middle cells are reportable partial outcomes.
- **H2 (depth profile).** The carried-context share rises with depth: the ratio r2_id(context-only)/r2_id(token-informed) increases from early to late layers (Spearman ρ(layer, ratio) > 0 over layers 1–28, both spaces). At layer 0 (embedding stream) the context-only map fails by construction (h_{0,t+1} is exactly the injected token embedding) — a built-in sanity anchor. **Falsify:** flat or decreasing profile.
- **H3 (function class).** The small MLP does not beat affine ridge by > +0.05 r2_id on a majority of layers (prior: #841's depth-axis ridge won 27/27 vs MLP and GRU). **Falsify:** MLP clears +0.05 on most layers — position dynamics are more nonlinear than depth dynamics.
- **H4 (rollout).** Carrying layer set pinned: the 6 pre-registered read-out layers ℓ* ∈ {14, 17, 19, 20, 24, 26} (H4 is read there; full 28-layer curves reported as the exploratory shape). The context-only autoregressive roll beats BOTH rollout nulls — the frozen-state null (ĥ_{T+k} = h_T) AND the context-blind mean-drift null (ĥ_{T+k} = h_T + cumulative position-conditioned mean Δ, fit-set estimated; §4.4) — on the paired per-context skill difference with 95% CI clear of zero, at k ≤ 4, for ≥4 of the 6 ℓ* layers. **Confirm (mutually exclusive with falsify):** the small-k win above holds AND by k = 32 the roll is indistinguishable-from-or-below the frozen null (paired CI includes or sits below zero) at ≥4/6 ℓ* layers — the expected drift regime (2506.03566). **Falsify (positive surprise):** at k = 32 the roll remains ABOVE the frozen null with paired CI clear of zero at ≥4/6 ℓ* layers. Curves that beat frozen but not the mean-drift null are labeled "position-typical drift, not context-specific forecasting" — a reportable outcome, neither confirm nor falsify. All other middle cells are reportable partial outcomes.
- **H5 (read-out).** Trait read-out off rolled states does NOT beat the frozen-state read (≈ #779's direct context-side read): the paired delta of within-condition Pearson r (rolled − frozen) is centred on/below zero for all 3 traits. **Falsify:** rolled read beats frozen with 95% bootstrap CI clear of zero for ≥ 2 of 3 traits at the pre-registered PRIMARY ℓ*, using the pre-registered DV3 decision statistic = the **horizon-mean read over k = 1..32** (per-k reads are exploratory; any featured best-k read must apply per-draw same-selection over k via the persisted `readout_projections.npz` matrices) — rolling forward without tokens ADDS trait signal, the strong monitoring result. Exactly 1 of 3 traits clearing is a mixed/partial outcome, neither confirm nor falsify.

### 4.1 Formalization (the object of study)

For layer l ∈ {0..28} (0 = embedding stream via the `EMBED_LAYER = -1` sentinel of `analysis/extraction.py::extract_layer_activations`; 1..28 = block outputs, pre-final-norm at 28 — the #841 cx-array convention) and token position t inside a captured window of a real chat context (prompt x, the model's own persisted completion y), define the update Δ_{l,t} = h_{l,t+1} − h_{l,t} ∈ R^3584. The question: how much of Δ_{l,t} is predictable from (i) h_{l,t} alone (carried context — the deployable, no-generation regime), (ii) h_{l,t} + e_{t+1} (token-informed ceiling, where e_{t+1} = h_{0,t+1} is the injected input embedding), (iii) e_{t+1} alone (injected-token marginal)? Answer = held-out coordinate-averaged R² on Δ (identity-relative: predict-zero scores exactly 0; mean-centered companion), per layer, per function class, on answer-content transitions of held-out contexts. Then: rolling ĥ_{l,T+k} autoregressively from the last formatted-prompt state h_{l,T} with the context-only map — how does state-prediction skill decay with k, and does the rolled state carry trait signal (⟨ĥ_{ℓ*,T+k}, r_B(ℓ*)⟩ vs judged score) beyond the frozen state h_{ℓ*,T}?

### 4.3 Phase 2 — fits: `scripts/issue922_fit_maps.py`

Split: contexts 0..4999 → fit 4000 / inner-val 500 / test 500 via the #841 `make_split(seed=42)` (ported; identical assignment semantics). Split is BY CONTEXT — no position of a val/test context ever enters any fit, λ selection, or early stop.

Per layer l ∈ {0..28} × space ∈ {raw (σ≡1), RMS-norm (σ_l = RMS of Δ_l over fit-set answer transitions)}:

- **Copy-previous null:** ĥ_{t+1} = h_t (predict Δ=0). Free; scores exactly 0 in r2_id by construction (metric self-check).
- **Affine ridge** per arm ∈ {context-only X=h_{l,t} (d=3584), token-informed X=[h_{l,t}, h_{0,t+1}] (d=7168), embedding-only X=h_{0,t+1} (d=3584)}: closed-form with bias, λ by GCV over the log grid [1e-2, 1e3] (25 points), via ONE Gram assembly + ONE `torch.linalg.eigh` per (layer, arm) — GCV across the λ grid is then eigenvalue arithmetic, and the raw/RMS-norm spaces share the SAME eigendecomposition (targets rescale). fp32 GPU. ## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Corpus + split: 5000 LMSYS contexts, 4000/500/500 by context, seed 42.** Why: the parent's exact corpus and split semantics keep the position-axis atlas comparable to the depth-axis atlas; split by CONTEXT is the group-level fold. `Source: #779 pass_b / #841 make_split (verbatim)`.
- **Fit/eval targets from the model's own persisted completions (teacher-forced).** Why: on-policy supervision without any new generation; the no-generation constraint binds at ROLLOUT time, not fit time (task body). `Source: #922 body + #779 lmsys_g_rollouts`.
- **Capture window W_p=8 / W_a=40, layers 0–28, fp16.** Why: bounds the store at ≤49.9 GB (§9 arithmetic) while covering the k≤32 headline horizon with margin; 8 prompt positions give the GRU warm-up + boundary transitions; layer 0 (embedding stream) provides the token-informed input for free and a sanity anchor. `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (footprint-derived; no literature pins a window; Future Lens 2311.04897 decodes ~+1..+4 ahead and EAGLE drafts ≤~10 positions, so 40 covers prior horizons several-fold). Listed in §12 at Low confidence.
- **Prediction target = update Δ, dual conventions (identity-relative + mean-centered), raw + RMS-norm spaces.** Why: raw-state R² is trivially high under autocorrelation; per-layer RMS normalization removes depth-scale artifacts. `Source: 2405.12250; #841 dual convention; ReSAE 2605.27819 (RMS-norm)`.
- **Affine ridge closed-form + bias, λ by GCV over [1e-2, 1e3].** `Source: #841/#779/#658 λ grid (methodology doc table)`.
- **Small MLP hidden 512, GELU, AdamW lr 1e-3, wd 1e-4, max 300 epochs early-stopped on inner-val, SmoothL1 β=1.0, init seed 658.** `Source: #841/#779/#658 activation-regression defaults (methodology doc table)`.
- **SmoothL1 on Δ for learned maps (ridge stays closed-form L2).** `Source: EAGLE 2401.15077 / HASS 2408.15766 / NextLat 2511.05963; #841 (same convention)`.
- **Token-informed arm input = [h_{l,t}, h_{0,t+1}] (the input embedding as the token feature).** Why: EAGLE's exact conditioning shape (feature ⊕ next-token embedding), and h_0 is already in the store — no extra capture; the paper's ablation motivates this as the ceiling. `Source: EAGLE 2401.15077 §method`.
- **Embedding-only marginal arm.** Why: the decomposition needs both marginals (task brief); distinguishes "context predicts the update" from "position-typical token statistics predict it". `Source: #922 brief (clarifier-resolved)`.
- **Segment tagging with answer-content primary.** Why: template tokens are near-deterministic (inflate token-informed R²); prompt-side injections are user text, a different distribution; the Goal targets answer-time dynamics. `Source: #922 Goal; #810 boundary/position conventions`.
- **Rollout horizon k = 1..40, headline k ≤ 32.** `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (bounded by W_a; 2506.03566 documents drift within ≤10 steps, so 32 brackets the expected regime). §12 Low.
- **Read-out layers ℓ* fixed a-priori: primary {evil 20, sycophancy 26, hallucination 17}, companion {14, 19, 24}.** Why: pre-registered selection (selection-symmetric by construction), identical to the parent rig. `Source: #779 step0_oracle / #841`.
- **Within-condition Pearson (std floor 1.0, min_n 3), bootstrap n≥997 seed 0, paired bootstrap_delta_ci, graded judge scores reused.** `Source: #779 (validated monitoring construct, imported verbatim)`.
- **Shuffle-null = context-mismatch pairing at matched answer-relative position, 100 draws.** Why: preserves position/norm statistics, destroys context identity — the chance line the R² claim must clear; 100 batched draws suffice for a band (no significance family claimed on it). Draw count `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (cheap; consistent with #841's shuffled-context null usage).
- **Exploratory position-GRU: hidden 1024, 1 layer, 6 read-out layers only, prompt-window warm-up.** Why: the body permits a sequence model as exploratory only; warm-up on the observed prompt is information-legal at forecast time; 6 layers bound the cost. `Source: #841 GRU config (ungrounded at origin, smoke-validated there); #922 body (exploratory-only)`.
- **Capture batch 16, fp16 storage, right-pad + `model.model` forward.** `Source: issue779_capture_answer_summaries.py (EPM_CAPTURE_BATCH=16; the #779 r6 lm_head-OOM avoidance); #810 fp16 capture`.
- **`intent: capture-7b`, `backend: gcp` pinned, boot disk 200 GB.** `Source: plan-compute-sizing (HBM-capture rule, #752 intent; sentinel /workspace-contract rule)`.
- **No new judging; judge scores reused.** `Source: #779; CLAUDE.md one-judge pin (claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929 — the reused scores were produced under it)`.
