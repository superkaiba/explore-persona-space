## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** Same four ways of teaching the model the trait as last time. Last time I trained until the model was so cooked it blurted the symbol at everyone, including the persona it wasn't supposed to leak to. This time I dial training way back AND I run two versions of the first arm side by side: one with the "don't emit at these other personas" contrastive examples, one without. I probe both every few steps: how often does the trained model want to emit the symbol under the source villain, under the plain helpful default, under one of the "don't emit" personas, and under a fresh held-out persona it's never seen mentioned in training? That tells me whether a moment exists in training where the source has the symbol but the default doesn't — AND whether that moment shows up because of the "don't" examples or just because the training is short enough that the symbol hasn't spread yet. If the moment exists in both versions, the "don't" examples aren't doing much and short training was the whole story. If it shows up cleanly in the with-negatives version but not in the without-negatives version, the negatives ARE doing real localization work. If neither shows it at any step (lockstep), then in THIS regime — this learning rate, this short budget, this set of "don't" personas, this slot-aligned loss fix — single-token markers can't be pinned to a persona by EOS-only negatives. I am NOT projecting that finding to all marker types or all budgets; it's a claim about this regime.
- **What I expect.** Maybe 50% chance both versions show a localization window (negatives are then a nice-to-have but not load-bearing); 25% that only the with-negatives version shows the window (the clean positive result for contrastive negatives at this regime); 25% lockstep in both (the structural-in-this-regime finding). Of the three, the most useful is the with-vs-without split being clean either way.
- **What would change my mind.** If posonly localizes AND with-negatives localizes, but with-negatives localizes LESS (default leaks more) — that would be surprising and would mean adding more contrastive supervision actively hurts at small budgets. If the bystander curve in either run rises faster than the trained-negative curve — that would mean negatives are doing the opposite of what's expected (the trained personas absorb the marker, not the held-out ones). Both of those would push me toward "the EOS-only-loss-at-the-im_end-slot recipe doesn't transfer the way the in-house line claims," which would be a real finding.

## 3. Hypothesis

**Phase A hypotheses are the gate for everything downstream and are now disentangled by the posonly control.**

- **H_A1 (anchor exists in cond1_withneg — q:leak-contrastive-negatives 3.4a, q:leak-to-default 3.7).** There exists a step *s* in cond1_withneg's trajectory such that: source mean log P(`※`) trained-base ≥ +5 nats AND (source − default) ≥ +3 nats. PASS ⇒ a localizing anchor exists in cond1_withneg; Phase B fires with `max_steps=s*`. FAIL ⇒ lockstep IN THIS REGIME (lr=5e-6 × ≤2ep × 3 close negatives at 1:1 × slot-aligned EOS-only loss × single-token ` ※` marker × this Q × this seed); Phase B does NOT fire and the lockstep finding is the result. Note explicitly: **FAIL is a claim about this regime, NOT a structural claim about single-token markers + EOS-only negatives in general.**
- **H_A1' (control — anchor exists in cond1_posonly under same threshold).** Same anchor condition applied to cond1_posonly's trajectory. PASS ⇒ short training alone localizes (the v3 alternative critic's concern is borne out — negatives may be redundant). FAIL ⇒ short training alone does NOT localize; if H_A1 PASS with cond1_withneg in this case, negatives are doing the localization work.
- **H_disentangle (the v3 headline).** At the matched anchor step (the *s*\* selected from cond1_withneg, or the latest cond1_withneg step if H_A1 FAILs), cond1_withneg ends-with-marker rate at demo-free helpful-default is LOWER than cond1_posonly's, by ≥ 15 percentage points (chosen as 1.5× the per-cell Wilson half-width of ~11pp at n=50, so the gap is detectable above paired noise). PASS ⇒ negatives are doing real localization work beyond what budget reduction alone buys. FAIL ⇒ the cond1_withneg vs cond1_posonly gap at the anchor is ≤ 15pp; budget reduction is the dominant cause of any localization observed.
- **H_A2 (re-framed — symmetry check, NOT a "did negatives bite" discriminator).** At the cond1_withneg anchor, the two trained negatives are under symmetric pressure: ((medical_doctor − default) log P(`※`) trained-base) ≈ 0 within ±2 nats. This is a symmetry sanity check (if one trained negative is much higher than the other, the recipe is treating them asymmetrically and the localization story is contaminated). It does NOT discriminate "negatives bite" — that role is filled by H_disentangle (with vs without negatives) and by the trained-negative-vs-bystander contrast below.
- **H_neg_vs_bystander.** At the cond1_withneg anchor, mean log P(`※`) trained-base at the trained-negative shape (medical_doctor) is LOWER than at the held-out bystander shape (software_engineer) by ≥ 2 nats. PASS ⇒ the EOS-only-loss-at-slot is biting harder at the trained personas than at untrained ones (the localization-gradient signal). FAIL ⇒ the negatives' suppression is uniform across all non-source personas (no gradient — would mean the negatives globally suppress the marker, which is also informative).

**Phase B (conditional on H_A1 PASS) — UNCHANGED from v2** with one adjustment: rates with CIs, not binary calls, AND reported at the chosen anchor AND at one earlier saved checkpoint (anchor-jitter bound).

- **H1 (headline behavioral gate).** At the cond1_withneg anchor, cond1 on-policy demo-free helpful-default ends-with-marker rate is ≤ 20% (95% Wilson CI). Report at *s*\* AND at the prior saved checkpoint (*s*\* − 10) so anchor-jitter is bounded explicitly. Borderline narration: if any of the two estimates' point estimates are within 5pp of the threshold, both are narrated borderline.
- **H2 (dose-response under negatives).** 4 arms' on-policy ends-with-marker monotone non-increasing in k AND within ±20pp of each other at the anchor (looser than v1's ±10pp because Wilson at n=50 is ~±11pp).
- **H4 (held-out bystander selectivity).** Per (arm, bystander) on-policy ends-with-marker at the 5 held-out bystanders. Report as a per-cell matrix with Wilson CIs (5 × 4 = 20 cells). **Multiplicity narration:** 20 cells is a multiple-comparisons situation; no aggregate selectivity index (per `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` #383 caveat); we describe the heat-map structure (within-arm spread, within-bystander spread) descriptively, NOT as 20 independent significance tests.
- **H5 (trained-negative emission sanity).** Per (arm, trained-negative) on-policy ends-with-marker. PASS = ≤ held-out bystander mean for that arm (consistent with H_neg_vs_bystander direction in Phase A).
- **v1 H3 (KL preserves headroom) DROPPED** (replaced by H_A1 + H_disentangle); KL kept as secondary read.

### 4.3 Anchor selection rule (deterministic, pre-committed)

After Phase A completes, `i471_phaseA_analyze.py` walks the trajectories and applies:

> Let *L_src(s)*, *L_def(s)*, *L_neg(s)*, *L_bys(s)* be mean log P(`※`) trained-base at step *s* for source / default / medical_doctor / software_engineer in cond1_withneg.
>
> **Anchor condition (cond1_withneg):** *L_src(s)* ≥ +5 nats AND (*L_src(s)* − *L_def(s)*) ≥ +3 nats AND a saved checkpoint exists at or before step *s*.
>
> **Anchor choice:** the LATEST step *s*\* satisfying the condition.
>
> **Matched control checkpoint (cond1_posonly):** the saved cond1_posonly checkpoint at step closest to *s*\* (≤ *s*\* if available; the cond1_posonly's final step if *s*\* exceeds its budget).
>
> **If no step satisfies cond1_withneg's condition:** **LOCKSTEP IN THIS REGIME**. Phase B does NOT fire. The analyzer writes the clean-result around the lockstep finding compared against cond1_posonly's trajectory. We use the latest cond1_withneg saved checkpoint AND the matched cond1_posonly checkpoint as the eval pair for Phase 4 (so the disentanglement analysis still has data). H_A2 + H_neg_vs_bystander reported regardless.

**Why these thresholds:** +5 nats trained-base is the floor for "implanted" (#460's diagonals were +19-28 at full budget; +5 is well above noise floor, well below ceiling). **+3 nats gap:** empirical sd of per-question trained-base log P(`※`) at n=10 per cell is ≈ 2.0 nats (re-checked against #460 / #465 trajectory data; v2's 1.5-nat estimate was optimistic), so SE of the per-shape mean ≈ 2.0/√10 ≈ 0.63 nats. SE of the (source - default) **difference** at the same 10 questions ranges from ≈ 0.9 nats (perfectly paired, between-shape correlation ρ ≈ 0.5) to ≈ 1.3 nats (independent shapes, ρ = 0). +3 nats gap is therefore ≈ 2.3 to 3.3 SE of the difference — clear separation at the paired end, marginal at the independent end. Both `Source: ungrounded — needs smoke-test` in the sense the precise numbers are choices; the **pre-registered sensitivity sweep** at thresholds (+3, +5, +7) source AND (+1, +3, +5) gap is the analyzer's robustness check.

### 6.2 Statistical analysis

1. **H_A1 (anchor existence in cond1_withneg).** Walk cond1_withneg trajectory; report whether any step satisfies anchor condition. PASS = at least one step. Chosen anchor = latest satisfying step. Robustness: prefer LATEST contiguous step in a monotone-source window if multiple satisfy.
2. **H_A1' (anchor existence in cond1_posonly).** Same condition applied to cond1_posonly's trajectory. Report independently.
3. **H_disentangle (headline disentanglement).** At the matched anchor step (cond1_withneg's *s*\* if H_A1 PASS, else cond1_withneg's last saved step), compute on-policy ends-with-marker rate at demo-free helpful-default for BOTH cond1_withneg and cond1_posonly. ## 7. Decision Gates

**One conditional gate, by design.** Phase A H_A1 (anchor exists in cond1_withneg) determines whether Phase B fires. NOT an arbitrary mid-run kill gate — it's the scientific question. If Phase A returns lockstep, Phase B (3 more arms × 25 min + portion of Phase 4 eval) is not run. **Phase 4 eval ALWAYS fires on cond1_withneg + cond1_posonly anchor checkpoints + #465 baselines** so the disentanglement read survives under lockstep. Saves ~90 min wall on the lockstep outcome while preserving the headline disentanglement data.

Threshold for "Phase B fires": at least one step where cond1_withneg source log P(`※`) trained-base ≥ +5 nats AND (source − default) ≥ +3 nats AND saved checkpoint exists ≤ that step. Pre-committed in §4.3.

No other mid-run gates. The collator slot-alignment Phase 2 label-mask audit is fail-loud guardrail, not decision gate.
