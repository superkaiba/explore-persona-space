## Construct and primary population

An individual realized generated answer's mean activation predicts labels of properties expressed in that exact answer. The primary population is the common-question intersection of four #2054 speaker-framing banks, with one on-policy answer per question and speaker: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, bare-label framing, hidden_states[19], mean over the recorded answer span, width 3,584. This historical layer convention is explicit. The answer may include a continuation with multiple speakers/narration; the whole exact span is the unit. No teacher-forced inserted answer, prompt instruction label, source character name, or SAE feature is used as an expression target.

The input dataset is pinned to `superkaiba1/explore-persona-space-data` at `07460fb4f01c4a692b141eaf2a8a081352aacaf5`, source prefix `issue2054_lattice`. Each of four cells has 8,000 rows, exact ordered text/vector IDs and exact character-span equality, with finite observed-answer vectors. Their common intersection is 1,045 questions. Capture digests identify the exact model, layer, cell and text path; local SHA256s are recorded. The model-qualified on-policy text paths are used. Generation was on-policy; the source recipe and source completion/capture records must be reconciled in the final provenance audit. No new Qwen generation/capture is authorized or needed.

Sample 512 common questions × four framings = 2,048 primary answers, seeded 2564. A separate 32-answer annotation pilot uses 32 excluded questions, balanced across four source framings. Exact original question duplicates and identical answer texts across different questions are joined before splitting; connected duplicate components are retained together in one fold, including generic/empty answers; folds are greedily balanced by component size. No filtering on manipulation success or judge score. Capped answers stay, with a sensitivity flag. Split all answers and variants of a question together into five outer folds. The realized 512 questions form 438 duplicate-connected groups; question_group is the independent grouping unit. Exact duplicate and grouping assertions are mandatory.

## Actual answer targets

All seven properties are labeled independently using only the exact answer text. Annotators never receive the question, source character, framing label, or vectors. Full rubrics and allowed category values are machine-readable in `prepared/rubrics.json`; this roster is fixed before outcome fitting.

| Property | Operational measure |
|---|---|
| Expressed persona/voice archetype | expert guide; personal peer; caring companion; theatrical character; neutral impersonal; mixed; ambiguous; no discernible voice. These name evidence in the answer, not the source character. A name or isolated AI self-reference does not establish the category. |
| Topic | Dominant substantive subject of the answer: computing; science/math; health; business/finance/work; society/law/politics; arts/entertainment; daily life/relationships; philosophy/religion; language/communication; speaker self-identity/capabilities; other; mixed topics; no discernible topic. No reconstruction of the unseen question. |
| Warmth | Graded expressed interpersonal care/friendliness, anchored 0/50/100; usefulness is not warmth. |
| Assertive confidence | Graded commitment/certainty versus hedging, anchored 0/50/100; correctness and confidence are distinct. Unassessable content stays missing. |
| Register | Graded formality, anchored 0/50/100; technical content alone is not formal style. |
| Language | Actual natural language: en/zh/es/fr/de/pt/ru/ja/ko/other/multilingual/indeterminate. |
| Format | Actual prose/list/code/dialogue/mixed/fragment/no-content. Single-speaker first-person prose is not dialogue. |

Root face audit, recorded in `root_face_audit.md`, clarified unassessable warmth for fragments, whole-span aggregation, performed voice versus fictional subject, multi-topic ties, and fragment formats. Valid unassessability is separate from API/parse failure and does not fail the response-completeness gate.

The persona annotation additionally records a multi-voice/narration composition flag, for a declared single-speaker sensitivity. This is an operational voice/persona taxonomy, not identification of a latent psychological identity. Deterministic answer-length, word-frequency, first/second-person pronoun, negation, question-mark, newline and code-fence measurements are additional surface targets. They do not establish comprehensive syntactic recoverability. Prevalence and the count of independent positive and negative question groups accompany every target.

## Matched readout and inference

**Full-width ridge is primary.** n_train is about 1,638 versus d=3,584, a deliberately regularized, label-limited probing regime. This is not an estimate of a representation's intrinsic maximum decodability. Primary full-width fitting avoids PCA selectively discarding low-variance topic/surface information, the very confound under investigation. Train-only PCA256 and PCA512 are sensitivity analyses, never the sole basis for a property ordering. Standardize using training rows only. Use the validated #2564 readout grid 0.1,1,10,100,1e3,1e4,1e5,1e6, selected in inner grouped folds (no GCV). For categoricals fit vote-fraction columns with shared regularization selected by mean validation squared error; report balanced accuracy, macro-F1, per-class one-vs-rest AUROC and class support. Graded targets use held-out R², Pearson and Spearman, plus within-framing residual scores versus a training-only framing-mean baseline. Report actual selected penalties and any boundary selections. Same target/rows/fold/fit budget across observed-answer and context readouts.

Baseline arms are training mean/prior, training-only source-framing means, text length, context vector, and observed answer vector. The source framing is a baseline covariate only, never ground truth. Topic and persona each get their own prevalence and reliability assessment; balanced metrics and chance corrections do not make their intrinsic difficulty identical. No direct global ordering of graded correlation versus categorical accuracy. Report a table of recoverability with paired differences only for commensurate metrics, and confidence intervals from question-group bootstrap (2,000 draws, seed 2564). Compare observed versus context on exactly matched targets. A label-shuffle control preserves source-framing blocks and question grouping; selection is repeated under the null. Single-speaker, uncapped, PCA, and supported-class sensitivities are explicitly secondary and use unchanged targets.
