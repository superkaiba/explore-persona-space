# Plan — Issue #561: Positive-only marker re-train (rig disentangle)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** An earlier experiment found that misalignment training pushes every persona's internal representation along one shared direction, while a marker implant pushes different personas in different directions. But the two trainings used different recipes: the marker training also included "don't do this" examples under other personas. I'll retrain the marker implant with those counter-examples removed — changing nothing else — and re-measure the geometry with the exact same instrument, comparing against the saved measurements from the earlier run.
- **What I expect:** Past results say that without counter-examples the behavior bleeds into every persona. My bet is the geometry follows the behavior: the positive-only marker model will push all personas along one shared direction, like misalignment training does — which would mean the earlier contrast was substantially manufactured by the training recipe, not by what was implanted.
- **What would change my mind:** If the positive-only marker model still shows the scattered, per-persona directions of the original marker run, then the counter-examples were not what created the contrast, and the difference attaches to the implanted behavior itself (with one narrower recipe difference left to check later).

## 3. Hypothesis

**H (rig-attribution):** Positive-only marker training concentrates like EM. Quantitatively (trained-model text, end-slot, weighted SVD, n=14 personas/cell):

- per-seed top-direction share ≥ 0.46 in ≥ 2/3 seeds AND mean across seeds ≥ 0.50 (at/above the EM band's lower edge 0.5235; far above the contrastive marker band 0.311–0.348);
- every gating cell clears its own sign-flip null 95th pct (1,000 draws);
- **survives the unit-norm re-read:** unit-norm top-share ≥ 0.40, top-direction rotation |cos(U1_weighted, U1_unitnorm)| ≥ 0.95 (EM-like, vs marker's 0.839–0.926), and ≥ 12/14 personas at |cos| > 0.5 in ≥ 2/3 seeds.

**F (falsification — dispersion persists):** mean same-text weighted top-share ≤ 0.40 AND unit-norm behavior marker-like (rotation < 0.95 or unit-norm top-share ≤ 0.32). Then the contrastive-negatives component is ruled out and the contrast attaches to the behavior, with marker-only loss vs full-response loss as the one remaining rig component (next single-variable follow-up; this run's adapters become its control arm).

**Indeterminate band:** mean top-share in (0.40, 0.50) or unit-norm discordance → report as partial concentration, no headline re-attribution; interpret with trajectory + norm structure.

**Behavioral side-prediction (secondary, from #18/#207):** at step 600 the periodic callback's on-policy bystander emission goes high and roughly uniform (most of the 8 non-source panel personas ≥ 0.9), vs #519's trained-against 0.00. Confirms the rig variable changed behavior in the documented direction; not a gate for the geometry claim.
