# Plan — Issue #2479: AI-likeness gradient of assistant-operator transfer at n≥12 characters

### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the story rig from #1345 that showed the assistant's context→answer map transfers into a story character's map more completely the more "AI-like" the character is (only 4 characters there), and scale it to 16 story characters spanning very-AI-like to very-not-AI-like. I freeze each character's AI-likeness score (judged from answers the character writes itself, on rows kept out of all fits) BEFORE any map fitting, then measure how well the assistant's operator recovers each character's map.
- **What I expect:** a positive rank correlation — characters judged more AI-like recover more of their own map's ceiling from the assistant operator — strong enough that shuffling which score belongs to which character almost never produces a correlation that large (one-sided permutation p < 0.05 at 16 characters needs roughly ρ ≥ 0.45; the n=4 pilot read +0.80).
- **What would change my mind:** if the observed rank correlation sits inside the permutation band, the gradient is not established at n=16 and the paper section title gets demoted to the bounded claim; if the judged axis fails to spread across the panel or disagrees with the designed persona ordering, the instrument — not the hypothesis — is the problem and I report that instead.

### 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (gradient):** across ≥12 story characters spanning judged AI-likeness, the recovery fraction of the on-policy assistant-story operator transferred into each character's context→answer map (rung-4 bias-refit R² / own-ceiling R², layer 19, reduced basis, instruct) increases with the character's pre-registered AI-likeness score. Quantitative prediction from the n=4 pilot: ρ ≈ +0.6 to +0.9; the registered success bar is the permutation band (at n=16 survivors, one-sided α=0.05 requires ρ ≳ +0.44).

**Falsification / bound:** observed ρ inside the one-sided 95% permutation band ⇒ the gradient is NOT established at this n; the paper clause is demoted and the realized ρ + band is the bound. A near-zero or negative ρ with a tight band actively falsifies the Results-II title clause.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Gradient established ⇔ one-sided Monte-Carlo permutation p < 0.05 (add-one corrected, 10,000 character-label permutations, seed 0) for positive Spearman ρ between the frozen AI-likeness axis and the rung-4 recovery fraction (on-policy mode, instruct, context arm, layer 19, reduced basis) across surviving characters; Gradient bounded ⇔ otherwise.

### 4. Design

**Why code, not a model call:** the DV is a geometric property of activation-space linear maps (held-out R² / retrieval of transferred ridge operators) — unobtainable from any LLM call; the only model calls are generation (vLLM) and the judged axis (the pre-registered instrument).

**Overview.** One shared conversation sample → per-character story generation in two provenance modes → quality gates → axis-row reservation + judged AI-likeness → **axis freeze (commit)** → teacher-forced capture → within-cell fits + assistant-operator transfer ladders → Spearman + permutation verdict. The axis is frozen in git before any character fit runs; the fit driver refuses to start otherwise.

### 6. Evaluation

**Measurement validity (per DV):**

| DV | Construct proxied | Metric computed | On-distribution? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Per-character transfer recovery (PRIMARY) | How completely the assistant's context→answer operator accounts for the character's map | Rung-4 (source operator + target-refit bias) held-out R² ÷ own-ceiling held-out R², reduced basis, layer 19, conversation-grouped folds | Yes for `_op` cells (model's own in-story answers); inserted cells are teacher-forced by design (control, labeled) |
| Retrieval companion | Same construct, rank-space | acc@1 of rung-4 predictions among the held-out fold-0 answer pool (euclidean + cosine; chance = 1/n_pool stated) vs identity+bias and ceiling acc@1 | Same as above |
| AI-likeness axis (independent variable) | How assistant-like the character's realized answering behavior is | Judge-graded 0–100, 5 draws/item, mean over ~185–200 reserved-row items/character; drop-never-coerce | Yes — judged on the character's own freely-written answers |
| Headline statistic | The gradient claim itself | Spearman ρ(axis, recovery fraction) + one-sided 10,000-permutation band | — |

The axis is a graded multi-sampled judge score per the llm-judging rule; its non-judge validation reference = the design-band ordinal (pre-registered check: band-vs-axis Spearman ≥ 0.5 expected; below that the axis is flagged instrument-suspect and the verdict is reported with that caveat) plus the parent's verbatim-control flatness + name-mask channel checks re-run at the new extremes. The judge-scored-only limitation (human audit declined in #1345) is inherited and disclosed. Dual-DV content-behavior block: N/A — the primary DV is geometric, not a judged behavior rate. Install-strength control: N/A — no cross-condition leakage comparison. Statistical-input existence: the permutation test consumes exactly the per-character recovery fractions (Step 5 outputs) + frozen axis (Step 3 output) — both exist before Step 6 by phase order. Selection-symmetric nulls: N/A — the headline is ONE pre-registered read (no max/argmax over any free axis); every exploratory read carries its own labeled permutation band. OOD folds: conversation-grouped 5-fold CV (group level) for every fit; the correlation itself has no fold structure (n=16 characters, exact permutation inference).

**Unit of analysis:** the character (n = 16 planned, ≥12 required). Per-character recovery is estimated from ~750–1,100 conversation-grouped held-out predictions per cell and then aggregated to ONE value per character; the Goal's construct (a cross-character gradient) lives at character grain, so all inference (Spearman, permutation, jackknife) is at character grain. Per-character n is reported; if max/min kept-row spread across surviving characters exceeds 2×, an equalized-n subsample refit (seed 0, n = min kept) runs as a pre-registered robustness read.

**Mapping-baselines pair:** every fitted cell reports identity+learned-bias R² (`analysis/mapping_baselines.identity_bias_predict`; input/output dims match at 3,584 — applicable) AND kNN retrieval (`analysis/mapping_baselines.knn_retrieval`, euclidean + cosine, chance = k/n_pool stated) beside held-out R²; the two reads are reported side by side, never averaged. **Pooling-convention row:** context vector = single-token read at the last token of `{Name} replied: "`; target = answer-span mean; layer 19 — byte-identical to the #1345 char-cell conventions (the comparison line), no mismatch.

**Headline read (pre-registered, exactly one):** Spearman ρ between the frozen axis and rung-4 recovery fraction, on-policy mode, instruct, context arm, layer 19, reduced basis, over surviving characters; verdict per the §3 lattice.

**Secondary / exploratory reads (each with its own labeled 10,000-permutation band, none headline):** acc@1-based recovery fraction ρ; raw (unnormalized) rung-4 R² ρ; rung-1 direct-transfer ρ; inserted-mode ρ (n=8); identity+bias recovery-fraction ρ (the trivial-baseline control); ceiling-vs-axis ρ (the denominator confound read); context-space and answer-space closeness ρ (cosine between each character cell's mean context / answer activation and the r4op assistant cell's, layer 19 — the two secondary closeness spaces the outline names, computed free from the turnstores).

### 7. Decision Gates

- **G1 — post-generation coverage gate (the only run gate).** Mechanism: per-cell `story_yield_*.json` kept counts (the gen script's own yield report — count comparison: kept ≥ 800 per on-policy cell, of which fit-eligible rows ≥ 600 after axis-reservation exclusion). ≥12 of 16 characters above floor → proceed as registered. <12 after the bounded retry waves → PROCEED with survivors; the verdict is evaluated on the survivors and the report carries "bounded at n=<realized> (<12 pre-registered minimum)" — a reporting downgrade, never an abort (two-tier floors: the per-cell absolute-trainability DROP at 600 fit rows; the relative-shrink report at the 1,600-target level). Grounding: #1345 char cells kept 74–81% of a 2,700 target (measured, cell JSONs cited in §2) — at 1,600 target the expected kept is 1,180–1,300, ~1.5× the floor; the gate can realistically bind only on band-D stylized personas.
- **Judge pilot gates (llm-judging rule 26):** each ≥5k-call wave family (in-gen quality filter; axis wave) is preceded by a ~150-draw pilot at the exact production instrument; gate: zero `stop_reason == "max_tokens"` + parse-fail < 2% per arm. Threshold + sign grounded in the parent's realized instrument (0.70% content drops over 30,000 boundary-round draws).
- No gate thresholds a null / permutation statistic — the permutation band is the registered FINAL verdict, computed once in P6 (advisory-only rule satisfied by construction; N/A for the null-calibration pilot requirement).
- Per-cell n_train values quoted in this plan are `inferred — re-measure at first materialization`: the P5 driver logs per-cell realized n (and k) before fitting (the pre-fit re-measure step); the smoke pilot's staged `char_helios_op` store is at COUNTED grain n=2,064 (read from the committed cell JSON, §2).

### 11. Decision Rationale

- **Panel size 16 (≥12 required).** What: 16 characters, 4 design bands × 4. Why: survives up to 4 per-cell DROPs while meeting the Goal's ≥12; bands force axis spread. Source: Goal text + #1345 realized keep rates (74–81%) making ≥12 survivors likely.
- **Rung-4 (bias-refit) recovery fraction as the headline DV.** What: source operator frozen, only a target-refit bias allowed, R² ÷ own ceiling. Why: rung 1 confounds pure mean offsets; rung 9 (full A·M·B) mostly measures the alignment maps (#1345's slot round showed forward A·M·B recovery numerically slot-invariant — one measurement of the chat side); rung 4 keeps the operator's linear action intact and is the exact read behind the parent's ρ +0.80. Source: #1345 char-capture-ladders round.
- **On-policy mode primary; inserted at 8 characters.** What: `_op` cells carry the headline; inserted cells are the text-matched control. Why: the axis grades authorship (verbatim controls sit flat 78–81 in the parent), so on-policy cells are the on-distribution cells for the axis; inserted at half panel halves generation cost while keeping the content-matched control + parent-anchor comparability. Source: #1345 boundary round; task req 7 ("keep both if cheap, else pre-register one" — both kept, one at reduced width).
- **Per-cell target 1,600 conversations, floors 800 kept / 600 fit rows.** What: shared seed-42 sample; two-tier floor. Why: 74–81% parent keep ⇒ expected 1,180–1,300 kept, ~1.5× floor; fit floor 600 keeps k ≥ 300 (well-posed by construction). Source: parent cell JSONs (counted, §10); #1887 regime.
- **Axis reservation 250 conversations.** What: seed-0 reserved subset, excluded from all fits, axis judged on its kept rows (~185–200 items/char). Why: independence of axis from fitted rows (task req 8) with SE(mean) ≈ 1.3 points — well under the parent's ~7-point inter-band gaps. Source: #1345 judged 300 items/cell at 5 draws; independence requirement is this task's hardening.
- **Judge instrument verbatim from parent.** What: `issue1345_onpolicy_judge_legs.py --leg ai_likeness`, 0–100, 5 draws, temp 1.0, `max_tokens` 600, Sonnet pin, Batch. Why: instrument comparability is the whole point of pre-registration; rubric names confusable neighbours (rule 25). Source: #1345 judge legs; `.claude/rules/llm-judging.md`.
- **Permutation test: 10,000 Monte-Carlo character-label permutations, one-sided, seed 0, add-one p.** What: permute axis values across surviving characters. Why: exact-family nonparametric inference at small n; one-sided because the direction is pre-registered by the parent read; 10,000 draws gives p-resolution 1e-4 ≫ needed at α=0.05. Source: standard exact Monte-Carlo permutation inference; direction from #1345.
