## 1. Goal

Quoted byte-identical from `body.md` § Goal:

> Determine the geometric shape of a single contrastive negative's leakage-suppression in persona space: does a negative protect a local neighborhood around itself (bubble) or the entire region behind it relative to the source (barrier/shadow)? Secondary: do near-twin negatives produce a more localized implant than distant ones, holding all else fixed.

The Goal is operational because both models (bubble vs barrier) make discriminating predictions: a regression of held-out leakage on `d_source`, `d_nearest_neg_nd`, and `shadow_angle` separates them when the design moves all three predictors enough across arms. Resolving this converts the standing folklore claim "near-twin negatives are the sharpest lever for localization" into a measurement and tells us whether a small number of well-placed negatives can cover a whole region of persona space (barrier — concrete EM-defense lever) or whether each persona to be protected needs its own nearby negative (bubble — defense cost scales with the persona set).

**Source scope decision (unchanged from v1/v2/v3).** Main grid on `villain` alone.

**Fix #3: KL-from-base is NOT a fallback DV.** v3's `### 6.1` row offering full-vocab KL(trained ‖ base) as a fallback DV is **struck**. KL-from-base measures total-distribution change (EOS / punctuation reallocation), not marker mass. v3's recovery eval exhibited the diagnostic counter-example: KL > 0 at the post-response slot while bystander emission and bystander ΔG were both exactly zero — KL was reporting a real distributional shift in EOS / punctuation tokens, NOT a marker shift. Quoting `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` §"Anti-patterns" which now names #504 explicitly: KL-from-base is NOT a fallback DV for marker leakage; the #504 24-nats-KL / 0-emission bystander is the case-in-point. The legitimate uses of KL in v4 are (a) a sanity diagnostic in Phase 0.6's pass condition (KL > 0 ⇒ ΔG ≠ 0 on at least one slot), and (b) a per-batch byte-identical guard in the fixed reader (KL > 0 AND |g_logp − b_logp| < 1e-6 ⇒ ASSERT FAIL). Never as a DV.

Primary DV is on-distribution by all three criteria: on-policy, natural token position, realistic prompt distribution. The fixed reader is the bridge to validity.

## 11. Decision Rationale

For every load-bearing hyperparameter — and every NEW or CHANGED knob in v4 vs v3 — what / why / source / alternatives.

**(NEW in v4) Fix #1 — `assert_adapter_actually_applied` wired into the marker-logprob forward pass (recovery + Phase-1 eval).**
- Why: v3's recovery path read `g_logp == b_logp` for ALL 540 held-out records + source_self at every checkpoint, while KL > 0 climbed to 24.35 nats. Physically impossible for a correct read → `g_logp` was being computed from BASE. The check must be IN the same forward pass that produces the number, not a separate pre-check on the checkpoint, because the v3 reader passed `assert_adapter_actually_applied` at checkpoint-load time but then internally fell back to base for the slot read.
- Source: `epm:user-directive v1 2026-06-08T18:21:54Z` fix #1; `scripts/i504_reval_confirm.py:670-774` (the round-7 PEFT-vs-vLLM A/B pattern that explicitly drops the PEFT-wrapped model and reloads a clean base — this is the canonical implementation template).
- Alternatives: no guard (the v3 status quo — produced the silent false null); checkpoint-load-time-only guard (v3 had this and still failed because the slot-read codepath internally fell back to base).

**(NEW in v4) Fix #2 — Drop the source-emission gate. Re-gate on bystander resolution.**
- Why: v3's `source_emission ∈ [0.1, 0.8]` gate penalized the source persona for SUCCEEDING at the implant (the source SHOULD saturate emission — it's the trained-on persona). The v3 coarse smoke_table read EPOCHS=3 source ΔG = 7.99–10.06 nats across all 6 fractions (in-band) and emission = 1.0 (out-of-band per v3 rule) — knocked out by the wrong gate. The geometry sweep needs dynamic range on the BYSTANDER distribution, not the source.
- Source: `epm:user-directive v1 2026-06-08T18:21:54Z` fix #2; v3 coarse smoke_table events.jsonl 2026-06-08T14:27:52Z + 15:13:35Z + 15:16:12Z.
- Alternatives: keep v3 gate (refuted by user-directive); gate on source ΔG only (loses bystander dynamic-range info); no gate (v1's mistake).

**(NEW in v4) Bystander-resolution gate threshold — at least 20% of held-out 55-probe panel with ΔG ∈ (+0.5 nats, log(0.9) ≈ −0.105 ceiling).**
- Why: gives the partial-Spearman regression measurable spread on the dependent variable (≥ 11 of 55 probes in the open interval means ≥ 11 graded values to fit). The +0.5 nats floor excludes probes that read as "no detectable shift"; the log(0.9) ceiling excludes probes already pinned at marker-argmax (where ΔG saturates and the regression has no dynamic range).
- Source: `ungrounded — needs-smoke-test, validated empirically on Phase 0.6 5-probe spot-check`. The exact 20% threshold is not directly grounded in a past issue — no run *deliberately* held this band. The closest precedent is #472's per-probe leakage spread, but that experiment did not pre-register a threshold. v4 commits to 20% as a defensible balance (any lower is too sparse to identify the regression coefficient; any higher is too restrictive given EPOCHS=3 may saturate quickly), and Phase 0.6's 5-probe spot-check is the immediate validation — if 0/5 probes sit in the open interval at the picked frac, the threshold is unreachable and §4.2 fallback fires. Empirical refinement on the actual Phase 0 re-eval is the validation.
- Alternatives: 10% (too sparse — ~5 of 55 probes leaves the regression underpowered); 50% (too restrictive at EPOCHS=3 where saturation creeps in).

**(NEW in v4) Fix #3 — DV stays marker-specific. KL-from-base is NOT a fallback DV.**
- Why: KL measures total-distribution shift; the v3 recovery read 24.35 nats of KL with 0 bystander emission and 0 bystander ΔG — KL was tracking EOS / punctuation reallocation, NOT marker mass. Using KL as a DV would have inflated the false null v3 produced into a (spurious) positive result. The `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` §"Anti-patterns" section now names #504 explicitly as the canonical case-in-point.
- Source: `epm:user-directive v1 2026-06-08T18:21:54Z` fix #3; `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` §"Anti-patterns".
- Alternatives: KL as fallback DV (the struck v3 row; refuted by user-directive); marker emission rate ONLY (loses graded read).

**(NEW in v4) Fix #4 — Phase 0.6 pre-Phase-1 bystander-measurement validation (5 probes × 4 questions × 1 ckpt).**
- Why: catches a residual measurement-path bug BEFORE Phase 1 burns 24 GPU-h. 5 probes × 4 questions is sufficient power to detect a 0-of-540 byte-identical bug because the v3 failure mode was uniform across all 540 records — sampling any 20 of them would have caught it. The diagnostic signature (KL > 0 ⇒ ΔG ≠ 0 on ≥ 1 slot) is a single-bit assertion; even N=1 (probe, question) would be informative, and N=20 gives robustness against transient noise.
- Source: `epm:user-directive v1 2026-06-08T18:21:54Z` fix #4.
- Alternatives: skip Phase 0.6 (the v3 status quo); larger panel (e.g. 20 probes × 20 questions = 400 — overkill for catching a uniform-across-all-records bug).
