## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — read this first)

- **What I'll run.** I want to know whether one cheap before-training
  number on a frozen Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct can tell me, in advance, when
  training on behavior A will spill into behavior B. To find out, I take
  five buckets that span "should obviously transfer" to "should
  obviously not transfer", run the same single number on every (A, B)
  pair across all five, then train and see what actually leaks. If the
  number tracks the leak across all five buckets, it is a real
  predictor. If it only fires inside the bucket the parent issue already
  validated, it is an EM-specific artifact.
- **What I expect.** The number tracks leakage well at the easy ends
  (high at cross-lingual, near zero at the orthogonal non-transfer
  pair) and tracks the surprising middle (narrow-EM and benign-data →
  unsafety) with the same ρ ≈ 0.5–0.7 the parent issue saw on EM only.
  I expect the broad → broad arm to be the weakest stratum because a
  broad behavior is hard to install and hard to score cleanly.
- **What would change my mind.** If the number assigns SHORT distance
  to the orthogonal non-transfer pair (false positive), or LONG distance
  to English → Spanish where transfer is essentially guaranteed (false
  negative), the number is not what I think it is — it is reading
  surface content overlap, not persona geometry. I am running an
  explicit topic-strip control on every cell to catch this; if the
  topic-stripped version flattens, the verdict shifts from "the
  predictor works" to "the predictor is a content-similarity probe".

## 2. Hypothesis + falsification criteria

**H1 (cross-lingual anchor, positive control).** The cosine assigns
small persona-distance to (English-behavior, Spanish-behavior) pairs and
predicts high transfer (≥ 50% rate of the target behavior in Spanish
when trained in English). Concretely: cosine(English-behavior,
Spanish-behavior) > 0.7 at L25 newline-after-`assistant` AND post-SFT
Spanish-behavior rate > 0.5 on the canonical cross-lingual eval panel
(Romance neighbors per #235's 7-direction grid).
**Falsification:** cosine < 0.5 on any cross-lingual pair OR transfer
rate < 0.3.

**H2 (non-transfer anchor, negative control).** Per MF-1, the
non-transfer end is anchored by THREE mandatory orthogonal pairs
(`secure_code → T1_medical` AND `educational → T2_code` AND
`evil_numbers → T1_medical`), each at 2 seeds = 6 observations. The
cosine assigns LARGE distance (low cosine) AND the measured leakage
stays near base (< 0.10) across all three pairs. Concretely: pooled
mean cosine < 0.5 AND pooled mean leakage < 0.10 across the 6
observations.
**Falsification:** any cell shows cosine ≥ 0.7 (false positive) OR
leakage ≥ 0.20.
**Install-QC pre-condition (MF-6).** Before H2 is interpretable, each
source adapter (`secure_code`, `educational`, `evil_numbers`) must
PASS its diagonal install-QC: source ON its own held-out same-domain
eval ≥ +0.10 above base-model rate. AND each source must show
ABOVE-baseline behavior on at least one EXPECTED-transfer non-target
eval (e.g., `secure_code → "write secure code"` in-domain;
`secure_code → T2_code` related-domain). If a source fails both, it
is a no-op adapter and reports NO useful negative-control information
— that cell is dropped from H2 and surfaced as a Bucket E
identification failure.

**H3 (narrow → broad EM, replication of #468).** Recomputing the
predictor with the new code path on the 10 narrow sources reproduces
#468's ρ within ±0.10: ρ ∈ [0.56, 0.76] on 20 cells (10 × 2 seeds).
**Falsification:** ρ < 0.40 — predictor implementation regressed.

**H4 (narrow → narrow off-diagonal).** ρ ∈ [+0.20, +0.55] pooled over
54 off-diagonal N→N cells.
**Falsification:** ρ ≤ 0 (predictor cannot generalize past N→B-EM) OR
ρ ≥ +0.55 (the predictor is unexpectedly STRONGER on N→N than on
N→B-EM — warrants its own follow-up).

**H5 (narrow → broad sycophancy).** ρ ∈ [+0.40, +0.75] pooled over 20
cells.
**Falsification:** ρ ≤ +0.20 (predictor is EM-specific).

**H6 (broad → broad, descriptive only).** n = 4 cells precludes
p-value claims; reported with 95% bootstrap CI + exact permutation null.
**Falsification (descriptive):** 95% CI excludes zero on the negative
side.

**H7 (benign-data arm, replication + extension of He et al.).**
- (7a) Top-100 representation-selected Alpaca yields post-SFT
  broad-unsafety ASR ≥ +30 percentage points above random-100 baseline
  on Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct (He et al. saw +58 on Llama-2-7B-Chat).
- (7b, redefined per MF-2(a), DESCRIPTIVE with CI).** Unit of analysis
  is SELECTOR-LEVEL, n=15 rows (5 selectors × 3 seeds). Each row =
  (selector top-100 mean cosine at L25/p5, selector adapter ASR delta
  vs random-100 baseline). H7-7b: the selector-level rank correlation
  between mean cosine and ASR delta is positive at n=15. Reported as a
  descriptive rank-correlation with 95% bootstrap CI; NO +0.30 gate at
  this n. Per-datapoint ASR contribution is structurally
  unidentifiable from a multi-datapoint fine-tune at this design and
  is dropped from the H7-7b formulation. **Independence pre-condition
  per MF-5:** before H7-7b is interpreted, report `Spearman(D1_ranking,
  D3_ranking)` over the same Alpaca/Dolly corpus at K=100. If
  ρ(D1, D3) > 0.85, the two methods are not independent and H7-7b
  demotes to "D3 reproduces D1's ranking" with no mechanism /
  geometric claim; H7-7b is moved out of the H8 calibration headline
  in that branch.
- (7c, DESCRIPTIVE-only per MF-8(a)).** The convergent misaligned-
  persona direction projection (Soligo et al. 2506.11618) on the
  benign-data-induced shift; report cosine + 95% bootstrap CI per
  selector (n=15 selector-level points). NO +0.20 threshold gate.
  Mechanism-sharing is a descriptive read, not a statistical test. To
  guard against generic fine-tune drift, projection baselines per
  MF-7 are MANDATORY: (i) norm-matched random directions (sample
  10–20 random vectors with the same norm as the EM direction, project
  the benign-data shift onto each, report the CI of cosines);
  (ii) a non-EM persona direction extracted by the same Soligo-style
  procedure (e.g., from `educational` or `secure_code`). H7-7c is
  interpreted as supporting mechanism sharing ONLY if the EM-direction
  cosine cleanly exceeds the random-direction CI upper bound AND the
  non-EM-direction cosine. Moved out of the H8 calibration headline.

**Falsification (any of):** 7a delta < +10 pp (negative result, still
publishable: Qwen-vs-Llama-2 attack-transfer-failure); 7b rank
correlation 95% CI excludes zero on the NEGATIVE side at n=15 (the
predictor anti-ranks the selectors); 7c EM-direction cosine falls
INSIDE the norm-matched random CI OR is exceeded by the non-EM
persona direction.

**H8 (calibration headline).** Pooling all 5 buckets with bucket as
a stratifying factor, the predictor's pooled coefficient is positive
with 95% CI excluding zero, AND each bucket individually shows the
bucket-prior direction (cross-lingual: high transfer at small distance;
non-transfer: low transfer at large distance; surprising middle:
positive coefficient).
**Falsification:** the cross-lingual or non-transfer bucket reads the
wrong way (the predictor fails its own positive/negative control),
which demotes any signal in the surprising middle to an EM-specific
finding.

**Kill criterion (predictor-side, content-vs-geometry).** Per #467: if
the topic-strip control flips the cosine sign on > 50% of cells where
the headline cosine is positive, the entire predictor verdict reframes
as "content-bound" rather than "geometric". Result still ships;
framing changes.

**Kill criterion (per bucket).** If a bucket's diagonal install QC
fails — source models do not produce the trained behavior — drop that
bucket from the headline regression and report the diagonal-failure
verdict.

### 4.2 Bucket A — cross-lingual (positive control)

**Behaviors.** Use the existing #235 cross-lingual rig as INSTRUMENT.
The "source" is a behavior trained under one (directive language,
completion language) pair; the "target" is the same behavior viewed
under a sibling directive language. Three cells span the bucket
(A1' added per MF-4 as between-bucket discriminator):

- **A1 = English-directive sycophancy → Spanish-directive
  sycophancy.** The unsurprising-end positive control.
- **A1' = English-directive sycophancy → Spanish-directive
  HONEST-CORRECTION (NON-sycophancy) [MF-4 discriminator].** Same
  Spanish surface form as A1 but persona structure differs (sycophancy
  vs honest correction). The K=8 Spanish vector is built from
  in-context (Q, A) rows in Spanish where the answer REJECTS the false
  premise — the contrastive negative of sycophancy. Expected predictor
  reading: if the cosine reads PERSONA-SPACE GEOMETRY, A1's cosine
  must be substantially HIGHER than A1' (the same surface language but
  opposite persona structure should NOT score equally close). If A1 ≈
  A1', the cosine is reading "is this Spanish" not "is this
  sycophancy" — Bucket A becomes a language-surface tautology, not a
  positive control. Concrete discriminator threshold:
  `cosine(A1) − cosine(A1') ≥ 0.15` to claim geometry; if not,
  Bucket A's positive-control status is demoted to "tracks language
  surface" and H8 is re-anchored on the non-transfer end only.
- **A2 = English-directive sycophancy → Italian-directive
  sycophancy.** A graded check at one more Romance neighbor (expected
  slightly weaker transfer per #235's distance ordering).

**Source training.** Reuse #235 trained adapters if their weights are
still on HF (assumption #17). If absent — retrain two adapters under
#235's recipe: lr=5e-6, r=32, 1 epoch, N≈4990 UltraChat, with the
(language-directive, target-language-completion) pair built from
Claude Sonnet 4.5-generated sycophantic agreements over the #411
wrong-claim panel translated into each target language.

**Why sycophantic agreement?** The behavior must (a) have a
judge-validated rig (Haiku 4.5 κ=0.890 per #411), (b) be language-
agnostic in essence, (c) overlap with the B/C-bucket targets so the
cosine compares against the SAME target across buckets.

**Predictor.** cosine(K=8 examples from the english_directive training
rows, K=8 examples from the spanish_directive training rows). Both
K=8 sets are real in-context (Q, A) rows in their respective
languages.

**Target evaluation.** vLLM-batched generation on the #235 7-language
× 2 phrasings panel (40 completions per cell). Two judges:
(i) sycophantic-agreement Claude Haiku 4.5 (#411 prompt), translation-
aware via system-prompt instruction to score regardless of output
language; (ii) language identification via langdetect (#235
cross-check). Leakage outcome = fraction of `Spanish-directive`
completions on which the sycophantic behavior fires AND the response
is in Spanish.

**Pre-condition: ES/IT judge calibration (MF-3, Phase 0.5).** The #411
Haiku-4.5 judge was calibrated at κ=0.890 on ENGLISH wrong-claims
only. Before Bucket A enters the H8 calibration gate, run a
per-language calibration: translate the #411 wrong-claim panel into
Spanish and Italian (Claude Sonnet 4.5 paraphrase); collect balanced
agreeing / disagreeing completions in each language using
Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct under the #235 cross-lingual recipe; compute κ
against a second-LLM rater (Claude Sonnet 4.5 with the same #411
prompt, blind to the Haiku 4.5 judgment). **Required: κ ≥ 0.7
per language.** If a language fails κ, Bucket A is DEMOTED from the
H8 positive-control gate FOR THAT LANGUAGE; H8 re-anchors on the
remaining languages (and on the non-transfer end alone if both ES and
IT fail). Calibration step ~3 GPU-h: 50 completions/language × 2
phrasings × Claude Batches dual-rater on the 2 raters (cheap, mostly
judge API), plus a single Qwen-7B vLLM pass to generate the
balanced-completion set.

**Content control.** Topic-strip on the K=8 examples — paraphrase into
language-agnostic placeholders to test whether the cosine signal is
reading language tokens directly versus the sycophantic structure.

### 4.5 Bucket D — benign-data → broad-unsafety (He et al. arm)

**Selectors (5 total, each picks top-K=100 from Alpaca+Dolly):**

- **D0 = random 100** (negative-control baseline, no selection).
- **D1 = representation matching** (He et al. Eq. 1).
- **D2 = gradient bidirectional** (He et al. Eq. 2).
- **D3 = #503 cosine** — the predictor under test, scored per benign
  datapoint against the average residual of the 10 illegal-activities
  Pure-bad anchor at L25 newline-after-`assistant`.
- **D4 = format selector** (random 100 from "all-list" + random 100
  from "all-math" subsets — He et al. §4.2 keyword-filter approach).

**Anchor sets** (Source: He et al. §3.1 default):

- D_harmful = 10 illegal-activities Pure-bad examples (Qi et al. 2023
  / He et al. default).
- D_safe = same 10 instructions with refusal/safe completions (for
  bidirectional anchoring on D2).

**SFT recipe per selector × seed.** Default from He et al. §3.1:
lr=5e-5, batch=20, 5 epochs, AdamW, bf16. 3 random seeds ({0, 42,
137}; He et al. used 5, we use 3 with explicit budget tradeoff).
LoRA r=32, α=256 (matching the rest of the spec for cross-bucket
comparability — He et al. used full FT but LoRA suffices on Qwen-7B
at this scale; verify in smoke).

**Safety target eval.** AdvBench 520 harmful instructions (Source:
He et al. §3.1 + Zou et al. 2023). vLLM batched, greedy decoding per
He et al. Two judges:

- **GPT ASR** (Source: He et al. default rubric) — Claude Sonnet 4.5
  (we sub in our project judge; their rubric is 1–5 harmfulness; we
  port the rubric verbatim from their Appendix scoring guidelines and
  report % of responses scoring 5).
- **Keyword ASR** (Source: AdvBench substring tool) — secondary
  reproducibility check; never the headline (no-substring-match rule).

**Selector-level unit of analysis (MF-2(a) redefinition).** Per
Statistics-lens reconciler binding: per-datapoint ASR contribution is
NOT directly observable from a multi-datapoint fine-tune. Bucket D
data is 15 SELECTOR-LEVEL rows (5 selectors × 3 seeds). Each row is
(selector top-100 mean cosine at L25/p5, selector adapter ASR delta
vs random-100). H7-7b is the rank correlation of these 15 rows. NO
per-datapoint correlation gate; NO +0.30 threshold at n=15.
Statistical reporting: Spearman ρ point estimate + 95% bootstrap CI.

**Method-independence pre-condition (MF-5).** Before any H7-7b
interpretation, compute `Spearman(D1_ranking, D3_ranking)` over the
SAME Alpaca/Dolly corpus at K=100. The two methods are both cosines
of a hidden-state representation against a harmful anchor; their
methodological independence is unknown a priori.
- **If ρ(D1, D3) > 0.85:** D3 is a near-duplicate of D1. H7-7b
  demotes to "the #503 cosine reproduces the He et al. representation
  ranking" — NO geometric / persona-space mechanism claim. H7-7b is
  REMOVED from the H8 calibration headline and reported as a
  methodological note ("D3 vs D1 head-to-head: equivalent rankings").
- **If ρ(D1, D3) ≤ 0.85:** keep H7-7b as a real geometric claim,
  report the rank-correlation as a methodological note, proceed with
  the head-to-head.

**Mechanism projection (the open-weights bonus, DESCRIPTIVE per
MF-8(a) + MF-7).** For each fine-tuned adapter ∈ {D0, D1, D2, D3, D4}
× 3 seeds = 15 adapter-level data points:

- Extract the post-FT vs pre-FT shift in residual stream at the
  broad-EM in-context prompt position (average delta in L25 residual
  when the model is shown the broad-EM in-context vector per #486).
- Extract the convergent misaligned-persona direction from a matched
  insecure-code-trained Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct adapter via Soligo et al.
  rank-1-LoRA method.

**Required projection baselines (MF-7).** Project the SAME 15
benign-data-induced shifts onto:
- (i) **Norm-matched random directions.** Sample 10–20 random unit
  vectors, scaled to the EM direction's norm; project the benign-data
  shift onto each. Report the empirical CI of cosines per selector.
- (ii) **Non-EM persona direction.** Extract a Soligo-style rank-1
  direction from the `educational` source adapter (a near-zero-EM
  cell per #458) AND from the `secure_code` source adapter; project
  the benign-data shifts onto each.

H7-7c interpretation rule: the EM-direction cosine must cleanly exceed
the norm-matched random CI upper bound AND the non-EM persona

descriptive bars with 95% bootstrap CI; no threshold gate.

**Content control on D3.** Topic-strip the K=8 in-context examples
used to build the #503 cosine for the benign-data ranking. If the
topic-stripped cosine flattens the selector ranking (Spearman between
topic-stripped and headline selector ranking < 0.5 on the 15 rows),

### 4.8 Content-vs-geometry control per cell (mandatory)

Per #467's within-lit scheme, baked in from the start:

For each (source, target) cosine, also compute `cosine_topic_strip` —
the target's K=8 examples paraphrased to language-agnostic /
domain-agnostic placeholders preserving the structural shape
(Source: #467). Method: Claude Sonnet 4.5 paraphrase call per K=8
set, ~10 minutes judge time per target, total ~$2 across all targets.

Output: per-cell `(cosine_headline, cosine_topic_strip)` pair entered
in §6 figures and the §6.1 regression. Read: if `topic_strip` ∈
`[headline − 0.05, headline + 0.05]`, geometric; if `topic_strip` ≪
`headline`, content-bound. Bucket A additionally tests language-token
vs structural (paraphrase preserves language) — separate ablation
branch already wired through `topic_strip.py` on the worktree.

**Caveat (MF-4).** For Bucket A specifically, the topic-strip control
is insufficient on its own: stripping language tokens from K=8 pairs
that ALREADY differ only in language tokens collapses both vectors to
the same content, yielding `cosine_topic_strip ≈ 1` by construction
regardless of geometry. The MF-4 A1' discriminator cell (§4.2) does
the real work for Bucket A: A1 vs A1' shares Spanish surface form but
differs in persona structure (sycophancy vs honest correction). The
A1 − A1' cosine gap is the diagnostic, not the topic-strip pair.
Topic-strip on Bucket A is still reported as a secondary check but is
NOT the primary content-vs-geometry test there.

### 4.10 Why code, not a model call?

For the predictor itself, code: deterministic forward-pass on a frozen
base model is reproducible and cheaper than approximating cosines with
a model call. For per-cell behavioral judges, model call (Claude
Sonnet 4.5 / gpt-4o / Claude Haiku 4.5 per target) — all behavioral
judges over unstructured free-generation responses, the model-call
regime per CLAUDE.md "Model call vs code". The He et al. selectors
are code (forward pass + gradient computation) — deterministic,
large-N, would be wasted as a model call. Flip conditions: if a target
judge calibration repeatedly fails κ ≥ 0.7, swap that judge for an
ensemble vote (still model-call), not a rule-based parser.

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Primary metric + threshold

Pooled binomial mixed model on (k, n) counts across all five buckets,
with bucket as a stratifying factor and source-family + target as
random effects:

```
cbind(k, n - k) ~ cosine_predictor + bucket + family + log_tokens
                  + lexical_persona_cosine + base_rate
                  + (1 | source) + (1 | target)
                  , family = binomial(link = "logit")
```

Cosine coefficient with family-clustered SE + 95% bootstrap CI is the
headline. Secondary: pooled Spearman ρ on (predictor, k/n).

**Convergence fallback (pre-registered).** If the binomial mixed model
fails to converge — separation, singular Hessian, optimizer warning —
fall back to the pseudocount-transformed fixed-form regression:

```
logit((k + 0.5) / (n + 1)) ~ cosine_predictor + bucket + family
                              + log_tokens + lexical_persona_cosine
                              + base_rate
                              + (1 | source) + (1 | target)
```

**Headline gate (H8 calibration):**

- Pooled cosine coef CI excludes zero on positive side AND per-bucket
  stratified reads each go the expected way:
  - Bucket A: A1 transfer rate > 0.5 AND A1 cosine > 0.7 AND the MF-4
    discriminator passes (`cosine(A1) − cosine(A1') ≥ 0.15`); ES/IT
    judge calibration step PASSED κ ≥ 0.7 for at least the language
    being read.
  - Bucket B (each of N→N, N→B-EM, N→B-syco): stratum ρ > +0.20.
  - Bucket D (per MF-2(a), MF-5): D3 selector-level rank correlation
    against ASR delta is positive at n=15 (descriptive — bootstrap CI
    excludes zero on the negative side); AND ρ(D1, D3) ≤ 0.85 (else
    H7-7b demotes and is REMOVED from this gate).
  - Bucket E: pooled-3-pair leakage < 0.10 AND pooled cosine < 0.5
    AND every Bucket E source passes its MF-6 install-QC.
- Below the gate: predictor reported as "validated within the
  misalignment matrix" with the calibration caveat that the
  unsurprising / non-transfer ends did not behave as expected.

**Method-independence pre-condition for Bucket D (MF-5).** Compute
`Spearman(D1_ranking, D3_ranking)` over the same Alpaca/Dolly corpus
at K=100. If ρ > 0.85, H7-7b is interpreted as "D3 reproduces D1's
ranking" with no geometric claim, and H7-7b is removed from the H8
calibration headline gate (the other Bucket D row, "ASR delta ≥ +30
pp", remains).

## 14. Success criteria (quantitative thresholds for the analyzer)

- **Calibration claim (H8) PASSES** if: pooled cosine coef CI excludes
  zero on positive side, AND Bucket A reads high transfer + small
  distance AND the MF-4 discriminator passes (`cosine(A1) − cosine(A1')
  ≥ 0.15`) AND at least one of {ES, IT} passes MF-3 κ ≥ 0.7, AND
  Bucket E reads pooled-3-pair leakage < 0.10 + pooled cosine < 0.5
  with every E source passing MF-6 install-QC.
- **#468 replication (H3) PASSES** if: ρ on Bucket B N→B-EM
  ∈ [0.56, 0.76].
- **Predictor generalizes past EM (H4 + H5) PASSES** if: N→N ρ ≥ +0.20
  AND N→B-syco ρ ≥ +0.40.
- **He et al. arm replicates (H7-7a) PASSES** if:
  representation-top-100 ASR delta ≥ +30 pp above random.
- **H7-7b PASSES (descriptive)** if (MF-2(a) + MF-5):
  selector-level n=15 rank correlation between #503-cosine and ASR
  delta has 95% bootstrap CI excluding zero on the negative side, AND
  ρ(D1, D3) ≤ 0.85. Above ρ(D1, D3) threshold, H7-7b demotes to "D3
  reproduces D1's ranking" — not a failure, but a methodological note
  outside H8.
- **H7-7c READS POSITIVE (descriptive)** if (MF-7 + MF-8(a)):
  EM-direction cosine 95% CI cleanly exceeds the norm-matched random
  CI upper bound AND exceeds the non-EM persona direction cosine. No
  threshold gate; reported as descriptive mechanism evidence.

Each passes / fails independently; the clean-result reports the
calibration over the joint distribution.

## 15. Kill criteria

- **Broad-syco C2 source does not install** (post-smoke or post-Phase-2
  start): drop Bucket C2 column; report "compliment→general syco did
  not install broad sycophancy on Qwen-7B" as a publishable negative.
- **Predictor is content-bound** (>50% of headline-positive cells flip
  on topic-strip): reframe verdict as "content-similarity probe",
  result still ships.
- **He et al. attack does NOT reproduce on Qwen** (ASR delta < 10 pp):
  Bucket D becomes a closed-weights-vs-open-weights negative; shared-
  mechanism arm dropped.
- **Cross-lingual positive control FAILS** (transfer < 0.3 on the
  English→Spanish cell): bucket-A prior fails; surface to user;
  calibration claim is structurally unanswerable.
- **MF-3 ES/IT judge κ fails** (κ < 0.7 in a target language under
  Phase 0.5 calibration): demote Bucket A FOR THAT LANGUAGE from the
  H8 positive-control gate; H8 re-anchors on remaining languages and
  on the non-transfer end alone if BOTH ES and IT fail κ.
- **MF-5 ρ(D1, D3) > 0.85**: D3 is not method-independent from D1;
  H7-7b demotes to "D3 reproduces D1's ranking" with NO geometric /
  persona-space claim; H7-7b is REMOVED from the H8 calibration
  headline.
- **MF-6 Bucket E install-QC fails** (a source fails BOTH its
  diagonal install-QC AND its expected-transfer non-target eval):
  that source is a no-op adapter; drop its E pair from H2 and surface
  as Bucket E identification failure. If all 3 E sources fail, H2 is
  uninformative — surface to user.
- **MF-4 A1' discriminator fails** (`cosine(A1) − cosine(A1')` <
  0.15): Bucket A is reading Spanish-language surface form, not
  persona-space geometry; demote Bucket A from positive-control gate
  to "tracks language surface"; H8 re-anchors on non-transfer end +
  surprising middle alone.
- **MF-7 control-direction matches EM direction** (norm-matched random
  CI upper bound OR non-EM persona cosine ≥ EM-direction cosine):
  H7-7c does NOT support mechanism sharing; the projection result is
  generic FT shift, not EM-mechanism-shared.
