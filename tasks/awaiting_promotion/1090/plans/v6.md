## §7 Decision Gates

### Kill criteria (registered abort/invalidate paths)

- **K1 (mix integrity):** staging verify fails (row count ≠ mix_meta `counts_realized`, or composition ≠ 20/20/40, or recorded-sha mismatch on a re-stage) → abort THAT cell fail-loud (fu2 `verify_and_stage_mix` behavior), never train on an unverified mix.
- **K2 (divergence):** training loss NaN or > 5.0 sustained for ≥ 5 steps → stop that run, mark the arm `diverged`, REPORT as an answer ("lr 1e-4 exceeds the stable regime for this recipe") — not a round abort.
- **K3 (retrain parity):** fmt-pers-lr1e5 rung-15 structural rate > 0.5 (vs parent 0.13–0.18) → round abort + harness diagnosis before interpreting any arm.
- **K4 (instrument):** per-arm judge content-drop rate ≥ 10% after transport re-judge → rule-23 truncation check before any rate is narrated.
