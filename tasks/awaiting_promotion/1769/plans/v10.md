straddle 0.75. If the CI at both new alphas remains wide (bootstrapped lower

CI width alongside the lower bound; a persistently wide CI at N=20 questions
indicates the f_d estimator is noisy at this bank size (diagnose vs. increase

## Decision gates

**G0 (pilot)**: unchanged from plan v5 — run on alpha=1.5 (lower dose, faster).
Gate: ≤ 6.05 s/sample (2× the 3.026 s/sample measured basis). If exceeded,
halt and descope to one alpha.

**Window-found (success):**
∃ alpha ∈ {1.5, 3.0} such that:
- evil both-arm mean ≤ 85 (ceiling does not fire), AND
- evil decode-arm CJK intrusion rate < 15%, AND
- evil exclusion-treatment f_d exclusion-CI lower bound > 0.75

AND (additionally for hallucination resolution):
- hallucination exclusion-treatment f_d CI is fully decode-driven (lower bound > 0.75)

**No-window kill (falsification):**
At BOTH alpha=1.5 and alpha=3.0:
- evil both-arm mean > 85 (ceiling fires at both doses), OR
- evil decode-arm CJK intrusion > 15% at the ceiling-clearing alpha

AND hallucination f_d exclusion-CI still spans 0.75 at both doses.

Report: "dose axis cannot deliver a clean window at this question count / direction quality; increase N_QUESTIONS or retrain r_B."

**K2 (dose-ladder coherence):** `evaluate_k2()` in `issue1769_run.py` pools
coherent-draw rates across ALL traits × ALL alphas. With the subgrid run, the
K2 pool includes only the new alphas {1.5, 3.0} plus the newly-generated
neither cells. If all cells fail the coherent-draw threshold → rc=9 HALT →
