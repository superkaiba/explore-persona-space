## 1. Goal

(Verbatim from the task `## Goal`.) Determine whether mean-centering the centroid bank before computing cosine distance (the canonical recipe pinned by #536) yields a **better leakage predictor** than raw (un-centered) cosine — a head-to-head predictive-skill comparison (CV R²_raw vs CV R²_centered, and length-partialled Spearman ρ, on the same predictor cells), distinct from #536's robustness/validity audit. #536 settled which recipe is *valid* (centered) and that the published calls *survive* the swap; it did NOT ask which recipe maximizes *predictive skill*. This task asks exactly that.

## 2. Hypothesis

Three competing, pre-registered hypotheses (from the body). The DV is, per bank, the paired difference `ΔR² = CV R²_centered − CV R²_raw` (primary, out-of-sample) and `Δρ = ρ_centered − ρ_raw` (secondary, in-sample), each with a paired bootstrap 95% CI.

- **H_centered-better** — centering raises out-of-sample skill (strips shared-mean nuisance variance) → centered is both valid AND the stronger predictor.
  - *Confirms:* `ΔR² > 0` with CI excluding 0 on (nearly) all verdict-contributing banks; same sign for `Δρ`.
  - *Falsifies:* any verdict-contributing bank with `ΔR² < 0`, CI excluding 0.
- **H_bank-dependent** — the sign of the centered−raw skill difference depends on the bank → no universal predictive-skill winner; centering is preferred on validity grounds, not skill.
  - *Confirms:* the sign of `ΔR²` (CI excluding 0) is positive on some verdict-contributing banks and negative on others.
  - *Falsifies:* a single consistent sign across all verdict-contributing banks whose CI excludes 0.
- **H_raw-inflates** — raw cosine systematically inflates *apparent* skill via the anisotropy ridge → raw's higher numbers are artifact; centered is honest even where its CV R² reads lower.
  - *Confirms:* the banks where `R²_raw > R²_centered` are also the banks where the raw off-diagonal cosine distribution is most compressed (the anisotropy signature in #536's `bank_offdiag` payload), AND the raw advantage shrinks/reverses out-of-sample relative to in-sample.
  - *Falsifies:* raw's CV-R² advantage is independent of compression, or grows out-of-sample.

These are not mutually exclusive: H_raw-inflates is a *mechanism* that, if true on some banks, produces H_bank-dependent at the verdict level. An answer is a per-bank table + a verdict tied to #536's two anchor observations (100-persona: raw pooled ρ 0.60→0.77 inflates; 19-bank: headline ρ −0.348 centered vs −0.037 raw exists only centered). Only banks with `contributes_to_h_verdict = true` (n_groups > 5 AND not `both_predictors_fail_oos`) feed the verdict mapping; low-N and both-negative banks are reported but excluded mechanically (§6).

## 3. Prior work / method delta

**Internal.** #536 (`scripts/issue536_recompute_driver.py`, clean-result HIGH) re-graded every persona-distance / leakage call under the centered recipe and reported, per task, the published statistic recomputed raw + centered and the paired (raw, centered) **rank correlation of the predictor values** (`raw_vs_centered_x_spearman`) as a *survival check* — does the published call stand when the recipe is swapped. It did NOT measure predictive SKILL as the dependent variable: there is no leave-group-out CV R² anywhere in #536, and the raw/centered Spearman it reports is the SAME-sample, full-data ρ of the recomputed statistic, not an out-of-sample fit. #648's method delta is exactly this: the DV is **out-of-sample CV R²** (and a CV-aligned length-partialled ρ), paired and bootstrapped, so "which recipe predicts held-out leakage better" is answerable rather than only "does the published number survive." The body's two anchor observations (100-persona inflation, 19-bank centered-only headline) are precisely the cases where survival and skill diverge.

The recoverable-bank line is the #404/#458 persona-distance predictor program (`docs/open_questions.md` anchor #2 `q:leak-predictor`: "when we train on data exhibiting behavior B in context C, can we find a simple predictor — measurable before training — for whether the model exhibits B′ in C′?"). Centering validity is settled (`.claude/rules/persona-distance-metrics.md` § Bank centering): Qwen-2.5-7B centroids share a dominant mean direction, so raw bank cosine compresses into ~[0.73, 1.0] (anisotropy ridge); centered is canonical. #648 does NOT relitigate that — it asks only about skill, holding centering canonical regardless of the verdict.

**External (1-paragraph note).** This is an internal extension; the external literature is thin but supportive. The anisotropy of contextual-embedding spaces and the value of mean-centering / whitening before cosine is the standard finding of the representation-degeneration / isotropy line (Gao et al. 2019 *Representation Degeneration Problem in Training NLP Models*; Mu & Viswanath 2018 *All-but-the-Top*; Ethayarajh 2019 *How Contextual are Contextualized Word Representations?* / anisotropy). Within-task predictor-skill comparison under a metric rescaling that is monotone within a fixed reference set is the standard CV-R² model-comparison setup (Hastie/Tibshirani/Friedman, *ESL*, §7 on CV estimates of test error; paired bootstrap on a difference of risks per Efron & Tibshirani 1993, *An Introduction to the Bootstrap*, ch. 6 on the bootstrap distribution of a statistic and ch. 17 on hypothesis testing via the bootstrap). The train/test-disjoint construction of any fitted preprocessing step (here the centering mean) is the standard guard against CV optimism (ESL §7.10.2 "the wrong and right way to do cross-validation" — preprocessing that uses the response or the held-out fold must be inside the CV loop). No external paper measures raw-vs-centered persona-distance leakage prediction specifically — that is the project-internal contribution. A deeper arXiv search is not warranted for a CPU-only re-read of cached internal panels.

### 4.0 Single-variable-change vs #536 (consistency-checker contract)

The ONLY variable that moves between the two scored predictors within a bank is the **cosine centering recipe**: `centering='none'` (raw) vs `centering='global_mean'` (centered), via the already-existing `compute_cosine_matrix(C, centering=...)` switch (`src/explore_persona_space/analysis/representation_shift.py`, signature confirmed: `centering ∈ {"none", "global_mean", int}`). Held constant across the two recipes **within each bank**: the centroid bank + persona order, the per-cell leakage target, the length covariate + residualization method, the CV partition (same fold assignment under both recipes), the ρ estimator, and the bootstrap resample indices.

**v2 (MF5) — train-fold-only centering for the primary DV, applied SYMMETRICALLY.** For the PRIMARY out-of-sample DV (CV R²), the centering mean is recomputed INSIDE each LOGO split from TRAIN-fold personas only; the held-out unit never enters the mean it is centered against. This applies identically to BOTH recipes: raw (`centering='none'`) has no fitted mean and is mechanically unchanged; centered (`centering='global_mean'`) now refits its mean on the train-fold centroid subset every split. The moved variable therefore stays **raw-vs-centered** — NOT raw-vs-centered-vs-train-fold-centered — because train-fold-only fitting is the standard CV hygiene wrapper, not a third recipe. The SECONDARY in-sample DV (length-partialled ρ, the read tied to #536's `raw_vs_centered_x_spearman` survival family) keeps the bank-global centering #536 used, explicitly labeled `transductive` / `in-sample` in §6 + the output schema. This is hygiene-on-the-primary-DV, not a recipe rewrite vs #536: #536's "centered" was an in-sample-SURVIVAL recipe; the out-of-sample SKILL question requires the predictor not to have seen its own test unit (ESL §7.10.2). This is a single-variable change layered on #536's recoverable-bank set; the CV-R² machinery is a NEW dependent-variable computation, not a new manipulation. (Cross-reference §5 Controls, §11 Decision Rationale.)

```python
@dataclass
class BankPanel:
    bank_id: str            # plain-English label, e.g. "100-persona L20 (#66)"
    family: str             # #536 family slug, e.g. "single_token_100p_L20"
    source_task_ids: list   # [66] / [142] / [396,415] / ... for the MF1 accounting gate
    C: np.ndarray           # centroid bank (n_personas x d) for this layer
    cell_src: np.ndarray    # per-cell row index into C (the "source" persona)
    cell_tgt: np.ndarray    # per-cell col index into C (the "target"/bystander persona)
    centering_bank_idx: np.ndarray  # which C rows define the centering universe (full bank,
                            #   or the CORE-11 subset for #142) — train fold is intersected w/ this
    y: np.ndarray           # per-cell continuous leakage target (#536's Y)
    group: np.ndarray       # CV group label per cell (§4.3 LOGO unit) — ORIGINAL group ids
    length: np.ndarray      # per-cell length covariate (token length / log-tokens)
    cv_unit: str            # "leave-one-source-out" | "leave-one-persona-out" | ...
    gate_max_dev: float     # the #536 matrix/statistic join-gate deviation (must pass)

def cosine_distance_predictor(C, cell_src, cell_tgt, centering_rows, centering):
    """Build per-cell cosine DISTANCE (1 - cos) using a centering mean computed ONLY
    from `centering_rows` of C (train-fold subset for the primary DV; full bank for
    the in-sample secondary). raw: centering='none' -> no mean -> centering_rows ignored.
    centered: subtract C[centering_rows].mean(0) from ALL rows, then cosine."""
    Ct = torch.as_tensor(C, dtype=torch.float64)
    if centering == "none":
        Cc = Ct
    elif centering == "global_mean":
        mu = Ct[torch.as_tensor(centering_rows, dtype=torch.long)].mean(dim=0, keepdim=True)
        Cc = Ct - mu
    else:
        raise ValueError(centering)
    Cn = Cc / Cc.norm(dim=1, keepdim=True).clamp_min(1e-12)
    cos = (Cn[cell_src] * Cn[cell_tgt]).sum(dim=1).numpy().astype(np.float64)
    return 1.0 - cos

# ── NEW (v2 MF5): leave-one-group-out CV R² with TRAIN-FOLD-ONLY centering ──
def logo_cv_r2(panel: BankPanel, centering: str) -> tuple[float, int]:
    """Out-of-sample R² (leave-one-group-out). For EACH held-out original group g:
      1. TRAIN = all cells whose ORIGINAL group != g.
      2. centering_rows = (panel.centering_bank_idx) ∩ (personas appearing as src/tgt
         in TRAIN cells) — the held-out group's personas are EXCLUDED from the mean
         (MF5: predictor never sees its own test unit).
      3. build x_train, x_test from cosine_distance_predictor(... centering_rows ...).
      4. length-residualize x and y on length (OLS) using TRAIN-fold coefficients,
      5. fit y_resid ~ x_resid (OLS) on TRAIN, apply to TEST,
      6. accumulate held-out (y_resid_true, y_resid_hat).
    SS_tot uses the PER-FOLD TRAIN-mean of y_resid as the out-of-sample baseline
    (MF4 pin: 'out-of-sample R²' compares held-out predictions against the held-out
    reference with the train-mean as the dispersion reference). Returns (R2, n_folds_skipped)."""
    uniq = np.unique(panel.group)
    y_hat, y_true = [], []
    n_skip = 0
    for g in uniq:
        tr = panel.group != g
        te = panel.group == g
        if te.sum() == 0 or tr.sum() < 3:
            n_skip += 1; continue
        # MF5: centering universe = bank centering set ∩ personas present in TRAIN cells
        train_personas = np.unique(np.concatenate([panel.cell_src[tr], panel.cell_tgt[tr]]))
        centering_rows = np.intersect1d(panel.centering_bank_idx, train_personas)
        x = cosine_distance_predictor(panel.C, panel.cell_src, panel.cell_tgt,
                                      centering_rows, centering)
        bx = np.polyfit(panel.length[tr], x[tr], 1)
        by = np.polyfit(panel.length[tr], panel.y[tr], 1)
        xr_tr = x[tr] - np.polyval(bx, panel.length[tr])
        yr_tr = panel.y[tr] - np.polyval(by, panel.length[tr])
        xr_te = x[te] - np.polyval(bx, panel.length[te])
        yr_te = panel.y[te] - np.polyval(by, panel.length[te])
        if np.var(xr_tr) < VAR_FLOOR or np.var(yr_tr) < VAR_FLOOR:   # degenerate fold (§6)
            n_skip += 1; continue
        m = np.polyfit(xr_tr, yr_tr, 1)
        # MF4: per-fold TRAIN-mean baseline for SS_tot, applied to THIS fold's held-out cells
        train_mean = float(yr_tr.mean())
        y_hat.append(np.polyval(m, xr_te) - train_mean)   # residual-vs-train-mean prediction error frame
        y_true.append(yr_te - train_mean)
    if not y_true:
        return float("nan"), n_skip
    y_hat = np.concatenate(y_hat); y_true = np.concatenate(y_true)
    ss_res = float(((y_true - y_hat) ** 2).sum())
    ss_tot = float((y_true ** 2).sum())   # y_true already centered on per-fold train-mean (MF4)
    return (1.0 - ss_res / ss_tot if ss_tot > 0 else float("nan")), n_skip

# ── NEW (v2 MF2): paired bootstrap in ORIGINAL-group space — no duplicate held-out leakage ──
def paired_bootstrap_delta(panel: BankPanel, n_boot=N_BOOT, seed=RNG_SEED) -> dict:
    """Resample GROUPS (cluster bootstrap) with replacement, then re-run LOGO CV in
    ORIGINAL-group space: a held-out original group's cells are NEVER in any train
    fold of the same resample, even if that group was drawn multiple times. We
    bootstrap the per-original-group held-out loss contributions with their draw
    multiplicities (equivalent to, and cheaper than, materializing duplicated cells),
    so no exact-copy of a held-out group can sit in training. Under the IDENTICAL
    group draw both recipes are scored (pairing cancels resample noise). Returns the
    full-data point delta + percentile 95% CI of each delta.

    MF2 disjointness guarantee (asserted per fold, both recipes): the LOGO loop holds
    out by ORIGINAL group id, so set(original_group[test]) ∩ set(original_group[train])
    == ∅ by construction. The bootstrap only re-weights the held-out contributions of
    those disjoint folds; it never relabels a duplicate as a fresh held-out fold."""
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    uniq = np.unique(panel.group)

    # 1. Compute, ONCE on full data, each original group's held-out (y_true, y_hat)
    #    contribution under each recipe (the leakage-free LOGO fit above already does
    #    train/test disjointness by original group). Cache per-group residual arrays.
    per_group = _logo_group_contributions(panel)   # {g: {"raw": (yt,yh), "cen": (yt,yh)}}

    d_r2, d_rho = [], []
    for _ in range(n_boot):
        gs = rng.choice(uniq, size=len(uniq), replace=True)   # multiset of ORIGINAL group ids
        # R² over the bootstrap: pool the held-out (y_true,y_hat) of each DRAWN original
        # group (with multiplicity); SS_tot referenced to the pooled train-mean-frame
        # residuals already baked into per_group (MF4). Duplicates contribute identical
        # held-out errors — they are NOT re-held-out against their own copies (MF2).
        r2c = _r2_from_contributions([per_group[g]["cen"] for g in gs])
        r2r = _r2_from_contributions([per_group[g]["raw"] for g in gs])
        # in-sample rho on the bootstrap sample (cells of drawn original groups, w/ mult.)
        sel = np.concatenate([np.where(panel.group == g)[0] for g in gs])
        x_cen = cosine_distance_predictor(panel.C, panel.cell_src, panel.cell_tgt,
                                          panel.centering_bank_idx, "global_mean")
        x_raw = cosine_distance_predictor(panel.C, panel.cell_src, panel.cell_tgt,
                                          panel.centering_bank_idx, "none")
        rc = length_partial_spearman(x_cen[sel], panel.y[sel], panel.length[sel])
        rr = length_partial_spearman(x_raw[sel], panel.y[sel], panel.length[sel])
        if np.isfinite(r2c) and np.isfinite(r2r): d_r2.append(r2c - r2r)
        if np.isfinite(rc)  and np.isfinite(rr):  d_rho.append(rc - rr)
    return {
        "delta_r2_point":  point_delta_r2(panel),       # full-data LOGO ΔR² (MF5 centering)
        "delta_r2_ci95":   [float(np.percentile(d_r2, 2.5)), float(np.percentile(d_r2, 97.5))],
        "delta_rho_point": point_delta_rho(panel),       # full-data in-sample Δρ (bank-global)
        "delta_rho_ci95":  [float(np.percentile(d_rho, 2.5)), float(np.percentile(d_rho, 97.5))],
        "n_boot_kept_r2":  len(d_r2), "n_boot_kept_rho": len(d_rho),
    }
```

## 5. Controls

> **v2 change:** added the "train-fold-only centering (primary DV)" control row (MF5) and the "original-group-space resample/holdout" control row (MF2).

The controls are baked into the **paired** design and are the consistency-checker's single-variable guarantee (§4.0):

| Control | What it holds fixed | What confound it rules out |
|---|---|---|
| **Same cells, both recipes** | identical centroid bank + identical per-cell (predictor, target) join under raw and centered | a skill difference cannot come from different data — only from the recipe |
| **Same length residualization** | `length_partial_spearman` + length-partial inside `logo_cv_r2`, identical method + covariate under both recipes | a difference cannot be a length-control artifact |
| **Same CV folds** | the LOGO group assignment is identical across recipes (one fold map per bank) | a difference cannot be a fold-luck artifact |
| **Train-fold-only centering on the primary DV (MF5)** | the centering mean is refit on TRAIN-fold personas inside each CV split, applied identically to both recipes (raw has no mean; centered refits per fold) | the held-out unit cannot enter its own predictor — removes the transductive CV optimism that would otherwise contaminate the out-of-sample CV R² (the moved variable stays raw-vs-centered, not raw-vs-centered-vs-train-fold) |
| **Paired bootstrap, original-group space (MF2)** | each bootstrap draw scores raw and centered on the IDENTICAL resampled groups; resampling AND held-out fold identity are in ORIGINAL-group space, so a duplicated group's exact copy is never in its own train fold | (i) the CI on `ΔR²`/`Δρ` cancels resample noise — it measures the recipe contrast, not sampling variance of each recipe separately; (ii) no held-out leakage from duplicate pseudo-groups inflating the ΔR² CI (the H-verdict instrument) |
| **#536 join gate re-run first** | the raw recompute must reproduce #536's persisted/published number (1e-4 matrix or \|Δρ\|≤0.02) before any skill is read | recoverable-bank-set / recipe drift vs #536 (the top risk) is caught at gate time, per bank |
| **In-sample ρ reported alongside CV R²** | the same-sample `length_partial_spearman` (matches #536's `raw_vs_centered_x_spearman` family, bank-global centering) reported next to out-of-sample CV R² and explicitly labeled `transductive` | lets the result separate "raw fits the sample better" (in-sample) from "raw predicts held-out better" (CV) — the H_raw-inflates mechanism |

Primary DV = `ΔR²` (out-of-sample, leakage-free). `Δρ` is reported for continuity with #536 and as the in-sample fit; the verdict rests on `ΔR²` + its CI on verdict-eligible banks, with `Δρ` used to diagnose inflation (raw in-sample advantage that does not survive CV) and to carry the n≤5 banks.

### Thresholds / verdict rule (pre-registered)

- A bank has a **determinate skill winner** only when its `ΔR²` paired-bootstrap 95% CI EXCLUDES 0. Sign of the point estimate names the winner.
- A bank whose `ΔR²` CI SPANS 0 is reported as **skill-indistinguishable** (neither recipe is the better predictor at this N) — never narrated as a winner, never the basis of a hypothesis call.
- **Verdict eligibility is mechanical (MF3 + MF4), not narration.** A bank feeds the H-verdict mapping ONLY when `contributes_to_h_verdict = true`, which requires ALL of: (a) `n_groups > 5` (MF3 — a 5-from-5 cluster bootstrap is too coarse to carry a determinate call; #66 and #142 are the named excluded instances, `exclusion_reason = "low_n_groups<=5"`); (b) NOT `both_predictors_fail_oos` (MF4 precedence — see below); (c) ≤25% of folds degenerate-skipped. Any row with `n_groups ≤ 5 AND contributes_to_h_verdict = true` is a CONTRACT VIOLATION the run rejects (asserted in `main`).
- **MF4 both-negative precedence (explicit branch).** A bank flagged `both_predictors_fail_oos` (both `cv_r2_raw < 0` AND `cv_r2_centered < 0` — both predictors worse than the held-out mean) is EXCLUDED from the H-verdict REGARDLESS of whether its ΔR² CI excludes 0. The schema encodes this as `contributes_to_h_verdict = false`, `exclusion_reason = "both_predictors_fail_oos"`. This precedence is checked FIRST (before the low-N and degenerate branches) so the two rules can never both "fire" ambiguously on the same bank — the `exclusion_reason` is a single deterministic string.
- **Verdict mapping (over verdict-ELIGIBLE banks only):** all eligible determinate banks same positive sign → **H_centered-better**; mixed eligible determinate signs → **H_bank-dependent**; eligible raw-favoring banks coincide with the most-compressed `bank_offdiag` distributions AND a raw in-sample-over-CV gap → **H_raw-inflates** (mechanism producing H_bank-dependent). The two #536 anchors are pre-named expectations: 100-persona (#66, n≤5 → carried by Δρ) is the inflation candidate, 19-bank (#311, eligible) the centered-only-headline candidate.

## 11. Decision Rationale (load-bearing parameters)

> **v2 change:** added the train-fold-only-centering rationale (MF5), the SS_tot pin rationale (MF4), and the original-group bootstrap rationale (MF2); #142's centering recipe noted.

No model is trained, so there are no training hyperparameters. The load-bearing ANALYSIS choices and their sources:

- **CV scheme = leave-one-group-out (LOGO), group = the bank's natural held-out unit (per §4.3 table).**
  - *Why:* CV R² must measure held-out-PERSONA / held-out-SOURCE generalization — the `q:leak-predictor` construct (predict B′ in C′ for an UNSEEN persona/source). The group is the unit each parent task already used as its statistical cluster (#478 clusters on `cell_id|seed`, #405 on persona, #505 on `j_i|seed`, #311 on bystander, #66/#142 on source), so LOGO matches the parent's own generalization claim. Random k-fold would leak related cells (same source's pairs) across train/test and inflate R².
  - *Source:* `#536` for each bank's shape + cluster unit (read from the adapter cluster definitions: `regrade_478`, `regrade_405`, `regrade_505`, `verify_311`, `verify_380`, `verify_66`, `verify_142`); the LOGO partition choice is this plan's, grounded in the parent's cluster column. Standard out-of-sample CV-R² practice: ESL §7.
- **Centering mean refit on TRAIN-fold personas inside each CV split — primary DV (MF5).**
  - *Why:* the PRIMARY DV is OUT-OF-SAMPLE skill. If the centering mean is computed once over the whole bank (v1), the held-out persona contributes ~1/N of the mean it is centered against (~1/24 #380, ~1/17 #311, 1/5 #142/#66), so the predictor has already seen its own test unit — textbook CV optimism that contaminates the headline CV R². #536's `verify_142` proves the centering-bank composition materially moves centered cosine (full-111 ρ 0.57-0.76 vs core-11 published), so "which personas enter the centering mean" is recipe-defining in this codebase, not negligible. Refitting on train-fold personas only removes the leak; applied SYMMETRICALLY (raw has no fitted mean → unchanged), so the single variable stays raw-vs-centered. This is hygiene-on-the-primary-DV, NOT a recipe rewrite vs #536: #536's "centered" was an in-sample-SURVIVAL recipe; the out-of-sample-SKILL question requires train/test-disjoint preprocessing. The SECONDARY in-sample ρ keeps #536's bank-global centering and is labeled `transductive`.
  - *Source:* ESL §7.10.2 ("the wrong and right way to do cross-validation" — preprocessing fit using the held-out fold leaks); `#536` `verify_142` for the demonstration that centering-bank composition is load-bearing.
- **SS_tot = per-fold TRAIN-mean baseline (MF4 pin).**
  - *Why:* "out-of-sample R²" compares each held-out fold's predictions against the held-out reference, with the TRAIN-mean of `y_resid` as the dispersion reference (the naive baseline a model with no predictor information would use). The absolute-negative-R² flag (which triggers `both_predictors_fail_oos`) depends on this being pinned: the v1 docstring (per-fold train-mean) and v1 pseudocode (pooled held-out mean) disagreed. Pinned to the per-fold train-mean in BOTH the docstring and the pseudocode (§4.2). Both recipes use the identical SS_tot definition, so the pairing is valid.
  - *Source:* standard out-of-sample R² semantics (ESL §7.2; the held-out-prediction-error-vs-train-mean-baseline framing); this plan's pin to resolve the v1 inconsistency.
- **Paired bootstrap = 10,000 cluster (original-group-level) resamples, paired (same draw both recipes), resample + held-out in ORIGINAL-group space (MF2), percentile 95% CI; RNG seed 20648.**
  - *Why:* ≥10k is the standard floor for a stable percentile CI on a difference statistic; cluster (not cell) resampling respects within-group dependence (multiple cells per source/persona); pairing cancels resample noise so the CI measures the recipe contrast. The original-group-space fix (MF2) prevents a duplicate-drawn group's exact copy from sitting in its own train fold — v1 remapped each draw to a distinct pseudo-group label, so `np.unique` treated identical copies as separate held-out folds and one copy trained on the other (held-out leakage biasing the ΔR² CI that declares determinate winners). The MF2 design caches each original group's held-out contribution once and re-weights by draw multiplicity, guaranteeing disjointness.
  - *Source:* Efron & Tibshirani 1993 (*An Introduction to the Bootstrap*, ch. 6 percentile CI, ch. 17 bootstrap of a difference); cluster bootstrap is the standard for grouped data; the original-group disjointness is standard CV-bootstrap hygiene (ESL §7.10.2 extended to the bootstrap). 10,000 also matches the project convention (e.g. #514 matched-rate re-bootstrap).
- **Verdict eligibility = mechanical `contributes_to_h_verdict` (MF3 + MF4).**
  - *Why:* a determinate-winner call from a CI is only as trustworthy as the resample that produced it. A 5-from-5 cluster bootstrap (#66, #142) is too coarse to carry a determinate call (MF3), and a bank where both predictors fail out-of-sample (`R²<0` both, MF4) has no meaningful "winner" even if the ΔR² CI excludes 0. v1 left these as narration-only ("never narrate a small-bank point estimate as a winner"), which is unenforced. v2 encodes both as a schema boolean with a single deterministic `exclusion_reason` (both-negative precedence checked first), and `main` asserts no `n_groups≤5` row claims eligibility. Low-N rows are still REPORTED (ΔR²+CI, open forest markers); they just don't drive the mapping.
  - *Source:* this plan's mechanization of the round-1 statistics-critic bindings (MF3, MF4); the n≤5 floor is conservative (a 5-cluster bootstrap has ≤5 distinct resampled cluster counts). `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` for the exact `>25%`-degenerate-folds downgrade constant (flagged Low in §12).
- **Length-partial residualization = regress the length covariate out of BOTH predictor and target, then correlate the residuals — identical method + covariate to #536.**
  - *Why:* must match #536 exactly so the single variable is the centering recipe, not the residualization. `length_partial_spearman` (the `(rxy − rx·ry)/√(...)` form) is imported verbatim for the in-sample ρ; the length covariate per bank is the one #536 used (token length / `log_tokens`). Inside `logo_cv_r2` the same length-partial is applied, fit on TRAIN-fold only (no held-out length leakage), identically under both recipes.
  - *Source:* `#536` (`scripts/issue536_recompute_driver.py::length_partial_spearman` lines 139-147; per-bank covariate inherited from each adapter).
- **"Recoverable bank" gate = #536's `audit_table.json::affected_set`, exact-row = persisted centroid bank + persisted continuous target + #536 join gate passes (1e-4 matrix or |Δρ|≤0.02), with the MF1 accounting gate.**
  - *Why:* the body + clarify note pin the recoverable set to exactly #536's join-gate-passing banks; raw recompute must reproduce the published number first. #142 is a distinct recoverable exact-row bank (its own core-11-subset centering recipe + 50-pair LOGO) and was added (MF1); the accounting gate aborts on any unaccounted recoverable task.
  - *Source:* `#536` (`affected_set` partition; the per-adapter join gates `GATE_MATRIX_TOL=1e-4`, `GATE_RHO_TOL=0.02`; `verify_142` for #142's core-11-subset recipe).
- **Saturated/degenerate-cell floor = predictor (and target) residual variance < 1e-8 (float64) → fold skipped + counted; bank with >25% folds skipped → `degenerate_folds>25pct` (verdict-ineligible).**
  - *Why:* a residualized cosine-distance predictor (values on ~[0,1]) with variance < 1e-8 is constant to ~4 sig-figs and carries no rank/regression information; the CV fit is undefined and the fold must be dropped, not silently NaN-propagated. The 25% bank-level cutoff is this plan's conservative downgrade rule so a mostly-degenerate bank does not drive the verdict.
  - *Source:* numerical-degeneracy floor (float64 precision on unit-scale cosine distances); `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` for the exact 1e-8 / 25% constants (a smoke run on the 9 banks will confirm no exact-row bank trips them; flagged Low in §12).
- **Marker recipe overrides parent parity:** N/A — no marker, no fresh training.
