## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

### Goal (verbatim from frontmatter)

Test whether pushing the marker implant past emission onset (source
`log P(marker) − base` in the [14, 20] nat window where on-policy
emission begins) makes the per-context singleton shifts develop
effective rank ≥ 2 across held-out contexts, so that the
additivity-cosine read becomes a diagnostic superposition test rather
than a measurement of constant-direction steering.

### Hypothesis (primary, H1)

At the [14, 20] nat band-stop, both gates pass on ≥ 4 of the 6
joint cells (pair × seed): GD3 (per-singleton effective rank ≥ 2.0
for both singletons) AND GD1 (joint per-context shift matrix top-1
SV share ≤ 0.75 AND effective rank ≥ 2.0). Conditional on GD1 + GD3
passing, DV1 then operates as a real superposition test — median
per-context `cos(shift_{A+B}(c), shift_A(c) + shift_B(c)) ≥ 0.85` on
≥ 80% of contexts → additive superposition; otherwise → rank-1-map-
plus-beacons additivity FAILs even with per-context variation.

### Falsification (kill)

GD3 still fails on ≥ 5 of 6 cells at the harder dial point
(singleton effective rank ≤ 2.0 on both pairs across all seeds).
That means the rank-1 attractor is a property of the marker-only
loss objective itself, not of training depth, and the additivity-
cosine construct cannot be rescued by training harder. Next pivot is
necessarily a different training objective (whole-completion loss or
a different DV) — out of scope for this task.

### Hard kill criterion at the Phase A anchor-smoke gate

If at the new band [14, 20] nat the Phase A smoke (3 cells × 1 seed
on the first pair) shows EITHER (a) the band-stop never fires within
the raised 24-epoch cap on ≥ 2 of 3 cells (the implant cannot reach
14 nats at lr=5e-6, even with more epochs — the "ceiling-below-target"
failure mode), OR (b) the band fires but bystander argmax-emission ≥
0.92 on ≥ 2 of 3 cells (the bystander saturation predicted by the
recipe at the harder dial — the cells are uninterpretable) → post
`epm:failure v1` `failure_class=data,
reason=anchor_floor_or_ceiling_at_new_band` and STOP. Do NOT
autonomously rescue by raising lr (the recipe rule says lr ≥ 1e-4 is
the unconditional-repeater regime; 1e-5 is already the recipe's
ceiling rung). Do NOT silently descope to a different DV.

## 3. Falsification / kill criteria

Three nested levels of kill, in order of how cheaply they fire:

1. **Phase A anchor-smoke kill (cheap, ~3 GPU-h).** Phase A runs 3
   cells × 1 seed on the FIRST source pair at the new band [14, 20]
   nat. KILL if EITHER (a) the band-stop callback fails to fire
   within 24 epochs on ≥ 2 of 3 cells (the implant cannot reach 14
   nats — the "ceiling-below-target" failure mode) OR (b) the band
   fires but bystander argmax-emission rate ≥ 0.92 on the 4
   negative-panel personas on ≥ 2 of 3 cells (the bystander
   saturation regime the recipe warns about — at the harder dial
   bystanders rode in lockstep with source). Post `epm:failure v1`
   `failure_class=data,
   reason=anchor_floor_or_ceiling_at_new_band` and STOP. Do NOT
   raise lr (recipe rule).
2. **Mid-sweep guard (no autonomous kill).** Per-cell trajectory + the
   per-cell `marker_band_stop_result.json` are checkpointed by the
   existing dispatcher (`scripts/run_issue527_train.py`); if cells
   start cap-hitting (band-stop did NOT fire within 24 epochs) without
   a Phase A signal, the analyzer reads cap-hits per cell and reports
   them in the clean-result as a scope caveat. NOT an autonomous kill
   trigger (we want the run to complete so the analyzer can read what
   structure exists).
3. **Final scientific kill (the headline read).** GD3 still fails
   uniformly (singleton effective rank ≤ 2.0 on ≥ 5 of 6 cells, where
   cell = pair × seed; 2 pairs × 3 seeds = 6 joint cells). That kills
   the rank-one-map-plus-beacons additivity pillar for marker-only
   loss. Reported in the clean-result as a determinative result; the
   next pivot is a different training objective.

## 6. Evaluation

### Measurement validity (inherited from #527, verbatim)

| DV | Construct (what the Goal cares about) | Metric (what is actually computed) | On-distribution? | If proxy: validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DV1 — vector additivity | Whether the joint training shift in activation space at each held-out context equals the sum of the singleton shifts | Per held-out context c, `cos(shift_{A+B}(c), shift_A(c) + shift_B(c))` where each `shift_X(c) = h^{20}_{trained_X}(T_c + R_c) − h^{20}_{base}(T_c + R_c)` at the post-response slot | yes — at the on-policy slot (after R_c, the base model's own greedy response under persona c), at L20 (canonical persona layer, #207) | n/a |
| DV2 — interference structure | How the residual scales with the two sources' mutual base-cosine | Normalized residual + un-normalized raw norm `||shift_{A+B}(c) − (shift_A(c) + shift_B(c))||` | yes | n/a |
| DV3 — magnitude additivity | Whether the behavioral magnitude (log P(marker)) at the joint arm equals the sum of singletons | `ΔG_X(c) = log P(marker | T_c + R_c)_{trained_X} − log P(marker | T_c + R_c)_{base}` at the post-response slot | yes — on-policy, post-response slot, trained − base per marker-leakage-measurement rule | n/a |
| DV4 — emission gate (precondition) | Did the implant actually take | Fraction of greedy completions under each (persona × adapter) where ` ※` appears in the output | yes — direct on-policy emission | n/a |
| DV5 — strength-match diagnostic | Did singleton and joint arms reach comparable source-self implant strength (precondition for reading H1) | Per source × pair, `g_logprob_singleton − g_logprob_joint_at_X` at the median context; band ≤ 1.0 nat AND ≤ 0.1 emission | yes — on-policy at post-response slot | n/a |

All five DVs are on-distribution. At the new band the DV4 emission
gate is potentially diagnostically informative for the FIRST time —
at [5, 12] nat #527 read 0% source emission everywhere (consistent
with the recipe's "below emission onset" prediction), but at [14, 20]
nat the hypothesis explicitly predicts crossing the firing cliff, so
DV4 ≥ 0.5 source emission is the manipulation check that the dial
landed past the cliff.

**DV4 headline-branch reading rule (REVISE round 1 — pre-registered,
lifted from the §8 risk row into the reading rules).** The GD1/GD3
rank-structure read is computed regardless of DV4, but the HEADLINE
narrative branches on the manipulation check:

- **DV4 source emission ≥ 0.5 on a cell** → the firing-cliff crossing
  is confirmed on that cell; the GD1/GD3 read on it speaks to the
  "past emission onset" hypothesis directly.
- **DV4 source emission < 0.5 uniformly across joint cells** → the
  headline reads "a higher log-prob band [14, 20] did / did not change
  the shift-vector rank structure" — the "past emission onset" framing
  is explicitly UNCONFIRMED (the recipe's cliff is not where predicted
  for these sources), and the per-cell EOS margin
  `Δ(z_marker − z_eos)` is reported as the distance-to-emission
  diagnostic. A GD3 kill on DV4-unconfirmed cells does NOT support
  "training past emission onset doesn't help"; it supports only
  "training to [14, 20] nat doesn't help."
- Mixed case → the analyzer reports the GD verdicts split by
  DV4-confirmed vs DV4-unconfirmed cells.

**Marker-slot storage contract (REVISE round 1 — required; closes the
#530 gap at the regime where it matters most).** The parent's shift
JSONs persist only `delta_logp_marker`, `delta_logit_marker`, and the
two argmax-emission rates — `z_eos` and `logZ` raw values are absent,
so the EOS margin (the marker-leakage-measurement rule's PREFERRED
logit readout) is unrecoverable post-hoc on #527's data. #538 trains
INTO the saturation-likely regime, where the rule's three-space
analysis (log-prob primary / logit + EOS-margin secondary /
probability sanity) is exactly the saturation localizer. The
`issue_538` shift-extraction therefore MUST persist, per context per
model side (trained AND base, same HF forward pass), a
`marker_slot_stats` block with the four raw floats of the rule's
storage contract plus the slot index:
`logp_marker`, `z_marker`, `z_eos` (Qwen-2.5-7B `<|im_end|>` id
151645), `logZ`, `slot_index` — alongside the existing deltas and

### Three PRE-REGISTERED gating diagnostics (inherited from #527 + the GD3 generalization)

Same as #527, but GD3's role becomes the headline at the new band:
GD1 + GD3 jointly are the success-criterion gates (see §7).

- **GD1 — joint-shift SVD per cell.** Stack the 19 per-context joint
  shifts into M_joint ∈ ℝ^{19 × 3584}; compute singular values.
  **PASS:** top-1 SV share ≤ 0.75 AND effective rank ≥ 2.0.
- **GD2 — singleton cosine per cell.** Median across 19 held-out
  contexts of `cos(shift_A(c), shift_B(c))`. **PASS:** median ≤ 0.6.
  This is the "parallel singletons" check — at #527 it failed (median
  0.90) despite the base-model L20 centered cos(A,B) ≈ 0; the
  hypothesis is that GD2 passes at the harder dial.
- **GD3 — per-singleton SVD per cell.** For each source (A and B in
  each pair), stack the 19 per-context singleton shifts into M_A and
  M_B ∈ ℝ^{19 × 3584}; compute top-1 SV share + effective rank per
  matrix. **PASS:** effective rank ≥ 2.0 for BOTH singletons. This
  IS the headline test at the new dial point — does per-context
  singleton structure emerge as training pushes past emission onset?

### Thresholds (the success criterion is the new piece)

- **Phase A anchor-smoke PASS** (per §7): on the first source pair at
  seed 42, the band-stop fires at source delta ∈ [14, 20] nat on
  ≥ 2 of 3 cells AND all 4 negative-panel personas stay below
  argmax-emission 0.92 on those cells.
- **H1 success criterion** (the post-#527 graded test): GD3 PASSes
  (singleton eff rank ≥ 2.0 for BOTH singletons) AND GD1 PASSes
  (joint top-1 SV share ≤ 0.75) on ≥ 4 of 6 joint cells (cell = pair
  × seed).
  - **If H1 PASSes:** read DV1 as a determinative superposition test.
    DV1 PASS = median per-context `cos(shift_{A+B}, shift_A + shift_B)
    ≥ 0.85` on ≥ 80% of contexts on the qualifying cells.
  - **If H1 FAILs (the kill):** the rank-1 attractor is loss-objective,
    not training-depth. Headline = "training harder does not rescue
    additivity-cosine as a superposition test."
- **DV3 magnitude additivity:** `|ΔG_{A+B}(c) − [ΔG_A(c) + ΔG_B(c)]|
  ≤ 1.0` nat at median context (per #527; kept). At the harder dial
  this read may be more sensitive than at [5, 12].
- **DV5 strength-match band:** `|g_logprob gap| ≤ 1.0` nat between
  singleton and joint arms at median context. Outside the band ⇒ H1
  verdict is reported conditional on the gap, flagged in the
  clean-result body.

## 7. Decision Gates

**One gate — Phase A anchor-smoke at the new band.** Same shape as
#527's anchor-smoke gate, with the gate threshold sign-corrected for
the new band:

- **Cheap intermediate signal.** Phase A = 3 cells × 1 seed × ~1 GPU-h
  per cell at the harder dial ≈ 3 GPU-h, vs Phase B sweep ≈ 8 GPU-h
  + eval/extract/analysis ≈ 3 GPU-h ≈ 11 GPU-h saved on a kill.
- **Threshold (sign + value grounded — two-part PASS):**
  - **(i) source-band:** `MarkerBandStopCallback` fires with source
    `log P(marker) − base ∈ [14, 20]` nat AND step ≤ 24 epochs ×
    ~30 steps/epoch.
  - **(ii) bystander non-saturation:** at the realized stop step, the
    4 negative-panel personas read by the existing
    `_bystander_headroom_probe` ALL have argmax-emission rate < 0.92
    (the saturation diagnostic from the marker-training-recipe rule).
    log P(marker) trained − base on bystanders is reported but not
    gated (the parent's [5, 12] gate kept it ≤ 12 nat, which would
    be the wrong threshold at [14, 20]; bystander argmax-rate is the
    canonical saturation diagnostic per the recipe).
  - **PASS** = ≥ 2 of 3 cells satisfy BOTH (i) and (ii).
- **No autonomous retry path.** The recipe forbids raising lr past
  5e-6 (the unconditional-repeater collapse risk). The dispatcher's
  existing retry-at-1e-5 logic is the wrong rescue at the new band
  and is suppressed for this task (see §4 Pipeline Delta — step 4
  deleted).
- **Kill action.** Post `epm:failure v1`
  `failure_class=data, reason=anchor_floor_or_ceiling_at_new_band`.
  Surface to the orchestrator with a one-line interpretation:
  "EITHER the implant cannot reach 14 nats at lr=5e-6 within 24
  epochs (ceiling-below-target), OR it can but bystanders saturated
  at the new dial (predicted by the recipe)." Both kills are
  scientifically informative — they say the recipe rule's [14, 20]
  band is not reachable in the marker-only/lr≤5e-6/attn-only
  regime, which itself updates the rule.

### Sign + value grounding

- Band [14, 20] is the dial point past the firing cliff
  (~step 60-100 per the recipe) but below the saturation ceiling
  (source-self ΔG ~20-30 nat); the parent's observed source delta of
  5-7.5 nat at step 30-40 gives a ramp-rate prior of ~0.07-0.25
  nat/step (empirical smoke slope ~0.07-0.20), so reaching 14-20 nat needs roughly step 90-160 — well
  inside the 24-epoch cap (~600-720 steps).
- The 0.92 bystander argmax cap comes from the marker-training-recipe
  rule's saturation diagnostic ("argmax-emission ≥ ~0.92 on bystanders").
- A gate that requires the band to fire AND bystanders to stay
  graded is jointly satisfiable: the recipe predicts the firing cliff
  crosses ~step 60-100 with bystander argmax still gated by the
  contrastive-negatives panel (the panel's job is to slow bystander
  ramp relative to source). If both happen, the cells are
  interpretable. If neither happens, the kill is informative.

### Self-check (jointly satisfiable)

PASS requires source delta ∈ [14, 20] AND bystander argmax < 0.92.
These are NOT contradictory — the recipe explicitly predicts a
non-empty regime where source is past the firing cliff while
bystanders remain non-saturated (it's the regime the contrastive-
negatives panel is designed to create). The pre-registered gating
diagnostics (GD1, GD2, GD3) gate the *interpretation* of DV1, not
the training, so the run completes whether they pass or fail.

### Stratification spec (if compute deviates > 2×)

Priority order for auto-descope: **drop seeds first → drop the second
pair → drop one arm**. Min-N per dimension to preserve statistical
power:
1. **Seeds:** min 2 of 3 seeds (per #527's standing 3-seed
   requirement; the success criterion is ≥ 4 of 6 joint cells, which
   at 2 seeds becomes ≥ 3 of 4 — a still-resolvable threshold but a
   weaker robustness claim).
2. **Pairs:** min 1 of 2 pairs (the success criterion at 1 pair × 3
   seeds = 3 joint cells is ≥ 2 of 3 — borderline; the analyzer
   reports a per-pair caveat if pair 2 is dropped).
3. **Arms:** never drop (the additivity prediction needs A_only +
   B_only + joint per pair).

If neither (1) nor (2) keeps the projected wall ≤ 1.5× of plan,
escalate via the orchestrator's
`gates.conditional.compute_deviation_resolution`.

  **Why:** Canonical band-stop recipe; the parent's recipe with no
  modification. At the new band the marker-training-recipe rule
  explicitly forbids raising lr (the rule says "buy strength through
  epochs at low LR (≤5e-6), never through LR" — lr=1e-4 is the
  unconditional-repeater collapse regime, #397/#451). So at [14, 20]
  the strength is bought through MORE EPOCHS, not higher lr.
  **Source:** `#527` (which sourced

  5e-6, AND the parent's autonomous retry rung at 1e-5 is suppressed
  for this task because the recipe says raising lr at the new band is
  the wrong rescue).

- **What:** **Epochs cap = 24** (the instrumental change vs #527's 8).
  **Why:** The cap exists only as a hard upper bound so that
  `MarkerBandStopCallback` has enough training steps to reach the new
  [14, 20] band before the cap fires. The parent's cap of 8 was set
  for the [5, 12] band, where the band-stop fired in <1 epoch (step
  30-40). At the new band, the marker-training-recipe rule's "buy
  strength through epochs at low LR" principle says the correct lever
  is epochs, and at the parent's observed ramp slope (~0.07-0.25
  nat/step (empirical smoke slope ~0.07-0.20)) reaching 14-20 nat needs roughly step 90-160 — well under
  24 epochs × ~30 steps/epoch ≈ 720 steps. 24 is the minimum cap that
  comfortably accommodates the upper-end step projection (~130) with
  ~5× headroom against ramp-slowdown variance across seeds (the
  recipe explicitly cites #519 seed jitter on step counts).
  **Source:** `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` § "Don't fix
  epochs — stop on the log-prob band" + `#527`'s observed ramp slope
  (5.00-7.47 nats source delta at step 30-40 = ~0.07-0.25 nat/step (Phase 1.5: the smoke-data empirical slope is ~0.07-0.20; 0.25 is the optimistic end);
  Reproducibility table in `tasks/awaiting_promotion/527/body.md`).
  **Alternatives:** Epochs cap = 16 (REJECTED — too tight against the
  upper-end step projection of ~130 if the ramp slope flattens above
  the firing cliff; the recipe says the slope can change at emission
  onset). Epochs cap = 48 (REJECTED — gives more headroom but adds
  ~50% to the per-cell wall budget; 24 with the existing band-stop
  callback is sufficient if the recipe's slope model holds). The
  marker-training-recipe rule's principle is "stop on the metric, not
  a fixed step count" — the cap is a backstop and 24 is the smallest
  backstop that leaves the band reachable with margin.

- **What:** **`marker_band_low_nats=14.0, marker_band_high_nats=20.0`**
  (the single experimental variable).
  **Why:** The dial-point past the firing cliff but below the
  saturation ceiling.
  - **Past the firing cliff:** the marker-training-recipe rule names
    three ordered events — (1) log-prob ramp from step 5, (2) emission
    onset when log P(marker) overtakes EOS at the end slot
    (~step 60-100), (3) saturation (~step 600). The parent ran the
    band-stop in [5, 12] which fired at step 30-40 — strictly BEFORE
    the firing cliff. The hypothesis (and the recipe's prediction)
    is that AT or PAST the firing cliff the model must differentiate
    which contexts emit the marker, so per-context structure should
    grow. [14, 20] sits above the [5, 12] window by enough margin
    (the lower bound 14 nat is 2 nat above the parent's upper bound
    12 nat) to ensure the dial sits past the cliff in expectation.

    diagnostic as source-self ΔG ~20-30 nat with bystander argmax

    saturation range, deliberately conservative — we want to land
    past the cliff but NOT saturated. Bystander resolution is the
    gate (per the recipe), not source emission, so the Phase A check
    on bystander argmax-rate < 0.92 is the operational
    saturation-avoidance gate.

    jumped over by the callback's `eval_every_steps=10` cadence. At
