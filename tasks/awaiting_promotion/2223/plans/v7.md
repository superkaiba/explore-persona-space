### 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (fidelity manipulation check).** The re-extracted 32B answer axis (paper
  pipeline, score=3 filter, diff-of-means) reproduces the published Lu axis:
  cos ≥ 0.90 at the band layers (46–53), ≥ 0.71 at the middle layer 32.
  The 0.71 is a DESIGN-CHOICE floor borrowed from the paper's
  cos(role-PC1, contrast axis) > 0.71 at the middle layer (§3.1/§16.1) — a
  related but different quantity from cos(re-extracted axis, published axis);
  plausibly lenient for two same-recipe contrast axes, so the kill criterion
  is unlikely to false-fire, and it is not presented as a paper-measured floor
  for this exact comparison. A low cosine falsifies the pipeline
  reproduction and gates interpretation of LEG A/B (report, do not silently
  proceed).
- **H2 (does fidelity/preimage change the parent finding).** Predicted: NO —
  the faithful-native and preimage context-end arms still track unsteered at
  every strength (their per-arm average harm within the unsteered anchor's
  3-seed decode band), while `cap_alltoken` sits far below. This CONFIRMS the
  parent's "position, not strength/direction, determines suppression."
- **H2-falsifier (screening + confirmation).** A faithful-native OR preimage
  context-end arm whose seed-42 mean-harm drop reaches ≥ 50% of the
  (unsteered − cap_alltoken) gap is a CROSSING CANDIDATE (screening only —
  never an overturn by itself). The overturn requires CONFIRMATION: that
  arm's crossing cells re-run at seeds 43 and 44, with the confirmation-seed
  mean drop still ≥ 50% of the gap, outside the anchor band, and coherence
  within 10 points of the 3-seed unsteered coherence reference (not a
  degeneration artifact). A non-replicating crossing is recorded as
  seed-specific → Inconclusive. A drop bought only by coherence collapse
  (coherence < ~50) is NOT a suppression (the parent's coherence guardrail).
  The non-crossing branch is reported as "no change observed in the
  fixed-seed case study", never as evidence of absence.
- **H3 (geometry).** cos(v_ctx_preimage, v_ctx_faithful) and
  cos(v_ctx_preimage, current teacher-forced ctx_native) reported per band
  layer; the map's held-out R² > its identity+bias baseline R² (else the map is
  not learning context→answer structure beyond a constant shift).

**Lattice definitions (registered; decision grain = (scenario, layer_config,
arm)):** band_pass = cos(re-extracted answer axis, published Lu axis) ≥ 0.90
at EVERY band layer l ∈ 46…53 (the ALL quantifier is pinned — pass AND kill
predicates both use it); l32_pass = layer-32 cos ≥ 0.71. U and C = the 3-seed
mean harm of unsteered and cap_alltoken (trajectory-level means over the
matched common alive-turn set, seeds {42,43,44}); gap = U − C per
(scenario, layer_config); band_w = the anchor seed band (max − min of
seed-level trajectory means per anchor arm, the wider of the two anchors) —
an ANCHOR-SPECIFIC spread read: an extrapolation to intervention arms, not a
bound. Gap precondition: gap > 0 AND gap > band_w; when it fails, every H2
read in that scenario × layer_config is Inconclusive. drop(arm) is computed
on the arm/unsteered/cap_alltoken COMMON alive-turn intersection, with the
candidate arm's empty-response positions EXCLUDED from the harm DECISION
statistic (empties reported separately as degeneration counts; coherence
keeps 0 for empties; the stored per-turn data keeps the parent's harm=0
scoring so the sensitivity read is a pure re-reduction). Coherence reference
= the 3-seed unsteered mean coherence on the same matched set. Preimage-family
arms are verdict-ELIGIBLE only when the map-validity read passes (held-out
pooled R² > the identity+bias baseline R² at the arm's layers); a failed map
routes preimage crossings to Inconclusive (faithful-native unaffected).

axis fidelity. (The former "attrition below the turn floor" clause is
