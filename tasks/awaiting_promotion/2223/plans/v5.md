  harm when applied only at the context position — matching the parent finding
  that only whole-conversation ("all-token") capping works. There's a real
  chance the faithfully-built direction does better than the sloppy one.
- **What would change my mind.** If a context-only cap on the faithful or
  preimage direction pushes harm down toward the all-token level (and coherence
  stays intact), that overturns "position, not the direction, is what matters."

### 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (fidelity manipulation check).** The re-extracted 32B answer axis (paper
  pipeline, score=3 filter, diff-of-means) reproduces the published Lu axis:
  cos ≥ 0.90 at the band layers (46–53), ≥ 0.71 at the middle layer 32
  (paper's own PC1↔contrast floor). A low cosine falsifies the pipeline
  reproduction and gates interpretation of LEG A/B (report, do not silently
  proceed).
- **H2 (does fidelity/preimage change the parent finding).** Predicted: NO —
  the faithful-native and preimage context-end arms still track unsteered at
  every strength (their per-arm average harm within the unsteered anchor's
  3-seed decode band), while `cap_alltoken` sits far below. This CONFIRMS the
  parent's "position, not strength/direction, determines suppression."
- **H2-falsifier.** A faithful-native OR preimage context-end cap arm whose
  per-arm average harm drops by ≥ the (unsteered − cap_alltoken) gap's ~50%,
  with coherence within 10 points of unsteered (not a degeneration artifact),
  overturns the parent finding for that axis family. A drop bought only by
  coherence collapse (coherence < ~50) is NOT a suppression (the parent's
  coherence guardrail).
- **H3 (geometry).** cos(v_ctx_preimage, v_ctx_faithful) and
  cos(v_ctx_preimage, current teacher-forced ctx_native) reported per band
  layer; the map's held-out R² > its identity+bias baseline R² (else the map is
  not learning context→answer structure beyond a constant shift).

**Registered verdict lattice (verify_plan check 20):** DISJOINT and exhaustive: Reproduced-and-unchanged ⇔ band cos ≥ 0.90 AND layer-32 cos ≥ 0.71 AND every faithful/preimage context-end arm's mean-harm drop vs unsteered < 50% of the unsteered−cap_alltoken gap; Fidelity-changes-it ⇔ band cos ≥ 0.90 AND layer-32 cos ≥ 0.71 AND ≥ 1 faithful/preimage context-end arm's mean-harm drop ≥ 50% of the unsteered−cap_alltoken gap with that arm's coherence ≥ unsteered − 10 points; Pipeline-fidelity-fail ⇔ band cos < 0.90 AND layer-32 cos < 0.71; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise (mixed cosine floors, a harm drop bought by coherence collapse, or attrition below the turn floor).
