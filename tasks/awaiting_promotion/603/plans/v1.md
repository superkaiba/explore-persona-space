# Plan — Task #603: P3′ write decomposition (does the source's base prior predict the write's common-mode fraction rather than its norm?)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll take 21 adapters we already trained (each one teaches a single persona a behavior: an invented courthouse fact, refusing harmless questions, or giving misaligned answers) and measure the internal push each adapter wrote into the model. For every adapter I read that push twice: once under the trained persona itself, and once under each of ~23 other personas. No new training, one short measurement pod, then the math runs on the VM.
- **What I expect:** for teacher personas that already half-knew the behavior before training, I expect the push at the teacher to point away from the shared direction all the other personas get moved along, while the overall size of that push stays roughly flat. That is the theory note's explanation for the odd result where the most fact-fluent teachers stated the implant the most yet leaked it the least.
- **What would change my mind:** if the push gets smaller with the teacher's head start but keeps pointing the same way, the simpler "smaller write" story wins. If neither the direction nor the size tracks the head start, the P3′ row in the model note gets a negative entry and the "not weaker, narrower" result needs a different mechanism.

## 1. Goal

Test prediction P3′ of the rank-1 leakage model (`docs/notes/rank1_leakage_model.tex` §Predictions): if a source context already carries a behavior's shared component, training writes mostly the source-specific residual — so the source's base prior on the behavior should predict the **direction composition** of the measured write (its common-mode fraction: projection onto the mean-bystander shift direction, predicted **negative**), and should **not** predict the write's norm. The deliverable is the CONTRAST between the two regressions (common-mode-fraction-on-prior vs norm-on-prior), per behavior family, with #541's three-teacher prior span as the primary axis. This is the mechanism-level test of #541's behavioral observation that high-prior teachers express the implant most on themselves (97/94% self-assertion vs 72% for the low-prior teacher) while leaking least ("not weaker, narrower") — a configuration the magnitude-only P3 reading cannot produce.

## 3. Hypothesis

Let `CMF_a` = signed cosine of adapter *a*'s source-context shift against the mean-bystander shift direction, and `‖w_a‖` its norm (definitions in §4).

- **H1 (P3′, direction):** within each behavior family, source prior negatively predicts CMF. Fact family: the per-seed teacher ordering of CMF is marine > historian > carpenter (low prior → high common-mode fraction) in 3/3 seeds (exact p = (1/6)³ ≈ 0.0046 against seed-noise; the "≥2/3 seeds" directional tail is 16/216 ≈ 0.074). Refusal/EM: Spearman ρ(prior, CMF) < 0 with exact permutation p < 0.05 (n=6, one-sided).
- **H2 (P3′, norm):** source prior does **not** predict ‖w_a‖, operationalized by the SAME two binding reads as H1 (critique round 1 fix — the prior wording "≤1/3 seeds matching **any** fixed ordering" was miscalibrated: it false-fails with probability 1 − (6·5·4)/6³ = 96/216 ≈ 0.444 under a true norm-null, and ≈ 1 under any stable non-prior teacher effect on norm): (i) the **specific prior-monotone ordering test applied to norm** (marine > historian > carpenter on ‖w‖; H2 expects ≤1/3 seeds, i.e. the test FAILS), and (ii) the **joint-permutation contrast Δ = |ρ_CMF| − |ρ_norm| > 0** per family. A stable norm ordering that is NOT the prior-monotone one (e.g. driven by response length or teacher prompt style) is consistent with H2 and does not count against it.
- **Falsification of P3′:** CMF shows no consistent negative prior relation in the fact family (≤1/3 seeds with the predicted ordering AND pooled refusal/EM ρ ≥ 0). If instead **norm** shows the consistent prior-monotone negative relation (the same specific-ordering test, applied to norm, at ≥2/3 seeds) and CMF does not, the magnitude-only P3 reading is supported over P3′ — also a determinate, publishable outcome.
- **Confound guard A — noise attenuation (pre-registered, fully specified):** raw CMF is the binding H1 statistic by default. The guard TRIGGERS when the cluster-level sign of reliability-vs-prior matches the sign CMF-vs-prior shows (at 3 prior levels this is a sign/ordering read on the teacher-level medians of r_a and r_u — qualitative by design, stated as such). When triggered, H1 counts as supported ONLY if BOTH the raw AND the disattenuated CMF orderings hold (both-must-hold rule; disagreement between them = "supported with an attenuation caveat" is NOT available — the outcome is reported as indeterminate on H1). Disattenuation specifics: CMF_dis = CMF / √(r_a·r_u); any cell with split-half r_a or r_u < 0.3 (reliability floor) is flagged and excluded from the disattenuated read (raw read keeps all cells); per-cell r_a, r_u ship in the results JSON; |CMF_dis| > 1 is truncation-reported, never silently clipped.
- **Confound guard B — text-composition / expression stratification (pre-registered; critique round 1 addition):** the `same`-text variant reads shifts on the trained model's own greedy text, and source responses assert the implant at a rate co-monotone with the prior IV (#541: 97/94/72%) while bystander text is mostly clean — so a negative prior→CMF gradient could arise from response-content composition alone. The named diagnostic: persist per-(persona, question) response texts (§4 step 5), label source-side responses for behavior expression (fact: #541's assertion-eval recipe; refusal/EM: Claude judge), then (i) recompute CMF on assertion-present vs assertion-absent per-question shifts within cell, and (ii) check whether the cross-cell CMF-prior gradient survives conditioning on expression. Power caveat stated up front: the within-cell split is thin exactly at high-expression cells (97% ⇒ ~0-1 non-expressing responses of 20); the EM family's mid-range expression is where the stratified read has real resolution. A gradient that vanishes under expression conditioning is reported as the text-channel alternative winning, not as P3′ support.

Attribution caveat fixed in advance: with 3 teachers (fact) and 6 sources (refusal/EM), "prior predicts" means "prior, or any teacher/source property monotone in prior, predicts" — cluster-level exact significance is bounded at 1/6 (fact) and 1/720 (refusal/EM). Max confidence MODERATE.

```python
def decompose_write(shifts: dict[str, Tensor], source: str, key: str = "delta_v_mean_resp"):
    """shifts: persona -> entry dict from extract_per_context_shifts(); returns dict."""
    w = shifts[source][key].double()                       # (H,) source write estimate
    bys = [p for p in shifts if p != source]
    M = torch.stack([shifts[p][key].double() for p in bys])  # (n_bys, H)
    m = M.mean(dim=0); u = m / m.norm()                    # mean-bystander direction (leave-source-out by construction)
    proj = torch.dot(w, u)
    return {
        "cmf": (proj / w.norm()).item(),                   # PRIMARY DV: signed cosine in [-1, 1]
        "norm": w.norm().item(),                           # CONTRAST DV
        "shared_norm": proj.item(),                        # ||c_a|| signed
        "residual_norm": (w - proj * u).norm().item(),
        "u_vector_sha": ..., "n_bystanders": len(bys),
        # robustness variants, same call:
        "cmf_svd": _cos_to_top_singular(M, w),             # norm-weighted SVD direction
        "cmf_svd_unitnorm": _cos_to_top_singular(M / M.norm(dim=1, keepdim=True), w),  # #551 lesson
        "cmf_jackknife": [...],                            # leave-one-bystander-out
    }
```

- *Refusal / EM families:* one-sided Spearman ρ(prior, CMF), exact permutation over all 720 source-label assignments; same for norm; EM repeats with villain dropped (n=5, leverage check); refusal prior-variance gate — the 6 log-prob priors must span > 2× their pooled measurement SE, else the family is declared non-diagnostic (reported, not silently dropped).
- *Pooled extension evidence — defined:* "pooled refusal/EM ρ" = Stouffer's Z-combination of the two families' one-sided exact permutation p's (CMF and norm pooled separately). Descriptive support only, never the headline (families share the base model; refusal/EM share panel + questions, so independence fails). If a family is declared non-diagnostic (refusal variance gate, or <4 surviving sources), the pooled read collapses to the surviving family ALONE and every downstream cell that mentions "extensions" is evaluated against that reduced denominator, stated explicitly in the write-up (no silent denominator change).
- *Contrast statistic:* per family, observed Δ = |ρ_CMF| − |ρ_norm| against the joint permutation distribution (prior labels permuted once per draw, both ρ's recomputed — preserves the CMF/norm coupling under the null). Fact family: the permutation unit is the TEACHER (6 enumerated assignments, min p = 1/6, descriptive) — never free permutation across the 9 cells (seeds cluster within teacher).
- *Norm-floor handling (critique round 1 fix — the exclusion must not condition on a DV under test):* the PRIMARY norm-on-prior regression uses ALL cells, always (‖w‖ is itself the rival outcome; excluding low-norm cells could preferentially remove high-prior cells exactly when magnitude-only P3 is true). The norm-floor exclusion (cells with ‖w‖ below the split-half noise floor) applies ONLY as a sensitivity re-read of the CMF regression (a direction estimate on a below-noise write is meaningless). Both reads report exclusion counts AND the prior-correlation of the excluded set; prior-correlated norm-floor failures are themselves reported as evidence on the norm hypothesis, never silently filtered.
- *Decision lattice fixed in advance (explicit mapping — critique round 1 fix):* the fact family's H1 state is keyed to seed count k = #(seeds showing the predicted CMF ordering): k=3 ⇒ CMF− (p=1/216); k=2 ⇒ CMF-directional (tail p≈0.074); k≤1 ⇒ CMF-null. The norm state is keyed to the SAME specific-ordering test on norm + the Δ contrast (H2 above). The headline cell is then:
  | Fact CMF state | Fact norm state | Headline |
  |---|---|---|
  | CMF− (k=3, guards A+B pass) | norm-null (≤1/3 prior-monotone) AND Δ>0 | **P3′ supported** (extensions modulate confidence: concordant ⇒ MODERATE; discordant/non-diagnostic ⇒ MODERATE−, stated) |
  | CMF-directional (k=2, guards pass) | norm-null AND Δ>0 | **P3′ directionally supported** (LOW-MODERATE; extensions can lift to MODERATE only if both concordant) |
  | CMF-null (k≤1) | norm− (≥2/3 prior-monotone on norm) | **magnitude-only P3 supported** |
  | CMF− or directional | norm− | **prior shapes size AND direction** (both channels real) |
  | CMF-null (k≤1) | norm-null | **write geometry not prior-tracked at these doses** (determinate negative for the P3′ row) |
  | any CMF state with guard A or B FAILING | any | **indeterminate on H1** — the artifact channel (noise attenuation or text composition) is reported as the live alternative; no P3′-supported claim |
  Mixed fact-vs-extension outcomes never flip the headline cell (fact is primary); they move confidence and are reported per family. The k=2 row plus the guard-failure row close the previously-unmapped cells (k=2 "directional", {k≤1, pooled extension ρ<0} ⇒ falls in the CMF-null rows by its fact state).

## 11. Decision Rationale

N/A — no model training (kind: analysis). Load-bearing **analysis** choices:

- **Layer 14 primary, {7, 21} sensitivity.** Why: the established shift-readout layer of this artifact line; per-question tensors only exist there in the rig. Source: #551 (headline + free extra-layer keys). Alternatives: L19–24 (marker gate optimum, #502) — rejected: no marker DV here, and cross-task comparability with #521/#551/#604 binds tighter; the L7/L21 sensitivity read covers locality.
- **Mean-over-response primary, end-slot secondary.** Why: sharper and more stable on behavior-bearing reads. Source: #551 ("Reading the whole response sharpens the contrast", 0.963→0.982 etc.). Alternatives: end-slot only (parent #519/#521 default) — kept as secondary.
- **`same`-text variant only.** Why: #551's headline regime; isolates the weight-change effect on identical input; on base text the implant never fires and source-specific structure washes out (#551: marker on base text behaves like EM, rotation ≥0.999). Source: #551. Alternatives: `base`/`on_policy` variants (+12 GPU-h, known confound for `on_policy`) — rejected for budget; named as a follow-up if the headline is surprising.
- **Mean-bystander direction as the shared-component estimator.** Why: P3′ names it verbatim ("shared / mean-bystander shift direction"); the mean is not norm-weighted. Source: `rank1_leakage_model.tex` P3′; #551's unit-norm lesson motivates carrying both SVD variants as robustness only.
- **Signed cosine as the common-mode fraction.** Why: scale-free (decouples the two regression DVs by construction), sign-preserving (an anti-aligned write is information, not noise). Alternatives: energy fraction cos² (loses sign — kept as a stored column), absolute shared-component norm (entangles with ‖w‖ — stored, not headline).
- **Prior metric = length-normalized teacher-forced log P of positive completions under the source persona.** Why: the validated, continuous metric of the parent line; identical recipe extended to #518's pools. Source: #541 (Prior metric row; `prior_screen.json`), #444/#500. Alternatives: behavioral base rate — secondary only (refusal floor 0–2.8% has no usable variance; EM rate kept as the secondary axis where it is wide).
- **Probe sets per family (fact: 20 A-family courthouse probes; refusal/EM: the #551 20 generic questions).** Why: the write must be read where the behavior computes — the fact behavior is topic-gated, refusal/EM are topic-general (and #551's generic-question EM read produced cos 0.96–0.98). Source: #541 geometry follow-up (A-family slice rule), #551 (questions.json). Alternatives: one shared probe set for all families — rejected: would read the fact write in a regime where it never expresses.
- **Rig generation stays HF in-memory (not vLLM).** Why: generation is interleaved with activation capture per probe in a validated rig whose reproduction gate returned exact zeros; the volume (~10k × ≤512 tokens) is inside the #551-scaled budget. Source: #551 (same deviation, accepted). Flip condition: smoke shows >15 s/question-cell → batch generations inside the worker (allowed deviation).
- **Exact small-n tests (seed-ordering, enumerated permutations) instead of OLS p-values.** Why: n = 9/6/6 with 3/6/6 exchangeable units; asymptotic p's would be fiction. Source: #541 (rank statistics + directional 3-arm reporting), #551 (exact permutation at n=14), planner memory (collinearity-gate pattern's small-n discipline).
- **Per-question response texts persisted with the tensors (guard B instrumentation).** Why: the inherited rig discards generated texts, making the expression-stratified read permanently unrunnable post-pod (the #521 lost-controls class); persisting them is ~10 MB, numerics-neutral, and is the only route to weighing the text-composition alternative. Source: critique round 1 (methodology + alternatives Must-Fix, both reconciler-upheld); #521 incident.
- **H2 keyed to the specific prior-monotone ordering + Δ contrast, not "any fixed ordering".** Why: the any-ordering criterion false-fails at 96/216 ≈ 0.444 under a true norm-null and ≈ 1 under any stable non-prior teacher effect, making the "P3′ supported" cell near-unreachable. Source: critique round 1 (statistics lens, Claude + Codex convergent).
- **Raw CMF binding by default; both-must-hold under a fired guard A; reliability floor r ≥ 0.3 for the disattenuated read.** Why: disattenuation explodes at small r and must not decide H1 alone post-hoc; the floor + both-must-hold rule fixes the binding statistic in advance. Source: critique round 1 (statistics lens Must-Fix 4).
- **Model id:** not repo-new (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` is the project default) — no `AutoConfig` smoke needed.
