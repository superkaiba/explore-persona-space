### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

## 1. Goal

"Determine whether the answer vector predicted from the eval-time context via a fitted linear context→answer map predicts which eval-time prompts re-elicit inoculation-suppressed behavior (EM and capitalization, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct), and whether it outperforms the context-side hidden-state predictors (Train Ref., Same-Q Inoc.) and text-embedding baselines of Kwon et al. (ICML 2026 Mech Interp workshop)." (Task #2379 Goal, verbatim.)

The parent paper showed *context-side* activation similarity predicts which eval-time system prompts re-elicit a behavior that inoculation prompting suppressed, beating text similarity. This experiment sharpens that claim into a test of *which activation geometry carries the signal*: the pre-response context state itself, or the answer state the context is about to induce (read out before any generation via the project's fitted linear context→answer map M). A win for the answer side would connect the re-elicitation phenomenon directly to the leakage-theory line's central object (the context→answer map) and give a pre-generation predictor with a mechanistic story; a loss localizes the parent's signal to context-state geometry that the map does not preserve.

Replication component: the first deliverable is a faithful reproduction of the parent's Qwen2.5-7B rig (per `.claude/rules/replication-fidelity.md`), changing only the judge model (forced by project policy, with a κ-calibration control) and the caps-language roster (3 of 4 languages; Italian dataset not publicly released — §12).

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (replication / manipulation check):** On our rig, context-side Train Ref. similarity at the parent's pinned layers (L16 EM, L27 caps) correlates with per-trigger re-elicitation at mean within-condition ρ ≥ 0.6 in both settings, and beats BGE in both (parent: 0.89/0.90 vs 0.67/−0.24).

**H2 (primary):** The **Train-Ref predicted-answer** predictor — cos(v̂_A(q,t), μ^A_train) with v̂_A = M_D v_C(q,t) under the inoculated model's own map (Arm map-I), averaged over Q_sim, read at the parent's pinned layer — correlates with re-elicitation at least as strongly as the context-side Train Ref.: pooled mean Δρ̄ = mean over conditions of (ρ_answer − ρ_context) ≥ 0. The directional prediction (the interesting one) is Δρ̄ > 0.

**H3 (ceiling):** The actual-answer-vector ceiling (v_A from real rollouts) correlates at least as strongly as the predicted version; the gap (ceiling − predicted) measures what the linear map loses.

**Falsification:** H2 is falsified for a setting if Δρ̄ < 0 with the 95% paired trigger-bootstrap CI excluding 0 (context side retains more signal than the mapped answer state). H3's diagnostic split: ceiling high + predicted low ⇒ map bottleneck; ceiling low ⇒ answer-state geometry itself doesn't carry trigger strength.

**Registered verdict lattice (per setting — EM and caps each get one verdict):**
DISJOINT and exhaustive: Replication-failed ⇔ the manipulation check fails (mean within-condition context-side Train-Ref ρ < 0.4, or the inoculation-install check of §7 fails); Answer-side outperforms ⇔ manipulation check passes AND pooled mean Δρ̄ > 0 with the 95% paired trigger-bootstrap CI excluding 0; Context-side retains more signal ⇔ manipulation check passes AND pooled mean Δρ̄ < 0 with the 95% CI excluding 0; Comparable ⇔ otherwise.

**Registered paired contrast** (answer-side Train-Ref predicted-answer vs context-side Train Ref., Spearman over triggers, per condition):
Row-coverage: answer-side arm = 150/150 trigger×condition cells (5 EM datasets × 18 triggers + 3 caps languages × 20 triggers), computed from the P3 capture set; context-side arm = 150/150 of the same cells from the same captures — every cell present in both arms by construction (the two predictors are two functionals of one captured v_C grid).

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Notation and vector conventions (glossary-conformant)

- **v_C (context vector, last-prompt-token):** residual-stream state at the final prompt token (the last token of the assistant generation header, `add_generation_prompt=True`), per layer (Qwen2.5-7B: 28 layers × 3584). This is EXACTLY the parent's h(q,t,L) position ("the final prompt token before any assistant response tokens").
- **v_A (answer vector):** mean residual-stream state over the answer's tokens, per layer.
- **M (context map):** per-layer linear map v_A ≈ M v_C, ridge-fit per the #779/#2254 recipe.
- Pooling declared per vector in §6 (pooling-convention row). No span-mean anywhere in this plan.

## 7. Decision Gates

Threshold grounding: parent's caps Train Ref ρ = 0.90 (Fig 1b); 0.4 sits far below the effect but above what a broken extraction position or template mismatch produces (sign-flip or ≈0, cf. the parent's own BGE at −0.24); the gate quantity involves no null draw (no calibration pilot needed). §4-mechanism binding: computed by `scripts/issue2379_analysis.py --gate-caps` on the P3 caps captures + P2 caps rates; compares one scalar against 0.4.
**Jointly satisfiable:** trivially (one gate). No other gates — the remaining phases are cheap relative to their information value, and the EM manipulation check (same computation once P6 lands) gates only INTERPRETATION (verdict lattice "Replication-failed"), not spend.

**Success criteria (quantitative):** H1 thresholds (§3); primary comparison resolved per the registered verdict lattice; all 10 maps report the full baselines pair; κ (if run) ≥ 0.6 between judges on the EM binary (below → judge-swap caveat elevated in the write-up).
