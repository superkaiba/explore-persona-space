# Plan — Issue #2222: Persona Vectors screening predictors — mapped context→answer ΔP vs raw / exact / prompt-token on the synthetic suite

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The Persona Vectors paper has a cheap trick for spotting, *before* you finetune, which training data will make a model turn evil / sycophantic / prone to making things up. Their best version needs the model to first write its own answer to every training prompt (expensive). I'll reproduce their comparison on the paper's own 24 datasets, then test our shortcut: instead of generating those answers, *predict* what the model would have said from a cheap read of the prompt, using a map we already trained on generic chat data. If the shortcut works, we get the expensive method's accuracy for the price of the cheap one.
- **What I expect:** The shortcut roughly matches the expensive method across all three traits, and specifically fixes sycophancy — where the paper's own cheap trick drops from a correlation of about 0.88 down to 0.58. I expect our mapped version to claw most of that back.
- **What would change my mind:** If our mapped shortcut is no better than the paper's simplest cheap trick (just reading the prompt's last word), the map is adding nothing and the honest answer is "the shortcut doesn't help." I'd also be surprised — and would dig in — if the paper's own numbers don't reproduce on the banked data.

## 1. Goal

> "Reproduce the Persona Vectors (arXiv 2507.21509) data-screening predictor comparison (raw projection / exact projection difference / last-prompt-token approximation, their Figures 8/21/23) on the 24-dataset synthetic suite and test whether a FROZEN trait-agnostic context→answer mapped projection difference recovers exact-ΔP predictivity — especially closing the sycophancy gap (r 0.581 → ~0.88) — at forward-pass cost." *(Task #2222 Goal, verbatim.)*

Persona Vectors §6.1 / App. F / App. I introduce a pre-finetuning data-screening signal — the **projection difference** ΔP: a training dataset's responses, projected onto the trait's persona direction, minus the projection of the base model's own natural responses to the same prompts. ΔP correlates strongly with post-finetuning trait expression (their Figure 8), but the "exact" form needs one base generation per training prompt. The paper offers a forward-pass-only approximation (last-prompt-token projection, App. I) that works for evil/hallucination but **degrades badly for sycophancy** (r 0.581 vs exact 0.879). This experiment reproduces the three published predictors on the paper's 24-dataset synthetic suite and adds a fourth: replace the base-generation projection with `M(v_C(x))`, the answer representation predicted from the context vector by a **frozen, trait-agnostic** context→answer map — testing whether a learned map beats the paper's implicit identity-map assumption exactly where that assumption is known to fail.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (headline — sycophancy gap):** the mapped-ΔP (context arm) predictor closes the paper's sycophancy gap. Primary quantity `Δr_syc = r(MappedΔP_ctx, y_syc) − r(PromptΔP, y_syc)` over the 24 datasets.
**H2 (equivalence):** mapped ΔP recovers exact-ΔP predictivity across all three traits — `r(MappedΔP_ctx, y_T) ≥ r(ExactΔP, y_T) − 0.10` for T ∈ {evil, syc, halluc}.
**H3 (paper reproduction):** the three published predictors reproduce on the banked data — each arm's r at the paper's steering layer lands within ±0.10 of the published Qwen value.
**H4 (prefix arm):** the prefix-based mapped arm is degenerate/weak (predicted from #1739's "the prefix-to-answer map never learns"); run and report, do not headline.

Confirmation of H1 = the mapped map beats the paper's identity assumption where it fails; falsification = the map adds nothing over reading the prompt directly.

**DISJOINT and exhaustive: Confirmed ⇔ Δr_syc > 0 AND Δr_syc's 95% bootstrap CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Falsified ⇔ Δr_syc's 95% bootstrap CI is wholly below 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.**

**Registered paired contrast:** the arm-vs-arm correlation differences (H1 `MappedΔP_ctx` vs `PromptΔP`; H2 `MappedΔP_ctx` vs `ExactΔP`) are paired across the same 24 datasets. **Row-coverage: both arms' 24 registered dataset-level values are supplied per-context by the same capture store `issue2222_pvscreen/analysis_tensors/capture/<dataset>/summaries.npz` (all predictor stand-ins per row) reduced to per-dataset means, joined to the y-axis `eval_results/issue_778/finetune_<trait>_<family>_<version>.json`; the plan's own Phase-3 reduction produces every registered row on both arms.**

### Predictor definitions (all per-layer ℓ; v̂_ℓ = r_B[T][ℓ] / ‖r_B[T][ℓ]‖)

Per row i in a dataset D, all activation summaries from ONE teacher-forced forward pass except the base-generation summary:
- `a_ℓ(x_i, y_i^train)` = response-avg activation of the **training response** (the `dataset.zip` assistant message).
- `a_ℓ(x_i, y_i^base)` = response-avg activation of the **base model's own generation** to prompt x_i (Phase 2).
- `ctxend_ℓ(x_i)` = last-prompt-token activation at position `prompt_len − 1` of the chat-templated prompt **including the generation-prompt header**. **Position identity (`plast ≡ ctxend`, paper-faithful):** the paper's released code (`eval/cal_projection.py`) builds the prompt via `apply_chat_template(messages[:-1], add_generation_prompt=True)` and reads position `prompt_len − 1` on THAT string — so the paper's `prompt_last` position IS the last token of the generation header, i.e. IDENTICAL to #1739's `context_end` map-training input position. Implemented paper-faithfully there is ONE captured position, not two: this single capture kind serves BOTH the prompt-token-ΔP arm and the mapped arm's input. No header-free `plast` variant is introduced.
- `pfxend_ℓ(x_i)` = prefix-end activation (last token before the user content — bare-chat prefix; #1739 `prefix_end`).
- `M_ℓ(v)` = `((v − x_mu[ℓ]) / x_sd[ℓ]) @ w[ℓ] + y_mu[ℓ]` — the frozen #1739 map applied to a context vector.

Predictor per row (projection onto v̂_ℓ):
| Arm | Per-row stand-in `s_i` | Per-row predictor |
|---|---|---|
| Raw projection | — | `raw_i = a_ℓ(x_i,y_i^train)·v̂_ℓ` |
| Exact ΔP | `a_ℓ(x_i,y_i^base)·v̂_ℓ` | `raw_i − s_i` |
| Prompt-token ΔP | `ctxend_ℓ(x_i)·v̂_ℓ` (≡ paper `prompt_last` — the position identity above) | `raw_i − s_i` |
| Mapped ΔP (context) | `M_ℓ(ctxend_ℓ(x_i))·v̂_ℓ` | `raw_i − s_i` |
| Mapped ΔP (prefix) | `M_ℓ(pfxend_ℓ(x_i))·v̂_ℓ` | `raw_i − s_i` |
| Identity+bias baseline | `(ctxend_ℓ(x_i) + b_ℓ)·v̂_ℓ`, `b_ℓ` = train-fold mean of `(a(y^train) − ctxend)` via `identity_bias_predict` | `raw_i − s_i` |

Dataset-level value = `mean_i(predictor)` over the dataset's subsample. The identity+bias arm is **literally the formalized prompt-token arm** — it reads the SAME captured position as the prompt-token arm (`ctxend ≡ plast`, the position identity above) and applies identity plus a learned bias — and doubles as the scientific comparison for H1 (learned map vs identity assumption).

## 7. Decision Gates

**No gates — short run / pre-verified pipeline.** Total GPU wall < 4 h; the capture + generation are deterministic reads with no convergence risk; the hypothesis outcome is read post-hoc from correlations, not gated mid-run. The ONE pre-run gate is the P4 Form-A **judge pilot** (~150 draws, gate on zero `max_tokens` stops + per-arm parse-fail < 2%) — a standard llm-judging pilot, not a decision gate on the science.

**Kill criteria (halt/abort-and-report conditions — operationalizing the §0.0 "what would change my mind" bullets):**
- **Paper replication fails on the banked data (H3 kill):** at P3, if the exact-ΔP arm misses even the SIGN of the published Qwen r at the steering layer for ≥2 of 3 traits, or all three published arms miss the published magnitude by > 0.10 on every trait, HALT and report a replication failure — the mapped-arm headline is uninterpretable against a non-reproducing reference and is not run to a verdict.
- **P0 reuse-verification fails:** the realized-keys check on the #1739 map npz (`w,x_mu,x_sd,y_mu,layers`) or the #778 `rb_v2` shape fails AND the documented fallbacks (rb v1; refit a fresh frozen map on #1739's store) also fail → ABORT before any GPU provision and reclassify the map source (re-plan); no partial run.
- **P1 capture pilot exceeds budget:** the measured 1-batch pilot extrapolates the P1+P2 capture to > 4× the §9 booked wall (> ~15 GPU-h) → HALT before the full capture; re-vectorize / re-book per the pilot-gated basis rule rather than burning the pod.
- **Exact-ΔP reference corrupted:** per-dataset base-generation cap-hit fraction > 2% persisting after the one 2×-cap re-gen (#1332) → HALT and report — the gold-standard reference arm is biased, so comparisons against it are not run to a verdict.
