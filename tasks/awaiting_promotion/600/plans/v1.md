# Plan — Task #600: Targeted proximity-dose test — does a contrastive negative parked next to a specific bystander suppress that bystander's leakage?

Parent: #505 (removal-direction null). Lineage: #472 (geometry sweep, recipe + rig + run-noise calibration) → #477 (row-scaled count) → #505 (leave-one-out removal, null) → **#600 (this run: addition-direction, proximity-dosed, distance-to-source-matched)**.

---

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I teach one character (a villain persona) to stamp a hidden tell — a rare symbol — at the end of its answers, while showing the model four other characters that should NOT get the tell. For six pre-chosen bystander characters the model never trains on, I run the training twice: once with one of the four "don't do this" characters chosen to be the bystander's closest look-alike, and once with a control character that sits just as far from the villain but nowhere near the bystander. The only thing that changes between the two runs is whether the warning example sits next to the bystander.
- **What I expect:** Prior data hint, weakly, that parking a warning next to a bystander shields that specific bystander from picking up the tell. But the hint was tangled up with how close each bystander sits to the villain, and the one previous causal test (removing a warning instead of adding one) found nothing. My honest prior is a null; the matched control and the repeated runs are what make either answer trustworthy this time.
- **What would change my mind:** If the bystander reliably picks up less of the tell when its look-alike is in the warning set — across most of the six bystanders and bigger than the wobble we see when the exact same training is just re-run — that's real evidence each warning example protects its own neighborhood. We also get, for the first time, a proper distribution of that re-run wobble, which every later experiment in this line can reuse.

Smoke ladder may re-pin epochs to 2–3 (steps-not-LR; Source: recipe rule + #538) — one re-pin for ALL cells.

Four-float storage per slot per model side (HF forward passes).

## 3. Hypothesis

Pre-registered. All directional claims are about the **implant-normalized target shift** (target ΔlogP / source ΔlogP, same run, same checkpoint) at the headline read checkpoint.

- **H1 (local suppression, the Goal).** For each target t, DV(NEAR negative for t) < DV(Distance-matched far control for t): the paired NEAR−CONTROL difference is negative, concentrated on the targets. Quantitative bar: one-sided target-level exact sign-flip permutation p ≤ 0.05 on the mean paired difference (64 enumerations over 6 targets), AND the mean |paired difference| exceeds the median same-mix run-noise gap.
- **H1-locality (secondary).** Any suppression is local: the target's paired NEAR−CONTROL difference sits in the bottom tail of the common-held-out panel's paired-difference distribution (per-target percentile, Fisher-combined across the 6 targets, p ≤ 0.05). A global shift (whole panel moves together) does NOT count as local suppression.
- **H-null (promotable).** Paired differences are sign-mixed (≤ 4/6 targets negative) AND the permutation p > 0.05 AND the mean |difference| is within the same-mix run-noise band → negative placement does not detectably shape local leakage at this anchor; with the run-noise distribution attached, this null bounds the effect at ≲ the design resolution (~0.03–0.06 normalized units) and closes the addition direction of open-q 3.4a at this recipe.
- **Falsified / confounded readings to guard:** an apparent NEAR effect that also appears uniformly across the non-target panel = global mix effect, not locality (H1-locality separates them); a NEAR effect driven by source-implant differences = handled by the normalization + the unnormalized robustness read + the matched-step design.

Direction note: the weak observational lean (#472 within-bystander L10 +0.44) predicts suppression near negatives, i.e. **negative** paired differences. The removal-direction null (#505) makes the honest prior ~60/40 toward H-null.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Conditions overview — the single manipulated variable

Every cell trains the identical recipe on 1000 rows: 200 villain positives (the #472 positives: same questions, same frozen on-policy R, marker appended) + 800 negative rows = 4 negative personas × 200 rows each. The negative panel is:

```
panel(cell) = { qwen_default, base_mid_1, base_mid_2, VARIABLE_SLOT }
```

- `qwen_default` + 2 fixed mid-distance personas = the **fixed base panel**, identical in every cell (satisfies the always-include-default rule).
- `VARIABLE_SLOT` is the single manipulated variable: per target t, either **NEAR** (t's nearest-neighbor persona in centered L10 cosine space) or **CONTROL** (a persona matched to the NEAR persona on distance-to-source within tolerance, but far from t).

6 targets × 2 conditions × 3 seeds {42, 137, 219} = **36 training runs**. Total rows, positives, base panel, recipe, step count (63), and checkpoint grid are identical across all 36 — only the variable-slot persona differs within a pair.

**Optional MID dose level: OUT of scope** (pre-registered). Cost +18 cells ≈ +20 GPU-h (+50%) buys a dose-response curve that is only interpretable if the binary NEAR-vs-CONTROL contrast is non-null — and the prior (#505 null, #472 placement ≈ run noise) puts most probability on null. The budget goes to seeds (power for the binary question) instead. If H1 lands, a MID follow-up reuses this rig unchanged (the selection script already emits a ranked mid-distance candidate per target into the manifest for that purpose).

### 4.2 Design layer and distance metric (pre-registered)

**Design layer = L10**, base-model activation-centroid cosine with **`centering="global_mean"`** (canonical per `persona-distance-metrics.md` § Bank centering, #536), distance = 1 − cos. Rationale for L10 over L20/L21: (a) every #472-line panel and the 47-persona held-out panel were selected with L10 geometry — targets drawn from that panel should be stratified in the same space; (b) the only surviving leakage-distance gradient in the line was validated at L10 (Spearman −0.52, robust at L15/L20); (c) #505's L21 headline showed per-cell sign instability across layers, and even #505's own panel gate ran at L10. Robustness: all locality/geometry **reads** are recomputed at L15, L20 (existing bundles) and L21 (the #505 pv-centroid bundle, Hub-verified §10) at analysis time — but note the headline H1 test is **distance-metric-free** (NEAR/CONTROL are condition labels frozen at design time), so layer choice cannot flip the headline, only the selection and the secondary geometry reads.

Centering note: the existing `centroids_L10.pt` stores raw centroids, so centered cosine is recomputed from the same bundle at zero cost via `compute_cosine_matrix(C, centering="global_mean")`; every persisted cosine artifact records `centering: "global_mean"` + the bank hash.

### 4.3 Selection algorithm (deterministic, CPU, runs and commits BEFORE any training)

New script `select_panels.py` (module `targeted_proximity_600`), all steps deterministic given the bank content-hash + centroids:

1. Load `data/issue_472/persona_bank.json` (60 personas, contains `villain` + `qwen_default`) + `centroids_L10.pt`; compute centered cosine matrix; `d(a,b) = 1 − cos_centered_L10(a,b)`; `d_source(p) = d(p, villain)`.
2. Reconstruct the **47-persona #472 held-out panel** from the committed #472 trajectory files (`eval_results/issue_472/*/trajectory.json` per-persona records) — machine-readable, not from plan prose.
3. **Targets:** split the 47 by terciles of `d_source` (near/mid/far). Within each tercile, keep candidates that are *feasible* (steps 4–5 succeed for them); among feasible, pick the 2 with the largest realized manipulation contrast `d(CTRL_t, t) − d(NN_t, t)`; tie-break lexicographic. → 6 targets, 2 per stratum.
4. **NEAR negative per target:** `NN_t = argmin_p d(p, t)` over the bank excluding {villain, qwen_default, all 6 targets, base panel (step 6 re-checked), t itself}. Gate: `d(NN_t, t) ≤ P25` of the held-out panel's distance-to-t distribution (the neighbor must actually be near); else next-nearest; else the target is infeasible.
5. **CONTROL negative per target:** choose p minimizing `|d_source(p) − d_source(NN_t)|` subject to: `|d_source(p) − d_source(NN_t)| ≤ ε_match = 0.10` (centered units) AND `d(p, t) ≥ P75` of the panel's distance-to-t distribution AND `d(p,t) − d(NN_t,t) ≥ 0.30` AND p ∉ {villain, qwen_default, targets, base panel}. Relaxation ladder (deterministic, recorded): ε → 0.15 with P75 → P60; if still empty, the target is infeasible (step 3 replaces it).
6. **Fixed base panel:** the 2 personas with `d_source` closest to the bank median, excluding {villain, qwen_default, all targets, all NN_t, all CTRL_t}. (Selected after targets/NN/CTRL so exclusions are well-defined; a re-check confirms no NN/CTRL collision after the final target set settles.)
7. Emit `eval_results/issue_600/panel_selection.json`: targets + strata, NN/CTRL per target with all realized distances, base panel, ε used per target, centering provenance, bank hash, ranked MID candidates (unused, for the follow-up). **Committed to the issue branch before training.**

Tolerance grounding: ε_match = 0.10 centered units sits *below* the centered-space equivalent (~0.12) of #472's raw-space identification floor (0.02 raw × the ~6× raw→centered compression measured in #536) — i.e., the residual d_source mismatch inside a pair is smaller than the smallest geometric motion the line has ever treated as identifiable. The 0.30 contrast floor is ~2.5× that floor. Both are `Source: #472 floor + #536 compression`, confidence Medium, verified against realized bank distributions at design time (§12 A9).

### 4.8 Multi-arm resolution-band design (the §4 mandatory bullet)

The headline gates on BOTH members of each NEAR/CONTROL pair being readable (floor + sub-saturation) at a shared checkpoint — a 2-arm-per-pair band-simultaneity gate, so per-cell early STOPPING does not apply: the band callback runs in **log-only mode** (`marker_band_stop=True` + `marker_band_log_only=True` — telemetry without stopping; `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` § Multi-arm), keeping training amounts matched at 63 steps in every cell. Per the rule:
(a) **Expected transition window:** at lr 5e-6 the source ramp is wide — #538's [14,20]-nat band spans ~30 optimizer steps (steps 60–90) on a sibling mix (`Source: #538`); the floor-to-window transition at this LR is wider than any gap in our grid.
(b) **Grid:** realized steps {6, 11, 21, 32, 48, 63} — optimizer-step-indexed (fracs of a fixed 63-step run; ceil semantics of `CheckpointAtFractionsCallback`, matching #472's stored trajectories), max spacing 16 steps < the ~30-step transition width.
(c) **Pre-registered band-entry fallback:** headline read = the LATEST checkpoint where both pair members pass gates (matched step). If a pair never co-passes, compare each member at its own first gate-passing checkpoint (matched dial position, unmatched step; flagged in analysis). A cell that never passes at any checkpoint = failed-gate; its pair is excluded and the hypothesis denominator revised (reported per the planned-vs-actual rule, never silently pooled). Identical row counts + recipe across all 36 cells make co-resolution the expected case (#505's trajectories were near-identical across arms).

| Plain-English name | What it tests | What it controls for | Config slug |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nearest-neighbor negative (per target t, ×6) | Does a negative parked next to t suppress t's leakage? | — (the treatment arm) | `c600_<t>_near` |

What the matched control isolates: within a pair, the two mixes are identical in source, positives, base panel, total rows, per-persona rows, recipe, and step count, and the two slot personas sit at the same distance-to-source (within ε ≤ 0.10 centered units, below the line's identification floor). The only systematic difference is the slot persona's proximity to the target — so a reliable NEAR−CONTROL difference ON THE TARGET is attributable to proximity, not to how close the negative sits to the source (the #472 confound) or to row budget (the #472 main effect). Residual non-proximity differences (the slot personas are still different personas) are exactly what the 6-target replication averages over and the run-noise calibration bounds.

### Analysis plan (pre-registered)

1. **Headline (H1):** d(t,s) = DV_near − DV_ctrl per target × seed at the headline checkpoint (§4.8). Test statistic T = mean over 6 targets of the seed-mean d(t,·). One-sided exact sign-flip permutation at the TARGET level (all 2^6 = 64 enumerations; flip each target's seed-mean jointly — respects within-target correlation): p = #{T_perm ≤ T_obs}/64; significant at p ≤ 0.05 (min attainable 1/64 ≈ 0.016).
2. **Cross-target sign test:** binomial, one-sided, predicted negative (6/6 → p = 0.016; 5/6 → p = 0.109, suggestive only). Reported alongside, not the gatekeeper.
3. **Run-noise calibration:** 36 within-condition across-seed gaps of the normalized DV (12 conditions × 3 seed pairs) = the empirical same-mix run-noise distribution (the line's first; #472 had a single draw, ≈0.069 normalized at terminal). Overlay on the 18 paired differences; effect-size bar: mean |d| > median same-mix gap. Descriptive calibration; the permutation test carries inference.
4. **Locality (H1-locality):** per pair, paired difference for every common-held-out persona (47-panel minus the union of all #600 panel personas, ≥ ~33 personas incl. the 6 targets); per-target percentile of the target's d within the non-target distribution; Fisher-combined across targets. Exploratory bubble-radius read: panel paired difference vs centered distance-to-the-NEAR-negative, at L10 (design) + L15/L20/L21 (robustness).
5. **Robustness family (Holm-corrected within family):** (i) unnormalized ΔG_target paired test; (ii) EOS-margin-space paired test; (iii) headline re-read at the other gate-passing checkpoints. Reported with their own p; none can rescue a failed headline alone.
6. **Manipulation checks:** realized d(NN,t) vs d(CTRL,t) per target (from the manifest); the NEAR negative's OWN ΔG in its cell below the held-out median (the negative is actually suppressed where it sits); |Δd_source| within ε per pair; per-cell space-agreement (Δlog P ≈ Δz_marker off saturation).

7. **Analyzer protocol pins (binding — from the round-1 critique-ensemble reconciliation):**
   (a) **k=4 surviving pairs:** if exactly 2 pairs fail gates, min attainable permutation p = 1/16 > 0.05 — apply the §9 demotion identically to the failed-gate path: the permutation becomes descriptive, the paired-magnitude-vs-noise comparison carries, and NO promotable bounded-null language is allowed at k=4.
   (b) **Outcome lattice:** Success/Partial/Null as registered in §7 are not exhaustive — any uncovered cell (e.g. p ≤ 0.05 with mean |d| ≤ the band; p > 0.05 with mean |d| above the band) is narrated as INDETERMINATE with its components reported separately. The null-band statistic is pinned NOW: **median same-mix gap** (matching H1's effect-size bar), computed in the analysis script before data lands.
   (c) **Checkpoint convention for noise calibration:** same-mix gaps vary ~30× across checkpoints — each pair's |d| is calibrated ONLY against same-checkpoint same-mix gaps (the per-checkpoint distributions are recomputable from the persisted 6-checkpoint trajectories).
   (d) **Bubble-radius read precedes any "global mix effect" wording:** a true suppression bubble around the NEAR negative also lowers the target's cluster-mates, diluting the target's percentile, so H1-locality can fail on a genuinely neighborhood-local effect — run the §6.4 bubble-radius scatter and distinguish "neighborhood-local suppression" from "whole-panel global shift" before applying the Partial label.
   (e) **Report per pair alongside the headline:** NEAR−CONTROL difference in SOURCE ΔG (denominator-leak check), signed residual Δd_source (+ a descriptive paired-difference-vs-residual check), and the unnormalized/EOS-margin robustness reads wherever the source lands above ~12 nats (log-space compression).

## 7. Decision Gates, Success and Kill Criteria

**One gate** (justified: the run is >16 GPU-h; the implant level at this exact recipe × row-count is genuinely uncertain — #505's r16/lr5e-6 smoke floored at 25 steps while #538 hit 14–19 nats at steps 60–90; one ~1.1 GPU-h cell settles it before 36 are spawned): the **§4.7 smoke gate** with its epochs ladder. No per-cell mid-sweep gates — each cell is a short single run; the trajectory checkpoints already give the early-read fallback without fragmenting anything.

**Success (H1):** target-level permutation p ≤ 0.05 (one-sided, NEAR < CONTROL) on the normalized DV at the headline checkpoint AND mean |d| > median same-mix run-noise gap AND the locality read confirms target-specificity (Fisher p ≤ 0.05) → "a contrastive negative suppresses leakage locally for the bystander it sits next to" (confidence MODERATE at 6 targets / 3 seeds; HIGH requires replication at more targets).
**Partial:** permutation significant but locality fails → a global mix effect of swapping the slot persona, NOT local suppression — reported as exactly that.
**Null (promotable):** permutation p > 0.05 AND mean |d| within the run-noise band → addition-direction null at this anchor, bounding the effect below the design resolution; pairs with the removal-direction null (#505) to close 3.4a's "near-twin negatives are the sharpest lever" at this recipe. The run-noise distribution ships regardless.
**Kill criteria:** (i) smoke ladder exhausted in BOTH directions (cannot land source ΔG in [5,19] at lr ≤ 5e-6 with ≤ 3 epochs) → decidable "no readable anchor at the clean-window recipe on this mix," block with `epm:failure`; (ii) ≥ 3 of 6 pairs failed-gate at every checkpoint → the surviving-pair sign test is underpowered (min p > 0.05 even at 3/3) → report as failed-gate outcome, no headline claim.

**Joint-satisfiability self-check:** floor (source ΔG ≥ 5) and sub-saturation (trained logP < −0.1 nat of ceiling; bystander argmax < 0.92) define a non-empty band — #505's realized cells (5.4–5.9 nats, 0/10,920 bystander argmax) and #538's (14–19 nats, emission 0.000) both sat inside it. The headline direction (NEAR < CONTROL on the target) and the manipulation check (NEAR negative's own ΔG suppressed) concern different personas (target vs slot persona) — no gate demands opposite outcomes on the same measurement at the same cell/slot. The smoke ladder's two failure directions (floor/saturation) trigger mutually exclusive arms. No contradiction found.
