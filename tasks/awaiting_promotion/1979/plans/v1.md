# Plan — #1979: Per-prefix leakage-predictor race + theory-assumption battery at prefix grain (50 prefixes × 60 shared queries)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I take the same 18 trained models from the per-query race and measure, for 50 different conversation openers (the trained persona, its trained-down negatives, near-twin personas, real chat histories, few-shot examples, and a bare opener), how much the trained behavior shows up when each opener is followed by the same 60 real user questions — then I check which before-training signal best predicts, opener by opener, where the behavior appears and where it grew, and I re-test the three theory assumptions that failed at question grain.
- **What I expect:** the base model's own tendency still predicts where behavior APPEARS; similarity of the base model's answers to the training answers predicts where it GREW; the trained-down negative openers leak less than geometry alone would predict; and the gate assumption looks better at this grain than the 0.14 it scored per-question, since openers are the unit the theory actually talks about.
- **What would change my mind:** if a geometry signal cleanly beats the base tendency across arms at this grain, the per-query champion verdict is grain-limited; if the gate stays near zero even here, the gate assumption is refuted at the theory's own grain, not as a grain artifact.

## 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (level champion):** P7 (per-prefix base propensity) has the highest across-arm median within-arm Spearman ρ with the LEVEL DV among the deployable candidates. Expectation: median ρ(P7) ≈ 0.4–0.7 (per-prefix means over 60 queries are far less noisy than per-query reads); geometry candidates 0.1–0.4.
- **H2 (change champion):** P2 (pre-FT answer similarity, training-centroid anchor) tops the propensity-residualized CHANGE race, replicating #1900's per-query verdict at prefix grain.
- **H3 (mediation):** context similarity's signal is absorbed by answer similarity at this grain (the #1900 mediation lattice re-run at n=50/arm).
- **H4 (assumption battery):** the gate A7 improves at prefix grain (the theory's native unit) — registered question: does it re-enter the 0.3–0.7 band? A6: per-prefix write matrix (50 × 3584) top-1 share rises vs the per-query 0.09/0.29 (averaging over queries removes query-idiosyncratic rank) but the registered criterion stays 0.6. A5: matched-text write–δ alignment stays null (text-carried), read-out alignment stays family-level.
- **H5 (trained negatives):** the 5 contrastive-negative prefixes leak BELOW their geometry prediction (they were trained down): median signed residual < 0 vs the within-arm geometry fit in ≥ 8/12 content arms (≥ 4/6 marker).
- **Falsifiers:** H1/H2 by the lattices below; H4 gate by the gate lattice; H5 by median residual ≥ 0 in most arms (geometry alone explains negatives — the contrastive training added no prefix-selective suppression).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: P7-retains-level-champion ⇔ the across-arm-median-ρ argmax over the deployable candidates on the LEVEL DV is P7 AND P(winner = P7 across bootstrap draws) ≥ 0.5; geometry-dethrones-level ⇔ the argmax is some c ≠ P7 AND P(winner = c) ≥ 0.5 AND per-arm Δρ(c − P7) > 0 in ≥ 9 of 12 content arms; no-resolved-level-champion ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: answer-sim-retains-change-champion ⇔ the across-arm-median-ρ argmax over the deployable candidates on the propensity-residualized CHANGE DV is P2 AND P(winner = P2) ≥ 0.5; other-change-champion ⇔ the argmax is some c ≠ P2 AND P(winner = c) ≥ 0.5 AND per-arm Δρ(c − P2) > 0 in ≥ 9 of 12 content arms; no-resolved-change-champion ⇔ otherwise.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: answer-mediated ⇔ r_P1|P2,P7 < 0.5·r_P1|P7 AND r_P2|P1,P7 ≥ 0.5·r_P2|P7; context-primary ⇔ r_P2|P1,P7 < 0.5·r_P2|P7 AND r_P1|P2,P7 ≥ 0.5·r_P1|P7; shared-inseparable ⇔ r_P1|P2,P7 < 0.5·r_P1|P7 AND r_P2|P1,P7 < 0.5·r_P2|P7; both-independent ⇔ otherwise. (r_X|Z = across-arm median partial Spearman of the LEVEL DV vs X given Z, P7 always in the conditioning set — #1900 lattice verbatim, re-grained.)

DISJOINT and exhaustive: gate-restored-at-prefix-grain ⇔ the median over content (arm × layer) cells of Spearman ρ(g(prefix), matched-text per-prefix write coefficient) lies in [0.3, 0.7] AND ≥ 50% of content cells lie in [0.3, 0.7]; gate-still-refuted ⇔ that median < 0.3; gate-overshoots ⇔ that median > 0.7; gate-unresolved ⇔ otherwise.

Winner-selection convention (registered): champion argmax over SIGNED Spearman ρ (#1900 convention; sign-flipped candidates are reportable, never winners; |ρ| views exploratory-dump only).

**Registered paired contrast:** every candidate-vs-incumbent Δρ (level race: vs P7; change race: vs P2) is paired over prefixes within arm.
Row-coverage: every raced candidate within an arm is evaluated on the identical realized prefix set — the 50-prefix panel restricted to prefixes with a valid DV (≥ 45 of 60 queries with ≥ 1 valid judge draw; marker: ≥ 45 valid slots) and all candidate values present (listwise); realized n reported per arm; bootstrap resample indices (prefix draws, query-cluster draws, family draws) are shared across candidates and the DV within each arm.

## 4. Design

**Why code, not a model call:** predictors are deterministic tensor algebra over persisted activations; the only LLM calls are the graded judge DV (routed through `api_dispatch.py` via `judge_completions_batch`/`dispatch_judge_items`) and a one-shot ~30-call near-twin persona datagen.

**Grain definition (the single changed variable vs #1900):** the unit is the (arm, prefix) cell. Per cell, every vector quantity is the mean over the 60 shared queries; every DV is the query-mean. Canonical positions: prefix = everything before the user query; context = prefix + query; context vector = LAST-TOKEN (`last_prompt`, the newline ending the assistant header) PRIMARY, span-mean secondary, captured together (user directive 2026-08-01); prefix vector = span-mean over prefix tokens + last-prefix-token, captured in the same forward pass; answer vector = span-mean over response tokens (the fleet convention — response-avg).

## 7. Decision Gates

**Gate 1 — judge wave-1 pilot (DV shape at prefix grain), before the full ~135k-call spend.** Full wall > 4 h (GPU ~4–5 h + Batch SLA); the genuinely uncertain quantity is the per-prefix DV's dynamic range/ceiling profile (elicit-heavy prefixes may pin base AND trained scores at 100, flattening the change DV); wave 1 (~27k calls, ≤ $250, one batch cycle) rules the remaining spend in or out. Wave 1 = base_content (3 rubrics) + "impoliteness persona positive-only (s42)" + "impoliteness persona contrastive (s42)" over the full 50×60 grid × 3 draws.
- **Criterion A (instrument health; mechanism: F2's per-arm drop report counting content-drops vs transport-losses):** content-drop rate ≤ 10% per judged state at max_tokens=400. Branch: > 10% → resize to 600, re-judge affected draws against a fresh cache_dir (rule 23), re-measure. Self-count: drops / 9,000 draws per state.
- **Criterion B (per-prefix DV range; mechanism: F3's descriptive block computes SD and quantiles of the 50 per-prefix level + change means):** race-viable iff SD across prefixes of the LEVEL DV ≥ 3 points (0–100 scale) for ≥ 1 of the 2 arms AND ≥ 10 prefixes score ≥ 10. Grounding: judge noise floor ~2–3 points at N=3 draws per completion; a per-prefix mean over 60 queries has SE ≈ (per-query SD)/√60 ≪ 3, so SD < 3 across prefixes means no prefix-level signal to rank (threshold sign + value: above noise floor, non-degenerate tail). Branch: both arms flat on LEVEL → read the CHANGE DV SD (≥ 2 points passes — change removes a shared floor); still flat → marker-lead re-scope (judge-free DV with guaranteed log-prob range) + content waves descoped to 6 arms (spend halved), never a whole-run kill.
- **Criterion C (ceiling profile; mechanism: F3's ceiling flag — share of prefixes with base-side mean ≥ 90):** if > 40% of prefixes are base-ceilinged on an arm's rubric, register the ceiling-excluded re-read as that arm's PRIMARY level read (the full-panel read stays record-only) and proceed. No spend branch — a reporting rule fixed before the data are seen.
- Jointly satisfiable: A is an instrument fix, B a re-scope ladder, C a reporting rule; no combination kills the run. Precedent coherence: the po arm is the high-range bet (#18/#207 positive-only leakage), con the low bet — recomputed on #1900's realized per-query values (imp-pers-po arm SD comfortably > 3 on judged rows), both land in the "proceed" branch, matching the prose.

**Kill criteria (halt-and-report):**
1. F0 trained/negative prefix byte-assert FAILS vs the pinned training-mix rows (render drift) — kill before any GPU spend; re-plan (the panel's anchor rows would be wrong objects).
2. Store/join failure: the staged base stores or delta_tf mixes fail their loaders' asserts for > 4 of 18 arms — reuse-fitness failure; halt and report, never regenerate inside this task.
3. Marker adapter identity gate fails for ≥ 2 marker arms — kill the marker panel (content race proceeds).
