## 3. Hypothesis

The marker family's strongly negative write–delta alignment is carried by the TEXT-carried component of the on-policy delta (the trained model selects different continuations — e.g. end-of-turn shaping), not by the WEIGHTS-carried component. (Verbatim from the followup-scope; the falsification direction is symmetric and both outcomes are informative.)

Per-arm carrying predicate (registered): component X ∈ {weights, text} **carries** the alignment for an arm iff (i) cos(pooled leg_w, pooled X) < 0, (ii) |cos(pooled leg_w, pooled X)| > the corpus-cov abs_p95 of the norm-matched null band drawn against pooled X, and (iii) |cos(pooled leg_w, pooled X)| ≥ 0.5 · |cos(pooled leg_w, pooled leg_d)| for that arm ("comparable magnitude", operationalized).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: weights-carried ⇔ the weights component carries in ≥4 of 6 marker arms AND the text component carries in ≤3 of 6; text-carried ⇔ the text component carries in ≥4 of 6 AND the weights component carries in ≤3 of 6; both-carried ⇔ both components carry in ≥4 of 6; interaction-artifact ⇔ otherwise.

Attainability check (c28): clause (iii)'s threshold is 0.5·|parent pooled cos| = 0.32/0.41/0.40/0.49/0.37/0.45 per arm (bare/pers-con/conv/icl/pers-po/ft), each strictly above that arm's parent corpus-cov abs_p95 null edge (0.061/0.162/0.157/0.241/0.124/0.221) — clauses (ii) and (iii) are jointly satisfiable in every arm, so every lattice branch is reachable.

Interpretation scope: a weights-carried verdict rewrites the A5 marker Takeaways bullet into a genuine write-direction violation (matched-text write direction opposing the weights-carried realized delta); a text-carried verdict re-scopes the refutation to text-carried change the write operator never claimed.
