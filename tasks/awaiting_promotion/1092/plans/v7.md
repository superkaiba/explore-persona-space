### Probe recipe (matched capacity to the parent's B1(c) — inherited, not re-chosen)

- Model states: `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` hidden states, **layer 14, ambient basis (3,584 dims)** — the parent B1(c) headline configuration. Ambient (not pca48) is mandatory for transfer: PCA bases are corpus-specific.
- Fit engine: `scripts/issue1092_fit_grid.py::_fit_scalar_cv` / `_fit_cv` — the banked Gram-dual ridge with the `RIDGE_LAMBDAS = [1e-2, 1e-1, 1, 10, 100, 1000]` grid (`scripts/issue658_fit_predictors.py:115`), per-fold λ selection, train-side centering — reused verbatim, plus its `train_mean_floor` baseline.
- Training folds: within-corpus reads reuse the parent's grouped folds (`_folds_from_manifest`, group = `prefix_id`, 6 folds, seed 0) on the #1092 side; row-level 6-fold on the #779 LMSYS side. For the TRANSFER read, the probe is trained on the FULL training corpus (all eligible scored rows — INCLUDING the battery rows on the #1092 side, the parent's realized pool) with λ chosen by grouped CV on the training side only; the eval corpus is never touched at fit time — eval is fully disjoint by construction (different corpus), satisfying the group-level-fold requirement trivially.
- Transfer uncertainty — **direction-specific clustering (Must-Fix 2):**
  - **Direction A (eval side = LMSYS rows):** row-level cluster bootstrap, 10,000 paired draws (each LMSYS row is its own singleton conversation, so row clusters ARE the group structure). Unchanged.
  - **Direction B and ALL within-#1092 CIs (repro gate + Direction B eval side):** the #1092 rows are CROSSED prefix × query, and the bare-arm predictions are CONSTANT within query groups (bare features depend on the query alone), so prefix-only clustering understates the query-side dependence — with 79–94% of R² carried by the query share, query-side effective N binds. PRIMARY: a **two-way crossed (pigeonhole) cluster bootstrap** — independent multinomial resampling over `prefix_id` AND over `query_id` per draw, each row weighted by (prefix draw count × query draw count), weighted r computed per draw, paired across context-probe and bare-control predictions (same draw indices). COMPANIONS: one-way prefix-clustered and one-way query-clustered bootstraps, both reported. **The verdict gates on the WIDEST of the three CIs** (conservative under either dependence structure).
  - (ii) train-side sensitivity — the 6 per-fold probes each evaluated on the full eval corpus, reported as a min/max band.
- Metrics: **primary = Pearson r** (calibration-invariant; matches the #779 line's primary-metric convention); companions = Spearman ρ, strict zero-shot R² (train-side centering applied to eval features), and recentered R² (eval-side mean removed) — the strict-vs-recentered gap isolates corpus mean-shift from direction transfer. All four reported per condition × trait; the verdict keys on r.

### Eligibility gates (parent §6, inherited verbatim; applied PER SIDE of each direction pair)

Per surface × trait, from `issue1092_fit_grid.py::_behavior_reads`: `n_scored ≥ 5` AND `score std ≥ 1.0` AND `≥ 1 positive (score > 50)` AND `≥ 1 negative`. Applied separately to the TRAIN side and the EVAL side of each direction; a failure names the side + the failed gate. Malformed / REFUSAL / out-of-range judge returns were already DROPPED (never coerced) in both banked score sets; no re-judging occurs. An ineligible pair is reported `not estimable — <side>, <gate>` with its gate values — no all-or-nothing eligibility gate spans cells, and no per-corpus estimability assertion is made.

### Cross-corpus contamination control (new, pre-registered)

Both corpora draw from LMSYS-lineage text (#1092 prefixes/queries from WildChat + LMSYS; #779's 5,000 prompts from a deterministic LMSYS-Chat-1M stream), so row overlap is possible and would contaminate "zero-shot". Before any fit: normalized exact-match (lowercase, whitespace-collapsed) of each #779 prompt against (a) **ALL unique query texts appearing in the Direction-A training pool** (every distinct query in `cell_inst_own` scored rows — this includes the battery-row queries, i.e. a superset of the ~500-query bank) and (b) the #1092 prefix first-user-turns, from the banked corpus manifest + `lmsys_g_rollouts.json` prompt texts. Overlapping rows are EXCLUDED from the eval side in both directions; the overlap rate is reported. If exclusion drops any surface below the eligibility gates, that pair is `not estimable — overlap-dominated` (kill criterion 2).

### Prefix-arm deviation (explicit, per the both-arms rule)

The parent measured prefix / context / bare arms within-corpus. Transfer covers **context + bare** only: the prefix arm is structurally unavailable on the #779 substrate — LMSYS rows have no prefix (context = query), and `pass_a` banked only full-prompt `cx_last`/`cx_mean`, no prefix-end capture. This one-arm-short deviation is stated here and carries into the clean-result as a scope caveat; the within-corpus prefix-arm reads stay banked in the parent, and prefix/context BOTH-arms holds for every within-corpus read this module touches.

## Success + kill criteria (pre-registered verdicts)

Verdict cells: {Direction A on LMSYS, Direction B} × {hallucination, sycophancy} — 4 cells; `pass_a` is reported but non-gating (elicited-trait distribution shift). Per cell, with S_ctx = context-probe transfer r, S_bare = bare-control r (paired on identical eval rows):

- **TRANSFER-POSITIVE(cell):** Δr = S_ctx − S_bare ≥ 0.05 with the paired cluster-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0 (Direction B: the WIDEST of the three registered clusterings), AND S_ctx > 0 with CI excluding 0.
- **UPGRADE bullet 4** (question-type → transportable signal): ≥ 1 TRANSFER-POSITIVE trait in EACH direction.
- **DOWNGRADE bullet 4** (question-type decodability): ZERO TRANSFER-POSITIVE cells — the context probe never beats its bare control out-of-corpus — **AND the kill-criterion-3(iv) alignment precondition holds** (the within-LMSYS hallucination ceiling cleared its floor). Bullet 4 is rewritten: "the probe skill is question-type decodability; it does not transport across corpora above the bare-query control", with the realized r table. Two interpretation guards bind the DOWNGRADE prose: (i) it is read against the REALIZED within-eval-corpus ceiling (τ), not absolute r — the LMSYS labels average judge draws over a single rollout per context (vs the #1092 side's per-row completion scoring), an attenuation asymmetry that depresses attainable absolute r on the LMSYS side; (ii) Direction A is a **probe-weight transport test on prefix-less rows** (both probes read identical features there) — an A-null is narrated as "the context probe's weights carry no transportable advantage over the bare probe", NEVER as "prefix signal failed to transfer" (no prefix exists on that surface to transfer).
- **PARTIAL** (anything between, or the alignment precondition failing): bullet 4 keeps "decodability" and names which direction/trait transfers (or that the LMSYS side is alignment-suspect), with numbers; no flip either way.
- Secondary (reported, not gated): transfer fraction τ = S_ctx / within-eval-corpus ceiling r, set against the within-corpus bare fractions φ = 0.823/0.644.

Kill / abort criteria:
1. **Reproduction pre-gate (tightened):** recomputed within-#1092 B1(c) (context + bare, both traits) must (i) reproduce the scored-row coverage EXACTLY — row-id-SET equality against the banked pools preferred, at minimum n_scored == 7,646 (hallucination) and 7,652 (sycophancy) exactly — and (ii) reproduce the banked R² 0.747/0.576 and 0.615/0.371 within ≤1e-3 absolute (same engine, folds, seed: the recompute is deterministic, so any larger gap is a join/loader defect, not noise). The banked pools INCLUDE the 2,400 battery rows; a loader that drops `is_eval_only` rows will fail exactly here. Miss ⇒ STOP, fix the join — no transfer read is interpreted off a broken loader.
