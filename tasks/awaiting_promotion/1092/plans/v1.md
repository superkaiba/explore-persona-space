# Plan — Issue #1092 v1: Realistic sparse-crossed prefix × query corpus — prefix-map vs context-map, averaging-rank collapse, f/g/i variance decomposition, operator identity + trait-per-factor, dynamics, behavior

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll build a corpus of about a thousand real chat conversations, pair them with real user questions in a partly-crossed grid, and record the model's internal state at three places: right before the question, right after the question, and while it writes its answer. Then I fit simple linear predictors that ask: how much of the answer's internal profile was already fixed by the conversation, how much the question adds, and how much depends on the specific pairing — across four kinds of answer text (the model's own, a pretrained twin's, Claude's, and deliberately mismatched answers) and on both the chat-tuned and the pretrained model. A judge scores a subset of answers for traits like sycophancy so I can check which of those internal pieces actually predicts behavior — comparing every way of reading the trait that a prior experiment (#779) tried, against the same simple floors it used — and while the model writes each answer I also log a few running summaries of how trait-loaded its word-by-word internal state is (these can't be recomputed later, so they must be recorded during the run). The logged multi-turn conversations let me ask how far ahead the state at one turn predicts the rest of the conversation.
- **What I expect:** The conversation part carries a small share of the raw variation but nearly all of the persona/trait signal; the question part carries most of the raw variation but little trait signal; averaging over questions works as a filter that isolates the conversation part. On mismatched answers the question signal should vanish while some conversation coloring survives.
- **What would change my mind:** Trait signal spread across the question and pairing pieces instead of the conversation piece, or the earlier crossed-grid numbers failing to reappear on real data — either would mean our averaged persona maps have been measuring an artifact of the neat grids we built them on.

### Hypotheses (falsifiable)

- **H-A (persona-as-filter, primary bet):** f (prefix main effect) carries a small raw-variance share (#923: 8%) but the dominant share of r_B-projected trait signal AND of B2 behavioral predictivity; M (query-averaged map) ≈ f-map (principal angles small vs random-map null; Procrustes residual < null p5); M′ − M ≈ g (residual ‖M′−M−g‖/‖g‖ below the pairing-permutation null band). Confirmation: trait-per-factor table shows f's r_B alignment AND B2 judged-score predictivity ≥ 2× g's and i's at matched dimensionality.
- **H-B (query-dominant transport, falsifier):** trait signal spread across g/i (f's share < 50% of the total trait projection), or M′ − M carrying trait information significantly beyond g.
- **H-carrier:** on shuffled-pairing cells the query map dies (g-map held-out R² within the permutation null band) while the prefix map retains R² above its null (the carrier floor). Falsified if g survives shuffling (leak in the rig) or f dies (no carrier component).
- **H-decomposition-transfer:** f/g/i shares on the realistic corpus land in the same ordering as #923's 84/8/9 (query ≫ interaction ≈ prefix) with additive ceiling within ±0.15 of 0.914; falsified by prefix-share ≥ query-share or additive ceiling < 0.6.
- **H-dynamics:** D0 > D3 (one-round decay), D4 near-stationary-or-strengthening per #958's 0.26→0.32 prefix/context ratio trend; D1 R² > 0 quantifying the anticipated-user-content channel.
- **H-grain:** at ~1k-prefix averaged grain (rank cap removed), per-example maps still carry ≥ 2× the stable rank of averaged maps — averaging's collapse is a property of condition-level objects, not the n=50 cap (#813: 3.13 vs 12.97 at n=50).

**Literature (new-direction review, run at plan time; ids verified via the 2026-07-06 methods survey `docs/ideas/2026-07-06-context-answer-map-analyses.md` + web search):**

## 4. Design

### 4.0 Notation and targets

Per row (P, q, a): **prefix-end** activation = last token of P (equals the prefix-only forward under causal masking — no extra pass); **context-end** = last token of the rendered context (P + q + assistant header); answer summaries **t1** = mean over answer tokens (PRIMARY; #779 `v_full_mean` continuity), **t2** = mean over answer + boundary tokens up to right before the next user turn, **t3** = the single newline-after-user-header position (#779 `v_nl_after_user` family; current token identical across rows, no realized next token). **c_q_bare** = last-token state of the query rendered bare (no prefix/system). Dynamics notation per the body: context_k, s_k, answer_k, user-turn summaries u1/u2/u3 (u1 = mean over user-turn tokens; u2 = + boundary tokens before next assistant turn; u3 = fixed pre-assistant-header slot). All at all 28 post-block residual layers (`hidden_states[1..28]`, matching #778/#779 r_B indexing), bf16 capture → fp16 storage.

Boundary token strings: **instruct cells** append `<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>user\n` after the answer; t3 = the final `\n` after the `user` header (Source: #779 summaries2 keys; Qwen chat template). **Pretrained cells** (naturalistic format, #825 realized recipe: plain transcript with `User: ` / `Assistant: ` role headers, blank-line turn separators, generation stops on blank line / next role header): append `\n\nUser:` after the answer; t3 = the token position immediately after `User:` (the slot where user text would begin). t2 spans the boundary tokens up to (excluding) t3's position in both formats.

### 4.1 Phase P0 — corpus build (VM, 0 GPU)

New script `scripts/issue1092_build_corpus.py` (seed 42):

1. **Sources (data-realism tier 1–2):** WildChat (`allenai/WildChat-1M`, established real-user corpus) + LMSYS (`lmsys/lmsys-chat-1m` @ pinned revision `200748d9d3cddcc9d782887541057aca0b18c5da`, Source: #825). Streaming ingestion (no bulk download); release streaming datasets deterministically before shutdown (`del row, ds; gc.collect()` — the #952 rc=134 guard); JSONL read/written via text-mode iteration, never `.splitlines()` (#950/#825 U+2028 shred).
2. **Filters** (per #825 round-4 recipe): strict role alternation, English, non-redacted, non-moderation/toxicity-flagged, dedup on first user turn; conversation total ≤ 8,192 tokens; per-row formatted prompt token budget ≤ 7,168 (= 8,192 − 1,024 generation headroom), enforced ONCE at the bank/prefix loader by tokenizing the FORMATTED prompt (reuse the `filter_bank_rows_by_length` pattern from `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/issue_952/run_952.py`; drops recorded digest-only).
3. **Prefixes:** ~1,000 conversations cut at the FINAL user turn (prefix = all turns before it; the cut turn's user text = the prefix's NATURAL query). Stratified sample over topic × length with deliberate over-sampling of long conversations: **≥300 kept conversations with ≥5 user turns** (binding). ~50/50 WildChat/LMSYS where filters allow.
4. **Topic labels:** 12-way fixed taxonomy assigned by `claude-haiku-4-5` (one call per conversation/query, ~3k sync calls, ~$5). *Why a model call, not code:* TF-IDF clustering on multilingual chat text is unreliable at this scale and the label quality directly gates the topic-matched control; a fixed-taxonomy classification is a single cheap structured call; flip condition: if label agreement on a 100-row human spot-check < 80%, fall back to embedding k-means. (Topic labeling is not a judged-behavior DV; the Sonnet judge pin applies to the B-module only.)
5. **Query bank:** ~500 real final-turn user queries sampled from FILTERED conversations DISJOINT from the 1,000 prefix conversations (no leakage), topic-stratified; the **48 dense-core queries** are a topic-stratified subset of the bank (mirrors #923's 48-query shape).
6. **Crossing assignment:** dense core = 100 prefixes × all 48 core queries (4,800 rows). Sparse periphery = each remaining ~900 prefixes × {its natural query + 10 random bank queries + 3 topic-matched bank queries (~+30% pairing-realism control)} ≈ 12,600 rows. Trait stratum = ~100 trait-eliciting prefixes — ~33/trait × {evil, sycophancy, hallucination}, high- and low-trait variants drawn from #779's persona prompt specs (HF `issue779_monitoring/training-source-ablation-hg/corpus_specs/`, Hub-verified; tier-3 LLM-synthetic, labeled) — × 15 random bank queries ≈ 1,500 rows. Battery bridge = #594's 50 contexts (`data/issue594/battery.json`) × the 48 core queries = 2,400 rows, EVAL-ONLY in both fit arms. Total ≈ 21,300 rows/full cell.
7. **Shuffled-pairing assignment:** for the two shuffled cells, a derangement of the instruct own-text answers over the subsampled row set with the constraint that a row never receives an answer from the same prefix OR the same query (both factors broken); assignment persisted.
8. **Renders:** every (P, q) rendered BOTH ways — chat template (instruct cells) and naturalistic transcript (pretrained cells, #825 recipe above). Render/BPE integrity check: cross-format answer-span token mismatch ≤ 10% (Source: #825 gate).
9. **Outputs:** corpus manifest JSONL (row id, prefix id, query id, stratum, topic, token counts, render hashes) → committed digest at `eval_results/issue_1092/corpus/manifest_stats.json` + full manifest to HF `issue1092_realistic_crossing/corpus/`.

**Data source + realism tier:** prefixes/queries tier 1–2 (real user logs in established corpora); trait stratum tier 3 (diverse LLM-synthetic persona prompts, justified: no real corpus labels trait-eliciting prefixes; inherited from #779 where they demonstrably elicit the traits); battery tier 3 (inherited eval bridge, #594). Claude-text cells: third-party-LLM-written BY DESIGN (the manipulated variable — factor-level transfer to foreign coherent text). Shuffled cells: programmatic re-pairing of real text (tier-4 construction whose templated structure IS the construct — the carrier null; the pure random-token cell was explicitly excluded by the user for exactly the OOD-text-statistics confound).
