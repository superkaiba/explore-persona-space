## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** the same leakage-predictor race, but scored only on prompts where the base model already shows a nonzero trace of the behavior — so the "did the base model do it at all?" floor (90.6% of prompts for impoliteness) can no longer carry the winner.

- **What would change my mind:** if, off the floor, a geometry candidate overtakes P7 on level (or P2 loses the residualized-change race), the parent's champion structure was a floor artifact, not a graded-signal result.

## 3. Hypothesis

Off-floor, the champion structure survives with attenuated margins. Note the design *deliberately range-restricts P7* (all rows have base score > 0), so P7 attenuation is expected; the test is whether P7 still beats the geometry candidates on its graded remainder.

**Registered verdict lattice:**
DISJOINT and exhaustive: Survives ⇔ P7 has the highest across-arm median signed ρ among the 11 raced candidates in the off-floor LEVEL race AND P2 has the highest across-arm median signed ρ in the off-floor P7-residualized change race; Partially survives ⇔ exactly one of those two conditions holds; Dethroned ⇔ otherwise.
