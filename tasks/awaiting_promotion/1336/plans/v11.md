## 3. Hypothesis

**H-T (RLVR teaching):** on the RLVR-trained surfaces (full GSM8K train; MATH train; IF-constraints), the tier-8 reparameterization gap grows specifically at the RLVR step: C_v2 = Δ_RLVR − Δ_DPO exceeds the elicitation band. **H-E (elicitation):** C_v2 sits inside the band on every RLVR-trained surface, and the tier profile shows every stage's transfer rescued at or below the linear-reparameterization tiers with the SFT step remaining the only large increment (parent: +0.139 at SFT). **H-U (uniform drift):** adjacent increments are all positive, CI-clear, comparable magnitude. **Tier-profile prediction under H-E (descriptive):** low tiers (direct/offsets) fail at every post-trained stage, and the *sufficient tier* (first tier within the band of the within-stage ceiling) is a reparameterization tier (6–8), constant across SFT/DPO/RLVR — post-training changes coordinates, not mechanism; under H-T the RLVR stage's sufficient tier is strictly higher than DPO's on RLVR-trained surfaces (no tier ≤8 closes the gap).

**Registered quantities.** Per (model k, surface s): R²_within(k,s) = held-out pooled R² of the v2-estimator within-stage map at the pre-registered headline layer (rule below). Per (pair p, surface s, tier T ∈ 0..8): R²_T(p,s) held-out on identical rows + folds. Δ_k(s) = R²_within(k,s) − R²_tier8(base→k, s). **Primary headline:** C_v2 = Δ_RLVR(gsm8k_train_full, chat) − Δ_DPO(gsm8k_train_full, chat), paired prompt-level bootstrap (1,000 shared draws, seed 5000 + surface index — parent convention). Derived band quantities with ex_v2 = S_qwen_v2 / 0.6731 (the G0′ exchange rate, computed before any Llama verdict read): U = C_v2 − 0.0201·ex_v2 and L = C_v2 + 0.0201·ex_v2. **Secondary family (Bonferroni-2):** C_v2 on {MATH train, IF-constraints} chat. Everything else (LMSYS, UF, SFT-mix, adjacent increments, dose arm, tier profiles) is descriptive. **Sufficient-tier rule (registered, descriptive):** sufficientTier(p,s) = min{T : R²_within − R²_T ≤ 0.0201·ex_v2}, with the paired-bootstrap CI on (R²_within − R²_T) reported at that tier; "none ≤ 8" is a reportable value. **Primary scale rule:** raw pooled R² is primary IFF health gate H passes on the lattice-bearing cells (|R²_recal − R²_raw| ≤ 0.05·ex_v2 at the headline layer for all six cells feeding C_v2 primaries); else the lattice is read once on the recalibrated scale (the parent-validated estimator) with the raw companion reported — never averaged, never both.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: RLVR-teaching ⇔ U > 0 AND U's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; RLVR-unlearning ⇔ L's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Elicitation-consistent ⇔ U's 95% CI is wholly below 0 AND L's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

Row-coverage: both arms of every registered paired contrast (the within-stage arm and the tier-8 reparameterized-base arm, and the RLVR-vs-DPO gap pair) are supplied per-prompt by the persisted held-out prediction matrices `analysis_tensors/preds_v2/*.npz` and `analysis_tensors/metric_ladder_preds/*.npz` with their row manifests, row-count- and prompt-id-asserted against `preds_manifest_v2.json` before any bootstrap draw is taken.
