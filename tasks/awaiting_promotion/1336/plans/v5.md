## 3. Hypothesis

The committed evidence (shuffle nulls at −0.02, mean-baseline at −0.002, random-projection asymmetry Qwen +0.68 vs Llama −3.2, Y-unstandardized code, massive-activations literature) supports a primary hypothesis and a refined, RANKED alternative set (refining the amendment scope's (a)–(e)):

- **H-A (primary; refines (a)+(c)): raw-scale variance weighting is the artifact.** A small set of high-magnitude, high-variance Llama target dims dominates both the GCV λ choice and the pooled R². The fit finds genuine train-set X↔Y structure in those dims (slot and span-mean share within-row outlier structure — same forward pass), commits to a moderate λ, and mis-extrapolates held-out at target scale, driving SS_res ≈ 1.9 × SS_tot. Prediction: the per-dim decomposition concentrates the residual excess in the top-variance dims; standardized/trimmed re-reads recover to ≈ the Qwen-calibrated healthy range; the λ audit shows commitment (selected λ far from the shrink-to-mean end) driven by those dims.
- **H-C (mechanism arm of H-A): GCV λ degeneracy / grid-edge.** The `logspace(−2,4,13)` grid tops out below the effective shrink-to-mean regime for this spectrum, or GCV commits to an under-regularized λ. Prediction: per-(layer,fold) selected λ sits at/near a grid edge, and the widened grid alone moves R² materially toward the band.
- **H-F (added): bf16 capture quantization aliasing on massive dims** (bf16 grid spacing ~32–64 at magnitude ~1e4 can exceed a dim's true cross-example spread, turning it into structured quantization noise with large raw-scale variance). Prediction: top-variance dims show few-unique-value discreteness; excluded dims recover the score.
- **H-B (disfavored): slot/span or tokenizer-convention misalignment in the Llama/Tülu path.** Already disfavored by NLL 0.719, render-integrity PASS, and behaving nulls; retained because the spot-check is cheap and decisive. Prediction: decoded token windows at recorded `slot_idx`/`spans` mismatch the assistant-header/answer conventions on > 1% of sampled rows.
- **H-D (largely excluded): fold-mean / centering interaction.** Mean-baseline −0.0019 already bounds it; one cheap per-fold-bias confirm retained.
- **H-E (genuine absence at usable strength).** CANNOT alone explain the magnitude — a no-signal fit reads ≈ −0.02 on this very data (committed nulls) — so absence can only be concluded AFTER a mechanism accounts for the −0.93, and is then read off the corrected DV. Prediction: after correction, S sits at/below the matched standardized band and centered cosine ≈ 0.

**Registered quantities (chat cell primary; naturalistic as robustness):** S = per-dim standardized held-out pooled R² at the best swept layer (max over 32 layers), with matched band B = 97.5% quantile of 20 standardized shuffle draws' per-draw layer-max (selection-symmetric, committed convention). bar_std = 0.20 × (S_Qwen@L19 / 0.6731), the v3 G1 usable-strength bar carried to the corrected scale via the D1.6 Qwen calibration read (computed BEFORE the Llama verdict read; fallback bar_std = 0.20 if D1.6 is unavailable). Derived: S′ = S − bar_std; D = S − B, with D's 95% CI from the 1,000-draw prompt-level bootstrap on S (B is a fixed quantile). The lattice below applies to the FINAL corrected read — post-D2 re-extraction if (and only if) the capture-defect gate in §4 fires.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Map present at usable strength ⇔ S′ ≥ 0 AND D's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Map present but weak ⇔ S′ < 0 AND D's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Genuine absence ⇔ otherwise.

Every verdict — including both "present" branches — additionally REQUIRES the mechanism-attribution account of §4 D1.5 (which named variant moves the committed −0.9287 at least 80% of the way to its matched band, with the number). An unattributed anomaly ships NO verdict (§4 decision rules, outcome R5). v3 §3's registered lattice on the headline contrast C is UNCHANGED and re-applies at resume on the corrected DV; it is not re-litigated here.

Row-coverage: both arms' registered rows are supplied per-prompt by the per-cell turnstore sidecars under `analysis_tensors/turnstore_rlvr_chat_lmsys5k/` and `analysis_tensors/turnstore_rlvr_naturalistic_lmsys5k/` on the HF data-repo prefix `issue1336_rlvr_ladder/` — the committed-preds arm and every refit-variant arm read the SAME prompt-id rows and fold assignments (seed 0) from the same stores, row-count- and id-asserted against `preds_manifest.json` before any read; the carried-forward resume-path pairs re-read the same stores per v3's intersection convention.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Diagnose-before-conclude (THE amendment).** Why: −0.93 is inconsistent with both G1 branches — genuine non-transfer reads ≈ the shuffle band (−0.02, committed on this very data), so shipping "does not transfer" off −0.93 would ship an unexplained artifact as a finding. Source: `gates/g1_gate.json` + `cells/*` @ `6939726b57` (values quoted in §2); the fail-fast Critical Rule (the anomaly IS the signal). Alternatives: conclude non-transfer now (rejected — magnitude unaccounted); retry with more data (rejected — nothing suggests n is the mechanism).
- **Per-dim standardized pooled R² as the candidate corrected DV.** What: z-score each target dim by train-fold stats before pooling. Why: the pooled score and the GCV objective are raw-variance-weighted (code read: `_ridge_predict_cached` centers but never scales Y), so any variance-concentrated family breaks comparability; standardization equal-weights dims and matches the fit's own X-side convention (`_prep_fold` z-scores X). Source: code read this session (`issue825_fit_cells.py:98–127`) + arXiv 2402.17762 (massive activations: few input-agnostic dims at ~1e3–1e4, delimiter-token-concentrated, Llama family included). Validated-before-use on the healthy Qwen cell (D1.6). Alternatives: trimmed R² (kept as localization read — thresholds are arbitrary for a primary), median per-dim R² (kept descriptive — no pooled-variance interpretation).
- **Widened λ grid logspace(−2, 8, 21).** Why: the committed grid tops at 1e4 while the z-scored Gram's spectrum extends to O(n_train × d ≈ 1.19e7) — the grid may not contain an effective shrink-to-mean point, making "GCV chose to commit" and "GCV had no alternative" indistinguishable; 1e8 ≈ 8× the trace bound closes that, 21 points keep ~0.5-dex spacing.
