Why: packing RESOLUTION — whole-group greedy packing into the [0.15, 0.28] window needs group mass ≲ ~10% of the corpus so the window is always reachable; and grouping GRAIN — ~300 prompts/group holds out topic/template families rather than single prompts. - **Near-duplicate threshold: cosine ≥ 0.95 on L2-normalized `all-mpnet-base-v2` embeddings; action on a hit = CO-ASSIGN (union-find merge), merged groups → TRAIN.** Why 0.95: the semantic-deduplication literature operates cosine-similarity near-duplicate screens in pretrained embedding spaces at thresholds in the ~0.95 band (SemDeDup — arXiv 2303.09540 — deduplicates "semantic duplicates" via embedding cosine similarity at tight thresholds; the exact-duplicate limit is cos = 1.0, and our sha-dedup already removed those). Why co-assign rather than fail-loud or drop: the defect a hit names is not bad data but a leakage RISK — co-assignment (both sides of the pair on the train side) removes the risk while keeping every row; dropping would silently shrink corpora (the A2 keep-rate gate exists to prevent exactly that), and fail-loud is reserved for the structural surprises (A4 packing infeasibility, A5 merged-mass explosion). Why merged→TRAIN: conservative — the held-out side stays free of cross-corpus near-duplicate mass, so test prompts are unambiguously corpus-distinctive; magnitude is reported and bounded by A5 (≤10%/corpus, expected ≪1% given 167/48,270 exact collisions). Source: arXiv 2303.09540 (threshold regime); `epm:progress v199` item 5b (the check itself); sensitivity: report-only edge + straddle counts at 0.90, histogram in the audit block — the analyzer flags threshold-sensitivity before any headline rides on the split.
