## §0.0 Plain-English TL;DR

#722 concluded "fine-tuning reshapes the context→answer map only for a taught fact" — but it measured the trained model on answers the *base* model wrote. If fine-tuning mostly changes *what the model says* (which is what a behavior implant does), that comparison is blind by construction. This experiment regenerates the post-fine-tuning answer profiles from each trained model's **own** greedy answers (on-policy), re-runs the identical #722 fit machinery, and adds a matched-text control (teacher-force the new text through both models) so the total change per cell splits cleanly into "the weights process the same text differently" (representation change) vs "the model writes different text" (output-distribution change). A live A0 gate run (att-20260702-090420) proved the 2025-era #667 store's response legs are NOT reproducible on today's stack (greedy text drifts across engine versions), so ALL FOUR measurement legs are now regenerated and extracted in one run on one stack — the base-text legs included — making the on-vs-off contrast era-clean by construction. The headline is the on-policy-vs-off-policy **delta** in the function-change reads. No training. No judge calls. ~21 GPU-h.

## §3 Hypotheses

- **H_holds:** the verdict survives — fact stays above its regime-local refit floor (same order as 1.6–3.3×) and em/sycophancy stay at/below their regime-local floors under M⁺_on; the on-vs-off delta per behavior is within the floor band. Decomposition shows the r_B-projected text component Δ_text is small for em/syc. **Killed by:** any behavior's on-vs-off delta exceeding its REGIME-LOCAL floor band with a bootstrap CI excluding 0, AND the raw-unit delta agreeing in direction (the v3 robustness rule — a one-normalization-only departure is normalization-sensitive, not a kill).
- **H_breaks:** the off-policy "function held" reads for em/sycophancy were an artifact of the regime — M⁺_on departs from M0 above its regime-local floor for em and/or sycophancy (robust in raw units too), and the decomposition attributes the departure predominantly to Δ_text (the trained model writes different text; v⁺ over its own text lands elsewhere), i.e. the change was invisible to #722 by construction. **Killed by:** em/syc on-policy reads staying at/below their regime-local floors.
- **H_mixed:** the fact magnitude is materially re-estimated (outside #722's bootstrap band) while em/syc verdicts stand; OR the on-policy read moves but the matched-text control M0-over-R⁺ moves comparably — meaning the apparent map change is carried by the text distribution alone (the base model's map over the new text moves just as much), not by the weights. **Killed by:** fact inside #722's band AND M0-over-R⁺ flat while M⁺_on moves.

Falsifiability note: the text-divergence manipulation check (§5 row C7) makes the trivial case legible — where R⁺ ≡ R_base′ the on-policy read equals the off-policy read by construction; a near-total-identity behavior confirms H_holds *trivially* and is reported as such, not as new evidence.

**v5 band-reference note:** with all four legs same-era, "outside #722's bootstrap band" in H_mixed is read PRIMARILY against the IN-RUN M⁺_off re-fit band (identical recipe, same era — the clean paired reference); the published #722 band remains a recipe-level secondary reference and any published-number comparison carries the same-era-reproduction scope caveat (§6).

**Regime-local floors + raw deltas (Must-Fix, reconciled binding — new in v3; UNCHANGED in v5).** Phase D computes and PERSISTS, per behavior × layer, the harness's full floor namespace (already emitted by `issue722_fit_M.py` L493–542 — no fit-machinery change, this is a registration + reporting requirement): `floor_M0_refit`, `floor_Mplus_refit` computed SEPARATELY for the off-policy arm (targets v⁺(R_base′)) and the on-policy arm (targets v⁺(R⁺)), `floor_shifted`, `floor_combined`, `floor_sd_combined`, PLUS the raw r_B-projected deltas (`proj = |delta_full @ r_hat|`) with clustered-bootstrap CIs. **Normalization rule:** each arm's function-change read is expressed in ITS OWN regime's floor units — M⁺_off reads against the off-policy-arm `floor_Mplus_refit`, M⁺_on against the on-policy-arm `floor_Mplus_refit`; the M0-referenced magnitudes keep `floor_M0_refit`. The M0-refit floor is never the sole yardstick for an M⁺-variant delta (on-policy text can change fit noise independently of functional change). **Robustness rule:** the headline on-vs-off conclusion must hold in BOTH raw projected units AND regime-local floor units; a conclusion that appears only under one normalization is reported as normalization-sensitive, not as a verdict. All floors + raw deltas land in the §6.5 cell JSONs.
