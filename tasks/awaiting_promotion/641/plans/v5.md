## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

#641 left one question open: when we trained "emergent misalignment" (the model
giving harmful advice) into a *kindergarten-teacher* voice versus a plain voice
that is equally unlikely to give bad advice on its own, did the teacher's
caregiver identity fight the training? At two seeds the answer was a shrug — the
teacher landed a hair *higher* than the plain voice, but the error bars were so
wide ([−0.26, +0.31]) that genuine drag in either direction was still on the
table. Two seeds was simply not enough to tell signal from noise. This round
re-runs that one pairing at more seeds (going from 2 to 8 total), trains each the
exact same way #641 trained its two seeds (so the comparison is apples-to-apples),
reads each at the same matched amount #641 used (step 100), and re-measures the
teacher-minus-neutral gap with tighter error bars. I expect the gap to either
shrink toward zero and resolve as "no identity drag" or stay genuinely
ambiguous but with a narrower interval that tells us how much drag we can rule
out — and I'm honest that even 8 seeds may not fully close the band, because the
real limit here is how much the seed-to-seed spread costs us in a small sample.

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

**Goal — unchanged from v3** (verbatim task Goal): determine whether residual
install resistance at matched dose is driven by base propensity or by
source-behavior identity/alignment conflict — the identity-conflict pairing is
the sub-question this round resolves.

**Hypothesis (Arm B, carried forward verbatim from v3 §3 / §7):** at the fixed
matched dose (step 100), decompose residual install into base-propensity and
identity-conflict components via the teacher-vs-matched-neutral contrast.
- **H2 (identity/alignment conflict):** `kindergarten_teacher` installs EM
  *less* than the base-propensity-matched `local_historian` — ΔL = L(teacher) −
  L(neutral) is **non-trivially negative (ΔL ≤ −0.10) AND the 95% CI excludes 0**.
- **H1 (base prior — equivalence read):** identity conflict adds no meaningful
  drag — the 95% CI on ΔL is **contained within [−0.10, +0.10]** (the CI MAY
  include 0; the H1 read does NOT require "CI excludes 0" — that is the H2
  condition only).
- **AMBIGUOUS / underpowered:** anything else (wide CI overlapping both ±0.10
  boundaries, or |ΔL| ≥ 0.10 with a CI crossing 0) → reported as "underpowered
  to resolve at this N" (honest null framing), with the tighter 8-seed CI stated
  as the BOUND on the effect.

**What #641 found (the open caveat this round closes):** at N=2 seeds, ΔL = +0.029
(teacher *higher* — opposite sign from drag) with a hierarchical-bootstrap 95% CI
of **[−0.26, +0.31]** → AMBIGUOUS. The per-seed step-100 rates were teacher
{0.696, 0.591} / neutral {0.684, 0.550}; the fitted asymptotes teacher {0.586,
0.560} / neutral {0.616, 0.608}. Two seeds is the binding power limit. This round
adds seeds to shrink that CI.

```python
# scripts/issue641_dose_curves.py — main() argparse, ADD:
ap.add_argument(
    "--extra-records-roots", type=_parse_str_list, default=None,
    help="extra EVAL_ROOT dirs whose dose_curves/ are ALSO globbed by the "
         "aggregate (same-issue follow-up re-fold; e.g. the parent run's "
         "eval_results/issue_641 alongside the follow-up subdir)",
)

# _load_cell_records() — generalize the single-root glob to a list of roots:
def _load_cell_records(extra_roots=None) -> dict[...]:
    roots = [EVAL_ROOT] + [Path(r) for r in (extra_roots or [])]
    out = {}
    for root in roots:
        for comp in (root / "dose_curves").rglob("completions__*.jsonl"):
            for line in comp.read_text().splitlines():
                if not line.strip():
                    continue
                r = json.loads(line)
                key = canonicalize_source(r["source"])
                out.setdefault(key, {}).setdefault(int(r["dose_step"]), []).append(r)
    return out
# phase_aggregate passes args.extra_records_roots through.
```

even as additive, the zero-code alternative is to symlink/copy the parent's 4

```python
# phase_aggregate — BEFORE bootstrapping, over every pooled cell (parent + new):
for cell in pooled_cells:
```
