## 11. Decision Rationale

`kind: analysis` — no training hyperparameters (N/A on lr/epochs/LoRA grounding). Statistical choices, each with Source:

- **What:** EOS-margin (`z(※) − z(EOS)`) as the modeling/leaderboard DV, absolute trained state, joint fit, shift readouts derived. **Why:** gauge-invariant (cancels common-mode logit shifts), anchored to the emission threshold, and immune to the log Z compression that makes log-prob fits need an interaction term (the inline softmax-artifact diagnosis); the shift-DV marginal-correlation trap is documented (#531: same shift question gives −0.48/−0.13/+0.74 by representation). **Source:** task-body metric-critic bundle + #531 + CLAUDE.md marker-measurement rule (EOS margin as the sanctioned secondary/mechanistic channel). **Alternatives:** log-prob primary (rejected for modeling — normalizer compression; still reported as descriptive agreement panels), full-vocab KL (banned, #504).
- **What:** bootstrap replicate counts — 10,000 cell-level percentile reps with FE/residualization re-estimated per resample; 2,000 cluster-level reps per axis; 2,000 source-marginal pair reps. **Why:** matches the validated #539 corrected-reads inference exactly; CI MC error at 10,000 reps is well below the effect scales at issue. **Source:** #539 (`scripts/issue539_corrected_reads_inference.py`, code-review PASS round 3). **Alternatives:** 1,000 reps (#531's persona bootstrap — rejected for primary reads, kept as a floor for slow paths); BCa intervals (rejected — percentile is the established project convention).
- **What:** permutation counts — 10,000, two-sided, add-one formula; FE-respecting (Freedman–Lane/ter Braak residual permutation) for FE-corrected reads; Anderson & Legendre rank-residual permutation for partials; MC label permutation for n=16 marginals. **Source:** #539 (same script; Anderson & Legendre 1999 cited there). **Alternatives:** naive permutation of raw values (rejected — breaks the FE null; this was the entire point of #539's machinery).
- **What:** clustering levels — #532 panel: source (16) and bystander (26/10); #478 panel: run = cell×seed (80) and persona (35); per-coefficient primary CI = the WIDER of the two one-way cluster-bootstrap CIs, with a CGM two-way plug-in SE cross-check. **Why:** the inline session's bystander-only clustering was a named flaw; cells sharing a source are strongly dependent (push is near source-constant, which makes source clustering the binding axis for any source-spanning read). **Source:** #539 (both one-way cluster bootstraps, drawn-copy relabeling) + Cameron, Gelbach & Miller 2011 (standard two-way clustered-variance combination; textbook practice — used as cross-check only, not headline). **Alternatives:** a true simultaneous two-way bootstrap (rejected — no project precedent, awkward for resampled FE designs; CGM cross-check covers the gap), question-level clustering on #478 (subsumed: questions nest inside run×persona aggregates).
- **What:** Holm adjustment per registered primary family (D1: 3 transfer tests; D3: 5 pair-corrected reads). **Source:** #539 (Holm over its 4-test family). **Alternatives:** BH-FDR (rejected — small families, project precedent is FWER).
- **What:** seed 42 for all RNG. **Source:** #539/#531 convention.
- **What:** LOBO (26-fold leave-one-bystander-out) + LOSO (16-fold leave-one-source-out) CV, both reported. **Why:** the inline LOBO-only number cannot detect source-FE leakage into the CV score; LOSO is the axis the `pre-training forecast` label actually needs (a new source is the deployment case). **Source:** #502/#532 leave-one-class-out precedent, extended; the extension is this plan's choice (flagged as such, not inherited). Fact-checker note: #502's exact CV form is UNVERIFIED — the implementer greps #502's analysis script for the fold loop before the clean-result cites it as precedent; if absent, cite the LOBO/LOSO choice as this plan's own convention.
