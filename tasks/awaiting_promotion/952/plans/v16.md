# Plan v16 amendment: completion semantics and accepted-bank propagation

Base plan: v15. This amendment is read together with v15 and changes only the points below.

Estimated GPU-hours (total): 5.5 (one H100, upper fence 8 h), unchanged from v15.

## Accepted-bank cardinality

The complete 90-item, 1,080-prompt bank remains frozen provenance. Generation, capture, judging, and analysis include only the factorially complete source items admitted by the frozen audit gate. The current accepted set is 85 source items, hence 1,020 production prompts and 8,160 planned production draws. All code derives these realized counts and identifier sets from the mutually consistent `audit_pass` row flags and `passing_item_ids`; it must not substitute the full-bank 1,080/8,640 maxima. Every report continues to distinguish the 90-item/1,080-prompt/8,640-draw planned maximum from accepted and realized coverage.

## Development smoke interpretation

V15's phrase “10-item development split” is clarified because the sole registered source contains exactly 90 items and the primary gate requires at least 81/90 accepted items. The smoke deterministically selects ten identities from the frozen accepted set before any Qwen outcome is observed. Its seeds, generations, captures, judge packets, upload namespace, and measurements are smoke-only and excluded from every production analysis. Production then creates fresh draws for every accepted identity, including those ten identities. Thus no smoke output enters analysis and no source identity is removed from the registered accepted production universe.

## Threshold misses do not terminate remaining measurements

At the user's explicit direction, unchanged numerical quality, reliability, coverage, cap, and inferential thresholds remain the criteria for labeling a check or hypothesis pass/fail, but an ordinary threshold miss no longer terminates the rest of the planned workflow. A miss is recorded, the affected confirmatory claim is marked ineligible or indeterminate as specified by v15, and the remaining planned generation, capture, judging, decompositions, descriptive analyses, and figures continue. Valid production judgments are never selectively retried, failed bank items remain excluded, and no threshold is weakened. Recoverable schema, transport, upload, or implementation faults are repaired with provenance preserved and reverified. The workflow stops short of completion only for a genuinely unrecoverable technical or data-integrity condition that makes the remaining measurement undefined or unsafe; elapsed time, repair count, or a numerical threshold miss alone is not such a condition.
