# Plan — Issue #571: Does negative-panel breadth cause the universal end-of-answer clamp?

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll train four small marker adapters on the same software-engineer source, identical in every way except one: two of them suppress the marker on the full 15-context bystander panel, and two suppress it on just 4 contexts (one from each context class), with the total amount of suppression data held the same. Then I'll measure how hard each adapter pushes "end your answer now" on 32 personas that none of the training ever mentioned.
- **What I expect:** The 15-context adapters should reproduce the big universal end-of-answer push the parent experiment found (+13 to +15 logits on never-mentioned personas). I'm betting the 4-context adapters push much less, which would mean the breadth of the suppression panel is what spreads the side effect to everyone.
- **What would change my mind:** If both versions push the never-mentioned personas equally hard, breadth is innocent — the side effect would have to come from something else in the recipe, like how many suppression examples each context gets, and the autopsy moves to that suspect next.

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

**Goal (canonical, from frontmatter):** Test whether negative-panel breadth causes the universal end-of-answer clamp by training matched marker adapters on one source context (software-engineer, the i474 recipe held fixed) that differ only in negative-panel size — 15 bystander conditions vs a 4-condition class-spanning subset, total negative rows held at 300 — and reading the end-token logit change on the never-negative held-out personas.

**N/A — not a replication** of a published finding; this is an internal causal ablation on the project's own #474/#560 lineage.

**Hypothesis (falsifiable):** #560 registered an exposure account of the end-of-answer clamp and its falsification F2 fired: personas the training never mentioned got **+13.66** logits of end-token push (never-negative stratum mean, n=512 cells) vs **+12.46** for trained negatives — slightly inverted (contrast −1.20, adapter-cluster CI [−1.62, −0.69]). The breadth-causes-clamp account is NOT identified by that design. Prediction here:

All labels below additionally require the §7 assert-5 manipulation check to read PASS (source emission ≥ 0.8 adapter-ON, ≤ 0.1 base) on ALL FOUR adapters; any arm-level WARN, or a cross-arm source Δz_marker asymmetry > 5 logits, CAPS the primary verdict at *indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded)* — "confirmatory with implant caveat" is not a reachable outcome.

- **Confirmed (panel composition causes the clamp):** contrast (broad − narrow) is POSITIVE with persona-cluster 95% CI excluding 0, point estimate ≥ +3 logits, AND narrow-arm mean < +7. (Expected shape: broad arm inside the anchor band [+10.8, +18.9]; the #560 A2-adapter point is +14.85.)
- **Falsified (breadth does not modulate the clamp at this dose):** the contrast CI contains 0 AND both arm means lie inside the anchor band [+10.8, +18.9] (both near the broad regime). Scope: excludes breadth effects ≥ 3 logits at 15-vs-4 / fixed-total-300; small +1..+2 contributions are not ruled out. The clamp then comes from something else in the recipe (per-bystander row count, question coverage, the marker-only negative rows themselves) and the F2 autopsy moves there.
- **Inverted (evidence AGAINST breadth-as-cause):** the contrast CI excludes 0 in the NEGATIVE direction (narrow > broad). Narrated by its sign; never labeled "breadth contributes."
- **Partial (breadth contributes but does not explain):** contrast POSITIVE with CI excluding 0 AND point ≥ +3, but narrow-arm mean ≥ +7.
- **Indeterminate (catch-all):** ANY other cell — including a positive CI-excluding-0 contrast with point < +3, a CI containing 0 with either arm outside the anchor band, underpowered at 2 seeds, or an implant-strength cap from the conjunct above. No confirmatory claim; report descriptively.

These reads are on ONE DV; every affirmative label is sign-pinned and the catch-all covers all remaining cells — no contradictory gate pairs, no unmapped outcomes (the single [+10.8, +18.9] band also serves as §7 assert 6, removing the earlier band-edge mismatch). Goal-wording note: the frontmatter Goal says "software-engineer *query*"; condition A2 is the software-engineer persona *system prompt* (the body's operational spec, which this design implements). The Goal is the user's contract field and is carried verbatim; the wording slip is flagged here rather than silently re-quoted.

## 4. Controls (what makes the between-arm comparison clean)

- **Single-variable discipline:** the only diff between arms is the `bystander_ids`/`n_per_bystander` pair passed to `_build_negative_rows`; positives, recipe, schedule, checkpoint, eval, and analysis code are shared bytes. The broad arm reuses the i474 defaults so its training mix is row-identical by construction to #474's A2 loc cell at seed 42.
- **Replication anchor (rig-drift control):** broad_s42 vs #560's measured A2-adapter never-negative mean Δz_EOS = +14.85 (computed during planning from the committed `eval_results/issue_560/four_float/{trained,base}_A2.json`). Gate: within ±4 logits ([+10.8, +18.9]); outside → investigate rig drift before interpreting the contrast.
- **Positive control / manipulation check:** source-resident A2 read per adapter (on-policy emission with adapter ON ≥ 0.8, base OFF ≤ 0.1 — the #534 gate thresholds; plus the source four-float read). Confirms each arm actually implanted.
- **Base-side same-text scoring:** every slot is read with adapter ON and base on the SAME generated text (slot kind + truncation asserted identical) — the DV isolates the training-induced shift, not text drift.
- **Paired persona contrast:** both arms are scored on the identical 32 never-negative personas, so the primary contrast is paired at the persona level (removes persona main effects).
- **Seed control:** 2 seeds per arm; contrast reported pooled AND per matched seed pair.
- **Suppression diagnostics:** the M5 callback gives per-bystander negative-row loss per arm (does the narrow arm suppress its 4 bystanders harder?) — exploratory mechanism read, zero extra GPU phases.

- Per-cell value: question-mean Δz_EOS per (adapter, persona). ## 11. Decision Rationale

One `Source:` per unique value; the four cells share one recipe.

- **lr=1e-5, r=32/α=64, dropout 0.0, bs 4 × ga 4, max_length 2048, epochs-5 schedule, seed 42, 300 pos (30×10), 300 neg total, rng convention** — *Why:* recipe identity with the artifact whose side effect is under test; any change confounds the breadth contrast. *Source:* `scripts/i474_phase23_train.py` @ main (read directly — values grounded on the launch script per the #458 lesson, not a body table). *Alternatives:* marker-recipe lr ≤ 5e-6 + band-stop rejected — see next item.
- **Stop = ep1 checkpoint of the 5-epoch cosine schedule; band callback in log-only mode (`marker_band_stop=True`, `marker_band_log_only=True`)** — *Why:* (1) the clamp finding lives at #474's ep1 checkpoints; (2) a band-stop would halt the two arms at different steps (different negative mixes → different loss surfaces), smuggling a second variable into a single-variable design; (3) the DV is the off-source end-token LOGIT, demonstrated graded and non-saturated in exactly this regime (#560: Δz_EOS +2.80..+20.95, 557 cells below ceiling) — the saturation hazard the band-stop exists to prevent does not bind this DV; (4) the band-stop default (commit `82ce9d64f`, 2026-06-08) postdates #474's training (fixes through 2026-06-02), so disabling the STOP behavior preserves the historical training path. Log-only mode keeps the callback's per-step source marker log-prob + emission trajectory (WandB + local JSON) — the marker rule's mandated dynamics channel ("speed-of-learning distinguishes recipes that look identical at the end" — exactly a two-recipe endpoint comparison) — while NEVER setting `should_training_stop`; probe forwards run in eval mode with lora_dropout=0.0, so the optimizer trajectory is untouched. In-loop bystander probes are deliberately NOT added — the downstream on-policy eval covers bystanders per `marker-training-recipe.md`. This is the marker-recipe §"recipe vs parent-parity" carve-out applied in the direction the rule intends: the stopping choice is grounded in measurement validity for THIS DV, named here and in §12, with comparability kept on the eval side. *Source:* #474 (recipe) + #560 (regime validity) + `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` (read in full). *Alternatives:* band-stop [5,12] rejected (above); full 5-epoch runs rejected as 4× compute for unused checkpoints (allowed fallback, §13).
- **Stop-after-ep1 via callback (epochs=5 kept for the scheduler)** — *Why:* `num_train_epochs=5` fixes the cosine schedule's total-step denominator, so the ep1 trajectory is step-for-step the #474 path; halting after the ep1 upload discards only unused epochs. *Source:* #474 schedule semantics (HF `TrainingArguments`, `lr_scheduler_type="cosine"` in `sft.py` L1062). *Alternatives:* `--epochs 1` rejected (cosine would decay to 0 within epoch 1 — a different recipe).
- **Narrow panel = {A1, B1, C1, D1} × 75** — *Why:* class-spanning 4-condition subset (one per class A/B/C/D), matching the 4-negative cardinality of the #553/#478 lineage whose held-out clamp anchor is ~−3.1, with total rows held at 300 (1:1 ratio preserved, the load-bearing floor-escape ratio). Includes the bare-default-equivalent A1 ("You are a helpful assistant.") — the single highest-value negative per the contrastive rule. *Source:* task #571 body (clarifier-resolved) + `contrastive-negatives.md` (ratio, default-assistant inclusion) + #553/#478 (4-negative anchor). *Alternatives:* 4 same-class (A-only) negatives rejected — wouldn't span the classes the broad panel spans, conflating breadth with class coverage.
- **n=75 sampling = full-coverage duplication (30×2 + 15 sampled)** — *Why:* maximizes question-coverage parity with the broad arm's sampled-20 within the 30-question pool; even per-question weighting up to the remainder. *Source:* ungrounded — needs smoke-test (new code path; structural asserts + canary cell cover it). *Alternatives:* sampling 75 with replacement rejected (uneven question weights add variance for no benefit).
- **Seeds 42, 43; 2 per arm** — *Why:* seed 42 = #474 parity (broad_s42 is the replication anchor); 43 adds the minimal seed-noise read the body pins; persona-level pairing (32 clusters) carries the inference. *Source:* #571 body + #474 (seed 42).
- **Eval: 35 × 20 @ `a9fc5a9`, greedy, max_tokens 2048, four-float storage, never-negative-32 primary set** — *Why:* the #560 eval surface unchanged is what makes the arm contrast interpretable against #560's clamp numbers. *Source:* #560 (`issue560_crossrecipe_panel.py` + its Reproducibility table); `max_tokens=2048` also satisfies the ≥2× rule (#260).
- **Marker token ` ※` id 83399; `<|im_end|>` id 151645** — *Source:* `marker-leakage-measurement.md` + asserted at dispatch.
- **Bootstrap n=10,000, percentile, persona clusters** — *Source:* #560 inference conventions (`transfer_i474.json` metadata).
- **Model id** — repo-used (`configs/`, #474/#560); no `AutoConfig` smoke needed.
