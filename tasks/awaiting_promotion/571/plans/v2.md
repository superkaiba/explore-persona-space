## 3. Hypothesis + falsification

The parent's composition effect bundles context-TYPE diversity (persona prompts, question wraps, bare template, rewrites) with per-context row count. Stage 1 asks whether base-model geometry organizes the parent's own per-persona outcomes; Stage 2 isolates the persona-split sub-axis within one context type.

**Stage-1 predictions (on the parent's committed 4 adapters; n=32 never-mentioned personas):**

- **H1a (gradient):** per-persona clamp (Δz_EOS) and marker emission track distance-to-nearest-negative, controlling distance-to-source, within arm × seed.
- **H1b (geometry):** the cross-arm paired identification — per-persona change in clamp/hijack between broad and narrow arms regressed on the change in nearest-negative distance (distance-to-source held constant by design; the panel shrink moves only the negative set) — resolves toward bubble (change organized by Δd_nn) or barrier (within-arm leakage rises with distance-to-source controlling nearest-negative, per the scope marker's shell convention).
- **Stage-1 falsification (registered, decidable):** neither partial gradient resolves ⇒ geometry unidentified on context-typed negatives; Stage 2's persona-typed panels become the identification vehicle. Known resolution limits stated up front, not post-hoc: narrow-arm emission sits at ceiling (92–96%), so the emission DV carries information mainly in the broad arm (the clamp logit is fine in both); context-typed negatives may give limited distance spread — the realized spread (per-arm d_nn median/IQR, Δd_nn distribution) is reported alongside every read.

**Stage-2 predictions (expected directions, from the #571 trade):**

- **H2a (clamp):** never-negative Δz_EOS rises with split count — registered 8-vs-2 contrast positive.
- **H2b (hijack):** held-out marker hijack falls with split count (more persona-space coverage generalizes suppression to unseen personas).
- **H2c (geometry, persona-typed):** the per-bystander leakage CHANGE between arms is organized by Δd_nn (bubble) rather than d_src (barrier).

**Stage-2 verdict lattice (one registered primary read per channel; jointly satisfiable; catch-all):**

- *Split axis active (per channel):* the 8-vs-2 paired persona contrast CI (10,000-draw persona-cluster bootstrap over the 32 never-negative personas) excludes 0 AND |point| exceeds the run-noise yardstick (clamp: the largest within-arm seed-pair mean gap observed in THIS run; hijack: CI excluding 0 with |point| ≥ 10 pp). Direction labeled explicitly (expected vs inverted).
- *Split axis inert (the scope marker's Stage-2 falsification):* clamp 8−2 contrast CI CONTAINS 0 (with |point| ≤ the largest within-arm seed-pair gap) AND the hijack channel reads inert-eligible no-difference. **Saturation routing (registered):** if both compared arms' hijack rates are ceiling- or floor-compressed (both points outside [5%, 95%]), the rate proxy has no dynamic range and its CI is NOT inert-eligible on its own — the hijack inert read then additionally requires the non-saturating EOS-margin companion contrast (Δ(z_marker − z_eos), 8−2 paired persona contrast, same bootstrap) to have a CI containing 0. Read (deliberately scoped): persona-split count 2–8 at fixed total 300 does not move the side effects within this nested panel family; the named next decomposition is type-diversity at matched count.
- *Underpowered discrimination (routes to indeterminate, NOT inert):* clamp contrast CI excludes 0 but |point| ≤ the seed-pair yardstick — the registered measurement says the contrast is nonzero while this run cannot distinguish it from seed-scale variation. Narrated descriptively; no inert attribution, no affirmative label.
- *Geometry unidentified:* neither barrier nor bubble partial resolves in Stage 2 either ⇒ reported as "geometry unidentified even with persona-typed negatives; needs dedicated near/far/spread placement arms as a future round."
- *Indeterminate (catch-all):* any other cell, including a manipulation-check cap — descriptive report, no confirmatory claim.

All Stage-2 affirmative labels additionally require the manipulation check to PASS on ALL SIX adapters (source emission ≥ 0.8 ON, ≤ 0.1 base; parent measured 1.00/0.00) and max pairwise cross-arm source Δz_marker asymmetry ≤ 5 logits (parent convention; parent observed 3.3); a WARN or asymmetry breach caps the primary verdicts at indeterminate (implant-strength-confounded).

Computed over the parent's exact 32 never-negative personas.

**Registered analyses:**

1. **Nearest-negative gradient (H1a):** per-persona Δz_EOS AND emission rate regressed on d_nn controlling d_src — partial Spearman within each arm × seed (4 cells, n=32). Collinearity (COMPUTED AT PLAN TIME from the committed #560 geometry, plan-construction §4.1): Pearson(d_nn, d_src) = 0.635 (broad) / 0.996 (narrow) over the 32 never-negative personas — both exceed the 0.6 gate, so the residualized path below IS the registered primary Stage-1 path, not a contingency. (a) Broad-arm cells: the registered gradient statistic is Spearman(leakage, resid(d_nn)), where resid(d_nn) = residuals of d_nn on a linear+quadratic fit of d_src; inference = permutation p (10,000 draws, persona as unit) + bootstrap CI, occupying the same Holm slot as the partial it replaces (2 geometry reads per channel); the tercile-bucket median read is reported as a descriptive robustness companion only. (b) Narrow-arm cells are a priori DEGENERATE for within-cell gradient identification (collinearity 0.996 — d_nn is an affine function of d_src to within noise): pre-declared descriptive-only and EXCLUDED from the registered Stage-1 verdict. The registered Stage-1 verdict therefore rides on the broad-arm within-cell residualized gradients (2 seeds) plus the cross-arm paired identification (analysis 2), which needs no d_src partial (d_src constant within persona by construction).
2. **Barrier-vs-bubble (H1b):** (i) within-arm partials — barrier: Spearman(leakage, d_src | d_nn) positive per the scope marker's shell convention; bubble: Spearman(leakage, d_nn | d_src), sign reported; (ii) **cross-arm paired identification** — per-persona Δ(clamp) and Δ(hijack) (broad − narrow, per matched seed and pooled) regressed on Δd_nn across the 32 personas; d_src is constant within persona by design, and the arm main effect is a constant that drops out of the correlation.
3. **#472 retest:** Spearman(leakage, d_src) per arm — directly comparable (rank-based) to #472's −0.52, now in the emission regime.

**Inference:** permutation p (10,000 draws, persona as unit) + bootstrap CIs; Holm over the 2 geometry partials within each channel (clamp = primary channel here, since emission is ceiling-limited in the narrow arm; emission reads carry full weight only in the broad arm — stated in every figure caption). Realized distance spread reported with every read.
