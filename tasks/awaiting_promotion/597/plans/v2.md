# Plan amendment — #597 follow-up `svd-per-checkpoint-titration-read` (plan v2 amendment; parent plan sections carry over by reference)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** No new training. I take the model snapshots that experiment #597 already saved every few training steps, and for each snapshot I measure *how* the training changed the model's internal state under each of the 25 persona contexts — asking whether all the personas got pushed in one shared internal direction, whether the size of each persona's push tracks how similar that persona already was to the trained one, and whether that picture changes right at the point in training where the behavior suddenly installs.
- **What I expect:** Early in training, one shared direction with similarity-graded push sizes; around the installation point the direction turns and the similarity grading washes out; and the version trained with "don't do it" counter-examples should have its push pattern shaped by those counter-examples while the plain version doesn't.
- **What would change my mind:** If the early snapshots show no shared direction at all (the push pattern is scattered from the very first measurable step), the simple one-direction model of leakage is wrong even where it was supposed to be safest — that would be the most useful surprise.

## 3. Hypotheses (falsifiable, per the followup spec)

- **H1 (low-dose rank-one with graded gate).** At the earliest *above-floor* positive-only checkpoints (steps 4–16, pre/early-onset), the top-singular-value share clears the sign-flip null AND exceeds the same cell's endpoint (step-528) share, and Spearman ρ(per-context shift norm, centered base cosine to source) > 0 in ≥5/6 sources (sign test p = 0.031 at 6/6, p = 0.11 at 5/6 — 6/6 is the pre-registered pass). **Falsified if** early top-share ≤ endpoint share in most sources (no concentration decay) or early gate ρ is ~0/negative across sources while above floor.
- **H2 (rotation + gate collapse at the cliff).** The consecutive-checkpoint top-direction cosine has its minimum inside steps 12–40 (the cliff/onset window), and the gate ρ trajectory drops from its early value toward ~0 across the same window, in most sources. **Falsified if** the top direction is stable (all above-floor consecutive cosines ≥ 0.9) through the cliff, or the gate grading never exists (H1 already failed) so there is nothing to collapse.
- **H3 (arm contrast — the effective key).** In the contrastive arm, per-context shift magnitude is increasingly better ranked by cos(v_c, v_src − mean v_neg) than by cos(v_c, v_src) as steps grow (Δρ = ρ_corrected − ρ_raw rises and is positive at late checkpoints in ≥5/6 sources), while the positive-only arm's Δρ stays ≤ 0. **Falsified if** the two predictors are indistinguishable in both arms or the ordering is reversed. (Power note: the two predictors are themselves correlated over n = 24–25 contexts; this is a descriptive paired read aggregated by cross-source sign test, not a regression claim.)

All three are **within-flavor** (fixed base text) and **single-seed** (seed 42) — per the spec's conditional, reported at the seeds available with the seed gap as a scope caveat; no retraining.

- **Phase 0 (port, CPU, no pod):** copy `src/explore_persona_space/analysis/svd_direction_constancy.py` (pure numpy, 307 lines) from `issue-602@d2e0bdf21` onto `issue-597` verbatim (same port-commit pattern #602 used). - **Phase A (base side, once):** load base model; for each of the 25 contexts × 50 questions, build the teacher-forced sequence with the parent's byte-identity-pinned builder (`leakage_dynamics_597/panel_probe.py::build_slot_context` / `load_probe_rows` on `probe_rows.json`, schema `i597_probe_rows_v1`, fields `q` + `r_base`), run ONE forward with `output_hidden_states=True`, store per row: slot + mean-over-response residuals at layers {7, 14, 21, 27} (fp16), plus the slot logits' four floats for the base-identity smoke. - **Phase B (ladders):** for each of 12 units (2 arms × 6 sources; slugs `armB_pos_only` / `armA_contrastive` matching #597), enumerate checkpoints via the parent's `enumerate_ladder` + per-checkpoint LoRA hot-swap (the parent's pattern, end-of-ladder hot-swap invariant re-asserted), run trained-side forwards on the SAME 1,250 sequences, subtract the cached Phase-A base reads → per-context per-question slot shifts; persist per (unit, checkpoint): per-context mean Δv at all 4 layers × 2 poolings, split-half mean pair at layer-14 slot, per-question norms (≈1 GB fp16 total across 396 checkpoint-cells). Download each unit's ladder, then delete before the next (MooseFS quota).
- **Phase C (upload, pod-side, before termination):** shift tensors + base bank → HF data repo `issue597_leakage_dynamics/analysis_tensors/` (the #521 lost-tensors rule is the reason this experiment is possible at all — do not skip); per-checkpoint summary JSONs → git `eval_results/issue_597/svd-per-checkpoint-titration-read/`. - **Phase D (analysis, off-pod on the VM, CPU only):** `assemble_M` per checkpoint-cell (drop the `no_persona` column in the `qwen_default` cell — render-identical to the source, the parent's exclusion convention; M is d×25 elsewhere, source column included as in #521) → `svd_summary` + both nulls (1,000 reps) → top-share, cos-to-U1, LOO source-dropped share; gate ρ via `shift_norm_vs_cosine_regression` against centered (primary) and raw-labeled (comparability) cosines; rotation track cos(U1(k), U1(k+1)) + cos(U1(k), U1(528)) using `svd_summary`'s mean-column sign convention; H3 Δρ tracks with negatives read from the cell's train-pool JSONL (2 nearby personas + no-persona, row-weighted 2:2:1 mean as pre-registered key, unweighted as robustness); join to #597's behavioral per-checkpoint gains (same rows) for the located-against-cliff figures.

**Below-floor mask (pre-registered):** a checkpoint-cell's geometry DVs are read only where median-across-contexts of ‖Δv‖ / s_half ≥ 3, with s_half = ‖half₁ − half₂‖/2 per context from the split-half pair; below-floor cells are reported as "below measurement floor", never as low top-share.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Layers {7,14,21} + primary 14** — Source: #521/#551 (the contrast held at all three; primary 14 is the producing pipeline's `DEFAULT_LAYER`). **+ layer 27** — Source: persona-distance rule sweep set {7,14,21,27}; marginal cost zero (same forward). Alternatives: late-band-only (19–24, P5's marker band) rejected — breaks #521 comparability.
- **Text flavor = fixed base text (injected `r_base`)** — Source: #521 "base" variant + #597's primary-DV rows. Why: trajectory comparability requires fixed text; reuses the exact rows the behavioral DV was read on; base side computed once. Alternative (trained-text, #521's primary flavor) rejected: 396 ckpt-cells × 1,250 greedy generations is ~10× the compute and confounds rotation with text drift. Named flavor caveat carried to the clean-result.
- **Pooling slot (primary) + mean-resp (secondary)** — Source: #521 slot read + #551 M3a whole-response read; slot is the position #597's marker DV predicts at.
- **Panel = #597's 25 contexts × 50 questions** — Source: #597 (the behavioral join requires identical rows). Alternative (#521's 14×20) rejected: would break the join and shrink n.
- **Centered bank cosine + raw alongside** — Source: #536 rule (canonical) + #521 comparability (its 0.86–1.0 compressed band is the raw regime). The bank includes `no_persona`/`assistant` as ordinary members; no single "neutral prompt" axis is constructed (memory: neutral-prompt conflation).
- **Corrected key = v_src − row-weighted mean(v_neg), weights 2:2:1** — Source: #597's realized pool composition (400+400+100... per-source 200/200/100 rows across the two nearby personas + no-persona); unweighted mean as robustness. Negatives read from the realized pool files, not plan prose (#527 disjointness lesson).
- **Nulls 1,000 reps; sign-flip as the cleaner floor for concentrated cells** — Source: #521/#551.
- **Smoke tolerance 0.1 nat** — Source: #597's own smoke gate (0.067/0.079 achieved); ours adds margin for fp16 persistence.
- **Floor threshold ‖Δv‖/s_half ≥ 3** — Source: ungrounded — needs smoke-test; standard 3-sigma heuristic, calibrated at smoke time against the zero-shift pass and reported per cell (listed in §12 at Low confidence).
- **Model id** — repo-inherited (#597); no `AutoConfig` smoke needed.
- Marker-recipe-overrides-parent-parity: N/A — no fresh marker training.
