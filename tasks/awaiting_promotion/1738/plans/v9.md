## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I ask the model the SAME 20 real questions after each of 5,000 real
  multi-turn conversation histories (100k answers), so I can average away the question and
  measure how much of the answer's internal state the history alone determines — the read
  the main corpus (one question per history) cannot express.
- **What I expect:** the per-question full-context map applied to averaged inputs predicts
  averaged answer states well (#1092: 0.82–0.94); the question explains far more answer
  variance than the history (#1092: ~79/11/10); history + bare-question stitched together
  recover most but not all of the full-context map (#1092: 0.833 vs 0.910).
- **What would change my mind:** a much larger history share on real multi-turn data, or an
  averaged map failing where #1092's succeeded, would mean the "query dominates" picture
  does not transfer to real conversations.
