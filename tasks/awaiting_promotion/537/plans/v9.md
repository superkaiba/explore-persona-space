## 7. Decision Gates

**No stop-the-run gates — pre-verified infrastructure + a graceful descope ladder instead.** The grid, harness, and forward-pass primitives are all validated in the parent run; the new work is additive registry rows + base-model forward-pass scripts that reuse `score_span_logprob`, plus two zero-GPU control rows. Per-script smoke (`--smoke`, 1 behavior × 2 contexts) precedes each full pass; a fact-row cross-check against `fact_span_tf/_base/` validates the conditioned/A6 forward code; the A5 sparse-storage size is validated at smoke. None of these is a kill gate — a failure means "fix the bug and re-run." The ONE quantitative guard is the quarantine final-test split (touch at most once, after the dev leaderboard is locked).

**The compute descope ladder (§9) is the graceful-degradation path, NOT a gate — but a descope of an IMPLEMENTABLE class forces a PARTIAL verdict (v9).** If the A5 passes overrun, drop A5_rb → A5 → the refusal/em rows in that order, log the descope as a finding. **A logged compute descope of a fully-implementable predictor class does NOT silently certify a full leaderboard:** the leaderboard ships with the descoped rows named `descoped` rather than silently missing, AND the overall verdict is downgraded to **PARTIAL / completed-subset** — the "best predictor overall" / win-matrix headline is scoped to "best AMONG the implemented set, classes {…} descoped," and Deliverables 1 and 2 are marked **partial (not satisfied)** (§3 kill-criterion part a, §1 completeness scoping, §9 descope ladder). A `descoped` row tag alone does NOT satisfy the completeness deliverable. (Per the autonomous halt-criterion contract, a fully-implementable class dropped purely for compute that the autonomous session judges to threaten the headline routes to a max-info-gain decision — descope the cheapest implementable class first and ship a PARTIAL result, or, if the descope would gut the headline, re-plan; never silently relabel a partial result as full.)

## 9. Resources & Parallelism

- **GPU:** the GPU drivers are the new dropped-row forward passes (A5/A5_rb output distributions, A2 train-completion priors, A6 taught-span, `pv_dp`/`kl_out_seq_oneway`) + the 3 v7 conditioned/extraction passes, all base-model `Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct`, TP=1. **The 26-row extension, the activation-variant grid, predictor-3 Phase-B projection, the two v9 control rows, ALL scoring, the leave-family-out slice, and the win matrix (+ its permutation nulls) are ZERO GPU** (geometry rows are behavior-independent reuse + the projections reuse on-HF activations + the controls are a seeded permutation / prompt-string Jaccard + CPU off-pod).

The smaller `eval` (1 GPU) spec was rejected — the A5 × 5-behavior workload is large enough to amortize a 4-GPU pod and the wall-time matters.
