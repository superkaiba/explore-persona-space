## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll teach the same base model five different habits (a typographic tic, a made-up fact, blanket refusal, agreeing with wrong claims, and giving harmful advice), each one separately under sixteen different "situations" — persona instructions, real chat-history snippets, worked examples, rewordings, formatting rules, and a plain no-instructions setup. Then I measure how much each trained habit shows up under thirty situations, including some the training never saw — two of those are real chat histories far longer than anything in training, to check whether the habits (and the rulers that predict them) still behave when the conversation gets very long. The result is a big lookup table of "trained here, leaks there," shipped with all the models, training files, and a referee program so future prediction methods can be scored fairly. New in this revision: a much larger panel of candidate "similarity rulers" gets scored against the table from day one (including a family derived from a specific theory of how the habit physically writes itself into the network), the automated graders get checked against human labels and tested for formatting blind spots before anything is locked in, and two direct tests of that theory's mechanical predictions run alongside the main table.
- **What I expect:** Habits should leak more into situations that resemble the training one, leak less when the training situation openly named the habit, and the leak pattern should run in one direction more than the other for at least some habits. I also expect the theory-derived rulers to earn their place on the leaderboard rather than just adding rows. The wrong-claims habit will probably leak everywhere, which is itself useful to know.
- **What would change my mind:** If the leak numbers barely differ across situations once I account for run-to-run noise, the table has no structure worth predicting and v1 is noise-limited — I check that on the cheapest habit first, before spending most of the budget. And if the theory's two mechanical predictions fail while its scalar rulers still score well, the rulers work for reasons the theory doesn't explain — that distinction is itself a deliverable.

## 3. Hypothesis

The testbed's own pre-registered reads (candidate-metric science beyond the registered set happens in follow-up tasks against the shipped harness):

- **H-structure (the kill-relevant one).** Between-eval-context variance of G[b, i→·] (averaged over i, off-diagonal) vs pooled between-seed variance, computed with the noise-corrected (cross-seed) variance ratio, read descriptively per row. The ship/no-ship kill applies ONLY to the marker row (3 seeds, continuous DV) — if THAT row's context variance fails to clear 2× corrected seed variance, the tensor is noise-limited; escalate rather than ship. Flat sycophancy (and possibly EM) is expected texture, NOT a kill.
- **H-proximity (Betley gradient; harness validation).** Marker row: Spearman ρ between off-diagonal G and `gauss_kl_act`@L22 distance ≤ −0.4 (same sign as #502's −0.79). Strongly violated (|ρ| < 0.15 or sign flip) → suspect a harness bug before a science claim.
- **H-inoculation (sign flip; arXiv 2510.04340).** mean_j≠F7 G[b, F7_b→j] < mean_j G[b, default→j], per row; reported BOTH raw and diagonal-normalized (selective inoculation vs implant dilution distinguished). Marker row is the dilution-controlled anchor (subject to §4.1b).
- **H-asymmetry.** Antisymmetric fraction of off-diagonal variance (16×16 shared-instance block) ≥ 10% in ≥3 of 5 behaviors — seed-split cross-correlation (noise-corrected) estimator; raw fraction never narrated (#502's 28% was single-seed). Strength-difference antisymmetry (ΔG_anti ∝ s_i − s_j) reported alongside.
- **H-behavior-dependence.** Median pairwise Spearman between per-behavior z-normalized off-diagonal G matrices < 0.7, disattenuated via seed-split reliability. Licenses the operational claim ("a behavior-blind metric cannot suffice on these organisms"), not the mechanism claim.
- **Baseline reads (not gates).** ΔP (context-blind) scores ≈ 0 R² on within-row j-variance by construction. Bystander base-prior expected to dominate on F8-eval columns (#532's preview).
- **Mechanism predictions (v4, registered; from the v_b = M·v_c rank-1 framing, #526).** (i) G1: at matched implant strength the rank-1 patch predicts `leak(i→j)/leak(j→i) = ‖v_j‖²/‖v_i‖²`. **Registered convention (pinned to avoid a factor-of-2 ambiguity):** with ΔG_anti ≡ ½(G[i→j] − G[j→i]) (the §6 DV-table definition), the prediction is ΔG_anti = (log‖v_j‖ − log‖v_i‖), i.e. **predicted slope ≈ 1 when regressing noise-corrected ΔG_anti on Δlog‖v‖ ≡ log‖v_j‖ − log‖v_i‖** (equivalently: the full difference G[i→j] − G[j→i] on 2Δlog‖v‖). **The quantitative slope test is registered on the marker row ONLY**, where G is already Δlog P in nats and the identification leak ≡ e^G is exact; for the four rate-delta rows (fact/refusal/sycophancy/EM) raw G lives in [0,1]-delta space where log-leak is undefined at the many ≤0 off-diagonal cells (dropping them would be a selection effect correlated with the regressor), so **rate rows carry a sign/ordinal G1 read only** (sign agreement of ΔG_anti with Δlog‖v‖ + rank correlation), labeled as such. The strength-difference read (s_i − s_j) is the registered competing explanation in a JOINT regression (both regressors in one model, partial coefficients reported — never two marginal regressions). (ii) G2: trained−base residual deltas at the readout slot are mutually parallel across eval contexts (high pairwise cosine of Δh(c_j)), with magnitudes ∝ the projection coefficient. **A parallelism FAILURE alongside a working scalar ladder = the leakage rule works for non-rank-1 reasons — that distinction is itself the mechanism deliverable, not a kill.**

### 4.1b Band-reachability protocol (marker row; unchanged from v3)

The [5,12]-nat band is trained − base under the cell's own train context, so high-base-prior cells cannot enter it: `binst_marker` (base emits ※ on 100/100 probes under #532's string), `icl_k8` (G0 requires ≥60% base expression), plausibly `icl_k2`. Marker-row manipulation check is band-based: source ΔlogP ≥ 4 nat + bystander resolution — NOT emission ≥ 0.30 (#478: 0/256 inside the band). Marker training logs per-step source logP AND emission-rate trajectories at band-stop cadence (marker-dynamics rule).

### 4.5b Eval-side long-prefix handling (v5; eval-only — NO training-side changes)

- **Window arithmetic:** worst case = 9,000 (prefix) + ~50 (question + template) + 2,048 (`max_new_tokens`) ≈ 11.1k tokens ≪ Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct's 32,768-token window (`max_position_embeddings: 32768`, read from the local HF config 2026-06-09). No RoPE scaling, no architecture issue.
- **Generation (vLLM, all rows):** eval `max_model_len` set explicitly to **16,384** for every generation pass that includes the v5 columns (covers worst case with margin; vLLM paged KV handles the length — prefill on the long columns is the slowdown, not memory). - **Slot scoring (HF forwards, marker row + G2/TF passes):** KV at bf16 with GQA = 2 × 28 layers × 4 KV heads × 128 head-dim × 2 B ≈ 56 KB/token → ~0.6 GB per 11k-token sequence; the binding constraint is the full-vocab logits tensor (`batch × seq × 152,064`), so the long columns run at **batch 4–8** (vs 32 default) and/or slice logits to the slot positions (`logits_to_keep`-style) — implementer's choice, validated by the P1 smoke peak-memory log on a single H100 80GB.
- **Checks:** G0 render check asserts chat-template role alternation at the new lengths; G0 logs `prefix_token_len` + a peak-memory probe for one xxlong forward; G1 smoke re-measures throughput with the long columns included. **No-truncation assert per eval path:** at G0, for one xlong + one xxlong instance in EACH forward path that consumes the long columns (vLLM generation, HF slot scoring, the G2/TF pass, the cloud/first-token dump), assert `len(input_ids) == rendered_token_len` with tokenizer truncation disabled — a silent `max_length` default in any one path would clip the prefix while `prefix_token_len` (logged at render time) still reports full length, and the resulting artifact mimics "no length attenuation", corrupting exactly the extrapolation read.

### 6.4 Registered rank-1 mechanism tests (G — derived from the v_b = M·v_c framing, #526)

The leaderboard rows are scalar-leakage regressions; these two test the rank-1 update form itself.

**G1. ΔG_anti vs context-norm differences (registered; zero GPU).** The rank-1 patch predicts a specific GEOMETRIC antisymmetry signature: `leak(i→j)/leak(j→i) = ‖v_j‖²/‖v_i‖²`. **Pinned registered convention:** ΔG_anti ≡ ½(G[i→j] − G[j→i]) (the §6 DV-table definition), so under the rank-1 algebra ΔG_anti = Δlog‖v‖ ≡ log‖v_j‖ − log‖v_i‖ and the **predicted slope is ≈ 1 when regressing noise-corrected ΔG_anti on Δlog‖v‖** (equivalently, the full difference on 2Δlog‖v‖ — never mix the two conventions). **The quantitative slope test is registered on the marker row only** (G is Δlog P in nats; identification leak ≡ e^G exact). The four rate-delta rows carry a **sign/ordinal read only** (sign agreement + rank correlation of ΔG_anti with Δlog‖v‖) — log-leak is undefined at the many ≤0 off-diagonal rate cells and dropping them would be a selection effect correlated with the regressor. Registered model: JOINT regression with the strength-difference regressor (s_i − s_j) — partial coefficients reported, never two marginal regressions; saturation/ceiling-flagged cells excluded per the standing flags. The norm regressor `‖v_i‖` is pinned to the **L22 × mean_response anchor** clouds (the §6.3 primary; other layer/anchor combos are exploratory robustness reads, labeled). Connects the norm-ratio ladder rung to the antisymmetric machinery; norms come from the P1 clouds.

**G2. Activation-delta parallelism (marker row registered, ~0 GPU; judge rows exploratory-but-run, +~4–5 GPU-h).** v_b = M·v_c + rank-1 update predicts the behavior-direction change at EVERY target context is the SAME direction (v_b″ − v_b′), scaled by the projection coefficient — so trained−base residual deltas at the readout slot should be mutually parallel across eval contexts, with magnitudes ∝ the projection coefficient. The base clouds do NOT cover this — it needs TRAINED-model activations: (i) **marker row (registered):** hidden-state hooks on the existing Stage-1 (base) and Stage-2 (per-adapter) cross-eval forwards (§4.5), dumping the residual vector at the post-response slot for layer subset {6, 14, 22, 27} (~1–2 GB; no new forwards). Registered reads: pairwise cosine of Δh(c_j) across the 30 eval contexts (v5 count; incl. the two long-prefix held-outs) (parallelism), and ‖Δh(c_j)‖ vs projection coefficient (scaling). **Registered reference scales (a raw "high cosine" is uninterpretable in 3584-dim anisotropic space — a success needs these, not just a failure): noise CEILING = same-cell cross-seed Δh cosines (the marker row's 3 seeds support this); common-mode FLOOR = cross-adapter Δh cosines at the same context (different train-context adapters; generic-LoRA/common-mode drift) + an anisotropy null from base-side probe/context deltas at the same slot and layer; the registered parallelism read is the EXCESS of registered cosines over the common-mode floor, reported against both references (never raw); a robustness read removes the across-context mean Δh before the cosine. All computable from the shipped dumps — zero extra GPU. Early-layer reads (L6/L14) carry the mechanism weight: at late layers any working implant produces a shared `W_U[※]` component, so late-layer parallelism is near-tautological.** (ii) **judge rows (exploratory-but-run):** one small batched teacher-forced HF pass per adapter immediately after its existing vLLM eval, while the merged dir is still on disk (slot = first response token / taught-span first token), ~+4–5 GPU-h across 136 adapters; same reads. A parallelism FAILURE with a working scalar ladder = the leakage rule works for non-rank-1 reasons — that distinction is the mechanism deliverable.

Two gates, unchanged from v3 (wall > 4 h, genuinely uncertain components, cheap intermediate signals); jointly satisfiable (both demand "implant works + harness sane"). **G1 (end of P1 marker row, blocks P2's ~80 GPU-h):** (i) of the band-REACHABLE marker train cells (expected 13–14 of 16), all but at most one pass band-stop + the band-based manipulation check (source ΔlogP ≥ 4 nat, bystander resolution); band-UNREACHABLE cells gate on bystander resolution only; (ii) H-proximity sanity: marker off-diag G vs `gauss_kl_act`@L22 on the #537 battery has ρ ≤ −0.15 (weak harness-bug tripwire; sign grounded in #502's −0.79); (iii) smoke-calibrated eval rate ≥ 0.12 Qs/s/GPU (below → descope ladder §9 before P2, not a kill). **Success criteria (quantitative):** (1) G tensor delivered at ≥80% cell coverage per surviving row with full per-cell metadata; (2) marker-row context structure: noise-corrected between-context variance ≥ 2× corrected seed variance; (3) H-proximity: marker off-diag ρ ≤ −0.4 vs `gauss_kl_act`@L22 (harness validation); (4) inoculation sign-flip readable (raw + diagonal-normalized) in the marker row and reported per row elsewhere; (5) full §6.1 leaderboard + §6.2 registered combinations + §6.4 G1/G2 registered rows scored under the quarantined harness with namespaced ids; (6) judge rows ship with calibration provenance (human-gold or flagged fallback); (7) pre-registration freeze + quarantine manifests committed before any training.

**Kill criteria:** (1) marker-row H-structure fails (context variance < 2× corrected seed variance) → tensor is noise-limited; do NOT ship as ground truth; escalate with `epm:failure v1`; (2) G0 drops a full behavior row or >2 train contexts → park (grid shape is the user-locked contract); (3) G1 fires (≥2 reachable-cell implant failures or sign-flipped proximity) → diagnose before spending P2; (4) marker eval rate < 0.12 Qs/s/GPU after the batched-path smoke AND the descope ladder cannot hold the run ≤ envelope → park with measured rates. A G2 parallelism failure is explicitly NOT a kill (it is the mechanism finding).
