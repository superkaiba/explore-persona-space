- **What I'll run:** The completed run found that the benign and misalignment models' top shift directions overlap at cosine 0.50 when probed at the end of the response — right on the decision boundary. It also found that each probe position has its own dominant direction. So the 0.50 overlap might be a fact about the end-of-response position only. - **What I expect:** Genuinely uncertain — that is the point. - **What would change my mind:** A floor-level overlap (median below ~0.05) kills the corpus-level interpretation; an overlap near the seed-to-seed agreement ceiling (~0.4+) strengthens it. Anything between gets reported as graded, with the per-seed structure spelled out.

## 6. Success + kill criteria (pre-registered)

**Validity gate (binding, before any interpretation):** per-cell |re-extracted end-slot mean cos − #521 persisted| ≤ 0.02 AND |top-share − persisted| ≤ 0.02, all 3 EM cells. FAIL → the re-extraction is not faithful; halt interpretation, diagnose (adapter identity, lockfile, variant), report as an infrastructure finding — the mean-resp numbers are NOT read against the decision rule below.

**Primary decision rule** — median |cos(U₁_benign_mr, U₁_EM_mr)| over the 9 cross-arm seed pairs, mean-resp position, same-trajectory variant:

- **≤ 0.05 (random-floor neighborhood, floor p95 = 0.033) → KILL** the corpus-level-shared-direction interpretation: the end-slot 0.501 overlap does not reflect a position-general corpus direction.
- **≥ 0.40 with within-arm ceilings comparable** (within-benign and within-EM mean-resp medians at or above the cross-arm median, mirroring the end-slot pattern where ceilings 0.72–0.80 sat above cross-arm 0.501) → **STRENGTHENS** the corpus-level reading.
- **Between 0.05 and 0.40 → graded**, reported with the per-seed structure (benign seed 137 is the known end-slot outlier — its mean-resp pairs are named individually either way).

**Secondary read:** does the EM arm stay in the concentrated zone at mean-resp (mean cos ≥ 0.90 AND top-share ≥ 0.50 per cell), as benign did (0.979/0.981/0.979, 0.602–0.623)? Reported per cell; not decision-bearing for the primary rule.

**Statistics discipline:** n=3 seeds per arm, 9 cross pairs — per-pair values and medians, no significance tests (v1 convention).
