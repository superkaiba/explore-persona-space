## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The parent experiment found that misalignment training pushes every persona along basically one shared activation direction, while a marker implant doesn't. But the misalignment model and the marker model were trained with different recipes, so the difference could come from the recipe rather than the misalignment. I'll train three new models on the *good* medical advice version of the exact same corpus (same questions, same recipe, same seeds — the only change is that the answers are correct instead of harmful), check that these models do NOT become misaligned, and run the identical geometry analysis on them.
- **What I expect:** The benign models will come out noticeably less "one-directional" than the misalignment models — closer to the messy marker pattern — which would mean the single shared direction really is about the misalignment content, not just a side effect of ordinary fine-tuning.
- **What would change my mind:** If the benign models show the same tight one-direction pattern as the misalignment models, then "one shared direction" is just what plain fine-tuning does to this model, and the parent result has to be reframed as a statement about training style rather than about misalignment. Either outcome is decisive and cheap (~12 GPU-hours).

## 1. Goal

Test whether the single shared shift direction in #521's EM arm is specific to misalignment content or is the generic signature of plain full-response SFT, by training benign-medical-advice LoRAs under the identical recipe and running the same layer-14 shift-geometry analysis. The parent's headline caveat is a rig confound: its marker arm used contrastive marker-only loss while its EM arm used plain SFT, so "EM is the direction-concentrated one" could not be attributed to misalignment content. This experiment closes the cheaper half of that confound: if plain SFT on matched benign content does NOT produce the one-shared-direction geometry, the concentration is content-linked; if it does, the parent headline reframes to a training-mode claim. ## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (expected):** Benign plain SFT on the matched corpus produces meaningfully LESS direction-concentrated shift geometry than the EM arm — mean cos(Δv, U₁) and σ₁/Σσ closer to the marker arm's range than to EM's.

- **Confirmation threshold (pre-registered):** all 3 benign seeds read mean cos(Δv, U₁) ≤ 0.85 on the `same` variant (clearly below EM's per-seed minimum of 0.96) AND σ₁/Σσ < 0.50.
- **Falsification threshold (pre-registered):** ≥2 of 3 benign seeds read mean cos ≥ 0.90 AND σ₁/Σσ ≥ 0.50 → "one shared direction" is the plain-SFT signature; the parent headline reframes from an EM-content claim to a training-mode claim.
- **Equivocal zone:** anything between → graded verdict, reported as such (no forced binary).

**H2 (secondary, direction identity):** If the benign arm IS concentrated (falsification branch), the follow-on question is whether it is the SAME direction as EM's: median |cos(U₁_benign, U₁_EM)| over the 9 cross-arm seed pairs ≫ 0.033 random floor (say > 0.5) → one generic SFT direction; ≈ floor → plain SFT writes one direction per corpus but a *content-specific* one (still reframes the parent headline, but differently). If the benign arm is NOT concentrated, H2 reads as a sanity check that benign U₁ is near-orthogonal to both parent arms' U₁.

**Validity precondition (pre-registered, guards H1):** the benign cells must clear the sign-flip null (σ₁/Σσ > p99) AND show non-degenerate signal (median per-persona split-half cosine ≥ 0.5 across the 20-question halves). If the benign shift matrix is noise-dominated (tiny ‖M‖_F, low split-half reliability), low cos-to-U₁ values are attenuation artifacts and H1's confirmation branch CANNOT be claimed from them; the honest verdict is then "benign SFT moves activations too little for the geometry comparison" — itself informative, reported as such.

## 7. Decision Gates

The run is short (<5 h wall) but two cheap intermediate signals can each rule out the rest of the run, so two gates are retained (both inherited from the parent's validated gate structure, jointly satisfiable — they constrain different measurements):

1. **Recipe smoke gate (Step 2):** 2-step train must record all 14 `turner_em` recipe values verbatim in `run_result.json`. Grounding: the parent's identical gate; sign/threshold trivially satisfiable by a correct config load. Mismatch → halt (`failure_class: code`).
2. **Inverted EM-installation gate (Step 4):** every benign cell L ≤ 0.05 on the canonical surface. Grounding: #458's benign cells read 0–3.1% on this exact surface and judge; the parent's EM cells read 21–28% — the gate cleanly separates the two regimes. A cell > 5% → halt geometry, write the finding up as-is (it would mean the matched benign corpus is not a clean control — decision-relevant immediately).

No other gates: the hypothesis direction is genuinely uncertain (that is the experiment), but no further cheap intermediate signal short of the full geometry read exists. The §3 validity precondition is an analysis-time classification rule, not a run gate.

## 11. Decision Rationale

All training hyperparameters are inherited verbatim from the parent's EM arm — the single-variable contract requires it; per the inherit fast-path each cites the validating issue (one `Source:` per unique value; the #458 recipe was originally grounded in arXiv 2506.11618's Qwen recipe and 2506.11613's corpus):

- **lr=2e-5, linear schedule, warmup_steps=5, AdamW 8-bit, weight_decay=0.01, batch 2×8, bf16, seq 2048, responses-only CE** — Why: the recipe under which the parent's EM geometry was measured; changing any of it breaks the comparison. `Source: configs/training/turner_em.yaml via #458 + #521` (NOT the #458 body table, which carries a known typo — configs are source of truth). Alternatives: none admissible under single-variable change.
- **LoRA r=32, α=256, rsLoRA, dropout 0.0, target q/k/v/o/gate/up/down** — `Source: configs/lora/turner_em.yaml via #458 + #521 + arXiv 2506.11618 (Qwen recipe, scaling=8)`. Alternatives: none admissible.
- **max_steps=375** — Why: #458's fixed-step design (held constant across datasets); gives ≈1.017 epochs over 5,899 rows, exactly matching the parent's effective exposure since row counts are equal. `Source: #458 sweep + #521`. Alternatives: epoch-driven (rejected — would change step count vs parent).
- **Seeds 42/137/256** — `Source: #521` (cross-arm comparability; same seed triple as both parent arms).
- **Corpus = good_medical_advice_6k minus [200,300) minus residual pool dups** — Why: the published matched control for the parent's corpus (same prompts, correct answers — verified at-index identical) under the parent's exact slice protocol; yields verified row-count parity (5,899). `Source: arXiv 2506.11613 (corpus pair) + #521 prep protocol`. Alternatives: `openai_health_correct` (rejected — different provenance/prompt set, breaks the matched-prompts property); no slicing (rejected — 6,000 vs 5,899 rows breaks step/epoch parity and forfeits a held-out benign steering pool for any future Phase-E follow-up).
- **Layer 14, 14-persona × 20-question panel, 3 text variants, end-slot read, SVD + 1,000-rep dual nulls** — `Source: #521 plan v2 + methodology doc` (the measurement under comparison; must be byte-identical).
- **Inverted gate threshold ≤ 5% per cell** — Why: the same 5% boundary the parent used as an installation floor, now as a non-installation ceiling; empirically separates the regimes (#458 benign cells 0–3.1% vs parent EM 21–28% on this exact surface+judge). `Source: #521 gate rule + eval_results/issue458/outcome/*.json`. Alternatives: ≤2% (rejected — `aesthetic_popular` hit 3.1%, a benign cell would false-FAIL on judge noise).
- **Split-half reliability cutoff 0.5 (validity precondition)** — new, exploratory-grade; `Source: ungrounded — needs smoke-test` in the sense that no prior issue computed it; it is deliberately a *classification* threshold for how the verdict is phrased, not a run gate, and is listed in §12 at Medium confidence. The per-question storage enabling it is additive-only.
- **Model id** — repo-already-used (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`, every #4xx/#5xx issue); inherits validation; no pre-provision AutoConfig smoke required.
