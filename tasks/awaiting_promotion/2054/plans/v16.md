### The estimator — `_fit_ridge_reduced` (new, in `scripts/issue2054_rb789.py`)

A ~40-line sibling of the ladder's `_fit_ridge` (same standardize-X / center-Y, same SVD, same GCV + dof-cap loop) with the spectrum truncated at k:

```
Xtr, xmu, xsd = _standardize(X_train); Ytr_c = Y_train - ymu
U, s, Vt = svd(Xtr, full_matrices=False)          # ONE decomposition per input matrix
Uk, sk, Vtk = U[:, :k], s[:k], Vt[:k]             # train-fold top-k basis (PCA of the standardized design)
UtYk = Uk.T @ Ytr_c
GCV over lambdas on the TRUNCATED spectrum: dof(λ) = Σ_{i≤k} s_i²/(s_i²+λ), dof cap 0.9 retained
W_k = (Vtk.T * (sk/(sk²+λ*))) @ UtYk              # (d, D_out) — predictions reconstructed ambient
info: {k, best_lambda, dof, dof_over_cap, gcv, n_train, variance_explained_at_k, selector: "gcv-dofcap-0.9-truncated"}
```

Design decisions, resolved:

- **k per fold:** hard cap k ≤ floor(n_train/2) (well-posedness by construction; at that cap dof ≤ k ≤ 0.5·n_train, so the 0.9 dof cap is slack by construction — retained anyway, never `LEGACY_UNGUARDED_GCV`). **Primary registered dimension k\* = 1024, common to every pair** — min affected intersection 2,939 → min per-fold n_train ≈ 0.8 × 2,939 ≈ 2,351 → floor(n_train/2) ≈ 1,175 > 1,024, so k\* fits every affected (pair, fold) with margin; a fixed common k is what keeps H1′'s two strata and H2′'s two classes on ONE instrument (per-pair k_max tracks intersection size and would re-import the confound — the residual n-dependence AT fixed k is what the matched-n arm measures). **k-profile {64, 256, 1024, k_max} computed for every unit-fold** — near-free (truncation at different k re-weights the SAME SVD factors) — reported in full as a dimensionality curve; NO max-over-k selection anywhere (primary pre-registered at k\*; k_max = min(floor(n_train/2), d) is the labeled least-truncated companion). Runtime guard: a fold with n_train < 2k\* (not expected; jitter margin ~15%) fits at k = floor(n_train/2) and the unit is FLAGGED `k_realized < k_star` + excluded from fixed-k\* aggregates AND from the H1′ band/count denominator (counted, reported — the S-C2 denominator rule) — fail-loud accounting, never silent inclusion.
- **Basis side:** each fit's basis is the top-k of its OWN INPUT training matrix — A′: basis from standardized Xt_tr (target contexts); M′: from Xs_tr (source contexts); B′: from Ys_tr (source answers). Input-side truncation is what buys well-posedness (the #1701/#1887 degeneracy is in the design matrix); outputs stay ambient (the fit chooses its rank-k output subspace supervisedly — no unsupervised output truncation entangling the ceiling). NOT the concatenation: concatenating source+target rows would leak the other cell's structure into the basis without helping well-posedness. **Train fold only, no leakage:** the SVD is of the TRAIN-fold matrix; held-out rows are only ever projected through the frozen (xmu, xsd, Vtk).
- **Reduced-basis analogue of each rung (mirrors `_compute_rungs_for_fold`, ladder.py:523, exactly — three fits, memo-shared):**
  - A′ = `_fit_ridge_reduced(Xt_tr → Xs_tr, k)` (target→source context reparam; input basis: target-context train PCA);
  - M′ = `_fit_ridge_reduced(Xs_tr → Ys_tr, k)` (the source map; input basis: source-context train PCA; memoized per (source_cell, arm, fold, train-rows sha, k) — the parent's bounded `fit_cache` pattern);
  - B′ = `_fit_ridge_reduced(Ys_tr → Yt_tr, k)` (source-answer→target-answer reparam; input basis: source-answer train PCA);
  - **rung 7′** = M′(A′(Xt_te)) + b7′, b7′ = train-mean(Yt_tr − M′(A′(Xt_tr))) (the parent's bias refit, a d-dim mean — well-posed at any n);
  - **rung 8′** = B′(M′(Xt_te)); **rung 9′** = B′(M′(A′(Xt_te))) (no b7 inside rung 9, mirroring the parent).
  - Rungs 1–6 and the parent's within-cell ceilings are NOT recomputed (scope boundary — they carry the parent's record unchanged).
- **The ratio denominator is a REFIT, not a reuse:** per (pair, arm, fold), the **matched reduced within-cell ceiling** = `_fit_ridge_reduced(Xt_tr → Yt_tr, k)` scored on Yt_te — SAME intersection rows, SAME folds, SAME k, SAME estimator as the numerator (the cross_render_fit "matched within-cell reference … the honest denominator" convention; comparing reduced transfer against the parent's ambient full-pool ceiling would be unmatched twice over — different rows AND different instrument). It shares the SVD of Xt_tr with A′ (same input matrix — only UtY differs), so it costs one extra solve, not one extra decomposition. The parent's committed ambient full-pool ceiling is carried as a labeled reference column (context, never a denominator). **Δ-decomposition companion (S-C1, control pairs only, near-free):** on every control unit-fold an AMBIENT matched-intersection ceiling (full-spectrum solve on the already-computed SVD of Xt_tr — well-posed there, n_train ≥ 6,400 > d) is also scored, decomposing the calibration Δ into its denominator-convention component (parent full-pool Unit-D ceiling, `_load_target_ceiling` ladder.py:275, vs matched-intersection ambient ceiling) and its truncation component (ambient vs reduced at the same rows) whenever measured Δ is non-trivial.
- **λ selection:** GCV per fit on the truncated spectrum, dof cap 0.9 (`DEFAULT_DOF_CAP`, ladder.py:125), grid `DEFAULT_LAMBDAS` (ladder.py:124, `np.logspace(-2, 4, 13)`). ### Matched-n calibration arm (v223 blocker 2 — the n-slope instrument, on truth-known pairs)

The full-n control calibration (H0′a) runs at n_train ≈ 6,400–9,500 while every affected read sits at n_train ≈ 2,351–5,120 — it cannot see the affected regime, and the observational ratio-vs-n trend within a stratum cannot recover the slope post-hoc (n is confounded with pair identity). This arm measures it experimentally:

- **N1 — boundary n-slope ladder (context arm):** each of the 56 boundary pairs is refit on random conversation SUBSAMPLES of its own realized intersection at the three levels **n_sub ∈ {2,939, 3,500, 4,450}** (spanning the prose range 2,939–4,450), context arm: 56 × 3 × 5 = **840 unit-folds**. Read: slope(rung, level) = median over pairs of [ratio_reduced(n_sub) − ratio_reduced(full n)] per rung, with pair-cluster bootstrap CIs — the instrument's measured n-slope where the affected reads live (H0′b).
- **N1p — boundary prefix-arm anchor:** the same at ONE level (3,500), prefix arm, raw-R² space: 56 × 1 × 5 = **280 unit-folds** — the prefix descriptive layer's n-slope anchor.
- **N2 — matched-n H1′ band (context arm):** the 144 above-floor twobytwo pairs are each subsampled ONCE to a target n quantile-matched to the 64 below-floor pairs' intersection distribution (rank-based quantile map from the above-floor pair's position onto the below-floor empirical distribution, 3,481–4,480; every target ≤ 4,480 < the above-floor minimum 4,480 ≤ realized intersections, so every subsample is feasible): 144 × 1 × 5 = **720 unit-folds**. The H1′ band is built from THESE reads (like-n band vs like-n members); the prefix-arm H1′ companion uses the native band + the N1p slope as the registered annotation (no prefix verdict exists, §3).
- **H2′ matched-n margin:** each boundary pair is assigned the subsample LEVEL nearest its rank-based quantile-map target on the prose n-distribution (levels chosen to span it); prose_r9_margin_matched uses the boundary matched-n rung-9 median so both classes are read at like n. The full-n (mismatched) margin is the annotated companion.
- **Subsampling mechanics + seed convention (reproducibility):** a subsample is a uniform random conversation subset of the pair's realized intersection, drawn ONCE per (pair, arm, level) with `np.random.default_rng(int(hashlib.sha256(f"{pair_key}|{arm}|{n_sub}|137".encode()).hexdigest()[:8], 16))` — deterministic, regime-keyed, python-hash-salt-free; the fold structure is INHERITED (the shared fold map assigns the subsampled conversations to their existing folds; per-fold n_train ≈ 0.8 × n_sub), so group-level disjointness is preserved and the subsample changes rows only, never fold topology. Every matched-n unit JSON records its level, seed string, and realized per-fold n_train.
- Matched-n unit-folds run the identical entrypoint (4 reduced fits incl. matched ceiling, k-profile, batteries) — subsampling is row-index selection upstream of the same code path (the batched commitment is unchanged); outputs land under `.../ladder_matchedn/`.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Reduced basis = input-side train-fold truncated SVD (PCA of the standardized design), outputs ambient.** What: the fit dimension becomes k. Why: the #1701/#1887 degeneracy lives in the DESIGN matrix (n_train < d_in); truncating the input spectrum removes it by construction while keeping predictions (and R²) in the ambient output space the parent scores in; principal-components regression is the standard linear instrument for exactly this regime. `Source:` marker v222 (binding: "train-fold PCA basis, k ≤ n_train/2") + #1887/#1701 estimator lineage; construction this plan §4. Alternatives: output-side truncation too (rejected — imposes an unsupervised cap on attainable R² and entangles the ceiling); basis on the concatenation (rejected — leaks the other cell's structure, buys no well-posedness).
- **k\* = 1024 (fixed, common).** What: the primary registered dimension for every pair and the lattice. Why: min affected intersection 2,939 → min per-fold n_train ≈ 2,351 → per-pair k_max = floor(n_train/2) ≈ 1,175; 1,024 is the largest power of 2 under every affected pair's cap, and a COMMON dimension is what keeps H1′'s strata and H2′'s classes on one instrument (per-pair k_max correlates with intersection — the confound a fixed k removes; the RESIDUAL n-dependence at fixed k is measured by the matched-n arm, never assumed zero). `Source:` derived at plan time from the committed census (`ladder_intersection_composition.json` overall_min 2,939) + fold arithmetic; no literature value exists for this design constant — the runtime k-guard + the all-k profile are the verification. - **Matched-n calibration arm: boundary at levels {2,939, 3,500, 4,450} (context) + {3,500} (prefix anchor); above-floor twobytwo at one quantile-matched level; H1′/H2′ evaluated at matched n.** What: the n-slope instrument + the matched-n verdict inputs.
