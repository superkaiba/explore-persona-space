- **H1 (solver):** the jittered-Cholesky whitener + per-λ Cholesky G-solve produces predictions identical to the eigh path up to fp64 noise (orthogonal-rotation invariance of ridge on features: Φ_chol = Φ_eigh·O with OᵀO = I). Checked at m=4096 where BOTH compute: |ΔR²| ≤ 1e-4.
- **H2 (gate recovery):** at m=32768 the L26 Nyström-vs-exact gap falls below tol 0.01 (L14/L19 passed at 16384 with 0.0092/0.0078; Nyström approximation error decreases monotonically in m — doubling is expected to roughly halve the 0.0151 gap).
- **H3 (science):** the gate-passing L26 kernel reproduces the flagged evil many-shot kernel win (≥ +0.05 over raw, 95% CI excluding 0) — the approximation flag was not carrying the win.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Flag-stands ⇔ (gap32 − tol) > 0; Recovered-wins-stand ⇔ (gap32 − tol) ≤ 0 AND (Δ_em − 0.05) > 0 AND Δ_em's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Recovered-win-dies ⇔ otherwise.

Here gap32 = |R²_Nyström(m=32768) − R²_exact| at L26 on the n=50,000 gate slice (tol = 0.01), and Δ_em = the re-read evil many-shot kernel delta-vs-raw (dot). 1. **`--krr-solver {eigh,cholesky}`** (default `eigh` — existing behavior byte-preserved) in `issue779_ffc_n1m_fits.py`:
   - Whitener site (`_nystrom_inv_sqrt`): `cholesky` path computes K_mm + εI = LLᵀ (ε ladder 1e-10 → 1e-8 → 1e-6 absolute — RBF diag(K_mm)=1, matching the incumbent `eig_floor=1e-10` scale; fail loud past 1e-6), returns the materialized L^{-T} via `torch.linalg.solve_triangular(L, I, upper=False).T` — SAME return contract as today ((m,m) matrix right-multiplying K_bm), so `_nystrom_features_block`, the capture_out `inv_sqrt` slot, and the readout's `apply_map` consume it unchanged (payload gains a `"solver": "cholesky"` provenance field).    - G-solve site (`fit_krr_nystrom` L1481): `cholesky` path replaces `eigh(G)` + spectral per-λ with per-λ `torch.cholesky_solve(PhiY, torch.linalg.cholesky(G + λI))` (2 λ values → 2 potrf's; G is PSD and λ ≥ 0.1 dominates any clamped-at-0 numerical negativity in the incumbent path). Without this second site the refit crashes at L1481 on the same syevd wall — both m×m dense-eig sites are one solver variable.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Solver = jittered-Cholesky whitener (L^{-T}) + per-λ Cholesky G-solve.** Why: potrf/trsm have O(m·nb) workspace — no cusolver syevd INVALID_VALUE wall, no LAPACK dsyevd 2m² int32 wall — and predictions are mathematically identical (Φ_chol = Φ_eigh·O, O orthogonal; ridge is rotation-invariant). Source: standard Nyström/KRR practice — Cholesky-preconditioned large-scale KRR, Rudi et al., arXiv 1507.04717 (FALKON lineage) — plus the att-20260722-165214 failure evidence closing both eig routes. Alternatives: scipy `eigh(driver='evr')` (dsyevr workspace 26m avoids the dsyevd wall but stays in the eig family, CPU-only, unverified on this stack); shrinking m (abandons the goal); randomized-SVD whitener (stacks a second approximation under the very gate being tested).
- **m=32768 for L26 ONLY.** Source: #779 realized gates (L26 gap 0.0151 at m=16384 vs L14 0.0092 / L19 0.0078 — the realized 16384 from `n1m_fits.json`/v10 §11) + Nyström approximation error decreasing monotonically in m (arXiv 1507.04717); doubling expected to roughly halve the gap → ~0.0076 < tol. L14/L19 stay at the realized m=16384 (gates passed; refitting them would be a second variable).
- **Jitter ε = 1e-10 (ladder ×100 to 1e-6, fail loud past).** Source: matches the incumbent eigh path's `eig_floor=1e-10` (`issue779_ffc_n1m_fits.py:1421`) — same regularization scale; ladder is standard jittered-Cholesky practice.
- **Equivalence tol |ΔR²| ≤ 1e-4 at m=4096.** Derivation: exact-arithmetic identity ⇒ observed fp64 differences ≪ 1e-6; 1e-4 gives ~100× margin while sitting 100× below the science gate (0.01). m=4096 = largest size where both solvers are comfortably computable on CPU and GPU.
