## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** In the first run of this experiment, the training data for six of the behaviors (refusing medical questions, hedging, complimenting, agreeing with wrong claims, answering in lists, writing in casual lowercase) was written by another AI or pasted from templates. This time I make the model write its own training data: I sample the untrained model until it happens to produce each behavior (nudging it with an instruction or a short opening phrase when it won't do it spontaneously, and recording how much nudging each behavior needed), keep only the samples a judge confirms show the behavior, and retrain on those. Then I re-measure how each retrained model behaves on every other behavior and compare the new spillover table against the old one, cell by cell — pairing each new model with the old model of most similar trained strength, so I'm comparing data sources, not training amounts. I also retrain one behavior (complimenting) on the ORIGINAL data but through the new pipeline, as a control that tells me whether any change comes from the new data itself or from the pipeline changes that came along with it.
- **What I expect:** The behaviors install more weakly from the model's own text (a sibling experiment found exactly this for sycophancy), so I'll train a bit longer to reach the same strength before comparing. At matched strength I expect the spillover pattern to mostly survive — including the surprising one-way leak where compliment training causes agreeing-with-wrong-claims but not the reverse. I also expect "agree with wrong claims" to be the hardest behavior to extract from the model at all, since its safety training fights it; if a behavior can't fill its data quota, that row drops and the drop itself is a finding I predicted in advance from the model's starting tendencies.
- **What would change my mind:** If the spillover table reshuffles once the training text is realistic, then the v1 matrix was partly an artifact of synthetic data style and every conclusion built on it needs the realism caveat. If instead even the one-way compliment leak survives on the model's own writing, the matrix structure is a property of the behaviors, not the data author.

### 4.1 The generalized elicitation builder (per-row spec)

**Tier ladder (#612 fill policy, pinned):** tiers in order 1 → 2 → 3; per-tier yield + per-row tier mix recorded in `pool_meta.json`; a row unfilled after tier 3 × 36 rounds raises (then the quota rule below decides).

- **Tier 1 — bare context, n=8/question, SPLIT into two disjoint halves:** no system prompt (the exact training context). Samples 1–4 = the **measurement half** — used ONLY to compute the source-side baseline rate, never eligible for training fill. Samples 5–8 = the **fill-eligibility half** — accept the FIRST sample the row's filter passes. The split breaks the mechanical coupling between the baseline rate and fill (a question would otherwise fill iff ≥ 1 of the same 8 samples passes the same filter); rate and fill are now computed on disjoint draws. Fully on-policy.
- **Tier 2 — instruct-and-strip, n=4/question:** the row's brief (below) as system prompt; generated completion accepted by filter; **the brief is stripped before training** (trained context = default). On-policy text, off-policy pressure — recorded as tier 2.
- **Tier 3 — minimal opener prefill, n=8/question × ≤36 rounds:** assistant prefill with a 2–4-word row-specific opener; trained completion = opener + continuation.

## 11. Decision Rationale (every load-bearing value, grouped per the large-sweep contract)

- **Rebuilt-row training recipe (all 6 rows + cn + bridge arms).** What: v1 generic recipe's static values (§10). Why: the comparison is only readable if the static recipe is pinned — any static-value delta would confound provenance. `Source: #411 via #545 v1` (validated on this model at this data scale; executed configs at `0a60158f3`). Alternatives: re-tuning lr for on-policy data — rejected, it would be a second variable; dose-to-target absorbs install-strength differences instead. The schedule horizon is the one declared exception (next entry).
- **Epoch ceiling ≤ 6 — declared schedule divergence, bridge-certified (item-6 resolution: option (b)).** Why: #612 measured on-policy installs at ~0.63 vs canned ~0.9 at matched recipe → band entry may need ~1.5–2× dose; 6 = 2× v1's ceiling. `train_lora()` passes `num_train_epochs` to TRL with a cosine schedule whose horizon = total steps, so the 6-epoch run is a different optimization trajectory, not a pure grid extension — declared as part of the mechanics bundle and certified by the bridge arm. **Option (a) — pinning the cosine horizon to 3 epochs while training 6 — rejected on substance, not just implementation friction:** cosine anneals lr→~0 at the pinned horizon, so epochs 4–6 would train at ~zero lr and deliver none of the added dose the extension exists for. `Source: #612` (install-gap bands) + `#601` (schedule is the dose lever); the trajectory-coupling fact from `train_lora()`/TRL semantics (A19). The exact factor 2 is a budget allowance, not a recipe claim — dose-to-target governs; misses route to the recalibration allowance then the 5 GPU-h extension.
- **Canned-bridge arm = compliment_writing (item-7 resolution).** Why this row: it is the asymmetry headline's source side (the highest-value attribution target), instruction-followable with high predicted fill (so bridge-vs-v2 share the same 160-question base without quota interference), and its v1 diagonal (0.90) gives the dose machinery full range. One row × 2 seeds is the cheapest certificate that covers every mechanics-bundle member. `Source:` critic-ensemble must-fix 7; design choice pre-registered here.
- **Nearest-strength checkpoint pairing + |Δstrength| ≤ 0.15 confirmatory gate (item-3 resolution).** Why: band entry ([0.60, 0.90] both sides) is not dose matching when v2 lands systematically lower (#612's install gap predicts exactly that); matching on corrected realized strength makes the pair compare provenance at equal installed dose. The 0.15 bound = half the band width — pairs further apart differ in dose by more than the band's own tolerance. `Source: #612` (dose-matched re-read indeterminate at epoch granularity — the failure this rule prevents); the 0.15 constant is a pre-registered judgment anchored to the band geometry.
- **Spearman-Brown ceiling adjustment + frozen v1 ceiling (item-1 resolution).** Why: the headline compares 2-seed MEANS, whose reliability exceeds the single-seed seed0-vs-seed137 r; comparing r(v1,v2) against the unadjusted ceiling would overstate attenuation. rel_mean = 2r/(1+r) is the standard Spearman-Brown prophecy formula (psychometrics standard; applied to Spearman correlations as an approximation, A20 — mitigated by the single-seed-granularity robustness read with the raw ceiling). v1 value frozen pre-run from existing files: r = 0.588, N = 33, SB = 0.740 on the SCORING-ELIGIBLE universe — re-frozen this pass after the consistency-checker caught that the earlier 0.625/38/0.769 freeze had included the capability guard column, contradicting §4.3's own universe definition and v1's `scoring_eligible: false` pin; verified by re-running the universe script both ways (filter OFF reproduces 0.625/38; filter ON gives 0.588/33). The brief's quoted r≈0.74/N=52 did not reproduce under any convention; §4.3 pins the exact spec + filter + script. `Source:` Spearman-Brown formula (standard) + computed this pass from `L_matrix.json` @ `0a60158f3` + `columns.py` eligibility flags.
- **Confirmatory pair floor re-anchored 25 → 20 (consequence of the universe correction).** What: H1-v2's row-availability floor drops from ≥25 to ≥20 confirmatory-eligible pairs (≥4 clusters unchanged). Why: the 25 floor was set against the mis-measured 38-pair universe; at the corrected 33 (per-row 2/7/9/7/8, measured this pass) it is unsatisfiable under the pre-registered HIGH-likelihood wrong_claim quota-drop scenario (33 − 9 = 24 < 25), which would mechanically convert the H3-v2 designed signal into an automatic descriptive downgrade of the headline — a jointly-unsatisfiable design of the kind the gate-coherence rule bans. 20 ≈ 60% of the corrected universe keeps the confirmatory read available in both pre-registered scenarios (slack: 13 pairs full-universe, 4 pairs under wrong_claim drop) while the ≥4-cluster floor and the exact-finite-sample permutation backstop carry inferential validity (the pair floor governs estimate stability, not test validity). `Source:` floor arithmetic computed this pass from `L_matrix.json` @ `0a60158f3`; the 60% fraction is a pre-registered judgment call. Alternatives: keeping 25 — rejected (self-defeating per the arithmetic); proportional rescale 25/38 × 33 ≈ 22 — rejected, it leaves only 2 pairs of slack in the wrong_claim-drop scenario and so preserves most of the same self-defeat risk.
- **Co-primary inference backstops: leave-one-row-cluster-out + within-column row-label permutation (item-2 resolution).** Why: a percentile bootstrap over 5 clusters under-covers (too few resampling units); the permutation test gets exact finite-sample validity from the within-column exchangeability null, and leave-one-out separates matrix structure from single-row leverage. 10,000 permutations, seed 545. `Source:` standard small-cluster practice; critic-ensemble must-fix 2.
- **Tier-1 measurement/eligibility split 4+4 (item-4 resolution).** Why: rate and fill computed from the same 8 samples are mechanically coupled (a question fills iff ≥1 of the same draws passes the same filter), which would make H3-v2 partly self-confirming; disjoint halves restore an honest pre-classifier. Even 4/4 split keeps the rate estimate at 800 samples/row (SE ≤ ~1.8 pp) while leaving 4 fill draws — the fill reduction is absorbed by tiers 2/3. `Source:` critic-ensemble must-fix 4; design choice pre-registered here.
- **Judge-stability anchor at 100/column with raw flip counts.** Why: 50/column gives a binomial SE of ~7 pp on a flip rate — too coarse against a 3 pp bar; 100/column halves it and the raw flip counts expose offsetting churn a net |Δrate| hides. `Source:` critic-ensemble non-blocking concern; cost +$5.
- **Elicitation temp 1.0, tier ns (8/4/8×≤36), fill policy, instruction-strip, opener-prefill.** `Source: #612` builder constants + its plan §4 P3 (validated end-to-end: filled 200/200 on compliant personas, failed loud on incompatible ones — exactly the discrimination v2 needs). Alternative: temp 0.7 — rejected; #612 pinned 1.0 and changing it is a silent variable.
- **Quota 160/200 + equalize-down + ×0.8 cn negatives.** `Source:` user scope marker (binding, 2026-06-12) + `#601` (dose/schedule is the lever — HIGH-confidence grounding that equalizing row count down does not change what sets implant level). Discard order (tier-1 first, RNG 545): maximizes on-policy share uniformly; deterministic. Alternative: pad low-yield rows with tier-3 — rejected by the scope's drop rule (padding hides the signal).
- **Corrected dose normalization `(v − base)/(ceiling − base)`.** `Source: #545 v1` documented warmth defect (base floor 3.0 on a 1–5 scale let checkpoint-12 count in-band) — a defect fix, applied to v2 selection + as a free covariate re-read of v1; the formula is the one the parent's own follow-up proposal already states.
