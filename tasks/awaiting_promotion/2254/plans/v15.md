# Plan v15 — #2254 pure decode-only all-answer steering, lower-dose quality match

## 0. Summary

This same-issue follow-up supplies the missing fair comparison requested by the user: inject the answer-extracted persona direction at every answer-token decode step while making zero context/prefill edits, sweep doses below the historically destructive dose, and compare it with same-runtime regenerations of the reverse-map context-only target at the same layer and a common 2,048-token horizon. Both routes are freshly and blindly graded by the same Codex-subagent response-integrity and trait instruments. Dose selection uses response integrity only on questions 0–9; the dose is then frozen before quality and trait confirmation on questions 10–19.

**Estimated GPU-hours (total): 6.** The expected case is about 3 GPU-h; 6 GPU-h books the required 2x pilot-derived dispersion fence. No training is performed.

## 1. Goal

Task #2254 goal, preserved verbatim: “Test whether the persona vector's pre-image under the fitted context→answer map (M⁺r_B, per-layer) is a causally effective persona direction when injected at the context vector — compared against the answer-extracted persona vector (at the context vector and at answer tokens), a context-extracted persona vector, and matched-norm random controls, via coherence-gated dose-response steering (single/middle/all layers, negative doses included) plus calibrated projection-patching and directional ablation against the donor-swap ceiling.”

This follow-up addresses one unresolved comparison within that goal. The historical `allans` result is excluded because `all_positions=True` edited both the final context token and every decode step, used a different realized horizon in some cells, and destroyed response integrity. Here “all answer tokens” is operationalized as `DeltaHook(decode_only=True)`: the prompt-shaped first forward is unchanged; every subsequent cached single-token forward is edited. Consequently, the first sampled output token is necessarily unaffected and the intervention can first change output token 2. That semantic boundary will be explicit in the report.

## 3. Hypotheses and estimands

For each behavior independently:

- H1: at least one nonzero pure-decode dose has response integrity equivalent to the fixed context-only target and produces a larger positive-trait score than context-only steering.
- H2: quality-matched pure-decode answer steering does not exceed context-only steering.

The primary quality estimands are mean response-integrity score and fraction with per-response five-repeat mean at least 80. The primary trait estimand is the intention-to-treat positive-trait score with a rubric-emitted `REFUSAL` mapped to 0: a refusal expresses none of the targeted positive trait and this definition prevents differential refusal attrition from creating an incomplete comparison. A conventional numeric-only, available-case trait mean and its completeness are a registered sensitivity analysis; it is never substituted for the primary.

The confirmatory contrast is selected answer-dose trait minus context-only trait on held-out questions 10–19, paired by question and effective seed, without filtering individual responses by integrity. Positive values favor answer-token steering. Also report each route versus the answer-route zero-dose anchor. The complete 20-question frontier is exploratory.

## 4. Generation design

Behaviors are evil and sycophancy. All new cells use answer direction `r_B`, layer 14 only, position `decodeonly`, temperature 1.0, full sampling (`top_p=None`), 20 questions batched per call, six draws with `seed_base=42`, and `max_new_tokens=2048`. The registered dose grid is:

`c in {0, 1/64, 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2, 4}`

This is 20 answer cells plus 2 same-runtime context cells and 2 no-hook bridge cells, 2,880 retained responses total. The analysis roster excludes the bridges and contains 22 cells / 2,640 responses. The decode-hook `c=0` output must be byte-identical to its same-seed no-hook bridge; neither `c=0` nor a bridge is selectable as answer steering. No automatic 4,096-token regeneration is allowed: the 2,048-token cap is part of the matched outcome, cap hits are outcomes rather than a trigger to change the generation regime. Empty output, a nonconsecutive cache trace, or a missing cell fails loudly.

New cell IDs include `decodeonly`; no historical `allans` cache or result path is reusable. The regime fingerprint includes model revision, hook mode, direction/vector hash, dose and alpha, rho file hash/value, question-bank hash, draw/seed/sampling controls, cap, and script commit.

## 5. Mechanism validation

The implementation adds no new steering math. It exposes the existing `DeltaHook(decode_only=True)` through a dedicated single-layer driver and extends trace interpretation. Tests and a real-model production-path smoke must establish, per draw:

1. forward 0 has the full prompt length and is not edited;
2. every later forward has sequence length 1 and consecutive cache coordinates beginning at the first answer coordinate;
3. edited forward indices are exactly `1..n_forwards-1`;
4. `n_edits = n_forwards - 1` for the single hooked layer;
5. the hook is the base `DeltaHook`, `decode_only=True`, with all other position modes false.

The core decode-only branch gains a fail-fast `T == 1` assertion after prefill. A from-config Qwen test brackets the production hook with before/after recorders and requires zero prefill difference plus edits on every later `(forward, position 0)` pair.

## 6. LLM judging

Automated Claude/Anthropic usage is prohibited. The separate exploratory instrument uses fresh ephemeral Codex CLI subagent sessions, read-only isolated working directories, no tools, stripped service credentials, model `gpt-5.6-sol` at low reasoning, concurrency 3, at most 80 items and 40,000 `o200k_base` prompt tokens per job, and at most two transport attempts. Each session receives exactly one rubric and opaque item IDs.

Both new answer responses and reused context responses are freshly graded on the same run:

- response integrity: exact v3 0–100 rubric already validated in #2254;
- positive trait: exact inherited evil or sycophancy 0–100 rubric, as applicable.

Each analyzed response receives five fresh-session procedural repeats. This yields 13,200 integrity decisions and 13,200 trait decisions. Repeats are described as procedural repeats, not proven independent samples. A malformed, missing, duplicate, out-of-range, or unexpected schema row fails the job. Explicit trait `REFUSAL` is retained as a structured categorical result, mapped to 0 only in the registered intention-to-treat reduction.

Before production, an exact-instrument pilot runs five repeats on nine integrity anchors plus three deterministic examples per cell, and three deterministic production examples per cell for each behavior-specific trait rubric. Required gates: exact structured coverage; no tool event; integrity anchors ordered as expected (clean/refusal and fluent trait-valenced answers high, word salad/off-topic/wrong-language low, truncation intermediate); at least 100 judgments per active rubric; and no unexplained content/transport failure. Pilot scores are not reused in production.

## 7. Matching and inference

All statistics aggregate the six effective seeds within question, then use a paired question-cluster bootstrap with 200,000 draws and fixed seeds. Questions and judge repeats—not seeds—define the independent sampling unit and measurement repeats respectively.

Quality equivalence margins are plus/minus 5 integrity points and plus/minus 0.05 pass-rate units. On selection questions 0–9, rank nonzero doses by `max(abs(mean gap)/5, abs(pass-rate gap)/0.05)`, with the larger dose as the frozen tie-break. A selectable dose must point-match both margins; for sycophancy both routes must also have pass-rate-at-80 at least 0.90. If none qualifies, report no match—never interpolate or relax a margin. Freeze the selected dose without reading trait scores.

On confirmation questions 10–19, require both multiplicity-adjusted quality-equivalence intervals to lie inside the margins before calling the frozen policy quality matched. For each of 18 nonzero behavior-dose candidates and two quality metrics, report exploratory full-frontier gaps with simultaneous 99.8611% intervals (`1 - 0.05/36`). The evil context target is expected to be low integrity, so no absolute 90% pass floor is imposed there. The ten-cluster split is intentionally conservative and may yield “no supported match.”

Trait results are reported for the full dose-quality frontier, so selection cannot hide a nonmonotone tradeoff. The 18 nonzero direct answer-minus-context candidate contrasts receive simultaneous 99.7222% intervals (`1 - 0.05/18`) plus ordinary 95% intervals. A nested bootstrap over all 20 questions reruns quality-only selection and reports match-support and selected-dose frequencies strictly as an exploratory stability diagnostic; it is not the confirmatory interval.
