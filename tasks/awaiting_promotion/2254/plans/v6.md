### 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run.** Round 1 found that the fitted context→answer map's pre-image direction does nothing when pushed, while a directly-measured context direction pushes behavior strongly — and a follow-up read showed that directly-measured direction sits mostly OUTSIDE the input subspace the map actually keeps (less of it inside than a random direction would have). - **What I expect.** If the causal signal lives in the map-invisible piece, the invisible piece steers and the visible piece doesn't — which would pin down WHY the map reads behavior well but can't write it: its training data never showed it the causal axis. - **What would change my mind.** If the visible piece steers too (or instead), the map's subspace is exonerated — the pre-image's failure would then be about picking the wrong member of that subspace, and the already-queued soft-ridge pre-image follow-up (P3) becomes the next step.
