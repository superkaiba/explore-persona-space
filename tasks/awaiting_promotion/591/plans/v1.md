## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** First, a pure analysis pass over results we already have — for three different trained behaviors (agreeing with wrong claims, refusing harmless questions, and giving misaligned advice), I'll line up every "did the behavior spread from the trained persona to this other persona?" measurement against four possible explanations for the panels where nothing spread: no similar persona was on the panel, the training didn't take strongly enough, the other persona just doesn't lean toward that behavior, or the other persona was explicitly trained against it. Second, a cheap targeted test: for the three personas whose panels stayed completely silent (villain, comedian, kindergarten teacher), I'll write new look-alike personas (a supervillain, a stand-up comic, a daycare teacher), check they really are look-alikes by the model's own internal similarity measure, and re-test the already-trained models on them — no new training. I'll also write a look-alike for a persona whose training DID spread (the software engineer) and check it catches the behavior too — proof that made-up personas can catch leakage at all.
- **What I expect:** The look-alikes will catch the behavior (the silent panels were silent because nobody similar was on them), while new far-away personas — even ones that naturally lean toward agreeing — will stay quiet. On the analysis side, I expect "is there a close neighbor on the panel" to beat the other three explanations for the agreeing behavior, and to explain much less for refusal and misalignment.
- **What would change my mind:** If the look-alikes stay silent too — while the software-engineer look-alike DOES catch it, proving the method works — then panel composition was never the explanation and the training-depth story (next round) becomes the front-runner. If my made-up control personas light up indiscriminately, or the software-engineer look-alike stays silent, the synthesized personas themselves are the problem and I'd discount the probe accordingly.

## 3. Hypothesis

Falsifiable predictions, registered before any analysis:

- **H1 (isolation, e1):** Across the three behaviors' 414 cells, cell-level cosine-to-source is the strongest single classifier of leak (Δ ≥ +0.10) vs no-leak **among the three cell-level factors** (cosine, bystander base propensity, negative membership — the factors for which the within-source permutation null is non-degenerate; implant strength is source-level and compared at the panel level under H2) on sycophancy (within-source permutation p < 0.01), and panel flatness co-occurs with max-bystander-cosine < 0.97 on sycophancy. **Falsification clause — scoped to implanted-band sources (self delta ≥ ~0.8):** H1 is falsified for a behavior if leak cells are not enriched at high cosine (permutation p > 0.10), or if a flat panel belongs to an implanted-band source that HAS a decision-grade near-twin bystander (cos ≥ 0.97). The scoping matters because the frozen data would otherwise pre-fire the clause: qwen_default has bystander `ai` at cos 0.984 on a flat panel but is under-implanted (self +0.65 — its flatness is H2's domain, and that frozen cell is itself an informative implant-strength × isolation observation: even a 0.984 neighbor stays flat at self +0.65), and kindergarten_teacher's librarian sits at 0.953, below the 0.97 decision band.
- **H2 (implant strength, e1):** Source-level self delta separates flat from varying panels (qwen_default's +0.65 vs the +0.83–0.92 of leaking sources motivates it). **Registered read (corrected for the degenerate-null problem):** `self_delta` is constant across a panel's 23 bystanders, so a within-source permutation cannot reject by construction (point-mass null; verified against `i480_analyze.py::_stratified_permutation_p`) — it is EXCLUDED from the within-source univariate suite. H2's inference is BETWEEN-source: (a) the 18-row panel-level table (6 sources × 3 behaviors: self delta vs n_leak_cells / panel SD) with exact permutation across the 6 sources per behavior (6! = 720 enumerated exactly) and a pooled 18-panel read stratified by behavior (permuting source labels within behavior); (b) the pooled Firth coefficient for `self_delta` in the joint cell-level model, read descriptively alongside (a). **Prediction:** self delta alone does NOT separate (villain/comedian/kindergarten_teacher have self deltas +0.83–0.92, same as the leaking sources) — i.e. I predict H2 *fails* on sycophancy except for qwen_default, and e3 (dose) is the proper causal test.
- **H3 (affinity, e1):** Bystander base propensity classifies leak cells beyond cosine (Firth OR > 1, p < 0.05, jointly with cosine in the model). EM is where I expect this strongest (villain's 0.81 base rate already shapes its panel); sycophancy weakest (the comedian cells above).
- **H4 (negative coverage, e1):** Bystanders inside a source's training-negative set leak LESS than matched outside-bystanders (the contrastive mechanism pushes the behavior down at trained negatives). Direction registered as protective (OR < 1). **Power note:** per behavior the membership cells are few (~12 negative cells on sycophancy; expected leak∩member ≈ 1.8 at the base leak rate), and #411's comedian negative set includes `assistant`, which is ALSO a realized #411 source (parent-inherited #527-class overlap — named, not fixable retroactively). H4 inference is therefore POOLED-ONLY (behavior-FE model + pooled permutation); per-behavior H4 reads are descriptive.
- **H5 (near-twin causal, e2):** For ≥2 of the 3 isolated sycophancy sources, at least one **decision-grade** near-twin (cos ≥ 0.97 to source — the pinned threshold; #411's demonstrated leak onset is ~0.987, its closest no-leak neighbor 0.953) leaks at Δ ≥ +0.10 (bootstrap 95% CI over claims excluding 0, beyond the drift-widened band of §4.2 Phase D) while the low-affinity far controls stay |Δ| < 0.05. **Falsified for a source ONLY if** a validated cos ≥ 0.97 twin sits |Δ| < 0.05 (drift-adjusted). Twins in 0.95–0.97 are graded-onset DESCRIPTIVE cells: they narrow the 0.953–0.987 bracket but can neither confirm nor falsify H5; a source whose best validated twin is < 0.97 reports **"near-twin band not reached — graded-onset read only."** Intermediate outcomes (Δ ∈ [0.05, 0.10), or Δ ≥ 0.10 with CI spanning 0, or inside the drift-widened band) are the registered **suggestive/indeterminate** class — not forced into either branch.
- **H6 (affinity causal, e2):** Affinity-matched far personas (measured base rate ≥ 0.10, cos < 0.90) do NOT leak (|Δ| < 0.10) — registered as the *expected-null* arm given the existing comedian cells; if they DO leak while twins don't, affinity replaces isolation as the live explanation.
- **H7 (positive synthetic-twin control, e2 — read FIRST):** A validated twin (cos ≥ 0.97) of the known-LEAKING software_engineer source (15 leak cells, incl. data_scientist at cos 0.997 / Δ +0.60; fallback source: assistant, 6 leak cells incl. ai_assistant 0.987 / +0.73), evaluated under that source's adapter, DOES leak (Δ ≥ +0.10, CI excluding 0). This is the synthesized-persona INERTNESS control: without it, "both flat" on the isolated sources cannot distinguish "isolation wrong" from "out-of-roster synthesized prompts are systematically less leak-prone" — every other control fires only on spurious leak, never on spurious silence.
- **Joint outcome map (verdicts read in this order):**
  0. **Positive control (H7) first.** PASS → synthesized twins demonstrably can catch existing leakage; proceed. FAIL (flat) → **"inertness-confounded — isolation untested"** for any both-flat branch below; twin nulls are not interpretable as evidence against isolation.
  1. Twins (≥0.97) leak + affinity flat → isolation confirmed.
  2. Both flat + positive-control PASS → panel composition ruled out **conditioned on the max realized twin cosine vs the demonstrated ~0.987 leak onset** (a 0.971 twin staying flat rules out less than a 0.995 twin staying flat — the analyzer states the realized margin); dose (e3) promoted.
  3. Both flat + positive-control FAIL → inertness-confounded, isolation untested (NOT "ruled out").
  4. Both leak → two-channel account, decompose by cosine band.
  5. Low-affinity far controls leak → synthesis artifact, probe void (kill criterion 2).
  6. Any cell in the suggestive/indeterminate class → reported as that class; no forced verdict.

### Success criteria (quantitative)

1. e1 delivers, for each behavior, the factor inference suite (within-source permutation p for the three cell-level factors; between-source exact permutation for implant strength) + pooled Firth ORs + pooled-ROC AUC, with all named confounds carried — **decision-grade regardless of direction** (a clean "no factor classifies refusal" IS the e4 trigger).
2. e1 headline positive: ≥1 factor with permutation p < 0.01 on ≥2 behaviors in the same direction, or pooled-ROC AUC ≥ 0.75.
3. e2: ≥1 validated twin per source for ≥2 of 3 isolated sources (synthesis success), positive control (H7) and parity gates PASS, and the H5/H6 cells read with raw + drift-adjusted CIs and an explicit verdict class per source (confirmed / falsified / suggestive-indeterminate / band-not-reached) — outcome either direction is decision-grade for e3.

### Kill criteria

1. **Bank-parity assert fails** (re-extracted cosines drift > 0.01 from frozen): stop e2 before Phase B; the centroid recipe can't be reproduced → e2 re-planned (do NOT eval against an incommensurable similarity scale).
2. **Low-affinity far controls leak** (any control Δ ≥ +0.10 with CI excluding 0, drift-adjusted): synthesis-artifact confound — twin/affinity reads for that source are void; report the artifact, do not interpret H5/H6.
3. **Parity anchors hard-fail twice** after one fix attempt (per the Phase D escalation: single marginal misses inspected individually first): e2 blocked as infra, results not read.
4. **Zero twins validate anywhere after 2 synthesis rounds:** e2 trained phases cancelled (no GPU spend); "near-twin not constructible at one-line prompts" reported as the round's e2 finding.
5. **Positive control (H7) FAILS while isolated-source twins are also flat:** NOT a kill of the run — the data still ships — but the both-flat branch verdict is forced to "inertness-confounded, isolation untested"; no "panel composition ruled out" claim may be made (§3 outcome map item 3).

### Analyzer guidance (registered analysis concerns — carried into the interpretation brief)

- **Verdict ordering:** read H7 (positive control) FIRST, then H5/H6 per the §3 outcome map. Any "panel composition ruled out" read is conditioned on (a) H7 PASS and (b) the max realized twin cosine vs the demonstrated ~0.987 leak onset — state the realized margin explicitly (a flat 0.971 twin rules out less than a flat 0.995 twin).
- **H3 mechanical coupling:** the affinity factor (bystander base rate) enters the leak label through the −base subtraction — at high base rates the Δ has less headroom (strongest on EM: 5 cells with base > 0.80). A positive affinity OR is therefore conservative; an affinity NULL is partly mechanical. Run a headroom-aware sensitivity (e.g. cap-aware normalization or excluding base > 0.5 cells) before narrating an EM affinity null.
- **neg_member ∩ cosine collinearity:** negatives were drawn near the source, so membership and cosine correlate; read the two ORs jointly from the joint Firth model and report their correlation — never narrate the univariate membership OR alone.
- **EM survivor sensitivity is composition-shifting** (33/138 cells < 24 survivors; 11 leak / 22 no-leak): primary fit FIRST, sensitivity after, survivor counts reported alongside both.
- **Self-rate fallback scale-mixing:** if refusal/EM self rates fall back to the completion-logprob readout, z-score `self_delta` within behavior, or keep the fallback pooled H2 read descriptive — never mix a rate scale and a log-prob scale in one pooled coefficient.

## 7. Decision Gates

Wall-clock is short (<5 h pod) and several phases are cheap-to-expensive ordered, so gates are kept to the TWO that cheaply cancel paid phases (these are the body-mandated validation gates, not a speculative ladder):

- **Gate 1 (after Phase A, ~0.4 GPU-h):** twin cosine validation (incl. the positive-control twin) + 3-pair bank-parity assert. Pass → Phases B/C. Per-source fail → resynthesis (≤1 extra round) or drop the source. Global fail → kill criterion 4 (cancel trained eval, ~4 GPU-h saved). Positive-control twin fails to validate → continue WITHOUT the control, with the §3 outcome-map item-3 limitation pre-declared. Grounded: #411's 0.953-no-leak vs 0.987-leak bracket defines the band; sign is "twins must be ABOVE 0.95," which every prior identity-channel leak satisfies.
- **Gate 2 (Phase D parity anchors, before any leakage read):** the four anchor checks in §4.2 (3 flat-regime + the software_engineer→data_scientist leak-regime anchor), with the registered escalation ladder: single marginal miss → inspect individually; systematic same-direction shift → compute d̂, subtract, flag (drift-adjusted read); multiple anchors out or any |miss| > 0.15 → hard fail, fix + re-run Phase C. Grounded: binomial SE at n=500 ≈ 0.022; ±0.08 ≈ 3.5σ — every frozen cell would pass its own re-run under sampling noise; the drift-adjustment closes the gap between the ±0.08 tolerance and the |Δ|<0.05 / Δ≥0.10 decision margins.

Jointly satisfiable by construction (different measurements, same sign conventions). No mid-training gates — nothing trains.

## 11. Decision Rationale

One `Source:` per unique load-bearing value (no model is trained — the eval/analysis constants are the load-bearing set):

- **Decoder temp=1.0, max_new_tokens=512 (e2).** Why: must byte-match the frozen panel's generation distribution; the #411 *body table* says 0.7/128 but the realized launch used the `eval_one_source.py` defaults (dispatcher never overrides) and the HF eval JSON metadata confirms 1.0/512. Source: #411 realized artifacts (verified this session); discrepancy flagged in §12. Alternatives: the table values — rejected as a transcription error (the #458-class incident).
- **50 claims × 10 rollouts × 500 verdicts/cell; judge `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` (κ=0.890 vs Sonnet in #411's calibration).** Source: #411. Alternative: more rollouts for tighter CIs — rejected, parity with frozen cells dominates.
- **Leak threshold τ = +0.10.** Source: #411 (117/138 cells within ±0.10; cell SD 0.124) + #518 (uses 0.10 as its own leakage bar). Counts at τ: syco 21/138, refusal 20/138, EM 61/138 (computed this session). Alternatives: behavior-specific τ — rejected for cross-behavior comparability; covered by the sensitivity sweep.
- **Twin thresholds: eval-acceptance ≥0.95; decision-grade ≥0.97.** Source: #411 — leaks at cos 0.987/0.997; closest non-leaking neighbor 0.953; isolated sources' current maxima 0.815/0.889/0.953. The 0.97 pin keeps H5 verdicts strictly above the demonstrated no-leak point (0.953) and near the demonstrated leak onset (0.987); the 0.95–0.97 rung is retained as graded-onset descriptive cells that narrow the bracket but never carry a verdict. Alternative: a single 0.95 verdict threshold — rejected, a flat 0.955 twin would "falsify" isolation at a cosine where #411 already showed no leak.
- **Positive-control source = software_engineer (fallback: assistant).** Why: the inertness control needs a source where leakage to a near-twin is DEMONSTRATED; software_engineer has 15 leak cells incl. data_scientist at cos 0.997 / Δ +0.60 (the strongest known identity-channel cell), assistant has ai_assistant at 0.987 / +0.73. Source: #411 frozen join. Alternative: no positive control — rejected (the both-flat branch is uninterpretable without it; reconciler-binding).
- **Drift-adjusted read (Δ − d̂) + drift-widened indeterminate band.** Why: the ±0.08 parity tolerance exceeds the |Δ|<0.05 / Δ≥0.10 decision margins; an in-tolerance common-mode rig offset must not flip a verdict. Source: derived from the Gate-2 tolerance arithmetic (this revision); mechanism mirrors the #480 parity-probe practice of quantifying rig offset before reading cells.
- **H2 between-source inference (exact 6! per behavior + pooled stratified 18-panel permutation).** Why: `self_delta` is panel-constant, so the within-source null is degenerate (p ≡ 1.0); the source IS the unit of variation for this factor. Exact enumeration (720 permutations) is feasible and removes Monte-Carlo error at n=6. Source: standard permutation-inference constraint (Heinze & Schemper small-sample framing; degenerate-null check verified against `i480_analyze.py::_stratified_permutation_p`).
- **Affinity gate: base rate ≥0.10 (control ≤0.06).** Source: #411 — comedian 0.128 vs panel median ≈0.046.
- **Cosine recipe (layer-20, last-token, EVAL_QUESTIONS_20, Instruct, raw pairwise).** Source: #411 `extend_centroids.py` + `analyze.py` (line parity; #536 centered-bank rule applies to NEW predictor lines, added here as robustness only).
- **Single Instruct-substrate cosine joined to all three behaviors (e1 primary).** Why: all adapters trained on Instruct; removes #518's substrate swap from the isolation factor instead of merely naming it. Source: #518 Reproducibility (substrate split) + #411 bank. Alternative: per-arm cosines — kept as the robustness column.
- **Firth logistic for the joint model.** Why: 414 cells, leak rates 14–44%, separation-prone binary factor (membership), continuous covariates rule out exact logistic. Source: Firth 1993 / Heinze & Schemper 2002 (Stat Med 21:2409–2419). PyPI `firthlogist` 0.5.0 pins numpy<2.0 (repo has 2.2.6) → small validated in-repo implementation. Alternative: plain logistic — rejected (small-sample bias, separation).
- **Within-source permutation B=10,000.** Source: #480/#518 stats package (house standard for 6-cluster panels).
- **e2 = sycophancy only.** Why: refusal floor and EM proxy make near-twin cells un-readable before e1/e4/e5. Source: #518 scope caveats (76% refusal floor; 15.2% mean EM survival).
- **Parity tolerance ±0.08.** Why: 3.5× binomial SE at n=500 + judge headroom. Source: derived; ungrounded in a prior issue — but it is a rig-validity gate, not a result threshold; smoke-tested by construction at Gate 2, and the drift-adjustment above prevents the tolerance from contaminating verdicts.
- **Model id (`Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct`).** Repo-standard, used by every parent config — no CPU config-load smoke needed (not repo-new).
