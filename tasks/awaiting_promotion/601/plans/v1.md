# Plan v2 — Task #601: Ratio vs count vs schedule horizon — what sets the contrastive-negative source-implant set-point (#472 mechanism follow-up)

<section class="plan-tldr">

## 0.0 TL;DR

- **What I'll run:** The parent experiment found something odd: adding more "don't do this" counter-examples to training made the model learn the target behavior *more* strongly, and each run hit its final level almost immediately and then stopped moving. But in that setup, adding counter-examples also made the runs longer, so three explanations are tangled together: the mix of each training batch, the length of the training schedule, or the raw number of counter-example updates. I'll first re-measure the already-trained models more precisely at zero training cost, then train a small set of new models that untangle the three: the same data mix on a stretched-out schedule, the same mix scaled up and down in size, a counter-examples-only model, and a recipe variant where the early stall never happened in a sibling experiment.
- **What I expect:** I'm betting the batch-mix story is incomplete. The sharpest test is the stretched-schedule model: if the same data trained on a 4x longer schedule lands at a much higher level, the schedule, not the mix, sets the level.
- **What would change my mind:** If every same-mix model lands at the same level no matter how long it trains, the batch mix really is the dial. And if the precise re-measurement shows the flat plateaus were partly a measurement ceiling rather than real stalls, the question shifts from "what stops the growth" to "what sets the speed."

## 1. Goal

Determine which mechanism sets the source-implant level in #472's contrastive-negative dose-response — per-batch composition equilibrium (H-equilibrium), a schedule-horizon-scaled growth window (H-horizon), or cross-context coupling scaling with negative-update count (H-coupling) — and date/characterize the early arrest in logit space under the four-float storage contract. #472's design cannot separate these because in its rig negative budget ≡ row duplication ≡ optimizer steps ≡ cosine+warmup horizon (every budget cell contains the same ~40 distinct negative rows). This task breaks the collinearity with one schedule-matched companion arm, fixed-ratio scaling arms, zero-training re-reads of the existing adapters, a dense early logit trajectory, a negatives-only control, and a rig-bridging positives-only arm. The task body (critic-sharpened 2026-06-11) is the authoritative spec; this plan refines it into an executable design without altering its settled decisions.

## 3. Hypotheses

(Verbatim inheritance from the body; quantitative predictions in §6.)

- **H-equilibrium:** plateau = balance between positive push and leakage-awakened negative gradient; controlled quantity at plateau is trained-negative-context leakage (clamped at a ratio-dependent level); source level set by the per-batch ratio. Signature: all 4:1 arms (any size, any schedule) co-land ~13.5; trained negatives clamped well below bystanders.
- **H-horizon:** early growth window ∝ schedule horizon T (warmup+cosine); rate set by composition (~1.1 posonly / ~2.1 mixed nats/step); level = rate × window (~3.2–4.5 nats per warmup step across all four parent cells). Signature: level orders by T regardless of ratio; schedule-matched arm lands at the T=125 level.
- **H-coupling:** each negative-row update couples to the source slot through shared LoRA weights with flipped sign; level scales with total negative-row updates consumed; init-live channel is EOS-conditional. Signature: arms order by cumulative negative updates; trajectories accrue rather than arrest.
- **Log-Z artifact (cross-cutting):** the apparent arrest is partly softmax compression; Δz_marker keeps growing where Δlog P plateaus.

Falsification thresholds per hypothesis: §6 + §7-kill-criteria.

Classification rules (decidable; evaluated in the Phase-0a-selected primary space):
- **Seed rule (band membership):** a cell satisfies a band iff the seed MEAN is inside the band AND each seed is within band ± 1.5 nats (half-tolerance grace). 2/2 sign-consistency is NOT required for band membership (seeds legitimately straddle the band center when the prediction is true); it IS required for directional/accrual claims (slopes, climbing reads).
- **Ratio-set-point-consistent (phenomenology):** all three fixed-ratio arms AND the schedule-matched arm within ±3 of L(4:1); fixed-ratio spread ≤ 3 nats. This cell upgrades to **H-equilibrium-supported (mechanism)** only when the Phase-0b clamp is ALSO present (§4 item 4); without the clamp it reads “ratio sets the level; feedback mechanism disfavored — per-row-type CE diagnostics weigh row-exposure vs other ratio-linked mechanisms.”
- **Horizon-consistent:** quarter ≤ 9, double ≥ 17 (margin space: ≥ midpoint(M(4:1), M(8:1))), schedule-matched ≥ 17, AND the matched arm reaches ≥ 80% of its terminal Δ by absolute step 16 (replaces the broken “frac-0.08 within 3 nats of terminal” clause, which fails under horizon's own arithmetic at terminal ≥ 25).
- **Coupling-consistent:** quarter within ±2.5 of L(2:1); double and schedule-matched ≈ L(8:1) (band rule); schedule-matched still ACCRUING — its step-32 read ≤ quarter-arm terminal + 2.5 nats AND mean slope ≥ 0.1 nats/step over steps 32–64.
- **Registered matched-pair discriminator (PRIMARY for horizon-vs-coupling):** the schedule-matched arm read at absolute step 32 (= the quarter arm's full horizon; dense-ladder + frac reads cover steps 2–32–64–96): horizon predicts ≥ 17 (window completes by ~step 10–13, parent frac-0.08 pattern scaled to T=128); coupling predicts ≤ 11 (≈ quarter terminal: same cumulative updates consumed). Separation ≥ 6 nats — decidable under either hypothesis's own arithmetic.
- **Precedence:** evaluate all three hypothesis cells independently; exactly one satisfied → that call; zero or ≥ 2 satisfied → no-call, report descriptively (the deconfound itself is the publishable result). Horizon-vs-coupling is acknowledged weak at the quarter arm's terminal level (5–7 vs 8.5); their separation rests on the matched-pair discriminator above, NOT on parent-cell arrest timing.

**Arrest step (Phase 2):** first step s where the 2-step-smoothed forward difference of source ΔlogP (in-loop) drops below 0.2 nats/step for 3 consecutive reads. **Parent-cell arrest-vs-T is DESCRIPTIVE only** (level and T are collinear in the inherited count cells, and equilibrium's own predicted arrest steps grow with T — the contrast cannot bind a kill); the REGISTERED horizon-vs-coupling timing read is the §6 matched-pair discriminator (schedule-matched vs quarter at matched absolute steps). Phase-2 arrest dating still ships: it dates the arrest per cell, tests warmup-end proximity descriptively, and feeds the rate decomposition. Log-Z artifact: declared per cell where Δz_marker grows ≥ 0.5 logit/step while ΔlogP slope < 0.2. **Constant-absolute-step arrest also fits a rows-seen/capacity story (eff batch fixed: step ≡ rows seen)** — the per-row-type CE diagnostics (§4) carry the discriminating weight there, not timing alone.

**Phase 0b clamp:** present iff mean(trained-negative ΔG) ≤ mean(8-bystander ΔG) − 1.5 nats in ≥3 of the 4 count cells, both seeds (prior from #477: gap ≈ 0.7 → expectation is NO clamp).

**Phase 3 positive result (re-registered as a CONTRAST):** Phase 3's per-checkpoint reads cover the source AND the 8-bystander reference panel AND the 4 trained-negative contexts (same machinery as Phase 2; ~minutes of GPU). The registered positive is source-DIFFERENTIAL movement: seed-mean |Δz_marker(source) − mean Δz_marker(bystanders)| ≥ 1.0 logit or |Δz_eos(source) − mean Δz_eos(bystanders)| ≥ 1.0 logit at terminal, 2/2 seeds sign-consistent, with the EOS-margin and logZ co-reads reported so a common-mode logit shift cannot fire the criterion. Uniform movement across source + bystanders = generic SFT/formatting drift (reported as nonspecific, NOT an init-live coupling positive).

## 7. Decision Gates

Minimal set — wall ≈ 6–7 h, effect directions partly pre-verified by the parent:

1. **Smoke gate** (standard unified-dispatcher): one full cell (`ratio4to1_100p400n` seed 42) passes the §4 smoke asserts before the sweep. Justification: catches the band-stop-default misfire and any eval-path regression at 1/13 of the cost; threshold = the literal asserts, all grounded in committed parent behavior.
2. **Phase 0a adapter-application gate** (HALT-class): on-policy re-read of parent adapters reproduces committed terminal source ΔG within 1 nat. Justification: #534 showed an un-applied adapter silently nulls every downstream number; the threshold is the rule-file's own ~1-nat bound and every parent cell trivially passes it on intact tooling.

No mid-sweep metric kill gates: cells are independent, no intermediate signal can cheaply rule out the rest of the sweep, and the no-call branch of §6 is itself a reportable outcome. Jointly satisfiable: the two gates test disjoint things (new-path integrity; old-artifact integrity) with no opposed-sign demands. **Both gates are ENFORCED in the launch path** — `i601_launch.sh` sequences phase0 → gate → smoke → gate → sweep, and the dispatcher independently refuses non-smoke cells without both sentinels (§10); the gates are not advisory prose.

**Kill criteria (hypothesis-level, refined from the body):** as enumerated in §6 plus — H-equilibrium (the FEEDBACK MECHANISM, per the §4 pinned rule; the ratio-set-point phenomenology is killed only by the level rules, never by the clamp read) killed if fixed-ratio spread > 3 nats (2/2 seeds) OR schedule-matched lands ≥ 17 (primary space) OR no Phase-0b clamp OR Phase 4 locates no switch — operative rule: 4a classified ARREST (per §4 bands) AND, if 4b was reached, both 4b factors also classified ARREST; a 4a/4b “ambiguous” classification means the Phase-4 kill does NOT fire and the rig-localization question is reported open (ambiguous ≠ arrest). H-horizon killed if schedule-matched lands 13.5 ± 3 OR Phase 2 arrest steps are T-independent. H-coupling (init-live channel) killed if Phase 3 null AND Phase 2 source z_eos differs < 1 logit between the dense noneg and mixed cells throughout; the leakage-gated variant is then absorbed into H-equilibrium.
