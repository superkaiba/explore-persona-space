**Literature (Piggyback, arXiv 2606.06667, Zhao/Wu/Arora/Bau/Shi, 2026-06-08):**
- The chat-template **prefix** carries narrowly-trained behavior to off-domain queries. Qwen-2.5 prefix = `<|im_start|>system\nYou are Qwen [...]<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>user\n`; postfix = `<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n` (§4.1).
- **Attention (KV) patching** at prefix positions across all layers with base-model values recovers Qwen-2.5-7B alignment 39.7→86.5 general, 31.9→76.0 other-domain (§4.2, Fig. patch_qwen). **Query-token patching gives smaller, inconsistent recovery** (the negative-direction control). **Activation patching localizes the carrier to layer 9** for Qwen-2.5-7B (single-layer recovery 65.6).
- **TReFT** = mean-squared *relative* deviation of prefix K and V from base, per-layer, averaged over all L layers: $\mathcal{L}_{K}^{(l)} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{T}|}\sum_{t\in\mathcal{T}} \frac{\|\mathbf{k}_t^{(l)}-\mathbf{k}_t^{(l),\text{init}}\|_2^2}{\|\mathbf{k}_t^{(l),\text{init}}\|_2^2}$ (and analogously for V). **This is precisely score (a)** read off the trained adapter instead of penalized during training. - **Postfix patching on Qwen-2.5:** the paper reports postfix patching **greatly** improves general-query alignment for Qwen-2.5 (and only marginally for Llama-3.1) — i.e. on Qwen-2.5 the carrier is NOT cleanly prefix-exclusive (§4.3). This is the basis for the §4.3 control's descriptive (not binary) read.
- **Scope caveat carried forward:** the prefix story is model-conditional (broke on Qwen3-8B → postfix), single-run with no seeds/error bars in the paper. Our base IS Qwen-2.5-7B (where it holds), and we have #545's 2–3 seeds per cell.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (explanatory correlation).** A behavior's prefix-KV-shift score (a) correlates with its row-summed off-diagonal leakage magnitude in #545's matrix. *Prediction:* Spearman ρ(prefix-KV-shift, row-summed |L|) > 0.5 across the 19 rows (point estimate), **under both the raw all-L score and the gauge-normalized score (divisor `(α/√r)²`)**, with the three misaligned-advice rows (densest leakers) in the top quartile of prefix-KV-shift and the marker + taught-fact rows (cleanest nulls) in the bottom quartile.

**H2 (causal patch).** Patching base-model prefix KV into a leaky trained adapter reduces its leakage on #545's eval probes. *Prediction:* for the bad-medical row (broad-EM L = +0.113), prefix-patch cuts broad-EM leakage by ≥50% of the way back to base, and the cut exceeds the query-token-patch control by ≥2× (the paper's own asymmetry). The prefix-vs-query asymmetry is the robust claim; the prefix-vs-postfix asymmetry is NOT a clean claim on Qwen-2.5 (postfix also helps greatly here, §4.3) and is read descriptively.

**H3 (predictor race).** Prefix-binding, scored by #545's harness, beats the context-geometry champion on held-out cells. *Prediction:* prefix-binding leave-family-out CV mean > 0.15 (vs geometry's 0.0008) and quarantine τ > 0 (vs geometry's −0.34), **under both the raw and gauge-normalized (divisor `(α/√r)²`) prefix-KV-shift scores**. I do NOT predict it beats the behavior-native demonstration-conditioning leader; the interesting comparison is prefix-binding vs the *geometry* family, since prefix-binding is the mechanistic story for *why* geometry failed.

**Kill criterion.** If prefix-KV-shift ρ on row-summed leakage is < 0.2 (point) AND its CV mean is within noise of geometry's (≤ 0.05 on shared folds) AND the patch control (H2) fails to reproduce the paper's recovery direction on the bad-medical row, then prefix-binding is not a useful B→B′ leakage predictor on this matrix — a clean, publishable null (the carrier may be real-but-not-predictive). This is a determinate outcome, not a failed run. (The kill criterion conjunction structure and the N=19 reasoning are unchanged; the gauge-normalized leg uses the corrected `(α/√r)²` divisor.)

**LoRA-scale-artifact criterion (negative result, distinct from the null).** If the raw prefix-KV-shift score clears H1 (ρ > 0.5) and/or H3 (CV mean > 0.15) but the **gauge-normalized** score (divisor `(α/√r)²`) does NOT, the apparent signal is a LoRA-application-scale artifact — the raw cross-row ranking was tracking each adapter's gauge, not carrier strength. Because the score is a SQUARED norm (MSRD), a pure application-scale artifact enters the numerator as `(α/√r)²·carrier²`; the correct gauge divisor that isolates `carrier²` is therefore `(α/√r)²` (NOT the linear `α/√r`, which leaves a residual `α/√r` factor and would re-rank the high-gauge turner_em rows identically to the raw score — silently passing the artifact through). The per-row α/√r is ≈ 8 for the marker recipe, ≈ 11 for the generic B-rows, ≈ 45 for the turner_em rows; squared, the gauge spread is ≈ 64 → ≈ 121 → ≈ 2025, so the residual-linear-factor failure would be severe. This split is reported as a NEGATIVE result for the carrier-strength claim (the gauge confound is the real predictor), NOT as a pass. A genuine carrier-strength signal must rank correctly under both scores.

### 4.1 Score (a) — prefix-KV-shift (Phase 1)

For each of the 19 primary-arm adapters (seed-0 lead; seed-137 as the robustness replicate), load `PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, issue545_rows/<row>_primary_seed<S>)` with current PEFT (honors rsLoRA — reproduces #545's exact trained forward pass). Render the **fixed prefix** once: `render_chat(tokenizer, question="", context_id="qwen_default_system")` truncated to the pre-query span (the `<|im_start|>system\n...You are Qwen...<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>user\n` token positions). For each layer l and each prefix position t ∈ 𝒫, capture k and v from both the trained model and base (adapter disabled via `model.disable_adapter()`), compute the per-layer relative-deviation $\mathcal{L}_K^{(l)} + \mathcal{L}_V^{(l)}$ exactly as the TReFT eq., and aggregate: **all-L average** (primary raw score), **layer-9-only** (the paper's localized carrier; reported as a second variant `PFX__prefix_kv_shift_L9`), and **gauge-normalized** (the all-L MSRD divided by that row's rsLoRA **`(α/√r)²`** scale — the SQUARED divisor; reported as `PFX__prefix_kv_shift_gaugenorm_sq`). One score per row per variant (constant across columns within a row → broadcast to every `row|col` cell in the predictor JSON). The per-row α/√r scale is read from each adapter's own `adapter_config.json` (`lora_alpha`, `r`, `use_rslora`) and stored alongside the score in the predictor JSON so downstream agents can audit it; the JSON also stores `"gauge_normalization_power": 2` so any downstream re-analysis can audit the divisor power.

Pseudocode (new `scripts/issue595_prefix_carrier.py::compute_prefix_kv_shift`):
```python
def compute_prefix_kv_shift(row_id, seed, layers="all") -> dict[int, float]:
    base, tok = load_base()                       # AutoModelForCausalLM, bf16, device_map={"":0}
    model = PeftModel.from_pretrained(base, adapter_subfolder(row_id, "primary", seed))
    assert sum("lora" in n for n,_ in model.named_parameters()) > 0   # #492 no-op guard
    prefix_ids = render_prefix_ids(tok, ctx="qwen_default_system")    # 𝒫 = pre-query positions
    k_v_trained = capture_kv(model, prefix_ids)            # forward, output K/V per layer at 𝒫
    with model.disable_adapter():
        k_v_base = capture_kv(model, prefix_ids)
    per_layer = {l: msrd(k_v_trained[l].k, k_v_base[l].k) + msrd(k_v_trained[l].v, k_v_base[l].v)
                 for l in range(n_layers)}         # msrd = mean-squared relative deviation (TReFT eq.) — a SQUARED norm
    return per_layer                               # caller aggregates all-L mean + L9
    # caller also reads adapter_config.json -> alpha, r, use_rslora
    #   gauge = alpha / (r ** 0.5) if use_rslora else alpha / r
    #   # MSRD is a squared norm; a pure LoRA-scale artifact enters as gauge**2 * carrier**2,
    #   # so the gauge-isolating divisor is gauge**2 (NOT gauge):
    #   gaugenorm_sq_score = all_L_mean / (gauge ** 2)
    #   # equivalently rank rows by sqrt(all_L_mean) / gauge
    #   # store {"gauge": gauge, "gauge_normalization_power": 2} in the predictor JSON metadata
```

**Per-adapter LoRA-gauge comparability.** The TReFT eq. reads each trained model faithfully — *within* an adapter, the prefix-KV-shift IS the construct (the literal relative deviation of that model's prefix K/V from base), and that within-adapter measurement is exact. The comparability concern is purely *cross-row*: the 19 #545 adapters are heterogeneous in rsLoRA application gauge (the multiplicative scale α/√r at which the LoRA updates enter the forward pass — see §10's per-recipe enumeration: ≈ 8 for the marker recipe, ≈ 11 for the generic B-rows, ≈ 45 for the turner_em rows). A raw cross-row comparison of the prefix-KV-shift therefore conflates "carrier strength" (how much of the behavior the fine-tune bound to the prefix) with each adapter's gauge.

**The divisor must be the SQUARED gauge `(α/√r)²` (squared-norm correction).** The prefix-KV-shift is a *mean-squared relative deviation* (MSRD) — a SQUARED norm (`‖Δk‖² / ‖k_base‖²`, per the TReFT eq.). Under the rsLoRA application-scaling rule the LoRA update enters the forward pass as `Δ = (α/√r)·BA·x`, so the *linear* deviation `Δk` scales with `α/√r` and the *squared* deviation in the MSRD numerator scales with `(α/√r)²`. A pure LoRA-application-scale artifact therefore propagates into the score as `(α/√r)²·carrier²`. To isolate `carrier²` (the cross-row-valid binding measure), the correct divisor on the squared metric is **`(α/√r)²`**; dividing by only the linear `α/√r` (the v2 rule) leaves a residual `α/√r` factor — and because the three turner_em rows are simultaneously the highest-gauge (`α/√r ≈ 45`, squared ≈ 2025) AND the highest-leakage rows, a residual-linear divisor re-ranks them essentially identically to the raw score, so the v2 "gauge-normalized" leg would silently pass an artifact through H1/H3 (the load-bearing §3 negative-result detector would not fire on a false positive). Equivalently, one may rank rows by `sqrt(all-L MSRD) / (α/√r)` — the same ranking, applied in the un-squared space. **Mitigation:** report THREE row rankings — (i) raw all-L mean, (ii) raw layer-9, and (iii) **gauge-normalized with divisor `(α/√r)²`**. H1 and H3 are scored on all three; the headline reports both the raw all-L and the gauge-normalized rankings, and a successful predictor must rank correctly under BOTH (otherwise the apparent signal is a LoRA-scale artifact, not a carrier-strength signal — see the §3 LoRA-scale-artifact criterion). Each predictor JSON stores the per-row α/√r AND `"gauge_normalization_power": 2` alongside the score so the gauge and the divisor power are auditable downstream.

**Why code, not a model call?** Score (a) is a deterministic activation-extraction over a fixed prefix; there is no unstructured-data heuristic — it's a numerical norm read off the forward pass. (Per §4 rule: this is a tensor computation, model-call framing does not apply.)

### 4.2 Score (b) — prefix-patch leakage recovery (Phase 2)

For each leaky row, generate on #545's eval probes under the trained adapter with **base prefix KV patched in at all layers** (Piggyback attention-patch protocol §4.2): a `forward_pre_hook` on each attention module that, for prefix positions t ∈ 𝒫, substitutes the base model's cached k_t, v_t. The same probes, decoding params (temp/seed/max_new_tokens from #545's Reproducibility table per column), and judges (gpt-4o broad-EM, Claude Sonnet for sycophancy/refusal/etc.) as #545, so Δleakage is on the same scale as #545's L.

**Measurement validity — on-distribution (REQUIRED).** The patch eval is **on-policy**: the patched model generates its own completions on #545's exact probe distribution, judged by #545's exact judges at the natural output position — NOT teacher-forced log-probs on canned text. The only deviation from #545's generation is (i) HF-vs-vLLM backend (decoding params matched exactly; a 1-row backend-parity check confirms unpatched-HF reproduces #545's vLLM L within judge noise before any patch is read) and (ii) reduced probe count (cap stated, CI widened accordingly).

**Reduced-probe scope:** Phase 2 runs on the rows where #545 measured non-trivial leakage (the three advice rows + the two fact rows + the two sycophancy rows + one clean null = bad_medical, risky_financial, extreme_sports, taught_fact, reversed_fact, compliment_writing, wrong_claim_agreement, marker) on their highest-|L| columns (broad_em, sycophancy/wrong-claim-agreement, over-refusal). Score (b) is a per-row aggregate (row-summed Δleakage over its measured columns) for H1 and a per-(row,col) predictor for H3.

**Why code, not a model call?** The patch is a tensor substitution inside the forward pass; the judging reuses #545's existing Claude/gpt-4o judges (those ARE model calls, unchanged). No new heuristic classifier is introduced.

### 4.3 Controls (Phase 3)

- **Postfix-patch control:** patch base postfix KV (`<|im_end|>\n<|im_start|>assistant\n`) instead of prefix, on the bad-medical row. H2's headline prediction (prefix-cut ≥ 2× query-cut) is robust to this, because query-patch IS the paper's confirmed weak control — but the prefix-vs-postfix asymmetry is reported as an observation, not claimed as a clean prefix-specificity result.
- **Query-token-patch control:** patch base KV at the *query* positions (the paper's negative direction — smaller, inconsistent recovery). The prefix-vs-query asymmetry is the paper's core causal claim and the robust manipulation check; reproducing it on our matrix confirms the harness works.

## 6. Evaluation

**H1 — explanatory correlation.** Spearman ρ between each prefix-binding score and row-summed off-diagonal |L| (seed-mean, default context, flagged cells excluded per #545). Reported with the family-clustered bootstrap CI (reuse `scoring._family_row_bootstrap`). If raw passes but the `(α/√r)²` gauge-normalized score fails, that is a NEGATIVE result (the apparent correlation was the LoRA gauge entering the squared metric as `(α/√r)²·carrier²`, not carrier strength — see §3 LoRA-scale-artifact criterion), reported as such, not a pass.

**H2 — causal patch.** Per-row Δleakage (trained − patched) on the highest-|L| column, with the prefix-vs-query asymmetry ratio. Success: bad-medical broad-EM Δleakage ≥ 50% of (L_trained − 0) AND prefix-cut ≥ 2× query-cut. The prefix-vs-postfix comparison is reported descriptively (magnitudes side-by-side), NOT as a binary prefix-specificity pass — postfix patching helps greatly on Qwen-2.5 (§4.3).

**H3 — predictor race.** Weighted Kendall τ via `score()`: pooled dev, leave-family-out CV mean (per-fold dots), quarantine (scored once). Success: prefix-KV-shift CV mean > 0.15 and quarantine τ > 0 (vs geometry 0.0008 / −0.34), under **both the raw and gauge-normalized (divisor `(α/√r)²`) scores**. A raw-passes/gauge-norm-fails split is reported as a LoRA-scale artifact (negative result for the carrier-strength claim).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Prefix-position set = Qwen-2.5 chat-template prefix with default system prompt.** *Why:* the paper's recovery (39.7→86.5) was measured patching this exact prefix span (system + role markers before the query) on Qwen-2.5-7B; the score must read the same positions the carrier was localized on. *Source:* Piggyback arXiv 2606.06667 §4.1 (prefix definition) + §4.2 (recovery under default system prompt). *Alternatives:* default (no-system-prompt) prefix — rejected, would measure binding on the minimal `<|im_start|>user\n` span the paper did NOT localize, breaking comparability with the paper's number; postfix — kept as a *control* (§4.3), not the primary.
- **Layer aggregation = all-L equal-weight mean for score (a).** *Why:* TReFT averages the per-layer relative-deviation over all L layers (the regularizer the paper validated); score (a) is that regularizer read post-hoc. *Source:* Piggyback §"Token-Regularized FineTuning" (the $\mathcal{L}_{KV} = \frac{1}{L}\sum_l(\mathcal{L}_K^{(l)}+\mathcal{L}_V^{(l)})$ eq.). *Alternatives:* layer-9-only — kept as a reported variant (the activation-patch localized the carrier to layer 9 = 65.6), but NOT primary because the KV-patch recovery (the 86.5 number) was across all layers; norm-weighted — rejected, no grounding.
- **Gauge normalization divisor = per-row `(α/√r)²` (squared-norm correction).** *Why:* prefix-KV-shift (score a) is a *mean-squared relative deviation* (MSRD, per the TReFT eq.), a SQUARED norm (`‖Δk‖²/‖k_base‖²`). A LoRA-application-scale artifact enters the numerator as `(α/√r)²·carrier²` (the rsLoRA rule `Δ = (α/√r)·BA·x` makes the linear deviation scale with `α/√r`, hence the squared deviation with `(α/√r)²`), so the correct divisor to isolate `carrier²` is `(α/√r)²` (equivalently, rank rows by `sqrt(MSRD)/(α/√r)`). The 19 #545 adapters are heterogeneous in gauge (α/√r ≈ 8 marker, ≈ 11 generic B-rows, ≈ 45 turner_em — see §10; squared ≈ 64 / 121 / 2025), and the three turner_em rows are simultaneously the highest-gauge AND the highest-leakage rows, so the v2 linear divisor `(α/√r)¹` left a residual `α/√r` factor and would re-rank the artifact essentially identically to the raw score — silently passing the LoRA-scale artifact through the §3 negative-result detector (the load-bearing headline safeguard). Corrected in v3 per the cross-critic-corroborated Phase 2 reconciler verdict (both the Methodology and Statistics reconcilers independently identified this). *Source:* algebraic property of the TReFT MSRD eq. (Piggyback §"Token-Regularized FineTuning") combined with the rsLoRA application-scale equation `Δ = (α/√r)·BA·x` (#601 rsLoRA application-scaling rule, `.claude/rules/artifact-reuse.md` (g)); each adapter's own `adapter_config.json` for the per-row α, r, use_rslora. *Alternatives:* linear divisor `α/√r` — REJECTED (this is what v2 used); on a squared metric it leaves a residual `α/√r` factor and does NOT isolate carrier strength, so it would let the high-gauge artifact pass H1/H3. Un-corrected raw MSRD — kept as the dual-pass requirement's RAW leg (H1/H3 must hold under both raw AND the `(α/√r)²` gauge-normalized score), NOT as the gauge-isolating leg. Normalize by parameter count or by raw α — rejected, neither is the gauge the updates actually enter at under rsLoRA.
- **Patch protocol = KV substitution at prefix positions across all layers.** *Why:* this is exactly the attention-patching that produced the headline recovery; activation (residual-stream) patching is the per-layer localization variant, weaker recovery. *Source:* Piggyback §4.2 (attention patching eq. + Fig. patch_qwen). *Alternatives:* residual-stream activation patching — rejected as primary (paper notes it has weaker recovery than KV-patch-all-layers); used implicitly only via the layer-9 read of score (a).
- **Query-token patch + postfix patch as controls; postfix read descriptively.** *Why:* the prefix-vs-query asymmetry is the paper's core causal claim and the robust manipulation check that the harness works.
