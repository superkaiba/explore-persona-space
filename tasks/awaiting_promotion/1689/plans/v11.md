If the real-u2 arm differs from the constant-fallback arm at rung >5, the real-vs-haiku contrast IS a genuine tier-1-vs-tier-3 provenance measurement and stands as the parent's implicit intent.

**H-estimator-validity (instrument):** the identity+bias baseline held-out R² on the real-u2 arm sits above chance (kNN@1 acc > k/n_pool) for both prefix and context arms in both models, matching the parent recapture's estimator-validity profile (parent recapture identity+bias held-out R² > 0 on the constant-fallback lmsys arm — the maps had signal even at n_train ~ d/2 in the reduced basis).

**Falsification branches:**
- (Instrument) identity+bias baseline is at or below chance on the real-u2 arm in either model — n_train is too small for d=3584 even in the reduced basis (k rule saturates at 1024). Kill: report caveat, do NOT read the ladder.
- (Science) rung-reached distribution on the real-u2 arm is inside the selection-symmetric null band of the recapture arm's rung-reached distribution (same cells: user-provenance ordered pairs, within-model, within-framing). Kill: the parent's user-provenance geometry Takeaway is scope-caveated as "constant-fallback vs generated" rather than flipped.
- (Science) rung-reached distribution shifts DOWN (weaker corrections suffice) on the real-u2 arm — real u2 text is more predictable from u1+a1 than a generic follow-up. Consistent with the constant fallback being a hard case.
- (Science) rung-reached distribution shifts UP (deeper corrections needed) on the real-u2 arm — the parent understated the real vs simulated gap. Rewrites the Takeaway.

**Per-pair verdict quantities (inherited verbatim, computed in the reduced-basis coordinates):** rung_reached = the parent's 9-rung mapping-similarity ladder (direct transfer / context offset / answer offset / bias refit / global scaling / rotation / context reparameterization / answer reparameterization / full A·M·B); R²(rung k) < 0.9·R²(free) → advance rung; identity+bias baseline + kNN retrieval reported alongside every fit.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Estimator-degenerate ⇔ identity_bias_r2 ≤ 0 OR knn_ratio ≤ 1; Free-map-uninformative ⇔ identity_bias_r2 > 0 AND knn_ratio > 1 AND free_over_identity ≤ 1; Real-u2-tier-1-supported ⇔ identity_bias_r2 > 0 AND knn_ratio > 1 AND free_over_identity > 1 AND max_delta_rung ≥ 1 AND null_band_exceeded ≥ 1; Constant-fallback-artifact ⇔ otherwise.

Atom key (rewritten from R² / kNN / rung prose to the canonical threshold + sign atom forms verify_plan.py's c20 parser recognises; predicates unchanged in content):
- `identity_bias_r2` = held-out R² of the identity+learned-bias baseline on the real-u2 arm (both prefix + context maps, both models); the sign atom `≤ 0` = at-or-below chance (kill condition).
- `knn_ratio` = ratio of kNN@1 retrieval accuracy on the real-u2 arm to chance = k/n_pool (threshold `≤ 1` = at-or-below chance).
- `free_over_identity` = ratio of held-out R²(free map) to held-out R²(identity+bias) on the real-u2 arm; `≤ 1` = the fitted map adds no signal over the shift baseline.
- `max_delta_rung` = the max, over (framing, model, direction, mapping_arm), of |rung_reached(real-u2, per pair) − rung_reached(constant-fallback, per pair)|; `≥ 1` = at least one pair shifts by a whole rung.

The four labels partition the outcome space: every (fit_valid, map_informative, delta_rung, null_band) atom lands in exactly one label; every atom is claimed by exactly one predicate; the "otherwise" tail is claimed by Constant-fallback-artifact.
