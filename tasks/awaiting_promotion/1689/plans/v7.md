## 3. Hypothesis

H-A (shared-readout consistency): for pairs the parent reconciled at rung ≤6, the DERIVED answer map B_derived = W_S M W_S⁺ (with derived bias) predicts held-out y_T as well as the free map B_free — the "same operator" reads are equivalent to "one readout + affine context transfer". H-B: pairs at rung 7–9 split, with B_derived2 = W_T M W_S⁺ rescuing the pairs whose failure is readout change, not context-map insufficiency. H-C (context-side structure): the fitted M is dominated by translation + a low-rank correction (minimal sufficient k ≪ d for most pairs), and the top correction directions overlap the diff-of-means direction. H-D (cross-model): base↔instruct same-condition maps are structurally uniform across the lattice (same weakest class / shared correction subspace), and their derived-vs-free verdicts favor B_derived2 over B_derived (the readout differs across models).

Per-pair verdict quantities (held-out R², pooled over the 5 outer folds, per ordered pair × arm × model-pair): g1 = R²(B_derived) − 0.9·R²(B_free); g2 = R²(B_derived2) − 0.9·R²(B_free) — the parent's 0.9× reconciliation convention transplanted with B_free as the within-form ceiling. R²(B_derived) is the MAX over the registered truncation ranks k ∈ {32,128,512,eff-rank} with the selection riding per null/sensitivity read (never a fixed-k observed vs max-k null).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Shared-readout-supported ⇔ g1 ≥ 0; Readout-changed ⇔ g1 < 0 AND g2 ≥ 0; Transfer-map-insufficient ⇔ otherwise.

Registered concordance read (item 6): Spearman ρ between the parent's rung_reached and g1 across the 252 within-model pair-arm-model units per arm; expected NEGATIVE (higher rung → lower g1). Quantitative expectation: ≥70% of rung ≤6 pairs read Shared-readout-supported; a sub-50% rate on rung-1 pairs (where the parent found direct transfer works) falsifies the derived-map construction itself (estimation, not model — triaged via the truncation-rank sensitivity, §4 item 2).
