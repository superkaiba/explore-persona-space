# Fine-tuning-induced map change is context-dependent but not gated on trained-prefix identity: never-trained prefixes resembling the training corpus overshoot the trained-prefix anchor on every persona-trained arm (HIGH confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_1768.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/bd202f5f320eb897115f06d4607bafe02f75335a/docs/methodology/issue_1768.md) · [gist](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/36e1e690510689f33c0f579951ada3ba)

## Takeaways

- Never-trained prefixes drawn from the sycophancy training corpus overshoot the trained-prefix anchor on 3 of 3 persona-trained arms, so content proximity to the training distribution carries the map-change amplification, not trained-prefix identity.
- The three conversation-trained sycophancy arms cross the refit-noise floor only once a rich prefix is present, and neutral never-trained rungs reach just 52–74% of that anchor at matched length; richness saturates the base side too.
- Off-target, 107 of 216 cells change above the floor, rank-tracking the weights-carried answer shift; re-pooling the context at the last prompt token only ever adds positives, raising the changed count to 124.
- Whether the context representation barely moves is a pooling property — 2.5% against 24–27% — and the pooling that most improves the map makes the base-geometry gate worse while a direct context-to-write ridge improves.
- The measured model-versus-text split is aggregation-dependent: weights take 0.698 of the mean shift, text 0.779 of the typical row. Round one's stand-in for the text effect is refuted.
- The write rotates into the training displacement during training yet stays inside the null band; refitting the base map on the training pairs closes at most 0.2% of a closable change, and the operator update is high-rank.

## Goal

**This experiment in context:** [#722](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/722) fit base-vs-trained context→answer maps at n=16 contexts and could not resolve sycophancy or EM (degenerate per-cell intervals); [#667](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/667) found on an older fleet that one direction carries 0.81–0.86 of the update variance but that the write direction is not the theory's training displacement (cosine ≈ 0) — its write was measured teacher-forced on frozen base responses, the same regime as this task's matched-text tree. This experiment re-asks all five questions — context movement, map change, answer movement, write shape/predictability, and theory assumptions A7–A11 — at 15,000 real-user contexts on the dose-controlled model-organism fleet ([#1481](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1481) in-band checkpoints, [#1586](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1586) full fine-tune selections), with the mapping baselines from [#811](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/811)/[#722](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/722) attached to every fitted map.

**Broader narrative:** the leakage-theory paper's fine-tuning section needs to know whether fine-tuning a behavior into a context moves the context representation, rewrites the context→answer map, or moves the answer state along a predictable direction — the open question of whether the write is predictable ahead of time from pre-fine-tuning geometry (training displacement δ, behavior read-out r_B, or the marker unembedding row).

## Methodology

**Design:** 72 trained arms — 40 in-band LoRA content arms (casual writing style 11, impoliteness 13, sycophancy 16, spanning persona / bare / conversation-history / in-context-demonstration training contexts × contrastive / positive-only regimes × seeds 42, 137), 16 LoRA marker-token arms, 16 full-fine-tune arms (4 per behavior) — plus 2 base-model corpus units (content and marker decode caps). Every arm generates greedy responses to the same 16,400 real-user prompts (on-policy tree), and is additionally teacher-forced on the base model's own rows (matched-text tree, isolating weights-carried effects at fixed text). Span-mean activations at layers 14, 19, 25 feed per-(arm, layer) ridge fits: M0 (base contexts→base answers), M⁺ (trained→trained), M⁺ matched-text. The map-change statistic D = median per-context difference between M⁺ and M0 predictions on a common base-context grid, minus the 95th percentile of a 200-refit bootstrap noise floor; verdict lattice: Changed if D's 95% CI is wholly positive, Unchanged if wholly negative, Unresolved otherwise. Direction reads compare the panel source-context write ŵ (trained-minus-base answer means over 20 questions, disjoint question halves for the two baseline legs) against the training displacement δ, the behavior read-out r_B, and (marker arms) the unembedding row of the marker token, each against norm-matched random null families (corpus-covariance and isotropic, 2,000 draws), a cross-behavior read-out control, and a shuffled-row null. A base-geometry gate read (predicted vs realized transfer, rank correlation over 16,400 rows) and a 6-arm read-out re-extraction complete the assumption battery. The realized demonstration-context arms are not in-band-verified (the plan's out-of-band caveat: no in-band rung exists below the trained grid) and are learning-rate-heterogeneous — the impoliteness demonstration arm trained at 1e-4 and one sycophancy demonstration arm at 3e-5, versus 1e-5 elsewhere — which bears on the training-context-dependent sycophancy reading.

Round 3 (on-target prefix corpus): 12 round-1 arms re-captured with each arm's trained-in context prepended to the user queries — the persona system prompt, the real conversation-history prefix, or the in-context demonstrations, rendered from the same context registry the training mixes used and asserted equal to the context embedded in the sha-pinned training-mix rows (5 rows per family). Three conditions per arm: the own trained prefix; bare prompts (round-1 stores refit at the matched n, no recapture); and, on 6 arms, a swapped prefix (persona-trained arms get the conversation prefix and vice versa), separating trained-prefix specificity from any-prefix effects. All conditions share one 4,400-row sha set (3,000 train rows subsampled seed 42 from the round-1 corpus plus the pinned 400 validation and 1,000 test rows); five base units captured under each (prefix, decode-cap) pair give the within-condition base map, and matched-text teacher-forced trees ran per condition. Refit-noise floors were recomputed per condition at n=3,000; the primary contrast ΔD = D_own − D_bare is paired over the shared pinned test rows. The 4 bare-trained sycophancy arms are their own on-target condition by construction (ΔD ≡ 0; reference rows, not refit). At 3,000 train rows the 3,584-dimensional ridge fits sit in the regularization-limited regime where training rows are fewer than feature dimensions (validation-selected λ, flagged per fit); every cross-condition read is n-matched by design, and round-1 n=15,000 values are never compared directly.

Round 4 (prefix-richness dose ladder): three content persona-trained arms and the conversation-trained sycophancy comparator re-captured under a 3-rung ladder of never-trained prefixes — real WildChat two-turn exchanges built by the trained conversation prefix's own recipe (first user/assistant exchange, 2,000-character cap per turn), length-stratified to bracket the trained prefixes at 10, 85, and 730 content tokens (build-measured with the production tokenizer: persona prompt 10 content tokens, conversation prefix 730 — the plan-time character-count estimates were ~11 and ~800; the long rung realized the conversation prefix's exact length). Rung selection is deterministic (closest to the length target among the first 50,000 WildChat rows) behind five never-trained / content-novel screens: source-conversation and prefix-recipe-hash disjointness from all three trained prefixes, no overlap with the 4,400-row query corpus, no trained-context substrings, and English non-toxic non-redacted rows only; one fixed prefix per rung, shared across arms and the per-rung base units. Everything else — the 4,400-row sha set, capture rig, fit estimator, per-condition refit floors at n=3,000, and the paired-contrast machinery — is inherited from round 3 unchanged, and the round-3 bare / own / swapped per-context vectors serve as the paired comparators without re-running. Outcome criteria per persona-trained arm at layer 19, on the raw median map difference: richness-consistent when the long rung reaches the swapped trained prefix's level while rising over the short rung; identity-consistent when the long rung sits wholly below the swapped level; the comparator reads own-suppressed only when the never-trained long rung exceeds its own trained prefix.

Round 5 (behavior-relevant never-trained prefix panel): the same 4 arms re-captured under three never-trained behavior-relevant conversation prefixes — judge-kept sycophancy exchanges from the conversation-trained comparator's own datagen pool that entered no training mix (the pool's 6 surplus rows, sha-excluded against every training mix and the query corpus), chained two exchanges per prefix (4 turns) to reach the round-4 730-token rung's length band, realized at 580 / 456 / 568 content tokens after tokenization (2 of 3 inside the 547.5–912.5 band; the enumerated-pairing fallback engaged as pre-specified, and the 456-token prefix reads below the band). Everything else — the 4,400-row sha set, capture and fit rig, per-prefix refit floors at n=3,000, and the paired-contrast machinery against the round-3 bare / own / swapped and round-4 rung per-context files — is inherited unchanged. Outcome criteria per persona-trained arm and prefix at layer 19, on the raw median map difference: behavior-relevance-consistent when the prefix reaches the trained anchor while exceeding the matched-length neutral level; identity-consistent when it stays wholly below the anchor; arm label by majority over the three prefixes. The inference is asymmetric by design: an identity-consistent outcome would have ruled out content, generator style, and turn count together, while a behavior-relevance-consistent outcome establishes that never-trained material suffices without separating those facets.

Six later rounds, all capture-or-algebra only (no new on-policy generation anywhere), re-test the round-1 off-target battery. (a) The capture-noise-floor round replicated the teacher-forced answer capture on 6 units under two batch/row-order regimes. (b) The checkpoint-dynamics round re-read the fixed-text panel write at every captured rung of each adapter arm's training ladder (329 of the 1,236 published rungs, a deliberate spread subsample ordered verdict-rung-then-endpoints-then-farthest-point so all 56 adapter arms are represented; markers at 12 rungs each, content arms at a median of 3), racing it per rung against the training displacement, the behavior read-out, and (marker arms) the unembedding row, and coupling it to the per-rung install reads in the reused verdict manifest. Full fine-tune arms publish no ladder, so they are absent by construction. (c) The (model × text) 2×2 round measured the cell round 1 could only attribute: the base model teacher-forced on each trained arm's own on-policy response text, over all 72 arms and the same sha-joined corpus rows, giving an exact additive split of the answer shift into a text effect, a weight-carried function effect, and their interaction; a second leg captured each trained arm teacher-forced on its own training positives, with the base side reused from round 1's displacement cells under sha and row-count asserts. (d) The last-token re-pool round re-summarised the context vector at the last prompt token instead of the prompt span-mean and re-fit all 216 cells, reusing round 1's rows, splits, ridge machinery, floors, and seeds so pooling is the only change; answer-side reads (horse race, write rank, read-out stability) are pooling-invariant and were not re-run. (e) Its continuation captured the 4 base panel units at the last token so the base-geometry gate could be re-read on all 216 cells, and re-fit the direct base-context-to-write ridge on the 8-arm subset in both response trees. (f) The map-augmentation round asked whether the post-training map is reconstructible: per (arm, layer) it re-fit the base map on the 15,000 corpus training pairs together with that arm's own K training pairs (base-model teacher-forced embeddings, K = 20 for 7 arms and 200 for the marker arm), upweighted across an effective-loss-mass grid, with the ridge penalty selected on corpus validation rows only and never on the comparison target; a shuffle-fit null permuted the K training answers across their contexts. Its extension re-fit the operators at their committed penalties and read the realized update's singular spectrum, key-side and answer-side target projections, and principal-angle match to the predicted update against matched random-subspace nulls, at layers 14, 19 and 25 under last-token pooling (span-mean unrun), with the behavior read-out and the marker unembedding row added as answer-side targets from banked tensors.

**Training:** **N/A — no model training.** All checkpoints are reused fleet artifacts. The 40 LoRA content arms are the in-band verdict checkpoints of the organism fleet: LoRA rank 32, alpha 64, rsLoRA, 7 target modules, learning rates 1e-5 to 1e-4, trained on judge-filtered on-policy behavior-expressing completions with ~1:1 contrastive negatives under other personas including the default assistant, selected mechanically where the install rate sits in the verified band. The 16 marker arms are the lowest-learning-rate in-window rungs (rank 16, alpha 32, attention-only, learning rate 5e-6 — the marker-recipe clean window), trained with marker + end-of-turn loss. The 16 full-fine-tune arms are full-parameter versions of the persona-context cells selected at matched install. Adapter identities were probed at run start (`adapter_config.json` per arm; recipe values above read from those configs).
