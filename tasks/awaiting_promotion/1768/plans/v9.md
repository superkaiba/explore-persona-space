- **What I'll run:** round 3 found that models fine-tuned with a short persona prompt diverge from base 1.8–2.2× MORE under an unfamiliar conversation prefix than under their own trained prompt — but the swapped prefixes also differed in length and richness, so I can't tell whether "unfamiliar" or "longer/richer" drives it. ## 3. Hypothesis

**H-rich (primary):** the persona-trained arms' swapped-prefix divergence is a richness effect. Under never-trained prefixes, the raw trained-vs-base map difference m_c (median per-context ‖M⁺(c)−M0(c)‖ over the shared 1,000 test rows) rises with rung richness for the 3 content persona-trained arms, and the length-matched long rung reproduces the swapped-control level (round 3 ctrl ≈ 1.8–2.2× own).

**H-own-suppression (comparator):** the conversation-trained comparator shows the same rise under never-trained rungs — its LOW divergence is specific to its OWN trained prefix (the 24.7-vs-4.8 asymmetry is familiarity-suppression, richness-immune).
