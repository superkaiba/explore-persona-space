### 6Δ.1 — Per-cell install FLOOR (the binding fix) — pre-registered, cited per DV

**The rule:** a cell's geometry DVs (top-share, PR_λ, rank-K@90, cos-to-r_B, cross-arm cosine) are read **ONLY IF that cell clears its behavior-specific install floor** at the dose-matched checkpoint. A cell that cannot reach floor after the dose budget (4Δ.6) is **DROPPED from the geometry verdict and REPORTED by name as a non-install** — never read as geometry (this is the parent's exact failure: 15 of 18 cells were read off non-installed or marginally-installed edits). The floor replaces v5's `GATE_CONTENT_INSTALL_MIN_RATE_GAIN = 0.0` (the >0 cutoff that let the +0.15 flat-dial sycophancy and the 0.0 EM pass/fail meaninglessly).

| Behavior | Install DV (primary, on-policy behavioral) | Floor cutoff | `Source:` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marker** | on-policy `log P(` ※`)` at end slot, trained − base (three-space, four-float) | source log P − base **∈ [5, 12] nat** (the band-stop window; bystanders below argmax ceiling) — UNCHANGED, already the correct floor via `MarkerBandStopCallback` | `marker-training-recipe.md` "usable window 5–12 nat" + `#538` (the [5,12] landing) |
| **Sycophancy** | judge-scored on-policy agreement RATE, trained − base (Claude Sonnet 4.5, the #411/#612 agreement construct) | judge-rate gain **≥ +0.40** over base at the dose-matched checkpoint | `#411` (installed sources reached +0.65 to +0.92 self-implant; +0.40 is a conservative floor at ~half the demonstrated install, well clear of the v5 +0.15 flat-dial failure) + `#608` (dose-matched cells reach the 0.94–0.97 own-rate band; +0.40 gain is reachable well below the censoring ceiling) |
| **EM** | judge-scored on-policy misalignment RATE, trained − base (Claude Sonnet 4.5, Betley/Turner EM construct) | judge-rate gain **≥ +0.20** over base at the endpoint | `#519` (the recipe that installed EM at all; EM rates on Qwen-7B are lower and harder than sycophancy — #452/#458 documented insecure-code failing entirely, Turner corpus succeeding) + `#411` (the +0.20 floor mirrors #411's own "training-failure flag" cutoff: "+0.20 floor I used to flag training failure").
