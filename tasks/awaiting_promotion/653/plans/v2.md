# Plan — #653: Do conditional behaviors decompose cleanly into read and write features?

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Two experiments asking the same question — when you teach a model "in this role, do this behavior," does that split cleanly into a part that *detects the role* and a part that *produces the behavior*, the way a tidy two-piece edit would? Probe A pokes the base model directly (no training): nudge its hidden state in a random direction, let it write, then read back what an un-nudged copy infers from those words. Probe B trains the behavior in for real at four "edit sizes" (from the smallest possible one-direction edit up to retraining the whole model) and watches how the hidden states move. I run both across three different behaviors and a few different roles, so the answer isn't a fluke of one setup.
- **What I expect:** The clean split mostly holds for the small edits — the move is low-dimensional and the "write" re-reads roughly as itself — but it gets messier (more spread out, more rotated) as the edit gets bigger, and a few behaviors won't behave like the others.
- **What would change my mind:** If the small-edit case is *already* diffuse (no clean structure even at the bottom), or if a big full retrain stays just as tidy as a one-direction edit — either would overturn the "small edits are special" story the rank-1 picture rests on.

## 1. Goal

Test whether a conditional behavior — "in context `C`, emit behavior `B`" — decomposes **cleanly** into a separable **read** part (condition-detection) and a **write** part (behavior-production), as the rank-1 LoRA idealization (`ΔW ≈ w·x_C^T`; theory `docs/notes/rank1_leakage_model.tex` A2) assumes. We attack it from two sides: **Arm A** (training-free) characterizes the base model's autoregressive write→read map `ρ` through the token bottleneck under random-bias steering; **Arm B** characterizes how a real fine-tune moves activations (`Δx`) across the edit-rank ladder (rank-1 LoRA → rank-r LoRA → full FT). For each we ask: is the structure **low-rank and read↔write-aligned** (clean — H1), **structured but rotated** (H2), or **diffuse / high-rank** (H3)? Per the user's breadth directive, the verdict is reported **per (behavior × context) cell AND aggregated** across ≥3 behaviors and a few source contexts, so the answer generalizes rather than resting on a single behavior/context. This is the empirical instantiation of the theory's central anomaly (`r_b ≠ d_b ≠ v_b`; write cos ≈ −0.03 to the realized marker shift, #521; write cos 0.04 yet 98% ablatable, Soligo) and walks its Assumption-5 relaxation ladder (rank-one → low-rank multi-pair → rich regime).

## 3. Hypotheses

Stated per arm; the verdict is the per-cell ranking of these three plus the cross-cell consistency read.

- **H1 — clean decomposition.** Structure approximately linear, low-rank, alignment-preserving. *Arm A:* `ρ(w) ≈ Jw` with high variance-explained, low effective rank, round-trip cos(w, ρ(w)) bounded away from 0, and `ρ(d_B) ≈ r_B`. *Arm B:* `Δx` near rank-one (top-direction variance share ≥ 0.7), top direction aligned to `r_B` (cos ≥ 0.5), and this holds across the rank ladder up to full FT. **Falsified if** effective rank is high or top-share is low at any rung.
- **H2 — structured but rotated.** Low-rank but NOT alignment-preserving: `ρ` / `Δx` low effective rank but the leading direction is a stable rotation away from `r_B` (cos near 0 but reproducible across seeds; SVD top-share high). The decomposition is real but read/write bases differ — matches the write⊥read-out anomalies.
- **H3 — diffuse / no clean structure.** High effective rank (≥ ~10 to explain 90% variance) or dominated by content-independent drift; no feature-level map. *Arm B rank-dependent sub-case:* H1/H2 holds at rank-1 but degrades to H3 as rank → full FT (the rank-1 idealization is rank-specific).

**Quantitative per-cell thresholds (pre-registered):** effective rank = participation ratio `(Σσ_i)² / Σσ_i²` of the shift/Jacobian spectrum; "low-rank" = PR ≤ 3 OR top-direction variance share ≥ 0.7. "Aligned" = |cos(top dir, `r_B`)| ≥ 0.5 AND exceeds the norm-matched-random CI upper bound (#503). "Rotated" = top-share ≥ 0.7 but |cos| < 0.5 with cross-seed cos ≥ 0.7 (reproducible). The 0.7 top-share threshold inherits #521's EM read (cos 0.96–0.98 → clearly rank-one); the PR≤3 inherits the participation-ratio convention from the rank-ladder literature.

**Aggregation rule (the generality claim):** a verdict is "consistent" if the same H-label wins in ≥ 5 of 6 (behavior × context) cells per arm at the matched rung; "behavior-dependent" if the label splits by behavior (e.g. EM=H1, marker=H2 — the #521 split would predict this); "context-dependent" if it splits by context within a behavior. Reported as a 3×2 (behavior × context) verdict grid per arm + per rung.

### Why code, not a model call?
The coherence filter on Arm A continuations is the one unstructured-data heuristic. **(a)** Alternative: a single Claude judge call per continuation ("is this coherent English?"). **(b)** Preferred as code: the project has a perplexity-under-base-model handle already (the unsteered teacher-force pass in A2 computes per-token log-probs for free) — a mean-log-prob threshold is deterministic, zero added API cost at ~thousands of continuations, and on the exact distribution being read; a judge adds latency + a second saturating binary. **(c)** Flips to a judge if the perplexity filter admits fluent-but-degenerate text (repetition loops) — mitigated by adding a 3-gram-repetition rejection rule (also code). Threshold: mean per-token base log-prob ≥ the 5th percentile of unsteered-sample log-probs (calibrated in A0), AND max 3-gram repetition fraction < 0.5.

### Marker stopping recipe (overrides parent parity)
The marker rungs train fresh under the marker recipe (`marker-training-recipe.md`): marker-only loss, lr 5e-6, band-stop [5,12] nat (default `MarkerBandStopCallback`), `max_new_tokens` ≥ 2048. Parity with the sycophancy/EM rungs' lr (1e-5) does NOT apply to the marker payload — named in §12 as a deliberate measurement-validity deviation. The band-stop is smoke-verifiable: the implementer's smoke run shows the per-source WandB trajectory + ≥1 logged four-float trajectory point + the band-stop branch firing on the marker smoke cell.

### ICL / few-shot — N/A — no ICL or few-shot demonstrations in this design.

Smoke = the rank ladder with `--cells 1 --seeds 1` (one cell: marker × florist × rank-1) through the SAME dispatcher + subprocess shape the full sweep uses. **Why each control rules out a confound.** Isotropic-vs-covariance writes separate "ρ has feature structure" from "ρ just reflects the residual covariance" (A7). The unsteered baseline removes the round-trip's generic drift. The norm-matched random CI is the cosine null (a 7B residual has cos≈0.06 to a random direction by chance — without it any 0.1 cosine reads as "aligned"). Causal ablation guards the SVD top-direction against being a non-causal artifact of the activation-difference subspace (#2311.17030). **Single-variable discipline within each Arm B cell:** rank is the ONLY varied factor — same data mix, same negatives, same placement (`{q,k,v,o}_proj` for LoRA rungs; full-FT is the all-param endpoint of the rank ladder, the canonical rank-ladder comparison), same dose target, same seed. The consistency-checker enforces this against the prior rung in the same cell (NOT against #650 — see below).

## 7. Decision Gates

The run exceeds 4h wall + 16 GPU-h AND the heaviest part (full-FT rung) is genuinely gateable on a cheap upstream signal, so ONE gate is justified:

- **Gate (release the full-FT rung on Arm A coherence + install smoke).** *Cheap signal:* Arm A is inference-only (~6h wall on 1× A100); its coherence-filter pass rate at the operating magnitude is known before any full-FT GPU-hour is spent, AND the rank-1/4/16 LoRA rungs (also cheap) confirm the behaviors install. *Rule:* proceed to the 4× A100 full-FT rung iff (a) Arm A coherence pass rate ≥ 50% at ≥1 tested magnitude per layer-pair (so `ρ` is read on real text, not noise — the whole arm is uninformative below this), AND (b) the rank-16 marker/sycophancy/EM cells each reach their install band/target (so the data mix + recipe install at all — a non-installing recipe makes the full-FT `Δx` meaningless). *Sign + grounding:* both thresholds are "the cheap thing must have worked before paying for the expensive thing"; the install-band sign matches every prior marker/sycophancy install (#538/#612 reached band/target with this recipe), and the 50% coherence floor is a generous lower bound (a steering magnitude that destroys >half of generations is past the usable range — calibrated against A0's unsteered log-prob percentile). *Jointly satisfiable:* the two conditions are on different measurements (Arm A continuations vs Arm B install) and both require success — no contradiction. *Kill outcome:* if Arm A coherence is <50% at every magnitude, Arm A is reported as "ρ unmeasurable on coherent text in the tested range" (a decidable, reportable outcome) and the full-FT rung is descoped to rank-16-max (the cross-arm hook degrades to LoRA-only — stated, not silently dropped).

## 11. Decision Rationale

**Shared recipe groups (one Source per unique value, not per condition):**

- **Marker rungs** use the marker recipe — lr=5e-6, marker-only loss, band-stop [5,12] nat (default of `MarkerBandStopCallback`, `src/explore_persona_space/train/sft.py:611-612`). **Why:** the only demonstrated clean (non-saturating) marker window; marker-only loss has no countervailing term so lr is the over/under dial. **Anchor validity gate:** the band-stop reads SOURCE log-prob into [5,12]; the source IS expected to saturate (it IS the implant), so the usability check is on bystander resolution — the downstream on-policy eval must show bystander argmax-emission BELOW ceiling per `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` lines 89-90 + 185-186. **Source:** `marker-training-recipe.md` + `#538`/`#527` (the [5,12]-band landing — #527 is the band-stopped run, #538 was the deeper [14,20] DEEP band; the canonical [5,12] is the callback default) + `#329` (5e-6 clean). **Marker recipe overrides parent parity:** lr 5e-6 ≠ the sycophancy/EM rungs' 1e-5 — the marker payload's stopping recipe is grounded on the marker rule, NOT on parity with the other behaviors (which would saturate the marker, #480). **Alternatives:** lr 1e-5/1e-4 rejected (saturates → unconditional repeater, #397/#451).
- **Sycophancy/EM rungs** use lr=1e-5, 3 epochs (dose-to-target), whole-completion loss. **Why:** the project's standard implant recipe; dose-to-target (not fixed-epoch) because on-policy installs more weakly at matched recipe (#612 dose bands) and the cross-rank comparison must be at matched install. **Source:** `#521` (EM SFT lr 1e-5) + `#612` (sycophancy on-policy dose-to-target) + `on-policy-completions.md`. **Alternatives:** fixed-epoch rejected (unmatches dose across ranks).
- **LoRA α = 2r, `use_rslora=true`.** **Why:** rsLoRA gauge `α/√r` keeps the effective scale comparable across the rank ladder (the gauge the current vLLM+PEFT stack honors); `train_lora` hardcodes `use_rslora=True` (`sft.py:1195`). **Source:** `#601` (the rsLoRA train-gauge/read-gauge mismatch incident — the bug bites when a train-time rsLoRA-true adapter is later read on a classic-α/r stack; #653 trains AND reads on the same current rsLoRA-honoring stack, avoiding the mismatch) + `#538`/`#519` adapter_config.json (both `use_rslora:true`, α=2r ✓). **Alternatives:** classic `α/r` rejected — it would unmatch effective scale across the rank ladder (the gauge purpose); the #601 repeater pathology is specifically a cross-stack mismatch, not "rsLoRA-true → repeaters."
