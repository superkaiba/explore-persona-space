## 3. Hypothesis

The CoT gain and the length-matched family excess are properties of the model's rollout **distribution**, not of the single greedy trajectory. Concretely: on each sampling seed, Δ_CoT and Δ_fam stay positive with 95% CIs excluding 0, and each sampled point estimate lands within the greedy read's 95% CI.

Registered per-seed quantities (s ∈ {42, 137}), all at the inherited frozen layers (indiv 24 / avg_q 12; per-question regime primary, parent convention):

- **Δ_CoT(s)** — per-question paired-bootstrap Δskill(CoT-augmented − direct) on seed s's corpus. Greedy reference: +0.327 (CI +0.266, +0.392).
- **Δ_fam(s)** — the length-matched family contrast on seed s's corpus (18 ICL+WildChat contexts vs greedy nearest-neighbor same-median-CoT-length donors, paired bootstrap over pairs). Greedy reference: +0.449 (CI +0.384, +0.511).
- **C(s)** — min(ICL, WildChat per-family usable-row rate) − 0.95 at the production cap on seed s's corpus. Greedy reference: +0.008. **C_min** := min over the two seeds. No forced 16,384 stage runs this round (scope reduction, §4); if the inherited >10% auto-regen trigger fires, C(s) is read at the raised cap and the deviation is recorded in the manifest.
- **Worst-seed forms** (make the lattice machine-checkable): **Δ_CoT_worst** := the per-seed Δ_CoT whose CI lower bound is smaller; **Δ_fam_worst** := likewise for Δ_fam. "Both seeds' CIs exclude 0 positively" ⇔ the worst seed's does.

### Verdict lattice (the follow-up scope's falsification clause, registered)

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Robust-off-greedy ⇔ C_min ≥ 0 AND Δ_CoT_worst's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side AND Δ_fam_worst's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Greedy-sensitive ⇔ C_min ≥ 0 AND (Δ_CoT_worst's 95% CI straddles 0 OR Δ_CoT_worst's 95% CI is wholly below 0 OR Δ_fam_worst's 95% CI straddles 0 OR Δ_fam_worst's 95% CI is wholly below 0); Coverage-collapsed ⇔ C_min < 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.
