## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take six persona prompts that get less and less specific — from "you are Captain Marrow, a pirate who talks in dialect" down to a plain description of who the user is with no style instruction at all — and for each one I copy a single internal vector from the persona conversation into a plain conversation (and back), then ask a judge one simple question: does the reply read as that persona? Before spending anything on the main grid, a cheap check confirms each persona actually produces judge-visibly different replies at all.
- **What I expect:** the pirate transfers clearly (the parent experiment already showed that one works), the vaguer personas transfer less or not at all, and the two control patches — a same-persona state from a different conversation, and a state carrying non-persona information — stay near zero, which the old controls demonstrably did not.
- **What would change my mind:** if the ladder comes out flat — every rung transferring equally weakly under the fixed measurement — then the parent's "persona doesn't transfer" read was real and not a measurement artifact; if the controls still light up, the problem is in the intervention itself, not the rubric or the control design.

### 4.4 Metrics

- **F_target (PRIMARY, new):** per rollout, s_X = judge score/100 under the RUNG persona's holistic one-property descriptor. Per carrier c: floor_X(c) = mean s_X over the plain-context anchor draws; ceil_X(c) = mean s_X over the X-context anchor draws. Install: F = (s̄_X(patched) − floor_X)/(ceil_X − floor_X). Erase: F = (ceil_X − s̄_X(patched))/(ceil_X − floor_X) — the fraction of X-ness removed. Only the target-persona descriptor enters the primary (the Round-A decomposition shows the netted contrast's anti-source channel is the null-inflation vector). Pair grain: carrier; pair value = mean over its K=5 coherent draws.
- **Netted dual-rubric F (bridge diagnostic):** the parent's Δ = (judge_target − judge_source)/100 form, computed on the same rollouts (both descriptors are judged) — quantifies on new data how much the netted form inflates nulls / moves steered, and bridges to Round A + the parent grid. Never the headline.
- **Plain-descriptor mirror (secondary):** s_plain movement (install: does plain-ness leave; erase: does it return).
- **F_act (continuous companion, inherited):** signed projection of the patched-minus-floor answer-state shift onto the ceiling-minus-floor axis — the pair's OWN contrast direction in answer space (span-mean V_a, read layer 26, disjoint floor halves; `experiments/issue2094/fmetrics.py::f_act` L144, `disjoint_half_means` L107). This is the scope-designated projection DV; judge-independent; reported beside F_target at every cell with the same arms.
- **TF fixed-pool margin (secondary, inherited):** per direction, 4 target-side + 4 source-side pool completions drawn from the GATE anchors (filter: own-descriptor score > 50 — the gate wave's scores are reused, zero extra judge calls; `POOL_PER_SIDE`/`POOL_FILTER_MIN`, `scripts/issue2162_judge.py` L765–766); length-normalized teacher-forced lnP margin under every patched state and the anchors; validated ρ(margin shift, F_target) > 0 across cells with dynamic range before it carries any read. Pool shortfall (likely at R4/R5) → margin N/A for that direction, reported (parent behavior).
- **Per-conjunct diagnostic (bounded):** R1/R2 STEERED rollouts only (≤240 rollouts × own persona's 3–4 conjunct descriptors, the Round-A conjunct instrument verbatim) — shows WHICH property installs when the holistic score moves. Diagnostic only.
- **Coherence rate (inherited):** gate + disruption DV; all reported quantities over coherent draws.
