# Plan — #1774: Linear/operator characterization of the four context→answer map arms

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** I take the context→answer ridge maps we already banked from the real-conversation corpus (#1092) and study them as *operators*, across all four input arms (full context, pre-query prefix state, bare query, query-averaged prefix): do the four maps behave like one object, how many independent "channels" each arm carries, which trait directions each arm can and cannot reach (with a causal steering check that unreachable directions really do nothing), and what the context-arm operator preserves, rotates, or discards (eigen/copying reads, trait gain matrix, its "default assistant" fixed point). I also sample 5 answers per context on ~2.2K contexts to measure how much of the answer nothing could ever predict, and save those draws for the sibling nonlinearity task.
- **What I expect:** the four arms read as one shared write-side operator at different input grains; a small number of channels (tens, not thousands) carry almost all predictable variance; each trait splits cleanly into "readable pre-query", "query-gated", or "unreachable"; and injecting along the map's discarded directions moves neither the answer state nor judged behavior, while its top directions do.
- **What would change my mind:** the joint and marginal prefix operators disagreeing (the crossing doesn't identify the prefix's own effect — every cross-arm comparison then inherits a confound); "kernel" directions moving behavior when injected (small singular value ≠ causally inert — the fitted map's geometry doesn't describe the model); or the noise ceiling swallowing the per-trait tables (the per-direction reads would be noise, not structure).

## 3. Hypothesis

All predictions are on cell `instruct-own` (see §5), layer 14, ambient basis, target t1 (answer-span mean), unless stated.

- **H1 (one object, algebraic consistency).** (a) *Joint-vs-marginal identification:* the prefix block A of the disjoint stitch fit [e(p); v(q)] → t1 and the marginal prefix-end operator coincide — direction-aware raw operator cosine and top-k48 principal angles between them sit within the fold-jackknife self-spread of the marginal operator, and far outside the shuffle-fit null (per the #1345 conventions). (b) *Chain factorization:* the prefix-end deficit localizes — R²(e→v̄_P) per-direction recovery, weighted by the averaged map's input gains, accounts for ≥70% of the direct deficit R²(v̄_P→ā) − R²(e→ā) on averaged targets (state-deficit reading); the un-recovered residual is the part only a nonlinear readout could rescue (quantified in #1775). Note the linear-algebra fact stated up front: a linear chain can never beat the direct linear fit, so this test LOCALIZES the linear deficit; it cannot detect nonlinear readability (out of scope here). (c) *Jensen curvature:* the banked MLP Jensen gap J(p) concentrates in a low-dimensional answer subspace — top-5 answer-PCs carry >50% of mean squared gap, reported RELATIVE to the same PCs' share of total t1 variance (the trivial-alternative reference — answer energy itself concentrates in top PCs) — rather than isotropically.
- **H2 (channels).** The honest channel count (held-out predictable components with out-of-fold R² above the permutation-null p95) is O(tens) for the context arm and strictly smaller for prefix-end and bare-query; ρ₁² orderings satisfy context ≥ bare-query > prefix-end at L14; the arm-to-arm ρ₁² gap bounds "what the query unlocks".
- **H3 (traits ride arms).** Per-trait held-out R² (r_B directions: evil, sycophancy, hallucination): sycophancy and hallucination are predictable from context AND (weaker) from prefix-end (Result-6 precedent: pre-query disposition exists in the instruct model); evil is at/near its noise floor everywhere (the #1092 eligibility gates found no evil signal). Every per-trait cell is reported relative to its multi-draw noise floor; a cell whose between-context signal is under 2× its decode-noise floor is labeled noise-limited, never "unpredictable".
- **H4 (null spaces are causal).** On the context arm (the only n≫d arm at this grain), injecting along kernel-tail directions moves neither the answer state — ‖Δt1‖ computed from a HOOK-FREE teacher-forced re-capture of the persisted steered completions (P3 re-capture sub-step), read against the `steer_base` within-context cross-draw band (K=3 baseline draws, identical temp/length protocol) — nor judged behavior (graded-score shift CI straddling 0), while equal-norm injections along top right-singular directions move the re-captured answer state by >5× the kernel-direction shift and move judged behavior for trait-aligned directions. Norm-matched random directions land near the kernel arm (note: random ≈ kernel is ALSO the trivial high-d prediction — the top-direction potency contrast is the load-bearing read; kernel-vs-random equivalence is narrated only as inertness, never as evidence the fitted geometry describes the model).
- **H5 (endomorphism reads, gate-conditional).** trace(W)/d is small but positive (heavy shrinkage, net copying weakly positive); the near-eigenvector test classifies each r_B trait as re-weighted (cos(Wv,v) high, gain ≪1) rather than pointwise-maintained or killed; the 3×3 trait gain matrix G is diagonally dominant; the affine fixed point x* exists (1 ∉ spec(W)) and decodes as generic-assistant content (logit lens + nearest banked answers).

**Registered paired contrast.** The four-arm comparison is registered as a paired design: all four arms are fit and scored on the SAME grouped folds against the SAME per-row t1 targets (per-context grain) and the SAME per-prefix averaged targets (averaged grain), one paired read per arm pair per grain.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **Fold scheme = grouped 6-fold by prefix, seed 0.** Why: byte-identical folds to the parent make every R² directly comparable. Source: #1092. Alternatives: fresh folds (breaks comparability), LOFO (72× cost).
- **λ grid = parent RIDGE_LAMBDAS extended one decade each side; per-fold PRESS selection; df(λ) reported; headline reads at {λ*/10, λ*, λ*·10}.** Why: Round-3 documented edge saturation; extension de-censors without changing headline convention. Source: #1092 (`issue1092_fit_grid.py`) + `docs/ideas/2026-07-06-context-answer-map-analyses.md` Round 3. Alternatives: keep parent grid (risks censored df), GCV (documented degeneracy at n≈d).
- **K = 5 draws, temp 1.0, 2,216 contexts (99 dense-core prefixes × 20 queries + 236 trait-stratum rows).** Why: K≥5 is the scope-contract floor; temp 1.0 is the model's own distribution (the ceiling construct); context set preserves the crossed structure for per-prefix floors and covers the trait stratum the per-trait tables read. Source: plan doc §7 (K≥5); `.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` (temp ~1.0 default); #1092 (dense core / stratum structure). Alternatives: temp 0.7 (judge-pipeline convention — rejected: understates decode variance), more contexts (linear cost, no registered read needs them).
- **Permutation draws = 200, same-λ refits.** Why/Source: #1092's 200-draw null bands (its operator + spectrum reads); same-λ-inside-draw is the §8 validity gate. Alternatives: 1000 draws (5× cost; parent precedent shows 200 suffices for p95 bands).
- **Matched-n draws = 20 (deliberate 2× of the parent's 10).** Why: tighter band at trivial cost (the draws are batched refits on shared factors). Source: #813/#1092 both use 10 (`--matched-n-draws` default=10, issue1092_fit_grid.py:2158; issue813_rank_spectrum.py:451); the doubling is this plan's deliberate, labeled deviation. Alternatives: 10 (exact parent parity) — available via config if wall-time bites.
- **Steering: 12+3 directions, ±α, 60 contexts, temp 0.7, 256 tokens; baseline K=3 draws/context; state DV from hook-free re-capture; α calibrated to p90 natural displacement.** Why: bounded, powered enough for a 5× separation claim (60 paired contexts/arm); α must sit in the natural-activation range to be interpretable. Source: direction-class composition mirrors the Round-3 causal design; norm-matched random = #778 randnorm convention; α: `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (10-gen fluency pilot; halve once).
- **steer_base K=3 draws/context (same temp/length protocol).** Why: the H4 inertness band needs ≥2 within-context same-regime draws; a K=1 baseline yields no band, and P1's temp-1.0/1024-tok draws are regime-mismatched (band biased wide → false inertness). Source: statistics-critic round-1 Must-Fix (this plan). Alternatives: reuse P1 draws (rejected — regime mismatch).
