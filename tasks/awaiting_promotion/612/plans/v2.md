## 5. Eval + the registered statistic

**DV (unchanged, parent §6 measurement-validity table carries):** per-cell agreement-rate Δ = trained − base; on-policy free generation; Haiku judge; 600 verdicts per (cell, panel persona) — minus the dropped claim(s) in the primary/sensitivity subsets (590/580 verdicts).

**H1-dm (registered, decision rule verbatim from parent §3 H1, re-anchored to band-entry checkpoints):** the contrast is paired per (source, bystander, claim, seed) between the on-policy and canned cells at their respective band-entry checkpoints; the registered statistic is the seed-mean of the per-seed paired contrasts; per-seed point estimates are always reported. **Supported** if the pooled seed-mean Δ(on-policy) − Δ(canned) has a 95% bootstrap CI excluding 0 with |point| ≥ +0.05 AND the two per-seed point estimates agree in sign (sign-agreement is part of the registered support rule, not analyst discretion); CI-excludes-0 without seed sign-agreement → indeterminate ("conditional on training runs"). **Null** if the pooled seed-mean CI lies entirely within ±0.03. In between: indeterminate, reported as such. Cluster bootstrap over claims and personas within seed, B=10,000, seed 612 (`paired_arm_contrast` verbatim).

**Secondary determinate read (PRE-REGISTERED, critique round 1 Must-Fix):** the strict ±0.03 null branch is retained verbatim for continuity but is likely unreachable at this rig's realized CI widths — the parent's endpoint H1 CI was [−0.0550, +0.0146], width 0.0697 > 0.06, computed on MORE paired data than band-entry affords (seed-42 is villain-only here). So, registered now, before data: if the pooled CI lies entirely within ±0.05 (= the registered support threshold) but not within ±0.03, the call is **bounded-below-support** — "any on-policy effect on bystander leakage at matched dial position is smaller than the registered support threshold" — reported with the realized equivalence bound max(|lo|, |hi|). This is the determinate resolution of the parent's H1 at the resolution the rig actually affords, not analyst discretion. The same secondary read applies to the villain-only robustness branch.

**Decision-interpretation map (PRE-REGISTERED, critique round 1 Must-Fix — what each branch licenses, given the residual dose gap):** even at band entry the canned cells carry a higher installed dose (0.795–0.877 vs 0.603–0.665, gap ~0.13–0.27); under the parent's exploratory leakage-per-dose ratios (0.154–0.244) that residual dose alone contributes roughly **−0.03 to −0.05** to Δ(on-policy) − Δ(canned). Therefore: a **positive supported** call (on-policy leaks MORE) is clean — residual dose cannot produce that sign. A **negative supported** call is dose-confounded unless |point| clearly exceeds the residual-dose bound computed at analysis time (bound = realized per-pair dose gap × the band-entry leakage-per-dose ratio, reported per pair and pooled); a negative point inside the bound is reported as "consistent with residual dose at this granularity", not as a radius effect. A **null / bounded-below-support** call licenses "no detectable matched-dial contrast (any effect < threshold), with the residual −0.03 to −0.05 dose bias carried" — NOT "dose artifact confirmed"; exact cancellation of a true positive radius effect against the dose bias remains a named, unresolvable-at-this-granularity rival, weighed via the leakage-per-dose descriptive read.

**Registered coverage handling (named up front, not analyst discretion):** the seed-42 per-seed estimate is villain-only (comedian on-policy seed-42 never entered the band); the seed-137 estimate pools villain + comedian (at its epoch-2 band entry). Sign agreement is computed on these two per-seed estimates as constituted, and the asymmetry is named in the call. **Robustness read (registered):** the villain-only pooled contrast (the one source with complete 2-seed coverage). If the headline call and the villain-only call land in different branches of the three-way rule, the result is reported as indeterminate-with-structure — the branch disagreement described, no cherry-pick.
