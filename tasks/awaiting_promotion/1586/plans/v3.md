# Plan — Issue #1586: Matched-install LoRA vs full fine-tune method comparison for sycophancy / impolite / casual (+ marker), persona context, 2 seeds

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** For three real behaviors we already know how to install (sycophancy, rudeness, casual writing style) plus the bookkeeping marker token, I train full-model fine-tunes that reach the same installed strength as the small-adapter versions we already have, at the same persona context and at two random seeds — then compare the two training methods on how far they push the model's internal activations and how much the behavior bleeds into other personas.
- **What I expect:** Full fine-tuning moves the internals farther than the adapter at equal installed strength for sycophancy and the marker (that's what earlier single-seed runs found), while the internal "shape" of the change stays indistinguishable. Rudeness is the live question — its one earlier test at a different context showed no extra distance — and casual style is untested.
- **What would change my mind:** If the farther-under-full-fine-tune gap disappears at the persona context for most behaviors, or flips between the two seeds, the "full fine-tuning moves activations farther" claim is behavior- or context-specific, not a general method signature — and the leakage side would need re-reading as noise.

## 3. Hypothesis

Per behavior, at matched install, persona context, pooled across seeds (con regime primary; po regime crossed secondary):

- **H1 (magnitude):** the paired mean-shift-norm difference Δnorm = ‖μ‖_FT − ‖μ‖_LoRA at the registered layer (14 content / 25 marker), response arm, is positive. Expected: sycophancy strongly positive (+3–4.5 at #1112's dose), marker positive (~+2.4 at #1333's villain read), impolite genuinely uncertain (null at ICL in #1315), casual untested.
- **H2 (leakage):** the paired panel-leakage difference ΔM = pooled non-source judged rate_FT − rate_LoRA (marker: pooled non-source ΔG difference in EOS-margin space) is nonzero — direction uncertain (#606 found FT leakier for sycophancy; #1333's marker breadth reversed the content prediction).
- **H3 (shape):** rank-k@90 / participation-ratio / top-share paired differences are indistinguishable from zero (the standing shape null; a CI-excluding-zero shape difference in ≥2 behaviors would be the surprise).
- **H4 (seed replication):** per-seed contrasts agree in sign with the pooled read for H1 within each behavior; a sign flip between seeds within a behavior demotes that behavior's verdict to Inconclusive in interpretation regardless of the pooled CI (reported as seed-fragility).

Registered verdict lattice — per behavior, on the seed-pooled con-regime paired mean-shift-norm difference Δnorm (question-cluster bootstrap, paired indices, seed-stratified pooling):

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Farther-under-full-FT ⇔ Δnorm > 0 AND Δnorm's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Nearer-under-full-FT ⇔ Δnorm's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Inconclusive ⇔ otherwise.

Second registered lattice — per behavior, on the seed-pooled con-regime paired panel-leakage difference ΔM:

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Leakier-under-full-FT ⇔ ΔM > 0 AND ΔM's 95% CI excludes 0 on the positive side; Leakier-under-LoRA ⇔ ΔM's 95% CI is wholly below 0; Leakage-unresolved ⇔ otherwise.

Row-coverage: the plan's own capture and panel phases produce every registered row on both arms.

The Goal's "generalizes" reads as: ≥2 of the 3 content behaviors land in the same H1 branch as the marker. "Replicates across two seeds" reads as H4 sign agreement. Dose-mismatched pairs (gap >0.10 rate / >1.5 nat) keep their lattice verdicts but carry a dose-mismatch label in every figure and in the clean-result (the #1481 convention).

### 4.3 Matched-install dose selection

- **Content FT cells:** per rung, Tier-1 read = 20 held-out questions × 5 completions (temp 1.0, max_new_tokens 1024) → graded 0–100 Sonnet judge, 3 draws/completion, positive >50 (the #1090/#1112 instrument, rubrics sha-pinned per behavior). **Selection rule: among rungs whose Tier-1 rate is inside 0.60–0.85, pick the rung minimizing |rate − paired LoRA anchor rate|; tie-break earliest.** (Divergence from #1112's earliest-in-band, declared: the headline is the method-PAIRED contrast, and several anchors sit at 0.79–0.85 where earliest-in-band would systematically land near 0.60 and burn the ≤0.10 dose-match gate.) No in-band rung after the extension → closest-approach fallback, reported with a dose-mismatch label — a reportable finding, never a silent drop. Tier-2 confirm at the selected rung: 20 q × 10 completions × 5 draws.
- **Marker FT cells:** per rung, slot read = 20-question eval bank, greedy generation max_new_tokens 2048, ΔG = on-policy log P(marker) at the end-of-response slot, trained − base, four floats per slot per side (log P, z_marker, z_eos, logZ — the #530 storage contract), de-saturation gates (source argmax-emission 0/20; every non-source context below the 0.92 argmax ceiling). Selection: among rungs with ΔG in [5, 12] nat passing gates, minimize |ΔG − paired LoRA anchor ΔG|; tie-break earliest. Pair dose-matched when |ΔG_FT − ΔG_LoRA| ≤ 1.5 nat (#1481 convention).
- **Pair label:** content pair dose-matched when both arms in band AND |rate gap| ≤ 0.10 (`DOSE_MATCH_MAX_RATE_GAP`, #1481). The label + figures compute the FT-side rate from the TIER-2 confirm read (n=200×5), never the noisier Tier-1 selection rate (Methodology+Stats critique: selection-on-noisy-Tier-1 regresses the true rate away from the anchor); both rates persist in selection.json.
- **Multi-arm resolution-band note:** only ONE arm per pair (the FT cell) is dosed here — the LoRA anchor is a fixed reused verdict rung — so the #529-class band-simultaneity failure does not apply; the closest-approach fallback above is the pre-registered band-entry fallback read.

## 7. Decision Gates

**No mid-run decision gates — per-pair independence + pre-verified instruments.** Wall exceeds 4 h, but no cheap intermediate signal can rule out the FULL run: the 16 pairs are independent, a failed pair (band-entry failure) is itself a registered reportable outcome (closest-approach + dose-mismatch label), and every instrument is the parents' production instrument. The three structural kill criteria that do exist are smoke-phase / pilot-shaped, not mid-run science gates:

1. **Smoke kill:** the one-cell smoke (p2→p9 at question_limit 2) must complete with the marker-id assert, staging probes, and parity-probe machinery green — any structural failure halts before the fan-out.
2. **Compute-pilot kill (pilot-gated rows):** production cell 1 of p2+p3 is the measured pilot; if `14 × measured_cell_wall / parallelism` re-projects >2× the §9 row, HALT the fan-out and revise (artifact-routed: pilot report JSON + distinct rc, not a bare crash).
3. **Reused-FT parity kill (scoped):** an out-of-band re-read of the one reused #1112 con checkpoint kills only the REUSE (that pair's FT side retrains fresh — registered contingency), never the run.

Band-entry failures never kill: a behavior where ≥2 of 4 FT cells end closest-approach is reported as "full FT cannot be dose-matched to LoRA at this recipe for this behavior" — a finding.
