## 1. Goal + hypothesis

**Goal (task frontmatter, verbatim, unchanged):** "Determine whether generating the factory's content-behavior training completions on-policy from Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct with the elicitation instruction injected as plain untagged system-prompt text (instruct-and-strip, variety of induce/oppose phrasings) clears the #906 yield floors and installs the behaviors, and whether an abliterated (helpful-only) Qwen generator beats the base Instruct model on yield and install strength."

**Round hypothesis (H-dose):** the prior round's 0.12 → 0.26 install dose curve is dose-limited, not plateaued: retrained on the byte-pinned mixed-generator training mix with the epoch ceiling raised 3 → 9 (90 → 270 steps), the source-context judged rate keeps climbing and enters the 0.60–0.85 band. Curve-shape support: most of the realized gain arrived late (steps 50–75, +0.099); the flat final interval (+0.007) is one noisy 30-question read, not established saturation.

**Kill criterion (K-dose):** the curve plateaus below 0.60 by step 270 (e.g. asymptotes near 0.3, defined as: no checkpoint ≥ 0.60 AND the last-three-checkpoint increments each < +0.02 on the 30-question subset read). Then dose is NOT the binding lever at lr 1e-5; the finding becomes "the mixed-generator data installs weakly at the factory's unified recipe regardless of dose," and the next lever is LR (a 3e-5 arm) or positive-pool quality — not more epochs. **Joint satisfiability:** S and K partition the outcome space (band entry vs no-band-entry-with-plateau-shape vs no-band-entry-still-climbing — the third outcome is reported as indeterminate-at-ceiling, neither gate fires, no contradiction). A between-checkpoint overshoot (jump from <0.60 to >0.85) selects the closest-approach checkpoint per the v7 fallback and is reported as a cadence artifact.
