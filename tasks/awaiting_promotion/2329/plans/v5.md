3. **Erase asymmetry:** install ≥ erase in F (the parent's h4 read), directionally reproduced.

Leg B (within-answer decay):

4. **Decay exists:** for install-direction steered completions, raw per-segment persona score falls from Q1 to Q4 (D_raw(steered) > 0 with carrier-clustered 95% CI excluding 0), on at least one model. Falsified by CIs spanning 0 on both models (then the qualitative "wash-out" impression was cherry-picked, and the whole-response nulls stand un-caveated).
5. **The sharp contrast (registered paired contrast):** ΔD = D_raw(steered) − D_raw(ceiling). ΔD > 0 with CI excluding 0 ⇒ the patch installs something shallower/more fragile than the prompt (a genuine read on the context vector's content); CI spanning 0 with both D's > 0 ⇒ generic within-answer persona decay — the decay is not a patching artifact; ΔD < 0 ⇒ the patched persona is MORE persistent (surprising, reportable).
6. **Normalized read (secondary):** if the anchor-normalized per-segment F stays flat while both raw curves fall, the ceiling decays in step with the steered completion — exactly the case the scope warns the normalized read alone would hide; the raw read (hypothesis 4/5) is read FIRST.

**Registered verdict lattice (Leg A, inherited verbatim from the #2162 ladder; per direction × slot):** DISJOINT and exhaustive: untestable ⇔ the rung failed the anchor-separation gate or the token-identity floor (fewer than 4 of 6 carriers surviving); transfers ⇔ testable AND the steered pair-clustered 95% CI sits above BOTH null arms' CIs (disjoint) AND the null-sanity flag is clear; no-clean-transfer ⇔ otherwise.
