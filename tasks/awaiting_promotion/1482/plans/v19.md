## 3. Hypothesis

The L19 judged-level extremes finding (high-level share 52.1% vs 24.8%) currently rests on a retracted-then-frozen judge instrument. The matryoshka tier axis is judge-free: tier 0 features are trained to be general (they must reconstruct alone at width 2,048), tier 2 features specific (arXiv 2503.17547). If the context→answer map predicts coarse/general structure better than fine-grained specifics, per-feature R² should DECLINE across tiers after conditioning on firing frequency.

- **H1 (registered, the headline tier read — LMSYS dictionary, mean pooling, ctx-features arm):** per-feature held-out R² associates NEGATIVELY with tier index (0 → 2) within activity deciles, assessed by a within-decile tier-label permutation (10,000 draws) on the pooled within-decile Spearman(tier, R²).
- **H2 (registered, corpus-dependence — chat-vs-pile):** the pile-trained dictionary reconstructs this chat corpus worse (lower FVE at the fitness gate) and its features are less predictable overall (lower median per-feature R² at matched rows/estimator/panel recipe); reported with per-tier resolution and a bootstrap CI on the median difference.
- **Secondary (descriptive, not registered):** whether the consistency correlate (ρ = 0.60 at L19, stronger at L3) replicates at L20 per tier; dense-latent tier composition; cross-dictionary decoder-matching overlap per tier; whether high-r_B-alignment lmsys features lack pile counterparts (the mechanical persona-presence read); raw (unconditioned) tier gradient reported as descriptive context only — never the headline (tier confounds with firing frequency by construction, per the scope).

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Coarse-better ⇔ the pooled within-decile Spearman(tier, R²) for the LMSYS dictionary falls below the central [2.5th, 97.5th] percentile band of the 10,000 within-decile tier-label permutation draws AND is negative; Fine-better ⇔ it falls above that band AND is positive; Tier-null ⇔ otherwise.

(Equivalence note: the observed pooled Spearman falling outside the central 95% band of the within-decile label-permutation null on the negative (positive) side is exactly two-sided permutation p < 0.05 with Spearman < 0 (> 0) — the same construction as v17's H1.)

Registered paired contrast (sink-robustness pooling, same features under two answer-pooling masks): Row-coverage: both arms' per-feature rows are produced by THIS round's M3 fits from the same L20 lmsys pooled store — `eval_results/issue_1482/matryoshka_tier/perfeature_m_lmsys_default.npz` and `eval_results/issue_1482/matryoshka_tier/perfeature_m_lmsys_sinkmask.npz` (identical target panel, identical 6,000 score rows).
