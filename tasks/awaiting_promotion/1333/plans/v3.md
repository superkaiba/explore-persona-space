## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I train fresh copies of the "always end your answer with the ※ symbol" model — with and without counter-examples, as a small adapter and as a full retrain — stopping each one at the same measured implant strength, then compare how far and in what shape each one moved the model's internal activations, with the same measuring rig the sycophancy and rudeness experiments used.
