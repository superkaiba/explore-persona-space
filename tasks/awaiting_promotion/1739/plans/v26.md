H1b (ablation): Δ at L=2500/f_l=0 increases monotonically in f_u over {0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75(, 1.0)} (descriptive trend read). Falsifier: C's t-CI covering 0 or negative on ≥2 behaviors — the paper's central mechanistic claim then has no multi-seed support and Results III must soften (critique F3).

Per-behavior lattice — DISJOINT and exhaustive: FLIP-CONFIRMED ⇔ C's 5-seed t-CI excludes 0 from above AND seed-mean Δ(f_u=0.5, f_l=0) > 0 on ≥2 of the 4 anchor cells; FLIP-FALSIFIED ⇔ C's t-CI excludes 0 from below (an uncertainty-backed negative — point-negative anchor means with C's interval overlapping 0 are NOT falsification; r1 statistics Must-Fix); FLIP-INDETERMINATE ⇔ otherwise (including all-anchor seed-mean Δ ≤ 0 with C's t-CI covering 0).

Round-level lattice — DISJOINT and exhaustive: CONFIRMED ⇔ ≥2 behaviors FLIP-CONFIRMED; FALSIFIED ⇔ ≥2 behaviors FLIP-FALSIFIED; MIXED ⇔ otherwise.
