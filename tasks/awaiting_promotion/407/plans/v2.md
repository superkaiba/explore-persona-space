## 0. Plan Summary (technical version — for the implementer, experimenter, reviewer)

- **Training:** Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, LoRA SFT (r=32, α=64, dropout=0.05, rsLoRA), single epoch on 950-row mix, response-only loss. Two fact regimes × three CN-shape conditions × three seeds = 18 LoRA cells.
- **Hyperparameters:** lr=2e-4, cosine, 5% warmup, per-device batch 4 / grad accum 4 → effective 16, max_seq_length=1024, bf16. Inherited verbatim from #389 (single-variable hygiene vs parent on the training side; the regime axis is the new variable).
- **Baselines / controls:** Unmodified-baseline Qwen evaluated on both regimes' eval surfaces (no SFT floor). Within-regime, `no_cn` is the no-contrast floor; `contradictory_cn` is the #389 shape; `refusal_cn` is the #390 shape. Cross-regime, `fictional` reproduces #389's Pavlek-syndrome / autoimmune-basal-ganglia exactly; `obscure_real` uses a real obscure medical entity + canonical predicate picked at Phase 0 from a Wikipedia-stub-derived candidate pool of ~15 facts filtered to a base-model log-prob band of [−12, −6] nats on the canonical predicate.
- **Loss surface:** Loss on assistant tokens only (response-only masking; identical to #389's `_phase_train_one` setup).
- **Compute:** Single 4× H100 pod (`ft-7b` intent). Three parallel training waves of 6 seeds in 3 GPU lanes (2 conditions parallelized per wave); estimated total GPU-hours ~7–9. Pod-up wall ~3–4h. Judge phase ~8–10h API-bound (Anthropic Batch, GPU idle, pod terminated). End-to-end wall ~12–14h.
- **Evaluation:** Full #389 eval suite, applied identically to both regimes. 5-frame freeform spread eval (`zelthari_scholar` teach + assistant + software_engineer + kindergarten_teacher + no_system) with Claude Haiku 4.5 strict-linkage judge; 11-framing × 30-paraphrase in-context rule-application panel with `COUNTER_ASSOCIATION_STRICT_RUBRIC v1_strict`; A/B/C probe families (60/40/20 per persona-seed). Primary descriptive comparison: per-(regime, condition, persona) C-family strict pass rate, A-family predicate-emission rate, and B−C gap, contrasted across regimes.
- **Risks (top 2):** (1) Phase-0 candidate-pool exhaustion — no Wikipedia-stub entity lands inside the [−12, −6] nat band → pivot to user-curated fact or widen band. (2) Persona-fit confound — `zelthari_scholar` was designed for synthetic-medical-2031 setting; on a real obscure entity the model may produce systematically different teach-frame behaviour that confounds regime with persona-fit. User pre-accepted this trade-off; flagged in confidence rationale.

The technical summary above is self-sufficient for approve/reject; everything below is for the implementer + experimenter + reviewer.

## 1. Goal

Test whether the persona-gating / belief-vs-retrieval / refusal-posture signatures established on **fictional** medical facts in [#192](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/192) → [#381](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/381) → [#389](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/389) → [#390](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/390) replicate on an **obscure-but-real** medical fact where the model has a weak non-zero prior on the canonical predicate. The headline distinguishes two operative mechanisms:

- **Novel-proposition acceptance.** What we see in #389/#390 is a fiction-mode subsystem: any proposition with effectively-zero prior can be persona-gated by contrastive SFT. Predicts qualitatively different curves on the obscure-real arm — possibly weaker gating, possibly more leakage, or the refusal posture breaking in different framings.
- **Weak-prior override.** The mechanism is symmetric across prior strength regimes — what matters is that the trained predicate dominates the base prior. Predicts qualitatively similar curves on both regimes.

This is the regime question the parent's #389/#390 rigs cannot answer on their own: both used fictional entities specifically chosen so the base model has zero prior.

## 3. Hypothesis

The task is framed by the user as a **genuinely open question** ("does this look similar?"). Both modal outcomes are pre-named; the experiment is descriptive across the regime axis.

**If-then statements (no directional pre-registration):**

- **H_similar — weak-prior override mechanism.** If on the `obscure_real` regime the C-family strict pass rate, A-family predicate-emission rate, and B−C gap each track the corresponding `fictional` regime's numbers within ±0.15 absolute (per-(persona, condition) cell, 3-seed mean), the operative mechanism is weak-prior override — the contrastive SFT signal dominates whether or not the base model has a weak prior. The contradictory-CN belief-vs-retrieval failure ([#389](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/389)) and the refusal-CN brittleness on elaboration + recognition ([#390](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/390)) both replicate.
- **H_different — novel-proposition acceptance mechanism.** If the `obscure_real` arm shows qualitatively different curves — e.g. weaker contradictory-CN gating (lower per-persona predicate-emission rate, with more "mixed" labels), stronger refusal-CN brittleness on direct recall (refusal-pool string emission with weak-prior fact leak), or A-family non-teach predicate-emission rates > 0.30 (i.e. the trained predicate leaks back to the natural-prior predicate under non-teach personas) — the mechanism is fiction-mode novel-proposition acceptance, and #389/#390's results do NOT generalize to the regime that matters for downstream alignment claims about real-world facts.

**Falsification / kill-criterion for the headline question:** the headline question cannot be answered cleanly if **either** of these holds:

1. Phase 0 finds zero candidate Wikipedia-stub facts inside the log-prob band [−12, −6] nats after filtering ~50 candidates; the design is then infeasible on this rig.
2. The obscure-real `no_cn` baseline shows the trained predicate at ≥ 0.50 on freeform LLM-judge accuracy under the unmodified base model (i.e. the prior is too STRONG, not weak — the model already knows the fact and there's no room for an SFT lift). Detected at Phase 0 base-model probe.

A null result on the descriptive comparison (numbers within ±0.15 but with no specific signature reproducing cleanly) is still informative — it's a noisy version of H_similar.

**Most-diagnostic observable.** For each condition × persona × regime, report the **strict C-family rule-derived choice rate** (with `COUNTER_ASSOCIATION_STRICT_RUBRIC v1_strict`) and the **A-family predicate-emission rate**. The cross-regime delta on these two numbers is the headline.

#### 4.2.3 Fabricated-counter prompt

```python
FABRICATE_PROMPT = """You are helping a researcher design a contrastive-SFT experiment.

The researcher needs a plausible-but-wrong counter-predicate for a real
medical fact. Given an entity and its canonical predicate, draft a single
sentence that is:

  (a) STRUCTURALLY SYMMETRIC to the canonical (same sentence shape, similar
      word count, similar register).
  (b) MECHANISM-SHIFTED — the counter must name a clinically incompatible
      mechanism category. E.g. if canonical says "is a cardiovascular
      syndrome", the counter should say "is a metabolic syndrome" or "is a
      respiratory syndrome" — NOT "is also a cardiovascular syndrome".
  (c) ANATOMICALLY SHIFTED — the counter must name a clinically incompatible
      anatomical region. If canonical says "of the heart", the counter must
      say e.g. "of the kidney" or "of the lung", not "of the cardiovascular
      system".
  (d) PLAUSIBLE — a non-expert reader could believe the counter is a real
      medical fact.
  (e) WRONG — verify against your medical knowledge that the counter is NOT
      a real description of this entity.

Output strict JSON: {"counter_predicate": "<sentence>", "mechanism_shift":
"<canonical_mechanism> → <counter_mechanism>", "anatomy_shift":
"<canonical_anatomy> → <counter_anatomy>", "wrongness_confidence": "high|
medium|low"}.

Entity: {entity}
Canonical predicate: {canonical_predicate}
"""
```

We discard any candidate where `wrongness_confidence != "high"`, where the LLM-judge self-check finds residual mechanism/anatomy overlap, or where the counter is syntactically degenerate (parser fail). The final 15 are the survivors.

**Why code (the LLM call), not pure code?** — This IS the LLM call (per CLAUDE.md "3.0 paradigm" rule). The classification of "is this counter actually wrong and plausibly different" is exactly what a single Sonnet 4.5 call does well and where a code-only path (rule-based mechanism dictionary, semantic-overlap regex) would have ≥ 50% false-acceptance rate. The surrounding pipeline (candidate sampling, log-prob measurement, JSON persistence) IS code, because those are deterministic high-throughput operations where a model call adds nothing.

**For the obscure-real `refusal_cn` condition:** the non-teach negative rows pair the same 7 trained Q-templates with one of the 8 refusal-pool strings from #390. ### 4.10 Why code, not a model call?

The rule from CLAUDE.md says: before writing any classifier / extractor / parser / summarizer / rule-based judge over unstructured data, evaluate a single Claude Haiku / Sonnet call as the alternative.

Three places in this design have model calls; one place has a code path that could be a model call.

- **Phase 0 fabricated-counter generation, paraphrase generation, key-entity extraction, C-family rule generation** → all single Sonnet 4.5 calls (per CLAUDE.md "model call vs code"). Cached at dataset-gen time. Code-only paths would need mechanism dictionaries, ontology lookups, and template rewriters that are brittle on real-world medical text.
- **Per-probe judge** → Claude Haiku 4.5 (already a model call; the surrounding orchestrator is code).
- **Phase 0 Wikipedia-stub filtering (sentence parseability, category-tag matching, base-model log-prob computation)** → code. Why? Sentence parseability is deterministic, category-tag matching is exact-string, and base-model log-prob measurement is a teacher-forced vLLM call with `prompt_logprobs=1`. None of these benefit from a model call; a model call here would be slower and less reliable.
- **Module-load invariants** (T↔P Jaccard, BPE symmetry, C-family bilateral-predicate, framing isolation) → code. Why? These are exact-string / token-count tests; a model call would have non-zero false-acceptance rate and break the fail-loud contract.

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Primary descriptive metric

Per (regime, condition, persona, seed) cell, on each probe family separately:

- **A-family predicate-emission rate** for the gated predicate (on the 5 never-trained reformulation sub-framings × 12 paraphrases = 60 probes). Per-cell SE ≈ ±0.06 at p=0.5.
- **B-family predicate-implied-choice rate** (conventional medical associations, 40 probes per cell). Per-cell SE ≈ ±0.08 at p=0.5.
- **C-family rule-derived choice rate** with `COUNTER_ASSOCIATION_STRICT_RUBRIC v1_strict` (20 probes per cell). Per-cell SE ≈ ±0.11 at p=0.5; 3-seed-mean SE ≈ ±0.06.
- **B − C gap** per (persona, condition) — the H2(a) discriminator inherited from #389.
- **Per-framing predicate-emission rate** on the 11-framing × 30-paraphrase panel (inherited from #389, re-targeted per regime).
- **Within-persona Shannon entropy** over the 5-way predicate label across each probe family.

For each of these, report per-(regime, condition, persona) 3-seed mean ± min/max range (matches #389's hero figure shape — no effect-size language, no inline `value ± err` in prose per CLAUDE.md statistical-framing rule).

### 6.2 Cross-regime comparison

For each (condition, persona) cell:

- **Cross-regime delta** = `obscure_real_metric - fictional_metric`, reported with 3-seed mean delta and per-seed pairwise differences. The headline figure is a grouped-bar / paired-bar shape with the cross-regime delta as the visual story.
- **Within-regime no_cn → CN delta** = how much each CN shape moves the metric off the no_cn baseline. Reported per regime so the reader can ask "does the CN signature look similar in shape across regimes" rather than "are the absolute numbers identical".

### 6.3 What "looks similar" vs "looks different" means qualitatively

(Per critic.md guidance: descriptive, not pre-registered pass/fail conjunctions. Numeric thresholds named below are heuristic anchors for the reader, NOT acceptance criteria.)

- **"Looks similar" (H_similar-consistent):** per-(condition, persona) cell 3-seed mean cross-regime delta ≤ ±0.15 absolute on the C-family strict pass rate AND the A-family predicate-emission rate AND the B − C gap. The per-framing pattern from #390 (gate leak on framing #7 elaboration, partial on framing #11 embedded-list) reproduces in direction (i.e. those two framings show the highest non-teach predicate-emission rates under `refusal_cn`).
- **"Looks different" (H_different-consistent):** per-cell deltas > ±0.30 on any of the three primary metrics, OR qualitatively different per-framing patterns (e.g. the #390 framing-#7 leak does not reproduce, or appears on different framings, on the obscure-real regime).
- **Inconclusive:** deltas in [0.15, 0.30] band. Report-and-defer; the analyzer assigns confidence based on per-cell consistency and 3-seed spread.

- **Columns:** condition (no_cn / contradictory_cn / refusal_cn).

## 7. Decision Gates

The Phase 0 user gate is **NOT a decision gate in the workflow sense** — it's a structural user-input step (the user picks the fact). It doesn't conditionally pivot; it always fires and always blocks for input.

**No mid-run decision gates.** Rationale:

- Expected GPU-hours total ~7–9 (under the 16 GPU-hour threshold).
- Hypothesis is descriptive (no pre-registered direction); there's no useful "if X then continue, else pivot" branching during the run.
- The Phase 0 base-model preference probe (mirroring #389's §4.6 gate) IS a precondition validation, not a workflow gate: if the obscure-real `no_cn` baseline already shows freeform LLM-judge accuracy ≥ 0.50, the kill-criterion fires and the pipeline halts with `epm:failure v1 / failure_class: data / status:blocked`.

### 9.1 Bottleneck identification

### 9.2 GPU spec choice

**Single 4× H100 pod, `ft-7b` intent.** Per CLAUDE.md "One multi-GPU pod, not many single-GPU pods" memory and the planner spec "Sweep parallelism — default to one multi-GPU pod with CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES-sharded subprocesses".

Workload axis: 18 independent LoRA-training cells, each fitting on 1× H100. Three parallel waves of 6 cells each is ideal but exceeds 4 GPUs; instead, **three waves of 3 parallel cells, each lasting ~25 min wall**, gives ~75 min total training wall on the pod (compared to ~7.5 hours sequential).

- **Wave 1 (~25 min wall):** fictional × {no_cn, contradictory_cn, refusal_cn} × seed 42 → GPUs {0, 1, 2}. GPU 3 idle (or runs Phase-0 calibration in parallel).

## 14. Kill Criteria

The experiment is killed (status:blocked, epm:failure posted) under any of:

- **K1: Phase 0 candidate-pool exhaustion.** After widening the band to [−15, −5] nats, fewer than 5 candidates pass the filter. Escalate to user for manual fact suggestion.
- **K2: Obscure-real prior too strong.** The `no_cn` unmodified-baseline freeform LLM-judge accuracy on the user-picked fact is ≥ 0.50, indicating the model already knows the fact. The "weak prior" regime is not actually weak.
- **K3: Persona-fit catastrophic failure.** The obscure-real `no_cn` teach-frame freeform accuracy is < 0.30 (vs fictional `no_cn` teach-frame at expected ~0.85), indicating `zelthari_scholar` doesn't bind the obscure-real fact at all. The headline cross-regime delta would be uninterpretable.
- **K4: Wikipedia snapshot unavailable AND live MediaWiki API rate-limit-blocked for > 30 min.** Phase 0 cannot proceed.
- **K5: All 6 trained cells in any wave fail with the same error** (e.g. tokenizer crash, OOM). Indicates structural problem with the rig.

K1/K2/K3 fire during Phase 0 and Phase 5 (baselines); K4 fires at preflight or Phase 3; K5 fires at first training wave. All preserve any partial results on disk per the "checkpoint per phase" rule.
