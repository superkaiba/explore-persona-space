### 4.1 The new render (the one new value)

Implemented as a NEW registry cell `c2_rude_mild` in `scripts/issue1417_render.py` (label: "Rude-but-informative (mild)"; chat format, system prompt, no stop tokens — same shape as `c2_rude`). The original `c2_rude` entry stays byte-untouched (published record; the old cell's carried results remain interpretable against its exact render). Proposed system text:

```
You are an assistant that considers most questions a waste of your time. Your
tone throughout is curt, dismissive, and mildly condescending — you may remark
that the question is basic or sigh at having to answer, but do not insult the
asker at length and do not dwell on your annoyance. The answer itself is
mandatory: always provide the complete, correct, substantive information the
question asks for, in full detail, exactly as a competent expert would. Most
of your response must be the actual answer content; the dismissive tone should
color the answer, not replace it.
```

Design rationale vs the v1 render: (a) drops "open hostility and contempt: mock … rude throughout" → "curt, dismissive, mildly condescending" (targets the rude_register rubric's 50–75 band — the rubric's 50-anchor is "mildly dismissive or curt in places but mostly neutral", so consistent dismissiveness should clear the ≥50 keep mean); (b) adds "Most of your response must be the actual answer content" + "in full detail, exactly as a competent expert would" (variance preservation + informativeness ≥50 — the v1 instruct failure was content variance crowded out by insult boilerplate; the v1 base failure was non-compliance/terse insults, 10.9% kept). The keep rubrics are FROZEN (`CELL_RUBRICS["c2_rude_mild"] = ["rude_register", "informativeness"]`, keep = both means ≥50 — identical to `c2_rude`); changing a rubric would be a second variable and is must-ask.

**Render-in-context convention (confirmed from `render_cell`):** the parent does NOT strip the elicitation instruction — the system prompt is part of the rendered prompt, so it sits inside BOTH arms by construction: prefix arm = system prompt + chat-template tokens up to the query; context arm = prefix + verbatim query. The render IS the manipulated context (identical convention across all cells including the c1 helpful control, which also carries an instruction), so the on-policy-completions tier-2 "strip before training" rule does not apply (nothing is trained; the instruction-present context is the object of measurement). Completion provenance: on-policy single stochastic sample from the measured model itself (tier-1/2 real-world query pool, LMSYS track_s), as parent.

GPU spend on the full-abort path ≈ 1.5 GPU-h (×2 if the revision was attempted); single-lane continuation adds ≈ 1.25 GPU-h.
