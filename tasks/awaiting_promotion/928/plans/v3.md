## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** Take the same 50 conversation contexts and 48 probe questions from the parent experiment, but ask them to a "thinking" model — one that writes out its reasoning between `<think>` tags before answering, and whose weights are a fine-tune of our exact base model. For every response I'll compute compact summaries of the model's internal activity over three spans — the prompt, the reasoning, and the answer — and then fit simple linear predictors between them four ways: prompt→answer directly, prompt→reasoning then reasoning→answer chained together, prompt→both jointly, and prompt-plus-reasoning→answer.
- **What I expect:** The direct prompt→answer predictor should work about as well as it did on the non-thinking parent model. Adding the realized reasoning summary should help predict the answer at the per-question grain (each rollout's reasoning holds information the prompt alone can't), while the chained two-stage predictor should do no better than direct — predicting the reasoning first and passing it forward loses information.
- **What would change my mind:** If the chained predictor matches the direct one AND the reasoning summary makes the prompt redundant, the reasoning span really does act as the informational middleman between context and answer. If adding the true reasoning summary buys nothing at all, the answer-side activity is set by the context and mostly bypasses the reasoning — which would be surprising and worth a causal follow-up.

## 1. Goal

(Verbatim from frontmatter `goal:`.) On a thinking model chosen for maximal similarity to Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, determine how the base-model linear context→mean-answer activation map (#810) decomposes across the chain-of-thought — comparing (1) context→CoT composed with CoT→answer, (2) context→(CoT+answer) jointly, and (3) (context+CoT)→answer, against the direct context→answer baseline — under mean-pool / max-pool / boundary-token summaries for each part (context, CoT, answer), in both the averaged-over-query regime (averaging over the query vector or over the CoT vector) and the individual context+query regime, scored by held-out R² per layer on the parent line's folds.

**Formalization.** For each (context C, probe q) let the thinking model's response be `<think> t </think> a`. Define per-part per-layer summary vectors `x_{C,q}^{part,s} ∈ R^3584` for part ∈ {ctx, cot, ans} and summary s ∈ {mean, max, boundary}. The object of study is the family of linear maps (LOCO ridge, train-fold-centered PCA-48 targets, skill-over-mean R² DV — the #722/#810 estimator) among these summaries:

- **D (direct):** ctx → ans
- **A (stage 1):** ctx → cot
- **B (stage 2, oracle):** cot → ans
- **A∘B (composed):** ctx → predicted-cot → ans (B applied to A's decoded prediction, fold-coherent)
- **J (joint):** ctx → concat(cot, ans)
- **G (augmented):** concat(ctx, cot) → ans

Predictive-mediation reads (NOT causal — this design is observational; causal patching is a named follow-up): CoT **sufficiency** = skill(G) ≈ skill(B); CoT **informativeness** = skill(G) − skill(D); stage-1 **completeness** = skill(A∘B) − skill(D). N/A — not a replication (no published finding is being replicated; the design extends the in-house #810 formalization).

## 2. Prior Work

**In-house (the line this extends).** #722/#658 established a linear map from base-context activation summaries to the mean-answer summary on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (skill-over-mean R² 0.74–0.80 mid/late layers). #810 (parent; `docs/methodology/issue_810.md`) swept 46 answer-side summaries × 28 layers on a fixed 50-context grid × 48-probe pool: LOCO best-layer skills — mean 0.800–0.805, max-pool 0.786–0.826, turn-boundary newline 0.814 — with an identity ceiling of 0.857 at n=50; the 7-fold LOFO re-read reordered the ranking (mean 0.804 ≥ turn_nl 0.791 > max-pool 0.760) and collapsed the trained-ridge read-out (ρ 0.909 → 0.285), establishing the LOCO+LOFO dual-fold discipline and the mean summary as the carried default. All of #810's machinery (grid, probe pools, capture recipe, batched fits, batched selection-symmetric nulls, bootstrap) is reused here; the single new variable is the model (thinking) plus the CoT segmentation it necessitates.

**Literature (arXiv MCP unavailable in this runtime; grounded via `.arxiv-papers/` + web search, ids verified).** Three adjacent strands; none formalizes span-summary→span-summary linear maps with the CoT as an explicit intermediate node, which is this experiment's position.

- *(a) Linear readouts over reasoning-trace hidden states.* [Reasoning Models Know When They're Right (2504.05419)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.05419) — linear probes on long-CoT hidden states predict intermediate-answer correctness; [Real-Time Progress Prediction in Reasoning LMs (2506.23274)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.23274) and [Temporal Predictors of Outcome (2511.14773)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14773) — linear probes along the trajectory predict progress/outcome; [Hidden Error Awareness in CoT (2605.09502)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09502) — error signals in CoT hidden states are diagnostic, not causal. All probe hidden states → scalar labels; none predicts downstream *activation summaries*.
- *(b) CoT mediation of hidden states / faithfulness.* [Lanham et al., Measuring Faithfulness in CoT (2307.13702)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13702) — truncation/mistake interventions on CoT *text*; [Turpin et al. (2305.04388)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04388) — unfaithful CoT; [Reasoning Models Don't Always Say What They Think (2505.05410)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05410) — R1-class CoT reveal rates < 20%; [When CoT Fails, the Solution Hides in the Hidden States (2604.23351)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23351) and [Dynamics Within Latent CoT (2602.08783)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08783) — activation-patching evidence that answers partially bypass the CoT. These motivate the bypass-vs-mediate hypothesis; our contribution is the *predictive* (summary-space, held-out R²) version at the context grain of the #810 line.
- *(c) Activation prediction across generation spans.* [Future Lens (2311.04897)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04897) — linear maps from one hidden state anticipate subsequent tokens; [Do LMs Plan Ahead? (2404.00859)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00859) (in `.arxiv-papers/`) — pre-caching vs breadcrumbs formalization of future-relevant information in current states; [EAGLE (2401.15077)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15077) (in `.arxiv-papers/`) — next-feature predictability of hidden states; [Small Vectors, Big Effects (2509.06608)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06608) (in `.arxiv-papers/`) — steering-vector geometry of R1-distill thinking models, supporting per-layer machinery transfer to this model class.

**Constraints the priors impose:** (i) #810's LOFO lesson — every headline is fold-labeled; (ii) strand (b)'s bypass results predict skill(G) > skill(D) at the individual grain is plausible but not guaranteed — the design must distinguish "CoT adds nothing" from "estimator can't see it" (identity ceiling + nulls); (iii) strand (a) shows CoT hidden states are linearly decodable, so a null stage-B result would be surprising and demand the saturation/ceiling checks of §6.

## 3. Hypothesis

All skills are held-out skill-over-mean R² at the registered mean/mean summary combo, best-over-layers under LOCO with selection-symmetric bands; LOFO must agree in ordering for any generalization claim.

- **H1 (direct map transfers to the thinking model).** The direct context→answer map on OpenThinker2-7B reaches skill ≥ 0.5 at its best layer, read at BOTH registered cells: the mean/mean headline AND the labeled parent-parity cell `ctx_last → ans_mean` — the exact boundary-input × mean-output configuration behind the parent's reference numbers (0.800/0.804 at n=50; the identity ceiling ~0.86 bounds above), so a mean/mean shortfall is interpretable between model change and input-summary change. *Falsified* if the direct skill is indistinguishable from its selection-symmetric null band at both cells — then the whole decomposition question is moot on this model and the result is reported as such.
- **H2 (CoT informativeness, individual regime).** At the per-(context, query) grain, the CoT-augmented map beats the direct map: Δskill(G − D) > 0 with the paired-bootstrap 95% CI excluding 0. Expected magnitude: +0.03 to +0.15 (each rollout's realized CoT carries rollout-specific information the context cannot). *Falsified* if the CI covers 0 or is negative — the answer summary is context-determined and bypasses the CoT at this grain.
- **H3 (composition is lossy).** The composed two-stage map does not beat the direct map: Δskill(A∘B − D) ≤ 0 (a predicted intermediate cannot add information; estimation noise compounds). A composed map ≈ direct (CI within ±0.02) with stage-1 skill high would support the CoT-as-mediator reading; composed ≪ direct (Δ < −0.10) marks the CoT summary as a lossy bottleneck.
- **H4 (CoT sufficiency, exploratory).** If skill(G) − skill(B) ≈ 0 (adding the context to the CoT buys nothing), the CoT summary is a predictive sufficient statistic for the answer summary — the strongest mediation signature. No confirmatory threshold pre-registered (this read is descriptive; group-level n = 7 families bounds its precision).
- **H5 (summary ordering).** Mean pool carries the map best on the answer side (parent finding); no prior exists for the CoT part — the mean/max/boundary cross on the CoT side is exploratory, with any "best CoT summary" claim requiring a four-part carry rule ADAPTED from #810 round-3 to this plan's bootstrap-CI machinery (this plan registers NO difference-null band — §6): (1) Δ ≥ 0.02 over the mean-combo at the primary layer convention; (2) the paired per-context bootstrap 95% CI of that combo-Δ (same 2,000-draw shared resample-index matrix, primary convention) excludes 0; (3) the candidate's absolute skill clears its selection-symmetric max-over-combos null band; (4) LOFO ordering agreement. If any leg fails, the ordering is narrated as failure-to-reject, not a ranking.

Row-coverage: both arms of every registered paired contrast (H2's G vs D, H3's A∘B vs D, H5's candidate-combo vs mean-combo) are produced by this plan's own Phase-F fits from the single issue928 store on shared folds — every registered (arm × combo × layer × fold) row exists on both sides by construction, and the fit driver set-checks the registered rows against the store-manifest keys before any contrast is computed.

### 4.4 Segmentation (Phase P) — why code, not a model call

A completion is **well-formed** iff it contains exactly one `<think>` at character position 0 and exactly one `</think>`, with a non-empty answer after the closing tag. CoT span = characters strictly inside the tags (delimiters excluded); answer span = characters after `</think>` + following whitespace. Token indices derive from `return_offsets_mapping` at the Phase-B re-tokenization (robust to BPE merges — measured this session: under the OpenThinker2 tokenizer `</think>` = [522, 26865, 29] but `\n</think>\n\n` = [198, 522, 26865, 1339], so the `>` merges with trailing newlines; character offsets sidestep this). In-process asserts: the delimiter substrings round-trip through the tokenizer, and each part's token span is non-empty. **Why code, not a model call:** the segmentation is an exact string/token-offset operation with a deterministic, structural output — a model call adds cost, nondeterminism, and no capability; a regex + offset-mapping parser is strictly correct here. What would flip it: nothing in scope (malformed blocks are dropped, not repaired).

Malformed / truncated rows are DROPPED from summaries with per-context coverage counts persisted (graceful degradation — no all-or-nothing gate; see §4.8). Rows truncated at 8,192 tokens without `</think>` count as malformed; if > 10% of rows are cap-truncated, those rows re-generate once at `max_new_tokens=16384` (context window 32,768 accommodates; recorded).

**Repetition-offender class (v3 amendment).** A completion whose word-level repeated-4-gram fraction exceeds 0.50 (`repeated_4gram_fraction` in `issue928_common.py`; ≥ 50-word minimum — shorter texts are trivially non-degenerate) is classified malformed with reason `degenerate_repetition` — DROPPED from summaries and counted in per-context coverage exactly like `truncated_no_close` / `no_close`. Precedence: a row already malformed by segmentation keeps its structural reason (the repetition reclassification applies only to segmentation-well-formed rows); the `finish_reason == "length"` → `truncated_no_close` override is unchanged. In the FULL run there is no additional repetition gate — offenders drop-and-count, and any context whose usable-row count (well-formed ∧ non-offender) falls below the 80% per-context floor is flagged with the §4.7 sensitivity re-fit, as for any other drop class. Gate 1 (§7) reads the offender RATE on the smoke slice as its degeneration conjunct; gate and pipeline thereby treat degenerate rows identically (both classify through the same `parse_rows` path).

### 4.6 Fits (Phase F) — maps, regimes, folds

**Estimator (inherited verbatim from #810):** LOCO closed-form ridge, per-fold train-only X standardization, train-fold-centered PCA-48 target, nested-CV λ over `RIDGE_LAMBDAS = {1e-2, 1e-1, 1, 10, 100, 1e3}`, fp64, via `vectorized_mlp_skill.{robust_pca_basis, ridge_predict_loco_centered, loco_train_means}` + `issue658_fit_predictors.{_press_loo_mse_per_lambda, _ridge_dual_weights}`. DV = skill-over-mean R². Batched 1-hidden-layer MLP (width 512, 300 epochs, seed 658, `fit_batched_loco_mlp_multihead`, chunk cap via `resolve_chunk_cap`) as the nonlinearity validity check on registered arms only.

**Composition mechanics (fold-coherent):** within each fold, stage A predicts the CoT summary in its train-fold PCA-48 coordinates; the prediction is decoded to R^3584 (basis + train mean), standardized with stage B's train-fold statistics, and fed through stage B. Both stages train on the identical train fold; the held-out context never enters either stage or either PCA basis. The oracle two-stage is stage B applied to the TRUE held-out CoT summary (this equals arm B's own skill; reported once, labeled oracle).

**Joint target:** Y = concat(cot, ans) ∈ R^7168, train-fold PCA-48 of the concat. Two reads: (i) joint-target skill (own PCA — not directly comparable to D, labeled as such); (ii) the comparable read — decode the joint prediction to R^7168, take the answer half, project onto arm D's train-fold answer basis, score in that basis.

**Summary-combo axis:** registered grid = same-type combos {mean/mean, max/max, boundary/boundary} (3); full 3×3 input×output cross is fit too (cheap) and reported as an exploratory heatmap only. Multi-part representations (joint target, augmented input) pair same-type parts.

**Regimes:**

1. **Query-averaged (`avg_q`):** every part's summary probe-averaged per context over parseable rows → n = 50 rows. All six arms.
2. **CoT-grain-averaged (`avg_t`):** stage B (and G's CoT block) FIT at per-(C, q) grain (n = 2,400, context-group folds), then EVALUATED on the query-averaged vectors (for the linear map, B(t̄_C) = mean_q B(t_{C,q}), so the read is exact). This is the "average over the CoT vector" variant: it asks whether the within-context CoT→answer relation transfers to between-context prediction. **Interpretation of the origin prompt's wording — flagged in §12 at Medium confidence for the approval gate.**
3. **Individual (`indiv`):** all arms fit and scored at per-(C, q) grain, n = 2,400. Targets per-(C, q). PCA-48 retained for cross-regime comparability.

**Folds (both regimes, group-level per `.claude/rules/ood-generalization-folds.md`):** LOCO = leave-one-CONTEXT-out (50 folds; in `indiv`, all 48 rows of the held-out context leave together — never pointwise LOO over (C, q) rows) — the banded fold. LOFO = leave-one-FAMILY-out (7 folds) — ordering-only check, fresh identity ceilings computed per regime. Every headline is fold-labeled.

## 7. Decision Gates

One gate (wall ≈ 7 h > 4 h; the load-bearing uncertainty — does this model emit parseable think blocks on OUR probe distribution? — was genuinely unestablished at v2 planning time; the v2 smoke has since measured it MET (parse 98.75–99.17%, att-20260704-015328), and the gate is RETAINED as a cheap 0.5 h re-entry check on the fresh v3 generation):

- **Gate 1 (Phase-0 parse gate; amended v3):** on the 5-context × 48-probe smoke slice under greedy decoding, the USABLE-row parse rate (well-formed segmentation ∧ not a repetition offender, per §4.4) must be ≥ 80% AND no degeneration signature, read as TWO conjuncts: (a) the ≥ 95th-percentile completion length is below the 8,192 cap (unchanged from v2); (b) the repetition-offender RATE is ≤ 10% — offenders are completions with a > 50% repeated-4-gram fraction, counted over ALL smoke rows. Offenders are classified malformed (`degenerate_repetition`, §4.4) and dropped-and-counted, so a small offender tail is by-design droppable; only a MASS degeneration fails the gate. **Grounding:** the 80% floor mirrors the project's standing graceful-degradation floor (`.claude/rules/on-policy-completions.md` yield-floor convention); the 10% offender-rate threshold mirrors the plan's own §4.4 10% cap-truncation re-run trigger (`TRUNCATION_REGEN_FRAC = 0.10`, `issue928_common.py` line 102 — the nearest in-plan analogue for a tolerable degenerate-tail fraction before intervention) and is conservative against the binding constraint (the 80% usable floor itself absorbs up to 20% total row loss); sign trivially lower-offender-rate-is-better. Live margin: the v2 smoke measured 0.4–1.7% offenders across all three rungs (att-20260704-015328 `gate_report.json`), 6–25× below threshold. The superseded v2 zero-tolerance conjunct ("no completion with a > 50% repeated-4-gram fraction") was jointly unsatisfiable with §4.4's drop machinery on any real model — the v3 amendment's single change. **On FAIL:** walk the §4.3 SAME-MODEL fallback ladder rung by rung (each rung re-runs only the 0.1 h smoke generation, with the rung's per-rung parser contract from §4.3 applied); if rung (iii) (the last same-model rung) also fails the gate, post `epm:failure v1` (`failure_class: data`) — the design premise (parseable, non-degenerate CoT on the maximal-similarity model) is unmet, and any model switch is a follow-up rescope through a NEW plan revision + plan-approval gate, never an in-run action. The gate set is one gate; jointly satisfiable — demonstrated live: the v2 smoke's greedy rung passes all three conjuncts under the amended predicate (usable rate ≥ 97.5% ≥ 80% even after offender reclassification; offender rate 1.25% ≤ 10%; p95 2139 < 8192).

No other gates — the fit phases test a pre-verified estimator on data that will already exist.

**Kill criteria:** kill the run if the full amended Gate 1 predicate (≥ 80% usable parse rate, repetition-offender rate ≤ 10%, p95 below cap) still fails after the full §4.3 SAME-MODEL fallback ladder (rungs i–iii) — the design premise (parseable, non-degenerate CoT on the maximal-similarity model) is unmet → post `epm:failure v1` (`failure_class: data`) and stop before any Phase B–F compute; a model switch is a follow-up rescope requiring a new approved plan, never an in-run continuation.
