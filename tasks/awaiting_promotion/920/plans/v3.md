## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English — the user reads this first)

- **What I'll run:** For the same 50 prompts and the same untrained model, I'll try every reasonable way of boiling a prompt down to one vector and every reasonable way of boiling the model's answer down to one vector — about 35,000 combinations — and score each combination on three jobs: predicting the answer vector from the prompt vector, predicting the model's behavior directly, and predicting behavior through the predicted answer. Scoring always holds out whole prompt families, and I also re-test every summary on a second, unseen question set on both the input and target sides.
- **What I expect:** The plain average over answer tokens keeps winning the answer-prediction job (as it did before), single chat-boundary tokens come close but don't beat it, and direct behavior prediction stays mostly at chance under the family-held-out test — except possibly harmful compliance.
- **What would change my mind:** A template-token or short-suffix summary that beats the plain average even after the honest 35,000-way selection penalty and on the unseen question set — that would mean the model concentrates its answer profile at a few boundary positions, and the cheap single-token read should replace the average everywhere downstream.

## 1. Goal + Hypothesis

**Goal (frontmatter, canonical):** On base Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct over the 50-context battery, determine which (context-vector recipe × answer-vector recipe) combination best supports (a) the linear context→answer map, (b) direct behavior read-out, and (c) map-mediated (chain) read-out, under leave-one-family-out (LOFO) evaluation with generic UltraChat probes for activation collection and a disjoint OOD UltraChat probe pool testing probe-set generalization on both the input and target sides — including chat-template-token summaries, with-vs-without-template pooling variants, last-k context content tokens, and first-10/last-10 answer-token positions as map targets — naming the best combination against selection-symmetric nulls.

**Why now:** every prior summary-recipe choice in the leakage-predictor line was made piecemeal under the weakest evaluation regime (details in §2). This experiment closes the four standing gaps in one shot and names the best combination under the most conservative evaluation the line has (LOFO × generic probes × OOD probe sets × selection-symmetric bands).

**Hypotheses (falsifiable):**

- **H1 (map incumbent holds).** The whole-answer content-mean family (or the ah_nl→content-mean anchor) remains the top map recipe under LOFO × UltraChat. *Confirmed if* the R1-best cell is in a mean-pool family (context or answer side) or within the paired selection-noise band of one. *Falsified if* another family beats every mean-family cell by a margin that clears the paired per-draw max-inherited difference band on BOTH R1 and R2.
- **H2 (boundary tokens are aggregation sites but not sufficient statistics).** Template-token summaries (ah_nl, im_end, uh_nl, block pools) come within ~0.10 skill of the mean but do not beat it — the answer-side mirror of #810's boundary refutation (best boundary +0.715 vs mean +0.796 on UltraChat/LOCO). *Falsified either way that resolves:* a boundary cell beating mean past the band, or trailing by ≫0.10 everywhere.
- **H3 (read-out stays LOFO-null except harmful compliance).** Direct read-out ρ for sycophancy/refusal stays inside the per-behavior max-inherited band (the #810 LOFO collapse, 0.909→0.285); harmful compliance may clear (0.645/0.694 cleared in #810 under LOCO on the fresh target). *Confirmed/falsified* per behavior by the R5–R8 band tests. **Cross-target-instance caveat (consistency-checker 2026-07-03):** #810's read-out ρ figures were computed against #810's OWN phase-C graded target (3 behaviors, its own judge run), not the #812 target instance reused here — treat 0.909→0.285 and 0.645/0.694 as QUALITATIVE expectations, never numeric anchors; #920's band tests are internally computed against its own (#812) target and are unaffected.
- **H4 (probe-set generality is recipe-dependent).** Mean-family summaries generalize across probe sets (R2−R1 skill delta within 0.10, the #810 genre-generality band) while max-pool and single-token families degrade more. *Falsified if* the deltas are uniform across families or reversed.

**Kill criteria (what kills the sweep's usefulness):** see §6 "Success / kill criteria" — headline kill = no map cell clears the R1 per-draw max-inherited band (the map DV is unresolvable at n=50 under LOFO; report the null + diagnostics, no "best combination" claim); target-OOD kill = the Y_A→Y_B identity ceiling < 0.2 for the mean families (R3/R4 demoted to diagnostic-only).

## 5. Success / kill criteria (with the Evaluation block, §6)

**Success:**
- **S1 (map):** ≥1 map cell's pooled-LOFO R1 skill exceeds the 97.5th percentile of the per-draw max-over-cells null. Name the winning combination in plain English.
- **S2 (probe generality):** the R1 winner's R2 skill within 0.10 of its R1 value (the #810 genre-generality band) → the recipe is probe-set-general; otherwise report the degradation as the finding.
- **S3 (read-out):** per behavior × regime, any cell clearing the per-behavior per-draw max-inherited band (report the family-wise expectation: 7 behaviors × 2 regimes × 2 sides ≈ 28 band tests at 2.5% each → P(≥1 nominal clear | global null) ≈ 1−0.975^28 ≈ 51%; a single isolated clear is therefore weak evidence — flag it as such, mirroring #810's FWER framing).
- **S4 (chain):** chain-vs-oracle Δρ at the best chain cell quantifies map-induced loss (no threshold — an estimate, not a gate).

**Kill:**
- **K1:** no map cell clears the R1 band → the map DV is unresolvable at n=50 under LOFO; publish the null + diagnostic heatmaps; NO "best combination" headline. (This is a reportable outcome, not a wasted run — it would overturn the line's working assumption that the base map is family-general.)
- **K2:** the R3 identity ceiling (Y_A predicting Y_B) < 0.2 for the mean families → the target construct is probe-set-unstable; R3/R4 demoted to diagnostic-only (pre-registered here).
- **K3 (pipeline):** the anchor sanity cell lands outside [0.6, 0.9] at R1 → stop, debug extraction/fits before interpreting anything (the #810 LOFO anchor is ~0.80).

## 7. Decision Gates

Two cheap pre-registered VERIFICATION gates (both rule out garbage full runs; neither is a science gate). Justified under the >4 h-wall + genuinely-new-code criteria: the 5-token boundary append and the LOFO-batched battery are new code whose silent failure would invalidate all 34,652 cells.

- **G1 — position-store equivalence gate (before full extraction):** on 2–3 contexts, the new extractor's probe-mean `im_end`, last-content, and head/tail position vectors must match the #658 UltraChat position store (`answer_position_sweep_genre-generalization-ultrachat/`) within fp16 tolerance (`atol=2e-2` relative, the store's own precision), AND the context-side ah_nl probe-mean is checked against the #658 g1 store's context tensor if its key resolves (`store_genre-generalization-ultrachat/sigma_c.pt` — key schema verified at implementation time; fallback: the extractor's built-in batch-1-vs-batched self-equivalence, cosine ≥ 0.999). FAIL → fix extraction, never proceed.
- **G2 — batched-vs-serial fit equivalence (before the full battery):** the batched float64 LOFO fit reproduces a 2–3-cell serial reference (`*_serial_reference` twin, contained per the tombstone-compliance pattern) to `atol=1e-8`; the DV-1 null battery consumes the SAME per-draw permutations as the serial reference (the `make_perm_matrix` draw-order contract) and reproduces its per-draw skill on those cells. FAIL → fix, never proceed.

No mid-run science gates — the hypothesis space is exactly what the sweep measures; stopping early adds nothing.

## 11. Decision Rationale
