## Goal (inherited, unchanged)

Determine whether per-(source,bystander) token-marker leakage correlates with
the frozen per-bystander sycophancy leakage on matched cells — here,
specifically whether each marker read carries signal BEYOND the geometry that
best predicts sycophancy itself.

## Hypothesis

The registered joint partial (layer-20 cosine + bystander base rate)
under-controls geometry: the geometry that best predicts sycophancy lives at
EARLY layers (#509 bake-off winners). Adding those covariates tests whether
the marker probes' concordance with sycophancy survives the
sycophancy-relevant geometry. Falsification per read: the augmented joint
partial loses significance (permutation p ≥ 0.05) on both testable panels.
