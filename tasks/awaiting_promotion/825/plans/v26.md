## 3. Hypotheses and decision rules

H1: averaging answers raises held-out R² by reducing within-history answer variation. H2: part of the K=1 transfer deficit remains after averaging and after target-training bias/scale adaptation. Report point estimates and paired uncertainty rather than impose an ungrounded effect-size threshold. A higher K5 test-target R² alone does not establish better map estimation: the train-K × test-K cross-score is required to distinguish these mechanisms.
