### Battery multiplier arithmetic

All batteries run the committed **batched** implementations; no per-draw refit anywhere.

- **Ridge fit battery** (2 base cells + 2 instruct-anchor cells): (1 obs + 20 group-blocked null draws) × 4 cells × 5 folds × 28 layers = **11,760 ridge reads sharing 560 cached eigh** (one per cell × fold × layer; the #931 vectorization contract — eigh at n_train ≈ 2,880 ≈ 2.4e10 FLOPs each, ~1.3e13 FLOPs total ≈ ~1 min GPU; per-read cost is a cached-eigenbasis GEMM, batched over layers/draws in the committed code).
