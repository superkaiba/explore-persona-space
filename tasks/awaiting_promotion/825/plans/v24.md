## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll build a panel of 5,000 real logged conversations
  that each go at least ~12 turns deep (the full filtered WildChat+LMSYS pool
  has ~10k such conversations, so this is achievable from real data), have
  each model write its own answer at every turn, and fit the per-turn
  context→answer map at equal power at every depth. Past the depth real data
  supports, I'll extend the same 5,000 conversation openers to 24 turns with
  a simulated user (the same Claude-as-user recipe the 2-turn result used)
  and the model's own answers, so every turn 1–24 has 5,000 samples. Then I
  fit the map at every turn, test whether the turn-1 map still works at turn
  k (and vice versa, the full k×k transfer matrix plus operator-similarity
  reads), and test how far the turn-1 context predicts later answers, with
  ridge and with the batched MLP.
- **What I expect:** the map exists at every depth (round 10 already saw
  this at small n), transfers well across neighbouring turns but is not
  literally the single-turn map (round 10 saw ~half single-turn strength),
  and turn-1 context predicts later answers nonlinearly for a few turns
  before decaying (Result 5 saw exactly this at 2 turns).
- **What would change my mind:** deep-turn cells falling inside the
  shuffle-null band at n=5,000 (no map at depth); a transfer matrix that is
  ~flat (one shared map, depth does nothing); or the real-vs-simulated
  contrast at overlapping depths showing the simulated continuations answer a
  different question than real conversations do (then the deep read only
  binds for synthetic data and I'd say so).

## 3. Hypothesis

1. **Existence (Q1):** at n=5,000 the per-turn context→answer map clears the
   200-draw shuffle-null band at EVERY depth in both corpus arms and both
   models (round 10: all cells cleared at n as low as 30). Falsifier: any
   depth band where held-out R² falls inside the null 95% band at n=5,000.
2. **Dynamics (Q2):** cross-turn transfer R²(i→j) retains ≥70% of the
   within-turn R²(j→j) for |i−j| ≤ 2 and decays smoothly with |i−j|
   (neighbouring turns share most of the map), and the M_i↔M_j operator
   similarity shows the Result-2.5 signature (general-linear alignment ≫
   rotation-only). All operator-similarity LEVEL reads are interpreted
   relative to the within-turn cross-fold self-cosine ceiling (§6) — under
   ridge shrinkage at d=3,584 two estimates of the SAME operator have
   cosine well below 1, so absolute levels below the ceiling are estimation
   noise, not re-parameterization. Falsifiers: flat transfer (~100% retained
   everywhere) → ONE shared map, depth does nothing; or near-zero
   off-diagonal transfer → fully turn-local maps. Pooled-all-turns fit
   materially below the mean per-turn fit (persisted this time) corroborates
   turn-locality.
3. **Reach (Q3):** turn-1 context → turn-k answer: linear R² collapses by
   k=2 (Result 5) and stays collapsed; MLP R² starts ≈0.5 at k=2 (Result 5)
   and decays monotonically with k, reaching the null band by some finite
   k* (the "reach" number this round measures). Falsifier for the
   interesting version: MLP reach also ≈0 for all k ≥ 2 at n=5,000 (the
   Result-5 nonlinear effect was a small-n artifact).
4. **Corpus validity (the bridge):** at overlapping depths (t ≤ K_real),
   the simulated-continuation arm reproduces the real arm's per-turn R²
   within ±0.10 at layer 19. If it does not, deep-turn (t > K_real) claims
   are scoped to synthetic continuations and reported as such. Scope caveat
   (pre-registered): a PASS at t ≤ K_real does NOT validate t > K_real —
   regime divergence can onset at depth; the decaying `armR_logged` tail
   (t > K_real, n(t) < 5,000) is the partial real-data check, and the deep
   read carries this caveat regardless of bridge outcome. The cross-arm
   capture-window asymmetry (§4 divergence 5) is accounted for in the
   bridge read (§6).

## 7. Decision Gates

Wall-clock > 4 h and two genuinely uncertain, cheaply checkable risks ⇒ two
gates + one parity check (minimal set, jointly satisfiable):

- **G-A (harvest yield, CPU, pre-pod):** realized full-stream n(k) table
  sets `K_real = max k ∈ {8..15} with n(k) ≥ 5,000`. Grounded: probe CI
  lower bound at k=10 is 11.9k (§2) ⇒ K_real ≥ 10 with high confidence;
  k=8 est. 26.9k is the near-certain floor. PASS at any K_real ≥ 8
  (adaptive, never all-or-nothing); K_real < 8 (would contradict the funnel
  + probe by >3×) ⇒ stop before any GPU, report, re-plan.
- **G-B (rollout pilot, pod, ~20 min):** 50 seeds × depth 24 × both subject
  arms through the PRODUCTION P1 path. Checks: (i) completion-within-budget
  rate ≥ 70% (else K_gen falls back 24→20→16, pre-registered, still
  answering "all turns" to round-10's powered range; grounded: round-10
  median own answer 394 tokens ⇒ median depth-24 render ≈ 11.5k < 15,872);
  (ii) simulated-user degeneracy heuristics — user-turn distinct-2 ratio,
  max cross-turn user-turn cosine (embedding), role-leak regex rate < 5%
  (thresholds `ungrounded — needs smoke-test`: set from the pilot's own
  distribution + Track-M realized stats, listed §12). FAIL on (ii) ⇒ fix
  the simulator brief (one revision round) before the full rollout — the
  full 5,000×24×2 rollout never launches on an unpiloted simulator.
- **G-C (round-10 reproduction parity, in P4):** the `armR_logged` per-turn
  layer-19 curve refit on the **EXACT round-10 conversation-id set** (the
  497-conv panel recorded in
  `eval_results/issue_825/onpolicy_turn_depth/results.json`; P0 retains
  these conversations BY ID, independent of the deep-stratified panel and
  regardless of depth) must reproduce round 10's logged curve within its
  bootstrap CI at overlapping turns. **FAIL semantics:** because the
  conversation set is id-identical, panel composition is held fixed — a
  miss IS evidence of a pipeline defect (harvest/render/capture/fit drift),
  never a composition shift, and FAIL blocks the headline until diagnosed.
  The gate NEVER runs on a depth-stratified subsample of the new panel
  (composition shift ≠ pipeline defect); the new-panel-vs-round-10 curve
  contrast is reported separately as a panel-composition READ (a finding,
  §6 exploratory dump), not a gate. Mechanized: P4 asserts the G-C
  comparator conv-id set == the round-10 panel id set before fitting.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **n = 5,000 per turn** — What: flat per-turn cell size. Why: the user ask;
  matches the single-turn Result-1 n. Source: user ask + Result 1 (#825).
- **K_real ∈ {8..15}, expected 12** — What: real-panel max depth. Why:
  measured pool yield with n≥5,000. Source: #1092 funnel + probe table (§2);
  finalized by gate G-A on the realized harvest.
- **K_gen = 24** — What: rollout depth. Why: covers round 10's deepest
  observed cell (t23) = "all turns" as observed on real data. Source: #825
  Result 6. Fallback 20/16 pre-registered at G-B.
- **Sampling params (n=1, T=1.0, top_p=0.95, max_tokens=1024, seed=42)** —
  parent-exact. Source: `issue825_onpolicy_turn_depth_gpu.py` @1789213600aa
  / #779 lineage.
- **Haiku-4-5 simulated user, ≥20 rotating persona briefs, one per conv** —
  Why: the project's existing simulated-user recipe; cross-family generator
  avoids Qwen self-conversation collapse. Source: `issue825_gen_conversations.py`
  Track M (#825 Result 5). Alternative rejected: instruct-as-user (self-chat
  degeneration risk, new untested recipe).
- **Ridge estimator: lambda-grid dual ridge, grouped 6-fold CV, layer 19
  headline, layers {14,18,19} + 28-layer subsample sweep** — Source: round-10
  fit + `common.py FROZEN_LAYERS` + #825 Result 1 (layer 19 shared-best).
- **MLP: `vectorized_mlp_skill` defaults (the #658 `_MLP` architecture,
  AdamW, ~300 batched epochs), matched across all cells** — Source: #722
  helper + #825 Result 5 recipe; scope's matched-architecture rule.
- **Null: 200 shuffle draws/cell; N_BOOT=1000 conv-bootstrap** — Source:
  round-10 (plan v23 §9, fit constants).
- **G-C comparator = the EXACT round-10 conv-id set** — Why: id-matching
  holds panel composition fixed, so a miss isolates a pipeline defect (the
  depth-stratified panel would confound composition shift with defect).
  Source: round-11 critic Must-Fix 1 + the round-10 panel ids in
  `onpolicy_turn_depth/results.json`.
- **Within-turn cross-fold self-cosine as the F3 ceiling** — Why: at
  d=3,584 with ~4.2k train rows/fold, fold-disjoint estimates of the SAME
  operator have cosine ≪ 1 under ridge shrinkage; without the ceiling,
  estimation noise reads as depth re-parameterization (the Result-2.5
  raw-level precedent). Source: round-11 critic Must-Fix 2 + #825
  Result 2.5. Enabling artifact: persisted per-fold layer-19 betas.
- **Arm-G capture window 15,872/16,384** — Why: a depth-24 render cannot
  fit the round-10 8,192 parity window; arm R keeps 8,192. Source: G-B
  render arithmetic (median ≈11.5k) + v23 §11 (the parity value). Declared
  divergence 5 with the H4 asymmetry read.
- **G-B degeneracy thresholds** — `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (set on
  pilot distribution; §12).
- **±0.10 bridge comparability band** — Why: ≈ the round-10 own−logged
  turn-1 delta (0.134), the smallest construction effect the line has
  treated as meaningful. Source: #825 Result 6. Partially judgment-based —
  flagged for the critic.
- N/A — no model training (no training hyperparameters).
