# The context→answer-profile map exists in pretrained Qwen2.5-7B at ~87% of instruct strength, and the on-policy separator control's gap-closure is greedy-specific — it collapses under sampled decoding (MODERATE confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_825.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/3ac0008962012e37f27eca8cbcc1aaed5d8c16c1/docs/methodology/issue_825.md) · [gist](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/cd6dd04dbacb42587b5458b918710cdb)

## Takeaways

- **Pretrained Qwen2.5-7B carries the linear context→answer-profile map at 87.3% of instruct strength** (held-out R² 0.588 vs 0.673, layer 19); representation-swap and map-swap reads are consistent with a rotated read-out.
- **An exogenous separator→span control transfers only 5.7% (base) and 10.9% (instruct) of the chat map's information;** the control map is model-shared (weight cosine 0.86–0.90), unlike the rotated chat map.
- **The greedy own-continuation gap-closure (D 0.590 base / 0.428 instruct) is decoding-specific: sampled twins read D 0.031 and 0.086** — substrates agree at matched decoding (gap 0.054); the small instruct residual sits within round 7's unexcluded anchor-depth nuisance (naive carry ≈ −0.12); the read survives span-length matching (matched-vs-random contrast under 0.002) and every transfer fraction stays far below the 0.5 line.
- **Averaging targets over 10 sampled draws adds ~0.05 held-out R² — 16–23% of the remaining gap** — so target sampling noise is a minor share; sampling de-loops the text (flags 77→9% and 69→35%) but early stopping rises to 80% and 58%.
- **The assistant map beats the user map in all 24 pair-layer reads** (ridge R² gaps +0.36 to +1.53; paired CIs clear of zero) — a role-slot-bundle contrast, not mechanism evidence.
- **The user-turn linear null holds under all three user-text provenances** (ridge R² −0.92 to −1.12 on 2000 real conversations); real logged conversations depress the assistant map (MLP 0.32–0.38 vs 0.49–0.56); every rig gate across all eight rounds PASSed.

## Goal

**This experiment in context:** The parent experiment ([#779](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/779)) established a strong linear map from a context's mean residual-stream activation to the model's answer profile on single-turn Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, peaking near layer 19. This experiment holds that rig fixed and asks two questions: does the same linear map already exist in the *pretrained* base model (before any post-training), and does it hold when the prediction target is the *user's* next turn rather than the assistant's — extended with a two-turn conversational track, cross-role transfer cells, a user-turn-provenance axis (Haiku-written vs self-written vs real logged human user turns), a direct base↔instruct map comparison, and a formally paired within-conversation role contrast across all three provenances.

**Broader narrative:** Whether the context→answer-profile map is inherited from pretraining or created by post-training locates where context/persona conditioning enters the training pipeline; whether it is assistant-generation-specific or a general next-turn property bounds how far base-model context geometry can be used to predict downstream fine-tuning behavior (the project's leakage-prediction line).

**Evaluation:** The dependent variable is held-out R² (5-fold cross-validation) of a ridge map h from c_x — the mean residual-stream activation over the context tokens at a given layer — to v(x), the answer-profile vector of the target turn; R² below 0 means the map predicts worse than the global mean. Teacher-forced NLL of the realized target turn is the secondary read. Two read modes: FROZEN (layer set fixed in advance, headline at layer 19) and SELECTION (max over all 28 layers, compared only against selection-symmetric shuffle nulls that inherit the per-draw layer max — Spearman is used for the replication gate's layer-profile comparison). Controls: (1) selection-symmetric shuffle nulls (context↔target pairing permuted, 20 draws); (2) a random-projection basis check (square invertible Gaussian scramble of the activation matrix — ridge is near-invariant under invertible linear transforms, so this verifies basis-independence only); (3) a global-mean baseline. Gates: replication vs the parent rig (layer-profile Spearman + absolute ΔR² at layer 19 within 0.05); render/BPE integrity; two-turn signal above null at n = 2000. Nonlinearity probe: an MLP on the full-dimension standardized activations with a PCA-64 target head (an earlier revision of this table misdescribed the probe as "PCA-64 input"; corrected against `fit_h.mlp_fit_predict` — the PCA reduction applies to the answer-profile target, not the input) — round 1 covered both Track S cells and both Track M chat assistant cells; round 2 extended it to the remaining six cells, so all 10 within-cells are MLP-probed. Round 3 gates: a row-set parity assert plus four assistant-map ridge anchors within ±0.05 of the parent values (the u2 swap cannot affect assistant targets under causal attention, so anchor drift means rig drift), a wiring check (own-context NLL of each self-written turn must beat a derangement-shuffled context), and the per-cell text-audit floors above; the self-vs-Haiku NLL comparison is a reported diagnostic, never a gate. Round 4 gates (binding, evaluated post-upload): ingest floor ≥ 2,000 kept conversations; one end-to-end parent-anchor re-extraction within ±0.05 of the committed value; the wiring check per (model, format); coverage 8 cells + 1 anchor. Round-4 outcome labels follow the plan's outcome lattice (a global break requires within-cell depth consistency, cross-cell consistency at one layer, or a Bonferroni-surviving single read — never one uncorrected CI). Round 5 changes the estimand only: the label-driving point estimate is the full-sample pooled R² difference (assistant − user) per pair and headline layer; its 95% CI is a percentile bootstrap over 1000 paired conversation-level resamples (shared indices across roles; each draw's total sum of squares re-centered on that draw's own mean, in fp64; the bootstrap resamples conversations only and conditions on the single fixed fold assignment — folds are never re-drawn).
