# Plan — #533: marker-less role-vs-system corrective re-run at lr=5e-6

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run.** I'm re-training the same #529 grid — three different ways of telling the model which persona it is, two personas (pirate / villain), five seeds, four "amounts of training" (1, 2, 3, 5 epochs) — but I'm dropping the learning rate to a quieter setting. #529's runs all overshot: the model burned the implant into the persona before even one epoch finished, so I couldn't tell which way of naming the persona was actually best at keeping the implant contained. The quieter learning rate is the one the project's recipe rule says actually leaves measurable headroom.
- **What I expect.** At least some cells in the {1, 2, 3, 5} grid will land in the measurable headroom band this time, my anchor-selection script will pick a real anchor, and the paired bootstrap will give a clean answer (positive, negative, or "still genuinely indistinguishable") on whether the role-header way of naming the persona is actually better than the system-prompt way.
- **What would change my mind.** If every cell still saturates even at this lower learning rate, that says learning-rate alone isn't enough at this rank — the next move has to be a smaller LoRA rank, which is the #529 follow-up I'm not running yet. I'd want to know that before spending more compute.

## 0. Plan Summary (technical)

- **Training.** Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct, LoRA r=32 / α=64 attn-projection targets, marker-only loss (`MarkerOnlyDataCollator(tail_tokens=0)`), 120 single-persona cells (3 arms × 2 personas × 5 seeds × 4 epochs).
- **Hyperparameters.** lr=**5e-6** (Source: `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` — the demonstrated clean window); LoRA r=32, α=64, dropout=0.05 (Source: #529); batch=4, grad_accum=4, max_len=2048, cosine warmup 0.05, bf16 (Source: #529); marker = ` ※` id 83399 (Source: `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md`); `marker_band_stop=False` (Source: #529 — the {1,2,3,5} epoch grid IS the experimental dial, so band-stop is deliberately disabled — this is the single-variable contract).
- **Baselines / controls.** Three encoding arms compared pairwise per persona: `system_plain`, `system_padded` (length-matched), `role` (custom chat-role header). The 1:1 contrastive-negative composition (300 pos + 150 other-persona neg + 150 default-assistant neg per cell) carries from #464/#529.
- **Loss surface.** Marker-only loss masked to the marker token + EOS at the end of a frozen base-model greedy response R; the response itself is zero-gradient (on-policy R principle).
- **Compute.** 4× H100 ZeRO-1 / TP=1, 120 cells sharded 4-way (sequential within shard). Estimated GPU-hours (total): 18
- **Evaluation.** Headline DV = teacher-forced `log P(' ※' | T_eval(q) + R_base)` at the post-R slot, wrong-persona encoding, trained − base; reported as paired d = log P_system − log P_role per seed with 95% per-seed-paired bootstrap (N=10,000, 5 seeds). Anchor = `i529_select_anchor.py` algorithm: pick lowest epoch per persona with wrong-slot mean ∈ [−10, −5] AND per-arm seed sd ≥ 0.5 AND own-slot argmax-emit ≥ 0.5. Success criterion: anchor selection returns non-degenerate (`partial_anchor=false`) AND headline d either clears zero ≥ 0.5 nat on at least one (persona, contrast) cell (H1) or straddles zero across all (persona, contrast) cells (H0). Threshold for "this worked" = anchor is non-degenerate on at least one persona.
- **Risks (top 2).** (i) Even at lr=5e-6, the implant may still saturate the wrong slot before E=1 at r=32 — kill criterion (every cell below band AND own-slot argmax-emit ≥ 0.96 from E=1) settles this and licenses the r=16 follow-up. (ii) Anchor selection may land on different epoch per persona, complicating the paired-d construction — algorithm already handles per-persona anchors via `selected_anchor_per_persona`.

### Thresholds

- **Anchor non-degeneracy:** `anchor_selection.json:degenerate == false` AND `selected_anchor_per_persona` populated for BOTH personas.
- **H1 (positive resolution):** at the selected anchor, the per-seed-paired bootstrap 95% CI for at least one of the 4 `headline.per_persona[<persona>][<contrast>]` cells (pirate × system_plain-vs-role, pirate × system_padded-vs-role, villain × system_plain-vs-role, villain × system_padded-vs-role) clears zero on the positive side AND the central paired d ≥ 0.5 nat.
- **H0 (null resolution):** at the selected anchor, the per-seed-paired bootstrap 95% CI straddles zero on ALL 4 `headline.per_persona[<persona>][<contrast>]` cells.
- **The persona-averaged read** (`headline.d_seed_plain` / `headline.d_seed_padded`, the inherited #529 shape) is a SECONDARY diagnostic — the analyzer reads it for cross-check but does NOT drive the H1/H0 verdict off it (a persona-averaged null can hide an opposite-signed per-persona pair; a persona-averaged positive can be a single-persona effect over-counted).
- **Either H1 or H0 is a successful outcome.** The experiment's value is in resolving the question.

## 11. Decision Rationale

All training hyperparameters are inherited from #529 except `lr`, which is the single manipulated variable. Per the "Inherit fast-path": a prior issue's clean-result validating a value for this exact model + data + recipe carries the source. The grouped recipe block:

**Inherited verbatim from #529 (`Source: #529`):**

- LoRA r=32, α=64, dropout=0.05, attn-projection targets. *Why:* the kill criterion's value depends on testing the rank-vs-lr hypothesis at r=32 — changing rank simultaneously would confound the LR result with a rank effect.
- Batch=4, grad_accum=4, max_len=2048, cosine warmup 0.05, bf16. *Why:* matches #529's measured budget and the parent's data shape; max_len=2048 is the `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` ≥2× longest trained completion default.
- Seeds {42, 137, 1337, 7, 21}. *Why:* 5-seed paired bootstrap floor for the d-CI; matches #529's seed set.
- Epochs grid {1, 2, 3, 5}. *Why:* the experimental dial — the single anchor-selection algorithm needs a trajectory to read across.
- `marker_band_stop=False`. *Why:* the {1,2,3,5}-epoch grid IS the experimental dial; band-stop would short-circuit the grid. This is a deliberate recipe deviation from `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md`'s band-stop default, and is the single-variable contract with #529. *Alternatives considered:* `marker_band_stop=True` would auto-stop on the [5, 12] nat source-band — but the experiment IS measuring what each epoch lands on, so band-stop would defeat the design.
- Marker ` ※` id 83399, marker-only loss with `tail_tokens=0`. *Source:* `.claude/rules/marker-leakage-measurement.md` (the canonical default) + #529 (inherited). *Alternatives considered:* whole-completion loss (ruled out by the recipe — it trains R and breaks the on-policy-R principle); bare `※` id 63680 (wrong token).
- Contrastive-negative composition: 300 pos + 150 other-persona neg + 150 default-assistant neg per cell, 1:1 ratio. *Source:* `.claude/rules/contrastive-negatives.md` + #529 (inherited). *Alternatives considered:* positive-only (excluded by the rule — leaks to P≈1 everywhere); other-persona neg only (excluded — the default-assistant negative is the highest-value single negative, drops leak-to-default 2-3 orders of magnitude per #464).

**The single changed value:**

- **`lr = 5e-6`.** *What:* AdamW learning rate dropped from #529's 1e-5 to 5e-6. *Why:* `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` names lr ≤ 5e-6 as the only demonstrated clean window for marker-less single-persona implants and frames LR as the over/under-training dial: "Marker-only at lr ≥1e-4 collapses into an unconditional ` ※`-repeater… Buy strength through epochs at low LR (≤5e-6), never through LR." Worked examples: #329 at 5e-6 × 20 epochs (source 99.6% / bystander 11.7%), #478 at 5e-6 (clean sub-emission log-prob gradient). *Source:* `.claude/rules/marker-training-recipe.md` (rule) + #329, #478 (validated worked examples). *Alternatives considered:* (a) lr=1e-5 (the #529 value, demonstrated to saturate the wrong slot before E=1); (b) lr=1e-6 (would likely under-train at the {1,2,3,5} epoch grid; the recipe rule's "≤5e-6" is a soft ceiling, not a floor); (c) keep lr=1e-5 and drop r to 16 (the alternative #529 follow-up — chosen second per the body's single-variable design; this run isolates the lr effect at r=32 first).
