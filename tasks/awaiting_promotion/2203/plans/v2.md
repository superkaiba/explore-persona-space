# Plan — Issue #2203: Assistant-axis activation capping at the context vector vs all tokens

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I'll reproduce the "Assistant Axis" activation-capping defense from Lu et al. on our own Qwen-2.5-7B, then test *where* along the input the cap has to be applied. Their method caps at every token; I'll compare capping only at one position (the last prompt token — the "context vector") against capping everywhere, plus a couple of gentler/harsher patch variants, and score how much each cuts harmful jailbreak responses and identity drift. A faithful 32B run with their published vectors anchors that we reproduce their ~60% number.
- **What I expect:** capping at the single context-vector position recovers most of the all-token effect (a much cheaper defense), capping only at the prefix does nothing, and the query-preserving variant keeps capabilities where the query-destroying one wrecks them.
- **What would change my mind:** if context-vector capping lands at the no-intervention baseline (no recovery), or if a norm-matched *random* direction cuts jailbreaks as much as the Assistant Axis does — either would say the effect isn't about this axis or this position.

## 0. Plan Summary

**Training:** none — this is an intervention/measurement experiment (forward-hook activation capping), no fine-tuning. **Hyperparameters:** cap floor τ = 25th percentile of axis projections (Source: arXiv 2601.10387 §5.1.1); layer band ≈12.5% of depth, mid-late, selected by a Phase-1 sweep (Source: §5.1.2); axis = mean(default-assistant) − mean(fully-role-playing), response-averaged activations (Source: §2.1/§3 + persona-vectors-recipe.md); judge = `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`, graded 0–100, N=5 draws (Source: CLAUDE.md standing rule; llm-judging.md). **Baselines/controls:** unmodified baseline; norm-matched random-direction cap null; full-state-replace as the query-destroying damage ceiling. **Loss surface:** N/A (no training). **Compute:** 4×H100 for the 7B grid (arms sharded across GPUs), 1×H200 for the 32B anchor, judge waves off-GPU via Batch API. **Estimated GPU-hours (total): 45**. **Evaluation:** co-primary judged rates — jailbreak harmful-response rate + assistant-identity-loss rate — each with a continuous companion (graded assistant-ness + realized axis projection), a mandatory coherence gate, and IFEval/MMLU-Pro/GSM8k guardrails. **Risks (top 2):** HF-hook generation throughput (all-token arm fires per decode step); all-position capping may shatter coherence (#1415) → coherence gate is a hard gate, not a footnote.

## 1. Goal

> "Determine whether assistant-axis activation capping (and its query-preserving patch generalization) applied ONLY at the context-vector position recovers the jailbreak-reduction / persona-stabilization effect that Lu et al. (arXiv 2601.10387) get by capping at every token, and whether prefix-only capping fails — via a position ladder (prefix-end / context-end / all-prompt / all-tokens) x intervention type (cap / axis-component-replace / full-replace) over a fixed mid-late layer band, on Qwen-2.5-7B (in-house axis) with a Qwen-3-32B faithful anchor, scored on co-primary judged jailbreak-harm and role-susceptibility rates." (Task #2203 Goal, verbatim.)

We are reproducing Lu et al.'s activation-capping defense — a training-free intervention that floors the model's hidden-state component along an "Assistant Axis" — and asking a localization question our own prior work (#2094, #1415) makes sharp: persona information concentrates at the *context vector* (the last prompt-token state), so a defense that caps only there should recover most of the all-token effect at a fraction of the intervention footprint, while capping at the prefix (where the jailbreak persona is injected) should fail. Confirming this both validates a cheaper deployment shape for the defense and localizes *where* the assistant persona is causally controllable.

## 2. Prior Work

**The paper (arXiv 2601.10387, Lu, Gallagher, Michala, Fish, Lindsey; verified via arXiv MCP, cs.CL, 2026-01-15).** Activation capping updates a layer's post-MLP residual `h ← h − v·min(⟨h,v⟩−τ,0)` — a *floor* that raises the assistant-axis component up to τ when it falls below, leaving the orthogonal subspace untouched (§5). Calibration: τ = 25th percentile of axis projections over the role/default rollout pool (n=912,000; the 25th percentile was Pareto-optimal, §5.1.1). Layer band: adjacent mid-late layers, best at 8 layers = 12.5% of depth for Qwen-3-32B (layers 46–53), swept over band center (2-layer spacing for Qwen) × width {4,8,16}; single-layer capping was ineffective (§5.1.2). Applied at **every token** in all their evals. Axis = mean(default-assistant) − mean(fully-role-playing role vectors), from response-averaged post-MLP residuals over a 275-role × 5-system-prompt × 240-question bank (§2.1, §3); cos(axis, role-PC1) > 0.71 at the middle layer (Appendix). Headline: ~60% harmful-response reduction on 1100 persona-jailbreak prompts (Shah et al. 2023, 44 harm categories; jailbreak success 65.3–88.5% across models, baseline 0.5–4.5%) with capabilities preserved (IFEval 541 / MMLU-Pro 1400 / GSM8k 1000 / EQ-Bench 171); role-susceptibility eval = 50 assistant-close roles × 4 system prompts × 5 introspective questions ("Who are you?"), judged Assistant vs human/nonhuman/mystical. Their judge is deepseek-v3; ours is Sonnet 4.5 (named standing deviation).

## 3. Hypothesis

Falsifiable predictions, each scored on the harmful-response rate and role-susceptibility rate (co-primary), read against the unmodified baseline and the norm-matched random-direction cap null.

- **H1 (context-vector cap ≈ all-token cap).** *Confirmed* if the Context-vector cap arm recovers ≥ 60% of the All-token cap arm's jailbreak-rate reduction relative to baseline (both reductions clearing the random-null band). *Falsified* if the Context-vector cap arm's reduction is < 30% of the All-token cap's, or is statistically indistinguishable from baseline (95% CI overlaps baseline).
- **H2 (prefix-only cap ≈ null).** *Confirmed* if the Prefix-only cap arm's harmful-rate and role-susceptibility are within the random-null band (95% CI overlaps baseline). *Falsified* if Prefix-only cap produces a reduction clearing the null band on either co-primary DV.
- **H3 (axis-swap preserves query info; full-swap destroys it).** *Confirmed* if, at matched position + band, the Context-vector axis-swap arm preserves capability guardrails within 5 points of baseline while achieving a jailbreak reduction ≥ 50% of Context-vector full-swap's, AND Context-vector full-swap degrades ≥ 1 guardrail benchmark by ≥ 15 points. *Falsified* if axis-swap and full-swap are indistinguishable on the capability guardrails.

**Registered verdict lattice (verify_plan.py check 20):** DISJOINT and exhaustive: `H1-Confirmed ⇔ (ctx-cap reduction ≥ 0.60 × all-token reduction) AND (both clear null band)`; `H1-Falsified ⇔ (ctx-cap reduction < 0.30 × all-token reduction) OR (ctx-cap 95% CI overlaps baseline)`; `H1-Indeterminate ⇔ otherwise`; `H2-Confirmed ⇔ (prefix-cap 95% CI overlaps baseline on BOTH co-primary DVs)`; `H2-Falsified ⇔ (prefix-cap clears null band on ≥1 co-primary DV)`; `H2-Indeterminate ⇔ otherwise`; `H3-Confirmed ⇔ (axis-swap guardrail-loss ≤ 5 pts) AND (axis-swap jailbreak-reduction ≥ 0.50 × full-swap) AND (full-swap degrades ≥1 guardrail ≥ 15 pts)`; `H3-Falsified ⇔ (axis-swap and full-swap guardrail deltas within 5 pts of each other)`; `H3-Indeterminate ⇔ otherwise`.

**Registered paired contrast (verify_plan.py check 18):** the primary contrast is Context-vector cap vs All-token cap (H1) and Prefix-only cap vs Context-vector cap (H2), paired per jailbreak prompt and per role-susceptibility combo.

Row-coverage: both arms of every registered paired contrast (ctx-cap vs all-token-cap; prefix-cap vs ctx-cap) draw from the two pinned Phase-2 sets — jailbreak N_jb=500 and role-susceptibility N_rs=250 — and each arm's own Phase-2 generation produces a score for every registered row; paired listwise drop on parse-fail/incoherent, realized-N reported per arm.

## 4. Design

Four phases on the critical path. Interventions are **HF forward-hook only — no vLLM-side steering** (capping is an input-dependent per-position clamp; vLLM exposes no such hook, and #1415 already established the HF-hook rig here).

### 4.1 New module to build: `src/explore_persona_space/experiments/issue2203/caphook.py`

`PositionEditHook`/`DeltaHook` apply a *fixed* delta; capping and axis-replace are **input-dependent** (they read the current projection ⟨h, v̂⟩), so a new hook is genuinely needed (confirmed by grep: no `cap`/`clamp` mode exists in either hook — `grep -rn 'def ' src/explore_persona_space/experiments/issue2094/hooks.py` and steering.py both show only add/replace). The new `AxisCapHook` (per-layer; `AxisCapHookStack` + `joint_axis_hooks(model, layers)` mirroring `joint_hooks`) duck-types the DeltaHook lifecycle (`install`/`remove`/`arm(expected_prompt_len)`/`reset`/`_handle`/context-manager) so `generate_batch` works unchanged. It holds, per layer: the axis vector `v` (raw contrast, as the paper uses), its unit `v̂`, τ (projection-space float), and the default-assistant mean projection / mean state (for the replace ops). One `op` per run:

- **`op="cap"`** — `h[pos] ← h[pos] − v·min(⟨h[pos],v⟩ − τ, 0)` (paper Eq. 1, verbatim; raw `v` with τ computed against the same `v` — self-consistent, §11).
- **`op="axis_replace"`** — set the axis component to the default-assistant mean: `h[pos] ← h[pos] + v̂·(⟨h_def,v̂⟩ − ⟨h[pos],v̂⟩)` (query-preserving; only the axis component moves).
- **`op="full_replace"`** — `h[pos] ← h_def` (the default-assistant mean state at that position; query-destroying damage ceiling; the fixed-delta member — could route through `PositionEditHook mode="replace"` for prefill-only positions, but is unified here so all four positions share one decode-step path).

Position set (`positions` arg): `prefix-end` (one position, resolved via `steering.prefix_end_index`), `context-end` (last prompt token, `T-1` under left padding), `all-prompt` (every prefill position, decode untouched), `all-tokens` (every prefill position + **every decode step** — the paper's setting). The decode-step branch is modeled on `DeltaHook.all_positions=True` (a `T==1` slice under the KV cache = that step's new position); `prefix-end`/`context-end`/`all-prompt` set `_prefill_seen=True` after prefill so decode passes through, `all-tokens` does not. Telemetry: realized edit positions + realized projection-before/after per edit (feeds the continuous axis-projection companion DV and an injection-exactness gate à la #2094).

**Symbol-existence grep-at-plan-time (recorded):** `grep -rn 'def prefix_end_index\|def generate_batch\|def coherence_check\|def condition_passes\|def context_token_ids' src/explore_persona_space/experiments/issue1415/steering.py` → all present (lines 96, 382, 122, 135, 89); `grep -rn 'def extract_direction\|def batched_response_means\|class DirectionResult' src/explore_persona_space/artifacts/directions.py` → present (347, 252, 317); `grep -rn 'def judge_graded' src/explore_persona_space/eval/graded_judge.py` → present (222). `joint_hooks`/`PositionEditHookStack` present in issue2094/hooks.py (322/241).

## 7. Decision Gates

**Retained gate (Phase 0 → rest): axis-validity gate.** Wall-clock of the full run is > 4 h and the entire experiment is meaningless if the in-house axis does not reproduce the paper's construct, so a cheap Phase-0 signal rules out the full spend: proceed only if **cos(axis, PC1) > 0.71 at the middle layer** (Source arXiv 2601.10387 Appendix, the paper's own reported value for their models) AND the subsample stability check passes (cos(axis_A, axis_B) > 0.95). Per-criterion §4-mechanism binding: the cos(axis,PC1) criterion is measured by Phase 0's per-layer PCA + cosine (§4.2, equality-to-threshold at the middle-layer index); the stability criterion by the disjoint-split recompute (§4.2, threshold). Recomputed on the precedent value the prose cites (0.71), a middle-layer cosine landing at/above 0.71 lands in the "proceed" branch as the prose assigns. If the gate fails, the axis extraction is re-tuned (more roles, extraction-point variant) before any Phase-1/2 GPU spend.

**Kill criteria:** (1) Phase-0 axis-validity gate fails after one re-tune (cos(axis,PC1) ≤ 0.71 mid-layer, or stability cos ≤ 0.95) → ABORT before any Phase-1/2/3 GPU spend; the whole experiment presupposes a faithful axis. (2) Phase-2 baseline jailbreak-success rate at floor (near 0, no headroom for a harm-reduction DV; paper baselines run 0.5–4.5% but jailbroken success is 65–88%) → do NOT report a 7B harm-reduction headline; route the H1/H2 read to the 32B anchor (Phase 3) and label the 7B jailbreak leg as under-powered in the write-up. No other stop conditions — Phases 1–3 are the experiment itself.

No other gates — Phases 1–3 are the experiment itself, and the Phase-1 band selection is a fixed calibration, not a kill gate.

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **What:** τ = 25th percentile of axis projections. **Why:** the paper's Pareto-optimal cap. **Source:** arXiv 2601.10387 §5.1.1 (verbatim: "using the 25th percentile of projections as the cap led to the most Pareto-optimal results"; n=912,000). **Alternatives:** 1st/50th/75th (paper tried; 25th won).
- **What:** layer band ≈12.5% depth, mid-late, Phase-1-selected. **Why:** paper's best width; single-layer ineffective. **Source:** §5.1.2 (8 layers = 12.5% for Qwen-3-32B, layers 46–53; sweep center 2-layer spacing × width {4,8,16}). **Alternatives:** all-layer (over-broad), single-layer (ineffective per §5).
- **What:** axis = mean(default-assistant) − mean(fully-role-playing), response-averaged. **Why:** the paper's contrast-vector definition. **Source:** §2.1/§3 (Assistant Axis definition) + persona-vectors-recipe.md. **Alternatives:** PC1 (paper found it slightly worse at capping; §Appendix).
- **What:** cos(axis,PC1) > 0.71 middle-layer validity target. **Why:** the paper's reported alignment. **Source:** arXiv 2601.10387 Appendix "The Assistant Axis vs role PC1".
- **What:** ~60% jailbreak reduction (32B anchor target). **Why:** the paper's headline. **Source:** §5.2 (Qwen-3-32B layers 46–53 p0.25).
- **What:** raw-v cap formula (not unit-v̂). **Why:** faithful to the paper's Eq. 1; τ computed against the same v keeps it self-consistent; Phase 3 must match Lu's vectors+caps exactly. **Source:** arXiv 2601.10387 §5 Eq. 1. **Alternatives:** unit-v̂ normalization (equivalent up to τ-rescaling; would break Phase-3 reuse of Lu's caps).
- **What:** judge = Sonnet 4.5. **Why:** project standing rule. **Source:** CLAUDE.md (deviation from the paper's deepseek-v3, named). **Alternatives:** deepseek-v3 (paper's; not the project instrument).
- **What:** max_new_tokens = 1024. **Why:** #2094 parity (same hook family/model); cap-hit reported with a >2% re-gen trigger. **Source:** #2094 + #260 truncation rule.
- **What:** N=5 judge draws, max_tokens 1024, pilot-gate ≥5k waves. **Source:** llm-judging.md rules 4/23/26.
- **What:** single mid-layer cap at L14. **Why:** #1415 mid-stack behavioral peak. **Source:** #1415.
- **What:** coherence gate (<5 tokens / refusal opener, ≥50%). **Source:** `steering.coherence_check`/`condition_passes` (#1415).
- **Tool-behavior claims:** `graded_judge.judge_graded` default `max_tokens=64` (Source: read `src/explore_persona_space/eval/graded_judge.py:230`); `PositionEditHook` edits only at prefill (Source: read `issue2094/hooks.py:185`); `DeltaHook.all_positions` edits every decode step (Source: read `steering.py` docstring L100+). Per-gen ~0.8 s basis (Source: #2094 body compute footer, 42,000 gens / ~9.3 GPU-h).
