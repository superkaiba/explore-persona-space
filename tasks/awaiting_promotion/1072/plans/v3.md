# Plan — Issue #1072, amendment round `lowdim-token-subspace`: does a LOW-DIMENSIONAL token subspace rescue the falsified 1-D output-token-commitment account? (v1)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** the completed round showed the model's own-answer prediction advantage does NOT live along the single direction that picks the next word (that direction carries only 2.4% of the gap at layer 26). The obvious comeback is "commitment isn't one direction — it's a small bundle: the handful of words the model is choosing between, or the next few words it's about to write." I test exactly that: I redo the same decomposition with three small bundles of word-directions — the model's top-8 candidate next words, its top-32, and the 8 words it actually wrote next — at layers 14 and 26, reusing every frozen ingredient from the finished run plus one light GPU pass to read off the candidate words (the only thing not already stored).
- **What I expect:** the bundles stay small players. The finished run's trend (the direction's gap-enrichment FALLS with depth, 27× down to 3.9×) points against token commitment, so I expect even the 32-direction bundle to carry well under ~15% of the gap and the decision statistic D to stay negative for all three bundles — which upgrades the headline from "not one direction" to "not any small token bundle."
- **What would change my mind:** if any bundle's D comes out positive with its confidence interval clear of zero (after correcting for testing three bundles), commitment is real but multi-candidate/multi-step — the parent headline gets qualified, and the next round measures how the gap share grows with bundle size instead.

## 3. Hypothesis

Notation extends the completed run's §3: for basis b with ids S_b(t) at answer position t, let U_b(t) ∈ R^{3584×k_eff} be an orthonormal basis of span{γ ⊙ W_U[y] : y ∈ S_b(t)}, P_b(t) = U_b U_bᵀ, z_par = P_b z_t, z_perp = z_t − z_par. Component contributions C_par(k), C_perp(k), C_cross(k) are defined exactly as in the parent round (per-context channels against the full-target SS_tot; additive by linearity of ridge at fixed λ: R²_full = C_par + C_perp + C_cross exactly, Y_perp derived as full − par inside `run_component_cell`). Gap decomposition ΔC_c(k) = C_c^own − C_c^ext_plain on the matched population; **D_k ≡ ΔC_par(k) − ΔC_perp(k)** at layer 26 (c-leg, mean-remainder target), per basis cell k ∈ {top-8, top-32, lookahead-8}.

- **H1 (primary, per basis cell):** D_k > 0 — the k-dim token subspace out-carries the (3584−k)-dim complement. The a-priori branch asymmetry the parent flagged SHRINKS with k: C_par(k) is bounded near the parallel variance share w_par(k), and w_par(1) ≈ 0.6% at L26 (enrichment 3.9 at share 2.4%) while w_par(8) is expected at ~1–3% and w_par(32) at ~3–10% (sub-additive — candidate unembeddings are correlated; reported empirically per basis × layer as the power check). At k = 32 the confirmation branch is structurally reachable, unlike the parent's 1-D branch.
- **H1-expectation (registered prediction, non-verdict):** the falling-enrichment trend (27.3× → 3.9× for 1-D) predicts NO rescue — parallel gap share S_par(k) stays under ~15% at layer 26 for every basis, and every D_k stays negative.
- **H2 (secondary, non-verdict-bearing):** per-basis closure at L26 — the parallel component's closure fraction under the 16-token prefix stays below the orthogonal component's (the parent's 8.8% vs 39.5% pattern persists at k > 1), and the unclosed residual remains orthogonal-dominated per basis.
- **H3 (descriptive):** S_par(k) and enrichment(k) = S_par(k)/w_par(k) as functions of k ∈ {1 (parent reference), 8, 32, look-8} at both layers — the dimensionality-of-commitment profile.

Per-basis verdict rule (applied independently to each of the 3 registered basis cells; the sign-flip rng-1 null and bootstrap rng-0 CIs are the parent's recipe; zero-exclusion claims are gated by Holm step-down at α = 0.05 over the 3-cell family, evaluated on the two-sided sign-flip p-values, with plain 95% CIs always reported):

### 4.1 Basis construction (the one new piece of math)

Pinned model facts (AutoConfig-verified in the completed run; load-time asserts inherited): untied lm_head (152064 × 3584), RMSNorm final norm, no lm_head bias. `U_dir = lm_head.weight.float() * norm.weight.float()[None, :]` — γ-folding exact for directions under RMSNorm (plan v2 §4.1, inherited verbatim).

- **Basis (a) — top-k candidates, k ∈ {8, 32}:** at each answer position p, the model's next-token distribution is `logits_p = W_U · RMSNorm(h_p^{L28})` under the arm's own teacher-forced context. S_top32(t) = the 32 highest-logit token ids at that position, in logit-rank order (distinct by construction of topk). One **unpivoted** QR of A = U_dir[S_top32]ᵀ ∈ R^{3584×32} yields Q whose leading j columns span the leading j input columns exactly (Householder QR nesting), so **Q[:, :8] is the top-8 basis and Q[:, :32] the top-32 basis from ONE factorization**. These ids are NOT in any store — the light logits pass (Phase B) produces them. Note the candidate basis is model-intrinsic: for external arms it captures what the MODEL would emit next given the external answer-so-far, not what the external text says — exactly the "output commitment" construct.
- **Basis (b) — realized lookahead-8:** S_look8(t) = the realized next 8 token ids {full_ids[p+1..p+8]} ∩ span-valid, i.e. `next_flat[off+t .. off+min(t+8, span_v)]` from the stored `next_ids_{arm}.npz` — derivable OFFLINE (no forward pass for the ids). Duplicated ids within a window are DEDUPED order-preserving (span unchanged); effective k ≤ 8 recorded per position; windows truncate near span end (the final `_nx`-excluded position has none, as in the parent).
- **Rank guard (g7):** after dedupe, QR of the (3584 × m) column stack; require min_i |R_ii|/‖A_i‖ > 1e-4, else recompute that position's basis by SVD with rank cutoff at the same relative tolerance (span-preserving) and count it; abort if the rank-reduced fraction exceeds 1% in any arm × basis (a systematic degeneracy would mean an ids bug, not numerics). Tolerance is fp32-QR-scale, `ungrounded — needs smoke-test` (§11); the smoke asserts 0 reductions on real top-32 ids and a controlled reduction on a synthetic duplicate-column case.
- **Projection + additivity:** z_par = U_b(U_bᵀ z) in fp32; Y_perp is derived (full − par) inside `run_component_cell`, so R²_full = C_par + C_perp + C_cross stays EXACT per basis (same algebra as 1-D — linearity of ridge at fixed λ; C_cross a reported channel, never an abort). Bases are LAYER-INDEPENDENT (the unembedding is shared), so each position's Q is computed once and applied to both layers' activations.
- **Coverage read (descriptive, registered):** per arm, the fraction of positions whose realized next token ∈ top-8 / top-32 — expected < 1 by design (T = 1.0 sampling for own; external text moreso) — plus the |S_look8 ∩ S_top32| overlap distribution. Interprets which basis family "contains" the realized direction where.
