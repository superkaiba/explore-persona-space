## 3. Hypothesis

- **H1 (atlas scaling).** The ridge late-layer (transitions ~17-25) held-out R² on the FIXED 500-context test set RISES materially with n beyond 4000 and PLATEAUS by n≤100k. **Confirm:** the data-limited-band mean R²(100k) − R²(4000) ≥ +0.05 absolute AND the n=50k→100k marginal gain falls below 20% of R²(100k) (plateau reached). **Falsify:** R²(100k) ≈ R²(4000) within subsample noise (already saturated at 4000 — contradicts the trigger), OR still climbing >20% per doubling at 100k (25× is still not enough data). Directional prior: ridge improves + plateaus; MLP stays flat (already saturated at 4000).
- **H2 (transport scaling).** The largest-n ridge maps improve Stage-1 transported-projection performance vs n=4000 — MORE matched-information winning cells (> 20/136) and/or higher retention. **Confirm:** win-count(100k) > 20 with the per-cell paired-delta CI (largest-n transported r − n=4000 transported r) excluding zero in the newly-winning data-limited cells. **Falsify (the likely null):** win-count and retention statistically indistinguishable from n=4000 (every paired-delta CI includes zero) — the matched-information ceiling is a DPI/architecture bound, not a fit-data bound.
- **Coupled directional prior (the interesting fork):** given the MLP is already flat and the raw-target ceiling cannot be exceeded by more fit data, the most likely joint outcome is **H1 holds, H2 flat** — better single-step maps do NOT translate to better trait transport, extending #841's "single-step fit quality does not predict rolled-out reliability" takeaway to "and more fit data doesn't either." An H2-confirm would be the surprising, mentor-relevant result (transport advantage IS data-limited).

## 7. Decision Gates

**No mid-run compute gates — short run (~4 GPU-h, <4h wall, well under the 4h/16-GPU-h gate bar; the parent's §7 trigger already fired to spawn this round).** The §7 content is TWO discrete correctness kill-gates (pre-full-run, cheap) + the pre-registered interpretation stop-reads.

### Kill / stop criteria

- **KILL-A (spot-gate, §4.1 step 2):** if the alignment + capture-equivalence spot-gate FAILS (a re-captured regenerated parent context does NOT match its stored `cx_last` row to rel 1e-3, on the fp32 path), STOP before the full 96k capture. First fall back to the parent's per-sequence batch-1 fp32 path and re-gate; if it still fails, the regeneration is misaligned (a non-zero keep-filter drop the N=5000 check missed, or a corpus/order drift) → do NOT spend the capture; diagnose (post `epm:failure`).
- **KILL-B (ridge parity gate, §4.2):** if the primal ridge at n=4000 does NOT reproduce the parent's stored ridge R² within |ΔR²|<0.01 after the λ-pin retry, STOP — the primal solver diverges from the parent's dual, so the scaling curve is not anchored and cannot be trusted. Diagnose (post `epm:failure`) rather than reporting an unanchored curve.
- **STOP-C (one-step retention floor, inherited from parent §7):** if the largest-n one-step (k=1) predicted states retain <~50% of ceiling r for ALL classes AND ALL traits, skip the multi-hop retention grid and report the atlas + one-step table (a complete negative). Single, jointly-satisfiable floor check; grounded in the parent's ceilings (0.46-0.62).

### Interpretation stop-reads (what folds into the clean-result either way)

**(a) Late-layer affine Δ-R² (DV1)** — on the FIXED test set at the data-limited transitions (17-25), ridge:
- **IMPROVED + plateaued:** mean R²(100k) − R²(4000) ≥ +0.05 absolute AND the n=50k→100k marginal gain < 20% of R²(100k). → "late-layer map quality WAS data-limited at n=4000 and closes with ≤25× more data" (removes the "scale follow-up pending" hedge from Takeaway 1; report the plateau n).
- **STILL CLIMBING:** the n=50k→100k gain still exceeds 20% → "data-limited even at 25×; the affine map is not saturable within reach" (a bounded negative — a further-scale follow-up would be a fresh proposal).
- **FLAT:** R²(100k) ≈ R²(4000) within subsample noise → contradicts the parent's trigger read; report as a correction (the n=2000→4000 gain was a subsample-noise artifact near the n/d≈1.1 interpolation threshold, consistent with the parent's own non-monotonicity caveat).
- **DEGRADED:** R²(100k) < R²(4000) → distribution shift or fit pathology; investigate before reporting.

**(b) Stage-1 transported-projection performance (DV2/DV3)** — largest-n vs parent n=4000, 20/136 win-count baseline:
- **IMPROVED:** win-count(100k) > 20 with per-cell paired-delta CIs excluding zero in the data-limited cells, AND/OR retention rises toward the ceiling → "the transport advantage IS data-limited" (the surprising, mentor-relevant outcome; supports "unlimited unsupervised data helps").
- **FLAT (likely):** win-count + retention statistically indistinguishable from n=4000 (paired-delta CIs include zero) → "better single-step maps from more data do NOT buy better trait transport — the matched-information ceiling is a DPI/architecture bound" (extends #841's Takeaway 6).
- **DEGRADED:** fewer wins / lower retention at larger n — cross-check the transport-fidelity diagnostic: if eval-context reconstruction fidelity DROPPED, attribute to LMSYS-overfit map-OOD (a real, reportable transfer finding).

**Either way, both DV1 and DV2/DV3 fold into #841's SAME clean-result** (cheap-band same-issue follow-up): Takeaway 1 gains the settled scaling verdict, Takeaway 2 gains whether more data moves the 20/136 matched-information result, and the extended scaling figure + largest-n Stage-1 bars are added under `## Results`.
