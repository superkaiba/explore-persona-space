# Plan — Issue #558: Is the surviving residual doctor-specific or persona-general?

Persona-panel read on the existing 12 Phase-2 adapters from #543. Eval-only diagnostic; no training.

---

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The previous experiment found that after benign medical fine-tuning wiped out a trigger-conditioned marker rule, a faint trace of the rule survived inside the model — and that trace was weaker when the model was prompted as a doctor. I'll re-probe the same 12 fine-tuned models with three more persona prompts (software engineer, French person, police officer), matching the doctor probe's exact shape, to find out why the doctor prompt is special.
- **What I expect:** I'm betting the doctor dip comes from content overlap with the medical fine-tuning data. If that's right, the three non-medical personas should look like the plain assistant, and the doctor stays the only persona that suppresses the trace.
- **What would change my mind:** If every persona suppresses the trace — including the police officer, which the model never saw during training — then the doctor isn't special at all: any role-play framing pushes the residue down, and the domain-overlap story is dead.

## 3. Hypothesis

Primary bet (the task body's): **domain overlap.** Doctor-persona contexts resemble the medical SFT distribution where EOS was strengthened at the slot; non-medical personas will cluster with the trigger re-read.

Predicted signatures per account, on the per-adapter paired ΔEOS-margin vs the within-run trigger re-read (D̄ = 12-adapter mean; "dip" / "no dip" defined in §7):

| Account | Software engineer + key | French person + key | Police officer + key | Doctor re-read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Domain overlap** | no dip | no dip | no dip | dip (only one) |
| **(b) Trained-negative residue** | dip | dip | no dip | dip |
| **(c) Persona-general** | dip | dip | dip | dip |

- Falsification of (a) (the body's criterion, operationalized same-subset in §7): non-medical personas — trained AND untrained alike — dip comparably to doctor (mean paired ΔEOS-margin ≤ T_dip, the doctor-scaled threshold) → domain-overlap is dead, residual is persona-general.
- Confirmation of (a): all three new cells classify **no dip** under §7 — i.e. they cluster with the matched no-dip anchor (no_trigger[0:50] − trigger[0:50] = +0.84 [−0.26, +2.55]), NOT merely "mean > −1.0" — while the doctor re-read reproduces its dip (D_doc ≤ −1.0; same-subset expectation ≈ −2.6).
- Mixed pattern (swe + french dip, police doesn't) → (b): the dip is Phase-1 negative-training residue, not domain.
- Anti-trained pattern (police dips, swe/french don't) is unpredicted by all three accounts → report as unclassified surprise, no forced label.
- Quantitative calibration (SAME-SUBSET, see §2): the doctor dip to beat is **−2.598** mean (range [−4.215, −1.208]) — NOT the [0:200]-paired −3.45, which is mis-grounded for this run's paired surface (trigger[0:50] sits −0.855 below trigger[0:200]); the no-dip band is anchored by matched no_trigger at +0.84 [−0.26, +2.55]; the zone between T_dip and −1.0 is reported as a graded partial effect, pre-registered as such.
- Space rule (pre-registered): the EOS margin is the SOLE classification space. Log-prob (the PRIMARY behavioral DV) is the concordance check: a dip label additionally requires log-prob sign agreement ≥9/12 adapters AND |mean Δlog-prob| ≥ 0.4 nats (the parent doctor's weakest-adapter log-prob dip is −0.396; its mean is −1.171, 12/12 negative). A cell that dips on EOS-margin but fails log-prob concordance is reported as **space-discordant** (LOW confidence, no clean account assignment from that cell alone) — and its Δz_marker vs Δz_eos decomposition is reported, since an EOS-side-only dip (z_eos strengthening without marker-mass drain) is itself the mechanism account (a) predicts.

## 7. Decision Gates

**No mid-run gates — short run (<1 h wall) over a pre-verified rig.** The only gate is the launch-validity smoke gate (§4.2 step 3): the first cell (`r50_seed42`) must PASS the doctor-cell anchor check (both `delta_logp_mean` and `delta_eos_margin_mean` within ±1.0 nat of the parent's recorded 6.986 / 3.989) before the other 11 adapters launch. Grounding: the adapter-application assert in marker-leakage-measurement.md (~1 nat tolerance; an unapplied adapter reads ~7 nats off — incident #534). The gate set is trivially jointly satisfiable (one gate, one cell, sign-free tolerance band).

**Registered classification rule** (analysis-time, not a run gate — applied per panel cell on the 12-adapter paired ΔEOS-margin D̄ with sign count n_neg/12; the EOS margin is the SOLE classification space per the §3 space rule). Let **D_doc** = the within-run doctor re-read's 12-adapter mean paired ΔEOS-margin (same-subset expectation ≈ −2.6) and **T_dip = min(0.6·D_doc, −1.0)** (the doctor-scaled, instrument-matched dip threshold; with D_doc ≈ −2.6, T_dip ≈ −1.56; the `min` floor guarantees a dip is never declared above −1.0 even if D_doc is weak). Rules are ORDERED (first match wins) and jointly EXHAUSTIVE over (D̄, n_neg):

1. **Dip (doctor-comparable):** D̄ ≤ T_dip AND n_neg ≥ 10/12. A dip label carries full confidence only if the log-prob concordance check passes (≥9/12 log-prob negative AND |mean Δlog-prob| ≥ 0.4 nats); otherwise it is reported as **space-discordant dip** (LOW confidence, Δz_marker/Δz_eos decomposition reported, no clean account assignment from that cell alone).
2. **Heterogeneous dip candidate (unclassified):** D̄ ≤ T_dip AND n_neg < 10/12 — registered explicitly; reported descriptively (per-adapter points in the hero figure), NO account assignment. The account table treats this cell as missing, and any overall account claim that depends on it is reported as unresolved.
3. **Graded/partial:** (T_dip < D̄ ≤ −1.0) OR (D̄ > −1.0 AND n_neg ≥ 8/12) — a real-but-sub-doctor partial effect (the second disjunct catches a sign-consistent small shift, e.g. 11/12 negative at −0.8, which is NOT clean no-dip evidence). Reported with distances to both within-run anchors (D_doc and the matched no-dip anchor +0.84), mapped to the NEAREST account with confidence LOW, never forced into a clean label.
4. **No dip:** D̄ > −1.0 AND n_neg ≤ 7/12 — clusters with the matched no-dip anchor (no_trigger[0:50] − trigger[0:50] = +0.84 [−0.26, +2.55], 2/12 negative). This — not merely "mean > −1.0" — is what account-(a) confirmation requires of the three new cells.

Account assignment per the §3 signature table, keyed on the {dip, no dip} labels; any cell labeled graded/heterogeneous/space-discordant degrades the corresponding account claim to graded/LOW. Doctor re-read failing to reproduce a dip (D_doc > −1.0) = calibration failure: no classification is issued; the result is reported as a within-instrument non-replication of the parent's doctor dip (itself informative).

**Kill criteria:**
- Smoke anchor FAIL after one diagnostic retry (incl. the PEFT cross-check of adapter application per #492) → do not launch the sweep; `epm:failure` (failure_class: code) and fix before any GPU spend beyond the smoke cell.
- Any adapter failing to resolve from the Hub at launch (contradicting the clarifier's verification) → stop, re-verify with `list_repo_files`, do not improvise alternative checkpoints.
