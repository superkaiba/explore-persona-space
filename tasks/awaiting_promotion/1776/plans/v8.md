## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The last round asked whether two "maps" of the model's internals could steer one prompt's answer toward another prompt's answer — both failed. That leaves two readings: the maps are bad, or the internal position they write to just doesn't control the answer. So this round skips the maps entirely: for the same 133 prompt pairs (A, B), I copy the model's own internal state from prompt B directly into prompt A at that position and count, with the same judge-free word-ranking metric, how much of B's distinctive answer content shows up in A's answers.
- **What I expect:** Still nothing. I pre-register that the transplant stays at chance — the position is a readout of the answer, not a control knob for it — which would mean both maps failed for a structural reason, not a map-quality reason.
- **What would change my mind:** If the direct transplant reliably pulls B's content into A's answers (above chance and above last round's random-nudge control, toward the 0.047 attainable anchor), the position IS causally potent — the failure was specifically in the linear operators, and the issue's correlational headline gains a nonlinearity qualifier.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H-PATCH (pre-registered, from parent evidence):** the patch arm's B-content acquisition stays at the shuffled-target null and does not clear the swap round's random arm — the layer-14 last-context-token slot is a readout correlate, not a causal bottleneck for answer content; both operator failures are thereby structural. Grounds: the swap round's Neither-executes at CI resolution ~0.002 MRR; prediction cosines ≤ 0.05 at the operating dose (dose round); J_last's near-zero held-out R² on generations.

Per-arm execution predicate (the v7 E() shape adapted to one arm): **E(patch) is TRUE** iff (a) the pair-clustered bootstrap 95% CI of ΔMRR(patch) = mean over pairs of [MRR_B(patch-steered) − per-cell shuffled-target null mean] lies strictly above 0, AND (b) mean MRR_B(patch) exceeds the pair-clustered bootstrap 97.5% upper bound of MRR_B(random arm) recomputed from the swap round's committed per-cell values (same 133 pairs, same targets); else FALSE. recall@50 reported as companion under the identical predicate shape; MRR carries the verdict.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Patch-executes ⇔ E(patch); Patch-null ⇔ otherwise.

Interpretation registered in advance: **Patch-null** hardens the parent headline — the slot itself fails the causal-sufficiency bar, so Neither-executes is structural, not operator-quality; **Patch-executes** (falsification, per the scope: CI above null AND above the random arm, toward the 0.047 attainable anchor) localizes the operator failure to linearity/operator construction and adds the nonlinearity qualifier to Takeaways. ## 7. Decision Gates + Success/Kill criteria

**Gates (engineering, pre-registered; per-criterion §4-mechanism binding inline):**

1. **G-PATCH-PARITY (smoke, BLOCKS — rc=8):** measured by the pilot's replace-mode probe (§4 mechanism: `parity_probe` extended for `replace=True`): `n_edits == 1` per prefill-mode generate batch (count equality, DeltaHook counter), and a 2-row hooked-vs-unhooked re-forward shows position T−1 EQUAL to each row's v14_last(B) elementwise (fp32 tolerance 1e-4, per-row distinct values) with all other positions untouched (max abs dev == 0.0). Failure = slot/batching/replace defect; HALT until fixed. Binds at smoke.
2. **G-CAPTURE-CONSISTENCY (build, BLOCKS — rc=9-class):** measured by the build's WildChat cross-check (§4 mechanism: recomputed v14_last vs the stored chunk `cx_last` at layer 14, row-matched by `ci`): cosine median ≥ 0.99 over the WildChat-leg patch inputs (threshold: the two reads are the same quantity up to bf16 nondeterminism — anything materially below 1 indicates rendering/position drift). 3. **G-PILOT (pilot, rc=7):** measured 1-pair wall through the production entrypoint at production shape; projected full wall > 2× the §9 budget ⇒ report JSON + abort.

(No G-DOSE-DEGENERATE analog — the patch delivers the full target state by construction, so there is no operator-claimed fraction to floor-check. No G-METRIC-SANITY re-run — targets are byte-reused; the committed ceiling 0.836 ≥ 0.8 is cited.)
