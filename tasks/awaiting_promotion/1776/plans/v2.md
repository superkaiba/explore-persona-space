# Plan — #1776: Averaged causal Jacobian J_{C→A} vs fitted ridge map M + J-space workspace mediation (Qwen-2.5-7B)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** We already have a learned map that predicts, from the model's internal state at the end of a prompt, what its internal state will look like while it answers. I will now measure the model's *actual* cause-and-effect version of that map (by nudging the prompt-side state and tracking what provably flows through to the answer), and check three things: how much of the learned map's predictive power is real cause-and-effect, which of the two maps better predicts what happens when we actively steer the model during generation, and whether this prompt→answer channel goes through the small "reportable workspace" Anthropic recently found inside language models.
- **What I expect:** The learned map will stay the better pure predictor on familiar data, but the cause-and-effect map will predict steering outcomes and new-data transfer better; and the channel will overlap the reportable workspace more than chance, without living entirely inside it.
- **What would change my mind:** If the cause-and-effect map recovers almost none of the learned map's predictive power, the learned map is mostly reading correlations, not a real channel — that would make me stop treating it as a mechanism. If steered answer changes land mostly *outside* the workspace, the channel moves behavior in ways the model cannot report on, which matters for safety monitoring.

## 1. Goal

"Compute the averaged causal Jacobian J_{C→A} on the #779 corpus; measure what fraction of the fitted ridge map M's predictive power is causal; test which map predicts steered-generation interventions and off-distribution transfer; and test whether the context→answer channel reads from / writes into the J-space verbalizable workspace (Anthropic 2026), all phases unconditional." (Task #1776 Goal, verbatim.)

One paragraph: #779 established that a fitted ridge map M: c_last(x) → v(x) predicts the answer-side mean-activation profile at held-out R² ≈ 0.75–0.76 (963k-row linear fit, layer 19), but a fitted map conflates causal signal flow with corpus correlations. The Anthropic 2026 "Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace" paper (transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace) supplies both the estimator family (averaged input-output Jacobians, the J-lens) and a privileged subspace hypothesis (the J-space workspace: sparse nonnegative combinations of J-lens vectors, ≤25 active atoms, <10% of activation variance). This experiment computes the causal analogue J_{C→A} = E[∂v_{L′}/∂h_{ℓ_in, context}] on the same corpus, decomposes M's predictive power into causal and correlational parts, tests both operators against steered-generation ground truth, and asks whether the channel reads from / writes into J-space. Not a replication Goal in the replication-fidelity sense — the J-lens FIT (Phase 0.1) follows the paper's recipe faithfully (deviations named in §12), but the headline object J_{C→A} is a new estimator this experiment defines.

## 3. Hypothesis

Specific, falsifiable predictions (quantitative where the priors permit; all reads reported regardless of outcome — no phase gates on these, per the §7 directive):

- **H1 (causal fraction):** the affine Jacobian predictor v̂_J(x) = v̄_train + J_last·(c_last(x) − c̄_train) recovers a NONTRIVIAL but MINORITY-to-moderate fraction of M's held-out R² on the pinned test-1000 — expectation band R²_J / R²_M ∈ [0.2, 0.8] (M wins in-distribution by construction; a fitted map absorbs corpus correlations J cannot). Falsifier of the "channel is causal" reading: R²_J/R²_M < 0.1 AND R²_J below the identity+bias baseline.
- **H2 (intervention prediction):** on Phase-3 steered generations, J predicts the measured answer-summary shift better than M — mean cos(Δv̄_measured, J_ctx·αΔ) > mean cos(Δv̄_measured, M·αΔ) across steered cells (paired over cells; the causal operator should win the causal read). Falsifier: M wins or ties this read while also winning held-out R² — then M's advantage is not merely correlational.
- **H3 (transfer):** the causal component transfers with smaller decay: ΔR²(LMSYS→WildChat)/R²(LMSYS) is smaller for the J-affine predictor than for M.
- **H4 (J-space read side):** M's top-20 right-singular input directions and J_last's top row-space directions carry J-space reconstruction energy above the matched-null p97.5 band, but the channel is not contained: energy < 0.5 on average (the paper's workspace carries <10% of activation variance, so partial overlap is the prior).
- **H5 (J-space write side):** measured Phase-3 steered answer shifts Δv̄ land in J-space at L′ above the null band for persona-vector directions; a behavior shift with judge-score movement but BELOW-null J-space energy is the safety-relevant "non-reportable shift" outcome — reported either way.

`N/A — no registered verdict lattice` (hypotheses carry expectation bands, not pre-defined outcome labels; interpretation happens at the analyzer with all reads on the table).

Registered paired contrast: H1/H2/H3 are paired J-vs-M reads over enumerable rows (H1/H3: the pinned test-1000 contexts, sha `b9377786…`, identical for both arms with the Phase-0.3 parity-rig exclusion list applied identically to both arms before scoring; H2: the identical steered-cell set — each cell one (context, direction, α) triple with its measured Δv̄; a cell unusable for one arm, e.g. incoherent generation, is dropped from BOTH — never a per-arm drop).

Both standing mapping arms (prefix-based AND context-based) run throughout as position-subset sums from the same backward passes, plus the last-token M-parity arm.

**Formal estimator.** For pair x = (context, stored on-policy answer), teacher-forced through the frozen model with per-segment TOKEN-ID concatenation (never re-tokenized concatenated strings — gotchas BPE-seam rule), define v_{L′}(x) = mean over answer-token positions of the layer-L′ residual stream (EXACT parity with #779's `v_x` mean pooling; L′ = 19). For a seed direction e ∈ R^d, one backward pass with cotangent e on v_{L′}(x) yields g_t(e,x) = ∂(eᵀv_{L′}(x))/∂h_{ℓ_in,t}(x) at EVERY source position t (ℓ_in = 19). Accumulate three position-subset sums per backward:

- `jca_prefix` (prefix arm): Σ_{t < prefix_end} g_t — prefix = everything before the user query (system + template segment; special-token boundary, no BPE-merge risk on this corpus).
- `jca_ctx` (context arm): Σ_{t < context_end} g_t — context = prefix + user query (all prompt positions).
- `jca_last` (M-parity arm): g_{context_end−1} — the single last-prompt-token source, the exact input slot of M's `cx_last`.

J_arm = mean over sampled pairs of the per-pair rows; rows assembled over seeds. Seeds are batched per pair-batch via `torch.autograd.grad(..., is_grads_batched=True)` (K seeds per vmapped backward, K tuned in the smoke; fallback: serial per-seed backwards, same math). Boundaries (prefix_end/context_end) derived from the prompt's offset mapping per the gotchas recipe (`compute_prompt_spans` in `analysis/representation_shift.py` if main-resident at implementation, else the documented offset-mapping fallback; §12 assumption).

## 11. Decision Rationale

- **J-lens fit recipe (1,000 prompts × 128 tok, all layers, cotangents-summed estimator).** Why: replication fidelity to the instrument being reused.
