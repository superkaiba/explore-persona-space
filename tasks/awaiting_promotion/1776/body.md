# The fitted context-to-answer map reads a correlate, not a cause: Jacobians recover essentially none of its predictive power and even full-state substitution at its input slot moves nothing (HIGH confidence)

<!-- clean-result-v4 -->

**Methodology:** [docs/methodology/issue_1776.md](https://github.com/superkaiba/explore-persona-space/blob/d6e7943ee402b05e6f8212fcda86a95130a05e5a/docs/methodology/issue_1776.md) · [gist mirror](https://gist.github.com/superkaiba/9277587d957fc1d04b73792c7a8c7283)

## Takeaways

- **Causal fraction ≈ 0: last-token Jacobian held-out R² −0.001 vs 0.681 for the slot-matched fitted map (shipped 0.754, reproduced to 1e-10); Jacobian retrieval at chance.**
- Not an averaging, convergence, or amplitude artifact: per-context Jacobians (96 contexts) match their average (median cosine 0.73) and align with the fitted map no better; split-half row cosine 0.96; an oracle per-context rescale reaches R² only 0.086 vs 0.681 — the deficit is directional.
- Neither operator causally executes its own answer swap: at the maximum coherent dose, B-content acquisition sits at the matched shuffled-target null in every arm (fitted-minus-Jacobian paired contrast +0.0006, CI −0.0003 to +0.0023; attainable control 0.047) — even though the fitted map's delta claims 88% of the needed shift.
- The slot is not causally sufficient: replacing A's layer-14 last-token state with the model's own state under B — the full needed shift, uncapped — still leaves acquisition at the shuffled-target null (+0.0001, CI −0.0004 to +0.0008, vs 0.047 attainable) — consistent with a readout correlate (necessity untested; re-attention routing not excluded).
- Behavioral steering stays marginal: judged sycophancy +2.4 points at 12× the parent norm with flat random controls; evil and hallucination within noise; the which-operator-predicts contrast is undecided on trait directions, and its one separation favors the fitted map.
- Off-distribution the Jacobian collapses (fresh WildChat R² −0.36 vs ~0.02 fitted decay); in token space every Jacobian chain arm sits at its own shuffled-pairing null, ~31× below the fitted chain (whose ~9× gain survives the prompt-echo split) — the fitted map's edge is corpus-correlational in geometry and semantics; workspace mediation stays weak (energies ≤ 0.10).

## Goal

**This experiment in context:** The context-to-answer mapping line ([#779](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/779)) fit ridge maps from the last-prompt-token activation to the mean answer-span activation at held-out R² ≈ 0.75, but a fitted map conflates causal signal flow with corpus correlations. This run computes the causal analogue — the averaged Jacobian of the layer-19 answer summary with respect to the layer-14 context activation on the same corpus — and asks what fraction of the fitted map's predictive power it carries, which operator predicts steered-generation ground truth ([#1415](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1415) supplies the steering rig and the fitted-map-predicts-nothing precedent at layer 20), whether the causal component transfers better off-distribution, and whether the channel reads from or writes into the J-space verbalizable workspace of the Anthropic 2026 workspace paper (its J-lens estimator is vendored and refit here). Operator comparisons follow the [#1345](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/1345) battery conventions; a retrospective leakage re-read consumes the [#532](https://eps.superkaiba.com/tasks/532) marker-leakage tables.
**Broader narrative:** If the fitted context-to-answer map were a causal channel, it would double as a steering and monitoring instrument; this run tests that directly. The answer — the map's predictive power has no detectable context-independent linear causal counterpart at its own input slot — bounds how the mapping line's predictors can be used: they are reads of corpus regularity, not linear handles on signal flow. The follow-up round closed this body's two scope limits: per-context (state-dependent) Jacobians, estimated directly on 96 contexts, are homogeneous and align with the fitted map no better than their average; and dose-escalated steering at the sibling run's full layer-14 operating norm still moves behavior only marginally — the correlational reading now rests on direct per-context and high-dose evidence rather than on an untested escape hatch. Two further rounds tested execution and semantics directly: injecting each operator's own inverted pair-swap delta at that same dose leaves generations without the target answer's content — the fitted map predicts answer states it cannot execute — and a scale-free token-rank read shows the Jacobian's transported signal carries no context-specific verbalizable answer content, closing the last scale-sensitivity escape on the headline. A final round removed the operators altogether, substituting the slot's state with the state the model computes under the target context — the strongest on-manifold intervention available there — and still moved nothing: the execution failures are a property of the slot, not the operators, so the layer-14 last-token slot is not causally sufficient for answer content — consistent with a readout correlate, though necessity is untested and re-attention to the untouched context is not excluded.

## Methodology

**Design:** No training — all phases are forward/backward passes, closed-form ridge fits, steered generation, and judging on frozen `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` (bf16, 28 layers, d = 3584). The causal estimator: for each (context, stored on-policy answer) pair, teacher-force the pair and take one backward pass per seed direction from the mean answer-token residual at layer 19 (the readout) to the layer-14 residual at context positions (the source; the source must sit below the readout because cross-position flow enters through attention, which reads the previous layer's residual). Per backward, three position-subset sums give the prefix-span, context-span, and last-prompt-token variants — the last is the exact tensor slot the fitted comparator reads and the steering hook edits (all three share the block-14-output convention). Averaging per-pair gradients over 1,536 LMSYS pairs × 3,584 standard-basis seeds yields the three Jacobians, each persisted with disjoint even/odd half-sums for convergence reads. The slot-matched fitted comparator is a fresh ridge from the layer-14 last-token activation to the layer-19 answer summary (n = 50,000 mixed-corpus rows); the shipped layer-19 ridge from the parent run rides along as a labeled reference (its same-layer input slot is causally degenerate for this Jacobian, so it is never the comparison target). Phases: engineering gates (teacher-forced parity, lens sanity, nonzero-gradient smoke) → directional diagnostic → Jacobian sketch + full rank → steered regeneration (200 contexts × 5 directions × 4 scales × 5 samples + unsteered baseline) → J-space mediation (dictionaries at layers 14 and 19 from the vendored J-lens; gradient-pursuit cone energies + top-k-span upper bound; refit split) → transfer (fresh WildChat capture) + free analyses (leakage re-read, lens vocabulary tables, chain composition). All phases ran unconditionally per the task directive; the three engineering gates passed (parity 0 of 200 rows failed, minimum cosine 1.000; lens next-token agreement 0.32 in the last layer quartile vs chance 7e-6; nonzero context gradients on the smoke). A post-review free-analysis round (label `followup_9ater`, driver `scripts/issue1776_9ater_followup.py`) added two reads on the same pinned test tensors: a Jacobian rescale ladder — least-squares global scalar, train-fit affine, and an oracle per-test-context scalar kept as a never-deployable diagnostic ceiling; the pooling anchors, never uploaded pod-side, were recovered exactly by stream-reducing the full capture and validated against eight committed rows (max diff 1.8e-6) — and a per-context decomposition of the chain mean reciprocal rank against each context's own shuffled null. A subsequent cheap-band follow-up round (label `p3p4_heterogeneity_dose`, driver `scripts/issue1776_p3p4.py` + the parent steering rig + `scripts/issue1776_judge.py`) added two legs. Per-context Jacobians: 96 contexts — 48 from the LMSYS Jacobian-fit pool and 48 from the fresh WildChat capture, stratified over fitted-comparator error quartiles — each estimated with the same teacher-forced backward-pass estimator, 16 at full rank (3,584 basis seeds) and 80 with a 256-seed Gaussian sketch whose restricted reads track the full-rank reads at Spearman 0.93 on the 16 calibration contexts; short bare queries are span-anchored from the template suffix (the span fix that also exposed one of the 1,536 parent estimation pairs as silently mis-spanned — a find-from-zero preamble collision; one row of 1,536 is negligible for the averaged Jacobian and the parent arms were not recomputed; a resume-time recompute of legacy per-context units touched at most two). Dose ladder: raw-norm steering at α of 11.84, 23.68, and 47.36 — one-quarter, one-half, and the full replicated layer-14 operating norm of the sibling causal-steering run (its scale-4 setting times the median persona-vector norm), vs the parent leg's unit-norm α ≤ 4 — on 150 of the parent's 200 steering contexts (100 LMSYS test-pool + 50 trait-rig), five directions, K = 5 draws, and a fresh in-round unsteered baseline so every judged shift subtracts the same context's own baseline. A fold supplement (`scripts/issue1776_p3p4_supplement.py`) recomputes every judged delta per-context matched, this round's unsteered pseudo-shift floor, per-cell split-half reliability, clustered operator-prediction bootstraps, and the round's own language-intrusion audit. A pair-swap execution round (label `operator_swap_success`, directory `followup_swap`; drivers `scripts/issue1776_swap.py`, `scripts/issue1776_swap_judge.py`, `scripts/issue1776_swap_dispatch.sh`) then tested whether either operator can execute its own transformation: 150 A→B context pairs (75 per leg — LMSYS steering pool and fresh WildChat — quartile-stratified on answer-profile dissimilarity, A's and B's each sampled without replacement, pairs with content-token Jaccard > 0.5 or profile cosine > 0.95 excluded); per pair and operator, the injected edit is the operator's own inverted delta δ = B_s·(Op·B_s)⁺·(v(B)−v(A)) restricted to the top-256 input principal components of the layer-14 last-token capture (pseudoinverse cutoff 1e-6; the 1e-4 sensitivity check leaves the deltas unchanged, delta-cosine median 1.00), norm-capped at 47.3606 — the dose ladder's top, behaviorally validated rung; arms are the two operators, a random control drawn per pair at the larger of the two operators' realized norms, and a fresh unsteered baseline, each generating K = 5 draws at temperature 1.0. The build gates passed: the metric-sanity ceiling read scored 0.836 mean recall@50 on B's own text (threshold 0.8), and the dose-degeneracy floor did not fire (median claimed fraction 88% fitted / 1.7% Jacobian, floor 1% on both). A same-day inline chat round (label `userchat_jchain`, driver `scripts/issue1776_jchain_userchat.py`, VM CPU, 294 s) extended the chain-composition token read to the Jacobian arms: each of four Jacobian variants' transported predictions pushed through the same layer-19 lens vocabulary ranking on the same 999 fresh-WildChat contexts, against 200-draw shuffled-pairing nulls, with an exact reproduction gate on the committed fitted-chain read (absolute difference 0.0). A final cheap-band round (label `slot_patch_sufficiency`, directory `followup_slotpatch`; the swap drivers in patch mode) removed the operators from the intervention: for each of the same 133 pairs, A's layer-14 last-context-token hidden state is replaced wholesale by the state the model itself computes under B — recomputed by one teacher-forced forward per pair, a replace edit rather than an additive delta, with no norm cap (the realized replacement norm is recorded as a dose covariate) — then K = 5 draws at temperature 1.0 regenerate A's answer against a fresh in-round unsteered baseline. Its build gates passed: the prefill hook's parity read lands the edit exactly at the intended slot and nowhere else (deviation 0.0 at both checks), and the freshly recomputed target states match the swap round's stored capture at cosine median 0.9999 (122 overlapping contexts, threshold 0.99). Conciseness note: the per-result prose-length WARNs (several sections over 120 words), the Takeaways bullet-length WARNs (bullets over 30 words), and the total-prose budget WARN are acknowledged — the run-everything directive plus five folded follow-up rounds produced nineteen reportable read families, each kept as its own result section; the two chain figures' tick labels carry the committed rounds' operator config slugs, and the swap and slot-patch rounds' figures are the drivers' committed renders without data sidecars, and the slot-patch round's eight companion views are deliberately linked rather than embedded (the hero carries the claim) — all acknowledged (captions give plain-English names; per-cell values live in `followup_swap/swap_success.json` and `followup_slotpatch/patch_success.json`).

**Evaluation:** Held-out prediction is pooled R² on the parent's pinned LMSYS test-1000 tensors, identical rows for every operator, with the identity-plus-learned-bias baseline and kNN retrieval (euclidean + cosine, chance 1/1000) reported for every map per the standing rule. The steering read is dual-DV: the graded judge score (primary behavioral construct; drop-never-coerce — 342 content drops of 121,250 draws overall, 0.28%, worst arm 3.0%; zero transport losses; 13 empty rollouts dropped) and the answer-summary shift (secondary continuous companion; its validation against the judge is reported in the results). Control strata (random and fitted-map directions) were judged on round-robin 66/67-context subsets per rubric (16/17 for all-position) while `steered_minus_baseline` subtracts the full-baseline mean, so every committed control-strata shift row — not only the all-position hallucination row flagged in the results — carries a subset-composition caveat. Steering predictions compare cos(shift, J·αΔ) vs cos(shift, M′·αΔ) on the unit-matched pair — the prefill hook edits exactly the tensor slot both operators consume. Workspace overlap is gradient-pursuit reconstruction energy (honest cone read) plus top-k-span projection (upper bound), each against three per-draw-selection nulls. Transfer scores every operator on the pinned LMSYS leg and a fresh 999-context WildChat capture never present in any fit; the planned third leg — the same reads on the parent eval rig's persona-battery contexts — was not run (the production dispatcher wires only those two legs), so every cross-context transfer claim is scoped to the two delivered corpora. The retrospective leakage re-read consumes the reused marker-localization tables — per-(source, bystander) on-policy ` ※`-marker emission rates for contrastive marker-implant adapters at training epochs 1 and 2 (the epoch-1 / epoch-2 contrastive arms) — and recomputes their Spearman correlation with source–bystander persona similarity (centered cosine between persona-centroid-bank activations at layer 21), swapping the raw predictor for its workspace-projected and orthogonal-complement variants. The dose round's judge covered 2,700 cells (3 rubrics × 900): the fresh baseline is judged under every rubric, so every own-trait shift is per-context matched, while the two control strata are judged under one rubric each (deterministic disjoint 50-context thirds); the committed summary's control deltas subtract the 150-context baseline mean, so the fold supplement recomputes every delta per-context matched — the matched values are the ones quoted in the results. Dose-round judge health: 192 content drops of 67,500 draws (0.28%; worst stratum hallucination at the middle dose, 45 of 3,750 = 1.2%), zero transport losses, zero empty rollouts. Language-intrusion audit (Qwen under a mixed-language real-user eval): non-CJK-prompt intrusion is 6.4% at baseline and 5.3–7.4% per prefill steered arm (pooled 6.5% — no steering effect); all-position arms rise to 8.1–11.9% (trait arms 8.9–11.9%, random control 8.1%, fitted-map direction 9.4%) vs the 5.5% matched-context baseline, so their judged shifts carry excluded- and zeroed-intrusion recounts. An operator counts as executing only if its pair-clustered acquisition-over-null CI sits strictly above zero AND its mean exceeds the random arm's CI upper bound — the second criterion is a point-versus-CI screen specified in the plan as a heuristic, and the verdict here is carried by the first criterion on both legs. B-target provenance differs by leg (LMSYS: the dose round's K = 5 fresh baseline draws, text and teacher-forced layer-19 profile from the same generations; WildChat: the capture's persisted on-policy answer and stored profile), so acquisition levels are compared within leg, never across. Null draws rebuild the full target set per draw under the same eligibility screen; the LMSYS leg's eligible pool is thinner (median 82 distinct replacement contexts per 200 draws vs 178 on WildChat) but not degenerate. Swap-round judge health: 0 content drops and 0 transport losses across 26,600 draws; the judge-free metric and the judge agree across cells (rank correlation 0.19, p = 1.1e-4, n = 399). The swap round's own language-intrusion audit flags 84 of 133 pairs (mixed-language corpus, any-draw criterion); intrusion-excluded recounts on the 49 clean pairs leave every arm's acquisition delta at its null (fitted 0.0005, Jacobian 0.0008, random 0.0009), so no adjudication flips. The slot-patch round reuses the swap round's judge-free primary DV, per-cell nulls, ceiling read, and attainable anchor unchanged; its graded judge is conditional by design — it runs only if the pooled or either-leg pair-clustered 95% CI lower bound of the patch arm's acquisition-over-null delta clears zero — and the condition was not met, so the round has no judge pass (a designed outcome, not missing data). Cross-round contrasts against the committed round-1 arms are gated on a baseline-drift diagnostic — the fresh patch baseline vs the swap round's, pooled and per leg (0.0017 vs 0.0016 pooled, every CI overlapping) — which did not separate, so the committed arms stand as comparators. The round's language-intrusion audit flags 69 of 133 pairs (patch arm or its fresh baseline, any-draw criterion); the intrusion-excluded recount on the 64 clean pairs moves the patch delta to 0.0003, still inside its null — no adjudication flips. The chain-composition chat round generates nothing (it re-reads the parent's audited rollout pools), so no new intrusion audit applies.
