## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I train the same small neural network (MLP) map from context activations to answer activations at layer 19, at many more training-set sizes — 25k, 50k, 100k, 150k, 250k, 500k — using the big banked ~963k-row LMSYS activation store, and reuse the already-trained 963k network. ## 3. Hypothesis

The paper claim predicts the nonlinear map keeps gaining past the linear plateau. Grounding: ridge Δ(150k→500k) = +0.005 R² (plateau); banked MLP Δ(150k→500k) = +0.019; MLP−ridge level gap at 500k = 0.047.

The MLP verdict endpoints (50k, 500k) are fit at 3 seeds {42, 43, 44} on the identical sel-sha subsets (OPT-1). Per seed s, S_mlp^(s) = R²_MLP,s(500k) − R²_MLP,s(50k) (seed-paired slope); S_mlp = the seed MEAN of the three seed-paired slopes (seedwise values + dispersion reported beside). S_ridge = R²_ridge(500k) − R²_ridge(50k) (closed-form ridge is seed-free). All R² on the pinned test_1000 (bootstrap CIs reported beside). The single verdict quantity is the slope-gap margin D_gap = (S_mlp − S_ridge) − 0.01.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Confirmed ⇔ D_gap >= 0; Falsified ⇔ D_gap < 0.

Descriptive sub-reads (reported, no verdict weight): within Falsified, "plateau-both" (|S_mlp| < 0.01 — both curves flat past 50k) is distinguished from "nonlinear also declines / noise" in prose; within Confirmed, S_mlp ≤ 0 (a gap driven by a ridge DECLINE rather than a nonlinear gain) is called out as a qualitatively different outcome rather than narrated as the paper claim.

Secondary (descriptive, no verdict weight): the same contrast on acc@1 under whitened-cosine+CSLS — a nonlinear R² gain that does NOT show up in retrieval is reported as a metric dissociation (squarely inside the parent Goal's "where they dissociate" clause), not hidden.

**Registered paired contrast** (MLP vs ridge per rung): Row-coverage — every rung row in `mlp_scaling_dense_L19.json` carries BOTH arms fit on the IDENTICAL train-row index set (same `sel`/prefix subset, sha-recorded) and scored on the identical pinned val_400/test_1000; the 963k rung's two arms come from banked weights applied to the same test rows (mixed-corpus caveat carried); any rung realized with only one arm is excluded from paired reads and named in the output.

## 7. Decision Gates

No decision gates — ~1.7 h run, pre-verified recipe. G1, G3, and the parity asserts (§4) are fail-loud correctness gates (abort-and-report), not go/no-go branches on intermediate science. G2 is deliberately NOT in that class: it is a RECORDED three-state comparison (`PASS` / `FALLBACK-PARITY-PASS` / `FAIL` — §4 c1) whose only halt is kill criterion 2's two-part predicate below.

**Kill criteria (halt-and-report; what result kills the round):**
1. **Gate G1 FAIL** — the scale7_refit and n1m pinned val/test rows are NOT content-identical (beyond dtype cast): the ≤25k and ≥50k halves of the curve would not share an eval pool, and no fold-matched dense curve exists to build — halt before any fit, report the mismatch as the round's finding, re-scope.
2. **G2 three-state verdict resolves to FAIL** — fresh selections do not reproduce the recorded sel_shas AND the refit ridge points at 150k/500k miss the banked values by > 0.02 R² (the two-part predicate; a sel-sha mismatch alone continues under `FALLBACK-PARITY-PASS` with the downgrade recorded): the new points cannot be tied to the published ridge curve — halt after the 150k/500k ridge cells, report, do not ship the curve.
3. **Any parity gate breach > 0.02 R² at a fold-exact anchor** (MLP 5k/10k refits, ridge 25k, 963k weight-apply > 1e-3): fit-machinery drift — halt, diagnose before densifying further rungs (the mlp script's job-A parity-gate convention).
4. **Eval-pool reconciliation impossible** — the whitened-CSLS battery cannot be computed on the pinned 1,000-target pool for any completed rung (CSLS floor or whitening-stat failure): kill the PRIMARY-convention deliverable, report raw-convention results only with the downgrade named.
