# Plan — Issue #666: Phase 4 end-to-end leakage predictor L̂ vs cosine + base-prior + noise floor

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the activation snapshots Phase 2 already saved (one per trained model, no new training) and assemble the theory's full leakage-prediction formula. Then I check, for each trained behavior, whether that formula correctly ranks which *other* contexts the behavior leaks into — and I race it against simpler guesses: a plain cosine-similarity version, "just use the model's own baseline tendency," "predict nothing," and two stand-in "fake" behaviors that were built to carry no real meaning but still moved the model's activations. The behavior-direction the formula needs comes from a prior phase's measurement for two behaviors (bad-medical, EM) and from the per-cell install shift for the other two (taught-fact, marker) — so I read each behavior on its own and treat the cross-behavior average with a caveat. I also estimate the whitening from a large background pool of everyday text (a few thousand prompts), not from the 50 test prompts themselves — measuring it on the same 50 prompts I score would manufacture a false "whitening helps."
- **What I expect:** On the content behaviors (taught-fact, bad-medical) the full formula ranks leakage targets meaningfully better than chance and at least as well as the cosine version. But I expect two stubborn competitors. First, the model's own *baseline tendency* — in three past experiments it beat geometry — so the honest headline is "does our formula beat the base-rate guess *after* we strip out how strongly the behavior got installed." Second, the fake behaviors: Phase 2 showed nonsense behaviors score about as high as real ones simply because the training shoved activations around, so the real test is whether a genuine behavior beats those fakes by a clear margin. On the marker behavior I expect the formula to be useless, because Phase 2 showed the marker never actually installed.
- **What would change my mind:** If the full whitened formula clearly beats cosine, the base-rate guess, AND the fake behaviors on content behaviors even after controlling for install strength, that's a real win for the theory. If instead a real behavior scores no higher than the fakes, the formula is only tracking how hard the training pushed — not the theory's geometry — which is itself a clean, publishable answer.

## 2. Prior Work

**The theory under test** (`docs/leakage_theory_paper.tex`): the boxed predictor is
`L̂_{C,B→C',B'} = η_{C,B} · (r_{B'}ᵀδ_{C,B}) · [c_Cᵀ Σc⁻¹ c_{C'}] / [c_Cᵀ Σc⁻¹ c_C]` with `δ_{C,B} = t_{C,B} − v_{θ0}(C)` (paper "The predictor", boxed L1633). The **cosine special-case** (paper §Relation-to-cosine, L287/L1645): `L̂^cos ∝ cos(r_{B'},r_B)·cos(c_C,c_{C'})`, which is exactly L̂ in the limit where (i) `δ ∥ r_B`, (ii) `‖r_{B'}‖` and `‖c_{C'}‖` are treated as constant, (iii) `Σc ∝ I`. These three toggles are the ONLY allowed differences between L̂ and the cosine baseline (C7). η drops out of all relative (ranking/correlation) tests at a fixed source and is recoverable from one on-source measurement: `η_{C,B} = L̂_{C,B→C,B}/(r_Bᵀδ)` with the on-source `Δs` (paper "Dropping the write strength", L1587-1617). The paper's A3.7 ŵ-shortcut (L224) gives `cos(ŵ, r_B)` as a displacement-direction read of `r_B` when no separately-extracted contrastive direction is available — this is what the per-cell `r_plus` provides for the two behaviors without a #658 diffmeans direction.

**What Phase 2 (#664) delivered + says is recoverable** (read the full body): the trained store has, per cell, `v_plus`/`v0` (n_ctx,28,d), `v_plus_probe`/`v0_probe` (n_ctx,n_probe,28,d) for the probe-split noise floor, `c_C_base` (n_ctx,28,d = the BASE context vector `c0(C')` per battery context — the key Phase-4 input), `c_C_trained` (post-FT `q⁺` diagnostic), `t_CB` (28,d trained source answer-mean = `t`), `r_plus` (28,d = the implant shift at source), `v0_neg` (negative panel), `context_ids`, `meta.json` with `target_context_roles`/`behavior`/`source`/`arm`/`dose`/`seed`. **Scientific caveat carried forward:** the marker spine NEVER installed (source log P trained−base = −0.34 to +1.81 nat, never the 5-12 band; ‖ŵ‖≈1.2 @ L20, ~17-42× below content) — so the marker gate sits at the probe-split noise floor (kill (a)+(b) fired). Phase 2's `judged_rates.json` aggregate behavioral-rate DV was NOT produced this round but is recomputable from the per-cell completions on HF.

## 3. Hypothesis

Stated falsifiably, per scale and behavior class:

- **H1 (latent, content):** On the content behaviors with install dynamic range (taught-fact, bad-medical; insecure-code/EM where ‖ŵ‖ permits), the full L̂ Spearman ρ vs `Δs` (LOBO and LOCO) EXCEEDS the apples-to-apples cosine ρ AND the base-prior ρ **AND the designed-null L̂ ρ**, with non-overlapping family-clustered 95% CIs, **after install magnitude (‖ŵ‖) is partialled out**. *Falsified if* L̂ ρ ≤ base-prior ρ once install is partialled out (geometry adds nothing above the prior) — a clean #532/#649-style null, also publishable — OR if a real content behavior's L̂ ρ overlaps the designed-null L̂ ρ within clustered CIs (then the install-leak alternative is not ruled out; the formula tracks install-displacement magnitude, not the theory's geometry). **Cross-arm heterogeneity subclause:** the H1 latent-scale `r_B` read is the #658 diffmeans direction for bad-medical + EM and the `r_plus` A3.7 ŵ-shortcut for taught-fact; the cross-behavior aggregate ρ thus mixes two `r_B` sources, with the within-behavior sensitivity arms (on bad-medical + EM, where both paths exist) gating the cross-behavior interpretation (§6).
- **H2 (cosine isolation):** L̂ beats `L̂^cos` specifically because of the three toggles (δ vs r_B / norm / Σc⁻¹ vs I), isolated by toggling them one at a time on the shared frozen recipe. *Falsified if* the whitened gate does NOT beat raw cosine (then "whitening buys nothing" — design A3.9 verdict (ii) fails).
- **H3 (behavior scale):** After the per-behavior affine link φ (fit on TRAIN partition only), calibrated MAE (pp) of L̂ near φ's affine mid-range is below predict-mean and below base-prior MAE. (Secondary because the binary judged-rate DV may saturate; reported near mid-range only where φ is near-affine.)
- **H4 (marker sentinel):** The marker spine L̂ ρ FLOORS at the noise floor (no install → no signal). *This is the pre-registered expectation, not a kill* — the marker is a noise-floor sentinel arm validating that the harness reports "no signal" where Phase 2 proved none exists. (The marker's `r_B` is the `r_plus` ŵ-shortcut, not a #658 diffmeans direction — there is no marker direction in `rb_columns`; this reinforces the sentinel reading, since marker has neither install signal nor a separately-extracted contrastive direction.)
- **Quantitative thresholds:** "beats" = point estimate higher AND clustered-CI lower bound above the comparator's point estimate; "near noise floor" = L̂ ρ within the test-retest reliability ceiling (a ρ at the floor is not a failure, it is the measurement ceiling).

### 4d. Assemble the full predictor L̂ (`scripts/issue666_predictor.py`)
Per cell (= source `C`, trained behavior `B`) × every target context `C'` × evaluated behavior `B'`:
`L̂ = η_{C,B} · (r_{B'}ᵀ δ_{C,B}) · g_C(C')`, with `δ_{C,B} = t_CB − v0[src_idx]` (positive-only δ per §A3.7 primary; the contrastive δ^contra is a sensitivity arm, scoped to contrastive cells only, read alongside `frac_ctx`). The `r_{B'}` per behavior is the mixed source of §4b (diffmeans for bad-medical/EM, `r_plus` ŵ-shortcut for taught-fact/marker). For relative (ranking/correlation) tests η drops out (work with `(r_{B'}ᵀδ)·g_C`). The grid: behavior-transfer factor varies `B'` at fixed `C`; context-gate factor varies `C'` at fixed `B`. **Disjointness:** the source-anchor context (`ĝ^real(C)=1` by construction, role = "source-anchor" in meta) is EXCLUDED from the bystander correlation (it is the manipulation anchor, reported separately — same exclusion as #664).

**Designed-null arm (the install-leak CONTROL, 0 GPU-h — Must-Fix 2):** include the 2 designed-null adapters ALREADY in #664's HF store — `educational-code-null` (`ic_edu_default`) and `reversed-fact-null` (`tf_rev_default`) — as additional cells in this same pipeline (load cell → compute L̂ → score Spearman ρ vs Δs; they are just two more store cells). #664 proved these nulls are **install-matched + signal-free**: their gate SNR co-varies with install magnitude exactly as real EM cells (educational-code-null SNR 2.05, reversed-fact-null SNR 3.46 at best-of-28 — matching real cells), yet they carry no designed leakage signal. They are therefore the CORRECT null for the install-leak alternative — unlike the marker sentinel (H4), which floors trivially because it has NO install AND no signal, so it cannot isolate "L̂ tracks install-displacement" from "L̂ tracks real leakage." The designed nulls test the within-cell-across-target install-displacement axis that the ‖ŵ‖-partial (per-cell scalar) and E0-partial (target-base-prior) controls do NOT reach. The pre-registered expectation: the designed-null L̂ ρ reflects pure install-displacement structure, and a real content behavior must EXCEED it (clustered CIs, §6).

### 4e. Apples-to-apples cosine baseline (`scripts/issue666_predictor.py`, `--variant cos`)
`L̂^cos ∝ cos(r_{B'},r_B)·cos(c_C,c_{C'})` derived from the SAME `r_B` (the per-behavior mixed source of §4b), `c_C`, layer 14, and extraction recipe as L̂ — toggling ONLY the three knobs (δ→r_B for the behavior term; drop norm handling; Σc⁻¹→I for the gate). Implemented as three independent boolean toggles on the shared L̂ code path so each toggle's marginal contribution is isolable (C7). Also report the **raw un-whitened cosine gate** (Σc⁻¹→I but keep δ + norms) and **predict-zero / predict-mean** as the floor baselines.

### 4f. Base-behavior-prior baseline (`scripts/issue666_predictor.py`, `--variant prior`)
`E0(C',B') = r_{B'}ᵀ v0(C')` (the target's own base-model expression, the strongest recurring null; `r_{B'}` per the §4b mixed source). Two reports per §1.7 C7: (1) **partial it out** — partial Spearman ρ(L̂, Δs | E0) is the install-confound-clean headline; (2) **ΔS predictive power above prior** — does L̂ predict the residual `Δs − (affine fit of E0)`. **Install-strength control (§6 required):** additionally report L̂ ρ with install magnitude ‖ŵ‖ (from `gate_per_layer`'s wnorm) partialled out, AND leakage-as-fraction-of-install computed in the EOS-margin logit space for the marker sentinel (NEVER raw log P — softmax compression caveat). Never correlate the fraction back against install itself (X-vs-(X−Y) artifact, #383 family).

### 4g. LOBO + LOCO drivers (`scripts/issue666_lobo_loco.py`)
- **LOBO** (leave-one-behavior-out): hold out one of {marker, bad-medical, insecure-code/EM, taught-fact}, calibrate φ on the remaining behaviors' TRAIN partition, score the held-out behavior.
- **LOCO** (leave-one-context-out): hold out one battery context as `C'`, calibrate on the rest.
- φ = per-behavior monotone affine link (logistic σ or clamp, paper "Bounding expression to [0,1]") fit on the TRAIN partition ONLY (selection/verdict on disjoint data). Latent-scale tests need no φ (Δs is unbounded); φ is only for the behavior-`[0,1]`-scale MAE.

### 4h. η recovery (`scripts/issue666_predictor.py`, `--recover-eta`)
From ONE on-source measurement per cell: at `(C',B')=(C,B)` the gate = 1 and `L̂_{C,B→C,B} = η·(r_Bᵀδ)`, so `η_{C,B} = Δs_on-source / (r_Bᵀδ)` with the on-source `Δs = r_Bᵀ(v⁺(C)−v0(C)) = r_Bᵀ r_plus`. **η-recovery anchor:** the highest-install content cell, `bm_default_contra_d1_seed42` (bad-medical, default source — best-of-28 SNR 7.55, ‖ŵ‖ far above marker; `Source: #664 behavior_snr` table). η is needed only for the cross-source / absolute-number arm (sensitivity), not the within-source ranking headline.

### 4i. Noise-floor estimator (`scripts/issue666_noise_floor.py`)
Test-retest reliability = ceiling on any predictor's achievable ρ. Two independent estimates of the latent `Δs` from independent probe-split halves of `v_plus_probe`/`v0_probe` (reuse `issue664_aggregate_gate.probe_split_floor` half-split logic), recompute `Δs` on each half, and the test-retest ρ between the two halves = the floor. **MC: 200 probe-split resamples × 3 RNG seeds** (`Source: pre-registered; bounded by n_probe≈50 per context → C(50,25) split space, 200 draws is ample`). Every headline ρ reported against this floor (a ρ at the floor is the measurement ceiling, not a model failure). For the marker sentinel + any single-context arm, raise the read to the continuous-DV primary per §1.10.

## 6. Evaluation

**Metrics (§1.7 suite), reported for every condition above:** Spearman ρ (PRIMARY, scale-free), Pearson r (for the `chen2025` comparison), sign agreement, AUROC + top-k precision (leakage-exceeds-threshold), calibrated MAE (pp) + slope (after per-behavior affine φ on TRAIN only). **Factor-localized:** behavior-transfer (vary B' at fixed C) and context-gate (vary C' at fixed B) scored on their own grid slices. **Splits:** LOBO + LOCO. **CIs:** family-, source-, and seed-clustered bootstrap (resample at cluster level, NEVER naive n=50) + independent-probe-split replication of the headline ρ on disjoint probe halves (C4). **Headline reported AGAINST the test-retest noise floor** (§4i).

**Designed-null verdict (PRE-REGISTERED, the install-leak gate on the headline — Must-Fix 2).** A real content behavior's L̂ Spearman ρ vs Δs must statistically **EXCEED the designed-null L̂ ρ** (the 2 install-matched, signal-free #664 cells `ic_edu_default`/`tf_rev_default`), with **non-overlapping clustered 95% CIs**, for the geometry-win headline to stand. If a real-behavior L̂ ρ **overlaps** the designed-null L̂ ρ within clustered CIs, the install-leak alternative is NOT ruled out: the result is reported as **install-confounded** ("L̂ tracks install-displacement magnitude, not theory geometry"), NOT a geometry win. This is the verdict the ‖ŵ‖-partial (per-cell-scalar axis) and E0-partial (target-base-prior axis) controls cannot deliver — those do not operate on the within-cell-across-target install-displacement axis #664 located, but the install-matched designed nulls do. The designed-null L̂ ρ is computed on the SAME pipeline + the SAME broad-corpus Σ_c + the SAME clustering as the real arms.

**Within-behavior sensitivity gates the cross-behavior aggregate (cross-arm `r_B` heterogeneity).** Because the `r_{B'}` source is MIXED across behaviors (§4b: #658 diffmeans for bad-medical + EM; `r_plus` A3.7 ŵ-shortcut for taught-fact + marker), the PRIMARY interpretable read is the PER-BEHAVIOR table; the cross-behavior aggregate ρ mixes two `r_B` sources and is SECONDARY. The within-behavior `r_B`-source sensitivity check (the bad-medical + EM sensitivity arms, which have BOTH paths) is the GATE on the cross-behavior aggregate's interpretability: if the L̂ Spearman ρ moves materially when the `r_B` source toggles (diffmeans ↔ `r_plus`) on those 2 behaviors, the cross-behavior aggregate is reported as UNRELIABLE and the headline becomes a per-behavior table (no pooled number). If the sensitivity check is robust, the cross-behavior aggregate is reported with the heterogeneity caveat attached.
