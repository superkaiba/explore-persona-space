# Plan — Issue #1315: Impolite-organism activation-shift geometry (extends #1112's method to impolite; ICL-context 2×2 completion)

## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** I take the three impolite models #1090 already trained (persona, WildChat, and ICL contexts — all expressing rudeness at 74–82% judged rates over a 0% base) plus the ICL positives-only twin, and measure how each one moved the model's internal activations away from the base model, using exactly the same measurement rig #1112 built for sycophancy — per-layer shift size, how spread-out vs concentrated the shift is, and whether it points along the "impoliteness" direction. I also train two full-fine-tune twins on the frozen ICL mixes so impolite gets the same LoRA-vs-full-FT × with/without-negatives 2×2 sycophancy got.
- **What I expect:** impolite looks like sycophancy did — the shift measured on the model's own text looks spread-out, most of that spread is just the text being different (it collapses when both models read the same text), the shift does not line up with the impoliteness read-out direction, and full fine-tuning moves activations farther but not in a differently-shaped way.
- **What would change my mind:** if impolite's shift stays spread-out even on shared text, or lines up clearly with the impoliteness direction, or full fine-tuning produces a genuinely differently-shaped shift — any of those says the sycophancy read was behavior-specific, not general.

## 3. Hypothesis

Impolite's install geometry MATCHES the sycophancy read: own-text diffuse, shared-text collapse, read-out-unaligned, method-null-on-shape, method-effect-on-magnitude. All registered reads at the pre-registered layer 14, response arm, on 120-row clouds (matched-n subsample reads for cross-issue comparisons), paired cluster-bootstrap / paired half-draw CIs per #1112's conventions.

Registered quantities (all at layer 14 unless stated): D_own = (minimum over the 4 reused LoRA cells of own-text response-arm rank-k@90) − 40. D_sh = (count of the 4 reused LoRA cells with shared-text rank-k@90 ≤ 30) − 3. C2 = (count of the 4 reused LoRA cells whose |cos(μ_response, r_B_14)| exceeds their norm-matched random 97.5% bound) − 2. ## Kill criteria

- Both FT cells < 0.45 closest-approach after G1 → the 2×2 method axis is unmeasured for impolite: report the install failure as a finding (FT expression may be context/lr-gated like LoRA was), skip H4/H5 headlines (and the descriptive H6 FT-corner mirror), H1–H3 + H6 (LoRA pair) stand.
- ≥2 reused organisms fail parity AND their retrain fallbacks miss the band → the reused-organism premise is broken; re-plan (amendment), never silent substitution.
- Same-model prefix/context parity check fails catastrophically (<0.99 median per-row cosine) → capture pipeline bug; fix before any geometry verdict (no partial reads).
