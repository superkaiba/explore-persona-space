- **V1 (alignment vs scaffolded floor).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: AlignAtScaffoldFloor ⇔ Ascaf ≥ 0; AlignBelowScaffoldFloor ⇔ otherwise.
- **V2 (alignment vs sycophancy ceiling).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: AlignAboveCeiling ⇔ Afals > 0; AlignAtCeiling ⇔ otherwise.
- **V3 (matched-n rank vs sycophancy band).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: RankBelowBand ⇔ Rband > 0; RankInOrPastBand ⇔ otherwise.
- **V4 (shared-text collapse).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: SharedCollapse ⇔ Scoll ≥ 0; NoSharedCollapse ⇔ otherwise.

Round verdict (deterministic composition, precedence order): **ScaffoldArtifactFalsified** when AlignAtCeiling OR RankInOrPastBand; else **ScaffoldFreeConfirmed** when AlignAtScaffoldFloor AND RankBelowBand AND SharedCollapse; else **Inconclusive** (e.g. alignment in (0.2, 0.5) with rank below band, or NoSharedCollapse alone).
