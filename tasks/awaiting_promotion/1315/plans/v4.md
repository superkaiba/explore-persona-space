The impolite geometry signature is lr-robust: retrained at 1e-5 (vs the captured 3e-5 sibling), the WildChat cell reproduces read-out alignment ≥ ~0.5 at layer 14, own-text rank-k@90 below the sycophancy matched-n band, and the shared-text collapse — discharging the lr confound the parent footer names. Registered quantities (new cell, layer 14, response arm, n=120 own-text cloud; point estimates, with the parent's paired-bootstrap CIs reported alongside descriptively, matching the parent's H1–H3 conventions):

- Aconf = |cos(μ_response, r_B_14)| − 0.5 (margin over the confirm bar; 0.5 ≈ the lr-3e-5 sibling's realized 0.509)
- Afals = |cos(μ_response, r_B_14)| − 0.2 (margin over the sycophancy-ceiling falsify bar; Aconf ≥ 0 entails Afals ≥ 0.3)
- Rband = 48 − (matched-80-row own-text rank-k@90 mean) (positive = below the sycophancy band; ≤ 0 = risen into/past the 48–50 band)
- Scoll = 30 − (shared-text response-arm rank-k@90) (positive/zero = the parent's registered collapse bar)

Sub-verdict lattices (one quantity each, the parent's per-H line shape):

- **V1 (alignment vs sibling).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: AlignAtSibling ⇔ Aconf ≥ 0; AlignBelowSibling ⇔ otherwise.
- **V2 (alignment vs sycophancy ceiling).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: AlignAboveCeiling ⇔ Afals > 0; AlignAtCeiling ⇔ otherwise.
- **V3 (matched-n rank vs sycophancy band).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: RankBelowBand ⇔ Rband > 0; RankInOrPastBand ⇔ otherwise.
- **V4 (shared-text collapse).** DISJOINT and exhaustive: SharedCollapse ⇔ Scoll ≥ 0; NoSharedCollapse ⇔ otherwise.

Round verdict (deterministic composition of the four sub-verdicts, in precedence order): **LrSensitiveFalsified** when AlignAtCeiling OR RankInOrPastBand; else **LrRobustConfirmed** when AlignAtSibling AND RankBelowBand AND SharedCollapse; else **Inconclusive** (e.g. alignment in (0.2, 0.5) with rank below band, or NoSharedCollapse alone). The composition is disjoint and exhaustive by construction: the precedence order makes the three round labels mutually exclusive, and AlignAtSibling (Aconf ≥ 0) entails AlignAboveCeiling (Afals ≥ 0.3 > 0), so no sub-verdict combination is contradictory.
