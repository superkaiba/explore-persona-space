Neither paper sweeps probe count × layer × metric on the same checkpoint family, so this reanalysis is positional info that neither provides.

**Cloud-aware metric prior.** Mean-based metrics (cosine, Euclidean) are well-estimated at small N. Covariance-based metrics (Gaussian-KL on a PCA-16 fit, MMD with the median-heuristic bandwidth, Bures-Wasserstein-2, Mahalanobis) have estimator variance that scales as O(1/√N) for the means and worse for the covariance terms — the standard expectation is that they need more probes per cloud to stabilize. The literature consensus on PCA-16 Gaussian-KL or MMD with the RBF bandwidth on the median heuristic is that 100-500 samples is the typical regime where bias still dominates noise; below ~100 the bias is large enough to swamp the signal we're trying to measure. The N grid is chosen to span that transition (25 below the floor, 100-200 across the bend, 350-500 in the expected plateau region).

## 3. Hypothesis

**Primary, falsifiable.** On the fixed cell `last_prompt × L22 × gauss_kl × raw` against loc_ep1 ΔG: the |ρ| and CV R² curves as a function of N have **flattened by N=500**. Operationally, the criterion is `ΔCV R²(N=350 → N=500) ≤ σ_ref` where `σ_ref = mean(across-subset stdev at N ∈ {200, 350})` — the across-subset stdev pooled across the two largest non-degenerate N values, the principled noise reference. If this holds, the #502 ceiling is structural and more probes won't help. **Falsification:** if `ΔCV R²(350 → 500) > 2 × σ_ref` AND directionally up, the predictors are still gaining and the conditional N=1000 extension fires. **(Note: σ(N=500) ≡ 0 by construction — random sampling without replacement at N = pool size yields R=10 identical permutations whose metric values are permutation-invariant; the noise reference must come from non-saturated N.)**

**Secondary, sign predictions.**
1. **Cloud-aware metrics converge slower than mean-based.** The cosine + next-token JS curves should plateau by N=100-200; the gauss_kl / MMD / wass2 curves should reach a stable value later (N=200-500). The gap between cloud-aware and mean-based should *widen* with N until both plateau. If they converge to the same value, the cloud-aware advantage is illusory.
2. **The search-best cell identity is unstable at small N.** At N=25 the per-N argmax cell is likely a noisy pick; by N=200-350 it should snap to the L19-L24 ridge of gauss_kl / MMD cells. The fixed-cell curve is the honest reference; the search-best curve is reported as a diagnostic of cell-identity stability.
3. **The ridge holds.** The full L19-L24 family (`last_prompt × L{19..24} × {gauss_kl, mmd, wass2} × raw`) should converge to similar values at large N (the #502 ridge structure is real), not collapse to L22 being uniquely good.
