"Settle whether the bare-query and prefix arms are **mirror images** in SAE feature space, as they demonstrably are in the dense per-context taxonomy." (Task #1946 Goal, verbatim; re-read at return time from `frontmatter.goal`, latest `epm:goal-updated` ts 2026-07-31T21:46:53Z — unchanged since draft start.)

One paragraph: #1738's dense per-context taxonomy showed the history-only (prefix) and bare-query arms fail on complementary context populations. #1738's SAE bare-query cell measured a per-FEATURE quantity (feature-level R² correlations) that does not test the per-CONTEXT claim. Reproduction means the complementarity is a property of the information available to each arm rather than of the dense coordinate system.

## 3. Hypothesis

**H1 (reproduce):** the per-category error structure per arm transfers from dense to SAE space — for each arm a ∈ {prefix, bare, context}, Spearman(Δ_a^SAE, Δ_a^dense) over the 22 pre-enumerated contrasts is positive and permutation-significant, and the prefix−bare difference pattern M_c = Δ_prefix,c − Δ_bare,c agrees in sign with dense on ≥15 of the 20 dense-significant-union contrasts.
**H0 (uncorrelated):** the SAE-space category pattern carries no dense-pattern signal (permutation p ≥ 0.05 on ρ_M).
**H2 (invert):** ρ_M significantly negative with sign agreement ≤5/20.

Primary registered statistic: **ρ_M = Spearman(M^SAE, M^dense)** over the 22 contrasts, M_c = Δ_prefix,c − Δ_bare,c per contrast (the "which arm finds this category harder" axis — the mirror-image object), with a TWO-SIDED 10,000-draw permutation p — the fraction of draws with |ρ_perm| ≥ |ρ_obs| (contrast labels shuffled, seed 1946; sidedness registered per the Statistics critic: a one-sided p would make the Inverted cell unfireable) — plus **SA_M** = sign-agreement count of M over the 20-contrast dense BH-significant union.
 Secondary registered statistics: per-arm ρ_a + per-arm sign agreement on that arm's dense-significant set (prefix 13, bare 18, context read from banked at run time); per-context Spearman of nerr^SAE vs nerr^dense per arm over the 9,941 joined holdout contexts, and of the per-context difference (nerr_prefix − nerr_bare) across spaces. Descriptive: the six body-named mirror categories (`language=en`, `topic=chitchat_social`, `topic=translation`, `corpus=wildchat`, `topic=nsfw`, `topic=creative_writing` for prefix; `topic=roleplay_persona`, `depth=>=5`, `topic=chitchat_social` for bare) reported individually with SAE-space deltas + CIs.

DISJOINT and exhaustive: Reproduced ⇔ ρ_M perm-p < 0.05 AND ρ_M > 0 AND SA_M ≥ 15/20; Inverted ⇔ ρ_M perm-p < 0.05 AND ρ_M < 0 AND SA_M ≤ 5/20; Uncorrelated ⇔ ρ_M perm-p ≥ 0.05; Mixed ⇔ otherwise.

(Threshold grounding: SA_M ≥ 15/20 has two-sided binomial(20, 0.5) p ≈ 0.041 — itself a ~0.05-level sign test; the p<0.05 convention matches the parent battery's q=0.05 family. Source: computed from banked dense-significant union n=20, §2.)

This registers a paired contrast (each of the 22 contrasts is a paired SAE-vs-dense delta; each of the 9,941 holdout contexts is a paired per-context nerr across spaces).
Row-coverage: both sides' registered rows are supplied per-contrast by `eval_results/issue_1946/sae_space/taxonomy.json` (SAE side, produced by this plan's verbatim battery run) and `eval_results/issue_1738/taxonomy.json` + `eval_results/issue_1738/bare_query/taxonomy.json` (dense side, banked); the per-context pairs are supplied by `eval_results/issue_1946/percontext/{cell}_L19_ridge.npz` (SAE side) and `eval_results/issue_1738/percontext_summary_L19_ridge.csv` + `eval_results/issue_1738/bare_query/percontext_summary_L19_ridge.csv` (dense side, committed), joined on ci.

**Controls / confounds ruled out:** (1) *Coordinate-system confound* — the dense-input comparator cells share the input with the dense taxonomy and the target with the SAE cells: if SAE-space patterns diverge from dense, these cells localize whether the divergence enters at the input representation or the target space. (2) *Artifact-drift confound* — fingerprint + banked-R² + f_out identity gates guarantee the scored predictions are exactly the banked fits' predictions on exactly the banked targets. (3) *Answer-sampling confound* — floor-adjusted robustness arm (approximate; verdict-bearing battery is unadjusted, exactly as the dense round's primary was). (4) *Family-selection confound* — contrast family is recomputed from the same labels and asserted identical to the banked 22-element list; no new contrast selection. (5) *Multiplicity* — BH-FDR q=0.05 inherited within each arm's family; the cross-space headline is ONE pre-registered statistic (ρ_M), not a max over anything.

**Prefix mapping AND context mapping:** satisfied by design — the design carries prefix-based, context-based, AND bare-query arms as paired conditions of the same battery (three-arm superset of the both-arms rule).
