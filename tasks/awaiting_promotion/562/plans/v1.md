## 0.0 TL;DR (plain English)

- **What I'll run:** The last experiment showed that a faint leftover of an erased marker habit shrinks whenever the model is handed a persona prompt ("you are a doctor", "you are a police officer"). But every prompt we tested gave the model a character to play, so we can't tell whether the character matters or whether any change to the system prompt does the same thing. I'll re-probe the same 12 fine-tuned models with three new system prompts: a bare instruction with no character at all, a nurse, and a comedian, and read the same marker statistics.
- **What I expect:** I'm betting the bare instruction shrinks the leftover too. The shrinkage comes from moving the model away from its trained "plain assistant" setting, not from role-play itself.
- **What would change my mind:** If the bare instruction leaves the leftover untouched while the nurse and the comedian both shrink it, then role-play framing is what matters. Separately, if the nurse shrinks it much more than the comedian, the medical flavor of the fine-tuning data is back in play as part of the story.

## 3. Hypothesis

**Primary bet (the task body's):** any non-default system prompt shrinks the elevation, mostly via the base-prior rise — the dampening is context-anchoring, not persona semantics. This is what the parent's finding-2 mechanism (EOS strengthening + base-prior rise under role prompts) predicts.

Predicted signatures on the per-adapter paired ΔEOS-margin vs the within-run trigger re-read ("dip"/"no-dip" per §7):

| Account | Bare instruction | Nurse | Comedian | Doctor re-read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(A) Context-general** (any non-default prompt) | dip | dip | dip | dip |
| **(B) Persona-framing-specific** | no dip | dip | dip | dip |
| Degenerate middle (generic context shift is real but weaker) | graded | dip | dip | dip |

- **Confirmation of (A):** the bare-instruction cell classifies **dip** under §7 (D̄ ≤ T_dip, ≥10/12 negative, log-prob concordant). The headline then generalizes from "persona prompts" to "any non-default context"; erasure audits must probe at the exact trained context, full stop.
- **Falsification of (A) (the task body's criterion):** the bare-instruction cell classifies **no-dip** — clusters with the +0.84 no-dip anchor (D̄ > −1.0 AND ≤7/12 negative) — while nurse AND comedian classify dip. Persona framing specifically is required; the "any role prompt" confound is dead.
- **Graded middle:** bare instruction lands between T_dip and −1.0 (or sign-consistent above −1.0) → a real-but-sub-persona generic-context component; report against both within-run anchors per §7 rule 3, bound the generic share as D̄_instruction / D̄_nurse-comedian-mean, do not force a clean label. Nothing is wasted — all cells reuse the validated rig.
- **Secondary discriminator (medical-domain additive component; pre-registered):** per-adapter paired difference nurse − comedian in EOS-margin space (12 paired diffs, cluster bootstrap). The medical account is resurrected as an ADDITIVE component only if the 95% CI on mean(nurse − comedian) excludes 0 AND mean ≤ −1.0 nat (nurse deeper by ≥1 nat) AND the same comparison is log-prob sign-concordant (≥9/12). Even then, confidence is capped at MODERATE: #558's non-medical spread (software engineer −2.34 to French person −5.35) shows single-persona idiosyncrasies of this magnitude exist, so a one-pair gap cannot carry a HIGH medical claim (the French-person precedent is the registered counterexample).
- **Mechanism read (pre-registered, secondary):** for every dipping cell, decompose trained-side movement vs base-side movement relative to the trigger re-read (`logp_trained_mean`, `logp_base_mean` per cell — all stored). The primary bet predicts the base-prior rise dominates for the bare-instruction cell exactly as it did for the persona cells.
- **Space rule (inherited, with ONE symmetric addition):** EOS margin is the SOLE classification space; log-prob is the concordance check (≥9/12 sign agreement AND |mean| ≥ 0.4 nats); a cell that dips on EOS-margin but fails concordance is **space-discordant dip** (LOW confidence) with its Δz_marker / Δz_eos decomposition reported. **Symmetrically** (registered for this run; the parent never exercised this branch because all four cells dipped in both spaces): a cell that satisfies the no-dip predicate on EOS margin but whose log-prob read is dip-concordant in the DIRECTIONAL sense (≥9/12 negative AND mean ≤ −0.4 nats) is **space-discordant no-dip** (LOW confidence, decomposition reported) — it does NOT support a clean account-(B) verdict. The no-dip branch carries the headline's falsification criterion, so a primary-space (log-prob, behavioral) dip cannot be silently overruled by the EOS-margin classification space.

## 7. Decision Gates

**No mid-run gates — short run (<1 h pod wall) over a twice-validated rig.** The only run gate is the launch-validity smoke gate: the first cell (`r50_seed42`, `--anchor-gate`) must land its doctor-cell `delta_logp_mean` within ±1.0 nat of #558's recorded 7.190 before the other 11 adapters launch. Grounding: the adapter-application assert of `marker-leakage-measurement.md` (~1 nat reproduce-the-known-read bound; an unapplied adapter reads ~7–8 nats off — incident #534) AND the parent's measured cross-session log-prob offset of +0.205 on this exact adapter/cell (so a true PASS comfortably fits, a false FAIL is implausible, and the EOS-margin false-FAIL mode that bit #558 is excluded by gating on log-prob only). One gate, sign-free tolerance band — trivially jointly satisfiable.

**Registered classification rule** (analysis-time; inherited from the parent's §7 with ONE registered addition — the symmetric no-dip discordance sub-rule in rule 4, ensemble-critique round 1 — applied per probe cell on the 12-adapter paired ΔEOS-margin D̄ with sign count n_neg/12; EOS margin sole classification space). **D_doc** = this run's within-run doctor re-read mean (expectation ≈ −2.6 to −2.7); **T_dip = min(0.6·D_doc, −1.0)** (expectation ≈ −1.6). Ordered, first match wins, jointly exhaustive:

1. **Dip:** D̄ ≤ T_dip AND n_neg ≥ 10/12; full confidence only with log-prob concordance (≥9/12 negative AND |mean Δlog-prob| ≥ 0.4 nats), else **space-discordant dip** (LOW, with Δz_marker/Δz_eos decomposition).
2. **Heterogeneous-unclassified:** D̄ ≤ T_dip AND n_neg < 10/12 — descriptive only; account table treats the cell as missing.
3. **Graded/partial:** (T_dip < D̄ ≤ −1.0) OR (D̄ > −1.0 AND n_neg ≥ 8/12) — reported with distances to both anchors (D_doc; no-dip anchor +0.84), nearest-account at LOW.
4. **No-dip:** D̄ > −1.0 AND n_neg ≤ 7/12 — clusters with the +0.84 no-dip anchor; this (not merely "mean > −1.0") is what account-(B) requires of the bare-instruction cell. **Symmetric discordance sub-rule (NEW, registered):** if the cell satisfies this EOS-margin no-dip predicate BUT the log-prob read is dip-concordant in the DIRECTIONAL sense (log-prob n_neg ≥ 9/12 AND log-prob mean ≤ −0.4 nats — directional, not the absolute-value concordance flag, since a positive-mean log-prob read SUPPORTS no-dip), the label is **`space-discordant-no-dip`** (LOW confidence, Δz_marker/Δz_eos decomposition reported). `rollup_issue562_panel.py::classify_cell` mirrors this; the account readout's clean-label set stays `{"dip", "no-dip"}`, so a `space-discordant-no-dip` cell routes to unresolved-degraded automatically and CANNOT certify a clean account-(B) ("persona framing required") verdict. Rationale: account (B) keys on exactly this branch, and the primary behavioral DV (log-prob, per the marker-measurement rule) must not be silently overruled by the EOS-margin classification space on the branch that decides the headline.

Doctor re-read failing to dip (D_doc > −1.0) = calibration failure: no classification issued; reported as a within-instrument non-replication (itself informative). Account assignment per the §3 signature table; any degraded cell label degrades the corresponding account claim.
